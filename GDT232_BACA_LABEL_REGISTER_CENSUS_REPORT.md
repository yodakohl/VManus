## Interpretation

The best current reading is architectural:

- `BACA-` is a candidate **graphical-label-register family**, especially in
  q13 and Pharma;
- the f82r `BACAB/BACACA` pair may additionally share a local array or
  apparatus-class template;
- the prefix is poor evidence for a word meaning water, flow, waterfall, pool,
  or a particular object.

The six prose and one radial occurrences are important counterexamples to a
universal label marker.  The f75v and f83r label occurrences also show that it
is not exclusive to the f82r paired structures.
