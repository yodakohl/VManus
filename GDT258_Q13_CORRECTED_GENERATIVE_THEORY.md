# GDT258 — corrected q13 generative theory

## Leading theory

The best current q13 generator is:

```text
page-level illustrated system
  -> local graphical labels through a section-specialized renderer
  -> an independent sequence of physical prose paragraphs
  -> each paragraph contains HPR2 fields separated by DY or line end
  -> a sparse minority of complete rendered groups can be reused across the
     graphical and prose channels
  -> content values remain unresolved and need not reside in PAGE_HOST alone
```

This is a hybrid **diagram legend plus paragraph-record** architecture.  It is
not a per-figure recipe list: f80r has 10 labels but five prose paragraphs,
while f82r has 13 labels but three paragraphs.  It is also not a demonstrated
label dictionary: only three of 23 aligned discovery-page label groups have an
exact all-reading prose counterpart, and the wider exact/PAGE_HOST key routes
are nonexceptional.

## Why this world leads

- Corrected coordinates recover five f80r and three f82r physical paragraphs.
- HPR2 fields remain reproducible after the old merged-record roles are
  removed: 43 covered fields on f80r and 51 on f82r.
- The `AQAB`/visible `okal-` family behaves as a graphical renderer with
  heterogeneous residuals, not a figure word.
- Exact `okaly`, `olky`, and `okal` groups demonstrate that graphical and prose
  channels are not disjoint.
- The external “clause/argument” projection is exactly a field-size threshold
  on all 94 corrected fields, so it contributes no semantic key.
- The best residual-position clue (`okal+AB` at slot 4/10 on two folios) is
  weak after full max-search calibration and reading-unstable at exact-member
  level.

## Competing worlds

1. **Independent formal renderers with no recoverable content** remains the
   mandatory null and is close to the leading world.
2. **Per-figure named records** is contradicted by label/paragraph count and
   ownership mismatch.
3. **A simple label-word dictionary** is contradicted by sparse exact reuse,
   broad PAGE_HOST recurrence, and failed folio transfer.

## Translation consequence

is independently known and whose full rendered tuple can be sought in a
corrected paragraph.  More PAGE_HOST role mining cannot supply it.  Until such
an endpoint exists, the content layer must remain latent.
