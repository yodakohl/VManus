# GDT002 exploratory branch contract

Branch: `yolo/gdt002-visual-grammar-constraints`

Base: GDT001 head commit `c7874a9f15b43e7e7af1fa67b769e23d6c341e37`

This branch is an exploratory visual-grammar role-constraint sandbox. Every
result is exploratory. Nothing here is a confirmed semantic role or
translation, and this branch must not be merged automatically.

## Isolation

- Never modify or merge canonical `VOYNICH_ACTIVE_STATE.md`,
  `experiments/semantic_assumptions/ACTIVE_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.tsv`, or
  `experiments/semantic_assumptions/CLOSED_ROUTE_FAMILIES.tsv` on this branch.
- Record GDT002 work only in `GDT002_YOLO_LEDGER.tsv` and GDT002 artifacts.
- Do not run OCR, CLIP, embeddings, automated image analysis, or batch visual
  classification. Direct native inspection is permitted by the active policy
  only when source-bound and explicitly marked `AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION`.
- Reuse existing human visual descriptions before making any new visual
  observation. Preserve raw source wording beside neutral geometry.
- Use f80r and f82r for discovery. Keep f84r formal correspondences sealed
  until the role schema, penalties, and discovery hypotheses are frozen.
- ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b remain alternate observations of one manuscript.
- Keep `OBSERVATION`, `FORMAL_STRUCTURE`, `SOFT_PRIOR`,
  `LATENT_ROLE_HYPOTHESIS`, and `INTERPRETATION` distinct.
- A future solver must score whole joint role worlds, pay for mappings and
  page exceptions, and compare with structure-matched permutations.

## First-checkpoint access rule

No image was opened for the first checkpoint. The visual inventory is sourced
only from cached human descriptions, with prior machine-authored visual
adjudications carried in a separate field. The mechanically generated f84r
formal projection is represented only by a hash commitment and row count.

## Independent theory generation

Codex may originate compact joint role worlds. It may not assign lexical
meanings or historical-language identities on the strength of visual
proximity. Weak observations may combine, but direct contradictions and every
page-specific exception must be explicit and costly.

## Claim ceiling

A positive discovery result could at most nominate anonymous roles such as
`PROCESS_STAGE`, `STATE_PROPERTY`, or `RELATION` for held visual transfer. It
does not establish a word, part of speech, sound, historical language,
plaintext, meaning, or translation.
