# GDT001 exploratory branch contract

Branch: `yolo/gdt001-global-decipherment`

Base: `origin/main` commit `f68381e519ee4f739f8003f1354d49691fab2db2`

This branch is an aggressive whole-manuscript model-discovery sandbox. Every
result is exploratory. Nothing here is a confirmed translation, and this
branch must not be merged automatically.

## Isolation

- Never modify or merge canonical `VOYNICH_ACTIVE_STATE.md`,
  `experiments/semantic_assumptions/ACTIVE_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.tsv`, or
  `experiments/semantic_assumptions/CLOSED_ROUTE_FAMILIES.tsv` on this branch.
- Record work only in `GDT001_YOLO_LEDGER.tsv` and GDT001 artifacts.
- Do not run image, OCR, CLIP, embedding, spectral, chemical, or
  palaeographic-acquisition work.
- The entire manuscript may be used for exploratory decoder discovery and
  postselection. This permission does not turn postselected output into
  confirmation.
- ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b remain alternate observations of one manuscript.
- No decoder may select the convenient edition without paying the common
  observation-lattice code cost.
- Every mapping, segmentation rule, exception, latent text/record, and model
  capability pays an explicit description-length cost.
- Failed runs remain in the branch-local ledger.
- Large checkpoints and downloaded language corpora stay under ignored local
  paths; only hashes, provenance, configs, compact decoders, and summaries are
  committed.

## Candidate language evidence

Only provenance-clean human-authored historical or near-historical corpora may
score language candidates. LLM-generated medieval-like text is forbidden.
Models may render decoded output but must not use an LLM to repair or beautify
it.

## Independent theory generation

The required ABBR_LANG, HOMOPHONIC_CIPHER, RECORD_NOTATION, and
NONSEMANTIC_GENERATOR families are a floor rather than an exhaustive theory
list. Codex may originate additional compact mechanistic systems when the
manuscript-wide regularities suggest them. A self-originated theory enters the
tournament only after it is expressed as an explicit segmentation, mapping,
grammar/transducer/generator, reverse probability, and common MDL account. A
verbal interpretation, attractive decoded fragment, or unpriced exception is
not a candidate decoder.

## Claim ceiling

A tournament winner is only the best complete exploratory generative account
under the shared GDT001 code. It becomes a confirmation candidate only after
an immutable decoder specification is exported and a separately approved
confirmation branch starts from `main`.
