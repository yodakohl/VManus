## What survives

The descriptive anomaly survives: among every f82r label window, the visually
attached f82r.10 component is uniquely aligned with the paragraph immediately
following it, and the fine member pattern is reading-robust. This remains a
useful local clue for generative theory.

What does not survive is inferential specificity. Paragraph-local clustering,
line length/opportunity, and physical order can produce equally strong minima
under rotations. Together with GDT261's independent-folio failure, the safest
theory is a page-local topic/morphotactic cluster rather than a general
graphical cross-reference key.

Do not mine another f82r cut, radius, or paragraph assignment. A future test
requires new source-bound attachment data and a frozen source-native query.
