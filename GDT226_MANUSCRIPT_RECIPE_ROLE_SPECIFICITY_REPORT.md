## What the result adds

GDT224's q13 effect is not merely the architecture of an arbitrary Herbal-B
page sample.  At the coarse resolution visible to a real-recipe-trained
instrument, q13 and Stars-B occupy the manuscript's most recipe-like
position/length regime.  This is positive evidence for a shared practical-
record compiler: both alternate long clause-sized fields with short
argument-sized fields far more like the readable recipe control than Herbal-A
does.

That is a document-level semantic prior, not a word reading.  It raises the
probability that q13 records organize procedures, parameters, materials, or
cases rather than continuous expository prose, but the present instrument
cannot choose among those possibilities.

## The essential confounds

All four Currier-B scopes rank ahead of both A scopes.  Register, hand,
section, content, and record length are therefore entangled.  Exact
record-field-count matching preserves a mean q13 advantage over Herbal-A
(`+.0996` JS), Herbal-B (`+.0436`), and Other-A (`+.1224`), but reverses it
against Stars-B (`-.0230`) and Other-B (`-.0116`).  Q13's global first-place
rank partly reflects its record-size mixture; it is not uniquely recipe-like
among comparable B records.

The classes are also deterministic functions of only four features: relative
field position, squared position, field span, and record field count.  Record
starts use editorial paragraph boundaries.  No surface string, PAGE_HOST,
wrapper, family, visual annotation, or semantic label contributes to the
score.  The published q13-versus-Herbal-B and Stars projections were already
known before this specificity synthesis.

## Consequence

The best current coarse interpretation is therefore:

> q13 and Stars-B share a practical-record-like clause/argument scaffold that
> differs strongly from Herbal-A, but the scaffold is compatible with a
> generic Currier-B record compiler and does not identify recipe content.

The next useful experiment should hold this scaffold fixed and ask where q13
and Stars-B differ within matched role/position cells.  A content-bearing
layer should diverge by register while compiler layers remain shared.  This is
more informative than another closure search or an isolated PAGE_HOST gloss.
