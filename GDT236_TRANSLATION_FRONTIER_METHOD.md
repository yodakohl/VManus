# GDT236 — translation-frontier architecture checkpoint

## Purpose

Consolidate the current public generator after the q13 lattice and transferred
label-prefix experiments.  This is an abductive theory checkpoint, not a new
semantic fit.  It corrects the obsolete semantic force of GDT181's f57/f77
local decoder using GDT182/GDT184/GDT185 and makes the next acquisition tests
explicit.

## Inputs

Only published result JSONs are read.  No transcription, image, f84 row, or
sealed payload is loaded.  The checkpoint binds GDT181, GDT182, GDT184, GDT185,
GDT227–230, and GDT233–235.

## Comparison

Three broad generators remain in the comparison:

1. compressed or abbreviated natural language alone;
2. semantic/technical notation alone;
3. a hybrid page-conditioned record compiler whose content channel may be
   lexical, abbreviated, code-like, or distributed.

The third is selected as the working generator because it covers the confirmed
record architecture and the historical abbreviation calibration without
pretending that an exact PAGE_HOST or label residual is a word.

## Promotion rule

A content unit may enter a provisional lexicon only after a source-bound
external referent recurs on at least two physical folios and a frozen
representation predicts it beyond section/register/layout nuisance.  Until
then it is rendered as an opaque address.  No prediction targets f84.
