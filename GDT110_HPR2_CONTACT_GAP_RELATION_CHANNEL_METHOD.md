# GDT110 — HPR2 contact/gap relation channel

## Question

Do the HPR2 compiler layers that looked relation/layout-associated in GDT103–
GDT104 predict the already acquired CONTACT versus CLEAR_GAP geometry?

This is exploratory reuse of exposed GDT002 observations, not a fresh visual
validation. The historical contact-capacity gates remain failed; one-sided
arrays are valid descriptive observations and are not deleted.

## Fixed panel

Use the 27 unique CONTACT/GAP loci in
`gdt002_exploratory_visual_formal_join.tsv`: 8 CONTACT, 18 CLEAR_GAP, and one
UNCERTAIN across five arrays and three physical folios. UNCERTAIN is missing in
the primary hard-state test and is assigned both ways as a sensitivity.

## HPR2 features

Apply the unchanged GDT109 extension of the GDT062 parser separately to ZL3b,
IT2a, and RF1b. A binary layer is present in the primary analysis when at least
two readings contain it. Test exactly:

- DY closure;
- explicit RIGHT_FAMILY;
- DY or RIGHT_FAMILY;
- B3;
- any stripped wrapper;
- O/OT frame.

B3 is the negative control. No feature is chosen after seeing its visual
effect. PAGE_HOST and raw-form character trigrams are additionally compared by
a five-neighbour whole-physical-folio-held predictor; edge-stripped PAGE_HOST
and compiler-active features complete the representation ablation.

## Exact control

For binary layer effects, permute CONTACT/GAP labels inside each array while
preserving every array's hard-state counts. The primary orbit has 2,520 worlds.
The statistic is the equal-array mean of feature prevalence among CONTACT
minus prevalence among CLEAR_GAP for arrays containing both states. Report
two-sided inclusive local and max-over-six probabilities, per-array effects,
and leave-one-array-out directions.

For predictive representations, use the same complete within-array orbit and
max over the four representations. All target predictions exclude the entire
physical folio from training. These are low-capacity diagnostics on three
folios; a positive cannot assign meaning.

## Claim ceiling and holdout

The experiment can support or weaken a transferable formal relation/layout
channel. It cannot establish contact semantics, a role, gloss, word, morpheme,
POS, sound, language, plaintext, meaning, or translation. f84r is excluded
before retention and is not opened, parsed, retained, queried, joined, scored,
or targeted.
