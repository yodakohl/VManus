# GDT237 — leave-one-section graphical-label prefix transfer

## Question

Does the partial GDT233 graphical-label prefix compiler generalize across
manuscript sections when its prefix inventory is rediscovered without the held
section?

## Frozen analysis

Use the first strict consensus-family group at every non-f84 locus.  In each of
the eight section folds, learn length-1–4 prefixes on the other seven sections
using the unchanged GDT233 rule: support at least five, at least four editorial
`L` rows, label rate at least `.50`, and one-sided hypergeometric `p <= .01`.
The union predicts editorial label kind on the unseen section.  Report every
fold, including sections with no labels or no predicted positives.

This is a formal-register test.  Editorial `L` is not authorial ownership or a
semantic category, and a prefix is not a word or morpheme.
