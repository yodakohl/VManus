# GDT225 — distributed closure is a partial, layout-driven rescue

## Result

**Status: `DISTRIBUTED_CLOSURE_PARTIAL_OR_GENERIC`.**

The two mechanisms frozen after GDT224—final-line B3 and a human-annotated
label block following the prose record in source order—partly reduce the q13
closer deficit, but do not recover a stable relocated closer.

| quantity | q13 | Herbal-B control |
|---|---:|---:|
| records | 33 | 22 |
| final closer-like field | 17 | 17 |
| final-line B3 | 2 | 2 |
| following label block | 9 | 0 |
| either distributed proxy | 11 | 2 |
| field closer or distributed proxy | 23 | 18 |

Among records missing a final closer-like field, the proxy rate is 6/16
(`0.375`) in q13 and 1/5 (`0.200`) in Herbal-B, a difference of `+0.175`
(one-sided Fisher `p=.4427`).  Within q13, the proxy is slightly more frequent
when the field closer is missing than when it is already present (`.375`
versus `.2941`, effect `+.0809`, one-sided Fisher `p=.4507`).  These are the
two frozen directions, but neither is individually strong.

Counting a field closer or either proxy as an expanded closure reduces the
pooled q13-versus-Herbal deficit from `.2576` to `.1212`, a `52.94%`
reduction.  That crosses the frozen record-weighted half-reduction threshold.
After weighting physical folios equally, however, the deficit falls from
`.3013` to `.1532`, only `49.17%`; the threshold is missed.  All three frozen
directions survive only 4 of 9 q13 leave-one-folio deletions, versus the
required 8.

## Why this is not a recovered closer

The apparent rescue is mostly a q13-specific source-layout fact: nine q13
records are followed by labels, while no Herbal control record is.  But the
label block is not selectively placed after records missing a closer.  It
follows 4/16 missing-closer records and 5/17 records that already have a
closer-like final field.  B3 has only two examples in each scope and cannot
explain the deficit by itself.

Moreover, source order is not spatial adjacency or authorial ownership.  A
label with a later locus number may annotate a figure or component and need
not close, index, or refer to the prose record before it.  GDT225 therefore
finds a real layout asymmetry and an arithmetic partial rescue, not a stable
distributed record-closure rule.

## Consequence

GDT224's weak recipe-like aggregate clause/argument balance survives, while
its missing closer should no longer be pursued through additional generic
boundary channels on this exposed panel.  The next useful route must test a
different externally grounded prediction—preferably the organization of the
clause-like and short-argument-like material—rather than selecting another
post-record marker.

This experiment assigns no label key, index, word, semantic value, language,
plaintext, or translation.  No f84 row was retained, joined, or scored.
