This is an explicitly post-result decomposition of the exposed GDT140 positive.
It asks whether that exact-assignment result is a single common-token artifact
or a distributed PAGE_HOST profile.  It cannot validate GDT140.

On the same fixed ten pages and 120 assignments, score exact PAGE_HOST bags in
six fully logged variants: all hosts; length at least 2, 3, or 4; and removal
of the 5 or 10 most frequent PAGE_HOSTs in the complete f84-free GDT062 source
inventory.  Weighted Jaccard and the original assignment direction are fixed.
Use a standardized maximum across all six variants.

Separately score every exact host observed in the ten-page panel by its summed
minimum-count overlap across the five pairs.  Enumerate all 120 assignments and
report local inclusive and max-host probabilities.  A host supported by only
one pair necessarily has weak assignment capacity and must not receive a
meaning.

The result localizes a mechanism only.  It assigns no botanical truth, plant,
component, semantic role, gloss, word, morpheme, POS, sound, language,
plaintext, meaning, or translation.  Reject all f84 rows before retention.
