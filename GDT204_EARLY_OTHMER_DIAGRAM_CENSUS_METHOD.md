# GDT204 — early Othmer labelled-diagram census

## Purpose

Screen the complete institutional catalogue descriptions for Othmer MS 1--7
before using any named medieval operation as a Voynich hypothesis.  These
seven manuscripts span practical alchemy, medicine, alchemical theory, and
Lullian figures from the fifteenth century or its immediate boundary.

The screen uses catalogue metadata only.  It does not inspect or transcribe
manuscript images.  Every diagrammatic record with a readable catalogue value
is retained, including negative topologies and later additions.

## Exact-homolog gate

An exact f77 homolog requires one catalogue record to document six ordered
states, five adjacent relations, four active outputs plus one inactive/hold,
and readable labels assigned to the relevant states or relations.  A group of
three operations, a list of elements, a furnace diagram, or a Lullian circle
does not satisfy that conjunction.

This source census supplies historical priors only.  No external Latin or
Italian label may be assigned to a Voynich group without the exact topology
and a separately frozen correspondence rule.  No Voynich transcription,
image, or f84r material is read.
