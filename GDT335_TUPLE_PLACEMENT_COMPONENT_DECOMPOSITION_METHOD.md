# GDT335 — tuple placement component decomposition

GDT335 freezes every GDT334 outer fold and its training-selected shrinkage
concentration.  It reruns no model selection.  The held tuple-versus-coordinate
gain is decomposed additively into the four original targets: physical line
entry, within-field position, field ordinal 1/2/3/4+, and physical line
quartile.  Scores are reported globally and by register.

The purpose is causal interpretation of the formal signal: distinguish local
line placement from a reusable record-field slot.  No new tuple, representation,
threshold, or target is selected.  No semantics or f84 data are used.
