# GDT210 source audit

## Institutional identification

The Morgan Library identifies MS G.74 as an Italian copy of Peter of Eboli's
*De balneis Puteolanis*, ca. 1400. Its page-level iconographic catalogue gives
readable bath identities and describes the depicted figures, pools, buildings,
and water flow. This supplies an external semantic ground truth absent from
Voynich.

The Biblioteca Angelica independently describes the work as a therapeutic
treatise/poem on the baths of Pozzuoli and Baia. Its witness has eighteen
epigrams paired with eighteen full-page miniatures. The Codex4D scholarly
record reports a wider tradition of twenty-eight manuscripts, thirteen
illustrated. These contextual records establish that illustrated bath-by-bath
organization is a real medieval document practice rather than a single
attractive picture.

## Four inspected Morgan pages

- **fol. 9r:** the catalogue identifies a domed bathhouse, bathing figures,
  an exterior fountain, and water drawn from a well. Direct inspection shows
  the arched building, a group in water, and a colored stream outside it.
- **fol. 19r:** the catalogue identifies nude female bathers under a cupola and
  water running beneath the structure. Direct inspection shows a shared green
  pool inside a domed architectural frame and a water band below it.
- **fol. 21r:** the catalogue identifies a three-level domed bathhouse,
  figures on successive levels, a central fountain descending through the
  levels, and two lower arcaded pools with bathing groups. Direct inspection
  confirms the multi-tier figure array, central vertical watercourse, and two
  separated lower pools.
- **fol. 25r:** the catalogue identifies nude male figures bathing in an
  arcaded pool in a domed structure before a cave. Direct inspection confirms
  the shared pool and architectural/cave enclosure.

## Provenance discipline

The images and catalogue descriptions are one institutional witness, not four
independent manuscripts. The Angelica description is an independent witness
to the work's illustrated bath-by-bath organization, not to the exact Morgan
miniatures. No Voynich text was used to select a page and no f84 material was
accessed.
