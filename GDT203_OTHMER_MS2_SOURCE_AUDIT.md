# GDT203 — Othmer MS 2 source audit

The University of Pennsylvania's OPenn catalogue identifies Othmer MS 2 as a
northern Italian alchemical miscellany dated 1450–1475.  Its folio 41r is a
full-page drawing of two named tools: `Sublimatoria vasa` and
`furnus sublimatorum`.  The Science History Institute independently describes
the codex as a practical/theoretical alchemical miscellany containing recipes
for subliming mercury and making alcohol.

This is useful positive context: a near-contemporary technical manuscript can
place readable apparatus categories in a full-page drawing beside practical
recipes.  It is not the needed f77 key.  The authoritative description has two
tools, not six ordered states, and documents no five-relation sequence or
four-output/one-hold pattern.

The Latin labels are recorded as external catalogue values only.  They are not
assigned to a Voynich group, image component, or process stage.
