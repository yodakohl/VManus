# GDT021 visual-anchor / record-phase atlas

GDT021 combines two previously separate weak evidence layers: GDT013's
visual-role-derived formal anchor sets and GDT016/GDT020's prose record-state
compiler.  It asks whether groups reached by a visual anchor occupy distinctive
prose field contexts.

The inputs are frozen and contain no f84r row.  The 2,860 GDT013 propagation
rows are canonicalized to unique `(role hypothesis, locus, group)` entries.
The comparison universe is the union of all propagated groups.  Role names are
provenance labels inherited from human visual annotations; they are not
meanings assigned to the prose occurrences.

For each of eight role-anchor sets, four context outcomes are tested:
after any earlier DY, immediately after DY, inside a field that closes with DY,
and line-final.  Tests are repeated for the union, SOURCE_FAMILY-only, and
RESIDUAL_HOST-only anchors: 96 cells total.

Every exact conditional test fixes page, compiled record state, normalized
position quartile, number of role-anchor members, and number of outcomes.
Hypergeometric stratum distributions are convolved exactly.  Leave-one-
physical-folio effects quantify concentration.  A 96-cell Bonferroni value is
reported; controls rank discovery leads and do not automatically stop them.

Claim ceiling: exploratory alignment of a visual-derived formal anchor family
with a prose field context.  No semantic role, object name, morpheme, word,
syntax, sound, language, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
