# GDT177 report — the recipe-role projection does not validate inside Q20

## Outcome

**POSITION/LENGTH ANALOGY ONLY; NO INDEPENDENT Q20 ROLE SUPPORT.**

GDT176's external calibration remains valid on readable medieval recipes. What
fails here is the semantic transfer to Q20. None of four frozen tests finds the
predicted source-native behavior. Therefore `INSTRUCTION_CLAUSE_LIKE`,
`SHORT_ARGUMENT_LIKE`, and `RECORD_CLOSER_LIKE` must remain descriptive
position/extent analogies, not provisional Voynich roles.

## Decisive held-folio host test

The exact-host update covers 1,038/1,444 supported ZL field events (71.9%). It
worsens log loss from 375.98 to 1,071.88 bits, a **−695.90-bit** gain, and drops
top-class accuracy from 1,444 to 1,160. Every one of eight held physical folios
is negative. Exact PAGE_HOST identity therefore does not preserve even the
coarse GDT176 placement class when it is forced to generalize across folios.

This also demotes the GDT176 `che`, `oke`, `okche`, `opche`, and `chey` atlas
entries. Their apparent class purity is a property of the positions and field
sizes where they occur, not evidence that they name an argument or operation.

## What remains useful

Q20 plainly has a record architecture, and readable recipes plainly have a
strong position/extent role architecture. GDT176 showed that the two can be
made to look alike at low resolution. GDT177 shows that this analogy does not
survive independent source-native predictions. It is therefore not a bridge
to translation.

The next translation-directed route should not refine these projected roles.
It must add an independently observed content class—preferably a repeated
diagram object/state or a known astronomical/medical table row—before querying
Voynich identities. Without that external endpoint, field-length semantics are
not identifiable.

## Claim ceiling

GDT177 rejects independent support for this particular Q20 recipe-role
projection. It does not reject Q20 record structure, medieval recipe influence,
or all notation/language hybrids. It establishes no word, role, language,
plaintext, or translation. f84r was not accessed.
