# GDT319 — fresh-surface `t` line-entry transfer

GDT318 exposed `t` as the strongest secondary positive physical-line-entry
coefficient. Test that post-selected lead prospectively by excluding every
exact source surface present anywhere in the GDT318 panel. On the remaining
corpus, freeze each opaque
`PAGE_HOST × frame × inner-D × right × DY × B3` cell having at least two `t`
and two non-`t` events, each class on at least two physical folios.

The frozen panel contains seven cells, 50 events, 20 `t` choices, and 31
folios. In leave-one-physical-folio-out folds compare ridge-10 exact-cell
priors against exact cell plus physical-line-start. Report held log loss,
cell/register-matched direction, coefficient direction, section contributions,
and an 8,192-world fixed-crossfit cell/register alignment diagnostic. The
diagnostic is not an exact retrained null.

Call `T_LINE_ENTRY_EXTENDS_TO_FRESH_SURFACES` only if held gain and matched
delta are positive, at least 24/31 fold coefficients are positive, at least
two powered B/H/S sections contribute positive gain, and diagnostic p is at
most .05. A failure rejects only the transferable secondary `t` line-entry
rule.

At most this establishes a stochastic `t` line-entry tendency after an opaque
cell is already known. It predicts no new license and assigns no prefix,
morpheme, POS, meaning, sound, language, plaintext, or translation. No f84 row
may be opened, parsed, retained, joined, or scored.
