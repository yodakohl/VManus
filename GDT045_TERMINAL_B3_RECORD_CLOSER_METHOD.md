# GDT045 — source-native B3 terminal record-closer transfer

## Question

GDT039 and GDT044 showed that residual hosts ending in display `m` are often
physical line-final in Herbal-B and Stars/Recipe. This pass tests whether that
positional function transfers to unseen registers and whether it is specific
among supported source-native final member classes.

## Source-native unit and holdout

The source is the f84r-free, all-reading-agreeing GDT016 group inventory,
restricted to physical lines whose complete group sequence is retained. Each
group is joined to the source-native family consensus only by its exact
locus/group key. f84r rows are skipped before formal parsing, never retained.

On all 213 complete-line residual-`m` groups, the final STA member is exactly
`B3` in ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b. The source-native `B3` class is therefore the
primary feature. HB+S/B are the already exposed discovery registers. Herbal-A,
other Currier-B, and other Currier-A are the transfer set.

Within every physical line, exact tests fix the number of B3 groups and the
one physical endpoint. Register-specific and pooled transfer effects are
reported with whole-folio deletion. A max-search atlas repeats the pooled test
for every transcription-stable final STA member with at least 20 occurrences;
Bonferroni correction covers that complete atlas.

## Claim ceiling

A positive result supports a **formal record-closing marker class**: B3 makes
a group much more likely to close a physical line. It does not imply that B3
is punctuation, a word, morpheme, sound, number, language feature, plaintext,
or translation. Precision and recall are reported because the marker need not
close every record and many records end without it.
