# GDT209 — readable quintessence-apparatus topology audit

## Question

Does direct inspection of institutional manuscript images from the
Rupescissa/Pseudo-Lull quintessence tradition support a specific visual homolog
for the frozen, non-f84 q13 apparatus inventory, or only the broad historical
plausibility of circulation and distillation imagery?

This is an external iconographic specificity test for the exploratory GDT208
content theory. It is not a word-decoder, source-identification, or translation
experiment.

## Frozen inputs

- the five-row, f84-free q13 visual inventory published by GDT208;
- the official HAB image of Cod. Guelf. 23.19 Aug. 4°, fol. 40r;
- the official OPenn images of Othmer MS 7, fols. 10r and 36r;
- institutional catalogue metadata and the 2021 academic study used in GDT208.

The external images were inspected directly. No OCR, transcription, automated
vision, embedding, segmentation, object detection, or image-similarity model
was used. Every new visible claim is marked `AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION`.
Interpretive source statements remain separate.

## Test

The comparison uses eleven predeclared topology axes: multiple containers,
paired containers, a closed return path, a top/bottom connection, side ducts,
figures inside the system, repeated figure stages, pools, color-coded material,
singular owned labels, and the exact six-state/five-edge/four-output-one-hold
f77 topology. Broad apparatus similarities and missing specific features are
both retained.

## Decision rule

- `SPECIFIC_DQE_ICONOGRAPHIC_HOMOLOG_SUPPORTED` requires a source-bound image
  with figures embedded in a connected pool/duct system and a repeated-stage
  topology comparable to q13.
- `BROAD_ALCHEMICAL_APPARATUS_ECOLOGY_SUPPORTED_SPECIFIC_HOMOLOG_ABSENT`
  applies when circulation/vessel features recur but the q13-specific figure,
  pool, stage, and f77 structures do not.

No visual match can assign a value to a Voynich source group without a
separately owned readable-label bridge.

## Claim ceiling

At most this experiment can raise or lower the historical plausibility and
specificity of the GDT208 medicinal-alchemical apparatus theory. It cannot
establish a source manuscript, language, sign value, word, operation,
material, plaintext, or translation. No f84 page is an input.
