## External evidence

The source freeze uses four provenance-bound records:

1. Wellcome MS.708 (Regensburg, 1443), whose institutional record identifies
   John of Rupescissa's *De consideratione quintae essentiae* as medicinal
   extraction by distillation from wine, plants, and minerals.
2. An academic primary-manuscript study of a 1451 illustrated copy, including
   its transcription/translation of a vessel in which material repeatedly
   ascends and descends through side pipes until quintessence separates from
   the four corruptible elements.
3. The British Library catalogue for Sloane MS 2560 (Germany or Austria,
   1450--1499), an illustrated *Donum Dei* cycle whose eleven painted
   miniatures include chemical union of a red king and white queen in a flask,
   black/white flasks, worms, a man, a flower, and red/white rose figures.
4. The exact BL-derived public-domain image of Sloane 2560 f7, inspected
   directly without OCR or automated vision.
