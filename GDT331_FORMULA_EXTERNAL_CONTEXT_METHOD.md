# GDT331 — repeated-formula external record context

GDT331 asks whether any of the 44 recurrent GDT328 field formulas carries a
stable surrounding physical-line template.  It uses no glyph similarity and
no same-field feature other than the formula identity used to define cases.

For each occurrence, the external context is:

1. previous-field group-count bin (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4+`, or absent),
2. previous-field closure (`DY`, `B3`, `OPEN`, or absent),
3. next-field group-count bin,
4. next-field closure, and
5. total fields-on-line bin.

The raw statistic is component agreement averaged across every pair of
occurrences.  In 8,192 deterministic worlds, occurrence labels are permuted
among fields with the same register, exact formula-field group count, and
exact field ordinal.  This conditions out the GDT329 position effect.  Each
candidate is standardized by its own null mean and standard deviation; the
primary diagnostic maximizes z over all 44 formulas in every world.

The panel is exposed and the analysis is exploratory.  It can distinguish a
portable formula from a stable surrounding record template, but it cannot
identify content, a semantic slot, language, plaintext, or translation.  No
f84 row is used.
