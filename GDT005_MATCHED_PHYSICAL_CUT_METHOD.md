# GDT005 matched physical-cut method

GDT005 is an exploratory, postselected control for GDT004. For each of the nine
GDT004 exact folio-held completions, choose one ordinary source group on the
same physical line by the frozen tuple:

1. smallest absolute nearest-basic surface-length difference;
2. smallest source-group-index distance;
3. smallest source-group index.

The target cut after a prepended `q` is compared with a pseudo-cut after the
first displayed character in the control. A target right-edge cut before
`dy`/`dar` is compared with a pseudo-cut the same number of displayed
characters from the control's right edge. These are display-coordinate probes,
not asserted grapheme boundaries; EVA multicharacter conventions make a finer
stroke-level interpretation unsafe without a separately marked blind packet.

The direct native inspection rubric is deliberately coarse:
`DISTINCT_PHYSICAL_SEPARATOR`, `NO_DISTINCT_PHYSICAL_SEPARATOR`, or
`UNRESOLVED`. A distinct separator must be word-sized or delimiter-like;
ordinary inter-sign spacing does not qualify. All observations are
`AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION` and reuse the exact official full-image hashes
and line contexts already bound by GDT004. No OCR or automated vision is used.

The nine targets are successful GDT003 fourth cells. Failed/absent fourth cells
have no physical ink object and cannot enter an image comparison; they remain
formal negatives in GDT003. The matched physical controls are observed ordinary
groups, not invented failed forms.

This is a postselected, source-aware single-review discovery pass. It can show
only whether coarse visible separators discriminate proposed cuts from matched
pseudo-cuts. It cannot establish microspacing, pen trajectory, grapheme
boundaries, morphemes, linguistic slots, language, meaning, or translation.
The f84r holdout remains sealed.
