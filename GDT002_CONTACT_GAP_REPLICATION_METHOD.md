# GDT002 complete-array contact/gap replication

Status: **REGISTERED BEFORE NEW TARGET-STATE REVIEW**.

This exploratory experiment supersedes, but does not erase, the failed f88/f102
extension. It freezes four source-count-matched candidate arrays on two physical
folios, pending exact native-image census:

- discovery physical folio f100: f100r/L2 (`f100r.6-.11`, six labels) and
  f100v/L1 (`f100v.1-.4`, four labels);
- transfer physical folio f99: f99v/L1 (`f99v.2-.9`, eight labels) and f99v/L2
  (`f99v.15-.20`, six labels).

Every source-assigned locus in every candidate array is included. Rows were
selected from source-stated counts, not a known CONTACT/GAP outcome. Nine calls from f99v/L2
and f100r/L2 already have frozen prior judgments; those records are retained
unchanged and rebound to new opaque IDs. The remaining fifteen calls are new.
Neither physical folio is observer-pristine, so a pass is exploratory transfer,
not confirmation. Before this registration, f99v/f100r full pages and unit
regions, plus the completion target regions f99v.16, f99v.19, and f100r.6, were
opened for localization without CONTACT/GAP judgment. f100v also has prior
repository visual exposure. No new target-state review occurred before this
freeze.

## Census and review

Before new state review, a source-aware localizer must confirm for each array
that its frozen locus set exhausts the visible source-described unit. Allowed
states are `EXACT_LOCUS_SET_EXHAUSTS_VISIBLE_ANNOTATED_UNIT`,
`EXTRA_UNMAPPED_INSCRIPTION`, and `UNCERTAIN`. Any non-exact state stops the
experiment before new review.

The fifteen new targets are randomized and sent independently to two crop-only
reviewers. Each sees only a marked context/target crop and the frozen states
`CONTACT`, `CLEAR_GAP`, `UNCERTAIN`. They receive no folio, locus, array,
transcription, source comment, formal feature, object name, or prior outcome.
Disagreement becomes `UNCERTAIN`; no text/full-page adjudication is allowed.
The nine inherited calls are not re-reviewed or altered.

No OCR, automated segmentation, object detection, embedding, classifier, or
other automated vision is allowed. New calls are
`AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION`.

## Visual gates

Each complete array independently needs at least one `CONTACT`, at least two
`CLEAR_GAP`, and zero `UNCERTAIN`. Both f100 arrays must pass before discovery
formal fitting; both f99 arrays must pass before transfer scoring. Any failure
stops before formal data are joined.

## Frozen formal channel

Only anonymous states `R0/R1` may be modeled. The formal channel is source-
native: symbol count and group count are nuisance/conditioning variables;
candidate signal is an unordered STA-family histogram plus synchronized
boundary-count/type profile. Exact ordered family sequences, members, roots,
literal surfaces, locus/page identity, catalogue class, and legacy
carrier/parser semantics are forbidden. ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b remain alternate
readings of one manuscript; alternative-bearing f100r.6 must be marginalized
with its frozen observation cost.

Fit state-conditioned Dirichlet-multinomial construction profiles on both f100
arrays and compare to an equally informed state-blind model. Score f99 without
refitting. The exact null enumerates within-array state permutations preserving
each array's observed counts. Require positive held codelength gain separately
in f99v/L1 and f99v/L2, positive pooled f99 gain, joint exact p <= 0.05, and
survival under a length/group-count matched null. Model/smoothing/selector costs
must be explicit. A later solver may run only after all visual gates pass.

## Claim ceiling

Even a pass supports only an anonymous physical-contact/source-native-
construction constraint across these two previously exposed physical folios.
It does not establish ownership, object type, semantic role, lexeme, POS,
sound, language, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
