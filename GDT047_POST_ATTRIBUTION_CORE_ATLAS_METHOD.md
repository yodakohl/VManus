# GDT047 — post-attribution residual core atlas

## Purpose

Re-rank exact Herbal-B↔Stars residual hosts only after subtracting the formal
systems recovered in GDT039–GDT045. The atlas is meant to identify the next
content-host candidate, not to re-promote closers or wrapper stacks.

The source is the f84r-free, all-reading-agreeing GDT016 group inventory.
Before ranking, this pass excludes groups with right-edge DY, residual hosts
ending in terminal M/B3, and carrier+D double stacks. Exact residual hosts must
occur at least three times and on two physical folios in both HB and S/B.
Rates are compared with Herbal-A and other Currier-B, with every target folio
deleted in turn.

The atlas joins the already published, f84r-free GDT012 human-annotation
inventory by exact residual host. Label hits are descriptive grounding
capacity only; object-class diversity is a counterexample to a unique
referent. No new visual observation is created.

Known attributions are carried forward: OD+AIN/AIIN short/long selection,
anonymous OT/OL/AL/AR states, and generic Currier-B prevalence. A residual host
with no annotated label occurrence is explicitly `UNGROUNDED`, not assigned a
meaning.
