# GDT115 — Q20 record-template channel decomposition

Status: `POST_GDT114_EXPLORATORY_DECOMPOSITION`

GDT114 found that the complete HPR2 profile of an OPEN predicts its unseen-
folio BODY template above raw/host character-trigram controls under an exact-
OPEN-length within-folio null.  GDT115 asks two harder questions:

1. does the result survive swaps restricted to the **same page** and exact
   OPEN member count; and
2. which anonymous HPR2 channel carries the transferable relation?

The 170-record/eight-folio panel, nested ridge fitting, three alternate-reading
sensitivities, nuisance variables, and leave-one-record-out other-BODY profile
are unchanged.  No coefficient is trained on its held physical folio.

The five GDT114 models receive a stricter 4,096-world same-page exact-length
null.  Thirteen fixed input→BODY block models are then scored:

- WRAPPER, FRAME and RENDERER crossed with BODY WRAPPER, FRAME and RENDERER;
- complete COMPILER → BODY COMPILER and BODY EDGE;
- complete EDGE → BODY COMPILER and BODY EDGE.

`RENDERER` is the anonymous vector RIGHT_PRESENT/DY/B3.  `EDGE` is the
PAGE_HOST final-character distribution plus host length/diversity.  These are
formal coordinates, not linguistic or semantic categories.

Each block model receives local and max-13 p-values under both within-folio and
within-page exact-length permutations.  The page null has less capacity but
removes recto/verso and page-ecology swaps.  All 13 tried channels are retained.

f84r is rejected before any formal row is retained.  It is not opened,
retained, queried, joined, scored, targeted, or predicted.  No recipe, heading,
semantic role, word, morpheme, POS, sound, language, plaintext, meaning, or
translation is assigned.
