# GDT181 — leading hybrid technical-compiler theory

Status: **LEADING_HYBRID_TECHNICAL_COMPILER_WITH_LOCAL_F57_F77_STATE_DECODING**.

## Best current overall theory

The strongest single generative account is a **page-conditioned hybrid
technical compiler**.  Voynich visible groups are not best treated as ordinary
indivisible words.  They are generated from at least three interacting layers:

1. a page/register-conditioned address or content layer (`PAGE_HOST`);
2. a record compiler that selects entry wrappers, local frames, right-edge
   renderers, checkpoints, and closers;
3. reduced local diagram codes when a page supplies a finite technical state
   system.

The hidden content under `PAGE_HOST` may include abbreviated natural-language
lexemes, technical identifiers, or both.  The current evidence does not
separate those possibilities.  What it does separate is the **observable
surface machinery**: ordinary historical abbreviation alone does not reproduce
the full Voynich algebra/record profile, while pure independent codewords do
not explain the internal structure and host-licensed rendering.  The hybrid is
therefore the leading theory, not a compromise among equal alternatives.

The transparent ten-axis abductive totals are:

| theory | score / 20 | reading |
|---|---:|---|
| compressed/abbreviated natural language | 9 | explains real abbreviation-like families and register variation, but not the local state codes or broad compiler residual |

| **hybrid technical compiler** | **20** | explains both the abbreviation-like substrate and the added record/diagram compiler |

These are exposed theory grades, not likelihoods or posterior probabilities.

The record grammar explains line reset, Currier/register differences, dense

whole strings remain so predictable.  `DY_CLASS` is an internal
checkpoint/field edge, while `B3_CLASS` is a probabilistic physical-record
closer.  Neither is translated as a word or punctuation mark.

`PAGE_HOST` is the best current location for an address/content contribution,
but it is not a recovered dictionary stem.  Exact identity matters strongly;
one-glyph neighbors also have structure; and the complete external-referent
atlas still fails.  The correct display for an unresolved item is therefore an
opaque address, not an English guess.

## What the theory explains jointly

- **Free/bound reuse and split/join forms:** a compiled field can realize the
  same address/state material across one or several manual groups without
  requiring modern word boundaries.
- **Right-edge `DY`:** a frequent field-edge realization, locally usable as a
  state coordinate on f57/f77, but not a universal suffix meaning.
- **Productive `q+X`:** a host-licensed left routing operation whose local
  string productivity need not be a spoken prefix.
- **Extreme transformation compatibility:** many page/register-conditioned
  address families share compiler realizations, creating dense grids; the
  system is distributed rather than a small clean morpheme table.
- **Line reset and record structure:** physical lines are compilation units;
  `DY` can chain fields and B3-like forms bias record closure.
- **Repeated identical labels such as `daldy`:** identity can mean repeated
  address/state/formula rather than a repeated ordinary lexical word.
- **Currier and register effects:** shared address families are rendered by
  different wrapper/right-edge ecologies.
- **Failures of simple language and cipher mappings:** the visible string
  mixes address content with compiler state and therefore is not a direct
  phonetic or substitution-cipher layer.
- **Failure of broad semantic host transfer:** address values may be local,
  sparse, or coupled to a schema; exact PAGE_HOST is not licensed as a global
  lexicon.

## What remains awkward

The two semantic scaffolds are exposed, post-hoc, and related through earlier
f57/f77 work rather than independent replication.  f57 ownership is
proximity-only.  A cached f77 puff order contradicts the proposed transition
order at all four positions.  The f67v1 universal-output transfer failed.  No
Q20 role, plant name, star state, source language, sound system, number system,
or plaintext clause has survived a transferable test.

The broad operation algebra also remains a warning.  GDT003 finds genuine
hidden completions, but absolute precision is tiny and the named `q` plus
right-edge subgroup loses to string statistics.  GDT160's incidence excess is
real yet nonunique, and GDT161 rejects a small stable operation-class system.
The generator must therefore remain host-licensed and distributed rather than
pretending to be a neat list of affixes.

## Conclusion

The best current theory is not “Voynich is random,” “Voynich is a simple
cipher,” or “every group is a word.”  It is a hybrid technical inscription
system in which a page-conditioned content/address inventory is compiled into
record fields and, on some diagrams, combined with compact state coordinates.

That theory has yielded the first executable local reading: f57/f77 form a
provisional four-quality state system and a six-stage transition process.  It
has **not** yielded a complete translation, a confirmed word, or a language.
The useful advance is sharper: we now know what can be translated locally,
what must remain an opaque address, and what kind of new evidence could turn
the compiler into plaintext.
