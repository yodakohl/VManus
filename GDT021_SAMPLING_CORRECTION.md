# GDT021 sampling correction

GDT021 is retained as an audit artifact but its quantitative inference is
superseded.  Its input `gdt013_prose_anchor_occurrences.tsv` is capped by the
GDT013 producer at `matches[:40]` for each anchor feature.  It is a compact
display sample, not the complete population of matching prose groups.

Consequently, GDT021's reported effect, exact p-value, multiple-test value,
and leave-one-folio stability must not be cited as full-corpus evidence.
GDT022 re-evaluates the identical frozen anchor list against every eligible
group in the f84r-free GDT016 inventory.  This correction changes no GDT013,
GDT016, or GDT021 bytes and preserves the historical audit trail.
