# GDT027 Q/L history-bit portability across right-edge states

GDT027 asks whether the Currier-B Q/L backward-link contrast found among DY
closures also appears when the same family motifs occur with AL, AR, ED, OL,
or other non-DY right states.

The frozen universe contains all QJB/QKB/LJB/LKB groups in the f84r-free
GDT016 inventory.  For DY and non-DY partitions, and for each sufficiently
represented anonymous state, it reports the raw Q/L by previous-DY table,
two-sided Fisher p-value, and conditional effects at page, physical-folio, and
section matching levels.  Every matched test additionally fixes current state
and line-position quartile.

Page matching is primary because it best controls local ecology; coarser
folio/section and raw results measure exploratory capacity only.  No semantic
interpretation is assigned to any right edge.
