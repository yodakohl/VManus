# GDT287 — joint wrapper/host factorization synthesis

## Purpose

GDT283 scores host characters with and without wrapper identity.  GDT286
scores wrapper identity from shape context, exact host, and exact host by field
position.  These frozen held-folio codelengths can be combined by the chain
rule into four explicit joint factorizations of the same observed wrapper and
opaque PAGE_HOST pair.

This is a post-hoc synthesis of fully exposed results, not a prospective test.
No model, corpus, parser, threshold, or null is changed.

## Models

For every GDT286 panel, define:

1. `INDEPENDENT` = wrapper under `SHAPE_CONTEXT` + host characters under the
   GDT283 no-wrapper model;
2. `WRAPPER_FIRST` = wrapper under `SHAPE_CONTEXT` + host characters under the
   GDT283 full-wrapper model;
3. `HOST_FIRST_STABLE` = host characters under the no-wrapper model + wrapper
   under the GDT286 `EXACT_HOST` model;
4. `HOST_FIRST_CONTEXTUAL` = host characters under the no-wrapper model +
   wrapper under `EXACT_HOST_X_POSITION`.

All four pay the same two-bit four-model selector.  Report held bits/event,
saving versus `INDEPENDENT`, winner, runner-up, and margin.  The component
codes are fixed hierarchical predictive instruments, so this ranks operational
factorizations rather than proving causal order.

## Claim ceiling and seal

At most this identifies the best of four exposed operational factorizations.
It cannot establish a lexical unit, morphology, abbreviation, notation,
language, meaning, plaintext, or translation.  Inputs are already-published
f84-free result tables; no f84 row is opened, parsed, retained, joined, or
scored.
