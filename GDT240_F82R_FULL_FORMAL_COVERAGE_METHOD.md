# GDT240 — f82r full source-native formal coverage

## Question

Can the partial GDT239 page dossier be extended to every human-catalogued f82r
locus without inventing missing HPR2/semantic fields?

## Method

Aggregate the already published GDT002 consensus projection by physical locus.
Preserve `STRICT_EXACT_FAMILY`, `EXACT_FAMILY_WITH_ALTERNATIVE`, and
`NO_EXACT_FAMILY_CONSENSUS` as distinct coverage states.  Concatenate consensus
groups only with an explicit `|` boundary.  Join the existing GDT239 field and
label dossiers by locus, recording their coverage rather than filling gaps.

The output is a full 45-locus formal census, not a semantic parse.  A missing
exact-family consensus remains explicit and no preferred ZL/IT/RF reading is
chosen.
