## Interpretation

GDT242 invalidated the old single-record relative positions.  GDT243 shows that
the **two-way long/short architecture** does not depend on those positions: it
survives even enormous uncertainty in the number of unseen fields.  This
restores a useful page-level structural statement:

> The observed f82r prose alternates larger clause-like field bundles with
> compact argument-like field bundles across all three physical paragraphs.

It does not restore the old semantic role names.  The external instrument is
largely responding to field extent, and its own calibration confuses tools with
short arguments and fails opener recovery.  GDT177 also showed that the
projected Q20 classes lack the expected unused structural features.  Therefore
`INSTRUCTION_CLAUSE_LIKE` and `SHORT_ARGUMENT_LIKE` remain shape analogies.
