# GDT246 — f80r full formal census method

## Question

What source-native formal information is actually available on f80r after the
GDT244/GDT245 role-coordinate correction, and do any complete graphical-label
family expressions recur verbatim inside prose?

## Inputs and rules

The experiment uses only the f80r rows of the existing GDT002 discovery
consensus projection, the corrected five-paragraph coordinate, the transferred
GDT233 label-prefix predictions, and the GDT245 artifact-status map. Every one
of the 53 human-catalogued loci is retained. Strict family consensus,
alternative-bearing, and no-exact-consensus states remain separate.

For strict loci, ordered source groups are joined with `|`; family n-grams are
not allowed to cross group boundaries. A label/prose recurrence exists only
when the complete family expression of a single-group graphical label equals a
complete internal prose group family. No substring search and no semantic
role assignment are used.

## Seal

All inputs are already published f80r/non-f84 artifacts. No f84 input was
read, retained, joined, or scored and no new f84 access occurred.
