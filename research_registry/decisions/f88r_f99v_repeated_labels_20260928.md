# f88r/f99v: repeated `otaly` and `otoky` labels

Status: **exact cross-folio label reuse confirmed; object identity and word meaning unbound**. This is a retrospective, source-nominated capacity audit, not a registered significance test or a translation. Both physical folios were previously admitted. No reserve, f84/f84r, or unadmitted page was opened.

## Nomination and prior limits

An [external progress report](https://github.com/geoffitect/voynich/blob/master/reports/PROGRESS.md#43-otaly--visually-confirmed-cross-reference) claims that `otaly` labels visually matching brown, branching root fragments on f88r and f99v. Its wider chain includes sealed f84r and was **not** followed. The current route, `context topic names`, `ideas search/duplicates`, and `vmanus-exp route-check` were inspected before this audit. The nearest strict predecessor [RLO001](../../experiments/semantic_assumptions/results/rlo001_repeated_label_ownership_capacity_report.md) found 57 labels / 57 types under **singular explicit ownership**; this broader proximity audit does not reverse that result. [W90](../proposals/translation_programs_20260912/work/W90/REPORT.md) had already inspected f88r and expressly left .5/.6 whole-versus-part ownership unresolved. The annotation source is [Stolfi-derived exact-locus comments](../../experiments/semantic_assumptions/results/existing_human_exact_locus_annotations.tsv). These comments carry `REL_PROXIMITY`, not singular attachment.

## Bounded reading and observation

The guarded `./vmanus-exp query-tsv experiments/semantic_assumptions/results/source_separator_transcription.tsv --selector locus --allow f88r.5 --allow f88r.6 --allow f99v.6 --allow f99v.9 --columns edition,page,locus,kind,ivtff_group_raw` returned twelve rows. Each of ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b has exactly these four same complete labels and `kind=L` readings:

| Complete form | f88r | f99v | Image/owner observation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `otaly` | `.6`, top row | `.6`, top row | f88r .6 is near W90's pale, long-rooted U5 and a large green leaf; the older comment calls the roots light. f99v .6 is near a dark forked fragment and a neighbouring large leaf. Both are positional descriptions; neither isolates one whole object or part. | Exact reuse across physical folios, but the proposed **same brown multi-fingered root** is not established. |
| `otoky` | `.5`, top row | `.9`, top row | f88r .5 is also in W90's U5 neighbourhood, although an older proximity comment associates it with a dark adjacent fragment. f99v .9 stands between two plant fragments, one dark and bifurcated. | A second exact cross-folio reuse; there is no secure shared object or material class. |

The official Yale image receipts were already recorded by [W90](../proposals/translation_programs_20260912/work/W90/SOURCE.json) for f88r (IIIF `1037112`, SHA-256 `aa266580695fc4a84cd031015c56f51f1b6ce807b6998c6ef4b8b68bae11983b`) and [GDT881](../../experiments/yolo/gdt881_f99v_text_graphic_stroke_interface/artifacts/SOURCE.json) for f99v (IIIF `1006247`, SHA-256 `111f6dfc34b8ecb9230cb5a0d144afef4cbd788048ddda2f440108941c91d5e5`). Both cached image hashes were verified and the images were manually inspected. Two independent image-only viewers, kept blind to the transcript, labels and reports, agreed that the top rows contain heterogeneous pale and dark branching fragments, but offered nonidentical coarse shape matches and did not identify a uniquely corresponding pair. Their counts and ordering are not owner annotations and do not resolve the label positions.

The existing `vmanus-work words profile FORM --json --limit 1` census of all 179 admitted text selectors adds a useful asymmetry: `otoky` occurs **exactly twice** in each reading, both times as these two pharmaceutical labels. `otaly` occurs 11 times in ZL3b and 13 times in IT2a/RF1b, with **only two labels** and respectively 9 or 11 running-prose occurrences across botanical, pharmaceutical and star/bath sections. Thus `otoky` is a local two-label candidate, whereas `otaly` is a broader reused form; neither distribution decides whether it names an object, part, material, class or address. Counts across alternate readings are not independent samples.

This audit was prompted by a claimed matching pair after the source was read. No whole-search countercontrol or blind pair-selection distribution was fixed. Therefore **no significance or independent confirmation** is claimed. Agreement among ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b supports only transcription robustness: they are alternate readings of the same manuscript, not three physical replications. On f88r two different strings can neighbour one W90 U5 drawing; on f99v their labels occur at different positions. A name, material, plant part, class marker and record address all remain plausible. `otaly` and `otoky` are useful candidates for a future independently anchored owner relation, not translated words.

Decision: retain the two exact repetitions as a structural positive, reject promotion of the external visual-match claim to a meaning binding. Reopen only if an independent source supplies singular ownership or a predeclared, whole-array image-feature contrast that discriminates the word/part/address rivals across physical folios. Do not reopen RLO001's strict contract from proximity alone.
