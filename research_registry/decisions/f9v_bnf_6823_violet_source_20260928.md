# BnF Latin 6823 f. 160v–161r: violet contrast, source-only


This was an exploratory source inspection after the existing f9v image and
source-profile decisions, not a prospective Voynich-word test. The
[fourteenth-century BnF Latin 6823 record](https://iiif.biblissima.fr/collections/manifest/d1a397a91c342e195a1dacd635a34fe1392b00c2)
points to the [Gallica IIIF manifest](https://gallica.bnf.fr/iiif/ark:/12148/btv1b6000517p/manifest.json).
Its original [f. 160v](https://gallica.bnf.fr/iiif/ark:/12148/btv1b6000517p/f328/full/full/0/native.jpg)
and [f. 161r](https://gallica.bnf.fr/iiif/ark:/12148/btv1b6000517p/f329/full/full/0/native.jpg)
were inspected natively through the institution's IIIF image service. The
1200-pixel versions inspected have SHA256
`72d42a2ef280aba1e12aa1d4a57698277abd28a63eecce49906514a89a41d3b0`
and `844bba313f598047842a9a3f0bbfbf8fdd44596adaa6013952bb878968f6dadc`.
The IIIF canvas labels are explicitly 160v and 161r. The separate
[BnF-derived image record](https://portail.biblissima.fr/fr/ark:/43093/ifdata45da8ce377dcbc3e4b941907f28fb61149b6d7b1)
indexes the opening as `viola alba` over a **two-page range**; that range alone
did not establish image/text ownership.

The original resolves the useful local boundary. On f. 160v, one painted
violet-like plant has purple blossoms and a new decorated-V text entry under
its roots. That entry continues at the top left of f. 161r. A later,
separate line on f. 161r begins visibly `Viola alba`; beside it is another
plant with pale outlined flowers, and its small `alba` caption is visible
next to the figure. The black `viola` caption on f. 160v lies at the gutter
beside that second plant across the opening. This is a historically dated
example of two adjacent violet descriptions with a written white-flower
distinction. The purple image's overpainted entry heading was **not** fully
transcribed, and the medieval term does not identify a modern species.

This source does not supply a f9v word owner. The source violet drawings
have heart-shaped leaves and stylized tubular/outlined blooms; the admitted
f9v image has different mixed leaves and five-part blue/pale blooms, and its
single page gives no independently named white-versus-purple pair. Thus the
source contrast improves the historical comparison but cannot select
`fochor = Viola`, `fochor = Jacea`, or the rival entry-address reading. No
new Voynich pixel or text, reserve, sealed page, or target test was opened.
