The proposed "two sides" premise needs correction. Q1 (f83r.47–49, 11 ZL
groups) is a small note in the **left arch/end-point field**. Q2
(f83r.52–55, 16 ZL groups) is by the **lower left outlet**. Both belong to
the larger two-figure coupled panel, but they are not simply the left and
right captions of the arch. The image shows the arched form joining the upper
figures and lower channel/outlet forms; it does not mark a directed handoff
from Q1's local feature to Q2's local feature. GDT002 had already found the
lower labels outlet-adjacent without singular figure ownership. This is a
local owner correction, not a new whole-panel interpretation.

The entire short texts contain two exact recurring ZL forms:

| Form | Q1 | Q2 | Prior restraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cheol` | .48 | .53 | In the GDT790 three-page artifact also .32 in f83r P5 and five lines on f77r/f82r; globally 139 ZL occurrences on 70 admitted selectors, 129 line-internal. |
| `sol` | .49 | .55 | In the same artifact five other f83r lines; globally 57 ZL occurrences on 37 selectors, 36 line-initial. |

Q1's `daiiin` (.49) and Q2's `daiin` (.54) are different whole forms. The
common `cheol` and `sol` therefore give a real within-panel recurrence, but
neither is an overtly identified participant. Their wider recurrence and
position profiles allow a shared field, routine line pattern, or separate
station notes as well as a single carried participant. The full Q1/Q2 lines
are preserved in GDT790; no one convenient snippet is scored as a sentence.
[GDT791](../../experiments/yolo/gdt791_thirty_page_visual_owner_spine/REPORT.md)
keeps exact label/prose reuse from merging panel owners, while the earlier
[IDEA521](../proposals/f83r_embedded_music_pair_20260922/REPORT.md)
already found insufficient frozen lexical capacity for a distinct music
extension over these two complete notes.

Decision: IDEA641 is **not tested** as a meaning/handoff claim. It has a
corrected physical owner map and exact recurrence, but no singularly established
physical endpoint shared by the two note fields, no direction, and no independently
bound written participant. Reopen only if such a local physical relation and
an overt target bridge can be established without assigning `cheol`, `sol`,
or a similar group a convenient meaning. No confirmed word.
