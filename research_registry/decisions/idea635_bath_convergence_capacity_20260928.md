# IDEA635: mixing versus successive baths, target-capacity screen

2026-09-28. Exploratory review of admitted prior work, before historical source
selection and before any fixed Voynich reading. IDEA635 requires two
independently conditioned inputs converging into one vessel, a resulting bath
state, and a second procedure in which the same conditions apply successively.

[GDT591](../../experiments/yolo/gdt591_bath_episode_continuity/REPORT.md)
finds coherent formal continuity among 92 Y-bearing bath hosts over 64
statements and 17 paragraphs. Its body/station alternation and AIIN-fill
attachment are internal to the inherited working reader; they establish
neither actual water nor heat, cold, mixing, vessel identity or temporal order.
[GDT595](../../experiments/yolo/gdt595_remaining_bath_default_source_atlas/REPORT.md)
assigns exploratory objects to 254 bath actions and retains six host-binding
rivals. [GDT596](../../experiments/yolo/gdt596_bath_object_compound_scope_phrasebook/REPORT.md)
factorizes those same readings: 247 actions have one participant, six have
two, one has three. The multi-participant clauses are generated from the
inherited role cards and do not independently establish two differently
conditioned fluid inputs or a single mixing endpoint. Their exact replay is
of the model's own clauses, not historical meaning accuracy.

The separate [W88 mixing check](../proposals/translation_programs_20260912/work/W88/REPORT.md)
retains `ychocthy` as a provisional mixing or discourse fork. Its f93r.24
partners `chotey` and `teey`/`teeys` remain unidentified; even `sheey teey`
at f29r.5 is compatible with more than one role. W88 expressly finds no
proven same-portion history. These contexts cannot supply IDEA635's two
known hot/cold inputs or distinguish convergence from succession.

Decision: **not tested**. The available target evidence licenses a formal
episode and several possible working paraphrases, but not IDEA635's owned
input-vessel-output graph or its temporal rival. Searching a historical pair
now would give no discriminating target consequence. Reopen only with admitted
target evidence that independently binds two conditioned inputs and a shared
result (or a clearly separate ordered pair), so that a frozen whole-reading
contract can choose between them. Another fluent bath paraphrase or old
HOT/END card does not meet that condition. Zero confirmed words.
