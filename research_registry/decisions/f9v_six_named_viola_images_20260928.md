# f9v and six named *Viola* images: bounded visual source comparison

28 September 2026. This is exploratory source inspection after the admitted
f9v image and the `fochor` name/address rival were already known, not an
independent Voynich-word test. The decision at issue was whether a nearby
*Tractatus de herbis* picture supplied a close, written-name owner for the
whole f9v botanical form. The smallest adequate check was to inspect a bounded
set of six readily available photographed `Viola` leaves rather than launch
a larger image search. Preparation, inspection, recording and publication were
budgeted at 30 minutes. The source comparison cannot itself decide what
`fochor` means.

The [Commons source-family index](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:De_viola_in_Tractatus_de_herbis)
is a **locator**, not an exhaustive medieval botanical census. Root directly
inspected the six linked manuscript photographs below, and the already
admitted f9v scan in `research_registry/work_batches/ten_hours_20260915/paired_root_cache/f9v.jpg`.
Institutional sources are linked where available. The images were viewed, not
copied into this repository. Commons catalogue dating is provisional where an
institutional catalogue was not independently read.

The inspected historical pictures show that written `Viola` could accompany
blue/purple flowers; **none** supplies the combined f9v silhouette of broad
five-part blue/pale flowers, lower lanceolate leaves, and upper deeply divided
leaflike parts. The repeated broad rounded leaves are especially different.
This limits a claim of direct picture copying from these six witnesses; it does
not refute the broader f9v wild-pansy/*Viola* visual hypothesis or prove a
different taxon. The six are a bounded convenience set, not a random sample
or all surviving *Viola* images. Medieval `Viola` labels also do not fix a
modern species.

No shared target text relation, caption or source-specific prediction was
found. `fochor` remains a singleton and its plant-name versus generic-entry
address alternatives remain open. No new Voynich page, reserve or sealed
material was accessed. Confirmed translated words: zero.
