# IDEA636: same-herb internal/external route capacity

2026-09-28. Exploratory target-capacity screen of the nominated admitted herbal/pharmaceutical and bath records, not a fixed historical-source comparison. The proposal requires one independently identified plant retained across two complete clauses, with separately written ingestion versus external application, different recipients/body sites and indications. No dated historical entry was selected or frozen because that target graph is absent from the nominated evidence.

The strongest existing cross-section inventory, GDT169, freezes forty human-nominated herbal-to-pharmaceutical physical-page pairs. Its five published singular or provisional pharmaceutical local queries have **0/5 exact PAGE_HOST** and **0/5 exact full-tuple** matches on their paired herbal pages; no replicated referent-stable host is selected. This does not disprove any same-plant relationship, but none is independently available for the required two-route test. GDT811's f88r `okol` label/prose reuse is an exact *string* bridge, without plant identity, treatment route or recipient. W89's 140-paragraph recipe-versus-description comparison remains paused: its concrete action glosses cannot be imported as confirmed ingestion/application verbs. In the admitted biological pages, GDT590 assigns provisional body-versus-station bath roles to complete passages, but its image pass finds no word-level visual owner and identifies no plant, swallowed administration or matched oral-external pair. The present human relation inventory in GDT388 has zero eligible ordered inscription edges; proximity to a plant or body does not create the missing target graph.

Decision: IDEA636 **not tested**. A complete historical entry contrasting internal and external use would be a useful source hypothesis but would supply missing target roles if compared now. Reopen only with an admitted complete target that independently binds the *same* material across two written clauses and distinguishes ingestion from application plus recipients before the source entry is examined. The conclusion is restricted to the nominated existing inventories, not a universal claim about all Voynich prose or medieval treatments. No new page, decoder, word assignment, historical source selection, significance claim or confirmed translation.
