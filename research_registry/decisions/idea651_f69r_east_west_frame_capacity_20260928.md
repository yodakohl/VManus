# IDEA651 — east-west event cannot yet orient f69r

Decision: `NOT_TESTED__NO_INDEPENDENT_FRAME_TRANSFER`. This is a capacity
review of the raw proposal, not a test of a Voynich word.

The proposed solar rising/setting event could carry an east-west meaning
independently of f69r. Its direction would orient the f69r ring only if an
author-visible relation also fixed the **same coordinate frame** across the
two diagrams. No such relation is present in the admitted f69r image or in
the prior f69r owner inventory. GDT1067 already shows that f69r's own
12-in-16 color geometry retains exact 180-degree partners and lacks a fixed
outer-label-to-bearing owner. The earlier DIRECTIONPLACEMENT003 audit found
49 matched clock-half rows, but only on physical folios f67 and f68; it did
not transfer a direction feature to f69r. GDT1068's unchanged titles do not
provide a text bridge either. An external solar event alone therefore
changes none of the eight admissible f69r phase mappings.

No target label, word, root or direction was scored. This leaves the positive
f69r wind-system analogy and IDEA649's polarity ambiguity intact. Reopen
only with both an independently owned east-west event **and** an explicit
f69r frame/label ownership relation, or a different prospectively fixed
asymmetry that distinguishes the paired maps. Another oriented historical
wind rose without a Voynich frame link is insufficient.
