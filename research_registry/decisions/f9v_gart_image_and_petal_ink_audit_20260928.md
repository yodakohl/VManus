# f9v: 1485 Yacea image and alleged writing on petals

2026-09-28. Exploratory, unblinded source/target comparison, not a registered
prediction test. f9v had already been visually exposed in GDT844/1063/1064.
The source was chosen after its historical name compatibility was known.
No new manuscript selector, reserve, f84/f84r, decoder or word gloss was used.

Sources: [official Yale f9v canvas](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006093/full/max/0/default.jpg)
(2812 × 3797, downloaded SHA256
`790a7493a31133cf9375fb7471d1d1167df4988bcadeb74ea2528a0ba562e693`),
the existing [1485 *Gart der Gesundheit* Yacea woodcut](../../experiments/yolo/gdt1064_f9v_jacea_historical_synonym/src/YACEA_GART_1485.jpg)
(SHA256 `282dc906c8e1aff68dda4cac713ca8dc12d2f5c6848066edd454048b56c75ea0`),
and P. Han's [2011 f9v floral detail study](https://www.as.up.krakow.pl/jvs/library/2-7-2011-05-25/pansyvm.pdf),
which proposes writing on some petals without transcribing a stable sequence.
The Yale full canvas and upper-left, upper-right and lower-flower native IIIF
regions were viewed. The 1485 illustration and its historically pansy-like
`yacea` caption had already been established by GDT1064.

| Feature | f9v | 1485 Yacea woodcut | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flower heads | Five spread, individually drawn open heads, blue/pale colouring. | Four discernible heads, mostly upright or side-facing closed/cupped forms. | No shared unusual flower placement. |
| Leaves and stems | Long basal lanceolate leaves, many narrow divided upper appendages, upright branch fan. | Wavy/lobed lateral leaves and side branches; different central stem organization. | General herb/pansy similarity, no distinctive tracing match. |
| Root | Long branching fibrous root fan. | Cut/looped stem end without a comparable root fan. | A conspicuous copying detail differs. |
| Written plant label | f9v has whole prose, but no author-visible word attached to a particular flower in the inspected image. | Printed `yacea freyschem krut` caption under the figure. | Historical name compatibility does not transfer to `fochor`. |

The dark flecks and jagged blue boundaries of the f9v petals do not yield a
bounded, repeatable, multi-character inscription in the official full image.
This **does not prove there are no intentional marks**: the public image may
miss fine strokes, and the tiny isolated flecks cannot be securely assigned.
But the alleged petal writing supplies no readable independent word owner now.
Do not treat a colour patch, petal orientation or an outside speck as a label.

**Decision:** retain GDT1064's positive historical fact that *Jacea/Yacea*
could denote a pansy-like herb. The inspected 1485 woodcut is not a close
graphic match that would license a direct image-model or matching-text
inference for f9v. A shared plant tradition remains possible. `fochor` is
still a C0 replaceable candidate for the pictured herb versus an entry address;
the full f9v text has no newly identified lexical anchor. Confirmed words: 0.
No source-to-target significance or independent confirmation is claimed.
