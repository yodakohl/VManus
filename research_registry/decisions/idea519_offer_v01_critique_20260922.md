# IDEA519 offer_v01: independent content critique

2026-09-22. Reviewed only the delivered, preserved `offer_v01/LEXICON.json`,
`CLAUSES.json`, `BINDINGS.json`, `COSTS.json` and `READING.md`, after finishing
the separate input preflight. No author contact, new word assignment, solver,
corpus access or modification of the offer. Root's earlier findings were known;
this is an independent source/contract check, not a blinded review.

**Decision: incomplete construction and reference contract; no consequence
replay warranted.** The offer preserves a substantial raw-word inventory and
42 provisional new values, but it does not yet compose those words into the
claimed complete argument. Some local source/rendering claims are concretely
wrong. That is different from proving the entire hour/day hypothesis impossible.

## What is actually retained

All nine full ZL/IT raw lines are present with their separate spans. The 15 old
ZL values listed in LEXICON match GDT1015. The 42 new ZL whole entries cover its
remaining types. The five IT-only forms remain explicitly unknown, with no
alias or packing claimed. Referencing the unchanged 80-group P2 source retains
that source; it does not itself supply a joint P1/P2 construction.

Those are real inventory virtues, not a complete semantic parse. In particular,
zero unknown ZL *lexical entries* does not mean zero unknown syntax, reference,
unit or event relations. The opening claim of a “vollständige ... Arbeitslektüre
der beiden ... Einheiten” is too strong while IT has five unknown forms and
neither branch has complete compositional argument rules.

## Decisive construction defects

CLAUSES declares four production IDs but actually uses five:
P1_TEMPORAL_FRAME, P1_RULER_SUCCESSION, P1_DERIVED_PHASE,
P1_BOUNDARY_TRANSITION and undeclared **P1_PHASE_CARRY** in C02 and C09.
This is an exact mismatch with both COSTS' four-production claim and the selected
four-production cap; it is not evidence of a semantic contradiction.

More basically, all four declared entries contain only unordered `forms` lists
and a `use` description. They supply no ordered pattern, argument-selection
rule, typed output, optional-slot conditions or treatment of the other words.
For example P1_TEMPORAL_FRAME lists DAY/HOUR/DAYLIGHT/TWENTY_FOUR, but C01 has
only the last of those, twice; C08 instead has DAY/DAYLIGHT but no HOUR or
TWENTY_FOUR. A reusable rule connecting these cases is not specified. The nine
German sentence renderings are consequently independent authored assertions,
not demonstrated applications of four complete productions.

The old 12 exact GDT1015 patterns are not instantiated or shown to cover these
gaps. Merely renaming P1_PHASE_CARRY to another ID would not repair the missing
argument/order contract. An explicit versioned revision could supply one, but
V1 must remain an unfinished account and must not receive a post hoc execution.

## Concrete source and fixed-value problems

1. **B02 cites a nonexistent token.** It refers to “qokedy DAY in P1C02 and
   P1C08”. C02 has qokain INITIAL_POINT and qokeedy HOUR, not qokedy. The old
   DAY form occurs only in C08. A new DAY_SEGMENT value at C02 does not become
   that missing old DAY token. This is a false source citation, not an arithmetic
   failure.
2. **The common C08 paraphrase overwrites an IT obligation.** ZL qekeey is newly
   assigned SECOND_START; IT instead has old qokeey=FIRST. Nevertheless the
   one `german` field and shared reading table say “Ein zweiter Anfang”. The
   unknown lchor caveat does not mark this already-known FIRST discrepancy.
   As rendered, this violates the fixed-value contract. FIRST of a separately
   delimited second process could in principle be meaningful, but no such
   domain/construction is supplied here; it cannot be inserted as an invisible
   rescue. This local rendering conflict does not prove all possible fixed-FIRST
   readings impossible.
3. **ZL THEREFORE has no constructed role.** The C08 r is correctly listed as
   THEREFORE in LEXICON, but its sentence supplies temporal prose (“nach der
   Nacht”) rather than a specified premise and conclusion. There is no parse
   showing where its inferential contribution occurs. This is missing semantic
   realization, not permission to replace it by THEN or omit it.
4. **THIS has no resolved typed antecedent.** C02 dy is rendered with an hour,
   but no required type, previous compatible mention or exact reference rule
   is given. The following qokeedy supplies an hour noun, not by itself a prior
   hour referent. Earlier new interval/segment words might support a declared
   construction, but V1 gives none. The appropriate verdict is missing binding,
   not a proof that no antecedent could ever be proposed.

## The promised additional dependency is not yet an evaluable claim

B01 says the C02 initial point and the two C09 ruler projections share alpha
and H. This is an additional assumed binding, correctly described elsewhere
as such. Calling it “written in two separate clauses” overstates what the
offered construction establishes. Repeated RULER nouns do not themselves
write equality or specify their projections.

There is no concrete endpoint function, elapsed count, ruler-valued relation
or comparison predicate for C09. No map specifies whether its two outputs are
H(alpha), H(alpha+k), or different results depending on the same alpha. Sharing
an input parameter is not the same as returning to the initial output. Without
that distinction one cannot derive an adverse assignment, decide the stated
independent-phase rival, or verify the claim that the new dependency exceeds
an asserted common source.

The two 24s are called “frame number” and “return measure”. Those are role names,
not explicit units or a cardinality relation. No typed count tells which objects
are counted by each, or which recurrence is being measured. Thus neither a
second 24-hour proof nor a contradiction with a seven-cycle has been established.
For example, a future *explicit* claim of returning to the same ruler after 24
forward hourly moves on H would conflict with a seven-cycle, but V1 does not
fix that interpretation; it must not be forced onto the vague “return frame”.

B04's “same bounded phase family” also leaves its phase/derivation relation
open. Its clause list omits C02, which contains the first old qokal=DERIVED,
while including C03's new qokaly=DERIVED_SEQUENCE. Those are different whole
values. Their relationship can be specified, but it is not supplied by writing
“qokaly/qokal DERIVED”. All four old DERIVED occurrences need actual arguments;
listing a common family is insufficient.

## Cost certificate and whole-account limits

The six named B entries do not establish six atomic nondefault bindings.
B01 bundles phase identity, two ruler projections and a common order; B02
bundles two count roles and day boundaries; B05 bundles inclusion, a single-day
scope and no phase reset. B06 is reader/source bookkeeping, not a new semantic
reference. Some obligations could follow from a genuinely shared production,
but no such derivation is currently available. Therefore the binding cost is
**not certifiable**, rather than demonstrably a particular integer above six.
The five-used/four-declared production mismatch is separately definite.

New compound noun values such as CLOSING_RULER_PHASE are not automatically
sentence macros merely because their labels contain several words. They become
hidden macros if the account relies on them to supply unlisted closure events,
phase identities, endpoint functions or complete claims. V1 does not provide
enough composition to certify its asserted zero sentence macros.

No explicit equation identifies P1's H/phase/day endpoint with the old P2 H,
initial phase and beginning; B01 is between two P1 clauses. Calling the old
source retained and speaking of a “global phase” does not define cross-paragraph
scope. Same order, same phase, same day instance and chronological succession
remain distinct additional assumptions. The retrospective rival remains viable.

The offer's own declaration that the rivals remain compatible is appropriate.
There is no demonstrated manuscript discriminator, no confirmed meaning and
no reason to reject the entire family simply because all values are hypotheses.
Preserve this V1 with exact defects; any authorized V2 must be explicitly
versioned and complete its construction, units, references and costs before a
fixed consequence is assessed. No solver can supply the missing semantic choice.
