## 29 September source-side correction: all twelve radial fields are months

An accessible photograph of the actual [Laon MS 422 f.6v wheel](https://www.ziereis-faksimiles.de/fileadmin/_processed_/wmimages/isidorus-hispalensis_014490_17-1e52ef5fa0.jpg), published by the facsimile vendor and tied to the [institutional manuscript](https://initiale.irht.cnrs.fr/en/codex/12840), was visually inspected. The [publisher's description](https://www.facsimiles.com/facsimiles/laon-computistical-miscellany) states the layout explicitly: **four season figures each span three sections, and each of those twelve sections corresponds to a month**. The picture shows the season figures inside the wheel, with month writing in the twelve outer sectors. Thus the middle item of each radial triplet is still a month; the source does not label four radial centres as seasons and eight flanks as months. The figures carry the four season roles in a different visual layer. Exact month spellings and a target rotation were not diplomatically transcribed.

This corrects the *source* premise of IDEA650's proposed centre-versus-flank **season/month word-role** test. The visually similar four-by-three partition remains, but its Laon ownership cannot support that lexical contrast. A different month-order or middle-month hypothesis would require its own independent target owner/phase and a new discriminating consequence; it is not a repair of IDEA650. No f68v2 Voynich image or word content was opened or newly admitted. The commercial photograph is a traceable primary-image reproduction, not a direct institutional IIIF receipt; the institutional Initiale viewer returned an access challenge here. An image-search caption also misidentified a different Laon wheel (the twelve winds, f.5v) as the seasons; it was not used as f.6v evidence.
