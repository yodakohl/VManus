This is a maintenance/curation pass, not a new manuscript experiment.

Unknown: which records repeat an identical claim, which preserve distinct rival hypotheses,
and which blocked candidates have a concrete route to a discriminating observation.

Decision: collapse repeated work into explicit hypothesis dossiers; retain variants and
counterevidence; distinguish immediate execution, conditional acquisition, source review,
and closed models. No numerical success probability is assigned.

Smallest adequate pass: parallel source review of semantic families and working glosses;
root assesses comparators/data stops; all imported records receive a coverage disposition
including unresolved ones. Lexical matches and shared report pointers never establish identity.
Budget: 15 minutes including primary reads, curation, validation and publication; checkpoint
at 8 minutes. At the checkpoint reduce speculative expansion; retain unresolved membership
explicitly. No decoder, new manuscript access, or control-corpus work is authorized by this note.

Priority order: a concrete observation that separates meanings with available independent
evidence; otherwise a specific obtainable prerequisite; otherwise source recovery or archive.
A theoretically powerful but absent bilingual key is not an executable top candidate.
