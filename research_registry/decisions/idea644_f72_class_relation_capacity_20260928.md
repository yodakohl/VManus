# IDEA644: f72 repeated labels versus a directed class relation

2026-09-28. Exploratory primary review, no new text access or fixed semantic
test. [GDT793](../../experiments/yolo/gdt793_okal_whole_record_candidate_discriminator/REPORT.md)
already owns the complete f72r2 outer-ring sequence: `okal` at slots 3 and 5,
`okaly` at 4 and 12. The later complete ring-E prose has one exact `okaly`
and one exact `okal`. It also retains other `okal*` forms and all intervening
groups in its 27-line record. [GDT794](../../experiments/yolo/gdt794_complete_label_multiform_slot_transfer/REPORT.md)
confirms the two-by-two within-ring collisions, while correcting the older
raw slot-4 series: raw list order is not a shared Kluge-A coordinate.

The repeated labels are genuine classes/recurrences, not two uniquely
identified members. GDT793's target-masked owner recovery was 0/3; on f72
both prose labels can attach to either of their two identical local copies.
A strict whole-form ordinal yields `okal < okaly < okal`. Thus IDEA644's
class-level formulation is a plausible way to avoid an individual-member
claim, but it cannot inherit a unique referent or order from these labels.

The prose does present `okaly ... okal` in that sequence, with `dalchdy
eteeey` between them in the local line. None of these intervening groups is
independently established as a directed relation operator, nor is the prose
order an author-visible label-to-label arrow. A directed class assertion and
a flat mention of both codes can therefore both accommodate the complete
record. Naming the intervening span a relation after seeing the labels would
not be a falsifier. This is the same exposed physical leaf, with no independent
meaning confirmation.

Decision: IDEA644 is **not tested**. Reopen only with a prospectively fixed,
independently constrained relation-bearing construction and its direction in
a complete admitted record, plus an independent owner/contrast able to
distinguish assertion from enumeration. Reassigning the four label copies,
reviving the ordinal, or reading the raw slot-4 sequence as one historical
coordinate does not qualify. Zero translated words.
