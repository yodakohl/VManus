source; no fixed text test, new image access, decoder, or word gloss.

The independent historical comparator is Heidelberg Cpg 644, 100v (1450–1470).
The [native source record](../proposals/laufenberg_f85r2_20260926/CPG644_NATIVE_COMPARISON.md)

contents. The partly legible caption makes an observation about urine appearance
conditional on a statement about the patient. The later phrase and exact
diagnosis are not securely transcribed. This source owns the relation
**vessel contents → observed sign → judgment**; it does not own the other
f85r2 figures, circle, or outer forms. The comparison is stronger than a
vessel silhouette alone, but its pose is not an exclusive semantic key.

[GDT1043](../../experiments/yolo/gdt1043_f85r2_native_attribute_binding/REPORT.md)

with flask inspection. The grip differs between two observers. No visible
contents, sampled fluid, patient, or word-to-item connector is established;
generic vessel or emblem remains possible. [GDT1042](../../experiments/yolo/gdt1042_f85r2_source_program_surface_census/REPORT.md)

in each alternative reading. East shares only `aiin,daiin` with north,
`aiin,oteey` with south, and `aiin,ol` with west in ZL3b; these shared forms
have no independently assigned observer, substance, sign, or judgment role.
The four paragraph starts differ, and no repeated two- or three-group sequence
is stable across all three readings. There is one exposed physical leaf, so
this comparison provides no independent target confirmation.

IDEA634's stipulated complete reading cannot be chosen between inspection
and emblem from the admitted observations. An interpreter could assign the
source's medical roles to any of several east groups and then invent
unconstrained meanings for the other sectors; such a reading would be a new
hypothesis, not a tested consequence. This also overlaps the older unreviewed
IDEA533 question of diagnosis versus treatment, but does not settle it. The prior
iconographic lead in GDT1043 is retained.

Decision: **not tested** for translation. The historical comparison supports
uroscopy as a provisional visual class, but there is no source-independent
target ownership of contents, observed property, or judgment. Reopen only if
an admitted target obtains an author-visible connector or other independent
binding for those roles, and a complete fixed reading can then discriminate
inspection from emblem and treatment across all four sectors. A clearer
transcription of the Cpg 644 caption alone will not fix the target gap.
