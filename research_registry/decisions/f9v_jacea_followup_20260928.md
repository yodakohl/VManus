# f9v: chronology, exception and referent capacity

2026-09-28. Exploratory follow-up to [the fixed source-profile decision](f9v_jacea_full_entry_capacity_20260928.md).


The [historical study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/304142518_Viola_jacea_Zur_botanischen_Fachsprache_in_der_Renaissance)
traces the *Jacea* transfer from knapweed/scabious-like plants to wild pansy
through the 1485 *Gart* and its reception. Its bounded review finds no
pansy/*Yacea* in the specified earlier *Circa instans* witnesses; its 1363
*Jacea* occurrence has no secure pansy referent. This makes the 1485 label a
later compatibility example, not evidence that a circa-1420 f9v writer used
that label. It does not prove that such usage was impossible around 1420.

Fuchs [1543, chapter 313](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/29723146.pdf)
does give a sharply worded source-side contrast: his Freyschamkraut is *not*
cold as some thought, but warm and dry. This was located after the f9v source
profile had been seen. IDEA627, as submitted, also requires a named comparison
herb and a prospectively fixed subject/condition/polarity; the Fuchs sentence
does not identify that comparison herb. It is therefore **not** an eligible
fixed-test source under that idea. Its late date and the absence of an
independently assigned f9v quality span are further barriers. The earlier
1485 chapter is reported hot and moist, so qualities cannot be borrowed
interchangeably across the witnesses.

The complete guarded f9v ZL3b projection has exact `fochor` only at .1,
`pchor` at the second paragraph's .5, `ychor` at .11, and separate `chor`
at .2/.3/.12. GDT766 does not license a shared `chor` morpheme; the later
forms therefore do not furnish IDEA630's independently bound antecedent and
reference. Absence of an exact `fochor` repetition does not contradict a
plant-name reading: later prose could use other words or omit a repeated name.
IDEA626's dated alias graph can keep historical homonyms apart, but without a
pre-1450 pansy-owned entry or an independent Voynich-to-entry link it likewise
cannot select a complete f9v reading. All three ideas remain untested for
manuscript meaning. No new target, reserve, or sealed page was opened.
