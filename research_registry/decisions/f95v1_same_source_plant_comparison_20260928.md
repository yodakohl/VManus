# f95v1: fixed same-source plant-image comparison (exploration)

Status before source-image access: source-only exploratory screen, no Voynich
word selection or new manuscript admission. f95v1 is the admitted Yale
1006243 panel; f95v2 is Yale 1006242. The GDT404 pooled drawing owner has
been withdrawn. Existing catalog guesses for f95v1 conflict: wormwood
(*Artemisia*) versus hollyhock (*Alcea/Althaea*). The first f95v1 group
`olteedy` is rare but a first position alone is not a plant-name rule;
GDT1062's other first-position labels have no confirmed meanings.

Unknown: whether either proposed plant has a historically labelled image
whose **flower/fruit architecture, leaf form, stem arrangement, and root form**
jointly resemble f95v1 better than the alternative and a nearby plant of
similar visual complexity. The early Carrara Herbal (BL Egerton MS 2020,
c.1390–1404) has catalogued wormwood f12v and marshmallow f52v; marshmallow
is a source-family proxy for hollyhock, not an identity. The BL white-bryony
f31r image is a same-source visual competitor. Fixed features to inspect:
round blue terminal heads versus small yellow/white clusters, palmate lobed
leaves versus finely divided or broad unlobed leaves, paired arching stems,
and multiple reddish tuber-like root arms. Major missing source anatomy is a
contradiction, not an artistic repair.

Decision: a distinctive multi-feature match would retain one C0 image-identity
lead for later independent text binding; a weak or tied match leaves both
names unselected. Even a match would not identify `olteedy`, because the
record-head-as-name rule and cross-context meaning remain unproved. No reserve,
f84/f84r, or new Voynich image will be opened. Smallest test: retrieve only the
three labelled institutional source images, compare complete plants by the
four fixed feature classes, and write every mismatch. Budget: 45 minutes total
(10 preparation, 20 source acquisition/comparison, 10 validation, 5
publication); stop expansion at budget limit.

## Completed source-image check

The [BL catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-001982947)
independently names the three inspected Egerton MS 2020 entries. Their
[wormwood f12v](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165354197.0x000018/canvas/c/35),
[marshmallow f52v](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165354197.0x000068/canvas/c/115),
and [white bryony f31r](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165354197.0x00003d/canvas/c/72)
were viewed in full after the checklist above was fixed. The admitted
[f95v1 Yale image](https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/2002046?child_oid=1006243)
was the target visual; no target text beyond its previously admitted panel
was newly opened for this comparison.

| Fixed feature | f95v1 | Wormwood f12v | Marshmallow f52v | White bryony f31r |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reproductive heads | numerous round blue heads | tiny grey-green clustered buds | few white/pink open flowers and brown clusters | round fruit green/yellow/red, not blue |
| Leaves | deeply palmate/lobed, several bilateral groups | deeply divided and elongate | broad serrate to lobed | broad star-like lobes |
| Stems | two main stems, long arching flowering arms | one thin upright branched stem | two separate upright plants | one climbing vine with tendrils |
| Roots | multiple massive reddish tuber-like arms | thin tapered root | thin branched roots | large two-armed pale tuber |

Wormwood matches lobing alone and contradicts head colour/abundance and
root mass. Marshmallow offers a loose leaf-family resemblance but has open
flowers and thin roots. White bryony has the strongest root and leaf
similarity, yet the tendrils, vine habit and red/yellow fruit disagree with
the observed Voynich architecture and blue heads. No one source explains
three of the four fixed classes without a major contradiction. Source style
alone is not a plant identity. The historical wormwood and hollyhock guesses
remain unselected; the marshmallow image is only a related-taxon proxy for
hollyhock and does not test a labelled hollyhock illustration.

No target word, including `olteedy`, is selected. A future route needs a
separately source-owned, more diagnostic picture match and an independently
tested record-head relation; rerunning these three source panels or adding
colour repairs would not change this decision. This is an exploratory
source-image result, not a statistical control or independent confirmation.

## Separate f95v2 source lead

The same [white-bryony f31r](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165354197.0x00003d/canvas/c/72)
and the independently labelled [Morgan M.652 f22v](https://ica.themorgan.org/manuscript/page/38/143825)
both portray a rounded fruit/flower cluster, conspicuously edged leaves and
divided roots. Those broad traits resemble f95v2, whose terminal ovals are
unpainted and whose leaves have marginal hair. Both source images show
climbing tendrils absent from the observed f95v2 drawing, and neither shows
its four upright spray-like stalks and paired hairy leaf groups. The BL
[black-bryony f31v](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165354197.0x00003e/canvas/c/73)
is a nearby named alternative with berry clusters but smooth heart-shaped
leaves and a slender root. White bryony therefore remains only an unselected
iconographic candidate for f95v2, not a plant identity or a reading of
`tchody`.
