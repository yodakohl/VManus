# Walters W.73 f.2r: written four-cardinal / twelve-wind source

29 September 2026. Source-only follow-up to IDEA650, GDT353, GDT952, GDT1067 and GDT1081. No new Voynich target content opened. **W.73 f.2r is an already used source in GDT952, not a newly discovered wind witness.** This note records its exact four-cardinal ownership and its limit for the current f68v2/f69r analogy.

The Walters Art Museum's [f.2r object record](https://art.thewalters.org/object/W.73.2R/) and [institutional manuscript catalogue and transcription](https://art.thewalters.org/files/pdf/W73.pdf), pp. 11–13, identify a late-twelfth-century diagram of twelve winds. The direct [museum scan](https://thedigitalwalters.org/Data/WaltersManuscripts/W73/data/W.73/sap/W73_000005_sap.jpg), locally viewed with SHA-256 `c008e0a5de99d253d938b76b7d0da6b1ff854186b832b21decf75eab4a1267c9`, has twelve names in one colored annulus and corresponding characterizations in twelve trapezoidal sectors around a T-O map. The catalogue transcribes four corresponding sector texts as `primus/secundus/tertius/quartus cardinalis`: Septentrio, Subsolanus, Auster and Zephirus, respectively N/E/S/W. The other eight winds are described in relation to them. This is a source-owned **four-plus-eight class within a twelve-place cycle**, rather than a numerical resemblance inferred from the diagram alone.

The diagram does not supply the missing Voynich owner. Its twelve wind names occupy the **same** ring, whereas the historical f68v2 inventory puts four sparse star-label centers and eight radial titles in **different visual layers**. The written Walters contrast is cardinal versus collateral *winds*, not center versus flank *months*; it cannot repair the Laon source correction in IDEA650. The f68v2 image/text is not currently admitted, and historical GDT353 already found no exceptional ordered title alignment. GDT952 already froze eight explicit W.73 f.2r name references against the f67r2 title/body register: its original implementation was invalid, and the corrected post-exposure diagnostic gave zero definite off-diagonal title recurrence; IT2a contradicted the fixed models while ZL3b/RF1b remained unresolved. For admitted f69r, the Walters source provides no target authorial north, polarity or singular sector-to-name link; GDT1067 and GDT1081 retain their stops. The Walters f.8v catalogue also combines twelve winds and lunar-tidal cycles, but that broader topical conjunction likewise supplies no target phase or label owner.

Decision: retain as a positive comparative source with **zero present lexical-test capacity**. A future test would need independently admitted target content, an authorial target orientation, and a unique mapping from one target inscription layer to the source's twelve positions. Do not select a favorable rotation, label a Voynich word with a wind name, or count this as independent confirmation of a target reading. f84/f84r and reserves were untouched.
