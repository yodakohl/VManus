# IDEA632: plant stages, part order and harvest condition

2026-09-28. Exploratory capacity screen, not a fixed Voynich test. IDEA632
requires two independently identified historical plant entries with different
written stage/harvest conditions, followed by complete target readings that
bind the same plant through successive stages. A mere part inventory must stay
an explicit rival.

The best already admitted target lead is f17r.5, quoted in
[GDT768](../../experiments/yolo/gdt768_chor_shor_part_identity_tournament/REPORT.md):
`ychekchy cthy chor shor cphor cphaldy dair cthey qody`. GDT768 supports
parallel nominal roles for `chor` and `shor`, but flower versus seed/fruit and
the reverse direction tie exactly (0.820437 each). Its complete working line
renders a part/quantity/preparation inventory, not a development sequence.
[GDT813](../../experiments/yolo/gdt813_f17_content_word_transfer/REPORT.md)
describes the admitted f17r picture as one connected plant with leaves, three
terminal heads and marked root-region forms; it does not identify those heads
as ordered flower, fruit and ripe seed stages or bind words to organs.

[IDEA368](../proposals/raw_f17r_seed_extraction_sowing.json) already supplied
the competing complete-line draft “development: leaves, flowers, fruit; when
ripe, extract seeds; sow them.” It explicitly pays for new unconfirmed values
of `ychekchy`, `cphor`, `cphaldy`, `dair`, `cthey` and `qody` and retains a
static-inventory and reverse flower/fruit rival. No independently owned
later sow-versus-store consequence is nominated. The earlier
[IDEA189 review](../proposals/translation_programs_20260912/HARVEST_SELECTION_REVIEW.md)
already shows that `cthy chor shor` alone does not establish extraction or a
continuing mother plant. W93's naming test also had zero applicable frozen
identity comparisons; a newly chosen plant name cannot serve as a free owner.

Decision: IDEA632 remains **not tested**. The present target cannot distinguish
ordered same-plant phenology from a static list or fix which of `chor`/`shor`
is flower. Searching more historical harvest instructions first would not
change that Voynich decision. Reopen on a source-independent exact target
owner/temporal/harvest relation, with two complete rival readings and dated
source-owned entry profiles frozen before comparison. No new manuscript image,
transcription, reserve, f84/f84r page or decoder was accessed. Zero confirmed
words.
