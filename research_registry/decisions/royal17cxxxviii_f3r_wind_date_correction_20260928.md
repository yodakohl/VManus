# Royal MS 17 C XXXVIII f. 3r: added wind drawing, not a c. 1420 key

The British Library's [primary catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002107378)
lists an Italian compass/wind-name drawing on fol. 3r. The host manuscript
dates to 1400–1424, but the catalogue's provenance explicitly dates the
**added f. 3r drawing to the late fifteenth century**. Its digital images are
currently unavailable. The item is therefore neither a c. 1420 exemplar nor
an image-checked one-to-one owner/orientation key for f69r. The catalogue
does not give a readable slot count or a Voynich-aligned inscription map.

This source-only correction does not change the positive f69r 12+16
wind-system family comparison. It leaves IDEA649's GDT1067 polarity symmetry
and GDT1068's zero whole-title matches intact. No Voynich word or direction
is assigned. Revisit this witness only if an accessible image reveals a
specific, independently transferable feature; its late date still prevents
use as a known earlier template.
