# IDEA519 offer_v02: independent content review

2026-09-22. Reviewed the complete frozen V2 offer against the selection decision,
SOURCE/SOURCE_BOUNDARY, the prior input-only preflight and V1 critique, and the
unchanged GDT1015 MODEL_v01/READING_v01. No author contact, new target access,
meaning assignment, semantic/numeric replay or modification of either offer.
Small read-only checks below concern file identity, source-token accounting and
the supplied patterns only; they do not execute the proposed interpretation.

**Verdict: preserve and publish V2 as a materially more explicit but incomplete
whole-paragraph attempt. It is not ready for consequence replay.** There is an
actual IT argument-assignment error and a production-cost mismatch. More
fundamentally, the proposed additional endpoint consequence is neither a
constructed dependency between two separately written statements nor a result
of defined endpoint operations. These findings do not establish that every
fixed-lexicon hour/day extension is impossible.

the nonexistent C02 DAY citation, preserves IT C08 FIRST separately from ZL

The packet still has only three ZL and four IT anchor-eligible whole lines;
the source's diagram interruptions and diplomatic limits remain. The alternative
readers are not independent manuscripts. The old P2 normalization and IT unit
boundary caveats remain in SOURCE_BOUNDARY. Nothing here confirms a meaning.

## Four names do not supply four reusable productions

CONSTRUCTIONS contains four top-level names but nine distinct ordered patterns:

| Name | Separately supplied clause patterns |
|---|---|
| P1_FRAME_COUNT | C01 |
| P1_PHASE_DERIVATION | C02, C05, C09 |
| P1_RULER_SUCCESSION | C03, C04, C07 |
| P1_SCOPE_TRANSITION | C06, C08 |

The latter entries select their arguments and relations by a `clause` field.
Their constructor is only, for example, `DerivedPhase(arguments in the clause
template order)`. C02 has eight slots, C05 eight different slots and two
derivations, and C09 six slots with no defined derivation relation. No common
argument-composition rule produces these three patterns. A shared output label
does not make their distinct input patterns one production.

Under the inherited accounting convention—one exact value-pattern per clause—
there are **at least nine authored alternatives**, before deciding whether
reader-specific optional/split alternatives incur further cost. This exceeds
the selected four-new-production allowance. Four nonterminal names could head
nine grammar rules, but COSTS currently charges four rules. Factoring them into
four genuine reusable rules would be a further design, not a property of V2.
The two transcriptions of one clause are not independent instances of reuse.

Even nine exact patterns currently provide chiefly tuple construction. The
prose adds relations not defined by those tuples: C02's hour ending at both
night boundaries, C03's transition through a phase change, C05's crossing a
threshold, and C09's closure after an order limit. Keeping all associated nouns
in ordered arguments is necessary but does not state these predicates.

## THIS<Point> is not itself a lexical contradiction

Old dy is THIS, described as a typed backward reference. GDT1015 C07 explicitly
says “typed most-recent demonstrative; not same individual referent”, with DAY
and HOUR as that clause's two required types. It does not declare that these
are the only possible semantic types of THIS in every future construction.

The V2 local trace is coherent at the declared argument level: C01 qokeol is
an earlier Point; C02 qokain INITIAL_POINT is then the immediately preceding
Point before dy; the later qokeedy HOUR is not borrowed as an antecedent. With
the newly supplied Point slot and local backward binding, dy can preserve its
old reference function. This is a proposed new construction/type instantiation,
not evidence for a new lexical sense and not an automatic violation of the old
DAY/HOUR examples.

It also is not a demonstrated use of GDT1039's completed-clause reference
registry. That registry's eligible types/publication rules were a separate
experiment. V2 directly binds a prior argument within C02. Importing that
registry would require an explicit Point/publication extension; it cannot be
claimed for free. The local V2 binding can be assessed as authored grammar
without importing that additional machinery.

## The endpoint equality does not meet the selected consequence

B01 lists scope C01/C08, but all actual endpoint arguments come from C01:
qokeol, the two daiin tokens, shckhy and shey. **C08 qoteor does not occur in
either endpoint expression or in an equality binding it to them.** Two numbers
in one statement do not satisfy the decision's requirement that two separately
written statements constrain the same time/phase/ruler argument.

There is also an internal expression discrepancy: B01.variables/function use
`step` for both endpoints, while comparison_contract and READING replace the
second with `return_to`. Neither operation is defined. FRAME_UNITS and
RETURN_UNITS name roles but still do not identify which objects each count
counts. The derivations themselves retain UNBOUND_C01_FRAME_UNIT and
UNBOUND_C01_RETURN_UNIT.

Even assuming the same starting point, sequence and count, equality does not
follow between two otherwise unconstrained operators. If both are explicitly
the same deterministic step with the same arguments, the equality is instead
immediate from those assumed identities and 24=24; it supplies no additional
written check. If `return_to` has a distinct specified meaning, that meaning
and its relationship to the first operation are still needed. These are logical
alternatives, not a numerical replay or a claim that V2 asserts an impossible
24-step return on the seven-cycle.

The physically timed interpretation is an additional open bridge, but it is
not necessary to demonstrate this formal incompleteness. No shared H, phase,
day endpoint or temporal continuity between P1 and P2 is constructed. Preserving
P2's 80 positions and old rules is preservation, not a joint derivation.

## Costs and publication ceiling

The six B labels remain an inventory rather than a certified count of six
atomic nondefault bindings. B01 bundles starting-point/count/unit/sequence
choices and endpoint equality; B04 bundles source choices and a proposed phase
chain; B05 bundles night/day identity and no reset. B06 is primarily reader
bookkeeping. Some could follow from a complete shared grammar, but that grammar
is not supplied. Therefore the binding cap is **uncertified**, not independently
proved to exceed six by some uniquely determined count. The nine-pattern versus
four-production problem is the separate definite cost defect.

No multiword lexical label is automatically a sentence macro. However, the
missing transition, closure and reference predicates cannot be credited to
PHASE_CHANGE/CLOSING_RULER_PHASE merely because their names suggest them.
The zero-macro claim does not fill those missing predicates.

Publish V1, V2, source limits and both critiques as the completed bounded
development attempt. Report exact token preservation, retained unknowns,
the IT role error, the actual pattern cost and absent joint consequence.
Do not publish a full semantic parse, fixed-content contradiction, successful
reference transfer, independent meaning support or a numerical PASS/FAIL.
No solver or replay is warranted on this incomplete contract, and this review
does not authorize another revision or candidate search.
