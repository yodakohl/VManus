# f4v mandrake source gate — stop before image admission

Status: source-only capacity screen, no new Voynich image, text or reserve
access. f84/f84r remain sealed. The already admitted f4v text begins with
the complete group `pchooiin` in all three readings; the existing 179-selector
profile finds one exact occurrence and one first position per reader. GDT1062
shows that a public table associates this written head with MANDRAGORA, but
its botanic reading was produced through a visual/cipher search, not an
independent meaning test. GDT1070 found that 80/89 admitted Herbal-A first
heads are manuscript singletons, so `pchooiin`'s uniqueness is not a
discriminator by itself. The exact f4v image is outside the current visual
admission and was not opened here.

Question before further access: do independent named medieval mandrake
images share enough unusual anatomy to fix a source-side f4v prediction that
could reject or support the public name? A positive source gate would require
the same diagnostic leaf arrangement, fruit placement and root architecture
in at least two independently labelled witnesses, rather than the generic
fact that a root branches or resembles a person. If yes, a separately
registered f4v image admission might change whether MANDRAGORA is retained
as a C0 picture hypothesis; it would still not prove `pchooiin` means it.
Smallest test and budget: inspect two labelled institutional source images
and their metadata before any new target image; 25 minutes for source,
comparison, check and publication, with no decoder or new target access.

The [Morgan M.652 f103v](https://ica.themorgan.org/manuscript/page/186/143825)
(mid-10th century) explicitly labels a male mandrake. Its picture has a
single upright human-shaped dark root/body, a compact crown of broad leaves
and red berry-like forms embedded among them. The independent [Morgan M.873
f61v](https://ica.themorgan.org/manuscript/page/122/159345) (1350–1375)
explicitly labels MANDRAGORA on a different picture: a broad fan of leaves,
two round fruits just beneath it and several thin descending roots. The
jointly visible description is only broad leaves above a branched root with
round reproductive forms; the spatial anatomy and root motif vary sharply.
That source-side union is too generic to be a discriminating f4v image rule.

Decision: **source gate failed for a new f4v admission under this question**.
Do not open f4v or select `pchooiin=mandrake` from its rare first position
and the searched public glossary. A newly located source lineage with two
concordant, unusually specific labelled mandrake drawings, or a separate
authorial written-name relation, would change the input; more generic
mandrake images or another first-head singleton would not. This is missing
source capacity, not a botanical refutation of f4v or a claim that the plant
is not mandrake.
