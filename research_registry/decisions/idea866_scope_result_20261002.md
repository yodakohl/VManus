# IDEA866: source restriction is not an observed patient state

Decision: **NOT_TESTED / missing_design**. This is a proposal correction, not
an executed Voynich experiment or a manuscript contradiction. The original raw
proposal, GDT1105 and GDT966 remain unchanged. No new target content was opened.

The complete owned PGP40129 pomegranate passage has a working feminine-dual
hot modifier with coordinated stomach/liver heads; quince has the single-head
form. That source-language contrast is retained. But the remedy is described
as beneficial for a restricted organ class: it does not assert current heat in
a patient. Pomegranate and quince also have different stated actions. They
are not a controlled efficacy contrast. Neither the woman's subsequent story
nor an omitted liver predicate supplies an explicit negative. The witness is
one leaf, dated only after the tenth century, not securely before1420.

The prereview note fixed three illustrative formulas, not exhaustive historical
readings or clinical laws. The accompanying script produces all16 assignments
and checks Ng => Dg => J, with both reverse implications false. This is only
logical accounting; the assignments are invented, not observed data.

| Hypothetical Hs,Hl,Bs,Bl | Dg | Ng | J | What separates them |
|---|---|---|---|---|

False heat does not imply false benefit. An unreported field is **UNKNOWN**,
not the zero in this table. Collective properties require separately justified
pair-level atoms; neither member properties nor dual morphology settle them.
The source does not independently select a joint-condition reading.

Independent critic review agreed on the defect. For a *property assertion*,
a fixed H(s) AND H(l) followed by an explicit NOT H(s), under the same owner,
domain, time and condition, would contradict that conjunction. H(l) alone
would remain compatible, not thereby confirmed. For the actual generic-benefit
wording, the first separating row instead requires an explicit benefit outcome.
The two designs must not be interchanged.

GDT1105 supplies neither a fixed shared singular/dual modifier family nor the
needed written contradiction. Its27 nominal runs retain46 unbound fields;
its owners, domains and thermal meanings are stipulated alternatives. No raw866
full target key or decisive consumer was nominated. A future concrete C0
reading may be authored without already confirmed vocabulary, but it must fix
that common key and all context before claiming a consequence. Another count
of matching positive fields is insufficient. No significance, translation or
independent confirmation claim follows from this review.
