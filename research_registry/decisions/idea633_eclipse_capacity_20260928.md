# IDEA633: solar/lunar eclipse reversal, capacity screen

2026-09-28. Exploratory target-capacity review, not a source-to-manuscript
test. The proposed relation is directional: in a solar eclipse the Moon blocks
the Sun from an observer; in a lunar eclipse the Earth blocks sunlight from
the Moon. The proposal demands a dated source that explicitly owns both
three-body relations and a complete admitted pair of Voynich records with
the corresponding participants and opposite directions. No source passage
was selected or frozen because the nominated target capacity fails first.

The current best visual leads are f68r2's separate Sun/Moon medallions and
f67v2's Sun/Moon-associated endpoints. [GDT1051](../../experiments/yolo/gdt1051_frozen_grammar_local_application/REPORT.md)
applies the existing word grammar to the complete f68r2 rings and retains
`okoaiin≈Sun` only as an unconfirmed working reading; upper-ring readings
even disagree on the second word. It finds no one-to-one Sun/Moon word
replacement. The [f68r2 source-topology audit](../../experiments/semantic_assumptions/results/fdtw_f68r2_sun_moon_ring_metadata_worth_report.md)
describes the owned medallion/star-ring layout, not an eclipse pair with
Earth, an observer and directed occlusion. [The f67v2 visual audit](../../experiments/semantic_assumptions/results/f67v2_rosettes_native_visual_scaffold_report.md)
retains a center/axis/corner scaffold without identifying object or
directional text values. The prior [RAW569 candidate](../proposals/laufenberg_f85r2_20260926/CELESTIAL_AUTHOR_REPORT.md)
*assigns* a lunar-eclipse synopsis to a different diagram text; it explicitly
remains an authored, alternate-reader-incompatible hypothesis, not an
independent eclipse-label identification. The [IDEA513 metadata gate](idea513_f67v2_prose_metadata_capacity_20260922.md)
also stopped its specific short f67v2 prose test before content access.

Decision: IDEA633 is **not tested**. Sun/Moon depictions alone cannot
distinguish an eclipse mechanism from ordinary celestial pairings. Reopen
only if an admitted paired target shows source-independent ownership of
Sun, Moon, Earth/observer, and the opposing occlusion relations, followed by
a prospectively frozen complete source/target contract. No new image,
transcription, decoder, reserve or f84/f84r access; zero confirmed words.
