This review uses the existing QH1 output in
`research_registry/decisions/qh1_variable_text_frames.json`; it performs no
new source census. “Index” below means the middle group's 1-based
`source_group_index`. Raw groups and separators remain the governing evidence.

## `chol A daiin`: two middle forms at f21v

The QH1 frame contains these two exact, all-reader-concordant rows:

| locus | middle index | raw triplet | readers | internal seams |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| f21v.4 | 5 | `chol daiin daiin` | ZL3b, IT2a, RF1b | four `DEFINITE_SPACE` values |
| f21v.6 | 5 | `chol todaiin daiin` | ZL3b, IT2a, RF1b | four `DEFINITE_SPACE` values |

This is a literal fixed-flank substitution across two lines on the same
folio: the left and right groups remain `chol` and `daiin`, while the middle
changes from `daiin` to `todaiin`. This is descriptive alignment, not proof of a grammatical slot or intended
substitution. The full lines also differ outside the triple (QI1). It does not
identify what either middle means.

GDT628 and GDT630 primary reports contain earlier `chol`/`daiin` material
and working quality/part readings, but those readings are semantic hypotheses
and do not establish this QH1 substitution as a meaning contrast. GDT851's
primary report establishes the same source-fidelity standard for exact
all-reader raw groups, while testing tandem repetition rather than this
variable-middle frame.

These three rows are literal-exact in all three readings. The first two have

recorded by QH3. IT2a/RF1b mark that seam definite:

| locus | middle index | raw triplet | readers |
|---|---:|---|---|

Together these rows show that `ol` and `aiin` can flank at least three
literal middle forms across separate loci while preserving reader agreement.
This does not distinguish a productive grammatical frame from coincidentally
shared frequent flanks; it supplies no semantic assignment.

The GDT630 report's separated and fused OL examples are a relevant structural
predecessor because they preserve raw boundary variation, but its plant-part
and quality glosses are not imported here. GDT851's exact-source report
likewise supports retaining reader-specific raw spellings and separators; it
does not report this `ol A aiin` contrast.
