# IDEA655: action-to-text preselection, 28 September 2026

**Decision: not selected for a fixed meaning test.** This is a capacity review,
not a falsification of a participant/action reading. It uses only the three
already admitted bath images f77r, f82r and f83r and GDT790's complete panel
ownership inventory. No text reserve or new page was opened.

The proposal requires a *physically unambiguous depicted action* whose agent,
patient or recipient can be carried into a complete owned prose block, and a
second admitted block with the same independently defined action. GDT790
establishes ten panel owners and thirteen prose records, but expressly stops at
panel ownership. Its 27 graphical label loci are mainly port, figure-zone,
inflow or outlet addresses. Only ten exact label/prose occurrences exist, and
none supplies a unique written action or actor/patient assignment.

The strongest visible candidates were inspected as a bounded preselection:

| Panel and record | Visible candidate | Why the proposed contrast is not yet testable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| f77r upper arch, P1 | Two endpoint women and an arched multiport connection | No singular pictured transfer act or label that names an actor rather than a port/zone. |
| f77r middle/lower, P2/P3 | Figure with vessel and several outlet-like forms | Contact, flow and possession remain alternative descriptions of the same drawing. |
| f82r top/middle, P1/P2 | Paired figures, central apparatus and a source-tube/recipient topology | The direction of a visible connection is not a unique agent/patient relation in the text; the only middle-panel label `oroldair` is attached to the cross tube. |
| f82r pool, P3 | Multiple figures with two vertical inflows | Figure labels and inflow labels do not isolate one actor or a recurring complete action. |
| f83r upper/lower, P1–P5/Q1–Q2 | Handheld spray-like form, loops, drips and coupled outlets | Even the upper hand contact identifies no exact action word; the labeled lower outlets are local stations. |

The relevant countercase is GDT792: exact `okal` label/prose reuse transfers a
whole-form structural role yet leaves address, member, class and value readings
equivalent. GDT590/595's bath actions and patients are inherited working
semantics, not independent picture-to-word evidence. Replacing them with
`actor`, `patient` or `transfer` here would merely assert the proposed answer.

No lexical, statistical or significance claim follows. Reopen IDEA655 only if a
new admitted image or authorial device fixes **both** a singular action-role
owner and a word/complete-passage consequence that differs from the impersonal
station reading. Re-viewing these gestures or counting nearby words is not a
changed input. Prior project exposure makes these images development material,
not an independent holdout. f84/f84r and reserves remain closed.
