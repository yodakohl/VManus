## Result and exact ownership

Franco's *Ars cantus mensurabilis* supplies a historical rule in which the same lozenge form means one or two minor-semibrevis durations depending on its position in a complete bounded group. The selected printed example has five notes, grouped3|2. It lasts the same amount as a2|3 alternative, but their internal onsets differ. A rival that lengthens the first rather than the second note of the pair even preserves the actual printed grouping and every group total, while predicting a different last onset.

## Complete connected short rule passage

The preceding chapterIV defines the shared form: “Semibrevis autem alia major, alia minor dicitur; harum tamen quelibet uniformiter ad modum losange sic [S] formatur.” Here `[S]` is the modern transcription's replacement for the engraved lozenge, not a source letter.

The following is the complete semibrevis subsection of chapterV, including its continuation beyond the selected example onto p123. Figure slots remain explicit and are wholly available in the cited scans. Latin is public-domain; modern English below is our paraphrase.

> De semibrevibus autem et brevibus idem est judicium in regulis prius dictis. Sed nota semibrevium plures quam tres pro recta brevi non posse accipi, quarum quelibet minor semibrevis dicitur, eo quod minima pars est ipsius recte brevis, ut hic patet:
>
> [CSI122,2: **Mentibus vocibus**, complete printed staff example.]
>
> Nec minus quam duas, quarum prima minor, secunda major semibrevis appellatur. Secunda major pro tanto dicitur, quia duas minores in se includit:
>
> [CSI122,3: **Sanguinem lavat**, complete printed staff example.]
>
> Sed si immediate due semibreves, pro recta brevi posite, tres sequuntur, vel e converso:
>
> [CSI122,4: **Quid plura cedit**, complete selected five-note printed staff example.]
>
> tunc divisio modi inter tres et duas, vel e converso, est ponenda, ut in predicto exemplo apparet.
>
> Pro altera autem brevi minus quam quatuor semibreves accipi non possunt:
>
> [CSI123,1: **Gaude virgo mater**, complete printed staff example.]
>
> Nec plures quam sex, ut hic:
>
> [CSI123,2: **Ave Maria gratia plena**, complete printed staff example.]
>
> Eo quod altera brevis in se duas rectas includit; per quod patet quorumdam error, qui quandoque tres semibreves pro altera brevi ponunt, aliquando vero duas.

The rule gives two ways of filling a recta brevis: three minors or a minor followed by a major that equals two minors. When a two-note group and a three-note group meet, the source requires a division between them. The concluding rule matters: an altera brevis contains two rectae and admits four through six semibreves; it does not license arbitrary durations for an unmarked group of two or three.

## All variables and steps for the selected printed exemplar

Native observations on p122,col2: a C-shaped clef, four staff lines, five lozenge noteheads, and one internal division after the third note. The first three notes share a staff level; the fourth and fifth each rise one staff-step. The text caption is **Quid plura cedit**. No extra rest, longa, note, terminal duration or lyric continuation is introduced. Absolute octave is left unassigned.

Let `u>0` be one minor's duration. Setting `u=1` is analytical normalization, not historical written numeral notation. Let `S` label the lozenge and `D` the division. These are modern descriptive labels with no Voynich assignment. The full observed type sequence is `S S S D S S`.

1. The actual `D` divides the entire example into group sizes3 and2. Group ownership is visible; it is not fitted to a desired total.
2. In the first group the source makes all three notes minor: `[u,u,u]`.
3. In the second group it makes the first minor and the second major: `[u,2u]`.
4. Both groups therefore last `3u`; the whole example lasts `6u`. The division adds no sounded time.
5. Full durations in units of `u`: `[1,1,1,1,2]`; onsets `[0,1,2,3,4]`; endings `[1,2,3,4,6]`.

These arrays are **computed consequences**, not a source-written table. The source rule is finite: group size2 or3, within-group position, and the known recta-brevis context determine every duration. There is no per-occurrence key, guessed exception, arithmetic cipher, generic pronoun, or modern instruction language.
