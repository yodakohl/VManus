## IDEA000374 — delayed age cohorts and production

Existing card: `research_registry/proposals/raw_f82r_cohort_transition_narrative.json`.
Status remains `RAW_UNREVIEWED`, untested. The complete exposed P1 paragraph is
`f82r.1`–`f82r.9` in
`research_registry/proposals/translation_programs_20260912/work/P28/READING_N2.md`.
Recorded repeated whole forms include `qokaiin` at `.3`, `.4`, and `.9`,
`qokal` at `.2`, `.4`, `.5`, and `.7`, `qokeedy` at `.2` and `.5`, and
`cheey` at `.1`, `.7`, and `.8`; all nine lines are mandatory context.

The proposed shared meanings are adult/reproductive cohort (`qokaiin`),
newborn or not-yet-reproductive cohort (`qokal`), production at the next
boundary (`qokeedy`), maturation (`qokeey`), and survival (`cheey`). The
content consequence is delayed production: with adults M and young J,
`M_next=M+J`, `J_next=M`, so equal current totals with different mixtures
produce different next totals. The owned source analogue is the complete
Fibonacci rabbit account in
`research_registry/work_batches/ten_hours_20260915/AGE_COHORT_POPULATION_SUPPLY.md`;
it explicitly distinguishes newly born pairs from pairs that reproduce at the
next month and supplies the full 1,2,3,5,...,377 progression.

What remains unknown is decisive: the P28 lines do not independently identify
population, units, starting mixture, time clauses, or numerical fields. The
same f82r page also contains the separate P2 raw card (IDEA000379), where some
of the same surface forms receive a celestial hypothesis; this is a useful
cross-card conflict, not evidence for either meaning.

The closest closed predecessors are separate experiments, not one combined
model. W97 is the fixed individual-genealogy contract on f83r and was
contradicted by self-reference; GDT969 is the separate Fibonacci-generated
root-trace/writer contract and was contradicted at its fixed input/high code
stage. Their falsifiers are respectively identity/pointer inconsistency and
fixed code-contract failure. IDEA000374 has a different falsifier: hold the
whole P1 paragraph and require a global cohort transition; if repeated forms do
not preserve adult/young roles or the same paragraph cannot distinguish
delayed production from immediate doubling using a written next-total
consequence, this card fails. A population account is not a genealogy repair.

Correction to the retained raw card: its historical predecessor shorthand
`W97 / GDT969` conflates these two reports. That raw proposal remains byte
preserved and unselected; this appendix uses the separated attribution above.

The proposed shared meanings are one ordinal hour (`qokeedy`), a complete
day-night unit (`qokedy`), ruling planet (`qokaiin`), night (`lchedy`), and
the next succession member (`cheey`). The content discriminator is the
relation between two cycles, not a seven-item list: if H is the seven-member

recorded in the existing source packet; its status and electronic-text limits
remain as stated in the raw card.

equal-hour convention, or clause boundary is read. The P2 complete paragraph
may instead be static material or another register. GDT951's fixed zodiac
tuple renderers were contradicted, but that does not test this content model.

The closest closed predecessor is GDT951's order-free tuple renderer, whose

IDEA000379 has a different falsifier: preserve one complete P2 paragraph and

derivations; if a shared assignment permits independent period-seven lists,

meaning fails. No tuple renderer or circular capacity repair is implied.

or nominal measurement/list reading gives a different state or leaves lineage
unbound. The complete paragraph must retain all unknown prelude groups; no
quarter word is assumed.

The main unknown is already recorded as the readiness blocker: ZL has 37
unread prelude groups and IT has 10; no written reference whole, common
quantity lineage, or final quarter result is independently owned. The source
records are existing guarded snapshots, not new text access.

The closest predecessor is IDEA079's idempotent-versus-cumulative repetition

whole f107r context, a shared `chey` must preserve an operand lineage across
the two occurrences; if the second occurrence is independently reset, if the
same operator yields the cap/zero rival, or if the unresolved prelude cannot
bind the required quantity without free per-position meanings, the serial
halving reading fails. A numerical Q/4 endpoint alone is explicitly
insufficient.

### IDEA000409 — one successor relation shared across P1 and P2

This card holds a repeated whole form to one abstract successor relation with
one direction and arity across both complete passages. Its source-owned
constraints are different and concrete: Fibonacci's complete rabbit account
distinguishes newly born from reproducing pairs and explicitly checks adjacent
totals (`1+2` and `144+233=377`); Cassius Dio XXXVII.18–19 binds a first-hour
planet to a daily successor through both a 24-hour advance and a seven-member
order. The target fields that would carry predecessor/successor identity are
not independently known. The card therefore fails if complete P1/P2 records do
not expose those fields or if a candidate requires changed relation arity or
direction between passages. It is distinct from W97's person-pointer test,
GDT969's fixed Fibonacci code, GDT608's formal composition, and IDEA000406's
order-sensitive intermediate state.

### IDEA000410 — one same-result relation over two derivations in P1 and P2

This card tests a shared scope relation whose two route clauses address one
result in each complete passage. Fibonacci supplies the written narrative plus
the consecutive-total margin calculation; Cassius Dio supplies the hourly and
skip-two derivations of the same next ruler. The target would have to expose
two route fields, a scope boundary, and one result field; none is currently
meaning-bound. This is a route-equivalence discriminator, not IDEA000408's
two-input convergence, IDEA000379's single-P2 cycle hypothesis, or a count/list
test. It fails if the target lacks two recoverable derivations or if the fit
needs independent meanings for every occurrence.
