## Result and exact ownership

A complete connected chapter supplies the requested identity contract. Water distilled from a glass distillatory is received in a named clean glass amphora; **the extracted water** must then be put back over **those three elements** retained at the bottom of the original distillation. The same bath is explicitly reused. The source subsequently fractionates the material further. It does **not** describe one unchanged, undivided substrate following a general circulation loop.

The mechanism is directly visible in **Wellcome MS708, f9v**, institutionally dated **1443, Ratisponae**. The chapter continues throughf10r and ends before the next Canon. The source work is Johannes de Rupescissa's *De consideratione quintae essentiae*. This packet also transcribes the entire corresponding **Basel1561 print, Cap.XII, pp45–48**, preserving its conflicts. Direct medieval collation confirms the critical return clause; it does not certify every character of a full diplomatic1443transcription.

## Written identity, without a false conservation claim

The1561wording is:

> Accipe iterum aquam extractam, & repone in vase super illa tria elementa, & repone in eodem balneo per septem dies, & bene misceantur ibi, taliterque claude, vt nihil distilletur.

A normalized, abbreviation-expanded reading of1443f9v is:

> accipe iterum aquam extractam pone in vase super tria elementa et repone in eodem balneo per septem dies

The manuscript has **pone** at the first return instruction, and lacks the printed **illa** before **tria**; these are not silently harmonized. Both readings explicitly refer back to the extracted water. The immediately preceding residue statement binds the three elements to the retained bottom fraction. **Eodem** explicitly qualifies the bath; the first return vessel is identified by its retained contents rather than an explicit phrase “the same glass vessel.”

The receiver and distillatory are actual named source roles; D/A/W/R labels in the JSON are only our bookkeeping. The bath's heating water is separate from process water. A source-theoretic element class is not automatically a unique physical portion. A water fraction becomes a carrier for another fraction after reunion; it need not remain chemically unchanged.

## Witness conflicts

The [Furnivall1866 print, pp12–13](https://archive.org/download/bookofquinteesse00furn_0/bookofquinteesse00furn_0.pdf), edited from Sloane73(c1460–70), was read natively. Its titlepage is **MDCCCLXVI**; the [Gutenberg revised1889 text](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/17179/17179-h/17179-h.htm) is a searchable companion, not another medieval witness.

1. English omits the explicit early extracted-water return. After the bath's inability to raise the other elements it says “and so [take] þo þre elementis” and proceeds to the seven-day bath. The bracketed **[take]** is an editorial supplement. Its later explanation that water is put again does not replace the missing early clause. The1443Latin independently attests that clause.
2. Latin1561p47prints **aqua nigerrima**; p48requires calcination of **terram nigerrimam**. This is an **internal printed conflict**. Native1443f10r appears to read earth at the bottom, and English explicitly reads black earth. These witnesses motivate a textual variant; they do not license silent correction of1561.
3. English second incorporation is7days; Latin1561allows7or8. English final bottom fire fraction is red water; Latin1561calls it red oil.

The exact writing of every minor1443variant remains uncollated. No critical edition or reconstructed authorial text is asserted.

## Consequences and honest rival limits

- Replacing the first receiver output with fresh same-type water preserves generic chemistry roles and a possible schedule, but violates **aquam extractam** as an actual-result reference.
- Returning that output over a newly supplied same-type residue breaks the joint retained-bottom/reference contract. A model cannot obtain this relation from repeated generic material names alone.
- One unchanged substrate following every step cannot represent separate receiver products, set-aside air, retained fire/earth and black residue. The complete sequence requires multiple concurrently available fractions.
- Reusing the earlier recovered water for the later4:1addition is **underdetermined**, not refuted. Identity, sufficiency and recovery quantity are unwritten there.

These distinctions constrain a hypothetical interpretation only after the complete text's operations, portion references, vessel roles and qualifiers have a shared writing contract. No such Voynich contract is supplied here. The historical mechanism is not a Voynich semantic discriminator by itself.
