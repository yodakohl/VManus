## A — complete transport planning with safety at every state

**New content:** a whole transport account describes actors, cargo, vehicle,
capacity, initial distribution, restrictions, crossings and a final safe
inventory. The same object persists across every crossing. A boat must be
where the next loading happens; disembarking changes the shore state; all
unattended states must satisfy the same compatibility rule. Complete accounts
may contain different numbers of crossings, nested reasons or explicit failed
attempts, under one globally fixed grammar. The source's seven- or nine-crossing
solution is an example, not a target length.

bundle across a river without damage, in a boat carrying at most two of these

goat. The man accompanies each crossing. The following state table includes
every trip; an empty cargo cell is the expressly narrated return by the man.
The usual unattended wolf–goat and goat–cabbage incompatibilities formalize the
problem's damage condition; the printed problem does not separately state those
two dietary axioms. This assumption must remain explicit.

**Different rival consequence.** Exchange the cargo of trips1and3 while keeping
all directions, cargo occurrence counts and final inventory fixed. The first
trip now leaves goat and cabbage alone together. The terminal inventory still
matches, but the global all-states safety condition fails immediately. A model
that merely enumerates transfers or checks the endpoint misses this distinction.
An exact source-only replay verified this difference; no unknown text was fit.

**XIX, independent capacity contrast.** Two adults each weigh one loaded-wagon
unit; the two children together weigh that unit; the boat can carry at most one
such unit. The complete source solution is: both children cross; one returns;
mother crosses; the other child returns; both children cross; one returns;
father crosses; the other child returns; both children cross. Source ownership
covers the initial weights, capacity, every return and the final no-shipwreck
assertion. The text says the children **jointly** weigh one unit; equal half-unit
weights are not needed and are not silently inserted. The printed solution
licenses each child rowing. A count-only two-passenger rule admits an adult
plus child, whereas positive child weight makes that load exceed the actual
weight bound. This is a semantic rival distinct from permuting the trips.

**Remaining symmetries and bindings.** Shore reversal and exchange of equivalent
children remain symmetries. Under the undirected safety graph alone, wolf and
cabbage are interchangeable endpoints; it cannot identify their natural names.
A shared syntax for direction, co-travel, exclusion and reference is still to
be proposed. No boat or safely separated cargo arrangement has been nominated
on admitted Voynich material. Figures near water do not by themselves establish
this ownership. A connected whole-text hypothesis may be explored without a
known noun, but should fail if it requires missing voyages, local identity
changes or unrecorded capacity exceptions.

## B — complete alloy reasoning with quantity and composition

quantities, desired grade, permitted additions, intermediate mixtures and
proofs. Its constraints are simultaneous: total weight is additive; silver
content is additive; fineness is silver content divided by total weight;
repeated named mixtures retain their composition. A grammatical clause may
assert, choose, compute, scale, explain or verify. The number and order of
legitimate explanatory clauses vary. No decimal/opcode field format is proposed.

## Supply decision and practical limits

A and B have complete owned worked examples with distinct intermediate-state
or constituent-content rivals. B additionally preserves the historical source's
own non-unique valid solutions. C and D add genuinely different content systems,
but have weaker witness/target ownership. All four remain raw; no manuscript
meaning or experiment is selected, and no decoder/control corpus was built.

The smallest useful later step would be a concrete joint writing-and-owner
proposal for one system, with all grammar alternatives and symmetries explicit.
A source example alone cannot choose among these meanings. A future fit that
uses per-token senses, silently inserts missing steps, or drops explanatory
clauses has not met the proposed whole-content contract. Conversely, failure
of a fixed serializer would exclude that serializer, not automatically this
entire semantic system.
