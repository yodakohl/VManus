## Exact source and editorial scope

The source is Max Wellmann’s *Pedanii Dioscuridis Anazarbei de materia medica libri quinque* (Weidmann 1906–1914), represented by the Harvard/Perseus EpiDoc 2018 cache. The XML header identifies the editor, publication dates, digital encoders and Creative Commons licence. The target passages are precisely **Dioscorides IV.20, Xiphion** and **Book III.126–128**. The Satyrion name occurs as the main head in III.128; in III.126, `σατύριον` is in the `RV` name apparatus, while the main text head is `ὄρχις` (“dog’s orchis” in its first naming clause). These locations must not be collapsed.

## Source-derived composite hypothesis and nearest rival

**R1 — paired-part/state operator.** A content system may encode one material with a physical part/state (upper/lower; thicker/thin; full/soft; larger/smaller; tender/dry), then a preparation route and an effect. The discriminating source consequence is not plant-name recurrence: changing the paired part/state changes effect direction or effect class while retaining the material frame. Xiphion supplies upper-root extraction/menstrual/sexual/hernia effects versus lower-root infertility; Orchis supplies larger/smaller reproductive outcomes and a tender/dry sexual inhibition contrast. The unmarked root versus explicit dry root, and boiled/wine routes, are preparation or material-state distinctions, not calendar duration.

**R2 — local morphology plus independent remedies.** Root geometry, size and the explicitly stated dry state are descriptive fields; the first III.127 root state is unmarked. Each application sentence is locally attached, and the apparent oppositions are item-specific reports. The same part/state operator need not recur across entries or predict an effect change.

**Smallest different consequence.** In an already admitted complete paragraph, R1 predicts a fixed paired-part/state relation that co-varies with route and a changed or mutually exclusive outcome; exchanging the paired state changes the outcome while retaining the material frame. R2 predicts no stable cross-branch polarity or cross-entry recurrence. A repeated name or shared whole form alone distinguishes neither.

## Limits and corrections

There is **no explicit elapsed time or calendar timing** in III.126–128 or IV.20. “Tender” versus “dry” is a material-state contrast. The reproductive claims are hearsay, and III.126’s final reciprocal-dissolution wording remains partly ambiguous even though the dry form’s inhibition/attenuation direction is clear. Xiphion’s upper/lower pair must not be silently equated with Orchis’ larger/smaller/full/soft pair. The source extract excludes critical apparatus notes from the main text but preserves the complete clauses; editorially supplied text and damaged abbreviations are identified in the table. The strongest failure is missing independent target binding for part/state, route and outcome, which would leave R1 relabellable as R2. This remains a source comparison and does not reopen GDT963.
