# Semantic content next supply

Bounded source-only proposals for the live composition baseline. This note
uses only already admitted reports and their cached artifacts; it does not
open Voynich text, images, f84/f84r, or reserves. The proposals preserve whole
forms, their reusable parts, order, residual identity, and entry context. No
Voynich form is assigned a confirmed English meaning.

## Proposal 1 — three-sided quantity–content–closure frame

**Possible whole reading.** A complete quantity expression can introduce a
measured content or part only when an exact neighboring whole supplies the
content candidate and a second ordered whole closes or completes that local
field. The reading is occurrence-scoped: the same residual whole remains
unresolved when one of the three links is absent.

**Multistep architecture.**

1. Keep the complete quantity whole and its internal parts intact.
2. Stratify by entry context and line position before choosing attachment
   direction: GDT760's working orientation is rightward at line start,
   leftward in the line middle, and unlicensed at line end.
3. Require a neighboring complete content candidate such as the report's
   `cheor/sheor` or `cheo/sheo` working pair, retaining the dry/moist rival
   labels as hypotheses.
4. Require a following endpoint/closure whole in the same complete field and
   preserve the observed part order. Without this third relation, retain only
   a quantity-contact observation.

**Why it may fail.** The apparent endpoint may be another item or a layout
convention; the dry/moist candidates are renderer-derived; quantity forms may
count actions rather than material; and the line-position effect may be a
field layout rule rather than syntax. GDT806's zero new roles warns that even
stable whole contexts do not independently bind content.

**Different consequence.** A pass would restrict a material/part reading to
three-link frames and weaken free global readings of quantity-adjacent wholes.
A null would leave GDT760's pairwise, position-conditioned observation in
place without upgrading it to a content grammar. This is the distinct design
registered as [IDEA000340](../../proposals/quantity_content_closure_sandwich_20260915.json).

## Proposal 2 — complete entry-field gating of residual content

**Possible whole reading.** A residual whole is a content carrier only inside
a complete entry field with an incoming compositional part sequence, an
occurrence-specific whole identity, and an outgoing state/endpoint relation.
The same surface may remain an opaque entry key or a general carrier when one
of those field relations is missing. This uses GDT608's part-order/residual
split and GDT805's entry-context profiles jointly.

**Multistep architecture.** First classify the local entry context and hard
chunk boundaries; then parse the complete reusable part sequence; then retain
the whole residual as the candidate field head; finally require the next
complete whole or endpoint to agree with the same field. A practical reading
would therefore be “entry field: [content candidate] → [state/result]” while
leaving the candidate's named substance open. `okal` and `ol` remain explicit
rivals from GDT805 rather than fixed meanings.

**Why it may fail.** GDT805's projected context axes are not independent
semantics, and GDT806 found no new role after stronger controls. A field can
also contain a title, amount, or process operator with the same order. Whole
residual identity may dominate the apparent part sequence, and entry
boundaries can be ambiguous.

**Different consequence.** A pass would make entry context a required
semantic gate and would prohibit upgrading isolated whole forms from local
neighbor counts. A null would retain the existing opaque/material rivals and
would leave composition as a formal predictor only. No card was added because
the role-gating question is near IDEA000009, IDEA000024, GDT805, and the
closed GDT806 scope.

## Proposal 3 — ordered action–argument–endpoint residual frame

**Possible whole reading.** The portable remainder seen after an action-head
change can be read as a complete argument frame only when its source/material,
action, target/result, and endpoint occur in the observed order. The whole
residual supplies the argument relation; entry context supplies the specific
referent. This is a clause-level reading of GDT496's 242/242 portable
remainders, not a claim that any component is a translated word.

**Multistep architecture.** Identify the complete action-family whole and its
reusable parts; verify that the remaining whole sequence preserves object,
relation, and order across the alternate action envelopes; bind the referent
from the preceding entry context; then require a following result/closure
position before producing a practical sentence such as “apply the previously
stated item to the target.” If the referent is absent, render the relation as
unresolved rather than inventing a noun.

**Why it may fail.** GDT496's portable remainder is a reader/workshop
construction, and its inherited argument varies across families. Stable order
can be a recipe convention; action heads can be labels; and a context-derived
referent can make every rival appear coherent. IDEA000107 also shows that
invariant type and invariant meaning are not separable without an independent
endpoint.

**Different consequence.** A pass would move content decisions from isolated
whole glosses to complete ordered frames and would preserve “previously
stated item” as the maximum safe rendering when no independent referent is
available. A null would retain GDT496's formal action-substitution result but
remove its use as a semantic content architecture. No card was added because
the proposed action/argument relation is already covered by GDT496,
IDEA000018, and IDEA000107; the added requirement of a complete endpoint is
recorded here as a review condition rather than a new claim.

## Boundaries and next review

These are exploratory hypotheses with working content labels, not selected
experiments. They do not alter GDT965/966, titles, marks, reserves, route
state, or any global ledger. The smallest follow-up for IDEA000340 is a
source-first enumeration from already cached artifacts with exact whole
boundaries, line-position strata, entry-context strata, and a predeclared
endpoint definition. Stop if endpoint labels require inherited renderer
meaning or if the three-way relation does not separate from matched pairwise
and independent-field controls.
