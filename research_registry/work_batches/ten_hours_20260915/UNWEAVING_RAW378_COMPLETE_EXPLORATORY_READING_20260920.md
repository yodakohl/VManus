## Complete reading

> Weaving, as opposed to unweaving, the cloth. Completion of the cloth is a prerequisite for marriage. In secret, the woman unravels it. By day she publicly pledges that prerequisite. Until the cloth is finished, she weaves it row by row. The first and second activities on the cloth have daytime and nighttime, respectively. A handmaid informs the wooers; they discover the concealed action at night. Then, under compulsion, the woman weaves it to completion.
