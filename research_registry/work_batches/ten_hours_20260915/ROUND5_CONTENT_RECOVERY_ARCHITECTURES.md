## A. IDEA000326 — evidential commitment and correction

**Primary example.** Pliny, *Naturalis Historia* XVII.35,
[Perseus chapter](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.02.0137%3Abook%3D17%3Achapter%3D35), reports a former belief about
box-tree cuttings and then says experience ended that belief. The same chapter
also gives five vine-support arrangements. This is a source-level distinction
between reported explanation, observed correction, and resulting classification.

**Target endpoint.** GDT768's already admitted complete f17r.5 line has the
consecutive part-like cells `cthy chor shor` followed by further fields; GDT625
records the complete f29v.1 local entry. A report/correction reading predicts
that a target must contain an independently visible attribution or correction
relation carrying one subject into a replacement consequence. A neutral
inventory reading predicts equal-status co-listing. A rare form, position, or
repetition cannot serve as the cue. Current target reports expose no such
independently bound cue, so this remains source precedent only.

## B. IDEA000324 — feature taxonomy with an exception

**Primary example.** Pliny, *Naturalis Historia* XV,
[Perseus Book XV](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Aabo%3Aphi%2C0978%2C001%3A15), distinguishes named fruit
variants by observable properties such as colour, shape, origin, flavour,
ripening, and seed presence; the later storage discussion distinguishes
general preservation procedures from fruit-specific exceptions.

**Target endpoint.** The already admitted f17r.5 sequence and the complete
f29v.1 prefix could be read as parent → selected variant/part → use/result, or
as a flat part/state inventory. The source architecture predicts mutually
exclusive values in a fixed feature slot and a changed downstream use only for
the exceptional class. The rival permits co-present fields and no exception
transition. Existing GDT625/GDT768 observations provide parallel nominal
fields but no independently fixed parent, feature slot, or exception marker;
therefore the target consequence is not presently licensed.

## C. IDEA000335 — institutional status transition

**Primary example.** Benedict, Rule ch.43, [OSB text](https://archive.osb.org/rb/text/rbemjo2.html), gives late arrival after the signal → last-place
placement → completion and public satisfaction; an abbot may permit choir
re-entry, but satisfaction remains due afterward.

**Target endpoint.** GDT928's already admitted complete-paragraph cache is the
registered endpoint. A status reading predicts a trigger, visible demotion,
repair, and re-entry, with permission changing re-entry timing but not deleting
repair. An ordinary procedural reading predicts ordered actions without a
persistent actor state. No current target report independently identifies
actor, authority, demotion, or satisfaction fields, so this too is a source
architecture rather than an executable target test.

## Decision

These three mechanisms are genuinely different—epistemic correction,
feature/exception taxonomy, and institutional state carry—but none currently
derives a target meaning without importing an unknown marker. Do not add a raw
card or select an experiment. The next useful advance requires an admitted
target observation that fixes one of the relevant relations independently
(attribution/correction, mutually exclusive feature slot, or status carry).
