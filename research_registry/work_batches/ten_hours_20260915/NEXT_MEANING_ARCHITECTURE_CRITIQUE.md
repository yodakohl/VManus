# Next meaning architecture: independent selection critique

2026-09-15. Source and primary-report review only. No target search, new image
inspection, code fitting, reserve access, contacts, or global-file changes.
This review follows GDT967's closure; it does not reopen its two failed models.

**Recommendation: develop one small latitude-workbook writing contract before
any further solver work.** Its useful unknown is whether one compositional
notation can read complete calculations whose explicitly written answers agree
with reused operands and opposite correction branches. A fixed-width table of
five freely named columns is not an adequate new design. The second nomination,
geometric construction, has a concrete operational semantics but is ready only
for the bounded ownership/completeness preflight specified below. I do not
recommend the current De balneis operator cards as the immediate target test.

**not** prohibit a complete provisional reading without previously confirmed

A fully specified external content hypothesis can have falsifiable joint
consequences even when some of its names remain unidentified.

[GDT607](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt607_boundary_word_disentanglement/REPORT.md)

behavior. A proposed writing mechanism must retain exact forms and explain
reusable combinations. It need not turn every distributional residual into an
extra semantic exception: legal compound types and contexts themselves can
produce different whole-form distributions. Conversely, neither a bare-letter
translation nor one arbitrary gloss per wholeword follows from these findings.

[GDT611](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt611_lexical_slot_permutation_audit/REPORT.md)

[GDT599](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt599_remaining_action_object_completion/REPORT.md)

[GDT936](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt936_clause_condition_motion_state/REPORT.md)

The positive structural baseline also does not rescue the failed GDT616

## Nomination 1: a typed latitude workbook

**Semantic hypothesis.** Some complete paragraphs are compact records of a
noon solar-altitude calculation. They state an observed altitude `h`, a
declination magnitude `d` and north/south condition, an explicitly written
corrected equinoctial altitude `e`, and an explicitly written latitude `phi`.
With the hypothesized degree constant `Q=90`:

```
north: e = h - d             south: e = h + d
phi = Q - e
```

The branch condition changes the behavior of one correction operator. Its
result is then reused by the complement operator. Computing an unwritten
answer is insufficient: the manuscript must contain both checks under the
same quantity and numeral code. Equal numeric value, quantity role, and
physical observation are three different things.

The source support is the **complete edited** Chaucer II.25, rubric and lines
1–60, in [the source dossier](ASTROLABE_II25_COMPLETE_SOURCE.md), transcription
SHA-256 `8a6691961db5dea8ef02046e3bc2ff5b0acd138f6f3656d501799d9c654bafd1`.
It supplies one exact `90-38=52` example, an approximate north correction
involving almost 56 and almost 18 with 38 plus odd minutes, an unnumbered
south addition/complement branch, and a fixed-star alternative. These are not
three exact worked calculations. The Oxford qualification and the opening
definitions must remain in the source receipt. The proposed worksheet is a
new generative notation for this algorithm, **not a claim that these complete
paragraphs copy Chaucer's complete prose**. It therefore does not silently
delete that prose or turn its alternatives into successive observations.

Provenance correction: root's native inspection established that the previously
claimed Rawl. D.913 fol.29r image shows a rete/zodiac discussion, not II.25.
The old dossier claim of native II.25 verification is not evidence. The edited
text remains usable on its own; manuscript location and diplomatic spelling
are separate unfinished source questions.

**Root's final candidate is the smallest defensible writing language.** One
complete paragraph contains exactly these seven instructions:

```
OBS_ALT(h) OBS_DECL_BRANCH(d) CORRECT ASSERT_EQ(e)
QUADRANT(90) COMPLEMENT ASSERT_LAT(phi)
```

The paragraph boundary closes the record; no additional END word, region name,
noon word or Sun word is invented. The solar/noon setting belongs to the fixed
worksheet interpretation, not to an additionally decoded lexical item. Every
written group must be consumed by an instruction or its numeric operand.

Treat the proposed spellings as **two fixed global models**: FUSED uses one
complete group for each instruction (seven groups); SEPARATE writes each of the
five numeric instruction names and numerals in separate groups, leaving CORRECT
and COMPLEMENT alone (twelve groups). Do not introduce a per-instruction or
per-paragraph menu of fusion choices after seeing targets. The initially
nominated explicit region/END architecture in my review has been superseded by
this more economical pre-fit candidate; no target result selected that change.

Every numeric instruction in FUSED has a fixed field prefix followed by its
numeric payload. For example, the ALT and EQ prefixes differ, but the payload
for value 38 is identical in both. Ten **distinct**, globally shared,
equal-width codewords spell ordinary decimal digits. Their common width is
finite because the target groups are finite. Every field prefix and opcode is
a fixed distinct literal string. NORTH, SOUTH and EQUINOX have distinct
DECL prefixes. SEPARATE uses the same principle with a whole numeral group.
There is no independently assigned wholeword value for every number, no null,
no per-paragraph key, and no retrospective orthographic normalization.

Root's proposed domains are `h,e,phi in 1..89`, declination magnitude at most24,
and `Q=90`. **Require NORTH and SOUTH to have d in 1..24; EQUINOX alone has
 d=0.** If zero is allowed for NORTH/SOUTH, three nominally different branches
can all implement `e=h` and never test opposite correction signs. The upper
bound24 is a finite hypothesis bound, not a newly established exact historical
solar limit. Preserve leading-zero rules explicitly; ordinary decimal notation
should not acquire optional leading zeros to help a fit.

Fixed instruction positions can supply context for decoding. Distinct prefixes
alone do not prove that all concatenated compounds are globally unambiguous.
If the model permits a fused whole to have different readings in different
instruction positions, that is a declared contextual interpretation; later
applications of the key must use the same complete grammar. It must not be
reported as an unqualified wholeword dictionary. No free per-word interpretation
is introduced by this warning.

`OBS_ALT` and `OBS_DECL_BRANCH` load their two quantities; CORRECT applies the
written branch to them. ASSERT_EQ checks the explicitly written intermediate
and stores it. COMPLEMENT uses that stored value and the explicitly written
quadrant; ASSERT_LAT checks the explicitly written result. Merely computing
answers that are not written does not pass. This is a short, connected,
human-usable calculation notation with directed quantity composition, exact
instruction identity, and entry context. Those features are compatible with
the structural baseline, although no historical evidence currently identifies
this particular notation, literal EVA alphabet, digit width or serialization.

For example, a **constructed**, exact north record can read: “Observed solar
altitude56 degrees; north declination18 degrees. Correct: equinoctial altitude
38 degrees. Quadrant90 degrees. Take the complement: latitude52 degrees.” A
constructed south record can use altitude20, declination18, equinoctial altitude
38, latitude52. A constructed equinoctial record can use altitude38,
declination0, equinoctial altitude38, latitude52. These are examples of complete
program meanings, not transcriptions of three Chaucer examples. The south
numbers are newly constructed, and the source's north example is approximate.

**Smallest adequate experiment.** Freeze both global serializations, domains,
source receipt, complete target intake, code conditions and computational caps
before any filters. Require one complete NORTH, SOUTH and EQUINOX paragraph on
three distinct physical leaves under one global key. Numeric values should be
jointly decoded variables, not the convenient example tuples above. The three
observations can concern different locations: **do not impose equal latitude
or equal physical observation without a written region-identity mechanism**.
The cross-paragraph binding is the shared digit code, quadrant and instruction
lexicon, together with execution of every written check.

Use every frame in an already fixed complete-paragraph panel as a candidate;
never search for an internal seven- or twelve-group window. Report all scope
exclusions and alternate readings separately. A panel restricted to12–24groups
cannot test the seven-group serialization; a broader inherited complete-paragraph
panel must be named prospectively if that branch is to run. No new raw scope or
boundary reconstruction follows a capacity failure. The already exposed pages
are discovery material, not independent confirmation.

First test the sound necessary literal/length/equality conditions and
three-leaf capacity; stop without an arithmetic decoder if their complete domain
is empty. If capacity survives, solve the exact joint numeric and writing model.
Independently re-encode the complete paragraphs and execute all seven
instructions. Query alternative full key/value assignments for every claimed
recovered quantity. Report all solution families or computational unknowns.
Freeze a complete candidate key before applying it to other admitted paragraphs;
one unsuccessful completed key does not refute every unenumerated key family.
A few sound synthetic counterexamples suffice for the evaluator; do not build
another large control corpus or fluency objective.

This is different from
[GDT909](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt909_worked_division_chains/REPORT.md):
there is no paragraph partition into three identical rows and no literal
wholeword next-divisor transfer. Equal quantities can occur under different
role prefixes. GDT909's zero does not logically imply zero for this model. It
is different from
[GDT902](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt902_treviso_complete_multiplication_register/REPORT.md):
there are no copied44equations, fixed8block count profiles, or erased global
background. The new content constraint is a generated, typed calculation with
written answers. Nevertheless, seven/twelve-group serializations and constant
opcodes remain restrictive. If they die on wholeword equality or length,
report a small notation exclusion, not newly tested astronomical meaning. Do
not repair it with gallows/header nulls, optional prose or another row width.

**Identifiability limits and decision.** Addition/subtraction alone permit
common positive scaling of all unknown quantities, including an unknown Q.
Hypothesizing Q=90 and a decimal degree grammar fixes that gauge; arithmetic
does not independently discover the unit. Abstract north/south names can also
be exchanged with branch assignments unless further written constraints
distinguish the models. Fixed decimal composition rules out many arbitrary
numeric relabelings, but uniqueness must be checked in the actual joint model.
A correct sum does not identify the Sun, a region or geographic direction.

A complete positive would supply a constrained connected provisional reading
and justify another fixed consequence under that key. Multiple keys leave the
reading unidentified; a complete negative closes this writing/algorithm/panel
conjunction. All outcomes are meaningful at that limited scope. The test cannot
alone distinguish an authentic astronomical workbook from an otherwise coded
arithmetic system with the same relations.

Budget recommendation: ten minutes to finalize the exact source-only contract,
then at most fifty minutes including implementation, exact inference,
validation and publication. If the contract still needs free filler or undefined
context expansion after the first checkpoint, stop before a decoder. With the
nonzero-branch and global-serialization conditions above, root's candidate is
ready for this bounded experiment. This is the strongest immediate next step.

## Nomination 2: execute a whole geometric construction

Existing [IDEA000125's proposal](../../proposals/geometric_construction_program.json)
already gives a concrete language: `JOIN`, `CIRCLE`, `INTERSECT`, `STOP`, with
two seed points, creation-order references and one shared compositional symbol
code. Keep that finite language; do not create another generic inverse-graphics
platform. Its meaning comes from execution in the plane: a circle through a
previous point imposes a metric equality, and a later intersection must exist
at the computed position. An incidence-isomorphic drawing with incompatible
radii is an actual counterexample, not another lexical permutation.

A minimal complete source example is Euclid I.1: two circles centred on the
given segment's opposite endpoints, a selected common intersection, and joins
back to the endpoints construct an equilateral triangle. All auxiliary circles
belong to the construction. The full proposition also proves its equalities;
an operational projection must be labelled as such, not a transcription of
every proof word. [The complete proposition](https://mathcs.clarku.edu/~djoyce/java/elements/bookI/propI1.html)
is available, and [CMI's source catalogue](https://www.claymath.org/online-resources/euclids-elements/)
provides a proposition-indexed medieval witness. This source is historical
support for constructive geometry, not a claimed Voynich exemplar.

The smallest actionable next step is **a bounded ownership/completeness
preflight, not target decoding**: compile this complete source construction and
its full output inventory; ask whether one already admitted, independently
bounded diagram-plus-text unit actually supplies two seed points and a complete
output domain for this language. That asks for a new author-visible construction
relation, not another inspection of generic circle counts or label proximity.
Only such a concrete packet could justify writing a target program. If none is
established within twenty minutes, park IDEA000125 without further machinery.

This does not require previously deciphered point names or a pre-identified
seed pair. Seed roles may be inferred jointly by enumerating pairs from a fixed
complete inventory of visible points. What cannot remain free is an arbitrary
new coordinate for every instruction, or a different selection of visible
primitives for each candidate. The complete output boundary and error model
are observations/model inputs; the role assignment can be a hypothesis.

There is currently no established eligible unit in the primary evidence read
for this review. This is a real limitation: GDT871's full originals include
faces, stars and coloured forms that a four-opcode geometric language does not
draw. They cannot simply be dropped as decoration after choosing a match. A
separately bounded geometric construction may be a legitimate output unit, but
its boundary has to be supplied by the page, not the fitted program. GDT392
found no independently owned direction markers; GDT878 did not establish the
required reusable attachment in its nominated panels; GDT213/GDT221 do not
supply a transferable component-label binding. No unchanged missing-input
inventory, new source-image collection, metric fitting to a known answer, or
relaxation of those results is warranted.

A future positive must read every group of the bounded instruction passage and
produce every primitive in the bounded output, under one key that works on a
second complete construction. Similarity of the whole seed frame is harmless;
independent adjustment of every point is fatal. A negative can be an impossible
reference, missing required primitive or incompatible fixed metric relation.
Without an independently justified metric-error model, a schematic drawing
cannot be counted as a failed exact construction. This route is materially
different from the latitude workbook, but currently less ready.

## Why IDEA000344/345 do not yet add meaning

These are the new **idea cards**, not experiments GDT344/345. Their source is
ALIM553. The older experiments are relevant negative controls: GDT344 did not
calibrate an event-path comparator; GDT345's corrected common-target-coordinate
test did not pass its maximum-two null gate (`p=0.350500366`). In that older
experiment, “source-conditioned” means conditioned on the preceding formal
state, not on a readable historical source.

The new guard model can preserve a real source claim: the conjunction of bath,
recipient and condition determines the source's stated beneficial/harmful
effect. A complete fixed source graph with shared compositional coding could
therefore reject some content hypotheses. GDT397 does not imply otherwise.
But a free GUARD word, a free EFFECT word and new recipient/state codes for
each chosen clause merely move the singleton dictionary to semantic labels.
An anonymous graph with the same incidence and polarity pattern still fits.
Matching four selected source contrasts is not a complete reading of the 32
bath entries, nor a general writing mechanism for the remaining words.

The raw cards also misstate completeness or force chronology:

- XI's fresh-water effect retains line130's sick recipient and heat-tolerance
  condition. Fresh, removed, cooled and renewed are conditional/contrasting
  claims; the complete text does not establish one executed patient trajectory.
- XIX continues beyond lines219–228 through lines229–230, which contain the
  actual avoidance closure. Its chronicity restriction and these final lines
  cannot be dropped while calling the sign contrast a complete entry.
- VII/XXXIII's opposite hydrops effects require their different source and
  condition contexts; a universal disease-to-sign map loses the source claim.

GDT967 already contradicts two fixed lexical-incidence consequences over its
literal panel. It does not disprove conditional medical description, but a new
operator encoding must explain prospectively why the same source content has
different written realization. Dropping repeated nouns, inventing implied
recipients or selecting only four easier clauses after that result would be a
repair without an independent writing rationale. I would keep IDEA000344 as a
source-semantic draft and revise IDEA000345's chronology claim, but select
neither for a new target solver now.

**Claim ceiling of this review:** two concrete architectures nominated, one
preferred source-only writing-contract task, zero new manuscript findings,
zero target calculations, zero confirmed words. Whole provisional readings are
permitted; generic fluency, a program that fills unwritten values, and renamed
formal roles are not substitutes for their complete consequences.
