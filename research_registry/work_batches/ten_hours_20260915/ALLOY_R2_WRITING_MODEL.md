## Exact semantic and writing contract

S0 is unchanged: three ordered grades, a target and mass, one to three branches,
at most twenty statements per branch, eight registers of each type, expression
depth at most six, and its existing numerical bounds of 400. All declarations
must be used; recipe references are immutable and backward. Positive portions
and scales, integral repeat-fill, written assertions and the final mass/fine
conditions remain mandatory. The source JSON and evaluator are hash-bound in
the raw R2 proposal. This is neither a fixed number of steps nor a fixed number
of words per source or target paragraph.

```
Q := NUM(n) | MIXED(NUM(w),NUM(p),NUM(d)) | QREF(i)
   | ADD(Q,Q) | SUB(Q,Q) | MUL(Q,Q) | DIV(Q,Q)
   | MASS(R) | FINE(R) | GRADE(R)
R := RREF(i) | PORTION(type,Q) | MIX(R,R) | SCALE(Q,R)
   | REPEAT_FILL(R,R,Q,Q)
S := SETQ(i,Q) | SETR(i,R) | ASSERTQ(property(R),Q)
   | ASSERTWEIGHTS(R,Q,Q,Q) | YIELD(R)
```

The exact R1 atomic arguments remain TYPE, NUM, QREF, RREF and literal
MIXED(NUM,NUM,NUM). A type emits its shared A/B/C component. NUM and references
emit their prefix component and canonical shared decimal digits. MIXED emits
MIXED followed by all three NUM spellings. No complete amount has an independent
word value. The header is GRADES(A,NUM(a),B,NUM(b),C,NUM(c)), TARGET(NUM(t)),
TOTAL(NUM(m)); then FIRST or ALTERNATIVELY precedes the branch's statements.

For every other constructor emit one core, in order:

```
head(C), slot(a1), ..., slot(ak)
slot(a) = complete atomic spelling if atomic, otherwise HOLE
```

SETQ/SETR indices follow the operator as shared decimal digits. Recursively
emit every non-atomic child after its parent core in original argument order.
Each HOLE owns exactly one typed subtree. No operation, assertion, argument or
hole is omitted, inserted or copied without an explicit S0 reference. There
is no added END instruction or line-triggered reset.

For a core with head C and remaining payload p1...pk, R2 writes one group:

```
H(C@e,m) H(p1) ... H(pk)
e = 0 iff first account group or first group after FIRST/ALTERNATIVELY
m = 1 iff this core directly absorbs a literal MIXED atomic argument
```

Otherwise each flag is one or zero respectively. The mixed flag does not
inspect the computed value of a deferred child or a referenced quantity.
Result class Q/R/S is already determined by the operator; it adds no spelling
component or independent context. There is no universal ENTRY or AGREE code.
Exactly one ordinary space separates groups; no spaces occur inside a group.
A recorded layout line break may replace a separating space with no semantic
or context effect. No other whitespace edits or silent groups are permitted.

The following is the complete statically licensed head/context table. Contexts
are fixed by grammar, not added after inspecting occurrences. In particular,
nested PORTION can never be the first statement after a branch marker.

| Heads | Licensed (entry,mixed) pairs | Allomorph count |
|---|---|---:|
| GRADES | (0,0) | 1 |
| TARGET, TOTAL, FIRST, ALTERNATIVELY | (1,0) | 4 |
| SETQ, ASSERTQ, ASSERTWEIGHTS | (0,0), (0,1), (1,0), (1,1) | 12 |
| SETR, YIELD | (0,0), (1,0) | 4 |
| ADD, SUB, MUL, DIV, PORTION, SCALE, REPEAT_FILL | (1,0), (1,1) | 14 |
| MASS, FINE, GRADE, MIX | (1,0) | 4 |

There are **39 operator allomorph components** and **18 shared payload
components**: digits 0–9, A/B/C, NUM/QREF/RREF/MIXED and HOLE. Choose one
injective prefix-free map H from these 57 components to nonempty lowercase
a–z strings of length 1–8, shared across every account. Allomorph spellings
need not resemble one another, but one head/context pair has exactly one
codeword everywhere, independent of quantities, register identity, branch
number, leaf, line, or any observed word. Payload codes never vary by context.
The complete code family is finite; no state expansion, per-word key, new
literal alphabet, semantic filler or occurrence exception is available.
These 57 global entries are ten more than R1's 47 and nineteen more than R0's
38. That explicit extra capacity needs assessment before any target selection.

## Meaning, source ownership and decision limits

The historical numerical source is Fibonacci's Liber abbaci, revised 1228,
chapter XI's three-coin subsection in Boncompagni's 1857 edition, printed
pp.152–154. The [owned source dossier](NEXT_GLOBAL_MEANING_SUPPLY.md) records
the complete native inspection, public scan link, hashes and printed/computed
distinctions; the [public edition scan](https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_CrdUBgtAZFoC/bub_gb_CrdUBgtAZFoC.pdf)
is retained without a new acquisition. R2 relies on the existing S0 technical
projection. Its source scope excludes the original introductory classifications,
table drawing, citations and repeated exposition exactly as S0 does; it is
neither a translation of complete Latin prose nor a license for this grammar.
The full source and later arithmetic audit retain all printed/computed claims.

Three claims remain separate: a legal typed program, a program whose written
assertions and header are arithmetically true, and a supported reading of an
independently constrained object. A well-typed recipe with weights (16,4,0)
has mass 20 but fine content 64 and fails the unchanged grade-5 header. Its
encoding is legal; its arithmetic is false. That retained rival cannot be
excluded merely by finding a legal surface parse. Likewise GDT971's free
whole-number-label countermodel warns that closure with known roles cannot be
transferred to unbound meanings. Shared digits and exact operators are major
assumptions, not consequences of observing two paths.

Unlike a free automaton, R2 has no learned transitions or arbitrary state
inventory: all contexts, argument positions, subtree ownership and numerical
operations are fixed. Unlike a source oracle, it permits new complete bounded
accounts with different values and trees; it does not require copying source
order or length. A learned globally shared code plus arithmetic and an owned
external consequence could support a joint reading without a previously
identified word. The missing item is still that concrete target consequence
and a bounded feasible selection test. Neither is supplied by this source-only
proposal. R2 is retained as one raw writing rival with an exact surface contrast;
it does not reopen predecessors or authorize automatic alphabet/context repairs.
