## 1. IDEA000327 — Tabula Smaragdina roles/processes

### Source scope

The Latin source text is a short medieval translation with uncertain recension history. Its relevant ordered clauses are explicit:

- `PATER EIUS EST SOL, MATER EIUS LUNA` — one implicit referent receives father/mother roles;
- `PORTAVIT ILLUD VENTUS IN VENTRE SUO` — a carrier relation;
- `NUTRIX EIUS TERRA EST` — a nourishing/supporting relation;
- `SEPARABIS TERRAM AB IGNE, SUBTILE A SPISSO` — an operation with two separated components;
- `ASCENDIT A TERRA IN COELUM, ITERUMQUE DESCENDIT IN TERRAM ET RECIPIT VIM SUPERIORUM ET INFERIORUM` — directed movement, return, and acquisition of upper/lower force.

The public transcription is [Tabula Smaragdina](https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Tabula_Smaragdina), lines 110–124 in the retrieved page. The associated medieval witness catalogue is Pal. lat. 1328, dated 1350–1400, linked in the raw proposal. The source supports a role/process *architecture*; it is not a codebook and does not establish any Voynich word value.

### Smallest executable consequence

Use only the already admitted GDT956 f66r whole-page endpoint and its fixed upper/lower group boundaries. Before any word gloss, freeze a structural record with three required observations:

1. one referent or object must occupy at least two distinct roles in the observed arrangement, analogous to source/carrier/nurse;
2. a visible, independently describable separation or transition must connect two states or components;
3. the arrangement must impose a directional before/after relation, including a later state or return relation, rather than merely upper/lower proximity.

The rival is GDT956-C: separate stable labels with no transition requirement. A positive result would deliver only **structural compatibility of this one f66r arrangement with a Tabula-like role/process schema**. It would not identify a substance, operation, language, word, or translation. Failure to observe the three conditions would leave 327 unselected and would not refute alchemical process writing in general.

### Why it is not selected now

GDT956's final report already records separated marginal groups and proximity but no complete reading; the upper mark is unexplained, and no visible relation binds a group to a definite object or state transition. The report's strongest conclusion is that stable-label and contextual readings remain live. Recasting the same proximity as `separatio`, `ascensus`, or `descensus` would silently add the source architecture to the target. The required target predicate therefore has no independently measurable positive capacity in the admitted endpoint. This is a **missing binding/observability limit**, not a source-text refutation.

## 2. IDEA000128 — Llull ordered chamber tables

### Source scope and predecessor result

The primary *Ars brevis* text explicitly states that the alphabet is memorized and that one letter has multiple significations. It gives the ten-letter alphabet B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, K (and the T figure), 36 two-letter chambers in the third figure, and moving three-letter chambers in the fourth figure; it also defines the general question categories (`utrum`, `quid`, `de quo`, `quare`, `quantum`, `quale`, `quando`, `ubi`, `quo modo`, `cum quo`). See the [Latin primary text](https://www.ramonllull.net/sw_studies/l_br/t_ars.html), especially its sections I–IV and figures III–IV. This is a genuine operational/combinatorial source, but it does not attest a cipher from those letters to Voynich strings.

GDT882 independently proves that the **different** fixed additive whole-line mechanism is impossible on its declared 413 complete concordant lines (rank20/unit lattice on the 20-character literal alphabet). That result does not refute Llull's semantic chamber method, but it does rule out inheriting the old ten-letter fixed whole-line additive model as a rescue.

### Required executable consequence and blocker

A valid test would need a complete source column or passage, preserving uncertain/overwritten cells, plus an independently fixed complete target passage. Fit source-column serialization under one global key and predict a held-out column exactly; retain all surviving keys. Success would deliver a conditional copy/combination register, not a unique sentence or meaning. The present record has neither the complete source collation nor an admitted target column with demonstrated chamber ownership. Narrowing the domain after seeing target matches would violate the card's reopening condition. No test is selected.

The source's multiple significations also weaken a one-gloss-per-visible-stem interpretation: context and figure select among meanings. The ten-letter literal morphism in the proposal is an explicit cryptographic hypothesis, not a historical fact from Llull.

## 3. IDEA000122 — shared consonantal channel for multilingual medical text

### Source scope and predecessor result

The inspected Cambridge and publisher records support a narrower claim: K14.22 is described as a trilingual Greek–Aramaic–Middle Persian pharmaceutical lexical list, and its language/classification analysis remains disputed. The source cache and feasibility decision state that no complete edition, common alphabet inventory, reversible normalization, or conversion table has been fixed. The source does not demonstrate successive complete paragraphs switching languages. A catalogue language label cannot supply a script-to-language channel.

### Required executable consequence and blocker

A source-only prerequisite would be to collate a complete K14.22 medical witness, preserve editorial uncertainty, enumerate observed base/final/vocalization/abbreviation signs, and test whether one reversible graphemic inventory accounts for the language columns. Only after that could a target endpoint be admitted and a shared-channel prediction be defined. Success would establish source compatibility of a common written channel, not the language of a Voynich passage or any word meaning.

The current missing edition and normalization are exactly the card's `missing_data` and `missing_design` blockers. Selecting a paragraph-level target reading now would add the unsupported assumption that lexical-list multilingualism generalizes to multilingual medical prose. No test is selected.
