Chapter I.1 is a linked set rather than a modern single recipe: it begins with a white vine and three associated natural objects, develops medicinal operations, and later gives a decan-linked disease and amulet branch before closing the first στοιχεῖον.

## Full source relation used here

The opening roster identifies white vine (`Ἄμπελος λευκή`), eagle, aetites stone, and an eagle-like marine fish. Early operations distinguish plant part, route, outcome, and amount: the root carried at the neck treats spasms/epilepsy; leaf juice in a one-cyathus dose treats dysentery; boiled stems in white wine stop dysentery/lientery; and equal leaf juice and white wine taken for seven days is prescribed for a woman with broken bones. Other sections give further explicit time scales: an enema is used for three, five, or seven days; epilepsy receives a daily dose for a year; and a spleen treatment is taken for thirty days. First shoots, when boiled and eaten, move urine and the bowel, while leaves, root, and fruit are assigned a hot/sharp power.

The same chapter also contains contrary or exception outcomes. Adding a little of the associated stone to the leaf decoction means the disease will no longer worsen or return (I.1.9). Later, a quartan disease is attributed to a bad demon sent under the first decan of Capricorn, described as not seeing or hearing and headless (I.1.35). The immediately following branch removes grape seeds from raisins, wraps them in cloth, and hangs them at the neck of an unaware/chaste person (I.1.36); the fish-head stone is offered as an alternative (I.1.37). The chapter then gives a wine/jug formula and an engraved eagle on the stone with a grape seed under it, worn for protection and advantageous encounters (I.1.38–39).

The source therefore fixes more than a plant-name list: part → preparation/route → bodily or flow effect → amount/duration; a mixture can block recurrence; and a named astral/decan cause is followed by an amuletic botanical/stone alternative. It does **not** prove that all sections form one causal medical theory, and “decan” is retained as a source term rather than converted into a modern astrological claim.

## Two whole-content readings

### A. Compound regimen with temporal and astral branch

A hypothetical record may encode a material core, its plant part, preparation and route, the bodily/flow effect, and a duration or amount. A later condition can reverse or prevent recurrence, while the decan clause switches the remedy from ingestive treatment to a wearable botanical/stone amulet. The same source item need not be one whole-word form: component roles can be reused across parts, routes, and temporal modifiers.

**Different consequence:** a complete target passage would need a linked chain in which a material/part relation controls an effect and a time/amount adjunct, with a distinct contrary branch (prevented return/worsening or adverse condition) and, if astral content is present, an alternate non-ingestive object route. A simple plant-name incidence model predicts none of these scope changes.

### B. Heterogeneous compendium with independent registers

The medical recipes, exact durations, disease-flow claims, decan etiologies, and amulets may be adjacent editorial strata. Shared white-vine or grape-seed references do not force one regimen; duration can be a local dosage convention and the Capricorn passage a separate occult note. The stone/fish substitution can be an independent amulet equivalence, not a continuation of the medical chain.

**Different consequence:** target records under this reading should allow medical operation/duration frames to recur without an astral switch, and astral/amulet frames to recur without plant-part or flow-effect carry. No cross-register link is required.

**Smallest discriminator:** within a source-frozen complete target passage, test whether one reusable component participates in (1) a part-specific bodily/flow operation with a fixed time/amount and (2) a separately marked contrary or astral/amulet branch whose route changes. The rival expects these frames to remain independently distributed. The observation is a relation-and-scope pattern, not a guessed word meaning.

## Costs and failure

The target would need already admitted complete passages with independently fixed operation, route, temporal, and exception/alternate-object positions; no such target instance was inspected here. The source itself is a compiled, heterogeneous Greek magical-medical text, so the compound reading may over-unify redactional neighbors. A target with ordinary medical procedures but no astral or amulet register leaves A indistinguishable from a generic procedure model. Conversely, recurrence of a duration or a flow effect alone is weak and cannot select A.
