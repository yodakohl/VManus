horizontal root. Its broad irregular leaves also differ from both source
descriptions. This is not an upper/lower pair selected by rotating the plant.

relation if the outlines represent the two bodies. III.126 does not securely
assign fullness to the upper or lower member, so this particular opposition is
not imposed on it. Yet f17r's leaf placement along the stem does not show the
source's basal ground-spread pattern, and the red outlines are not securely
identified as bulb bodies. This remains a partial visual candidate only.

compact bodies. f20v has incomplete root visibility at the page edge. Those
