## 1. IDEA000325 — Celsus conditional diagnosis (strongest)

**Primary source.** Celsus, *De Medicina* 4.15.1.1, [PHI Latin
text](https://latin.packhum.org/loc/836/2/260), especially the sequence
`longus ... acutus`, `initio sanguinem mittere`, `tum venter soluendus`,
`primum quae reprimant, deinde calida quae diducant`, and `post haec malagma`.
The source gives diagnosis, conditional fallback, ordered opposite treatment
effects, and a named endpoint in one continuous passage.

**Already admitted target endpoint.** The complete f29v.4 line recorded by
GDT625 is `ysho otshy okaiin cthy oltchy s shot sho okaiin`; f29v.1 is its
registered local companion. The exact line and all current working roles are
in [GDT625 REPORT](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt625_ordered_quality_state_transitions/REPORT.md).

**Whole-content alternatives.** A: the line is a conditional process record:
condition/branch → first restraint → opposite warming/drawing phase → terminal
preparation. B: it is an inventory of co-present part/state fields, with no
diagnostic branch or temporal endpoint.

**Smallest possible distinction (not yet licensed).** The source does not entail
that `okaiin` marks a phase, encloses two treatments, or recurs with the same
relation in f29v.1. Those are additional analyst assumptions. A future test
could compare a predeclared scope model against the independent-field rival,
but no current observation licenses that model. The strongest failure is that
no condition, endpoint, carrier identity, or phase marker is independently
identified, so the two readings may remain relabellable.

## 2. IDEA000336 — Cyranides timed medical/astral branch

The source dossier [CYRANIDES_FLOW_TIMING_ASTRAL_SOURCE.md](CYRANIDES_FLOW_TIMING_ASTRAL_SOURCE.md)
records the complete I.1 relation: plant part → route → bodily/flow effect →
amount or duration, a mixture preventing recurrence, then a decan-linked
disease and wearable botanical/stone alternatives.

Reading A predicts one target passage linking a part-specific treatment and
fixed duration/amount to a marked route switch or contrary branch. Reading B
allows independent medical and occult frames. A target passage would have to
show the same reusable relation across both route types; recurrence of a
duration or flow effect alone is insufficient. GDT811’s four-page working
theory and GDT928’s one-anchor-only result provide context, but no current
target endpoint independently binds an astral/amulet switch. Keep as plausible
raw source architecture, not a near-term selection.

## 3. IDEA000090 — paired head/item construction (reject for this round)

GDT765 gives exact f22r.4 pairs `ofchy daiin` and `schor daiin`, with a
working head/item rendering; GDT768 preserves the parallel two-part structure
but leaves flower versus seed/fruit direction tied. The rival—independent
nominal fields in a pharmaceutical inventory—predicts the same observed
surface arrangement. Without an independently fixed semantic endpoint, this
card supplies no new whole-content discriminator beyond the already tested
role contrast.

## Decision

Retain IDEA000325 as the only practical next content test. It asks whether a
complete admitted line has a nested ordered treatment scope, a consequence
that Celsus fixes and GDT625 did not test. Do not import Celsus words as
Voynich meanings. IDEA000336 remains a source-grounded raw rival; IDEA000090
is not a new route.
