## Architecture A — article-bearing head–modifier versus predicative property

**Source constraints.** The selected records contain article-bearing adjacent
pairs such as `אלמעדה אלבארדה` (recto 21), `אלמואד אלצפראויה` (recto 17),
`אלרמאן אלחלוא` (recto 18), and `אלנוע אלמערוף` (recto 19). They also
preserve unprefixed property sequences such as `אלכוך. בארד רטב` (recto 5)
and `אלרמֹאן בארד` (recto 14). `אלמא אלבארד` (recto 2) is a supplemental
source example outside the selected records. Exact offsets and source
spellings are in the source dossier.

The full recto 15–16 clause is also retained: `מקויא ללמעדה / ואלכבד
אלחארתין מסכן ללקי`. The source translation explicitly renders a hot stomach
and liver, so `אלחארתין` can be treated as a working dual-agreement modifier
over the coordinated pair while leaving case details open.

**Conditional whole reading.** A shared construction model first identifies a
head candidate, then distinguishes `[article + head] [article + modifier]`
from `[head] [unprefixed property sequence]`. In a complete medical paragraph,
the first frame can be read provisionally as an attributive property of the
head, while the second is a predicative/state sequence. The model must carry
the frame choice through the record and preserve the article feature; it may
not assign a q, r, l, or other Voynich symbol value from this source analogy.
The output is a whole conditional reading architecture, not a list of
translated words.

**Why it may fail.** `אל` may be an orthographic article only in some
contexts; unprefixed properties may be ordinary predicates, apposition, or
editorial variation. Formulaic medical prose can copy the same frame without a
stable semantic distinction. One leaf cannot establish universal Arabic
agreement, and the source dossier has no independently confirmed target
construction or antecedent binding.

**Smallest discriminating test.** Freeze every complete source occurrence in
the three records, classify article-bearing and unprefixed frames by the exact
surface strings, and specify a rival that treats both as one flat property
sequence. A future target test would need independently delimited complete
constructions and a held frame class before any semantic score; a count of
`אל` prefixes alone is insufficient.

**Distinctness.** This is a construction-choice and modifier-scope question.
It does not choose paragraph language (IDEA000122), switch a whole from
definite to anaphoric use (IDEA000017), or assign a fixed warm/genitive value
(GDT814).

## Architecture B — possessive suffix recurrence as an anaphoric participant link

**Source constraints.** `אמראה` explicitly introduces a woman before the
first `פם מעדתהא`; the exact phrase occurs at verso 2 and verso 4 in
the selected pomegranate record. Supplemental `טיב ראיחתה` occurs at verso
21 outside the selected quince boundary. These suffix-bearing forms are
structurally separate from noun–noun candidate `חב אלרמאן אלחאמץֹ`; `עקל
אלטביעה` is retained as a possible verb–object construction, not counted as
idāfa without lexical context. The repeated exact phrase is a source-internal
recurrence, not a translation imported from a missing English panel.

**Conditional whole reading.** A shared construction model separates a
suffix-bearing noun `[N + possessor suffix]` from an article-bearing noun–noun
frame. When a complete record introduces an entity or body-part candidate, a
later suffix-bearing phrase carries an anaphoric participant relation across
the clause. The model must preserve the suffix as a bound slot and require a
single antecedent chain for all repeated occurrences; it cannot assign the
suffix a fixed English pronoun without independent binding.

**Why it may fail.** The final letters may be ordinary lexical or inflectional
material rather than possession; `פם מעדתהא` may be a copied formula rather
than a productive relation. The explicit woman antecedent supports a working
feminine antecedent for `-הא`, while the exact referent and semantic scope of
the body term remain open. A flat noun sequence can mimic suffix recurrence,
and any longer participant chain must still be checked against source syntax
rather than supplied by analyst prose.

**Smallest discriminating test.** Build a source-only inventory of every
suffix-bearing word in the three bounded records, retaining exact surface
forms and offsets. Freeze at least one repeated and one non-repeated suffix
construction, then compare an antecedent-carrying model against a free local
word model. A future target test requires held complete constructions and an
independent antecedent criterion; repeated suffix counts alone cannot choose
anaphora.

**Distinctness.** IDEA000017 concerns whether a whole changes use after prior
mention and explicitly lacks an endpoint binding. This proposal is narrower:
it tests a written suffix construction against article-bearing noun–noun
frames, with exact source recurrence. It also does not reuse GDT814's fixed
warm/property or possessive-genitive word assignments.

## Architecture C — coordinated definite property pair as an oppositional state

**Source constraints.** `אלחרארה ואלברודה` occurs in the selected pomegranate
record at recto 19 and in the selected quince record at verso 12. A further
occurrence at supplemental verso 19 lies outside the selected quince boundary.
The conjunction `ו` is attached to the second surface word. Other selected
article-bearing pairs (`אלרמאן אלחלוא`) and unprefixed property sequences
provide local contrasts. The exact pair therefore recurs across two selected
records, but all occurrences belong to one historical witness.

**Conditional whole reading.** Treat the coordinated pair as one ordered
two-property construction `[definite property A] ו[definite property B]`, not
as two unrelated ingredient tokens. In a complete record, the pair can
provisionally encode a contrastive state axis whose order is part of the
reading; adjacent head/property phrases then specify the carrier. The model
must preserve conjunction attachment, order, and recurrence and should allow
the pair to remain semantically unnamed until an independent endpoint exists.

**Why it may fail.** The pair may simply be a conventional list, a copied
phrase, or two nouns coordinated without an oppositional relation. Repetition
on one leaf is not independent replication. `אל` may not uniformly mark
definiteness, and thermal or polarity values must not be imported from a
translation or from GDT814. A flat two-token list and an ordered state-pair
model can be observationally equivalent without an independent contrast.

**Smallest discriminating test.** Freeze the two selected occurrences and
register supplemental verso 19 separately, together with all complete
neighboring clauses. Compare an ordered paired-state model against a flat
coordination model, requiring a held occurrence to preserve both member order
and the same construction role. Stop if the source supplies no independent
polarity/contrast criterion; no target fitting follows from the source
recurrence alone.

**Distinctness.** This proposal targets the coordination relation and its
ordered scope. It does not assign a fixed thermal word, revive GDT814's
possessive-genitive account, or infer any Voynich letter value. It is also
separate from GDT915/916's terminal r/l co-variation, which has no established
grammatical agreement.

## Shared ceiling and decision

All three architectures use complete source constructions and multiple linked
constraints rather than an arbitrary unknown phase marker. None establishes a
language for the Voynich manuscript, a translation, or a confirmed lexical
meaning. Missing confirmed meanings are therefore a limitation on later fixed
testing, not a reason to discard these exploratory source hypotheses. The
minimum changed input needed for a real experiment is an independently frozen
target construction scope plus a rival-specific endpoint that separates the
architecture from flat token reuse or formulaic copying.
