# GDT959 next-content review

## Result

I find no defensible new content-binding test within the currently admitted
f66r material. The strongest bounded candidate is a source-specific extension
from figure classes to the rest of the page, but its required ownership
relation is absent. Launching it now would amount to assigning meanings to
unbound marks rather than testing a consequence.

## The one candidate considered

Use each surviving GDT957 fifteen-position calculation as a provisional
geomantic register, retain its Turner figure names and elemental class, and
ask whether the 34 isolated inner marks (or the nearby prose rows) are the
register's twelve houses or figure-derived annotations. Turner1655 gives a
real content vocabulary for that question: after constructing the figures he
assigns house domains (for example, substance, journeys, illness, marriage,
and enemies) and gives figure-specific judgements within each house. A fully
specified version would have to test all 32 complete IT2a readings, all
available ZL3b/RF1b candidates, and every one of the 34 marks and prose rows
under one fixed physical ownership map. It could then distinguish a source
reading that predicts the same house/figure content at repeated bounded
locations from a rival in which the margin is only a list.

That is not currently an executable discriminator. The f66r evidence audit
records separate baselines for the outer margin, inner marks, prose blocks and
lower annotation, with no explicit equality or ownership relation. GDT957
registered and tested only the complete outer fifteen-word list; it explicitly
left the 34 marks and prose outside the model. The earlier marker and heading
tests did not establish a margin-to-inner binding. Turner's [1655 source](https://www.princeton.edu/~ezb/geomancy/agrippa.html)
supplies house meanings conditional on a chart and question; it does not supply a rule that
an adjacent Voynich mark must be a house label or that this drawing asks a
particular question. Choosing such a relation after seeing the surviving
figure assignments would be arbitrary semantic assignment.

## What GDT959 actually leaves

The only repeated known family is the remainder `ary`: ZL has `rary`, `dary`,
`fary`, while IT and RF have `rary`, `fary`. Four cross-edition known-family
survivors remain (two top-down table-B candidates and two bottom-up table-C
candidates). Three table-A cases are upper-only because of the printed
`Amitia` uncertainty. These results constrain a local initial/remainder
classification. They do not choose a figure, element, house, planet, or
ordinary-word gloss. Extending the same family test to another partition
would repeat the closed local filter, not add content.

Accordingly, the smallest honest next step is to retain the source-specific
house/inner-mark proposal as a conditional future route only if a separately
selected physical ownership relation becomes available from already admitted
records. With the present records, there is no source-grounded observation
that could make two complete content readings diverge. This is a specific
scope failure of the proposed binding, not a claim that the f66r marks are
meaningless. No new target image, body payload, reserve or semantic choice
was opened here.
