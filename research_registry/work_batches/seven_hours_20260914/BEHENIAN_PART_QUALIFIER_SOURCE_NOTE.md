# Behenian part-qualifier alternative (bounded producer note)

This source-only follow-up supplies one alternative to requiring an identical
whole target form for every repeated plant identity. Agrippa's complete
fifteen-row roster explicitly writes unqualified **Mandrake** in the Goat-Star
and Spica rows, but **the root of Mandrake** in the Tail of Capricorn row. It
also writes **flowers of Perwinckle** in the Tail of the lesser Bear row and
**Perwinkle** in Spica. These are visible source relations; no modern plant
identification is used. See `BEHENIAN_SOURCE_ROSTER.json` and the EEBO primary
text, Book I, chapter XXXII:
<https://quod.lib.umich.edu/e/eebo/A26565.0001.001/1%3A16.32?rgn=div2%3Bview=fulltext>.

The proposed reading is a reusable base item plus a part operator. Its fixed
consequence, for a later source-frozen comparison against GDT960's admitted
fifteen-group endpoint, is that related positions may share an internal
component while qualified entries add a second component or role change. The
nearest rival treats every complete row label as opaque and predicts no
part-conditioned structural change. The test must preserve the distinction
between exact source strings and normalized plant identity, and must not assign
Mandrake or Perwinkle to a target form.

This target consequence was not inspected here. The raw proposal is in
`research_registry/proposals/raw_behenian_part_qualified_composition.json`.
`ideas add` was attempted after the required route/duplicate screen but was
blocked by the registry's stale imported-source guard; refreshing the shared
registry was outside this task, so no global refresh or review was performed.
