## Rival complete readings

These are deliberately provisional content readings; every token in the
selected line is accounted for.

**A — two botanical material roles.**

`pchaiin` = record opener; `ofchy` = floral mass/material head; first
`daiin` = its value or portion; `cfhy` = a field/transition operator;
`doroiin` = unknown modifier; `ypchol` = unknown modifier; `sy` = unknown
relation or quantity term; `schor` = floral head/flower-part item; final
`daiin` = the corresponding value or portion. In this reading, the line
contains two parallel specifications, with `ofchy` and `schor` naming
different material roles that happen to receive the same `daiin` field.

**B — one abstract specification system.**

`pchaiin` = record opener; `ofchy` = a grade/index field; first `daiin` =
grade value; `cfhy` = unknown field separator; `doroiin` = unknown modifier;
`ypchol` = unknown modifier; `sy` = unknown relation or quantity term;
`schor` = generic subrecord/item marker, without a distinct botanical role;
final `daiin` = another grade/value entry. Here the repeated `daiin` is
parallel bookkeeping, and the apparent `ofchy`/`schor` distinction is a
layout distinction rather than a material distinction.

The exact semantic changes are therefore: `ofchy` material head versus
abstract index, `schor` distinct floral item versus generic item marker, and
the relation between each head and its adjacent `daiin` (material portion in
A, scalar grade in B). The five unassigned forms remain visibly unknown in
both readings; no old English gloss is being imported.

## Candidate consequence and why it is not yet a result

The finite, already exposed consequence is a family-and-position check. Under
A, the remaining exact `ofch*`/`*fchy` forms should continue to occur in
specification-head positions with complements that behave like material,
portion, or state fields, while `schor`/`chor` forms should occupy item-head
positions. Under B, those families should instead share one scalar/index
distribution; any apparent material split is accidental layout. The check can
be made on the cached occurrences and their complete neighboring lines,
without new images, labels, or reserves. A useful discriminator would be a
cross-family position/complement pattern that is present for the full exposed
families and differs from the other reading before assigning English values.

The present record does not meet that standard. The three `ofchy` and three
`schor` cases are exactly the small positive set already used by GDT765; the
reported “flower mass/flower head” terms are working readings, not independent
observations. The neighboring notions “dry,” “hot,” “portion,” and “value” are
also provisional axes. Expanding them into a claimed semantic win would make
the result depend on the interpretation it is supposed to test. Conversely,
the absence of a previously translated operator cannot show that no operator
is written: the unresolved forms could carry it.

Thus A and B give a concrete, fully delimited rival pair and a finite next
check, but no qualifying untested semantic consequence is established within
this bounded review. The correct decision is to retain this as a near miss,
not select it as positive decipherment evidence. The qochey material is weaker
for this purpose because GDT749 already tested its outside-role consequence
and found competing end/transition readings rather than a new discriminator.
