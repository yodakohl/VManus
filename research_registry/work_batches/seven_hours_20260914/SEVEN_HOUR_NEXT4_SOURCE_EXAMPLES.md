## 1. Quaestio status chain (new source framing; target f73v)

**Finite source.** Thomas Aquinas, *Summa theologiae* I q.1 a.1, has a fixed
article sequence: objections (`arg.`), contrary authority (`s. c.`), resolution
(`co.`/`Respondeo`), and replies (`ad`). Primary text:
https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/sth1001.html (the same sequence recurs in the
finite articles of q.1).

**Target and two readings.** The admitted f73v annotation record preserves a
three-role disagreement: illustration/December says Sagittarius while a field
says Scorpio (`HIST:8d4cf69878a708ea`, `HIST:bc3acab09bcf6d10`). Reading A is a
metatextual report: observation -> rival source -> adjudication/correction.
Reading B is an ordinary annotation/transcription error with independent fields.
A predicts a later already-admitted page-level reuse with distinct report and
resolution roles; B predicts no ordered response or correction marker. The
smallest discriminating observation is a written/author-visible response link,
not another disagreement. Strongest failure: the conflict may be entirely modern
metadata and supplies no manuscript semantic evidence. This refines, but does
not replace, `IDEA000315`/`RAW_P32`.

## 4. Directed graft transformation graph (existing IDEA000321; target f57v)

**Finite source.** Columella, *De arboribus* 26.1.1, enumerates three graft
methods and compatibility/season conditions. Palladius, *De insitione* XIV

flesh or thorniness, including a self-exchange exception. Primary pointers:
https://latin.packhum.org/loc/845/1/30/704-709 and
https://remacle.org/bloodwolf/erudits/palladius/livre14.htm .

**Target and two readings.** The admitted f57v record has four figure-near
inscriptions and four between-figure inscriptions, with no visible leader or
bracket (`HIST:cfad7da3682d3dcb`; `RAW_P38`). Reading A is a directed graph:
endpoint host -> bridge method/compatibility -> transformed endpoint/result.
Reading B is two parallel spatial registers. A predicts direction-sensitive
bridge reuse and an endpoint change; reversing a pair may change legality or
result. B predicts no such co-variation. The smallest observation is a repeated
directed bridge plus a downstream endpoint consequence; adjacency alone is
insufficient. Strongest failure: four pairs and no ownership marks cannot
separate the graph from layout coincidence. No new raw card is warranted.
