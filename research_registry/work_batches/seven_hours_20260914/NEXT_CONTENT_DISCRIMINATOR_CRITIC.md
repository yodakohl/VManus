# A delayed result relation is the only useful next content test

The strongest executable route left in the admitted material is a **delayed
result/participant test** over the five open GDT705 cases, with A057/C017 and
A056/C018 retained as positive-form controls. This extends the content graph
after the first nominal successor has already been exhausted. It is not another
purpose-list equality, and it does not repair an old gloss.

The best concrete working passage is the already exposed A072 frame at
`f86v5.2`:

```text
ytol …  →  otam …
cool this drug material  →  then: a measure of cold preparation
```

The first clause and the following record are both complete in the retained
reader. The only strong shared content is cooling/cold. The material head,
measure, and preparation identity are not repeated. Thus the proposed reading
is explicitly provisional: “the cooled drug material is entered as a cold
preparation measure.” Its closest rival is the descriptive-register reading:
“a cooling instruction is followed by a separate cold-measure record.” The
rival does not claim that `otam` is the output of `ytol`.

The four other open frames provide the necessary content contrast:

| case | retained complete reading | conservation debt |
|---|---|---|
| A066, `f86v3.13` | heat an equal part → dry material, hot, stage II | equal-part reference, material and stage are supplied only around the target |
| A046, `f76v.10` | measure a drug composite → moist measured raw-material amount I | material changes; stage appears only on the right |
| A006, `f105r.31` | fill into three bundles and close → finished composite | bundle number and input identity disappear |
| A043, `f56r.6` | prepare a warm dry mixture → finished measured middle-stage dry portion | measure, portion and stage appear only on the right |

These are not five chances to choose whichever German sentence sounds best.
They are a fixed set of material debts. A057/C017 is the control where soaking,
heating, and the middle stage are written again in `shkeol`; A056/C018 is the
control where measuring and completion are mirrored by `dchey`. GDT705 already
showed why neither surface can become a portable result word: the other `sheky`
and `dchey` occurrences do not carry the same outcome.

## The finite discriminator

Keep every GDT705 whole-form value and operation role fixed. For each of the
five open cases, inspect only the already retained complete paragraph window,
including the first later action after the nominal record. Admit a delayed
action-to-result edge only when all three conditions hold on the written
material:

1. the later record repeats at least two properties of the earlier action,
   with one being an operation/state change and one being a participant,
   measure, or stage;
2. the participant is written again or is the sole unambiguous anaphoric
   target, so “same material” is not supplied merely because the line is short;
3. the next action treats that result as its input, or the record closes with
   an explicit terminal state. A nominal item that merely follows an action is
   not enough.

The process/conservation model predicts at least two such delayed edges on
different physical pages among A066/A072/A046/A006/A043, and predicts that the
carried participant remains stable while state or amount changes. The
record-register rival predicts no consumed output: action and nominal fields
can share a line while their participants remain independent. The exact
outcome is the count and full list of admitted edges, plus the first
counterexample. Require two pages before retaining a content lead; zero or one
closes conservation as unsupported at this scope. No result is promoted to a
word meaning either way.

This consequence is genuinely different from GDT948/949. Those trials filled
complete paragraphs with alternative singleton meanings and found that process
and inspection readings could be made equally fluent. The present test never
adds a missing noun or changes `qotchy`, `pcheey`, `dchey`, or `sheky`; it asks
whether a written intermediate product is actually reused by a later action.
It also differs from GDT705’s completed first-token census: A006 and A072 are
not reclassified as immediate results, and the five held candidates remain
held until the delayed-input condition is checked.

The likely result is a closure rather than a semantic win. A072 currently
matches only cold after cooling, and A046 currently changes the material head;
those are precisely the observations that make the rival credible. If no two
cases meet all three conditions, the next decision is to leave the material
transformation family and retain only occurrence-bound local process fragments.
If two do meet them, the output is a small connected participant/state graph
for those occurrences, suitable for a later complete reading. It still cannot
confirm a lexeme, component, language, plaintext, or physical efficacy.

The test needs no new image, page, source corpus, reserve, or sealed folio.
All source content and candidate frames are already exposed in GDT700, GDT701,
GDT705, and GDT769; historical recipe comparisons can supply architecture
only, never target spelling or a hidden semantic anchor.
