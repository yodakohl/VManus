## IDEA000328

`raw_geomantia_temporal_conclusion_roles.json` is **RAW_UNSCREENED**.

The medieval *Geomantia* passage printed from manuscript witnesses says that
four Matres represent the past, four Filiae the present, four Nepotes the
future, and Testes plus Iudex the conclusion. It also records exceptions to
the ordinary first-house assignment for special querent cases. Primary text
and manuscript discussion: Beccarisi 2019, pp. 554–603, especially the
diplomatic excerpts on p. 587:
<https://www.e-theca.net/QuadernidiNoctua/Issues/2019%2C%20volume%205/Alessandra%20Beccarisi%20%E2%80%93%20Qualche%20considerazione%20sulla%20Geomantia%20attribuita%20a%20Guglielmo%20di%20Moerbeke.pdf>.

The already admitted GDT957 f66r object has 15 groups, but its injective
figure-key comparison supplied no meaning evidence. Reading A predicts a
4+4+4 temporal block structure followed by two dependent witnesses and one
dependent conclusion. Reading B treats all 15 groups as independent labels.
The smallest distinction is a structural block change plus reuse/attachment
from groups 1–12 into groups 13–15, tested without English glosses. If that
attachment is absent, this proposal remains untested and does not rescue the
geomantic key.

The direct rival is GDT957's independent/injective label model. The earlier
Llull card IDEA000128 was checked and is a different combinatorial mechanism;
it is not duplicated here.
