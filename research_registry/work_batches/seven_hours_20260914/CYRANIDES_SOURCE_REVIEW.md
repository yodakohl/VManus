# Cyranides: a concrete four-class source, no selected target code

The complete Book I roster contains24 alphabetic chapters, each linking a botanical
object, a bird, a stone and a fish or other marine animal. This is a more specific
historical architecture than GDT735's generic descriptive/prescriptive field mixture.
The primary Greek digital edition is [Open Greek and Latin, Cyranides, Book I](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenGreekAndLatin/First1KGreek/master/data/tlg1482/tlg001/tlg1482.tlg001.1st1K-grc1.xml),
based on the1898 edition. It is not a Voynich source identification.

All24 opening rosters were read. Chapters2–6 were read completely; in chapters7–24,
all source sections selected by the disclosed engraving/enclosure strings were
read in addition to the rosters. The first11sections of chapter1 were also read.
This is a complete roster audit and a bounded instruction audit, not a claim to
have translated all353sections of Book I or collated an original manuscript.
The first chapter is also available in the [CTS reader](https://fgh.perseids.org/read/greekLit/tlg1482/tlg001/1st1K-grc1/1.1.1-1.1.11).

The connected content is concrete. Chapter2.6 places a crow and marine creature
on a stone and encloses botanical and animal material;19.6 combines an engraved
bird/marine-creature scene with the plant root below the stone;20.6 adds a bird
part and explicitly allows a substitute. These are historical recipe claims,
not verified medical effects or instructions for current use. The four objects
are not always all ingredients in one operation. Chapter5.9 instead depicts a
deity,7.8 includes an extra scorpion, and21.23–24 supplies competing inscribed
names attributed to different source authorities. Thus the source distinguishes
object identity, depicted image, material included, and text inscribed.

The roster order varies. Chapters1/10/14 give respectively plant–bird–stone–marine,
plant–marine–stone–bird, and plant–stone–bird–marine. The shared chapter index is
not a shared physical object: chapter10 names all four entries Κιναίδιος and14
names all four Ξίφιος, while most other chapters use distinct names. Chapters1/3
share the bird and marine name;21 shares bird and stone names. These contrasts
could matter to a future compound reference model, but neither a target index nor
its category meanings has been identified.

## Decision after primary predecessor review

GDT635's fixed initial-head census already contains760different bodies:616with
one head,87with two,33with three and24with all four. GDT735 retained the96complete
grid cells but removed its old p/s/r/l Latin-initial glosses. GDT736/737 show
transferable record-position effects and limited/nontransferring body-affinity
claims. GDT887 rejects a different fixed Tacuinum compiler, not all natural-object
associations. These primary reports were read; none is overwritten.

A global four-category plus24-alphabet-index interpretation of **every** old
head/body token is already incompatible with the known760bodies under a one-to-one
index rule. Restricting that codebook to the96complete cells leaves its boundary
selected from the target. The coincidence24complete bodies/24chapters is therefore
not positive evidence. No new target access is needed to expose this limitation.

The bounded critic suggested checking for additional complete four-head bodies
outside those24within the same760-body inventory. Root rejects that suggested
criterion: those24were defined as **all** complete bodies in that inventory, so
failure is guaranteed by selection. No new experiment or significance calculation
will be built around it. The original critique remains separately available as
SOURCE_MODEL_CRITIQUE.md; it is an assessed proposal, not an executed result.

A future restricted class/index model would need a predeclared target domain or
chapter-marker interpretation and actual cross-category content consequences.
An independently translated anchor is not required to draft that hypothesis;
no such draft has yet specified those assignments without selecting the96cells
for their completeness. Consequently no decoder, name, category-prefix gloss or
new target trial is selected in this pass. The source's image/material/inscribed-name
distinction is retained as an untested content idea. It does not turn drawn
figures into names or physical body parts.

## Reproducibility and limits

CYRANIDES_SOURCE_RESULT.json binds the original506183byte XML, extraction code,
all24opening rosters and audit-selection scope. Run validate_cyranides_source.py
for refetch, chapter/section-count and roster checks. Its pass concerns source
bytes and extraction, not the historical interpretations or manuscript meaning.
The digital source declares CC BY-SA4.0; extracted Greek source text carries that
license and attribution to Open Greek and Latin and its1898 base edition.
The local complete source cache is not published. f84/f84r, all reserves and
f116v stayed closed; no Voynich images or new passages were opened.
