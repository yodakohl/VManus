# p9 institutional-resolution addendum

This addendum records a second inspection of p9 only. I fetched the institutional IIIF image service at its native page size (6132 x 8176) and a native-resolution rectangular region around the wheel centre. The earlier JSON and note are unchanged.

Sources inspected:

- Full native page: `https://www.e-codices.ch/loris/csg/csg-0756/csg-0756_009.jp2/full/6132,/0/default.jpg`, SHA-256 `53af7f792390b1c2409366625994489c2d136d2147bce5119e79722c34f76880`.
- Native centre region: `https://www.e-codices.ch/loris/csg/csg-0756/csg-0756_009.jp2/2200,2800,1400,1500/full/0/default.jpg`, SHA-256 `8cd39c594ee74703ab443e1c51749f18797c3e1da3c4d7cb973219f240d90537`.
- Service metadata: `https://www.e-codices.ch/loris/csg/csg-0756/csg-0756_009.jp2/info.json`; reported dimensions 6132 x 8176.

The native view confirms that the centre contains a red labelled ring inside the two larger red named bands, plus grey cursive inscriptions in the central sectors. The red and grey writing is partly crossed by the vertical/horizontal rules and by neighbouring ring strokes. At this resolution I can confirm the presence and separation of these coloured layers, but I cannot securely segment or transcribe the central red labels or the four grey inscriptions. The grey marks do not yield a secure reading of *aqua*, *aer*, *ignis*, *terra*, or another quality/element term. No explicit element or quality label, legend, ownership mark, arrow, or prose assignment was identified.

Thus the higher-resolution check strengthens the layout observation (separate red central ring and grey inner writing) but does not license an element classification. The cardinal labels remain the page-level orientation labels recorded previously. No later classification source was used to repair the unreadable text, and this remains a partial source witness rather than a complete early program.
