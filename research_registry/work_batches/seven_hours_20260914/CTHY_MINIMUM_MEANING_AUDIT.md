# cthy: minimum meaning audit

This is a provisional content card, not a confirmation claim. I checked the
live route, the bounded lexical results, and the primary reports GDT625,
GDT768, GDT809, and GDT948. No new image, target, reserve, or sealed page was
opened. The old GDT625/92 herbal result is retained as prior evidence, not
promoted to an English lexeme.

## Candidate

The strongest current whole-form working value is **cthy = leaf material / a
leaf drug (Blattgut, folium)**. It is a nominal content item in a technical
record, not an operation and not a value assigned to `cth-` as a component.
The most compact provisional readings leave every other form visible:

```text
f17r.5  ychekchy cthy chor shor cphor cphaldy dair cthey qody
         <entry> LEAF-MATERIAL <part> <part> <dose> <portion> <fraction> <form> <finish>

f29v.1  kooiin shor chetchy ol ls shytchy cthy shy cho shy daiin
         <root/head> <reproductive-part> <state> <carrier> <unknown>
         <state> LEAF-MATERIAL <state> <unknown> <state> <value>
```

These are deliberately incomplete readings. They do not turn the neighbouring
forms into Latin words or assert that a line is a recipe. The useful claim is
only that cthy can occupy one of several written content fields alongside
other possible plant-part fields.

## Why this is the best provisional value

GDT625 reports 92 exact cthy occurrences in ZL3b, 99 in IT2a and 86 in RF1b;
the older synthesis counts 90 of 92 in Herbal, 50 pages and 88 loci, with zero
line-first occurrences, 66 middle and 26 final positions. Its 32 retained
contacts with `chor`, `shor`, or `dair` include both `PART_THEN_CTHY` (25) and
`CTHY_THEN_PART` (7). Ten contacts are adjacent. This is the shape of a
reusable content field more than a local process verb, while the result still
does not identify which visible part it names.

The already opened visual notes give the candidate concrete, bounded support:
f18r has rich foliage beside the exact `qokchy cthy` line; f29v.1 records
rootstock, reproductive organs and leaves in one plant; f45v records repeated
`chor`/`cthy` part contacts; and f15v has four cthy occurrences beside a
leaf-dominated drawing. GDT768's exact line `cthy chor shor` places three
successive possible content cells before later dose/fraction fields. The same
report finds 14 chor/cthy lines and 8 shor/cthy lines, but explicitly treats
this as parallel register evidence, not identity evidence.

GDT809's repaired comparison is a useful warning against overclaiming. The
cthy axis has a 12:0 base association and remains 13:0 after its declared
ablation, but the external folios overlap the training population. `folium`,
`herba`, and an unnamed botanical head all receive the same contextual score
(11). Thus the association is compatible with a botanical content role; it
does not select leaf. GDT948 likewise says that no `cth-` element receives a
productive translation and that its complete paragraph readings have no
independent semantic confirmation.

## Closest rival

The closest rival is **cthy = above-ground herb / broad aerial plant material
(herba)**. It preserves the same whole-form and Herbal concentration while
allowing cthy to denote a plant-level material rather than one organ. A
secondary structural rival is simply “nominal botanical field with no part
identity.” Neither rival is defeated by recurrence, by a visible plant, or by
the spelling’s `cth` sequence.

The central negative evidence against the leaf-specific gloss is the absence
of an independently written ownership marker: the text never says that cthy
means a leaf, and existing visual annotations do not bind every one of the 85
triple-stable GDT768 occurrences to a separable leaf object. Some pages show
multiple plausible parts, and the reports acknowledge that broad aerial herb
remains live. The old Herbal majority also has section concentration and
cannot by itself distinguish a leaf field from a whole-herb field.

## One executable distinction in admitted material

A small text/annotation audit can make the two hypotheses predict different
observable records without assigning meanings to neighbouring words. Freeze
the already exposed GDT625 cthy contact table, the GDT768 12 complete lines,
and the eight existing visual judgments. For each exact cthy occurrence,
record only:

1. whether cthy is line-initial, medial, or final;
2. whether a declared non-leaf part surface (`chor`, `shor`, or `dair`) occurs
   on the same line before or after it; and
3. where an existing visual note explicitly separates a leaf cluster from a
   reproductive or root structure.

The **leaf-field** prediction is a non-initial peer pattern: cthy should occur
with separable non-leaf part fields in both written orders, and a visual note
with multiple parts should place it at the leaf field rather than at the whole
plant. The **aerial-herb** prediction is a class/head pattern: cthy should
occasionally open a plant entry or stand without a separately enumerated
non-leaf part, and its visual referent should be the whole above-ground unit.

The existing text already has the leaf-compatible side of this contrast—0/92
line-first, 32 part contacts, and both contact orders—but that is not a
predeclared semantic win. A fair decision rule for a follow-up is: retain
leaf as the working default only if the already exposed part-bearing visual
records all have a separately noted leaf object **and** the exact text audit
retains both contact orders; otherwise downgrade to the broader aerial/general
class. If the visual notes cannot make the ownership column without reopening
images, report `UNRESOLVED` rather than filling it from the text. The current
notes supply enough support to retain the default provisionally, but not the
ownership audit needed to distinguish it.

This consequence is not self-confirming because it does not count a smooth
German rendering, a recurrent spelling, a cth substring, or a selected image
as a positive hit. It asks for a fixed positional relation and an explicitly
recorded visual separation, with a downgrade path. It also leaves open the
possibility that cthy is a broad aerial class used in the same nominal field.

## Coverage and decision

The candidate has repeated whole-form exposure, Herbal concentration, written
part-field contacts, and a concrete but limited visual fit. Its missing bridge
is direct cthy-to-leaf ownership. The closest rival has equal contextual score
in GDT809 and remains compatible with all reported cthy/chor/shor lines. No
confirmed lexeme, component, or complete translation results. The next useful
decision is therefore a bounded ownership audit on existing annotations; a
new global cthy census or another singleton gloss would not distinguish these
models.
