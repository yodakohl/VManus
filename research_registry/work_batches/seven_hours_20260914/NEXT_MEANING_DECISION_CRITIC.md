# Next meaning decision: a visible state field over learned entries

The strongest remaining bounded route is a **state/quality field attached to
learned circular entries**, using the already admitted f70v, f71v and f72r
images and their source-native label atlas. It is a different question from
which wind, zodiac or decan occupies a sector. No permutation of named places,
no Latin spelling, and no component value for `ok`, `ot`, `al`, or `dy` is
needed.

## Concrete mechanism

GDT795 has two useful facts that should be used together. Complete catalogue
families do not preserve a fixed nominal member across the three circular
pages, but a transferred formal prefix has a strong **local adjacency** signal
(35/87 consecutive pairs versus a permutation mean of 23.398, calibrated
`p=.000800`). GDT797 then gives a small, exact surface panel: `AQABBA` is
represented by `okaldy` in the pool/figure domain and `otaldy` in the
plant/drug domain, while the four source/host events split perfectly by whole
surface. That split blocks a portable substance or action reading, but it does
not decide whether the recurrent family carries a graphical status field.

The candidate reading is therefore two-layered:

```text
individual learned member entry
+ a locally copied status/quality field
→ entry displayed for a visible figure state
```

“Status/quality” is a working semantic axis only. It does not mean benefic,
covered, hot, or any other chosen English value yet. The native visual state
must be encoded from the existing GDT797 inspection plan (the fixed
f70-R0/f71-F9/f72-R0 torso-state comparison), with a finite category list
frozen before counting. The exact catalogue block is GDT795's 101 admitted
labels on f70/f71/f72, including the four complete recurring-family cards:

* f70v1.5 `okalal`, family `AQABAB`, source member 09A;
* f72r1.5 `okalam`, family `AQABAB`, source member 09A;
* f70v1.14 `otalaiin`, family `AQABAG`, early inner member 02A;
* f71v.15 `otalaiin`, family `AQABAG`, early inner member 03A.

The repeated complete forms are not treated as identical objects. Their value
is that a state-field model predicts a relation between the **whole family
and visible state**, even when the individual member designation changes.

## Falsifiable bounded test

Use only the already exposed f70/f71/f72 circular panels and the retained
source-native occurrence atlas. For every label, record its fixed ring/page
position, complete surface/family, and one predeclared native torso-state
category. Keep the family discovery cards marked and report them separately.
Fit no word meanings. The status model predicts that recurrent transferred
families, especially `AQABBA`, have lower state dispersion than matched
families after conditioning on ring position and page. It also predicts that
the local transferred-prefix adjacency signal is retained within, rather than
independent of, the same state class.

The closest rival is the retained GDT795 architecture: the repeated prefix is
copying/register texture and all complete labels are learned member entries.
It predicts no family-state association after the same position/page
conditioning. A leave-one-page-out table is enough: train on two panels and
predict the third state's family distribution, with a fixed within-panel
permutation control. Require every held panel to have the status model's
directional prediction and a positive exact permutation margin before keeping
the status lead. Otherwise close it as graphical texture. This is a decision
rule for a C0 working lead, not a probability of decipherment.

If the status model survives, the next content reading is a constrained
two-step card—“entry/member, then visible state/quality”—for the enumerated
circular labels only. It may guide a later search for the same state field in
an already exposed paragraph, but it cannot export `AQABBA`, `okaldy`, or
`otaldy` as a substance, verb, planet, or reusable substring. If it fails,
the useful conclusion is sharper than another missing-anchor audit: local
formal blocks explain the recurrence without a presently supportable semantic
state, and the circular family should be left as catalogue texture.

## Why this is genuinely new enough

GDT948 and GDT949 made complete multiword process/number readings, but their
singleton gloss freedom and absent object or operation bindings left their
semantic alternatives tied. Extending those paragraphs would mostly add more
chosen values. GDT797 tested only four host-domain events and explicitly left
the status/facies interpretation at C0 with a six-permutation `p=.333333`.
GDT795's failed fixed-member transfers do not test visual state; its positive
local adjacency result supplies the structural reason to test a field layered
over learned entries. The proposed outcome can change the next research
decision while retaining every known counterexample and without reopening any
page, reserve, sealed folio, or source corpus.

The test should be abandoned if the retained image annotations cannot define
the finite torso-state categories before family counts are inspected. A
convenient category invented after seeing `AQABBA` would reproduce the exact
singleton-repair problem that stopped GDT948/949.
