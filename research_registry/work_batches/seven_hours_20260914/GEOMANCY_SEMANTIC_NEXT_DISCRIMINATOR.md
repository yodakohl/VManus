# GDT957: semantic consequence review

## Fixed result and information limit

GDT957 tested the late Turner common program against all fifteen f66r outer
margin groups in both directions for ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b. The IT2a margin has
15 distinct whole forms and is compatible with 16 mother assignments in each
orientation. Every surviving assignment uses 15 distinct figure values and
leaves one of the 16 named figures unused. ZL3b and RF1b retain multiple
assignments because two groups are uncertain. No independent confirmation leaf
exists, and no word meaning is established.

This matters for semantics: the IT result supplies a one-to-one relabelling of
15 unique forms into 15 figure values, but no repeated target form or repeated
figure relation from which a content word could be checked. The 16 survivors
are the possible omitted figure, not 16 semantic readings supported by the
page. The full f66r JSON records show many paragraph and marginal forms, but
the fixed GDT957 contract makes only the fifteen outer groups register
positions; the surrounding text has no source-defined geomantic selector.

## Candidate consequence 1: house-domain reading

The Turner text assigns meanings to figures in houses. One could provisionally
read the 15 positions as a chart and ask whether the image and adjacent f66r
text supply the domains associated with, for example, house 6 (illness,
medicine, servants, domestic animals) and house 12 (enemies, imprisonment and
animals). The two rival orientations would then place different figure names
at those positions, giving different content expectations.

This is not an executable independent discriminator on the admitted material.
The margin contains isolated labels around one drawing, while the source text
does not say that a Voynich margin is a question with a querent, a house
selector, or a house-by-house answer. Choosing which visual feature counts as
illness, an animal, a medicine, or an enemy would be role assignment from the
same hypothesis. A mismatch would be an unbound-purpose failure, not a
semantic refutation; a match would be a selected visual analogy.

## Candidate consequence 2: complementary/reflected figure families

The source dictionary has fixed binary operations such as complement and
vertical reflection. A possible follow-up would predeclare that target forms
assigned to one such pair must show an independently observable relation in
the already exposed f66r material: exact recurrence, a known morphological
family relation, or a fixed positional pairing. This could distinguish
surviving assignments if that relation were independently specified.

No such independent relation is available. The fifteen margin forms are
distinct; paragraph forms such as `qokal`, `qokol`, and other `qok*` strings
cannot be treated as the same figure or as semantic derivatives under the
GDT957 injective whole-form rule. Conversely, declaring shared stems,
one-edit relations, or a particular outer-margin pairing after seeing the 16
assignments would invent a decoder. The source's semantic opposition between
named figures does not entail a Voynich spelling relation.

## Decision

There is no noncircular semantic discriminator available inside the admitted
f66r full-page/source915 records under the fixed GDT957 model. The model can
be retained as a conditional formal compatibility result, but the next
meaning decision should be **stop geomantic semantic expansion** until an
independent selector or source-defined content relation is admitted. Neither
the 16 IT survivors nor the historical house/name glosses license assigning
`rary`, `rals`, `qor`, or any other f66r form an English meaning.
