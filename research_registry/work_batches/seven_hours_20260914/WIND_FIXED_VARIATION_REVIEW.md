# GDT958 fixed-variation review

## Decision

This can be a genuinely different, bounded falsifier of the fixed GDT952
whole-title reference model, provided the variation rules are frozen before
the f67r2 body comparison. It is not yet a meaning test. A zero result would
close the added hypothesis that the W.73 reference network survives through
these already observed complete-form variants; a positive result would show
only conditional structural compatibility.

## What is genuinely new

GDT952 required each source wind's complete named title to occur literally in
the assigned f67r2 body. Its corrected comparison found zero definite
off-diagonal title recurrences (IT2a is contradicted even under its upper
unknown allowance; ZL3b and RF1b retain capacity uncertainty). GDT958 adds a
different, explicit hypothesis: a complete title or phrase may reach a body
form through one of two previously observed form relations, while retaining
the group sequence and all eight source edges. This is materially different
from searching for a convenient substring or repairing an unread token.

The supporting libraries are limited and should be described accurately.
GDT800 supplies 155 nonempty l/m paired stems and a strong terminal-position
association, but its A09 homolog is unique and its direction is unresolved;
it selected a structural boundary-favoured terminal surface, not an
allograph, inflection, or meaning. GDT915 supplies 22 known RR/LL phrase
pairs with exploratory same-ending co-variation; mixed cells remain, and
GDT916 did not establish transfer to new ordered stem pairs. Therefore these
are frozen structural pair libraries, not established linguistic rules.

## Required frozen contract

1. Use the corrected GDT952 complete title/body records and fixed ownership,
   all three readings, and the eight certain directed source edges. Preserve
   the excluded ambiguous Circius edge and all unknown statuses exactly.
2. Define LM_ONLY and LR_PHRASE_ONLY as finite reachability over the exact
   witnessed GDT800 and GDT915 pairs. COMBINED must have a fixed composition
   rule and order (or one explicitly defined finite closure); no post-hoc
   choice of the model or operation order is allowed. A relation may apply
   only to an exact complete group or exact licensed two-group phrase. No
   substring, new stem pair, group split/merge, spelling repair, or invented
   boundary is allowed. Repeated application must not create unbounded edits.
3. Freeze every candidate title variant before reading or comparing its
   assigned body. The same declared reachability rule must be used for every
   one of the 12! assignments, with no selected example or preferred wind
   orientation. A cross-role title/body invariance is an explicit working
   assumption, not evidence supplied by GDT800 or GDT915.
4. Keep definite match, contradiction, and unresolved capacity separate. A
   possible variant through an unknown group is not an observed match. Report
   zero additional matches as a closed extension only when all models,
   readings, assignments, and eligible complete records have been evaluated.

## Closest failure mode and consequence

The nearest rescue is applying an l/m or RR/LL operation to only the fragment
that happens to resemble a wind name. That would simply reintroduce the
fragment rescue rejected by GDT952's whole-title contract and must invalidate
the test. The other danger is treating GDT915's association as universal
agreement; GDT915 directly contains mixed RR/RL cells, so the closure must
remain a finite license, not a rule that forces all endings to agree.

Under the frozen contract, zero added complete edge matches in every model
closes this reference-invariant extension and leaves the GDT952 wind result
unchanged. Any added matches, including COMBINED-only matches, remain a
conditional finite graph compatibility result: they do not identify a wind,
prove a title/body synonym, or establish l/r morphology. Only a later,
separately selected content reading could give the graph semantic force.
