## Where the specified development budget stops

The first two proposed new schemas already use the entire allowance:

1. `DeferredClause := FINALLY UnaryOperation`. It needs an explicitly bounded
   later preparation and a specified final patient. Old finite operations
   execute in written order; a manner label cannot change that for free.
2. `MediumClause := WITH_MEDIUM PartitionOperation SupplyKind`. This reverses
   the medium/operation order of old M1. Its only allowed medium remains
   already-contained WATER; this does not license oil or new supply.

The result/property construction for IT .2 and common .3 is an additional
unlicensed obligation. So are the postposed Q-part measure, the final
fronted-input/output-powder frame and the final rinse-patient/distant-supply
frame. They are not bundled into a universal “arguments may occur anywhere”
rule to remain numerically below two. This is not a proof that every possible
grammar requires that many rules; it is the honest cost of this attempted
construction inventory.

The nine listed nondefault choices/obligations are: the extent of FINALLY;
its final patient; the first sieve's powder/coarse output association; IT's
FINE assertion; proper-subportion scope shared by both PARTLY instances;
FINE on the worked portion in .3; Q on the new fine branch; the last POWDER Q
as the coarse branch's grinding result; and J as the last rinse supply.
This already exceeds eight; separate counting of PARTLY's two instances
would increase it. No claim is made that this accounting is an optimized
lower bound over all readings.

## Unsuccessful alternatives preserved rather than erased

The JSON retains the complete lexical skeleton and changed assignments of
the earlier serial-oil attempt. It proposed OIL_IMPREGNATED_PLANT_MEAL,
THINLY DRY, WITH_MEDIUM SIEVE OIL, followed by the same whole later sequence.
The specific oil-medium binding contradicts V2's water-only WITH_MEDIUM.
Changing that medium to WATER under ordinary serial DRY leaves no retained
water. These are specific incompatible bindings, not a universal rejection
of every scope alternative.

A second whole-word variant used SPREAD where the present draft uses DECANT
and WATER for yty. SPREAD supplies no free rinse-water stock, and Q cannot
quietly measure water: Q is fixed plant-solid mass. This variant remained
unfinished. DECANT and yty=POWDER are explicitly new developmental choices,
not undisclosed repairs to old words or a completed test.

The literal N1 parse of `otchol chol` means COARSE_GRIST and FINE of the same
input, contradicting V2's disjoint grades. Making FINE instead the result of
grinding the **whole** R removes the positive coarse branch needed by the
intended next separation. The present PARTLY proposal avoids that particular
state conflict only by paying for a new resultative/subportion construction.
Selecting an older partition's coarse branch would be another explicit
reference choice. GDT811's known f4r counterexample concerns its former
obligatory two-slot grammar; it does not establish or reject this new meaning.

The result is therefore a fully preserved whole lexical attempt with exact
unresolved obligations. No new target is selected, no old lexical/material
contract is edited, and no simulator or corpus calculation is used to turn
the unfinished story into a semantic result.
