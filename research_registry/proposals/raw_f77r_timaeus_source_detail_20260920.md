The account is qualified as probable. Earth meets fire and is broken by its
sharpness, whether surrounded by pure fire, air or water. Its fragments continue
moving until they meet and reunite as earth; they never become a different form.
Water broken by fire or air can become one fire corpuscle plus two air corpuscles.
The fractions from one dissolved air particle form two fire corpuscles.
A small quantity of fire enclosed by a large quantity of air and water, or earth,
moves within the surrounding rushing bodies; when defeated and broken up, two
fire corpuscles unite as one air form. Defeated, disintegrated air combines into
water at two whole air forms plus a half per whole water form.

Thus the proposed whole account owes not only element names, quantities and
conditions but continued motion, encounter/reunion, relative surrounding amount,
and probable assertion status. The previously proposed predicate inventory is
expanded here before registration by MOVES, MEETS, ENCLOSES and LIKELY; none
has an identified target word. The exact “air and water, or earth” source wording
is retained as a collation/scope issue, not flattened into three independently
interchangeable agents. No source-conditioned transformation is asserted to be
an unconditionally executable physical program.
