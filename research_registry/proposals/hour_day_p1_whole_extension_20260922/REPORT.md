V1 merely asserted a common phase. V2 proposes comparing
`step(S0,24,FRAME_UNITS)` with `return_to(S0,24,sequence)`. The step/return
operations and unit identity are not defined by the complete account; the
second endpoint written in C08 does not occur in this comparison at all.

and units, equality follows from that assumption and the repeated number. If
they are distinct operations or units, their relationship is still unspecified.
Neither branch supplies a new cross-clause constraint on the old P2 account.

Consequently there is no justified numeric experiment here. In particular,
we do not invent a return after 24 ruler steps, derive a conflict with seven,
and call that conflict a manuscript result. The draft never fully binds that
claim. Shared versus separate P1/P2 phase, chronological versus retrospective
scope, and the unresolved reader branches remain viable alternatives.
