Uncertainty: potentially useful for a future paired image/text check, but catalog-level linkage alone does not resolve the relation.

For example, the official item for fol. 20 identifies an iris illustration: [NYPL item](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/5e66b3e9-0517-d471-e040-e00a180654d7). This exclusion is based on the holding library's stated date, not on visual resemblance.

## Screen result

No inspected candidate establishes the required source capacity. Egerton 747 is the strongest follow-up because its plant pictures are text-linked, but this screen did not find and verify a single named drawing with both structures and then read its corresponding source passage. Sloane 4016 and M.873 have useful image/caption records but do not supply the needed explicit written relation at the checked locations. The remaining records lacked an accessible named, matching entry. Keep the result at **missing-input / no capacity evidence located**, rather than treating the negative screen as proof that no historical source exists. Any next search should stay bounded and first nominate an entry from its own primary facsimile/catalogue evidence, then check its corresponding source passage.
