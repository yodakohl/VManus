## C. The ass repeats an action after its material changes

Owned source: Babrius, Fable CXI, “The Ass Carrying Salt”, Davies1860 English
verse translation from Sir G.C.Lewis, pp102–103; full poem including final moral,
not adjacent fables or the editor's commerce note. Ancient fable content is
separate from this nineteenth-century witness; no medieval transmission witness
was collated. The hosting page's title says James Davies while its printed-book
attribution says Rev.John Davies. This metadata discrepancy is retained; the
witness is designated Davies1860 here.
[Complete primary translation](https://www.elfinspell.com/ClassicalTexts/Babrius/Part1-Fables80-129.html).
Owned HTML SHA256: 1825e311bbacfa7af46b0777df1e34d0a7d9a0dd4e20891875589db4f4593711.
The complete CXI was directly read in this task. No claim of native scan collation.

Complete content: hearing salt is cheap at the coast, a merchant buys it and
loads his ass for home. The ass accidentally falls in a stream; salt dissolves
and it rises more easily to the bank. The merchant buys more salt and loads
it more heavily. At the same stream the ass deliberately slips, loses the salt
and rises triumphantly. The merchant changes his plan to carrying porous
sponges inland. The ass again falls deliberately; the sponges soak up water,
and it carries the heavier load home. This witness calls it a double bale.
The moral says earlier success can be followed by failure. There are three
crossings: accidental salt, deliberate salt, deliberate sponge. A two-event
salt/sponge abbreviation would omit the deliberate successful middle trial.

Concrete consequence: the same ass and intentional maneuver persist through
trials2/3, but the material-dependent weight change reverses. The error is a
belief generalized across a changed load, not an inverse action or new actor.
A shared story grammar must distinguish accident from purpose, a reported
expectation from an actual outcome, and a fresh load from the persistent carrier.
“Double” is this witness's literary quantity, not a universal absorption law;
no other translation's tenfold value is substituted. Mere eventual light/heavy
labels without load identity, wetting and expectation ownership would freely fit.

Closest conflicts: IDEA377's safe crossing depends on capacity and unattended
pairs; this fable instead depends on the sign of one material response. IDEA378's
concealed unweaving actually reverses an operation; this story repeats one
operation and invalidates the learned expectation. GDT785 keeps literal sal=salt
as C0 rival and explicitly gives its visual resemblance zero weight. This card
does not use sal as salt. Old action renderer defaults are not measured facts.
