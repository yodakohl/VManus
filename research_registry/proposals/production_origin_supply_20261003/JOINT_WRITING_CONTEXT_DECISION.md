# Joint writing process and lexical context after GDT1160

2026-10-03. Bounded decision review only. No target query, fit, new control, code or decoder repair. Recommendation: **do not implement a joint target model from the present proposal**. GDT1160 is a positive supervised source-ranking result; it does not provide the missing target candidate domain.

## The one potentially useful path

Model complete written groups as emissions of a shared latent lexical sequence through a constrained writing process. Preserve the observed order and spaces; represent directed components, pay for whole-form residuals, and condition rendering on actual position/context. Learn the sequence context jointly, but retain uncertainty over complete readings rather than declaring a winning hidden label a meaning.

This would use real positive constraints: GDT608 requires both directed composition and exact whole-form residuals; GDT316 supports fresh q-compatible surface choices after DY; GDT915 supports familiar phrase dependencies, with GDT916 retaining its failure on new stem pairs. None authorizes deleting all q, merging all terminal variants, calling a component a letter, or imposing unrestricted agreement. Actual whole-form frequencies and section/leaf distributions remain constraints on every candidate meaning.

The desired gain is to separate content identity from writing variation, then let context rank the resulting alternatives. That is more specific than changing the language model to a neural architecture. However, the separation itself currently lacks a discriminating target observation.

## Why an unconstrained joint fit would not identify that separation

In a model P(written sequence | layout) = sum over latent sequences of P(writer output | latent sequence, layout) P(latent sequence | context), a common permutation of latent names changes no observation. This ordinary label symmetry is harmless if reported honestly: useful relations can remain identifiable up to renaming.

The more serious freedom is reallocating information between lexical identity, writing state and whole-form exceptions. If both the latent context model and the emission process can adapt freely, different apparent lexica can explain the same word frequencies and position effects. A bottleneck or MDL preference may select a compact representation; it does not thereby show that its merged forms have the same meaning. This is not a universal argument that unsupervised recovery needs an already translated anchor. A sufficiently fixed writing law, a content-preserving intervention, or a constraining joint external relation can break the relevant symmetry without supplying individual translations.

## Exact predecessor consequences

- **GDT609:** the mixed abbreviation architecture is historically motivated; 34 slots and macro budgets are engineering regularization, not an observed Voynich writing law.
- **GDT616:** the original full synthetic constraint system is UNSAT. Four forbidden child-gate violations are needed in its nearest diagnostic relaxation. This is not a successful latent channel awaiting a neural optimizer; no generator repair is selected.
- **GDT207:** diplomatic character statistics help relatively but the fixed one-source-sign/one-letter target screen remains rejected. Adding mapping freedom needs a different observation, not a better score alone.
- **GDT276:** compiler-conditioned host character and preceding-host context already improve coding. Its operational abbreviation-heavy label did not identify a language.
- **GDT398:** latent predictive equivalence gave an apparent paid gain, but only five of eleven folds improved; frequency beat it by 11,686.7 bits and all matched worlds surpassed its gain. A new latent lexicon must defeat this concrete shrinkage counterexample.
- **GDT829/GDT928:** the fixed repeated-context reflow and multi-anchor complete-paragraph routes did not supply their intended same-content comparisons. Their criteria are not relaxed here.
- **GDT835/GDT995:** full writing compatibility can genuinely eliminate wrong readings, but their particular wrong control maps are already resolved. They are not unsolved neural targets.
- **GDT1160:** contextual ranking works when identical retained marked forms have independently recorded alternative expansions. It neither recovers the writing channel nor justifies importing German expansions into Voynich.

## Smallest prediction that would change the decision

For one predeclared family, fix two rival complete analyses that disagree about whether a surface difference belongs to lexical content or to writing state. On a content-preserving contrast whose identity does not come from the fitted model, freeze the predicted alternative written form (or exact finite set) under a changed observed writing condition. Retain the rest of both complete records and every occurrence of the family. A further crossed case must vary content while holding the writing condition fixed, preventing topic/layout from standing in for identity.

Success could license that **specific lexical-equivalence/channel constraint** when constructing a finite reading lattice; failure could reject that channel account. This would shrink alternatives before lexical ranking, without pretending to have named the latent word. Improved masked-token likelihood alone would not make this decision.

The essential missing input is one such operational contrast or an independently constrained source-to-written relation that plays the same role. It is **not** necessarily a known plant name, pointer for every token, or a complete bilingual passage. The reviewed existing inputs do not instantiate it. No follow-up capacity audit, control corpus or new decoder is selected in this note. Without that contrast, joint training currently changes a compression model rather than a defensible reading choice.
