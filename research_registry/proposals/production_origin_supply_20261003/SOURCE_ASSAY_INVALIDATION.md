- Latin: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/L/Roman/Texts/Pliny_the_Elder/33*.html#127

The passage gives a heated-shaving test with white, red and black outcomes and different judgments. It then describes pretreating the iron testing implement with urine, producing deceptive whiteness. It ends with a separate breath test of polished silver. The treatment belongs to the implement before the assay; the deceptive visible result belongs to the shaving. No successful purification is asserted by that deceptive result.

The bounded consequence is **same visible sign, different evidential force**, conditioned by how the instrument was prepared. A whole reading cannot collapse observation, material quality and acceptance into one variable. This is a claim about the source's account, not proof that its tests work.
