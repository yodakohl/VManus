4 October 2026. The user authorizes continuing the feasibility check before a
contextual inverse experiment. This is an exposed-source descriptive audit,
not a fitted experiment, fresh confirmation, or repair of GDT1166.

## Decision before computation

The finite supplied declaration relation covers many source groups, but that
does not establish coverage of unseen written forms by other-book reference
words or enough ambiguous cases for a contextual comparison. GDT1160 supplies
conditional context benefit with training inventories; GDT1164/1166 failed
their different unsupplied-candidate contracts.1166's reference-only ceiling
was45.366%. Its failure, and the three local counterexamples in REPORT.md,
remain. The seven exposed manual cases demonstrate limited plausibility only.

Question: under the unchanged source declaration and six leave-one-book-out
reference splits, how much exact recovery is even possible for written forms
absent from the other five books, and where could context improve on global
reference frequency? This decides whether to design a small source-context
comparison, not any Voynich reading or language. Poor coverage stops this
closed-vocabulary design; adequate coverage alone does not justify a decoder.

Smallest adequate work: reuse the pinned projector and declaration; census
all selected groups, all reference-supported candidate domains, exact-answer
coverage, frequency-only credit, and their maximum possible improvement.
Inspect reference context support without training or selecting a context
scorer. Validate domains independently and retain complete occurrence rows.
Working estimate: preparation10min, implementation20min, validation10min,
interpretation/publication15min. At expansion beyond this55min task estimate,
reassess rather than add models or corpora. This is not a replacement of the

## Fixed inputs and splits

Only the six XML books and two declarations already pinned in SUMMARY.json.
No Voynich input. All sources and some answers have previous exposure; no
analyst blinding. The existing projector supplies full groups and record
boundaries, with spelling, case, spaces, unknowns and provenance unchanged.
Keep all11,724 previously selected groups, including unresolved obligations.
Editorial selected-group labels define this diagnostic panel, not a proposed
blind detector of abbreviations in Voynich.

Each whole book is held in turn. The other five supply (a) the set of complete
written forms, (b) exact expanded-word counts, and (c) ordered expanded bigram
counts within their complete records. Unknown expansions are not vocabulary.
No expansion pair from the held book enters any of those supplies. Source
graphic classes and their possible strings remain given from the declaration.
Do not infer native glyph identity, hidden key, or a complete historical writer.

A known written form is NOVEL if its exact full native character tuple occurs
nowhere in the other five books, including ordinary unselected groups. Otherwise
it is SHARED. Unknown written forms are retained as UNKNOWN_WRITTEN obligations,
not invented lexical types. Unknown reference answers remain zero-credit
obligations and are separately counted. Distinguish novel written forms from
expanded answers absent from the reference vocabulary.

## Candidate capacity and context support

For every known written type, intersect the unchanged ordered declaration
relation with the entire other-book exact expanded vocabulary. Use a trie;
no candidate caps, spelling normalization, lemma matching, gold-injected
answers, or free letters. Empty domains stay empty. Score three different
ceilings: rule compatibility, reference vocabulary presence, and their
intersection. Neither ceiling is an observed decoder accuracy.

The fixed frequency comparator orders compatible reference words by decreasing
reference count, then exact Unicode string. It uses no held answers. Retain
all top-frequency ties separately. Maximum improvement is intersection
coverage minus this comparator's exact occurrence accuracy, also summarized
with equal written-type weights within books. It is oracle headroom, not a
predicted gain. Report per book and NOVEL/SHARED, not just frequent pooled words.

For each reference-supported ambiguous held focus, map each adjacent raw
written group to its full reference-supported declaration domain. A candidate
has left/right support when the other-book bigram inventory contains it
beside any member of that neighboring domain, in the appropriate order.
No held expanded neighbor is supplied. Count whether candidate supports
differ, whether only the gold candidate has any support, and whether another
candidate alone has support. This is a sparse support diagnostic, not a
ranker or a significance test; membership does not normalize candidate
frequency. Do not delete cross-record failures or widen the window afterward.

Keep full candidate lists, counts, support witnesses, focus and raw-neighbor
locators in compressed audit artifacts. Source locators identify historical
control books, never Voynich folios. Independently recompute every domain with
an anchored regex over the reference vocabulary and every summary from the
saved rows. Input/source hashes and complete-panel conservation must pass.

## Interpretation fixed before results

Zero headroom means this specific comparator cannot benefit on this panel;
small headroom or sparse supported contrasts limits the scientific scope of
a future test. Positive headroom plus unequal supports supplies opportunities,
not proof that a learned model will find them. No new success percentage is
selected after this census; any later accuracy contract must be prospective.
Related books, editorial restorations, incomplete graphic identities and
unknown spelling choices remain limitations. No source fit, new corpus,
threshold repair, unknown-key stage or target attack follows automatically.
