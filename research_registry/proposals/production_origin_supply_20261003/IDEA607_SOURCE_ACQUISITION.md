## Acquired bytes and boundaries

1. [The Book of Quinte Essence](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17179):
   Furnivall's revised1889 edition of Sloane73, with both books and the final
   explicit. The tract occupies printedpp1–25. Its English adaptation is itself
   an abridgement; it is not the complete Latin original. The separate Spheres
   text, editor summaries and notes must not silently enter its denominator.
   HTML and plain text were both retained; the HTML preserves thorn/yogh and
   expansion typography better. Gutenberg supplies a public-domain-USA notice.
   This work was already used in GDT378: today's acquisition restores raw
   readability, not an additional independent historical work.

2. [Regimen sanitatis Salernitanum](https://www.tha.de/~harsch/Chronologia/Lspost11/Regimen/reg_sana.html):
   Latin web transcription identifying the Tesdorpf/Tesdorpf-Sickenberger1915
   edition. All104 offered sections I–CIV are present consecutively. The ending
   is the final named section, not a broken download. The whole offered edition
   is inspectable; coverage of every historical recension and exact agreement
   with the print have not been established. The underlying medieval text is
   public domain; no explicit digital reuse license was identified. Raw bytes
   remain local and are not publication payload.

These are different medical text traditions: alchemical therapeutics versus
health/diet verse. Both have anonymous/pseudepigraphic authorship; two securely
identified independent named authors have NOT been established. Shared medical
inheritance prevents treating the texts as statistically independent samples.
