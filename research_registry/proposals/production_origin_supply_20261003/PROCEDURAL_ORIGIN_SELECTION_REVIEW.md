## The meaningful different falsifier and why it is not newly executable

The strongest candidate would distinguish:

1. split material M into outputs A and B; retain both;
2. later return the actual A to its associated B;
3. a subsequent written clause depends on their shared preparation lineage, so a fresh same-kind A would give a different claim.

This could express complete meaningful content without a plant-name anchor. The distinction is not simply “same word repeated”: it needs a written identity/lineage relation and a downstream consequence that differs even when material kinds agree. Otherwise generic references, material types and fresh stock produce the same reading.

IDEA392 already retains the historical extracted-water/retained-residue example and precisely this actual-output versus fresh-same-type alternative. Its source notation has29 positions,48 local bindings and67 predicate heads,43 singletons; those are authoring costs, not a bound proving that Voynich cannot express it. Its reviewed reopening term requires a whole script realization with differing consequences. GDT1133's missing check and GDT1137's kind-level equivalence do not supply that changed input. Adding a lineage field to an invented interpreter would enforce an analyst rule, not extract a new manuscript consequence.
