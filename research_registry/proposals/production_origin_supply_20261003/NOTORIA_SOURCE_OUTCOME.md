## What the original actually supports

Yale Mellon1, catalogued about1225 in France or northern Italy, is an early
witness of Ars notoria. Root directly viewed official images of ff1r–2v.
A separate reader inspected ff10r/v and18v; another independently read the
complete medical block on f2v. These are readers of one witness, not separate
historical replications. Image IDs, dimensions, hashes and native regions are
retained in the three linked receipts/reviews below. The dating is the
institutional catalogue's attribution, not a new palaeographic determination.

The f2v result depends on the entire branching context. A silent patient is
not always treated by the same rule: children and people unable or unwilling
to answer receive a separate instruction involving the practitioner's first
thought after the prayer. The clause following nescio is partially uncertain;
only the explicitly readable worsening clause is retained. The opening reads
`coram eo dic secrete`; an initial suggestion of an ear instruction was a
reading mistake and has been removed, with correction provenance in the review.

This supplies a historical production architecture: a stable verbal object,
ordinary instructions referring to it, and several context-dependent uses.
It does not supply a known generator for all the opaque words. Similar forms
in such a formula need not encode small differences between its applications.

## Decision for Voynich research

Retain907 as a concrete untested production alternative. It is different from
ordinary encrypted continuous prose and from an arbitrary meaningless-text
model: complete utterances can have an explicitly assigned use without each
syllable being an ordinary lexical item. The manuscript has not been shown to
implement it. Existing structural composition and whole-form residuals alone
cannot choose among these architectures.

Do not convert daiin into a divine name, AND, a repetition count, or any other
new gloss. No term-frequency likeness, short near-variant family or shared
circle design supplies a target unit/instruction relation. GDT954's fixed
seven-paragraph refrain and GDT919's complete Sator equation remain failed;
GDT954 already documented near-variant names in Picatrix. That general fact is
not rediscovered or counted as a new target result here.

A new test would require a fixed common writer and whole target construction
where stable formula reuse and content-changing word composition predict
different written continuations or arguments. No such target construction is
nominated by this source study. Consequently no new target census, genre
classifier, inverse model or relaxed refrain search is authorized by its result.
Confirmed Voynich words remain0. The gain is a directly verified source
mechanism and its constraints, not decipherment progress.
