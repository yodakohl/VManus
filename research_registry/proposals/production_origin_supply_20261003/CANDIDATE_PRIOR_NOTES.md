## Claim-bearing prior evidence and counterexamples

**chodaiin.** Bounded exact registry search returned IDEA000209/365, whose
questions concern other words in hypothetical passages. Targeted exact lookup
in the structural brief/map/baseline and material ledger did not locate a
word-specific supported meaning. This is limited retrieval, not a declaration
that the form was never studied. Do not inherit daiin, aiin, cho or ch values
from substrings, and do not treat shodaiin/chedaiin as established morphological
alternants. The complete-word cross-section distribution is already a required
countercheck against a narrow picture gloss.

**dol.** GDT302's positional alternant atlas is genuine formal evidence, but
its 145-form/37-opaque-host normalization is not semantic morphology. Later
material-measure/dose wording appears in historical renderer records; those
are not independent meaning evidence and are not adopted here. The current
whole-form profile is mostly internal, with both starts and ends. No amount,
material or organ interpretation is supplied by the visual assay.
Primary: `GDT302_HOST_POSITIONAL_ALTERNANT_ATLAS_REPORT.md`.

**dy.** GDT302 reports exact dy as a stable LAST positional alternant in its
frozen atlas; that is a physical-position association, not an exceptionless
boundary or the word "finished". The broader profile's 146 internal ZL uses
and repeated dy are obligatory retained observations. GDT316/317 establish
q-choice effects following a **structural DY-closure indicator**, not a test
restricted to the independent raw word dy. Do not export those class results
to a whole-word meaning or count embedded -dy as free dy. GDT608's d+y merge
has a right closure tendency but fails naive left-head composition; learned
unit dy is not automatically the separate raw word dy.
Primaries: `GDT302_HOST_POSITIONAL_ALTERNANT_ATLAS_REPORT.md`,
`GDT316_FRESH_Q_POST_DY_REPORT.md`, `GDT316_FRESH_Q_POST_DY_METHOD.md`,
`GDT317_Q_POST_DY_CONTROL_CALIBRATION_REPORT.md`, and GDT608 REPORT.md.

**kol.** Exact registry search was empty, followed by targeted structural
brief/map/baseline and ledger lookup, also without a specific reviewed claim.
This is not evidence of no previous research. The admitted profile supplies
an A/Herbal concentration, not a plant-part meaning. Keep kol separate from
kal, qol, okol and embedded kol sequences. The differences between ZL's 24 H
uses and IT/RF's 19/18 are real alternate-reading sensitivity, not replication.

**oky.** The old 13-event OKY/OKCHY workshop comparison proposed a take/use
contrast within an already interpreted passage; its own report keeps synonymy
alive. It is not independent evidence that oky means an action or reference.
The present corpus has 84/88/86 exact occurrences, so that small rendered
example cannot stand in for the full distribution. No part value is exported
from ok, k or y. Primary retained only as provenance:
`experiments/yolo/sidequest_semantic_oky_okchy_four_hundred_twentieth/FOUR_HUNDRED_TWENTIETH_REPORT.md`.

**sho.** GDT302 calls its FIRST tendency provisional. GDT810/HISTORY explicitly
traces the old moist-preparation wording to sh+o semantic composition; its
three supposed related contacts pool one sho with two sheo. Those do not
constitute three sho confirmations. W17 tests five sho occurrences under an
already hypothetical liquid lexicon; its global internal-reference variants
have no antecedent at f22v and f45v, and differ in their later f102v2 reference.
That is a conditional reading exercise, not independent evidence for liquid,
anaphora or an organ. Keep sho distinct from sheo, shol, shor and schor.
Primaries: `GDT302_HOST_POSITIONAL_ALTERNANT_ATLAS_REPORT.md`,
`experiments/yolo/gdt810_quality_arity_value_run/HISTORY.md`,
`research_registry/proposals/translation_programs_20260912/work/W17/REPORT.md`.
