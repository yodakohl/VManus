## Documented assignments

Wagner's Table3 separates body-text sources from illustration assignments:

|Witness / entry|Text source|Illustration|
|---|---|---|
|Egerton747 f4r, Appium emoroidarum|Pseudo-Apuleius, Botracion statice|Divided basal leaves, no flowers|
|Casanatense459, Tertia speties|Botration species3–4 plus Pseudo-Apuleius|Derived from Egerton emoroidarum, with flowers|
|Casanatense459, Apium emoroydarum|Pseudo-Apuleius synonyms; Circa instans section4|Different umbellifer; following Apios remains unillustrated|
|Clm28531 f1v, Appios|Dyascorides alphabeticus, Apios|Corresponds to Casanatense emoroydarum image|

Clm also retains an emorroidarum entry with the earlier illustration and associated text sources. Wagner reconstructs chapter splitting followed by reassignment of illustrations. This is a scholarly transmission reconstruction, not our native verification or a reciprocal swap.

## Exact locator capacity and remaining gap

- Secure in Wagner: Egerton747 f4r; Clm28531 f1v; Masson116 p30 and Sloane4016 f5v for the emoroydarum/Apios entries.

No image-number-to-folio conversion was inferred. Original WDL and LOC JSON endpoints did not resolve through the web tool.

## Research consequence

There is a concrete medieval precedent for illustration attachment changing while identifiable entry texts persist. It supports separating text identity from nearest-picture identity in future hypotheses; it does not establish such displacement in Voynich. A possible next source-only packet would verify the four assignments above from exact manuscript locations before defining any target consequence. Do not reopen or relax the failed GDT1158 panel.
