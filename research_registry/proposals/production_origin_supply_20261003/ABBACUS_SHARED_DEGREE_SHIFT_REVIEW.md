## Actual historical source

Jacopo da Firenze's *Tractatus algorismi* is dated 1307; its Vatican witness
Vat. lat. 4826 is a mid-fifteenth-century copy. The algebra occupies
ff.36v–43r. Høyrup reproduces its twenty-case register and translates actual
rules/examples in Appendix A. Power names include cosa, censo, cubo and
censo di censo. The three arrangements of degrees (2,1,0) recur at (3,2,1)
and (4,3,2). The fourth rule's loan example yields
x²+40x=200 and x=√600−20. The fifth rule instead has the number added to
the square side, requiring subtraction under the square root and two
branches. The manuscript announces fifteen additional cases but supplies
fourteen; hypothetical missing cases must not be manufactured. These are
historical prose rules, not modern symbolic notation on the manuscript page.

Source: [Høyrup 2006, pp.8–9 and Appendix A pp.38–39](https://rucforsk.ruc.dk/ws/files/36102953/Hoyrup_2006_Jacopo_da_Firenze_and_the_beginning.pdf).
The study includes direct historical translations; this review did not inspect
the Vatican original. Compound-power terminology must stay witness-specific.

## Precise mechanism, formalized here

Let a term consist of a coefficient and an anonymous power class U_d. A
degree shift S_k maps **every term on both sides** from U_d to U_(d+k),
preserving coefficients, side membership and the full additive arrangement.
For example, the three configurations are:

| Arrangement | Original degrees | Shift +1 | Shift +2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| aU₂+bU₁=cU₀ | 2,1;0 | 3,2;1 | 4,3;2 |
| bU₁=aU₂+cU₀ | 1;2,0 | 2;3,1 | 3;4,2 |
| aU₂=bU₁+cU₀ | 2;1,0 | 3;2,1 | 4;3,2 |

This is a shared relation over complete records, not an interpretation of one
changed ending. Its surface analogue need not be literal affix addition:
one independently fixed compositional channel would have to realize the same
class successor in all term positions, including the far side of the relation.
Whole-form exceptions cannot be supplied record by record.

Mathematical qualifications in this review, **not newly attributed historical
statements**: cancelling a common x^k preserves solutions only on x≠0;
multiplication can introduce x=0. Positive-quantity scope would avoid that
case only if actually justified. Normalizing by a leading coefficient needs
it nonzero. For x²+bx=c, the usual positive solution is
√((b/2)²+c)−b/2. For bx=x²+c, the two branches
b/2±√((b/2)²−c) require nonnegative discriminant; equality gives one repeated
solution. The branch distinction is stronger than recognizing repeated powers.
No target zero, coefficient, equality or positivity word is assumed.

## Distinct observational consequence and limits

A complete reading could predict that several differently positioned term
classes change by one shared relation while coefficient expressions and the
side/operation skeleton remain invariant. A second homologous record would
have to reuse that relation; a mixed shift, changed coefficient or unexplained
side movement contradicts the fixed account. This can be checked anonymously,
before identifying lexical roots. It does not require a pretranslated equals
word, provided the full candidate grammar jointly proposes its role and pays
for that choice in every occurrence.

But surface synchrony alone does not identify algebra: case agreement, item
classification and formal template copying can have the same relation. The
multi-step solution branch, or another consequence that distinguishes these
rivals, must be part of a complete candidate account. Selecting three similar
strings and naming their differences degrees would not supply that account.
