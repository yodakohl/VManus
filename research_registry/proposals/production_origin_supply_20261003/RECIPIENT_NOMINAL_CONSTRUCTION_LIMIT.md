## Content consequence and falsifier

The Adrianum source has different administration cases involving condition polarity and carriers. A genuinely discriminating recipient account must write a positive-P and a negative-P case and bind their different continuations to those cases. A chronological material account instead performs successive operations; a case-list account must not execute every alternative merely because it is mentioned.

The smallest strict extension would add one fixed negation rule `CHEY C(P,x) = NOT P(x)` with immediate scope, plus an explicit administration/carrier consumer on each branch. Its written falsifier is simple: it fails to realize the claimed opposed-P pair if no such negative-P constituent occurs in the nominated packet. The actual CHEY is followed by TAL, and its whole line contains none of KEDY/OKEDY/QOKEDY. Thus this strict extension cannot express the required opposite-polarity pair here. TAL is not made an alias for P, and distant QOKEDY is not silently brought into CHEY's scope.

This does not refute negation in other grammars or assert that every case-list must contain this particular construction. It rejects this finite extension as a test of the source card. A carrier meaning or longer-range scope could be proposed separately, but would be another paid construction whose context must be read; none is selected here.
