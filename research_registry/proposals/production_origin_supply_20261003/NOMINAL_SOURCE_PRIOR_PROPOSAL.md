Definitions sent to root before annotation:

- **WATER:** historically asserted ordinary physical water, including mineral,
  spring, sea, rain or meltwater when identified as water. This follows the
  author's material category, not a modern chemical assay. Exclude wine, bodily
  humors and named aqua-vitae/Quinte-essence/herbal distillates unless ordinary
  water or an aqueous constituent is explicitly identified. Ambiguous water
  nomenclature in a named distillate is uncertain, not automatically explicit.
- **AIR:** physical atmospheric or elemental air, including explicitly identified
  moving/inhaled air. Wind, breath or vapour alone does not establish AIR; neither
  heat, cold nor a season is an air mention.
- **BASIN_ART:** an artificial concave receiving/holding space with basin, pool
  or bowl function. An explicit artificial bath pool or open bowl qualifies.
  Generic vessel, flask, glass or closed still does not qualify without stated
  bowl/pool geometry or basin function. A bath establishment alone is not a basin.
- **PIPE_ART:** an artificial hollow conduit conveying material between places.
  A specified tube/pipe/channel qualifies. An aperture, every container, a stick,
  a stem or a procedure alone does not.
- **BASIN_NAT:** a natural concavity/chamber/pool denoted as a collecting/holding
  reservoir. An unambiguous reservoir name, or an anatomical referent prospectively
  classified as such, can qualify without a separately written storage event.
  Generic organs are not automatically reservoirs.
- **PIPE_NAT:** a natural physical conduit denoted as a conveying route. An
  unambiguous duct/channel name or a prospectively declared anatomical class
  such as vein/artery can qualify without a separately written flow event.
  Every organ and every plant stem is not a conduit; hollow-conduit denotation
  or an explicitly identified antecedent is required.

This denotational correction was supplied to root before annotation following
the pre-registration reviewer's symmetry challenge. Material nouns and typed
container/conduit nouns must meet the same explicit-denotation rule; the natural
categories must not quietly acquire an extra event predicate. Root's registration
must fix the anatomical denotation list and ambiguity policy before counting.

Each complete unit gets explicit/uncertain/absent per concept with source spans.
Quotes, negations and conditions invoke their concepts even when events do not
occur. Explicit anaphora requires an identified antecedent. Heading/topic carry
is separately marked; implied ambient air, inferred vessels and modern anatomy
do not become written concepts. Absence requires reading the whole unit.
