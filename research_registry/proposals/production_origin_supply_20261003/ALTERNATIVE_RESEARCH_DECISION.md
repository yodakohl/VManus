# Alternative content route: formation evidence without inherited glosses

2026-10-03. Bounded selection review; no new manuscript data, images, fits or reserve access.

**Decision: retain a compositional content hypothesis, but select no new test from this review.** The strongest alternative to an invented local schedule is the existing OL/OR and CH/SH–CTH formation evidence. It reduces the number of independent lexical cards an exploratory author needs. It does not currently supply an observable distinction between a static property, a processed material and an amount/action construction. Repeating the old paragraph comparison would not change that.

The retained positive is specific. [GDT628](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt628_chol_measure_frame/REPORT.md) reports 48/54 lattice cells and 23/24 core OL cells, with direct, fused and separated value-like expressions. [GDT629](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt629_part_quality_degree_clause/REPORT.md) records `chor chol daiin` at f21r.12 and f32v.10 and, at the same physical spans on f49r.6 and f100r.22, ZL/RF `choldaiin` against IT `chol daiin`. These are alternative transcriptions of one manuscript, not independent semantic confirmations. [GDT631](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt631_prefixed_cth_quality_parts/REPORT.md) supplies five identical remainders with both CH and SH prefixes and the same-span `sh cthey`/`shcthey` bridge at f21r.9. Its dry/moist and plant-part words remain hypotheses. In particular, `chcthy shol` and `shol chcthy` cannot be discarded as inconvenient cases.

A historically concrete authorship model is a compact materia-medica register that combines a material/part class, a reusable modifier and a value field. The rival is a recipe register combining material state and quantity or action. Both are legitimate C0 authoring models without translated seeds. The formation evidence constrains their reuse; it does not choose the thermal orientation, numerical values, material names or whether CHOL is “dry”, “dried” or “crush”. The complete source span's fusion versus separation also cannot choose those meanings: a property phrase and an action/amount phrase can each be written with either boundary convention.

The proposed narrow experiment was checked against its actual predecessor. [GDT809](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt809_record_conditioned_whole_head_semantic_tournament/REPORT.md) already compares these alternatives with one 16-entry dictionary across four complete cached paragraphs, f17r.4–6, f21r.8–12, f32v.7–11 and f29v.1–4. It proposes values at 46/145 positions, retains 99 unresolved positions, `chol chol`, `daiin daiin`, and both CHOR–CHOL–DAIIN sites. Static degree versus material/portions remains unresolved. Its 735 unobserved candidate-specific requirements are not counterevidence. This predecessor already permits partial authoring in complete contexts; requiring a near-complete paragraph lexicon is therefore unnecessary, but removing that requirement alone creates no new semantic test.

The smallest genuinely discriminating consequence would be a **written dependency under a fixed attachment rule**: a property construction returns a property of a particular bearer, whereas an action construction introduces an event or a changed material that a later construction actually uses. Repetition alone is insufficient: two repeated qualities may have different bearers or times; two actions may describe the same operation. None of the reviewed bridges establishes the bearer, time or later consumer independently of assigning another unknown neighbour the desired meaning. Introducing that neighbour separately at each occurrence would be circular fitting. I cannot presently name an unused observable criterion that avoids this donation.

Accordingly, no new source-only control, thermal restoration or large compiler is recommended. An author may still develop a narrow, explicitly partial CHOL construction with unchanged reuse and UNKNOWN neighbours; that is exploratory drafting, not a new discriminating test or a preference over its rival. A subsequent test needs the actual new attachment/consumer claim specified first, with the known repeated and contrary contexts retained. W94's ratio failure, W95's unbound heads, W96's parked amount/grade branch, GDT913's 18 failed frozen lexicons, GDT1047's universal-host failure and GDT1058→1105→1106 do not reopen merely because the glosses are renamed. The proposal also is not a rescue of GDT838's exact recoded-passage search or the failed unpaired controls GDT1159/1164/1166.

This review therefore stops at a useful formation prior and a concrete limit: **permission to draft a partial reading is broader than evidence selecting a word meaning.** No positive lexical identification or new research gate is claimed.
