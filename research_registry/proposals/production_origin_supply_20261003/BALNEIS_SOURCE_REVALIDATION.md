## Research consequence

Acquisition is not the barrier. The existing full text is available for a
prospectively defined source-sense annotation, but that annotation has not
been performed here. Existing AQUA witnesses should be reused rather than
recounted as a new finding. Any future analysis must distinguish lexical
nouns, indirect reference, bath names, bathing acts and built containers;
it must preserve contradictory and ambiguous uses. This single poetic
bath work cannot establish a universal frequency bound for medieval
medical prose, nor select between the GDT827 whole-paragraph hypotheses
without an independently justified source-to-target observation model.
