## Smallest nonduplicate consequence

For a future complete authoring, one preparation and one complaint with two
explicitly distinguished conditions already suffice: recommendation under C,
prohibition under D. Both independently written entry organizations must
preserve those condition/polarity bindings through one shared reading.
The raw proposal's two-by-two inventory is an authoring budget, not a minimum
implied by the manuscript.

This is stronger than matching names or transposing a co-occurrence matrix:
swapping C/D or dropping the prohibition changes the practical recommendation
while leaving the two entities' co-occurrence unchanged. A single all-positive
incidence pattern does not provide that contrast. Conversely, **absence from
an open-ended list is not prohibition**. Missing mention must remain unknown;
it cannot be promoted to a negative edge to make the converse unique.

The second assertion must be read from its own written construction. A program
that generates it automatically from the first will always pass by definition.
The hypothetical identities and condition expressions may be inferred jointly,
but adding an individual exception or fresh meaning for each side would remove
the claimed shared-writing constraint. This does not require a previously
confirmed plant name.
