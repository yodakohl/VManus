## The account

A compiler assembles short medical entries from exemplars. An entry can state its own subject and preparation while inheriting an earlier entry's indications through a written reference. A copying workshop renders these records using productive contractions, conventional complete forms and position-sensitive spelling. This is a composition of two historically motivated mechanisms, not a newly identified Voynich production history.

The existing `selective_entry_inheritance.json` supplies an actual source instance: the two Acharistum entries in the Reichenau Antidotarium have a full first indication list, a subsequent backward reference and unequal ingredient lists. Its institutional transcription was previously read; this review did not recollate it. GDT609 supplies period-relevant mixed abbreviation precedents, but its 34-slot architecture is an engineering choice and GDT616's fixed implementation remains failed.

The joint account accommodates the positive constraints as follows:

| Constraint | Required mechanism, not a recovered reading |
|---|---|
| Spaces carry hierarchy; unseen forms reuse directed parts | A productive writing system preserves bounded lexical or constructional groups and ordered abbreviation operations. No universal parser is supplied by the existing BPE tree. |
| Exact whole forms retain residual information | Conventional abbreviations and memorized entry phrases retain lexical/contextual exceptions; the account cannot discard them after decomposing a word. |
| Physical and paragraph position affect forms | Copying realization depends on position. GDT1155 forbids importing the same unaltered l/m rule at line descent and drawing interruption. Paragraph position is not automatically a new semantic referent. |
| Known r/l phrase pairs transfer, new pairs do not | Learned formula families can support restricted concordance without a productive universal suffix rule. Ordinary lexical grammar remains a rival. |
| Short forms recur broadly | Both repeated medical predicates and common grammatical/reference constructions are possible. The account does not name any actual form, assign every short form a function, or explain narrow nouns by frequency alone. |

These are compatibility requirements. They are **not** evidence that this particular compiler generated the manuscript. A free abbreviation channel appended to a plausible medical story would merely recreate the identifiability problem in `JOINT_WRITING_CONTEXT_DECISION.md`.

## A real content consequence, and its exact limit

Let a first complete entry contain indication set I₁ and ingredient list R₁. A second writes a reference to the first plus a demonstrably different list R₂. Freeze the reference's scope and replacement rules before interpreting the second entry:

| Complete account | Second entry means |
|---|---|
| Selective indication inheritance | I₁ with R₂ |
| Whole-entry inheritance without replacement | I₁ with R₁; incompatible with the independently written R₂ |
| Ingredient-list inheritance without replacement | Retains R₁, and does not supply I₁ |
| Whole-entry inheritance plus a fixed ingredient override | I₁ with R₂, exactly the same consequence as selective inheritance |

This concerns which treatment is prescribed for which conditions, rather than a classifier score. It can reject two specific complete accounts. It cannot distinguish the first and fourth: those are equivalent on this observation, even with perfect transcription and a genuinely bound reference. A shorter description would be a modeling preference, not a second semantic observation. A literal-prose rival that explicitly supplies I₁ can also be equivalent at the level of practical meaning.

The useful distinction would therefore be **I₁/R₂ versus I₁/R₁**, not naming the operator COPY or discovering a generic anaphora class. No reviewed native packet presently supplies that independently unequal content consequence under one fixed construction. This is a conclusion about this candidate, not an assertion that translation logically requires an already confirmed word.

## Why this does not authorize another immediate test

The strongest version is already retained in `selective_entry_inheritance.json`; `table_compilation.json` covers related header inheritance. No new raw card is warranted. GDT611 shows that exchanging semantic names inside formal families can leave the available frame evidence unchanged. GDT1099 gives only one already exposed p/y paragraph-head/reprise opportunity, not an entry-reference law. GDT1153 rejects the particular rule choosing a continuation because it alone appeared earlier; it must not be revived as antecedent resolution. GDT915 and GDT916 distinguish known phrase behavior from new-pair generality, not prescription from indication.

Consequently this review does not recommend another reference detector, layout classifier, control corpus or weakened suffix grammar. The account becomes worth authoring only when a complete candidate makes the treatment/indication distinction above constrain the same written material through one fixed reading. At present, neither the abbreviation layer nor the medical-entry layer independently narrows that reading. Calling their conjunction a big-picture solution would conceal the same two freedoms instead of removing either.
