# Positional covertext: source comparator, not a selected Voynich route

Status: bounded source review; no new proposal card, experiment, target access or word assignment. 2026-10-03.

## Actual source mechanism

Paolo Bonavoglia, *A Partenio's Stegano-Crypto Cipher*, HistoCrypt 2020, section 6.1, printed pp.39–40, describes Trithemius's *Polygraphia*, composed 1506–1508 and printed 1518. This is a later comparator, not evidence for a 1420 source. The paper reproduces the first three pages of the historical lists in figure 5. The review here reads the research paper; the full 1518 primary and all 384 lists have not been collated.

Source: https://ep.liu.se/ecp/171/006/ecp2020_171_006.pdf

Each successive plaintext letter selects a word from the next 24-letter alphabet/list. The 384 successive lists supply roughly interchangeable words at each position, making the output resemble a religious text. Consequently grammatical compatibility of neighboring output words can arise from table organization rather than the semantic relation between the concealed plaintext letters. The paper gives a warning about a dangerous man whose covertext resembles a devotional passage. This is a concrete separation of overt grammatical meaning and encoded message, not proof of either mechanism in Voynich.

Do not conflate this with the later Partenio mechanism: the latter uses ten digits and nine or fifteen lists. Do not transfer those numbers, or a proposed modern cycle length, into Voynich. Trithemius's list order also is not automatically periodic or reset at every paragraph.

## Why no new RAW card was added

A literal historical table has a finite consequence: fixed list index and output token together determine the encoded letter; the same payload at different indices generally has different output words. A free Voynich table, however, can simply absorb every observed whole-form/context association. No presently specified table-size bound, independent reset law or shared code column makes that model constrain an actual whole reading. A position classifier alone would not identify a concealed message, nor distinguish it from ordinary grammar.

GDT1160 establishes contextual abbreviation ranking on a supervised source task. It does not establish that Voynich words are context-indexed code entries. Productive components and whole-form residuals in the structural baseline likewise fit multiple mechanisms; they cannot license a free table. GDT912's incomplete marginal table is not filled or reopened here; GDT601's literal Naibbe failure remains intact. Lexical searches for Trithemius, covertext and steganography found no direct indexed card, but empty searches do not establish novelty.

This mechanism is useful as a limit on inference: recovering convincing surface grammar need not recover intended content. It is not presently a faster meaning route. No synthetic-control infrastructure or target test is recommended solely from this source fact.
