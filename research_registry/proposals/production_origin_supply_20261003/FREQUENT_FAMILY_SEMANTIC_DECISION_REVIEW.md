**Use the recurring construction as a constraint on a reading, rather than choosing an isolated noun for daiin. ## Strongest positive and its actual ceiling

[GDT626](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt626_mobile_operation_lexicon/REPORT.md) records 5,176 occurrences of the bounded raw pattern `HEAD+a+(n/in/iin/iiin)`, under 545 heads; 28 heads occupy three cells and 15 all four. The D family is `dan/dain/daiin/daiiin` (17/193/721/17 in that historical scope). Multiple cells of one head coexist on 136 lines, 96 reader-stable. [GDT627](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt627_value_head_role_atlas/REPORT.md) adds 27 repeated immediate-neighbour frames for D, including all four forms in `chol __ <line end>` across 13 witnesses. This is a useful substitution/parameter architecture: an economical productive account should explain the shared ending and the changed whole, rather than donate four unrelated meanings. It does **not** establish that the cells are ordered, cardinal I–IV, or mutually different meanings in every use. Roman-minim resemblance supplies a historical hypothesis; hot/cold/dry/moist heads remain inherited hypotheses, with the global thermal sign unresolved.

[GDT1149](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1149_minim_line_entry_control/REPORT.md) supplies the strongest exact-free-form placement constraint. In complete multiline P paragraphs, bare `ain/aiin/aiiin` occur internally 564 ZL/511 IT times and at continuation starts zero times; D counterparts occur internally 739/759 and at continuation starts 182/175. Exact aiin contributes 422/389 internal cases. Thus paragraph opening alone does not explain the zero. Other a-initial words supply eight continuation exceptions per reading, but also strongly avoid that position: no statistically distinctive minim mechanism was established. These are alternate transcriptions, not independent manuscripts. This suggests testing attachment or an entry-sensitive realization; it does not prove either.

The older [GDT040](../../../GDT040_AIIN_NESTED_WRAPPER_REPORT.md) supports an ordered carrier+D+AIIN construction in Currier B, while the corresponding double cell is absent in its Herbal-A scope. [GDT1051](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1051_frozen_grammar_local_application/REPORT.md) retains exact hosts, wrappers and residual internal o under two different frozen decompositions. Neither licenses a universal parser or a free-part translation.
