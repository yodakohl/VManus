## What unaligned readable text can identify

A fixed readable corpus gives named wordforms and asymmetric sequence probabilities. It can therefore break lexical permutations without supplying a translated target word:833 demonstrates this concretely. This differs from [GDT611](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt611_lexical_slot_permutation_audit/REPORT.md), where renaming formal slots leaves the measured likelihood unchanged. Confirmed meanings are not a prerequisite for exploratory constructions.

The dangerous freedom is simultaneous adaptation of writer rules, lexical identities and sequence grammar. A free writer can encode each observed wholeform as an independent card; a free latent language model can then learn whichever resulting sequence was chosen. Exact roundtrip, lower code length or fluent output alone cannot distinguish that from a reading. Even a finite genuine writer may leave different expansions observationally equivalent; unused rules and same-output parameter permutations must be separated from different plaintexts. Whole-form residuals may be paid and retained, but cannot silently become a fresh carrier for every spelling.

External primary work supports feasibility, with narrower assumptions. [Ravi and Knight2011](https://aclanthology.org/P11-1002.pdf) infer channels using unrelated monolingual text; their word-substitution experiments posit a one-to-one writer, and the broader translation experiments are partial recovery, not unknown historical abbreviation. [Luo et al.2019](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1303/) use constrained cognate correspondences and minimum-cost flow, reporting67.3% Linear B cognate recovery. [Luo et al.2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.5/) jointly infer segmentation/cognate alignment through phonetic historical-change priors. These establish neither an unrestricted channel nor complete plaintext from arbitrary structural grammar. Their restrictions are substantive identifying information.

## Actual new question and smallest adequate next action

**Question:** with no aligned expansion pairs, known symbol values, per-surface expansion dictionaries or oracle role labels at inference, can an independently bounded historical abbreviation family infer productive writing rules and recover every retained group of whole held texts from a separate readable corpus? This is different from merely using a neural language model with832's supplied deck or weakening906's grammar.

Before selecting a test, freeze a short literal writer contract: admitted operations and their conditioning, finite output domains, segmentation/space treatment, optional versus compulsory abbreviation, rule/wholeform costs, and explicit unknown/OOV policy. The contract must derive from historical practice rather than fitting the target's desired meanings or rescuing616. Do not call a TRAIN substring list a complete language grammar.

The already owned Nuremberg marked/expanded editions provide a possible **representation-level** panel: use one whole book's written stream with all carrier identities consistently anonymized, unrelated books' expanded text as the reference, and the held expansion pairs only after fit/prediction locks. No original letters or exact expansion pairs may initialize the hidden key. All complete groups, unusual spellings and ambiguity remain scored; no handpicked easy sentences. However, its single editorial marker erases native sign distinctions. It cannot identify the original native abbreviation inventory or certify a medieval writer. Source feasibility and finite-contract adequacy are unknown; no such extraction/test was run here.

The minimum comparison must distinguish inferred productive rules from the same writer with those rules supplied, plus a reference-frequency/order baseline; retain the whole inferred key, complete readings and materially competing readings. Independently reencode the actual predictions. Freeze complexity and budgets before gold; report duplicate formulas/source relationships rather than claiming fresh independent transmission. A positive likelihood or restart agreement cannot substitute for exact source recovery.
