## One finite index sentence model with an actual falsifier

This long-form contrast is diagnostic only, **not historically preferred**.
The compact positive authoring options below are the more plausible next
drafts; no preference for an injective literal writer follows from this review.

For clarity, the following are anonymous semantic-role variables, **not
assignments to any Voynich forms**. An expanded instruction has this fixed
tree:

    INDEX(body,
      LET r = REMAINDER(AD_YEAR + signed_adjustment, modulus)
      IN IF r = ZERO THEN modulus ELSE r)

Its source values would distinguish the two Honorius rules; none is fitted
here. The body's identity does not determine the modulus or adjustment.
This model specifies current cycle position, unlike a duration or a diagram.
The crucial obligation is the same modulus argument in two different steps.

Under a **transparent literal realization**, with one invariant whole-group
realization for each scalar and every repeated scalar written again, the two
modulus occurrences must produce an exact repeated group within each ring.
The upper ring has no such repeat in any reader. Therefore that narrowly
defined fully expanded realization is already incompatible with the exposed
upper ring, before deciding which number means nineteen or twenty-eight.
If it additionally writes YEAR, REMAINDER and ZERO as three distinct invariant
whole groups in both instructions, the cross-ring intersection cannot supply
all three even in IT. This second obstruction depends on the additional
lexical-realization assumption; historical prose does not guarantee it.

This is not a failed full decoder: no complete lexical assignment was built.
It is a necessary-condition argument for one explicitly stated sentence law.
Changing numeral values cannot fix it, and writing exceptions for just these
two records would abandon that law.

## A legitimate compressed rival removes that obstruction

The following *different* finite grammar defines a conventional one-based
wrap operation, so its modulus is stated once:

    INDEX(body, WRAP_ONE_BASED(AD_YEAR + signed_adjustment, modulus))
    WRAP_ONE_BASED(z,m) = 1 + ((z - 1) mod m), for positive integer m.

The zero case is part of this shared operator, not a silently dropped source
duty. Another possible grammar uses an explicit reference to the already
written modulus rather than spelling its value twice. Either can genuinely
encode the full computation. Neither currently has a demonstrated Voynich
realization. They must be proposed and costed before fitting, not introduced
as retrospective exceptions after a literal model fails.

With a shared finite constructor and arbitrary unresolved argument forms,
eight or eleven groups can accommodate such trees in numerous ways. Merely
filling every group with a new terminal does not make one a supported reading.
In particular, a numeral dictionary assigning each singleton its needed
source number adds no constraining consequence. A conventional composite
operator must transfer as the same operation; it cannot mean a different
entire source sentence at each occurrence.

## Minimal competing sentence requirements

### Short complete accounts worth actually authoring

The shortest plausible interpretation is not an imperative spelling out every
step. It may be a reckoning heading with typed parameters. These three finite
accounts have complete semantics without a list of invented neighboring glosses:

1. **Geometric caption:** “The circle of body B in frame F.” Its logical
   content is `CIRCLE_OF(B,F)`. This caption is a complete referring expression,
   not a claim to an unexpressed motion or an arbitrary property list.
2. **Return specification:** “Body B's return in frame F takes quantity Q
   of unit U.” Its content is `RETURN_INTERVAL(B,F,Q,U)`. No calendar input is
   required, and changing year alone does not change this idealized quantity.
3. **Reckoning specification:** “The B-cycle for era-year Y, adjustment a,
   cycle size m.” Its content is `CYCLE_INDEX(B,Y,a,m)=WRAP_ONE_BASED(Y+a,m)`.
   One conventional index constructor supplies the remainder and zero
   convention for both records. It cannot acquire extra operations separately
   for the second body. The Honorius alternatives instantiate different a,m;
   they do not supply target numerals.

The third is a source-grounded **proposed compression**, not a claim that the
Honorius witness literally writes just this short heading. The witnessed
algorithm fixes the constructor's interpretation; target use of that
convention would still be an explicit authoring assumption. It is much more
plausible than insisting that every procedural occurrence of a modulus be
written out identically. A common era/year or frame could be a discourse
parameter, provided the same bounded rule supplies it to both records. No
infinite omitted-text license is needed.

For a genuinely complete target draft, the unknown debts are specific:
which raw groups realize the constructor and its argument constituents; which
groups realize shared versus body-specific parameters; whether any parameter
is implicit by one fixed convention; how the extra lower groups modify or add
a clause; and how the ZL/RF opening difference is retained. The eight versus
eleven/twelve groups do not themselves answer those questions. These accounts
therefore complete the *semantic sentence*, not the manuscript translation.

One particularly useful source distinction is the two lunar indices with
the same modulus but different offsets. An account must distinguish their
index kind or adjustment; just “lunar cycle nineteen” collapses different
procedures. This is an obligation for authoring, not grounds to identify
any observed lower-ring group with either procedure. A lower repeated heading
could introduce two specifications in ZL/RF, but IT does not have that exact
repeat. That is an explicit reader-dependent alternative, not an endorsed
parse or a reason to normalize IT's forms.

These are actual finite semantic sentences. They are **not complete parses**
of either ring. Their expression obligations depend on a chosen writer: an
implicit common frame/year, inflection, incorporation or variable clause
length can change the expected surface repetition. Such mechanisms are not
forbidden, but each must be a shared rule with all-occurrence obligations.
An invariant heading need not occupy precisely the first raw group if its
placement rule says otherwise; it cannot move ad hoc to whichever group fits.

On the present exposed evidence, the common anonymous constructor can be
renamed from a diagram relation to a duration relation or an index relation,
with its unresolved arguments retyped. No known numeric result, repeated
year with a changed output, common frame, or later typed consumer breaks that
renaming. This is conditional interchangeability of the incomplete models,
not a claim that all possible complete readings are observationally equal.
The calendar model's distinctive future consequence is dependence on year;
the duration's is invariance under year in its fixed frame. A single pair of
different owners is not that contrast.
