# Gart1485: complaint register points to prescriptions, without restating their conditions

Decision: **SOURCE_REFERENCE_ARCHITECTURE_CONFIRMED_FOR_SELECTED_CASE; NO_TARGET_BINDING**. The first indication entry in the selected complaint register is a navigational list. It is not a second full statement of the corresponding prescriptions. IDEA000916's full-proposition reciprocity is one possible proposed writing mechanism, but this actual historical witness does not justify requiring it.

## Consequence for actual research

A jointly compiled indication index can preserve access to a treatment without repeating its full qualified proposition. Its short entries may be mostly chapter/authority/paragraph locators rather than a parallel dictionary of plant and disease names. This changes the proposed IDEA916 authoring obligation: full condition/polarity repetition is not a historical default. If a Voynich hypothesis posits that richer redaction, it must justify it from its own written account. A proposed pointer register instead owes actual consistent destinations and address grammar; it does not inherit medical word meanings from this source comparison.
