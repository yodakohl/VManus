## One policy to attempt

1. An operation word initially denotes an operation description. It does not
   by itself create a performed event. A governing frame must supply its mode,
   participants and any actual occurrence. This leaves a bare operation pending.
2. An operation description has at most one explicit FULLY degree slot. Read
   left to right: FULLY fills a preceding still-open operation's degree slot;
   if that slot is already filled/closed, it opens a prefix modifier requiring
   the next operation. NOT between an operation and FULLY prohibits that complete
   operation, rather than deleting either token. A modifier cannot be reused by
   two operations. This is an explicit new arity/closure assumption, not a
   discovered Voynich law or a ban on all possible redundant natural-language
   modifiers. AS_POSSIBLE can qualify a degree, preserving its separate token.
3. CALLED takes a prepared-subject expression and the maximal immediately
   following contiguous run of operation descriptions as a designation. Every
   repeated operation occupies its own label slot; none executes. A following
   nonoperation ends this label run. This rule is not applied to ungoverned
   duplicate words or to degree modifiers.
4. FOR expects a purpose expression; LONGER/HOWEVER_LONG expect a duration-
   bearing operation; BECAUSE links an explained proposition to a reason;
   AS_JUST_SAID expects a prior discourse/instruction referent. A compatible head
   supplies a pending typed slot. Intervening terms must keep their own pending
   obligations; they cannot be silently removed. If two attachments remain
   possible, retain both. No operation-description-to-proposition coercion is
   silently added to force a match.
5. Negation, temporal comparison and quotation do not change the identity of
   an operation. A mode is not read from l- versus qo-, and duplicate operation
   words are not automatically two performed actions. An existing explicit
   source reference may be retained as an assumption but is not counted as a
   surface derivation. Remembering a norm, resuming a prior prohibition and
   introducing a conditional cause still require an actual construction.

This policy deliberately supplies no nearest-good-clause fallback, implicit
IF/BECAUSE, magic closure at a printed line end, remote fixed position operand,
new food portion, third boil or changed water identity. These are not meanings
proved absent; they are unresolved obligations if the surface rules need them.
