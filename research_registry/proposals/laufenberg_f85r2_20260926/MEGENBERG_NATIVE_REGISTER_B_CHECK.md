# Cgm 38 register-only navigation check (B)

This is a limited visual locator check, not a transcription or content reading. I inspected only the five cached register images specified for this check (`1v`, `2r`, `3r`, `4r`, `4v`) and the KdiH Cgm 38 entry. No body image was opened.

KdiH 22.1.13 identifies Cgm 38 as a 14th-century, Bavarian witness and locates the *Buch der Natur* register at 1ra–5rb, with the main work beginning at 9ra (KdiH, “Inhalt,” lines 77–89: https://kdih.badw.de/datenbank/handschrift/22/1/13). It explicitly calls the register a “Register mit Blattverweis.” The intervening 5va–8v leaves are genealogical notes, so the catalogue’s f.9 start is a real discontinuity after the register, not simply an unlabelled continuation.

On the supplied 1v image I can locate the entry reading approximately “Von dem doner vnd pluzen” near the foot of the left column. Its adjacent red locator is present, but the Roman numeral is too uncertain at this image display to report as an exact number responsibly; I will not promote a guess. This is the key unresolved locator detail.

The sample register pages do show subject entries for birds/worms and plants/stones (including visible “Von dem vogel” and stone/plant headings on 4v). But this bounded set does not establish that their red locators are catalog folio numbers with a uniform +8 relation: KdiH’s distinction between a register locator and the manuscript’s numbered leaves is not itself a numeric alignment proof, and I did not inspect body leaves to match any listed entry to a body folio. Thus I cannot confirm the requested offset from this check. The red annotations are visibly grouped/abbreviated, and several numerals are not unambiguous at the supplied display size.

Result: the catalogue independently confirms a register on 1ra–5rb and a main-text start at 9ra; the exact red Donner/Blitz reference and the claimed offset of eight remain unverified here. Root’s separately registered body-leaf observation can resolve the foliation question if it reaches a directly legible comparison; this note makes no claim about chapter text or manuscript genealogy.
