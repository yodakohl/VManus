## Direct observations

Four draped, bearded male busts occupy the quadrants of a red cross inside two outer circular boundaries. Their extended hands meet neighbouring figures at the top, bottom and lateral joins. They do not carry a flower, staff/strand or raised vessel. The centre contains writing, not a solar face. There are no four external plumes in the visible crop.

The central inscription is readable as STIHIA / ID EST TEMPORA / VEL ELEMENTA. Spatially owned labels read:
