Marion Dolan, *Astronomical Knowledge Transmission Through Illustrated Aratea Manuscripts* (2017), printed124, figure3.8, reproduces the complete circular diagram at592×611 embedded pixels. The PDF is publicly hosted by Utrecht University; exact URL, hashes and extraction command are in CAVA_DOLAN_NATIVE_RECEIPT_20261004.json. Its caption identifies an early-eleventh-century Bede manuscript but gives no shelfmark or folio. The source image was viewed first as a rendered book page and then as its directly extracted JPEG, with no enhancement or OCR. This is a diagram crop, not the complete manuscript leaf.

## Direct observations

Four draped, bearded male busts occupy the quadrants of a red cross inside two outer circular boundaries. Their extended hands meet neighbouring figures at the top, bottom and lateral joins. They do not carry a flower, staff/strand or raised vessel. The centre contains writing, not a solar face. There are no four external plumes in the visible crop.

The central inscription is readable as STIHIA / ID EST TEMPORA / VEL ELEMENTA. Spatially owned labels read:

| Quadrant | Season | Element | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upper left | AUTUMNUS, broken across lines | AER | OCCIDENS |
| Upper right | HIEMPS, broken across lines | AQUA | SEPTEMTRION, broken across lines |
| Lower left | AESTAS, broken across lines | IGNIS | MERIDIES |
| Lower right | VER, vertically broken | TERRA | ORIENS |

These readings directly preserve the unusual spring–earth and autumn–air pairing reported by Obrist. Do not replace them with a familiar seasonal scheme. The outer annulus contains extended quality-writing and repeated IUNGITUR. Its complete diplomatic text and each exact quality-connection endpoint have not been independently transcribed here; the earlier published interpretation remains attributed to Obrist.
