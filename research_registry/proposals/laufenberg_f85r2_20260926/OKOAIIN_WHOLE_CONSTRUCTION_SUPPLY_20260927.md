## Recommendation to the coordinator

measure a descriptive overlap but would not choose source/effect, orientation,
preservation, property, naming or reference. The new source/effect trial is
the most explicit finite suggestion here; its source predicate and literal
LIGHT meaning create obligations rather than evidence. Unless an existing
independently owned construction supplies one of those obligations, leave it
unselected instead of inventing complementary translations for the paragraph.
