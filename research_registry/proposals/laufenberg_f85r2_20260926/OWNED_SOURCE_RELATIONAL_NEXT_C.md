## Retained lead argument

The full paragraph103 also includes white/black lead, several origins and
extraction accounts, composition/softness, impurity, alleged softening power,
melting conditions and colors used cosmetically. The selected clauses are
not represented as the entire paragraph:

> And with wiping and cleansing, this uncleanness of lead may be taken away for a time, but never for always; a man may wipe off the uncleanness but alway it is lead although it seemeth silver. But strange qualities have mastery therein and beguile men, and make them err therein.

The temporary effect belongs to removal of uncleanness. Persistent identity
belongs to lead. Silver belongs to its seeming classification. Error belongs
to people deceived by qualities. Exchanging these owners/scopes changes the
argument; merely listing lead, silver and cleansing cannot preserve it.
The wording permits actual temporary cleaning while blocking permanent
cleaning and blocking an inference from silver-like appearance to being silver.

The passage links cleaning and seeming in one argument, but does not use an
explicit causal marker saying that the operation causes the precise silver
appearance. It does not identify a deliberate fraudster, give a duration or
observe two controlled states. Neither the gold medical ambiguity nor a modern
chemical explanation supplies those missing details.
