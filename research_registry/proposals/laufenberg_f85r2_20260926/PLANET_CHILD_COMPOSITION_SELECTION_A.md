## Source content that can support real reuse

The fixed unit is Md2 271vB5–32, including its seven-planet recap, general
human/planet-child rule, all qualifications and final `etc.`. The preceding
Moon conclusion and Mercury article are not premises that can be borrowed to
supply a convenient additional contrast. The complete source plan's C01–18
remain the coverage inventory.

There are at least two independent dimensions of reusable **content**:

1. **Event reference:** birth and conception concern the same generic human,
   but are different event kinds. Event-qualified attribution must retain its
   event; the final qualification explicitly returns to birth. A shared event
   schema can carry different kinds without pooling them or inventing two
   observed horoscopes. Whether OR is an alternative convention, a disjunctive
   event condition or a distributive assertion requires a fixed scope choice.
2. **Comparison by respect:** greatest power and greatest received properties
   are distinct comparisons. A common comparison operation can preserve their
   different respects and scopes; it cannot silently identify their maxima.
   Donor provenance connects the second comparison to naming: the name source
   must be a contributor to that same human's profile. Naming does not erase
   the other contributors.

The common receipt relation also recurs with two, three and all contributors,
and the same planet qualifies the final birth by day or hour. These are real
source repetitions, not three invented example people or seven individually
named target planets. They provide further ownership obligations, not free
meanings for the apparent letter counts.

The source does not force unequal power/share maxima. A single conventional
head with an explicit scope/order-alignment bridge remains a serious rival.
An ordinal alignment can suffice; lack of a numerical scale does not by itself
refute it. Nor does the source prove that birth and conception actually select
different planets in a named case. A hypothetical writer may express the
conditional distinctions without presenting them as observed data.

Root's suggested **semantic direction**, not assigned by this review, is a
property-respect nominal followed by graded possession and then a leading/
maximum comparison. It is source-plausible only with an important additional
role distinction:

- In the power comparison, the **planet q is the bearer** of power at an event.
- In the received-properties comparison, the **human p is the possessor** of
  properties, while **q is their planetary donor/provenance index**.

Both comparisons ultimately range over planets q. A bare
`HAS(property)(owner,state)` lift followed by a generic maximum does not specify
that change from bearer to donor index. The writer must explicitly retain the
comparison index, holder, event/profile scope and contribution provenance.
A typed comparison frame or a written index/role projection can do this, but
it is an added construction and must have a closed, stated signature. Calling
both nominal roots “properties” supplies neither that frame nor its arguments.
This is the most material concrete condition on the proposed direction.

This source direction need not force event indexing into every prefix. Event
scope can be supplied by an openly written syntax/binder. Root's latest
provisional plan is a typed event variable ranging over birth/conception,
rather than deleting one alternative globally or manufacturing two observed
cases. That is a prospective scope choice to freeze with the source critic's
assessment, not a choice or target production made by this review. The source
plan's A/B analyses are alternatives, not an exhaustive theorem excluding

as IT's alternate S.16 form; `qodaiin` recurs at S.16/W.21. The whole block
must own every application, not just the one clause where a chosen function
looks attractive. Reader disagreement is not a supply of independent positive
cases. The split `aiinog`, marked forms, and outside repeated `ar` obligations
remain literal; no all-`ot` deletion, i-run normalization or reader selection

## Bounded conditions for the recommended authoring attempt

Root may select the complete four-block packet,108ZL/107IT/109RF, as one
exploratory synopsis target. Preserve every group and all assigned outside
occurrences; do not force four source themes or figures. The full28-line source
and all C01–18 must be fixed before fitting. Rarity of sole power, generic mixed
receipt, the2/3/all examples, little/much, nature/strength qualification,
adversative naming, final same-planet birth/day-or-hour and `etc.` all remain.
No universal seven-contributor rule, hard2/3/7-only support list, sole-power=
sole-contributor identity, or tie-breaking algorithm is licensed.

A useful budget is **45 minutes for whole authoring including its decision,
self-review and freeze, then20 minutes for independent literal/type/source
review**. No automatic decoder, corpus test or repair chain is warranted.
Within that budget:

1. Freeze the new scope/typing contract and exact family cuts before expanding
   the remaining dictionary. Choose the event policy, C07 strength bearer and
   C12 distribution scope openly. Preserve the rival attachment/OR readings.
2. Require two shared operations, each exercised on at least two distinct
   input values, with typed compositional outputs. The double chain is one
   concrete candidate graph, not a forced choice. Count every new component,
   whole value, synonym, bound/free distinction and residual exception.
3. For the graded-possession/comparison direction, write planet index, holder,
   event/profile and donor provenance. A leading planet must belong to that
   same person's contributor profile before it names that person. Do not
   manufacture observed contrary rankings merely to defeat the aligned-head
   rival.
4. Account for the third qodain/bare-ain obligations and every repeated whole
   before claiming either layer works. Literal alternate mismatches may reject
   the proposed common contract; unknown words remain gaps, not automatic
   contradictions or costless higher-order rescue constructors.
5. Give the whole source argument actual clauses. A table of all85 glosses or
   a generic source AST is insufficient. If one operator, owner/index bridge,
   event return or final qualifier remains unwritten, freeze that exact partial.

Possible outcomes change the research decision. A complete constrained writer
would merit compatibility review only. A fixed local type/owner contradiction
rejects that writer. A complete story with merely free whole outputs fails the
requested compositional improvement. A partial lacking the third application
or profile link remains a documented authoring obstacle, not global UNSAT.
If all written consequences also fit the aligned-head or alternative-event
rival, retain that underdetermination without building a simulator.

This recommendation permits constructive whole-source exploration. It neither
claims that two target operators have been found nor demands preconfirmed
words before attempting it. Confirmed meanings remain zero.
