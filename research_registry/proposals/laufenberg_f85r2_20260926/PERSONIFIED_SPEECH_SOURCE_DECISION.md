## Fixed comparison questions

For each seasonal voice, inventory (1) explicit self-naming or self-identification, (2) first-person/deictic forms, (3) any named or grammatically indicated addressee, (4) descriptive claims about the speaker versus imperatives/advice to a reader, and (5) the order of those functions through the entire poem. Compare the same features with the already fixed physician/regimen unit (171–175), preserving its separate instructional frame. Do not assume four equal stanza lengths, four target seasons, or a fixed mapping to any manuscript feature.

Decision at stake: whether a uniform personified-speaker mechanism is genuinely supported by repeated source constructions across Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter and whether the physician unit repeats or departs from that pattern. A mixed result remains informative; likeness or source precedent alone cannot assign target readings or establish a decipherment.
