## 2. The common construction remains limited to three derived forms

The old law still maps `PrototypeKind` to one comparison-head type. It derives exactly `otar=ot(ar)`, `otaiin=ot(aiin)` and `otchedy=ot(chedy)`. Its argument domain is FIRE/WATER/CRYSTAL; its result never changes to a RespectSet or qualification operator.

Consequently `ot(odar)` is ill-typed: `odar` already denotes a RespectSet. No alternate decomposition through an undeclared `od` or `d` is licensed. `otodaiin` is also not derived by this law. The adjacent ot-initial wholes therefore receive three different roles—comparison head, respect set, and qualification operator—because two of them are newly atomic. The draft explicitly discloses this cost; it does not pretend that a shared prefix already explains them. The local completion adds three independent word entries, one prototype cell, one combinatory schema with three terminal extensions, and two boundary choices. It supplies **no additional productive `ot` case or compression result**.

This is a limitation of the explanatory gain, not an inconsistency in a grammar that explicitly permits whole-word exceptions. A later requirement that every ot-initial form use the same constructor would reject these new atomic entries under that stronger requirement; that requirement is not part of this frozen packet.

## 3. Source qualification: reason for naming versus property negation

Root's source check quotes the eleventh-heaven passage as beginning `sol furin genant sin / Nit dar umb dz er brenne`, followed by light and shining-like-fire wording. The quoted negative clause directly rejects burning **as the reason for the fiery designation**. In strict scope, `not BECAUSE(designated-fiery, burns)` does not by itself entail `not BURNS`.

The whole literary context and the kind of heaven being described may make categorical nonburning a plausible interpretation. This reviewer has not reopened the pixels and does not dispute the reported letters. The point is the logical strength of the supplied translation: categorical `BURNS(H11)=false` needs that contextual interpretation as an explicit additional premise. The quoted clause alone is not an independent proof of the stronger proposition.

This affects both main and designation-only rival because both retain the same new negative qualifier. It does not cause a Voynich contradiction or license editing `otodaiin` after freezing. A reason-negation qualification would be a distinct, separately declared rival with different truth conditions, not an accepted repair. Root has been notified and will retain the distinction separately from the frozen source report and draft.

The positive shining statement has separate support in the reported following lines. The draft's use of a literary FIRE/LIGHT prototype is sensible as a declared comparison premise, not a universal physics claim about every possible fire.

## 4. Main versus designation-only rival

The comparison main entails positive H11 light, conditional on the guessed values and FIRE profile. The designation-only rival entails a designation in the LIGHT respect and shares the same categorical nonburning assertion. It leaves H11 light open. Its countermodel with H11.LIGHT=false correctly demonstrates non-entailment; the alternative model with H11.LIGHT=true shows that the rival is compatible with the source's shining statement.

Accordingly the rival is incomplete **for recovering the positive light assertion from these five groups**, not empirically false. Conversely, the main does not itself assert the designation/naming relation that the source explicitly uses. The report already retains that asymmetry. It would be misleading to call the main a complete recovery of every source proposition while scoring the rival only for missing positive light. Under the source-scope issue above, neither reading's categorical nonburning follows from the quoted negative phrase alone.

This local consequence distinction is real but conditional. It does not select the general `ot` meaning, establish a word, or eliminate a whole-line designation account that writes light elsewhere. A comparison of complete accounts must require the same source obligations and preserve both omissions.
