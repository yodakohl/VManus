# IDEA000570 source-specific contract review B

## Decision

IDEA000570 has a distinct content contract from RAW558, but not a distinct general-purpose set/profile data structure. Its specific addition is a complete short C54 general-complexion introduction that permits 2-, 3-, or 4-member complexion supports for a person while one complexion takes the upper hand. RAW558 models a different primary domain and argument: material elements common to humans, plus treatment-benefit nonuniversality. The predicates `ElementOfPerson` and `HasComplexion` cannot be equated by shared English words such as “mixture,” “nature,” or “profile.” RAW558 already distinguishes membership from predominance at the conceptual level, so IDEA000570 should be framed as a local domain/cardinality consequence and an application of a profile schema, not as the first discovery that a head need not exhaust membership.

The 28 profile/head states are the number of combinations licensed by the declared finite model: choose a 2-, 3-, or 4-member subset of four complexion kinds, then choose one member as head. They are neither 28 observations, 28 people, nor a target count. C54 provides cardinal alternatives, not these particular member combinations. The construction makes those alternatives explicit logical witnesses only.

## What HEAD alone loses

`HEAD(profile)` is insufficient as a representation of the source’s asserted complexion information. Two different supports can share a head: e.g. `{MELANCHOLIC, PHLEGMATIC}` and `{MELANCHOLIC, PHLEGMATIC, SANGUINE}` both project to MELANCHOLIC while differing in count and whether SANGUINE belongs. Conversely, the same two-member support can have different heads. Thus HEAD cannot recover MEMBERS or COUNT, and neither MEMBERS nor COUNT alone recovers HEAD. A whole reading that assigns only the dominant label would omit source content if the written material also encodes the non-dominant memberships or their count.

That consequence is conditional on reading the target construction as a closed support with a shared person/context. An open mention-list may leave unmentioned members undecided; it does not establish absence. The profile’s `support closure` is therefore a material modeling cost, not a harmless restatement. Likewise, unique `d` above every other member is a formalization of “one takes the upper hand”; the source does not give weights or a fully ordered ranking of lesser members.

## Serious compatible rival: all material elements, partial complexion manifestation

A strong rival keeps all four material elements present in a person while allowing only two to four complexions to be manifested, predominate, or be relevant in the descriptive account. This accommodates both RAW558’s all-element material claim and C54’s complexion cardinality if the domains are distinct. It is not defeated by a 2–4 complexion support, by the cardinality arithmetic, or by HEAD/Profile projections. It is defeated only if a future written reading explicitly binds the same counted domain to both claims, or supplies another independent consequence that makes that identification unavoidable. There is no basis here for identifying four figures, four elements, four complexions, or any target terms with one another.

The competing all-four-*complexions*-in-every-person account is a different rival. Under an explicit same-domain and closed-support interpretation, it conflicts with C54’s two/three-member cases. The all-elements rival does not, because “element” and “complexion” name different modeled sorts. A one-exclusive-label account also loses the source’s multiple-member claim if it treats the label as the whole profile; an open list remains compatible but fails to encode the asserted cardinality/closure.

## Relative to RAW558, RAW559 and RAW539

RAW558’s whole source unit is the 1491 printed elemental/complexion argument, with an all-elements/all-humans composition claim and a separate not-universal treatment-benefit claim. Its current review reports no complete semantic account: some pointwise owners and composition bridges remain absent, N.5–6 is weaker than source prose, and the .24 Element-to-Person bridge is unresolved. The RAW570 profile does not fill those gaps, add treatment effects, or inherit its target seed. It can motivate a separate test of whether an explicit profile preserves membership and predominance jointly, but that requires its own assigned written owners and closure.

RAW559’s modal ring concerns state and outcome predicates under fixed whole-form assignments, with one same-regimen/recipient/horizon profile and an ALL-vs-SOME rival. Its reviewed blockers are profile feasibility, target owner, and polarity. A 2–4 complexion set is neither its outcome universe nor evidence for a multi-state medical outcome profile; no modal rule or living-state constraint transfers from either direction.

RAW539’s partition/phase contract concerns a year/month/season partition, coverage and boundaries. It is already a warning that set membership and partition vocabulary have prior proposal stock; set notation alone is not novel. It does not state person-level complexion cardinality or predominant-member projection. IDEA000570 differs in its source predicate and finite cardinality restriction, not by inventing set mathematics. The current 28-way enumeration is not a statistical or target-side distinction from the 12-month/four-season design.

## Smallest adequate next authoring scope and remaining source debt

The smallest useful future test is a complete, prospectively bounded target passage that actually writes a same-profile owner/context, at least two reuses of that profile, both a membership/count consequence and a predominant-member consequence, and explicit support closure. Every group in the chosen complete block(s) must be accounted for, while every occurrence assigned under the chosen lexicon elsewhere in the already-owned 24-locus projection remains an extension obligation. If no single block contains those linked claims and reuses, the smallest adequate unit expands to the complete relevant N/E/S/W passage set; selecting only the attractive repeated word or inferring membership from a figure count would not test HEAD versus PROFILE.

Source-side debt is modest but real: preserve the complete short 34r general introduction rather than just the “upper hand” clause; keep its rubric and paragraph boundaries; retain the working-reading status and common-photograph exposure of the two readers; and do not use the following melancholic article as if the full four-type chapter were completed. The source’s generic claim supplies 2/3/4 possibilities and a predominant complexion, but not the exact member subsets, named owners, temporal persistence, proportions, a complete ranking, or absence of corresponding material elements. The profile therefore requires explicit choices for closed support, unique head, and a common person/context. Source compatibility would not confirm a target meaning.

No selection or target interpretation is made here. This is a bounded contract review based on the supplied proposal/source reports; no new target data, images, or source material were accessed.
