## S1: the smallest usable query and its complete context

Three existing native accounts supply the core rather than one favorable crop:

- [V70 R1](../../../experiments/yolo/sidequest_theory_candidates_v70/V70_R1_IMAGE_FIRST_WORKSHOP_REPORT.md)
  describes the lower paired scene, the arch, a left descending blue channel
  and the right S-shaped extension with a multi-ended blue body.
- [V70 R3](../../../experiments/yolo/sidequest_theory_candidates_v70/V70_R3_TECHNICAL_IMAGE_REPORT.md)
  independently reported the local contacts in that old round, including both
  lower extensions and the absence of demonstrated edges to the small marginal
  stations. Its interpretation as an apparatus is not adopted as a fact here.
- [W52](../translation_programs_20260912/work/W52/REPORT.md) supplies the later
  whole-original account: three upper-left figures in layered shell-like forms,
  two lower figures, the arch, unequal lower terminations, long text at right
  and between drawings, and shorter inscriptions in the lower image field.
  [The September6 orientation](../../../docs/visual_overview/FIGURE_ORIENTATION_2026-09-06.md)
  also records the winding right extension and points near terminations.

For a prospective comparison, retain the following conjunction without changing
it to fit the first promising source:

1. Two depicted human bodies are **within** distinct lower enclosing forms.
2. One broad arched double contour links their enclosing regions.
3. There are separate extensions below both enclosing forms.
4. The extensions differ together: left taper/fringe-like lines or points;
   right winding extension and rounded body with multiple outward ends.
5. The surrounding page contains separate upper/left figure vignettes and a
   substantial right-hand/interstitial text field; it is not documented as one
   completely connected machine.

Blue distribution is a recorded secondary comparison, not a required substance
or flow label. No exact number of terminal rays, exact hand object, repeated
identity of a person, source/sink, return circuit, or truncation is supplied by
these reports. The reported red band on one enclosure is not promoted into the
core, because the other checked descriptions do not independently specify it.
Lack of a documented edge is not a claim that every faint stroke was resolved.

The most economical conventional rival is a paired figurative composition
whose terminal differences distinguish attributes or accommodate the page.
Finding two containers connected by an arch would be insufficient. Even finding
all five conditions would initially support a specific correspondence: deriving
a transmission relationship would require witness context and evidence that
the conjunction is not an ordinary reusable template. Calling the details
unusual does not provide that evidence.

**Smallest possible next step, if root selects it:** one bounded lookup against
an explicitly fixed readable witness/program, comparing the whole conjunction
and its surrounding scene. Stop if the witness supplies only containers, blue,
people, or a connecting arch. A positive search must identify the exact source
entry and show which text belongs to that source image. This still would not
identify which Voynich paragraph is copied, whether the text is a description,
therapy, commentary or mnemonic, or any Voynich word value. No automatic source
roster expansion or target dictionary follows.
