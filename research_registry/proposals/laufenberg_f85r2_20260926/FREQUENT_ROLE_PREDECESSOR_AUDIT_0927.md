## Direct reference and grammar proposals already reviewed

These are existing design decisions, not newly failed manuscript experiments:

* **IDEA000017, definite versus anaphoric use:** the
  [primary review](../../decisions/ig_root_design_scope_review.json) observes
  that definiteness and anaphora coexist. Prior mention can change discourse
  behavior without changing a word's lexical function. Owner history must not
  come from GDT573's output or other inherited determiner labels.
* **IDEA000022, required local antecedent:** the
  [primary review](../../decisions/hj_root_design_scope.json) rejects the
  implication “anaphor therefore overt antecedent in the previous two fields”.
  A visually supplied or longer-range antecedent is possible. A lexical rival
  can have the same antecedent rate. A restricted locally overt model remains
  testable if its candidate inventory, scope, antecedent identity and genuinely
  differing rival consequence are fixed independently.
* **IDEA000032, coordination over a shared-predicate list:** the
  [primary review](../../decisions/jg_root_method_scope_review.json) retains
  the restricted possibility, but a noun can also occur in a list. Selecting
  shared-predicate contexts and then measuring shared predicates is circular.
  Clausal-coordination examples do not refute the narrower list model.
* **IDEA000053, apposition versus separate X-daiin fields:** its reviewed
  requirement is a specified identity/alias consequence that separate entries
  do not also permit, plus complete comparison units beyond the motivating
  f22r lines. IDEA000055 is an accidental duplicate, not another test.
  The fixed decision is in
  [the four-design review](../../decisions/ls_root_four_design_review.json).

The bounded searches did not establish an exhaustive history of every
determiner, resumptive or delimiter model. An empty or low-ranked search would
not establish absence. In particular the route screen also points to GDT802's
retired daiin-neighbor lead; root should retain the existing WORD_EVIDENCE
warning about position and family identity before selecting any neighbor test.
That report was not added as a seventh primary in this bounded audit.

## What the next design may use

The durable positive constraints are a productive written family, real
same-frame variation, repeated X-daiin spans under different whole forms,
transcription-sensitive internal boundaries and nontrivial repeat behavior.
They justify testing how a role composes; they do not determine its name.
One must explain the whole family's behavior without treating all similar
forms as identical lexical items or assuming their differences are numeric.

There is no ready new meaning discriminator in these six predecessors. One
small **conditional structural falsifier** is available for a deliberately
narrow new contract: if exact daiin is asserted to require a nonempty overt
left host within the same independently delimited paragraph, any authenticated
paragraph-initial occurrence falsifies that contract. GDT812 established an
absence only in its stated smaller scope. A 179-selector extension could
therefore be informative *if the same broader census has not already been
done* and paragraph boundaries are available without inventing them. This
would test an obligatory local-host rule, not prove anaphora, a determiner,
intensity or a delimiter. Line-initial counts are not paragraph-initial counts;
successful absence would remain compatible with several rivals. This audit
does not select or authorize that experiment on root's behalf.

Do not spend a new experiment rediscovering the known doubles/triples against
a nonrecursive one-host-only model, or computing antecedent-frequency
differences without distinct rival predictions. The retained counterexamples
must first be addressed in the explicit candidate contract. Any corpus-wide
grammar test should report structural compatibility separately from meaning
selection; neither renderer fluency nor more counted contexts closes that gap.
