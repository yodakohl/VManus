## Why no additional card was registered

For the first argument, an added card could easily invent a clean chain such as one giver granting one particular art to one particular practitioner, whose use then causes one specific protection event. The owned report supports the broad mediation account, but does not establish all those token identities or event references. The opening speaker's art and the concluding plural arts cannot simply be declared the same individual object. Direct protection and protection through arts likewise cannot be silently made one event with two interchangeable descriptions. A generic source/means/recipient diagram would restate known content without supplying that missing written dependency.

For the second argument, a cause list and a statement of combined effects do not establish that every listed cause is necessary, that each is sufficient, that they are exclusive alternatives, or that removing one prevents every harm. No AND/OR gate, priority ranking or inverse diagnosis was supplied. Turning the passage into such a rule would add the consequence being sought rather than recover it from the permitted evidence.

Unconfirmed meanings are permitted in exploration, and this decision does not demand previously confirmed target words before a RAW can exist. The narrower problem is that these proposed additions would add generic causal machinery or unestablished reference identity without a distinct source-bound contrast. The source relations remain useful context for a future complete reading; they are retained here rather than multiplied into candidate cards.
