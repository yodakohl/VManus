## Is the shared-carrier construction licensed?

At the level the card carefully qualifies, yes. The source explicitly says that “one and the same spirit is named by divers names,” then associates natural, vital, and animal names with its working in liver, heart, and head. The preceding narrative gives an ordered account of generation/purification, movement toward the heart, further purification, passage into brain cavities, further refinement/change, and subsequent brain/spinal destinations. Thus a connected account preserving one spirit-substance under function/site-specific names is source-licensed. The source does not say the substance is unchanged: it is repeatedly purified, made subtle, and changed into animal spirit.

This license is not for one countable, indivisible parcel continuously occupying every named site. The account has branches, parts spread to limbs, part retained in brain cavities, and later onward motion. “One and the same spirit” supports continuity of the named substance/account, not numerical identity of each distributed portion. “Processed carrier” is a useful analytical role name, not source terminology and not a modern physical conservation model.

The principal pronoun chain mostly continues the generic masculine singular “spirit”: the same spirit turns heartward; the same spirit passes into the brain and changes; “he” goes to the middle cavity, then memory bearing likeness-prints, and later passes from hindmost brain through spinal marrow to moving sinews. The account does not establish a single parcel traversing all branches. Nor does it precisely say that every print originates in one uniquely identified cavity; it says the likeness-prints are made in the other cavities and borne to memory. The whole id00090 scope includes both the memory deposition and later spinal/motion route, so memory cannot be selected as a terminal sink while the onward duty is dropped.

The closing Austin-attributed limitation is unusually explicit: the spirit is not the rational soul, but its “car” and proper instrument; through it the soul is joined to the body, and without its service no act is perfectly exercised in the body. This licenses an instrument/user distinction and a qualified dependency. It does not license identifying the spirit with the soul, making spirit sufficient for cognition, or asserting that the soul cannot exist without it. The subsequent impairment account remains attributed historical doctrine and should not be converted into modern diagnosis or fact.

## Minimum content consequence beyond labels

A later whole account would need more than three site labels or three unrelated agents. The smallest source-connected distinction is: one spirit is processed and named according to its liver/heart/head work; that same source-described carrier has distributed and retained portions along branched paths; likeness-prints are carried to and deposited in memory while the carrier continues to moving sinews; and the carrier serves, but is not identical to, the rational soul whose bodily exercise depends on that service. These are linked relation consequences, not a four-part synonym list. An account that assigns three names without a common referent loses the explicit identity; one that equates carrier with prints or soul loses distinct source roles; one that ends all motion at memory omits the continuing spinal route.

These constraints make the proposed content potentially more than arbitrary labels. They do not certify a new primitive or research novelty: the card itself records related priors for site/stage, instrument, conduit, carrier/cargo, and processed material. The defensible new content is this particular conjunction of same-spirit naming, represented likenesses, further motion, and soul/instrument qualification within one complete paragraph. Every organ, branch, faculty, attributed claim, and closing impairment consequence remains mandatory; none may be cut for a more convenient carrier story.
