## Complete primary unit and attributed argument

[Corpus Thomisticum, Aquinas, *De mixtione elementorum*](https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/opx.html), complete item [69885], is an electronic transcription of the 1976 Leonine text. The whole unit, including both rejected positions, was read; no manuscript image or regional witness was inspected. Its argument is medieval natural philosophy, not a medical recipe.

Aquinas first considers unchanged elemental substantial forms surviving mixture. He rejects that account: distinct actual bodies would yield juxtaposition or perceptual mixture, not his true mixture. He then rejects attenuated substantial forms, arguing against an intermediate substance/accident and degrees of substantial form. Qualities can instead vary and form an intermediate quality according to mixture proportions. That quality disposes the compound's substantial form; the elements' powers persist virtually, without their forms remaining actual. The conclusion is neither unchanged ingredients nor total loss of their powers. The opening literal-retention thesis must not be cited as his conclusion. No mass equation, persistent particle identity, extraction procedure, quantitative mixture law, or clinical outcome follows from this unit.

## Reusable positive distinction and serious alternatives

For future authorship, keep three assertions separate rather than allowing one “conserved” predicate to imply all three:

1. **Token persistence:** a specified individual before the operation is numerically the same individual afterward, with possibly changed location or distribution.
2. **Kind continuity:** an output or surviving constituent has a specified kind, without establishing which earlier individual it is.
3. **Power inheritance:** a specified output retains a causally relevant power associated with an input, without establishing unchanged particles or actual ingredient forms.

These are analyst distinctions, not newly assigned Voynich meanings. In a prospective complete reading, each assertion needs its own written subject, relation, operation/context and relevant temporal scope. Two repeated forms denoting the same kind would still not establish token identity; a surviving effect would not establish unchanged material parts. A process word alone cannot discharge those obligations.

The serious rivals are (A) literal redistribution of persisting individuals, (B) production of a different kind with inherited powers, and (C) a mixed account retaining some constituents while generating another product. The old RAW565 scope already permits C. An observation that merely reports a resulting quality is compatible with more than one account. A genuinely discriminating future consequence would have to demand an unchanged identified constituent, a changed kind on its lineage, or an explicit denial of actual constituent survival—not just repeat a quality name or a transformation verb. Those are prospective obligations, not consequences already observed in this manuscript.

No new quantified law, number scale, compulsory single product, universal recipient invariance, modern material ontology, or recoverability guarantee has been introduced. Existing proposals remain unchanged. No current evidence warrants merging the legal, Platonic, Galenic and Thomistic source contracts into a single historical model.
