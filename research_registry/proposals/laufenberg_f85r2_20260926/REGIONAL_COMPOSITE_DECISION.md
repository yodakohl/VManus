## What counts as a joint program

Record these separately: annual/life phases; medical actors/attributes;
cosmic/calendar circle or ring; bands/clouds/winds; and written ownership.

- J1: same surface or explicitly connected single diagram/scene.
- J2: adjacent surfaces forming a catalogue- or text-attested sequence.
- J3: only coexistence within the codex, not a composite scene.
- U: not established by the inspected evidence; not a manuscript absence claim.

Do not combine an isolated doctor with a circle on another leaf and call it
J1. No object uniquely identifies a season, age, physician, saint or planet.
Temporal sequence, verbal analogy and spatial arrangement are distinct.
Describe complete programs, including inconvenient figures and unmatched
parts, rather than selecting four congenial motifs.
