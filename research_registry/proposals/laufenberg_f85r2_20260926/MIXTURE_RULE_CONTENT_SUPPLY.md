### What a proposed uniform law would have to assume

Three different objects must be kept separate: an ordinal degree label; a cardinal contribution of a quality; and a physical ingredient amount. The chapter's qualitative measure is not automatically any one of these. Ingredients of the same stated degree are not thereby proven interchangeable in every preparation, recipient or application context. No actual patient outcome is specified by the balance calculation.

For a **conditional analyst model only**, suppose all listed physical weight units are commensurate, contributions add linearly, and opposite qualities at the same degree have equal nonzero strength per unit. Write that common strength as `q_n > 0`. Further suppose the stated added quantities in the three rows used below are total additions, with no unmentioned medium, loss or conversion.

Then the IV/II and IV/I rows require `q4 = 2q2` and `q4 = 4q1`, so `q2 = 2q1`. The II/I row instead requires `q2 = 3q1`. These conditional requirements force `q1 = 0`, contrary to the proposed positive scale. This is a small manual compatibility argument, **not a test of Voynich or a refutation of the historical text under every interpretation**. The IV/III row and the potentially ambiguous III/II row are unnecessary to it and remain unchanged.

In particular, replacing1¾ with4/3 or replacing the final3 with2 would be a repair, not source reading. A translation/copying issue, an asymmetric scale, differing measure conventions, or context-dependent calibration remains possible. This task does not choose among them. The literal chapter and editor's qualifications are retained.

### Serious rival and why another card is not earned

A pair-calibrated balancing account can associate a distinct quality contribution or conversion with each kind/degree/measure context. It need not obey the symmetric `q_n` model above. This is a serious reading because the chapter itself separates measure from weight. But without the missing calibration relation, giving every pair a fresh coefficient can fit the listed outputs freely; it supplies no shared predictive operation. A nonlinear universal rule is also not furnished merely by changing the word “average” to “ratio.”

A degree-ranking account would preserve hotter/colder order but would not on its own calculate how much of another ingredient to add. Conversely, an additive constituent ledger needs a historically justified mapping from ranked degree to cardinal contribution and an explicit unit/denominator. Both are substantive meaning obligations. The selected chapter offers cases and qualifications, but this bounded reading has not recovered a complete shared mapping.

Positive reusable constraint: any whole account of this chapter must separately own the material amounts, qualitative measures, intended balance and attributed numerical checks. It must keep the preliminary reference, all seven cases, the named examples and the closing analogy. Equilibrium of primary qualities must not silently become clinical efficacy or universal cancellation of every other medicinal action. Nothing here licenses a guessed numerical value from a figure count.
