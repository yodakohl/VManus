# Karlsruhe88v: reason-negation is not automatically event-negation

2026-09-26. This is a substantive qualification of the earlier source reports,
including COSMIC_CONTINUATION_NATIVE.md and RESEMBLANCE_SOURCE_CHECK.md. Their
bytes are retained because frozen560 cites them. The active reading must carry
this qualification. It was raised by the independent local-draft reviewer in
RESEMBLANCE_FIRE_REVIEW_B.md; root agrees after reconsidering the already-read
sentence. No further image or reserve was opened for this linguistic check.

The visible sequence is approximately:

> Der xi himel ob in / sol furin genant sin /
> Nit dar umb dz er brenne / [Sin?] liechte hie bekenne

The following couplet explicitly speaks of shining and compares it with fire.
The secure distinction is therefore **the fiery name is not explained by
burning; its light/shining supplies the stated comparison**. The clause
`Nit dar umb dz er brenne` literally negates a reason for a designation. Taken
alone, it does not entail the unconditional proposition `NOT BURNS(H11)`.

| Level | What is supported |
|---|---|
| Written scope | Denial of burning as the reason for the fiery designation. |
| Plausible contextual interpretation | A luminous, nonburning heaven, contrasted with ordinary fire. |
| Stronger formal premise | BURNS(H11)=false in the model; this requires the contextual interpretation, not just the isolated reason clause. |

This is not a discovery that the heaven burns. Neither a positive burning
assertion nor physical heat is supplied by the text check. The correction
concerns the strength and scope of the claimed entailment. No independent
historical doctrine is imported to close it.

For the frozen560 five-group draft, both the comparison and designation-only
rival use `otodaiin` to deny BURNS outright. They consequently share this
stronger interpretive premise. Their formal difference concerning LIGHT is
unchanged, but categorical nonburning cannot serve as an independently fixed
source falsifier without declaring that premise. The draft remains a
conditional local hypothesis; it is neither a source-complete translation nor
an identified negation word. A future reason-negation construction would be a
new declared candidate, not a repair silently substituted into the freeze.

Earlier labels such as “explicit denial of burning” are retained historically
but superseded at the active claim level by **“explicit denial of burning as
the reason for the fiery name; nonburning is a plausible stronger reading.”**
The positive wave/cold/moist/crystal/water comparison is unaffected.
