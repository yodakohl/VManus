## Additional repeated-LIKE direction discrepancy

G23 explicitly defines `Compare(exemplar,described)=Like(described,exemplar)` and says symmetry is not assumed. C10's formula accordingly puts autumn/spring in the described position and winter in the exemplar position:

`Like(Pattern(r0,AUTUMN),Pattern(r0,WINTER))` and the corresponding SPRING clause.

G24, however, says the same finite repeated-comparison syntax “predicates Like(P0,PA) and Like(P0,PB)”. Here the written ACCOUNT_PATTERN supplies the current winter account P0 and chdy/sary supply the autumn/spring accounts. Under the explicit G23 convention, those arguments are reversed relative to the C10 formula. G24 might have intended the surface notation `Compare(P0,PA)`, but that is not its frozen text. The two orientations cannot silently be equated when symmetry is expressly withheld.

Disposition: **unresolved rule/formula direction discrepancy; no repair**.
