# Regional programs: root check and consequence

Root read the frozen eleven-object report and viewed its single complete
Cod.Sang.760p47 image after the source author. The2200px institutional rendition
shows the open written book, growing flower, two suspended objects and two
small medallions together. The flower is not held; the suspended objects are
not a flask raised to the eyes. No further picture or Voynich data was opened.
This informed reinspection is not an independent source witness.

Root also read the three exact KdiH records for Cgm28, Munich578 and C54.
They support the catalogued displacement/cross-reference distinctions:

- Cgm28 has a misplaced temperament picture and an explicit red reference
  redirecting the reader to its text elsewhere.
- Munich578 has physically pasted planetary pictures in spaces associated
  with bathing and bloodletting. Text loss and displaced gatherings limit
  reconstruction; present proximity is not their original semantic relation.
- C54 explicitly introduces a body figure and later refers to numbers which
  were not added to its painted linking lines. A written link and an incomplete
  visual addressing system can coexist.

These three cases are not independent random controls and do not establish
any comparable displacement or unfinished numbering in Voynich. In particular,
they cannot excuse a failed target mapping. They show why the evidence needed
for a correspondence is the actual written/physical relation, not proximity.
The frozen report keeps the different source families, regional uncertainties
and manuscript losses visible. Edinburgh121v remains an exact catalogue lead;
the present packet has no verified photograph or native endpoint inventory.

Decision: **REGIONAL_PROGRAMS_WITH_EXPLICIT_OWNERSHIP_COUNTERCASES**. The
joint medical/celestial tradition remains a reasonable working context.
No new source in this bounded roster was established to contain the whole
requested conjunction on one surface. That is not an exhaustive absence claim,
a probability estimate, proof of origin, or a negative Voynich translation.
No source feature changes a target word value or admits another target leaf.
