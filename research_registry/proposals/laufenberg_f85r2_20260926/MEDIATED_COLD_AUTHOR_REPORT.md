## What was actually assigned

`aiin` is tentatively a dissipative-loss process constructor, taking a named
ResourceAspect. `ain` is an escape process constructor, taking an explicit
Location and Carrier, in that order. The proposed prefix preserves those
arguments and adds the inhibiting Condition:

```text
qod(P)(args, condition; case) = INHIBITS(condition, P(args; case), case)

qodaiin(R, c; k)   = INHIBITS(c, DISSIPATIVE_LOSS(R; k), k)
qodain(l, x, c; k) = INHIBITS(c, ESCAPE_FROM(l, x; k), k)
```

Only those two exact decompositions are licensed. `odain` remains an opaque
whole; the letters `ain` inside it do not authorize another decomposition.
Marked or alternate forms are not normalized. `or` is a COLD **kind**, requiring
a written owner/case before it can denote a Condition instance.

Seven additional whole forms receive explicit typed-reference hypotheses:
`odain` and `los` refer to the most recent prior ResourceAspect; `an`, `chedy`
and `ar` to the most recent prior Condition; `chckhy` to a Location; `ykeedy`
to a Carrier. References stay in the same *written* Case and do not search
forward. These are seven free assignments, including **three semantic synonym
costs**. They are not transcription aliases. No previous meaning of these
forms is inherited.

Three actual contiguous spans then have matching lexical argument types:

| Full span | Hypothetical reading | Still missing |
|---|---|---|
| S.16 G002–G004 `qodaiin odain an` | That condition inhibits loss of that usable aspect. | Actual aspect, bearer, condition and Case. |
| W.19 G002–G005 `qodain chckhy ykeedy chedy` | That condition inhibits that carrier's escape from that location. | Actual location, carrier, condition and Case. |
| W.21 G005–W.22 G002 `qodaiin los ar` | That condition inhibits loss of that usable aspect. | A W-owned aspect and its relation to the wind carrier. |

These are **open applications**, not translated propositions. In particular,
the third span keeps precisely the same loss operator as the first. It cannot
be reassigned to expulsion, thickening or a special wind action. Under the
reference rule it also cannot borrow S's nutrient aspect across the paragraph
boundary. If it concerns wind availability, the aspect's wind owner must be
written in W. Availability does not mean strength.

The lexical analysis of LOSS supplies the dispositional relation
`DEPLETES(LOSS(R), R)`; it does not assert that loss occurred. ESCAPE does not
automatically deplete an unspecified property of its carrier. Consequently the
wind case still needs an explicit escape-to-availability connection. This is a
substantive difference between the two intermediate structures, not two names
for the same signed path.

## A concrete alternate-reader incompatibility

Under the unchanged component law, IT's first argument after `qodain` must be
a **Location**. The fixed whole `odain` is a **ResourceAspect reference**.
These types differ; no location/aspect conversion is licensed. Unknown words
elsewhere cannot repair this immediate application. This is a failure of this
exact partial contract under IT, not a rejection of every mediated reading.
The draft gives the two source IDs and a small replayable inference trace.

Condition-reference value, not ZL `or`'s COLD_KIND value. All other unassigned

Even if that frame were supplied, two specific content debts would remain:

1. For the nutrient case, a whole nutrient cannot silently replace its usable

   retention. The source's heat/dissolution explanation, preservation of

2. For wind, the carrier that escapes and the available aspect used in the later

   strength. The separate thickening statement, the strength guard and the
   support of surplus expulsion are not written in this partial. W.19's Condition
   reference also cannot use a later `or` as a forward antecedent.

Therefore **no mediated PATH is derived**. Inhibition alone supplies neither
the supporting operation nor its guard, and certainly not a net benefit or
achieved expulsion. Naming S and W as cases in this report is accounting, not a
written binder or evidence for a shared person, time or physical episode.

## Source review and decision limits

The entire [Albertus chapter](https://reader.lombardpress.org/text/albertusdeanima/admda-l2t2c8)
was read before authoring. It distinguishes appetite, attraction, retention,
digestion and expulsion rather than providing a simple four-part picture key.
Cold's indirect preservative help coexists with its direct impediment to vital
motion. The retained two-case synopsis omits the other aids, instruments,
digestive stages and closing spirit-motion case, as fixed in the decision.
No whole-chapter equivalence is claimed.

Root's source clarification is retained: preservation is not the entire spatial
retention function. In the wind passage, the preferred reading attaches the
strength condition to wind, but attachment to cold is grammatically possible.
The target cannot settle that source antecedent by convenience.

The complete RAW532, RAW343 source account/correction, RAW030/root correction,
GDT344 and GDT816 primaries were reviewed. Their limits remain: different owners
and frames matter; conditional source cases are not automatically executed
trajectories; dependent outcomes do not identify a causal connective; and
matching whole values across contexts does not supply a coherent joint frame.
No old glosses or failed experimental claims were imported.

A temporal or phased account can retain the same dependencies and outcomes.
A protective-goal/topic operator can also relate these process nouns without
encoding the local causal assertion proposed for `qod`. There is no independently
bound target discourse relation here to choose among them. The typed argument
restriction is real **within the authored candidate**, but does not identify
the historical meaning of the prefix.
