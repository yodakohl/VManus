## Source-content difference and a possible discriminator

The source's honey example describes bitterness arising through boiling. The original N4 places a transition assertion in chol itself; the rival places only the bitter result there while retaining N3's causal sequence, the explicit analogy and E3's boiling relation. The rival is less explicit **locally**, but it does not replace the paragraph's heating account with a timeless chemistry claim or deny change. The frozen source omissions remain unchanged. [Adopted primary unit](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Galen/Natural_Faculties/2%2A.html).

The W5 appraisal is already author-added synopsis material, not a translation of the source's final digression/authority paragraph. “The argument is clear” has no new source-completeness deficit relative to “the argument becomes clear”; both leave that entire source obligation omitted. Neither licenses the inference that the honey product was newly generated.

A genuine discriminating consequence would need a fixed, independently written aspect condition with the **same subject, property and time scope**. For example, if a clause explicitly says that an argument has remained clear throughout the relevant interval and that no clarity transition occurred in it, HAS_PROPERTY is compatible while a BECOME assertion located in that interval conflicts. Conversely, an independently specified transition would support transition content, but it still would not uniquely assign that content to chol if another written causal or temporal expression already supplies it.

The present two spans contain no such negative-transition or onset-specific requirement. No new locus or data acquisition is proposed or performed here. There is therefore a real conditional difference between the two meanings but **no current written discriminator selecting BECOME**. This is narrower than GDT827's multi-gloss transformation/flow substitutions: one operator's aspect changes, and the rest of the authored causal/provenance account stays fixed. It is still semantic underdetermination, not confirmation of the stative alternative.
