## Genuine consequence and strongest rival

Once written arguments are fixed, Q entails pre-existence and preservation of c's kind/owner alongside stirring at t1. A one-state HAS or NOT predicate does not entail those historical/identity relations. Conversely, Q does not entail absence, contrary quality or prescribed correction. This is why the card is more than a renaming of537/554's operator.

The strongest rival is **a new episode of an existing condition**. It keeps the same hidden underlying c but introduces an episode z at t1. The source can permit this; its vernacular does not decide our reified condition-versus-episode ontology. A written subsequent relation that refers to c itself rather than z could distinguish the models, but no such relation is presently read. A newly created condition that was absent before is only an easy negative control, not the serious rival.

Detection is another distinct relation. Recognizing a pre-existing condition need not make it stir, and stirring need not make it visible or known. A detection account needs a written observer/report scope; identifying the observer with the patient is an extra assumption. Detection could accompany stirring, so it is not contradicted by source compatibility alone. It lacks ownership of the stirring claim only when no other written clause supplies it.

For a concrete future comparison, hold p,c,t0,t1 and HEALTH fixed. Main Q asserts that c already exists hidden before the later stirring of c. An episode account can instead put the later event on z while preserving c as a latent disposition. A target-written SAME-CONDITION relation would distinguish the accounts only if it explicitly identifies the later stirring bearer with its earlier antecedent under a fixed condition/episode ontology; an EPISODE-OF relation could instead own the distinction. Neither may be silently invented after seeing which one fits. Main Q does not prohibit an additional episode, and an episode account need not deny that its underlying condition stirs: without that exact written-reference commitment the accounts can coexist. The identical comparison applies to PAIN_ILLNESS using the second base, with no change of Q. These are prospective conditional consequences, not observations supplied by the present target strings.
