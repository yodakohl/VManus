# Bounded pre-1500 source screen: paired luminaries and labelled stars

29 September 2026. Fixed image contract: one astronomical image with two separate, individually labelled Sun/Moon medallions and an open field containing many individually labelled stars. Existing source screens were checked for duplicates before adding a new candidate. No Voynich text was viewed or queried.

The already documented Morgan M.126 f.153v is not a new hit: its institutional image shows SOLIS and LUNA plus five separately named planetary stars in one blue field. It fails the fixed contract because the luminaries do not have separate local inscription rings and the five labels identify planets, not the many individually labelled points in the target description. The previously recorded Ghent, University Library MS 92, ff.91v–92r and Morgan M.721 ff.1v/5v are also duplicate checks, not new hits; the former is one shared zodiac/text circle, while M.721 has a shared firmament disk or separate diagrams without individually named star points.

One additional Morgan primary was checked: **MS M.524 f.5r**, catalogued to London, 1255–1260. The actual [folio image](https://ica.themorgan.org/icaimages/5/m524.005r.jpg) depicts a cloud-edged arc enclosing solar and lunar images and stars; the Morgan [folio record](https://ica.themorgan.org/manuscript/page/9/110814) describes both luminaries as inscribed SOL ET LUNA. This is a shared arc in an Apocalypse scene, not two distinct medallions; its stars have no individual labels. It fails the fixed contract.

**Result:** no new full hit in this bounded screen. The primary images checked provide partial iconographic parallels only. No claim about source, meaning, or textual ownership follows.
