# Decision: verify Symeon Seth honey-entry provenance

## Unknown

The owned Noble PDF presents a full English translation of entry 86 from Symeon Seth’s *Syntagma de alimentorum facultatibus*, but does not identify a Greek/Latin edition or witness. Can one published edition be identified from reliable metadata and its complete honey entry be inspected, to verify the recipient/season/preparation clauses without harmonizing them to the translation?

## Decision effect

A verified, accessible edition whose full entry materially supports the translation would let that entry carry a more secure historical source claim, while retaining any textual variants. Failure to identify or access such an edition leaves the Noble PDF useful as a translated comparator with unresolved provenance; it does not justify an access-repair chain or broader honey search.

## Limit

At most three targeted discovery queries and one complete edition entry. No broader honey research, Voynich access, target pixels/text, or HONEY_WHOLE draft/results. Stop if a primary edition cannot be verified and inspected within this bound. Preserve differences and uncertain readings rather than silently harmonizing.
