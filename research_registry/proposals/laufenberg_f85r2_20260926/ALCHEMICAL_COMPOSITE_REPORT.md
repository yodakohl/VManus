# Aurora comparison: a composite personification, no readable f85 binding

The selected opening picture in Zürich Ms.Rh.172 combines a human-shaped
composite, bird-like forms, a fish-shaped attribute, a blue winding band, fire-like
strokes and face-like forms. It is a concrete multi-attribute comparator
for technical personification. **It does not supply a source-specific reading
of the f85 diagram or its text.** Its surrounding inscriptions remain
incompletely read, and the adjacent verso notes are not established as its
explanation. No additional illustration was substituted after this result.

## Source and fixed scope

The [institutional catalogue](https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/de/description/zbz/Ms-Rh-0172/)
identifies a fifteenth-century alchemical miscellany, an incomplete Aurora
text and surviving numbered miniatures. Its description distinguishes the
opening picture pages from later additions on an originally blank1v. The
precise1420/30 dating sometimes attached to this work is not independently
established by this check; the catalogue's broader dating is retained.

Before pixels, the [decision](ALCHEMICAL_COMPOSITE_DECISION.md) selected exactly
`1r-3` and contiguous `1v-4` from the official manifest. An external discovery
description nominated the recto as a composite element harmony. That label
was not promoted to a verified inscription. All3 discovery queries were
used. The same public catalogue was retrieved directly after browser
timeouts; there was no access-repair chain or additional source unit.

Both full downloaded images were natively inspected. The manifest lists
2656×3776; the returned files are2657×3780, as checked by B. The exact file
hashes and institutional image URLs are in the [receipt](ALCHEMICAL_COMPOSITE_RECEIPTS.json).
No OCR, transformed image, AI reconstruction or extra historical page was used.

## Decision and retained alternatives

Keep this image as one documented composite-personification example. It
does not improve the current target-word binding over the broad alchemical
ecology already retained in GDT195/208. The actual grouping also differs from
f85's separately placed figures. Ordinary allegorical composition remains a
serious account; no operating apparatus or ordered transformation is inferred
merely from joining unlike attributes.

The image is completely inspected, but the historical text unit is **not
completely translated**. A securely read complete marginal argument would be
new input; until then there is no source-derived constructor to apply to the
Voynich groups. Stop this bounded screen without another picture, new RAW,
decoder or f85 word assignment. No exclusion of alchemy generally follows.

GDT195's dependent four-quality/four-output consequence and208's absent exact
topology remain. The older q13 lineage report was unavailable at its indexed
path and after targeted filename lookup; its imported summary was used only
for navigation. No claim that this is the project's first encounter with the
manuscript is made. No Voynich access, reserve, outside contact, significance
