## Movement is stated in the source text

The first observer deliberately left the last word of the 88r heading
uncertain. Root reads it as `Mobile`, but the black text is the stronger
basis: it explicitly calls the ninth heaven `primo mobile`. The following
short verses call it the first movement and say it moves the eighth heaven.
The eight physical baselines immediately right of the diagram are represented
below; this is a working visual transcription, not a critical edition.

1. `Wie [or: dis?] 9 himel dar obe`
2. `Jn gottes ere vñ lobe`
3. `Jst primo mobile gena[n]t`
4. `Jn guot tü[t]sche also bekant`
5. `Die erste bewegung heiss[t?] er`
6. `Also sprechent die weis[e?]`
7. `Wan er beweget mit sinem gang`
8. `Den achte[n?] himel vmb lang`

Uncertain inflections do not affect the clear `primo mobile`, `erste
bewegung`, `beweget`, and `achte ... himel` content. Thus movement is supported
by adjacent readable words, while the curved drawing alone would not prove it.
No claim is made that the curved strokes encode a particular rotation speed,
direction, or astronomical number. The separate eighth-heaven heading reads
approximately `Der achte himel. dz firmament`; the first report's expanded
article is not a diplomatic reading.
