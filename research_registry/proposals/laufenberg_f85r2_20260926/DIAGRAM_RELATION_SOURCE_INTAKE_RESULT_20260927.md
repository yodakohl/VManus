## Source identity and interpretive limits

The Morgan's institutional catalogue identifies Philosophy nourishing the arts
in a celestial enclosure; several recipient identities are tentative. This is
an iconographic interpretation, not a recovered Latin sentence on the leaf.
See the [catalogue](https://ica.themorgan.org/manuscript/145359),
[exhibition](https://www.themorgan.org/exhibitions/online/imperial-splendor/leaf-schoolbook)
and [complete observation/receipt](MORGAN_M982_RELATION_INTAKE_20260927.md).

For Marc.gr.516 the source is Cantarella's 2019 Harvard dissertation,
Fig.0.13, printed465/PDF494. Her analysis describes caption/quality reversals
and cautions against uniform season–age–colour correspondences. Those cautions
are retained rather than normalising the diagram. The lower inscription's
proposed seasonal subject is interpretive. [Institutional metadata](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/e4c223cf-662d-4634-b3d7-61f80818bcae).

Root's [first observations](MARC_GR516_ROOT_FIRST_20260927.json) tentatively read
the yellow-panel quality as hot. That first reading remains frozen and is
**not accepted**: the separate observer reads a cold/dry fragment, consistent
with the scholarly transcription. This differs from the neighbouring hot/dry
quality busts. The green panel's complete wording remains image-unverified.
The [independent packet and short report](MARC_GR516_INDEPENDENT_20260927.md)
