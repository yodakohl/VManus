**Decision: no distinctive lower-f68r2 motif match.** The f85r2 rays make a
solar visual interpretation plausible, and the absence of a crescent separates
it from f68r2's upper face. Yet the lower f68r2 face does not share the broad
swirl or curling rays, while all three use generic frontal faces and writing
around a circle. The deliberately restricted two-face comparison therefore
cannot identify f85r2's centre as the same Sun or as `Homo`. The Worcester
diagram's central `Homo` is written text, not a matching face. Historical
microcosm and solar-cosmological interpretations remain live rivals; neither
transfers a name to the target script. The image confirms no word, topic order
or source-copy relation. This is a known-page, unblinded exploratory result,
not an independent image replicate or statistical test.
