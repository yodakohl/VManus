# Source-scope correction after the honey synopsis

27 September 2026. This correction leaves the frozen source reviews, author
packet and all their hashes unchanged. It affects a historical source summary,
not a Voynich word or the frozen Galen-based reading.

The [earlier root Seth review](HONEY_SETH_ROOT_REVIEW.md) described one late
clause as concerning “poorly prepared honey products.” That wording is too
specific. After reading the [preparation review](PREPARATION_CONTENT_SUPPLY.md),
root inspected the entire already-owned printed p69 image again, including the
late clause and apparatus. The image SHA-256 remains
`84142aa08bb1484ea7b2c07e5cfa18c476c1c6864f77603f97978b06d2dd0f55`.
The [1868 edition scan](https://archive.org/download/b23984235/b23984235.pdf#page=83)
is the same source, not an independent witness.

The clause first identifies products prepared with honey and calls them hard
to digest. Its subsequent `μὴ καλῶς πεπτόμενα` can concern their insufficient
digestion, leading to the stated liver/spleen obstruction. It must not be used
as a securely identified failed cooking operation. The earlier explicit
boiling and skimming instructions are different expressions. Preserve this
process-owner ambiguity; withdraw “poorly prepared” as an established
translation of the condition. No modern clinical conclusion follows.

Two further limits in the complete preparation review are retained: the
opening honey description does not explicitly say RAW, and the boiled-alone
branch does not fix the same gastric-action outcome as the diluted,
boiled-and-skimmed branch. Three preparation descriptions therefore do not
provide three known values of one common effect variable. No new preparation
mechanism or duplicate RAW proposal is justified by filling those missing
cells from neighboring clauses.

Root also read the complete284-byte derived karopos entry and the receipts
behind the [uroscopy source note](UROSCOPY_CONTENT_SUPPLY.md). It juxtaposes a
grave prognosis with a possible favourable expulsion explanation, but does
not fix equal patient/consistency conditions across them or specify how to
determine the cause. The note's two-case rival and precise-folio uncertainty
remain. This is a useful additional source example for533, not a newly
executed diagnostic-inference test or independent support for honey565.

No current meaning, rule, transcription or access scope is changed. The two
source notes do not select a next target reading and are not decipherment
progress in their own right.
