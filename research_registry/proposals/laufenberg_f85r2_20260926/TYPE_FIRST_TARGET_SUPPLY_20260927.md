partitive "of it" would change that declared candidate. A content expression,
property or referential term can occupy the inscription without those particular
extra arguments. That is **conditional type pressure**, not proof of a noun or
adjective. Elliptical, existential and metalinguistic uses remain real rivals;
the drawing can contribute context without identifying its exact referent.

whole-paragraph content accounts. Therefore a new generic caption-arity test

**Use now:** when authoring a new complete reading containing this exact form,
state once how its singleton and two-word uses are licensed. Do not choose a

## Why no additional card or generic type classifier

The useful restrictions above are already primary results. Their intersection
can stop a proposed complete reading from silently using incompatible type
assumptions, but this intake adds no independent observation that chooses a
positive type or historical word. A multi-step mechanism still has to specify
its written arguments and context rules; merely calling an opaque form a
"state" or "referent" does not supply those rules.

  the role classifier's23 true positives,42 false positives and60 misses do not
  calibrate semantic priors. Its quality/stage reference labels were themselves
  candidate roles; they are not independent historical meanings.

Other bounded screens were not promoted: the two GDT874 complete multigroup
label/prose matches are reader-specific and lack authenticated ownership;
the Rosettes5×3 geometry is real but its one exposed panel supplied no scored
content typing; f57/f77's known state-transition analogy supplies no new type

the corresponding generic idea space. No new source paragraph dictionary,
