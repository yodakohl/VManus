# Root native observation of the fixed Aurora opening unit

27 September 2026. Written after viewing both exact full images at their
recorded original resolution, before reading any B observation. The source
nomination and general alchemical hypothesis were known. No OCR, enhancement,
crop, Voynich image or additional source page was used.

On `1r-3`, one central, largely human-shaped standing composite dominates a
rectangular red field. Its head has a conspicuous rounded golden surround
with a profile-like face. A large dark bird with raised wing overlaps a red
fish-shaped form held across the upper body. A blue winding band crosses
diagonally through this group; one hand appears to grasp its lower portion.
One leg is human-shaped, while the other includes a bound, straw/bundle-like
yellow structure ending beside red flame-like strokes. At lower left lies a
pale, crescent/profile-like face form. A separate small bird-like creature at
right holds a long horn or tube to its beak and stands on a raised gray form.
Gray/brown shaped ground fills the bottom of the image. Exact species,
materials and allegorical identities are not visually identified.

This is a conjunction of anthropomorphic, animal, winding-band and fiery
attributes within one composite image. It is not four separately bounded
human figures around a central annular diagram. The golden head and pale
lower face invite a celestial reading, but no securely transcribed caption
in this review identifies them as Sun and Moon. Likewise, the winding blue
band does not identify one particular physical substance by colour alone.

All four surrounding inscription zones were inspected: horizontal above,
vertical at left, vertical at right, and horizontal below. The ink is faded
and uneven. I cannot provide a secure complete transcription or a complete
proposition assigning the depicted components their roles. Partial apparent
words do not establish a four-element key. No word from these margins is
used as an independently read attribute name.

On `1v-4`, an upper prose annotation and a separate lower red-initialled
verse/recipe block are visible; the rest of the page is mostly blank. The
lower incipit is compatible with the catalogue's `Ad bonam pastam…` and the
corrected word above its first line is visible. I do not claim a full reading
of either block. The catalogue identifies this verso as later additions to
an originally blank page, so mere physical adjacency cannot make the recipe
the explanation of the recto picture.

Root's comparison is therefore limited: this exact opening image supports
the availability of elaborate composite personification in the broader
alchemical tradition. It supplies neither the f85 topology nor an owned,
fully readable relation that binds its pictured attributes to the Voynich
text. The complete picture was inspected; its inscriptions were not fully
deciphered. Do not describe it as a complete translated historical unit.
