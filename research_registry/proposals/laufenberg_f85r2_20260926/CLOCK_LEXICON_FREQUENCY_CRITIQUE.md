# Clock lexicon: a genre-sensitive prior and its decision limit

2026-09-27. Independent bounded review; source bodies, new target data and
reserves were not read. All numbers below are existing GDT1048 profile cells,
not a new census. No probability, concept count or source annotation was run.

## Decision

A source-defined, within-work distribution can guide research priorities more
usefully than one global WINTER count. It can expose whether a proposed meaning
normally belongs to a narrow kind of discussion or appears in several kinds of
discussion. It still cannot calibrate a universal Voynich frequency or identify
a word. The useful immediate target is the precise scope of **qokal = ZODIAC**,
not a three-way frequency contest between WATER, HOURS and ZODIAC.

Do not start full Megenberg annotation before resolving that scope. Retain
GDT1030 as a local hypothetical clock account. Give its globally portable,
strict display-part interpretation of qokal low priority; it carries a concrete
cross-context burden the source fit has never discharged. Keep a broader
celestial/astrological reading as a separately declared rival, not as a silent
repair or confirmation of the old physical-display denotation. No competing
meaning is identified by this judgement.

The target profile also records strong CurrierB/hand2–3 concentration for qokal

after ignoring writing tradition and position. Existing section codes are
descriptive metadata, not decoded genres. GDT1048's independent verification

the section/Currier/hand fields; retain that provenance limitation.

There is an additional logical distinction: a local referent and a portable
lexical meaning are not the same object. The parent water is the water of that
apparatus, but a global WATER hypothesis need not make all other occurrences
refer to the identical reservoir. Likewise, distinguish the local zodiac display
from the broader possible meaning of its noun. Do not manufacture an absurd
global claim by exporting local object identity to the entire manuscript.
Conversely, do not drop the frozen physical-display type while claiming an
unchanged extension of GDT1030.

## What within-work stratification really improves

Megenberg offers source-defined human, natural/celestial, animal, plant and
mineral discussions within one compiled work. That reduces between-author,
between-language and whole-book topic-mixture differences. It may show, for
example, that a celestial concept has concrete nonastronomical uses rather
than being confined to an astronomical chapter. This is a useful challenge
to an overly narrow prior; no such source finding has been made in this review.

For ordinal comparison the defensible object is a concept's *relative topic
distribution*, not an exact word count or a number of paragraphs. A possible
soft bridge is: the candidate has the same lexical core across the declared
domains, its choice among synonymous written realizations is not itself driven
arbitrarily by domain, and comparable discourse functions tend to preserve
some topic ordering. State these assumptions before looking for agreement.

Constant differences in total vocabulary richness or synonym use might then
cancel in a relative profile. Domain-specific spelling, selection or synonym
use would not cancel. Neither manuscript nor source establishes this bridge by
itself. Source-defined topics cannot simply be equated with Voynich picture
sections, and ancient/medieval astrology may link categories that look separate
to a modern reader. Thus the result may adjust an ordinal working priority;
it is not q, a likelihood, a hard cross-genre exclusion or a classifier.

A one-sided obligation is safer: an obligatory meaning has to be available in
every context in which the form is used. Absence of the form does not imply
absence of the concept, because a word need not express every mention. This
asymmetry is especially important for the singleton WATER assignment.

## Smallest useful candidate question

**Does the proposed portable qokal meaning denote a zodiac display/device part,
or a zodiac-related referent whose non-device uses remain legitimate?**

This is a real distinction, unlike asking whether the word is merely frequent.
The local clock paragraph supplies a device referent, but has not established
the universal lexical boundary. Decide the two contracts before importing a
source prior:

- DISPLAY: every use denotes a physical zodiac representation/device part.
- CELESTIAL/ASTROLOGICAL: a wider zodiac concept can occur in a device display
  but also in other explicitly described relationships. This broader hypothesis
  must pay for its referential transitions and is not unchanged parent1030.

Do not conflate either contract with an unspecified circle, sign, month,
region or influence. A generic rival is not automatically preferable merely
because it can fit more contexts.

The cheapest useful source question, **if separately selected**, is whether
one prospectively fixed nonastronomical Megenberg domain supplies an explicit
zodiac relationship that preserves the DISPLAY sense, or instead illustrates
only the CELESTIAL/ASTROLOGICAL sense. Read complete source passages and their
arguments. A source-word search can locate positive examples for this bounded
witness question; it cannot establish complete semantic absence or provide a
frequency denominator. No universal distribution inference follows.

| Source result | Changed research decision |
|---|---|
| Actual physical-display use in a non-device discussion | Weaken the simple domain objection to DISPLAY and specify the concrete relation that makes the transfer possible. The Voynich mapping still needs evidence. |
| Only celestial/influence examples inspected | Do not use those as support for DISPLAY. If pursued, develop the broader rival under its own definition; source examples alone do not prove no display cases elsewhere. |
| Both senses appear or distinctions remain ambiguous | Source topic breadth does not separate the rival meanings. Preserve ambiguity; no larger source-frequency job follows. |
| No suitable positive example in the bounded search | Record missing witness, not rarity or absence. This outcome cannot eliminate either target reading. |

This witness question is cheaper and more relevant to the narrow qokal type
than a whole-work count, but it is only worth doing if root would actually
choose between developing DISPLAY and a separately specified wider rival.
If both outcomes lead to the same next experiment, do not execute it. A
30-minute inclusive ceiling for decision, source reading, review and report
would be adequate for this witness question, not for complete concept absence.

If the goal is instead an ordinal *frequency* prior, the minimum expands:
all source-defined domains need complete concept annotation, sense separation,
and a prospective topic/realization bridge. That is no longer this cheap
question. Do not purchase that larger work merely to rediscover that the
33 freely fitted values have unfulfilled global obligations.
