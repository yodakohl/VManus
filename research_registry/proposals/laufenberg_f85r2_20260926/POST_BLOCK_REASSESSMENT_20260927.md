# After the ten-hour block: what could select a meaning?

2026-09-27. Read-only scientific reassessment followed by this continuity
decision. No new experiment, target occurrence census, image opening, source
acquisition or reserved confirmation was performed. The earlier frozen
results remain unchanged. A separate bounded critic reviewed the next-source
proposal and relevant predecessors; that is additional reasoning, not meaning
confirmation. No outside person was contacted.

## Current position

We have substantial formal knowledge and many explicitly written candidate
interpretations. We do not have a preferred identified dictionary, recovered
language or independently confirmed word. The last ten-hour block did not
establish that one of its semantic accounts is more likely than its rivals.

GDT608 retains directed reuse of component profiles alongside indispensable
whole-form residuals. This is formal evidence, not identified linguistic
morphemes or a sound alphabet. Its whole-form model beats the simple component
model on all 23 held folios. Neither a default letter-substitution model nor
free whole-word meanings alone account for this combined requirement.

The last ten candidates mainly asked whether a selected historical content
could be represented after authoring words, argument roles, boundaries and
rules on the same exposed f85r2 page. That is useful construction work, but
it is weaker than showing that this page selects the proposed content.
The error would be to count a successful construction as increased historical
probability without a discriminator. More restarts or reviewer agreement do
not supply the missing observation.

Concrete examples:

- IDEA595 has an actual two-input season/profile calculation and a full manual
  ZL content allocation. Only 3 of 23 qo-containing ZL occurrences are derived;
  the whole account uses 113 free whole values and has unresolved formal
  conflicts. Separate SUMMER_HEAT/WINTER_HEAT whole values reproduce the fitted
  calculations. Neither component reuse nor full manual coverage identifies
  SUMMER/WINTER.
- IDEA602's four product quantities remain real hypothetical calculations;
  four independent duration values have the same four-value count. Its whole
  extension also has explicit fixed contradictions. It does not select numbers.
- IDEA605 supplies all six chosen source contents in five local ZL packages,
  using 46 whole values and no internal morphology. Those packages cover
  51 groups, with 26 assigned groups outside and 79 unknown. Early references
  remain unbound; the human clause crosses the S/W paragraph boundary. Its
  paid order/scope assumptions remain conditional, not manuscript evidence.

Failures in IT/RF constrain a fixed literal candidate. They do not by
themselves prove that the manuscript has different meanings: the readings
are alternatives of the same object. Conversely ZL-only compatibility does
not establish that ZL is correct. Existing uncertainties remain unresolved.

The new source comparisons are historical evidence for possible illustrated
knowledge traditions. Cgm38 attests the thunder account; GDT1043 retains the
plant/staff-strand/raised-vessel combination and its alternatives. These are
not word bindings. The original composite Laufenberg hypothesis remains
plausible, with no direct-copy, four-season or urine-word identification.

## Recommended next work and its decision value

The immediate objective should be an externally constrained relation that
couples picture roles, complete written constructions and the known word
structure. A new interesting source alone is insufficient. We have not yet
located a ready observation that distinguishes the latest fitted meanings
without further freely chosen meanings or attachments. Do not claim that such
a decisive test is already available.

One concrete, bounded acquisition opportunity is the closest Karlsruhe
comparison, Cod. K2790. This is a recommendation for changed source input,
not authorization of another freely filled f85 whole reading:

1. Obtain a genuinely more readable original rendition or explicitly separate
   textual witness/edition for the unresolved local verses. The particular
   92r continuation was inspected in a bound 1000-pixel rendition; repeated
   inspection of the same images is not new input. Foliation must remain tied
   to institutional canvases, with the known catalogue discrepancy retained.
2. Prioritize the unresolved **relations** in the physician-to-complexion
   passage: the complete element/body correspondences, their qualifications,
   and repeated expressions. The fixed complete unit is the 12 lower 91v
   lines plus 21 continuation baselines on 92r, ending before Sanguinea.
   Adjacent complete Autumn/Winter poems are a separate future scope, not
   silently added to make this task succeed.
3. Before any fresh Voynich values, state what the resulting account would
   require of text ownership and recurring constructions on f85r2, and what
   a competing account would require differently. Use the already recorded
   target geometry and all affected forms; do not turn four figures into four
   preassigned seasons or an external caption into a target word owner.

An inclusive **60-minute ceiling** is adequate for an initial attempt:
10 minutes for a changed readable input, 30 for the complete bounded source
unit, 10 for the actual competing consequences, 10 for review/publication.
If the first input remains unavailable, stop that acquisition. If readable
new source content still leaves every target assignment free, record source
progress only and do not launch another semantic writer. Neither outcome
refutes the broad Laufenberg/f85 hypothesis.

Success at this stage is a specific newly supported source relation plus a
concrete target consequence that at least one serious fixed rival cannot
absorb without changing its declared mapping. It need not identify a word
with certainty. A future exploratory reading may use unconfirmed meanings;
the source relation must simply constrain their choice rather than being
invented by them. Any scored relation packet still requires the existing
GDT388 gates. No new decoder, reserve access or large control construction is
proposed here.

already inventories all 33 baselines, many with unreadable spans.
