## Decision

**GDT946 did not already try the proposed whole shared-origin reading.** It
did try complete local line hypotheses around a flow/residue passage. At
f80v.36 it compared `olshedy = ein Anteil` with `olshedy = Öl`, and `olal =
der` with `olal = neben`, yielding two whole German line renderings. Its P/M
packages tested immediate typed neighbors at every recorded `olal`, not
whether the same material was written as venous-cavity contents, what organ it
came from, and where that material later went. GDT946 explicitly leaves the
earlier event and material identity unbound at its chosen line.

The new question can therefore be different if it fixes a whole relation with
separate arguments for (1) a vessel/cavity versus its contents, (2) the
contents' anatomical origin, and (3) the destination/path toward the stomach,
while testing whether a single written material is preserved across those
relations. An adjacency of source and destination words, or reuse of a
“portion/material” noun, would not by itself identify one moving witness.
This is a candidate contract description, not a reading or a selection.

## Prior results and limits that remain binding

GDT946's original fixed countercases remain in force for its strict local
packages: P and M agree at only the selected f80v.36 line, but the post-census
known-side audit finds seven role conflicts at three loci—f107r.21,
f108r.43 and f113v.22—across seven reader positions. The outcomes are two
compatible, seven known-side contradictions, two unbound and five not
testable positions per fixed package. Thus all 32 exact P/M packages were
rejected. That rejects those typed immediate-neighbor contracts; it does not
prove every sense of `olal`, every material participant, or every whole
flow/origin account impossible. Keep the original preregistered statuses and
the separately reported necessary-condition audit distinct.

GDT945 is a further fixed constraint if a new proposal reuses its `qoker` or
`qokey` source/goal readings: the full f80v.33 line admitted two distinct
source-vs-genitive renderings, but the fixed immediate-object `qokey`
packages failed 43 reader positions at 16 loci on 12 leaves. It did not
choose SRC over GEN, identify an earlier event or bind its participant to the
later residue. The f80v.32 GDT944 formation/separation alternatives also
remain conditional and underdetermined: their common source/product line
does not prove identity with the f80v.33 flow participant or the f80v.36
residual participant. No new proposal may silently carry forward those
unproved identity links.

Exposure is substantial: GDT946 preregistration says its three motivating
paragraphs were read before registration; GDT945 had already exposed all 21
`olshedy` loci, older project exposure included GDT652, and these are same-
manuscript alternate transcriptions. The GDT946 report records zero
independent meaning tests and zero unexposed confirmation leaves. These
results are consistency/falsifier knowledge, not a holdout or a success prior.
