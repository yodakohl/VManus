# B reconciliation addendum: Cgm 38 fols. 44r–44v

Recorded 2026-09-27 11:04 UTC, after the B report and receipt were frozen. I read `MEGENBERG_NATIVE_BODY_ROOT_FIRST.md` and `MEGENBERG_NATIVE_ROOT_CORRECTIONS.md`, then re-viewed only the existing cached `cgm38_44r_20260927.jpg` and `cgm38_44v_20260927.jpg`. The earlier B report and receipt are unchanged.

The root locator is right, and it resolves my earlier uncertainty about the seasonal unit's exact columns. On fol. 44r, column b (lower part), the question begins “Nu maht dû fragen …”. Its winter account continues onto fol. 44v, column a. That continuation contains the autumn/spring extension and rare exception, the attributed eastern lands report, the eastern summer cloud obstruction, the eastern-winter comparison and thunder, and the sunset-lands comparison. The closing sunset/summer qualification ends the selected seasonal unit in 44va.

The next Pliny three-kinds account begins under a red rubric lower in 44va and continues for about seven lines into 44vb. The following red “Von dem nebel” heading is in 44vb after those lines. The four provided images suffice to locate these successive boundaries; the 45r continuation was not consulted. My earlier wording that placed the question only on 44v and left the Pliny boundary uncertain was over-broad/incomplete and is superseded by this locator addendum. No substantive divergence from K01–K10 or from the prepared source transcript is visible in the seasonal passage.

The root correction note records three errors in its own earlier description of preceding 43r–43v material: the text names the heart, the person turns the face toward the strike, and the washerwomen are seen from hills above streams. These concern material before the bounded seasonal unit and are withdrawn as first-pass misreadings, not manuscript variants. I did not reopen 43v in this reconciliation, so I am reporting the root's correction as a correction record, not adding a fresh observation.
