## 1. IDEA770: relative stellar reach and a solar exception

The [raw proposal](P_RAW_STELLAR_WIND_TYPED_RELATIONS.json), [complete source review](P_SOURCE_REVIEW.md) and [root review](P_ROOT_REVIEW.md) were read, followed by the entire 1,789-word [Visio II §I Latin entry](P_COMPLETE_VISIO_II_1_LATIN.txt). The [electronic primary text](https://www.clerus.org/bibliaclerusonline/it/fnk.htm), columns 754B–D, describes different ray destinations and excludes one principal head from solar contact. The [institutional photograph record](https://mavcor.yale.edu/material-objects/hildegard-bingen-s-liber-divinorum-operum-second-vision) identifies Lucca MS1942 f9r as thirteenth-century. Electronic Latin and the medieval photograph are separate evidence layers, not a diplomatic collation.

Before any target opening, the exact physical subaccount would require this constructor:

1. Keep four principal heads in their stated cyclic adjacency and distinguish their eight subordinate head occurrences from sixteen stellar emitters. Do not substitute sixteen direction names for those stars.
2. In **each** interval between adjacent principal heads, retain four stellar roles in the relative order **near / middle / middle / near**. One stable REACH relation assigns the two middle emitters to thin air and the two near emitters **only** to black fire. Across four intervals this generates eight of each terminal class; it is not sixteen separate fitted properties.
3. Bind one solar emitter and apply one CONTACT relation to the same four principal-head referents. Leopard, lion and wolf are positive recipients; bear is explicitly excluded. Repeated pictures of the Sun do not create three separate Suns.
4. A complete chosen written account must preserve the producer, endpoint and scope identities when the roles recur. Reach, breath, planetary ray, rivulet and measuring filament cannot all be silently collapsed into a generic connector. The inconsistent repeated `cervi` length clause remains unresolved and cannot serve as an extra secure edge.

This is a consequence beyond naming or rotation: after head adjacency and near/middle roles are fixed, exchanging a near and middle star while keeping its terminal clause unchanged violates the account. Likewise, exchanging the bear recipient with a positive recipient without changing the contact/exception scope violates it. A global orientation change need not matter; an arbitrary role permutation does. A pure twelve-wind/sixteen-direction list does not already derive either relation. A rival generic cosmography can reproduce them only by adding the same reach/scope relations, rather than obtaining them from counts.

The consequence is available **on the source**, not yet on f69r. GDT1081's full native report supplies twelve painted spokes, sixteen outer sectors and twenty-two radial items, but no observed corresponding principal heads, near/middle stellar intervals, two ray-terminal media or solar recipient set. GDT1067 grants an ideal overlay and still retains eight paired fits; this new source does not choose one. A C0 author may assume those target roles, but must supply a complete allocation and stable relationship construction; no such allocation is in the card or its linked primaries. Selecting convenient objects to make twenty-two radial owners, or inventing one unrelated whole-word value per sector, does not meet that obligation.

**Exact stop: MISSING_COMPLETE_TARGET_CONSTRUCTION; source alternative retained, no new semantic test.** This is the same material condition in IDEA770's reviewed gate, not a new negative result or another native-owner census. The relative-role constructor above makes the missing obligation concrete, but provides no changed target input. A source-only endpoint collation could improve confidence in the photograph/text relationship; by itself it would not produce a target constructor or change the selection decision. No such collation was started.

## 2. IDEA789: circular quality carry versus conditional generation

The [original raw card](AM_04_wind_rose_route.json) proposes four route legs, three names per leg and quality carried to the next sector. Its source identifier must be qualified: the actual manuscript page is **BnF Latin18499 f26r**; the older filename containing f67v2 refers to a target comparison. The [BnF primary catalogue](https://mandragore.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cgfbt74042j) binds the wind rose, manuscript and folio. The full [AO report](AO_REPORT.md), [root reading](AO_ROOT_READING.md), [peer reading](AO_PEER_READING.md), fetch and closure receipts were inspected, not merely the catalogue roster.

The source is materially richer than a static list. Provisional recovered clauses describe water/reflux quantity, sending toward land, lateral impact at a land-side location, and a named wind arising. The important prospective participant chain is therefore:

**flow/quantity condition → directed sending → impact against land at a specified side → generated wind → naming or quality predication of that wind.**

Fixing a whole case would constrain different roles jointly: the input flow cannot become the output wind merely by reusing one word; the impact location cannot become the sending source; and the name following generation cannot be the prior-flow actor. Alternative branches would have to preserve those types and change only the actual written condition/location/result. This would be a distinct falsifier from wind-name reference matching, compass polarity and W93's symbolic definition equality. A wind travelling onward to another named wind does not derive that generation graph.

But **zero complete conditional cases were certified** in AO. Subject/object assignments and abbreviated directions remain uncertain; the Circius branch retains an apparently inconsistent first direction without correction. The peer's provisional Auster clause describes passage toward “us” across two zones. Its temperature argument is incomplete, and it is not a passage through twelve radial names. Principal quality inscriptions do not automatically predicate the same qualities of all three names in a sector group. Neither the full reading nor the catalogue supplies the proposed circular quality handover.

**Exact stop: INCOMPLETE_SOURCE_RELATION_GRAPH_FOR_THE_PROPOSED_ROUTE.** Do not select IDEA789's four-leg carry account from these inputs. Do not call it universally refuted: unread prose remains. A separately fixed flow-impact-generation account would need an actually recovered complete case and its alternative, rather than a new itinerary supplied by the author. No source words were completed from textbook wind theory here.

The official source service already exposes a 1920×2952 native full image; AO used that full image. Another identical fetch or a label roster is not new evidence. A bounded search for the same work located only a Biblissima catalogue lead for Latin6956/Metz380; direct work/manuscript pages returned an access challenge, and no exact corresponding wind folio, institutional scan or complete case was recovered. This remains **unverified acquisition navigation**, not a new witness receipt, source graph or selection reason. No contacts or access workaround were attempted.
