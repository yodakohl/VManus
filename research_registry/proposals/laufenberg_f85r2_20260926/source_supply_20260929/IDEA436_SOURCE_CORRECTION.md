# IDEA436: the cited Theophilus source does not contain its casting sequence

This is a source correction found during the second bounded raw-supply task,
not a manuscript test or a general rejection of casting interpretations.
The old card and its source packet remain unchanged. No registry review,
ledger or route update was made by this producer; root owns that integration.

[IDEA436's proposal](../../raw_theophilus_form_polarity_survivor_20260920.json)
claims that complete Theophilus I.1–I.9 supports preparation of a negative
form, filling it, destruction of the form and survival of a cast. It cites
[this exact source packet](../../../work_batches/ten_hours_20260915/THEOPHILUS_I1_I9_SOURCE_CONTENT.json).

All nine packet chapters and their complete normalized Latin were inspected.
Their contents are:

| Chapter | Written subject and operations |
| --- | --- |
| I.1 | Prepare flesh-coloured `membrina`; heat, grind and mix pigments; vary complexion. |
| I.2 | Dissolve and strain `prasinus`; use it on a fresh wall. |
| I.3 | Add pigments to `membrina` to make `posc`; delineate listed painted body parts. |
| I.4 | Make `rosa` from simple `membrina`; redden specified painted regions. |
| I.5 | Make `lumina`; apply highlights at specified regions. |
| I.6 | Make `veneda`; fill painted pupils, add white, add a white boundary and wash. |
| I.7 | Retrieve earlier `posc`, add pigments and paint the listed shadow regions. |
| I.8 | Add pigment to `rosa`; paint so that the earlier colour remains visible above and below. |
| I.9 | If the face is dark and one highlight is insufficient, add white and paint fine strokes over the earlier highlight. |

There is no negative mould, separate filling body, destroyed shell or surviving
metal cast in this cited unit. Painting a face or filling a painted pupil is
not the claimed mould-filling operation. In I.8 the earlier colour explicitly
remains visible; I.9 adds strokes over a preceding highlight. These do not
describe destruction of an outer form.

The card's own falsifier says to reject its source account if the complete
owned trace lacks a destruction step. That condition is met for the **cited
I.1–I.9 source binding**. The result is a misattributed source architecture,
not a failed Voynich decoding experiment. It supplies no reason to erase
IDEA390's real colour/reference/layer observations or to rule out a different,
properly sourced casting mechanism.

A future casting proposal would need an actual complete primary passage
owning the distinct mould, fill, destruction and survivor identities, with its
source scope fixed before target fitting. Merely changing the chapter label,
finding the word `form`, or pointing to a generic metalworking chapter would
not repair this source claim.
