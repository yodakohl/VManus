## A consequence that a bare verb lookup cannot cover

The preceding florin account supplies an additional argument-sensitive condition. It permits repeated florin quenching in burning water, with white wine as a substitute, and afterward directs mixing the treated liquid into Quinte Essence. It **forbids direct florin quenching in Quinte Essence**. The same named donor and quench action therefore do not license one undifferentiated beneficial result for every destination. The receiving material and permitted intermediate route matter. Iron must also not touch the intermediate liquid; the donor is described as almost its previous weight after the work.

The prohibition is explicit; the antecedent of “it were lost” remains unresolved. Do not select the coin or Quinte Essence as the uniquely destroyed object. The passage does not prove that no other direct gold procedure can work. These obligations apply to an account selecting this complete recipe, not to every exploratory gold hypothesis. This is a retained source distinction, already documented in E, rather than a newly acquired input.

## Fair shared law and fair rival

A finite reading of this source can give the generic retention claim a shared donor/property/carrier relation, then give the two recipient clauses their independently written effects. Its retention relation should operate on an open material argument rather than expand into a case for every named metal. Actual same-liquid references must carry the earlier carrier forward; actual property-of-Saturn reference must preserve its owner. The general conclusion and Venus extension must remain represented, rather than being auxiliary outputs with no use.

That does **not** establish one uniform recipient update equation. A fair rival retains exactly the same generic liquid-retention capacity and anaphors, but makes softening/hardening conditional on the particular material and operation context. It can attribute the route to retained matter or mixture rather than an abstract transferred virtue. It is not eliminated by near-equal florin weight, which is not exact nontransfer of matter. The rival becomes unfair only if it drops the generic capacity, replaces the earlier liquid with a new unrelated one, loses the property owner, or ignores the forbidden direct route. Two canned endpoint sentences alone would omit those commitments; an argument-sensitive rule/table retaining them is not refuted by this historical text.

The elementary source countercases are consequently precise: split the carrier between the first and second operation; attach first-branch softness to the carrier instead of Mars; remove its Saturn provenance; treat direct florin-in-Quinte-Essence quenching as the instructed successful route; or consume the florin wholly. Each changes stated content. In contrast, putting both histories into one compatible world, or merely reversing their names, does not select a law. No counterfactual claim that an unmentioned metal must become soft/hard follows without fixing the interpretation of the generic extension.

## Medical and wind comparisons constrain overgeneralization

The owned complete Quinte Essence pp2–3 continuation has celestial influence on a preparation and a specified anatomical effect: Gerapigra draws from head/neck/chest, explicitly not the members below; other preparations affect knees/legs/feet under another stellar association. It supports an influence-source/preparation/affected-region relation, not copying the stars' intrinsic qualities into every bodily recipient. Its mineral/celestial Sol distinction is explicitly source-defined; no manuscript name is assigned from it.

The full owned Md2 13v–14r source review fixes a west wind that is cold/moist by nature yet warm locally because it crosses warm land. That source gives causal mediation and retained subject identity, but does not assert intrinsic coldness was erased or a uniform all-property-copy rule. Its north attachment and east diagram/prose discrepancy remain. These comparisons support shared **role separation and qualified attribution**, not one demonstrated universal causal operator across metals, medicine and winds.
