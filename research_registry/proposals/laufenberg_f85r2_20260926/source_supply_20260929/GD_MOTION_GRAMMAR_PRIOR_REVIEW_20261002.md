## Consequence for release

Literal assembly requires QOKEY=qok+ey, QOKEDY=qok+edy, QOKEEDY=qok+e+edy and QOKEEEDY=qok+ee+edy. QOKY=qok+y and QOKYLDDY cannot be absorbed by deleting the terminal E. Whole residuals or new separately charged constructions are permitted; an asserted identical assembly would be a literal error. LK EY forms remain unlicensed here. QOK nominal/finite and LK instrumental arguments must actually be supplied and consumed; no global q-verb or l-instrument claim is inherited.

The pulse/unmarked/continuous map is unconfirmed. In an actual-event frame, pulse and continuous conflict only when the same event is bound over the same full interval. Same paragraph, patient or main event does not bind instrumental streams that way. In capability frames, one patient may be capable of both pulsed and continuous flow; capability alone is not a contradictory actual history. The unmarked member must add no pulse, continuity, LIQUID or completion obligation absent from its frozen parent.

Reject the proposed common extension if its own reduction changes a frozen parent, misassembles a spelling, or produces an unavoidable typed contradiction under its explicitly fixed bindings. Existing structural results otherwise constrain the reach of a completed C0 account. They neither select these meanings nor justify rejecting it merely because a former quality/whole-word account used different unconfirmed meanings. Parent frequencies are descriptive recurrence, not a meaning selector. This review supplies prior constraints only; it does not evaluate native P4 or choose an experimental outcome.
