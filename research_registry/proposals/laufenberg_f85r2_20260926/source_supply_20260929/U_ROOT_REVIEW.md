## What the new sources actually add

producer's interpretation. The four peripheral figures have the expected
ascending, seated ruling, descending and prostrate arrangement. There is a
separately crowned central figure and a real spoked wheel. The left/top labels

the edited18a. This is an informed second look at the same image, not another
witness or independent palaeographic test. The catalogue's whole-manuscript

The repeated regn- material is genuinely useful as a readable example of one
status expressed by future/present/perfect verbs and a distinct BE+WITHOUT+NOUN
construction. It is not four unrelated names. Nor are four identical finite
verb templates present. Perfect tense alone does not assert present absence;
explicit privation does. Replacing those two values by one LOSS label would
discard a real source distinction.

Pliny XIII107–110 was read as a whole herb/root account, with the preceding
tree/homonym boundary and the separate Euphrates report retained. The new

but no written status/phase operator and its arguments have yet been derived
across their proposed whole target units. Assigning arbitrary values to all
remaining f85r2 or f2v words would repeat the already known fitting freedom.
Replaying W96's identity collisions or another guessed cyclic world would not
supply a new manuscript consequence. No new corpus census was run.

The useful retained offers are precise source constructions, not failures of
all temporal or status interpretations. Their RAW status is preserved. A
future selection must first show a concrete shared written operation over
at least two different written arguments, with whole contexts and alternatives
retained. This is an authoring requirement, not a claim that a historical source
must match the manuscript literally or that all morphologies are concatenative.

accounts and their actual repeated constructions. Root will review those
inputs before choosing a new test. It will not inherit quarantined legacy
