## What was obtained

`εν + μ at gap0 → μεν` and `αυος + τ at gap2 → αυτος`. The mu dots are below
ε; the tau dots above ο. The connected αυ interpretation is edition-assisted.
These are source-supported local insertions into retained spelling, not a
complete pixel-to-letter recognizer or a Voynich correspondence.

A third example prevents silently making the rule total: in τοῖς the marks
lie over an οι span, near iota. Choosing the nearest stroke would put τ in
the wrong place. Allen also reports tau dots below iota to avoid iota's own
upper dots; consequently above=tau/below=mu is not an unconditional classifier.
The doubled rounded note is graphically recognizable, but its inflected
lexical expansion is edition-assisted and is not ordinary duplicate deletion.

For the265v sentence, the full observer table preserves all11 positions:

The count is seven ordinary-only positions, one mixed word, the article and

## Exposure, method and decision

All source images and the edition were already acquired and viewed in September.
The new measurement was exact word/carrier alignment, expressly unfinished in
that earlier review. Registration2ad0edc7c preceded the fresh views locally;
the push completed during the first view, not a claimed public-before-view
blindness barrier. Root froze02:42:31, C02:45:58 and B02:48:48UTC. No other
new observer packet was read before each freeze. Root subsequently read the
packets and inspected a pixel-preserving crop of the same native file with
nearest-neighbor display enlargement. Initial packets remain unchanged.

The crop clarified shapes but did not finish the ordinary ligature inventory.
Allen explicitly expands ordinary abbreviations in print. Feeding those
expanded words back as independently observed carrier strings would manufacture
a successful reconstruction. No such transfer or decoder was run. The complete
source image and printed page incidentally show the continuation; it was not
used as a fitted or blind test. No new source fetch, OCR, Voynich admission,
reserve, f84/f84r or f116v access occurred.

The source-local positive is the two explicit insertions and their concrete
limits. It changes the usable description from a free supplement to an
annotated operation requiring a justified carrier span and insertion gap.
It does not establish a total spelling channel, select Greek for Voynich,
resolve f9v or add a translated Voynich word. The original September review
and all decoder failures remain unchanged. A further reconstruction requires
resolved native carrier/ligature evidence; another tau/mu/kai summary is not
an adequate successor. No automatic continuation was selected.
