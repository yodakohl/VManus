## IDEA000431: entry context selects LOCAL versus CARRY

The exact card proposes an ordered payload P(op,args,residual) and a deterministic SCOPE(entry_context,wrapper_state) chosen before reduction. LOCAL consumes current-clause arguments and closes at its boundary; CARRY leaves one typed payload open for a compatible later update. A final incompatible entry or explicit reset closes it. The intended complete-passage contrast is one carried update and no final leakage, versus independent local payloads or unrestricted carry.

This is a state-transition consequence rather than a spelling-to-value function table. For fixed compatible inputs and fixed selector/reset rules, it would compute whether an earlier payload is modified and which argument survives. A single newly selected whole gloss would not be the output.

But the card supplies **no named complete passage, exact forms/segmentations, finite selector truth table, compatibility rule or written reset**. It explicitly says these are dependencies. “Compatible” cannot be determined afterward to preserve the desired result, and clause boundaries cannot be supplied by an English paraphrase. An omitted repeated argument has to be demonstrably an omission of that argument, rather than merely a short group.

Its direct predecessors are [GDT563](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt563_complete_state_microphrase_edition/REPORT.md) and GDT318. GDT563 renders active action/argument memory in a creative working edition; its own claim ceiling distinguishes editorial context from unseen manuscript signs. [GDT1037](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1037_galen_fixed_entry_context_join/REPORT.md) actually rejects the fixed line-start/ASCII-previous-dy join of the original Galen denotations: literal lines still contain four collision cells. That closed model stays closed. A different stateful LOCAL/CARRY constructor could be different, but merely adding “entry context” is insufficient. [GDT1122](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1122_joint_written_participant_entry/REPORT.md) already supplies conditional TOPIC/CURRENT participant consequences and exposes late scope debts; carrying state or joint C0 fitting alone is not novel.

The exposed FT packet also already supports newly authored local and imported-trace accounts. Their different cross-record graphs do not independently identify scope. Reusing that difference as the response would test the author's new policy against another author's policy, not establish a written trigger.

Smallest adequate next test: first nominate one already admitted complete passage containing two compatible payloads and a written third-entry/reset contrast. Fix the finite selector, reduction, compatibility and reset rules, preserving whole residuals and reader alternatives. Compute the update/reference graph without new per-occurrence licenses. A same-selector LOCAL/CARRY conflict, reset crossing or unbound carried argument would reject that fixed constructor. A successful complete replay would retain a C0 scope model, not confirm meanings. The present card has not supplied this input, so no implementation is selected.

## IDEA000109: modifiers on distinct semantic fields commute

The exact card proposes M_a(M_b(x)) = M_b(M_a(x)) when their targets are disjoint, while an outer-scope modifier can change or invalidate the reversed output. This is a real algebraic restriction: after fields and operators are fixed, arbitrary outputs for each observed function-input cell are disallowed. Directed surface composition can coexist with commutative semantic updates; the law does not imply that the written strings must be symmetric.

Its source [GDT575](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt575_repeated_relation_modifier_scope_atlas/REPORT.md) inventories 4,609 slots in a German working edition, including seventeen outer/inner pairs. Its method explicitly takes the GDT574 rendered edition as its only text input. The slot scopes and head attachments are analytical values, not independently typed manuscript semantic operands. [GDT577](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt577_interrupted_modifier_attachment_topology/REPORT.md) preserves 125 interrupted slots, including fifty exploratory attachment candidates and one retained renderer-history conflict. It does not observe the result of a semantic modifier swap. The card's related IDEA000018 remains conditional because inherited renderer roles do not independently establish action endpoints.

The raw card names no actual matched swapped construction and no fixed target fields/operators. Swapping a stored recipe in software and obtaining the result defined by one's own update functions would validate that software, not test the manuscript. Disjointness inferred from the desired commuting result would make the law circular. Conversely, a real surface-order effect in GDT608 alone would not refute semantic commutation.

Smallest adequate next test: nominate two actual admitted construction pairs with their original source IDs, reader bytes and operand correspondence. Assign the target fields and two operator laws before calculating the outputs. C0 fields are allowed; the laws must compute a result beyond per-whole lookup. An independently constrained output or incompatible scope/result relation can then reject the fixed disjoint-field law. A consistent complete construction can retain the C0 constructor even when semantic verification remains absent. Currently neither the matched pair nor a result constraint is supplied. This is missing input/design, not an executed commutation failure or proof that semantic fields cannot exist.
