## Price and limits

The alternative must pay for a new OTCHDY whole meaning and a declared title/metadata production. Under A's convention of one unit per paid lexical/part value and one per production, the simplest **additive** example costs +1 lexical unit and +1 production unit. It can use G1's exact-whole priority rather than reinterpret CHDY or QOKCHDY. This is a proposed accounting example, not a fitted minimum or calibrated likelihood. Removing unused earlier costs or changing the broader composition theory would require a complete separately priced model.

A's finite caption mode G7 already separates specified caption use from prose use, but preserving all other occurrences still has to be declared and checked. If the rival needs an additional Q1-only context restriction, it must price that restriction as well. This local proof does not show that a procedure-title account covers f77, the four f83 captions, P4/P5/Q2 or every composed form. It does not repair, replace or rescue a frozen full hypothesis retrospectively.

A's missing-Component counterfactual is valid for a different intervention: overwrite/remove the ambient Component register with a bare Procedure value, or require the next consumer to take the immediately preceding token's output. Neither intervention follows merely from reading the preceding token as a non-invoking title, and neither occurs in the actual Q1 trace. An invocation Event E0 would arise only if a newly declared heading-invocation rule created it; generic procedure/title status does not require E0. A is correct that a Procedure cannot simply occupy a Component argument, but the title construction never attempts that substitution.

**Decision consequence:** retain the explicit SHEDAL-to-Transit material dependency and persistent C_H/F47 later relations as conditional content. Withdraw the broader inference that those consumers by themselves select OTCHDY's nominal semantic sort. They constrain already provided Component context and the later Fluid input. They do not distinguish a Component reference from a separately paid non-invoking title that leaves that context intact. This finding neither establishes procedure semantics nor selects a historical domain. Confirmed target words remain zero; f84/f84r and reserves remain closed.
