### Smallest actual constructor consequence

If a complete independently catalogued pair later supplies the proposed contrast, use a small **role-substitution constructor**, not a new lookup for each resulting whole. Freeze one full typed source process G, the externally attested changed input role r, and replacement value b. `SubstituteRole(G,r,b)` propagates b only through references to that role's entity; it preserves other inputs, operation order, destinations, conditions and identities. The output is the complete later state/event graph, including every downstream consumer whose material argument should change. A competing content-preserving paraphrase constructor leaves that role and all its downstream consumers invariant.

That supplies a calculable distinction: replacing a material with a different kind changes all dependent material arguments; merely reordering two conjunctively supplied ingredients does not. A lexical table for a whole entry cannot satisfy this constructor test merely by returning a stored graph. The known CoReMA pair demonstrates the latter kind of variation, but is not a new held contrast and has no source-owned target binding. No such constructor was implemented here.

The smallest future source test is two complete entries plus an externally keyed third continuation or entry excluded from constructor definition. Fix the change from source-owned headings/body, compute all propagated consequences, and compare the **whole** held content. Success with actual changed roles makes the constructor eligible for a separate C0 target hypothesis; unchanged content narrows the candidate to a paraphrase/argument-order rule; a missing full entry remains missing data. Failure of the frozen constructor rejects that constructor, not historical recipes or translation in general. A target meaning hypothesis still needs independently stated inputs and actual record capacity; no source comparison alone translates a Voynich group. Planning estimate after those exact sources exist: 60 minutes for full-entry reading and argument collation, 30 for constructor/proof, and 30 for validation/publication. Reassess the design if that estimate proves inadequate; this is not a time-based abort of necessary checks. The present task did not begin that computation because its defining substitution/held source are absent.

## IDEA000121: one witnessed editorial plan, not discretionary montage

The [actual raw proposal](../../idea_pipeline_vernacular_redaction_montage.json) is materially different: one externally collated multi-work redaction determines preserve/omit/split/transpose/append operations, and one global whole-word bijection must cover the entire generated text. It forbids resetting the key between works or rearranging chapters during fitting. It requires complete vernacular text, an externally fixed chapter plan and a source-specific continuation. This is not a permission to optimize source switches until matches appear.

The [Wellcome MS.617 catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/cz4h3uvr) dates the witness to 1451 and documents continuous chapter numbering across medical works, omissions, splits and a fever transposition. It names another Italian excerpt, MS.533, and says the proposed Zuane attribution needs direct comparison with a named Naples witness. The [MS.533 record](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/vxfmfvy3) catalogues an abbreviated Italian text, but supplies incipits/explicit rather than its complete entry text and directs physical access through the conservation studio. No contact was made. Catalogue description is not native manuscript collation.

Targeted exact-shelfmark/institutional searches did not provide the required complete public Naples parallel during this assessment. Noninstitutional teaching-copy excerpts were rejected as primary evidence; they do not license a native ingredient/gloss contrast or prove a global stemma. This is a scoped retrieval result, not proof that no digitization or scholarly collation exists. No uncertain attribution was promoted to an established shared redaction. A separate precise follow-up locator is the pair Girolamini CF1.9 / Biblioteca Nazionale di Napoli VIII.C.94: the [institutional catalogue-author bibliography](https://docenti.unisa.it/005764/ricerca/pubblicazioni?anno=0) lists the latter as Tesoro dei poveri. The native complete-entry pair remains uncollated here; classroom-copy claims about glosses becoming ingredients were not accepted as source facts.

The novelty boundary comes from [GDT887](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt887_tacuinum_joint_entry_reconstruction/REPORT.md), [GDT893](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt893_global_source_montage_word_code/REPORT.md), [GDT894](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt894_forced_source_adjacent_copy/REPORT.md) and [P29](../../translation_programs_20260912/work/P29/REPORT.md). The specified Tacuinum whole-word compiler had no solution; GDT893's permissive source switching gave a conditional local RF fit, whose added literal continuation law failed in both directions in GDT894. P29's complete ink recipe had no fully bound correspondence under its fixed redaction alternatives. IDEA121 does not erase those failures. Only a different *complete source-owned editorial plan and lexical packet* would justify a new fixed model. [GDT892](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt892_joint_abugida_paradigm_reconstruction/REPORT.md) was a missing eligible-control capacity stop, not an unsuccessful fit or a gate that later engineering could silently satisfy. The older medical architecture in GDT211/213/215 supplies neither this plan nor a source key.

The smallest useful computation would serialize the independently collated editorial plan once, without target fitting, then predict an excluded cross-work chapter successor and its complete text. A correct known-label permutation without lexical content changes no meaning decision. A complete distinctive continuation with a fixed lexical key would justify a separately registered source/constructor test; inconsistent order rejects that exact plan; absent full collation remains missing source input. No whole-word solver should be implemented before that source packet exists.
