## The actual candidate direction

I considered a **Latin medical description of contrasting qualities and
applications**, using the already exposed complete f83r P4 native packet. This is a candidate
content register, not a claim that a pictured object is being described or that
Galen/Vitruvius is its source. Medical writing includes descriptions, comparisons
and prescriptions; the claim must live or die on a connected lexical reading.
Latin inflection would supply explicit lexical and grammatical obligations;
it does not supply a manuscript-to-language bridge. Neither word statistics,
EVA nor the learned98 units selects Latin. This candidate is therefore not
chosen, and another language remains possible.

A tempting first hypothesis is CHEEY/CHEDY = calidus/calidum and
SHEEY/SHEDY = frigidus/frigidum. It replaces four material/state values with two
ordinary adjective paradigms. **This is not yet a selected reading.** The -us/-um
opposition is a concrete linguistic hypothesis, but masculine accusative and
neuter nominative/accusative share -um. The surrounding written governor and
subject have to decide which construction is claimed. Merely calling EY
nominative and DY accusative is false precision. Changing hot/cold to other
second-declension adjectives can preserve the table, just as changing the two
material names preserved1137. No fact currently chooses these adjective stems.

The proposal becomes less arbitrary only when one key spells **all** its Latin
words, rather than a table assigning those four wholes. For example, a written
preposition requiring an accusative must itself be spelled by that key; its
scope cannot be supplied as an English annotation. An adjective predicated of
a written noun must agree with that actual noun. A reference must identify the
same written lemma, with the new inflection demanded by its actual governor.
The connective, governor, noun and reference cannot be invented independently
for each position. Repeated words retain one full Latin analysis or a fixed
source-attested ambiguity resolved by ordinary syntax.

This is a **prospective authorship contract**, not the missing completed account.
I cannot supply a finite sign key, all32 Latin words and those governors from the
available evidence. Do not register the four-word table as satisfying IDEA864's
reopening condition or as a result about case.

The material historical bridge is absent: an attested writing law must actually
restrict this candidate's signs/expansions, rather than merely prove that some
medieval authors abbreviated. GDT609 does the latter and explicitly does not
identify its key. The available Galen English translation is not native medieval
Latin spelling evidence. A source convention plus an arbitrary new whole-root
table would preserve the present nonidentifiability. This is sufficient to
decline the attempt now, without commissioning another source-only calibration.

## Smallest adequate attempt, if chosen later

Use only GDT1137's bound97 native groups: IT32 for the candidate reading,
ZL33/RF32 preserved as alternate barriers. This advice opens no target body or
image. Work by hand in one compact table; no fitter, corpus build or extra cache.

Before any completion, freeze a finite sign inventory, its segmentation, a
global output key and one forward writing law. The inventory is a hypothesis
about the existing transcription, not an identified alphabet. Prefer literal
outputs of one letter and explicitly listed abbreviated outputs of at most
three letters. Permit at most three independently attested whole-form formula
abbreviations, zero empty-output signs, and no per-target-word overrides. Every
exception must be fixed before the complete account, including when an
abbreviation is obligatory versus optional. These bounds are an exploratory
economy choice, not a historically proven Voynich capacity profile.

The complete table must give every exact IT group: output spelling, attested
lemma/form, morphology, its written grammatical attachment, and contribution
to the complete connected passage. It must forward-spell back to the original
form under the same law. No homonym, silent word, source-word insertion, changed
segmentation or misspelling waiver may appear only where needed. Punctuation
and any allowed ordinary Latin ellipsis must be declared; ellipsis cannot
supply an absent substantive for each inconvenient adjective. No free English
action attached outside the decoded Latin is allowed.

Use two actual complete constructions in this same paragraph to test one
unequal grammatical consequence: changing the same lemma's case must change
the legality or referent of its written consumer. Freeze the specific cases
and consumers when they exist. This is not permission to invent them through
semantic annotations. If only the four adjective labels can be supplied, stop
the attempt rather than expand the dictionary.

Audit all repeats, including QOKEEDY at .25/.27/.30, QOKEDY before both CHEDY and
SHEDY, SHCKHEDY twice consecutively, and OLDY in both tail constructions. Each
literal reuse must survive under the same word/key. The doubled SHCKHEDY needs
an ordinary linguistic reading, such as an attested repetition construction,

## Rival and outcomes

The strongest current rival is the generic record/type account preserved by
GDT1137: common kind/state constructors plus compatible reference, with freely
renamable labels. The explicit-earlier and latest-compatible selectors remain
equivalent here. A Latin reading must gain a constraint that this generic
account lacks: one lexical key plus ordinary grammatical relations that force
some different words and exclude some apparently available substitutions.

If no complete key-and-grammar account exists under the frozen law, close that
candidate law; do not infer that Latin or all abbreviation is impossible. If a
complete account exists but other keys yield equally grammatical incompatible
meanings, retain the accounts as C0 alternatives and report their freedom.
If one complete account makes a genuinely unequal written consequence that
survives review, retain it as a narrower plausible reading and nominate a
separate already-owned transfer only afterward. None of these outcomes opens
a reserve or establishes a confirmed English lexeme. Existence of grammatical
Latin alone does not select Latin over another language or a generative text.

The next unknown is therefore whether the **same finite spelling law and
ordinary lexical grammar can support the whole paragraph**, with two actual
unequal written consumers. It is not another question about whether an
assigned type object can flow through code. The present advice has not solved
that unknown; it identifies a small meaningful authorship task and declines
to mislabel the four-word suggestion as its solution.
