## The admissible new positive

An adequate native positive has all these separate findings for a pair: distinctive visible structural concordance sufficient to support coidentity as an explicit hypothesis; a securely different anatomical extent, such as a complete plant and a root-only fragment; singular fragment-to-inscription ownership; and the retained neighboring fragments that could otherwise explain the label or shared shape. Generic leaf shape, stylization or mere proximity is not enough. Continuity, cut edge and possible drawing simplification must be reported separately.

Two such fully qualified relations can justify a **bounded exploratory whole/part construction hypothesis** using already established directed composition. They can constrain a candidate to preserve plant-kind identity while distinguishing anatomical extent. This is manuscript-side information, unlike importing an external herbal story and choosing its expected roles. No confirmed plant name or English ROOT anchor is required to formulate that candidate.

However, repeated root-only examples are not independently observed ROOT semantics. A root drawing may still have a plant name, class name, property or preparation label. A depicted organ difference is not an observed temporal transformation: even a cut-looking boundary does not establish which process produced it. The allowable claim is object/organ relation capacity, not the text instruction RootOf(K), an extraction output or a new pharmacological meaning.

Two f102r2 fragment positives share one pharmaceutical drawing context. The initial nine, now twelve named selectors are not twelve independent physical leaves. Root must map foldout canvases and regions to physical keys; shared contexts and historical development exposure must be retained. None supplies fresh held-folio confirmation.

## Exact later consequence that anatomy could make testable

The concrete part candidate is a finite constructor `Part(K,O)` preserving one plant-kind K and changing anatomical argument O. It competes with `DerivedName(K,D)` or an unchanged species/kind name whose drawing happens to show a part. A jointly renamed English world cannot erase the part candidate's requirement that O tracks the observed organ rather than a free name modifier.

The smallest useful textual pressure would therefore be **crossed reuse**: the same proposed operation or organ value must track the same independently annotated organ across different K values, while a different organ of the same K must produce a different argument/consequence. The whole/part owner endpoints must be supplied by actual written construction rules, with residuals and alternate readings retained, not by seven source-index links. An explicit finite candidate can remain C0; this is not a demand for confirmed words before authorship.

The initial five-pair plan and its seven-pair preaccess amendment do not guarantee this crossing, and this critic has not examined bodies to claim it exists. If all qualified positives are roots, they provide a possible root-part comparison but no observed organ-variable contrast. If the text cannot furnish a shared directed construction on the actual paid forms, anatomy alone leaves the part-versus-name/property interpretation unchanged. Do not expand a glossary merely to manufacture the crossing. This is a prospective limit on the next decision, not a new test or decoder specification.

## Outcomes and decision

If the metadata audit finds a reserved or unidentifiable canvas, that pair closes without substitute. If native anatomy finds no clear coidentity/organ contrast or singular ownership, no textual semantic candidate follows. If it finds two fully qualified relations, retain the anatomical findings and permit consideration of a bounded part candidate, while explicitly keeping name/property rivals and the missing written reuse consequence. Neither outcome reopens any prior frozen formal test.

Accordingly there is no unchanged missing-geometry stop that invalidates the new inspection in advance: direct, all-pair anatomical evidence is precisely the proposed changed input. There is a real ceiling on what it can buy. **Inspect to establish relation capacity; do not represent the two-positive threshold as selecting meanings or funding free filler in an almost-full reading.** No additional pair mining, morphology census, relaxed score, image substitution or decoder follows from this audit. Original failures and zero confirmed lexemes remain unchanged.
