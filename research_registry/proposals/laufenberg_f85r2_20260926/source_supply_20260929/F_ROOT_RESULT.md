The sources also prevent three convenient shortcuts. Carduus explicitly
includes a spineless cultivated counterpart: lack of visible prickles alone
cannot disprove that broad class. Dipsacus says **one head per stem**, while
its image has more than one head per plant: a picture count needs its stated
bearer. And its head-associated remedy uses **worms inside the head**, not
the head as the administered material. Atractylis uses *folia, coma et semen*;
equating *coma* with *capitulum* would add an unproved source identity.
IDEA737's two different procedures for the same named head part therefore
remain missing. These are historical source constraints, not Voynich meanings.
