# I — normalized working transcript of the whole Sloane age chapter

**Status: provisional, edition-assisted, not diplomatic.** The four primary page images are authoritative. This working text expands common abbreviations, joins line-end divisions, adds punctuation and regularizes some i/j/u/v forms. Square brackets mark unresolved or supplied readings; drug spellings and the disputed age numeral especially require collation. The 1911 other-manuscript edition was consulted after native whole-page viewing. This is not an independently transcribed Sloane critical edition. No modern drug identities are silently substituted.

The full sense inventory and precise source/edition distinction are in `I_SLOANE_COMPLETE_WORKING_READING.md`.

## f31r, rubric and left-column chapter opening

Li .xx. chap[itres] comment l'on se doit garder es .iiii. eages et tarder de viellece et soi maintenir ione.

Entendu aues comment vous deues faire vo enfant norrir ou premier eage et ou secont. Or vous dirons comment cascuns se doit garder es autres eages. Premierement deues savoir que communaument li fisicien dient que les eages sunt quatre, si com adolescentia, iuventus, senectus, senium.

De le premiere dient qu'ele est caude et

## f31r, right column

moiste, et en cest eage croist li cors et dure iusques a .xxv. ans ou .xxx. Li seconde est caude et sece et demeure li cors en se force et en se vigor et dure iusques a .xl. ans ou .xlv. Li tierce si est froide et sece, que li cors commence a decroistre et a afoiblir, et dure iusques a .lx. ans ou .lxv. Li quarte si est froide et moiste par abondance des froides humors qui abondent par le defaute de la calour naturel, et dure iusques a la mort. Et en cest eage va tos iors li cors a neant.

Ce sunt quatre eage, mais se nous volons parler un poi plus soutilment si poons dire qui en a .vii., si com le premiere quant li enfes est nes et dure iusques a tant que li dent li commencent a venir, et c'est infantia. L'autre, dentium plantatura, et c'est quant li dent sunt venu, et dure iusques a .vii. ans. La tierce pueritia et dure iusques a .xiiii. ans. Et sachies que ces trois se contienent sor le premiere ke dite vous avons desus, ki a a nom adolescentia; et de ces .vii. eages vos avons dit les .ii., si com d'infantia et dentium plantatura.

Or vous dirons qu'il estuet faire es autres.

## Non-silent uncertainties and checks to retain

- The narrow opening beside the initial was subsequently checked directly in the larger column detail. The whole transcript remains edition-assisted and normalized, not diplomatic.
- The larger 31v detail supports `.ii. fois` before wine, correcting the first provisional `.iii.` copy. This is a working two-drinks reading; it is distinct from the earlier clear three-times-daily feeding sequence. The1911 base text has wine without that repeated count.
- The intervention threshold on 32r is **.xxxv. (35)**. The initial provisional `.xlv.` (45) reading is expressly corrected by the original numeral detail; there is no 45/35 manuscript variant established here.
- Several small verb endings and the sexual-activity verb interpretation remain uncertain. The middle-age activity verb is `travellier` (work/exertion), clarified by the 32r left-column detail.
- The passage after daily fasting medication says to drink afterward; the precise missing/implied drink noun at this point should not be supplied as certain wine without checking the image. Wine is explicit in adjacent age clauses and in the other-manuscript edition, but those are different evidence.
- Ingredient conjunctions can matter: the second recipe's last binder is visually `ou en chucre` in this working transcript, not a certified requirement to use both honey and sugar. The complete sense account is corrected accordingly in the associated scope note.
- Final drug-name spellings and whether `lin aloes` is one lign-aloes name or two items remain open. The general closing category of warm electuaries is legible.
