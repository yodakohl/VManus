## Useful multistep distinction, kept separate from target claims

Three distinct relations occur in the complete primary:

1. Sun is the listed ruler of sight (as well as brain, heart, sinews and right-side parts).
2. Specified configurations involving Moon and maleficent bodies are said to
   produce injury to vision; the text distinguishes one-eye and both-eye cases
   through additional configurations. This is not a new assignment of sight's ruler.
3. Under stated beneficent dominance, disease is said to be mitigated;
   Jupiter with Mercury is specifically linked to drugs and good physicians.

The same body-related term can therefore be relevant in a luminary account,
affliction account and treatment account without naming a luminary and without
changing its referent. That is a source-grounded alternative to compulsory
caption naming. It does not show that the target uses this construction, nor
that eye/brain/heart/drug is any particular Voynich form. These historical
astrological claims are not efficacy evidence. The complete chapter retains
its configurations, temporal conditions and cure qualifications.
