# AA / IDEA000782: physical line ends are not paragraph ends

Append-only author correction, 2026-09-29, received from root immediately after the single IDEA000782 add had completed. The registered card and `AA_CONSTRUCTION.md` remain byte-preserved; read this note with them.

The references to GDT759's **34 line-final s occurrences** need a narrower interpretation. That count concerns **physical line ends**, not verified ends of complete source paragraphs. AA's declared free-s rule permits the immediately following group across a physical line break when it is still in the same complete paragraph. Therefore those 34 positions are **known boundary cases to inspect before any global extension**, not established missing-right-argument cases and not a demonstrated contradiction of that rule.

No complete boundaries for those 34 cases were inspected in AA, and no new query is made to do so. A literal s at a verified paragraph end would conflict with a mandatory same-paragraph right argument; whether any of the 34 meets that condition remains unassessed here. A local derivation does not establish global viability either.
