A possible future decision gate is whether independently measured physical gaps actually license joining particular uncertain boundaries under a source-fixed estimator, rather than merely reproduce transcription labels. Positive evidence could refine the structural boundary representation; failure could retain the present provisional boundary treatment. It would not by itself choose language, historical subject, a semantic gloss or a cipher key. Before any content intake, the exact folio coverage and allowed selectors, estimator validation, dependence on the same image/transcription inputs, available boundary identifiers and the already-tested consequence must be established. This is a prospective review gate, not a proposed rerun or implementation. No whole repository or ancillary bundle was downloaded.

The retained local baseline matters. GDT601 rejects the literal published Naibbe key on the tested target. GDT602's strong recovery uses oracle U/P/S segmentation; its unconstrained recovery fails to recover the control plaintext. GDT603 demonstrates end-to-end recovery of a known-capacity generated control. GDT604 then closes the exact whitespace-token U/P/S target attack on its tested languages and conditions; it does not close every cross-separator, mixed-length or noncipher architecture. GDT605 learns reusable multiglyph units crossing uncertain spaces but its one-unit/one-letter attacks fail. GDT608 retains directed composition and heterogeneous parts, not an alphabet or meanings. General glyph/token/space caution and generative fit therefore add no new decision by themselves.

Two specific countercases remain. GDT831's original held ink/paper control failed on f81r; a validator arithmetic PASS does not rescue that decision, and a newly listed gap estimator is not automatically an answer to that failure. The reviewed Averyanov intake distinguishes latent plaintext reuse from exact surface-word repetition: recoding can hide a copied unit, and an inverse deleting recent identical surface words can remove a legitimate repeat. Even known-key A,B,A admits original-plus-copy and original-repeat explanations without an ownership mark. No universal impossibility was established. The bibliographic records do not solve this identifiability problem.

Decision: retain the physical-gap archive as a concrete obtainable **method-review resource**; retain Turenne's claimed new corpus as unverified availability; classify Parisel's boundary route and Averyanov's selected mechanism as already represented in primary predecessors. None currently supplies a new historical-meaning discriminator or selects a theory test. No new Voynich page, image, reserved unit, contact, decoder or scoring was used.
