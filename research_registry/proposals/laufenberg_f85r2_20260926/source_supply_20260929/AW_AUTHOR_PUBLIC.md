### Composition rules (four; all C0)

1. `.6–.7` is one ingredient specification: `fcho` supplies H; `tcheychedy` names H's coma/tuft; `otol dol` is “leaves of H”; `ol dair` coordinates seed. The H relation is not implied by word shape; it is an authored construction. The preparation M is a separate product of grinding/ingestion; later holding/release refer to raw H.
2. `qo` takes that preceding ingredient list as its object. `ar daiin` opens a linked “with” adjunct; its open scope includes the following pepper and wine groups across the physical line break. `dary` applies ingestion to the prepared mixture. This asyndetic cross-line adjunct is a costly local grammar claim.
3. `dchor ctho daiin` opens the benefit/against relation and `.11` supplies its scorpion-sting-pain target; `.12` adds a distinct relief/ingestion qualification. `otcho rol` opens the later attributed claim only, scoped through `.19`. P is a class-level forward/back-reference: `dain` explicitly names the scorpion-stung class in the second claim and the earlier remedy target is hypothesized to be that same generic class, not an unmentioned unique patient.
4. `dain` introduces P and `cthy` the scorpion-sting condition; `shey/doiir/dchoty/dol` all resume H; `n/soty/doiin` all resume D; `shy/dan` resume P. The holder is P; `schey` marks concurrent holding, `qokchy` its duration, `cths` absence and `otchol chey soty` the no-pain proposition. `chokeol/dchol` mark release of H; the same D then affects the same P. The final `.19` clause assigns the release to P. No hand or before-contact timeline is added.
