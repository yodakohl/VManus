## What follows and what does not

The fixed derivation has one new matching written surface beyond its motivating
pair. This gives a real location for further exploratory authoring. It does not
establish that shckhy means holes, that the new form quantifies over sign regions,
or that either suffix is a morpheme. DOM and RANGE predict the same spelling;
OWNER-PROJECT remains another possible semantic construction. The absence of
alolsheaiin is reported without repair and does not refute productivity.

The counts make the global burden explicit: the hypothetical DAYS, HOLES and
MOTION values have respectively 126, 51 and 423 ZL occurrences, while the two
observed quantified forms have 3 and 1. This is not a numerical disproof, but the
one fitted clock paragraph does not already account for these distributions.
All 33 old glosses remain guessed; no global frequency-to-part-of-speech rule is
invented. The three aiin-final atomics retain their old values and do not acquire
universal quantifier semantics.

A metadata-only selector-first query bound the f108v.10 hit to the full ZL/IT
paragraph f108v.7–11. RF has no paragraph flags, so those five lines can only be
a comparison window there. No paragraph content beyond the profile neighbours
was opened for this selection check. The other old chckhaiin contexts were not
selected as substitute targets; their loci remain reported above.

**Decision:** nominate the whole f108v.7–11 unit for a separate exploratory
attempt with old 33 values fixed and the derived shckhaiin value explicit.
No complete extension is presently authored. It must preserve every unknown
word, reader difference, owner and argument obligation; a list of fresh singletons
will not be treated as semantic transfer. No existing 504/524 failure is reopened.
No new parser, clock-world simulator, new image or reserve use. Independent
meaning confirmation capacity remains zero, with no whole-search significance
or first-word claim.
