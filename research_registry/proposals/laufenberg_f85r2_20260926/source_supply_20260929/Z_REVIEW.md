## Precise next authoring object, retained rather than executed

The smallest honest continuation of IDEA774 is one versioned replacement of the existing person-binding contract across **both whole units**, not a rerun of the old assembly trace. It must state these changes together:

1. Replace old actor_elision's D=W equation with explicit addressee D, assembly-specific wearer role W(A), and one rule for unmarked imperative actors. State how the instruction episode continues into the commentary; RAW492 B01 cannot silently provide person identity while the new rule claims to derive it.

3. Remove W from `cheedar`'s lexical definition if WEAR is supposed to derive W(A)=D. Otherwise the conclusion is preinstalled in the predicate. The new writer must provide WEAR's actor and assembly arguments from its declared construction.
4. Make an explicit decision about C03. Keeping “Wearer, engrave” as a vocative to the current addressee already yields D=W(A) at C03. In that version, C08 cannot be reported as a later independent derivation of that equality. Making `qokaiin` a beneficiary/topic instead would change C03's actor binding and its old `chkain` construction; this would be a further assumption, not a consequence of the minim difference.
5. Apply the same choice to C06's written `qokaiin shcthy`, C07's unexpressed actor, C08's WEAR/YOU pair, C09's implicit beneficiary and C11's `otedy okedy qokaiin`, while retaining all other clauses and the 88-value cost ledger. No clause-ID lookup may assign the desired person.

Under a practitioner/beneficiary reading the wearer may instead be a different person, but the same written C03/C06 contacts must then receive a different declared argument construction. A role name or a new implicit-person constant by itself does not license that shift. Choosing either writer would be C0 authorship; the owned words do not currently choose between them.

The later `BY SAME WEARER` could constrain the selected writer's continuation, but unchanged B01/B06 already enforce that very identity.
