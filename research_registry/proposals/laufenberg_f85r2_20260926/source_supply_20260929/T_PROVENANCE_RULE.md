# C0 rule v1: provenance modifiers and optional replacement plans

New manual construction for IDEA776, written after source/target exposure.
The scope and budget are in T_ROOT_DECISION.md. This is not a confirmed grammar
or an unchanged rerun of GDT1023/GDT1038. No new world simulation is proposed.

## Lexical realization

Delete only the atomic ALTERNATIVELY analysis of exact saiin. Every saiin expands
to s, aiin = ALSO, OF; a separated s aiin has the same two terminals. Other
GDT1038 atomic-or-binary alternatives remain available, with their original
ambiguity and fixed word values. In particular, sy and qokeedy are not forcibly
split. Keep old raw characters, separators, group IDs and source flags.

The inventory therefore has 70 original and 87 original-plus-extension atomic
entries instead of 71 and 88. This is not a net model-compression claim: the
following construction, scope and type choices are new and may cost more.

## Modifier and attachment

M1. OF consumes its following written nominal source. It returns an unattached
source modifier; neither an alternative branch nor a physical ingredient is
created. The source is a BirdKind or BirdBody according to its old lexical entry.
No lookup by desired eagle/hawk answer is allowed.

M2. Within the current nominal field, attach that modifier first to the nearest
already open, compatible head to its left. ALSO can intervene without replacing
that head. If no such head is open, keep the modifier pending until the next
compatible written head on the right. BUT, a new finite assertion or an operation
boundary ends that field; an unconsumed modifier is then unresolved. The parent
statement/operation boundaries are still fitted inputs, not a newly learned
sentence grammar. Multiple compatible heads retain the declared nearest-left
preference; this preference is a new assumption.

M3. The two inherited typed applications are kept distinct:

- FORM + OF(BirdKind): representation-content field.
- PHYSICAL_PART + OF(BirdBody): material-origin field.

M4. One new type lift is required for WING_TIP + OF(BirdKind). It yields a
physical part specification whose source body has that kind:
`WingTip(p) AND exists b[BirdBody(b) AND Kind(b)=k AND Origin(p,b)]`.
This does not place b in the assembly, change the image, identify a particular
bird individual, or decide wing versus feather anatomy. The existing atomic
WING_TIP_OF(k) already has that qualitative interpretation; using the same
relation compositionally is nevertheless a new constructor/type license.

## Reference, replacement and optionality

R1. A new nominal description remains pending while its local modifiers and
replacement target are read. THAT_PART resolves the nearest compatible already
completed MaterialPart **role**, excluding this pending new description. It
does not search all objects: a depiction and a grape seed are not the parent's
MaterialPart role. Completion timing and the role-type partition are explicit
assumptions. An absent compatible completed role stays unresolved. The parent
P role was already supplied by its fitted declarations; its recovery here is
not an independent discovery of identity.

R2. `new_part_spec INSTEAD_OF THAT_PART` builds a Replacement descriptor
`ReplaceSpec(target_role, new_part_spec)`. It does not remove an already assembled
object. Following PLACES and its unchanged accompaniment/pose arguments produce
a proposed instruction fragment for that same role. Body, wearer, seed and stone
references not written in that fragment retain the parent's counted omissions.

R3. ALSO adds its completed payload to the appropriate discourse collection:
a provenance modifier augments its head's assertions; a position requirement
augments the plan's constraints. A completed Replacement instruction is instead
added to the alternative fragments for its target role. The previous fragment
remains available. This last typed addition is an explicit **new optional-plan
rule**. Mere INSTEAD_OF would express replacement; it does not by logic alone
make replacement optional, and a bare ALSO does not make any instruction optional.
There is no appeal to the old chey gloss's words 'in this alternative branch'
as independent evidence for R3.

R4. At THEN/enclosure, select exactly one completed fragment per role-alternative
set. If no replacement alternative was added, the original fragment remains the
sole member. Do not execute both or apply two bird provenances to one body part.
Common constraints on the same role apply to every alternative. Depiction,
seed, wearer and stone remain outside this role-local alternative set.

The old whole-paragraph parse still supplies an instruction program, clause
boundaries, referent introductions, source availability and common prerequisites.
R3/R4 replace its hard-coded C06/C07 ALTERNATIVELY trigger; they are not established
by physical position, an observed instruction being carried out or source meaning.

## Manual consequences to account for next

1. C02 ols aiin skal: OF(BIRD_KIND) attaches left to FORM; this is image content.
2. C02 cthal s aiin chky: OF(BIRD_BODY) attaches left to PHYSICAL_PART; ALSO adds
   provenance. Fused IT saiin supplies the same terminals. No Replacement payload
   exists, hence no new plan branch. The complete IT clause still has cheen/ram
   unknown and is not thereby translated.
3. C05 s altedy / IT saltedy: ALSO adds REQUIRE_POSITION, not a replacement
   option; source-content/diplomatic unknowns in the whole clause remain.
4. C07 saiin shedal shecthy chey tal: OF(HAWK) waits for WING_TIP, receives the
   M4 lift, and INSTEAD_OF resolves the prior completed P role. After its PLACES
   fragment, ALSO adds the H-derived alternative to P's E-derived fragment.
5. C08 THEN/ENCLOSE consumes one selected fragment while retaining the eagle
   depiction and the same seed. This is a consequence under all the above
   assumptions; the old source already stipulated that outcome.

The construction is deliberately reusable without C02/C07 occurrence IDs, but
its type and scope conventions were authored from those known examples. A
directed rule with fitted types is not automatically productive morphology.
No new semantic prediction on an independent passage has been supplied.


## Known global boundary countercase, retained before any wider application

GDT1047 already reports 4 ZL and 3 IT paragraph-boundary contradictions to a
mandatory written **right** host for exact aiin. Therefore M1 above cannot be a
universal paragraph-local writing rule for that whole form. This C0 derivation is
limited to the five declared already authored units. No implicit argument,
paragraph revision or postposed-source exception is added to repair that global
countercase. An OF meaning in some other construction is not disproved, but a
future general grammar would be a changed, separately declared hypothesis.
