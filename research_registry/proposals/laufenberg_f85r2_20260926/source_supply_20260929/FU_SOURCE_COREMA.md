# FU CoReMA: a complete material alternative and third counterpart

**Result:** three complete medieval black-chicken recipes explicitly replace unavailable gingerbread with honey and flour. The selected prepared base supplies the subsequent egg preparation and chicken coating; black chickens retain gold-clove decoration. A locally excluded third entry preserves those relations. This supplies actual historical content for a conditional material hypothesis, not a translation or a successful general recipe comparator.

The strict proposal “one ingredient changes while the entire ordered procedure stays identical” is not supported: the alternative contains two materials, and the witnesses differ in pounding position, tool explicitness, cooling placement and later process reference. The authorized follow-up permits a nonillustrated source. It therefore does **not** fulfill RAW615's illustrated-manuscript requirement.

## Full content comparison

All three entries name black chickens. The primary path burns gingerbread; the alternate path, under lack of that gingerbread, burns honey and flour in a pan until black. B4/W1 explicitly pound the primary gingerbread in a mortar before the conditional; GR1 places cooling and mortar pounding after it. In B4/W1 cooling follows the alternate pan-blackening: branch-only versus common cooling is not fully resolved by the unpunctuated text. No universal cooling/pounding scope is imposed.

The common suffix passes/strains the resulting material with eggs and pours it onto the chickens. B4/W1 name a cloth; GR1 does not. `mit` establishes eggs in the preparation but does not establish a separate “mix first, strain second” operation. Likewise, honey and flour jointly form the alternate material; a modern explicit MIX verb would be a model inference, not another source verb. B4/W1 specify `semel mel` (annotated wheat flour); GR1 says `melb` (annotated generic flour). This is a specificity difference, not evidence that GR1 used another grain.

After application, B4/W1 say to stud the chickens with cloves when they are roasted; this is a later condition, not a new literal fire command. GR1 says to do as written before, then stud them. Black chickens receive gold(en) cloves, red silver(en), green both; GR1 also adds white with black. B4 lists red/black/green, W1 black/red/green; these separate recipient branches keep their roles despite order change. The text does not specify a metal-coating technology or make gold a coating-base ingredient. This is a real written content distinction, not merely an editor's shared dish label. [B4](https://gams.uni-graz.at/o:corema.b4.recipes), [GR1](https://gams.uni-graz.at/o:corema.gr1.recipes), [W1](https://gams.uni-graz.at/o:corema.w1.recipes).

These unusual shared details support our identification of parallel recipe witnesses. They do not establish copying direction, a stemma, independent traditions or a catalogue assertion that these exact units are one critical redaction family. B4/W1 are nearer in wording than GR1. Similarity in entry number or English editorial title was not used as semantic proof.

## Frozen output law and third result

The rule is a conditional material constructor followed by a shared consumer chain:

`LACKS(gingerbread) ? alternate_base(honey, flour) : primary_base(gingerbread)`

`PASS(chosen_prepared_base, with eggs) -> POUR(preparation, chickens)`

`WHEN_ROASTED(chickens) -> STUD(chickens, color-dependent cloves)`

The last explicit condition applies to B4/W1; GR1's reference remains separately represented. Each witness retains its own preparation operations. Substitution changes the material ancestry of the passed base and then the poured preparation; it does not change the guard's named unavailable ingredient, the eggs, chicken destination, cloth where named, or the black-chicken decoration recipient. This is the falsifiable propagation consequence. It requires two successive consumer arguments to follow the selected branch rather than keep the unavailable primary material.

|---|---|
| P1: named black chickens; honey+flour conditional on lacking gingerbread | Preserved explicitly. All three ingredients are not jointly required. |
| P2: both alternatives converge on preparation with eggs, then pouring onto the same chickens | Preserved, using contiguous singular material reference; no second destination appears. |
| P3: black chickens receive gold cloves | Preserved. Other color branches also remain. |
| P4: record actual operation/tool/later-process scope, without enforcing invariant sequence | W1 keeps B4's primary-path pounding, explicit cloth, alternate cooling and roasted-chicken condition; garnish order differs. This records limits, not a separate successful invariant-order test. |

P1–P3 are supported by the locally excluded complete counterpart; P2 includes ordinary pronoun/null-argument interpretation, not an explicit chemical ancestry statement. No disconfirming outcome was repaired after the third read. This is one related-witness documentary check, not an independent confirmation score or evidence that an unseen decoder generalizes.

## Distinct content accounts and next decision

An additive account that uses gingerbread **and** honey/flour contradicts the lack-of-gingerbread conditional. An account choosing the alternate but leaving gingerbread as the later egg/coating input breaks the continuous material reference. A global lexical replacement of gingerbread also fails: the guard must still test lack of gingerbread, not become “if you lack honey/flour, take honey/flour.” A branch-aware material account preserves the guard, changes both dependent consumer arguments, and preserves the chicken and garnish roles. These are actual source-content contrasts.

The smallest adequate computation is the two-path table already documented in JSON: track availability, selected base, egg preparation, application destination and decoration; evaluate the two material ancestries through both consumers. No anonymous retrieval, graph corpus, whole-entry lookup table or decoder was implemented. Memorizing each complete entry could reproduce these results, so this comparison alone cannot establish compositional target meaning.
