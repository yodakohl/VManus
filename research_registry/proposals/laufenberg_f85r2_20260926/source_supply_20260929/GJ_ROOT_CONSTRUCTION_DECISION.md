The first row is a manual conditional consequence of the proposed rule and already frozen words, not an independently observed semantic contradiction. ## Exactly how prior structural knowledge constrains this choice

- [GDT608](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt608_compositional_stem_orientation/REPORT.md) establishes directed external form profiles and whole-form residuals. It does not identify subject positions or lexical valencies. [GDT1094](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1094_f25v_source_package_whole_reading/REPORT.md) already ran the frozen word decompositions on this paragraph; running them again cannot turn `ch`, `sh` or `d` into semantic operators.
- [GDT915](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt915_terminal_lr_phrase_transfer/REPORT.md) retains known-family r/l co-variation, including mixed endings. It cannot authorize exceptionless agreement or equate `chor` and `chol` as inflections of an identified verb. The route retains GDT916's absent new-pair generalization.
- [GDT611](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt611_lexical_slot_permutation_audit/REPORT.md) already shows that formal shared slots leave lexical renaming alternatives. Greater syntactic fit would not by itself select these43 English senses.
- [GDT933](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt933_paragraph_participant_reference_contrast/REPORT.md) already demonstrates that typed backward reference can fill slots by carrying assumptions through unread spans. Repeating that repair is not a new argument for the plant or hare referents.
- [GDT1047](../../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1047_bare_value_left_host/REPORT.md) records boundary-sensitive daiin host capacity and family-level counterexamples. It does not prove a plant pronoun, a universal delimiter, or a noun/verb category. Neither frequency nor host availability settles the1143 guess.

No new candidate is selected by this note. In particular, no nearest-noun program, corpus-wide parser, new generic statistics, new source collection or routine rerun is justified by these outcomes. Continued research should select among genuinely different existing proposals after primary review, rather than interpret this design non-start as another thousand-trial failure or as a universal lack of possible evidence.
