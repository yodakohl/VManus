## Decision from this account

**Retain BB as a sparse unselected exploration; do not choose SUN, MOON or
LIGHT as manuscript meanings.** BB and W assign different semantic types at
the exact same3 physical product positions, but all available following
content is still untranslated. The preserved observations therefore do not
select light against time. The new nominal account removes a mandatory
argument debt at the cost of giving up the upper ring's proposed directed
light assertion; it does not improve semantic evidence. No meaning should be
reported as more probable merely because its account is easier to render.

The next useful continuation needs an actual written consumer: for example a
jointly authored duration-with-start/end relation versus light-with-exposure/
obstruction, retaining the entire unit and known countercases. No confirmed
anchor is required to author it, but two differently named unknown neighbours
are not a discriminator. Do not add40 independent glosses or run another
unchanged census merely to keep these models alive. This work did not execute
an exhaustive all-prose test and did not reopen W/Y; their original decisions
and source bytes remain frozen.
