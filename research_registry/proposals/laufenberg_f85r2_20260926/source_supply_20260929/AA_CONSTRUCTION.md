## Exact authoring contract

The meaning choices are **all C0**, including the two bases:

1. `chor` names a reproductive-head material stock, denoted C within its complete paragraph. This broad anatomical choice does not distinguish flower from seed/fruit. It is an additional AA hypothesis, **not an exported component of schor's C1 image compatibility**.
2. `chol` names a dried drug-material stock, denoted L within its complete paragraph. This is a **new nominal whole-word choice**, not preservation of the old DRY predicate and not a fact proved by the older quality renderer. It is not EXTRACT_STEP from IDEA766. No drying action or earlier source of L is supplied.
3. `s` introduces one proper material portion of its explicit material argument: `PORTION(N) = x` with `material(x)`, `proper_part_of(x,N)` and `x != N`. A nonempty remainder is part of this C0 definition. Neither the portion's size nor its anatomical identity is specified. This is a nominal existential introduction, not an extraction instruction, a measurement or an assertion that a real treatment occurred.

The same operation applies when written separately as `s N` and, **only for the two declared whole forms**, as `s|chor` or `s|chol`. Free `s` consumes exactly the next written group within the same complete paragraph. It cannot skip an unknown group. A derived form already contains its operand and does not consume the following `daiin`, `okchey` or other group. Spaces remain evidence; only the observed local `schol` / `s chol` correspondence is offered a shared construction. No global whitespace deletion or arbitrary s-prefix splitting is licensed.

For this draft, each occurrence of the same exact material base in one complete paragraph refers to its one paragraph-specific stock. The base inside `schor` or `schol` resolves to that same stock. A later bare occurrence may make the owner explicit: this is an openly added forward-reference rule, not a temporal action. Different paragraphs have **different variables**: C22, C32, L32, L29. There is no assertion that they are the same plant, preparation or individual across leaves. The owner is not supplied by a picture.

Each portion occurrence introduces its own existential material referent. It does not automatically equal another portion of the same stock. In f32r only, the draft additionally stipulates that C32 and L32 are different stocks and that the selected portion of C32 is not also a part of L32. Different spellings alone do not establish either fact; these are the cost of making the nearest-stock rival extensionally different. Without that added separation, the two owner descriptions could happen to identify the same material and the owner contrast would be weaker.

Everything else remains untranslated. In particular `qokchor`, `okchor`, `cthol`, `qotchol`, `ypchol`, `shor`, `sy`, `sheor` and `dsh[o:a]r` are **not** silently decomposed by this local rule. The proposed family consists of two specified derived words plus literal free s; it is not a recovered general prefix grammar.
