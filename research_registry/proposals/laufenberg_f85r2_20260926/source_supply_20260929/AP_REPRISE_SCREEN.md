## Candidate and discriminating obligations

The tentative alternative was to ask whether exact `daiin` could introduce a relation like “so / likewise / in the same way.” Three contracts would differ if their semantic roles were independently bound:

- **Operation reprise:** an earlier written operation is the antecedent; `daiin` carries that operation forward to a later written patient while preserving the recipient unless the text changes it. It requires a recoverable prior event and explicit patient/recipient accounting.
- **Property reprise:** an earlier written property/state is the antecedent; the later bearer must be compatible with that property. It does not inherit an operation or license a new action or recipient.
- **New list item:** `daiin` contributes an independent item at an independently visible list boundary. It requires no prior event or property and inherits neither operation nor recipient.

Those duties would make the models different if filled by stable whole forms and independently readable context. Here the slots cannot be filled without choosing the very values under question. A placeholder such as “previous action” or “same property” gives no concrete patient, recipient, compatible bearer, or boundary. It therefore supplies no actual C0 reading beyond three labels for possible uses of “likewise.”

## Decision, novelty, and remaining unknowns

No raw proposal was added with `ideas add`. The outcome that might change the research decision is a complete, already-owned or separately authorized passage where every proposed daiin use can be assigned from fixed independent evidence to an antecedent event or property, or to a visible independent item boundary, and where the resulting patient, recipient, bearer, and later effects differ across the three contracts. The inspected f25v passage does not provide those independent role bindings. Adding guessed glosses, reusing W08/W13 values, treating `similiter hic` as Voynich semantics, or reading a noun-like token as an item would manufacture the discrimination.

Still unknown: whether `daiin` has any relational use; what scope/antecedent it would take; whether recipients persist or change; how list boundaries are written; how immediate repetition is composed; and whether reader disagreements alter any candidate. No hypothesis is selected, no translation or universal numeral is proposed, and no new target access or corpus extraction is authorized by this screen.

## Root qualification, 2026-09-29

This producer's abstention is not a failed semantic experiment or a prohibition on exploratory reprise readings. The screen supplied three generic role contracts but did not actually author a complete new passage reading; it establishes no impossibility of doing so.
