# AH: solar source does not yet supply an organ-cycle writer

29 September2026, post-exposure authoring consideration. No new target was
opened. Root read the whole288-group W/Y ring-plus-f89 account and primary
GDT1051, GDT1079, GDT759, GDT788 and GDT983 reports, together with the relevant
earlier workshop DY correction. This note records an unselected authoring
attempt, not a new test or another global missing-input audit.

The AE source now distinguishes leaf orientation from flower opening and
same-plant reclosure. The two earlier target proposals do not supply these
roles:780 derives a solar return interval;781 requires a light recipient. A
plant organ may receive light without turning, and opening is not illumination.
Changing LIGHTS to TURNS/OPENS would therefore be a changed meaning rule, not
new support for the unchanged781 candidate.

One tempting form construction was examined explicitly. In the complete
f89v1 unit, ZL has daldy and daldaldy, while IT splits the relevant endings
as dal dy and dal daldy; RF retains an uncertain initial component in the
second sequence. A proposed dal=TURN, dy=completed-event and repeated
dal=iteration could manufacture a turn/turned/turned-again account. It was
not adopted: no organ, solar phase or event scope is thereby written, and the
reader difference concerns grouping as well as lexical identity. Bare dal's
existing191/201/158 occurrences and daldy's17/16/8 do not independently select
TURN; daldaldy has only one exact ZL occurrence and none in IT/RF's raw groups.
These counts use the same179-selector word-profile contract, not the whole
manuscript or new independent leaves.

In particular, the old formal DY closure is not a generic translation of the
letters dy. Workshop iteration94 explicitly distinguishes free D:Y from that
closure, correcting the earlier interpretation. GDT1051 preserves the exact
raw/host/whole differences; neither structural parser supplies a TURN value.
GDT983 also leaves action-repetition, reprise, emphasis and graphic expansion
unselected. Its qoqo-specific absence of matching contexts does not refute this
different dal proposal, but cannot be borrowed as supporting it.

Decision: retain AE's primary-source content and do not register this invented
dal cycle as a new candidate. No additional glossary, parser or corpus census.
This is an authoring choice, not an empirical rejection of the plant-cycle
idea or an update to GDT788/983. The next concrete candidate under review is
AG's separately authored reciprocal transfer head on the already owned whole
f75v context; its scope, same-material reference and relation type remain to
be examined before any fixed test.
