## Concrete candidate: two texts about one pictured owner

The specific already admitted **image relation** is not an arbitrary fourfold
enumeration: each f68v2 isolated star has two short inscriptions within its
own sector, and that sector differs from the neighboring many-star sectors
and from the boundary rays. Fix those physical pairs before assigning values.
Once fixed, a complete account cannot move one paired inscription to another
star or treat a neighboring boundary ray as the same label merely to rescue
its meaning.

The historical semantic discriminator is particularly clear in
[Walters W.73 f1v](https://art.thewalters.org/files/pdf/W73.pdf), PDF pp9–10:
Latin and Greek names share an individual wind bust, while its spoke carries
a separate characterization. Same pictorial ownership therefore does not
force same semantic function. The source supports contrasting complete
accounts of a paired register: **two designations of one entity** versus
**designation plus a property/action of that entity**. It does not establish
that the f68v2 stars are winds, that either inscription names a star, or which
target expression performs either function. This source is already used in
the investigation; its institutional transcription was reread, not discovered.

An adequate next trial would author both inscriptions for every isolated star
as complete, jointly referential expressions under one frozen account. An
alias account must preserve one referent across both designations, including
all other admitted uses of any proposed complete forms. A designation/property
account must preserve the same entity but distinguish the designation from
what is predicated of it. It cannot silently replace identity with an action,
let either caption describe a convenient neighboring cluster, or reinterpret
every repeated form with a new owner. Those are genuine multi-term obligations;
an exact echo count alone does not settle them.

**Changed input versus predecessors:** GDT1086 explicitly corrected its own
assumed cluster owners to single-star/two-inscription owners. Its frozen
ray-to-cluster echo test was never run. GDT353 tested eight-title alignment
between f68v1/v2, and GDT1085 tested Trier's twelve homogeneous wind/month
sectors. A complete paired-expression account with the corrected owners is a
different falsifier, not a rescore of those decisions. However, GDT1086's
twelve-locus transcription admission was purpose-bound to its original fixed
test. No new text query was made; root must register any new purpose/scope
before using those loci for this different trial. I sent this exact proposal
and changed-input distinction to root before creating an experiment; none was
created. Preparation/implementation/validation/publication need a decision
note if root selects the trial.

This is an **unselected concrete C0 content obligation**, not an existing
semantic binding, an access recommendation, or GDT388-ready edge. Whether the two pictured inscriptions
are securely individually owned, and which actual complete forms they contain,
must be established under the selected purpose before a content account can
be tested. Existing whole-caption pair membership is a physical constraint;
alias/attribute roles remain the expressly competing interpretations.

### Can those accounts predict different observations now?

Root requested the complete four-pair discriminator before any new access.
The prerequisite is not satisfied by the established structural grammar.

| Source-inspired complete four-pair prediction | Competing account that produces the same observation | Actual decision changed |
|---|---|---|
| Both inscriptions of every star have the same formal expression architecture, as two designations might. | A name and a single adjective, nominalized state, or nominalized action can both be atomic labels; two aliases can also have different compound architectures. | No semantic choice. Formal symmetry alone cannot select identity/alias. |
| One inscription in every pair uses an identical ordered predication pattern, while the other has designation-like structure. | A compound alias can have ordered internal structure; a descriptive label can have it too. Calling the construction predication requires an independently licensed complete target assertion grammar. | Without that license, only a formal register contrast; no alias/state/action selection. |
| Each member of a pair repeats in different running-text contexts or neighboring boundary rays. | Alias designations, an object's name and a property, or a shared subject plus separately predicated material can all recur. W.73 itself separates named alias layers and characterizing spokes. | Exact reuse may constrain a declared complete C0 account, but cannot supply the output type or relation by itself. |
| A shared written construction explicitly marks equivalence in all four paired units. | Apposition, paired categories or an independently asserted correspondence can have the same surface arrangement. | It selects alias only if the construction's equivalence function and its two arguments are independently licensed, not assigned from these pairs. |

The established baseline proves directed composition, hierarchy and whole-form
residual effects, not the semantic types “name,” “property,” “action” or
“proposition.” Importing those types now would make the desired distinction
an assumption. A caption's length or morphological family cannot repair that
gap. Nor can both inscriptions be treated as Latin/Greek wind twins merely
because W.73 supplies that readable example.

**Concrete result: NO_TEXT_ACCESS_RECOMMENDATION_FROM_PAIR_OWNERSHIP_ALONE.**
I do not propose a new overlap count or new extraction to answer a question
whose predicted observations currently leave the same meaning decision open.
The corrected topology still matters: it fixes which complete expressions
must share a pictured owner when some independently justified content account
is available. The useful next discriminator is either an explicitly owned
historical two-caption star construction with independently fixed semantic
roles and distinctive complete-content consequences, or an independently

to all four pairs. That would permit a whole-pair semantic falsifier; another
wind image, arbitrary bilingual vocabulary, new rotation, or selected repeat
would not. No new source search or experiment was authorized by this conditional
