## Finite requirements for both complete accounts

1. **Literal completeness.** Account for every raw written group of every
   selected reading in P4, P5, Q1, Q2 and all four captions, retaining native
   lines, spaces/uncertain separators, raw entities and embedded-record
   boundaries. Include a unique source-group/span-to-analysis index: no
   omissions, duplicated semantic contributions, changed characters or silent
   regrouping. Any multi-group construction cites its exact ordered span.
   Different readings are alternatives of one leaf, not repeated confirmation.
2. **One frozen dictionary and grammar per account.** List every semantic
   value, type, arity, composition/attachment rule and initial condition before
   critique. Distinguish inherited rules from new C0 choices. Each repeat
   retains one complete-form value or a previously declared finite contextual
   rule; every contextual override, sense, referent introduction, default,
   coercion and unexpressed dependency is priced individually and propagated
   to all applicable occurrences. Related spellings cannot borrow component
   values, deletion rules or synonymous meanings without a separately listed
   assumption. No dictionary coverage count is semantic evidence.
3. **Visible derivation and actual arity.** For every proposed construction,
   show inputs, expected argument types, actual source-backed arguments,
   outputs, state change and consumer. A lexical noun is not an action merely
   because narration supplies a verb; a procedure name does not execute itself
   unless the frozen rule says so. A type signature is an assumption when its
   semantic output type has not been independently established. Manual
   contribution tables are not an executable parser/grammar PASS.
4. **Referents and records.** Track named entities, component instances,
   procedure identities, events and participants explicitly. Cite the written
   introduction/reference/consumer for each. Picture proximity and label reuse
   do not authorize a particular woman, channel end, patient, input, output or
   cross-record handoff. Silent panel-level topic ownership is distinguished
   from token-level ownership. Record resets and any continuation across
   P4/P5/Q1/Q2 require frozen, charged rules; they cannot follow whichever
   dictionary needs them.
5. **No flexible unknown repair.** Uninterpreted groups may remain explicitly
   unresolved, but cannot restart interpretation, clear state, supply a missing
   argument, change a referent, cast between types or close an event for free.
   If a needed unresolved contribution has no frozen analysis, mark that
   dependency missing. Do not invent a late gloss or branch after critique.
   A new corrected version would require new authorship/freeze and would not
   overwrite this version's result.
6. **Whole content and claimed picture binding.** Give a connected account of
   all four records and captions without treating physical lines as automatic
   sentences. If the account claims an image binding, explicitly connect its
   named participants and asserted actions to the complete frozen native
   configuration and retain adverse details. A text unbound to the image is
   not contradicted by failing to narrate it; it earns no image-binding support.
7. **Retained predecessors and dependencies.** Apply the shared predecessor
   and counterexample packet to every claimed inherited rule. GDT790 physical
   record ownership and GDT792 cross-topology exact-form roles do not supply
   a component/procedure meaning. FO/FP/FR and FS paint partial decisions remain
   frozen, with zero confirmed words/complete paragraphs. No new dictionary
   coverage, renderer fluency or manual-state match supersedes those decisions.

## The decisive OTCHDY comparison

Each author must nominate at least one **actual written consumer** inside the
fixed complete packet and provide its unchanged shared baseline rule, source
span, argument slots and resulting object/event reference under both rivals.
The comparison keeps all other shared known rules fixed; a new C0 rule is
identified and costed rather than called known. If each author chooses its own
consumer/coercion grammar, apparent type separation is constructed, not a
manuscript discriminator.

For the component account, say which component identity/instance the consumer
uses and which later object/reference consequence differs. For the procedure
account, say which procedure specification/identity it uses, whether an event
is actually invoked, its participants and which later event/reference
consequence differs. These are demanded descriptions, not permission to add
new operators. An unconsumed heading or differently named silent topic cannot
win merely by receiving a different type tag.

The nominated difference must be evaluated through the entire packet under
both frozen accounts. A distinction that disappears through an arbitrary cast,
late UNKNOWN, referent substitution or author-specific grammar is not a win.
If the component and procedure can be globally renamed while preserving every
consumer, reference and observable consequence, record **semantic invariance**.
If both remain different but equally compatible constructed accounts, record
**equally admissible C0 semantics**, not preference or lexical confirmation.

Multiple statuses may apply to different obligations. The postreview must give
all defects and dependencies, not stop after the first objection or at a time
estimate. If a crucial shared rule is itself unconfirmed, state the conditional
result explicitly. A complete hypothetical account is allowed; independent
confirmed meanings are not prerequisites for exploratory authorship. Their
absence limits the conclusion rather than automatically invalidating the work.
