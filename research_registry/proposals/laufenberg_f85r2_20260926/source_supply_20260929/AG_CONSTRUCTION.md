## Exact shared relation and constructor

Let `R(P,S,D,e)` mean transfer event e has material continuant P, source S and destination D. Source and destination are ordered. This exact same R is the value of free qokeey and the inner qokeey in qoqokeey. No FIRE, UNWEAVE, FLOW_EVENT or PREPARE value is substituted in one occurrence.

The chosen new cut is `qo | qokeey`. The inner whole qokeey remains opaque; its own qo is not parsed. Neither keey nor bare qo is given a meaning by this rule. Parent's AB/IDEA783 keey=FLOW_EVENT is a separate C0 nominal proposal, not evidence for this relation. Combining the proposals would require an additional explicit derivation and type account; AG does not do that.

`RECIP(R)(P,A,B,E)` requires distinct events e1 and e2 in one chosen operation episode E such that R(P,B,A,e1) and R(P,A,B,e2). It does not itself impose temporal order. chey=THEN supplies e1 before e2 in the positive branch. The outer head is stipulated to prescribe/assert realization of both directions, not merely name a possible bidirectional graph. This truth-commitment is essential to the new contrast and is a paid assumption.

The grammar is explicitly restricted to this named unit:

`[qo|qokeey] [olkain [qol sheedy]] [qokeor sheedy [qokal or] chey [qokar ol]] [aiin]`

The complete operation E is the bracketed group5–11 body of .39. Group12 scopes over that body. Groups2–4 introduce the material description. The .39 physical line end is chosen as clause closure; no written universal end marker has been recovered. The head does not secretly range over all of .38–42, let alone the next paragraph. Unknown .38/.40–42 material remains outside its chosen local scope.

## A different written consequence, and its limit

Under old PREPARE, GDT982's NOT_ONCE reading was internally possible: execute B→A and forbid A→B, once. With the new positive reciprocal head over exactly that same body, this reading fails the head's required reverse-direction event. This is an explicit consequence of the new meaning and scope assumptions, not new empirical manuscript evidence for chey=THEN. The manuscript has not supplied an independently understood outcome that selects the reciprocal head.

THEN_ONCE and THEN_REPEAT both satisfy the proposed directional obligation. The old NOT_REPEAT same-portion/no-reset failure remains as GDT982's conditional result; it is not newly discovered. A purely nominal/descriptive reciprocal graph can name both possible directions without prescribing both events. Choosing that rival removes the new execution obligation. PREPARE, emphatic R, repeated R, and a noun reprise also remain live rivals; compatible two-leg content alone does not eliminate them.

Under a same-direction DUP reading, two copies of R can share or change arguments; spelling duplication alone fixes neither. RECIP fixes a role exchange of the same two endpoints and same P. This is the substantive difference from the old surface expansion question. No claim is made that a literal doublet forbids a reciprocal real-world episode.
