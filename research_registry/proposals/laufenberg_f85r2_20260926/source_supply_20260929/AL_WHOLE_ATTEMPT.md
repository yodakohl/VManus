## Actual proposed reading

For the complete IT2a line f75v.43 I propose:

> **“Infusion procedure: transfer a portion of prepared herbal liquid from B
> to A; then let it rest gently.”**

The 12 written groups in this line all receive a concrete role in that
sentence. `qokeey` is the fixed four-place predicate R(P,S,D,e): P is the
portion of liquid described by `chesy sol shey ... olkedy ... qol`, S=B from
the inherited `or`, D=A from the new `qotedy`, and e is the instructed event
introduced by the predicate. The inferred imperative/procedure force is an
explicit grammar guess below. The coordinated `tal` event takes the same P.
This is one complete, connected statement under the proposal, not a complete
translation of the paragraph.

The later sequence supplies a concrete possible continuation of that same
infusion episode. In `.45`, retain `qokar=TO_B`; use the inherited `ol=FROM_A`
at `.44#5` to supply S=A across the line break, and carry the previously
introduced P forward. The two adjacent qokeey tokens both denote the one
return event R(P,A,B,e2), with identical participants and event identity. The
new line/episode rule makes e2 later than the `.43` event e1; thus the same
portion is described first going B→A and then A→B. This is a concrete account
for both participants and both events, not the bare claim that repeated
spelling means “mention twice.” The use of `.44#5 ol` and the carried P are
paid, vulnerable scope/anaphora assumptions. `.44#2 ol` is not used.

In IT2a `.48`, `qokeor` remains the distinct inherited value POUR. I read the
line's later `ol` and `qokar` as its explicit FROM_A and TO_B arguments, with
the same current P as material, so this is a further proposed pour from A to
B. This relies on AG's already stated POUR→TRANSFER inclusion for this
construction. ZL3b/RF1b have `qokear` at the corresponding position; I leave
that form unresolved and do not substitute `qokeor` there.
