The differentiating structure in Q3 is not the name equivalence alone: persistent material A contacts intermediate liquid B; B is subsequently mixed into C; direct A-in-C quenching is prohibited. The text's asserted near-equal weight excludes reading A as wholly consumed. It does not assert exact mass conservation. The pronoun in “for þanne it were lost” has not been resolved to a unique referent; the prohibition itself is explicit. Modern marginal summaries misleadingly compress the repetitions into fresh water fifty times; the primary gives liquid replacement only when too much is wasted.
