## Strongest rival and the actual information asymmetry

At unchanged proposed **whole-word values**, no observed quantity currently separates the accounts. The disjointness and one-stock assumptions are construction-level commitments. Written recurrence alone does not prove that `.9` consumes the `.8` stock, that its two noun phrases have disjoint referents, or that `.10` consumes the earlier fine branch. The V2 contract declares these bindings. Its FRESH rival expressly changes the unmarked-input-list rule. An overlapping-description rival can change object identity without changing the noun names.

Consequently the current mass-conservation result cannot discriminate the manuscript from those rivals. Even a partition reading would only select “supplies two positive quantities” against restatement within that world: addition versus two equal portions remains unresolved, Q has no numeric value, and daiin has no confirmed translation.

The information asymmetry worth exploiting is therefore **stock identity under a written continuation**, not another source-domain resemblance. A real continuation that forces the same antecedent to be consumed twice, or independently marks separate retained outputs, would distinguish these meanings. None is already independently bound here. The present question makes that exact dependency visible instead of concealing it inside another fluent reading.
