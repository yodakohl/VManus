## Exact current semantic obstacle

ES's actual constructors state two different effects:

- CHEEY = CLEAR(x); CHEEDY = CLEAR(x) attained through an actual earlier clarifying event.
- QOKEEY = LIQUID(x) and CAPABLE(x,FLOW); QOKEEDY = actual FLOW(x,e,I), without requiring every subject to be liquid or claiming completion.

Uniform attainment of the base property does not give the second contrast: a material can acquire flow capability and remain stationary. Uniform present actuality does not give the first: a material can be currently clear without any actual clarification operation. These are countermodels to proposed operator equations, not observed manuscript histories. Completion/cessation would contradict the deliberately unmarked actual-flow output if silently added. The liquid restriction also cannot survive into all QOKEEDY subjects without changing the frozen member.

Shared existential quantification alone is too weak to claim a lexical discovery.
