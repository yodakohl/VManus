## Known limit that prevents a global reading claim

M1 requires a following written source. GDT1047 already finds paragraph-final
exact aiin without a right host: four ZL and three IT contradictions under its
fixed boundary contract. Thus this M1 convention is not a universal paragraph-local
grammar. No implicit source, different paragraph or postfix exception is added.
Those would be different contracts. GDT1047 does not refute every possible OF
meaning; it does reject exporting this particular right-host requirement.

No new count, subset, synthetic world or alias search was run to soften that
countercase. The new version is a local C0 construction with a known generalization
limit. Old 1038 results, including failure of mandatory decomposition under its
old terminal streams, remain intact.

**Decision:** retain the explicit local construction as a draft; do not select
a general decoder or a further simulation. A next step on this branch must bind
the same modifier/replacement operation in another complete written context,
with actual arguments and a stated scope that addresses the retained boundary
countercase. Another expected eagle/hawk result or another decomposition count
does not do that. Independent meaning confirmation: zero. Confirmed words: zero.
