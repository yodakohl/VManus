## One concrete exploratory architecture

Use a **finite content-bearing construction grammar with exact whole residuals**. Its goal is a complete plausible manual reading under explicit C0 assumptions, not recovered meaning or a new machine.

1. Freeze one exact whole-value dictionary shared by the complete authoring units, with finite paid typed/contextual overloads. Use the same function at every repeat. Preserve native source IDs, uncertainty, spaces and alternates. Do not map unknowns to names by default.
2. Retain the existing formal cuts and directed composition as constraints. Hypothesize only a small explicit semantic constructor inventory. Each claimed semantic part must affect an argument, result, condition or scope; an intentionally null part is separately declared and charged. Exact whole residuals retain specializations/exceptions rather than being erased.
3. Permit opaque unconfirmed names inside finite paid nominal constructions. Each name becomes actual content: an argument of a Spec, relation, assertion or instruction. A catalogue that absorbs leftovers while leaving the procedure unchanged does not establish useful semantic composition.
4. Separate Kind/Spec/Instance and Plan/Event/Result. Publish exact producer inputs, captured versus current constants, operation order, closure/stage policies and subsequent consumer arguments. Predicates on a Kind are not performed-instance events. Asserted constraints are not physical outcomes.
5. Derive the entire native unit in one ordered row-to-rule/argument/output table. A paid shared construction may recur; a whole-line macro per line is charged and cannot be called productive grammar. Any first unavailable input/source group makes that unit partial. Account every alternate without pretending three readers are three manuscripts.

A suitable already admitted authoring scope is FV's complete IT f83r.9–17 plus f83v.21–33, using the existing cache packet rather than a fresh extraction. This nomination selects no interpretation for any word and promises no fit. The substantive difference from FW/997/416 would be **one cross-unit model whose nominal constituents are consumed as actual operands and whose reused semantic parts compute content beyond complete-word/line lookup**. A model containing the same inert names, whole aliases or ineffective parts would not be different merely because it uses a new domain.

The following complete content example specifies the requested calculation without assigning a Voynich glyph or gloss:

> Combine named materials A and B. Take a portion P of the combined material and place P on surface R. Combine the remainder with named material C. Take a portion Q of that material and place Q on R. Compare the two deposited portions: the later one includes C and the earlier one does not.

Pay four literal constants A/B/C/R and five constructors: NAME introduces a Kind; COMBINE takes ordered material arguments and unions their stipulated constituents; PORTION produces a fresh portion with parent provenance and copied content; PLACE makes a fresh deposit owned by explicit R with an immutable content snapshot; COMPARE takes two produced deposits and computes added/removed constituents. Material/remainder state, reference binding and snapshot preservation are explicit assumptions; no chemistry or empirical efficacy is inferred.

| Written step | Computed consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Combine A/B | M1.contents={A,B} |
| Portion and place on R | P1.parent=M1; L1.contents={A,B}; L1.owner=R |
| Combine remainder/C | M2.contents={A,B,C} |
| Portion and place on the same R | P2.parent=M2; L2.contents={A,B,C}; L2.owner=R |
| Compare L1/L2 | added={C}; removed={}; actual earlier outputs consumed |

Replacing C by D changes the computed final added set to{D} under the same constructors. That output is not a stored complete-entry result or a decorative part tag. This symbolic example is **not a manuscript translation or observed experiment**. Portion/deposit vocabulary overlaps FR and supplies no novelty on its own. Only a source-bound complete derivation with shared effective construction would meet the proposed architectural distinction. Unknown actual source attachment remains unknown until such a candidate is authored.
