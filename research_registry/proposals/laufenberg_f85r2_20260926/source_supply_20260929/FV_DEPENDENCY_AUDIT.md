## Counterfactuals under the fixed rules

Deleting a selector contribution while keeping the active program's nominal participants is an abstract dependency intervention, not permission to repair a now-incomplete literal clause.

* Delete old `.16 G001`: no completed U exists. R11 ENCLOSE lacks its SelectionOutput operand; WEAR lacks its sealed operand; R10's purpose reference is unresolved. Nominal slot, specification, stock, core and carrier cannot supply U.
* Delete new `.29 G007` with availability true: specification, old-role reference, replacement descriptor, ready N.pA and hosting plan remain, but no U or assembly is produced. All three later `chedy` operands are unresolved; neither retention requirement nor finished-assembly release can use a donated current material. This changes graph consequence even though the participants remain.
* Delete `.29 G005` or old role `.22 G003`: the replacement descriptor lacks its source-linked compatible target. Existing A sample does not replace that input. Delete proposed specification or sample preparation: respectively no ReplacementSpec or no eligible physical realization; nominal A alone cannot satisfy R09.
* Set availability false: under paid R08/D07 scope, N07–N13 are inactive. The recorded false case has no sample, U, assembly or release and no later consumer invocation. Earlier base and old loose component may exist. This is a valid inactive branch, not a failed executed dependency and not evidence of a lacks-old guard.
* Leave actual availability unknown: retain true/false conditional cases. There is no warranted executed-selection assertion.
* Remove the later consumers alone: U may still exist, but the demonstrated two-consumer requirement fails. The first two references in .30 cannot be double-counted to fill the missing .31 event.

The false-guard scope itself is fitted and paid: it extends to the work/release close and nests later handling/completion conditions. Written WHEN does not independently establish that full scope. Changing the scope is a different model, not a rescue within the frozen one.

## Costs, rivals and decision

The frozen ledger correctly discloses 122 whole cards: 51 new, 35 changed/respecified and 36 reused unconfirmed; three exact binary licenses plus the qokeedy atomic/binary choice; 17 rules, 12 defaults, 22 fitted clause productions and four alternation classes; 3/5 internal selector steps; two nominal kind values; two scope frames; two opaque kind frames; four stock prerequisites; one forward-actor convention; 11 first-mention frames; and eight type bridges. Those are inventories of authored commitments, not calibrated likelihoods or code lengths. The old stock-realization ambiguity prevents treating that list as an unambiguous complete type derivation for both alternatives.

In particular, FV changes `chedy` from the old protective assertion to selected-output reference, `tchedy` from following-stage execution to selector, `shcthy` from physical placement to placement-plan construction, and several material/role meanings. It adds the five-step `dalshdy` meaning and positive availability scope. This is a new C0 model. T7767 and GDT1023/1038/1047 retain their original failed decisions; documentary FV consistency does not validate their earlier lexicon.

Renaming opaque kinds A/B, material IDs or role labels consistently leaves the typed graph and all consequences invariant. Thus kind identity remains exactly underidentified even if the dependency is accepted. A surface name can name a physical component while keeping this same graph; that is not a distinct operational rival. A genuine name/context-metadata account or attached reusable protocol would have to change the specification/sample/plan types, selection/result constructor, reference eligibility and consumer effects, and pay the corresponding changed cards, productions, defaults and bridges. Under unchanged R09/R10, a title or plan cannot supply U. That rejection is conditional on the authored grammar; it is not manuscript evidence that titles or reusable procedures are impossible. No rival grammar was manufactured or counted here.

CoReMA supplies actual historical choice-and-dependent-use structure, but its lacks-primary guard, prepared base and egg/pouring consumers differ from FV's positive availability, opaque material and retention requirements. It confirms neither target words nor efficacy. Formal composition, frequency, native paragraph flags and repeated raw groups do not independently select FV's English assignments. Some card prose still says bird/stone despite the explicit opaque-kind/unknown-carrier limitation; those residues carry no verified referent credit or additional causal rule.

**Research decision:** retain the new IT written-dependency candidate as conditional `COMPLETE_C0_WRITTEN_DEPENDENCY`, with `OPERATIONAL_EQUIVALENCE_LIMIT` and the old-B realization caveat. Retain whole-reader `NO_CAPACITY_DEPENDENCY` for both ZL and RF units. Do not claim all-reader, whole-folio, translation or semantic-selection capacity. The frozen predeclared application audit can now test transfer of these exact producers, output-reference rules, productions and two distinct consumers; it must not add a stock bridge, new clause shape, alias, guard scope or donated output after exposure. This audit opens no application. A transfer failure would reject that scope of the frozen candidate; a transfer success would establish additional C0 consistency, not confirmed meanings.
