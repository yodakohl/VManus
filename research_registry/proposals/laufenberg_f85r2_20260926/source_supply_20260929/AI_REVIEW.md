## Complete textual content, before visual classification

1. 5r-A calls the Moon the lowest planet and describes **its heaven** as transparent. It expressly leaves sight through the heavens open to the starry sphere. Do not assign this transparency to the lunar body.
2. 5r-B switches to **the body of the Moon**, calling it solid/dense and non-transparent. It writes the Moon between Sun and **us**, with straight alignment and blocked rays; old-to-new transition belongs to this solar-obscuration account.
3. 5r-C says the Moon lacks its own light and that the Sun lights the exposed part. Its appearance changes: greater Sun separation increases the light returned above Earth, approach diminishes it. Illumination fraction, body identity, and appearance are therefore not automatically the same kind of description. The small phase details are not a complete ordered set of labels.
4. 5v-A writes direct opposition and Earth's intervening position between Moon and Sun. An additional conditional phrase is not fully secure; AI does not erase it or infer that every opposition causes an eclipse. Full-Moon phase is an inference from the geometry, not a written Luna plena label on these pages.
5. 5v-B attributes lunar waxing/waning effects to humours, animals, plants and sick bodies. This historical medical passage belongs to the complete unit; it is neither modern evidence nor a decoded target medicine statement.
6. 5v-C adds attributed human-character effects, including a gendered historical stereotype. It is preserved rather than omitted; these effects do not identify another depicted celestial person.
