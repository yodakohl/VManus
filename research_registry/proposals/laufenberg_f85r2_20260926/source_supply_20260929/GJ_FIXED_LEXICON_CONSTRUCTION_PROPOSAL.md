## Candidate reusable construction, stated precisely

One candidate reusable construction is a local typed-predicate frame:

1. Each predicate is hypothesized to head one clause. A clause has typed slots supplied by that predicate's fixed meaning: `sos/chor/deeaiir` take a remedy and affected condition/recipient; `okeeaiin/chol` take binder and held object; `shol` takes a growing plant and optional place; `qokcho` takes a thing named and an overt name complement. This is one general valency construction with lexical valency declarations, not a separate per-locus rule.
2. Every slot must be filled by an overt compatible constituent inside that same hypothesized clause. A preposed constituent is permitted only as a local topic if an overt resumptive subject follows in that clause. The frozen adjacent `daiin daiin` topic/resumption rule is local and retains both words.
3. No argument can be supplied from a different hypothesized clause, the illustration, a source passage, or a hypothesized paragraph topic. A line break alone neither defines nor rules out a clause boundary. The parse must independently establish its clause boundaries from the proposed constituents. No constituent may fill two slots unless the construction overtly marks coordination or resumption.
4. A causal naming complement requires an overt `qokcho` head and a complete local cause proposition whose participant identities are overt or locally resumed. `dshey` “therefore” alone does not turn neighboring propositions into one argument.

This construction is explicit and reusable, but the GDT1143 lexicon contains whole-word guesses, not compatible case/role features or clause delimiters. Adding flexible word order, silently duplicating one `daiin` into agent and possessor, or leaving clause scope unconstrained would be additional mechanisms. Under this proposal, boundaries and bindings therefore remain unresolved. Other explicitly motivated constructions remain possible.

## Consequence

This specific local typed-argument proposal does not recover the intended complete reading from the unchanged inventory. Its clause boundaries, roles, and attachments are under-specified; the examples do not establish that another reusable construction is impossible. I recommend **no further construction experiment on this frozen account**. If future work reopens f25v, it should first bring a genuinely discriminating role marker or independent lexical constraint; adding more English whole-form glosses or assuming a source-shaped paragraph is not that input. This conclusion is design-level only: it does not falsify the base or β historical reading, establish a control result, or change any word meaning.
