## The useful construction and its price

A possible whole reading could carry:

```
PreparationDescription:
  ingredient-kind provenance
  other explicitly introduced ingredients
  operation history and stated endpoint conditions

EffectClaim:
  preparation reference
  recipient/context scope
  named bodily action or property
  polarity or comparative degree
```

BOIL would take a preparation description as its input in both explicit preparation branches. The water mixture, boiling and continual skimming remain separate conditions; the final skimmed condition is not a numerical cooking clock or a new word meaning “the whole recipe.” No proof of a terminating mechanical loop is supplied. Boiling alone is a different explicitly described input, not a new meaning of BOIL.

The common HONEY designation can preserve **ingredient kind or source food family**. It does not identify a physical batch across generic alternatives, preserve quantities, guarantee chemical identity of every product, or make the water mixture identical to honey alone. A particular portion can be tracked within an explicitly written preparation, but that would be a further binding. Discarded foam also does not license inventing a named active constituent or assigning it the entire pungent effect.

The effect claim must retain its named object. A negative gastric-action claim does not erase urination, distribution, nourishment or other fields. “More nourishing” is a comparative historical claim, not demonstrated successful nutrition of a particular patient. The old/cold and prime/hot recipient descriptions remain separate obligations; they cannot silently assign every preparation case to one unchanged patient or be used to hide a troublesome result.

This frame is coherent, but its parts are already available in the earlier proposals. To derive the source effects from BOIL, MIX or SKIM without reading them as separately asserted consequences, a new mechanism would be required. Seth does not state that foam is the entire pungent constituent, that removing it sets pungency to zero, that boiling always has one monotone effect, or that all preparation differences are reducible to temperature. Adding those laws would manufacture the needed outputs. Merely appending one effect record to each named preparation would reproduce a branch table, not supply a new reusable consequence beyond334.

The serious rival is a **food/preparation-type lexicon**: distinct names for a honey-water preparation and boiled honey, with separately expressed ingredient provenance and effects. It can retain the same underlying honey kind without being a process-state grammar. Its coexistence with the historical account is not refuted by repeated ingredient naming. Likewise, a result-state adjective may classify a preparation without asserting a currently executed operation. The source alone does not choose between these written constructions.

That is a concrete constraint for authoring, not a new target test now.
