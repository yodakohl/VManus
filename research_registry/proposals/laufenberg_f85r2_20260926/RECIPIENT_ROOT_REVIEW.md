## Concrete source gap, without retrospective repair

The fixed source is the complete owned Galen III.9 paragraph, p277 in the1916
Loeb translation: [source](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Galen/Natural_Faculties/3%2A.html).
It compares dogs' liking for human faeces with the suitability of liver residues
for spleen, gall-bladder AND kidneys. The packet has all three suitability
claims but only the gall-bladder and kidney cases become explicit analogy
targets. G07/E3 and G09/S1 introduce and project the spleen description; neither
asserts an analogy. G08/G11 are fixed to gall-bladder; G16 ranges over its two
written target pairs. No rule generalizes this relation to the spleen.

This is a genuine gap in the packet's full-source-coverage claim. Root froze
the finding before the author's [separate response](RECIPIENT_AUTHOR_REVIEW_RESPONSE.md),
which agrees and preserves the original files. It is not a contradiction in
spleen suitability, the conjunction modifier or origin projection. An unstated
rhetorical bridge cannot be counted as already derived. No extra rule is added.

A simple semantic counterexample explains the distinction: keep three material
witnesses satisfying D0 and their respective recipient predicates; make the
explicit gall-bladder/kidney analogy instances true and the spleen analogy
instance false. The stated projection and analogy rules do not force that
last relation. This is a counterexample to the claimed entailment, not an
observed medical case, a manuscript reading or a complete calibrated null.

Other source duties have explicit places, subject to declared assumptions:
general organs via Omega; attraction/rejection and alteration/retention through
the large G05 construction; four faculties necessary, not sufficient, for
