The human example is modal; it does not specify a deterministic winner function, numerical pressure law, universal priority rule or guaranteed transfer in every state. The vein wording gives a channel system, not necessarily one individually identified vessel. Material amount, unchanged composition and individual particle identity are not established by channel reuse.

The following is an **author's proposed formalization**, not a translated target construction. Let `GT(a,b,basis)` compare traction strictly, with an explicit basis; it is not a ranking of every physiological activity. Use two sorts of basis, `NATURAL` and `CURRENT(t)`. Keep organ identities constant. A local pair can then have:

```text
GT(liver, stomach, NATURAL)
G_liver(t1)  -> GT(liver, stomach, CURRENT(t1))
G_stomach(t2)-> GT(stomach, liver, CURRENT(t2))
```

Here the two guards stand for the source's complete stated circumstances, not freely chosen numeric values. No equation derives current strength from appetite alone. Both guards, their subjects and times must ultimately have written scope. Transitivity or antisymmetry is used only within the same declared basis. Treating the first and third assertions as a strict cycle requires erasing that basis distinction. Conversely, assigning a fresh basis whenever a contradiction appears would merely repeat181's missing written-switch problem.

The retained connection can be represented separately as `CONNECTS(C,a,b)`, with event records containing direction, material and time. Reversing the **route projection** exchanges endpoint roles while retaining C. It does not, without an additional assertion, reverse an entire event including its material state, quantity or physical portion. The source discusses plural veins/stomata; a single abstract C must be declared a channel system if that is intended. Stomach, intestines and the alimentary region likewise cannot silently become interchangeable object names.

This gives a useful serious rival: two episodes use the same connection but different portions or material states. That rival can agree with route reversal. A proposed SAME_PARTICLE or unchanged-material assertion would be additional content requiring written ownership. A permanently one-way connection is a different rival; a mere difference in contents is not evidence for a second connection. None of these source distinctions establishes a target binder.
