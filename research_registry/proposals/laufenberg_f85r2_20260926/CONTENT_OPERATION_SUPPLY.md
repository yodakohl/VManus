## The nontrivial consequence, and its limit

As an **analytic expansion of the old assumed necessity meaning**, let
`N(r)` denote the relevant nourishing occurrence at part `r`, and let
`Cap(r,f)` denote possession of faculty `f`. The old direction is:

`N(r) => Cap(r,A) & Cap(r,R) & Cap(r,M) & Cap(r,X)`.

Here `A/R/M/X` merely abbreviate the four already guessed Faculty values.
This notation adds no Voynich meanings. It makes the following differences
visible:

| Completely written case, if a future account supplies it | Necessary-contribution account | What would be an extra claim |
|---|---|---|
| `N(r)` together with absence of retention capacity | Incompatible under the assumed implication | Treating the missing capacity as optional |
| `N(r)` together with absence of alteration capacity | Independently incompatible under the same implication | Substituting transport for alteration |
| All four capacities present, with no nourishing occurrence asserted | Admissible | Inferring actual nourishment from possession alone |
| Capacity for alteration present while no alteration event is occurring | Admissible | Equating disposition with activation |

The first two rows are two applications of one stipulated rule, **not two
observed manuscript tests**. None of these cases has a completed target
derivation. An author cannot win by writing a truth table that simply repeats
the definition of `qotom`.

A serious rival can keep the same Faculty nouns but give the whole paragraph
the force of an instruction: activate the four faculties for a specified
material and purpose. That does not require the crude claim that mere
possession guarantees success. It differs in speech act, activation events,
participants and intended outcome. A further sufficient-process rival would
need to state its background conditions; it should not be rejected merely
for failing in an unconstrained world. The old seed alone does not decide
between complete accounts of these kinds.

## Assimilation is a distinct operation, but not a new card here

[540](ideas/10_galen_retention_assimilation.json) already requires a complete
arrival/residence/assimilation argument and its departure countercase. The
old source account cannot be completed by replacing assimilation with any
of these **analyst-defined rival endpoints**:

- relocation: material reaches a region;
- enclosure: a recipient contains material;
- likeness alone: material shares a property with the recipient;
- literal identity: material and the entire recipient become one referent.

A future typed account would have to distinguish these from its declared
assimilation endpoint, and say what persists or changes. The source wording
does not furnish a modern token-identity or mass-tracking convention; that
would be additional modelling. Merely adding `BECOMES_PART_OF` as a new whole
word would not demonstrate a reusable constructor. No material-to-blood
chain, fixed reaction product, automatically unique episode or cessation rule
is supplied here.

That endpoint distinction is worth keeping in540's full obligations. It does
not justify a new parallel draft while root develops550's typed descendant.
This task did not inspect that descendant or its results. The older
[550 root review](UNTIL_S_ROOT_REVIEW.md) remains the relevant public limit:
event types and identity, actuality of departure, endpoint composition and
ordered attachment were open. Those costs cannot be concealed by choosing
the word assimilation.
