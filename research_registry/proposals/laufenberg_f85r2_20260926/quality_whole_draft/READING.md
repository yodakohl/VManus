## What this does and does not establish

The repeated `or` has one recursive PART_OF value in all seven occurrences.
`aiin` is BODY in all five, `shedy` DIET in all four, and the remaining repeats
retain their entries. Those are author choices, not discoveries. In particular,
the first line uses two nested part operators; the independent `oraiin=SOFT`
entry prevents any claim that the apparent surface compound has been decoded.

All marked groups and uncertain separators are retained in the structured
record. IT2a has 107 groups and RF1b 109; neither is repaired to this reading.
For example, IT2a S.16 has qodain=GENTLE under the fixed lexicon, not
qodaiin=NOT_MOIST; IT2a/RF1b W.20 begin with ar=AND rather than or=PART_OF.
The packet therefore has no complete three-edition agreement claim.

The correction rival changes only qodar/qodaiin. Its three occurrences lack
written action/patient binding under the frozen grammar. B takes intention
from the trial qo itself; it does not demand a second intention word. This
is a limitation of the invented scaffold, not a victory for negation. No
same-owner HOT/NOT_HOT or MOIST/NOT_MOIST contrast occurs in A either. The
proposed target-level discriminator remains uninstantiated.

The appropriate result is a complete but heavily underdetermined A draft,
an explicitly incomplete B semantic parse, and no meaning confirmation.
