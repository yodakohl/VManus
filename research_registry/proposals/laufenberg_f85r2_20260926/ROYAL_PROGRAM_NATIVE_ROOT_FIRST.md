## Initial comparison, preserving the mismatches

This is an actual connected cosmos/wind/weather/time/calendar/season/world-age
program. A more concrete motif than generic circularity is the eight veined,
elongated internal forms in the explicitly wind-labelled51r construction.
The f85r2 plume interpretation remains hypothetical: number, placement and
head representation differ, and53v shows that pointed radial forms also occur
in a planetary layout. The source does not identify a Voynich glyph or fix a
unique interpretation of an outer plume.

The four external angels on50r, seven planets on53v, four outward-facing
seasons on54v and six world-age sectors on58v are distinct arrangements.
They must not be merged into a fictional single matching fourfold diagram.
Likewise flowers in a spring-month scene, feast vessels and work tools do
not reproduce an aged person with support/beads followed by a physician.
No complete such attribute sequence was observed in these18 faces.

The circles use different centres and inscriptions. Blue can be a starred
heavenly field or ornamental background; water is indicated more locally in
one planetary figure. Smooth concentric boundaries and repeated decorative
border strokes do not provide a demonstrated f85r2 wavy-boundary equivalent.
No direct copying, shared workshop, Upper Rhine circulation or source genealogy
is established. This source is the catalogue's earlier southern-French Occitan
witness. Whole-image coverage is complete; full textual understanding is not.

588 retains its fixed source;595 remains unselected. No target data, reserve,
