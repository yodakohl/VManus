The critical meaning-bearing phrases are clearer than some inflectional
endings: four elements; all humans mixed; some take more of one; all nevertheless
possess all; predominance names constitution; hot/cold and dry/moist oppose;
not every thing is wholesome for every person. The unit ends by allowing
conduct contrary to constitution, qualifying a purely deterministic reading.
The exact scope of the last qualification needs a philological edition if
used as a fine-grained discriminator; no deterministic equality is imposed.

## Complete argument and actual consequences

| Step | Printed location | Historical proposition, paraphrased |
|---|---|---|
| Analogy |175body1–6|Fourfold human nature is compared with the year's divisions; domains are related, not identified.|
| Composition |175body9–176body6|Living beings are constituted through the four elements.|
| Material/effect correspondences |176body7–9|Fire supplies warmth; water blood/moisture; earth flesh/bone; air breath. This is the source's historical account, not modern physiology.|
| Common membership |176body10–13|No human is unmixed from these elements.|
| Different predominance |176body14–18|Individuals receive more fire, water, earth or air.|
| Conflict |176body19–23;177body1–4|Contrary qualities can contend, causing disorder and harm.|
| Naming without exclusive membership |177body5–10|A dominant contribution names/inclines the constitution even though all elements remain present.|
| Individual regimen |177body11–18|Conduct/treatment must consider this inclination; one thing need not benefit all people.|
| Qualification |177body19–178body3|Conduct can run counter to inclination; exact syntax is retained as uncertain.|

The useful semantic distinction is **contains X** versus **predominantly X**,
and descriptive constitution versus an intervention appropriate to it. It is
not a list in which each person consists of exactly one element. Nor does the
text license replacing every NOT-HOT property with a cooling treatment. The
proposed qo correction/negation rivalry remains a trial assumption until the
written subject, state and action constructions are supplied.

## Karlsruhe correction and witness fidelity

Before reading the printed parallel, root read the first Karlsruhe92r line
at1000 then2200pixels as approximately `Das wasser blut v[n] für hitze.
Erd fleisch v[n] bein luft ...`. The earlier second-reader claim of water

supported in that line. The readable print confirms the kind of element/body

root's manual reading. Exact source witnesses and observation order are kept
