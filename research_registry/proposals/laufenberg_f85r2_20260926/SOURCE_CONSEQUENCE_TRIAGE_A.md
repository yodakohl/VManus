## Why578 is stronger than another two-name substitution

The complete owned GalenIII.14–15 argument gives more than a mode label followed
by one suitable outcome. Evacuation concerns nearby accessible sources and
lighter matter first; the tube example supplies a concrete water/sand ordering.
The quality account permits heavier matter when more naturally related, and
later restricts that kind of attraction to proximity. Conduit-mediated
evacuation can operate over a distance. The closing wine/artery discussion
retains suitability **and** thinness, vessel width and attractive efficiency.

Thus a proposed mode qualifier has two distinct downstream obligations:
priority/permission and locality. Deleting the stored mode while keeping one
universal rule for both would lose content. A whole written account could
fail because its qualifier preserves neither, only one, or the wrong modal
strength. This is more informative than declaring two records and never
using their fields again.

It still does not force record storage or morphology. A vocabulary with two
operation names can preserve every assertion, including all scopes. To claim
compositional advantage, a later author must show the **same written mode
construction** used with two explicit modes and later references using those
same mode-bearing expressions. An operator that just indexes two bespoke
paragraphs is a lookup table, not a new consequence. Nor is merely storing
both flags in a named frame sufficient.

The smallest consecutive source unit retaining578's full declared challenge
is completeIII.14–15, not three selected passages. It includes arterial motion
and source neighbourhoods; the two mechanisms and cavity/coats distinction;
the historical vascular-communication arguments, including the unobserved
ends of supposed septal passages and the teleological/inlet-outlet reasoning;
the distance comparison; garden channels; local nutritional relay; intermediate
humours, marrow and tissue examples; and the final wine qualification. An
author unable to carry these obligations should not claim a whole-unit result
from the tube and lodestone examples alone. This substantial source debt is
the main practical reason not to launch it automatically.
