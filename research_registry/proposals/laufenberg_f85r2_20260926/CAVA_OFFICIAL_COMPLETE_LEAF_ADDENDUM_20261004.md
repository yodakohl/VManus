## Executed disputed-clause check on the new pixels

Root viewed the complete official image at original resolution. A reader who
had supplied the earlier uncertain `SOLV…` alternative then inspected this
new image without being supplied root's new reading. Both now read the
upper-left outer arc as **`AER CALIDVS ET HVMIDVS SOCIATVR IGNI`**. The latter
reader explicitly identifies the open C, angular A and final R in SOCIATVR,
and the terminal I of IGNI. This is agreement on improved pixels of the same
witness, not independent manuscript evidence or a statistical test.

The earlier dissolution reading is withdrawn. The accepted source fragment
says that hot, moist air is associated with fire; it does not say that air
dissolves into fire or dissolves fire. The hypothesized new transformation
predicate therefore supplies no reason to reopen the Voynich quality graph.
The earlier conflicting readings remain in the Dolan addendum as an exposure
and correction record, not as equally accepted alternatives after this check.

The upper-left inner arc remains less secure: root reads
`PER CALOREM AQVE PER HVMOREM`, while the counter-reader reports uncertain
`[…] [C?]ALOREM AQVE [F?]RIGOREM`. No majority vote resolves this discrepancy,
and no complete diplomatic annulus is claimed. The upper-right outer start
`AQVA HVMIDA ET FRIGIDA` is readable; its faded continuation is left open.
