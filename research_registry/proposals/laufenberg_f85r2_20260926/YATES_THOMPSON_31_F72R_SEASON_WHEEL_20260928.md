# Yates Thompson MS 31 f. 72r: labelled four-figure season wheel

28 September 2026. Exploratory source-only comparison after GDT1043 and the
existing Royal MS 19 C I f. 54v control. The [British Library catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002354846)
dates Yates Thompson MS 31, a Catalan *Breviari d'Amor*, to 1375–1399 and
lists f. 72r among its full-page miniatures. The [institutional f. 72r
image](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165716982.0x00008f/full/max/0/default.jpg)
was viewed natively by two project readers. It shows a single joined wheel
with four differently posed human figures around a central disk. Four
season names are placed next to figures around the outer frame, and further
written material follows the ring. Exact expansion of the small curved ring
text is not needed for this comparison.

The page is a useful earlier example of explicit season ownership in a
four-person circular image. It also exposes the limit of resemblance: none of
the figures carries a flower, a staff with hanging beads, or a raised vessel
under inspection, and no Voynich-like outer plumes are visible. Extended
hands occur, but a gesture alone is not a two-attribute match. Existing
f85r2 [GDT1043](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1043_f85r2_native_attribute_binding/REPORT.md)
therefore remains a provisional composite-iconography lead, not a matched
four-season text key. No Voynich word or block receives a season value from
this source. No new Voynich target, reserve, or sealed page was opened.
