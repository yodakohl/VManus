## What follows from these complete units

III.1 explicitly motivates names for alteration/assimilation/nutrition,
attraction and retention from their activities. These are not an inventory
of mutually exclusive physical agents. The first general term can have more
specific descriptions. The source distinguishes a power belonging to a part,
its activation, and the resulting presentation or assimilation. Its account
of retention requires a power resident in the receiving part, rather than
one arriving from elsewhere. Attraction brings appropriate material to the
part; arrival is its stated purpose. The whole argument also includes elapsed
time, continued departure preventing adherence and complete assimilation,
and the conditional premise of purposeful Nature. A reading of only the
three activity names does not reproduce that argument.

III.9's first paragraph extends the discussion from the spleen to liver,
veins, arteries, heart and other nourished parts. It attributes attraction of
appropriate material, rejection of foreign material, retention and alteration.
The faculties are necessary for nutrition. This is neither a measurement of
a current event nor the converse statement that possessing all faculties
ensures an event. The following comparison of residues is a separate paragraph
and is not inserted into553's owned source unit.

Thus the proposed generic process-to-faculty construction has a historically
motivated CONTENT distinction. The source does not identify `qod`, `qok`,
`ar`, their cuts, application directions, or a target organ. It does not
supply the claim that E and S refer to the same physical organ. Shared
specification is not shared individual; shared individual is not automatically
a shared event. A written realization relation would be additional target
grammar, not a fact recovered from a common subject heading.
