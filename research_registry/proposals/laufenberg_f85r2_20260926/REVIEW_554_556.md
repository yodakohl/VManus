# Independent review: RAW554–556, with RAW558 comparison

RAW555 imposes the broadest fixed local composition obligation among RAW554–556. Its one `or` = OPPOSITE rule must type all exact free `or` occurrences separately in each reader, as well as fused and split compounds and the double-opposite form. The first concrete gap is `or shedy` at f85r2.13 G001: `shedy` has no declared QualityTerm type. That is an unresolved typed input, not a linguistic contradiction. The 20 assigned groups per reader are literal inventory counts, not semantic evidence.

RAW554 offers a potentially crisp assertion/negation contrast, but existing proposal 07 already gives `dar`/`daiin` HOT/MOIST and `qo` NOT on the same surface. RAW554 mainly re-factors that proposal compositionally and adds a typing burden for bare `ar` and `aiin`. GDT627/GDT812 also make `daiin` as measure, magnitude, or degree a live alternative, while GDT624 is close prior quality-grammar work. RAW556 has a distinct SUPPLIES relation but only two outside-locus seed forms; the first, `otar` at .1 G008, immediately requires an unprovided Element subject.

The most relevant rival to RAW555 is unreviewed IDEA000548, which assigns the same `or`/`aiin` surface to immediate-carrier selection, including `or or aiin`. This is a competing contract, not confirmation or an automatic stop. Any later test must distinguish the two over a complete block.

Conditionally, RAW558 is a better next drafting scaffold than another isolated quality-operator proposal because it frames separate, consequential source claims: universal element membership and the failure of universal treatment benefit. Its explicit scope rivals and countermodel make the difference concrete. It is not ready to test: the benefit construction is exact only in ZL W.20; IT/RF have different spellings, and ZL's tail plus numerous other `or` contexts remain unbound. Its own whole-source obligation still includes predominance/naming, element/body effects, conflict, suitable regimen, and an uncertain will/conduct qualification. Draft those clauses and all-reader bindings before code. IDEA000411's prior warning also applies: a complete grammar can still fit without discriminating a reading.

These judgments use only the existing safe 24-locus projection and specified primary reports/proposals. No new target data were accessed; raw group counts remain separate from meaning. Existing route stops on the older source/text-equivalence claim remain intact but do not decide these local raw hypotheses. No global candidate selection is recommended.
