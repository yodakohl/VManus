# Decision: test a specific bridge, not four-way resemblance

## Question and current stop

The unknown is whether a medieval source program already in this dossier
explicitly relates the four natural/vegetative faculties (attraction,
retention, alteration or digestion, and expulsion) to a seasonal, age,
complexion, or cosmic illustration program. GDT891 supplies four named
vegetative powers in a different diagram, while the Laufenberg materials
preserve seasonal, age, physician, and complexion programs. Their separate
existence does not establish a historical bridge, a common sequence, or a
bijective four-role mapping. A bridge would need an explicit relation in a
primary witness, not shared cardinality or general Galenic vocabulary.

## Decisions affected

- A primary passage or diagram explicitly connecting these programs would
  justify considering a faculty-mediated source hypothesis alongside the
  seasonal/complexion account. It still would not identify any Voynich figure,
  owner, word, or mapping.
- A bounded source unit that defines the faculties but does not connect them
  to the other programs leaves the proposed bridge unsupported in that unit;
  retain the programs as distinct source comparators. This is not evidence
  that no medieval bridge exists.
- A conflicting order, number, or functional scheme would constrain any
  proposed composite account and block automatic transfer of a four-role
  template.

## Smallest adequate source check

Use at most four targeted searches and inspect at most three primary source
units. First screen the existing Laufenberg source reports and the GDT891
primary report for an explicit cross-reference, not only adjacent discussions.
If they identify one bounded source unit that names both a faculty and a
season/age/complexion/cosmic relation, inspect that complete unit and its
reported diagram/figure relation. If no such locator emerges from the bounded
screen, inspect no speculative additional witnesses: report the exact gap and
stop. Galen III.1/9 rechecking is reserved to root in this work block.

The comparison must keep faculties distinct from the four humours, four
elements, four seasons, and four bodily qualities. It must preserve contrary
schemes, incomplete diagrams, uncertain labels, and non-bijective ownership.
No target query, target image, reserve, sealed data, new decoder, or contact is
in scope.

## Budget and outputs

Maximum 30 minutes total, including this preparation, no more than four
searches, source inspection, reporting, and validation. Outputs are this
decision plus `FACULTIES_TRADITION_BRIDGE_REPORT.md` and
`FACULTIES_TRADITION_BRIDGE_RECEIPTS.json` in the existing dossier. A negative
result must be stated as a bounded-screen gap, never as absence from the
tradition.
