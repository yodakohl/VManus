## Concrete positive input: one referent satisfies both restrictions

The complete23-line C54 Item remains the source unit, with all U1–U8 duties
in [the working transcript](C54_CELESTIAL_UNIT_TRANSCRIPT.md). Its closing
R14–16 construction makes one stellar referent both nearest to the repeatedly
referenced planet and running in the relevant sign. Its nature then enters
the final consequence. The sign's assignment to that planet is a separate
directed relation; it is not supplied by the restriction itself.

A source-content formulation is:

`D(q) = stellar(q,c) AND nearest(q,p,c) AND runs_in(q,s,c).`

The same variable q belongs to every conjunct. With an explicit witness of
D, its nature is `nature(q,c)`. Replacing this by separately finding some
nearest q and some same-sign r loses that common-reference consequence.
For a declared individual-bearer example, q nearest but outside the sign and
r inside it does not satisfy D merely because both separate searches succeed.
This is a logical illustration, not an observed configuration or target case.

The old [RAW568 construction](ideas/38_recipient_relative_material_restriction.json)
already fixes `FOR_RECIPIENT(b,c)(D) = lambda x: D(x) AND PROPER_FOR(x,b,c)`.
It explicitly preserves the same x and D's source argument. The C54 instance
uses that same description-restriction law twice with different predicates.
It adds two celestial relation predicates, a common context, the retained
planet/sign chain and an ontology choice. Those are real costs; calling the
result one prefix would not make them disappear.

The closest serious rivals remain **collective reference** and **scope of
the nearest comparison**. A collective gestirn may satisfy both predicates
without being one physical star; distributive properties of its members need
separate specification. Nearest overall and then within-sign filtering differs
from nearest among same-sign bodies. A static per-sign configuration may also
preserve every stated relation without any changing neighbor. These are
compatible interpretive branches, not straw failures of conjunction.

The [root C54 check](C54_CELESTIAL_ROOT_CHECK.md) is authoritative for the
later qualification: R9 may contain Maß, so the frozen producer's categorical
day/night-disappearance paraphrase is not a settled law. Final mon remains
Moon/month; R6–7, the distance relation and the Sun-in-planet bridge remain
qualified. None is repaired to help the restriction example. A future whole
C54 author must retain the entire Item, not promote its final relative clause
into a complete reading.

## Why repetition alone does not supply this construction

[GDT580](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt580_adjacent_relation_resumption_voice/REPORT.md)
already implements immediate resumption under inherited meanings and explicit
head attachments. It expressly cannot invent an owner. Its reversible output
is not evidence that a repeated Voynich component denotes common-reference
restriction. GDT943 likewise preserves a complete conditional clause but
leaves the hypothesized owner operation unselected outside its fitted seed.
The old [shared-degree card](../ji_shared_degree_scope.json) also distinguishes
shared scope from counting repeated values and retains its one-quality/two-value
countercase. None of these old meanings is inherited here.

The concrete future obligation is stronger than matching repeated spelling:
the repeated construction must receive an already introduced argument,
preserve it in both predicates, and retain the explicit planet/sign/person
reference when a later property or name is predicated. A complete hypothetical
reading may posit that structure openly. It cannot replace it by fresh
unnamed witnesses per occurrence or by an English paraphrase whose ownership
is absent from its written derivation.
