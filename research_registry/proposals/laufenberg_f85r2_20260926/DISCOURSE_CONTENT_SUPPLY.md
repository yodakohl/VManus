## A. Earlier expectation and the repeated late-stage assessment

The two **whole, fully read local inscriptions** are:

- **9:** *Putavi quod viverem. Vita me decepit.*
- **10:** *Versus sum in cinerem. Vita me decepit.*

The abbreviation expansion in9 is inherited from the source report. Its
working translation uses “I thought I would live”; no “forever” or numerical
duration is present. Both clauses in each annulus must be retained, not just
the repeated ending. The following notation describes source content; it is
neither a target code nor an implemented semantics:

```text
ASCRIBE(c9, x9, BELIEVED(x9, LIVE(x9,h), t_b))
ASCRIBE(c9, x9, DECEIVED(Life, x9, t_d9))
ASCRIBE(c10,x10, CHANGED_INTO(x10,Ash,t_a10))
ASCRIBE(c10,x10, DECEIVED(Life,x10,t_d10))
```

`ASCRIBE` locates a proposition in a depicted persona's discourse context;
it does not claim a physical utterance. Past/perfect relations are relative
to the respective context. The belief precedes its retrospective context;
the other completed events are not silently assigned its exact date.
`h` marks the living proposition's unresolved temporal horizon, not an extra
word claimed to occur in the source. Nor is `x9=x10` established just because
both inscriptions contain first-person forms.

The source-derived **role distinction** is substantial: the persona is the
subject of believing/living, the object of the repeated deception predicate,
and the subject/patient of the change-to-ash statement. Life owns the repeated
third-person predicate. A future shared reference grammar must preserve those
roles instead of assigning every clause to its most prominent figure.

The tempting new mechanism would connect the earlier embedded proposition
to its later disconfirmation and carry that assessment into10. Its additional
costs are explicit: same-person linkage across the two scenes; a resolved
living horizon; a link from the refrain to that exact earlier belief; and
an event or assessment identity across repetitions. None is merely the
meaning of the prefix “past” or a free paragraph-boundary default.

**Serious rival:** a finite earlier expectation of continued life can have
been fulfilled, while later mortality defeats a broader confidence in life.
Alternatively each age exemplar voices the same moral assessment without
recounting one deception event. These readings preserve the literal clauses
and their roles. They differ from a model that treats the refrain as an explicit
rebuttal of exactly `LIVE(x9,h)`. An added “forever,” identical event identifier,
or unexpressed future endpoint would select the stronger account by assumption.

If a completed target account independently writes that precise horizon and
reference, the two models can differ: the prior proposition is false at its
stated horizon, or it is true while a different expectation is disappointed.
Without that completion there is no formal contradiction to count. Merely
adding `BELIEVED` already yields the familiar non-entailment of its complement;
IDEA326 and397/415 contain that commitment distinction. The Arundel pair is
better local source input, not a newly discovered general operator.

**Explicit accounting of this source sketch:** six relation symbols
(`ASCRIBE`, `BELIEVED`, `LIVE`, `DECEIVED`, `CHANGED_INTO`, temporal precedence),
two substantive constants (`Life`, `Ash`), two persona slots, two discourse
contexts and five temporal slots. These are not all distinct physical events.
No target whole-form or component value is assigned. A full target grammar's
number of productions and lexical values remains unknown.

## B. Divine governance and the king's worldly claim

The complete central statement combines seeing-all-together and governing
the-whole-by-reason. The complete king statement combines kinghood, worldly
rule and worldly possession. Their minimal graph has separate owners `g,k`,
separate predicates `SEE`, `GOVERN`, `RULE`, `POSSESS`, and explicitly retained
domains `U,W`. The picture identifies the central owner differently from the
king; it does not write an identity or disjointness equation for their domains.

A coherent theological reading can contrast limited human confidence with
divine government and the ending in mortality. But the source supplies no
recovered connective saying that the king's claim is false, that one ruler
excludes another, or that divine seeing is a formal `KNOWS` operator applying
to every future proposition. Those are separate interpretive bridges.

**Serious rival:** both forms of government coexist, with worldly rule inside
a broader divine order. Another reading hears the king's words as boastful
persona speech without asserting their truth or falsehood. The late-stage
refrain can make the boast poignant without logically negating kingship.
Consequently `RULE(k,W)` and `GOVERN(g,U)` alone produce no contradiction.

The cost is at least two owners, two domain slots, four substantive relations
plus kinghood and a reason/manner argument; source-wide scope and any link to
the later scenes remain additional. There is no target assignment. This is
a richer source case for IDEA534/538's frame/voice distinctions, not a new
two-valued “higher/lower” law. Arbitrary universal/exclusive quantifiers would
repeat the free scope allocation that the previous proposals already expose.

## C. Attributed persona voice versus a physical speaker

The complete8–10 run gives a connected concrete case: an ill patient alongside
a physician; a bier with a reading cleric; a tomb with a recumbent figure.
Its inscriptions belong to the stage personas. An ordinary account of the
picture therefore needs to distinguish at least the **represented subject**,
the **content attributed to that subject**, and any **physical reading or
speaking action**. That is a multistep discourse obligation, not “all people
in a scene have the same role.”

**Serious rival:** the cleric physically reads a first-person text representing
the dead person. His reading action and the first-person referent differ.
Alternatively the inscription voices the persona without narrating any
physical recitation. Both preserve the source. A physical-speech rule requiring
a living speaker cannot simply be applied to the tomb's persona; but a literal
speaker model with explicit quotation can coexist with the persona account.
Neither a resurrection nor an invisible new narrator is supplied for free.

This would require inscription/content ownership, a quotation/reference
construction and, only if actually claimed, a separate speaking/reading event
with its own agent. It adds no target values here. IDEA397/415 already separate
narrator, reported speaker and proposition; IDEA538 already requires explicit
indexical context. Arundel now makes their practical cost sharper, especially
in relation to IDEA563's patient/physician distinction. It does not make that
general distinction a new RAW mechanism.
