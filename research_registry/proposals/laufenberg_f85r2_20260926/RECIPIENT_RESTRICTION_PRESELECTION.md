## Why the human-faeces case is not a free second input

The source does offer a second origin-like attribution: human faeces. That is
useful, but unchanged568 defines **RESIDUE_FROM**, not a universal OF operator,
and its second operator fixes **PROPER_FOR**, not any favourable relation.
There are two honest prospective generalizations, neither adopted here:

1. Introduce `FROM(a,c)(D)(x) = D(x) AND ORIGIN(x,a,c)`, using a faeces
   description/human source and a residual-matter description/liver source.
   This adds a generic origin relation, a new description-argument interface,
   and a source interpretation of the human genitive. It cannot be reported
   as a previously owned second RESIDUE_FROM input. Human kind and organ role
   also need an explicit common source-role interface; no particular human
   or physical liver is identified by this general text.
2. Generalize the recipient step to a predicate-indexed qualification with
   separate PLEASING and PROPER parameters. This preserves the source's
   distinction, but adds the predicate parameter and its typed application
   rule. Omitting that parameter and identifying the two predicates would
   strengthen the source. The dog then demonstrates pleasingness; it is not
   a second demonstrated nutritional-suitability case.

Together these could form a plausible **new** content hypothesis. Their core
is generic conjunction of relations on a shared variable; the source does
not fix the order of those conjunctive stages or provide a noncommuting law.
Inventing an order-sensitive effect to meet a compositional quota would add
another unsupported commitment. This review supplies no target realization
or newly discriminating written consequence for that generalized language.
These origin/predicate generalizations do not justify a whole free-word
authoring pass under the claim that unchanged568 already owns two repeated
operations. The narrower factorization below avoids changing either content
predicate and provides the conditional recommendation in this final note.
The limitation is exact capacity/contract change, not lack of confirmed words.

## Minimal falsifier preserving the original material argument

For each recipient b, a source-faithful assertion of a qualified residue must
use a **single bound witness within that assertion**:

`exists x [RESIDUE_FROM(liver,c)(x) AND PROPER_FOR(x,b,c)]`.

A fixed reading fails this obligation if it instead supplies independent
existentials for origin and suitability. The smallest countermodel has two
material witnesses u and v: only u is liver residue, only v is proper for b,
and u differs from v. Separate existence claims are true; the required shared
witness claim is false. This is a logical countermodel, not an observed bodily
case. The same check can be applied to two recipient claims without requiring
their respective witnesses to be identical or different across claims.

Dropping the origin after recipient restriction, changing liver to the
recipient organ, or silently creating a new product likewise violates the
fixed law. But checking this definition alone is not a manuscript test. It
becomes an informative written consequence only when a complete reading gives
both arguments and uses the derived description again under that unchanged law.

## Strongest compatible rival

The serious rival supplies separate material descriptions M_s, M_g and M_k,

Consequently, source truth alone does not select internal description

## Separately proposed factorization of the original restriction law

Parent supplied a more precise alternative before this note froze. The
one-origin finding above stands, but it does **not** exhaust ways to factor
original568. In particular, it does not scientifically disprove568's original
criterion of repeated same-witness restriction. The two-input limit above
applies when RESIDUE_FROM is counted as one of the two required repeated
operations. It does not apply automatically to the following factorization.

Use neutral names A and B here. Parent's illustrative letters d/qo are **not
target assignments** and none of572's frozen meanings is inherited:

```
A_c(b) = lambda x: PROPER_FOR(x,b,c)
B_c(P) = lambda D: lambda x: D(x) AND P(x)

A_c : Recipient -> MaterialPredicate[c]
B_c : MaterialPredicate[c] ->
      (MaterialDescription[c] -> MaterialDescription[c])
```

MaterialPredicate and MaterialDescription here may share the underlying
predicate domain; their different discourse purposes alone do not establish
different mathematical sorts. The meaningful type step is from an ordinary
unary predicate to an operator taking a description and returning a description.
The context index requires compatibility; it is not permission to coerce an
incompatible clinical, temporal or material domain silently.

This factors the **same original568 law**:

`B_c(A_c(b))(RESIDUE_FROM(liver,c))(x)`

is exactly the conjunction of liver origin and propriety for b, on the same x.
Neither factor changes PLEASING into PROPER or invents a second source organ.
For b=spleen,gall-bladder,kidneys, A receives three recipient inputs and B
receives their three recipient-specific predicates. These are distinct
parameterizations, not source-proved extensionally different predicate sets:
the paragraph does not prove those suitability sets unequal or disjoint.

### Source support versus added architecture

**A source-supported opportunity exists for two layers, but the factorization
itself is an added semantic/grammar hypothesis.** The paragraph's opening
general organ account uses appropriate-material predicates in attraction;
its closing clauses qualify origin-bearing residue descriptions. Spleen and
liver are explicitly named in the general account; other nourished organs
are generalized. Applying that generic scope specifically to the closing
gall-bladder/kidney roles requires keeping their organ/nutritional membership
explicit. It is not an observation of additional feeding events.

A later writer could therefore supply actual A_c(b)(x) applications within
the general appropriate-material claims for at least two recipients, and
actual B_c(A_c(b))(D)(x) applications in at least two residue claims, preserving
the third final recipient unchanged. If it merely produces A_c(b) as a term
inside B and never applies a bare predicate in an owned assertion, it has
shown a curried implementation of one restriction, **not the stronger proposed
two-layer written-use requirement**. The source's general statements must
retain their generic/capability status; inventing observed portions to obtain
extra applications would add content.

The original source does not dictate these functions, their separate lexical
realizations, morpheme cuts, application direction or order. It does not
guarantee that there is an appropriate material with non-liver origin.
Consequently this is a plausible controlled construction to try, not an
attested medieval compositional grammar or already demonstrated target capacity.

### Exact output difference and limit of the proposed gain

The bare output `A_c(b)(x)` asserts suitability and says nothing about origin.
The modified output `B_c(A_c(b))(D)(x)` entails **both D(x) and suitability of
that same x**. In a logical case with PROPER_FOR(v,b,c) and NOT D(v), the bare
application is true and the modified one false. The source does not supply
that particular counterexample; it supplies the two different scopes which
the proposed writer must preserve without adding an origin restriction to
every bare suitability claim.

There is no further physical transformation, transfer, causal prediction or
new medical law hidden in this distinction. Beyond generic conjunction, the
content gained is the explicit source/recipient ownership and retention of
the origin-bearing description in subsequent use. The separate-material-name
rival above can preserve all those truths through separate clauses. Only a
fixed written account can show that two reusable factors replace independent
compound values, and whether later uses actually depend on their projections.
An exact extensional synonym that satisfies both projections is not refuted
merely for lacking the preferred internal representation.

Extra factorization costs, relative to the original one-step FOR_RECIPIENT
law: one recipient-to-predicate rule; one predicate-to-description-modifier
rule replacing the single curried restriction; explicit predicate application
and modifier application interfaces; a shared compatible context choice and
its written binding; and a requirement for **actual uses of both output levels**.
No new content predicate, origin value, event, cross-recipient token identity
or numeric quantity is supplied. No target compression is measured. The fixed
source description, three recipients and one suitability relation were already
part of568; counting them again as discoveries would be misleading.
