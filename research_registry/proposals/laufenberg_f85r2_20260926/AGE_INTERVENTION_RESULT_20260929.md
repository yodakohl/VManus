## Source consequence and what the draft fails to express

Conditionally reading W as the earlier and S as the senior advice realizes
the difference between a one-intervention and two-intervention prohibition.
Reversing those contexts reverses that difference. Neither assignment supplies
a written age guard, the numbers35/65, or the later rule's priority. Figure
attributes cannot silently provide those missing words.

More decisively, `shedy` occurring after the W closer does **not** mean that
purging is preferred. Absence from one prohibition entails neither permission
nor preference. The actual source asserts medicinal purging's greater
suitability; this draft has no written comparison construction. It therefore
does not satisfy even the complete selected source core, much less the rest
of the chapter or all Voynich block contents. No fifth convenient word or
new phrase-as-sentence production was added to hide this gap.

The same surface positions also permit a nonmedical list qualifier, a
recommendation restricted to a named condition, or entirely different named
actions. Without meanings, the structure does not select prohibition, need,
bloodletting or purging. Swapping source labels, changing scope constituency
or treating unknown material as operators are further unresolved alternatives.

## Frequency and grammar remain obligations

The cached179-selector profiles were checked before final authorship. ZL
`ain` occurs101times on45selectors, `aiin`423/100, `chedy`466/73 and `shedy`388/64.
Those frequencies do not select a meaning. In particular the two proposed
procedures are widely reused, not private labels of these figures; their
adjacent repeats (8 chedy,9 shedy) and shedy's label-kind occurrence still
need an account. Conversely, ain's lack of ordinary line-initial occurrences
is compatible with a postfix use but does not prove a syntactic closer.
Profiles distinguish line edges from sentence or scope boundaries.

The spelling relations ch/sh and ain/aiin are retained without asserting
venesection-versus-purging or negation-versus-need morphology. Directed
composition/whole-form residuals remain requirements of a fuller theory.
GDT999's failed specific aiin-ykar-ain-cheedy transfer is not reopened, nor
are the earlier f85 NOM/QOD/faculty readings. The new scope construction
has not been given a universal-corpus claim.

## Decision, reproduction and exposure

Retain the explicit partial C0 construction and its reader disagreement;
classify IDEA764 **nonconfirming, whole reading incomplete**. Do not extend
its dictionary automatically or call the two structural projections two
independent successes. A useful next development would have to express the
actual age/priority and positive preference while preserving these meanings
and accounting for the intervening text; no enlarged alias or word quota
is licensed by this outcome.

The public decision was `c17943705` before the current authorship pass, but
source and target were already project-exposed. The draft itself is openly
post-exposure. `AGE_INTERVENTION_AUDIT.py` replays all473 retained groups and
the six exact closer projections; its PASS validates the inventory and authored
rule display only, not meaning. No new source/image, admission, f84/f84r,
f116v, reserved page or contact was used. Independent meaning/confirmation
