# Augsburg 1491 scans 00171–00175: Winter ending and complete regimen transition

2026-09-26. Fixed source-only audit under [HEAT_PRINT_PARALLEL_DECISION.md](HEAT_PRINT_PARALLEL_DECISION.md). All five cached full scans were inspected, without crop, enhancement, OCR or new image retrieval. The source is the Augsburg 1491 *Regimen sanitatis* witness held by HAB, shelfmark 167-9 Poet, as identified in the existing source receipts. No target transcription, pixels or IDEA551 lexicon was read.

**The requested complete Winter passage cannot be recovered within this fixed range.** Scan 00171 starts mid-unit with a list of meats. Its Winter rubric and the earlier food/animal/digestion/warmth/three-meals stretch lie before the selected scans. The Winter ending and its next rubric are visible. No neighboring scan was opened to repair that boundary gap.

There is a positive separate result: from the next rubric on 00171 through the upper part of 00175, the print gives a complete, approximately 64-verse regimen argument. Seasonal bodily change, different responses of sick/healthy and old/young people, animal examples, medical judgment and theological qualifications all belong to it. This is broader than a four-season quality list, but it does not supply the missing Winter mechanism.

## 2. Every visible Winter-ending line

This is a working transcription of all fourteen lines before the next rubric on 00171. Long-s is rendered as `s`; u/v and abbreviations are lightly expanded for readability. A bracketed letter/name remains uncertain. It is printed-witness evidence only.

1. `Von rindsfleisch vnd schweinen`
2. `hesin vnd von hir[c]zinen`
3. `Vnd des geleich ob du icht bist`
4. `gesund vn dir sunst nicht gebrist`
5. `Baden soltu messigklich`
6. `vnd gar warme halten dich`
7. `Dis zeitte leret der maister mich`
8. `ist einem alten weib geleich`
9. `Die von alter dorret krümen`
10. `vnd ringet mit dem tode vmben`
11. `Das leben vnd ist überladen`
12. `mit alter vnd mit seinen schaden`
13. `Vnd ist nackend vnd bloß`
14. `on alle hilffe sigeloß`

The surviving continuation mentions beef/pork, apparently hare and deer, and similar foods **with a health/no-other-defect condition**. Because its governing sentence began earlier, this fragment alone is not used to reconstruct the precise missing imperative. It next counsels moderate bathing and keeping warm. The season is compared with an old woman, withered/bent by age, wrestling with death for life, burdened with age and its harms, naked/bare and helpless. The force of the last adjective is provisionally “without victory/power”; a polished edition-level interpretation is not claimed.

The health qualification, moderate bathing, warmth instruction and adverse old-age conclusion are all retained. Nothing in these fourteen surviving lines mentions digestion, an internal organ, three meals, or the animal behavior from the disputed earlier stretch. Their absence **from this ending** cannot be used to deny their occurrence in the missing opening.

## 3. Complete following regimen argument

This is a sense account of the complete unit beginning under the new 00171 rubric and ending before the complexion rubric on 00175. It preserves its less convenient qualifications and changes of topic rather than extracting only the old/young heat couplet.

The opening speaker says his art was learned from God and has sustained people. The physician picture follows. The text asks the reader to understand that the year has four different natures and that bodies likewise change their natures. **Something beneficial at one season can harm at another**, so conduct must answer to the time. The sentence crosses 00172/00173; its harmful counterpart cannot be omitted.

Animals lacking human reason serve as examples of adapting or preserving themselves. The print names birds, including stork and swallows, and then wolf and leopard; one bird name and the exact winter/weather clause are not fully resolved here. Each acts according to its kind and the time. The wise person should likewise adapt, expressed through turning one's cloak toward the wind. This is an argument for responsive conduct; it is not yet the missing Winter animals/digestion clause.

The sick and the healthy have different conditions: **what helps one can hurt the other**. Old and young also have different changes/responses with cold and heat. This requires judgment and attention to experienced physicians and wise predecessors. The direction and magnitude of those age-specific responses are **not specified in the quoted old/young passage**. The text does not here state the Hippocratic ranking of fasting tolerance or assign maximum innate heat to growing bodies.

The argument then widens: human life is exposed to many small and great harms; the stars' properties, powers of air, water, fire and earth, and their combined effects can bring death and suffering. Invisible spirits and the theological explanation through sin/divine displeasure are also included. God nevertheless protects people, directly and through arts imparted to human beings, which should help body and soul. The reader should follow those beneficial teachings, since art and instruction flow from God. That completes the unit before the four-complexion rubric.

The complete argument is therefore:

`seasonal variation → bodily variation → different useful/harmful conduct`,

qualified by `sick/healthy`, `old/young`, `kind-specific animal examples`, `medical judgment`, and the text's wider cosmological/theological account. It is **not** an explicit `cold outside → increased heat inside → greater food requirement` chain. That narrower question still depends on the missing Winter opening.

## 4. What this print can and cannot clarify

The printed ending independently makes its health condition, moderate bathing and warmth instruction readable. The following complete speech directly supports a distinction between seasonal state, human bodily response and appropriate conduct. It also asserts an age distinction in response to cold/heat without reducing young/old people to season names.

These are observations of the **printed witness**. No uncertain Karlsruhe91v letter, animal verb, referent, meal frequency or causal connector has been restored from them. The print places a longer regimen/physician speech between Winter and the complexions; the earlier manuscript account must retain its own actual sequence and wording. Related witnesses do not become independent origins or proof of a particular copying path simply because both survive.

**Decision: INCOMPLETE_SOURCE_UNIT_AT_FIXED_START.** The Winter mechanism remains unavailable in scans 00171–00175. Preserve the positive complete regimen argument and the exact visible Winter ending. Any later, separately authorized inspection of earlier scans must be a new scope, not a retroactive enlargement of this packet. No target meaning, dictionary value, source identity or Hippocratic borrowing is confirmed.
