The new construction separates **common kind**, **case output**, and **material provenance**. Two input portions can belong to the honey kind without being the same portion, dose, mixture or treatment. Each case binds its own recipient, condition and times. An asserted product is a positive claim, not a statement that every other product is absent. The asserted contrast between cases is a separate proposition. No age, constitution, disease or seasonal condition silently overrides another.

The origin question then uses a different written relation. Pure release of a named output requires an earlier constituent of that output kind, in the input portion, connected to the output through a declared lineage with no change of kind. Generation by alteration requires a change claim on a declared precursor lineage. A whole input called honey may contain a constituent called bile: comparing the whole's name with a constituent's name does not establish transformation. Input/output labels are roles of MaterialPortion, not disjoint sorts that force new physical individuals.

The strongest countermodel is retained constructively. Two hypothetical honey portions can have the same constituent-kind inventory containing both blood and bile. One common recipient-dependent selector releases one kind under one profile and another under the other profile. That model can reproduce the positive product cases and a claimed outcome contrast. Its hidden stock and selector are added rival assumptions; they are not source facts. A generation account can reproduce the same outputs. A mixed account can release some constituents while generating others.

Thus recipient dependence alone does not choose the origin mechanism. Nor does common food kind imply uniform composition or recipient-independent release. The author's uniformity argument supplies an additional causal bridge U. With P denoting the preformed-only hypothesis and D the separately asserted contrast, the proposed reconstruction has `P ∧ U → ¬D`. D rejects the conjunction; rejecting P alone requires U. The whole target account would need to express that premise and its attribution/counterfactual scope. It cannot manufacture it by defining release as uniform.

A meaningful later consequence would concern a particular output and lineage, bound independently of which causal model is being evaluated. Explicit absence of a preformed witness or an explicit kind change is incompatible with pure release for that output. Explicit preformation, the same lineage and no kind change are incompatible with generation-by-change on that lineage. Mere silence, an unspecified pre-existing quantity elsewhere, or a different output supplies neither contradiction. This is a constraint on complete claims; no current Voynich claim has been bound to it.
