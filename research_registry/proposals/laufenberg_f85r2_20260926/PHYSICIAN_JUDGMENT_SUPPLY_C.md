## Candidate A: an example changes the argument's domain, not its actors into one another

**Owning passage:** verses5–26; immediate example-and-command sequence15–26, wholly on scan00173 after the temporal continuation. The source has already instructed the reader to govern himself as each time requires (13–14). It then says animals without reason nevertheless do this (15–16), names birds including stork and swallows, and describes preservation in winter/weather. The first bird name and some weather wording remain uncertain and are unnecessary to this construction. Wolf and leopard follow (21); each according to its kind can preserve itself according to the time (22–23).

The reinspection supports these local anchors, with long-s rendered s and abbreviations left lightly normalized:

> Also thünd doch die thier zu hand / die gar kein vernunfft hand

> yedes nach seiner arte / Kan sich [ge-/be]halten nach yede[n] zitte

The first syllable of the preservation verb is not fixed by this focused rereading; the proposed relation depends on the clause's capacity and reflexive preservation, not that spelling.

The next line addresses a wise person, but its last word is not securely fixed here. The following instruction is clear:

> Deinen mantel soltu keren dar / do yeder winde wähet har

The source order is directive → animal example → renewed human directive. It is not a chronological experiment in which watching an animal caused an observed human action. Reading the animal material as a reason-giving example is supported by *Also* and the return to second-person *Deinen/soltu*. Calling it a strict logical a-fortiori proof would add a formal validity claim not printed in the passage.

**Input → carried relation → written output:** changing circumstances and kind-qualified animal self-preservation provide an example of responsive conduct; the addressee remains a human reader, and the written downstream output is a directive about that reader's cloak in relation to the wind. Animal kind, animal capacity, human addressee, the human's garment and normative force remain distinct. The native wording does not state a numerical bearing or identify a universally correct physical maneuver, so the raw does not supply one.

**Concrete distinguishing consequence:** a rendering may retain the animal capacity and the human directive without asserting actual human compliance or preservation. It may not give the animals the reader's cloak, turn wolf/leopard into medicinal ingredients, or transfer one species' precise unmentioned behavior unchanged to the human. A flat list of creatures plus a garment loses the written return from example to instruction.

**Rivals/costs:** a merely adjacent animal catalogue; a literal single-subject action sequence; or a claim that the normative instruction is logically entailed by animal facts alone. The source's analogy is interpretive discourse structure, with explicit links but no written formal proof rule. The cloak line can serve as a practical/proverbial concretization; this task does not resolve that rhetorical register into an external geometry. No existing target figure is assigned an animal or cloak role.

## Candidate B: bodily cases motivate judgment; an experienced group becomes the object of a trust directive

**Owning passage:** verses27–38, crossing scan00173/00174. The source first contrasts sick/healthy recipients and old/young responses to cold and heat. Directional age effects, a named treatment and a fixed persistent patient remain unstated. Verse34 then says judgment/wit is needed. Reinspection favors **witze**, rather than using the first B inventory's tentative word as a clean transcript.

The next lines identify the old/grey among physicians and wise people, attribute much prior experience to them, and tell the reader to believe them. The exact grouping of the two coordinated/genitive descriptions remains a qualified reading; no rigid modern professional set intersection is imposed. The decisive local wording is:

> Hand empfunden vil hie vor / den glaube ob du nit bist ein thor

**Input → carried dependency → written output:** differing bodily responses motivate the need for judgment; a newly described adviser group is credited with prior experience; *den* points back to that group in the reader-directed instruction to trust. The group owns the experience, while the reader is addressed by the instruction. The text does not give the specific contents of advice in these two lines, certify every possible statement by every physician, or report that the reader already believes them.

This supplies an additional reference distinction: the earlier old/young recipient contrast and the later old/grey adviser description share age vocabulary, but do not establish the same individual referents. A role description about physicians/wise people intervenes before the anaphoric trust instruction. The source gives no warrant to identify an earlier old patient with a later adviser merely from an age adjective.

**Concrete distinguishing consequence:** a source-faithful account can assert past experience of the adviser group and a present directive to the reader without asserting the reader's own past observations, completed belief, a successful treatment or infallible truth. If it turns *den* into a medicine or carries a prior patient identity into the adviser group without justification, it changes the written relation. If it only repeats that people vary, it omits the downstream instruction and its referenced group.

**Rivals/costs:** a simple empirical outcome claim; an unrestricted authority-implies-truth rule; the physician speaker alone as owner of every first-/third-person predicate; or a known patient becoming an adviser by automatic identity carryover. The experience-to-trust connection is a reason-giving sequence, not an experimentally established reliability coefficient. The *if you are not a fool* phrase is admonitory rhetoric; the raw does not construct a diagnostic fool/non-fool treatment branch.
