## Comparison and decision

87v/88r cosmic circles: undulating texture can occur within an explicitly
named heaven whose description invokes cold/moist water. The relevant question
for Voynich is therefore not simply 'water OR sky'. A historical author can
use water or fire qualities to describe different celestial domains.

field, do not form a blue lobed outer boundary, and are not four detached
feather-like appendages. The source also supplies no combined sun/four-figure
panel here. Its yellow and red colors do not establish the target blue's
meaning. No celestial identity is assigned to a target plume or word.
