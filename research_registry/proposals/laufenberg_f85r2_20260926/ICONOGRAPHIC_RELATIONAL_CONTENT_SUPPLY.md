## A. Egerton: the most useful already-owned architecture

Use the complete four-banner unit, not a selected relative clause. Under the retained scholarly reading, its graph introduces four humours, also calls them complexions, continues a nature/kind phrase with an unresolved term, and ends with an element relation whose verb/antecedent is uncertain. The alternative final opening *they* can preserve plural reference without the same relative-clause parse. Neither alternative identifies the nearby singular figure as the subject of every clause. [Whole sentence-ownership review](EGERTON_SENTENCE_OWNERSHIP.md); [native report](EGERTON_VERSO_REPORT.md); [informed root review](EGERTON_VERSO_ROOT_REVIEW.md).

A hypothetical writer would need three separate relations:

```text
CARRIES(figure, text-unit)             visual placement
ABOUT(proposition, introduced-set)    grammatical/discourse reference
UTTERED_BY(proposition, speaker)      speech attribution, if asserted
```

None of these implies either of the others. In particular, moving a continuation to another drawn carrier need not replace its introduced subject. Conversely, a genuinely written speaker introduction could change who speaks without changing what the proposition concerns. This is a proposed typing distinction, not three identified target words.

The serious rival is **distributed recitation of a shared explanation**. Several personifications could utter different parts of one definition while retaining the same plural subject. That rival is compatible with the source's collective reference. It prevents the tempting but invalid conclusion “collective argument therefore one speaker.” Four independent self-descriptions are a different, more restrictive rival and cannot simply be assumed from four adjacent figures.

The precise future authoring obligation would be the introduced set, both naming descriptions, every continuation and its antecedent, the unresolved nature/element content, and any claimed speaker changes. No one-to-one mapping from four humours to four elements, identification of a central sun, or order around a target ring follows. The literal source reading order is not a target permutation rule.

This would change a whole reading only if its written references force two clauses to preserve one subject while their depicted carriers differ, or expressly distinguish speaker from subject. Merely assigning a collective noun to a repeated form would leave that relation stipulated. Source incompleteness is also substantive: the unresolved lower-banner content cannot become a generic SAME/RELATED operator that automatically makes the chain close.

**Why no new card:**538 already requires explicit speech transitions,534 explicit cross-unit reference, and057 separates local captions from other discourse. Egerton's exact collective continuation is stronger source input than those broad analogies, but the source-level distinction was already recorded. This task supplies no further written reuse law. The two possible accounts are concretely different:

* **CURRENT_MONTH:** a current calendar condition governs advice to an otherwise unspecified reader. Natal description remains a separate proposition.
* **NATAL_RESTRICTION:** the advice concerns the previously described sign-child, with the month still relevant to conduct. Generic wording does not by itself rule out inherited discourse restriction.

A person born under another sign, presently in that month, is the distinguishing hypothetical case: only CURRENT_MONTH necessarily extends the advice to that person. This case is **not observed or asserted by the source report**. Neither model may replace a birth condition with current time or silently identify the reader with the sign-child.
