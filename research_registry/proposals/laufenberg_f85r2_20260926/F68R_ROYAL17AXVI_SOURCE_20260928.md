# Royal MS 17 A XVI: f68r2 topology near-miss

28 September 2026. Bounded, exploratory source-only check after the existing
f68r2 paired-opening and Ghent/Morgan comparisons. The [British Library
catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002107247) describes Royal
MS 17 A XVI as an almanac **for 1420**; it calls f. 2r an **added
fifteenth-century** diagram, so the date of that addition is not fixed by the
almanac year. A researcher directly inspected these institutional images:

| Folio | Original image | Visible relation |
|---|---|---|
| 2r | [BL IIIF](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165704077.0x000003/full/891,1024/0/default.jpg) | One orbit sketch with Sun/Moon marks and a planet-distance list; no connected star field or two local written rings. |
| 25r | [BL IIIF](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165704077.0x000031/full/822,1024/0/default.jpg) | Named fixed-star positions form a table, not a ring adjoining the two luminaries. |
| 27r | [BL IIIF](https://bl.digirati.io/images/ark:/81055/vdc_100165704077.0x000035/full/816,1024/0/default.jpg) | A single concentric Sun/Moon volvelle has zodiac/month bands and a shared rim; it has neither two separate written medallion rings nor a labelled interior star field. |

The source attests several relevant celestial devices in one physical book, but
distributes them across different folios. It has no author-visible relation
that could own both Voynich f68r2 opening phrases to the two separate rings.
**NO_CAPACITY** for a heading or word assignment from this witness. The
negative conclusion is confined to these inspected folios, not all Royal
material or all medieval astronomy. Existing Ghent MS 92 and Morgan M.721
comparisons remain as recorded; no Voynich target, sealed data or reserve was
opened in this source check.
