# Informed comparison addendum to first-pass Berlin 22r note

My initial whole-page observation `BERLIN_MERCURY_PAGE_B.md` is frozen
unchanged. After freezing it, I read `BERLIN_MERCURY_PAGE_ROOT.md`, which
provides a full 49-baseline working transcription from the same image. This
is an informed comparison of one photograph, not a second witness.

The root reading confirms my first-pass scope observations: 22r is a
two-column text page with no figure; it begins mid-sentence in the Gemini
placement discussion, names Mercury in the left column, and continues with
the child's qualities on the right. It is a substantial Mercury continuation,
not the full Mercury unit. The page's explicit associates in the good/bad
clause are planets (`gut bey guten planeten ... pos bey bosen planeten`),
with a separate qualification that Mercury is good by nature.

My broad statement about learned/celestial arts was under-specific. The
root's line-by-line reading identifies astronomy, geometry, measuring land,
figures of the stars, heavenly spheres and elements; it also reads
disputation, natural matters, magic/divinatory arts, future finding, verse,
reckoning, painting, carving and engraving. This is a connected arts-and-
learning description, not a medical or urine-treatment instruction. The
root's exact words remain a working transcription rather than an edition.

My first note left the women clause unresolved. In the root's reading,
`Er hat dy frawen lieb` is legible as a positive statement that the child
likes/loves women; I withdraw the stronger uncertainty I recorded as a
possible evaluative/social relation. The clothing word remains uncertain
(`graws?` in the root transcription), so neither a definite grey garment nor
a specific color/fabric should be asserted. The forehead line is also
bracketed by the root (`[stirn?] ist eng`); that does not resolve my
first-pass uncertainty about a confident forehead description. No forehead
feature should be used as a secure cross-witness difference.

The page therefore supplies a clearer planet-associate clause, an
intellectual/celestial arts constellation, and a readable women preference;
it supplies no local picture-text linkage. These refinements do not alter
any target interpretation or the fixed Md2 planetary conclusion.
