## Why a type label is not yet an additional observation

For `cheor chor` / `cheol chol`, the same observed two-form pattern can be
represented as agreement on a binary grammatical feature, two lexical formulas,
a shared production state, or two descriptions with different participant
properties. Frequencies, directed component profiles and the four r/l cell
counts are unchanged when those interpretations are exchanged. Calling the
feature ENTITY, QUALITY, CASE or PROCESS does not break that equivalence.

This is a limit of the evidence reviewed, not a proof that all future text-only
reasoning is impossible. A restrictive reading can have genuine consequences.
For example, [the RAW507/508 primary review](../../decisions/galen_composition_507_508_review_20260922.md)
shows that a fixed extensional operation conflicts with the old NOT/TO/MUCH
assignments. Those meanings and the operation are paid assumptions; the conflict
does not select the correct meaning or make every r/l change one operation.
Unbounded whole-form exceptions would erase this useful constraint again.

## One conditional opportunity; presently not a runnable semantic test

**Use known r/l alternatives to distinguish intrinsic information from relational
role, rather than to guess a metal, season, or grammatical name.** Retain the
five GDT915 evaluation families that actually contain both RR and LL:

| Fixed whole-form alternatives | ZL RR,RL,LR,LL |
|---|---:|
| cheor chor / cheol chol |1,0,0,4|
| chor or / chol ol |1,0,2,2|
| okar otar / okal otal |2,1,0,1|
| qokar or / qokal ol |2,3,0,2|
| shor chor / shol chol |1,0,0,3|

These are a retained candidate bank, not five new discoveries or a preference
for whichever pair fits an image. Include the mixed alternatives and complete
hosts; the table does not prove that adjacent forms are grammatical partners.

The two substantive hypotheses are distinct:

- **Intrinsic feature:** the ending contrast expresses a property of the
  independently identified referent. Keeping that referent and its property
  fixed while changing its relational role should preserve the ending.
- **Relational role:** the ending contrast expresses the referent's place in a
  fixed relation. Keeping the referent fixed while reversing that role should
  change the ending under the declared mapping.

The strongest nuisance rival is fixed phrase/production-state selection. A
same-ending effect alone is predicted by that rival too. The smallest useful
new consequence is a crossed reuse packet: independently identify the same
referent in two contrasting roles, and another referent in those same roles;
fix the raw forms, partner relation and relevant property before scoring endings.
Then identity/property tracking and role tracking make different predictions.
The referent need not have a translated name. A single observed contradiction
can reject a deterministic contract; one fitted crossover cannot confirm it or
supply project-wide significance.

**Actual missing input:** occurrence-to-referent identity and the role contrast,
not more counts of these forms. They may eventually come from already admitted
visual or diagrammatic evidence, or from a separately justified textual relation.
This review has found no such additional packet and makes no claim that the
parallel visual review will find one. Without it, do not execute a new type test.
A hypothetical source story that supplies both identities itself does not make
the endpoint independent of that story.

## What frequency can do now

Use the existing whole-form profiles as obligations and nuisance checks, not
as a substitute for the missing type binding. A frequent portable content noun
owes plausible use at every retained occurrence; a rare spelling does not make
its concept rare because other realizations may exist. Currier/hand, position,
exact whole form and known entry dependencies must remain in the explanation.
No source-to-Voynich likelihood or universal seasonal upper bound exists.

For example the frozen clock lexicon assigns `qokal` a physical zodiac display
but `okal` a whole-period qualifier. Shared visible structure does not by itself
justify a type-preserving q rule that would equate their types; nor does the
structural baseline force an arbitrary rule that turns one into the other.
These exact assumptions remain reviewable in [GDT1048's frozen lexicon](../../../experiments/yolo/gdt1048_clock_prelude_frozen_values/artifacts/FROZEN_LEXICON.json)
and its [frequency critique](CLOCK_LEXICON_FREQUENCY_CRITIQUE.md). No old gloss
is adopted here. A source-frequency recount would not settle this type issue.

The immediate root decision is therefore evidence selection: accept a concrete
independently bound crossover if the parallel evidence review supplies one;
otherwise do not spend the next experiment on another formal-to-type classifier.
Explicit exploratory readings remain allowed, but their new type laws should
be finite and their distinguishing consequence should be stated before fitting.
No confirmed word, semantic type, significance or independent confirmation is
claimed by this triage.
