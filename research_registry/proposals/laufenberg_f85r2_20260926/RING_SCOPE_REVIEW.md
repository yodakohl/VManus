## Why these counts follow

With one quality, the ALL reading requires at least one absent-quality state
and at least one dominant-quality state. Each of those classes contains3of
the9states. Inclusion–exclusion gives512−64−64+8=392subsets. For both axes,
exclude4empty outer rows/columns in the3x3state grid:512−4*64+(2*8+4*16)−4*4+2
=322. The empty subset never satisfies the requirements.

Under SOME, otar demands that every outcome lacks heat, while otodar demands
that every outcome has dominant heat. Dominance implies presence; any nonempty
profile therefore contradicts that conjunction. The same proof holds for
moisture. For a singleton, ALL and SOME agree, so neither can satisfy a pair.
The source-restricted domain has4states, hence15nonempty subsets; neither
quality is absent in any of them, so even the ALL pair fails immediately.
These are logical counts, never p-values or estimated probabilities.

## Relation to the actual historical source

The inspected source says living beings are constituted from the elements,
all humans are mixed, and fire/water give warmth/moisture. If the model's
future states remain living-person states with those contributions, literal
PRESENCE of both qualities persists. Then its contrasting successful abstract
profiles are unavailable. Common membership and predominance must remain
different; changing presence to a temperature threshold or predominance would
change RAW559. The source also mentions eventual death and contrary conduct,
but it neither identifies a target regimen/horizon nor supplies the proposed
profile of absent and dominant qualities. A future explicit account could
address that question; none is supplied here. The conditional restriction is
therefore NOT a proved description of every target outcome or every historical
physiology model.

## Decision

**Defer whole-line authoring of the unchanged modal model.** Its compositional
contrast is mathematically real and ties both quality pairs together, but it
requires a historically admissible mixed profile as well as written common
regimen/recipient/horizon and asserted-polarity binding. None is identified.
This is more specific than merely missing a few dictionary entries: if the
source-faithful living-state restriction applies, the planned positive model
also loses its witness. Do not fill35words first and then quietly loosen that
restriction to manufacture the desired comparison.

The9-form derivation remains a raw hypothesis. The complete ring quartet is
exact only in ZL; IT/RF have unbound G010 variants. No full target clause was
read, no actual meaning was refuted or confirmed, and no ALL-over-SOME target
preference follows. The source-constrained RAW558 authoring continues separately.
