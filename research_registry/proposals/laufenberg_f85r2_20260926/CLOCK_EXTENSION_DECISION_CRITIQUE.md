## What IX.8.10 can add to a fixed extension

The already owned source entry is
[VITRUVIUS_IX8_8_15](../../work_batches/ten_hours_20260915/CONTEXTUAL_FULL_CONTENT_RAW_SOURCES_20260920.json).
Its complete paragraph10 distinguishes the actual Sun passing through celestial
signs from the dial's index. It gives daily index motion and varying periods,
month/day limits, and then the transition to regulating water flow. These are
concrete obligations, not a license to assign arbitrary seasonal vocabulary.

The most useful predeclared checks are:

1. **Actual Sun versus depicted index.** RAW503 fixes `yshe` to the physical
   index of a usually solar-depicted kind. That value cannot silently become
   the actual celestial Sun. If an occurrence is claimed to represent the
   actual Sun, the fixed interpretation must provide a valid composition or
   fail; a fresh sense is a counted revision, not unchanged transfer.
2. **Celestial versus apparatus ownership.** `qokal` currently denotes a
   physical zodiac display, `char` the persistent display wheel. A celestial
   trajectory and a wheel-borne zodiac must not be identified merely because
   both source clauses mention signs. An analogy is possible, but any new
   domain-transfer construction must be declared and counted.
3. **Preserve existing hour/month and motion types.** Reused `qokaiin`,
   `qokeain/otain`, `aiin` and the reference constructions must retain their
   arguments and denotations. Daily progression does not select an automatic
   actuator; varying periods already contradict the full-source equal-duration
   rival in GDT1030. Neither may be presented as a newly discovered manuscript
   result simply because paragraph10 is rendered.
4. **Retain the entire paragraph's obligations.** The water-regulation
   transition is part of the nominated source unit. A fluent solar/index
   sentence that drops that transition is not a complete extension.

There is also an order cost: the nominated target precedes RAW503's paragraph,
whereas source paragraph10 follows source paragraph9. This need not invalidate
a shared-content hypothesis, but it cannot be described as an automatically
predicted forward literal parallel. The reorder is an explicit assumption.

These checks do not require independently translated endpoint words. They
require the proposed meanings to stay fixed and expose consequences that new
vocabulary is not allowed to erase. If arbitrary new argument attachments,
new composites and unrestricted domain conversions can absorb every conflict,
fixed33 alone leaves too much freedom to alter the research decision.
