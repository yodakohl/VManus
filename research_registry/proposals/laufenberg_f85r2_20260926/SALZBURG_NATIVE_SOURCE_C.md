## Result

The source does contain a concrete joined program: seven planet pages with
separate lower life-age/hour scenes, seven personified arts with discipline-
specific activities and named masters, a wagon on which the arts cooperate
around Theology, then a cosmos and Albertus ending. The labels distinguish
these domains. The old-looking Saturn is paired with lower Infancia; that
alone blocks equating the central planet's depicted bodily age with the lower
life-stage subject. Sun and Moon each use two satellite picture slots while
their labels specify only one house; image-slot count is not entity count.

The arts provide more than co-occurrence. Grammar's retained Latin assigns
futile pursuit of arts to a person ignorant of partes. Logic's retained Latin
says teachers cultivated her sisters in vain without her. These are two
explicit negative-dependency constructions with different subject and
prerequisite expressions. The familiar inference that they describe necessary
learning resources is retained as a working interpretation; neither gives a
positive guarantee of success. Their local speaking frame can be compared
without treating every teacher, art, tool and lower master as one actor.

The visual sequence supports progressive preparation and differentiated work
on wheels, followed by cooperation at the wagon. It does not independently
prove that every page follows one literal piece of wood. Arithmetic and Music
both explicitly mention numbers/proportion, but no clause found here says
their two instances share one measured quantity. That proposed referent bridge
is not silently filled in.
