## Bounded visual findings and limits

- The source program changes organization and number: layered cosmos with four outer winged figures (50r); sixteen wind heads, eight principal plus eight collateral in the heading (51r); overlapping day/night circles without figures (53r); seven planet figures around a radiating blank disk (53v); four outward-facing seasonal figures around a blank gold disk (54v); twelve rectangular month scenes, with unequal actor counts (55r–57r); and six world-history sectors around one central winged figure (58v). No single four-role map describes the whole unit.
- Centres differ visibly: earth disk, blank wind centre, intersecting time fields, silvered radiating disk, blank gold seasonal disk, and a full central winged figure. They cannot all be called solar faces from the visible evidence.
- Visible attributes have local settings: floral headwear on the spring figure differs from two bunches held by the April-context figure; feast vessels, cauldrons and liturgical chalice are not visibly medical urinals. Tools, staffs/poles, cutting blades and instruments also differ. No physician identity or human life-age series has been established by this pass.
- White scrolling/wave-like ornament occurs on rectangular month-scene frames. This does not turn smooth zodiac or history annuli into wave bands. The seven-figure wheel also has local pale waves/cloud forms around one figure; the exact identity of that figure and the central ray count remain uncollated.
- Six ages of world history are supported by the heading and adjacent named sacred-historical narrative. They are distinct from four seasons, seven planets and twelve months, and are not evidence for a human lifespan ladder.

No RAW proposal is authored or selected. Exact small head counts in the ark, some instrument/tool identities, certain ring boundaries and several labels remain uncertain. These are observations to compare after this first-note freeze, not silently harmonized with root's withheld notes.
