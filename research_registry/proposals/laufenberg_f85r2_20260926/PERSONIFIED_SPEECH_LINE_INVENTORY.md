### Spring voice attribution and consequence

The first-person caption on 00162 explicitly says “I am called Spring,” but the next page opens with third-person `Des glenczzeite vahet an` (“Spring’s time begins”) and then `vernymm was ich dir sage` (“hear what I tell you”). The second line proves a speaking narrator addressing the reader; it does not prove that narrator is the personified season. The two-line caption and the ensuing instructional narrator must therefore remain separate unless additional textual evidence joins them. The ending on 00164 compares Spring to a young maiden and says something hidden in a person stirs during this time, whether health or illness; that is a distinct consequence about an existing human state becoming active, before the advice to act carefully.
