The crop confirms a source arrangement combining four female figures, local humoral inscriptions and a divided circular field. It also distinguishes a child-bearing figure and a plausible spinning activity from interchangeable symbolic props. It does not supply a physician/urinal, a staff-plus-hanging-strand compound, a flower or a central face. Thus it is not a stronger joint f85r2 identification and does not license transferring Flegma, Sanguis or an age to any target token.

This source is useful for **attribute discrimination**: an elongated object with a thin connection can belong to textile work; a held small outlined body can be a child. Those alternatives are supported here by connected local geometry, not a generic resemblance alone. A higher-resolution complete folio would be necessary to resolve the left implement and uncertain labels. It is not necessary merely to establish the present negative comparison.
