# Exact Cava clause search: published transcription, no second witness

Bounded source-only search, 4 October 2026. Current route, pictures topic,
existing Cava identification/native records and targeted Cava idea cards were
read. No additional Cava leaf or Voynich material was acquired.

**Result:** no independently inspected parallel manuscript with the same
multi-clause annulus and layout was found. A useful new counterreading was
located in the publisher's public sample of Vera Jerjen, *Arbeiten an Welt-
und Selbstbild im ‚Welschen Gast‘ Thomasins von Zerclære* (2019), pp12–13.
This prints all four Cava inscriptions, explicitly identifying the same Cod.3
and its cosmological florilegium; it is not a second manuscript witness.

Jerjen's transcription supports the air clause's two respective qualities.
It explicitly preserves anomalous spellings in the water and earth clauses.
For fire it gives **SICCITATEM** and **COMMVNIS NICAT**, disagreeing with our
provisional ariditatem and compound-word division. These differences are a
reason to compare the actual letters, not silently adopt the publication as
native proof. The sample discusses the co-ownership and two-line quadrant
layout but does not reproduce the diagram in its ten supplied pages.

The source tradition remains non-exclusive: Isidore's *De natura rerum* XI
provides an explicit quality-mediated elemental circuit, but not this exact
set of inscriptions. The [Cologne critical edition](https://www.uni-koeln.de/~ahz26/edition/oisnae11.htm)
has different clause wording and the conventional spring–air pairing rather
than Cava's spring–earth pairing. This is a counterexample to replacing
uncertain Cava text with a standard doctrinal table.

Additional search exposure: a 2018 forum post transcribes the same Cava air
quadrant, and an online Latin Hexaemeron transcription contains closely
related air/fire formulations. Neither supplied an independent diagram
witness; neither was used to settle native uncertainty or to claim an exact
source attribution. No fresh primary manuscript satisfies the requested
parallel-witness gate. The useful addition is an attributed scholarly
counterreading of the already acquired source, not a new bound Voynich path.

## Root consequence after source check

Root verified the retained sample PDF hash and visually checked its printed p13. COMMVNIS NICAT is present in the printed page, not merely a text-extraction artefact. The exact black-to-red word reconstruction COMMV|NICAT in our earlier addendum is therefore retained only as a disputed reading, not an established diplomatic spelling or securely identified single-word seam. No missing letters are deleted to obtain standard COMMVNICAT. The general clause-continuation observation has separate support from the red IVNGITVR and its surrounding annular clause; this does not repair the uncertain fire word.

GDT1169 had already executed and published before this counterreading was acquired. Its preregistration and observations remain untouched. The stronger source premise of a secure split word is downgraded; its target result does not change: neither reader found an explicit path among the eight inscriptions. The source correction cannot turn that lack of a bound target path into support for a new reading. This is a source-reading correction, not decipherment progress.
