## Fixed scope and smallest adequate work

Read two or three complete named historical plant/drug entries, preferentially
from cached public primary transcriptions. Cover a medical/physiological record
and an entry that actually includes morphology, if available. Distinguish an
edited text from a native page collation and an illustrated witness from a text
edition merely belonging to an illustrated tradition. Record full entry bounds,
all functional components, and actual image/text ownership evidence. No plant
species is inferred for Voynich from these controls. A small purposive set does
not establish frequencies or a universal genre template.

Independently, inspect existing native target reports only for at most three
compound signatures. Require exact admitted scope, complete owner/context,
source-backed observation, uncertainty, ordinary-convention rival and prior
experimental use. No new target images, transcriptions, pixel processing or
page admission; f84/f84r/f116v and reserves stay closed. At most two new external
source images may be inspected only if required to establish one selected
entry's relationship; old images are not new independent evidence. No contacts.

For the user's correction explicitly separate: pictured thing; entry subject;
identification/description; quality; preparation; indication/action; dose/time;
caution/correction; and nonmedical/allegorical explanation when relevant.
Presence of a leaf, colour or count does not require its verbal mention. A text
may describe identifying shape, but no drawing count becomes a numeral gloss.
Similarity in image transmission does not require identity of neighboring text.

## Decision consequences and limits

If a precise signature survives, nominate that signature alone and state which
external source-entry observation would change the reading decision. A shared
picture with unrelated text supports picture transmission only. A written
conceptual connection permits a bounded joint working hypothesis, subject to
whole contexts, existing formal constraints and whole-corpus word profiles; it
does not confirm a word. Scoring a new relation would still require its gates.

If only ordinary motifs or uncertain fragments remain, stop the shared-innovation
search before a wider witness crawl. If historical entry ownership cannot be
established, record that exact capacity gap without calling the source absent.
Do not manufacture an error, a literal caption, an invariant herbal template,
a new free dictionary, or a semantic claim from validator agreement.
