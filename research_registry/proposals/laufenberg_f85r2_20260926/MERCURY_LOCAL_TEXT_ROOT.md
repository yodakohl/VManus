## Whole content and scope

270v relates Mercury to Venus, Gemini as its house and Virgo as exaltation,
then denies power in Sagittarius and Pisces. Relative power/favor is not
a modern quantitative dignity scale. The next sentence gives goodness with
the good and badness with the bad while retaining intrinsic goodness.
Its associates and mixed-company combination law are not specified in this
short sentence. Context-dependent behavior and native character differ,
but no arbitrary finite function over all companions is defined.

Mercury's child is described through speech, mastery, self-praise, inquiry,
future/natural matters, verbal creation, reckoning and manual/visual crafts.
The continuation includes learning, retention, restlessness/travel, bodily
traits, preferences regarding women/clothing, friends/favor, and a final
attributed some-say description. That attribution is not silently given
the same evidential status as every preceding generic assertion. The craft
noun provisionally read organ remains uncertain. No physician noun, urine
diagnosis or treatment instruction was securely read in the complete
Mercury unit. Broad natural arts fit several pictured occupations; they
are less specific than an explicit urine-medicine caption.

271v begins a fresh named Moon account: distance/order, circuit/dwell times,
sign-dependent power, partly uncertain inflow/course wording. Its child
is compared with lunar instability and described through movement, speech,
temperament, health/age, occupations, bodily details and fortune.

The right-column general conclusion is a new paragraph about the whole
planetary chapter. It calls a human a planet's child under birth OR
conception and a strong eastern-rising condition. It immediately qualifies
single-planet attribution: sole power is rare; a human receives multiple
planets' properties, some from two, three or all, little or much. Naming
follows the largest share together with a birth/day/hour clause. The exact
conjunction of rising power, conception, birth and time-ruler conditions
is not collapsed into one modern natal-chart algorithm.

This is a direct written **mixed influence versus predominant naming**
argument in the same planetary program as the physician-like Mercury.
It gives no numeric weights or observed individual profiles. Its planetary
domain differs from C54's complexion domain and Laufenberg's material
elements. Child is classificatory here, not biological planetary parenthood.
