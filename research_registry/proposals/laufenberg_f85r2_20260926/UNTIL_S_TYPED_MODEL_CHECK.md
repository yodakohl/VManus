## Separate source-compatibility layer

Galen III.1 is not used to fill target gaps silently. The frozen source obligation account gives a process sequence: presentation before adhesion, adhesion before assimilation, both latter processes requiring time; it also describes continuing departure as preventing attachment/assimilation and frames attraction as directed toward presentation. It includes other obligations absent from the target draft, such as proper juice, a resident retentive faculty and the teleological inference. The relation table below keeps that comparison conditional.

| Reading | Target-only model | Compatibility if target events are mapped to the same single Galenic episode | What is needed for the comparison |

| UNTIL | Witness U exists. | Order `p < h₀ < assimilation-start < c` can satisfy the stated process order. Remaining source obligations stay omitted; this is partial compatibility, not a complete source account. |

A goal statement alone does not entail that an attraction process ceases at the exact moment of presentation. It can support a directed relation to presentation without settling the process’s precise end. Therefore the two guards do **not** create two independent source contradictions. The adhesion-order contrast is the sharper one, but it still depends on the additional same-event/source bridge. If the target events are not mapped to one source cycle, or if distinct episodes are permitted, the source does not decide the target rival. Conversely, once the grammar’s one-cycle commitment and source stage order are both stipulated, the AFTER h₀ > c ordering cannot satisfy them; this is a conditional incompatibility, not an observed target falsification.

## Exact premises that change the result

- **Both readings survive:** use only the target-authored propositions above. U satisfies them with adhesion-state end at assimilation completion. A satisfies them with adhesion beginning after assimilation completion. The missing target-only premise is an independent assertion that adhesion-onset precedes assimilation onset/completion.
- **AFTER is incompatible on adhesion:** add (i) that target h₀ and c are the same episodes as Galen’s adhesion and assimilation stages in the unique cycle, and (ii) the source order `h₀ < assimilation`. The AFTER definition supplies `c < h₀`. The three inequalities cannot all hold.
- **AFTER attraction is incompatible:** also require (iii) that the target’s a is the source attraction episode whose directed goal is p, and (iv) a no-retrocausation/order condition that such an episode cannot begin after its already completed goal. This still does not prove that UNTIL’s exact cessation boundary is source-entailing.
- **No event identity/unique episode:** if p, h₀, and c can refer to different episodes despite shared x/r, neither source incompatibility follows. That would reject assumption 2 of this authored grammar, not add a lexical value.

Under target-authored constraints alone, UNTIL and AFTER both survive. A conditional Galen compatibility comparison distinguishes AFTER only when the listed identity/stage-order premises are supplied.
