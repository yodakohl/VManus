# Decision: seek one complete honey-condition passage

## Unknown

Does a medieval regimen source give a concrete, bounded account in which the
same honey product or food kind receives different outcomes under explicit
recipient conditions such as age, cold/warm complexion, phlegmatic/bilious
constitution, or excess? The current owned Laufenberg reports establish
seasonal food advice, age contrasts, warmth instructions, and a separate
four-complexion/element argument, but the bounded searches found no honey
clause joining those facts. Their co-presence in one printed work is not
itself a honey mechanism.

The relevant RAW565 proposal separates a claimed product, recipient-conditioned
outcome, and provenance of product constituents. This source check does not
read or evaluate any whole draft/result. It asks only whether a primary
historical passage makes a useful same-product condition contrast concrete.

## Decision effects

- A complete primary passage explicitly names honey and gives both a
  favorable and unfavorable recipient/condition case, with qualifications
  intact, would support a historical source constraint for an exploratory
  recipient-conditioned reading. It would not establish any target word or
  prove a causal mechanism.
- A passage that only praises honey, gives a generic quality classification,
  or discusses unrelated remedies would not supply the contrast; retain it as
  general regimen context only.
- If the bounded source check finds no suitable primary unit, report this as
  a scoped gap, not as absence from medieval medicine or from Laufenberg.

## Smallest adequate check and limits

Start with the already owned Laufenberg and southern-German housebook reports
and receipts. Use no more than five targeted discovery queries and inspect at
most two complete primary source units (and at most two new external images).
Prefer the same Augsburg 1491 *Regimen sanitatis* if a complete honey passage
is actually located; otherwise a clearly named 15th-century regimen witness is
useful but remains a separate provenance. A source unit must include the full
bounded passage and neighboring qualifications, ingredients, recipient
conditions, and any excess/contraindication clauses. Do not promote a single
line or general Galenic authority into the required contrast.

No Voynich text/image/access, reserves, contacts, modern clinical claims, or
semantic execution are in scope. Maximum 30 minutes total, including this
decision, discovery, inspection, receipts, and report. Deliver the report and
receipts in this dossier, then stop.
