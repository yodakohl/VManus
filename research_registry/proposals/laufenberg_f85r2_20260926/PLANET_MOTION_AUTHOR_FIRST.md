an explicit ordered `Sum(QuantityExpr,QuantityExpr)` tree. This is a declared
inclusion, not an unannounced cast. No commutative rearrangement or numerical
normalization is used. `Planet` is a named planetary kind, not an individual
event, a target figure or a discourse binder. `PeriodRole` is a finite
relation constructor. `Proposition` is the resulting historical assertion.
