# IDEA608: independent decision critique

2026-09-27. Bounded design review only. No source body, semantic count, target
row, image or reserve was opened. This note does not select or execute IDEA608.

## Recommendation

Do not make a Galen-only WINTER/SUMMER census the next substantial meaning
selection experiment. It cannot calibrate a Voynich likelihood, and its expected
low-seasonality outcome would leave the existing winter decision unchanged.
The current word-selection audit already denies that freely assigned gloss any
preference. The missing source-to-target relation is not repaired by a complete
or carefully annotated source census.

A smaller, explicitly asymmetric use is legitimate: a bounded countercheck of
the intuition that an ordinary medical argument seldom expresses seasons. If
Galen unexpectedly sustains seasonal content across much of its argument, that
would weaken this intuition and prevent an overconfident frequency objection.
This is an optional source challenge, not independent translation progress. It
does not require pretending that an ordinary clinical book represents Voynich.

## A useful heuristic prior is possible

It is reasonable to prefer hypotheses that explain already known distribution,
formal relatives and constructions with fewer separately supplied contexts.
That is an explicit research judgement, not a claim of mathematical certainty.
We need not treat WINTER, a broadly used scalar, a reference-like construction
and an unrestricted polysemous device as equally promising merely because no
likelihood has been calibrated.

However, the present data justify only a relative explanatory burden. The
obligatory WINTER reading owes broadly recurring winter content wherever the
form occurs; a conditional reading owes its rule for when winter is meant.
The latter cannot keep the convenient winter examples while silently treating
every difficult occurrence as another sense. An unrestricted generic rival
does not win by flexibility: it still needs a concrete proposed meaning and a
consequence that can fail. No old number or command gloss becomes established.

The honest working judgement is therefore: the portable seasonal gloss has low
priority unless an independently motivated seasonal organization or a stated
sense-conditioning construction makes its broad distribution plausible. This
does not require a fabricated numerical prior, an invented q, or proof that no
medieval text could repeat winter frequently. The judgement is already available
from the existing audit; the Galen pilot is not its prerequisite.

## Minimum adequate endpoint and relation, if the optional pilot is selected

Use the complete authorial English text of the fixed Brock edition as one
declared observation layer. Read every native chapter in full, annotating all
authorial paragraph units while preserving chapter context. Exclude modern
prefaces, index and apparatus from the denominator. If paragraph boundaries
are editorial or unstable, record that fact and keep the native chapter
footprint separate. No chapter-to-Voynich-paragraph equation is licensed.

For each concept distinguish locally expressed seasonal reference, anaphoric
reference with an identified antecedent, continuing heading/topic scope, and
uncertainty. Cold, heat or a storm alone does not establish a season; indirect
but explicit reference may do so. Seasonal mention in a negation, conditional
or quotation still invokes the concept and should not be dropped merely because
the event is not asserted. Use the already owned Greek only to resolve relevant
translation ambiguity; retain unresolved cases. This is an endpoint for this
edition, not complete recovery of ancient seasonal expression from English.

Whole-chapter reading is the minimum semantic coverage, not a keyword scan of
candidate passages. Searching may check missed spellings but cannot establish
negative units. All uncertainty remains visible, with no guaranteed perfect
recall claim. A bounded second reading of ambiguous and prospectively selected
negative passages is adequate quality control for exploratory guidance.

On the target side, IDEA608's A-subset-C implication is conditional on a local
obligatory-concept reading and a fixed complete-unit population. It can state
the seasonal demand p without q. A source footprint is evidence about its own
units. A qualitative analogy requires a stated shared discourse function and
sensitivity to unit scale and inherited topic. If no such relation is plausible,
report the source example and target demand side by side without transferring
an incidence ceiling. A medical label alone is not a sufficient bridge.

Suggested optional cap: 60 minutes including registration, text extraction,
full reading, limited review and publication; stop incomplete if full reading
does not fit. This is a ceiling, not a claim that completion is guaranteed.
No automatic second source or infrastructure follows.

## Smallest more useful immediate alternative

Spend at most 20 minutes applying the already available word-selection evidence
to the actual seasonal draft and record a practical priority decision. Separate
three claims: obligatory WINTER at every daiin, a prospectively conditioned
winter sense, and an as-yet-uninterpreted recurring construction. Use the known
global distribution, daiin repetitions and the retained formal/constructional
counterexamples as explanation duties. Do not recount data or rebuild tools.

The current authoring record supplies no independent seasonal organization and
no conditioning rule. Therefore demote the portable WINTER assignment now;
preserve the original draft and retain the formal family as an unresolved
construction. Require the next attempted reading to state its intended scope,
one independently motivated semantic anchor and one contrasting consequence
before new free values are written. This is a concrete selection decision,
not proof of a replacement gloss and not another general method project.

If that priority decision has already been adopted, do not repeat it as a new
experiment. Move to a candidate with a supplied anchor/consequence. The optional
Galen countercheck can remain a cheap challenge to the heuristic, not the gate
that prevents all subsequent research.
