## Smallest adequate test and decisions

Apply one fixed procedure to every original source-role/whole-page case.
Preserve all original source-unknown and length-excluded cases explicitly.
For every other literal case, each locally recurrent source atom can denote
only a repeated substring of the target. Retain every substring satisfying
the full one-atom ordered projection, including minimum lengths for all
intervening atoms and the exact beginning/end when no atoms intervene.

For every adjacent pair of locally recurrent atom types in the original
source, retain both values only if they are prefix-incomparable and their
complete two-atom projection can cover the whole target with all other atoms
relaxed to positive-length spans. Repeated values remain the same everywhere;
no span is inserted between adjacent retained source atoms. Iterate support
deletions to a fixed point or the declared limit. Record each deletion and
its complete contemporaneous partner domain for replay. Local singletons and
cross-record dictionary consistency are not solved by this necessary test.

- An exhausted domain with a verified finite certificate contradicts that
  exact complete source-role/page conjunction. If every page for a required
  role is excluded, the original edition-level conjunction becomes impossible.
- Nonempty domains retain a necessary possibility, not a complete fit.
  Their explicit sets may show which codes still cannot be distinguished.
- A resource limit leaves the case computationally unresolved. No retry,
  longer solver, added atom, repaired boundary or automatic model expansion.

At the checkpoint, publish the full case table and certificates/results, then
reassess. No joint solver or additional search is included in this block.
If the procedure supplies no decisive restriction, park it; a cleaner checker
or another count of attempts is not decipherment progress.
