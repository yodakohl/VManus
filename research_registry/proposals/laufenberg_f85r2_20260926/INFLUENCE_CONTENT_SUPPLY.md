The minimal content distinction is:

```text
complexion of p -> principal inclination of p
capacity of p, through p's will -> conduct contrary to that inclination
```

It is not:

```text
complexion of p -> every action of p necessarily conforms
capacity to act against inclination -> the contrary action actually occurred
contrary conduct -> all bodily/celestial influences disappeared
```

These arrows summarize source claims and rejected overextensions; they are
not mathematical causal equations. The person, complexion, inclination,
conduct, and will must retain their roles. No fixed action-kind, time,
intervention, quantitative bodily change or historical person's identity is
introduced by this sketch. Its capability force must not become permission,
an imperative, a chosen intention or an accomplished outcome without further
written content.

## Strongest compatible rival and genuine possible consequence

**R_BODY_INFLUENCE_WITH_VOLUNTARY_CONDUCT** preserves bodily constitution and
its influences while allowing conduct to oppose the associated inclination.
For example, the source can allow a person to regulate conduct without
asserting that he ceases to possess his elemental mixture. This is compatible
with the main restricted reading, and source words about moderating nature
do not by themselves decide how much bodily change is involved.

An unrestricted rival in which complexion necessitates every relevant action
would lose the source's contrary-conduct capacity under the working reading.
But rejecting that unrestricted account does **not** reject necessity in every
other domain. A separate claim of unavoidable bodily effects could coexist
with voluntary behavior, depending on its exact object and scope. Conversely,
a reading granting unrestricted control over all outcomes overstates the
passage too.

A concrete future content consequence would be the difference between an
inclination and a completed act: a person can retain the former while a written
contrary act is possible or occurs. Inferring every action from complexion,
or inferring a change of complexion solely from one contrary action, would
then require an extra rule. **The present source does not supply two witnessed
action episodes or a controlled unchanged-constitution observation.** No new
two-case computation can therefore be presented as observed source evidence.

The stronger astronomical proposition remains unestablished in this unit.
It does not explicitly enumerate the scope of stellar compulsion, define
moral responsibility for every act, or assert a general exception-free
relationship between divine governance and human will. The earlier complete
physician speech contains stellar/elemental dangers and divine protection
through arts, but it is a different unit. Combining its premises with this
conclusion would need an openly authored cross-unit argument; adjacency alone
does not supply the missing implication.
