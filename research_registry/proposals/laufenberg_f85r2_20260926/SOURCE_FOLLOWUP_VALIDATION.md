The assertion that each of the three inspected seasonal woodcuts depicts a flower-bearing figure is supported by the native observations of scans 00162, 00165 and 00167, bound to the eight-image receipt. It is a picture claim, not a claim that all three captions mention flowers: only Spring’s caption explicitly says it is adorned with flowers; Summer’s compares the season with a beautiful woman; Autumn’s says it has fruit and loses strength.
