# Independent source critique: mediated cold paths

## Scope and evidence

This review assesses only the complete Albertus Magnus, *De anima*, II.2, cap. VIII, at the [LombardPress Reader](https://reader.lombardpress.org/text/albertusdeanima/admda-l2t2c8), lines 24–38. I read the Latin unit from its opening enumeration through its final spirit statement, alongside the already completed source report and the full RAW card. I did not inspect target records, images, or an author’s target draft. The chapter is a medieval physiological account, not current medical guidance.

## What the chapter actually connects

The chapter states an ordered set of local mechanisms, with different objects and scopes:

| Source passage | Explicit content retained in a source-compatible reading | What it does not itself establish |
|---|---|---|
| Retentive power, lines 28–30 | Retention keeps food in one place until it receives its due alteration. Cold helps *per accidens*: it holds the nutritive material in its essence and figure so heat does not dissolve it and its power does not vanish. Cold is said to help against putrefaction as well. | No quantified conserved amount, universally monotone resource variable, or successful downstream digestion. The “until” phrase is a process-specific duration, not a global chronology linking all later cold effects. |
| Expulsive power, lines 35–36 | Cold helps *per accidens* by containing the windiness in the surplus being expelled so it does not evaporate, and by thickening it; when the relevant feminine “which” is strong, it forcefully ejects surplus. Heat and moisture also aid expulsion by different mechanisms; cold and dryness contract. | No assertion that the retained nutrient of the earlier retention clause is the windiness in this expulsion clause. “Strong” is an explicit condition, not a universal outcome. The closest feminine antecedent of *quae* is *ventositas*, though *frigiditas* is grammatically possible; I keep that local reference qualified rather than turning it into a certain translation. |
| General qualification, lines 37–38 | Cold helps these powers only accidentally; cold immobilizes and mortifies. The text says spirit is the instrument and vehicle in all these powers, and cold “impels or hinders” the spirit from its motion, therefore directly obstructing the powers’ works while benefiting accidentally. | No specified spirit event, extent of obstruction, or net comparison with the preceding indirect assistance. The disjunctive *impellit vel impedit* and generic formulation should not be collapsed into a quantified negative result for a specific operation. |

The opening says the several subservient powers are reduced to attraction, digestion, expulsion, and appetite “before” the other three; retention is then treated in detail. That organization is not a transparent diagram of four coequal boxes. The chapter also says the antecedent powers work about one and the same subject, and later that spirit is the vehicle in all these powers. This supports a shared general subject/instrument frame. It does not identify one concrete food portion with a portion of expelled wind, or supply a common episode or time horizon for every mechanism.

## Strongest source-compatible rival to the abstract PATH composition

A strong rival can preserve every stated causal relation while keeping the chapter’s explanations local and operation-specific:

1. In a retention context, cold incidentally prevents heat-mediated dissolution of nutritive material and keeps it in place until the required alteration. This is an explicit local explanation of retention.
2. In an expulsion context, cold incidentally contains and thickens the windiness associated with the surplus; forceful expulsion is conditional on the relevant windiness being strong. This is a second mechanism with its own object and guard.
3. Across the powers generally, cold directly obstructs vital operation through its action on spirit’s motion, while its local retention/expulsion assistance is accidental.

This rival does not delete the nutrient-preservation relation, the contained/thickened wind and strength qualification, or the spirit-motion hindrance. It also does not claim distinct people or wholly disjoint operations: the chapter gives a same-subject statement and a common spirit vehicle. It differs from a PATH calculus by refusing to turn these prose explanations into one reusable, locally monotone algebra whose rule licenses new cases merely because an inhibition, loss, and support edge can be typed. In the chapter, the cold–retention link and the cold–expulsion link are separately qualified explanations. The wind mechanism additionally includes thickening and a strength threshold; it is not simply another instance of nutrient-like conservation.

The RAW proposal already does important work to avoid overclaim: it keeps retention and expulsion as distinct cases, states the strength guard, preserves both contributions, and denies any net sign. Its further analytical commitments remain real costs, however: five abstract sorts, typed primitive relations, same-denotation joins inside each path, and a monotonic directional composition law. Those are a useful formalization of selected source content, but they are not themselves sentences or universally asserted laws in this chapter. The rival is therefore not a historical contradiction of the candidate. It is a reminder that source fidelity alone cannot make the abstract path composition the unique reading of the source mechanism.

## What would distinguish the accounts

The source contains specific causal language: nutrient is retained against heat-induced dissolution; cold contains and thickens windiness; a strong windiness expels surplus; cold impedes the spirit’s motion and thereby obstructs operations. It also contains the explicit contrast *per accidens* versus *per se*. These facts distinguish both accounts from a bare “cold is beneficial” label.

What is not present is a single cross-clause statement identifying the nutrient-loss event, the expelled windiness, and the spirit-motion event as the same timed case; a quantitative monotonicity law; or a net result. The common subject and spirit frame are not equivalent to such event/resource identity. Nor does the source say that a later phase is required: phased interaction is a viable analytical reading, not a translated temporal claim. The candidate’s strongest differentiating content would need to be a target-side written common-path relation or a shared mediator whose exact denotation persists across the clauses, with the retained guard and scope fixed. No such target content is assessed here. Within Albertus itself, the local mechanism clauses are present, while the extra common-case/monotone calculus is underdetermined.

## Decision

Keep RAW566 as an explicit exploratory formalization with useful, source-attested local causal content. Do not treat its general PATH composition or same-case joins as entailed by Albertus. The best source-compatible rival is the operation-specific account above: it preserves each causal claim and the common subject/spirit setting, but does not posit a uniform monotone composition law or common temporal horizon. No explicit content in this chapter distinguishes that rival from the abstract calculus at that level. A future target test must therefore expose a written cross-clause identity/scope consequence, not merely re-find the three cold-related effects or call them “paths.”
