## Most specific opportunity: one relation, opposite caption perspectives

Hypothesis: two complete captions anchored at opposite endpoints describe the
**same proposition and the same participants**, while the inscription form
changes with grammatical perspective. For example, a source could explicitly
say A supplies B and B receives from A. Those paraphrases are an abstract
illustration, not a transcription of either source now under intake.

The price of the hypothesis is a finite rule tying the marked form to a stated
argument role, plus a fixed referent and scope account. It cannot just mean
"different wording is allowed." A second configuration in which the same
participant changes role should then require the corresponding marked form;
holding the form fixed would contradict that particular role-marking rule.
The proposed discrimination is role-sensitive expression versus endpoint
names, not a claim that a single suffix means a particular verb.

This is a **refinement of IDEA000050**, not a new mechanism. Its primary design
review already requires independently tracked endpoints, an actual reversed
pair, a fixed marker/frame response and a restricted symmetric rival. It
correctly notes that directional relations can keep a marker while argument
order changes, and symmetric relations can have ordering conventions. A new
source must therefore own the complete statements and the scope of their
relation; merely seeing two attached names does not supply the needed pair.

The strongest immediate rival is lexical endpoint naming. A single pair of
captions could give each endpoint its own name or local namespace. A second
role-swapped reuse is needed to distinguish that from the finite role rule.
Another rival is a genuinely symmetric predicate. Endpoint perspective must
not be mistaken for a reversed historical event or reciprocal physical flow.

No manuscript endpoint is nominated in this pass. GDT915 supplies known
whole-phrase r/l co-variation, but its mixed-ending cells contradict universal
agreement and GDT916 did not establish transfer to unseen stem combinations.
Consequently this idea may not inherit an r/l voice or case value. An eventual
target reading could posit one explicitly restricted construction, with all
its occurrences retained, but that would be an exploratory assumption.
