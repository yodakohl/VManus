## Finite additional grammar proposals

G01. A simple or compound nominal forms its declared typed term; no hidden
     subject, element, patient, season or time is supplied.
G02. A written adjective modifies the adjacent declared noun/attribute term;
     the exact attachment is frozen per clause, not freely retried.
G03. A subject and unary predicate form a clause; zero copula is allowed.
G04. A written copula ol applies a predicate to the same written subject.
G05. A written coordinator combines same-type constituents. Lists of same-type
     effects may also be asyndetic; this separate choice is counted.
G06. Binary predicates may occur between their two arguments (SVO).
G07. The specifically declared predicate-first frame takes two following
     arguments in their stated order. E.8 uses it for AIR/BREATH supply.
G08. Attribute application may be `owner attribute` or `attribute owner`.
     These are TWO extra placement licenses, separate from Q1/Q2. A Domain
     owner creates an image-domain; it does not become an individual.
G09. A head's explicit genitive marker introduces its declared argument slot.
     It cannot independently choose an arbitrary relation.
G10. A written postposed owner quantifier may bind the argument slots of the
     listed attributes in that one clause. It cannot cross a clause boundary.
G11. A relation between uninstantiated attributes is a schema-level statement.
     It does not itself introduce a universal patient or assert an individual's
     state. Any proposed pointwise source interpretation is separately costed.
G12. A proposition followed by qokal and a criterion preserves that proposition
     and appends a relevance-of-criterion claim. A nominal evaluation datum
     followed by qokal and a criterion asserts the same relevance relation.
     This is TWO typed attachment frames for one RELATIVE_TO value. Their
     source adequacy, especially W.20, is an unresolved audit obligation.
G13. A written modal scopes its listed proposition. A Domain in a modal
     possibility statement stays a domain; it does not name a particular person.
G14. A relational proposition may be nominalized as the explicitly stated
     conflict event that a following CAN_CAUSE predicate takes as its argument.
G15. Parallel predicates may share their one overt subject, including an
     asyndetic continuation. The exact sharing is listed, not inferred afresh.
G16. A source term followed by CONTRIBUTION and an effect asserts membership
     in its effect-set: FIRE CONTRIBUTION WARMTH. This does not say warmth is
     the only contribution of fire; no exhaustive equality is inserted.
G17. The distinct-predominance frame in N.5–6 compares both written domains.
     Its stronger proposed distribution, each of the four elements predominant
     in some person, is a NEW scope assumption, not a consequence of Q1/Q2.
G18. Clause-level adjuncts attach only where frozen; no implicit IF or time
     shift is introduced. Paragraphs are not presumed sentence boundaries.

## Scope rivals: the actual fixed consequence

The seed supplies NOT(forall p in Persons forall t in Treatments BENEFIT(p,t)).
It does not supply forall p forall t NOT BENEFIT(p,t), nor forall p NOT(forall t
BENEFIT(p,t)). With two people P,Q and two treatments A,B, benefit at P/A,P/B,Q/B
but not Q/A satisfies the chosen proposition and falsifies both stronger rivals.
This is a logical countermodel, not observed treatment data or a Voynich result.
Any source-level preference for the weak reading is conditional on the guessed
lexicon, source connection, frames and whole-clause completion.
