## Decision

**A single, separately registered 25-minute capacity census is defensible.**
The useful endpoint is whether either explicitly paid writer supplies any
complete four-cell candidate for subsequent whole-paragraph authorship. It is
not whether lead, tin, soldering or NOT has been identified. The two diagonal
denials are an additional source consequence, beyond a reciprocal AB/BA pair.
No logical defect makes that finite availability question uninterpretable.

The likely limitation is scientific yield: these unusually literal writers
are not established by the source or the structural baseline. A zero result
only retires these writers; it does not test the broad Megenberg connection.
Their low implementation cost, not an assumed high probability of success,
justifies one bounded screen. Do not expand it after observing the outcome.

ATOMIC14 enumerates six fixed permutations of `P,ARG1,ARG2` and two
whole-clause negator placements. All four form variables are distinct.
The 12 skeletons remain distinct even under arbitrary variable renaming.

ROLE10 has four orientation/alignment assignments for each of two negator
placements. After discarding actual form identities, its skeletons are only
`N a b N c d c b a d` and `a b N c d N c b a d`. **This does not justify
dropping six literal branches per family:** known r/l forms are constants,
and different orientations predict different literal runs. Conversely,
176 raw branches are not 176 independent forms of semantic evidence.

Freeze and generate all 22 ordered-bank entries times eight branches. The
exact frozen bank hash is part of the input contract. Preserve each branch's
actual four forms and role map, then group only identical complete literal
predictions, retaining every underlying assignment. Do not canonicalize away
literal constants or interchange a free N with an endpoint constant. Reject
an ineligible non-distinct set as an explicit schema failure; do not invent
replacement forms. The GDT915 report lists distinct X/Y forms for its 22
nominated pairs, but this review did not parse their occurrence payloads.

Within a candidate, A/B names can swap without identifying lead versus tin.
Argument positions and first/second role families are syntactic assignments;
the symmetric truth table cannot independently identify semantic direction.
Reversing arguments can yield a different surface-order branch, but it does
not distinguish J from its converse on this two-kind table. The asymmetric
soft/hard prelude is an additional future obligation, not an already solved
name binding. Overlapping candidate windows and alternate transcriptions
are not independent confirmations.

## What a surviving kernel cannot decide

- DIFFERENT, XOR, cross-kind compatibility and multiple other predicates have
  this same two-kind table. A match cannot identify soldering.
- Complementing J and swapping the marked/unmarked polarity interpretation
  preserves the observable contrast. N is not thereby translated as NOT.
- ROLE10 requires a shared relation constructor and cross-role material
  identities. GDT915 establishes neither. Separate lexical entries or a
  formula could have the same surface pattern.
- A strict asymmetric comparison cannot realize both crossed positives under
  one fixed axis. That excludes only that complete conjunction of assumptions,
  not every comparative or context-dependent reading.

These are retained alternatives, not a demand for certainty before trying a
working hypothesis. A complete, economical paragraph reading could give a
reason to prefer a hypothesis while keeping these limits explicit.

## Smallest sufficient census and outcome decisions

One pass over all admitted complete native primary P paragraphs is sufficient.
Freeze the exact paragraph eligibility rule, input hashes and 179 selectors.
Check all 12 ATOMIC14 and all 176 ROLE10 raw branches, with all four cells in
one native contiguous 14/10-group run. Preserve native positions and uncertain
groups as barriers: filtering uncertain groups out before matching would
silently create forbidden gaps. Uncertainty outside a core may remain in the
whole-paragraph record; it must not disappear from later authorship.

For every surviving occurrence retain paragraph/physical-leaf identity,
native span, branch IDs, all predicted/observed forms, every role assignment,
full paragraph remainder and same-paragraph IT state. Do not promote an
IT-only pattern into a primary ZL candidate or treat RF's absent native
paragraph fences as semantic contradiction. Candidate grouping must not
erase zero branches or failed alternate-reader observations.

Independent fixtures should reject a missing diagonal clause, one changed
polarity marker, a role-identity mismatch, an intervening group and a
paragraph-boundary crossing; positive fixtures cover both placements and
both writer families. This is adequate to validate a finite nomination
screen; a general decoder and search-wide null are unnecessary unless a
later statistical claim is proposed.

| Result | Research decision |
|---|---|
| No candidate in either family | Close both fixed writer branches. No gap, ellipsis, reader, suffix or order repair in this run. |
| At least one complete core | Permit a separately bounded whole-paragraph authorship assessment carrying every prelisted source duty and every N/P occurrence. No name or negator claim yet. |
| Many candidates | Retain all ambiguity; use only an unchanged additional source consequence to choose the next bounded assessment. No favorable fragment selection or automatic solver expansion. |
| Extraction or eligibility failure | No semantic result; record the incomplete capacity check rather than silently changing its denominator. |

The next authorship assessment is worthwhile only if it adds a shared grammar
and explicit meanings for the full paragraph, rather than assigning each
unexplained singleton whatever the source currently needs. This is the real
interpretability gate after a hit. There is no need to demand a 100% guarantee
or independent held-out verification at the nomination stage.
