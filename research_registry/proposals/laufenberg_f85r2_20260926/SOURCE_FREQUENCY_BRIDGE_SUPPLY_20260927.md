# One conditional frequency bridge, 27 September 2026

**Added IDEA000608, RAW_UNREVIEWED.** Its [proposal](ideas/semantic_unit_demand_encoding_bridge_20260927.json) replaces word-count equality with a weaker semantic obligation: if every use of a written form contains concept C, every complete unit containing the form must also contain C. A form may abbreviate several plaintext words and still obey this implication.

This is not endpoint registration, counting, experimental selection or a reopened607. Root's separate COMPLETE_SOURCE_ROSTER_DECISION concerns source availability. No new full-source body, concept count, target row or image was opened by this producer pass. Existing primary reports were read as predecessors, including their previously published results.

The candidate compares four explicit encodings:

- **One-sense whole:** every occurrence introduces C.
- **Compressed construction:** every occurrence still includes C as part of a larger meaning. Packing does not remove the incidence obligation.
- **Inflected/allomorphic family:** every declared member retains C; use the union of member-bearing units. Family membership itself must be justified.
- **Multivalent or parameterized construction:** only specified uses contain C. This is a different semantic claim with a written scope or escape budget; unrestricted choices remain non-discriminating.

Let p be the fraction of target units containing the fixed form/family. Its obligatory-concept reading requires concept incidence at least p. If an independently justified bridge supplies an upper concept incidence q for that same population, at least `max(0, 1 - q/p)` of form-bearing units must instead be concept-free under a multivalent escape. This concerns **units**, not the percentage of word tokens assigned another sense. Concept presence also differs from asserting that the concept is currently realized: negated, conditional or quoted mentions require the prospectively fixed annotation/scope policy.

The hard limitation is explicit: **three books' observed maximum is not automatically q**, and source paragraphs are not automatically equivalent to Voynich paragraphs. Without a defensible unit/genre bridge, report only the required semantic demand and the independent source observations. Do not reject a translation or invent a probability. If realistic registered coarsening, source uncertainty or encoding alternatives erase the contrast, frequency must not select a meaning.

This can change a concrete decision. A proposed portable WINTER gloss may require winter to appear in most complete units; a generic construction that only sometimes concerns winter makes a different claim. If the obligatory interpretation demands a source/encoding mismatch or more concept-free uses than its frozen allowance, it loses priority within that bridge. If source footprints overlap, or the bridge is missing, the route ends without semantic preference. No keyword classifier or additional corpus architecture follows.
