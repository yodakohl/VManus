# f13r diagnostic for the narrow `-shor` radiate-head idea (2026-09-29)

**Decision: narrow visual extension fails on one postselected, previously admitted page.** GDT1089's fixed blind inventories scored RADIATE_HEAD YES on both `tshor` first-head pages f15r and f53v. After that result, a search of the GDT1070 first-head roster found f13r's `torshor` (GDT791 f13r.1 confirms the exact ZL first group). If the visible terminal `-shor` by itself meant a radiate flower head in this first-head context, f13r would be a natural positive prediction. This is an exploratory test selected after seeing the two `tshor` drawings, not a registered population test or an independent holdout.

The official Yale f13r image was already admitted through GDT791; the project had prior image exposure. Two original GDT1089 image readers were separately given only the f13r image and the **unchanged six-code rubric**, without the head, other text or the proposed component. This undercuts only the **specific** radiate-disc extrapolation. It does not test a broader inflorescence reading: all three pages can have multi-unit heads, and the first-group `tor-` may matter. There is no image pointer proving that the first written group names the drawn reproductive part, and `shor` also occurs away from the first head. No component or word meaning follows. We do not newly admit f93r or revise GDT1089's registered result. A further image-only head-ending hunt would be another adaptive selection with the same ownership problem; require an independently owned contrast before promotion.
