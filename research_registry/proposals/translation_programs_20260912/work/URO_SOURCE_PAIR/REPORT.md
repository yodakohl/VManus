## Actual consequence and limits

The visible second heading supports a scoped reading: **ring description as previously given; newly specified liquid colour and case description.** This rules out reading the second page as lacking any ring instruction. It also does not mean that every field on the first page is repeated. This is a concrete historical example of selective carry-forward, not proof of the same mechanism in Voynich.

The selected pair varies liquid description while retaining the ring description. The proposal instead needs different ring conditions with fixed liquid to discriminate its competing diagnosis models. That capacity remains absent in this pair. Nor does changed diagnostic wording isolate the cause of a changed diagnosis: the full conditions have not been transcribed, and the complaints may overlap. A generic liquid-colour diagnostic model can still fit these observations. No causal, significant or unique inference follows.

**Decision:** retain IDEA143 as source-only, now with a concrete selective carry-forward example; do not build or transfer a diagnostic decoder. No Voynich vessel, ring owner, equivalent heading or conditional phrase has been selected. No translated Voynich word, new page admission or independent semantic confirmation. The next potentially useful manuscript question is whether a short repeated expression replaces one identifiable field across related entries while other fields vary. That is a proposal for duplicate review and an explicit discovery scope, not an executed finding or an instruction to assign any frequent word to “as before”.
