## Concrete competing readings

1. **Repeat:** `X, again X`. The later absence of X is compatible, but does not identify an action or establish how many times it occurs.
2. **Identity/coordination:** `X, the same X` or `X and X`. The later absence is equally compatible. Written type equality does not establish referent identity.
3. **Construction-level closing hypothesis (post hoc):** the entire `X marker X` construction ends the current passage about X while text about other forms continues. This fits the four symmetric cases as a literal no-later-X description. “Passage about X” remains an interpretation; no topic or treatment has been independently identified.
