## Research decision

Replace the five-example evidential picture with this complete cached inventory. Retain the four shared symmetric sites and the qokain … ar comparison as concrete material for a future reading contract; do not promote the old three-form SAME alias. The stronger claim that there is only one available symmetric case is not supported by the broader scope (the preceding audit correctly said one among its five selections).

A next semantic test would have to distinguish the roles of the middle form using consequences elsewhere in the complete passages. Merely expanding X dar X to “X again X” or drawing equality edges supplies no falsifier. No such semantic test is selected here. This census stops at its declared scope; no longer/fuzzier window search or change to GDT928 follows automatically. Durham's explicit inherited-field heading remains a source example without a matched Voynich heading.
