## A definite old-value substitution: dal

The frozen dal is **BY_OBSERVATION_IN**: it marks the time region used as the
basis of a judgment. LEXICON retains that definition. Nevertheless ACCOUNT
f83r.41 assigns its surface value **EXCLUDING_PES** and writes:

`CONCERNING(EXCLUDING_PES(PUBLIC_MANY(O)), KNOWS(O,OCCURS(SECOND(A))))`

READING then calls this the inherited “Ausschlussbedingung”. No such inherited
unweaving meaning exists. This is an actual conflict between the frozen entry
and its operational use, not merely a missing argument or an unconfirmed gloss.
The new form kain is EXCEPT elsewhere; it does not authorize replacing dal.
The old music-source context must not supply active predicates in this family.

SOURCE.reader_scope also retains music-specific commentary such as PES and
PREDECESSOR/ENTER/FOLLOWER. Those strings describe the prior rival source
account, not the present unweaving meanings. Retaining historical countercases
is appropriate if labelled; using them as current meanings is not. The dal
formula shows that this distinction matters materially here.

## The two shey clauses do not yet preserve the old operator

Old shey is “Negate the following epistemic condition in the current phase,
not the occurrence of its action content.” In the earlier complete clause it
precedes lchedy and the selected phase is E. V2 instead makes its production
itself read `NOT_KNOWS(subject,OCCURS(SECOND(A)),time=E)`, while all14 P5 rows
have `scope=AFTER_COMPLETION`. Positive lchedy defaults to the later context;
negative shey is sent to E by word identity/polarity.

This is not the required written, shared scope construction. A late report
about an earlier epistemic state is possible in principle, with distinct report
time and content time, but the rule deriving those two times must be supplied.
It cannot follow solely from which polarity word occurs.

The actual neighboring new values do not currently supply that rule:

- In .36, shy is ONE, lshedy is EARLIER_GROUP, aiin is JOINTLY, and qoky is W.
  ONE selects a participant; an earlier group is not an operator selecting
  earlier time E. The formula drops the group reference as an explicit operand
  and inserts both KNOWS-content and E. After shey the text has group/person
  terms, not the frozen following epistemic condition.
- In .39, kain is EXCEPT and chckhal is COMPLETION_BOUNDARY. An exception to a
  supplied case and a boundary do not establish the entire earlier phase E.
  The formula adds NOT_KNOWS-content and E; the prose further introduces “bis”
  although no UNTIL value is written in this row. The preceding qokain supplies
  an OTHER_PARTICIPANT reference, not the missing epistemic predicate.

No common derivation connects both rows to E while retaining the old
current-phase/following-condition function. The inherited explicit earlier-
period word otaiin and recall operator tchedy are not written in this block.
They cannot be supplied silently from the earlier paragraphs.

This diagnosis is not a proof of a whole semantic contradiction. In particular,
the .36 negative concerns W, whereas the positive formulas mostly concern O;
.39 selects an unspecified other member of O. Collective knowledge, each
member's knowledge, and the worker's knowledge are different claims. Without
the missing individual, quantifier and time bindings, one cannot declare that
the same person both knows and does not know at the same time. Nor does the
earlier model include a general axiom that every actor knows every occurrence
of their own activity.

## Knowledge mentioned is not automatically knowledge asserted

The new clauses themselves distinguish several scopes which the final
semantic-state summary collapses. In .32 the knowledge proposition is inside
RECORDED/REQUEST_CONCERNING; in .37 it is REQUIRED; in .41 it is CONCERNING.
A recorded request, requirement or topic does not by itself assert its content.
The old construction explicitly preserved that distinction for FINISHED.
Writing lchedy four times therefore does not establish four positive knowledge
assertions. The .34 PUBLICLY/ALL construction could propose an actual knowledge
claim, but its assertedness and collective-versus-distributive argument need
definition. The inherited discovery already supplied a later knowledge fact;
repeating its description is not independent support.

The old lkedy definition also requires the **preceding** unchanged state
predicate to be placed under a requirement. In .37 `sol lkedy lchedy ...`,
the supplied formula makes lkedy govern the **following** KNOWS expression.
That does not demonstrate preservation of its frozen argument direction.
Likewise .31 ldy is a state-typing operator on a preceding state expression;
`RECORD(FOUR_CONDITIONS,COMPELLED,STATE(C))` does not identify the preceding
state predicate to which that operator applies.

## Four argument lists are not yet four complete productions

P1–P4 name argument lists and broad prose operations. No terminal/type-matching
rules derive those arguments from the actual words, specify omitted operands,
or control nesting, argument permutation and scope. The claimed order
`polarity, subject, proposition, time`, for example, is not mapped to either
negative row or the positive knowledge rows. “P1 + P2 + P4” is a list of
intended operations, not a derivation tree. P5 rows use `P2_EPISTEMIC`, while
the declared identifier is `P2_EPISTEMIC_STATE`; even the name correspondence
is implicit.

Concrete remaining obligations include:

| .32 | The explicit cloth operand required by old REQUEST_CONCERNING; a rule consuming its knowledge/content terms |

In .42 asserting ATTAIN in a new phase and calling it “the same completion”
does not yet define one shared event identity. In .43 an attestation before
completion need not be a true completion claim; neither factivity nor the
opposite is licensed silently. An old ONSET/WHEN construction exists but is
not written here or explicitly instantiated by the new rules. Thus these are
real missing content relations, not a demonstrated temporal impossibility.

There are four production **names** and six binding **names**. Actual rule and
binding costs remain uncertified while these argument choices are unresolved.
The six B entries primarily identify C/W/O/discovery/completion and choose a
global scope; they do not explicitly bind the companion, other member, four
conditions, report collection, retrospective frames and new attainment event.
Some might be ordinary consequences of a complete reusable grammar. None
should be counted as an arbitrary extra by fiat, but the present names alone
do not certify the four/six caps. Compound or synonymous new whole values are
not automatically forbidden sentence macros; missing clauses cannot, however,
be credited to their labels without a compositional rule.
