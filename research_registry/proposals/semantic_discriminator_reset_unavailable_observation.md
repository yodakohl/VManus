## One useful discriminator, stated narrowly

The potentially useful observation is a text-independent **crossed referent square**: four unequivocally owned inscriptions on depicted objects in which two independently recognizable visible attributes occur as A1B1, A1B2, A2B1 and A2B2. Attributes need not have botanical names; reproducible visible equivalence is sufficient. Ownership must not be assigned by the inscription we want to explain. The prospective rule would be a specific, frozen factorization of those inscriptions into two reusable written operations associated with the two visible changes. It is not a claim that all lexical systems, names, or ciphers must factorize.

The distinguishing prediction is the missing fourth inscription from the other three under the frozen operation composition. A second, prospectively excluded square with different complete carrier forms is needed to distinguish reusable operations from an interpolation tailored to four labels. The operations, order and admissible segmentation must be fixed before the held inscription is exposed. Failure falsifies that particular factorization, not composition or language generally. Success would justify treating the operations as referent-associated transformations for further held testing, not naming a morpheme or translating the carriers.

This needs neither HOT/COLD gold nor assumed invariance of all neighbor distributions. It does need independently observed changes in what is depicted and unambiguous ownership. Semantic scoring on new ordered relation evidence would additionally require the existing relation gates; the local visual observation itself should not be rejected merely for lacking fifty edges.

## Why I am not recommending implementation now

This observation is **not new**: the internal-parallel-passages proposal explicitly mentions crossed reuse of drawn plant parts and says no sufficiently unambiguous text-independent four-object packet has been established. I found no such packet in the bounded primary checks below. That is an availability limit of this audit, not a census proving no square exists anywhere in the admitted images.

GDT351 supplies four concrete near-misses, rather than a generic missing-gold objection: three alleged repeated referents have no separately mapped plant label; the fourth inscription lies between two plants and has no singular ownership. The source does not license repairing this by proximity. PHF001 separately reconstructs 17 stable complete labels with no exact repeated type; its counting dataset cannot simply be advertised as a ready repeated-label square. The container/plant capacity validator likewise establishes a stop, not a decoded owner-class distinction.

The alternative text-only route cannot silently substitute for this missing observation. GDT838 found zero matching cross-folio ordered equality patterns among its 14 eligible 16-group windows and explicitly stopped that fixed recoding screen. This is not a universal impossibility theorem, but shorter or relaxed windows would be a new design, not an available held pair.

The tempting source anomaly at GDT747 f104v.23 (tchey, qokchey, qochey) also does not supply independently known attribute changes. Its HOT/COLD/DRY interpretations were inherited complete-card values. GDT749 already removed discovery frames, documented poor calibration of the global role classifier (23 true, 42 false, 60 missed), and left qochey contested. A different distribution score on the same serial example would not turn that example into a visual attribute intervention.

## Decision and smallest adequate next action

No decoder, new corpus, new null ensemble, label classifier, or image-admission work is warranted on this candidate now. Do not create a rerun merely to report another missing binding. Reconsider only if an existing source audit or a separately authorized native observation actually supplies a named four-object packet with explicit ownership and independently recorded crossed attributes. More pages, an old evidence file, or a matching reopening keyword is insufficient.

If such a packet is produced, the first budget should be **ten minutes for source ownership and attribute-independence adjudication only**, before any text matching. PASS changes the decision from unavailable observation to a concrete factorization proposal requiring preregistration; FAIL preserves the source-level reason and ends that candidate. No full scientific time budget is proposed without the necessary observation because implementation scope is not yet concrete.
