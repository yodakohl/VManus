# GDT033 Herbal-B additional-field content

GDT033 asks what the additional DY-delimited fields isolated by GDT031/032
contain. It uses the frozen f84r-free GDT016 source-native inventory and
existing human page descriptions only. No new image observation is made.

An `additional field` is a DY-closed field after the first DY-closed field on
the same physical line. Hosts are GDT016 residual hosts after its frozen
renderer-prefix and optional DY-closure analysis. Candidate hosts must occur
in at least five additional fields on at least four pages and have at least
two surface-token or stripped-prefix realizations. Rates are fields containing
the host divided by all additional fields on that page.

The human feature library is frozen as sixteen transparent rules over the
catalogue's `illustrations` or `text_description` fields: exact Gheuens/
Rapaport tags `daisy in a cup`, broad many-fingered calyx, grass, root
platform, leaves pointing to one side, and parallel/fused leaves; one declared
union `FLOWER_HEAD_ARCHITECTURE`; literal-description rules for bulb/tuber,
large/extensive root, multiple plants, blue flower/bud, strictly phrased
fingered/frilled leaves, and multiple stems/stalks; and three layout rules for
split/interrupted text, text avoiding the drawing, and text above the plant.
The emitted manifest retains the exact rule, source URL, source-field hash,
and positive human description.

For each host-feature pair, the statistic is the difference in mean page host
rate. Ten thousand deterministic permutations move complete visual-feature
vectors only among pages with the same hand and broad illustration profile.
Local two-sided and maxT-across-library p-values are descriptive discovery
controls. Whole-physical-folio deletions, a full-Herbal-B occurrence
sensitivity, construction diversity, and all existing non-f84 exact human
label annotations are reported. The label atlas supplies no exact owned
Herbal-B plant-label case, so labels can contradict or weakly contextualize a
lead but cannot validate it.

Semantic-core prioritization is explicitly abductive and postselected. It
prefers a non-closure host with at least three realizations, positive
leave-one-folio effects, at least four feature-associated folios, and at least
60% page precision. Any provisional gloss is a risky functional hypothesis,
not a word, morpheme, POS, sound, language, plaintext, or translation. f84r is
not retained, joined, opened, or scored.
