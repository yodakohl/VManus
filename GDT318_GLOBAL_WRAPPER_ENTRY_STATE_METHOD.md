# GDT318 — global wrapper entry-state compression

Use every f84-free Voynich opaque
`PAGE_HOST × frame × inner-D × right × DY × B3` cell with at least ten events,
three physical folios, and two observed wrapper classes. Selection is not tied
to `s` or `q`. The frozen panel contains 126 cells, 5,607 events, and 91
physical folios. Wrapper truth is withheld.

The fixed eight-way class inventory is `NONE, ch, che, d, q, s, sh, t`.
In leave-one-physical-folio-out folds compare an exact-cell Jeffreys prior
against shared multinomial multiplicative selectors for:

1. physical line start;
2. immediately preceding physical-group DY;
3. both coordinates together.

Cell counts, selector coefficients, and standardization are learned inside
each training fold. Use Dirichlet-1/2 cell counts and ridge 10 on shared
selector coefficients. Report total held log loss, gains by section and
wrapper class, fold coefficient directions, and an 8,192-world fixed-crossfit
cell/register max-three alignment diagnostic. The diagnostic is not an exact
retrained null.

Call `GLOBAL_WRAPPER_ENTRY_STATE_TRANSFERS` only when the joint model has
positive held gain after a two-bit four-model selector charge, at least two of
B/H/S contribute positive gain, at least 75/91 folds have positive
`s × LINE_START` and `q × PREV_DY` coefficients, and max-three diagnostic
p is at most .05.

This can establish only a shared formal rendering state machine after an
opaque cell is already known. It cannot predict unseen cell licenses or assign
a prefix, morpheme, POS, meaning, sound, language, plaintext, or translation.
No f84 row may be opened, parsed, retained, joined, or scored.
