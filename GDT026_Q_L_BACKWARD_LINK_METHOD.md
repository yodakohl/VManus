# GDT026 Currier-B Q/L backward-link state

GDT026 tests whether the QJB/QKB versus LJB/LKB contrast in Currier-B
DY-resolution groups describes entry into a checkpoint chain, continuation
through it, exit from it, or merely local repetition.

The universe is the 1,421 frozen Q/L subtype groups in the f84r-free GDT016
inventory.  Q-family is compared with L-family after fixing page, current
anonymous state, and normalized line-position quartile.  Five contexts are
tested: previous group is DY, next group is DY, line start, line end, and chain
entry (not previous DY but next DY).  A five-test adjustment is reported.

The previous-DY test is repeated after removing exact-token repetitions,
exact-family repetitions, and identical residual-host repetitions across the
boundary.  These ablations distinguish a backward state flag from literal
copying.

The test identifies formal direction only.  “Backward link” means dependence
on the immediately preceding anonymous checkpoint; it is not a linguistic
tense, agreement, conjunction, or semantic continuation marker.  No f84r row,
image, or payload is opened.
