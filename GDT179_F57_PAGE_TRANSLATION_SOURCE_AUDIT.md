# GDT179 external-source audit

## Frozen witness

The external comparator is Walters MS W.73, *Cosmography*, made in England in
the late twelfth century.  The Digital Walters catalogue describes f.7v as a
microcosmic–macrocosmic harmony diagram relating world, man, and year.

Its official description explicitly gives the clockwise phase beginning at
the top: Fire, Air, Water, Earth.  It also supplies the paired primary
qualities and the corresponding seasons and bodily humours.  These statements
are represented in `gdt179_w73_comparator_manifest.tsv`; the source URL and
retrieved-byte SHA-256 are bound in `gdt179_source_provenance.json`.

Primary source record:

`https://t.thedigitalwalters.org/Data/WaltersManuscripts/html/W73/description.html`

## What the source supports

- a historically real four-element wheel;
- an authorially described top-and-clockwise phase;
- the standard element/quality incidence square;
- linked season and humour registers in that witness.

## What it does not support

W.73 is not identified as the Voynich exemplar.  It does not contain Voynich
script, the f57 four-person centre, the same circular-band inventory, or an
owned mapping from the eight f57 inscriptions to readable values.  The source
therefore fixes a comparator architecture only.  It licenses no Voynich word,
sound, language, authorship, direct-copy claim, plaintext, or translation.
