# GDT029 host-licensed Q/L construction grammar

GDT029 asks whether the GDT026 previous-DY association predicts QJB/QKB versus
LJB/LKB after the residual host is known. The complete universe is the 1,633
Currier-B Q/L groups in the frozen, f84r-free GDT016 inventory.

Every model is trained without one physical folio and scores that whole unseen
folio. A stronger sensitivity withholds every occurrence of one residual host.
Dirichlet-1/2 multinomial naive Bayes uses only these frozen feature sets:

- prior;
- anonymous state plus position quartile;
- that context plus previous-DY;
- context plus exact residual host, with or without previous-DY;
- context plus padded residual-host character 1--3-grams, with or without
  previous-DY;
- the latter plus previous-DY × host-gram interactions.

The primary contrast is host-gram versus host-gram+history on held folios.
Its forty paired folio gains receive a deterministic 200,000-world sign-flip
test and folio bootstrap interval. Exact-host models are the whole-form
frequency control; host-held folds test generalization beyond memorized hosts.

This is a comparison between two formal representations of the same source
groups, not independent linguistic evidence. f84r is excluded upstream and is
not opened, retained, joined, or scored. No meaning, morpheme, sound, language,
plaintext, or translation is assigned.
