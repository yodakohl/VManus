# GDT092 — HPR2 generative-theory synthesis

This checkpoint synthesizes GDT056–GDT091 without opening a new endpoint.  It
compares three abductive models—compressed natural language, pure technical
notation, and a hybrid page-conditioned content lexicon plus record compiler—
against the bound experiment results.  It must select one leading theory.

The component inventory records formal evidence, counterevidence, and the
maximum permissible latent function.  Novel predictions target only future
non-f84r data or source-only held structures.  No component receives a sound,
historical-language identity, POS, translation, or English lexical gloss.
f84r remains entirely sealed and receives no prediction in this checkpoint.
