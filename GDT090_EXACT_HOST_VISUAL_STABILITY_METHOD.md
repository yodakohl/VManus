# GDT090 — exact-host visual-description stability

Use the unchanged GDT089 panel and eleven published neutral descriptor
patterns.  Enumerate every exact PAGE_HOST occurring on two different
physical folios.  Compare the positive-descriptor Jaccard similarity of each
same-host pair with cross-folio pairs matched to the target descriptor-vector
size (within one positive descriptor).  The anchor locus stays fixed.

The primary statistic is mean Jaccard across all eligible exact-host pairs;
50,000 deterministic matched worlds give its one-sided probability.  Each
pair also receives an inclusive enumerated local rank.  This tests broad
descriptor-vector stability, not the separately reported single descriptor
associations.  It is exploratory and postselected.  f84r remains absent.
