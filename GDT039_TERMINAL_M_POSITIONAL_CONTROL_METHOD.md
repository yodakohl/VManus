# GDT039 — terminal-M control for the DAM field-role lead

## Question

Does GDT038's provisional DAM final-open-field pattern transfer to related
hosts on entirely different physical folios, and is that transfer specific to
DAM/AM rather than the broader source-native terminal-`m` positional system?

This is a formal attribution test. It assigns no function or meaning.

## Frozen split and population

The five physical folios containing exact GDT038 residual-host DAM are the
discovery set: `f39`, `f46`, `f95`, `f106`, and `f112`. Every primary score
excludes those folios. The held pool is all remaining Herbal Currier-B and
Currier-B Stars/Recipe strict-consensus prose in GDT016, with f84r excluded.

Only lines whose retained group indices exactly exhaust source-native
`group_count` are eligible. This prevents a missing alternative-bearing group
from creating a false line end or field boundary.

## Declared families and outcomes

The nested families are terminal `m`, terminal `am`, terminal `dam`, exact
residual host `am`, `d|am`, and carrier-wrapper (`ch/che/sh`)+`am`.

Three positional outcomes are scored:

- membership in the final open field after the last `DY_RESOLUTION`;
- physical line end;
- physical line end when the final group is not a DY closure.

For each test, family membership is exchangeable only among positions on the
same complete physical line. The exact null is the convolution of each line's
hypergeometric distribution, preserving line length, family count, and the
number of outcome positions. Local and 18-test Bonferroni values are retained.
Leave-one-physical-folio-out minimum rates/effects and separate Herbal-B/S
rates are reported.

Specificity is tested by repeating the same exact calculation for terminal
`am` within terminal `m`, terminal `dam` within terminal `am`, and exact `am`
within terminal `am`. A nested family must outperform its parent to support a
DAM/AM-specific field role.

## Decision

The DAM lead is attributed to the broader terminal-M positional system if:

1. terminal-M placement transfers on the held folios;
2. terminal-AM and terminal-DAM have no positive exact enrichment within
   their parent terminal family; and
3. the direction survives deletion of every held target-positive folio.

This can reject a DAM-specific positional role without rejecting DAM as a
reusable formal host. No word, morpheme, POS, sound, language, plaintext,
meaning, or translation follows. f84r remains sealed.
