# GDT191 — context-keyed PAGE_HOST word-codebooks

## Question

GDT190 rejects one global mapping from frequent PAGE_HOST identities to
frequent historical-language words. Could the mapping instead change with a
known manuscript context, as the page-conditioned compiler theory permits?

## Frozen family

Use exactly five partitions: `GLOBAL`, `CURRIER`, `SECTION`, `HAND`, and
`PHYSICAL_FOLIO`. Within every partition stratum, select its deterministic
eight most frequent non-f84 PAGE_HOST identities (or all identities when fewer
than eight exist), and bijectively map them to the equally sized most frequent
words of one of the six frozen GDT001 historical-language packs.

Physical lines preserve order. Unselected hosts reset the mapped word run.
Each stratum receives an independently paid `log2(k!)` mapping. Language is
common across strata and pays `log2(6)`. The five-way partition selector is
common to candidate and null. The matched null is an independently integrated
order-1 KT identity channel in every identical stratum, over exactly the same
runs and events. Three deterministic best-pair-swap descents are retained.

The route passes only if a paid partitioned codebook beats its matched null and
the complete collection of stratum mappings is identical across all three
starts. Finer record keys, nonbijective expansion, and phrase codes are outside
this test. Optimized target words are model states, not readings. Every `f84*`
row is rejected before formal parsing or retention.
