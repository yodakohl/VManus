# GDT009 abductive semantic bootstrap

Status: `YOLO_POSTHOC_MEANING_SEARCH`

## Question

Given the selected HPR-1 hybrid procedural-register architecture, what is the
most coherent *provisional functional reading* of its recurrent pieces?  This
pass is deliberately abductive.  It searches for a theory that explains many
facts at once; it is not a confirmation test and it does not require a lexical
meaning to be independently identifiable before the theory may be stated.

## Evidence and separation of claims

The builder uses only already published, non-holdout artifacts:

- source-native GDT002 module occurrences, minimal pairs, split/join evidence,
  and human-annotation joins;
- the frozen structural grammar;
- GDT003 nested held-folio results and strong string baselines;
- the selected GDT008 HPR-1 architecture.

Every row is typed as `OBSERVATION`, `ABDUCTIVE_INFERENCE`,
`PROVISIONAL_FUNCTION`, `SPECULATIVE_GLOSS`, or `COUNTEREXAMPLE`.  Display
strings are transcription displays, not sound values.  ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b
are alternate readings of one object.

## Search procedure

1. Reconstruct position, free/bound reuse, label density, reading stability,
   and human-annotation coverage for AR, OL, DAL, DAR, SY, TE, TEE, and DY.
2. Compare five explicit joint semantic worlds against twelve observations.
   Fit values are transparent post-hoc 0/0.5/1 judgments with stated weights
   and a complexity penalty.  They are not probabilities.
3. Choose one world.  Assign each recurrent unit a concrete functional class
   and an English mnemonic while retaining counterparses and counterexamples.
4. Parse real loci and freeze risky non-f84 predictions that could distinguish
   the leading world from its rivals.

The search is joint: a weak DAL clue is not killed in isolation if DAL, DAR,
DY, q, che, line reset, and the boundary renderer together form a coherent
record system.  Conversely, direct contradictions such as plant-label AROL
against `AROL = water` remain fatal to that narrow gloss.

## Holdout

f84r remains sealed.  This pass neither reads nor joins any f84r formal row and
creates no f84r parse.  The prior frozen GDT008 f84r predictions remain
untouched.

## Claim ceiling

The result is a deliberately speculative functional interpretation.  It may
say that a form behaves *as if* it marks scope, reference, state, or closure.
It does not establish a language, sound, morpheme, POS, dictionary word,
plaintext, translation, authorship, date, or origin.
