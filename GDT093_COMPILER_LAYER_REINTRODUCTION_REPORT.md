# GDT093 — compiler-layer reintroduction

## Outcome

**COMPILER_LAYERS_DILUTE_PAGE_HOST_DARK_LEAF_SIGNAL**

For the GDT089 `DARK_LEAF` endpoint, PAGE_HOST alone gains
+4.342 held bits.  Adding WRAPPER changes this
to -2.125; adding RIGHT_FAMILY to
-3.400; adding B3 to
-2.567; and adding WRAPPER+RIGHT to
-1.946.  PAGE_HOST is the best of
the five variants on 5/8 descriptor axes.

The PAGE_HOST DARK_LEAF cell retains a local permutation p of
0.0228 but fails the expanded 40-cell
max search at p=0.3635.  Compiler layers
therefore dilute this specific archived content signal; they are not thereby
proved semantically neutral.  The result supports the HPR2 generation order
in which content-host identity precedes wrapper/right/B3 rendering.  f84r was
absent.
