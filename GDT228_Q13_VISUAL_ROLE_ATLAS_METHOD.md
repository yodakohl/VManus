# GDT228 — q13 visual geometry to abstract-role atlas

## Purpose and postselection

GDT228 explores whether two page-level geometric facts already stated by the
public human catalogue align with the GDT227 abstract interlinear.  It was
selected after GDT227 was exposed, and both tried axes were inspected in a
scratch calculation before this method was written.  It is therefore an
explicitly postselected hypothesis-generation atlas, not confirmation.

The two fixed normalized visual axes are:

1. `MULTIPLE_BOUNDED_REGIONS`: the catalogue explicitly describes at least
   two pools, sub-pools, or separate bounded water regions on the page;
2. `EXPLICIT_LINEAR_PATH`: the catalogue explicitly says that a tube or drawn
   linear structure connects, enters, leaves, ends at, or leads to another
   unit.  Mere presence of a tube is negative.

The 18 retained q13 pages are f75r through f83v.  Every f84 page is absent from
the manifest and rejected before retention.  The visual facts are normalized
`EXISTING_HUMAN_ANNOTATION`, not new image judgments.

For each axis, compare page-weighted fractions of instruction-clause-like,
short-argument-like, and closer-like fields.  Report exact unrestricted page
label permutations, exact within-physical-folio swaps over discordant page
pairs, leave-one-folio directions, and a deterministic max-six diagnostic over
both axes and three endpoints.

No role name is literal Voynich semantics.  A positive result can nominate a
page-level content/organization hypothesis only.  It cannot assign any field,
host, group, pool, path, action, material, word, or translation.
