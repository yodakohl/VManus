# GDT229 — provisional q13 semantic-role lattice

## Outcome

**`PROVISIONAL_Q13_SEMANTIC_LATTICE_BUILT_NO_LEXICAL_KEY`.**

The branch now has a field-by-field working semantic scaffold rather than only
an abstract grammar.  The leading abductive world is a hybrid
therapeutic/hydraulic practical record: longer fields carry description,
indication, or procedure-like material; short fields carry component,
material, case, quantity, or local-state values; sparse edge fields carry
identity/setting or closure functions.  These are broad alternatives, not
translations.

## Complete interlinear

The public lattice covers all **701 fields in 33 mechanical records on 18
non-f84 q13 pages**.  Its leading candidate bundles are:

| leading bundle | fields |
|---|---:|
| material/case/quantity argument | 272 |
| component/parameter/local state | 155 |
| setting/hydraulic description | 148 |
| practical description/indication | 105 |
| caution/outcome/formal close | 17 |
| identity/location/access header | 4 |

The split between the first two short-argument labels is intentionally a
page-level hypothesis: 155 short fields occur on pages with several bounded
regions and are provisionally promoted to component/parameter/local-state
candidates; the remaining 272 retain the broader material/case/quantity
bundle.  Likewise, 148 long fields on pages with an explicit path are promoted
to setting/hydraulic-description candidates.  No field is assigned to a
particular visible object.

means a component, material, state, or anything else.

## Why this world leads

The ranking combines four already published observations:

1. readable bath records consistently contain identity and indication layers,
   often hydraulic description and procedure/caution;
2. readable bath imagery weakly exposes setting/access and hydraulic systems,
   but not indications, procedures, or outcomes;
3. q13 has a readable-recipe-like aggregate clause/argument balance and is
   especially close to Stars-B at that coarse level;
4. multi-region q13 pages weakly favor short argument-like fields.

Together these make the hybrid world more economical than either a pure
indication list or a pure apparatus key.  The nonsemantic record-renderer
world remains mandatory because no exact PAGE_HOST dictionary transfers and
the q13 closing system is unstable.

## Awkward facts

- The visual modifiers are page-level, not field ownership.
- The multi-region result was postselected and has max-six p=.386.
- The explicit-path role-composition test itself is null; its hydraulic use
  here comes only from the external readable-manuscript calibration.
- CoReMA position/length features confuse ingredient and tool roles and do not
  demonstrate Voynich semantics.
- Exact q13 PAGE_HOST placement is locally stable, but q13-to-Stars exact-host
  role transfer is negative.
- Only one existing q13 label has connected-component evidence; the remaining
  linked loci are mostly proximity-only.

## Next discriminators

The lattice makes three concrete acquisition predictions:

1. newly acquired pages with several independently bounded regions should
   contain a larger share of short fields than comparable one-region pages;
2. a readable q13-like hydraulic diagram should concentrate component/state
   labels in short constructions while retaining longer setting or procedure
   material in prose;
3. a source-bound repeated component or state should preserve its broad field
   bundle across pages even if the exact PAGE_HOST changes.

These predictions can now fail without rewriting the lattice.

## Claim ceiling

The strongest current meaning hypothesis is document-level: q13 may encode
therapeutic/hydraulic practical records whose long and short fields distribute
description/indication/procedure versus component/material/case/state values.
No individual field, PAGE_HOST, source group, figure, pool, material, action,
condition, word, language, plaintext clause, or translation is established.
No f84 row was retained, joined, or scored, and no new f84 access occurred.
