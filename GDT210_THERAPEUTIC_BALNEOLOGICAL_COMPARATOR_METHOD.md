# GDT210 — therapeutic balneological comparator

## Purpose

Compare the frozen, non-f84 q13 visual inventory with a readable medieval
therapeutic-bath manuscript and with the GDT209 alchemical-apparatus controls.
The question is which content theory explains the visible package more
specifically:

1. literal therapeutic bathing/hydrotherapy;
2. alchemical circulation with personified material states;
3. a hybrid therapeutic regimen rendered with hydraulic/process notation.

This is exploratory, post-hoc theory generation. It does not decode text.

## External source

The primary comparator is Morgan Library MS G.74, *De balneis Puteolanis*,
Italy, ca. 1400. Four institutional images were selected because the Morgan's
own human catalogue descriptions document water flow, bathing figures, pools,
and architectural levels. Image selection did not use Voynich strings.

The official images were inspected directly without OCR, automated vision,
embeddings, segmentation, object detection, or image similarity. New visible
claims are `AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION`; Morgan's iconographic descriptions
are `EXISTING_HUMAN_ANNOTATION`.

## Comparison

Thirteen fixed axes are reported without a fitted composite score. They cover
figures in water, shared pools, vertical tiers, multiple pools, visible
watercourses, architectural enclosures, serial illustrated units, text-image
pairing, readable bath identity, cross-page ducting, visible liquid color,
local label ownership, and the exact f77 topology. Each axis records whether it
favors the bath, alchemical, shared-medieval, or unresolved explanation.

Because the comparator was found after GDT208/GDT209, axis counts are
descriptive. They rank theories; they are not p-values or confirmation.

## Decision

Therapeutic balneology becomes the leading q13 content theory only if the
readable comparator jointly supplies figures in water, repeated shared pools,
vertical staging, and a visible watercourse—features absent as a package from
the inspected alchemical witnesses. Alchemical circulation remains a live
secondary mechanism if it better explains closed loops or cross-page ducts.

## Claim ceiling

The result may identify a provisional page genre and the likely literal role
of the figures. It cannot assign a Voynich source group to a bath, disease,
procedure, person, place, material, or operation; it cannot establish a
language, plaintext, translation, or direct exemplar. No f84 page is an input.
