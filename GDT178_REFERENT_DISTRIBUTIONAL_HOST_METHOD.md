# GDT178 — full-atlas referent distributional host test

## Question

GDT169 found no exact PAGE_HOST/full-tuple invariance over 38 source-bound
human-nominated page pairs. GDT140's smaller exposed panel was strongest under
PAGE_HOST character trigrams. GDT178 asks whether the full GDT169 atlas shares
a **distribution of related short host forms** even when exact host identity
changes.

This is exploratory and the panel is exposed. It is a new representation test,
not independent confirmation of GDT140.

## Frozen representations

For each complete non-f84 page, construct occurrence-weighted bags of:

1. exact PAGE_HOST IDs (`HOST_EXACT`, integrity anchor);
2. boundary-marked PAGE_HOST character bigrams (`HOST_CHAR2`);
3. boundary-marked PAGE_HOST character trigrams (`HOST_CHAR3`);
4. boundary-marked raw source-token trigrams (`RAW_CHAR3`); and
5. PAGE_HOST length bins (`HOST_LENGTH`, nuisance control).

No feature is selected after relation scores are seen. ZL/IT/RF are already
collapsed in the published GDT062 view and are not replications.

## Pair scoring and controls

Reuse all 40 source-only GDT169 candidates; 38 are expected to have complete
formal pages. For each asserted pair, compare weighted-Jaccard similarity with
every target page matching section, Currier, and hand and lying on a different
physical folio. Report partner rank and standardized excess.

Use 20,000 deterministic matched-target worlds. Each world draws one target
from every pair's fixed pool and reports the mean standardized excess for all
five representations. Publish local and max-five tails. Also report the
cross-source-corroborated, asserted-same/strong, internal-Herbal, and
Herbal-to-pharma subsets, plus leave-one-pair and leave-one-physical-folio
effects. Shared pages and human sources remain dependent.

The five GDT152 locally owned/provisional labels are a separate secondary
query: rank each label's host-char3 bag against pages in the same matched pool.
They cannot rescue a negative page-level result.

## Interpretation

A positive HOST_CHAR3 result would support related short-form profiles on
externally nominated pages, not a plant word. A RAW-only result is ordinary
string/register similarity. A HOST_LENGTH result is a nuisance explanation.
Failure means the earlier five-pair trigram lead does not extend to the full
referent atlas.

No botanical identity, word meaning, language, plaintext, or translation is
licensed. f84r remains excluded and must not be accessed.
