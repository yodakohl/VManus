# GDT002 exploratory visual–formal discovery

Status: **EXPLORATORY SEARCH COMPLETE; NO SEMANTIC ROLE FROZEN**.

This checkpoint supersedes the earlier validation-first discovery policy but
does not rewrite its outcomes. The old capacity/census/visual-gate STOP rows
remain true descriptions of those preregistered procedures. Here their
observations are reused permissively for postselected hypothesis generation.
The f84r formal holdout remains commitment-only and was not opened, joined, or
scored.

## Discovery pool

The canonical CONTACT/GAP panel contains 27 unique physical loci after nine
inherited duplicates are removed: 8 CONTACT, 18 CLEAR_GAP, and 1 UNCERTAIN,
across five arrays and three physical folios. All 27 have source-native family
coverage. The one UNCERTAIN row remains in the joined atlas and is missing in
the primary binary scan; both CONTACT and CLEAR_GAP assignments are reported
as sensitivities. ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate readings, never three
samples.

Two additional visual channels are scanned separately rather than pooled:

- 23 existing human-linked f80r/f82r layout positions;
- 30 non-f84 BFE enclosure observations.

Pooling would manufacture section/Currier/hand associations: every
CONTACT/GAP row is P/Currier-A/hand-1 label material, whereas f80r/f82r is
B/Currier-B/hand-2 label material.

The search generated 808 ranked candidate rows across exact family expressions,
family components and n-grams, source-native construction length/grouping,
boundary profiles, edition-specific primary STA members, and postselected
depth-two AND/OR combinations. Controls include 2,520 exact within-array
CONTACT-count permutations, cyclic rotations, leave-one-folio diagnostics,
three-reading mask comparisons, uncertainty assignments, and a low-capacity
length/group matched sensitivity. The reported conditional-library maximum
holds fixed the joint library selected on the observed data; it does not rerun
that selection under every permutation and is not a full max-search correction.
None of these ranks is a confirmatory probability. The f80 formal subanalysis
has coverage for 8/10 positions, and the BFE scan for 29/30 nonholdout rows.

## Strongest weird correlations

### 1. ZL primary member `A1` is depleted in CONTACT loci — `WEAK`

`MEMBER_TOKEN_ZL3b:A1` occurs in 5/8 CONTACT and 17/18 CLEAR_GAP loci.
Its mean within-array contrast is -0.6833; exact array-permutation p=.02778,
but conditional-library maximum p=.50675. The direction persists after deleting each folio and
is nearly edition-stable (minimum mask Jaccard .9565), but exact
length/group matching has only three worlds and gives p=1. It is also sensitive
to the lone uncertain f89 call: uncertain→CONTACT preserves p=.02778, whereas
uncertain→GAP weakens it to p=.36111. Raw MDL gain is 3.515 bits and becomes
-4.343 bits after charging the searched mask library. This is the strongest
single formal primitive, not evidence that `A1` means contact or gap.

### 2. `K`/`KA` component presence — `WEAK`

Family `K` occurs in 2/8 CONTACT and 3/18 CLEAR_GAP loci. The pooled difference
is small, but its informative-array effect is consistently positive on f99
and f100: mean +.4167, exact p=.08333, unchanged in direction under every
folio deletion and all three readings. It is length-confounded, loses under
the three-world exact length/group sensitivity, has GAP counterexamples on
f100v, and fails the conditional-library maximum diagnostic (p=.99921). Raw MDL gain is 1.970 bits;
selector-paid gain is -5.888 bits.

### 3. Total source-family symbol count — `UNSTABLE`

This has the largest array-conditional mutual information: .39579 bit/row,
exact CMI p=.05952 and conditional-library maximum CMI p=.16825. CONTACT rows average 5.75
symbols versus 4.89 for CLEAR_GAP. The common-direction statistic is weak,
however, and f99v/L2 reverses sharply: +1.0 on f100r/L2, +1.0 on f99v/L1,
-1.2 on f99v/L2. It fails leave-one-folio direction stability. Raw MDL gain is
2.578 bits; selector-paid gain is -5.280 bits. This looks like array-specific
geometry/length dependence, not a reusable formal role.

### 4. Drawing-interrupted two-group labels — `LIKELY_PAGE_CONFOUND`

Both two-group loci are CONTACT and no GAP locus is two-group. Both are on
f99v/L1, and the internal separator is explicitly `DRAWING_INTERRUPTION` in
all readings. The visual contour being measured helped create the formal
boundary, so this is a mechanical visual/transcription coupling rather than
independent grammar evidence. It is retained because it is real, not promoted
because it is tautological.

### 5. f82r row topology: `AQA` prefix — `LIKELY_PAGE_CONFOUND`

All four formal-covered upper-row positions begin `AQA`; zero of six lower-row
positions do. Within-page exact p=.00476; conditional-library maximum p=.27619. This is the
largest clean human-layout contrast, but it is one page, one physical folio,
with no leave-folio test. It nominates a precise future prediction—`AQA`
prefix may predict row/register class—but does not establish a semantic role.

### 6. f82r apparatus positions and `ACA` trigram — `LIKELY_PAGE_CONFOUND`

All three apparatus-position loci contain `ACA`, versus one of ten figure
positions (exact p=.01399; conditional-library maximum p=.53846). Again this is one page and
one folio. It is interesting as a target for later independent apparatus
arrays, not a semantic assignment.

### Postselected joint rules

The strongest depth-two rule is `K present OR CAC prefix`: 3/8 CONTACT versus
4/18 CLEAR_GAP, mean within-array effect +.6833, raw p=.02778, conditional-library maximum
p=.50675. It remains `WEAK` because it was built after inspecting the same
panel, loses selector-paid MDL, and has no independent validation. The atlas
contains every tested rule rather than only this favorable one.

## Interpretation

No candidate earns confirmation-grade status, but the search is not empty.
The most useful risky leads are a member-level `A1` depletion, a weak `K/KA`
component tendency, and the sharply page-local `AQA` row/register contrast.
Their weaknesses are now explicit: uncertainty dependence, length confounding,
array direction reversal, one-page concentration, postselection, and negative
selector-paid MDL. These are exactly the kinds of dirty hypotheses the YOLO
branch is meant to surface.

No semantic role, lexical value, POS, sound, language, plaintext, meaning, or
translation is claimed.
