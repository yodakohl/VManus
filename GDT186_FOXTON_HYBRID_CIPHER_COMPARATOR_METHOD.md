# GDT186 — Foxton hybrid scientific-cipher comparator

## Question

Could the leading Voynich hybrid-technical-compiler theory be an instance of
a historically attested scientific concealment practice, rather than an
ordinary phonetic alphabet or a simple whole-text substitution cipher?

The comparator is John de Foxton's Latin *Liber Cosmographiae*, Trinity
College Cambridge MS R.15.21, completed in 1408.  It is unusually close in
date and document ecology: astronomy, astrology, medicine, physiognomy,
phlebotomy, prognostication, diagrams, tables, and a deliberately invented
cipher alphabet.  Byrhtferth's earlier computus diagram in Oxford, St John's
College MS 17 supplies a second, weaker comparator for cryptic writing placed
inside a cosmological diagram.

This is a source and mechanism audit.  It does not assign a Foxton cipher
letter, Latin word, sound, rubric, or meaning to any Voynich form.

## Frozen sources

The source manifest records the official Trinity manuscript catalogue, John
Block Friedman's scholarly article and its page-level Persée payload hashes,
and the University of Glasgow OG(H)AM project account.  The extracted claims
are paraphrases with page references; the copyrighted article is not copied
into the repository.

The external facts were frozen before the architecture comparison was
written.  Most important are:

1. Foxton uses an artificial substitution alphabet in a Latin compendium.
2. 135 words are enciphered, rather than the whole manuscript.
3. operative words, rubrics, ingredients, and diagram keys are preferentially
   concealed;
4. a key noun can be supplied by a diagram/list and omitted from following
   descriptive paragraphs;
5. part of a word can be ciphered while the rest remains ordinary Latin;
6. ordinary Latin abbreviation conventions continue inside cipher;
7. Giovanni Fontana supplies a contemporary full-text artificial-alphabet
   counterexample; and
8. Byrhtferth's computus diagram places uncertain pseudo-Ogham/cryptic writing
   inside a cosmological system diagram.

## Comparison rule

Each architecture axis is classified without a fitted score as `MATCH`,
`PARTIAL`, `CONTRADICTION`, or `UNRESOLVED`.  A direct Foxton-style selective
substitution requires readable ordinary-language substrate, a stable
letter-for-letter key, and selective concealment.  The broader historical
hybrid mechanism requires only that diagrams/rubrics can carry coded
headwords while grammatical or abbreviated material remains separately
rendered.

The direct model is rejected if either (a) the expected readable substrate is
absent manuscript-wide or (b) a simple substitution/abbreviation layer cannot
account for the independently established page-conditioned line-reset record
architecture.  The broader mechanism remains open only as a source-prior and
must make a new Voynich prediction before receiving semantic weight.

## Claim ceiling

GDT186 can establish historical plausibility for selective scientific
concealment, diagram-keyed headwords, partial-word ciphering, and abbreviation
inside cipher.  It cannot establish that Voynich uses Foxton's alphabet,
Latin, English, a rubric dictionary, a particular source, plaintext, or a
translation.  f84r is not read, retained, parsed, joined, or scored.
