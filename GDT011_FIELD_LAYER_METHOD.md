# GDT011 distributional field-layer recovery

Status: `YOLO_GENERATIVE_GRAMMAR_REFINEMENT`

## Question

Can attachment behavior recover positional classes without assuming the
GDT009 slot names?  Ten operations are compared to the same host on the same
page: prepend `t`, `s`, `d`, `q`, `ch`, `sh`, `che`, `o`, or `ot`, and append
`DY`.  Each operation is scored on normalized position, line-initial,
line-final, and nonprose status with 20,000 within-host-page permutations.
Forty primary tests are charged by a transparent Bonferroni search adjustment.

Classes are assigned mechanically:

- `ENTRY`: earlier and more line-initial after adjustment;
- `EARLY_CARRIER`: earlier or reliably non-final after adjustment without the
  paired position-plus-line-initial signature of `ENTRY`;
- `LOCAL_OR_UNRESOLVED`: no adjusted directional result;
- `CLOSURE`: later and more line-final after adjustment.

The q/DY bracketing hypothesis is separately challenged.  Complete
`H, qH, Hdy, qHdy` rectangles are counted globally and within page; lines with
separate q-only and DY-only groups test whether q actually precedes DY.

The source universe is the already published non-f84, all-reading-exact,
module-bearing GDT002 group set.  Results are functional field constraints,
not language, words, POS, or translation.
