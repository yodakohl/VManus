# GDT204 — readable medieval operation groups, but no f77 key

Status: **READABLE_ALCHEMICAL_DIAGRAM_ECOLOGY_EXPANDED_EXACT_F77_HOMOLOG_ABSENT**.

The complete institutional catalogue screen covers Othmer MS 1–7 and retains
24 candidate or explicit no-candidate records.  It adds a much stronger
historical vocabulary prior than generic apparatus resemblance:

- MS 2, fol. 41r names sublimation vessels and a sublimation furnace.
- MS 6, fol. 12v names element, sulfur/medicine, corruption/generation, and
  alembic diagrams.
- MS 6, fol. 13r organizes nine named operations as three triplets:
  `liquefaccio / elementatio / diuisio`,
  `inhumatio / distillacio / calcinacio`, and
  `inceratio / congelatio / sublimatio`.
- MS 6, fol. 14r connects circles named for water, air, fire, stone, and earth.
- MS 7 contains named furnaces and repeated titled circular figures.

These records show that readable apparatus labels, operation taxonomies,
element diagrams, and practical recipes coexist in the correct broad
manuscript culture.  They do not supply the required six ordered states, five
relations, and four-output/one-hold topology in one readable record.  The exact
f77-homolog count remains zero.

The operation names are an external comparative vocabulary, not translations
of Voynich labels.  No Voynich transcription, image, or f84r material was
accessed.
