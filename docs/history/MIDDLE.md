# Historical review: GDT337–GDT616

This is a retrospective map, not a research plan. I read the current route only to establish routing and scope; its newly selected cipher direction is not treated as a conclusion. I inspected index metadata across GDT337–616, then the primary reports listed below and targeted claim-registry and ledger passages. No experiment, source-transcription query, image review, external API call, or repository edit was performed. Findings below describe what the reports actually support; their original recommendations are historical, not adopted instructions.

## 1. GDT337–398: acquiring an endpoint and calibrating what an instrument can identify

This period contains several different scientific questions, not one unsuccessful search repeated sixty times. One track sought externally readable diagram correspondences; another tested anonymous formal prediction; another investigated visual ownership and relation capacity; the final track tested the research instruments on synthetic worlds.

[GDT337](../../experiments/yolo/gdt337_external_homologue_census/REPORT.md) audited fourteen external witness families and eleven candidate correspondences against existing diagram inventories. It found genuinely useful comparators, including a readable 28-member wheel with numbered records and a movable pointer. The blocker was on the manuscript side: no frozen start/direction or sufficiently independent matching topology. Its zero viable endpoints means “these correspondences cannot presently support this transfer test,” not “no historical homologue exists.” Fitted rotations or band orders would have supplied precisely the missing answer.

[GDT347](../../experiments/yolo/gdt347_fixed_graph_control_transport/REPORT.md) retained a real held-data formal signal: a three-edge compatibility graph improved probability coding by 56.651 bits after graph cost. Exact next-state recovery nevertheless declined by two. Its failure to transport to readable and artificial controls was carefully bounded: those controls had no inner-D/DY changes under the frozen parser. “Manuscript-specific” therefore referred to that observation layer, not proof that ordinary writing cannot contain an analogous mechanism.

Visual-form correlations received meaningful falsification. [GDT362](../../experiments/yolo/gdt362_remaining_complete_array/REPORT.md) tested a previously selected AQ/contact direction on the remaining complete array: contact 0/3 versus clear-gap 1/5 reversed the expected direction. The earlier supportive held array had only p=.60; aggregating it with the discovery sample would conceal postselection. A foldout-canvas correction was disclosed before continuing image review, and formal identities remained closed until visual calls were frozen. [GDT369](../../experiments/yolo/gdt369_order_preserving_geometry_null/REPORT.md) then showed why another apparently strong geometry association weakened when the null preserved spatial clustering: ordinary permutation maxT p=.002929 became approximately .060 under the global order-matched diagnostic. These are scoped rejections of particular associations and null choices.

[GDT372](../../experiments/yolo/gdt372_external_prespecification_capacity/REPORT.md) quantified a real design tradeoff. Reducing 81 candidate comparisons to three substantially improved simulated power, but no tested design passed every gate. Even a single externally chosen candidate had a 17.2% null-pass rate under the likelihood-only rule. Prespecification alone did not replace an evidential null.

The relation programme also preserved successes while correcting interpretations. [GDT381](../../experiments/yolo/gdt381_relational_topology_transfer/REPORT.md) found a topology that transferred across readable comparator domains. Its spectacular Voynich held AUC .916 was not promoted: target membership and its predictor reused source-side topology, and source-only AUC was already .895. Holding out folios did not remove endpoint-definition overlap. [GDT394](../../experiments/yolo/gdt394_latent_role_bottleneck_transfer_audit/REPORT.md) further showed that anonymous role scores were useful low-dimensional compression, but lost to equally small generic source bottlenecks. Their gains were not new information beyond the source features from which they were calculated.

[GDT388](../../experiments/yolo/gdt388_acquisition_ready_relation_edge_protocol/REPORT.md) explains today's strict evidence packet. The archive's 1,059 relation-channel rows included 95 explicit positives, but none provided a normalized second inscription and directed inscription-to-inscription edge. Attachment, contact, enclosure, and proximity were useful geometry; they were not interchangeable with a readable referential relation. The frozen gate required at least fifty singular directed edges on five folios, exact ownership, held folios, and a mobile matched null. Registry summaries of GDT389–393 record subsequent censuses and normalization failures; I did not read those primary reports in this pass.

Crucially, the synthetic work corrected overly pessimistic conclusions too. [GDT395](../../experiments/yolo/gdt395_adversarial_synthetic_identifiability_benchmark/GDT395_ADVERSARIAL_SYNTHETIC_IDENTIFIABILITY_REPORT.md) demonstrated substantial lexical-identity and entity-partition recovery by particular decoders that a panel rule suppressed because required replication decoders emitted near-singleton clusters. Ten endpoints were unscoreable interface HOLDs. [GDT396](../../experiments/yolo/gdt396_repaired_synthetic_identifiability_voynich_surface/GDT396_REPAIRED_SYNTHETIC_IDENTIFIABILITY_REPORT.md) repaired interfaces, yet an unrelated recurrent-relation suite suppressed eighteen otherwise passing routes. Some semantic-style claims also invented relationships in a semantics-light world.

[GDT397](../../experiments/yolo/gdt397_bounded_observability_ontology_audit/GDT397_BOUNDED_OBSERVABILITY_REPORT.md) separated these problems: exact visible identity was recoverable; conditional backward-target ranking worked when the query pivot was supplied; blind pivot detection remained uncalibrated. Some purported semantic false positives were real formal closure sensitivity mislabeled semantically. Identical observations could still support different denotational interpretations. This is an identifiability limit for those observation-only claims, not proof of manuscript meaninglessness or universal futility of internal analysis.

Finally, [GDT398](../../experiments/yolo/gdt398_opaque_joint_tuple_predictive_equivalence_preflight/REPORT.md) rejected its particular latent-equivalence proposal: small average gains over exact identity were dominated by frequent forms, matched nulls performed better, and removal of the largest cluster reversed the gain. It licensed no free synonym or allomorph merges.

## 2. GDT399–600: an increasingly complete, explicitly speculative German working edition

This was a different programme: build a coherent, inspectable rendering of chosen component recipes and broad working values. Its many PASS statuses chiefly certify coverage, deterministic composition, trace preservation, and internal consistency. Those achievements should neither be dismissed as nonexistent nor counted as hundreds of independently discovered meanings.

[GDT399](../../experiments/yolo/gdt399_creative_scope_rebuild_after_visible_resegmentation/REPORT.md) starts from an important repair. Targeted registry passages describe removal of one-edit imports that had silently supplied absent components: `cheo` had borrowed R or L from neighbouring spellings. Pass1026 resegmented 203 surfaces/239 events; GDT399 rebuilt 3,888 events, 627 statements, and 4,374 focus attachments rather than patching the obsolete inventory. The useful discipline was “one edit is not automatically an allograph.” The existing nineteen German core values remained assumptions throughout.

[GDT404](../../experiments/yolo/gdt404_random_four_page_factorized_admission/REPORT.md) admitted a single random four-page draw: f1r, f24v, f81r, f95v. Of 688 events, 470 repeated old surfaces exactly; 169 used clear new compositions, and 49 singleton microforms remained ambiguous. The report itself warns that once a surface is assigned fixed components, the factorized parser is deliberately receptive. Thus parser survival was weaker than independent semantic prediction, while unchanged exact recurrences and explicit uncertain boundaries were genuine transferable bookkeeping results.

[GDT410](../../experiments/yolo/gdt410_provisional_core_rival_tournament/REPORT.md) compared broad German alternatives across complete statements: HALTEN versus BEWAHREN, EINSTELLEN versus BESTIMMEN, and so forth. These were operationally motivated working choices, not recovered historical lexemes. [GDT416](../../experiments/yolo/gdt416_owner_local_imperative_sentence_compiler/REPORT.md) made 4,576 cards into imperative clauses using eleven templates, carrying an active action 1,598 times and an argument 2,096 times within owner boundaries. Fluent instructions were therefore explicitly generated by a renderer with inheritance; they were not directly read off the manuscript.

[GDT423](../../experiments/yolo/gdt423_leave_one_page_action_grammar_replay/REPORT.md) found 97.78% green-or-amber replay among its selected simple action events when each stored page was excluded from the teaching deck. That tested reuse of chosen action/slot recipes, with actionless and complex populations explicitly outside scope. The GDT424–458 index sequence then describes finite exception cards, intake rules, identity/execution separation, stop handling, and corruption recovery. Those are software/generalization properties of this analysis system.

The local-label work increasingly separated memorized names from reusable shells. [GDT459](../../experiments/yolo/gdt459_local_nomenclator_content_atlas/REPORT.md) retained 107 whole labels rather than forcing their decomposition: its minimal segmenter recovered known working recipes only 58.08% of the time. Seventy-six other local events received formula readings. Its “nomenclator” was a working architecture, not identification of a historical codebook. [GDT474](../../experiments/yolo/gdt474_locus_bundle_meaning_triptych/REPORT.md) compared address, instruction, and catalogue renderings, selecting a mixed model with fourteen heuristic grammatical repairs versus 89–146 for uniform alternatives. This was intelligibility under fixed roots. [GDT485](../../experiments/yolo/gdt485_fluent_reversible_microrecord_edition/REPORT.md) then smoothed 135 microrecords while preserving exact technical backprojections. A roundtrip proved preservation, not truth.

[GDT514–515](../../experiments/yolo/gdt515_second_random_four_page_full_admission/REPORT.md) added f31r, f66r, f20v, and f4r through a second unrepeated draw. All 276 exact GDT405 lock contacts retained their recipes; genuine `x` exceptions remained local. The edition reached thirty pages, 5,122 prose events, and 744 separate local cards. [GDT517](../../experiments/yolo/gdt517_thirty_page_surface_recipe_intake_compiler/REPORT.md) made surface-to-recipe parsing executable: the retrospectively chosen recipe appeared among candidates for all 159 novel surfaces, but ranked first for 117. That distinction matters: candidate coverage was not perfect blind analysis.

Subsequent passes repaired concrete editorial problems. [GDT538](../../experiments/yolo/gdt538_final_159_phrase_consistency_edition/REPORT.md) filled 152 placeholder phrases and corrected the noun POSTEN being spoken as a verb. Its inventory was corrected to 149 prose plus nineteen local occurrences. [GDT554](../../experiments/yolo/gdt554_statement_semantic_template_audit/REPORT.md) found reusable short transitions but no recurring three- or four-card templates in its sample; “zero exact context contradictions” was expressly reconstruction from already chosen recipes/states.

[GDT581](../../experiments/yolo/gdt581_grammar_content_boundary_audit/REPORT.md) identified 269 focus occurrences where fluent prose suggested the wrong grammatical attachment. It repaired voices while preserving 15,889 slots and distinguishing 4,026 inherited aliases from written occurrences. Its model-governor “host” must not be confused with GDT388's independently visible endpoint ownership.

[GDT582](../../experiments/yolo/gdt582_concrete_stem_default_fill/REPORT.md) filled every slot through 42 analysis classes, 181 register realizations, and learned names. Its 99.20% productive-content coverage meant reusable defaults under fixed segmentation. [GDT585](../../experiments/yolo/gdt585_learned_name_compound_atlas/REPORT.md) openly acknowledged that the previous name palette had been largely a freely selected apothecary list: visible root fragments had received water, wine, vinegar, egg, or wax aliases. It replaced primaries with image-conditioned descriptions while retaining rivals. Neither palette was a decoded dictionary.

[GDT595](../../experiments/yolo/gdt595_remaining_bath_default_source_atlas/REPORT.md) and [GDT599](../../experiments/yolo/gdt599_remaining_action_object_completion/REPORT.md) completed bath/object readings using written carriers, contextual inheritance, bounded rightward complements, and defaults. GDT599 filled 793 remaining action objects across 313 statements, including 54 root defaults and eleven local decisions; 125 review cases remained. This is why completeness and unresolved semantics coexist. GDT600 has a substantial rendering report, but its index status is REGISTERED_UNSCORED: I do not treat report existence as verified completion/publication.

## 3. GDT601–616: cipher controls, useful recoveries, and failure localization

[GDT601–604](../../experiments/yolo/gdt604_naibbe_frozen_target_attack/REPORT.md) form a particularly informative sequence. The literal published Naibbe table fit its modern control but failed Voynich order tests despite 79.86% token coverage. GDT602 recovered 99.97% of control characters with oracle segmentation; GDT603 then recovered approximately 95.68% end-to-end without the key or internal segmentation, conditional on known architecture/capacity. GDT604 applied that model to 68 training and 23 held folios: three incompatible languages produced attractive scores, but unstable keys and worse real-versus-destroyed typicality. This rejected the concrete whitespace-token U/P/S attack, not ciphering generally.

[GDT605](../../experiments/yolo/gdt605_multisymbol_unit_alphabet/REPORT.md) found learned units crossed uncertain separators much more often than certain ones on held folios. Its 98-unit BPE inventory was reproducible and useful, but not thereby an authorial alphabet. The particular one-unit/one-letter fits failed. GDT606's variable-output nomenclator could generate several language styles without stable words. [GDT607](../../experiments/yolo/gdt607_boundary_word_disentanglement/REPORT.md) corrected its apparently stable whole-word class to a frequency/mobility/output-capacity bucket. [GDT608](../../experiments/yolo/gdt608_compositional_stem_orientation/REPORT.md) retained directed exterior-context transfer from components, while exact merged identities predicted better overall. These were formal dependencies, not morphology or meanings.

[GDT609](../../experiments/yolo/gdt609_historical_mixed_abbreviation_prior/REPORT.md) selected a historically motivated abbreviation FST prior. Its 34-slot counts were expressly engineering regularization, not a documented codebook. [GDT610](../../experiments/yolo/gdt610_consensus_carrier_control_audit/REPORT.md) showed consensus could stabilize the wrong answer: all eleven planted whole-word outputs became perfectly stable and all eleven were wrong. [GDT611](../../experiments/yolo/gdt611_lexical_slot_permutation_audit/REPORT.md) found transferable exact formal frames, but zero passing lexical/family assignments; swapping names such as bath and illness left scores unchanged.

[GDT612](../../experiments/yolo/gdt612_historical_fst34_target_attack/REPORT.md) invalidated its own heuristic/control pair: the objective ranked planted truth last, several truth parameters lacked training exposure, and the copied FST specification was never executed. It did not test the exact GDT609 model. [GDT613](../../experiments/yolo/gdt613_observation_complete_fst34_recovery/REPORT.md) found contradictions in the exact flattened grammar plus demanding observability/length contracts before recovery. Pure real-language cross-entropy improved truth ranking against archived pseudokeys, but still failed some mutation distinctions.

GDT614 required at least eighteen paid subtrees under its fixed binding, exceeding eight. GDT615 optimized the binding and passed a necessary coverage bound, then failed because one mandatory paid node had an unobservable child composition. [GDT616](../../experiments/yolo/gdt616_joint_child_feasible_binding/REPORT.md) jointly searched bindings and paid locations; independent solvers proved the registered necessary conditions UNSAT. A later diagnosis found four child-gate relaxations sufficient. The index's latest “relaxation PASS” must not overwrite the terminal failure. These are exact conclusions about constructed FST34/V2 worlds, not proofs against Latin, abbreviation, composition, or natural language in Voynich.

## Provenance, user choices, and coverage limits

Targeted registry passages explicitly document user authorization for GDT373's record-conditioned internal rewrite route and GDT395's synthetic benchmark. The inspected workshop reports repeatedly call themselves a creative working reconstruction. I did not recover the original user message commissioning that entire sidequest, so attributing every later default or page choice to the user would be unjustified. Page draws and recipe locks are documented; specific broad-core selections were investigator judgments.

Actually read in full: primary reports GDT337,347,362,369,372,381,388,394,396,397,398,399,404,410,416,423,459,474,485,514,515,517,538,554,581,600,601–605,607,608,612–614,616; GDT395's main report and conformance correction. Substantial targeted report excerpts: GDT582,585,595,599,606,609–611,615. Index metadata covered GDT337–616; registry sections covered the Pass1025/1026 repair, GDT373/395 authorization, and GDT388–397 genealogy; ledger rows verified key dated transitions. Broad searches located those passages but were not substitutes for reading the entire archive.

Gaps: most intermediate workshop passes were mapped from index metadata rather than independently audited; I did not rerun validators or inspect underlying result rows, source segmentation, original images, solver code, or historical web sources. Later corrections may narrow surviving source-level claims. Root-level HANDOFF/WORKLOG files were absent, and no archive reconstruction was attempted. Accordingly this report explains the project's development and claim types, not a fresh verification of every numerical result.
