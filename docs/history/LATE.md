# Late research history: GDT617–GDT828

This is a selective primary-report reconstruction, not a revalidation of 212 experiments. Current-route routing and targeted ledger corrections were consulted; no experiment, corpus query, image inspection or attack was performed for this task. Later corrections govern interpretation, while older reports remain useful evidence of what was actually attempted. The latest user instruction calls for understanding before choosing a plan; no successor attack is recommended here.

## 1. External plaintext acquisition became an architectural analogy

GDT617 proposed an unusually concrete success condition: obtain at least five illustrated-herbal concordances, pair five distinct Voynich folios using pictures without names, fit one global primitive-to-0–3-Latin-letter transducer on four, then recover an untouched fifth heading plus twelve content words. Its present-stage registration expressly opened no Voynich targets. Institution metadata supplied a real transmission-family lead, not an established Voynich correspondence.

The source work was substantial. Targeted ledger entries document corrected manuscript locators, an unnoticed recto/verso physical-leaf collision, a calibrated scan offset, ten acquired source pages, and source/control access sequencing. GDT621 completed five manually verified BnF Latin 6823/Clm pairs: balsam, chervil, liquorice, cucurbita and dittany. The BnF readings were publicly fixed before the Clm locator controls opened. This is useful external source knowledge and provenance discipline.

However, neither acquisition nor the two-reader agreement made the Latin transcription infallible. GDT621 later corrected substantial readings: balsam's tree-versus-shrub contrast, liquorice's root identification and the attribution to Isaac in cucurbita. Its conclusion explicitly says twelve isolated words were too narrow for reliable palaeography; full sentence context mattered. The frozen payload records the original reading, while forward corrections supply the current interpretation. GDT617 also discloses pre-publication consultation of Wagner's concordance, making resulting candidate selection developmental rather than prospectively sealed.

The promised held Voynich plaintext test was not completed in these reports. GDT622 instead moved to a historically attested architecture: learned drug names plus compact hot/cold, dry/moist and degree fields, directly exemplified by Clm 667. It assigned tentative values to a real Voynich four-corner spelling pattern. Formal minimal pairs were a useful discovery target; the historical analogue did not label their axes. Proposed plant identities were inherited visual hypotheses, and several alleged quality contacts were eleven or twelve lines from the proposed name.

GDT623 immediately reversed the moisture orientation. Its main argument matched a frequent Voynich corner to dry's high frequency in the herbal comparator; the thermal direction remained unstable under a Herbal-only cut. It also withdrew convenient long-distance name/property attachment and rejected shody=moist despite superficially similar spelling. This was a meaningful correction, but neither frequency alignment nor a locally plausible plant hypothesis constituted a recovered word key. The phase established historical possibility and reusable source inventories, not a bilingual anchor.

## 2. Productive spelling families became an extensive practical renderer

GDT626 replaced a generic operation interpretation of daiin with an explicit four-form dan/dain/daiin/daiiin family. Its census reported 5,176 minim-family tokens under 545 heads and repeated same-head variants within physical lines. The structural recurrence matters. Reading the four cells as I–IV was an analogy to medical number notation, with grammatical/abbreviation rivals retained. Its stronger claim that family membership barred verbs or conjunctions was later corrected in GDT812: a productive family alone does not establish part of speech.

GDT631 extended a ch/sh plus cth family, reporting bare/prefixed counterparts and same-span split/fused readings. This usefully tested complete families rather than isolated resemblances. Yet material, leaf and dry/moist meanings depended on the inherited semantic assignments. Its own chcthy-shol contacts were uncomfortable under one dry/moist bearer; resolving them as separate cells required attachment evidence that did not yet exist.

The research then optimized concrete reading coverage. GDT650 added seven whole-form defaults covering 101 formerly unknown positions. GDT664 supplied 140 further surface defaults plus occurrence-specific renderers; GDT666 supplied 151. These reports produced readable recipes with drying, heating, soaking, powder, wood, doses and preparations. Their software distinguished whole words, local joins, repeated atoms and unresolved variants, and documented scope-dependent rendering rather than silently conflating every occurrence.

That engineering was useful, but the objective often became closing an existing one-hole line using previously guessed components. GDT664's O+L wood-preparation analysis and GDT666's 48-role sheet generated much of the concrete prose. Coverage and exact replay could therefore increase without new semantic evidence. A complete sentence assembled from those cards was an internally consistent interpretation, not a decoded sentence.

GDT675 shows both the benefits and costs of checking beyond a motivating page: 51 outside occurrences required nine explicit result/action or scope splits. It did not simply copy the original command wording everywhere. Nevertheless, absence of a contradiction among largely untyped neighbours did not independently confirm the meanings. GDT700 similarly narrowed one action-output reference across a state checkpoint to a single B-tier local hypothesis and retained a competing material-bearing example. Its carefully bounded graph prevented invented universal reference rules, but all named actions and materials still depended on inherited glosses; GDT388 intake remained not score-ready.

By GDT734 the dictionary contained 1,606 readings for 1,602 forms. A real software defect accounted for 305 missing projections from 71 already active wholes; a separate 20-form speculative tranche added 226 positions. The report explicitly distinguishes those two contributions and says that the 531-cell improvement is not translation accuracy. The renderer's source conservation and precedence machinery survive as infrastructure; its pharmaceutical vocabulary does not inherit truth from that machinery.

## 3. Provenance audits substantially reduced semantic claims

GDT735 identified a foundational problem: EVA p/s/r/l are modern transcription labels, so the mnemonic pulvis/semen/radix/lignum supplies no Latin-letter evidence. All 24 material-to-head assignments explained the same 96 structural cells equally well. Twenty-two historical sources supported mixed names, fields and recipe conventions, but none supplied the proposed four interchangeable material-head code. This removed an attractive historical-looking rationale without discarding the observed spelling grid.

GDT736 replaced the material names with H1–H4 record-position classes on 24 shared bodies. GDT737 then tested 120 additional bodies. The location effect transferred strongly: H1/H2 were initial in 199/328 occurrences versus 24/483 for H3/H4. The second proposed affinity axis failed its declared transfer: H1–H4 was no longer among the two strongest pairs, and surviving H2–H3 similarity was heavily ain-dominated. This is one of the clearest useful separations of a positive structural result from failed semantic generalization. It is a held-body test within an inherited corpus, not untouched-folio plaintext recovery.

The consequences were material: 80 of 82 inherited held-form cards still contained the retired powder/seed/root/wood patients and were quarantined. GDT754 broadened the provenance audit to 172 active GDT664/GDT666 PRODUCTIVE_COMPOUND cards covering 889 positions. Only four had any positive later role indication; 168 remained composition-axis hypotheses. All 172 old concrete sentences ceased being active spoken translations, while their history and explicitly hypothetical profiles were retained.

GDT768 subsequently removed near-family context donors before comparing chor/shor. The apparent dry/moist opposition failed this ablation; reversing flower and seed/fruit assignments produced exactly tied scores. Their similar context and position profiles were useful observations, but those profiles did not themselves prove nominal POS or botanical organ identity. The displayed flower/fruit direction was explicitly a replaceable presentation choice. This phase improved claim provenance while showing how much supposed confirmation had been the renderer rediscovering its own assignments.

## 4. Image-native hierarchy supplied constraints, not object names

GDT791 integrated 30 previously visually reviewed pages through 35 source selectors: 1,007 lines and 5,866 legacy tokens. Annotation depth varied: only three pages had detailed panels and records. Five of 235 inherited statements crossed a visible record boundary; two f77r inheritance aliases were quarantined and a raw governor clipped. This repaired a concrete text/layout mismatch. Exact label/prose reuse was kept as string identity, not owner identity or meaning transfer.

Circular-label work also resisted easy numerical decoding. GDT795 rebuilt 101 source-native labels, keeping separator and alternate-reading differences. Its optimistic held-folio family test had 26 eligible targets but only four exact-member contacts, collapsing to two recurrent families that also occurred at other positions. Shared rotation/reflection failed. Local prefix adjacency did survive its permutation comparison (35/87 neighbouring slots versus a null mean about23.4). The defensible result was local graphical/register organization, not a day, degree or catalogue-member word.

GDT800 found a much broader surface regularity: among 155 paired nonempty stems, terminal m was final in450/653 events, versus l in310/3484. The direction persisted after conditioning on stem and other metadata. Its numerous nonfinal m and final l instances excluded an obligatory final-allograph rule. The strong result is boundary association; neither abbreviation, sound, case nor meaning was identified. GDT806, conversely, found no robust semantic-role winner among six tested wholes after target-specific controls and broad denominator checks.

GDT809 corrected a contextual ranking that had effectively treated a botanical page as leaf-specific word ownership. Folium, herba and an unnamed botanical head consequently tied. Its purported external record tests also overlapped training folios, a limitation now disclosed. Complete paragraphs retained repetitions and unknowns under shared descriptive-degree and recipe-amount dictionaries. GDT810 then falsified one concrete extension: proposed paired qualities did not predict multiple following values outside the motivating f32v case; single-quality chol had a repeated-value counterexample.

GDT811 read four complete pages, distinguished labels from prose, and found f4r otchol chol incompatible with an obligatory two-slot interpretation of the two f17r brackets. Its 179-text-plus-35-visual union was190 selectors, explicitly not190 newly admitted visual pages. All17 proposed text-only relations were ineligible for scored ownership evidence.

GDT812 admitted f21r/f32v/f100v/f101r, using four of twenty discretionary admissions. Current visual scope became34 page keys/39 selectors, with16 unused; text scope remained separately defined. The displaced f101r vessel furnished no label-relocation test because the page had no local labels. More seriously, Yale1006204 was72v, not72r: the earlier GDT791/GDT811 f72r visual authentication through that image was quarantined. Correct selector-bound text survived; the later correctly sourced inspection covered f72r3, not a newly audited whole foldout.

## 5. Whole-passage hypotheses culminated in explicit underdetermination

GDT812 retained numerical, quantity/intensity, comparison and storage-time rivals. Its eventual four-whole scalar baseline was C0, with no dictionary replacement. GDT813 compared root-powder and root-is readings of f17r.11, checking54 complete loci. An isolated okaiin label made a content interpretation worth considering but did not prove powder or logically eliminate every copular use. Both smooth German renderings left the surrounding paragraph untranslated.

GDT818's condensation sentence assigned four new tail meanings expressly to complete a known line: “Dessen Dampf wird Wasser, wenn die Luft kalt ist.” Its vapour/basin and becomes/contains alternatives kept substantive debts. GDT819 corrected inherited source handling: RF che@152;aiin and shee@152;y are single opaque groups, not cleaner-created word pairs. The underlying global correction had already existed since August9; its reuse here was a repair, not a fresh discovery or blanket revalidation of older experiments. Real repetitions remained.

GDT824 preserved joined qolchedy as one source cell while trialling a source-plus-becomes interpretation; it did not solve repeated predicates or identify antecedents. GDT827 finally removed the protected water/becomes core and compared transformation, flow, manual-treatment and network wholes on the same50 loci/150 alternate lines. Transformation and flow both completed the showcase sentence through different guesses. Manual actions fitted known repetition patterns but incurred attachment debt. No model supplied a complete paragraph or independently owned nouns.

GDT828 made that debt logically precise: right-immediate nominal attachment failed at f81r.27 under imperative press; left-immediate attachment failed at f77r.34 under imperative stroke. Those are failures of two typed constructions, not proof against the word senses alone. RF's opaque .27 groups prevented an all-three exact conflict. IT also placed .19 and .27 in different paragraphs. Exposed independent reviewers were not blind witnesses, and software validators confirmed preservation rather than semantics.

## Scope, contamination and remaining audit gaps

Useful surviving assets include corrected source groups/separators, exact occurrence inventories, explicit alternative readings, external historical transcription, layout hierarchy repairs, and bounded positional associations. None yields a confirmed Voynich lexeme or clause. Family grids, fluency, renderer coverage, historical plausibility and repeated-reader agreement are distinct evidence types and cannot be multiplied into independent semantic confirmation.

The historical seal record is imperfect: GDT622 discloses an unguarded review search displaying an f84v row, excluded from its results. Therefore “f84 was never exposed” is not a defensible historical summary. Current rules seal f84 and f84r; current visual admission and the179-selector inherited text scope must remain distinct. GDT819 further shows that a documented correction can fail to propagate into later reasoning.
