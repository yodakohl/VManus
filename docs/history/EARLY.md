# Early VManus research history: selected primary-source audit

Scope: predecessor research and GDT001–GDT336. This is a retrospective account,
not a new experiment or attack recommendation. I read the current route first,
mapped all available early GDT index entries using metadata, obtained compact
lookup pointers, and selected turning-point reports. No manuscript data table,
image, sealed material or test execution was used. Numbers below are reported
results, not independently rerun measurements.

## 1. Before GDT: semantic ambitions and an explicit structural reset

The available Git history starts with README revisions on 2026-08-03. The
version-0.26 README called itself a “first translation,” offered a local
`daldy` outlet-position reading and a full-disc/red-crescent record reading,
while admitting that prose remained unknown. The same historical document
already corrected an image-recognition failure: only 1 of 29 candidate-
constrained star-label assignments survived comparison with documented spatial
transcription. It distinguished recovered label locations from meanings.
These are historical claims and corrections, not endorsements of those
semantic values. Source: `git show e782c6ad:README.md`, opening 95 lines.

A 2026-08-06 commit created a structural reset. The archive README says the
bulk snapshot was retired that day; superseded caches, OCR artifacts and other
working material were removed on August 8. Only a curated reproducible subset
and a 105-row legacy ledger survive, with an integrity manifest. Deleted
untracked material is not recoverable from Git. Consequently an audit cannot
infer “never tested” from an absent archived artifact.
Source: `archive_pre_reset_2026-08-06/README.md`; Git commits `900c22f3`,
`f4eed40b`, `2e8d4919`.

The retained predecessor experiments support several specific observations.
The typology-neutral audit distinguished visible gaps from compatible internal
joins, found recurring components that reconstruct many unseen groups, and
recovered page identity from low-level distributions. It explicitly did not
equate gaps with European words or identify a language. Its phrase “layered
compositional system” is the authors' interpretation of those tests.
Source: `archive_pre_reset_2026-08-06/semantic_assumptions/results/typology_neutral_structure_report.md`.

A separate writing-order test found an interior positional score rising along
lines and dropping at the next line, after length and form controls. Its safe
tag was a line-local positional/production gradient, compatible with spelling,
selection or scribal production. This observation does **not** establish that
each line is a sentence or a complete semantic record. The later abstract
interpreter organized approximately 4,000 lines per reading into formal frames,
with 78.6% three-reading coarse-frame agreement but only 49.4% exact-edge
agreement. That gap matters: transcription-dependent detail was substantial.
Sources: archived `writing_order_line_reset_report.md` and
`abstract_line_interpreter_report.md` in the same results directory.

## 2. GDT001: a large decoder tournament, not one initial guess

The current GDT001 summary retains 4,224 runs covering direct, contextual,
periodic, block, multigraph, nomenclator, abbreviation, null-symbol,
latent-space, consonantal, reading-order and several structured alternatives.
It also records frozen line-state switching keys and move-to-front dynamics.
Therefore “try homophony,” “ignore spaces,” “try another historical language,”
or “use a GPU” are not historically new descriptions of this project's work.
Source: `GDT001_CURRENT_SUMMARY.md`.

Its winning complete-source model was an adaptive context mixture at 2.960465
bits/source symbol. This is an important adversarial baseline: a proposed
language interpretation should not claim progress merely by fitting frequent
strings. But the report itself shows why the winner is not a nonlanguage
diagnosis. Its gain survives global sign renaming and is larger on a Timm
copy/modify synthetic text. Good compression identifies predictability, not
the historical production mechanism.

The strongest named-language compression effect, a 512-group latent-character
medieval-Czech fit, only narrowly exceeded an anonymous bottleneck and produced
unstable partitions. Boundary-erased homophony with inserted plaintext spaces
also lost its tested comparison. The summary expressly calls all results
exploratory and branch-local, retains no reading, and limits particular
negatives to their tested settings. Its breadth is substantial; it is not an
exhaustive mathematical exclusion of ciphers or languages.

Chronology caution: GDT001 and GDT002 are long-lived families. GDT002's current
primary summary cites GDT360–372. Numerical order and an index status of
UNREGISTERED therefore cannot be used as a precise event timeline or as
evidence that an experiment lacks substantive results.

## 3. GDT003–100: formal algebra becomes a proposed record compiler

GDT003 supplied a genuine but limited predictive result. In nested held-folio
tests, training-discovered edge operations improved average precision over
the same-candidate string baselines: 0.007899 versus 0.002828. Absolute
precision was only 0.000909 over more than one million predictions. The
popular q-plus-right-edge subgroup performed worse than its strongest string
baseline. The defensible contribution is limited local compositional
predictability, not discovery of q or dy morphemes.
Source: `GDT003_NESTED_HELDOUT_REPORT.md`.

By GDT051, many local studies were synthesized as HPR2, a layered technical
record compiler: optional opening, fields with wrappers and an opaque host,
DY transitions and B3 closure. GDT100 revised the “host equals content lexicon”
idea into a more cautious content-address inventory. Both syntheses explicitly
reported ungrounded content and no translation. They also used stronger
language about line resets and the compiler being the leading generator than
the original positional observation alone warrants. My interpretation is that
this was an abductive architecture organized around recurring regularities;
it was never an identified historical encoder.
Sources: `GDT051_REVISED_HYBRID_REGISTER_COMPILER_REPORT.md`,
`GDT100_HPR2_THEORY_REVISION_REPORT.md`.

## 4. GDT159–170: better controls expose limits of the instrument

GDT159 improved comparison quality by admitting manuscript-surface,
abbreviation-preserving references. It explicitly failed to find a
capacity-matched corpus simultaneously early-fifteenth-century, Latin,
technical/medical and abbreviation-preserving. Genre-priority, date-priority
and small apothecary panels were kept separate. That remaining gap limits
universal historical-language conclusions.
Source: `GDT159_DIPLOMATIC_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`.

GDT160 found specific left/right operation pairing beyond degree-preserving
graph controls. Voynich's absolute excess density was 31.8 times the largest
external comparator excess. However, every adequately powered external
corpus also had positive pairing excess. The abstract null relabels graph
edges without requiring each relabelled edge to remain a literal edit.
This supports unusual surface-incidence organization under the chosen
representation; it does not establish linguistic morphology or a cipher.
Source: `GDT160_COMPATIBILITY_PAIRING_NULL_REPORT.md`.

The most consequential calibration correction is GDT168 followed by GDT170.
GDT168 constructed both a true lexical codebook and a distributed record code.
Several diagnostics rejected the true lexical codebook, while compatibility
and renderer alignment could look strong in both worlds. Hence negative
host-neighbour prediction does not eliminate real lexical addresses.
GDT170 then removed the oracle component boundaries supplied to GDT168:
480,000 blind parses recovered zero exact true hosts, edges or complete
decompositions in either world, despite partial identity and closure signals.
This is a documented limit of the parsing instrument itself.
Sources: `GDT168_SYNTHETIC_ARCHITECTURE_CALIBRATION_REPORT.md`,
`GDT170_FULL_OBSERVATION_INSTRUMENT_REPORT.md`; targeted clarification in
`VOYNICH_ACTIVE_STATE.md`, lines 1265–1284.

## 5. GDT179–202: concrete f57/f77 semantics are withdrawn

The index records provisional f57 quality-state and f77 process readings,
synthesized in GDT181. I did not reread those original positive reports;
the decisive primary reconciliation is GDT202. It records nonuniqueness after
feature selection, failure to recover the proposed fourfold reference table,
absence of an exact readable homolog, failure of the ot-axis selection, and
renderer transfer of only 1/4 archived cases and 0/4 on the comparable panel.
All seven predictions were reconciled; none recovered a semantic value or
plaintext. Eleven semantic entries were withdrawn, while six formal-only
entries remained. Source: `GDT202_HYBRID_THEORY_RECONCILIATION_REPORT.md`.

The associated broad cipher closure has concrete limits. GDT188 concerns
fixed bijections and specific Latin comparator invariants. GDT189 tests
injective letters after the chosen compiler stripping. GDT190 maps the top
8/16/32/64 hosts to frequent words; GDT191 varies top-eight dictionaries by
known context. GDT192 permits one/two-letter emissions with local search;
GDT193–194 test static consonantal and homophonic variants. All fail their
specified paid comparisons, with unstable keys. These are substantive
negatives for those models, not exhaustive exclusions of variable units,
arbitrary languages or all context-dependent encoding.
Sources: `GDT188_BIJECTIVE_CIPHER_INVARIANCE_REPORT.md` and GDT189–194 reports
listed below. GDT186's Foxton comparison establishes a historical hybrid
analogy, not a Voynich key.

## 6. GDT242–255: apparent recipe roles came from coordinates and size

GDT242 found three physical f82r paragraphs collapsed into one historical role
record because paragraph starts were absent from the filtered frame. GDT244
found five f80r paragraphs collapsed into two. GDT245 propagated the defect:
six dependent layers were suspended, role counts withdrawn, and the semantic
lattice archived as a hypothesis. The underlying formal parses did not acquire
new meanings simply because their coordinates were repaired.
Sources: `GDT242_F82R_PARAGRAPH_COORDINATE_CORRECTION_REPORT.md`,
`GDT244_F80R_PARAGRAPH_COORDINATE_CORRECTION_REPORT.md`,
`GDT245_Q13_ROLE_INHERITANCE_CORRECTION_REPORT.md`.

GDT255 then showed that **94/94** corrected fields matched exactly the rule
“one/two groups = short-argument-like; three or more = instruction-clause-like.”
Recurring host identity added no identifiable semantic-role information.
This is especially strong negative evidence because it identifies the complete
confound, rather than merely reporting an insignificant effect.
Source: `GDT255_Q13_ROLE_SIZE_IDENTIFIABILITY_REPORT.md`.

## 7. GDT297–336: from causal decomposition to modest formal prediction

GDT297 found that for all 59 inspected hosts, renderer tuples were in bijection
with complete source forms. A successful exact-host renderer could therefore
be memorizing whole-form alternants. GDT298 withdrew the causal
host-to-renderer claim and adopted a joint form lexicon with shared tendencies.
Sources: `GDT297_HOST_SURFACE_ALIAS_AUDIT_REPORT.md`,
`GDT298_LEXICALIZED_HYBRID_GRAMMAR_REVISION_REPORT.md`.

Later work recovered a small transferable contribution rather than a universal
grammar. GDT321's s-at-line-start and q-after-DY terms saved 0.023987 held
bits/event and 120.46 charged bits; positive fitted coefficients in all folds
did not mean all folios gained (42/91 did). GDT322 made that rule executable.
GDT327 represented 8,448 groups as 1,676 opaque tuples on 91 folios, explicitly
leaving 2,841 events unlicensed or unknown. This is useful coverage, not 8,448
translated words. Sources: GDT321, GDT322 and GDT327 reports below.

GDT333's aggregate tuple-placement gain failed to transfer universally: only
three of five held registers improved, and no tuple improved in all five.
GDT336's nested placement correction improved top-one retrieval from 1,932
to 1,971 among 6,626 events. That is a real, small formal prediction gain;
the report explicitly denies a standalone decoder or any lexical meaning.
Sources: `GDT333_UNIVERSAL_TUPLE_ROLE_TRANSFER_REPORT.md`,
`GDT336_PLACEMENT_CONDITIONED_TUPLE_RETRIEVAL_REPORT.md`.

## 8. What the history supports, and what remains unaudited

The retained contributions are source-aware representations, strong
nonsemantic prediction baselines, constrained surface regularities, several
genuine held-folio improvements, and explicit demonstrations of confounding
and instrument failure. The record does not support confirmed words, clauses,
POS, cipher units or a uniquely selected production mechanism.

Non-GDT closure labels also require their original scope. IL011 excludes one
fixed local self-citation generator as sufficient for a directional residual
(0/62 eligible controls matched it); it does not exclude every nonsemantic
process. CDA001 found zero admissible external-anchor families under its
eleven-family registry, despite thousands of unused cached groups. Its
“no cached-data route” conclusion concerns that constrained anchor search,
not a proof that no algorithm could ever decipher cached text. Sources:
`experiments/semantic_assumptions/results/il011_timm_direction_control_report.md`
and `cda001_cached_data_route_exhaustion_report.md` in that directory.

This is a selected audit, not a full replication. I have not reviewed every
GDT004–158 local test, the entire external-homolog/visual programme, all
pre-reset ledger entries, or the complete later correction genealogy.
GDT001's current summary and GDT002's later synthesis were read, not all their
individual implementations. Earlier claims about physical lines, source
spaces and decomposition must also be checked against later transcription
corrections before reuse. These gaps are explicit; no fixed research plan is
drawn from this partial history.
