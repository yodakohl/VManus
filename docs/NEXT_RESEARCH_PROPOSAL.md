# Proposed next investigation: repeated passages under different line wrapping

2026-09-05. Status: PROPOSAL_ONLY_NOT_EXECUTED. This follows the user's request
to think further and propose a plan after the historical review and discussion
of whether the manuscript's structure disfavors cipher theories. It selects no
language, cipher key or word meaning. The earlier two-phase proposal stays inactive.

## Decision

Prioritize one short test of an observed but unexplained writing feature before
constructing a new inverse-writing model. Search the admitted source cache for
naturally repeated extended passages whose physical line wrapping differs.
At aligned positions, test whether the GDT800 terminal l/m variation follows
the changed line position or stays associated with the passage.

This is a conditional investigation. We have not established that enough such
passages exist. The first deliverable is a complete, auditable capacity dossier,
not a fitted decoder or a new morphological atlas. If the necessary contrasts
are absent, finish this route promptly rather than relaxing the definition.

## Why this question

GDT800 establishes a strong l/m association with line position, including many
exceptions to an obligatory rule. GDT801 separates part of this association
from higher boundaries. GDT802–803 already examine neighbouring words and show
thin exact-context capacity. Another overall count or neighbour classifier
would add little. Repeated extended passages with changed wrapping would
provide a more controlled comparison of textual and spatial explanations.

The old successful renderers do not supply the answer: GDT297's exact-host
choices were in bijection with whole-form alternants; GDT338 does not establish
stable renderer-invariant equivalence; GDT345 does not establish a general
productive operator. Preserve the observed forms rather than assuming a shared
underlying word or automatically replacing m with l.

External transcription work likewise distinguishes observed shapes from a
known alphabet or secure word segmentation:
[Zandbergen 2022](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3313/keynote1.pdf).
Positions can influence several glyph distributions simultaneously:
[Feaster 2022](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3313/paper12.pdf).
Neither result identifies the manuscript's semantic or cryptographic mechanism.

## First work unit: establish whether the test is possible

1. Freeze the source representation and matching rule before extracting pairs.
   Use the existing source-group atlas and the explicit 179-selector text
   allowance through the guarded query tool. Preserve native separators, line
   and paragraph boundaries, alternate readings and opaque extended entities.
   No EVA byte count is declared the true grapheme count.
2. Detect extended literal parallel contexts with the target terminal masked
   and line-final status hidden. A concrete initial specification to evaluate
   is twelve fixed transliteration atoms on each side, not approximate words,
   inferred roots, synonyms or edited text. The atom lexer must be stated and
   source-checked before this becomes an executable preregistration.
3. Retain every qualifying occurrence, including unchanged targets, opposite
   changes and different scribal hands. Reveal wrapping only after the set is
   fixed. Identify connected repetition families so many pairs generated from
   the same passage do not masquerade as independent observations.
4. Report usable crossed-layout families and conservative independent folio
   blocks. The three readings are sensitivity channels, not three replications.
   Same-hand comparisons are primary where identifiable; otherwise report the
   hand confound and do not silently pool it away.
5. Decide capacity before treating a target effect as evidence. Fix the effect
   worth detecting and power requirement in the executable specification.
   As a lower-bound illustration, even eight uniformly directed independent
   informative contrasts are needed for a two-sided exact sign test at .01;
   this is not adequate power by itself or permission to choose a test after
   seeing the results. Ties and dependency reduce usable information.

This phase is intentionally capped at one fixed extraction and capacity report.
Sparse contexts in GDT802 make failure plausible. Do not shrink flanks, permit
more edits, swap the terminal family or open further pages to rescue it.

## Conditional second unit: test the actual distinction

Only if capacity is adequate, validate the complete extraction-and-comparison
procedure on independent controls: a genuinely layout-conditioned reversible
writing variant, a text-fixed variant under rewrapping, and copied meaningless
strings. GDT168/170 motivate testing the observation pipeline rather than
giving it oracle boundaries. Controls establish whether the comparison can
distinguish the named mechanisms at the available sample size.

Then compare the fixed manuscript pairs, retaining uncertainties and exceptions.
The key outcomes are:

- Variation tracks changed wrapping: support for a layout-associated component
  in these contexts. A common cause such as scribe or copied source can remain;
  this is not automatic proof of causation, allography or semantic identity.
- Variation stays tied to aligned text position despite rewrapping: evidence
  against a layout-only account in those contexts, not proof of morphology.
- Mixed, confounded or too sparse: unresolved; no new normalization or meaning.

The obligatory final-allograph hypothesis was already rejected in GDT800 and
is not being rerun. This test concerns changed wrapping of aligned passages.
Textual alignment is not an authorial semantic reference edge; any later claim
of new relation evidence still requires the applicable GDT388 packet gates.

## Bridge toward decipherment

A supported effect could become a small probabilistic observation model that
retains both written realizations and uncertainty. Freeze that model before
testing further passages, and establish what distinctions it actually explains.
It must not become a global m-to-l replacement or another hand-completed table.

Only then build an inverse-writing hypothesis constrained by the measured
effect. Its objective must be actual sustained plaintext recovery with shared
rules and predictions on material excluded from rule fitting. A compact known-
key control is a competence check; it must not grow into another FST34/V2
synthetic-world construction programme. Reading fluency and roundtrip alone
are insufficient. Language and output values remain to be justified.

Existing external herbal sources stay available but off the critical path:
GDT617–621 establish external entry correspondence, not Voynich plaintext.
Sixteen remaining visual admissions are reserved for a specifically justified
confirmation or source question, not general plant-name searching. f84/f84r
remain sealed. Previously exposed cached pages will not be called untouched.
