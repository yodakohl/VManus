# GDT263 — f80r two-label/paragraph address

## Result

**F80R_TWO_LABELS_PER_PARAGRAPH_ADDRESS_LATTICE_FAILS**

The attractive 10-label / 5-paragraph cardinality does not yield a formal
address lattice.

| reading | aggregate adjusted evidence bits | equal-or-stronger circular shifts | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ZL3b | 5.073 | 30 / 43 | 0.697674 |
| IT2a | 6.611 | 18 / 43 | 0.418605 |
| RF1b | 4.553 | 34 / 43 | 0.790698 |

No individual label has a within-label-adjusted tail below 0.1. Several label
windows are so broadly recurrent that they hit most prose lines; other labels
have no useful hit capacity. The observed page ordering is less informative
than many simple rotations of the same fixed line content.

This rejects the specific generator:

```text
ordered label pair n -> physical paragraph n
```

It does not reject local graphical/prose reuse or the hybrid legend+paragraph
document model. It does remove the most economical attempt to turn f80r's
10:5 cardinality into a translation address.
