# GDT025 Currier-conditioned closure allomorph audit

GDT025 explains why the QJB lead has Currier-B capacity only.  It compares
anonymous DY-resolution forms in Currier A and B using the frozen, f84r-free
GDT016 inventory.

Three fixed diagnostics are reported:

1. terminal residual-host `E` versus `EO` immediately before a DY closure;
2. the frequency of DY-to-DY internal transitions;
3. post-DY behavior of QJB/QKB/LJB/LKB in Currier B and their nearest
   insertional QJAB/QKAB/LJAB/LKAB counterparts in Currier A.

The subtype tests condition on page, current anonymous state, and normalized
position quartile.  Currier-B Q/L tests are repeated after deleting exact-token
repetitions and same-family repetitions across the boundary.  Fisher tests on
the two aggregate 2x2 tables are exact and two-sided.

This is a register-conditioned formal grammar, not a claim about phonology,
dialect identity, or linguistic allomorphy.  “Allomorph” is convenient model
terminology for alternate formal realizations.  No f84r row, image, or payload
is opened.
