# GDT234 — label-prefix residual homolog test

## Outcome

**`LABEL_PREFIX_EXPLAINS_MOST_HOMOLOG_SIMILARITY_CONTENT_RESIDUAL_NOT_RECOVERED`.**

Removing the transferred GDT233 graphical-label prefixes generally destroys,
rather than sharpens, formal similarity among labels in the same human visual
unit.

| scope | pairs | improved | degraded | unchanged | mean similarity delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| at least one prefix stripped | 157 | 18 | **129** | 10 | **-.275** |
| both prefixes stripped; both residuals nonempty | 13 | 2 | **9** | 2 | **-.204** |
| all visual-unit pairs | 391 | 18 | 129 | 244 | -.111 |

This directly localizes the repeated formal material: it is primarily the
graphical/register layer, not a newly exposed shared content residue.

## Key paired structures

- f77v left/right tube positions: `AQAB|LJBA` / `AQABAC` become `|LJBA` /
  `AC`; similarity falls from `.444` to `0`.
- f82r left/right “waterfalls”: under the exposed BACA sensitivity,
  `BACAB` / `BACACA` become `B` / `CA`; similarity falls from `.667` to `0`.
- f78r Northwest/Northeast “grapes”: no strict prefix is removed, so its
  `.625` similarity remains unresolved rather than validated as content.
- f83r hedged left/right tube-end pair has no shared residual before or after
  decomposition.

## Meaning consequence

The strongest label similarities no longer support a direct content gloss.
They support a two-layer generator:

```text
GRAPHICAL_LABEL := REGISTER_OR_LOCAL_CLASS_PREFIX + OPAQUE_RESIDUAL
```

The residual is now the correct place to look for instance/state/content, but
this atlas supplies no recurring residual tied to an independent referent.
`B` and `CA` cannot be called left/right, source/destination, state values, or
anything else.

The result is post-hoc and the prefix learner has only partial label recall.
It nevertheless prevents a concrete error: the shared `dar-`/`BACA-` part of
the f82r pair should not be translated as water or flow merely because the two
labels are near waterfall-like structures.
