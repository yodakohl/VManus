# GDT230 — q13 opaque content-host candidate atlas

## Question

Which exact q13 PAGE_HOST identities are stable enough across folios and
compiler wrappers to deserve an external content test, and does exact host
identity predict the broad field class better than field size, closure, and
record position alone?

This is a lexical-candidate prioritization pass over the public GDT229 lattice.
It assigns no meaning.

## Inventory and models

Each of the 1,896 source groups is one occurrence.  A candidate PAGE_HOST must
occur at least five times on at least three physical folios.  For each candidate
report recurrence, raw surface diversity, wrapper diversity, right-family
diversity, dominant GDT227 abstract field class, and purity.

The held-folio comparison has two fixed predictors:

- `HOST_LOOKUP`: modal abstract class of the exact PAGE_HOST on the other
  q13 folios;
- `PLACEMENT_BASELINE`: modal class on the other folios for the same field
  group-count bin (`1/2/3/4+`), field endpoint (`DY/LINE_END`), and record
  position quartile, with endpoint+size then global backoff.

The class endpoint is itself the external CoReMA position/length likeness.  A
positive host increment therefore nominates an address candidate; it does not
prove lexical semantics.  A stable host whose increment is zero or negative is
classified as placement-stable but nuisance-explained.

## Priority labels

- `EXTERNAL_CONTENT_TEST_PRIORITY`: at least eight occurrences, four folios,
  two wrappers, purity at least .90, and positive held-folio increment;
- `PLACEMENT_STABLE_NUISANCE_EXPLAINED`: same stability criteria but no
  positive increment;
- `WRAPPER_LIMITED_STABLE`: purity at least .90 but fewer than two wrappers;
- `PROVISIONAL`: purity at least .80;
- `WEAK`: everything else.

The thresholds rank this exposed atlas only.  They are not confirmation gates.
All inputs are already f84-free; no global source table is opened.
