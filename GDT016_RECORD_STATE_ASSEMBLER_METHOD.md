# GDT016 record-state assembler

Status: **YOLO exploratory generative grammar**

GDT016 projects every strict, all-reading-agreeing, non-f84r
`CONFIRMED_PROSE` group into one provisional state.  Priority is:

1. terminal `DY` -> `DY_RESOLUTION`;
2. exact O/OT-framed AR, AL, or OL core;
3. residual AR, AL, OL, ED, or KAL family;
4. recovered entry (`d/s/t`), q, or ch/sh/che carrier;
5. `OTHER`.

This hierarchy is a deliberately lossy compiler, not a linguistic parse.
Every original token, source-native family, recovered prefix, closure, and
position remains in the inventory.

Two tests follow.

First, a first-order line-reset state model and a state-unigram model are fit
on all but one physical folio and scored on the unseen folio.  Dirichlet-1/2
smoothing and the global frozen state alphabet are common.  The held lines are
also shuffled twenty times within line, preserving every line multiset and
length, to test ordering rather than composition.

Second, all directed adjacent transitions are compared with 2,000 within-line
state permutations.  These controls preserve page, line, length, and the
complete state multiset.  Search-adjusted values cover the full eligible
transition atlas.  Controls rank exploratory leads and do not kill them.

One directional test is kept outside that exploratory multiplicity: the
already published GDT015 prediction that a `DY_RESOLUTION` state is followed
by one of `OT_AR_LOCAL`, `OT_AL_LOCAL`, or `OT_OL_LOCAL`.  It is pooled before
permutation and evaluated one-sided against the same within-line reorderings.
Its status is an inherited provisional replication, not a newly selected
transition and not semantic evidence.

Frequent raw and run-collapsed line templates are reported with physical-
folio and section coverage.  f84r is excluded before any state is retained.

Claim ceiling: transferable anonymous record states and field ordering only;
no morpheme, sentence syntax, word, POS, sound, language, plaintext, meaning,
or translation.
