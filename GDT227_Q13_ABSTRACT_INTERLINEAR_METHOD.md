# GDT227 — q13 abstract interlinear and cross-register address atlas

## Purpose

GDT226 provisionally localizes a practical-record-like clause/argument
scaffold to q13 and Stars-B.  GDT227 turns that coarse result into a usable
working interlinear.  It does not assign meanings.  It asks which exact
surface groups and HPR2 PAGE_HOST identities repeatedly occupy the externally
projected abstract field classes, and whether those placements recur across
folios and transfer between q13 and Stars-B.

The role projection is frozen from GDT226.  Each source group is parsed by the
published HPR2 rule after learning its O/OT licensing inventory from all
non-f84 source rows.  The retained interlinear contains q13 only; Stars-B is
used only for an aggregate cross-register placement check.

Report:

* every q13 field with its source tokens, PAGE_HOST sequence, compiler cells,
  and frozen abstract role likeness;
* exact raw-token and PAGE_HOST recurrence, cross-folio coverage, and dominant
  role purity;
* leave-one-folio modal-role prediction when the exact identity has appeared
  on another folio;
* transfer of exact PAGE_HOST modal role between q13 and Stars-B.

These are placement statistics.  Because the external role is itself a
function of position and span, high purity is not independent semantic
evidence.  Hand and section also distinguish q13 from Stars-B.  No host may be
given an English gloss from this atlas.

Every f84 row is rejected from the raw source stream before its columns are
parsed.  The earlier limited public-metadata exposure is disclosed; no new f84
access is authorized.
