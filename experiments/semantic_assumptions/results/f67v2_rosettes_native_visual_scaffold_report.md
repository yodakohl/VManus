# f67v2 / Rosettes native visual scaffold

Decision: **COARSE 1/4/4 SCAFFOLD RETAINED; EXACT TRANSFER STOPS**.

The official Yale images support one genuinely new manuscript-internal
relation. f67v2 and the Rosettes foldout each occupy a nine-position scaffold:
one central position, four positions on the principal axes, and four corner
positions. This is more specific than circularity alone and supports a shared
visual-vocabulary or scaffold candidate.

The detailed diagrams are not isomorphic. Rosettes contains nine full circular
compartments joined by a road network. f67v2 instead has one central
square-rosette, four directly attached axial structures, and four detached
corner face clusters that point inward without forming nine equivalent
compartments. Its text is divided among floating blocks, radial lines, and
labels; Rosettes distributes much larger and unequal text inventories within
rosettes, around rings, and along roads.

Sun-like, T-in-O-like, connected-dot, scalloped, and fanning motifs occur in
the two drawings, but they are repeated, differently embedded, or incomplete
at damaged margins. They do not independently select one 0/90/180/270-degree
rotation. In particular, the proposed 180-degree relation does not yield a
complete landmark-by-landmark or text-slot correspondence.

The result therefore retains only a coarse `CENTER / AXIS / CORNER` scaffold
candidate. It does not authorize aligning or scoring individual strings from
the two pages, and it does not show that one page is a key, legend, map, or
explanation of the other.

This is source-bound native AI visual inspection, not literal human
annotation. No OCR, automated transcription, CLIP, embedding,
image-similarity score, batch recognition, automatic glyph reading, or
decoder claim was used. No object identity, direction, place, map, word,
sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation follows.
