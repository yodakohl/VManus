# f17r / f116v Voynich-surface recurrence result

Decision: `PASS_F116V_COMPONENT_RECURRENCE_F17R_UNIQUE_HOLD_EQUIVALENCE`

The two f116v marginal forms are not exotic one-off strings. Exact `oror`
occurs at nine physical loci, including six confirmed-prose loci, and exact
`sheey` occurs at 153 physical loci, including 146 confirmed-prose loci. The
two forms also share one other prose row, f112v.10, although not adjacently or
in the same order. Exact adjacent `oror sheey` remains unique to f116v.1.

The f17r forms behave differently. Exact `oteeeon` and `oiil` occur only at
f17r.13 in the frozen interlinear, and exact adjacent `oteeeon oiil` has no
other locus.

The physical-locus unit matters: the two target rows appear in ZL3b and RF1b,
but those readings are two descriptions of the same manuscript occurrence,
not replications. IT2a omits the marginal rows.

The result establishes that the f116v marginal writer used two ordinary
manuscript surfaces before continuing in plain-script-looking writing. It does
not show that either surface translates, glosses, or labels that continuation.
The unique f17r forms are merely unattested elsewhere; uniqueness supplies no
reading. No word meaning, language, cipher, plaintext, or translation follows.
