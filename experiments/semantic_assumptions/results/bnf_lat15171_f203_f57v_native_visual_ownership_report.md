# BnF Latin 15171 f203 / f57v native visual ownership

Decision: **SOURCE SECTOR CO-OWNERSHIP PASSES; f57v TRANSFER STOPS**.

The earlier NAL 112 metadata check found a strong readable comparison family
but could not tell whether its four directions and four season-plus-quality
groups belonged to the same four diagram positions. NAL 112's public IIIF
manifest is only partial. Biblissima independently catalogues BnF Latin 15171
f203 under the same title, *Saisons et humeurs*, and links the full official
Gallica scan.

The Latin 15171 diagram resolves the source-side question. One circle is
divided into four large sectors around a small central pinwheel. Each sector
visibly contains a direction, a season, and the season's two qualities. The
directions proceed clockwise from the top as East, South, West, and North.
Thus the readable registers are not merely neighboring lists: they share a
four-sector ownership coordinate.

This still does not create a Voynich key. The comparison diagram contains no
four human figures. On f57v, four figures surround the central rosette; four
short inscriptions lie near those figures, while four other inscriptions
occupy the gaps between figures and point outward. Those two target registers
are angularly offset, and no leader, bracket, enclosure, or other drawn device
connects one figure, one nearby inscription, and one inter-figure inscription.
No source-target start or orientation is independently fixed.

The new image therefore strengthens only the existing fourfold
year-direction-quality diagram-family prior. It closes the source's internal
ownership gap but leaves the target's ownership gap intact. Assigning East,
South, West, North, a season, or a hot/cold/dry/moist value to any f57v string
would require choosing a rotation and an unshown relation.

This is source-bound native AI visual inspection, not literal human
annotation. Exact source spellings came from previously validated human
Mandragore metadata. No OCR, CLIP, embedding, image-similarity score,
automatic glyph transcription, fitted target statistic, or decoder claim was
used. No direction, season, humour, quality, element, word, sound, language,
