# EBA001 raw multi-exposure marginalia acquisition

Decision: **PASS — THREE-CAPTURE STABLE DARK TRACE; MECHANISM UNRESOLVED**.

VManus acquired six previously unopened public 16-bit TIFF capture products:
three file-labelled `MB365UV` captures each for f17r and f116v. Every object is
8176×6132, single-channel, uncompressed, nominally 16-bit, and 740 dpi. Exact
object IDs, byte counts, embedded TIFF `DateTime` values, and SHA-256 hashes are
retained. The 602,385,474 source bytes and all derived crops remained outside
the repository.

The registered f17r upper marginal line and the corrected f116v target—the
initial mixed-script span of the lowest of four principal marginal lines—are
visibly dark at corresponding apparent manuscript coordinates in all three
captures. Gross mounting and edge shadows show capture-specific patterns. This
establishes repeat-capture persistence and rules out a single-capture transient,
a processed-composite-only artifact, and those particular visibly moving
shadows as complete explanations.

It does **not** establish an absorptive mark or ink and does not exclude static
relief or a fixed shadow. The public filenames and TIFF metadata do not identify
lamp direction, and the display of each capture was independently stretched;
no radiometric comparison is claimed. On f17r, a broad region of differing
tonality overlaps the middle-right part of the line, where strokes have lower
visible contrast. No material identity, causation, or layer order follows.

This is the required new external observable, but not a semantic anchor. It
identifies no chemistry, date, writer, character, reading, gloss relation,
sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
