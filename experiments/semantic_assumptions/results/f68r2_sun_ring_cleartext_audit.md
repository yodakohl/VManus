# Public f68r2 Sun-ring cleartext audit

Decision: **STOP_NO_READABLE_CLEARTEXT_ANCHOR**.

The public Quire 9 catalogue says that the Sun-ring text only *appears* to contain non-Voynich characters and is almost impossible to read. The public transcription guidance is stronger: at this exact location it cannot determine which marks belong to the unknown script and which, if any, are cleartext.

The three native manual readings do not supply a consensus plaintext ending. ZL3b and RF1b preserve four special illegible marks, mapped by STA to `Ys Yp Yn Yd`; IT2a instead reads an ordinary final `koiin` group. These are alternate readings of one locus, not independent votes.

Therefore proposed readings such as `Suna` are not admissible anchors. No SUN word, language, sound value, cipher, plaintext, or translation follows. Reopen only if a qualified human palaeographic source reads the complete sequence with explicit uncertainty and independent manuscript support.

Public sources: [Quire 9 catalogue](https://www.voynich.nu/q09/index.html) and [transcription special topics](https://www.voynich.nu/extra/sp_transcr.html).
