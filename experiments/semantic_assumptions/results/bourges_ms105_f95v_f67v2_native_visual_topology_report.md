# Bourges MS 105 f95v / f67v2 native visual topology

Decision: **TOPOLOGY MISMATCH; NO f67v2 TRANSFER**.

Native inspection of the official IRHT image resolves the second Biblissima
wind-rose candidate. Bourges BM MS 105 f95v contains twelve head positions in
one continuous outer annulus. Each head blows inward through a visibly drawn
radial cone toward the inner wheel. Four circular stations on the principal
axes give the interior an explicit fourfold organization. The source does not
show four detached corner clusters or a four-crowned-major/eight-minor head
hierarchy.

That geometry does not match f67v2. The fixed target projection has detached
corner clusters of 3, 4, 4, and 3 faces clockwise from northwest, fourteen
faces total, rather than twelve positions in one annulus. Its four central-axis
strings do not visibly connect to the face clusters. The diagrams therefore
share a wind-directional circular genre and fourfold organization, but not a
one-to-one unit inventory or ownership topology.

There is consequently no common slot coordinate, start, or orientation from
which a source inscription could be transferred. Bourges joins
Sainte-Geneviève MS 1029 as evidence for the wider twelve-wind-wheel family,
but it is not an exact f67v2 homologue and supplies no key.

This is source-bound native AI visual inspection, not literal human
annotation. No OCR, automated transcription, CLIP, embedding,
image-similarity score, batch recognition, automatic glyph reading, or
decoder claim was used. No direction, wind name, word, sound, language,
cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation follows.
