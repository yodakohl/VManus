automated comparator scores, batch image analysis, PCA/multispectral image
processing, and an AI-lab workflow. Those inputs violate the active ban on
automated/neural image interpretation and cached machine-derived visual
features. The paper's later constitution, lunar-mansion, status, and
cross-reference assignments are interpretations rather than independently
readable authorial values. Its clock positions and nearby labels therefore do
not supply the required label-owner-value key.

The separately public Stolfi/VIB zodiac inventories remain useful human layout
descriptions, but their clockwise label ownership is an inferred convention,
not an author-visible connector, and their proposed `day?` values are not
readable translations. They do not repair this lead.

No image/OCR output, candidate token association, or claimed semantic value
