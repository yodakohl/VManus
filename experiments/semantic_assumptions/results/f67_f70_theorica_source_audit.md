# TPQ001 public-source audit

Decision: `STOP_NO_ONE_TO_ONE_THEORICA_LABEL_DONOR`.

The proposed seven-circle/*Theorica planetarum* analogy does not supply seven
external names that can be transferred to the Voynich diagrams.

The official catalogue dates Gotha Chart. A 472 to about 1460 and records 17
disk diagrams. Its consecutive f3r--f8r titles are `Circulus orbis signorum`,
`Circulus anni`, `Circulus Saturni`, `Circulus Jouis`, `Circulus Martis`,
`Circulus Veneris`, and `Circulus augum planetarum`. That is a seven-diagram
run, but only four titles are individual classical planets; Sun, Moon, and
Mercury are not the other three titles. The same catalogue places the more
elaborate planet instruments elsewhere in the book rather than in one
seven-page name sequence. [Official Gotha catalogue](https://bilder.manuscripta-mediaevalia.de/hs//projekt-Gotha-pdfs/Chart_A_472.pdf)

The wider *Theorica* tradition does not repair this. Scholarship describes the
canonical figures by shared motion models: Sun, Moon, lunar nodes, the three
superior planets together, and Venus/Mercury together. Variants reorganize or
add figures, so “seven circles” does not imply seven one-to-one planetary
plates. [Pantin, *The Illustrated Printed Page as a Tool for Thinking*](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-11317-8_2)

There is an additional uniqueness warning inside the public Voynich
catalogue: both f68v1 and f70r2 are human-described as having an apparent Sun
face. Those descriptions are not authorial identities, but they prevent us
from using the visible Sun motif as a unique seven-item key. The full
f67--f73 scope is public catalogue data, not a user-supplied grouping.
[Public Quire 9 catalogue](https://www.voynich.nu/q09/index.html),
[Public Quire 10 catalogue](https://www.voynich.nu/q10/index.html)

The older proposal itself remains historically useful because it correctly
points to astronomical paper instruments as a comparison class, but its exact
Gotha block analogy fails the label-donor requirement. [Published proposal](https://ciphermysteries.com/2020/03/30/might-gotha-chart-a-472-be-connected-to-voynich-q9)

This does not show that the Voynich diagrams are non-planetary or
non-astronomical. It only blocks the shortcut “seven circular pages = the
seven classical planets in a recoverable external order.” No Voynich word,
page, or diagram receives an English label.

Reopen only if a human-catalogued contemporary or earlier source supplies
seven explicitly named one-to-one diagram units plus a non-post-hoc complete-
block order or topology correspondence.
