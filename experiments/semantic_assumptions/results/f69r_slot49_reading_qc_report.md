# f69r.49 manual reading QC

Status: **`ed` FAVORED; POST-HOC SENSITIVITY ONLY**

Direct human inspection of two separately digitized scan families favors
`ed` over IT2a's `em` at f69r.49. The 2014 high-resolution scan shows an
initial `e`-like component followed by a loop-and-descender component
consistent with the known central `d` at f69r.45 after allowing for radial
orientation. The independent 2004 scan is compatible with it. This agrees
with the pre-existing exact-locus note that René confirmed `ed` against the
original manuscript.

The source record remains transparent: ZL3b has `e[d:g]`, RF1b has `ed`, and
IT2a has `em`. These are alternate readings of one glyph, not votes. A
14-check provenance validator reproduces the two image hashes and dimensions,
the independent 2004/2014 digitization record, the f69r Yale-object mapping,
all three source lines, and the original-manuscript annotation. It does not
automate or pretend to validate the visual judgment.

F69C001 remains a validated nonconfirmation. The QC authorizes only one named,
post-hoc `ed`-resolved sensitivity calculation; it does not authorize changing
the source transcription, rerunning the frozen target as confirmation, or
inferring a joined orientation, start, handedness, sound, word, root, lexeme,
language, plaintext, direction name, or translation.
