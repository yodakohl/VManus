# Haruna 2011 manuscript-K f45v source clarification

Decision: **STOP_K45V_ART_HOMOLOGUE_NO_SHARED_INSCRIPTION_OR_SLOT_MAP**.

The source prescreen resolves `K` as British Library Harley MS 4940, a complete fourteenth-century *Breviari d'amor* witness. The British Library independently describes it as Matfre Ermengaud's work, mid-fourteenth century, in French and Old Occitan.

`Illustration n° 29` is the Breviari illustration inventory item after verse 6112, `La rose des vents`; it is not a displayed figure 29 in Haruna's paper, whose figure captions stop at 22. Footnote 138 locates that inventory item at K f45v.

The comparison is explicitly art-historical rather than inscription-identical: the paper says that details explained by inscriptions differ between the two authors, while similar pictorial ideas are used. Therefore Sainte-Geneviève's four central cardinal names cannot be copied onto K, and neither source supplies a shared individual bust/name map, four-by-three placement, start, orientation, or f67v2 slot coordinate.

Only public human catalogue prose and embedded PDF text layers were read. No figure or manuscript image was rendered or inspected; no OCR, automated vision, or decoder claim was used. No wind, direction, inscription, bust, face, slot, label, word, sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation transfers.
