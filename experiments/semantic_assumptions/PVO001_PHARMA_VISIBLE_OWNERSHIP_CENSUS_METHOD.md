# PVO001 pharmaceutical visible-ownership census method

## Purpose

PLC001 stopped before label identity because existing human comments yielded
only two mixed physical folios, one mixed page, and zero opposite-state label
pairs with identical source-group length vectors. PVO001 asks a prior source
question using direct inspection of the complete pharmaceutical page block:
does the manuscript itself display singular author-visible ownership devices
that can add independently owned plant-fragment labels?

This is an image-only capacity census. It opens no Voynich transcription,
label surface, formal family, root, role, or score.

## Complete physical-canvas panel

The public source atlas identifies pharmaceutical logical page parts
f88r/f88v, f89r1/f89r2/f89v1/f89v2, and f99r through f102v2. Yale's official
manifest exposes these on 12 author-visible canvases rather than 12 simple
recto/verso pages: `88r`; `88v and 89r`; two `89v` parts; `99r`; `99v`; `100r`;
`100v and 101r`; two `101v` parts, the second also containing `102r`; and two
`102v` parts. The manifest canvas, not a reconstructed logical page, is the
visual unit. Bind all 12 exact Yale canvas IDs and labels before opening any
image body. No sampling or visual selection is allowed.

Opaque ID is `PV` plus the first eight uppercase hexadecimal characters of
SHA-256 `PVO001_OPAQUE|<canvas-id>`.

## Frozen direct-inspection rubric

Inspect each official canvas exactly once in opaque-ID order. Writing qualifies
as a `SINGULAR_VISIBLE_OWNER` only if an author-visible device uniquely joins
it to one plant fragment: a leader/connector touches both, a bracket or cell
encloses exactly that writing and fragment, or the writing is placed inside the
drawn body of exactly one fragment. Ordinary whitespace proximity, nearest
neighbor, row alignment, text between multiple fragments, paragraph prose,
and modern catalogue arrows/comments do not qualify.

Record for each canvas only:

- `OWNER_PRESENT` when at least one singular visible owner exists;
- `OWNER_ABSENT` when the resolved canvas contains writing and fragments but no
  qualifying device;
- `UNCERTAIN` when damage/resolution prevents the distinction.

Also record the number of distinct qualifying owner devices, without reading
their contents. Do not classify root-only/leaf-bearing state in this pass and
do not map a device to a transcription locus.

## Capacity gate

PVO001 reopens a formal owner-state design only if:

- at least four distinct physical canvases contain a singular visible owner;
- both q15 and q19 contain at least one;
- at least one canvas whose mapped logical source pages exclude the already
  mixed PLC001 folios f89 and f102 contains one;
- at least six total distinct visible-owner devices exist; and
- at most two canvases are uncertain.

Failure closes this acquisition route before any label or formal text access.
Pass licenses only a second prospectively frozen object-state/ownership mapping
pass; it does not license transcription access yet.

## Claim ceiling

PVO001 can establish only the presence and distribution of author-visible
singular ownership devices. It cannot say what any label names, identify a
plant or plant part, assign ROOT or LEAF, establish wordhood, language,
plaintext, meaning, or translation.
