# LM002 leaf-margin / `cho-che` regime method

## Question and route boundary

LM001Y established that a frozen source-native leaf-margin panel has adequate
visual support: 29 `SMOOTH`, 13 `TOOTHED`, and two excluded `UNCERTAIN` physical
folios satisfy every original capacity gate. LM002 asks one narrow question:
does that visual state align with the already confirmed formal high/low
`cho/che` page-side regime?

This is not a vocabulary search. The only formal target is the previously
published `SOURCE_ALL_SEPARATORS` folio-side state from
`parisel_cho_che_folio_states.tsv`, with both its EM and literal-threshold
assignments already fixed. No literal string, root, role, family, member,
template, candidate word, plant identity, or English gloss may be selected or
scored. The three ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b readings are alternate descriptions of one
manuscript and must never be treated as independent samples.

## Frozen physical panel

Join the published LM001 held observations, LM001X extension observations, and
LM001Y complete-residual observations to their already frozen selection
metadata. Exclude the two `UNCERTAIN` folios before formal-target access. Retain
all 42 admitted physical folios, their `TOOTHED`/`SMOOTH` label, acquisition
phase, Currier, hand, quire, folio-rank quartile, page side, and exact page ID.

The score-blind capacity artifact must contain no `cho`, `che`, rate, count,
threshold state, EM state, responsibility, formal target hash derived from row
content, score, effect, or p-value. It may bind the already public whole-file
SHA-256 of the target table without parsing it.

## Two exact conditional views

Only cells containing both leaf states are movable. Whole physical-folio leaf
labels are permuted inside each cell with their cell state counts fixed.

1. `PHASE_QUARTILE_SIDE`: exact cell is acquisition phase × Currier × hand ×
   folio-rank quartile × recto/verso. The frozen geometry must have five mobile
   cells, 13 mobile folios, and exactly 108 assignments.
2. `PHASE_QUIRE`: exact cell is acquisition phase × Currier × hand × quire. The
   frozen geometry must have five mobile cells, 16 mobile folios, and exactly
   324 assignments.

The more restrictive phase × Currier × hand × quire × quartile × page-side
partition has only 36 assignments and cannot reach `.01`; it is recorded as an
unscored capacity diagnostic, not silently promoted to inference. A broader
phase × Currier × hand × quartile view has 5,120 assignments and is likewise a
diagnostic only.

## Frozen target statistic

For each exact view, assignment, and reading, compute in every mobile cell the
mean high-state outcome on assigned `TOOTHED` folios minus its mean on assigned
`SMOOTH` folios. Average cell contrasts equally. Call the reading effects
`D_ZL`, `D_IT`, and `D_RF`. The common-direction statistic is

`T = max(min(D_ZL,D_IT,D_RF), min(-D_ZL,-D_IT,-D_RF))`.

Thus mixed reading directions cannot be called support. The inclusive exact
tail is the fraction of the complete conditional orbit having `T >= T_obs`.
The observed assignment is included. Fixed, nonmobile cells contribute
nothing. The EM state is primary. The independently fixed literal-threshold
state is a required robustness view, not a second sample. The same calculation
on `classifiable_groups` is a prespecified text-volume confound control.

## Target-free calibration and target gates

Before any row from the formal target table is opened, an independent
nonimporting calibration must reconstruct the geometry and show:

- zero full passes in 64 state-label-independent null worlds;
- eight of eight passes for a distributed exact-state plant and eight of eight
  for a reduced plant frozen before calibration;
- zero passes for one-cell, one-Currier, one-phase, page-side-only,
  quire-only, text-volume-only, reading-disagreement, and one-phase-reversed
  adversaries;
- scalar/optimized, row-order, reading-order, state-complement, and exact-orbit
  invariance.

A target pass requires all of the following, without amendment after target
access:

1. EM-state inclusive exact `p <= .01` in both exact views;
2. common-direction minimum effect `T >= .25` in both exact views;
3. the two exact views choose the same direction;
4. at least four of five mobile-cell contrasts have that direction in every
   reading in each exact view;
5. Currier-A and Currier-B mobile contrasts have the same direction in every
   reading under `PHASE_QUARTILE_SIDE`;
6. at least three of the four available acquisition-phase × Currier blocks
   have strictly positive aligned contrast in every reading, with none below
   `-.10` after orienting to the common direction;
7. the largest absolute mobile-cell contribution is at most `.40` of the sum
   of absolute mobile-cell contributions in every reading and exact view;
8. the literal-threshold state retains the same direction, `T >= .20`, and
   inclusive `p <= .02` in both views;
9. `classifiable_groups` has no aligned association at `p <= .05` in either
   exact view; otherwise stop as text-volume-confounded.

Synthetic calibration failure stops LM002 without formal-target access. Target
failure closes only this fixed cross-modal regime test. No target-side
diagnostic, subgroup, alternative feature, literal form, or threshold may be
mined afterward.

## Claim ceiling

Even a complete pass would establish only that one already confirmed formal
page-side regime covaries with a source-bound leaf-margin state beyond the two
frozen nuisance partitions. It would not show that the prose describes the
plant or its leaves, would not assign `cho`, `che`, `o`, `e`, or any other form
a meaning, and would not identify a plant, sound, language, cipher, plaintext,
or translation.
