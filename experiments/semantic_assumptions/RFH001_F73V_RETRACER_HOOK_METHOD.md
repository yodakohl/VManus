## Allowed outcomes

1. `ORIGINAL_HOOK_VISIBLE_BENEATH_HOOKLESS_RETRACING`
2. `CURRENT_F_LIKE_GLYPH_WITHOUT_SEPARABLE_ORIGINAL_HOOK`
3. `UNRESOLVED_SOURCE_IMAGE`

Outcome 1 requires all five gates:

- the Grove-8 outer-band label and f-like second glyph are unambiguously
  localized;
- a bounded hook-shaped authorial ink feature is visible at the target glyph;
- the hook is physically continuous with a lighter under-stroke rather than a
  neighboring letter, star line, nymph contour, stain, bleedthrough, wash, or
  compression artifact;

- overlap, differing ink boundary, interruption, or another physical feature
  supports the hook-bearing stroke predating the hookless retracing.

If only one current f-like glyph is visible without separable states, choose
outcome 2. If localization or layer chronology cannot be resolved, choose
outcome 3.
