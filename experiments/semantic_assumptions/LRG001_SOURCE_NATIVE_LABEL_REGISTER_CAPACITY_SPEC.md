# LRG001 source-native label-register capacity

Status: `REGISTERED_CAPACITY_ONLY`

## Question

Can the complete, three-reading-consensus STA-family representation support a
fair held-physical-folio test of a manuscript-wide `L`-versus-prose structural
register?

This is not a test that labels are names or nouns.  `L` and `P` are manual
IVTFF layout roles.  A future positive result could establish only a
transferable **label-associated construction profile**.  Only after that
result could the frozen profile be projected, without retraining, onto the
corrected drawing-reset prose segments as an identifier-like structural
coordinate.

## Capacity rules

Use only rows in `source_sta_family_consensus_groups.tsv` with
`strict_zero_alternative == 1`.

- Positive rows: `kind == L`.
- Control rows: `kind == P` and `grammar_scope == CONFIRMED_PROSE`.
- A cell is the exact `(page, symbol_count)` pair.
- Keep a cell only when it contains both positive and control rows.
- The physical folio is the leading `f` plus decimal folio number, so foldout
  panels such as `f102r1` and `f102v2` belong to one physical folio `f102`.
- Preserve every row in every eligible cell.  Do not select nearest controls,
  equalize classes, inspect family identities, or use EVA spelling.
- Future assignment nulls must permute the fixed count of `L` states inside
  each exact cell.  Physical folios, not rows or readings, are inferential
  units.

The capacity gate requires at least 300 label rows, 100 mixed cells, 15
physical folios, four sections, at least two multi-folio sections, and at least
one section represented by five physical folios.  These thresholds are fixed
before a family surface, member code, classifier score, or individual form is
opened.

## Novelty boundary

This route is distinct only in the following narrow sense:

- the archived label-content coordinate used the unavailable partial-parser
  root layer rather than complete source-native STA families;
- the direct-locus object tests used a sparse legacy annotation subset and
  were later ruled invalid for closing a stem route;
- EAS004 used 118 explicit-array slots rather than all manual `L` rows; and
- the source-native diagnostic-transition target asked whether a fixed prose
  edge set transferred to diagnostic text, not whether labels differ from
  exact same-page, same-length prose.

No generic word-frequency, label-recurrence, illustration-tag, object-owner,
or language-class test is reopened.

## Claim ceiling

Capacity can authorize target-blind calibration only.  It supplies no label
profile, identifier, name, noun, part of speech, object ownership, language,
word meaning, plaintext, or translation.
