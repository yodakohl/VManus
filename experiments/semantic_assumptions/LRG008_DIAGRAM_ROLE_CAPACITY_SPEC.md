# LRG008 label-versus-diagram-role projection capacity

Status: `REGISTERED_ASSOCIATION_UNOPENED_CAPACITY`.

## Question

LRG001 established a source-native construction profile that separates manual
IVTFF label (`L`) groups from exact-page/exact-length prose on held physical
folios. LRG008 asks a new role question: does that already frozen profile also
separate `L` from other diagram writing (`C` circular and `R` radial), or is it
only a generic short diagram-text profile?

This does not test C-to-L string echo, infer object ownership, or relearn a
profile. The eventual target must reconstruct the two LRG001 parity profiles
unchanged and apply the opposite-parity profile to each target folio.

## Score-blind capacity

Read only these fields from the complete consensus-group table:
`consensus_group_id`, `locus`, `page`, `section`, `kind`,
`grammar_scope`, `strict_zero_alternative`, and `symbol_count`. Do not read or
store `family_surface`, member codes, EVA, a profile score, or a feature.

- Keep `strict_zero_alternative == 1` and `kind in {L,C,R}`.
- Form exact `(page, symbol_count)` cells.
- Retain a cell only when it contains at least one `L` row and at least one
  `C` or `R` row. Preserve every selected row.
- A physical folio is the leading `f` plus its decimal number.
- Before any family identity or score is opened, retain only folios with at
  least three mixed cells, eight rows, three `L` rows, and three `C/R` rows.

The panel passes only with at least 40 cells, 280 rows, 140 rows in each side,
ten pages, six physical folios, three sections, both `C` and `R` controls,
three folios supporting each control role, and at least two odd plus two even
folios. The complete product of within-cell fixed-quota assignments must have
log size at least 100.

Capacity may emit row IDs, metadata, exact roles, quotas, counts, hashes, and
gates. It may not emit source-native family identities or their association
with role.

## Authorized successor only on pass

Freeze and calibrate a target-blind rank statistic before reconstructing any
target score. Within each cell, average-rank the fixed opposite-parity LRG001
profile scores. Contrast `L` with pooled `C/R`, averaging cells within pages,
pages within folios, then folios. A deterministic fixed-quota null, role-block
sensitivities, folio deletion/concentration, section, parity, null, distributed
signal, one-folio, one-role, one-section, one-parity, page-only, length-only,
and random-folio controls are mandatory. Thresholds must be frozen from those
synthetic worlds before the manuscript profile projection is opened.

On a validated pass, the strongest allowed claim is that the pre-existing
label-associated structural profile distinguishes manual labels from other
diagram text on this fixed panel. It still does not establish identifier,
name, noun, owner, object, word, sound, language, meaning, plaintext, or
translation.
