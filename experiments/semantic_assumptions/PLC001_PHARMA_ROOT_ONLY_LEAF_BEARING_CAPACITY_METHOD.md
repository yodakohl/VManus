# PLC001 pharmaceutical root-only versus leaf-bearing capacity

This filler-blind audit asks whether existing human exact-locus comments can
support a fair formal comparison of pharmaceutical labels attached to
root-only fragments versus leaf-bearing fragments.

Select only unhedged exact-local `@Lf` rows. Parse individually introduced
`plant <page>[row,item] - description` clauses. Retain a row only when its
directional ownership clause references exactly one described plant and does
not say `between`. Classify that owned description as `ROOT_ONLY` when it
mentions root/tuber/bulb and no leaf, flower, stem, twig, sprout, berry, or
inflorescence; classify it as `LEAF_BEARING` when it explicitly mentions leaf
or leaves. All other descriptions are excluded.

Before any family/member association, require a current source-native mapping,
strict zero alternatives, and exact family agreement reconstructed from all
three STA readings. Capacity requires at least three physical folios per
state, three mixed folios, two mixed pages, and at least twelve within-folio
opposite-state pairs with the same ordered source-group length vector.

No label surface, family/member association, root/role, classifier, effect, or
null may be computed. Human `root-only` and `leaf-bearing` drawing descriptions
are structural object states, not Voynich words.
