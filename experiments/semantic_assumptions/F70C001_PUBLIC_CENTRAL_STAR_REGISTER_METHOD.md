# F70C001 — public central-star register audit

Status before execution: `REGISTERED_DESCRIPTIVE_SOURCE_AUDIT`.

## Question

Public human catalogues describe the adjacent f69r and f70r1 diagrams as
having a six-pointed central star with six text items between the arms.  Is
that repeated layout accompanied by a repeated compact text register, and is
any such compactness merely a generic inner-versus-outer ring effect?

## Frozen evidence

- the cached public `voynich.nu` Quire 10 and Quire 8 HTML sources;
- the public manual ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b EVA transcriptions;
- the public Stolfi exact-locus comments and their validated current-locus
  projection.

No image decoding, OCR, automated vision, retained formal parser, root,
grammar role, or English gloss is used.  ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate
readings of one manuscript.

## Arrays

The source-defined local arrays are:

- f69r `K1`: six central-sector labels between star arms;
- f69r `E1`: 22 surrounding outward radial items;
- f70r1 `X1`: six inner labels between star arms;
- f70r1 `Y1`: nine surrounding inward radial phrases;
- f70v2 `S1` and `S2`: the public inner and outer zodiac-label bands, used
  only as a same-source comparator against a universal inner/outer rule.

For each raw manual reading, count only source groups separated by confident
spaces, uncertain small spaces, or drawing interruptions.  Editorial glyph
entities such as `@152;` remain inside a group and may not be silently dropped
as the incomplete retained parser once did.

## Decisions and ceiling

This is a descriptive audit, not a discovery p-value.  It may retain
`LOCAL_CENTRAL_STAR_COMPACT_REGISTER` only if all six central items are one
source group in all three readings, both surrounding arrays have a larger
mean group count in all three readings, and the public prose independently
states the corresponding letter/word/phrase roles.

It may not generalize compactness to all inner circles unless the f70v2
inner band is shorter than the outer band in every reading.  It may not join
the f69r glyphs, align them to the f70r1 words, identify planets, apsides,
spheres, winds, numbers, initials, abbreviations, words, meanings, plaintext,
or a translation.
