## Strict formal eligibility

The drawing projection joins the existing human/current-locus crosswalk by
source record ID. A row is strict only when it is primary-eligible, its page
and `RING:GROVE_N` position agree, and every consensus group at the locus is a
complete `kind=L`, `DIAGNOSTIC_NONPROSE`, zero-alternative group with exact
`1..N` indexing. Group identities and formal features are not constructed or
scored in this screen.

Only strict page-ring strata containing both TAIL and NO_TAIL are candidate
strata. A target is worth preregistering only if at least two physical folios
remain and every folio has at least two strict TAIL and two strict NO_TAIL
labels. This prevents a claimed cross-folio bridge from depending on one
positive drawing.

The sole additional public f70 tailed-star note (`f70v1.S1` inner #1) is
checked as a possible rescue, but its current crosswalk row is not primary-
