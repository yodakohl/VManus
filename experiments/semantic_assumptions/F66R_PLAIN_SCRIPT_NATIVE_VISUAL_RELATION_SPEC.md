## Bounded questions

1. Is the bottom plain-script-looking cluster physically integrated with a
   Voynich-script string as an equation, correction, overwrite, continuation,
   or pointer?
2. Do the two strings share one baseline or a repeated one-to-one alignment?
3. Is either string visibly attached to the small illustration in a way that
   creates an explicit bilingual relation?

## Decision rule

A bilingual or correction lead requires an author-visible relation between
the readable and Voynich-script strings: an equality/correction device,
pointer, interlinear alignment, overwriting, or repeated paired placement.
Mere proximity in the same lower-margin illustration area is insufficient.

## Exclusions and ceiling

No OCR, automated transcription, CLIP, embedding, image-similarity score,
external decoder claim, or proposed marginal reading is used. The visual
observations are machine-authored rather than literal human annotation.

The result may classify only physical relation. It cannot establish a reading
of the plain script, identify the drawn object or animal, or assign a word,
sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation to either string.
