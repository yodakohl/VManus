## Bounded questions

1. Does the readable comparison diagram visibly group one direction, one
   season, and two qualities inside each of four sectors?
2. Are those registers co-owned by the same source sectors, rather than merely
   listed elsewhere on the page?
3. Does the source also reproduce f57v's four human figures, four nearby
   inscriptions, four inter-figure radial inscriptions, and their ownership?
4. Does any common start/orientation map one source sector to one f57v figure
   and both associated target inscriptions without rotation or choice?

## Decision rule

The source-side ownership relation passes only if the four season, direction,
and two-quality groups visibly share four sectors. A transfer to f57v requires
the same owner class and register geometry, an explicit target relation, and a
fixed common orientation. Source-family agreement alone cannot gloss a
Voynich string.

## Exclusions and claim ceiling

No OCR, CLIP, embedding, batch recognition, image-similarity score, glyph
transcription, decoder claim, or fitted target statistic is used. Exact source
spellings come from the previously validated human Mandragore metadata, not
from machine transcription of the image. Native visual observations are
machine-authored rather than literal human annotation.

A pass may establish only a readable source-side year-direction-quality
ownership topology and a source-family prior for f57v. It cannot assign any
direction, season, humour, quality, element, word, sound, language, cipher,
plaintext, meaning, or translation to a Voynich locus.
