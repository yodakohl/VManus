# RFO001 recurrent figure-label ownership method

Status: **PREREGISTERED BEFORE TARGET IMAGE-BODY ACCESS**.

## Selection

Use only the current human layout/annotation tables and the literal manual
ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b surfaces. Retain a locus when it is editorial kind `L`, consists
of exactly one source group in every reading, and all three alternate readings
give the same nonempty literal surface of at least two characters. Group loci
by that surface and retain every complete surface type which:

1. occurs on at least three physical folios (the leading `f` plus digits);
2. has at least one existing human exact-locus annotation at every occurrence;
3. has `FIGURE` in the intersection of the non-`LABEL` human object tags over
   every occurrence.

No image, parser root/role, English gloss, or object-description phrase enters
the rule. Freeze every type and occurrence that passes; do not select a
favorite after image inspection.

## Direct source-bound ownership inspection

Inspect each frozen locus once on its exact official Yale canvas. Record one
of:

- `SINGULAR_FIGURE_OWNED`: all five gates below pass;
- `SLOT_OR_GROUP_ASSOCIATED`: a local figure slot or group is visible but one
  figure is not uniquely assigned;
- `PROXIMITY_ONLY`: nearness is visible without an ownership device;
- `LOCALIZATION_UNRESOLVED`: the target cannot be localized securely.

The five gates are:

1. the target inscription is securely localized;
2. exactly one plausible human figure occupies its local visual slot;
3. an author-visible enclosure, partition, leader, reserved column, or
   unambiguous local stack assigns the inscription to that slot;
4. no equally plausible competing figure shares the assignment;
5. the assignment is visible without relying on the editorial prose, clock
   position, or transcription order alone.

The panel passes only if every frozen occurrence is
`SINGULAR_FIGURE_OWNED`; otherwise it stops. Prior full-canvas exposure for
unrelated topology/layout work is disclosed and does not count as an ownership
judgment.

## Ceiling

A pass would establish only that one exact manual surface recurs in three
singular figure-owned label positions. It would not establish `FIGURE`,
`WOMAN`, `NYMPH`, a name, a grammatical category, sound, language, cipher,
plaintext, meaning, or translation. The surface's occurrence in prose and its
productive internal construction must remain explicit.
