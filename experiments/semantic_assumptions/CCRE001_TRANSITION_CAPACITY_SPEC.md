# CCRE001 C-to-L transition-capacity gate

Status: `REGISTERED_SCORE_BLIND_LENGTH_ONLY`.

The validated circle cross-role panel contains 16 f67--f73 pages on five
physical folios and a 12-page zodiac sensitivity on four folios. Before any
STA family identity, transition, similarity, or role echo is scored, this gate
uses only source-group ownership and the stored number of STA symbols in each
group.

A page is retained only if, in each of ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b and separately for C and
L roles, it has:

- at least two groups containing a within-group transition; and
- at least ten total within-group transitions, computed as
  `sum(max(symbol_count - 1, 0))`.

After that fixed filter, all five primary folios must still have at least two
pages, all 12 zodiac sensitivity pages must remain, the primary synchronous
within-folio assignment orbit must have at least 8,192 mappings, and the zodiac
orbit must remain exactly 5,760. Failure stops the route without opening a
family identity or score.

The output may contain only page/folio/class, group counts, transition-bearing
group counts, transition totals, eligibility, hashes, and gates. It may not
contain a family sequence, n-gram, role echo, meaning, plaintext, or
translation.
