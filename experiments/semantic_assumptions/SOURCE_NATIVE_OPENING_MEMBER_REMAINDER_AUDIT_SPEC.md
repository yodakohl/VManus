# Exact-member safeguard for the `NONE`/`DA` remainder construction

Status: **FROZEN_POST_TARGET_FORMAL_SAFEGUARD**

## Distinct question

The earlier favored-member transition atlas asked whether individual exact
member pairs were enriched inside the six favored family-edge shells.  This
audit asks a different question: when `NONE` and `DA` groups were called the
same remainder at coarse family level, do they still share the same complete
remainder after every STA member position is resolved in ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b?

The prior external-context target is already a validated nonconfirmation.  No
outcome is rescored here, and this audit cannot rescue or rerun that target.

## Frozen universe

Reconstruct the 53 family remainders retained by the original score-blind
capacity rule and the 197 remainder-by-physical-folio cells containing both
`NONE` and `DA`.  These cells contain exactly 1,207 target rows (892 `NONE`,
315 `DA`) on 59 folios.  Strip zero member positions from `NONE` and the first
two member positions from `DA`, exactly matching the family-level operation.

Every member-code list must have exactly one code per family symbol.  Treat the
three transcriptions as alternate readings, never replications.

## Resolution inventories

Report capacity without storing a remainder identity, locus, page, or row:

1. `FAMILY_ONLY`: the existing mixed cells and rows.
2. `ALL_READING_TRIPLET_EXACT`: a remainder signature is the ordered sequence
   of `(ZL3b, IT2a, RF1b)` member-code triples.  A cell is mixed only when that
   exact signature occurs under both operations in the same family-remainder
   and physical-folio cell.
3. `ALL_THREE_CONSENSUS_EXACT`: retain only rows whose complete remainder
   member sequence is identical in all three readings, then require the same
   exact consensus sequence under both operations in the same cell.
4. `ZL3b_EXACT`, `IT2a_EXACT`, and `RF1b_EXACT`: reading-specific diagnostics
   only, with no replication claim.

For each inventory store mixed exact cells, rows inside them, `NONE`/`DA` row
counts, physical folios, and family remainders.  For the two all-reading
inventories also store globally shared exact signatures and those occurring on
at least two folios under each operation.

For `DA` rows in `ALL_READING_TRIPLET_EXACT` mixed cells, inventory the two
stripped prefix positions as exact reading triples.  This is a structural
member-code census, not a morpheme or sound assignment.

## Decision and ceiling

Call exact-member capacity retained only if the triplet inventory has at least
100 mixed cells, 500 rows, 40 folios, 30 family remainders, and 150 rows of
each operation.  Passing establishes that the family-level natural
alternations are not wholly a coarse-member illusion and may identify a
stable exact formal prefix.  It does not authorize another external-context
target and cannot establish detachment, allography, morphology, pronunciation,
wordhood, syntax, language, cipher operation, meaning, plaintext, or
translation.
