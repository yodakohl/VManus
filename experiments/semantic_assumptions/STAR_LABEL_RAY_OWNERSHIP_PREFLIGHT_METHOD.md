# Direct star-label ray-count ownership preflight

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: `SOURCE_AND_NATIVE_VISUAL_OWNERSHIP_ONLY`

Before testing whether an attached label's formal construction varies with a
star's ray count, require at least two physical folios with eight or more
unhedged, exact-local, explicitly attached label/star records and at least two
ray-count states each. This is an ownership gate, not a text score.

Candidate rows come only from the active human exact-locus annotation atlas.
The ray count must be explicit in the human local comment and the row must be a
label context. The official Yale canvas for f70v1 is used only to decide
whether its old question-marked angular pairings have an author-visible
leader, enclosure, reserved cell, or other singular ownership relation.

No Voynich label surface, root, family, formal feature, OCR, CLIP output,
embedding, or inferred star name enters this preflight. ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b
remain alternate readings of one manuscript.

A stop supplies no number, ray-count word, star name, sound, language, cipher,
plaintext, meaning, or translation.
