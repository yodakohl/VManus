# Haruna 2011 manuscript-K f45v source clarification

Date: 2026-08-11

## Purpose

Resolve the paper's siglum `K`, determine what `illustration n° 29` denotes,
and ask whether the comparison supplies a shared inscription or slot map for
Voynich f67v2. This is a source-only clarification of the already screened
Haruna paper, not a decoder-claim audit or manuscript-image analysis.

## Permitted inputs

- Haruna-Czaplicki 2011, public embedded human PDF text layer.
- Occitanica's human manuscript-sigla catalogue.
- The British Library's human `Digitised manuscripts` list.
- The previously validated Haruna twelve-wind-bust result.

No figure or manuscript image may be rendered or inspected. OCR, automated
vision, Voynich string scoring, and external decoder claims are forbidden.

## Fixed questions

1. Does Occitanica identify `K` as London, Harley 4940, a complete
   fourteenth-century *Breviari d'amor* manuscript?
2. Does the British Library independently list Harley MS 4940 as Matfre
   Ermengaud's *Breviari d'amor*, mid-fourteenth century, in French and Old
   Occitan?
3. Does Haruna's inventory define item 29 as the wind rose after verse 6112,
   while the paper's displayed figures stop at figure 22?
4. Does footnote 138 locate the comparison at K f45v?
5. Does the paper explicitly state that inscription-explained details differ
   between the two authors even though similar pictorial ideas are used?

## Decision

Retain Harley 4940 f45v as a human-published art-historical homologue only.
Stop if the sources do not publish an individual inscription-to-bust map,
four-by-three placement, visual start, orientation, or a common owned target
slot coordinate.

## Claim ceiling

The sources may establish the identity, date, language, and comparative role
of manuscript K and distinguish the Breviari's internal illustration number
from the paper's displayed figure numbers. They cannot transfer any wind,
direction, inscription, bust, face, slot, label, word, sound, language, cipher,
plaintext, meaning, or translation to Voynich f67v2.
