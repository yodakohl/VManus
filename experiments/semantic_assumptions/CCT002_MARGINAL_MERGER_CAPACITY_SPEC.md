# CCT002 marginal-preserving merger-null capacity

CCT001 stopped unscored because exact pair-frequency-bin shells left only two
movable collision pairs.  CCT002 is a genuinely different null, not a relaxed
rerun: within each exact `(ASCII length, ch/sh prefix, site index)` shell it
keeps every observed `o` member fixed and bijectively permutes the complete
set of observed `e` members.

Every null mapping therefore contains every raw member exactly once and
preserves the complete `o` and `e` member-frequency marginals, the collision
pair count, exact length, prefix, and site position.  For any additive
member-specific nuisance terms `a(o)+b(e)`, the sum is invariant under every
bijection.  Pair-frequency covariance is deliberately not conditioned away:
whether the true surrounding-string counterparts pair members that transfer
together is the estimand.

This capacity pass may read the already opened 2,223 target groups solely to
reconstruct collision membership and aggregate shell sizes.  It must emit no
raw type, canonical template, member frequency, pair identity, state score,
page/folio score, or merger score.  It must bind the validated CCT001 capacity
stop and preserve its exact 35-pair/586-event reconstruction.

Capacity requires at least 24 collision pairs, at least 16 pairs in shells of
size at least two, at least 8,192 distinct joint bijections (`sum lgamma(n+1)`
at least `log(8192)`), pair events on all eight leaves and three readings, and
an explicit additive-marginal invariance control.  Independent reconstruction
is mandatory.

A pass authorizes only a new target-blind synthetic calibration of the
marginal-preserving null.  It does not authorize a manuscript association
score and establishes no canonical form, word, sound, language, cipher,
meaning, plaintext, or translation.
