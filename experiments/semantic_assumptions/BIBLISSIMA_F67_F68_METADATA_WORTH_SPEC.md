# Biblissima f67/f68 diagram metadata worth screen

## Purpose

Screen one complete human-curated Biblissima iconographic family before any
historical image or paper is opened.  The targets are the special-circle
acquisition gaps around the f67 wind diagrams and the f68 astronomical
diagrams.  This is a catalogue-metadata worth screen, not a visual comparison,
source attribution, or lexical transfer.

## Frozen sources and selection

Open only the public HTML pages for:

- the Biblissima `schéma` descriptor; and
- every linked iconographic record whose human title either begins exactly
  `Rose des vents` or contains both `soleil` and `lune` case-insensitively.

The first selection must contain exactly 18 distinct records and the second
exactly three.  A record that satisfies both definitions would be retained in
both panels, although none does in the frozen source.

Retain only human catalogue fields: title, type, folio/page, inscription,
rubric, date, descriptors, manuscript, text, place, and named source.  The
page-embedded IIIF `info.json` URLs may be recorded as unopened locators.  Do
not request any source URL, thumbnail, image, IIIF information document,
canvas, manifest, manuscript page, PDF, paper, OCR output, automated visual
output, or decoder claim.

## Frozen human-review gate

A wind record is worth qualified human topology inspection only when its
human metadata jointly provides:

- a date through the fifteenth century;
- `cercle` and `vents` among the descriptors;
- at least one human-figure descriptor from `homme`, `femme`, `tête`,
  `de profil`, `de face`, or `en médaillon`;
- a named provenance source; and
- at least one page-embedded IIIF information URL.

Identification-inscription metadata is retained as an extra strength, not a
requirement.  This deliberately admits Bourges BM Ms. 105 f.095v and
Bibliothèque Sainte-Geneviève Ms. 1029 f.135 for qualified human inspection.

An astronomical record is worth inspection for the f68 gap only when its
human title names the Sun, Moon, and stars together.  Eclipse diagrams naming
only the Sun and Moon do not pass.

## Source-transfer gate

No retained record supports transfer to the Voynich manuscript unless its
human metadata additionally fixes all of:

- a complete readable ordered register with explicit ownership of its labels;
- a one-to-one common slot coordinate with the target Voynich diagram; and
- a preserved start and orientation.

Cataloguing a circle, winds, people, heads, medallions, or identification
inscriptions does not by itself establish any of those relations.

## Ceiling

The strongest permissible result is a provenance-clean request for qualified
human inspection of two exact historical pages.  No direction, wind, person,
head, sex, object, Sun, Moon, star, slot, label, word, sound, language, cipher,
plaintext, meaning, or translation may be transferred to f67 or f68.
