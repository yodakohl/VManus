# f77r second segmented-system capacity audit

Status: **TOPOLOGY-ONLY PRETEXT SCREEN**.

## Purpose

The provisional post-hoc f77r construction requires a second independently
owned sequence of short labels in consecutive apparatus segments, with visible
mixed active and inactive boundaries.  Only such a system can test whether the
f57-derived state changes predict graphical emission without reusing f77r.

This audit inspects human layout descriptions and exact official images only.
Candidate strings, state bits, and transition scores are prohibited.

## Admission rule

A candidate must have:

1. at least six short labels in one ordered apparatus;
2. each label inside one segment rather than merely near a separate object;
3. boundaries shared by adjacent segments;
4. at least one visibly active/emitting boundary and at least one visibly
   inactive/non-emitting boundary;
5. topology fixed before any candidate label string is opened for this test.

The strongest metadata candidates are f69r `K1`, f70r1 `X1`, and f78r `X1`.
Official images are Yale canvases 1006198, 1006199, and 1006214.

## Decision ceiling

If no unit passes, retain f77r as a post-hoc lead only.  An all-active wheel
cannot test the inactive half of the rule, and scattered apparatus labels are
not a segment sequence.  No quality, element, object, word, sound, language,
cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation follows.
