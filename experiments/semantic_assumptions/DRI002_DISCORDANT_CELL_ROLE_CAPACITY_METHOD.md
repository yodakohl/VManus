Status: **PREREGISTERED BEFORE NEW TARGET IMAGE ACCESS**.

DRI001 retained a five-state source-native visual document-role rubric and
found two exact `(section, editorial-kind-run)` cells whose paired pages had

pages to support a later within-cell structural association. It does not score

## Complete selection

From the published DRI001 result, retain every cell with
`role_agreement=false`. Rebuild every page's exact collapsed run of editorial
IVTFF kinds from `existing_human_locus_roles.tsv`. For each retained
`(section, kind-run)` cell, select every page not already present anywhere in
the DRI001 thirty-page panel. Do not rank or sample the remaining pages.

Bind each selected logical page to its exact official Yale canvas and record
the official dimensions. Opening IIIF `info.json` for this binding is allowed;
no selected image body, transcription surface, family, member, root, parser
role, or prior target judgment may enter selection. Prior whole-canvas
exposure is disclosed conservatively for every page.

## Visual capacity census

Inspect all selected logical pages once under the unchanged DRI001 rubric:

- `PROSE_DOMINANT`
- `OBJECT_WITH_PROSE`
- `REPEATED_OWNED_RECORDS`
- `DIAGRAM_PARAMETER_ARRAY`
- `MIXED_OR_UNRESOLVED`

Record the same five visible evidence booleans and a short visible basis. The
new judgments must not use transcription identities or formal features.

Combine the new judgments with the two already-published DRI001 judgments in
each cell. A cell has usable role mobility only if at least two distinct
non-unresolved roles each occur on at least two physical folios. Continue to a
separately frozen structural association only if **both** discordant cells pass
that gate. Otherwise stop before formal scoring.

This capacity census can establish only whether visible page roles vary with
replication inside exact section and editorial-kind-run cells. It cannot
establish a heading, caption, field, class name, word, POS, sound, language,
cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
