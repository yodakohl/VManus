# F69F70C001 — f69r/f70r1 central-slot component audit

## Status and question

This is a disclosed post-hoc audit. The six central labels were already visible
in F70C001. It asks only whether the public human clock alignment supports the
idea that each short f69r item is contained in the corresponding f70r1 group.

## Frozen views

1. Pair `K1.1` through `K1.6` with `X1.1` through `X1.6`, the physical order
   independently recorded by the human clock annotations.
2. In each native ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b reading, score complete source-group
   substring containment. Compare the fixed score with all 720 assignments of
   the six f69r groups to the six fixed f70r1 groups.
3. Report the best rotation/reflection only as an optimization diagnostic and
   compare it with the same optimization over every assignment.
4. Separately collapse the official STA sequences to their leading family.
   This deliberately generous view checks the apparent optimized 6/6 result
   against all 180 distinct permutations of the repeated family multiset.

ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate readings of one manuscript, not independent
samples. No OCR or automated image recognition is used.

## Decision and ceiling

Retain a component-key lead only if the human-fixed alignment is exceptional
under the exact assignment null in every reading. An optimized alignment cannot
rescue a nonexceptional fixed alignment. No outcome identifies a glyph sound,
abbreviation, planet, direction, number, word, plaintext, or translation.
