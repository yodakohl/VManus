The five-doorway outcome requires all of:

1. five distinct author-drawn openings or inter-column compartments;
2. three locally aligned short baselines contained within each opening;
3. visible separation between adjacent three-row bundles;
4. no continuous baseline carrying writing across multiple openings; and
5. the same ownership geometry in at least four of five openings, with the
   fifth no worse than physically damaged or ambiguous.

The paragraph outcome requires three continuous or visually unified baseline
tracks spanning multiple openings, without repeated five-bundle separation.
Mixed evidence, damage that prevents the distinction, or a different layout is
not resolved in favor of either model.

## Decision and ceiling

A five-doorway result establishes only one local five-record, three-position
author-visible schema. A paragraph result establishes only one local paragraph
layout. Either result is one physical foldout and cannot by itself support a
word, field name, list identity, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or
translation. Any later content test needs a separate preregistration and must
disclose the accidental filler exposure.
