# Source-native opening-operation capacity audit

Status: **FROZEN_SCORE_BLIND_CAPACITY**

The validated construction atlas shows that `DAQKJ`, `DAQK`, and `DAQ` are
overwhelmingly source-group-opening paths. This audit asks a new, narrower
question: do exact family-sequence remainders recur under different lengths of
that opening chain often enough to test a detachable structural operation?
It does not reuse the old partial-root cross-operation target.

For every strict confirmed-prose source group, assign exactly one operation by
the longest matching physical prefix in this order:

`DAQKJ`, `DAQK`, `DAQ`, `DA`, otherwise `NONE`.

Strip that prefix and exclude an empty remainder. For all ten unordered pairs
of the five operation states, count only:

- exact remainder types seen with both operations;
- groups and physical folios represented by the shared remainders;
- remainders for which each operation occurs on at least two physical folios;
- shared remainder types occurring in Currier A and Currier B.

No remainder identity, locus, page, neighbor, group position, context feature,
score, or semantic field may be stored. A pair is capacity-eligible only if it
has at least 20 shared remainders, at least 10 two-folio-per-operation
remainders, at least 50 groups in each operation, at least 20 union folios, and
at least five shared remainders in each Currier register. If multiple pairs
pass, the sole future candidate is selected score-blind by descending
two-folio-per-operation remainder count, then descending smaller operation
group count, then descending folio count, then lexicographic pair ID.

This audit may authorize a separately frozen held-folio test of whether one
opening operation changes external structural context for an otherwise exact
family remainder. It cannot establish detachment, wordhood, prefix meaning,
sound, language, cipher operation, plaintext, or translation.
