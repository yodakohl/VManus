# Exact-group cross-Currier ecology diagnostic

## Purpose

Determine whether the partial whole-form position sharing seen across Currier A
and B survives a same-section comparison, rather than being explainable only by
their different section mixtures.  This is a descriptive confound audit of the
already exposed 25-form panel, not a new confirmatory test.

## Frozen selection

Use the same strict zero-alternative confirmed-prose first/last endpoint rows
and only the 25 exact family surfaces fixed by the prior cross-Currier audit.
For each section, a form has common support when it has at least five endpoint
events and two physical folios separately in Currier A and B.  A section is
eligible only with at least ten such forms.  Stop unscored unless exactly one
section is eligible; do not pool sections or change the threshold.

Inside the eligible section and each register, compute each supported form's
Jeffreys-smoothed first-versus-last log odds against every other endpoint
surface in that same section/register.  Report the complete form table, A/B
Pearson and midrank Spearman correlation, direction cross-tabulation, every
one-form deletion, and each section-specific coefficient's agreement with its
already published global-register coefficient.  No p-value or semantic label
is defined.

## Claim ceiling

This audit can determine only whether the partial A/B whole-form position
sharing is visible inside the sole adequately overlapping section.  It cannot
separate Currier from hand, topic, production chronology, dialect, language,
encoding regime, part of speech, sound, morpheme, word meaning, plaintext,
cipher, or translation.
