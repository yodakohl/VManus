# CDA001 — cached-data route exhaustion audit

Status: **SOURCE-ONLY ROUTING AUDIT**.

## Question

After RTA001, LTG001, IGR002, and the corrected TGC001 stop, does the current
cache contain a genuinely different, identifiable experiment that can move
toward translation without external-claim validation, OCR, automated vision,
embeddings, an exact-label decoder, a broad visual classifier, or a rerun of a
closed route?

This is a worth screen, not a new score. It reads only validated compact
artifacts and the manual ZL/IT/RF consensus table. It opens no image or target
association.

## Candidate reservoirs

1. **Translation anchors.** Retain the six mandatory gates from the frozen
   acquisition registry. Any current semantic route requires at least one
   admissible family.
2. **All-reading-stable groups.** Count strict-zero-alternative confirmed-prose
   groups of length 1--8 whose complete STA member sequences agree in ZL, IT,
   and RF. Delete every physical locus already selected by IGR001, IGR002, or
   the published TGC001 geometry. Separately delete every physical folio used
   by those three panels. This is an IGR/TGC-only exposure definition, not a
   claim of no exposure in any earlier experiment.
3. **Physical-layer acquisition.** Retain the NVA001 and NVA002 decisions about
   ordinary native-visual capacity and public image layers.
4. **Completed model routes.** Bind the final RTA001, LTG001, IGR002, and
   corrected TGC001 statuses so that a renamed continuation cannot silently
   reopen them.

Large all-reading-stable counts do not establish a usable route. Using their
manual identities as targets is the exact-label decoder already excluded.
Predicting only their symbol counts or editorial boundaries is a broad visual
segmentation classifier without independent physical-boundary truth: agreement
among three editions of one manuscript is not an independent ink segmentation
annotation.

## Decision

Return `NO_CURRENT_CACHED_ROUTE` when all conditions hold:

- zero of eleven translation-anchor families is admissible;
- NVA001 exposes zero new capacity-qualified ordinary-image routes;
- NVA002 exposes no new public folio or image layer;
- TGC001 has zero whole-folio-fresh rows;
- RTA001, LTG001, and IGR002 retain their registered nonconfirmations; and
- every large unused transcription reservoir would enter only through an
  excluded decoder/classifier or a closed route.

The smallest translation-bearing addition is a provenance-clean human
diplomatic panel with singular author-visible Voynich-text to independently
readable-value bindings, repeated contrasting values on at least five physical
folios, unique locus mappings, uncertainty records, and an untouched
whole-folio confirmation set. A single qualified reading of f68r2.31 or a
second f2r.15-like record reopens acquisition only; it does not itself meet the
full translation-anchor capacity.

## Interpretation ceiling

This audit can establish only that the present cached evidence does not contain
an admissible next experiment under the active rules. It does not prove that
the manuscript is untranslatable or that no future evidence can translate it.
It supplies no word, sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or
translation.
