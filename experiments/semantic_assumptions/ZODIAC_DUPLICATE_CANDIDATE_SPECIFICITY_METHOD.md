# ZODIAC_DUPLICATE_CANDIDATE_SPECIFICITY — post-result diagnostic

Date: 2026-08-10

This is an explicitly post-result diagnostic of the two candidates emitted by
`ZODIAC_DUPLICATE_SOURCE_NATIVE_OVERLAP`.  It does not alter that frozen
descriptive result and is not a confirmatory test.

## Inputs

- corrected public voynich.nu page catalogue v2;
- complete source-native STA group alignment;
- complete source-separator metadata;
- the frozen duplicated-zodiac result.

No OCR, image model, retained formal parser, English gloss, label proximity, or
day-order assumption is used.  Public sign identities come only from the
catalogue's illustration descriptions; tentative-identification prose remains
excluded.

## Diagnostics

1. Reapply the frozen candidate rule to all three perfect matchings of the four
   15-figure Aries/Taurus half-pages.  Count candidates by matching and view.
2. Inventory each frozen candidate across the entire manuscript by edition,
   page, physical locus, source group, role, and nearest basic EVA rendering.
3. For every exact STA-family whole-group surface, retain it only when it is
   present on both pages in all three readings and occurs on exactly those two
   pages anywhere in the manuscript.  Count such surfaces for every manuscript
   page pair and every pair among the twelve public zodiac pages.
4. Repeat item 3 with a circular-role witness required in every reading on both
   pages.

The readings are alternate descriptions, not independent samples.  The 24
matching comparisons are correlated views of the same four pages.  The minimum
layout-matching p remains 1/3.

## Decision ceiling

An exact two-page repeat may remain a scene-associated lead only if its full
support reconstructs.  It is not privileged if alternative half-page
matchings produce at least as many frozen-rule candidates or another zodiac
page pair has the same globally pair-exclusive whole-group pattern.  No result
can identify a sign name, month, day, word, morpheme, sound, language, plaintext,
or translation.
