# Source-native circle-block `qo` capacity audit

This is a descriptive, score-blind capacity audit. It does not test an
association and has no target outcome.

## Frozen scope

- Use the validated strict-zero-alternative source STA group scaffold.
- Include every row whose physical folio is f67 through f73, including f71.
  This is the complete 26-page circular-illustration block already bound to
  the public human catalogue by the circle-block diagnostic.
- Treat `D-A-Q-K-J`, `D-A-Q-K`, `D-A-Q`, and `D-A` as longest-prefix opening
  paths and all other groups as `NONE`.
- `consensus_group_index = 1` is only a source-transcription locus/line start.
  It is not a graphical slot, authorial record, clockwise start, or object
  ownership relation.

## Capacity question

Count operation states by physical folio, IVTFF kind (`C/L/P/R`), and
locus-first versus later position. Then form cells keyed by exact family
remainder and physical folio for `NONE` versus bare `DA`. A transferable
circle-start test requires at least three mixed folios and at least three cells
in which both states have first and later observations. These deliberately low
floors still prevent a single exposed folio from defining the relation.

If either floor fails, stop without scoring. No longer path may be silently
substituted, no operation states may be pooled post hoc, and no prose rows
outside f67--f73 may be imported.

## Claim ceiling

The audit can establish only whether the public circle block contains the
natural exact-remainder variation needed for a fair bare-`DA`/literal-`qo`
locus-position test. It cannot establish graphical record starts, a circle
marker, wordhood, detachment, sound, language, cipher, meaning, plaintext, or
translation.
