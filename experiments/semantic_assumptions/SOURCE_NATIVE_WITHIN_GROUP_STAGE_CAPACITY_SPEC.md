# Source-native within-group stage grammar: capacity and novelty gate

Status: **REGISTERED_SCORE_BLIND_CAPACITY**

## Question

Can the complete source-separated STA-family layer support a new lossless
within-group parser with ordered, latent structural stages? This is not a
meaning test. Candidate stages must receive neutral names such as `S0`, `S1`,
and `S2`; they are not prefixes, roots, suffixes, sounds, words, or parts of
speech unless independent evidence later establishes that.

## Distinctness

The route is distinct from:

- the confirmed first-versus-last **group** classifier inside a physical locus;
- the failed smooth coordinate over successive groups in a locus;
- the failed first-family-to-last-family coupling test inside one group;
- legacy root/role parsing, whose source is unavailable and whose retained
  nodes omit complete source groups;
- PP001 and IL026 context/trigram tests across written words or parsed roots.

The new unit is one complete manual source group represented by its lossless
official STA-family sequence. The latent stage path is internal to that group.

## Score-blind capacity

Use only synchronized groups with `strict_zero_alternative=1` and
`grammar_scope=CONFIRMED_PROSE`, and require an ordinary physical folio ID.
Freeze physical folios before any stage fit by
`u64(sha256("SNWG001|" + folio)) mod 5`: bucket 0 is TEST, bucket 1 is CAL,
and buckets 2--4 are TRAIN.

The public capacity table may contain identity, manuscript metadata, exact
symbol count, and split only. It must not contain a family sequence, member
code, prefix, suffix, target stage, fitted parameter, score, or English gloss.

As a cheap nonduplication gate, inventory exact cross-folio parallel capacity
without exposing identities: complete locus sequences repeated across folios,
contiguous family-sequence n-grams, and exact three-reading member-code
n-grams. This is descriptive capacity only and may not nominate a passage.

## Continuation gate

Continue only if all 94 physical folios survive, TRAIN/CAL/TEST each contain
both Currier registers and at least 1,000 groups per register, every split has
at least 20 folios and 5,000 groups, group lengths are 1--11, and the masked
table contains zero target sequence fields.

The next step may be only a separately frozen monotone-stage parser plus
target-free synthetic/null controls. No held TEST likelihood or positional
family profile may be opened before that freeze.

No capacity outcome establishes an authorial word, stage meaning, morphology,
sound, language, cipher, plaintext, or translation.
