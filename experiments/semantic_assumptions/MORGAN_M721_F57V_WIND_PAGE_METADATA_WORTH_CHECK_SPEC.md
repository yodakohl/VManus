# Morgan M.721 f13r / f57v metadata worth check

## Question

The Morgan Library's human-curated entry for *La sfera*, MS M.721 fol. 13r,
describes four wind faces with readable direction labels and a wind-name wheel
on the same page.  Does that public text justify image, codex, paper, or roster
validation as the missing complete f57v four-person/two-register homologue?

Fetch only the official Morgan HTML page.  Do not open any linked image,
thumbnail, IIIF canvas, paper, PDF, OCR output, automated visual output,
decoder claim, or wind-name roster.

## Fixed evidence

The page must identify the manuscript, folio, place/date, and all three
human-authored descriptions:

- a Sun/Moon diagram;
- four winds personified as faces and individually labelled with the four
  `PLAGA ...` direction phrases;
- a wind-name wheel in the lower right margin.

## Worth bar

Escalation requires the same integrated diagram to expose four person owners,
both corresponding four-item label registers, author-visible one-to-one
connectors, and a preserved start/orientation that maps to f57v without
choosing a wind roster after seeing the target.  Two separately catalogued
diagrams on one page do not meet that bar.  Direction/wind labels also do not
license replacement of the already exposed humoral/element comparanda.

## Ceiling

This check can retain M.721 f13r as a close same-page four-wind comparandum or
stop it before deeper validation.  It cannot assign a wind, direction, quality,
element, label, word, sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or
translation to f57v.
