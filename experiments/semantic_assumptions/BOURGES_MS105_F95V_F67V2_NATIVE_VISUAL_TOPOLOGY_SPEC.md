Date: 2026-08-11

and Initiale worth checks. Those human sources selected Bourges BM MS 105
f95v before its IIIF image was opened: an eleventh- to twelfth-century wind
rose with frontal and profile heads, a circle, cardinal points, and blowing.

unconnected strings on the principal axes around the central rosette.

## Bounded visual questions

1. How many source head positions are visibly present?
2. Do they form one continuous annulus or detached corner clusters?
3. Does each head have an explicit inward radial breath connection?
4. Does the inner wheel have a fourfold principal-axis organization?
5. Does the source reproduce f67v2's 3/4/4/3 grouping and fourteen-face
   cardinality?
6. Is there a common source-target slot coordinate, start, and orientation?

## Decision rule

A source-to-target topology transfer requires the same unit cardinality,
grouping, and ownership relation plus an independently fixed start and
orientation. Twelve continuously arrayed wind heads are not interchangeable
with fourteen detached corner faces merely because both diagrams are circular
and fourfold.
