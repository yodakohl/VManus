# PIP001 complete plume-intervention panel

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE THREE NEW TARGET-IMAGE REGIONS ARE OPENED**.

## Question

Across the complete human-authored source comments that explicitly propose a
plume as an intervention or correction, how often does the visible source
preserve a physically separable plume above otherwise coherent baseline writing?

This is a physical writing-process and transcription-reliability question. It
does not test a glyph reading, sound, language, cipher, or translation.

## Complete mechanical selection

Use `transcription/sources/Stolfi_text25e1-52.evt` only. Select every comment
that contains `plume` and also contains at least one of `correct`, `added`, or
`darker ink`. The next locus row is the target. This yields exactly five
physical loci:

- f26r.1, already inspected in the processed-correction screen and unresolved;
- f31r.7, already inspected in SIA001 and secure;
- f37v.22, unopened for this target question;
- f81v.13, unopened for this target question; and
- f81v.19, unopened for this target question.

The two exposed outcomes are fixed and cannot be reclassified here. The three
new target regions must remain unopened until the method, selection, and
source-only validation are published. Earlier unrelated full-page exposure, if
discovered, must be disclosed.

## Source and outcomes

Use only official Yale IIIF canvases bound by the official manifest. Inspect
source-native pixels without enhancement, OCR, CLIP, embeddings, automated
segmentation, or automated image recognition.

Classify each new target once as:

- `SECURE_VISIBLE_SEPARABLE_PLUME_INTERVENTION`: all five gates pass;
- `VISIBLE_PLUME_CHRONOLOGY_UNRESOLVED`: gates 1--3 pass but separate/later
  physical state is not established; or
- `NO_SEPARATE_PLUME_OR_UNRESOLVED`: the plume or its anchor is not secure.

Frozen per-target gates:

1. the registered locus and annotated target word/glyph are securely localized;
2. a bounded plume exists materially above the ordinary baseline writing;
3. the plume has one unique author-visible baseline host;
4. a separable ink-density, stroke-edge, or noncontinuous-placement boundary
   distinguishes the plume from the baseline writing; and
5. the baseline host remains physically coherent without the plume.

## Panel decision and use

The complete five-locus panel passes as
`PASS_MAJORITY_VISIBLE_SEPARABLE_PLUME_INTERVENTION` only if at least three of
five loci pass all five gates, with at least two positives among the three new
targets and positives on at least three physical folios. Otherwise stop as
`STOP_NO_MAJORITY_VISIBLE_SEPARABLE_PLUME_INTERVENTION`.

Even a pass authorizes only a compact plume-bearing physical-intervention
inventory. It does not establish that the plume changes one character into a
different character, and it does not authorize automatic correction of the
transcription.

## Claim ceiling

A pass would establish only that human plume-intervention comments often
correspond to visible separable supralinear strokes at multiple physical loci.
It establishes no correct glyph identity, correction intent, sound, morpheme,
word, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
