# DIC001 — drawing-interruption continuity capacity

Status: **SCORE-BLIND CAPACITY ONLY**.

## Question

The manual IVTFF sources distinguish ordinary apparent spaces from text
interrupted by a drawing.  An older parser deleted the aligned drawing marker
and concatenated its neighboring groups.  DIC001 asks a prior structural
question: is there enough source-native, multi-folio evidence to test whether
grammar continues across a drawing or restarts on its far side?

This is different from word-boundary prediction, exact-y spacing, graphical
array records, and circular-to-label echo.  The target is the explicitly
marked boundary type itself, not a selected string, glyph pair, label, image
class, or semantic value.

## Capacity universe

- Use only the hash-fixed strict shared source-native structural interlinear.
- Use only `CONFIRMED_PROSE` loci.
- A target is an internal boundary whose three manual readings unanimously
  say `DRAWING_INTERRUPTION`.
- A control is an internal boundary whose three readings unanimously say
  `DEFINITE_SPACE` on a page containing at least one target.
- Mixed, uncertain, unaligned, missing, and line-end boundaries are excluded.
- Physical folio is the numeric `f...` stem, so panels of one folio are not
  independent.
- The public capacity table contains identity, metadata, positions, and the
  two boundary labels only.  It contains no family surface, member code, EVA,
  root, role, image feature, OCR result, or English gloss.

## Capacity gates

The route may proceed only if the target has at least 200 boundaries, 30
physical folios, 50 pages, two Currier registers, three sections, no folio
above 15% of targets, and every target folio contains an ordinary-space
control.  At least 20 folios must contain five targets.

A pass authorizes only a separately preregistered target-blind continuity/
restart instrument and synthetic controls.  It does not authorize reading any
family identity during calibration.

Claim ceiling: capacity can establish only that drawing-interruption grammar
is testable.  It cannot establish whether text is syntactically continuous,
whether the drawing belongs to the text, or any word, sound, POS, meaning,
plaintext, language, cipher, or translation.
