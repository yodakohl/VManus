# Special-circle boundary rebuild capacity

This score-blind audit asks whether the new provenance-complete 45-array
special-circle inventory can support a fresh reconstruction of the lost
historical array-slot boundary claim. It does not inherit EAS001 membership,
scores, controls, thresholds, or conclusions.

For every linear adjacency in each array, retain only two transcribed endpoints
that both map to the current source-native structural interlinear. Require the
stored consensus `family_surface` at both endpoints to equal the family
sequence independently reconstructed from ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b STA codes. The
candidate edge is then the last source group of the left locus to the first
source group of the right locus. This is a root-free endpoint representation.

The source audit may count linkage and all-reading stability but must not fit or
apply the DIC001 reset-likeness model, calculate boundary effects, run a null,
or emit endpoint identities. During development, one diagnostic printed 20
anonymous endpoint family pairs before this method was registered; no score,
association, best edge, or result was computed. The audit is therefore
score-blind but not fully analyst-blind.

The seven extant circle folios occupy four physical bifolio production units:
`f67+f68`, `f69+f70`, `f71+f72`, and `f73+missing-f74`. A future confirmatory
boundary test would require at least seven independent production units and a
one-sided complete sign-orbit floor at most `.01`. With four units the floor is
`1/2^4=.0625`; row, slot, array, page, and folio counts cannot override it.

This is capacity only. It establishes no graphical record boundary, slot
function, object ownership, word, sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning,
or translation.
