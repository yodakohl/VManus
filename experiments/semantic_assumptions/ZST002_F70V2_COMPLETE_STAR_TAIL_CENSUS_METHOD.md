# ZST002 — complete f70v2 star-tail census

## Question

Can a complete native-visual census of the two figure/star rings on `f70v2`
supply the new, source-bound within-ring tail positives required to reopen the
stopped ZST001 capacity route?

This is new data acquisition, not a regrade of ZST001. ZST001 completely
graded five rings on f72r3 and f73r but inspected only one documented f70v1
positive, which lacked a strict position mapping. The present census includes
every one of the 29 mechanically positioned f70v2 ring records: all 19
`S1`/OUTER records and all 10 `S2`/INNER records. Preserve both the catalogue
item number and the existing human-mapped `RING:GROVE_N` position; the two
orders are cyclically rotated and must not be equated. No record is ranked or
sampled. The central-star record and all 15 position-unmapped f70v1 records are
excluded before image inspection.

The exact official Yale canvas is `1006200`, dimensions 3945 by 3772. Prior
whole-canvas exposure is disclosed. Selection may use source record IDs,
ring/ordinal metadata, current-locus mappings, and strict-consensus metadata,
but no transcription surface, family/member identity, root, parser role, or
formal association.

## Fixed visual rubric

Inspect the complete 29-slot panel once, without reading label text:

- `TAIL`: the held star has a visible contour and a distinct drawn line or
  tapered extension continues away from the star beyond the hand/arm.
- `NO_TAIL`: the held star has a visible contour and no independent extension
  beyond the hand/arm.
- `UNCERTAIN`: damage, crop, overlap, hand/arm ambiguity, or insufficient
  resolution prevents the distinction.

Do not count a figure's arm, a neighboring radial line, paint wash, barrel, or
star point as a tail. Record confidence and a short visible basis for every
slot. No OCR, CLIP, embedding, automated image recognition, sex inference, or
label transcription may enter a grade.

## Strict join and capacity gate

Use the already frozen crosswalk and consensus table. A slot is strict only if
it is primary-eligible; its existing `RING:GROVE_N` key names the selected ring
and a unique complete-ring position; and every consensus group at the current
locus is a complete
`kind=L`, `DIAGNOSTIC_NONPROSE`, zero-alternative, contiguous `1..N` group.
The selection records strict eligibility but does not serialize group
identities.

The f70 acquisition passes only if at least one ring contains both strict
states and the union of mixed strict f70 rings contains at least two `TAIL`
and two `NO_TAIL` slots. A pass would supply the second robust physical folio
beside the already published f72 result; f73 remains an underpowered
sensitivity. Any formal association would require a separate preregistration.

## Ceiling

This census can establish only whether f70 supplies replicated, strictly
mapped drawing-state capacity. It establishes no star-tail word, zodiac name,
sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
