# Biblissima f57 fourfold-register metadata worth screen

## Purpose

Screen one human-curated Biblissima iconographic family for the exact f57v
acquisition gap before any image or paper is opened.  This is a source-worth
screen, not a visual comparison or lexical transfer.

## Sources and selection

Open only the public HTML pages for:

- the Biblissima `schéma` descriptor;
- every linked iconographic record whose human title contains one of the
  following literal subject families: `Âges, humeurs, saisons, éléments`,
  `Éléments et humeurs`, `Éléments et leurs qualités`,
  `Éléments, saisons, humeurs`, `Quatre éléments (Les)`,
  `Saisons et humeurs`, or `Zodiaque, éléments et qualités`;
- the Biblissima manuscript record and Mandragore textual record for BnF Latin
  4922 f.1v.

Retain only human catalogue fields: title, type, folio/page, inscription, date,
descriptors, manuscript, text, place, and named source.  Do not open any
thumbnail, image, IIIF manifest body, Gallica canvas, manuscript page, PDF,
paper, OCR, automated visual output, or decoder claim.

## Frozen gates

A source may be marked worth qualified human image inspection when it has:

- an in-range manuscript date through the fifteenth century;
- a human inscription naming all four relevant semantic families (ages,
  humours, seasons, elements) or their literal values;
- an official digital-witness URL.

It may support source transfer only when human metadata additionally states:

- four people, portraits, philosophers, figures, heads, or faces;
- explicit one-to-one ownership of two four-item text registers;
- a preserved start and orientation.

The inspection gate is deliberately weaker than the transfer gate.  Passing
it identifies a precise task for a qualified human; it does not identify any
Voynich label.

## Ceiling

The strongest permissible outcome is a provenance-clean human-review lead for
diagram topology.  No age, humour, season, element, person, slot, label, word,
sound, language, cipher, plaintext, meaning, or translation may be transferred
to f57v without an explicit common owned coordinate.
