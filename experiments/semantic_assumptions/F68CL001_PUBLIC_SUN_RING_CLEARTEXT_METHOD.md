# F68CL001 — public f68r2 Sun-ring cleartext audit

## Question

Does the text around the Sun face on f68r2 contain a human-readable plaintext
word that can serve as an external lexical anchor?

## Evidence and exclusions

- Use the public human-authored Quire 9 catalogue and the public Voynich
  transcription special-topics page.
- Reconstruct locus `f68r2.31` directly from the native ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b
  manual files and the official STA projections.
- Treat the three readings as alternate descriptions of one physical locus.
- Do not use OCR, automated image recognition, machine-generated readings,
  online translation, or visual similarity to a proposed language word.

## Decision rule

A lexical route can open only if a public human source supplies a readable
plaintext sequence and the manual readings preserve a compatible sequence.
If the public source explicitly leaves script identity unresolved, or the
manual readings disagree between illegible marks and ordinary Voynich
transcription, stop before assigning any word.

## Claim ceiling

This audit can establish only whether the presently documented f68r2 Sun-ring
ending is an admissible readable plaintext anchor. It cannot identify the
language, author, Sun term, sound values, cipher, plaintext, or translation of
the manuscript.
