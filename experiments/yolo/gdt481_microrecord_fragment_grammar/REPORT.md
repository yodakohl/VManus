# GDT481 microrecord fragment grammar

## Outcome

The productive repeated layer is the single composed event, not a fixed
two-event phrase. The 183 events reduce to 149 strict meaning templates; 27
recur and cover 61 events. Their broad role layer has 118 shapes; 39 recur and
cover 104 events. In contrast, 48 adjacent pairs yield 47 strict templates and
45 role shapes.

Sixteen recurrent strict event families cover 38 events under different
surfaces. Eight strict event templates cross registers and cover twenty events.
The strongest is `CATALOGUE[DANACH · NAME]`: five surfaces—`oteeeon`,
`otchody`, `otork`, `otora`, and `otdordy`—occur across four registers with the
same OT-opened following-entry reading.

Other cross-register exact compositions include `AUSGANG·NAME` (`arom` /
`arody`), `POSTEN·WÄHLEN` (`ys` / `cheys`),
`DANACH·ZIELORT·POSTEN` (`otalshy` / `otalsy`),
`FORTSETZEN·NAME` (`olay` / `olkchs`), and
`NAME·AUSGANG·NAME` (`yfary` / `ytarem`). `SETZEN·WERT` and
`NEHMEN·GRAD I·WÄHLEN` also cross registers with the same surface but retain
their owner-local German phrasing.

## Singleton-record coverage

Among GDT480's 107 unique whole-record templates:

- 28 contain at least one recurrent strict event; eight consist entirely of
  recurrent strict events.
- 59 contain at least one recurrent role event; 39 consist entirely of
  recurrent role events.
- none contains a recurrent strict pair; only four touch a recurrent role pair.
- 48 retain no recurrent event or pair even at role level.

The pair result is especially clean. Only `okeey | ary` repeats strictly, twice
on f72r. It is the same surface pair both times. No strict pair crosses a page
or register, and all eleven OL-bound cross-bundle pairs are unique. Fixed
two-event idioms therefore add almost nothing to the present six-page model.

Of the 48 remaining fragment tails, 45 are already single-event records; two
have two events and one has three. The next productive decomposition must work
inside those events, on ordered component subfragments, rather than widening
the record window.

Every event and pair retains a concrete GDT479 reading. No meaning, model,
boundary, name, surface, recipe, record, or page changes. Validation passes
118/118 checks.
