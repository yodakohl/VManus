# GDT1175 method

Question: can the unchanged productive29-root writer retain the next complete
cached recipe within its predeclared spelling-cost allowance?

The current B6 writer was constructed on b6.7. GDT1174's finite-message writers
failed their fixed statistical screen; the new productive version has only a
construction demonstration, not statistical support. Generalized composition
is not assumed to rescue earlier native transfer failures.

Inputs: the already-owned CoReMA b6.8 XML unit, the frozen productive writer
and its public description, and the manually declared source account below.
The extract carries the complete source unit and attribution. No target data.

`src/run.py` loads the unchanged module and frozen source trees, writes the
whole recipe, reverse-reads it, and counts every working glyph and literal
occurrence. `src/validate.py` independently counts literal payload in the
written surface, verifies hashes, exact source coverage and roundtrip. Source
interpretation is human evidence, not something these programs can prove.

The result is conditional on the explicit source assumptions. Read the complete
account, not just total counts. Retained unfamiliar words do not become newly
understood concepts. Arity records their declared grammatical attachment and
charges for it; no whole phrase is replaced by a novel short code.

The exact decision and exclusions are in PREREGISTRATION.md. Reproduction:

```
python3 experiments/yolo/gdt1175_fixed_writer_b6_8_transfer/src/run.py
python3 experiments/yolo/gdt1175_fixed_writer_b6_8_transfer/src/validate.py
```

The cached source extract is sufficient for replay; the full cache is not
required. Writer dependencies are relative repository paths. Local checkpoint
publication exception applies to this construction test; no new manuscript
finding is claimed.
