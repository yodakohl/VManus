## Fixed hypotheses and assumptions

For every one of the exact 22 ordered pairs of stems from GDT915, a written
pair `A+x B+y` is provisionally an event on one channel. It states the state
before and after a step. F reads x as input, y as output; R reads y as input,
x as output. Initial state is free at each physical paragraph boundary.
Successive occurrences of that same stem pair must satisfy output(previous)
= input(next), in manuscript order. Both global conventions are tested.

Example consequence, specified without claiming occurrence: `cheor chol`
then `cheol chol` fits F (r→l, l→l), but fails R (l→r, l→l).
`cheor chol` then `cheor chor` fails F and fits R. Thus this observable need
not be a word-count identity. r and l are abstract binary states; swapping
their meanings globally changes no equality. Fresh/dried, singular/plural,
open/closed and lexical categories are NOT identified by this test.

Assumptions: each family retains its participant/channel for the entire
paragraph; only its exact recognized phrases update that channel; other
legible groups are neutral for this particular state; writing follows event
order; there is no invisible reset. These strong assumptions are part of the
hypothesis. A contradiction cannot be excused by an unregistered intervening
operation, referent change, new boundary, omitted phrase or switched reading.
Different families may share physical words; their channels remain separate.
This does not assert an independently authored relation or a full translation.

## Scope and extraction

Use GDT928's existing complete paragraphs and original GDT915 22-family list.
Keep every admitted paragraph and every family, including zero-case families.
Only `anchor_eligible` lines license events: every raw word is lower-case and
every internal separation was a definite source space. No normalization,
cross-line word pair, fuzzy form, paragraph repair or new source admission.
Full paragraphs, including uncertain lines, are retained in the input.
Within each eligible line collect every matching adjacent two-word pair.
Successive events for a family may cross lines within the same paragraph.
A chain crossing any ineligible line is UNRESOLVED, never a match. This is
declared before extraction. All chain-bearing paragraphs are published whole.

## Evaluation and ceiling

Publish every event, chain prediction and contradiction, plus 22 × 2 rows
per reading. The construction is contradicted by any clear mismatch.
Zero clear chains means untested. A surviving row means only not contradicted
on these cases; it does not select a word meaning or establish probability.
For fully legible chain-bearing paragraphs enumerate the exact extrema over
permutations of the whole event types using a count-state recursion. Report
mobile physical leaves separately. This is capacity, not a null p-value and
not a complete-search control. Do not rank meanings or claim significance.
The two readings are alternate transcriptions, not replication.

There is zero independent semantic confirmation capacity. Any derived
inscription-to-inscription packet is exploratory and ineligible under GDT388:
endpoints/direction come from text and the tested construction. The executable
gate must be reported as such, not bypassed to score semantic evidence.
