# GDT583 method — object-conditioned action subreadings

## Question

Can the four intentionally broad GDT582 actions `T`, `SH`, `CHD` and `S`
receive useful physical or register-specific occurrence readings without
changing their portable core, stealing an argument from another action, or
opening a new page?

## Inputs

The pass pins five GDT582 artifacts: the 15,889-slot complete default ledger,
5,122-event edition, 793-statement edition, 744 local cards and the fixed set of
twenty complete passage checks. Their hashes are recorded in the manifest and
independently pinned by the validator. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Method

### 1. Fixed target universe

The target is every already segmented `T`, `SH`, `CHD` or `S` action slot:
384 + 794 + 341 + 402 = 1,921 slots. No surface is resegmented and no slot,
root or argument is added.

### 2. Two argument scopes

For each action the compiler reconstructs two fixed contexts:

- **direct host** — slots on the same event/card with the exact primary
  governor key; for `T`, a grade hosted by a visible action chain ending in
  that `T` is also admitted;
- **complete host** — every GDT581 slot carrying that exact governor key,
  including a bounded argument written in a later event.

This distinction matters for examples such as `G407-E0360`: the visible `S`
inherits `AIIN` and `Y` through its complete host, so the local reading can be
“Seihe den Pflanzenauszug” without pretending that the isolated `s` surface
contains a noun.

### 3. Action direction

Self-actions are ordered by their fixed slot positions inside one card. The
nearest previous and next action are recorded. Nothing crosses an event/card
boundary. This creates three deliberately occurrence-bound `T` readings:

- `T→SH`: `Erwärme` (10 slots);
- `SH→T`: `Kühle ab` (12 slots);
- Herbal/Pharma `T→CHD`: `Trockne` (3 slots, local bold reading).

A grade alone only licenses “temperiere auf den Grad”; it never chooses warm,
cold or dry.

### 4. Ordered rule deck

Twenty-nine explicit first-match rules cover all 1,921 slots. Register rules
come first where the register is decisive (`CHD=berechnen` in Celestial,
`CHD=behandeln` in Biological); object/state and relation rules then supply
`zerreiben`, `seihen`, `sieben`, `abtrennen`, `umleiten`, `ziehen lassen` and
`einweichen`. Every ladder ends in an honest broad fallback.

The twelve GDT507 `CH→SH` shared-argument bridges override imagery and remain
`halten`. The lone observed Biological `S→CHD` carrier also remains “auswählen;
behandeln”; it cannot turn three unobserved register pairs into a universal
“seihen und zerreiben” phrase.

### 5. Two output channels

The exact channel rebuilds all 1,623 affected running events, 158 affected
local cards and 591 affected statements with every slot ID, governor and rule
visible. A separate editorial channel smooths the fixed twenty inspection
passages into readable German while retaining event order.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The pass succeeds only if every target slot receives exactly one deterministic
rule; all GDT582 source projections and affected event/statement memberships
remain exact; all target and non-target slots remain visible in traces; the
twenty fixed passages stay four per register; and an independent validator can
rederive every first-match decision without importing the generator or rule
module.

The outputs are an exploratory working theory, not recovered plaintext. They
may say that a particular fixed occurrence is read as “erwärmen”, “abseihen”
or “zerreiben” in this edition. They do not establish `T`, `S` or `CHD` as a
manuscript word with that portable meaning, and they add no language,
historical codebook, object identity, page, parser, surface or root.
