# GDT708 method

## Question

Do the four retained full-bundle holds and the five shorter live prefixes form
a variable practical block, and does the next untested terminal field turn one
of them into a concrete local action/result reading?

## Inputs

- GDT707's 32 action-adjacent bundle census;
- its cumulative 19-edge graph and 479-token/51-line relation overlays;
- the nine exact candidates A012, A014, A024, A043, A073, A070, A029,
  A017 and A004;
- no new page, image, transcription or word value.

Both f84 and f84r remain forbidden.

## Method

For each candidate, start at the first semantic item after the action and keep
the shortest coherent sequence. Cut analytically at the first material change, new
operation, grade reset, next action, or restart after an already sufficient
state/product value. This cut need not be manuscript punctuation or a written
patient change. Do not skip an intervening item.

The four full holds receive an additional order test: locate the first field
that explicitly names a finished or closed material-bearing product. A local
action/result edge is added only when that terminal field restores both the
action's material head and its degree and when the action can plausibly produce
the described state. Quantity, material and state fields inside the path remain
visible hull-only carriers rather than invented pairwise edges.

The five earlier stopped triples are separately recut at their better two-item
prefix. Their third item is recorded as the first blocker, not discarded.

## Decision and claim ceiling

- A012 admits C021 because #6 restores `Arzneikompositum` and `Mittelstufe`
  together and adds an explicit completed product state.
- A014 and A024 remain object-block holds because `nehmen` or `abmessen` does
  not generate the added processing states.
- A043 retains its immediate #3 hold, but the longer path stops when #4 changes
  middle degree to grade end.
- All five two-item prefixes remain holds.

C021 is occurrence-bound. It does not assert that every attribute belongs to
one patient, that `Q|M|S+G|P` is portable syntax, or that any surface has gained
a word meaning.
