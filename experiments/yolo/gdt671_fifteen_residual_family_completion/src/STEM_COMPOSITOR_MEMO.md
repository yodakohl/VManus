## Learned exact forms

`daiiy` is identical in ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b at f47v.2. It lacks the `n` needed
for `AIIN_III`; pretending `D_MEASURE+AIIN_III+Y_START_OR_CLOSE` would insert an
invisible letter. Two full-panel positions are insufficient for a productive
`iiy` role.

`otoiir` has no V47 `iir` role. ZL3b and RF1b agree on `otoiir`, while IT2a
has `otoiis`; this is not bilateral support for either `AIIR_FRACTION_III` or
`IIN_FORM_III`. It therefore remains `LEARNED_OTOIIR_WHOLE` rather than
silently borrowing an absent `a` or changing final `r` to `n`.
