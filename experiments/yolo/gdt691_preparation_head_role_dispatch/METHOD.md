# GDT691 method

## Question

Can the 140 V63 preparation-noun spans be separated into actual heads,
compound heads, contextual relations, genitive sources, and quantity objects,
then rendered with concrete working values without changing a written ordinal,
licensed verb, material head, or page?

## Inputs

- GDT690 V63: 51 lines, 479 token positions, and 725 exact noun spans.
- GDT688: 113 action-licensed verb occurrences and written ordinals.
- GDT652: exact accepted/anchor O-preparation surfaces only.
- Two versioned source decks: 54 exact token decisions and seven historical
  preparation controls.

All inputs are compact derived artifacts over already admitted pages. No mixed
sealed/unsealed source is materialized; f84 and f84r are forbidden.

## Method

1. Select the 140 V63 noun spans whose class is `PREPARATION`.
2. Classify each mention independently from its exact character span:
   `HEAD`, `COMPOUND_HEAD`, `LOCATIVE_CONTEXT`, `GENITIVE_SOURCE`, or
   `QUANTITY_OBJECT`.
3. Keep source provenance separate from prose role. Exact accepted GDT652
   O-preparation surfaces are joined by exact surface; provisional bounded
   `ol*` scope, productive `s` heads, and all other learned/unexported surfaces
   remain separate routes.
4. Apply the 54 exact `(surface, expected V63 gloss)` decisions. Re-render 32
   contextual relations explicitly; keep the other three explicit rather than
   deleting them or using an antecedent-free pronoun.
5. Use the smallest coherent working families visible in the current edition:
   finished SH-preparations use `Mazerat`; abgezogene preparations use
   `Auszug`; heat alone never supplies `Absud`; terminal dry wholes use
   `Masse/Trockenmasse`; O-CKH retains `Arzneizubereitung`.
6. Rebuild all 51 lines and exact V64 preparation-term spans. Preserve every
   V61 action ordinal and require no additional exact verb-form loss relative
   to V63.
7. Independently replay populations, roles, routes, rules, line spans, terms,
   verb ordinals, input/output hashes, and every generated byte.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

One practical main value is allowed where a complete local workflow or bounded
family makes it useful. Rivals remain in the rule deck and apparatus. A local
working value is not exported as a free substring value. In particular,
P041–P051 are `LOCAL_CONTEXT_WHOLE`, not a completed O/Q fraction morphology.

V64 is an exploratory practical renderer, not recovered plaintext. Mention
roles and output ordinals are reproducible; German heads are replaceable. No
language, phonetics, universal o-value, ingredient identity, disease, patient,
cure, page, transcription, or sealed datum is identified.
