# Artifacts

PREDICTIONS.json owns the two complete paragraph pairs and all 190 fixed queries.
PREFLIGHT.json records the complete synthetic checks. After execution, ROWS.json
contains every answer, complete positive code/parse/world witness and projected
tuple; CANDIDATES.tsv gives one row per query. RESULT.json summarizes marginal
domains without assuming arbitrary domain combinations are compatible.
INDEPENDENT_QUERIES.json and VALIDATION.json retain independent finite checks,
including unresolved limits. No artifact supplies independent word meanings.
