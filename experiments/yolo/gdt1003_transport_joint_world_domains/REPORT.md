## What the positive solutions actually contain

Ten of the 24 tuple representatives contain a two-cargo, three-voyage paragraph;
the others use one cargo and one voyage per paragraph. Thus the earlier failed
syntax samples did not exhaust physically valid shared readings. For example,
P2-4-T08 has dcheor=OTHER_CARGO, s=G, shodol=M. The complete f100r reading states
initial colocation and capacity, moves W out, returns empty, retains two THEN
clauses, states the goal and safety condition, ferries G out and concludes that
all are safely together. The complete f96r reading with the **same dictionary**
moves its sole G out and also satisfies its full initial/capacity/safety/goal/
conclusion scope. No extra line, omitted cargo or repaired reference is needed.
This is an illustrative member of the complete published census, not a preferred
or independently confirmed translation.

Every representative has zero instantiated hazard pairs. The 30–36-group
paragraphs cannot hold the mandatory 26 groups, a six-group distinct-cargo PAIR
and the minimum six groups for three voyages. Thus this population has no capacity
to confirm a wolf–goat–cabbage safety relationship. General safety statements can
be vacuous here. The more elaborate original GDT993 seven-voyage hypothesis and
its actual conditional safety distinctions remain unchanged.

The apparent uniqueness **shodol=M is inherited syntax**: shodol is word4 of
f100r, the only possible initial clause requires @Agent at word4, and @Agent has
only M. This is not a new semantic discovery. Other word-value exclusions may
also follow from syntax; this experiment did not predeclare a complete syntax
ablation and does not attribute every exclusion to world reasoning.

Each pair needs 61–62 additional whole-word aliases, including 2–6 distinct new
THEN forms per representative. Productive composition is not explained. All
shared tuples are exhausted, but the full dictionaries and parses within each
tuple are not. Variant checks describe the saved representative only; they do
not exhaust variant possibilities over every dictionary with that tuple.
OTHER/FIRST and THERE/GOAL/CURRENT distinctions, synonyms and cargo-label
permutations therefore remain explicit dependencies or ambiguities.

## Validation, exposure and decision

The primary run took about four seconds. Twenty complete synthetic world cases
passed before public registration a30933012. The independent validator checks
46 full positive witnesses and 284 bit-executor replays. It agrees on 22 SAT and
168 UNSAT primary queries, plus two UNSAT tuple-exhaustion queries; no negative
check is unresolved. See VALIDATION.json and the public/execution receipts.
These are implementation/consequence checks, not independent meaning evidence.

All four physical leaves were already exposed to the project. Source flags are
false for ZL f112v and IT f96r, true for IT f24v and IT f100r; every pair therefore
contains a source-uncertain member. Both members participate in fitting. There
is **zero independent confirmation capacity**, no reserved leaf, no new image
and no f84/f84r or f116v access. Alternate transcription editions remain readings
of one manuscript. No whole-search null was run and no significance is claimed.

**Decision:** retain both complete shared hypotheses and all 24 projected tuples.
GDT1002's five-way and two conflicting pair exclusions remain closed; GDT993 and
GDT997's original qualified positives remain. These are usable concrete candidates
for a separately justified component/meaning test, not a reason to append a new
61-word glossary or to open reserves. A next test must expose a discriminating
consequence beyond this assumed grammar, with complete word ownership; no
automatic alias, source, reference, morphology or serializer repair. Confirmed
translated words: **0**.
