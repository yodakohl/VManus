# GDT373 — internal functional-operator roadmap

## Purpose

GDT373 registers the remaining internal functional/grammatical hypothesis
families before any common search is run.  It is a route audit and discovery
freeze, not evidence for a function, role, language, or translation.

The new object of study is a **record-conditioned rewrite over atomic GDT327
joint tuples and source-native boundaries**.  It is not a substring edit, a
PAGE_HOST decomposition, a synonym claim, or a next-state operator chosen with
target information.

## Fixed evidence boundary

- GDT327 joint tuples remain atomic.  PAGE_HOST is not factored or mined.
- Physical lines, source-native hierarchical separators, drawing-reset
  segments, DY, B3, record/field order, and layout/register metadata may be
  used.
- Every `f84*` row is forbidden and must be rejected before any formal field is
  parsed or retained.
- ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate readings of one object, not replications.
- No visual grounding, external semantic atlas, lexical gloss, substring
  similarity, or historical-language hypothesis enters discovery.

## Deduplication rule

A proposed family is genuinely new only if its endpoint differs from all four
closed or constrained ancestors below:

1. **GDT003 / GDT078 string paradigms:** character-level transformations and
   rectangle completion.
2. **Minimal-pair/synonym work:** isolated surface substitutions or proposed
   equivalence of words.
3. **Earlier scope/order work:** marginal boundary association or a first-order
   field-state chain without a transferable rewrite.
4. **GDT345–347 operator/manifold work:** predicting the next formal coordinate
   state from the predecessor and layout.

The admissible endpoint is instead: a variable record rewrite that recurs over
multiple opaque host records, predicts a held variant or downstream record
change, and transfers across physical folios.  A candidate that reduces to one
of the four ancestors is reported as a duplicate and is not promoted.

## Candidate signatures frozen before forms

Every mined candidate must expose the fields in
`artifacts/gdt373_candidate_signature_schema.tsv`.  The compulsory behavioral
axes are symmetry, valency/arity change, scope, host diversity, optionality,
mutual exclusion, downstream-state change, record-length effect, cross-folio
support, cross-register support, held-folio gain, and null-adjusted evidence.

No candidate receives a semantic gloss.  Anonymous labels such as
`COORDINATOR_LIKE`, `EXCLUSION_LIKE`, `RELATION_LIKE`, `RESUME_LIKE`,
`REPEAT_LIKE`, and `SEQUENCE_LIKE` are permitted only as behavioral summaries.

## Common discovery instrument required next

GDT374 must use one shared mining and scoring framework for every admissible
family:

1. Build f84-free records from source-native physical lines and higher reset
   segments, retaining hierarchical spaces, DY/B3, and atomic GDT327 tuples.
2. Align near-isomorphic records without using glyph/string similarity.
3. Infer only a predeclared edit grammar: prefix/suffix/internal tuple
   insertion or deletion (`X<->NX`, `X<->XN`, `XY<->XCY`), paired two-site
   rewrite (`AXB<->APXQB`), exact tuple replacement, boundary split/join,
   duplication, and prior-record-conditioned shortening/resumption.
4. Rank by cross-host recurrence and held-folio predictive gain, not raw
   frequency.
5. Compare against placement, exact-record, tuple-frequency, and matched edit
   baselines.  Nulls preserve page/register/layout/position, source record
   length, tuple frequency/recurrence, host identity where applicable, and
   boundary opportunity.
6. Charge the full enumerated candidate library.  Report local and max-search
   evidence and leave-folio/register diagnostics.
7. Calibration on readable structured corpora is allowed only with the same
   observable boundary and opaque-identity interface.

## Promotion rule

Discovery is exploratory: weak, asymmetric, and one-sided observations remain
in the atlas.  Promotion to a prospective test requires all of:

- at least three opaque base records/host contexts;
- at least two physical folios;
- positive held-folio gain over the strongest matched baseline;
- no dependence on one register unless explicitly registered as
  register-specific;
- a frozen anonymous behavioral signature and rewrite rule;
- an untouched non-f84 prospective panel selected without inspecting the
  candidate's tuple identities.

Failure closes only the registered behavioral family/endpoint, not all
possible grammar.

## Claim ceiling

At most, GDT373 establishes a ranked, deduplicated roadmap and a frozen common
instrument contract.  It assigns no function, morpheme, POS, language,
plaintext, meaning, or translation.
