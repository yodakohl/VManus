## What the current reading sounds like

The result is not one repeated sentence grammar. It alternates among three
small card types:

- address cards: “Adressspur: danach → Positionswert”;
- action cards: “Setze den Positionswert von der Ausgangsposition”;
- catalogue cards: “Sternstelle »om« — Ausgangszuordnung.”

GDT475's record joins make combinations readable. For example, the first
pharmaceutical record on f89r is one three-locus operation: establish and take
the drug post, then continue with drug entry `qk`, then continue with drug entry
`d` at the marked place. The two later clauses no longer need isolated implicit
verbs because OL inherits the active operation.

Order is explicit wherever present. All 41 OT slots open or bridge to a
right-hand next unit. All 28 OL slots keep a unit active through a forward,
bridge, or backward scope. Sixty events contain at least one such slot; the
other 123 retain their ordinary active reading. Joint forms such as `otol` are
therefore printed as two operations—start the next unit, then continue it—not
as memorized giant words.

## Preservation

The build changes zero component meanings, learned name cores, surfaces,
recipes, events, or pages. The exact-only packages `ykyd` and `yddy` remain
exact-only. Every event, bundle, and record ID is printed in the readable book,
and the deterministic validator passes 83/83 checks.

This is the definitive edition of the current creative working theory for
