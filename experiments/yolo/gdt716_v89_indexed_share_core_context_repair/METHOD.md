# GDT716 method — V89 indexed-share repair

## Question

Can thirteen exact cards from the already selected GDT693/GDT694 indexed-share
model receive predictable portable cores without baking generic drug or Ansatz
patients into the word?

## Inputs

- canonical V88 lexical, context, confidence, held-queue, and span artifacts;
- GDT693's exact selected-share rules, 479-position reader, R/N pair census,
  revision rows, and verb-preservation rows;
- GDT694's exact residual migrations, 479-position reader, composition census,
  and verb-preservation rows;
- GDT711 family evidence, with `F_R +8` as the only score path.

## Method

The tranche contains eight direct GDT693 rules and five GDT694 residual
compositions. Each has one exact active position. The core preserves the share
index, written dry/hot/cold/result field, and any licensed action. A concrete
referent is allowed only in the named local realization.

Every target binds two exact primary rows: the rule/migration row and its exact
position row. The three action cards additionally bind an exact verb-preservation
row. `F_R` is credited once per reading; deletion of a noun, fluent German,
reference frames, and action prose add zero. All components remain bound and
nonexportable.

All 311 non-target lexical rows and 466 non-target contexts must preserve V88
fieldwise parity. Both spans and the f7r.2 one-shot renderer are rebuilt and
must remain semantically identical.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A target passes only if its rule, exact position, share index, composition,
score derivation, local anchor, core/context boundary, and export restrictions
all replay. Every target remains W1; no W3 row or span position is touched.

This is an exploratory working dictionary, not confirmed plaintext. It adds no
language, historical lexicon, free morpheme, page, image, transcription, f84,
or f84r. Historical status remains `H0_NONE`.
