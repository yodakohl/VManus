# Sidequest Pass 800: two recurrent-strip tests

This is a fast creative workshop pass over the same seven prose pages. It asks
whether two visually tempting groups behave like replaceable small words. It
does not add pages or alter the 381-event edition.

## Result

`CH / SH / CTH` is promoted from the memorized wall strip to the productive
core. The shortest readings remain:

- `CH` = **ENTNEHMEN**;
- `SH` = **HALTEN**;
- `CTH` = **BEREITEN**.

All three occupy the identical registered recipe `ACTION + E + Y`:

- `dchey` = **dies kurz entnehmen**;
- `tshey` = **dies kurz halten**;
- `qcthey / shcthey` = **dies kurz bereiten**.

The fixed tail contributes the same `KURZ + DIES` every time. Four real source
events therefore permit twelve direct workshop readbacks without changing the
picture owner or any other card in the statement. This is exactly the kind of
small taught choice a group of scribes could learn.

The two cards containing more than one member are not failures. They are
ordered action stacks: `SH + E + CTH + Y` says to hold briefly into the prepared
state, and the longer form adds `CHD`, the transfer step. The actions must not
be collapsed into one vague stem.

## The tempting O family does not work the same way

`O / OR / HO` remains three separate wall-strip values:

- `O` = **VORGANG**;
- `OR` = **ANSATZ**;
- `HO` = **ZUTAT**.

Only bare `OR` and `HO` share a position. There is no corresponding bare `O`
card and no common three-member tail. More importantly, the supposedly related
values coexist inside single cards:

- `CTH + O + OR` = prepare the batch in the operation;
- `O + Y + K + OR` = add the current batch in the operation;
- `HO + CH + OR` = take an ingredient for the batch.

A workshop writer could not treat those as interchangeable renderings of one
root. They name three different grammatical levels: procedure, preparation,
and ingredient. Visible `o` is therefore not licensed as WATER, OIL, or a
universal substance stem here.

## Updated compact architecture

The second grammar now has eighteen paradigm cores and thirteen remaining
recurrent wall-strip values. No unseen surface is needed for this promotion:
the decisive CH/SH/CTH mini-deck is already complete on the fixed pages.

Next, compare the remaining operation-like strip values `SHED / CHK / P / LSH`
under shared grade and endpoint tails. The goal is another small verb-choice
deck, not a universal letter-by-letter language.
