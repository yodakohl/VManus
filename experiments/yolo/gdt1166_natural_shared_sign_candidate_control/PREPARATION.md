# Frozen source preparation policy

GDT1166 source preparation only. No fit, candidate selection, oracle-coverage calculation or recovery scoring is performed by `src/prepare.py`. This policy is fixed before the preparation command below. The eight original public XML files in `sources/` are byte-pinned in the script and `src/SOURCE.json`; all original text, markup, revisions and metadata remain available there. CoReMA text is CC BY 4.0; no facsimiles are an input. This is a changed representation of previously known CoReMA, not a newly discovered corpus or a rescue of GDT155/1159.

## Input and record boundaries

Control writing comes only from `o:corema.b4/TEI_SOURCE`, Berlin Ms. germ. qu.1187, dated 1437–1475 by the edition. The five external readable-reference streams are the original B6, Br1, Bs1, Gr1 and W1 TEI files; no B4 expansions enter their vocabulary/counts. The character and editorial declarations are the previously acquired original streams. Every direct `/TEI/text/body/ab/seg` is a complete edition record boundary for this control; nested heading/transposition segments are not extra records. This does not certify that every surviving physical recipe is complete.

B4's physical-page metadata contains both recto and verso for all 42 leaves 71–112. Preparation independently checks this and assigns the ordered first `floor(42/2)` physical leaves 71–91 to training and 92–112 to held input. Record leaf sets use the current `pb` inherited from earlier siblings and every subsequent `pb/lb/cb` in the record's original document order. All words from a record crossing the cut remain conserved as BRIDGE and enter neither training nor held scoring. The resulting source-only split is 130 TRAIN records, 138 TEST records, and one BRIDGE record, R0131 on leaves 91/92. These numbers describe source partitioning, not recovery capacity.

## Character and word projection

The native stream uses literal written characters and consistent documented glyph shapes only. A referenced glyph is replaced by its declared Unicode codepoint when numeric and graphic-symbol fields agree. Private-use codepoints remain opaque shape classes. Different value-bearing references with the same consistent graphic key collapse: overline `bar_d/e/em/en/in/m/n`, comma `combcomma_e/er/r`, stroked-p `pbardes_per/perir`, and ur-shaped `urrot/tur/uitur`. The raw reference, normalized value, transcription name, glyph semantic name, and `am/abbr/ex` boundaries never appear in model input. A `g` element's text is not used as a fallback. Undeclared/missing references and contradictory codepoint/graphic fields become one unsupported atom -1. In particular, `tlbar` is not merged with `lbbar` merely because both say U+2114; `etcamp` is also conflicting under this rule.

`ex` letters are removed entirely from native input. `abbr` and `am` are unwrapped without adding separators or indicator bits. Their membership remains only in the gold/conservation ledger for selecting the fixed evaluation obligations. Ordinary carriers before and after a silent `ex` remain adjacent; no sign is invented. Written double-oblique hyphens and other documented punctuation glyphs remain native atoms.

The operational gold-only `abbreviated` flag means that some surviving projected part of the word is inside an `abbr` ancestor; it is not inferred from a visible shape, and the learner cannot read it. A structural audit finds all 1,166 B4 `ex` and all 1,166 B4 `am` nodes have an `abbr` ancestor (zero outside). Thus this definition does not omit a standalone B4 restoration/mark under the admitted schema. Split values are exactly uppercase `TRAIN`, `TEST` and `BRIDGE`. The ledger's `reference` is always a string; uncertainty is conveyed by separate boolean `input_unknown`/`expansion_unknown`, never by treating an unknown string as a valid expansion.

Tokenization is **whitespace only, with no outer-punctuation stripping, case folding, Unicode normalization or spelling repair**. Literal whitespace and `lb/pb/cb` outside `w` end a token. Whitespace and these breaks inside `w` do not end it; this is explicitly given editorial word-join information. Physical hyphen glyphs remain in the joined token. No `abbr`, `am`, `ex`, heading or `seg` boundary is used to split an interior word. Record ends terminate tokens. XML whitespace is an editorial transcription convention, not a measured manuscript-gap census. Modern `note` text, `anchor`, `ptr`, `listTranspose` and `transpose` instructions are excluded from both character streams but remain in the raw source and conservation ledger; no transposition is applied.

Final-state projection drops `del` and keeps `add`. Additions containing `unclear`, `supplied` or `gap` become UNKNOWN rather than a fabricated final reading. `unclear`, `supplied`, `expan`, `gap`, unsupported `choice`, and untyped `metamark` in native writing each retain an UNKNOWN span; their restored characters/length are not supplied as native atoms. `handShift` is metadata. B4 has no `choice` or `expan`, but these branches prevent silently treating expanded-only blocks as observed writing. Literal question marks remain literal source characters; only explicit uncertainty markup creates the uncertainty flag, with raw source retained for audit. No new inference about what an editor's question mark means is made.

The B4 readable gold stream follows the **native token boundaries**, keeps `ex` letters, removes `am`, and uses the declaration's normalized mappings for consistent ordinary glyphs. This includes `=` for a written double-oblique hyphen; it is not silently removed. Empty normalizations of punctuation remain empty. Missing/conflicting glyphs, ambiguous additions and explicitly uncertain/supplied spans set `expansion_unknown`; no value is guessed. Restoration whitespace would also set `expansion_unknown`, rather than introducing model-visible boundaries. Each ledger word retains its native character array, opaque array, readable reference, flags, `abbreviated`, `input_unknown`, `expansion_unknown`, record ID, zero-based word index and occurrence ID. Empty readable strings remain unsupported obligations, not vocabulary entries.

External readable-reference tokenization is independent of B4: same whitespace/`w` and final-state rules, `ex` retained and `am` removed, consistent ordinary glyphs normalized. Its explicit `expan` text may be used as readable plaintext because this reference view does not claim an observed native spelling. Unclear/supplied/conflicting/unsupported spans are represented as UNKNOWN; reference words containing an UNKNOWN span are excluded from usable vocabulary counts and counted separately. No source sign name is a plaintext candidate. No filter based on eventual B4 gold match is applied.

External direct `/TEI/text/body/ab/seg` record starts and ends also insert separators explicitly, even if adjacent XML records have no intervening whitespace. Nested heading segments remain transparent. A synthetic adjacent-record fixture verifies this prospective rule.

## Anonymization and separation

The full B4 observed consistent character alphabet is sorted by Unicode, then a Python `random.Random(20261003)` permutation assigns integer IDs. Only IDs and total alphabet size are available to the fitter. Full-alphabet size is disclosed, but held-only IDs have no fitted meaning; they must remain unsupported at prediction, not be assigned using held examples. The complete character/ID mapping is a preparation-only audit artifact. Unknown atom -1 has no learned shared value and is not evidence that two unknown spans were the same word.

`TRAIN_INPUT.json` contains `schema_version`, `n_atoms`, `unknown_atom`, `records:[{record_id,words:[[atom,...],...]}]`, and `words:[{atoms,count}]`. Unknown-containing words remain in its inventory. `REFERENCE_INPUT.json` supplies independent `words:[{word,count}]` and source/hash accounting only. The fitter may read exactly these two files; no B4 aligned expansion or abbreviation-membership flag is supplied. `HOLDOUT_INPUT.json` has the same anonymous record/count schema, opened only after the model-selection prediction lock. `SOURCE_GOLD_LEDGER.json` and `OPAQUE_KEY_PRIVATE_TO_PREPARATION.json` are forbidden fitter inputs; their role is auditable source conservation and post-lock scoring, not a claim that human historical exposure is absent.

The scoring contract retains all held new abbreviated known written types, including unsupported short forms and out-of-vocabulary expansions. Unknown-written or unknown-expansion abbreviated occurrences each remain a unique, mandatory zero-credit obligation, even when an unknown representation also appeared in training. They are not described as proven distinct lexical types. The scorer must report K known novel types and U unknown obligations separately, with the fixed conservative denominator K+U. Source preparation does not calculate that panel or its oracle/recovery counts.

## Checks and command

Run `python3 experiments/yolo/gdt1166_natural_shared_sign_candidate_control/src/prepare.py --fixtures-only` for source pins and synthetic projection fixtures; run the same command without the flag to materialize inputs and source receipts. Fixtures check that wrapper/removal changes do not expose restored-letter positions, value aliases share a written glyph, `w` joins retain the hyphen, ordinary line breaks split tokens, deletion/addition do not concatenate both readings, editorial notes do not become text, and unknown/conflicting glyphs or supplied letters cannot be recovered through display text. These are source-projection checks, not semantic or decipherment evidence.
