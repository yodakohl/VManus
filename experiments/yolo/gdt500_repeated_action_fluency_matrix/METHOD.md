# GDT500 method

## Question

Can the fifteen GDT499 phrases with two identical action slots receive concise
German defaults while retaining both technical slots, exact source wording,
evidence status and full 495-cell coverage?

## Inputs

- the complete 495-cell GDT498 matrix;
- the complete 352-cell GDT499 priority atlas;
- the exact fifteen-cell GDT499 repeated-action queue.

No manuscript page, image or transcription is opened.

## Method

1. Independently recover all source recipes containing a repeated action root
   and require exact agreement with the fifteen-cell GDT499 queue.
2. Admit only three fixed two-action recipes: `CH+CH`, `CHD+CHD+Y` and
   `CH+CH+E+Y`, each in all five registers.
3. Split the old phrase into first action clause, second action clause and
   punctuation/grade tail. Require the second clause to be the exact repeated
   form of the first; active-argument `CH+CH` may replace the repeated object
   with `es` as already rendered.
4. Keep the first clause and express its two action slots with exactly one
   `zweimal`. Separable celestial `aufnehmen` becomes `Nimm ... zweimal auf`;
   the grade tail remains after the compressed clause.
5. Store both source clauses and the tail beside the compressed default and
   reconstruct the old phrase byte for byte. Publish a complete 495-cell deck,
   exact edited/unchanged and observed/composed subsets, plus rule/register
   summaries.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A phrase may change only when it belongs to the exact GDT499 fifteen-cell
queue, contains exactly two identical action slots, uses one of the three fixed
recipes and roundtrips to the old source phrase. `zweimal` is an editorial
rendering of the already doubled technical action, not a new root meaning or a
claim about historical wording. Recipes, component traces, observations and
composition labels cannot change; no spelling or occurrence is predicted.
