# GDT685 method

## Question

Do the 540 already admitted exact `chol`, `shol` and `tol` surfaces support the
predicted universal nouns `Trockenansatz`, `Feuchtansatz` and `Kaltansatz`, or
does the shared `X+OL` family carry only a state whose material head must be
supplied by local context?

## Inputs

- GDT628's exhaustive `OL_OR_CARRIER_OCCURRENCES.tsv`, productive carrier
  matrix and `chol` value realizations;
- GDT630's complete value-expression and immediate part-attachment tables;
- GDT642's accepted exact `E+OL` whole-word heads;
- the GDT664 stem model and GDT671 V48 token glossary;
- GDT683's free-`ol` audit and complete V57 reader;
- GDT684's 479-position semantic-debt audit and frozen totals;
- seven local V58 line specifications in `src/V58_LINE_PATCH_SPECS.tsv`.

No page, image or raw transcription is opened. The circuit uses only cached,
already admitted artifacts. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Method

1. Select every exact `chol`, `shol` and `tol` row from the GDT628 occurrence
   atlas and require 343, 163 and 34 positions respectively.
2. Retain reader agreement, page, locus, ordinal, neighbours and the original
   carrier role for every position; do not infer a word boundary from an EVA
   substring.
3. Compare the three surfaces with all 935 core-bearing `X+OL` tokens in the
   24-cell quality lattice and record occupied cells and alternate-reader
   variants.
4. Join the 43 `chol`, 13 `shol` and one `tol` value realizations to their
   exact direct, fused or separated form. Separately preserve the seven
   visible plant-part contacts.
5. Contrast the family with exact `E+OL` wholes whose published card contains
   an explicit `Drogenstoff` or `Drogenteil` head. This tests whether the noun
   is carried by bare `OL` or by the whole licensed surface.
6. Enumerate every visible target/`ol` adjacency. Distinguish eight genuinely
   separate free-`ol` contacts from two apparent contacts that another reader
   binds into a compound.
7. Assign each target occurrence one reproducible local mode: visible part
   head, local materia candidate, degree with head open, state with head open,
   contrasting quality cells, or adjacency to a separate `ol`.
8. Compare five renderer models: state plus outer head, generic nominal
   fallback, universal `-Ansatz`, `CHO/SHO+L`, and three memorized wholes.
9. Apply the winning state-only default at the eight exact V57 target
   positions. Rewrite practical prose only where a local head is visible;
   otherwise state the quality and leave the head open.
10. Recompute the GDT684 strict, mechanical, broad and provenance-aware debt
    layers. Rebuild all generated artifacts byte-for-byte in a temporary
    directory and validate counts, hashes, joins, action licenses and sealed
    exclusions.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The universal noun passes only if one fixed preparation head explains the
quality lattice, degree expressions, visible part heads, explicit `E+OL`
heads and target/free-`ol` contacts without duplicating or inventing a noun.
Otherwise the renderer must keep the portable state and dispatch the head
locally.

GDT685 assigns replaceable working defaults to all 540 exact target positions
and revises eight positions in the 51-line reader. It does not identify an
ingredient, liquid, plant, disease, patient, cure, language, phonetic value or
historical codebook. Sixty-four target positions retain alternate-reader
variants. `tol = kalt` remains a working thermal orientation, and a local
`CHO/SHO+L` boundary remains possible where separately licensed.
