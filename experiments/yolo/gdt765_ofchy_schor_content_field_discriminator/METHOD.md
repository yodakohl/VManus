## Method

1. Enumerate every raw `ofchy` and `schor`, then retain only target tokens on
   which the three alternate readings agree.
2. For each admitted target, keep the entire written line and build the
   shortest exact rightward span that contains its visible specification.
   The three `ofchy` tails are respectively value III, dry form plus part, and
   hot fraction plus amount. The `schor` positions supply a value field, an
   initial item head, and an initial item plus hot/dry-grade field.
3. Enumerate two formal neighborhoods: all exact `ofch*` wholes and all exact
   `*fchy` wholes. These are analogy decks only. The method never assigns
   `ofch`, `f`, `ch`, `y`, or another substring a German value.
4. Enumerate 67 exact `chor`-family-head plus value pairs and the twelve exact
   `H-head X daiin` triples. This tests whether `schor daiin` belongs to an
   established nominal field pattern rather than an action clause.
5. Read f22r.4–6 as one local record. Its first two lines contain four exact
   value cells: `ofchy daiin`, `schor daiin`, `ol daiin`, and `dar daiin`.
6. Keep two layers in the renderer. The portable layer says “named drug” and
   “plant-part item.” The deliberately concrete layer says “flower mass” and
   “flower head.” `daiin` remains value III in the portable layer; the concrete
   f22 reading chooses three units as a replaceable amount interpretation.
7. Audit `cfhy` separately. Six exact occurrences support a field-transition
   default; the older “take,” “wring,” and free flower-substring readings do
   not enter the active renderer.
8. Rebuild every artifact in a temporary directory and require byte identity.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The best role must cover all exact target contexts without creating a stacked
quality or redundant unit phrase. A concrete identity is an explicit working
choice, not a confirmed decipherment: it stays active while it fits all known
positions and yields to a clearer rival when one appears.

GDT765 may select `ofchy` as a learned material/preparation head with the bold
default `Blütenmasse`, select `schor` as an H2 plant-part item with the bold
default `Blütenstand`, and render only the six observed exact spans plus the
fully enumerated f22r.4 line. It may not export a substring value, revive
`s = Samen`, turn EVA labels into Latin spelling, fix `daiin` globally as
amount or degree, or claim a confirmed word, substance, unit, plaintext,
language, new page, f84, or f84r.
