# GDT406 method

## Question

How much of each of the 49 GDT405 amber singleton recipes is independently
supported by packages already present on the admitted 26 pages?

## Inputs

- the 3,888-event Pass-1026 ledger from the original 22 pages;
- the 688-event GDT404 ledger from the four random pages;
- the 49 amber recipe locks and 46 locked atoms from GDT405.

No transcription row, image, or page outside those inputs is opened.

## Method

For every amber singleton, remove that surface from the 4,576-event corpus.
Count its adjacent atom pairs and up-to-four-atom spans on other surfaces,
pages, and specifically the older 22-page deck. Also rank the locked primary
recipe descriptively against its recorded one-edit rivals with a smoothed
bigram score. This ranking is a risk diagnostic only: it cannot change the
GDT405 recipe.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Package support stratifies Amber into strong-old, current-cross-page, partial,
or isolated support. **No singleton is promoted to Green:** GDT405 requires a
new exact recurrence with the same visible boundary. No new atom, meaning,
page, parser factor, or segmentation is admitted here.
