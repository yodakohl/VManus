## Notation clarification after observer freeze

Some frozen prose loosely calls RF's marks uncertainty notation. That is
imprecise: `@NNN;` encodes an extended-EVA character, and curly braces mark
connected/ligatured components. Neither notation alone establishes reader
uncertainty. The observers' inability to resolve the photographed strokes is
a separate visual judgment. Literal strings and frozen observations are
preserved; no alias or new canonical character follows. See the alphabet
author's [extended-EVA and ligature description](https://www.voynich.nu/transcr.html).

## Consequence and limits

The ALL/PERSON/BENEFIT semantics were guesses before this audit and remain
guesses. A repeated short shape supports a shape comparison, not ALL. An
unresolved first shape cannot be used to silently import the ZL construction
into IT/RF. This audit tests physical support for the chosen strings; it is
not an independent meaning test, p-value, or rejection of a language or genre.
The images were previously admitted, the seed was already known, and both
observers are models looking at the same manuscript pixels.

The frozen all-target/source draft can be assessed on its stated ZL terms,
with this limitation attached. No reserve is opened and f84/f84r remain sealed.
Stop this unchanged native audit: another rerender or consensus vote would
not provide a new semantic discriminator.
