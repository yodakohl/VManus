## Exploratory persistence rule

A useful interpretation is not withdrawn merely because the ten pages cannot
prove it uniquely. Preserve a leading hypothesis while it remains compatible
with all occurrences and makes the overall reading simpler. Replace it only
when it becomes clearly contradictory or another explanation covers more
evidence with fewer assumptions. Report confidence and rivals instead of
collapsing every unresolved result to a neutral label.

Each agent receives one unchanged perspective from
`SIDEQUEST_FOUR_AGENT_BACKGROUNDS.md` and may not read sibling V13 outcomes.

## Candidate expansions

- associative relation: WITH / RELATED TO / APPLIED TO;
- inherited relation: SAME RELATION / AS ABOVE / WITH THE CURRENT ITEM;
- belonging/source: OF / FROM / BELONGING TO;
- medium/location: IN / WITHIN / IN THE MEDIUM OF;
- coordination/list: AND / ALSO;
- portion/share/partitive;
- argument-slot or relation-frame notation without ordinary prose expansion;
- ordinary high-frequency formula word with context-dependent functions;
- an explicit mixture governed by one learnable rule.

These English phrases are provisional source-class paraphrases, never decoded
lexemes. A candidate may combine related expansions if it specifies when the
choice changes.
