## Executable three-state rule

The technical-register model tracks:

```text
H = active heading, owner or relation anchor
P = current payload block
E = whether a relation edge awaits a following payload
```

Writing rule:

1. Medial `A–L/O–B`: attach B under the locally active relation anchored by A
   or H.
2. Repeated `A–L/O–B–L/O–C`: attach B, then continue the same relation to C;
   B and C may be sibling entries rather than a literal binary chain.
3. FIRST `L/O–B`: inherit H from the current record or preceding committed
   field and add B under it.
4. ONLY `L/O`: retain/repeat the inherited relation as a complete elliptical
   entry.
5. LAST `A–L/O`: keep E open across the physical line; the next record-local
   payload fills it.
6. `...L/O–COMMIT`: commit the current block as attached under inherited H;
   no overt right operand is required inside that field.

This one rule covers all positional types without making a physical line a
sentence.

The best general reverse parse is now:

> Keep the current entry or heading active. Add the next opaque item under the
> same relation. If another L/O follows, add another related item. If the cell
> closes, commit the related block; if the physical line ends after L/O, carry
> the open relation to the next line.

This makes the sidequest parser more readable without assigning lexical
content to any operand.
