# GDT759 construction pass registration

## Fixed universe

The pass uses the 26 directed pairs in
`src/EXACT_CONSTRUCTION_PRIORS.tsv`, the four fused `s`-value forms in
`src/FUSED_QUANTITY_FAMILY_PRIORS.tsv`, all admitted reader-exact occurrences,
and all admitted three-reader lines returned by the guarded query. No pair is
added from its output frequency.

## Registered outputs

- complete 122-span construction atlas and three family slices;
- 26-pair observed/unobserved summary;
- every fusion/separation boundary candidate plus the exact normalized subset;
- four fused seed-family revisions;
- all 154 `s` context dispatches and fourteen `odol/ols` adjudications;
- a compact exact construction dictionary and revised thirteen-line reader;
- historical comparator/provenance rows, result and validation artifacts.

## Interpretation rule

Observed exact pairs may receive only their registered whole-span rendering.
Unobserved pairs stay unlicensed. A complete-line normalized cross-reader match
may support fused/separated expression identity, but never a glyph, sound or
free substring meaning. Historical forms rank functions and cannot identify a
Voynich spelling.

## Boundaries

No new page, image or transcription is admitted. `f84` and `f84r` remain
forbidden. ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b are alternate readings of one manuscript. All
meanings and confidence labels remain replaceable working hypotheses.
