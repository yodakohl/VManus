# GDT759 method

## Question

Do the GDT758 meanings begin to compose into repeatable useful phrases when
the full exact constructions are counted globally? In particular:

1. are `s/or/ar + an/ain/aiin/aiiin` stable amount expressions, including
   fusion/separation variants across the three manuscript readings;
2. do `chor/cthy` and `chol/qokchol/sheol` form reusable plant-part plus state
   phrases in either written order;
3. do the global positions of `odol/ols` favor measured/strained preparations
   over imperatives or generic results?

## Inputs

- GDT758's guarded context loader, 1,141-row follower occurrence atlas and
  thirteen-line reader;
- the GDT734 exact-whole dictionary solely to recover and quarantine the old
  seed-based fused-family defaults;
- the GDT755 historical expression/source bank and GDT729 quantity comparator
  deck for attested measure, part and equal-amount conventions;
- the existing 179-page allow-list, token cache and three-reader line cache,
  reached through the inherited guarded loaders and `./vmanus-exp query-tsv`.

`f84` and `f84r` are rejected by the selector gate before any other cross-row
field is materialized. No page, image or transcription is newly opened.

## Method

The source deck fixes 26 directed exact pairs: twelve quantity/value pairs,
twelve part/state pairs and two `ols`/value pairs. The builder enumerates only
adjacent tokens for which both written wholes are exact across ZL3b, IT2a and
RF1b. Each observed span inherits one concise German working rendering and two
explicit rivals; unobserved pairs receive no rendering licence.

For quantity boundaries the builder queries the permitted cross-reader lines
and locates lines in which at least one reader separates a head/value pair and
another fuses it. It then merges every target head/value pair mechanically. A
bridge is exact only when the complete three reader lines become identical
after this normalization. Lines with other differences remain local boundary
candidates, not exact bridges.

The builder also:

- compares separated-pair counts with exact fused-counterpart counts;
- dispatches all 154 exact `s` occurrences between ordered-value spans,
  observed `s om`, line-final formula use and a low-confidence fallback;
- follows all fourteen `odol/ols` positions and all three exact `ols` value
  spans;
- replaces `abgeseihtes Endprodukt` with the less overcommitted but still
  concrete `abgeseihte Zubereitung` in the one affected `ychor` line;
- writes every one of the 122 exact spans with its original line, confidence,
  evidence, alternatives and zero component-export credit.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

An exact construction is spoken only where the directed two-whole span was
observed. A fused form may receive an overlay only when it belongs to the fixed
four-form `s` value family; the cross-reader bridge is evidence for expression
identity, not for a free character value. A zero-count combination remains
unlicensed even when both component candidates sound plausible.

`Drachme` is the concrete leading model for `s + value` because medieval
measure signs productively combine with written values and the inherited
historical bank ranks the dram sign as a frequent abbreviated recipe item.
`Unze` and an *ana*-like equal-parts formula remain explicit alternatives;
nothing in EVA shape selects among them.

The experiment licenses replaceable exact-whole or exact-span renderer
defaults only. It identifies no confirmed word, unit, number system, language,
sound, component, plaintext sentence, plant species, liquid or treatment.
