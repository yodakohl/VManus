# GDT647 method

## Question

Does the GDT646 beginning/middle/end reading remain useful when propagated to
every observed whole surface in three predeclared quality-headed families,
without turning terminal `y/ey/eey` into a manuscript-wide suffix rule?

## Inputs

- Frozen GDT646 V23 allow-list, exact glossary, working dictionary and three
  line editions.
- GDT646's 24-cell quality atlas and dated 1415 humoral-subdegree comparator.
- Cached ZL3b token and ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b line projections.
- The inherited GDT631--GDT646 reader chain needed to replay V23.

No new page, image or transcription is opened. f1r is excluded; f84 and f84r
are rejected before row materialization.

## Method

1. Replay all 4,128 V23 physical lines and both frontier decks byte-for-byte.
2. Generate a fixed 120-cell whole-surface lattice:
   `(empty|o|qo)+(k|t)+(ch|sh)+S`, `(ch|sh)+S`, and
   `(k|t|ok|ot|qok|qot)+S`, where
   `S={y,ey,eey,dy,edy,eedy}`.
3. Census only complete ZL3b surfaces on the inherited 179-page allow-list.
   Record all-reader exact, split-normalized and reader-variant occurrences.
4. Give each observed whole the position reading beginning/middle/end; `d`
   marks completion. Keep `o` and `qo` bound. Do not transfer a suffix,
   substring, component or absent cell.
5. Append exact-whole overlays for every changed or newly admitted surface,
   rebuild every line, and retain five observed reader-warning surfaces as a
   separate tier rather than hiding them.
6. Manually reality-check strong and adverse passages. Repeated identical
   position phrases may be scoped once in natural German, while the tokenwise
   edition stays fully explicit.
7. Replay the builder independently in a temporary directory and compare every
   generated byte; independently recensus all 107 surfaces through guarded
   projections.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Retain the migration if the complete predeclared family contains 100 or more
observed surfaces, at least 100 have an all-reader whole-surface anchor, every
observed occurrence is audited, no absent cell is promoted, and the new line
edition adds concrete coverage without changing any out-of-scope exact gloss.

The result is an exploratory exact-whole working dictionary. It does not prove
that `y`, `e`, `ee`, `d`, `k`, `t`, `ch`, `sh`, `o` or `qo` is an independent
word, morpheme, sound or plaintext symbol. Naked CTH forms, p/s/r/l material
forms, reversed compounds, EEE forms, `aN`, `oiin`, `air`, `al`, `ol` and `or`
remain outside the migration.
