# GDT357 external source audit

## Critical edition

The archived National Library scan supplies the full 1956 Abrahamyan edition:
382 PDF surfaces, including the study, figures, edited texts, tables, and
indexes. A direct visual sweep found several calendar, numerical, and circular
figures in the introductory study. The most relevant circular plates are:

- PDF surface 76 / printed p. 72: an open annular scheme with a central circle,
  radial divisions, and ten outer circular cells;
- PDF surface 96 / printed p. 90: a manuscript reproduction with interlaced
  circular geometry.

Neither is the Penn 209r–210r eight-curved-band rendering. The visually
inspected PDF surfaces 169–200 (printed pp. 159–190) contain linear printed
text/tables and no reproduction of that spiral subseries. This is an edition
content observation, not evidence that the medieval work never used such a
diagram.

No OCR or automated text recognition was performed. The PDF's pre-existing
machine text was not used to create visual or semantic claims.

## Public witness availability

The 2025 v3 index contains 2,579 openly indexed digitized manuscripts. It
contains LJS 443 as a post-1416 calendrical collection. It contains no record
whose Matenadaran shelfmark is MM 1973 or MM 1999, the two witnesses explicitly
named in the audited Sarkawag scholarship. This blocks a public facsimile
cross-check through that index; it does not assert that the manuscripts are
undigitized in every institutional system.

The earlier 2022 v2 index (1,363 rows) gives the same negative result for those
two shelfmarks. A 1427–1429 Istanbul miscellany (APIA 00248) is publicly
catalogued with astronomical/calendar texts, but its metadata neither assigns
the Sarkawag commentary nor supplies a parallel folio key; access also requires
an HMML login. It is therefore a generic comparator lead, not a parallel
witness.

## Capacity conclusion

The critical edition is now recovered and auditable, removing one acquisition
uncertainty. It does not remove the scientific blocker: no source in this pass
binds LJS 443 209r–210r to eight values, a start, direction, order, or an
independent keyed witness.
