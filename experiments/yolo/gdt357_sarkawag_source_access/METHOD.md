# GDT357 method: Sarkawag source access and edition-figure audit

## Question

Does a newly recovered copy of A. G. Abrahamyan's 1956 critical edition, or a
complete public-digitization index, provide the exact LJS 443 folio concordance,
slot values, start, direction, or order required by the GDT356 stop?

## Frozen scope

This pass uses only external Armenian-manuscript scholarship and metadata. It
inherits the three external surfaces and work attribution from GDT355–GDT356,
but does not access a Voynich image, transcription, or formal row. All f84
material remains forbidden.

The recovered book is the National Library of Armenia scan of A. G.
Abrahamyan, *Hovhannes Imastaseri matenagrut'yuně* (Yerevan, 1956), retrieved
from a Wayback capture of the library URL. Its PDF has 382 surfaces and SHA-256
`b099488d34a6107447f90543d4255a6602cd1cdf4dfe7cdb2a6273132b00d302`.
The repository does not redistribute the 38 MB scan.

The public-witness control is version 3.0 of the *Index of Digitized Armenian
Manuscripts* (2025), 2,579 rows, SHA-256
`0c9bd071290efee1da5820e31eea1b0375e29b4da7ebcce70cd78bd6161f189a`.

## Inspection protocol

1. Inspect a direct thumbnail census of all 382 edition surfaces.
2. Inspect the selected diagram/facsimile surfaces at full page scale.
3. Inspect PDF surfaces 169–200, corresponding to printed pp. 159–190 cited
   for the Kharnakhoran material.
4. Record visible geometry only. Do not run OCR or automated text recognition.
5. Query the public index mechanically for LJS 443 and the two specifically
   named Matenadaran witnesses MM 1973 and MM 1999. Absence is an index-coverage
   fact, not proof that a manuscript has never been digitized or photographed.
6. A transfer key is eligible only if a source binds the selected Penn folios
   to explicit slot values plus authorial start/order or another invariant
   correspondence. Work-level calendar or lunar content is insufficient.

## Decision rule

If neither the edition nor a public parallel witness supplies that exact key,
retain the external topology as a period comparator and close acquisition from
these public sources. Do not expose or score the Voynich target.
