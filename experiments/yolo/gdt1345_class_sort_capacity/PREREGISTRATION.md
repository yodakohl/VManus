for ZL3b, IT2a, RF1b. No raw TSV/image/new admission. f84 and f84r remain forbidden;

on at least two physical leaves (strip r/v and foldout suffix from page number).

or replacement of the full result. No alleged independent holdout.

For every distinct pair u,v record whether some whole word places u before v,
not necessarily adjacent. Both orders imply different classes. The resulting
undirected graph has an edge exactly when both orders occur. Retain each directed
edge's occurrence count, distinct types, physical leaves and earliest witness

branch search. Its size is a certified lower bound, even if other directed cycles
would require more classes. A deterministic greedy partition into acyclic induced

ordered and replayed on every retained whole group. Report an exact minimum ONLY
when lower and upper agree. Otherwise preserve the interval; no solver escalation.

checks full certificates, enumerates maximal cliques by a different algorithm,

three-cycle (no pair conflicts but one class impossible), opposite pairs and
repeated symbols. No translation or historical use follows from capacity.
