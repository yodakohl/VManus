## Decision

Retain LOC-OWNER only as an explicitly costed possible derivation. **Park
further automatic QOD extension:** no non-development goal comparison is
available under the fixed grammar. A future whole W account may hypothesize
the missing roles, but it must reuse a constrained written construction;
another unrestricted paragraph dictionary would not test this operator.

The separate yp review is a retained predecessor: Boolean non-adherence does
not derive a sustained departure event, while mandatory detachment conflicts
with the fixed counterworld having departure and no prior adhesion in the same
attempt. That review is not rerun as a new experiment here. General typed
composition remains possible; none of its new component meanings is confirmed.

There is no significance claim or whole-search null. GDT388 scoring is not
requested: these are conditional model bindings on already exposed text,
not new visually owned relation evidence. No image, raw mixed corpus, external
contact, reserve or additional manuscript surface was accessed. ZL/IT/RF are
alternative readings of one manuscript. Confirmed translated words remain0.
