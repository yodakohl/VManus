# GDT801 preregistration

## Frozen question

Does the GDT800 terminal-`m` enrichment remain on GDT791 events that are
strictly internal to the annotated paragraph/statement/record/panel hierarchy,
and can any higher-level closure add information beyond physical line edge?

## Frozen primary population and gate

1. Join only by source selector, locus, token ordinal and exact surface.
2. Keep `RUNNING_EVENT` for the primary analysis; local labels are separate.
3. Define strict interior before testing: neither paragraph-start nor
   paragraph-end line and no statement, record or panel endpoint.
4. Condition on the complete nonempty preceding surface. Require physical-edge
   OR above 3 and exact `p<.01` for stem, stem×hand, stem×topology and
   stem×physical-page.
5. A paragraph, statement, record or panel increment is scoreable only with
   both `l` and `m` at target endpoints, at least one target endpoint internal
   to a physical line, and support on at least two physical pages.

## Frozen diagnostics

- Remove each higher closure from the joined running population and re-estimate
  the physical-edge effect.
- Control the full GDT800 population by exact line length and by
  source-selector×line length.
- On deep pages, compare normalized record/panel line progress by exhaustive
  within-page terminal permutation.
- Project local labels only from the frozen 155-stem inventory; require a
  complete `l/m × local/running` crossing before a register interpretation.

## Claim ceiling

No terminal sign or EVA character receives a word, sound or semantic value.
The strongest possible result is a physical-line-edge surface association plus
an explicit higher-scope capacity verdict.
