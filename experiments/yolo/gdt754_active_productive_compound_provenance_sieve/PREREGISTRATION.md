# GDT754 execution contract

The target universe is fixed mechanically: every currently active V99R7
exact-whole card sourced to a GDT664/GDT666 row whose card type is exactly
`PRODUCTIVE_COMPOUND`. All of its allowed cached occurrences are retained; no
attractive form, page or passage is sampled.

The source composition, source prose and source rival are preserved before
the spoken renderer is changed. Later support is admitted only by exact
complete-form equality from the named evidence artifacts. GDT738 may act at a
specific manuscript coordinate; no occurrence-local result is promoted to all
occurrences. GDT753 may replace only its two exact whole forms. Absence from a
later experiment is recorded as absence, not as negative evidence.

The default fallback is a compact role-axis hypothesis derived from the old
composition, with literal identity left open. Thus this pass removes circular
literal prose without requiring exploratory assumptions to be proven before
they remain in play. Component-export credit is zero for every output row.

The bridge deck contains exactly the 24 highest-priority complete forms under
the deterministic ranking in `src/run.py`. It compares historical register
families only. It cannot interpret EVA initials or substrings and cannot
declare a historical match.

This contract freezes the deterministic final pass and validation of the
route named by GDT753. It is not a claim that the older GDT664/GDT666
compositions were blinded when originally proposed.
