# GDT754 method

## Question

How much currently spoken V99R7 prose is inherited from GDT664/GDT666
`PRODUCTIVE_COMPOUND` cards rather than independently learned complete-word
evidence, which later experiments actually constrain those forms, and what
whole-form defaults can remain without losing the exploratory hypotheses?

## Inputs

- the active V99R7 dictionary and guarded compact-cell cache;
- the complete GDT664 and GDT666 decision decks;
- GDT737 negative head-noun quarantine decisions;
- GDT738 occurrence-level whole-role adjudication and renderer patch;
- the GDT745, GDT746, GDT748, GDT749 and GDT750 later whole-role censuses;
- the GDT753 `qokeol/okeol` provenance correction;
- the inherited safe-page and transcription guard chain.

## Method

Select every active exact-whole dictionary card whose source decision is
GDT664 or GDT666 and whose source type is exactly `PRODUCTIVE_COMPOUND`. Join
those forms to every allowed cached cell and count their complete current
footprint before changing any renderer value.

For every selected form, retain verbatim source prose, named source
composition, role axes, rival and source strength in a background inventory.
Then join only exact complete-form rows from GDT737, GDT738, GDT745, GDT746,
GDT748, GDT749, GDT750 and GDT753. No character or substring match counts.

Assign the spoken whole-form layer by this precedence:

1. preserve an exact occurrence-level GDT738 patch at the same coordinate;
2. preserve the GDT753 shared `qokeol/okeol` heat/middle hypothesis;
3. retain a later positive complete-form role where one exists;
4. otherwise compress the old component composition to named role axes and
   mark it explicitly as a whole-form working hypothesis.

Patient and carrier nouns constructed from old analyst components are never
spoken as recovered lexical identities. Every target still receives a
nonempty default, while the complete old claim remains available in the
background columns.

Rank a 24-form bridge deck by current cell footprint, later evidence and
source strength. Attach only a broad historical comparator register: measure
or degree, recipe action, state or quality, or learned drug/preparation
headword. This deck proposes comparisons; it does not claim a historical word
match.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A source `PRODUCTIVE_COMPOUND` is not independent evidence for its own
components. It may survive as an exploratory whole-form role bundle until a
better idea replaces it or makes it impossible, but it cannot confirm a
lexeme, operation, ingredient, preparation or patient. Later evidence changes
the spoken layer only at its demonstrated whole or occurrence scope.

GDT754 may inventory, demote and rerender these 172 complete forms and name 24
historical comparison targets. It assigns no value to an EVA character,
prefix, morpheme, sound, abbreviation or substring and identifies no confirmed
lexeme, plaintext clause, literal operation, patient, ingredient, plant,
disease, cure, person, vessel, unit, historical word match, unseen form, new
page, image, transcription, f84 or f84r.
