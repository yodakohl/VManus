# Sidequest V50–V59: ten bounded improvement passes

Status: creative ten-page workshop exercise, not a scientific decipherment.

## Frozen scope

- Herbal: `f10r`, `f11r`, `f55v`, `f56r`.
- Biological: `f81v`, `f82r`, `f83r`.
- Circle/Astro: `f67r2`, `f68r1`, `f69v`.
- `f84` and `f84r` remain sealed.
- V49 is the inherited baseline. Exact card identity, formal formula, atomic
  working gloss, and local creative expansion remain separate columns.
- A physical line is not assumed to be a sentence.
- No visible fragment is assigned a sound, language, or etymology.

## Four fixed perspectives

Each pass uses the unchanged R1–R4 backgrounds in
`SIDEQUEST_FOUR_AGENT_BACKGROUNDS.md`. The three delegated roles may not read
one another's pass output; R4 synthesizes only after all three freeze their
answers.

## Improvement rule

A revision beats its predecessor only if it does at least one of the following
without losing complete ten-page coverage:

1. uses a shorter, more learnable invariant default;
2. removes a contradiction across occurrences;
3. explains a previously awkward complete field or record;
4. reduces silent context inserted into a card value;
5. separates formal function from local content more cleanly;
6. makes the handwriting system easier for several circa-1420 scribes to use;
7. makes a historically ordinary source expansion more coherent.

Merely sounding more fluent is not an improvement. A rejected proposal remains
recorded, and `UNBEKANNT` is preferable to a phrase-sized fake word. Complete
local default translations may remain speculative even when their components
are unknown.

## Compact publication rule

V50–V58 publish only four role reports, one selected delta, one compact
validation, and any small table essential to the decision. V59 alone republishes
the complete dictionary/interlinear/field edition. This avoids ten redundant
copies of hundreds of rows while preserving every material revision.
