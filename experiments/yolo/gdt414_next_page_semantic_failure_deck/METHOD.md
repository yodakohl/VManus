# GDT414 method

## Question

Can the complete GDT413 working dictionary be converted into a compact error
deck that distinguishes a harmless new owner-local expansion from a genuine
portable-meaning failure?

## Inputs

GDT413's 4,576-event semantic edition and forty-six-component dictionary,
GDT408's complete leave-one-page event replay, and GDT412's nineteen-core
dictionary. No additional page, image, or transcription is admitted.

## Method

Expand every occurrence of the nineteen roots into an 8,505-row pressure
ledger joined to its leave-one-page support class. Build a complete 19×5
register matrix of counts, pages, and companion atoms. For each root, select
the least-supported existing event as the comparison boundary. State one
broad default, allowed owner-local expansions, a common overreading to avoid,
and explicit green, amber, and red next-page conditions.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The deck may broaden a concrete local paraphrase while leaving the root
unchanged. It must flag red when a root changes structural category, needs a
second portable meaning that owner/hull cannot carry, lexicalizes a local
label, adds an unseen atom, or repairs meaning by crossing scope. It is a
creative consistency protocol, not proof that the German defaults are true.
