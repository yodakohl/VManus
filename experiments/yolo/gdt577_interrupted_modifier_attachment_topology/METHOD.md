# GDT577 method

## Question

Can every same-root relation/modifier repetition that is interrupted by other
atoms be assigned to a small local head topology, so the next German voice can
preserve written order without flattening the structure to “zweimal”?

## Inputs

GDT576 supplies the complete 5,122-event current edition. GDT575 supplies all
96 duplicate groups and the disjoint seventeen outer/inner scope pairs.
GDT407 and GDT515 supply the fixed focus attachments for the old 26 and new
four pages. GDT416 is consulted only for the one discovered renderer-history
conflict at `G407-E1755`.

No new page, image, transcription, event, recipe, atom or meaning is admitted.
Sealed f84/f84r remain untouched.

## Method

The exact GDT575 selector `SAME_ROOT_INTERRUPTED` yields 62 groups in 59
events and 125 written slots.

For E, EE and AR, each slot must replay its existing GDT407/GDT515 focus
attachment exactly. These 75 assignments are not re-estimated.

O and D_ADDR were not focus families in those sources. Their 50 candidate
heads use one compact editorial rule:

1. OT, OL and DY divide a recipe into local carrier intervals.
2. Inside the interval, choose the nearest visible action; a distance tie goes
   left.
3. If the interval begins after a written control and has no action, keep that
   control as the sequence head.
4. Otherwise use the last active action on the same page and owner.
5. A head identity is event plus atom position, never merely an action root.

The 125 assignments are then classified into five topologies: different
action occurrences, a bracket around one head, two slots on the same side of
one head, one carried context head, or action plus sequence head. Every slot
retains its atom position, root, scope, head, placement and provenance.

## Renderer conflict and decision

GDT416 automatically called any repeated relation “outer” then “inner”.
Consequently its `G407-E1755` clause scoped the two AR slots. GDT565 later
generated the same written recipe as a plain ordered sequence, and both fixed
AR focus rows are `SINGLE/SINGLE`, not repeat/scope rows.

The atlas therefore keeps the common inherited OK attachment but quarantines
the whole event from the next voice rewrite. It neither silently drops the old
scope possibility nor promotes it to an eighteenth scope pair.

## Claim ceiling

The 75 fixed bindings are inherited structure. The 50 O/D_ADDR assignments are
explicitly exploratory editorial candidates for readable sequencing. Neither
set confirms syntax, plaintext, word meanings, language, chronology, genre or
object identity. No slot may be deleted or compressed to a count.
