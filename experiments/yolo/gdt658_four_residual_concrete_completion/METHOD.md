# GDT658 method

## Question

Can the four residual whole surfaces exposed by GDT657 receive concrete,
compositionally predictive meanings from their already visible families, so
that every affected V34 line has a complete default reading?

## Inputs

- GDT635 and GDT636 for the local materia-head architecture and its historical
  comparators;
- GDT640 for the bound `CHO/CHEO` preparation heads;
- GDT657 for the frozen V34 dictionary, glossary, complete passages, one-hole
  frontier, page allow-list and ordered multi-quality model;
- the guarded ZL3b token and three-reader line transcriptions.

The inherited 179-page allow-list is unchanged. `f1r` is excluded, `f84` and
`f84r` are forbidden, and no image or additional manuscript page is opened.

## Method

Only four exact surfaces may enter V35:

1. `otam` is read through the complete K/T × AM neighbourhood (`kam/tam`,
   `okam/otam`, `qokam/qotam`, with `cham/sham` as additional heads), its 44
   positions and its strong line-final preference.
2. `shedefam` is read through the visible `F + body` grids and the inherited
   `SHE` moisture, `D` state and `AM` measure architecture. The single reader
   warning remains attached to the whole card. Period materia-medica
   abbreviations choose among flower, leaf, fruit and an opaque structural
   F-head; they do not turn bare `f` into a global word.
3. `schos` is read through the exact `s | cho | s` reader boundary elsewhere
   on f93r, the `rchos` material sister and the productive initial materia
   heads. Initial and final `s` are separate slots; neither is copied globally.
4. `chokcheo` is read as an ordered nesting and compared with the observed
   `kcheo/tcheo`, `qokcheo/qotcheo` and longer CHEO family. Its outer `CHO`
   preparation head may not be flattened into the inner `K + CHEO` body.

For every target the builder records the short German default, visible
composition, rival reading, every occurrence, reader agreement, line
position, local context and the family cells that motivate the choice. It then
replays the four cards sequentially into the complete V34 glossary, dictionary
and line-coverage tables. Complete and one-hole passages are recomputed from
the full allowed corpus rather than patched by hand.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

All four surfaces receive a default. A reader-stable family composition is the
primary reading; where the family does not uniquely name the content head, the
most practical period-compatible meaning is used and its rival is retained on
the same card. No surface is left as “work item”, “material”, “operation” or
another generic placeholder.

The result is an exploratory exact-whole V35 codebook. It may predict meanings
inside the explicitly audited families, but it does not establish free
characters, substring inheritance, phonetics, a language, plaintext or an
exact botanical ingredient. Absent family cells are controls, not dictionary
entries.
