# GDT658 — four residual concrete completion

GDT657 made exactly four previously unknown surfaces the last hole in an
otherwise readable V34 line: `otam`, `shedefam`, `schos` and `chokcheo`.
GDT658 gives every one of them a short concrete whole-surface default, shows
the complete family that motivates it and rebuilds the working dictionary and
all line readings as V35.

The intended result is a usable working translation, not a claim that the
four forms are ordinary-language words. Component labels remain local to the
displayed families; reader disagreements and the strongest alternative
meaning stay visible.

See `REPORT.md` for the readings, `METHOD.md` for their construction and
`artifacts/README.md` for the reproducible outputs.
