## Decision

fraction of the text. A one-compound interpretation can have a small and
structured residual at substantial use. The recurring residue deserves an
account; declaring it noise would manufacture a fit. No compound is promoted
to a phonetic glyph or meaning, no full sourcewriter is claimed, and no automatic
multicompound/error-correcting extension follows. Existing minim-family primary
