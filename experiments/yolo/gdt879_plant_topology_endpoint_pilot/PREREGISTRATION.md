## Finite observation rubric

Work with the above-basal, stem-like connected structure. Record whether a
single basal hub/main connector can be identified independently of any text.
If no clear separation from the lower root-like structure exists, retain the
ambiguity instead of choosing a convenient root. Root-like lower components
are excluded only when that boundary is clear.

A terminal unit is a separately outlined blade/head-like body reached by a
distinct narrow stem or stalk. Lobes/teeth/paint spots inside one continuous
outlined body are not separate terminals. Overlapping/compound attachments
must be marked uncertain. This is a graphic rubric, not a taxonomic leaf count.
A branching junction is a continuous stem-like attachment from one incoming
path to two or more outgoing stalk paths; crossings without visible junction
are not joins. Ignore stroke width, angle, color, species and detailed margins.

For each page record: basal hub clear/uncertain; terminal count or bounded
count interval; an approximate locator for every counted terminal; visible
junction relations as parent→children where distinguishable; at least one
explicit example of a fork, non-fork or unresolved crossing; and whether the
hierarchy can be represented without observer-specific segmentation choices.
Do not count duplicated coordinates as another terminal. Preserve all three
pages, including missing/ambiguous graph observations.

## Pilot decision and ceilings

Proceed to design review only if at least two pages yield both exact agreement
on the counted terminal units and compatible rooted attachment relations in
the two independent packets, and the agreed graphs differ in a topology fact
beyond global size/position. This is a feasibility threshold, not statistical
evidence, a translation or a confirmation of text-picture correspondence.
If units or attachment choices remain unstable, stop this endpoint definition;
do not repair it by changing the ontology, choosing a favorable observer or
counting leaf teeth. Any later revision needs a genuinely different source
question and explicit decision note, not an automatic extraction project.
