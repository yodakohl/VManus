# GDT679 method

## Question

Can all 24 residual surfaces in V52's eight three-gap lines receive concrete
component-based or learned-whole meanings which preserve one practical core at
all 57 exact admitted occurrences, close the eight lines and improve their
usable prose without generic filler or hidden reader repairs?

## Inputs

- GDT671's f84-free 4,128-line V48 panel and literal token coverage;
- the published GDT673, GDT674, GDT675, GDT677 and GDT678 position overlays;
- GDT678's complete 51-line V52 reader and frozen result counts;
- 24 manual card specifications, eight source-line readings, thirteen reader
  decisions, seven historical architecture records and the three-reader
  synthesis;
- the mixed transcription table, accessed only through `vmanus-exp query-tsv`
  with 41 explicit locus allow-values and `--forbid-prefix f84`.

## Method

1. Rebuild the exact current overlay rather than reading a later prose
   translation as data. The five published passes contribute 358 disjoint V48
   positions, leaving 7,822 open positions before GDT679.
2. Locate exact whitespace-delimited occurrences of the 24 surfaces in the
   admitted panel. This yields 57 positions on 41 lines and 35 pages.
3. Materialize only those 41 loci from the mixed reader source. The guarded
   command selects 41 rows, rejects 98 `f84*` rows before materialization and
   skips 5,247 other rows.
4. Align ZL3b to IT2a and RF1b with exact tokens preferred over low-cost
   two-/three-token joins and splits. Every non-both-exact target receives an
   explicit decision; no variant is silently normalized.
5. Apply one card core to every exact ZL3b occurrence. Reader-conditioned
   renderings may clarify the local surface, but may not change the card into
   an unrelated material or operation.
6. Replace exactly three open positions in each source line, record new action
   ordinals, and retain token-parallel as well as natural practical readings.
7. Recalculate the complete 51-line deck and the global V48 overlay. Do not
   assume that only selected lines close.
8. Generate eight forward family predictions and a sourced historical atlas.
   Historical parallels constrain the mixed recipe/degree/measure architecture
   only; they do not assign sounds or Latin words to Voynich signs.
9. Independently rebuild ten result files in a temporary directory and compare
   every byte. The validator also checks counts, reader variants, token
   alignment, prose specificity, hashes, privacy markers and the f84/f84r seal.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The pass requires 24 unique cards, all 57 exact positions represented, no
unrecorded non-both-reader position, all eight source lines closed, zero hard
generic renderer terms, the predicted V53 and global counts, and a byte-exact
rebuild.

Cards are exploratory defaults, not confirmed plaintext. A card may remain
despite a named rival until a context makes it impossible or a better reading
explains the same complete occurrence circuit. GDT679 does not identify the
language, phonetics, historical codebook, exact plant, disease, patient, cure
or carrier liquid.
