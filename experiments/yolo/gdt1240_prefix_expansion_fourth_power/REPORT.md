## The hand-checkable obligation

Let C be a fixed prefix-free set of written codewords. If whole u and ur
belong to C*, left cancellation forces r to belong to C*. This fact concerns
the written parse, independently of what each code means or expands to.
Existing1236certificates supply short repeated-form proofs:

- IT/ZL: whole she and shee force e; whole she and shek force k.
- RF: whole sh and she force e; whole she and shek force k.
- Whole s directly forces its singleton code in every reading.

The rare witness is a material limitation even though the code-forcing
forms recur. It must not be silently declared an error to rescue a table.

Because k,e,s are themselves codewords, a longer prefix-code entry cannot
start with any of them. Thus keeees must parse k|e|e|e|e|s. Assign each
code its fixed nonempty literal expansion: X, U and Y respectively. Its
whole source word is X U U U U Y. U may be several letters, nonprimitive,
and part of a longer repeated stretch. The test covers every such U; it
is not merely a search for four identical source letters. Source-word
reversal preserves the condition and supplies no additional experiment.

Distinct codes are allowed to have identical expansions, and the writer
may choose among unlimited alternative spellings. Hence the old1202bound
on two complete-word aliases does not already settle this larger class.
The source envelope is deliberately generous: each native occurrence may
choose any word from the entire fixed source population, with unlimited
reuse. Even that relaxation lacks a source for the retained witness.
