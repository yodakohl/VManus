## Validation, decisions and ceiling

forbidden seam inside an atom. These test implementation, not the scientific
validity of the model. A separate validator reads the frozen inputs directly,
checks complete clause/source coverage, independently compiles tree orders,
rebuilds all case coverage and necessary certificates, and replays every SAT
and alternative witness. Solver UNSAT is labelled as a solver result unless
independently proved; no general proof or accuracy claim follows from a PASS.

Complete witnessed fits would be conditional whole-paragraph candidates, with
all assumptions and ambiguity retained. Contradiction parks only this conjunction;
source/computation unknowns do not count as contradictions or compatibility.
