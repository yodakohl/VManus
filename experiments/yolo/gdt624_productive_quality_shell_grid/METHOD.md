# GDT624 method

## Question

Does the GDT623 quality core extend beyond `qo-` into one productive family of
complete words, and can the extra wrapper, `e`, `d`, and final `y` slots receive
short defaults that improve real Herbal readings?

## Inputs

- the f84-free GDT327 page inventory;
- guarded ZL3b token rows and guarded ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b line readings;
- the GDT623 report, result, and working dictionary;
- five manually inspected historical binding comparators.

The frequency panel has 179 pages. `f1r` is removed before either query;
`f84` and `f84r` are rejected before payload materialization. The already
opened f31v image is an explicit manual extra for one local root contact and
does not enter any frequency count.

## Method

The builder generates exactly

```text
(bare|o|qo) + (k|t) + (ch|sh) + e? + d? + y
```

and counts exact whole-token occurrences. This is a 3×2×2×2×2 lattice of 48
cells. For each line and surface, the three-reading stability count is the
minimum exact token multiplicity across ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b. The readings are
alternate observations of one manuscript, not three replications.

Six sets of one-bit edges are enumerated: `k↔t`, `ch↔sh`, no-`e`↔`e`,
no-`d`↔`d`, bare↔`o`, and `o`↔`qo`. An edge receives a local witness only when
both complete surfaces occur on the same physical line. Wrapper triplets
require bare, `o`, and `qo` versions of the same core on one page.

The `e`-length census independently generates five lengths for six stems and
two tails: `{ch,sh,k,t,ok,ot}+e{0..4}+{dy,ody}`. The empty fourth extension is
retained as an explicit boundary. The historical and local position evidence
then ranks meanings:

1. `e`: forward-bound or attributive quality form;
2. `e/ee`: degree or strength as the live rival;
3. `d`: grammatical DY binding or state closure;
4. operation/preparation: rejected as the default of an isolated grid word.

The route screen returned GDT535, GDT528, GDT510, GDT599, GDT622, and GDT623.
GDT535's statement-specific initial-q null and GDT528's single licensed
terminal-d variant are retained as local precedents, not global meanings.
GDT599's noncanonical workshop prose is not imported. GDT624's new endpoint is
the exact complete 48-cell family plus direct quality-to-part contacts.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Every generated surface receives a short working default when it occurs and
has a three-reading-stable witness. Atom values are inherited without changing
the GDT623 orientation. Unknown tokens in rendered lines remain in angle
brackets; they are never replaced with generic instructions.

The result is a complete exploratory reader for this registered family. It
does not automatically decompose arbitrary words outside the family, make `d`
a verb, assign a numbered degree to `e`, identify a language or sound system,
or constitute a full manuscript translation.
