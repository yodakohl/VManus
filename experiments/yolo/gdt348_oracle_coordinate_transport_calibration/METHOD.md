# GDT348 method — oracle-coordinate transport calibration

## Question

Did GDT347 fail to transport because the common surface parser exposed no
inner-D or DY changes in the controls, or because the frozen Voynich pair
potentials are genuinely incompatible with known lexical and distributed
systems?

This is an instrument-ceiling test. It does not alter the Voynich graph, score
new Voynich data, factor PAGE_HOST, merge tuples, or assign semantics.

## Frozen controls

Use only the byte-frozen GDT172 lexical A and factorial B oracle/observation
layers and the byte-frozen GDT173 human-grown B2 oracle/observation layer.
Their 21 medieval-source units are split once by SHA-256. Five complete source
units are held out identically in all three systems. All copies of a source
unit across registers remain in the same side of the split.

## Oracle crosswalk

Map authored fields to the six GDT345 coordinates without reading any coupling
score:

- `true_line_frame`: empty→`NONE`, nonempty→`O`;
- `true_lexical_left == d`: `inner_d=1`, otherwise `0`;
- empty `true_lexical_right`: `right_family=NONE`; nonempty right categories
  are ranked by frequency in the 16 training units and mapped to the non-NONE
  GDT347 training right-family states ranked by frequency. If more than five
  control categories exist, the remaining rare categories collapse to the
  fifth state;
- `true_closure == y`: `dy_closure=1`;
- `true_closure == k`: `b3=1`;
- `true_record_operator` maps literally for empty/`q`/`d`/`s` to
  `NONE`/`q`/`d`/`s` as `canonical_wrapper`.

The authored field marker, positional right marker, literal escape, B2 lexical
closure, host, source form, and lexical ID are not mapped. This deliberate
partial crosswalk is reported as a limitation. No mapping is selected by a
graph score, and no category permutation is searched.

## Transitions and score

Within each authored record, order rows by `true_record_slot`. Adjacent rows
form transitions. A physical-line change maps to the frozen GDT345
`LINE_RESET/SAME_FIELD/FIRST` layout; other transitions map to
`SAME_FIELD/SAME_FIELD/MIDDLE`, with the final within-line transition marked
`LAST`. Record-crossing transitions are excluded.

Learn only the independent source-conditioned coordinate marginals from the
16 training units, using the unchanged GDT346 smoothing constants. Apply the
three exact GDT347 pair-potential tables and weights unchanged to the five held
units. The graph topology and its one-time 8.829722735-bit cost are inherited;
there is no new topology or alignment selector.

Report total and source-unit-balanced held gain, exact next-state recovery,
edgewise gain, support coverage, and a 4,096-world coupling-destruction null
that shuffles each target delta coordinate within held source unit × exact
layout × source-coordinate strata. Alternate renderings/register copies are
not independent replications.
