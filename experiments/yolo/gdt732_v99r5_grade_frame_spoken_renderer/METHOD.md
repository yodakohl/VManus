# GDT732 method

## Question

Can the 175 audible V99R4 grade-frame readings be spoken as immediately
readable German states without changing their underlying working meanings,
modality, formal stage, workflow closure, confidence, evidence or dispatch
scope?

## Inputs

- GDT558's carrier-envelope constraint and GDT647/GDT717's established
  modality, stage and closure distinctions;
- GDT671's f84/f84r-free 179-page cache: 4,128 lines and 32,339 aligned token
  cells;
- GDT696's three local action/patient/relation artifacts as byte-parity
  controls;
- GDT727's 479 exact active contexts and five active-reader artifacts;
- GDT730's canonical 1,586-row V99R4 dictionary, including confidence and
  positive/counterevidence;
- GDT731's practical-blocker rules and occurrence-impact report;
- `src/GRADE_SPOKEN_RENDER_POLICY.tsv`, a ten-rule frozen rendering deck.

No page, image, OCR, raw transcription, f84 or f84r source is opened.

## Method

1. Select the 175 complete-dictionary readings whose German working meaning
   contains an explicit grade frame. Require their canonical occurrence sum
   to be 2,431: 162 global readings/2,401 positions plus thirteen active
   readings/thirty exact positions.
2. Preserve every original V99R4 column byte-for-byte. Append only a spoken
   rendering, formal stage sequence, workflow closure, modality class,
   renderer mode, dispatch scope and policy-rule IDs.
3. For one explicit modality, render grade beginning/middle/end as
   `leicht`, `bis zur Mittelstufe` or `vollständig` plus the already stated
   condition: `erhitzt`, `abgekühlt`, `getrocknet` or `angefeuchtet`.
   `angefeuchtet` is never strengthened to `eingeweicht`.
4. Do not choose one process axis for a mixed state. Keep both qualities and
   speak only `Anfangsstufe`, `Mittelstufe` or `Endstufe`. Keep subsequent
   workflow closure separate as `… erreicht; abgeschlossen` or `… erreicht;
   fertig`.
5. Apply four exact clause-local rules to the only readings containing two
   explicit stage clauses, preserving their order and punctuation. No stage,
   verb, patient or substance may be imported from a neighbouring cell.
6. Project global readings by exact whole surface. Project active readings
   only at the exact `(page, locus, token ordinal, surface)` positions in
   GDT727. Retain all 1,784 occurrences of those thirteen active surfaces
   outside the thirty licensed positions as unchanged leak controls.
7. Publish aligned before/after cells for all 2,431 targets and all 1,661
   affected lines. Every one of the other 29,908 cache positions remains
   unchanged.
8. Recount grade-bearing cells over the entire target-only V48-baseline
   projection, not merely the affected lines. Classify the residual 4,752 as
   1,784 target active surfaces outside their thirty positions, 2,908 other
   active surfaces outside their exact scopes, 52 inherited V48 cells already
   superseded at exact V99 positions, and eight legacy contextual alias/merge
   cells. Retain the current V99 context value on those 52 rows.
9. In the fifty-passage reader, list each residual grade cell separately with
   ordinal and lineage class so the fluent-looking `Nachher` line cannot hide
   an out-of-scope remainder.
10. Hash, but do not rewrite, all three GDT696 and five GDT727 inherited
    artifacts. Independently reconstruct all dictionary, occurrence, scope,
    modality, closure, residual and parity invariants.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all 175 readings and 2,431 licensed positions render with zero
remaining target grade markers; modality and closure counts stay exact; all
thirty active positions are reconstructed; all 1,784 same-surface controls
and 29,908 non-target cells are unchanged; and every original V99R4 field and
all eight inherited artifacts remain byte-stable.

This is a scope-safe spoken renderer over an exploratory working dictionary.
`Vollständig` is a hypothesis for the end of a degree scale, not proof that a
workflow is complete; formal `OPEN/CLOSED/FINISHED` remains separate. The pass
does not establish plaintext, a historical lexicon, ingredient or disease
identity, a new action, productive component meanings or a new page.
