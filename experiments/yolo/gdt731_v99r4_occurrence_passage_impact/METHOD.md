# GDT731 method

## Question

What do the 94 V99R4 single-default decisions actually change when projected
onto every already cached exact occurrence, and which audible abstractions
still prevent the resulting passages from reading as practical prose?

## Inputs

- GDT671's 179-page allowlist and complete cached V48 line table: 4,128 lines
  and 32,339 aligned ZL3b tokens, with no f84 or f84r;
- GDT729's V99R3 complete dictionary for the before values;
- GDT730's explicit 94-row decision deck and V99R4 complete dictionary for the
  after values, confidence and positive/negative evidence;
- three GDT696 relation-overlay artifacts as byte-parity controls;
- `src/PRACTICAL_BLOCKER_RULES.tsv`, an explicit five-rule readability deck.

No page, image, OCR, raw transcription, f84 or f84r source is opened.

## Method

1. Preserve all 4,128 cached lines in stored order and require exact alignment
   between each whitespace token and its ` | `-separated inherited V48 gloss.
2. Exact-match only the 94 GDT730 whole surfaces. At each matching ordinal,
   render the V99R3 global default in the before channel and the V99R4 default
   in the after channel. Never decompose or substring-dispatch a form.
3. Keep every non-target token and inherited gloss cell unchanged. Join cells
   with semicolons and apply whitespace/punctuation cleanup only.
4. Bind each changed occurrence to its reading ID, family, score, confidence
   level, positive evidence, counterevidence, scope and retained rival.
5. Group the 1,039 occurrences into line comparisons; separately retain the
   351 affected V48-complete lines and rank the fifty most target-dense
   passages by target count, completeness and locus. This ordering does not
   measure semantic importance.
6. Count slash, standalone `oder` and case-insensitive `menge` only inside the
   changed cells before and after. These are renderer-choice markers, not an
   accuracy score.
7. Apply the explicit blocker deck to the complete V99R4 dictionary and the
   affected passage cells. Keep legitimate `Ansatz`, `Zubereitung`, `Portion`,
   `Fraktion`, `Rohstoff` and named drug families out of blanket genericity.
8. Hash the GDT696 local-relation layer without rewriting its clauses; local
   actions and patients must remain separate from this global-default impact
   projection.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The pass must recover all 94 surfaces and exactly 1,039 positions in 911 lines,
with all 31,300 non-target positions unchanged. It reports concision and
single-default gains plus surviving blockers honestly; it does not equate
shorter defaults with practical informativeness or call a semicolon-joined
technical inventory polished translation. No plaintext, language, phonetics,
ingredient, species, disease, cure, historical key, new page or component
meaning is claimed.
