# Method

The complete procedure and fixed decision table were locked in PREREGISTRATION.md
before pixel access. CANVAS_METADATA.json retains the official label/source;
TARGET_LINES.json is the exact f45r.10 slice of1198's already exposed packet.
One whole view locates the line, one source-native rectangle compares its groups.
OBSERVATION.json records the single informed judgment, then OBSERVATION_SEAL.json
binds it before src/run.py reduces it. src/validate.py independently recomputes
that reduction and checks source/admission chronology and exact receipts.
No relation scoring, new frequency test, visual classifier or lexical inference.
The full image is a scoped localization source; the recorded analysis is only
f45r.10. Source image dimensions/receipts are checked, not guessed from a preview.
