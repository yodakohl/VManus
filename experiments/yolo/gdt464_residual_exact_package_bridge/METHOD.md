# GDT464 method

## Question

Can the seven GDT463 whole-label residuals be reduced through exact existing
cards, already populated recipe arms, or replicated owner families without
reopening the rejected low-precision `ch`, `om`, `ey`, `ody`, and `dy` edges?

## Inputs

- GDT407's 4,576-event running-text edition;
- GDT413's unchanged 46-component working dictionary;
- GDT459's 183 address events and its old factor-gate results;
- GDT463's complete 107-label hybrid dictionary;
- GDT441's unchanged factor-gate reader.

## Method

Four bounded bridges are tested.

1. **`of`**: require the q-rendered complete base card `qof=O+LOCAL_CHAR_F`
   and perfect recipe alignment in every bare `of…` running extension.
2. **`ainy`**: require exact `ain=AIN` and `y=Y` cards, perfect `-ain`
   extension alignment, a previously promoted address anchor, and a green
   factor read for `AIN+Y`.
3. **`eey`**: define a terminal grade-II boundary that excludes both `-ey`
   and the longer `-eeey`; require at least 90% exact `EE+Y` alignment and a
   green factor read.
4. **`yddy`**: require the exact three-card spelling `y|d|dy`, the old GDT459
   `Y+D_ADDR+Y` parse and green gate, and independently populated overlapping
   arms `Y+D_ADDR` and `D_ADDR+Y`.

The strict GDT461 owner-family search is also rerun over all 183 address
events. A substring must occur in at least three unique surfaces, remain in
one content class, and span both relevant class pages. Mixed-class `of` is
therefore evaluated only as a functional card, never as a star-family name.

Every accepted channel is applied to all affected labels, not only the seven
named residuals. All other label fields remain unchanged.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Promote a channel only when its named gate passes exactly. A full formula must
cover every surface character; otherwise the unreconstructed span remains a
visible learned owner-bound name core. Do not use the five rejected weak edges
as fallback segmenters.

This experiment may revise function/name boundaries inside the existing
address deck. It may not add a new core meaning, page, object identity,
generated surface, plaintext claim, language claim, or confirmed lexeme.
