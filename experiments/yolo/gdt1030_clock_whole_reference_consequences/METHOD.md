# Method

Use the fixed contract in PREREGISTRATION.md and complete RAW503 in src/SOURCE.json.
SPEC fixes nine candidates, CASES fixes24 qualitative worlds. model.py compiles
all40 raw groups into the four declared constructions and computes positive
motion closure plus separate reference/calendar constraints. It is a compiler
for this fitted hypothesis, not a reusable Voynich grammar or decoder.

independent.py imports no model/executor. It supplies named-case predictions,
reverse tag reconstruction, implication-proof and month/history checks.
validate.py also reconstructs the parent's128-record lexical ranking and all
literal selected records. PREREG_LOCK binds source/code/predictions before use;
artifacts/PUBLIC_REGISTRATION records the public pre-test commit.

Reproduce after registration, from repository root:

```sh
python3 experiments/yolo/gdt1030_clock_whole_reference_consequences/src/run.py
python3 experiments/yolo/gdt1030_clock_whole_reference_consequences/src/validate.py
```

Full outputs retain positive proofs and separate invented-world constraints.
No false event is inferred from lack of a causal path. Temporal endpoints are
an explicit witness convention and never obtained from hole count. Full parent
source-hour variation is checked separately from the weak unit interpretation.
No observed manuscript meaning, engineering certification or p-value is produced.
