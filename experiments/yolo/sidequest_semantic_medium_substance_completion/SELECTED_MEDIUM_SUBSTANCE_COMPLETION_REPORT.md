# Selected medium/substance completion

## Result

The ten-page creative working edition now separates four things that the prior
German translation repeatedly collapsed:

```text
ZUTAT  ->  ANSATZ  ->  AUSZUG  ->  KLARAUSZUG
                     |
                     +-> WASSER / learned specialist medium
```

The selected short working roots are:

| visible candidate | short default | composition it predicts |
|---|---|---|
| `AIR` | **WASSER** | inlet water, basin water, start water, conduct water, close water run |
| `CHEO` | **AUSZUG** | take extract from a source; add extract to the next operation |
| `OR` | **ANSATZ** | next batch, previous batch, ingredient batch, batch portion |
| `HO` | **ZUTAT** | exact `cho|sho` ingredient card; `HO+OR = ZUTATENANSATZ` |

`SHEY = KLARAUSZUG` remains a recurrent learned result card rather than a
globally split `SH+EY` word. `SHECTHY = TEMPERIERT` is now a state card, not a
second word for water. This is still a deliberately speculative workshop
translation, not a decipherment claim.

## Why this is better than the previous edition

### AIR becomes a real candidate root

The five containing cards now read with one material value and different
operational hulls:

```text
CHAIR       Wasserzulauf
KAIR        Beckenwasser
OKAIR       Wasser in Gang setzen
SCHEDAIR    Wasser weiterführen
DAIRYDY     Wasserlauf schließen; Schluss
```

This is the boldest decision of the round. The older `LAUFFLÜSSIGKEIT` and
`STRÖMUNG` readings were safe but not very predictive: they restated the local
motion. `AIR=WASSER` instead stays invariant while `CH`, `K`, `OK`, `CHED` and
the terminal hull supply inlet, basin, activation, transfer and closure. None
of the five fixed-page contexts forces wine, oil or finished extract.

The direct fallback remains `AIR=STRÖMUNG` if a later authorized page places
an AIR card in a clearly nonaqueous medium. No global `O=WASSER` rule is
introduced.

### CHEO and SHEY become successive products

`CHEO` occurs in two complementary cards:

```text
CHEO+AR     Auszug aus dem Ansatz entnehmen
OK+CHEO     Auszug dem nächsten Posten zugeben
```

The shorter noun **AUSZUG** is preferable to `AUSZUGSMEDIUM`: the first card
describes a recovered product, which can then become input to a later step.
After pressing, standing and finer straining, the separate recurrent `SHEY`
card names the **KLARAUSZUG**.

### OR now names the object, not the whole act

The family becomes a compact compositional table:

```text
OR          Ansatz
OT+OR       nächster Ansatz
OL+OR       voriger Ansatz
HO+OR       Zutatenansatz
OR+AIN      Ansatzportion
```

`ANSATZ` is shorter and more useful than the old `ZUBEREITUNG`: it can be
resumed, selected, portioned and moved through later operations.

### HO repairs the exact-identity contradiction

The exact `cho|sho` card occurs four times on f56r. Three contexts had been
expanded as pictured plant material; one had been expanded as honey. A single
card cannot keep both dictionary meanings. **ZUTAT** is the smallest useful
common value:

- the picture may specialize three occurrences as plant ingredients;
- the fourth can remain a further ingredient, possibly honey;
- `CHOCHOR` becomes predictably `HO+OR = ZUTATENANSATZ`.

This does not license blind splitting of every longer surface containing
`ho`.

## Learned specialist cards

The model remains a mixture of productive abbreviations and memorized whole
cards. Specific media are not forced into a false universal stem:

| exact card | selected whole-card default |
|---|---|
| `SCHOAL` | **WEINSUD** |
| `DL` | **BADZUSATZ** |
| `TSHEY` | **SPÜLWASSER** |
| `DSHEDY` | **FRISCHWASSER; SCHLUSS** |
| `RSHEAL` | **WARMWASSER** |
| `SHECTHY` | **TEMPERIERT** |
| `KCHY` | **TRANK** |
| `KCHOAR` | **GEBRAUCHSAUSZUG** |
| `SKAR` | **WARMAUSGUSS** |

The words are intentionally short. For example, `SCHOAL` no longer means the
entire instruction “boil in wine”; it names the learned product **WEINSUD**.
The operation comes from its statement position. Likewise, `KCHY` is **TRANK**,
not “give as a drink”.

Oil remains unlocalized. Honey remains a possible local realization of
**ZUTAT**, not a dictionary word. No selected card is allowed to mean oil or
honey merely because a plausible recipe could contain it.

## Continuous readings after the repair

### H1-S001 — f10r

> Take a root portion of the pictured plant, clean and divide it, put it in the
> vessel, admit water and collect the working item; set the prescribed measure,
> use it and keep the remainder.

The critical prediction is the compact `CHAIR = CH+AIR = WASSERZULAUF` between
vessel and collection.

### H3-S001 — f11r

> Take the flowering herb, prepare a wine decoction, wring it out, let it stand
> for the prescribed time, strain it again, take the clear extract and let it
> cool; close.

This passage now distinguishes **WEINSUD**, coarse separation, rest, fine
separation and **KLARAUSZUG** without placing a sentence inside one card.

### H5-S004–S005 — f56r

> Set the current item, add the extract and strain it off. Add the next
> ingredient, set the item and take the use extract.

The former Honey/Pflanze conflict disappears: the second instruction may still
be expanded as honey in a fluent herbal reading, but the card itself says only
**ZUTAT**.

### B1-S002 — f81v

> Set the measure, bring the basin water to the working site, take portions from
> the same stock, add the bath additive and continue with the previous batch;
> hold it longer at the site, lead the item through the passage and close the
> operation.

Water (`AIR`), batch (`OR`), additive (`DL`), site (`AL`) and passage (`CKH`)
are five different dictionary roles.

### B3-S021 and B3-S030 — f83r

> Set the measure and bring the ready item to the site. After the settling site
> it is tempered; move the current item ready at the next site and close.

> Set the current item at the prescribed measure, conduct the water onward,
> transfer it again and close.

`SHECTHY=TEMPERIERT` removes an unnecessary second water noun, while
`SCHEDAIR` retains the explicit water root.

## Historical fit of the architecture

The comparison is architectural, not a language identification. Northern
Italian technical collections close to 1420 freely mix abbreviated operations,
measure signs, named media and memorized specialist preparations.

The fifteenth-century Bolognese recipe manuscript 2861 is especially useful:
its edition notes resolved abbreviations and measure signs, while its recipes
distinguish cold, warm, hot, clear and prepared waters, wine, oil, honey and a
clean recovered liquor. That is close to the selected division between a
productive base medium, modified whole cards, an intermediate extract and a
clear result. See the [University of Bologna manuscript description](https://bub.unibo.it/it/bub-digitale/manoscritto-bolognese)
and its [public transcription](https://bub.unibo.it/it/allegati/manoscritto-bolognese-traduzione/%40%40download/file/manoscrittobolognese-traduzione.pdf).

The [Othmer MS 1 catalogue](https://openn.library.upenn.edu/Data/0025/html/OthmerMS1.html)
describes a north-Italian compilation completed before December 1438 with more
than 500 recipes spanning medicine, alchemy, metals, cosmetics and wine. It is
a good period analogue for a large learned nomenclator surrounding a much
smaller productive process grammar. British Library [Royal MS 18 A VI](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002107422)
similarly combines medicinal waters, wine and herbal material in a
fifteenth-century technical collection.

These witnesses make the mixed architecture plausible. They do not prove the
specific assignments `AIR`, `CHEO`, `OR` or `HO`.

## Selection among the four perspectives

The medical/apothecary pass favored recurrent liquid and extract roles but
kept `HO=PFLANZENSTOFF`. The material-workshop pass correctly preferred
`OR=ANSATZ` and `CHEO=AUSZUG`, but generalized most concrete liquids away. The
bathhouse/technical pass independently converged on all five selected central
decisions:

```text
AIR=WASSER
CHEO=AUSZUG
OR=ANSATZ
HO=ZUTAT
SHECTHY=TEMPERIERT
```

The central synthesis adopts that root system and then shortens the learned
whole-card deck. The row-by-row comparison is in
`MEDIUM_SUBSTANCE_MODEL_COMPARISON.tsv`.
