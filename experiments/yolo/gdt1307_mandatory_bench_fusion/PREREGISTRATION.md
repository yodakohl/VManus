## Complete fixed teaching channel and necessary law
Use the existing22working units as labels. Source primitives are the21labels
other than ckh; meanings are unassigned. For one chosen orientation, scan each
nonempty source word once left-to-right. On the selected pair(ch,k) or(k,ch),
write one ckh and advance two primitives; otherwise write the literal primitive
and advance one. Preserve word spaces. Decoder expands ckh to the selected pair
and leaves every other label literal. The disjoint scan guarantees exact source
recovery for arbitrary finite source words; overlapping opportunities follow the
same left-to-right rule. No extra signs, zero outputs, hidden boundaries,
context/style exceptions or alternate literal values are supplied.

A canonical output cannot contain the selected unfused pair. For forward order,
whole chky decodes to[ch,k,y] and re-encodes as[ckh,y], so chky is not canonical.
For reverse order, whole kchy decodes to[k,ch,y] and likewise re-encodes as[ckh,y].
Both orders therefore fail if both whole forms are retained. Other analogous
gallows rules cannot repair these witnesses, which contain no t,p,f. This is
not a rejection of optional fusion, contextual ligatures, different atomic
bindings, featural modification or abbreviation generally. It assigns no meaning
to chky,kchy,ckhy or any constituent.
