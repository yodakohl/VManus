# GDT753 method

## Question

Across every reader-exact occurrence, does the complete-form pair
`qokeol/okeol` repeatedly support the inherited directional role division
`qokeol` = process or material at middle stage and `okeol` = preparation? If
not, where did the two concrete German sentences enter the renderer, and what
is the strongest working default that can remain?

## Inputs

- the GDT751 fixed q/base and non-q pair decks;
- the guarded cached line and complete-cell context inherited through GDT752;
- GDT744's unchanged historical field-channel rules;
- the GDT664 `okeol` and GDT666 `qokeol` target decision decks for provenance.

## Method

Select `qokeol/okeol` as the target fixed by GDT752. Select five other q/base
pairs and five non-q prefix/base pairs deterministically by base length and
log-distance from the target's two reader-exact occurrence counts. Position,
semantic labels and role outcomes do not enter matching.

For every reader-exact occurrence of all 22 surfaces, walk left and right at
most five cells. Stop at a physical line edge, a strict initial-head boundary,
a close field, or another member of that pair. Pair members and their inherited
meanings cannot anchor their own field. Only fields reaching a real boundary
on both sides are active; a radius-five edge remains censored.

Clean reader-exact W2/W3 whole anchors supply the unchanged GDT744 role axes.
The target directional hypothesis requires:

1. `MIDDLE_STAGE` plus `PROCESS` or `MATERIAL` around the q-form on at least
   two pages;
2. `PREPARATION` around the base form on at least two pages;
3. a positive q-minus-base rate difference for the first role; and
4. a positive base-minus-q rate difference for preparation.

The same rule is applied to all ten matched pairs. Separately, trace the
current two concrete German values to their exact source decision cards and
record whether they were learned wholes or analyst component compositions.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

If the target gate fails, do not speak the old directional command/patient and
preparation prose as recovered whole meanings. Preserve any useful shared
family hypothesis explicitly as a hypothesis and retain the complete-form
relation lead. External field labels may describe an occurrence's context but
do not become its lexical identity.

GDT753 may correct the spoken working renderer for these two complete forms.
It may not assign a value to EVA `q`, `o`, `k`, `e`, `ol`, any character,
prefix, morpheme, sound, abbreviation or substring. It identifies no confirmed
lexeme, literal operation, patient, preparation, ingredient, plant, disease,
cure, person, vessel, unit, plaintext, new page, image, transcription, f84 or
f84r.
