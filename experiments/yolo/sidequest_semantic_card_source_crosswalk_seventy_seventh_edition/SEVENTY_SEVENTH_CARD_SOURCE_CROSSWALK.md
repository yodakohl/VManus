# Endliches Karten-zu-Quellen-Wörterbuch

## AIIN · Sollwert

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `PORTION,TEMPERATURE,DURATION,CALENDAR_VALUE,CELESTIAL_VALUE,WEATHER_VALUE,LIGHT_VALUE,PLANET_VALUE,QUALITY_VALUE`.

A prescribed value can fill only a registered value slot.

## AIN · Anteil

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `PORTION`.

A share or portion does not identify its material.

## IIN · Stufe

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `TEMPERATURE,DURATION,CALENDAR_VALUE,CELESTIAL_VALUE,WEATHER_VALUE,LIGHT_VALUE,PLANET_VALUE,QUALITY_VALUE`.

A stage grades an already selected process or readout.

## AL · Ziel

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `TARGET,STATION,BODY_WORK_AREA,STAR_PLACE,MANSION_PLACE,CONDITION_PLACE`.

A destination can resolve only to a local target-like slot.

## AR · Quelle

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `OWNER,PREVIOUS,ROOT,HERB,WATER,EXTRACTION_MEDIUM,EXTRACT,INLET`.

A source points backward or inward; it does not name the source substance.

## AIR · Lauf

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `RUN,WATER,WASH_LIQUID`.

A run may carry a registered liquid but cannot invent one.

## OK · ansetzen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `ITEM`.

Set the current item into work.

## OL · weiter

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `PREVIOUS,ITEM`.

Continue the previous/current item.

## OT · danach

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `ITEM`.

Select the following item without naming it.

## OR · Ansatz

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `EXTRACTION_MEDIUM,EXTRACT,RESULT`.

The preparation channel can resolve to medium, extract or result.

## Y · dies

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `ITEM`.

This/current item only.

## E · kurz

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `DURATION,TEMPERATURE`.

Short grade modifies duration or temperature.

## EE · länger

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `DURATION,TEMPERATURE`.

Longer grade modifies duration or temperature.

## EEE · vollständig

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `DURATION,TEMPERATURE`.

Full grade modifies duration or temperature.

## CLOSE · Ende

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `NONE`.

Closure licenses no content noun.

## CHD · umsetzen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `ITEM,TARGET`.

Transfer acts on an item toward an already licensed target.

## CTH · bereit

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `RESULT`.

Readiness may expose only the registered result slot.

## CKH · Durchlass

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `OPENING,INLET,OUTLET,RUN,FILTER`.

A passage chooses visible/local path slots.

## CKHE · trennen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `FILTER,EXTRACT,RESULT`.

Separation may yield a registered extract/result.

## CHK · wärmen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `TEMPERATURE`.

Heating licenses temperature, not a named substance.

## SHED · absetzen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `DURATION,RESULT`.

Settling can consume duration and expose a result.

## SOLK · sammeln

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `RECEIVER,RESULT`.

Collection selects receiver/result only.

## HO · Zutat

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `SHOOT,LEAF,FLOWER,HERB,ADDITIVE`.

An added material must already occupy an ingredient-like slot.

## CHEO · Auszug

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `EXTRACTION_MEDIUM,EXTRACT`.

Extract never expands directly to wine, medicine or dye.

## KCH · bearbeiten

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `ITEM`.

Generic processing leaves the content in the current-item register.

## TY · Teil

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `PORTION,ROOT,SHOOT,LEAF,FLOWER,HERB`.

Part selects a registered divisible material.

## SH · halten

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `DURATION,ITEM`.

Hold affects the current item for a licensed duration.

## CHEEY · Ergebnis

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `RESULT`.

Result selects only the current program result.

## cfhy · auswringen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `FILTER,EXTRACT,RESULT`.

Wringing may produce the registered extract/result.

## cphy|ocphy · nachseihen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `FILTER,EXTRACT,RESULT`.

Re-straining may produce the registered extract/result.

## ches|chety|chty · teilen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `PORTION,ROOT,SHOOT,LEAF,FLOWER,HERB`.

Partition operates on a registered material.

## cho|sho|tshol · Zutat

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `SHOOT,LEAF,FLOWER,HERB,ADDITIVE`.

The learned addition card uses the same bounded ingredient slots.

## dchey · Wurzelteil

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `ROOT`.

The learned card licenses only root.

## dchol|schol · vorher

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `PREVIOUS`.

The learned card points to the previous preparation.

## lshedy|lsho|rshedy · waschen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `WASH_LIQUID,BODY_WORK_AREA,FILTER`.

Washing selects liquid, local target or filter path.

## qokylddy · befestigen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `OUTER_APPLICATION,BIO_CLOTH,BODY_WORK_AREA`.

Fastening requires a registered application, cloth or local target.

## skar · ausgießen

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `OUTLET,RUN,RECEIVER`.

Pouring selects an outlet, run or receiver.

## sotodan · anwenden

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `OUTER_APPLICATION,BODY_WORK_AREA,TARGET`.

Application resolves only to a registered target slot.

## dl · Zusatz

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `ADDITIVE`.

The learned card licenses only additive.

## talam · verwahren

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `VESSEL,RECEIVER,RESIDUE,RESULT`.

Storage selects a registered container or stored content.

## dain · Tuch|Portion

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `CLOTH,BIO_CLOTH,PORTION`.

The exact card chooses cloth or portion by register.

## ody · kühlen|markieren

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `TEMPERATURE,LOCAL_LABEL`.

The exact card chooses cooling or marking by register.

## os · Gefäß|Feld

Erlaubte Quellen-Slots: `VESSEL,BASIN,STATION,PANEL`.

The exact card chooses a container/field owner by register.
