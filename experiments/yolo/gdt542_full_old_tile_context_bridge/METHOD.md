# GDT542 method

## Question

For the final GDT540 prose targets that lack a complete old recipe but are
fully tileable by old multi-component recipes, do the old deck and its actual
statement contexts also support every tile boundary, portable action/argument
skeleton, and ordered tile path?

## Inputs

- GDT407's 4,576 old running events and 715 fixed statements;
- GDT516's 159-surface family atlas and original support tiers;
- GDT540's final 145 prose-surface context contracts.

No unadmitted page, new root value, alternate segmentation, or generated
surface is used.

## Method

1. Recompute the support tier of each of the 145 final GDT540 recipes. A tile
   is a complete GDT407 recipe with at least two atoms; the deterministic
   dynamic programme maximizes covered atoms, then minimizes the number of
   tiles. Singletons may fill gaps only in the fragment tier.
2. Retain the 29 recipes completely covered by two or more old complete-card
   tiles. Enumerate every old event that carries each chosen tile.
3. For every boundary between consecutive tiles, count that exact adjacent
   atom pair inside every old complete recipe. This asks whether composition
   crosses a wholly novel seam.
4. Remove local/rendering atoms and compare the remaining ordered sequence of
   the nineteen portable roots. Replay GDT407's two-slot same-statement state
   and compare the context modes of exact old skeleton carriers with the
   GDT540 target mode.
5. Search each old statement for the complete tile sequence in order, without
   requiring adjacency. Record the minimum-span paths; adjacency is reported
   separately.
6. Assign each target the strongest available bridge: adjacent sequence,
   ordered same-statement sequence, exact portable skeleton with compatible
   context, or complete old tiles plus old seams.

## Result conditions

- the final 145-target tier recount is deterministic and every migration from
  GDT516 is named;
- all 29 selected targets are completely covered by old multi-atom cards;
- every reported tile instance and every intertile seam replays from GDT407;
- every skeleton bridge contains the target's observed context mode;
- every statement path preserves tile order and page/statement boundaries;
- the reader delegates exact and non-tile targets to the earlier layers.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The experiment ranks already assigned working compositions; it does not
require the weakest tier to fail. Complete old tiles plus old seams are kept as
a reasonable default even when no whole old skeleton or sentence path exists.
An old seam proves only that the local atom transition is licensed somewhere,
not that the complete target recipe occurred before. Portable-root skeletons
discard renderer/local atoms and therefore do not establish phonetic
allography. No page, recipe, segmentation, root meaning, plaintext, language,
object identity, or historical codebook changes.
