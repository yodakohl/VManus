# Sidequest Pass 823: halten versus stehenlassen

The complete contexts retain the existing distinction:

> `SH = HALTEN`
> `SHED = STEHENLASSEN`

SH occurs 25 times and keeps the item under active control. SHED occurs 15
times. Thirteen uses close the local step; two `SHED+AL` cards leave the item at
a target and continue working with it afterward.

That distribution is exactly what the workshop verbs predict. HALTEN keeps an
operation live. STEHENLASSEN releases active handling and creates a pause or
placed intermediate state.

ABSETZEN is a plausible local expansion for liquid, but it would invent
sediment separation everywhere. RUHEN loses the transitive instruction to the
scribe/operator. ABSTELLEN is shorter but ambiguously means either put down or
shut off. HALTEN would collapse the useful active/released contrast.

No dictionary value changes in this pass.
