- `GDT803_14_STABLE_CONTEXT_DECK.tsv`: every all-fold stable recurrent context
  that entered the interpretation pass.
- `GDT803_450_CORE_CONTEXT_OCCURRENCES.tsv`: complete occurrence audit for the
  seven selected outer forms.
- `GDT803_12_BIDIRECTIONAL_BRACKETS.tsv`: the exact left/target/right spans and
  their complete cached ZL3b lines.
- `GDT803_7_OUTCOME_BLIND_CONTROL_MATCH.tsv`: exposure-only candidate/control
  assignment.
- `GDT803_GROUP_POSITION_POPULATION_CARD.tsv`: candidate/control residuals by
  population and line position.
- `GDT803_RARITY_ENUMERATION.tsv`: one-sided group rankings over 5/8/10-nearest
  exposure matches.
- `GDT803_BRACKET_ENUMERATION.tsv`: exhaustive cross of the 1,595 left and 120
  right ten-nearest group controls.
- `GDT803_EXACT_MATCHED_EVENT_PAIRS.tsv`: deterministic same-stem/distance,
  different-folio candidate/control matches.
- `GDT803_IDENTITY_RARITY_PAIR_ATLAS.tsv` and
  `GDT803_IDENTITY_RARITY_SUMMARY.tsv`: hard held-stem-and-folio exact identity
  versus outcome-blind rarity audit.
- `GDT803_STYLE_SENSITIVITY.tsv`: section/language/hand sensitivity.
- `GDT803_EXACT_PASSAGE_CARD.tsv`: every informative exact match plus all core
  `m` counterexamples.
- `GDT803_FIELD_ROLE_BRIDGE.tsv`: pre-existing broad role comparison with zero
  semantic export.
- `GDT803_CANDIDATE_ADJUDICATION.tsv`, `GDT803_STRUCTURAL_CARD.tsv`,
  `RESULT.json`, `VALIDATION.json`: compact decisions and validation.
