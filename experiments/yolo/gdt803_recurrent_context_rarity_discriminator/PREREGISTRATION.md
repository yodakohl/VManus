# GDT803 exploratory design record

This document records the design used for GDT803.  It is deliberately not
represented as a blind pre-outcome registration: the fourteen stable context
cards and their coefficients had already been exposed by GDT802, and the four
left `qok`-near surfaces were compacted after inspection.  Consequently the
matched-set enumerations rank the resulting hypothesis but do not supply an
independent significance test.

Before the GDT803 builder was scored, the following independent discriminator
was fixed:

- learn complete-context identity while excluding the test physical folio and
  target stem;
- hold section, language, hand, target stem and exact distance cell fixed when
  comparing `l` and `m`;
- compare identity with outcome-blind training rarity;
- use alpha 20, minimum 5 events/3 stems/3 folios;
- use whole-stratum sign flips, fixed seed 802803;
- keep left and right separate;
- reject all word/component/translation export.

The group analysis additionally freezes the exact seven named surfaces,
exposure-only distance, injective matching, 5/8/10-nearest enumerations and
same-stem/distance different-folio event pairing.  All counterexamples are
retained.  `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.
