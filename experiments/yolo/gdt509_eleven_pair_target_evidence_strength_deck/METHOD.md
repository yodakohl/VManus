# GDT509 method

## Question

Can the eleven pair-front working translations be published in one compact
deck that preserves their phrases while making the genuinely different support
mechanisms impossible to confuse?

## Inputs

- GDT413's fixed component values;
- GDT506's complete eleven-card frame compatibility rank;
- GDT507's four contextual bridge cards;
- GDT508's final local Source package bridge.

## Method

For every GDT506 target, retain identity, recipe, pair handgrip, argument
policy, phrase, selected witness and old counts. Assign exactly one route:

1. `A_LOCAL_FRAME_REDUCTION`: compatible ordered reduction in the target
   register;
2. `B_CROSS_REGISTER_FRAME_REDUCTION`: compatible ordered reduction only in
   another register;
3. `C_LOCAL_CONTEXT_BRIDGE`: old pair order plus a local same-argument context
   chain;
4. `D_LOCAL_REPEATED_PACKAGE_PROJECTION`: old pair order plus GDT508's local
   repeated-package projection.

Routes describe mechanisms, not posterior probabilities. Cards are sorted by
route and their old GDT506 priority only for reading convenience. A separate
five-handgrip table verifies complete target coverage.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Every source target must appear exactly once and keep its current phrase,
`COMPOSED_WORKING` evidence label and component trace. A route may summarize
only witnesses already published by GDT506–508.

No card becomes observed, confirmed plaintext or a surface/occurrence
prediction. No root value, phrase, page or foreign carrier frame changes. f84
and f84r remain forbidden.
