## Two separately motivated proposals, not a compulsory new dictionary

The physician's chedy=wird? tries one change-of-state predicate. It does not
include is, makes, becomes filled, an auxiliary, or an imperative. It makes
the first four words of f77r.35 a specific clause candidate in R:

```text

Dampf? dessen? wird? Wasser?
```

The proposed owner of dessen is the earlier otedy .25, a guessed water source.
The resulting content would be condensation. That is an actual material
assertion, not a generic instruction. Every word AND the clause grouping are
still hypotheses. The remaining four ZL words sheedy qokaiin chedaiin chealy
are not a cooling instruction or a causal explanation. Q's warm stays humoral;
the natural physical possibility of condensation supplies no thermal word key.

Root's separate ol=oder? proposal comes from the complete f83r last paragraph:


This preserves the existing scalar family; it does not identify it. The two
first lines each contain three terms followed by ol. The quantity pair cannot
be detached from them and made into a complete translation. All three ol
must serve the same alternative-coordination meaning; their larger scope is
unidentified. The RF cheal and final split forms remain different unknowns.

A limited historical comparator is §50 of the Latin Questio de aqua et terra
in the Princeton Dante Project. Its discussion of relative distance uses
the short phrase “parum vel multum”. That shows scalar alternatives in a real
Latin natural-philosophical sentence; it is not a Voynich quantity/degree key,
does not supply the same word order, and does not make this a bath passage.
This turn consulted the modern online text, not a circa-1420 manuscript.

## Wider written challenge: neither addition is ready as a universal default

- f76r.23 ZL/IT contain chedy chedy before qokain. The extension literally
  gives wird wird Wasser. Another such duplicate occurs in f72r3.26 ZL/IT.
  They are not licensed as two hidden procedures or two unmarked sentences.
  A construction or written-duplication explanation remains to be found.
- f81r.4 contains ol ol in all three readers. f81r.5 has two consecutive ol
  in ZL and three in IT/RF. Ordinary binary alternative coordination has no
  written intervening alternative here. Nesting alone does not supply one.
  Extra ellipsis, scribal duplication or a different interpretation might be
  possible, but none is read. Thus the unmodified everywhere-or model loses
  priority; keep viel oder wenig as a local seed, not dictionary adoption.
- Non-P uses are whole C records, not isolated one-word labels. They do not
  themselves refute either predicate or conjunction, but their variants matter.
  No direct known-noun chedy known-noun triple exists in the39-selector deck;
  this does not erase f77r's different four-word noun-marker-chedy-noun span.

GDT769's broader contextual ol conjunction/medium/source menu was a role tie,
not a proved word. It cannot be used to switch oder to von/mit here.
Keep base/extended literal arrays side by side so the debt stays visible.
