# GDT785 preregistration

This experiment is an exhaustive exploratory census, not a blind holdout.
Every one of the 33 reader-exact complete `sal` occurrences is admitted; the
f88r.22 `chorcholsal` interpretation is withheld from candidate scoring and is
used only as a downstream compositional check.

The live candidates are nominal drug/material, named drug, preparation,
record-item marker, salt, liquid/medium, quality/state and amount/unit. Salt is
retained as a genuine concrete C0 rival, but the visual coincidence between
EVA `sal` and Latin *sal* contributes zero points. Seed and the earlier
`s + al` analysis remain quarantined because GDT777 has zero reader-exact
split pairs among five raw candidates.

One preferred word must cover all occurrences. A position-dependent change
between “item,” “ingredient,” and “preparation” is not allowed. German syntax
may reorder a noun and its adjacent quality or degree in readable examples.

Ceiling: C2 complete written whole; C1 nominal functional role if selected;
C0 concrete word and longer-form core echo; no confirmed lexeme, plaintext,
specific substance, component export, or access to a new or sealed page.
