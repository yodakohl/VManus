# GDT780 method — two cardless complete-whole bridges

## Question

Can the independently supported whole roles `eees=Mengenfeld` and
`sheeol=Endzustand` replace their reader-exact generic `ol` fallbacks without
using target-local evidence or changing any other GDT779 decision?

## Inputs

The fixed parent is GDT779's 376-row renderer and result. Its residual census
defines the 25 reader-exact cardless cases. GDT758 supplies the complete-form
value-follower statistic for `eees`; GDT745–GDT748 supply the whole-form,
distribution, passage and counterframe record for `sheeol`. Every source is
hash-locked in `src/SOURCE_LOCK.tsv`.

## Method

1. Load and hash-check the eleven frozen inputs.  The two rowwise additions
   bind GDT769's exact `eees aiin` target context and GDT747's exact `sheeol`
   target with local-support tier L0.
2. Reconstruct the complete 25-row reader-exact cardless intake from the parent
   renderer, using the residual census only as a cross-check.
3. Load the two complete-whole cards from `src/TWO_WHOLE_SPECS.tsv` and verify
   their evidence numerically from the locked source artifacts.
4. Apply the pure selector to all 376 rows. Existing contextual rows have
   precedence; selected fallbacks consume their complete right token once.
5. Emit the complete renderer, two-row atlas, 25-row intake, two-card
   dictionary/evidence audit, two-row target-independence audit, passage
   patches, residual census, relation packet and machine-readable result.
6. Independently reconstruct selection, evidence, precedence and consumption,
   then byte-replay all runner outputs.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The selector is exactly:

```text
parent contextual = 0 AND right reader exact = 1 AND complete right surface in fixed two-card deck
```

Occurrence IDs, pages, loci, neighbors, frequency, edit distance, substrings
and meanings are forbidden selector inputs. The short German values are
replaceable whole-span renderer defaults. GDT780 does not identify `ol`,
`eees`, `sheeol` or a substring as a lexeme; it does not identify a number,
unit, ingredient, liquid, disease, treatment or plaintext clause; and it opens
no new page, image, OCR, transcription, `f84` or `f84r`.
