## Why this beats the alternatives

`aii` is not globally fixed. The complete context control contains nineteen
old and seven current short-`aii` surfaces with many different recipes. In
particular, `IIN`, `A_ADDR+IIN`, and visible local-character readings remain
valid in their own carried contexts.

The target-specific comparison is narrower:

- `AIIN+CH+T+Y` preserves both the completed square and exact `cthy`;
- `IIN+CH+T+Y` preserves the right card but not the square's left value;
- `A_ADDR+IIN+CH+T+Y` remains the serious `saiis`-style alternative, but it
  adds an address atom absent from the four-corner relation;
- the old direct reading with two local `I` atoms explains every character but
  misses the recurrent value card.

The page itself agrees with the value reading: f31r contains four exact
`aiin=AIIN` cards and four `daiin=AIIN` cards before the target.
