# GDT795 — Source-native family transfer over the Kluge register

Status: **PARTIAL — complete family atlas recovered; fixed position code fails;
local graphical blocking survives**.

## Outcome

GDT795 reconstructs the complete source-native family sequence for all 101
already admitted labels with a Kluge-A member. It does not split a visible
circle label into invented pharmaceutical words. Instead it retains the three
alternate readings, their source groups and their full member-code sequences.

The reconstruction is substantially cleaner than the earlier visible-string
view:

| source agreement | loci |
|---|---:|
| identical family sequence and boundaries in all three readings | 81 |
| identical compact family sequence, boundary majority 2/3 | 11 |
| family sequence majority 2/3 | 9 |
| full source-member sequence identical in all three readings | 55 |
| full source-member sequence majority 2/3 | 36 |
| full source-member sequence all different | 10 |

The 101 labels yield 74 boundary-bearing family signatures, 73 compact
signatures and 93 visible complete surfaces. Only eleven family signatures
recur, for 38 events. This is far too much individual material for a small
universal position vocabulary.

## What the families do not encode

The same Kluge-A member can be compared across diagrams 156 times. The full
ZL3b source-member sequence agrees in **zero** of those pairs. Boundary-bearing
families agree twice; transferred prefixes 39 times; residuals three times.
None exceeds its within-diagram permutation control.

The optimistic leave-one-physical-folio-out exact-family test has 26 eligible
targets. A matching family occurs at the exact held A-member for four targets
and within one member for six. Those four exact events reduce to two recurrent
families, not four independent keys:

- `AQABAB` gives `okalal` and `okalam` at 09A;
- `AQABA` gives `okody` and `okaly` at 21A.

Both families also occur at other A-members. `AQABAB` appears at 04A and 25A;
`AQABA` occupies eight distinct A-members. Even the two 09A rows differ in the
last full source member, B2 versus B3. Consequently neither `AQABAB=9` nor
`okal=nine` is licensed.

One shared rotation or reflection never recovers three different family
signatures. The best pairwise T15 transforms recover 1, 1 and 0 exact
signatures; f70v2 against f72r2 recovers two after a 22-member shift, no better
than the optimized null (`p=.998004`). The only near-stable relative interval,
`AQABAC` to `AQABBA`, spans seven members on f70 and six on f72. It is retained
as an analytical rival, not as external diagram-relation evidence.

Thus the families are not a reusable day, degree or catalogue-member code.
Edit similarity is mildly better than the visible surface, but still only weak
position texture: boundary-family top-1 credit is .069966 over 99 targets and
its permutation `p=.083832`. It cannot name a position or export a component.

## Positive result: a local graphical field

The useful signal is local rather than homologous. Across 87 genuinely
consecutive source slots, 35 neighboring labels share the transferred formal
prefix. Within the same arrays after permutation the mean is 23.398 matches;
the deterministic one-sided calibration gives `p=.000800`. Boundary families,
full member sequences and residuals do not show the same adjacency effect.

This selects the following working architecture:

```text
individual learned member designation
+ locally blocked graphical/register prefix or status material
+ optional source-group boundary variation
```

The prefix is a formal renderer class, not a recovered word. In particular,
the result does not resurrect free meanings for EVA substrings or for the
analyst families A/B/C/Q.

## Historical working theory

The best historical architecture is now a **sign-conditioned register of
learned 30-member designations, represented on some pages as 15-member half
registers, with a smaller local status/quality field**. This is an architecture
prior, not an identification of the register.

That mixture is historically plausible. Wellcome MS 8515 (about 1425) combines
calendar/computational material with zodiacal medicine and humoral content;
BSB Clm 826 (Prague, 1401) contains circular schemes joining lunar houses,
months and zodiacal material; Morgan M.785 organizes zodiac signs with separate
decan registers. These comparators support mixed registers and circular
arrangement, but none supplies a Voynich word value:

- https://wellcomecollection.org/works/w9nkm98w
- https://bildsuche.digitale-sammlungen.de/index.html?bandnummer=bsb00095113&c=viewer&l=de&nav=&pimage=22&v=
- https://www.themorgan.org/manuscript/144038

An intentionally creative next rival is `AQABAC = favorable/benefic facies or
quality class`. It occurs at Pisces 03A, the Taurus half-page 08A and Gemini
11A, so it crosses the ring split. That reading is **C0 and untested**: different
historical decan orders reverse the apparent benefic pattern. It may survive
only if one global orientation and offset predict held signs better than an
opaque class. It is not installed by GDT795.

## Why this advances the route

The experiment replaces two ambiguous levels at once. We now know which parts
of the repeated `okal*` resemblance survive source-native transcription, and
we know that family resemblance predicts local copying/register blocks much
better than the same nominal member across charts. The next test can therefore
look for a small visible or historical status field while treating the rest of
each label as an individual learned entry. It no longer needs to force every
label into one numerical code or generic process sentence.

## Scope and ceiling

alignment source was queried through the exact-locus guard: 394 rows were
selected, 2,122 sealed-prefix rows were rejected before materialization and no
sealed row entered an artifact. No new page, image or transcription was opened.

The empty GDT388 relation packet validates as acquisition-valid but not
score-ready because the one interval rival is an internal catalogue comparison,
not an independently annotated visual relation. GDT795 confirms no Voynich
lexeme, morpheme, number, day, degree, planet, star, substance, action, disease,
treatment, language, cipher or plaintext clause. The independent validator
passes 220 checks and two byte-identical builder replays over all thirteen
canonical machine outputs.
