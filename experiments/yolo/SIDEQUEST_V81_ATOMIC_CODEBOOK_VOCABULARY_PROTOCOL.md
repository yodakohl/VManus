Frozen: 2026-08-22, after V80 and before any V81 target reveal.

Status: creative ten-page sidequest, not a GDT experiment and not a

## Narrow question

Can a **small atomic portable vocabulary**, attested entry-for-entry in genuine
1370–1450 codebooks, improve the V80 ten-page working reading without turning a
whole sentence into one supposed word?

This round is deliberately source-first. It tests historical dictionary
granularity and occurrence consistency, not visual or phonetic resemblance.

## Phase 1 — historical source freeze before target reveal

Each role independently selects one genuine 1370–1450 cipher key,
nomenclator, codebook or coded sign list and freezes at most 60 exact entry rows
before reading the V81 card manifest or V80 occurrence contexts.

The phase-1 inventory must contain:

1. exact source-language entry;
2. exact opaque code/sign where preserved;
3. historical key identity;
4. archive shelfmark or unambiguous source object;
5. date or dated correspondence;
6. exact folio/page/table row;
7. facsimile or documentary-edition citation and stable locator;
8. transcription confidence;
9. a SHA-256 freeze binding the inventory before target reveal.

A row without an exact entry↔code pairing is a historical control and cannot
license a word. Previously used Lavinde 1379 key 13/key 26 rows and the Fi1
1414 `et`/`per` rows remain controls but do not count as new V81 evidence.

## Phase 2 — bounded target reveal

Only after all four source freezes exist, reveal one centrally generated
manifest containing every V80 exact prose card with at least three independent
source positions. Selection uses only V80 frequency and anonymous ID. Audit
every occurrence of every revealed card; do not add candidates after seeing
attractive matches.

Candidate construction may not use surface similarity, EVA spelling,
substrings, edit distance, PAGE_HOST, coordinates, sound, abbreviation shape
or presumed language.

## Admission rule

A proposed portable word survives only if all are true:

- it is an atomic category of at most three ordinary German words, not a
  sentence or hidden argument bundle;
- the exact historical entry↔code row satisfies Phase 1;
- one exact Voynich card receives one invariant default across every audited
  occurrence;
- the card appears in at least two formal records;
- the default is usable in every occurrence without changing its core sense;
- it improves at least two complete V80 statements rather than merely naming
  one page or picture;
- a silent formal role, frequency, position, Close, owner or exemplar-copy
  explanation is explicitly compared;
- all contradictions and repair costs are published.

Historical attestation licenses category granularity only. It never proves
that a Voynich card carries that category.

## Required role output

Each role produces:

- one frozen source inventory and freeze JSON;
- one decision table for the complete revealed card panel;
- one full-occurrence audit for every promoted or near-promoted card;
- revised continuous readings of every affected V80 statement;
- one compact report naming the best new atomic mapping, the strongest rival
  and the exact reason every rejected mapping failed;
- validation with explicit counts and seals.

`EXEMPLAR_VALUE_UNKNOWN` is a valid and preferable result when no atomic
mapping survives. Existing `FORMAL_LINK_OR_SLOT` and
`FORMAL_RELATION_OR_ENTRY` remain formal controls; `ET?`/`PER?` remain optional
master glosses unless new evidence changes only their ranking, never their
historical identity.
