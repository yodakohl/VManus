# GDT779 method

## Question

Can the residual V99R7 complete-word cards replace every matching reader-exact
generic `ol` fallback after old unsupported patients and source-composed prose
have been removed, without occurrence-conditioned selection or component
export?

## Inputs

The final 376-row GDT778 renderer is the fixed parent. The V99R7 complete-word
dictionary supplies card identity, old whole-card provenance and old evidence;
it does not automatically license its literal German wording. GDT754 supplies
the source-composition quarantine and GDT755 supplies the later complete-word
`qockhey` candidate. `RESIDUAL_V99R7_EXACT_WHOLE_SPECS.tsv` freezes the 44
sanitized defaults, rivals, confidence and evidence before the runner.

No mixed transcription source is parsed. Exactness, line text, page label,
physical folio, locus and token identity come only from the already guarded
GDT778 parent and its locked transitive cache.

## Method

1. Hash-check all fixed inputs and require exactly 44 unique complete surfaces
   in the authored deck.
2. Starting only from the 181 rows with `gdt778_renderer_contextual=0`, join the
   complete `right_surface` to the V99R7 card registry. Emit all 99 raw
   card-bearing candidates and keep every row with `right_reader_exact=1`.
   Separately enumerate deck matches outside that fallback pool as a shadowing
   control; all already contextual rows must remain inherited and unchanged.
3. Apply the selected whole default directly to all 50 exact matches. No page,
   occurrence ID, locus, frequency, neighbour, edit distance, prefix, suffix or
   body may choose or alter a row.
4. Sanitize `cheeor`, `cheor`, `lkedy`, `loeees`, `olshdy` and `qokar` without
   deleting their old provenance. Replace the source-composed `qockhey` prose
   only with its later complete-word GDT755 candidate. Scope `daiin`, `kaiiin`
   and `chcthy` only to exact `ol + complete whole`; do not globalize them.
5. Consume each complete right token once. The 50 selected rows are inherited
   fallbacks and therefore must add 50 noncolliding tokens to the inherited 155.
6. Emit the 50-row atlas, 49 nonexact exclusions, precedence-shadow audit,
   376-row renderer, 44-card dictionary, 49-locus passage patch set,
   provenance/sanitization audit, residual census, result, and an explicitly
   ineligible GDT388 relation packet plus crosswalk.
7. Independently reconstruct the selection, semantics gates, consumption and
   all reported counts, then byte-replay every generated artifact and report.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The fixed expectation is 99 raw candidates/76 forms, 50 exact rows/44 forms on
49 loci, 33 page labels and 24 physical folios, with 49 nonexact exclusions.
All 50 must be genuine fallback replacements: contextual coverage 195 to 245,
fallbacks 181 to 131 and unique right-token consumption 155 to 205. Forty-one
forms retain globally exportable V99R7 whole cards; the other three receive only
the new exact-span scope. Any first-occurrence selection, nonexact admission,
old literal-patient leak, source-composed `qockhey` prose, substring export or
consumption collision fails.

The precedence control must also reconstruct all 179 full-parent matches of the
raw 76-form deck: 99 fallback plus 80 contextual, with 127 reader-exact split as
50 fallback plus 77 protected contextual. The final 44-form deck has 68 raw and
63 exact parent matches, of which 13 exact rows are protected contextual
shadows. Source curation must partition the 44 cards disjointly as 32 direct
inherited, six patient-sanitized, three new exact-span scopes, two
composition-derived complete wholes with zero component export, and one GDT755
complete-whole replacement.

Success licenses only replaceable complete-whole defaults inside enumerated
reader-exact `ol + whole` spans. It confirms no EVA character or substring,
lexeme, historical spelling, language, plaintext clause, specific ingredient,
disease, treatment or recipe. No new page, image, OCR, transcription, `f84` or
`f84r` may be opened.
