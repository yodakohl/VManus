# GDT777 method — registered `ol` fields across split and fused surfaces

## Question

Can the corrected H1–H4 form registries turn exact complete right wholes and
four bounded split right fields into concrete `ol` field readings, while
predicting composition without reviving the disproved `p/s/r/l` letter-name
dictionary?

## Inputs

The 376-row GDT776 renderer is the fixed parent. GDT736 supplies its 96
training forms and GDT737 supplies 273 held forms plus current body candidates.
GDT759 supplies the later context-sensitive `s` amount rule and observed
split/fused amount architecture; GDT762 supplies the quarantine of old
Pulver/Samen/Wurzel/Holz head-patient prose. The GDT769 loader supplies only
the already guarded line environment needed to verify exact adjacent tokens.

Every external input is path/hash locked. Mixed transcription data are not
parsed directly, and sealed pages remain forbidden.

## Method

1. Union the GDT736 and GDT737 registered complete forms, retaining their
   record role, content body, current candidate, confidence, and quarantine
   state. Resolve duplicate readings explicitly rather than by dictionary
   overwrite.
2. Scan all 376 GDT776 rows by surface and exactness. Select a complete
   registered right whole or a one-glyph right token whose exact successor
   produces a registered fused form when concatenated.
3. Audit the corresponding split and fused surfaces across the guarded cache:
   frequency, physical folios, register, line position, and immediate outward
   neighbors. Similarity can strengthen boundary equivalence; disagreement
   leaves two constructions with a shared registered body.
4. Build one short display default per selected registered form. Strip all
   retired substance-head patients and keep uncertainty in dedicated rival
   and counterevidence columns.
5. Apply collision-safe renderer precedence: split registered field, complete
   registered whole, inherited GDT776 contextual span, inherited fallback.
   A split field consumes both right tokens; a whole field consumes its right
   token. Every consumed token ID must be unique.
6. Emit changed passages, a working dictionary, provenance/correction tables,
   compact result JSON, and an explicitly ineligible GDT388 relation packet
   with source crosswalk.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Useful progress is measured by deterministic coverage, shorter and more
specific field text, correct multi-token consumption, and a reproducible bridge
between separated and registered forms. The corrected pre-run expectations are
23 spans, seventeen registered forms, fourteen new contextual rows, nine
sharpened rows, and 149→163 coverage. The original 24-span estimate and the
nonexact `s al` correction remain visible in `PREREGISTRATION.md`.

The selected outputs remain replaceable whole-span readings. Split/fused
composition gives no free meaning to EVA transcription characters and confirms
no lexeme, plaintext, historical codebook identity, substance, disease,
treatment, or operation. Structural tags stay visibly distinct from the German
working default. New pages, images, OCR, transcriptions, `f84`, and `f84r` are
outside scope.
