# GDT777 route declaration — registered whole and split-field composer

Declared: 2026-09-03, after GDT776 and before the GDT777 runner is written.

## Fixed cached cohort

Start from all 376 rows of the published GDT776 renderer. A candidate is
admitted without occurrence-ID lookup when:

1. its complete exact right token is itself an H1–H4 form registered by GDT736
   or GDT737; or
2. its complete exact right token is one of the one-glyph transcription forms
   `p`, `s`, `r`, `l`, the following token is reader-exact on the same line,
   and their surface concatenation is an H1–H4 form registered by GDT736 or
   GDT737.

The first pre-run estimate was 24 exact spans representing eighteen registered
forms, including `s al`. The immediate token-exactness audit corrected that
before the runner was finalized: the `al` successor at f80r.51 is not
reader-exact and is excluded by the unchanged surface rule. The corrected
expectation is 23 spans, seventeen registered forms, fourteen fallback
replacements, and nine sharpened contextual rows, still raising contextual
coverage from 149 to 163. The admitted split occurrences are `r aiin`, `r ain`,
`s chey`, and four already licensed `s aiin` spans. These are route expectations
to be checked by the builder, not hand-selected IDs.

No new page, image, OCR, transcription, `f84`, or `f84r` may be opened. The
GDT769 guarded cache may supply exact token identity and immediate line
neighbors only.

## Composition and renderer rule

For a split field, the renderer consumes both right-side tokens and emits one
field whose content body is the registered fused form's body candidate. It may
use the registered form and body as a whole-card analogue, but may not export a
meaning for `p`, `s`, `r`, `l`, or any other EVA substring. A split form and a
fused form need not be spelling variants or interchangeable lexemes; they may
only share the bounded content-body interpretation supported by the registry.

Renderer precedence is:

1. exact `ol + split registered field`;
2. exact `ol + registered complete whole`;
3. GDT776 contextual span;
4. GDT776 fallback.

Every selected form receives one short German working default. Alternative
dimensions and stronger rivals remain in separate evidence columns rather than
inside slash-separated display prose. Retired Pulver-, Samen-, Wurzel-, Holz-,
and Drogen*-patients from the old `p/s/r/l` model are forbidden as inherited
support; a modern corrected whole/body card may still support state, value,
part, material, or record position.

## Useful comparisons

- count fused and split forms in the already guarded environment;
- compare their register, line-position, and immediate-neighbor profiles;
- distinguish exact same-body composition from literal spelling identity;
- enumerate all consumed token IDs and reject overlaps;
- report old versus new contextual coverage and each changed display.

Profile disagreement does not kill the field candidate: it changes the label
from boundary-equivalent to distinct constructions sharing a registered
content body. The useful success condition is a collision-free, concrete
whole-field renderer whose changed rows can all be reconstructed from the
surface rule.

## Claim ceiling

GDT777 may publish replaceable exact-span meanings, a bounded split/fusion
composition rule, and corrected registered-whole fields. It may not publish a
free EVA component dictionary, historical abbreviation identity, language,
plaintext sentence, specific substance, disease, treatment, or operation. Any
relation packet is descriptive acquisition only until every GDT388 gate passes.
