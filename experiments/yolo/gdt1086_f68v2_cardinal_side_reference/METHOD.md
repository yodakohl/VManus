# GDT1086 — f68v2 four-field/eight-ray local reference test

## Decision note before new text access

The newly admitted image (GDT1085) has four star-label fields and eight separate written rays. The result did not bind Trier's twelve same-role wind/month sectors. Walters W.73 f.2r, already used in GDT952, supplies a different *source-side* relation: its four principal winds occur every third position, and seven of the eight collateral-wind descriptions explicitly refer to a neighbouring principal wind by name (including the Zephirus alias Favonius). The source is not a target identification. GDT353 tested the eight radial titles against f68v1, not whether the four interleaved label fields are referred to by neighbouring rays. GDT1085 used image only. Thus the new unknown is a **within-f68v2 cross-layer reference**, not a chosen wind identity or rotation.

If the local textual echo exists, it would justify retaining a concrete four-owner/eight-description *working* model for this diagram, still without translated wind words. If it does not, this particular W.73-style reference model loses its only direct target consequence; a generic 4+8 visual analogy remains but cannot carry a lexical reading. The smallest test is all twelve prelisted loci in all three alternate transcriptions, no other page and no decoder. Total work budget 80 minutes: preparation 25, extraction/implementation 25, validation/report 20, publication 10. At that checkpoint stop expansion.

## Frozen target and relation

Image geometry is inherited from GDT1085: four star fields at page-clock 12, 3, 6 and 9, and eight written rays at roughly 11, 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 10. The historical f68v2 text atlas identifies exactly four interleaved star-label loci `f68v2.8`, `.11`, `.14`, `.17` and eight radial-title loci `f68v2.18`, `.7`, `.9`, `.10`, `.12`, `.13`, `.15`, `.16` (the latter ordered by GDT353). These twelve loci, not the outer ring, neighboring diagrams or prose, exhaust the target.

Before inspecting the text, bind physical adjacency by the image: C-top with F1/F2, C-right with F3/F4, C-bottom with F5/F6, C-left with F7/F8, where F1..F8 are the GDT1085 image-clock ray positions. Use the GDT353 clockwise source order only to bind locus identifiers to F1..F8: `.18,.7,.9,.10,.12,.13,.15,.16`. The four interleaved label identifiers are assigned in clockwise order beginning at top as `.17,.8,.11,.14` **only if** the text-blind inventory's angular notes verify that order; a mismatch invalidates the prospective comparison rather than allowing post-text reassignment. A source-only inventory check comes before extraction of any transcription content.

For each of ZL3b, IT2a, RF1b, preserve every raw group and source separation. A definite source-space delimits words. Uncertain distance is not silently collapsed; mark affected groups ineligible for an exact echo. The primary echo is one identical, all-lowercase a–z complete group of length at least four occurring in one star-field label and either of its two bound neighboring radial inscriptions. Report all 8 ray-by-field binary hits, all specific repeated groups, and the corresponding 24 non-neighbor ray-by-field comparisons. Also report whole-form-only and no-length-threshold counts descriptively; they cannot change the decision. No morphology, spelling repair, selected rotation, chosen reading, guessed synonym or new decoder enters the gate.

The source analogy retains a cross-layer local-reference lead only if **at least six of eight** rays show a primary echo with their bound field **in every reading**, every one of the four fields is echoed by at least one of its two rays, and local echo count exceeds the maximum over the three other possible quarter-turn assignments in each reading. These are descriptive gates, not a significance test. All failure cases and unmatched forms remain in the table. If the atlas cannot securely assign label IDs to physical fields, report `INVALID_OWNER_MAPPING` with zero lexical claim rather than inspect a favorable mapping.
