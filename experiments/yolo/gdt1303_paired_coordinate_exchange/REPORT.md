## The table-independent obstruction

For three equal output groups of even lengthL, the source words must satisfy
`w1[1:] == w2[1:]` and `w2[:-1] == w3[:-1]`. When pair boundaries coincide with
word starts, all three words are identical. At the other phase, identical internal
pairs fix every interior letter. The two identical cross-word pairs additionally
fix the last letters of words1/2 and the first letters of words2/3. Only the two
outer endpoints can depend on outside context. It would be WRONG to demand three
entirely identical source words in both phases: under whole-pair swapping the
source words00/10/11 can produce01/01/01 with suitable outer flanks.

The necessary rule therefore does not depend on the selected2x11 alphabet table,
its ordering, a Voynich glyph renaming, or any numerical entropy tolerance. It
requires fixed disjoint pair decoding, preserved word lengths/spaces and no
undeclared reset within the triple. Variable-length blocks, moving spaces,
state-dependent tables or different source content remain outside it.
