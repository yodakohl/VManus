# GDT552 method

## Question

Can each of the five direct-interface rests surviving GDT551 receive a bounded
ordered bridge from old same-statement card boundaries, old one-atom-separated
cards, or an already licensed learned-stem family, without pretending that the
missing old within-card direct pair is a universal rule?

## Frozen targets

```text
aiicthy  AIIN>CH
chap     A_ADDR>P
ofaram   AR>AM_ADDR
rotaiin  R>OT
shso     SH>S
```

The exact target recipes, visible routes and complete working readings come
unchanged from GDT549/GDT551. Inputs are the old GDT407 4,576-event running
edition, the admitted GDT539 546-event current prose edition, GDT444's observed
focus-separated action pairs and GDT526's already licensed `cha+p` route.

## Census

For every frozen ordered pair:

1. count old direct within-card adjacency;
2. enumerate old occurrences with exactly one visible recipe atom between the
   two roots;
3. enumerate consecutive old cards in the same statement where the last atom
   of the left card and first atom of the right card form the pair;
4. repeat steps 2–3 on current non-target cards as reinforcement only;
5. note when an old card-boundary surface also reproduces the target's visible
   seam suffix/prefix;
6. test the exact target-specific bridge named below.

## Target-specific finite gates

- `aiicthy`: at least one old adjacent-card path must be exactly an `AIIN` card
  followed by a `CH+T+Y` card.
- `chap`: GDT526 must still rank `cha=CH+A_ADDR` plus `p→P` first with its 2/2
  suffix license, and at least two old `A_ADDR+X+P` one-gap cards must exist.
- `ofaram`: at least one old `AR>X+AM_ADDR` card and one exact old card-boundary
  `...ar|am...` visible seam must exist.
- `rotaiin`: at least one old `R>X+OT` card and one exact old card-boundary
  `...r|ot...` visible seam must exist.
- `shso`: at least five old `SH+X+S` one-gap witnesses, one exact old
  `sh|s...` card boundary, and the five GDT444 focus-separated witnesses must
  survive.

## Decision rule and ceiling

Promote a target out of the support queue only when its complete finite gate
passes and recipe, surface reconstruction, visible trace and both German
working readings remain exact. The missing old within-card direct pair remains
explicit. A cross-card or separated bridge supports this already observed
target card; it never licenses an arbitrary new adjacent pair or predicts a
surface. No page, transcription, recipe, segmentation, root value, plaintext,
lexeme, language, cipher, historical codebook identity or object may change.
