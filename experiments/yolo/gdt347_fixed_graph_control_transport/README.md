# GDT347 — fixed compatibility-graph control transport

This experiment freezes the exposed GDT346 coordinate-pair graph once on
Voynich and transports its exact weights unchanged to the historical and
synthetic GDT278 controls. See `METHOD.md`.

Status: **MANUSCRIPT_SPECIFIC_FORMAL_CONVENTION** under the frozen observation-
layer decision. The graph gains 65.481 held Voynich bits (56.651 after its
one-time topology cost; `p=.000244`, 13/17 positive folios), while no powered
native or matched GDT278 control transports. The gain is distributional rather
than decoder-level, and the control parser exposes no inner-D or DY changes;
therefore this is a manuscript-specific formal-coordinate convention, not
proof of a unique authorial compiler or language.
