# GDT347 method — frozen Voynich compatibility graph transport

## Question

Does the local cross-coordinate compatibility detected by GDT346 describe a
generic writing/parser effect, a compiler-like formal system, a
Voynich-specific convention, or only GDT346-local overfit?

This is a transport calibration, not a semantic experiment. Exact tuples stay
atomic. PAGE_HOST is not factored. No meaning, morphology, language, plaintext,
or translation is assigned. Every input and output is forbidden from retaining
an `f84*` row.

## Frozen Voynich graph

The six coordinates, in order, are `local_frame`, `inner_d`, `right_family`,
`dy_closure`, `b3`, and renderer-normalized `canonical_wrapper`. Coordinate
states and source-to-target deltas are exactly GDT345 V2.

GDT346 already exposed its 91 physical-folio training folds. Before any GDT278
control is scored, freeze every pair selected in at least 60% of those folds,
up to three pairs. This gives one topology, never reselected by corpus or fold.
Fit its scope-conditioned multiplicative pair potentials once on a deterministic
Voynich training partition. The partition holds out 20% (rounded, minimum one)
of the folios inside each of the five frozen registers by SHA-256 order. The
held Voynich partition is scored only after topology and weights are frozen.

The graph topology costs `log2(C(15,k))` bits exactly once. Raw transported
gain and the one-time-cost-adjusted gain are both reported. The topology,
potential weights, split, smoothing constants, hashes, and coverage rules are
serialized in `gdt347_frozen_graph.json`.

## Control transport

The sole control panel is the already frozen native-order GDT278 event
inventory and its pre-score architecture manifest. The same observation parser
has already exposed the six formal coordinates for each visible group. Within
each control, adjacent groups on the same page create transitions exactly as in
GDT345; line, field, and record resets are reconstructed mechanically.

For each control folio, only the independent target-coordinate marginals are
estimated from the other folios, using the frozen GDT345 smoothing equations.
This is necessary because control alphabets differ. The Voynich pair topology
and every pair-potential weight remain unchanged. Unknown pair-potential cells
have the frozen neutral factor 1. No control may fit, rescale, reverse, select,
or recalibrate a graph edge.

The primary view is native order. The exact GDT278 matched overlay is a fixed
sensitivity where available. ZL/IT/RF are not control replications.

## Endpoints and comparability

For each corpus and view report:

- eligible and scored transitions and physical folios;
- independent and frozen-graph codelength;
- raw graph gain and one-time-cost-adjusted gain;
- positive held-folio count;
- exact next-state recovery;
- mean graph-potential coverage and each frozen edge's non-neutral coverage;
- coupling-null mobile events and inclusive p;
- section/register/hand or source-subcollection transfer where those strata
  are genuinely present.

A panel is powered/comparable only with at least 500 scored transitions, at
least two physical folios, at least 100 coupling-null-mobile transitions, and
at least 10% non-neutral frozen-potential coverage. Low-capacity and
all-neutral panels remain explicit `NOT_COMPARABLE`, not negative evidence.

## Coupling-destruction null

Run 4,096 fixed-prediction worlds per panel. Independently shuffle each true
delta coordinate within held folio × exact boundary/layout context × that
coordinate's source value. This preserves source-conditioned coordinate
marginals and layout while destroying only their joint realization. The graph
and all marginal predictions stay fixed. Inclusive p uses `(1+exceed)/(4097)`.

## Architecture groups and decision

Architecture labels come only from the pre-existing GDT278 manifest:

- ordinary/structured natural language;
- real diplomatic abbreviation;
- historically learned abbreviation;
- lexical codebook;
- factorial technical notation;
- irregular human-grown hybrid shorthand.

No score may change a label. Decisions are mechanical:

- `GENERIC_WRITING_MECHANICS` if powered significant positive transport occurs
  in both ordinary/structured natural language and real diplomatic text;
- `COMPILER_STYLE_FORMAL_GRAMMAR` if it is absent from powered ordinary text
  but occurs in at least two powered compiler-like synthetic/learned systems;
- `MANUSCRIPT_SPECIFIC_FORMAL_CONVENTION` if held Voynich transport is powered,
  positive, significant, and no powered control transports;
- `GDT346_LOCAL_OVERFITTING` if the held Voynich panel itself is nonpositive or
  nonsignificant;
- otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE_CONTROL_TRANSPORT`.

The graph must not be called compiler-like merely because ordinary controls
lack DY capacity. Any decision reports actual comparable coverage.

## Claim ceiling

At most GDT347 classifies the portability of three formal coordinate
couplings. It cannot establish an authorial grammar, morphology, word, sound,
semantic role, language, plaintext, translation, PAGE_HOST decomposition,
tuple equivalence, or any f84 result.
