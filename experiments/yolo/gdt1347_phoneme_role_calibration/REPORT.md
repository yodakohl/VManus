## A concrete nonphonetic writer

Three fixed-field commands can be written as

    R2 V5 R7 V1 R3 V9

with the meaning SETaddress2to5, SETaddress7to1, SETaddress3to9. R and V here name
two disjoint inventories for explaining the construction; the learner sees only
opaque symbols. No sound is used to generate their sequence. Endpoint trimming
leaves four alternating roles, giving3switches versus2expected from the exact
bag: excess1/3per adjacency. This analytic specificity challenge was known BEFORE
scoring and was not presented as an unexpected manuscript discovery.

The second writer allows address paths and value-digit strings of length1or2
independently. It retains field widths and repeated symbols. Role changes still
separate the three commands; every field is decoded exactly. This avoids relying
only on perfect alternation. Both corpora have8192distinct training and8192distinct
test records and were generated without native fitting or pronunciation input.
The validator reconstructs all32768procedural messages, including their widths.

Neither artificial writer is asserted to fit Voynich's full word lengths,
frequencies, images or other constraints. They refute phonetic specificity of
THISstructural gate, not the possibility of a richer discriminating hypothesis.
The shuffled control instead destroys order while keeping each selected CMU
record's unit bag and length; it is not a meaningful alternative writing system.

## What was learned and what was not optimized

The signed-cut objective is unchanged mathematically from1264: observed cross-
class adjacency minus expectation under each interior's own symbol bag. Exact
rational pair weights are aggregated and rounded at10^-6, ties-to-even. The old
22-vertex exhaustive executable was NOTextended or invoked. A preregistered
39-symbol heuristic uses64deterministic starts and at most500improving single-
vertex moves per start. No sound labels or class-size constraints enter the fit.

The selected cuts equal the known-label TRAINscore for CMU and both procedural
sources.37,37and39of64starts respectively reach the best retained score; there
are two distinct terminal masks per source. The shuffled source has46terminal
masks and5starts at its best retained score. All final states are locally optimal;
no iteration limit is hit. GLOBALoptimality is not claimed. Correct label recovery
is directly measurable regardless of whether a better unvisited cut exists.

The learned labels are frozen for the8192TESTrecords. On CMU they predict all
phone classes at inner ANDouter word positions correctly. Perfect nucleus counts
therefore follow automatically. The truth labels are never used to choose the
winning start; orientation alone is resolved on TRAINtruth, with a fixed tie rule.
For shuffled data the learned cut exceeds the true-class training score but does
not generalize, illustrating why a fitted objective score alone is insufficient.

## Research decision and relation to earlier work

Retain this as a successful KNOWNPHONECLASSrecovery control and a failed ORIGIN
specificity test. The three-way separation matters: an algorithm can recover
sound classes on sound data, recover procedural roles on procedural data, and
fail on destroyed-order data, without identifying which meaningful process
produced an unknown text. A stronger score is not automatically stronger evidence
of speech. No threshold or extra feature is introduced after these outcomes.

1346used historical written LETTERS and different source collections. This is
not a paired spelling-versus-phoneme intervention on the same texts, so it does
not prove that its earlier failure was caused solely by ch or any specific
segmentation. Its exact recovery counterexamples remain.1264/1265native structural
positives,1278's original predictive failure and the old Q20P001phonotactic family
failure remain unchanged. Existing ASJPcontrols remain available but small; they
were not rerun and their native target rows were not accessed.

The control gives no Voynich vowel, consonant, source language, word or syllable
boundary. Correct native units and an independently justified interpretation
remain open. No native experiment, changed reading, image, raw TSV, f84/f84r,
f116v/f1rbody or reserved material was used. External access was limited to the
official pronunciation source, not public Voynich solution approaches.

## Reproduction and validation

Protocol, both programs and sources were hash-locked before packet selection or
scores. A prospective agent review fixed orientation ties, capped-search handling
and unseen-unit accounting before execution. A separate validator reconstructs
the dictionary selection and class table, both disjoint source splits, control
inverses, shuffled records, all256final optimizer scores/local optima, all32768
held event scores and recovery metrics;25small exact-bag fixtures pass.

The validator does not independently replay procedural random-number generation
or every optimization trajectory. It checks the stated final records, inverse,
scores and local optima. Deterministic full-pipeline reproduction is a separate
check, not another independent manuscript observation. Arithmetic verification
cannot turn a single contemporary pronunciation resource into universal phonology.

Run src/run.py then src/validate.py with Python3 and NumPy; exact commands, input
hashes, outputs and retained upstream license are in experiment.json. No new
optimizer, source roster, native decoder or phonetic claim is selected here.
