# GDT670 passage selection memo

## Selection

The twenty passages were selected from the 28 source-frontier rows in GDT669 `NEWLY_EXPOSED_ONE_HOLE_LINES.tsv`. Every selected `locus` and `zl3b_line` is copied from that source. The set contains all twelve mandatory loci, plus examples for `ytey`, `cphy`, the SHK family (`shkaiin`), the KCH family (`kchaiin`), and four contrasting fraction/process cards (`ycthar`, `oeor`, `choeky`, `otodar`).

## Passage policy

- Final GDT670 `CARD_SPECS.tsv` supplies every new target meaning.
- Inherited V46 token renderings supply all other visible tokens.
- Nominal states remain nominal. In particular `oschotshl`, `otair`, `otarain`, `rtain`, `ka`, `cphy`, `shkaiin`, and `kchaiin` do not acquire an implicit take, add, cool, or heat command.
- Explicit process cards remain executable. `qetal`, `ofalsheky`, `chedol`, `qoiiin`, `dcheodaiin`, `qokokchy`, `ytey`, `ycthar`, `choeky`, and `otodar` retain all written operations in visible order.
- Repeated tokens remain repeated rather than being compressed into “again” unless the German rendering still exposes the repeated state.
- No carrier liquid, ingredient identity, vessel, disease, patient, or cure is inferred.

## Coverage

The set spans compound materials, seed and root preparations, flower/raw-material cards, powder and composite preparations, fractions I–III, measures and doses, cold/hot/moist/dry stages, inherited references, measuring commands, finishing commands, and a multi-operation reheat sequence. This diversity is intended to expose direction errors during final synthesis rather than merely showcase the easiest nominal lists.
