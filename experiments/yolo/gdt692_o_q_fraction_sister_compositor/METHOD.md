# GDT692 method

## Scope and complete target

The source is GDT691 V64: 51 lines, 479 written token positions and 36 already
admitted pages. GDT692 opens no page and does not access f84 or f84r. It edits
the complete current occurrence set of these sixteen surfaces:

```text

qokar qotar qodar qokaiir
```

The set has 41 positions. Two GDT686 amount/head carries are also rerendered.
The source tables declare every composition, literal spelling, expected V64
gloss, V65 main, rival, occurrence count, action flag and bound span.

## Working alternatives

Four readings remain live:

1. independent product wholes such as *Auszug* or *Holzauszug*;
2. one shared `Fraktion I/II/III` sister lattice;
3. `Klasse/Stufe I/II/III`, with numbers denoting qualities or process states;
4. an extract-fraction hybrid activated only by local separation or product
   context.

V65 implements alternative 2 because it keeps one head across all sixteen
written sisters and preserves GDT654's repeated AR-versus-OR distinction:
selected mixture share or technical class versus divided portion. It retains
alternative 4 at six strong local wood contexts.
Alternative 3 is now the strongest historical challenger and is the next
same-deck renderer to test; V65 therefore does not pretend that *Fraktion* is
settled plaintext.

unconsumed visible tail. These are bounded compositions, never universal
character translations.

The builder requires the exact source count and V64 gloss for every target.
Thirty of the 41 surfaces change; eleven already agree with the selected

## Product and historical guards

The seven global V65 *Auszug* occurrences are exactly `qoteed`, `olord`,
`oteed`, `okeeodar`, `qokeod`, `kchod`, and `keeod`, all inherited draw-off or
product workflow cards. Six strong local *Holzauszug* readings remain explicit
rivals at f75r.3, f80r.17, f80v.35, f83v.12, f86v3.13 and f86v6.5.

Two near-period comparisons constrain the next interpretation. The late
fourteenth-/early fifteenth-century *Anonimo Mediano* calls a liquid *prima
colatura* only after straining it, putting it in a vessel and reserving it. In
Tadhg Ó Cuinn's 1415 *Materia Medica*, “hot in the first degree” is a quality
description, while boiling and straining are separate operations. Thus
I/II/III may be classes or degrees, and K/T may be humoral qualities rather
than physical temperature. The exact sources and implications are preserved
in `src/V65_HISTORICAL_HEAD_ANALOGUES.tsv`.

## Validation and ceiling

The validator checks all hashes and populations, all nine literal component
spells, sixteen surface replays, exact-whole extensions, 41 target positions,
32 revisions, two non-overlapping spans, six contextual rivals, two historical
comparators, term counts, 113 action ordinals, sealed scope and byte-exact
rebuild of every generated artifact.

V65 is an exploratory compositor, not recovered plaintext. It identifies no
language, phonetics, carrier liquid, extraction method, ingredient, disease,
patient or cure. *Ansatz*, *Fraktion*, *Klasse/Stufe* and *Auszug* are competing
German renderer heads.
