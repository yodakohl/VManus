## Why Auszug retreats but does not disappear

wholes. That produced fluent local German, but changed the head on only one
side of written contrasts such as `okar/qokar`, `otar/qotar` and `otar/otair`.
V65 therefore keeps one fraction head by default.

## Historical reality check

The working *Fraktion* head is internally neat but not yet historically best.
It was inherited for a concrete reason: GDT654 found repeated `AR/OR` sister
contrasts and used *Fraktion* for a selected mixture share or technical class,
against *Portion* for an actually divided amount. It never claimed that AR was
a distillate. The historical check nevertheless shows that the German word
*Fraktion* can misleadingly imply a separated product and must now compete
directly with the less loaded *Stoffklasse/Stufe* rendering.

In the late fourteenth-/early fifteenth-century [Anonimo
Mediano](https://corpus.atliteg.org/opera/anonimo-mediano/86), a *prima
colatura* follows grinding, dilution, cloth straining, separate collection and
reservation. That is much more process evidence than the bare AR/AIR/AIIR
ladder supplies.

Tadhg Ó Cuinn's 1415 [An Irish Materia
Medica](https://celt.ucc.ie/published/G600006.html) independently calls
wormwood hot in the first degree and dry in the second, then separately says
to boil and strain it. Thus numbered hot/cold values can be qualities rather
than temperatures or separated liquids.

Consequently the strongest next alternative is not a return to global
*Auszug*, but a renderer with `Klasse/Stufe I/II/III` and K/T as quality tags.
Only local separation, collection, amount binding or product use should
promote *Fraktion* or *Auszug*. GDT692 records this as an open competition,
not as a reason to discard the nine-piece spelling model.
