## Why these numbers constrain a writing model

For each nonempty fixed nucleus code select one working unit contained in it.
At mostKcodes (orKfamilies sharing a fixed unit per family) require at mostK
representatives. Any word containing a complete such code must contain a chosen
representative. Therefore any word missed by the set contains none of those
complete nucleus codes. Minimizing misses over ALLpossibleK-unit sets supplies
a necessary lower bound without assigning a sign or word meaning.

A hit does not prove a nucleus: it may be an ordinary consonant occurrence, and
longer actual codes may be absent even when the chosen representative occurs.
Consequently, the optimum is only an optimistic coverage limit. It does not
construct a vowel code, establish that the exceptions suffice, or locate actual
unwritten vowels. The per-word code-alignment, intact-carrier and group/word
assumptions from1042remain essential. Clitics or split phonological words may
instead break those premises; no such explanation was adopted.

Primary computation accumulates word-support weights by a subset-zeta transform.
For representative setR, missed weight isF(Sigma minusR), whereF(M)sums all word
supports contained inM. ExactKand atmostKhave the same minimum by monotonicity.
The independently written validator enumerates representative combinations with
occurrence/type bitsets and does not use the transform. Canonical ties use the
smallest numeric representative mask, never a linguistically favorable choice.

## Changed research decision

Do not turn1042's valid zero-exception theorem into a practical exclusion of all
few-vowel languages. A relatively small population of absent carriers is not
excluded by the necessary coverage bound. This is a correction of the possible
strength of that interpretation, not a reversal of the original theorem.

Conversely, these small bounds do not establish an abjad. No vowel omission,
consonant value or Semitic morphology was observed. Independent sources would
still need a complete writing account and enough independently justified
exceptions.1228/1230/1255/1239remain failures of their specific source/model
contracts; their tests were not rerun and no failed source was reopened.
Consonant-letter repeats cannot automatically be called spoken consonant clusters:
under vowel omission they may have intervening unwritten vowels.
