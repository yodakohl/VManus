# GDT664 method

## Question

Can the 140 distinct residual surfaces exposed by the V40 one-hole frontier be
given concrete recipe meanings that agree with shared stems, close every one of
the 146 source lines, and yield readable workshop passages rather than generic
process prose?

## Inputs

- GDT663 V40 dictionary, glossary, occurrence audit, coverage tables, complete
  passages, and 146 newly exposed one-hole lines.
- The same guarded 179-page ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b panel inherited from GDT663. `f1r`,
  `f84`, and `f84r` remain outside the material.
- `src/CARD_SPECS.tsv`: 140 exact-surface working defaults.
- `src/STEM_MODEL_SPECS.tsv`: structural roles, practical readings, scope, and
  explicit non-export rules.
- `src/INHERITED_OL_REVISION_SPECS.tsv`: nineteen practical O+L revisions at
  256 inherited positions.
- `src/INHERITED_SOL_REVISION_SPECS.tsv`: two practical seed-family repairs at
  four inherited positions.
- `src/MANUAL_PASSAGE_SPECS.tsv`: thirty complete passage readings.

## Method

1. Rebuild the safe source census through the guarded query interface and
   verify the inherited V40 artifacts before translating anything.
2. Require the fixed 140 surfaces to occur exactly 1,141 times and in the same
   order as the 146 V40 frontier rows.
3. Dispatch long cards by exact whitespace-delimited surface. Their component
   traces explain the working composition but do not silently translate
   arbitrary substrings.
4. Treat the short forms `o`, `ch`, `qok`, `m`, and `sa` occurrence by
   occurrence. An alternate-reader join wins only when a local token alignment
   supports the exact concatenation. Label and one-token lines keep a label or
   free default. Free `o=Ansatzwasser`, `m=Handvoll`, and terminal `-g=Gran` are
   deliberately exploratory concrete readings.
5. Render V41 over all 4,128 physical lines. Re-promote the inherited action
   renderers, apply only the listed practical O+L and SOL revisions, and keep
   their structural V40 tags separately visible.
6. Require every inherited one-hole line to become complete. Then compare the
   generated prose with thirty manually smoothed workshop translations and
   search both channels for open markers and generic work-language.
7. Replay the builder in a temporary directory and require byte-identical
   outputs.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all 140 cards and 1,141 target positions are present, all 146
frontier rows close, every accepted reader merge has local alternate-reader
support, the listed inherited revisions have exact dimensions, no unrelated
token gloss changes structurally, and the generated artifacts replay exactly.

The result is a complete, concrete V41 working translation layer and a stronger
compositional theory. It is not a confirmed plaintext, language, plant
identification, or historical glyph identification. Every English/German value
remains replaceable when a later page supplies a better reading.
