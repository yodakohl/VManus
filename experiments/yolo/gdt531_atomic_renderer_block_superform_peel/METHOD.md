# GDT531 method

## Question

Can an exact old superform with one additional visible block decide among the
three live analyses of `saiis`, while remaining selective across the complete
159-form current workshop edition?

## Inputs

- GDT407's 1,558 invariant old surface recipes and event contexts;
- GDT522's position-matched visible-edit/recipe-edit signature atlas;
- GDT529's finite candidate ranks;
- GDT530's complete revised 159-form working edition and executable intake.

No page beyond the already admitted thirty is used.

## Atomic block-peel construction

For every current surface, compare its GDT530 working recipe and GDT529
rank-one candidate with every invariant old surface. A route exists when:

1. an old surface is exactly two or three visible characters longer;
2. deleting one contiguous visible block yields the current surface exactly;
3. deleting one contiguous block of one to three atoms from the old recipe
   yields either the working recipe or the rank-one candidate exactly; and
4. the visible block, atom block, and their left/inner/right positions have an
   old GDT522 edit signature with support at least 3, conditional probability
   at least 0.5, and reliability at least 0.6.

This produces 29 licensed routes over 15 current surfaces. Twenty-eight support
a recipe that was already both the working selection and rank one. Exactly one
supports rank one against a different working selection:

```text
saiisol = S+A_ADDR+IIN+S+OL
- final ol / final OL
-------------------------
saiis   = S+A_ADDR+IIN+S
```

The old right-edge signature `ol/OL@RIGHT/RIGHT` occurs in 29 of 33 eligible
comparisons (`p=0.830985915`, reliability `0.935483871`). The exact old carrier
`saiisol` occurs once on f77r. No equally licensed block-peel route supports the
previous `S+IIN+S` analysis.

## Competing decompositions

The visible form also permits the exact-card tiling `saii|s`, yielding
`S+AIIN+S`, GDT529 rank two. It has real family support: old `saii=S+AIIN`
occurs once and `saiin=S+AIIN` occurs twenty times. It remains the runner-up,
because the selected analysis is licensed by one exact larger carrier of the
whole target plus a position-matched removal signature. The previous
`s|aii|s=S+IIN+S` analysis is rank three and has no corresponding full
superform carrier.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A block peel may revise a working recipe only when it produces the existing
GDT529 rank-one candidate, no licensed route supports the previous working
recipe, and the target has no contrary invariant exact old recipe. Under that
rule only `saiis` changes.

This changes the working edition, not the finite candidate generator. The
accumulated top-one count rises `153→154`, top-two rises `157→158`, and rank
sum falls `173→171`. Five working discrepancies remain.

The selected atoms retain their established workshop defaults:

```text
S      = WÄHLEN
A_ADDR = HIER
IIN    = STUFE
S      = WÄHLEN
```

The compact reading is “Wählen; hier die Stufe wählen.” This is an exploratory
functional reading, not confirmed plaintext or evidence that every visible
`ol` is a detachable suffix.
