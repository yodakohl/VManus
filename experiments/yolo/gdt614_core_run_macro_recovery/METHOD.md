# GDT614 method

## Question

Does the smallest repair licensed by GDT613 produce natural, fully observed
truth worlds in which all 34 primitive cards, eight paid merge cards, 56
default merges, and every implemented transition are identifiable and blindly
recoverable?

## Inputs

GDT608 supplies the directed 98-unit/64-merge tree. GDT609 supplies the
historical mixed-abbreviation role inventory. GDT613 supplies its frozen Latin
train/held blocks and exact infeasibility result. A post-hoc repair search made
after GDT613 supplied the 64/64 structural CORE_RUN assignment and candidate
deck; GDT614 freezes both before its scored construction. No Voynich target
sequence, target key, candidate plaintext, aligned translation, or f84/f84r
material is an input.

## Registered repair

The model identifier is
`HISTORICAL_MIXED_ABBREVIATION_FST_34_CORE_RUN_MACRO_V2`. It makes the exact
changes licensed by the post-hoc feasibility witness:

1. `connector:1` and `context:1` move from output length one to length two;
2. the primitive WHOLE card and four paid WHOLE cards become embeddable
   `MACRO_CORE` atoms;
3. one body may contain an adjacent run of core atoms.
4. unexercised V1 edge-connector, connector-only, and boundary-compound
   branches are absent; connectors are internal and layout nulls are bounded
   to the two word edges.

The role counts remain 18 literal, four syllabic, three prefix, three suffix,
two connector, two context, one macro, and one null. The eight paid cards
remain four short and four macro cards. The complete fixed deck and grammar
are machine-readable in `artifacts/REGISTERED_MODEL.json`.

## Construction and recovery

Stage A deterministically reconstructs the already observed design world, but
adds the missing hard part: exact labelled occurrences for all 64 named merge
nodes, the eight paid children, all 42 cards in train and held, and every one
of the 21 registered transitions. A single ordered, multiplicity-preserving,
span-bearing parse and top-level unit tiling is selected per plaintext type;
ambiguous potential support or a card bit-mask is not enough.

The ordinary-abbreviation interpretation and the nomenclator interpretation
remain separate. Here a macro is a side-licensed learned stem/syllable and NULL
is an edge layout event, not a free cipher null or an atomic whole-word code.
The historical evidence and exact licenses are recorded in
`HISTORICAL_MODEL_NOTE.md`.

Stage B creates two further worlds by deterministic within-role output
permutations and paid-location search. The generator may reject a seed, but it
may not change the deck, grammar, exposure thresholds, objective, or stop
rules. The plaintext partitions stay frozen.

Stage C evaluates truth against the complete registered local mutation set
before optimization. Only an oracle pass opens eight blind starts per world.
Recovery is scored on primitive role+output, paid merge location+type+output,
the complete labelled unit stream, and held plaintext.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Failure to build three worlds or to cover one registered transition stops as
generator infeasibility. Failure of any
truth parameter to beat its local alternatives on independent confirmation
stops as objective non-identifiability. Only three oracle passes followed by
at least 7/8 exact recoveries in every world pass GDT614.

A pass licenses a separately registered target attack. It does not itself
assign any Voynich form a sound, word, object, operation, plaintext, language,
or meaning.
