## Question

the descriptive, prescriptive, quantity/part or ambiguous pharmaceutical

or ingredients concretely remain?

## Inputs and scope

The builder reads only four already published inputs:

- GDT735's historical result and slot census;
- GDT739's complete 1,373-row radius-five window deck;
- GDT743's complete 202-position target renderer.

No page, image or transcription is added. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Boundary-clipped microfields

For each target the builder walks left and right for at most five cells.

- A new GDT738 target or a strict initial-head neighbor stops either side
  before that cell.
- On the left, a `CLOSE` cell belongs to the preceding field and is excluded.
- On the right, a `CLOSE` cell is included and ends the current field.
- A physical line edge completes a boundary. Reaching radius five without a
  boundary marks that side censored.

This yields 140 fully bounded and 62 radius-five-censored microfields.

## Historical channel dispatch

Rules execute in this order:

1. `PASS|PROCESS` plus an amount, ingredient, material, part or preparation:
   `PRESCRIPTIVE_RECIPE`;
2. `PASS|PROCESS` without a typed object: `PRESCRIPTIVE_PROCESS`;
3. hot/cold/dry/moist plus material/preparation/part:
   `DESCRIPTIVE_MATERIA`;
4. a quality without a typed carrier: `DESCRIPTIVE_QUALITY`;
5. amount or part: `QUANTITY_OR_PART`;
6. ingredient/material/preparation alone: `MATERIA_OR_INGREDIENT`;
7. otherwise `OPEN`.

`PASS` means a processing passage, not automatically an imperative. Opposed
quality pairs are printed as competing anchors instead of being collapsed.

## Recurrence and renderer tiers

The recurrence key is the exact target surface plus field channel. A complete
template needs occurrences on at least two fully bounded pages and at least two
different anchor signatures. This does not identify the target surface; it
licenses only an occurrence-level field card.

- `F3`: current occurrence fully bounded and its template recurs;
- `F2`: recurring fully bounded template, but the current occurrence is
  radius-censored;
- `F1`: only window-level recurrence or a single context;
- `F0`: no historical channel anchor.

F2/F3 cards enter the working renderer. F1 candidates remain visibly
provisional. Every card preserves the inherited GDT743 target rendering and
keeps the literal content slot open.
