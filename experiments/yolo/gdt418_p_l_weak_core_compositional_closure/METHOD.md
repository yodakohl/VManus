# GDT418 method

## Question

Do the two weakest exact-recipe portability cores still support their selected
values when every occurrence, position, companion, and shared recipe is read?

## Inputs

GDT416 supplies all 4,576 imperative clauses. GDT417 supplies the exact
cross-register recipe inventory. No new manuscript material is admitted.

## Method

Expand every P and L atom occurrence separately, retaining card position,
neighbors, coactions, arguments, relations, grade, close, owner, imperative
clause, and cross-register tier. Aggregate by exact recipe and by root. Compare
four concrete meanings for each root on register breadth, position, coaction,
complement, and owner neutrality. Publish explicit future composition cards.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The five-part score is an exploratory working comparison, not statistical
proof. `L=VERBINDUNG` never licenses a physical pipe or direction by itself;
`P=EINSETZEN` never supplies the object being inserted.
