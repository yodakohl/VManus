## Repetition boundary

Equality means the complete, verbatim `ivtff_group_raw` of neighboring source
groups. No substring, cleaned-fragment equality, lexical interpretation or
`@152;=d` substitution is licensed. Both appearances of a doublet survive, and
a raw triple yields two overlapping adjacent-pair records.

The independent bounded reconstruction finds67 exact within-record pairs and no
exact pair across eligible adjacent P-line boundaries. The67 are reader-specific
records, not67 independent manuscript observations or a global lexical count.
Within-record includes C records; it does not mean every pair lies on a straight
horizontal physical text line.
Opaque exact repetitions include `che@152;y` and `qokee@152;y`; their entities
remain part of the compared whole. This is not equivalence with chedy/qokeedy.

Within-record pairs retain their actual separator state. Confident space, uncertain
small space and drawing interruption are not interchangeable syntactic markers.
Cross-line candidates require two consecutive source P records of the same P
stream, with both records selected. They cannot jump an omitted P line, cross a
paragraph end/start or cross pages. Intervening L records remain separate records
and do not reset the enclosing P stream; their words are not inserted into it.
The cross-line search is restricted to the nine complete inherited P blocks;
other selected singleton records are not silently promoted to complete paragraphs.

Because the observed cross-line exact inventory is empty, matching an empty TSV
is not enough to test boundary logic. A synthetic source-only example must admit
an exact pair across an eligible P-line boundary; separate controls must exclude
the same spelling across a paragraph boundary or a missing P record. A label
between consecutive P records should remain preserved without suppressing a
legitimate cross-line candidate. None of these synthetic cases is manuscript
evidence or a grammatical sentence-boundary rule.

The independent validator passes seven synthetic boundary/identity checks and

Those synthetic checks exercise the independent validator's implementation,
not an imported runner API; the observed runner outputs are independently
