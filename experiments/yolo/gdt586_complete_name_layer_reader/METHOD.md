# GDT586 method

## Question

Can all 109 GDT585 owner-bound values be installed in one complete reader for
the current thirty pages while preserving the real separation between 793
running statements and 744 local cards, keeping the exact GDT582 rival channel,
and rereading all nineteen GDT585 groups in their richest licensed context?

## Inputs

GDT586 uses only already admitted, unsealed artifacts:

- the GDT582 15,889-slot ledger, 793-statement edition and 744-local-card
  edition;
- the GDT584 591 polished statements and 158 polished local cards;
- the GDT585 109 assignments, 89 name-bearing label readings and nineteen
  group readings.

No page, image, transcription, segmentation, surface, slot, event, statement,
record or bundle is added. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Exact architecture audit

Every GDT585 assignment is joined to GDT582 by exact `slot_id`. This yields:

- 107 `LOCAL_NAME` spans on 89 of the 744 local cards;
- two owner-bound running `LOCAL_X` slots, in G515-S046 and G515-S050.

The 89 local event IDs have no intersection with the event IDs of the 793
running statements. Their inherited guard explicitly forbids running-sentence
inheritance. A shared physical page is therefore used only for display and
never as an attachment edge.

## Reader construction

The running edition keeps the GDT584 paragraph for all 591 covered statements
and the GDT582 paragraph for the remaining 202. Only the two exact `LOCAL_X`
values are replaced occurrence-wise. The other 791 statements remain byte-for-
byte equal to their selected base.

The local edition keeps GDT584 as the reversible base for its 158 covered cards
and GDT582 for the remaining 586. On the 89 name-bearing cards, the
grammar-aware GDT585 label reading becomes the primary channel; the complete
selected GDT582/GDT584 card remains beside it as the legacy channel. The other
655 cards remain byte-for-byte equal to their selected base.

No raw-core or substring replacement is allowed. The eight visibly colliding
raw cores `cho`, `d`, `dy`, `e`, `ody`, `y`, `yd` and `yt` illustrate why an
exact slot join is necessary.

## Rival reconstruction

The old value is taken occurrence-wise from
`gdt582_15889_complete_default_ledger.tsv`, not only from GDT585's declared
legacy-alias field. That distinction restores sixty numbered star values for
which the GDT585 assignment had recorded `NONE`. GDT585 primary, exact GDT582
old value, strongest rival and composition atom remain separate columns.

## Nineteen-group context pass

All 21 group events are local. The maximum context is therefore their exact
G475 record and source order:

- C007 expands to all four cards and three bundles of G475-R078;
- C010 expands to both cards and bundles of G475-R085;
- C001 restores the source order `P1003-E0079|P1003-E0080`, while its reverse
  left-to-right order stays in the visual composition channel;
- C019 exposes the complete local stream span R129→R130→R131. Its two proposed
  endpoints remain separate records and owners with `P1008-E1408` between
  them, so the pair is visual only.

The other sixteen cases already exhaust their one- or two-card source records.
For every case the source-order grammar, full primary context, full legacy
context, composition hypothesis and best current rereading are published
separately.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass requires all 109 exact slot joins, 793 ordered statements, 744 ordered
local cards, 30 page displays, 19 context readings, the 591/202 and 158/586
base splits, exactly two changed statements and 89 changed local cards, 791 and
655 unchanged base texts, sixty restored star rivals, the C001 order repair,
the intervening C019 record and complete exclusion of sealed pages.

This is a complete exploratory working reader over fixed earlier units. It may
select GDT585 image-conditioned names and local composition readings while
retaining their rivals. It does not turn local cards into running sentences,
confirm a manuscript lexeme or substring stem, identify a species, substance,
illness, remedy, star or calendar value, or license a new page or surface.
