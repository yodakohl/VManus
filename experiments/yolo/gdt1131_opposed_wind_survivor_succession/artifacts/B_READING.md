## Connected reading

Suppose a wind of kind K is blowing from source P within the observer's horizon H while the Sun is at that source and dry material M is available. P and Q are adjacent sources in this same horizon. Turn attention to Q, where this case has not yet registered a wind, and then return to P and bind the wind there to its present state. As the Sun withdraws from P, that wind ceases. Subsequently the Sun approaches adjacent Q and raises dry material there; from this raised material a fresh wind is generated. Bind that new wind to its solar material origin and its current state. Its generation follows the preceding wind's cessation, and its source is adjacent to the preceding source. Recall the preceding wind and its solar withdrawal cause, then turn to the new source and bind the wind now blowing there. Bind that latest generated wind again: it is a different physical wind from the preceding one, has the solar material origin just described, and comes from the adjacent source. Thus this wind is the solar successor of the wind that ceased.

Continue with that same newly generated wind as the preceding wind for the next succession, retaining its identity, source, horizon and ordered time. Recall it and its written state: as the Sun withdraws from its source Q it ceases. Attention turns toward P. Subsequently the Sun approaches P and raises dry material there. A fresh wind is generated from this material, with a fresh identity distinct both from the just-ended wind at Q and from the original ended wind at P. Bind this new wind and its solar material origin. Its source P is adjacent to Q and its identity differs from the just-ended wind. Bind the latest generated wind again: its generation follows the preceding cessation; the preceding wind ceased because of solar withdrawal; the new wind is blowing, comes from the adjacent source and has the solar material origin. Recall this latest wind and its fresh generation: its identity is distinct and its generation is later than the preceding cessation. Recall the preceding wind, reaffirm that it ceased, and return attention to the latest generated wind. Thus this wind is the solar successor of the preceding wind. Bind the latest wind's current state once more, and certify that the previous wind's solar withdrawal and cessation preceded this generation before binding the latest wind again and reaffirming the succession.
