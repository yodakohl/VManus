## Scope

targets are the four recurrent attached-terminal exact cards with 12/10/8/8
events. Other terminals may be used only as context or counterexamples.

## Required decisions

The selection must decide whether the four families are best treated as:

1. punctuation-like closure variants;
2. exact categorical answers/status values;
3. lexical actions/results/products;
4. route/station selectors;
5. inherited/ditto values;
6. a mixture with an explicit rule.

It must state what a one-card committed field contributes, whether terminal
identity survives outside one page/stencil, and where semantic content resides
relative to COMMIT. One architecture must win; a visually or historically
attractive interpretation may not hide incompatible occurrences.
