# V9 adversarial terminals: a workshop cadence/model-sheet hypothesis

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: **independent speculative candidate, not a GDT result and not a
translation**. This candidate was prepared from the frozen V9 context and a
guarded GDT327 slice of only `f10r`, `f11r`, `f55v`, `f56r`, `f81v`, `f82r`
and `f83r`. The circle pages have no GDT327 events. No `f84` or `f84r` material
was accessed.

## Decision

The strongest adversarial reading is a **mixture**:

```text
NONTERMINAL SEQUENCE = opaque copied content, cipher, label, or exercise matter
TOP TERMINAL FAMILY  = one of four taught graphic cadence exemplars
DY                    = the shared completion stroke/construction
ONE-CARD FIELD        = an isolated complete cadence exemplar
```

Thus the four exact identities are preserved, but as four **graphetic model
classes**, not four answers, medical products, actions, or route values. The
closest controlled paraphrase is:

```text
[copy the local model sequence] — FINISH IN CADENCE alpha/beta/gamma/delta
```

A terminal-only field says `COPY CADENCE beta AS A COMPLETE MODEL`, not
`[silent medical question] = beta`. This is not mere punctuation: cadence
identity carries a real workshop choice (ductus/model family), while the
shared attached close realizes completion. It is also not random filler. It
is a controlled practice or pattern sheet in which copyable sequences and
their permissible endings are rehearsed inside short ruled cells.

This wins among the adversarial mechanisms because it explains, in one rule,
both ordinary field-final copies and otherwise troublesome one-card fields.
It also predicts the conspicuous wrapper locking without assigning four
unowned meanings.

## Terminal-only fields and the f82r.27 run

Sixteen of these 38 high-frequency events are complete one-card fields:
alpha 3/12, beta 5/10, gamma 5/8, and delta 3/8. The field-length ranges are
also broad: alpha closes lengths 1–6; beta 1–4; gamma 1, 2 and 4; delta 1–4.
This is the decisive reason not to call the four forms ordinary lexical
sentence-final words. A one-word imperative or result is possible, but sixteen
such cells demand a recurrent genre convention.

The current itemized-form model supplies that convention by positing a silent
question/slot. The adversarial model supplies it more economically: a short
cell can display a cadence by itself, while a longer cell displays that same
cadence attached to a practice string.

`f82r.27` is the sharpest case. Its seven consecutive committed fields have
card lengths `1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1` and exact shape:

```text
[65df3c…] | [98bdc4… + 78b3b3…] | [beta] | [7f68f6…] |
[ff1783…] | [beta] | [f2af63…]
```

Six of seven fields are terminal-only; beta repeats at cells 3 and 6. A
medical checklist must infer seven invisible prompts, six invisible operands,
and two coincident identical answers. A model-sheet reading requires only an
ordered cadence palette with one two-card demonstration and a deliberate
repeat of beta. Its controlled alternate translation is therefore:

> Copy cadence A; copy head B and finish C; copy beta alone; copy cadence D;
> copy cadence E; repeat beta alone; copy cadence F.

No letter name, sound, or ordinary-language wording is implied. The repeated
beta is a rehearsal/checkpoint, not DITTO: a ditto mark would inherit the
previous value, whereas the immediately previous cell is E, not beta.

## Why the other rivals lose

| mechanism | attraction | failure on this census |
|---|---|---|
| punctuation-like variants | all four are terminal and share DY | punctuation alone contributes nothing to 16 one-card fields; it also underuses exact identity |
| categorical answers/status values | naturally fills a one-card checklist cell | the questions are never observed; the four values roam across field ordinals, and the inference is circular (`answer` from empty cell, `question` from presumed answer) |
| lexical actions/results/products | medieval recipes often use recurrent technical words | all four are Biological-only, exact terminal, and unusually often isolated; no action, product, or result is independently pictured |
| route/station selectors | Biological drawings make silent spatial arguments conceivable | no occurrence has an externally identified station, direction, or route, and identities recur across pages rather than staying local to one apparatus |
| inherited/ditto values | isolated cells and repetition invite ellipsis | four distinct families are too many for a generic ditto, and f82r.27's beta repeat skips a different intervening cell |
| cipher or nomenclator code values | whole-card opacity and exact recurrence are historically possible | codes normally encode content rather than being obligatorily terminal; this needs the same unobserved form slots as the status theory unless the page is itself a key/model sheet |
| inflectional/allographic word families | common attached closure could be a shared ending | GDT327 does not license morpheme segmentation, while wrapper locking is stronger than a four-way semantic contrast requires |
| random filler | explains lack of translation | fails the exact cross-page identities and the f81v.17/f82r.7 copied stencil |
| **controlled cadence/model-sheet copying** | preserves exact identity, wrapper habits, stencils, and isolated models | wins internally, but has major codicological and iconographic liabilities below |

Natural language remains possible upstream. The narrow claim is that these
four terminal families need not be lexical even if neighboring cards are.
Likewise a cipher remains possible upstream: an exercise sheet could train
enciphered groups and their endings. The four mechanisms are not collapsed.

## Historical comparators and limits

The historical case is functional, not genealogical:

- Medieval *probationes pennae* could consist of strokes or scribbles, but
  also words, complete phrases, and drawings. They were normally placed in
  margins or flyleaves, which supports rehearsed forms but cuts against a
  whole planned codex ([Universidad Complutense overview](https://www.ucm.es/quidestliber/probationes-pennae)).
- British Library Add MS 88887 preserves fourteen Gothic specimen alphabets,
  cadels, flourishes, borders, sample text, and later additions on parchment.
  The catalogue allows use as transmission models for assistants or as a
  customer sample book. Thus a costly, curated workshop pattern book is not
  an anachronism or contradiction in terms, although this witness is later
  than c. 1420 ([British Library catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-000179016)).
- The Göttingen Model Book, dated 1440–1460 and localized provisionally to
  Mainz, is explicitly an instruction for producing foliage ornament. It is
  close in period and proves that a compact workshop book could preserve
  executable visual procedures ([Göttingen digitization and catalogue](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/DE-611-HS-3572793)).
- Surviving medieval model books served as repertories of letters, figures,
  and ornamental motifs for reuse; sketches could also be compiled to build a
  visual vocabulary ([Metropolitan Museum, *Pen and Parchment*](https://blog.metmuseum.org/penandparchment/drawings-and-the-artistic-process/)).
- Cipher keys and nomenclatures are also historically available in the
  fifteenth century: early keys could contain alphabetic substitutions,
  logograms, homophones, nulls, and codebook entities. This establishes a
  viable rival mechanism, not a Voynich identification
  ([Somogyi on fourteenth- and fifteenth-century Italian codebooks](https://ojs.ppke.hu/verbum/article/view/405),
  [Megyesi et al. on historical nomenclatures](https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188404)).

These comparators do **not** exhibit a Voynich-like 12/10/8/8 cadence system,
the same plant/bathing/circle repertoire, or the same field grammar. No donor,
school, region, or plaintext follows from them.

## Direct challenge to the medical/itemized-form model

The medical reading currently gains most of its fluency from silent content:
the picture supplies an owner, the stencil supplies a question, and an isolated
terminal supplies an answer. None of those three links is independently owned
for these 38 events. Calling a terminal-only field a categorical answer and
then using it as evidence that a hidden categorical question existed is
circular.

The recurrent `1C | 3C | 1C | 4O` stencil does show copied form, but it does
not show medicine. The shared alpha ending in the three-card second field of
`f81v.17` and `f82r.7` is equally expected if an apprentice copied a graphic
model. Across the full census alpha is not restricted to that field or that
stencil. More generally, field ordinal is too mobile to support four fixed
medical questions, while page/register confinement is exactly what a local
exercise deck predicts.

The illustrations are the medical theory's strongest advantage, but they do
not decide what the adjacent strings do. Pattern and model books could preserve
figures, plants, letters, and ornaments for reuse. On the fixed pages alone,
the Biological drawings could be silent medical referents, drawing exercises,
or both. The adversarial explanation is therefore:

```text
illustrated workshop repertory
  + copied local graphic/text models
  + short ruled practice cells
  + four reusable cadence exemplars
```

It explains less iconographic intention than the medical model, but explains
the terminal evidence with fewer invisible semantic objects.

## Risky predictions and contradictions

The cadence hypothesis should lose if an independently annotated visual or
readable parallel maps alpha/beta/gamma/delta consistently to four states,
actions, products, or stations. It also predicts:

1. family choice will track wrapper/ductus, neighboring graphic structure,
   field length or copied line model better than field ordinal or pictured
   referent;
2. terminal-only runs will disproportionately contain rare cadence variants,
   repeats, and graded recombinations, rather than stable question columns;
3. alpha's che/t copies will be visually intelligible permitted variants of
   the sh-dominant model, not semantic exceptions;
4. similar stencils may preserve an ending even while nearly all preceding
   cards vary, because the ending is the practiced cadence;
5. no consistent medical English gloss will survive all 38 occurrences
   without multiplying silent prompts.

Its strongest contradictions are serious:

- classical pen trials are usually marginal or on flyleaves, not hundreds of
  carefully integrated parchment lines;
- known pattern books generally display recognizable alphabets, ornaments or
  figures, whereas the Voynich strings look like sustained text;
- the images are costly and thematically organized; a pure handwriting
  exercise does not explain why plant, Biological and circle repertoires were
  assembled;
- exact cross-page formulas and apparently coherent long fields could reflect
  real language or code rather than practice matter;
- no independent mark identifies an exemplar, correction, lesson, master
  copy, or trainee hand on these pages.

Accordingly this is the **strongest rival**, not the new default. Relative to
the V9 required choices it selects option 6, an explicit mixture: semantic or
coded content may occur before the close, but the four recurrent terminal
identities are graphic cadence values and COMMIT itself is structural. The
current medical/itemized-form interpretation remains plausible only if it can
acquire an external slot or referent that the cadence model cannot mimic.
