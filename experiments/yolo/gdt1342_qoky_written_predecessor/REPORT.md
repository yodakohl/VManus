## What was and was not tested

Whole page scope is a new, deliberately permissive version of the earlier
unexecuted paragraph proposal. Full spellings remain optional: qokeedy qokeedy
is allowed. Thus the failure is not GDT1274's compulsory-saving contradiction.
GDT1031 tested probabilistic shortening of six other stipulated groups, including
qoky/r, and stopped for capacity; GDT1153 tested a different continuation rule.
None previously executed this exact census. The old correction that qoky had
not yet been tested remains historically correct; this experiment now tests
and rejects the explicitly declared whole-page prerequisite.

Existence does not test whether the nearest producer is selected, what any form
means, whether the four forms share a meaning, or whether a process executes.
A positive case is particularly weak with common candidate producers. No
frequency advantage is scored. No assumption of a verb or syntactic argument
is supplied by qokedy adjacency. GDT1146/1163 still reject only their fixed
constructions; the recent K(A)/K(B) scaffolds remain unconfirmed attachment.

A missing antecedent is scored only when the consumer seams are clear and
**every earlier P group** is lowercase literal text with clear outer seams.
If any earlier group is uncertain and no reliable producer exists, the case is
SOURCE_UNCERTAIN. A reliable earlier producer establishes necessary availability
even if other groups are unclear. Complete line counts and a second guarded
extraction retain every P group from the fixed179-selector admission.
This is source completeness under the frozen transcription, not proof that all
original handwriting is correctly read or that no physically lost passage exists.
No source repair, forward reference, different producer family, initial value
or prior-page import was added after seeing the result.
