## Fixed rule and source

Every exact standalone qoky in P prose optionally replaces an occurrence of
one of qokey, qokeey, qokedy, qokeedy written strictly earlier in P prose on the
same page selector. Full spellings remain freely optional even after repetition.
No input is preloaded; no lookahead, implicit producer, alias, caption producer,
cross-page import or normalization. Whole P-page scope is newly declared and
more permissive than the earlier discussed paragraph scope. It is not an
already tested contract. Latest versus any prior is deliberately NOT tested:
existence is a necessary condition for either resolver.

Use the fixed 179-selector GDT631 PAGE_ALLOWLIST, already used by words profile,
and original source_separator_transcription.tsv through selector-first
vmanus-exp query-tsv with explicit allow values and columns. The locked allowlist
must exclude f1r, f84*, f116v; f84 and f84r remain sealed. No native image,
reserve access, new historical source or reconstructed group. Readings remain
separate. All source P lines and raw groups are preserved, with original row and
group indices, seams, Currier/section metadata and complete-line counts.
Historic exposure is not a new holdout. f83r is motivating development, all
other admitted pages exposed comparison; report both without pooling readers.

## Source-aware decision

Clear seams are DEFINITE_SPACE, LINE_START, LINE_END. A reliable group has only
lowercase ASCII letters and both seams clear. All other raw spellings/seams
remain recorded as uncertain, never silently removed.

For each exact qoky:

1. Unclear consumer seams: CONSUMER_UNCERTAIN, not a counterexample.
2. At least one earlier reliable exact family group: AVAILABLE, necessary only.
3. No such producer, and any earlier P group is unreliable: SOURCE_UNCERTAIN.
4. Otherwise: MISSING_PREDECESSOR, a counterexample to the fixed literal rule.

The full preceding P prefix is checked, not just the previous line or selected
family forms. Complete original line groups must be present. Uncertain prefixes
cannot prove absence. A source-complete prefix is complete under the frozen
transcription, not independent palaeographic certification. A physically missing
passage outside that representation is not silently supplied.

Report every case, producer IDs, uncertain-prefix IDs, and full consumer lines.
If any strict additional-page case is MISSING_PREDECESSOR, the fixed rule fails
in that reading. Otherwise report NECESSARY_ONLY or NO_ASSESSABLE_CAPACITY.
Unresolved cases prevent universal confirmation. Development failures likewise
remain explicit. No significance, semantic edge score or word meaning is claimed;
GDT388 semantic gates are not bypassed by this prerequisite census.

## Assumptions and scope of inference

Opaque occurrence-reference hypothesis only; this does not assign qoky's English
meaning, identify four forms as synonyms, select a source language, or establish
the source script's word boundaries. Names or type identifiers could be shortened
without executing anything. Failure does not reject all abbreviation or anaphora,
nor IDEA1009-1011. Earlier failed constructions remain unchanged. Validator must
independently enumerate full-prefix conditions and compare a second guarded read.
