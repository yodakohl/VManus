# GDT763 method

## Question

Does the three-occurrence `sho|sheo pcheey` construction behave most like a
field-bearing dry/Form-II record head, a source or content complement, a
parallel bound field, or a post-moist result? In parallel, do the sixteen
known `ol` amount positions support a content head, patient, context field,
source, unit, or specific oil reading?

## Inputs and guard

GDT763 uses the guarded context loader inherited through GDT762 and the same
already admitted cache. It opens no new page, image, or transcription. `f84`
and `f84r` remain forbidden. The run consumes the GDT736 training head grid,
GDT737 held head forms, GDT760 amount and content atlases, GDT762 state-side
and `ol` contacts, and the GDT735/GDT755 historical role banks.

The 94 GDT762 H1 semantic repairs and the combined GDT754/GDT737/GDT738
quarantine take precedence. Non-reader-exact slots are never assigned a
semantic role. A local display guard also suppresses residual literal
powder/seed/root/wood prose when an older GDT734 card is shown in an H1
neighborhood; this display guard does not create a replacement meaning.

## H1 sequence census

The GDT736 and GDT737 registries yield 369 exact head+body surfaces, including
89 H1 forms. Every reader-exact H1 occurrence is enumerated with three left
and five right slots. Each slot records reader status, quarantine status,
formal axes, a separately labelled working role, semantic source, and exact
whole value. Roles may overlap.

The primary geometry-matched comparison is reader-exact H1 on a paragraph
start line in medial position. It contains 52 occurrences: the three
`pcheey` targets and 49 other H1 controls. Result evidence is searched
symmetrically within three slots for `PROCESS`, `PASS`, or `CLOSE`, and also
through line-final and paragraph-end placement.

The gapped construction `TARGET X daiin` is counted only when all three local
tokens are reader-exact. Its ordinary control comprises non-H1,
non-quarantined surfaces with exactly three occurrences on three admitted
pages, all medial and all on paragraph-start lines.

## State side to record class

For every H1-H4 occurrence, the run records whether its immediate exact left
neighbor is one of GDT762's eleven working dry-side or moist-side wholes. The
rates use the 1,158 dry and 788 moist right-neighbor opportunities already
enumerated in GDT762. Dry/moist are working whole-side labels, not recovered
words.

## `ol` slot dispatch

The sixteen fixed GDT762 amount positions are classified in this order:

1. `ol ... amount ... ol` is bilateral and remains unresolved.
2. A nonpreferred right-side `ol` is a context/second field only when the
   preferred left candidate is reader-exact.
3. An otherwise licensed `ol` is a patient when the complete amount span is
   immediately followed by a reader-exact `PROCESS_CLOSE` field.
4. Remaining licensed positions, plus nonpreferred positions whose preferred
   competitor is nonexact, are head candidates.

No source reading is selected without an exact source, vessel, or relation
marker. The placement profile is compared with all 45 GDT760 content edges
and with eighteen exact amount/position/side-matched content controls.

## Historical role comparison

Eight already registered period examples distinguish compact
head+quality+degree entries, explicitly marked source/product relations,
process-to-result sequences, named oil, named powder, and dry-process
commands. They calibrate the kind of field pattern expected; they receive
zero EVA spelling and zero target-assignment credit.

## Claim ceiling

GDT763 may revise complete-whole working roles and exact-span renderers for
`pcheey` and `ol`. It does not identify a plaintext word, ingredient,
substance, oil, water, wine, unit, syntax relation, component value, language,
sound, or glyph value.
