## Frozen observations and decision

For each of twelve historical candidate loci, record one of: same-layer bounded wind/month-like written sector, distinct-layer written item with a unique authorial leader to a single sector, distinct-layer item without such a leader, missing/not visible, or ambiguous. A star, angular proximity, color, symmetry or an analyst-drawn overlay does not count as an authorial leader. Record all visible named cardinal marks, asymmetric sun event, arrow/start mark, and whether it unambiguously fixes the rotation and handedness **without reading Voynich text**. Count any extra or missing written sectors and all layer changes, not just best matches.

`BOTH_GATES_VISIBLE` requires all twelve candidate items to have individually unique same-layer/leader ownership **and** an independent authorial orientation that maps the full twelve-cycle to Trier's east/top, south/right, west/bottom, north/left structure. Partial ownership or an unfixed rotation is `NO_CURRENT_LEXICAL_CAPACITY`; uncertain marks never become positives. Even a positive would authorize only a later separately registered text test with source-owned full predictions, rival month-only and non-wind accounts, and physical-leaf capacity disclosure. There is no p-value or translated word from this image pass.
