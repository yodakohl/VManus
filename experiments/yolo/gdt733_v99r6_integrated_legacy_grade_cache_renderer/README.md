GDT733 replaces the split V48/V99/GDT732 views with one precedence-ordered
renderer over the existing 179-page, f84/f84r-free cache. It retains all
32,339 token cells and emits 32,319 practical units after applying sixteen
licensed two-token spans once and attaching four punctuation cells
structurally.

cells and 6,866 further unconditional V99R4 defaults. It also speaks the 4,692
remaining occurrence-local legacy grade cells without turning their active
surface readings into global word meanings. The older analytical phrases
`am Anfang/in der Mitte/am Ende des Grades` disappear from the integrated
practical output. Indexed expressions such as `Grad II`, `Menge III` and
`Mittelstufe` deliberately remain and are counted separately.
