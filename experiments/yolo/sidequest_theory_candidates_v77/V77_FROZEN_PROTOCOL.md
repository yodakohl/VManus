# V77 frozen protocol — exact-card dictionary attestation audit

Frozen: 2026-08-22, before the V77 source corpus or card decisions were
inspected.

## Scope

V77 uses exactly the fixed ten-page V71–V76 packet. It re-audits the 14
reusable exact control-card identities and a bounded, frequency-selected set of
the most recurrent remaining exact cards across every occurrence. It adds no
page, surface form, stem, sound, language or PAGE_HOST meaning. `f84` and
`f84r` remain sealed.

## Source-first order

Each role must perform these operations in order:

1. Select genuine surviving cipher keys, nomenclators or coded sign lists
   dated 1370–1450 without consulting the desired V60/V69 glosses.
2. Transcribe or quote the entries actually visible in a facsimile or a
   scholarly documentary edition, including the code/sign where preserved.
3. Freeze the resulting historical entry inventory and its source hashes or
   stable URLs.
4. Only then reveal the legacy card handles and compare their minimal proposed
   lexical/function category with the independently enumerated entries.
5. Audit every occurrence of each retained card in Herbal and Biological
   records. A proposed value must remain invariant or be withdrawn.

Ordinary recipes, Herbals, dictionaries, modern summaries, similar-looking
abbreviations and general historical plausibility cannot attest a dictionary
word.

## Required attestation row

Every admitted word must contain all of:

- anonymous exact tuple/card ID;
- minimal proposed editorial gloss;
- exact source-language entry;
- historical key identity or archive shelfmark;
- date or dated correspondence;
- facsimile/edition folio, page or entry location;
- codebook type;
- opaque code or sign, if preserved;
- full citation and stable source locator;
- confidence;
- statement that this licenses only historical dictionary granularity and
  does not identify the Voynich form.

Missing any required element forces `EXEMPLAR_VALUE_UNKNOWN` or
`FORMAL_LABEL_NOT_WORD`.

## Four independent roles

- R1 workshop master: teachability and occurrence invariance of the bounded
  exact-card deck.
- R2 historical medical/Herbal scribe: primary-source inventory of period
  codebooks and strict documentary attestation.
- R3 technical register writer: atomicity, cross-record invariance and a
  machine-checkable no-leakage decision table.
- R4 chancery corrector: independent source verification, false-friend audit
  and final adjudication.

The four roles write separate prefixed artifacts and may not read sibling V77
outputs before freezing their decisions.

## Decision ceiling

Even a surviving row establishes only that a meaning category existed in a
contemporary codebook and remains a coherent creative default across these ten
pages. It does not establish a Voynich word, plaintext, sound, language or
historical relationship to that codebook.

V77 must publish the complete bounded candidate list, every occurrence, the
source-first entry inventory, all withdrawals, and a validator. V78 may use
only V77-surviving attested handles; all other values must be visibly bracketed
master-exemplar expansions or formal nonword labels.
