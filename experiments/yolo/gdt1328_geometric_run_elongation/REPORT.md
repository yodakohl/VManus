## What the writer predicts

The complete formal writer takes source words without adjacent e/e or i/i and
independently stretches each such source slot. After each repeated unit the same
cell-specific continuation probability q decides whether another follows. Its
reader collapses maximal e/i runs; other units and word boundaries are unchanged.
This is an invented restricted symbol channel, not a full natural-language source
or historical handwriting reconstruction. Every source word and q table is paid.

Given n occurrences of the SAME collapsed form/slot on the SAME exact page and
their total length S, the geometric model predicts a uniform positive composition
of S into n labeled lengths. No continuation probability needs to be estimated.
Null draws preserve those totals but may exceed the largest observed run; simply
shuffling existing lengths would fail to test the proposed law.

Dispersion D=sum L*(L-1) has exact conditional expectation2*S*(S-n)/(n+1).
The score is centered on that expectation and divided by the possible D range;
cells are averaged within physical leaf, then leaves equally. Negative scores
mean lengths are more even than this conditional null predicts. The panels
include cells with identical observed lengths when their null remains mobile.
This is necessary: (2,2) must be compared with (1,3) and (3,1), not discarded.

## Decision and limits

Stop the independent memoryless-elongation account under the specified admission,
source-slot and boundary assumptions. A future constructive writer must account
for the stable lengths, not treat extra e/i copies as this particular free noise.
This does NOT reject every stochastic spelling rule, optional arch repetition,
nongeometric length choice, whole-form spelling memory, context-sensitive motor
habit or genuine multi-unit construction. It does not show that length changes
meaning, that e/i are vowels, or that a collapsed source form is one native word.
The other directed word-shape structure remains unexplained by this channel.

Selection is a real caveat: strict transcription/interior-gap admission must not
censor lengths within a cell. Null redraws change individual word/line lengths
and do not reconstruct ink geometry or the physical line-breaking process.
Unmodeled line geometry, serial dependence, transcription or selection can
violate the null. Page/word-specific q alone cannot explain the measured departure.
No new image check or corrected transcription is claimed. All source data were
previously exposed; no reserved confirmation was consumed.

GDT1170's whole-word repetition result, GDT1320's optional fusion result and
GDT1310's local allograph limits remain unchanged. Old grade interpretations
633/558/646 are frozen historical guesses, not supported meanings reused here.
No new semantic relation packet was scored. No automatic truncated distribution,
new conditioning, source language, glyph map or decoder follows this result.
