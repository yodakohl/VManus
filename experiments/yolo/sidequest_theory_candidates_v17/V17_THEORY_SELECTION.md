## Best current process reading

The recurrent Biological deck now reads as a recognizable wet-treatment
workflow rather than an inventory of anonymous values:

```text
take the next measured portion
→ add it to the vessel
→ mix two portions / stir evenly
→ keep lukewarm or heat once
→ bathe or immerse
→ leave standing / settle
→ strain through cloth
→ route through connected channels
→ rinse or apply at the pictured place
→ finish the local instruction
```

Herbal uses a compatible prose layer: prepared decoction, fresh preparation,
same batch, stated measure, present portion and continuation to the following
detail. This makes the shared cards ordinary recipe language while allowing
the many rare Herbal cards to carry plant-specific names and properties.

## Remaining hard choices

Concrete rivals remain recorded rather than erased:

- `OKEEY/QOKEEY`: selected **keep lukewarm**; alternatives are mix thoroughly
  and add warmed water.
- `LCHEDY`: selected **leave standing in the lower vessel**; drain and cool to
  ordinary setting remain plausible.
- `DCHEDY/SCHEDY/TCHEDY`: selected **finish this treatment**; strain and wash
  through once remain live.
- `OLCHEDY/QOLCHEDY`: selected **reserve mixed liquid**; drawing off the clear
  liquor is the main rival.
- `CHAR/DAR/SAR`: selected **from the same batch**; sequence/continue remains
  the main rival.
- f56r `CHO/SHO`: selected **thereafter take the following detail**; plant-topic
  resumption and flowering tops remain concrete alternatives.

These ambiguities are now localized. They do not justify returning the rest of
the deck to formal or semantic blanks.
