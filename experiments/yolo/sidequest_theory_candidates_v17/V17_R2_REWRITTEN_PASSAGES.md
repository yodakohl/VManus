## f10r — complete rewritten article

### Card-for-card reading

- **f10r.2:** The pictured simple bears the local name 65F32. Keep it in a
  covered jar; repeat the foregoing preparation until it is evenly joined and
  pounded to powder. Of the reddish stem make a small drink for stomach pain;
  apply it to the affected place in the stated measure, and keep the root.
- **f10r.5:** Use the freshly pounded preparation while warm, together with the
  foregoing preparation, when it is ready.
- **f10r.6:** It is found in [the pictured habitat]. When ready, take the
  prepared liquor—the expressed juice—boil it gently, and use this present
  portion in the stated measure on this treated member.
- **f10r.8:** Gather before flowering. Of the prepared liquor take a handful;
  add the foregoing batch likewise in the stated measure, and repeat the
  foregoing preparation.
- **f10r.9:** Its flower appears [as drawn]. Preserve the expressed bitter
  liquor in oil and use this portion on the present member.

### Fluent workshop copy

> Of the pictured simple, keep the root in a covered jar. Pound the reddish
> stem to an even powder. Give a small drink for the stomach and apply the same
> preparation outwardly in the stated amount. Use the fresh pounded matter
> warm. Before flowering gather enough for a handful; press out the bitter
> juice, boil it gently with the foregoing batch, and preserve the remainder
> in oil.

The physical lines are reflowed clauses, not seven independent recipes. The
repeated `Y` card is read as repeated deictic selection of the portion/member
currently being handled.

## f82r — complete rewritten application sequence

### Card-for-card reading

- **f82r.2:** Strain the application and set it aside. Keep the combined
  mixture covered, add the measured ingredient to this portion, bathe with the
  tempered herbal liquor, mix both portions with warm water at the second
  opening, and proceed to the next basin.
- **f82r.3:** Mix thoroughly, strain clear, continue at the second conduit,
  pass it through a cloth and through the joined conduits, then take up the
  next portion.
- **f82r.4:** Take the next portion under the same setting in the broad vessel.
  Draw it off, add the prepared oil and warm water, pour it over the local
  place, and apply it.
- **f82r.7:** Add clean water and keep it for the stated time over the local
  place. Give the ordinary lukewarm bath, keep it warm, mix thoroughly, apply
  at the first opening, and continue until the liquor runs clear.
- **f82r.19:** Add the measured ingredient, repeat the foregoing preparation,
  add the ingredient again, bathe with the tempered herbal liquor, and draw
  off the clear liquid.
- **f82r.23:** When the liquor runs clear, keep the same setting. Mix
  thoroughly; keep the immersed part in it for the stated time. Immerse the
  present member fully, let the liquor drain at the lower outlet, and close the
  outlet.
- **f82r.26:** Begin rinsing with the tempered herbal liquor at the affected
  place. Add cool water and prepared oil in equal stated portions; take the
  next portion with warm water.
- **f82r.27:** Draw it off. Pour in warmed water at the second opening. Bathe
  with the tempered herbal liquor, bind the treated place after the stated
  draught/application, repeat the herbal bath, and finish with equal shares.

### Fluent workshop copy

> Strain the compounded liquor, cover it, and add the measured ingredient.
> Temper it with warm water and carry it through the cloth and the joined
> conduits into the next basin. Draw off one portion, add the prepared oil, and
> pour it over the affected place. Keep the member in an ordinary lukewarm bath
> for the stated time, mixing until even and rinsing until the outflow is
> clear. For the stronger repetition add the ingredient again and use the
> tempered herbal liquor. Finally drain by the lower outlet, rinse with cool
> water and oil in equal portions, refill at the second opening, bathe again,
> and bind the treated place.

This is a practical bath/irrigation sequence rather than a catalogue of
independent values. Its minimal recurring action deck is TAKE, ADD, MIX,
TEMPER, STRAIN, PASS-THROUGH, BATHE, APPLY, DRAIN and TIME/MEASURE.
