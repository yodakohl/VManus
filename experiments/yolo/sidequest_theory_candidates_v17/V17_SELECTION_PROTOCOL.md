# V17 — recurrent core-card semantic stress and rewrite protocol

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: maximally abductive ten-page sidequest. This is not a canonical GDT
experiment or a decipherment claim.

## Question

V16 supplied a meaning for every visible group. V17 asks which concrete
meanings should form the stable **common teaching deck** rather than merely a
plausible one-page story.

Analyze every exact prose card occurring at least three times on the ten fixed
pages. The frozen V16 census currently yields 30 such cards. Every occurrence,
including awkward occurrences and cross-register uses, must be read in full
local sequence.

## Four independent perspectives

Reuse R1–R4 exactly from `SIDEQUEST_FOUR_AGENT_BACKGROUNDS.md`. No agent may
read another V17 result before completing its own candidate.

## Fixed scope and seal

- Herbal: f10r, f11r, f55v, f56r;
- Biological: f81v, f82r, f83r;
- Astro pages remain translated by V16 but are not used to infer prose-card
  identity because they have no GDT327 tuple mapping;
- no new pages, substring mining, phonetics or language matching;
- use guarded f84-free source data only; never open f84 or f84r.

## Required work

For each recurrent exact card:

1. list every occurrence with at least two left and two right cards where
   available, its field/line boundary and current V16 reading;
2. propose exactly three concrete rival meanings before choosing, including
   the V16 incumbent;
3. insert each rival into every complete local field/line and score:
   - contextual grammatical fit;
   - cross-page semantic consistency;
   - Herbal/Bio portability where applicable;
   - historical source-phrase plausibility;
   - number of silent arguments or special senses required;
4. select one concrete default and at most one explicitly conditioned second
   sense;
5. rewrite every affected physical line and at least one whole Herbal article
   plus one whole Bio record using the selected deck.

Every card must keep a concrete meaning. A losing incumbent is replaced by a
better concrete phrase, never by `UNKNOWN`, `OPAQUE`, `CONTENT`, `PAYLOAD`,
`ITEM`, `VALUE`, `STATE` or a purely formal tag.

## Priority ambiguities

Resolve these disagreements explicitly:

- `OKEEY/QOKEEY`: gently warm versus mix thoroughly;
- `CHEDY`: mix until even versus generic action;
- `OKY/QOKY`: lesser portion versus apply/use;
- `OR`: prepared liquid versus source/from relation;
- `OKAIN/QOKAIN`: put it in versus add measured ingredient;
- `OKAL/QOKAL`: combine portions versus work in warm/clean water;
- `VAL-Q`, `VAL-QE`, `VAL-S`, `VAL-L`: concrete action/condition differences;
- `CHAR/DAR/SAR`: of the same versus then/continue;
- `O56`: thereafter versus recurring plant part or topic resumption;
- `CKHY`: connected channels versus immersion/configuration.

Also test whether `AIIN`, `Y`, `L/O`, `qokaiin`, and `CTHY` retain their V16
defaults when embedded in the newly rewritten deck.

## Deliverables per perspective

- one candidate report;
- `V17_Rx_RECURRENT_CARD_DECISIONS.tsv` with all 30 cards, three rivals,
  scores, selected meaning, confidence and revision status;
- `V17_Rx_ALL_OCCURRENCE_READINGS.tsv` covering every occurrence of the 30
  cards and its rewritten local sequence;
- `V17_Rx_REWRITTEN_PASSAGES.md` containing at minimum complete f10r, complete
  f56r and complete f82r readings;
- explicit list of V16 glosses retained, improved or reversed.

## Selection rubric

| criterion | points |
|---|---:|
| every recurrent card and occurrence covered | 20 |
| one meaning works across all occurrences | 25 |
| resulting Herbal and Bio prose is coherent | 20 |
| historical source phrases are ordinary for c. 1420 | 15 |
| simple enough for a multi-scribe teaching deck | 10 |
| few special senses and silent repairs | 10 |

The final synthesis updates the V16 complete dictionary and regenerates all
776 meanings; no group may lose its default during revision.
