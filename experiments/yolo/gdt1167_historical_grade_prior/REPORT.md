## Research decision

Do not reuse the I–IV mapping as an established prior, and do not replace it with a frequency-selected II assignment. Any further numeric/degree reading must supply an independently motivated construction-level relation that distinguishes a grade from an amount or other property; a confirmed word is not required to draft that hypothesis. Reopening requires changed bound evidence, not another permutation search on these same counts. The complete annotation/result artifacts preserve both the weakening and what remains undecided.
