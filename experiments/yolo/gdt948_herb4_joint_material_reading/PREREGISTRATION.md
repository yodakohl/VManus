## Exploratory construction
1. Write one fixed whole-form dictionary for all four paragraphs. Full scope
   alignment is mandatory. Each form keeps its value and role. Unknown names may
   remain opaque named materials; that role itself is an assumption. Explicit
   unknowns are permitted and prevent a claim of complete translation.
2. Jointly specify clause spans and each operation's written participants,
   resulting material/state, identity relations and every supplied element.
   Distinguish grammatical support from guessed binding. Exact spelling alone
   does not prove same physical individual. No source line is automatically a
   sentence boundary. Hypothetical segmentation is recorded explicitly.
3. Write a complete descriptive catalogue rival using the same nominal and
   property vocabulary. Event descriptions do not assert temporal performance;
   their readings must be printed, not dismissed for making fewer process claims.
4. Enumerate all proposed event/binding consequences across all four paragraphs.
   Check each participant source, temporal availability, operation precondition
   and output/state assertion, including contradictions and missing information.
   Distinguish a required desired state from an achieved observed state.
5. Separate writer-supplied event semantics from constraints actually supplied by
   another source expression. Simultaneously constructing a coherent story is
   exploration, not successful held prediction. Opaque material renaming and
   operations with identical represented effects stay observationally equivalent.
6. Up to two documented draft revisions are permitted within this pass. Earlier
   versions and reasons remain visible; no repair may be counted as prospective
   success. A frozen-candidate evaluation lock precedes final mechanical audit.

## Deliverable and claim ceiling
Full candidate/dictionary/alignment table; four process and four descriptive
paragraph readings; complete participant/event trace; contradictions, unknowns,
singleton dependence, remaining semantic alternatives and a research decision.
A validator checks source preservation, consistent dictionary application,
complete cases and claimed model consequences, not historical truth.
No significance claim, no calibrated posterior, no confirmed plant names or
translated words. If one meaning can be swapped without changing checked
consequences, report the actual alternative. Fluency and restart agreement cannot
break that tie. Reserved pages remain closed unless a later authorized stage
establishes a nearly complete plausible overall reading; this pass opens none.
