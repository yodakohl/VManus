## Decision and limits

Stop this fixed shared cross-kernel comparator. The result does not decide
whether placement encodes content, whether whole forms carry
meanings, or whether there are useful family-specific context associations.
No within-family substitute model, changed half definition, lower capacity
threshold, extra targets, other reading or new data were tried. All previous
route stops remain intact.

Equal target length and character inventory refer only to literal ASCII
transcription strings. This is not evidence for established phonemes, an
alphabet or identical physical ink glyphs in the two positions. No native
image adjudication was part of this pass.
