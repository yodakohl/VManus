## Why the exclusion covers every key

For each observed order2context, the bound grants an independent target context
and independent injective next-symbol assignment. SPACE and BOS retain fixed
roles. Positive next-symbol counts are sorted largest first and paired with
the cheapest different target consonant costs; fixedSPACEcost is added. Take
the best permitted targetcontext separately for each native row.

Any actual global key is among those local choices, but also has to agree across
all rows. Removing that consistency can only LOWER its best attainable cost.
Thus even an impossible collection of perfectly favorable local choices losing
to KT proves every legal global key loses. This is the missing type of global
certificate that193/194's local optimality checks did not supply.

The bound does not cover194homophony: there, several source signs may map to one
consonant, and sorted DISTINCT target assignments would not be a valid lower
bound. No homophony score or decoder was run.
