## Precise necessary family
The next written whole group is F(previous exact whole group, source command),
with K possible command values and a fixed shared deterministic function. F may
be arbitrarily complicated. Two scopes: one global F, or separate F per exact
page. Starting word/anchor may be freely reset at physical line boundaries;
only consecutive ORIGINAL same-line strict pairs are used. No hidden state,
position/clock input, older words, source lookahead, homophonic random choice,
silent payload, skipped group or reset inside a retained pair. Source commands
need not be letters; K=27is the separately specified small-character subcase.

Generously permit arbitrary exact repetitions of current word as zero-payload
echoes; only NONSELF successor degrees enter the bound. For any current w,
all different observed following words must belong to the set{F(w,c):c in Σ}.
Thus K>=number of distinct nonself followers. This holds without assuming an
inverse or bijective F; readable writers obey the same necessary restriction.
Additional hidden states, probabilistic output or multiple source units per
transition are outside the model, not tested or selected repairs. The cost of
actually specifying a transformation family remains, even if capacity passes.
