# Sidequest Pass 829: literal-plus-owner record edition

All eleven prose records are now printed in two visibly separate layers.

The literal layer emits exactly one short German value for every component
token. It contains no pictured plant name, basin name, pronoun resolution, or
extra vessel supplied by the fluent editor.

The workshop layer then adds the active image/station owner, ordinary German
case and pronouns, and the smallest local object needed to make a readable
instruction. These additions may make a good working translation, but they do
not silently become component meanings.

The edition covers 39 components, 381 visible events, 116 statements, and all
eleven Herbal/Biological records. Every statement shows its surface sequence,
literal component sequence, and fluent reading side by side.

This separation improves the creative theory rather than weakening it: we can
now change an owner-level interpretation (for example bath versus apparatus)
without rewriting the reusable dictionary.

Next, use the layered edition to locate fluent words that repeatedly appear but
have no literal component source. Recurrent hidden words are candidates for a
missing component meaning; one-off grammatical glue is harmless.
