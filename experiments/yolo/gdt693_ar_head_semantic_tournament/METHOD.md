# GDT693 method

## Question and scope

GDT692 left one scoped semantic decision open: do the admitted
`AR/AIR/AIIR` target and control constructions denote a fraction, a selected
share, a preparation stage or a material class? GDT693 compares all four
choices over exactly the same 51 lines, 479 written token positions and 36
admitted pages, then selects one R head for the 63-position scope. It does not
claim that every inherited R-bearing surface in the reader has been migrated.

The direct target is unchanged: sixteen exact surfaces at 41 positions. The
comparison also includes 22 existing AR/OR control positions so that a fluent
choice cannot erase GDT654's contrast between AR and OR. Those controls contain
fourteen AR roles and ten OR roles; `aror` and `oroiir` contain both. No new
page, transcription or image is opened, and f84/f84r remain forbidden.

## Inputs

The builder inherits:

- GDT692's complete V65 token and line editions, its 41-position target table,
  113-verb audit and two bound amount/head spans;
- GDT654's nine bare/O/QO AR-versus-OR pair cells, covering 1,369 inherited
  occurrences, 1,069 of them reader-exact;
- GDT688's line and reader modes;
- explicit source decks for sixteen target surfaces, eleven control surfaces,
  six R/N terminal pairs, four candidate heads, the selected component model
  and six local product rivals;
- two selected-token overrides: one neutral German grammar repair and one
  token-isolated carry repair for an already bound quantity/head span.

All target and control rules name the expected V65 value. A mismatch stops the
build instead of silently applying a new gloss to a changed source.

## Four scope-wide renderers

Each candidate is rendered across every target and every AR-bearing control;
none may choose a different R noun merely because one local sentence sounds
better.

| candidate | common R reading within scope | main test result |
|---|---|---|
| fraction | selected technical fraction I–III | preserves the morphology, but can falsely suggest physical separation |
| share | selected material share I–III | preserves every tested contrast and supports measuring, recursion and portioning |
| stage | material/preparation on stage I–III | makes R nearly synonymous with the independent N value channel |
| class | material of class I–III | preserves much of the type contrast, but measured and recursively nested classes are less direct |

The selected head is `share`, rendered in German as *Anteil* or, where the
material head must be explicit, *Drogenanteil/Stoffanteil*. *Fraktion* remains
the runner-up only in the technical sense “selected constituent of a mixture.”

## Why no contextual mixed head is selected

The decisive evidence is a set of six exact terminal pairs in the current
51-line deck:

```text
aiir     / aiin
dair     / dain
olkaiir  / olkaiin
otair    / otain
qokaiir  / qokaiin
sair     / sain
```

Together they occur 30 times. In every pair the complete left body is fixed
and only terminal R versus N changes. The selected type system is therefore:

```text
A + repeated-I index + R  -> selected material share I/II/III
A + repeated-I index + N  -> head-dependent grade, amount or batch I/II/III/IV
OR                         -> independently divided portion
```

The repeated minim index does not choose a noun on its own. R and N type the
index differently. In addition, AR can be portioned by OR: `aror` reads *eine
Portion des Drogenanteils I*, while `oroiir` reads *eine Portion des
Ansatzanteils III*. These relations are natural under *Anteil*, possible but
less direct under *Klasse*, and awkward if R itself is made a process stage.

Context is not discarded. O still places the share in an Ansatz or
Zubereitung; QO remains its bounded frame; L supplies Holz; K/T supply
hot/cold quality; D supplies an already licensed measuring action. What is
rejected is only a context-dependent change of the R head itself.

## Selected rendering and controls

All 41 direct targets move from the V65 fraction head to the share head. The
fourteen AR-bearing controls move with them. The ten OR-bearing controls retain
the word *Portion*, including the two AR+OR/OR+AR composites. Hence the exact
change count is:

```text
41 target changes + 14 AR-control changes = 55 semantic head changes
55 head changes + 2 selected-reader repairs = 57 total revisions
```

Pure OR never becomes *Anteil*. This preserves GDT654's operational
distinction: AR selects which material share is meant; OR identifies an
actually divided portion of it. The neutral repair of `qodor` changes only
German case marking, from *eine Portion Ansatz abmessen* to *eine Portion des
Ansatzes abmessen*; its semantic head remains *Portion*.

Two GDT686 relations remain indivisible in line rendering:

```text
f86v3.13#5–6  drei Portionen des Anteils I des heißen Holzansatzes
f86v6.5#4–5   Anteil I des heißen Holzansatzes; drei Portionen davon
```

Span consumption prevents duplicated heads and retains the established
right-/left-bound quantity relation. It changes neither token count nor
ordinal provenance. The selected token channel also changes isolated
`qodaiin` from *drei Portionen der folgenden Fraktion* to *drei Portionen des
folgenden Anteils*, so token, span and line views no longer disagree about the
right-carried head.

## Product and historical guards

Six positions retain *Anteil I des heißen Holzauszugs* as a local rival:
f75r.3, f80r.17, f80v.35, f83v.12, f86v3.13 and f86v6.5. The rival changes the
locally selected referent from Holzansatz to Holzauszug; it does not turn R
into an extraction morpheme.

The late-fourteenth-/early-fifteenth-century
[*Anonimo Mediano*](https://corpus.atliteg.org/opera/anonimo-mediano/86) uses
*prima colatura* only after grinding, dilution, cloth straining, separate
collection and reservation. [Tadhg Ó Cuinn's 1415 *Materia
medica*](https://celt.ucc.ie/published/G600006.html) separately states a
drug's hot/dry degrees and its later boiling or straining. These comparators
support the distinction between technical selection, quality value and an
actually separated product; they do not identify a Voynich word or sound.

exact temporary-directory replay of all twenty generated files.

and identifies no language, phonetics, ingredient, carrier liquid, extraction
