# GDT693 report — V66 selects Anteil as the scoped common R head

## Outcome

GDT693 replaces V65's spoken *Fraktion* with **Anteil** at the admitted 41
targets and fourteen AR-bearing controls. The selected meaning is not an
arbitrary piece of material but an **indexed, selected material share**. The
separate N channel continues to express a head-dependent grade, amount or
batch, while OR continues to express an actually divided portion.

The final reader uses the same 51 lines, 479 written positions and 36 admitted

Final status:
`PASS_V66_SCOPED_INDEXED_R_SELECTOR__55_HEAD_PLUS_2_READER_REVISIONS__6_R_N_PAIRS__OR_PORTION_PRESERVED`.

## The tournament began with four scope-wide heads

Four complete renderers were built before one was selected:

| head | advantage | failure or limit | decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fraktion | compact; preserves AR/OR and R/N; natural under measurement | modern readers easily infer filtrate, distillation cut or physical separation | runner-up |
| Anteil | preserves AR/OR and R/N; natural under recursion, measurement and portioning | remains a broad modern working word | selected |
| Zubereitungsstufe | locally fluent in several O contexts | duplicates N's existing grade/value job and makes “measure a stage” awkward | rejected as common scope head |
| Stoffklasse | keeps a type distinction without implying separation | nested and measured classes are less natural than selected shares | live rival |

A context-dependent mixture initially looked attractive: *Stufe* in an
O-frame, *Klasse* in a nominal register, *Anteil* next to measurement and
*Fraktion* next to an extract. That policy was rejected because it optimizes
German prose by changing the value of the same visible R terminal. Local
context may choose the preparation or product referred to, but V66 keeps one
R head at all 41 targets and fourteen AR-bearing controls.

The 30 current occurrences show that the repeated-I index is shared but its

therefore make these exact pairs close synonyms precisely where their visible

The compositional controls point the same way. GDT654's nine inherited
bare/O/QO AR/OR pair cells contain 1,369 occurrences, 1,069 reader-exact.
`aror` is rendered **eine Portion des Drogenanteils I**. `oroiir` becomes
**eine Portion des Ansatzanteils III**. A selected share can receive a
quantity or be divided into a portion without becoming the quantity or the
portion itself.

## What the selected reader says

Representative target changes are:

```text
ar        Drogenanteil I
aiir      Drogenanteil III
oair      Anteil II des Ansatzes
odar      Anteil I des Ansatzes abmessen
okar      Anteil I des heißen Ansatzes
otair     Anteil II des kalten Ansatzes
olkar     Anteil I des heißen Holzansatzes
qokaiir   nimm den heißen Drogenanteil III
```

The recursive `otarar` becomes **Unteranteil I des Anteils I des kalten
Ansatzes**. This is not elegant literary German, but it exposes both written R
selectors instead of silently deleting one. The exact-whole `dy` result in
`otardy` still marks the selected cold-framed share as finished; it does not
become a free productive suffix.

The 57 revisions divide exactly as intended:

| source | revised positions |

The first repair changes *eine Portion Ansatz abmessen* to **eine Portion des
Ansatzes abmessen**. It repairs German case marking without changing OR's
meaning. The second changes isolated `qodaiin` from *drei Portionen der
folgenden Fraktion* to **drei Portionen des folgenden Anteils**. This aligns
the token view with the already selected bound-span and line view.

The two bound readings remain:

> f86v3.13#5–6: drei Portionen des Anteils I des heißen Holzansatzes

> f86v6.5#4–5: Anteil I des heißen Holzansatzes; drei Portionen davon

Thus *Anteil* names the selected material unit and *Portion* names the measured
or divided quantity. The line renderer does not duplicate either head.

## Product rivals remain local

Six `olkar` contexts make a Holz product plausible through soaking, heating,
macerate context, subsequent additions or established amount binding. Their
apparatus reading is **Anteil I des heißen Holzauszugs** rather than **Anteil I
des heißen Holzansatzes**. They occur at f75r.3, f80r.17, f80v.35, f83v.12,
f86v3.13 and f86v6.5.

This is a change of local referent, not a second meaning for R. The selected
main therefore does not infer extraction wherever `olkar` occurs.

## Why Fraktion remains a serious runner-up

V65 used *Fraktion* in the technical sense “selected mixture share or material
class,” never as a claimed distillate. In that restricted sense it passes the
same morphological tests as *Anteil* and remains the closest rival.

It loses the main channel because ordinary modern German strongly invites a
separated-product reading. A near-period positive control illustrates the
difference: the late-fourteenth-/early-fifteenth-century
[*Anonimo Mediano*](https://corpus.atliteg.org/opera/anonimo-mediano/86) calls a
product *prima colatura* only after cloth straining, separate collection and
reservation. V66 reserves *Auszug/Holzauszug* for independently supported
product contexts instead of allowing the R head itself to assert that
workflow.

Likewise K/T continue to be hot/cold quality tags. [Tadhg Ó Cuinn's 1415
*Materia medica*](https://celt.ucc.ie/published/G600006.html) describes
wormwood as hot in the first and dry in the second degree, then gives boiling
and straining as separate operations. V66 therefore adds no heating, cooling
or extraction verb from a nominal R form.
