# GDT501 method

## Question

Do the fifty GDT499 old-values-only cells have exact support from shorter old
recipes or ordered action pairs, and which cells remain supported only by
their individual component values?

## Inputs

- all 4,576 exact GDT416 imperative clauses;
- all 81 GDT421 ordered action-pair profiles;
- the exact fifty-cell GDT499 old-values-only frontier;
- the complete current 495-cell GDT500 phrase deck.

Only cached admitted material is read; no page or image is opened.

## Method

1. For each target recipe, enumerate every unique order-preserving proper
   subrecipe with at least two components. Record deleted positions, deleted
   tokens, contiguity and whether the target action root remains.
2. Match each subrecipe as an exact complete GDT416 recipe in the target
   register and in every other register. Publish event IDs, surfaces, pages and
   all observed German clause forms for every candidate×register witness.
3. For recipes with two action roots, join their ordered action pair to the
   complete GDT421 pair profile, preserving event, recipe and register counts.
4. Assign four working tiers: an exact action-retaining N−1 recipe in the same
   register; another exact local action-retaining partial; a cross-register
   partial, local action-neutral backbone or attested ordered pair; or
   individual component values only.
5. Rank within tier by longest and densest local partial support, then backbone,
   cross-register N−1 support, ordered-pair events, complexity, recipe and
   register. Retain the current GDT500 phrase verbatim.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Partial support never turns a target into an observed full recipe. A GDT416
witness must be an exact complete shorter recipe; a GDT421 pair must already be
attested. Absence leaves a working composition open and is not an impossibility
claim. GDT500 wording, component meanings, recipes and evidence labels cannot
change, and no manuscript spelling or occurrence is predicted.
