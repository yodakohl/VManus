## Exploration selected with prior knowledge

The paragraphs contain known constructions: cthy chor shor (GDT768),
chor chol daiin (GDT629/GDT759), repeated states (GDT625), and repeated
daiin (the broader phenomenon was studied in GDT686). These are convenient
places to compare interpretations, not newly discovered patterns.
Seven declared probes preserve repetition, intervening unknown text and
reader boundary alternatives. Their counts cannot select a semantic winner.

The descriptive model uses plant-part / quality / degree hypotheses.
The recipe model uses ingredient / material-state / portion hypotheses.
Their shared meanings, positive evidence, objections and uncertainty live in
JOINT_LEXICON_SPECS.tsv. Dry quality, dried material and an instruction to
dry are kept distinct. Shared spelling does not establish shared referent.

Historical passages are documented in src/HISTORICAL_PASSAGE_REVIEW.md.
They support the existence of these expression types, not EVA-to-Latin
identity, phonetics, a particular source language or a plaintext match.
