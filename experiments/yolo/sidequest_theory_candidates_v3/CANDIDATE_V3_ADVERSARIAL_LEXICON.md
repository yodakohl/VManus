# V3 adversarial lexicon: medium-and-process register

Updated: 2026-08-21

Status: independent speculative sidequest candidate. This is not a GDT result,
plaintext, or a claimed translation. It deliberately challenges the current
content lexicon while retaining those parts of the formal architecture that
are difficult to replace.

## Scope and source discipline

The page set is fixed:

- Herbal: `f10r`, `f11r`, `f55v`, `f56r`;
- Biological: `f81v`, `f82r`, `f83r`;
- circle/astronomical: `f67r2`, `f68r1`, `f69v`.

The theory was developed from `VOYNICH_CURRENT_ROUTE.md`,
`SIDEQUEST_SCRIBE_WORKSHOP_CURRENT.md`, and guarded f84-free slices of the
GDT327 interlinear and ZL3b surface lines. No other candidate or sidequest
archive was read. `f84` and `f84r` were not accessed.

## Leading alternative

The strongest rival is not a different cipher mechanism. It is a different
content lexicon inside the same learnable card apparatus:

> The seven prose pages are a practical **medium-and-process register**. A
> pictured plant, vessel, body or conduit supplies the silent subject. Entries
> specify a working medium, portions/components, operations on the current
> referent, and completed preparation states. The cards function more like a
> compact apothecary/bath-house work sheet than like an imperative sentence.

The generator is:

```text
PICTURED OBJECT OR APPARATUS
  -> choose preparation/application record
  -> optionally establish MEDIUM/MATRIX
  -> add COMPONENT/PART entries
  -> operate on CURRENT/REUSED referents
  -> commit intermediate or final cells
  -> render the same card under local wrapper/line conventions
```

This preserves the strongest formal observations:

- exact whole-card recurrence matters;
- B pages contain many short committed fields;
- A pages often have long open dossiers;
- wrappers are primarily rendering/construction choices;
- a physical line need not end a statement;
- picture and page can silently provide repeated arguments.

It rejects the current miniature dictionary as the uniquely best content
assignment.

### Why `qokaiin` can be a medium rather than a head

The exact card has nine fixed-page occurrences. Six are literally field-first;
the f82r line carry makes seven of nine logically entry-like if the repeated
boundary copy is counted once. But **broad right-context diversity does not
distinguish an instruction head from a carrier material**. Water, wine, oil,
decoction or a bath liquor can precede many different additives and treatments.

Its distribution supplies a coherent alternative:

- `f55v.5`: field-first on the only Herbal-B page in the set;
- `f81v.2`: field-first under a surface realization without visible `q`;
- `f82r.3 -> f82r.4`: field-final and then immediately field-first across a
  physical line;
- `f82r.26`: field-internal;
- `f83r.3`, `.6`, `.11`, `.14`: field-first.

The f82r repeat is particularly natural as a copied matrix label:

```text
... MATRIX /
MATRIX modifier component close | next component series
```

That reading is at least as economical locally as:

```text
... INSTRUCTION_HEAD /
INSTRUCTION_HEAD argument sequence
```

The alternative does **not** depend on the visible `q`: `f81v.2` shows the
same exact card with surface `okaiin`. The content claim attaches to the exact
card, not to a guessed prefix.

### Why L/O can be PART rather than a connector

L/O is mostly internal, but internal position alone does not identify a
relation. A repeated quantity/portion card also occurs between ingredients.

The most suggestive fixed sequences are:

```text
f10r.8  qotchor chor otol CHOL cholor CHOL daiin dar
f81v.2  ... sar OL kain olkain al OL rol dl
f81v.18 CHEY OL cheky OL SHEDY-CLOSE
```

If L/O is approximately PART/PORTION, the repeated pattern becomes:

```text
ingredient-A : PART ; ingredient-B : PART ; process/result
```

rather than a generic `X LINK Y LINK Z`. This is also why this candidate does
not revive `OL = WATER`: the exact same card behaves better as a repeated slot
or portion entry than as a fluid noun.

The main contradiction is that L/O can itself be field-first or field-last on
f83r and can form a one-card field on f81v. It may therefore be a whole
`ONE-PART` card or a slot instruction, not a preposition-like unit.

### Why AIIN can be a process card rather than a parameter

The exact AIIN card is positionally broad: six FIRST, nine MIDDLE and five LAST
over the seven pages. It never immediately takes attached closure in fifteen
available next-event opportunities. That profile is compatible with a reusable
operation that leaves its object/result to another card. Its Herbal line-final
uses are particularly comfortable as an instruction or process closure:

```text
f11r.4  dchol chy kchy dy DAIIN
f56r.5  chochor cho chodaly DAIIN
f56r.19 otchey keol DAIIN
```

It can mean MIX/APPLY only as an expansion class. `DO/HANDLE/CONTACT` is the
safer role. A true numerical parameter ought to show more consistent adjacency
to independently known amounts or units; the fixed pages supply none.

### Why Y can be a pointer rather than an item tag

The exact Y card is overwhelmingly an open, interior form and changes surface
wrapper freely. A deictic/current-item card would be useful precisely because
the drawings and previous cells leave the referent implicit.

The recurring construction then reads:

```text
Y -> AIIN -> Y
CURRENT/THIS -> PROCESS-CONTACT -> CURRENT/THIS
```

This is a compact two-place or reflexive process relation, not a typed amount
frame. It explains why both operands can be the same exact card even though no
two independently symmetric pictured quantities exist.

The awkward Herbal form contains one extra Y:

```text
f10r.6  ... DY CHY TAIIN SHY
                 Y  AIIN Y
```

On this account the first free `DY` is a current-object recall before the
two-place construction: `CURRENT; THIS process-with THIS`. That is possible in
a pointer-heavy shorthand, but it is not a clean natural-language phrase and
is an important cost.

### Why CTHY can be an operation rather than a state

The seven occurrences concentrate on f10r/f11r/f83r. Four occur in a compact
run on f83r (`.14`, `.15` twice, `.16`, with another at `.24` depending on the
fixed exact inventory accounting). The surface sequences allow a repeated
operation more readily than a permanent quality:

```text
f83r.14 qokaiin shcthy dal sy
f83r.15 ... shecthy chey tal shcthy ...
f83r.16 ... chedy qokain checthy ...
f83r.24 soiiin checthy chety otaiin olsaly shedy
```

`WORK/TREAT` is the robust class. `HEAT/BOIL` is a deliberately bolder guess:
it makes sense in herbal decoctions and medicated baths and explains repeated
treatment stages, but no flame, temperature sign or readable recipe anchors it.

## Consecutive parses

The parses below preserve every visible group. Brackets name unidentified
cards; vertical bars are frozen GDT327 field boundaries. Parentheses mark only
the adversarial source-class expansion.

### f82r.2-f82r.6: working-medium continuation

Surface:

```text
f82r.2 dchedy | qolchedy | qokain dy qokeedy | qokal lcheckhy lched
f82r.3 qokeey lcheckhedy | qokaly solkaiin chckhy qokaiin
f82r.4 qokaiin octheol chkeey ldy | oteey qokal sheckhy qoky
f82r.5 sol lkchedy qokeedy qokal cthol chedy qoteedy qokal
f82r.6 sar shedy qol shedaiin sheckhy okal sheky qotaiin chedol
```

V3 parse:

```text
f82r.2 [entry-CLOSE] | [entry-CLOSE] |
        [component] CURRENT [state-CLOSE] |
        [component/component treatment/open]

f82r.3 [preparation-CLOSE] |
        [component] [modifier] [treatment] LIQUID-MATRIX /

f82r.4 LIQUID-MATRIX [modifier] [component] [state-CLOSE] |
        [following component series]

f82r.5 [component series continuing within the same pictured apparatus]
f82r.6 [component series including one handled/mixed entry]
```

The strongest claim here is structural: `qokaiin` is the same local entry
continued across line space. The content fork is whether that entry is a
command head or the matrix being elaborated. The matrix reading predicts that
the following cards describe different constituents or conditions of the same
liquid/application rather than coordinate objects of one verb.

### f83r.3: the best pseudo-translation

Surface and frozen fields:

```text
olkeedy |
qotal chkeedy |
chey daiin chey lchedy |
qokaiin qotal dar
```

Maximally concrete V3 expansion:

> [First preparation, entered.] [A measured/selected component, entered.]
> **This one: work or mix it with the current one; enter the resulting
> preparation. In the liquid matrix, add/use the specified component.**

This is not a translation. Only the bold relation is produced by the proposed
lexicon:

```text
CURRENT — PROCESS/CONTACT — CURRENT — RESULT-COMMIT
MATRIX — COMPONENT — SPECIFIER
```

It explains a complete four-field line without making AIIN a number, Y an item
type or qokaiin an imperative.

This looks less like a single sentence than a checklist of inputs and prepared

### f83r.14-f83r.16: repeated process card

The attractive feature is the recurrence of CTHY as an action in the same local
procedure. The cost is that almost all other cards remain unknown; “heat” is
not independently visible.

## Water, habitat and preparation

This candidate tested three possibilities without assigning AROL or OL to
water.

### 1. `qokaiin = WATER`

Possible, but too specific. Its distribution fits a liquid medium on a Herbal-B
page and bath/apparatus pages. Against it:

- it is absent from the three fixed Herbal-A pages;
- none of its nine occurrences has an independently owned water referent;
- every Biological page is broadly water-compatible, so the visual match has
  poor discrimination;
- `f82r.26` places it field-internally.

Decision: retain **LIQUID/WORKING MEDIUM**, but subordinate literal WATER at
`.24`.

### 2. a habitat statement on Herbal pages

The plant image might motivate “grows in water/moist ground,” but the fixed
cards do not separate habitat from preparation. The sole qokaiin Herbal
occurrence is on f55v, whose B-like closed fields look more procedural than the
open Herbal-A dossiers.

Decision: habitat is weaker than preparation and is not part of the leading
parse.

### 3. preparation liquid

This is the strongest option. Fifteenth-century medicinal practice routinely
used plant waters, decoctions, oils and medicated baths. The Wellcome catalogue
records a mid-fifteenth-century French paper manuscript whose first section is
explicitly “recipes for medicinal waters from plants.” A history-of-medicine
study notes that late-medieval water therapy adjusted water by temperature,
herbal extracts/decoctions and mode of application. These make a matrix-first
worksheet historically ordinary, although they cannot identify a Voynich card.

Relevant comparators:

- [Wellcome Collection, recipes for medicinal waters from plants](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/f6nzyzh4);
- [PubMed, hydrotherapy prescriptions in the late Middle Ages](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12168234/);
- [Cambridge, late-medieval practical books and apothecary weight shorthand](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-british-studies/article/here-is-a-good-boke-to-lerne-practical-books-the-coming-of-the-press-and-the-search-for-knowledge-ca-14001560/8217EBC4F6CE53F1084709587B7C2E12).

These sources establish plausibility only, not donor relation or origin.

## Circle pages under the alternative

The medium/process lexicon is not exported into the circles. The fixed circle
pages have separate local namespaces and no GDT327 prose tuples.

- `f67r2`: a layered selector with 7 and 12 member inventories can supply
  conditions under which a preparation is used, but can equally be a standalone
  astronomical table.
- `f68r1`: a central plus 28 noncentral star catalogue supplies named entries,
  not an ordered process.
- `f69v`: the alternating LONG/SHORT 28-entry ring is compatible with a
  calendrical schedule but has no confirmed text distinction or medical link.

Thus the manuscript can still be a medical-practical miscellany without a
single WHAT/HOW/WHEN compiler spanning all ten pages. The Astro section is a
separate lookup annex until a cross-page pointer exists.

## Decisive fixed-page predictions

These predictions use only the ten already exposed pages and can be checked in
a later iteration without adding pages.

1. **Matrix continuation:** when qokaiin is line-final and repeated line-first
   on f82r, the post-repeat field should share more exact component/closure
   families with other qokaiin-first fields than with matched ordinary heads.
   Failure favors generic entry-head.

2. **Portion symmetry:** in L/O-repeated fields, left and right operand spans
   should be more homogeneous in field size/closure class than for matched
   repeated interior cards. Failure rejects PART and restores generic relation.

3. **Process completion:** AIIN at Herbal line/field ends should be followed in
   the continuing record by result/preparation-like closed construction classes
   more often than matched portable cards. No downstream change rejects
   PROCESS.

4. **Pointer reuse:** a Y occurrence should preferentially reuse the formal
   class of a prior local item or flank the same operation class. If its local
   antecedent is no more recoverable than for matched frequent cards, reject
   CURRENT/THIS.

5. **Repeated treatment:** the CTHY run on f83r.14-.16 should sit at homologous
   places in successive process fields and should predict a similar following
   closure/state family. Pure position heterogeneity rejects WORK/HEAT.

6. **Preparation not habitat:** the f55v qokaiin line should align structurally
   with Bio matrix fields rather than with the open descriptive Herbal-A lines.
   If it aligns better with Herbal description ecology, habitat or generic head
   regains probability.

7. **Water ceiling:** literal WATER may be promoted only if an independently
   visible fixed-page distinction creates qokaiin-positive and qokaiin-negative
   comparable fluid opportunities. Mere occurrence on a bath-like page is not
   enough.

## What this theory explains better

- It turns the f82r cross-line qokaiin repeat into content continuity rather
  than an unexplained duplicated command.
- It gives the two `Y-AIIN-Y` cases a relation without inventing a quantity or
  symmetric dose.
- It explains repeated L/O in `X-L/O-Y-L/O` as recipe portions.
- It places the f83 CTHY cluster inside a recognizable repeated-treatment run.
- It allows water to be mentioned beside a plant without assigning WATER to
  AROL/OL or insisting the plant is aquatic.

## What it explains worse

- qokaiin's strong field-entry skew is exactly what an entry head predicts.
- PART, PROCESS, POINTER and HEAT are mutually supportive guesses; none is
  independently anchored.
- The same data can be paraphrased as a generic form without any material
  meaning.
- Astro remains disconnected.
- Almost all rare cards still lack even a source-class expansion.

## Final choice

The leading adversarial theory is therefore:

```text
PICTURE-ADDRESSED MEDIUM/PROCESS WORKSHEET

qokaiin : working liquid/matrix, possibly but not necessarily water
L/O      : part/portion slot
AIIN     : handle/mix/apply/contact operation
Y        : current/this/same referent
CTHY     : work/treat, possibly heat
DY/B3    : commit the local preparation cell
```

This is more concrete than the current entry-head/link/parameter/tag/state
lexicon and yields coherent consecutive parses. It should replace that lexicon
only if the fixed-page predictions above favor matrix continuation, portion
symmetry, downstream process change and local pointer reuse. Until then it is
the strongest content-level alternative, not the new default.
