## Selected synthesis

```text
PICTURED/DOSSIER ADDRESS
  → ENTRY or reactivated address head
  → ITEM / RELATION / PARAMETER packets
  → PREPARATION or CONFIGURATION state
  → exact payload-bearing COMMIT card
  → optional open continuation
```

The likely upstream style is a normalized formulary order comparable to, but
not identified as, Latin recipe memoranda:

```text
ADDRESS → RECIPE/ENTRY → ITEM/RELATION/PARAMETER
        → PREPARATION/STATE → PRODUCT-COMMIT → APPLICATION/CONTINUATION
```

A vernacular imperative chain may underlie that register syntax. The page and
picture can silently supply the principal object and page-local roles; this is
drawn-argument ellipsis, not a universal picture dictionary.

> For the pictured owner, take or activate the current item under the stated
> measure or setting. Relate it to the prepared condition and commit the local
> cell. Continue with the linked item, retain its parameter, and close the

## Fluency stopping rule

Every word in a reported pseudo-translation must be tagged conceptually as:

```text
P = independently pictured or inherited
F = formal card role
S = explicitly speculative source-class expansion
U = unsupported filler
```

Stop at the first `U`. Articles may be supplied for readability, but a plant
part, liquid, action, body part, amount or direction may not silently enter the
translation. No exact card may change anonymous function to rescue a line.
