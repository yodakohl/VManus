# Sidequest V3 translation-selection protocol

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: frozen before reading V3 outcomes. Speculative YOLO sidequest only;
not a scientific result or translation claim.

## Fixed scope and seal

Only f10r, f11r, f55v, f56r, f81v, f82r, f83r, f67r2, f68r1 and f69v may be
used. f84 and f84r remain sealed. Alternate transcriptions are readings of one
manuscript, not replications.

## Fixed scoring

| criterion | points |
|---|---:|
| consecutive real-field translation coverage | 25 |
| lexicon/function consistency without ad-hoc switching | 20 |
| compatibility with exact-card grammar and renderer | 15 |
| source-language plausibility around 1420 | 15 |
| explanatory gain over anonymous V2 parses | 10 |
| handling of counterexamples and ambiguity | 10 |
| risky fixed-page predictions | 5 |

Maximum: 100. Scores are abductive comparisons, not probabilities.

## Required output decisions

The selected synthesis must publish:

1. one compact provisional card/function dictionary;
2. at least one multi-field continuous pseudo-translation;
3. a clear distinction between silent pictured/inherited material, anonymous
   formal roles, source-class expansions and fluent English paraphrase;
4. every place where a card has to change function;
5. the strongest alternative lexicon;
6. which V2 guesses survive, weaken or fail;
7. a stopping rule preventing fluent prose from hiding unsupported cards.

The winner must be one coherent theory. A losing mechanism may be imported
only if it reduces role switching or improves consecutive parse coverage.
