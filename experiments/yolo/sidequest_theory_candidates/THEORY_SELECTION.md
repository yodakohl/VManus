## Selected synthesis

The new basis is not a pure codebook. It is:

```text
ordinary technical language or source table
  → omission of picture/register-predictable material
  → phrase-, relation-, value- and state-level abbreviations
  → exact learned cards inserted into register forms
  → hand/register allography, joining, closure and line reflow
```

The selected content hierarchy is:

1. practical medical workshop miscellany;
2. Herbal simple dossiers plus Biological application/configuration forms;
3. Astro as practical lookup annex;
4. integrated WHAT/HOW/WHEN only as a secondary fork;
5. pattern/exercise book as the leading nonmedical adversary.

## Why the other candidates did not win

- The abbreviated-language candidate is incorporated rather than rejected: it
  supplies the most plausible upstream source layer. It loses only because
  exact whole-card legality and variable form stencils are more directly
  observable than lexical continuity.
- The medical candidate supplies the strongest content ecology. It cannot by
  itself explain why wrapper/rendering behavior dominates and why Astro lacks a
  formal bridge.
- The Astro candidate demonstrates that medical timing books existed. It does
  not demonstrate that these ten pages form one concordance.
- The pattern-book candidate is an important adversarial control. It should be
  promoted only if slot-preserving variable-fill prediction beats both exact
  phrase reuse and content-owned recurrence on the fixed material.
