# GDT1091 — held image contrast for the two f22r fields

Registered 2026-09-29 **before any new target/control image pixels** are
opened. Exact scope is in the dated image-admission note. This is a fixed
exploratory contrast on previously admitted text and newly admitted images,
not independent confirmation or a decoded word.

## Decision and predecessors

GDT765 records all three all-reader exact `ofchy` loci (f22r.4, f26v.5,
f39v.1) and all three `schor` loci (f22r.4, f32r.4, f42v.10); the two words
occur in separate proposed `X daiin` fields on f22r.4. The f22r drawing has
three dark blue cup/bell terminals and many red/pale beaded axes on one plant,
with **no visible field-to-organ pointer**. GDT1090 finds `schor` compatible
with reproductive heads on its three pages, but its chosen feature union is
postselected; nine of fifteen other two-code unions also yield an all-positive
word. GDT1080 failed to direct the `chor/shor` flower/fruit choice, and the
IDEA687 six-source screen did not bind the f22r organs to a written source
relation. These limitations remain.

Genuinely unknown: do **both other exact `ofchy` pages** show the unusual
f22r-style dark blue fringed terminal cups, while matched Herbal-B images and
the `schor`-only references generally do not? A positive differential would
prioritize `ofchy≈cup-like flower/fruit material` and `schor≈other
reproductive head` as a *paired working reading* of f22r's two fields. A
failure would retire that specific direct-visual mapping; it would not refute
all material/item meanings because an entry may mention unpictured parts.
The two-image contrast changes this reading decision and needs no decoder,
new text, external botanical taxon or new transcription rule.

## Frozen image roster and blinding

Exactly nine official Yale page images are scored: `f22r` (known cup/spike
reference); targets `f26v`, `f39v`; four Currier-B/Herbal/hand-2 controls
`f31r`, `f33v`, `f40v`, `f43v`; and `schor`-only references `f32r`, `f42v`.
Controls were chosen from the guarded 179-selector source metadata before
viewing their pixels: the two nearest distinct non-target physical leaves to
f26 (f31 and f33), and the two nearest to f39 (f40 and f43), using the
available page on that leaf; f31r was already image-admitted. No image
feature or target string was used to choose controls. f22r, f32r and f42v
were already viewed and are explicit calibration/contrast pages, not held
confirmation. The five new images are listed in `src/PAGE_ADMISSIONS.tsv`.

Two separate image readers receive the same nine downloaded images under
randomized IDs `B01`–`B09`, but no folio map, target/control status, text,
source species names, GDT765 glosses or each other's assessments. The private
mapping is committed by SHA-256
`1c2b7cf7d33caf92a179964e6c74e9498f1c6c8a3cf3c4d89a2f31e0d63ec170`
before pixels. Their tables are frozen and hashed separately before the map
is revealed and joined. Root has prior text/image exposure and may not
change their primary codes after the join. Any root review is labelled later.

Each reader records YES/NO/UNKNOWN plus a short visible-geometry note for:

* `BLUE_FRINGED_CUP`: at least one distinct **dark-blue filled terminal
  bell/cup** on a stalk with a **pale scalloped or fringed open lower rim**,
  as in the previously described f22r architecture; merely blue pigment or
  a blue cluster is NO.
* `OPEN_CUP_TERMINAL`: any clearly hollow/open bell/cup terminal on a stalk,
  regardless of colour or fringed rim. This is a wider sensitivity only.
* `MULTI_UNIT_SPIKE`: many small discrete units arrayed along an elongated
  flowering/fruiting axis, exactly GDT1089's geometric definition.
* `SPINY_ROUND_HEAD`: rounded terminal with pointed bracts/spines, exactly
  GDT1089's geometric definition.

UNKNOWN is required when ink, pigment, attachment or damage prevents a
decision. Readers describe shapes only, not botanical taxa, semantic words or
source identifications. Do not reinterpret a different blue flower as a
strict cup after seeing results.

## Scoring and limits

Primary `CUP_DIFFERENTIAL_COMPATIBLE` requires both readers to mark strict
`BLUE_FRINGED_CUP=YES` on **f22r, f26v and f39v**, on at most **one** of the
four matched controls, and `NO` on both f32r/f42v references. If either
target is UNKNOWN/disagreed, result `CAPACITY_INDETERMINATE`; if one target
is definite YES and one definite NO, `PARTIAL_ONE_TARGET`; if both target
NO, `NO_TARGET_SUPPORT`. If both target YES but two or more controls YES,
`NONDISCRIMINATING_COMMON_CUP`; if a required reference/control code is
UNKNOWN/disagreed, report the exact unresolved cell rather than silently
score it negative. The broad cup and spike/spiny codes are reported across
**all nine** images as nonselecting sensitivities. A strict cup positive with
spikes also present on both targets weakens two-organ separation; report it
without exception repair. There is no p-value, discovery of a species name,
or confirmed `ofchy`/`schor` translation from a two-leaf target.
