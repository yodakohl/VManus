# GDT791 reconstruction contract

This file is the explicit executable contract for GDT791. It is retrospective
with respect to the already published GDT581 and GDT790 counts and is therefore
not presented as a blinded statistical preregistration.

## Fixed scope

- Exactly the 30 released physical pages and their 35 ZL3b selectors.
- `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.
- No new page, OCR, transcription reading or hidden semantic source.
- All 30 prior visual page reviews are admitted; only f77r, f82r and f83r carry
  deep panel/record/component annotation.

## Required outcomes

- 1,007 source lines and 5,866 source tokens.
- 612 running-prose lines, 392 local-label/marker lines and three empty lines.
- 5,122 running events plus 744 local cards.
- 235 deep-page legacy statements projected to 240 record-local fragments.
- Exactly five cross-record statements, four of them also cross-panel.
- Exactly two event-to-event aliases quarantined at a record boundary, 283
  source-free owner defaults reparented locally and one raw governor clipped
  while its local effective host survives.
- Ten exact label/prose bridges remain string-reference edges with zero record
  merge and zero meaning-transfer credit.

Any mismatch blocks the integrated result. A passing result changes boundaries
and inheritance only; it licenses no lexical meaning.
