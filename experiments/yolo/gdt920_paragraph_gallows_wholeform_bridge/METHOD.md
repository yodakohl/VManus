Build ZL3b frames separately per page, ordered by numeric source_row_index. A start flag opens a new frame (abandon an unfinished old frame); append each stored row until an end flag closes it. Ignore outside rows; abandon unfinished page tails. Retain completed frames with >=2 rows and all kind P. Align IT2a/RF1b by the exact ordered ZL loci; exclude a frame if a locus is absent or not P. Do not reconstruct RF paragraphs from absent flags. No paragraph may cross a page.

A word is a complete raw [a-z]+ group with left separator LINE_START or DEFINITE_SPACE and right LINE_END or DEFINITE_SPACE. Only individual word boundaries determine eligibility; uncertainty elsewhere does not invalidate a paragraph. The header is its complete first stored line. Every distinct eligible header word with exactly one p or f and zero k or t is an anchor. Keep all its source occurrences; deduplicate its score within the paragraph. The body consists of eligible words from all remaining lines. Retain frames with at least one eligible body word, including frames with no anchor.

Two predeclared maps preserve every other character: A: p->k,f->t; B: p->t,f->k. No prefix deletion, alternate segmentation, word selection or glyph normalization.

## Score and complete controls
A physical leaf is the numeric f identifier, including both faces and all panels. Sort leaves lexically; frames by page and numeric first-row index. An exchangeable leaf has >=2 retained paragraphs and >=1 anchor. For map g, header paragraph i and body paragraph j, M_g[i,j] is the sum, over distinct anchors in i, of mapped-word count in body j divided by all eligible body tokens in j, then divided by the total anchor count across the leaf. Thus zero matches remain and opportunity/paragraph length is normalized.

T_g is the mean across exchangeable leaves of sum_i M_g[i,i]. The exact conditional expectation mu_g is the same mean of sum_i mean_j M_g[i,j]. Residual R_g=T_g-mu_g. In each of 1024 worlds, Python random.Random(920000+world_index), index 0..1023, independently shuffles a list of body indices on each lexically ordered leaf. The same permutation is used for A and B. Recompute both scores and max(R_A,R_B); compare the observed maximum to the world maxima, counting ties within 1e-12 against it. This accounts for choosing between these two maps only, not the complete project search.

A map has adequate capacity only if at least five distinct anchor words occur on exchangeable leaves and at least five leaves have any anchor whose mapped-body rate varies among available bodies. A positive provisional bridge additionally requires R_g>0, joint max rank (1+worlds>=observed)/1025 <=0.05, and that map's capacity. Otherwise report nonconfirmation or capacity stop. Alternatives are descriptive: calculate their complete matrices and residuals, but do not replace ZL primary or select their map. No significance claim about the whole search, semantic relation packet, word identity or confirmed plant name.

Output every frame/exclusion, every anchor and both predictions including zeroes, body matches, exchange expectations, per-leaf scores, every conditional world, capacities and remaining ambiguity.
