# V21 selection — executable Astro consultation

Date: 2026-08-22

Status: **concrete ten-page working theory, not deciphered astronomy**.

## Selected use

The three circle pages now form one practical consultation sequence:

```text
f67r2  SAFETY FILTER
       choose the governing planet and zodiac/body sector;
       avoid invasive treatment of the governed body region

f68r1  OBSERVATION / IDENTIFICATION AID
       identify the Moon's current one-of-28 station from its drawn place

f69v   WORKING RULE TABLE
       consult the corresponding one-of-28 entry and favour or withhold
       the named application, then continue the station schedule
```

This is the strongest current way to make Astro serve the Herbal/Bio workbook:
Herbal selects a preparation, Bio selects an application configuration, and
Astro restricts when and where it is safe to use.

## f68r1: spatial identification catalogue

The centre remains the Moon governing 28 stations. The 28 noncentral labels
remain spatial addresses, not an invented clockwise list. A practitioner could
use the star pattern or drawn location to recognize the current station.

No semantic names are assigned to the individual station labels. Their complete
defaults are “the station at this drawn locus,” which is concrete enough to use
without fabricating an Arabic, Latin or vernacular mansion name.

## f69v: 28-position working schedule

The 28 radial positions are the operational table. The present exploratory
reading keeps the alternating favourable/withhold interpretation:

```text
odd working slot   favour the named application
even working slot  withhold the named application
```

This is a usable default, but its greatest weakness is obvious: LONG/SHORT
alternation may be graphic capacity, paired template, condition/result or a
checking aid rather than polarity. It remains live because it produces a simple
teachable rule; it is not promoted above those rivals.

## The missing join

There is no visible, demonstrated f68→f69 label identity, common start or
direction. The workflow therefore requires one of two external mechanisms:

1. the user knows the conventional 28-station order and converts the recognized
   f68 station into its ordinal before consulting f69; or
2. f68 and f69 are related 28-member instruments but not directly joined.

`V21_28_STATION_CONSULTATION.tsv` records every tempting row correspondence as
`NOT_VISIBLE_CONVENTIONAL_INDEX_REQUIRED`. This is a working-index table, not a
claim that f68r1.9 equals f69v.4.
