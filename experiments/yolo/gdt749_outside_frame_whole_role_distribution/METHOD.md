# GDT749 method

## Target and exclusion deck

The target deck is fixed directly from GDT748: sixteen complete EVA surfaces
with recurrent S2/S3 serial roles, plus conflicted `qochey`. The builder finds
1,684 target occurrences in the inherited 179-page cache. It marks the exact
57 GDT748 form-bridged discovery coordinates and removes them from every
outside test. This leaves 1,627 written occurrences, of which 1,311 are exact
across ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b, on 154 already allowed pages.

No surface is decomposed. Alternate readers are one manuscript and serve only
as an exactness control.

## Raw whole-distribution profile

Each outside target profile is compared to GDT746's fixed deck of forty-six
known complete wholes. The reused features are manuscript section,
FIRST/MIDDLE/LAST/SINGLE line position, immediate left and right known-whole
axes, nearest known closure within five cells, and the exact left/right whole
surfaces. Jensen-Shannon similarities and GDT746's fixed weights produce a
ranked forty-five- or forty-six-word reference list.

For each proposed axis, the top-five count is compared with that axis's own
frequency in the full reference deck. Thus three DRY cards are not treated as
equivalent to three HOT cards: DRY occupies 25/46 reference cards, HOT only
10/46. The unadjusted and deck-adjusted ranks remain useful descriptive
profiles.

## Required known-whole calibration

The identical ranking rule is then applied to each of the forty-six known
reference wholes while that surface's self-comparison is removed. A
quality/stage axis is provisionally predicted when it occurs at least twice in
the top five, is enriched at least 1.35-fold over its deck expectation, and has
a best matching rank of five or better.

This calibration yields 23 true-positive, 42 false-positive and 60
false-negative quality/stage labels: precision 35.4 percent and recall 27.7
percent. Consequently the raw distribution tiers are retained for navigation
only. They cannot strengthen or defeat a word card.

## Baseline-adjusted immediate contexts

For the weaker working decision, each exact outside occurrence is classified
as prior-axis only, same-dimension rival only, both, or neither according to
its immediately adjacent eligible W2/W3 complete-whole cards. The same four
counts are computed over 1,158 exact occurrences of the forty-six reference
wholes. Conflicted positions are excluded from the prior/rival share, and the
target share is compared with this reference baseline.

The final K tiers are intentionally modest:

- K2: at least eight polarized outside positions, at least five prior-only
  positions, and a prior-share lift of at least 0.10 over the reference base;
- K1 weak compatibility: at least three prior-only positions and nonnegative
  lift;
- K1 rival lead: a raw rival profile, at least five polarized positions, and a
  prior-share deficit of at least 0.15;
- sparse, baseline-like and `qochey` cases remain explicitly open.

These thresholds rank replaceable working hypotheses. They are not
probabilities or confirmation levels.

## `qochey` split

The three hypotheses are scored separately against references containing all
required axes. For each hypothesis the artifact records reference-pool size,
top-five count, deck expectation, best rank and immediate outside support. This
preserves both the strongest old serial frame and the newly stronger outside
END_STAGE lead.

## Relation and claim ceiling

The strongest same-page complete-whole relation is sent through the executable
GDT388 intake. It is expected to remain invalid and not score-ready solely
because formal access is unsealed. All output roles belong to complete forms;
literal identities and component export remain zero.
