## Research decision

Retain1320's limited positive and the old compulsory-fusion counterexamples.
Do not infer a required whole-word spelling dictionary from1320. Direct adjacent
shape context is a plausible contributor worth retaining as a working explanation,
but this test neither proves its sufficiency nor supplies an actual medieval
renderer. Do not repair a failed gate with another context radius, hand features,
new smoothing or a selected BH/HB direction. No native alphabet, phoneme or word
meaning has been assigned. The incomplete1001port-planning teaching writer is
not fitted or validated by this result.

The old four-pair OUTER-WORD flank search remains stopped with no matches. This
comparison conditions on units INSIDE a stipulated expanded source word and
predicts observed joining choices; it is neither a reopened meaning-equivalence
search nor proof that the expanded strings mean the same thing.
