# GDT673 method

## Question

Do GDT672's eighty f1r transfer cards retain their working meanings at every
exact occurrence in the already admitted f84-free 179-side V48 panel, and
which cards require a named contextual split rather than global spelling
transfer?

## Inputs

- GDT672's eighty transfer cards and 214-position f1r token reader;
- GDT671's 4,128-line V48 coverage table and exact 179-page allowlist;
- the cross-transcription line table, read only through `./vmanus-exp
  query-tsv` with 179 explicit allowed page selectors, explicit columns and
  forbidden prefix `f84`;
- 39 manual P/W context decisions, three semantic rivals, three
  reader-conditioned rivals and twenty explicit value-attachment decisions.

## Method

1. Match all eighty card surfaces against every ZL3b token in the frozen V48
   panel. The panel is read from GDT671's already guarded coverage artifact.
2. Preserve each P/W card literally and inspect its complete left/right line
   contexts. A card is applied only if the manual audit names no contradiction.
3. Never transfer an O card by surface alone. Compare `y`, `s`, `d`, `r` and
   `ok` against the occurrence-specific V48 gloss, neighbor and reader-join
   condition and assign HOLD, conflict or untestable per occurrence.
4. Align every source position independently to IT2a and RF1b with GDT671's
   exact dynamic program. Exact means position-aligned identical ONE, not the
   same spelling somewhere else in the line.
5. Apply the accepted P/W overlay only to positions still open in V48. Report
   all touched lines and newly closed lines, but do not emit a 179-page edition.
6. Bind explicit adjacent values to their material heads. Keep four possible
   process-stage bindings open where direction is not fixed.
7. Rebuild every artifact byte-identically and validate all source keys,
   counts, reader alignments, decisions and hashes independently.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

An exploratory card remains usable until a named context contradicts it. A
learned W card may transfer as the same exact learned whole but does not become
a productive role. O cards always remain occurrence-scoped. Reader variation
is exposed rather than averaged away.

The output is a concrete working overlay, not confirmed plaintext, language,
phonetics, historical codebook identity, substance, plant, disease, patient,
cure or manuscript-wide semantics. f84 and f84r remain forbidden.
