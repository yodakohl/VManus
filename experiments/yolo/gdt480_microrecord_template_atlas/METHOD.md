# GDT480 method

## Question

How much of the complete GDT479 edition reduces to recurrent microrecord
templates once owner-specific learned names are replaced by ordered slots, and
which templates survive across pages or registers?

## Input and normalization

The only inputs are GDT479's 183 event, 146 bundle, and 135 microrecord tables.
No page or transcription source is opened.

Within each record, learned plant, celestial, bath-station, and pharmaceutical
names become ordered `{N1}`, `{N2}`, ... slots; repeated cores retain the same
slot. The one drug-family marker becomes an owner-neutral family slot. The
nineteen component meanings, exact order, event and bundle separators, active
coordinate/instruction/catalogue model, and GDT478 state operations remain
literal.

The German phrase template separately replaces quoted headwords with ordered
slots and converts owner-local nouns such as Positionswert/Mengenwert,
Zielposition/Zielgefäß, and Sternstelle/Droge to fluent neutral forms. This
changes presentation only; the strict key comes from the component trace.

## Two template levels

The strict template preserves the complete ordered meaning trace, grammatical
model, event/bundle topology, and OT/OL scope. Two records share it only if all
of those match after name-slot replacement.

The role shape replaces exact components by `ACTION`, `ARG`, `REL`, `ORDER`,
`NAME`, or `MOD`. It is a backoff description of sentence shape, never the
reading used in place of a strict template.

Each template reports record, page, register, surface, recipe, and phrase
variation. Recurrence is classed as cross-register, cross-page, same-page, or
singleton. Every record receives both levels regardless of recurrence.

## Integrity

The validator rebuilds all artifacts byte-for-byte; checks the full GDT479
mapping, template membership, recurrence classes, owner-neutral vocabulary,
surface and coverage counts; verifies the three exact cross-register families;
and confirms zero component, model, boundary, surface, recipe, or page change.
It passes 84/84 checks.
