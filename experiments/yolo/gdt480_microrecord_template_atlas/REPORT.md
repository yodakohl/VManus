# GDT480 microrecord template atlas

## Outcome

All 135 complete GDT479 records now have two explicit defaults: a strict
owner-neutral meaning template and a broader role shape. The strict layer has
120 templates. Thirteen recur and cover 28 records; the role layer has 105
shapes, of which 21 recur and cover 51 records.

| Layer | Templates | Recurrent | Records in recurrent forms | Cross-page | Cross-register |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strict meaning | 120 | 13 | 28 | 5 | 3 |
| Role shape | 105 | 21 | 51 | 13 | 8 |

Eight of the thirteen strict recurrent families cover eighteen records with
different visible surfaces. They are therefore not just duplicate-word hits.
The other five families are exact repeated surfaces. All thirteen recurrent
strict families produce one stable owner-neutral German phrase.

## Three cross-register meaning templates

The strongest result is small and concrete:

- `CATALOGUE[DANACH · {N1}]` occurs four times on f71v, f77r, and f88v. The
  surfaces `otchody`, `otork`, `otora`, and `otdordy` all read as a named entry
  with a following-entry mark; OT opens the right-hand sibling.
- `COORDINATE[DANACH · ZIELORT · POSTEN]` occurs as `otalshy` on f72r and
  `otalsy` on f89r: “Adressspur: danach → Zielort → Posten.”
- `CATALOGUE[{N1} · AUSGANG · {N2}]` occurs as celestial `yfary` and
  pharmaceutical `ytarem`: two named entries joined by an Ausgang assignment.

These are the best current whole-record compositions because they preserve the
exact model, component order, name-slot topology, and OT/OL scope across owner
classes. They are working predictions for how the already visible components
compose, not new word meanings.

## What does and does not compress

The largest broad role shape is `CATALOGUE[NAME · ARG]`: five records reduce to
three strict meanings. Eight role shapes cross registers and cover 22 records;
thirteen recurrent role shapes join more than one strict meaning and cover 33
records. This supports a reusable card grammar—action, argument, relation,
order, and learned-name positions recur—even when the exact roots differ.

Whole microrecords nevertheless remain diverse: 107/120 strict templates and
84/105 role shapes are singletons. Forcing 135 records into a tiny sentence
list would erase real component combinations. The productive next level is
therefore shorter than a complete record: recurrent event and adjacent-event
fragments inside these records.

No component meaning, selected model, boundary, learned name, surface, recipe,
record, or page changes. Every sequence keeps its GDT479 default. The
deterministic validator passes 84/84 checks.
