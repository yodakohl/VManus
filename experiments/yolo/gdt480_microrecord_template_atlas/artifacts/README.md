# Artifacts

- `GDT480_MICRORECORD_TEMPLATE_ATLAS.md`: readable recurrent strict templates
  and the complete role-shape inventory.
- `gdt480_135_record_template_assignments.tsv`: both template assignments for
  every GDT479 record, with original reading and surface.
- `gdt480_strict_semantic_templates.tsv`: all 120 exact owner-neutral meaning
  templates and their recurrence, surface, recipe, and phrase support.
- `gdt480_role_shape_templates.tsv`: all 105 broader grammatical role shapes.
- `gdt480_template_coverage_summary.tsv`: recurrent, cross-page,
  cross-register, multi-surface, and multi-component coverage.
- `gdt480_result.json`: compact counts and preservation claims.
- `gdt480_validation.json`: deterministic 84-check validation report.

All outputs are regenerated by `src/run.py` and checked by `src/validate.py`.
