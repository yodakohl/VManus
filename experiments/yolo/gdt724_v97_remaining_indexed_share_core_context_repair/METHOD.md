# GDT724 method

## Question

Can the nineteen remaining CLOSED_AR_SHARE_ROUTE cards be closed with the
already established indexed-share model while retaining useful material,
state, quantity and action information, but moving replaceable product heads
and verbs out of portable word cores?

## Inputs

- V96 active lexical, position, held-audit, complete-dictionary and bound-span
  artifacts from GDT723.
- The seventeen exact GDT694 residual migrations and the two exact GDT693
  revisions that actually own the nineteen current cards.
- GDT693's selected R/OR model, GDT716's earlier core/context repair template,
  and GDT692's superseded fraction renderer as an explicit rival.
- src/V97_19_AUDIT_SPECS.tsv, which fixes all positions, neighbours,
  decisions, portable cores, local renderers, evidence and counterevidence.

## Method

1. Replay each target against its exact V96 lexical, context and held-audit
   row plus its true GDT693 or GDT694 semantic-source row.
2. Preserve the indexed share and every independently active quantity, state
   or material identity. Do not infer a free or universal ar word.
3. Separate the exact local renderer from the portable whole-card core. In
   particular, abmessen remains local to fdar and lldar; product words such as
   Droge, Ansatz, Auszug and Arznei remain local where used.
4. Compare three renderer models for every target. Retain an existing whole
   when stripping it would delete active information.
5. Rebuild all 324 active lexical readings, all 479 contexts and all 1,586
   dictionary readings. Keep scores and confidence levels unchanged.
6. Re-render bound span B003 once as
   vollständig getrocknete Charge aus erhitztem Holzanteil I; its visible
   left l supplies Holz, so karchees never exports Holz by itself.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Select a revised core only when it preserves the exact indexed-share relation
and active signals while removing an unsupported portable patient, product or
action. Retain a card when its existing exact-whole value is already the
smallest useful reading. Every target remains W1, exact-surface-bound,
component-export 0, score delta 0 and H0_NONE.

This is an exploratory German working dictionary, not recovered plaintext.
It establishes no language, phonetics, historical codebook word, ingredient,
disease, patient or cure. No new page, image, transcription, f84 or f84r is
opened.
