# GDT911 — one source-exposed syllabic metre collision test

## Decision note and disclosure

IDEA000141 proposes joint quantitative metre, grammar and one shared writing
rule to recover unknown wording. GDT605 supplies a stable 98-unit BPE inventory,
not evidence that those units are syllables. Its ordinary letter experiment,
GDT606 mixed-code score instability, GDT884 fixed Laufenberg equations and
GDT906 empty fixed-CV grammatical solution set remain unchanged.

The new unknown is whether even a strictly injective syllabic channel plus
quantitative metre and grammatical agreement identifies one six-verse reading.
A concrete counterexample changes the immediate decision: do not build an
unconstrained syllabic Latin decoder on the assumption that metre identifies
meaning. If the proposed pair fails, record that failure; it neither validates
the method nor licenses a repair series. If quantity or grammar cannot be
established, record an inconclusive source test. No global elapsed-time limit
is reimposed; scope is one passage, one alternative and one independent audit,
including publication. Stop once this question is answered.

This is an EXPLORATORY, SOURCE-EXPOSED certificate test. Root saw the Macer
text and conceived frigida/fervida before registration. It replaces the proposed
blind six-verse challenge with a smaller necessary identifiability check; it
is NOT a withheld-key recovery trial, success-rate sample, full decoder,
exhaustive Latin search or manuscript experiment. Source selection is disclosed,
not random. A found collision cannot disprove identification from a longer
passage or an independent key/semantic anchor.

## Fixed input and channel

Choulant, Macer Floridus (1832), printed pp57–58, verses716–721, main text.
Retain all six verses and all words. A is historical. B changes ONLY frigida
to fervida: B is a constructed grammatical alternative, not an attested variant.
The excerpt ends before the condition in vv722–723. Do not present it as a
complete independent medical prescription; this edge does not disappear by
punctuation normalization. The missing condition is a limit of this test.

Each of at most98 opaque symbols has one nonempty orthographic Latin syllable
image throughout the packet, with injective images. Use the explicit syllable
splits in SPEC.json, the same in A and B except fri/gi versus fer/vi. No nulls,
contextual keys, output padding, word splitting/merging, or per-verse changes.
Preserve word boundaries. Remove verse boundaries from the encoded stream.
Vowel quantity is lexical and contextual, not a freely assigned property of
an opaque symbol; e.g. written da can have different lexical quantities.
No claim that the selected Voynich BPE units obey this channel is made.

## Certificate and quantity contract

Check whole-packet equality patterns independently by all occurrence pairs.
Canonical first-occurrence symbol names only make the certificate reproducible;
they supply no deciphering information. Publish both injective keys and complete
back-decodings. Their inequality and identical ciphertext suffice to establish
at least two readings; do not claim these are all surviving Latin readings.

Publish a syllable-by-syllable hexameter witness for each verse. Syllables long
by position obey ordinary Latin consonant rules across words; natural lengths
follow the listed lexical/morphological analyses. Standard mute-liquid common
quantity is permitted (podagrae), and final anceps. No added words, synizesis,
hiatus, elision or invented medieval licences are used in this packet. Changed
words are independently dictionary-bound: frīgĭdus versus fervĭdus (Lewis and
Short1879); the first syllable of fervidus is heavy by rv, its second short.
Both feminine nominative singular forms agree with virtus, and both yield LSS.
The independent audit must flag any unsupported quantity or sentence boundary.

A finite boundary witness is recorded, not inferred from Voynich physical lines.
Even supplying the correct boundaries as extra information cannot distinguish
two equal cipher readings with the same six scans. Erasing those boundaries
cannot restore uniqueness. We do not run or certify a general boundary decoder.
