## Why the former meaning fails

- f10r locally contains `Y–Y–AIIN–Y`, ends the open Herbal line and has no
  attached commitment;
- f83r contains `Y–AIIN–Y–LCHE-C`, begins a short Biological field and is
  followed by an exact committed value;
- the outer Y identities are equal but are not independently identified as two
  distinct objects, quantities or sides;
- wrappers differ as expected from register/position rendering;
- no antecedent value is independently owned in both records;
- eight other `A–B–A` shapes show that repeated outer identity is not itself a
  semantic equality sign.

Consequently `PAIRED_EQUAL_VALUE` is rejected. `SHARED_ACTIVE_REFERENCE`,
`DYADIC_RELATION_FRAME`, `INDEXED_CHECKLIST_FRAME` and
`ORDINARY_FORMULAIC_PROSE` remain compatible. Ordinary copied formula prose is
the strongest concrete null; shared reference is the strongest semantic rival.

The chancery agent's page×position shuffle gives useful descriptive scale but
is not confirmatory: target recurrence is locally rare, yet searching for any
cross-page triple makes recurrence much less surprising. Preserve the formula,
