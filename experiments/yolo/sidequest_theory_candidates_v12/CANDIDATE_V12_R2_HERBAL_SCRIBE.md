## Decision

The historically safest result is the frozen
`NOT_DISTINGUISHABLE_WITH_TWO_OCCURRENCES` architecture. The two instances do
establish a cross-register formula of three exact cards:

```text
Y — AIIN — Y
```

They do **not** establish whether that formula means shared reference, equal
value, an anonymous dyadic relation, a checklist coordinate, or ordinary
formulaic prose. In particular, the Herbal occurrence supplies no two
independently recognizable ingredients, measures, qualities, or operations;
the Biological occurrence supplies two positions but no independently owned
operand meanings. A late-medieval recipe scribe could use compact formulae for
all of the proposed functions, so historical plausibility cannot break the
internal tie.

The controlled reverse reading is therefore:

> Repeat exact card Y, then exact card AIIN, then exact card Y; treat the two Y
> positions as formally ordered occurrences only. Do not infer equality,
> coreference, ingredient identity, quantity, or direction.

## Antecedent, operand, and consequence audit

### Possible antecedents

1. **Immediately prior card.** In f10r the candidate is another exact Y; in
   f83r the target starts a new field after a closed two-card field. These are
   different antecedent types.
2. **Prior field or active record state.** This is formally possible in f83r,
   but Herbal f10r has no field boundary before the target and offers several
   earlier opaque cards. Selecting one would be post hoc.
3. **Picture/page topic.** The f10r plant can own the article, while f83r's

   shared visible value or pair. A page topic is too broad to identify AIIN's
   referenced object.
4. **Measure or standard introduced earlier.** No exact measure sign or
   externally read quantity has been established in either record. AIIN also
   begins six fields and ends five, so a uniformly overt local antecedent is
   absent.

- In f10r, g7 and g9 can be called left and right slots, but g6 makes the left

- In f83r, field position cleanly orders the two Y slots. Yet repeated exact
  identity cannot show whether they are two entities, the same entity twice,

Therefore the two outer Y events are **positionally distinguishable but not

## Historical source-text test, 1400–1450

Late-medieval medical writing makes the proposed mechanisms plausible but also
supplies the critical controls:

- Recipe formulae and their abbreviations can be highly stable across scribes.
  A five-witness study finds recipe-specific abbreviations such as the
  drachm/ounce signs, *recipe*, *semis*, and *ana* substantially more
  consistent than abbreviations in running text. It describes *ana* as
  instructing the same amount of an ingredient as previously
  ([Oxford, *Digital Scholarship in the Humanities*](https://academic.oup.com/dsh/article/37/3/765/6401180)).
- That comparison does **not** license `AIIN = ana`. Historically, *ana* acts
  over an intelligible ingredient list and amount relation. Neither target
  witness provides two independently identified ingredients or a quantity.
- Precise apothecary-weight notation was not universal. A survey of
  fifteenth-century practical miscellanies notes that one physician's book
  repeatedly uses scruple/drachm shorthand, while comparable Harley and
  Sloane recipe collections instead use general measures such as handfuls and
  spoonfuls, when they specify quantities at all
  ([Cambridge, *Journal of British Studies*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-british-studies/article/here-is-a-good-boke-to-lerne-practical-books-the-coming-of-the-press-and-the-search-for-knowledge-ca-14001560/8217EBC4F6CE53F1084709587B7C2E12)).
  Thus “amount” is possible in this milieu but is not the default explanation
  for a mobile card.
- An early-fifteenth-century English medical codex from Yorkshire organizes
  many recipes as distinct items, marks recipe initials in red, uses boxed
  catchwords, and combines medicinal liquids, recipes, bloodletting days, and
  later additions by several hands
  ([Durham University, Cosin MS V.iv.8](https://reed.dur.ac.uk/xtf/view?docId=ark%2F32150_s1sn009x84b.xml)).
  This supports inherited topic, formulaic continuation, and copying practice,
  but not a modern table/database interpretation.
- The same practical-miscellany survey records explicit source-language
  continuations such as “another way” and “for the same.” Such reference
  language is historically ordinary, but it is ordinarily recoverable from
  a recognizable entry relation. Here neither occurrence stands as an
  identified recipe heading or explicit alternative.

The most plausible upstream source structure remains broad:

```text
illustrated Herbal article: descriptive or practical running entry,
                           aggressively abbreviated and physically reflowed

Biological record:         short inherited form cells,
                           some locally committed
```

A reusable source formula could survive both structures, but the surviving
evidence does not say which formula it was.

## Falsifiers and fixed-page predictions

The selected non-identification should be replaced only by evidence that
discriminates the operands, not by a third visually attractive occurrence.

1. **Equal-value falsifier/winner:** within the fixed pages, independently
   identify two coordinated ingredient/value operands around Y–AIIN–Y and show
   the same explicit measure or state applies to both. Without both operands,
   equality remains barred.
2. **Shared-reference winner:** predeclare an antecedent rule using only record
   boundaries and exact identities, locate the active value before both target
   paths, and recover a common downstream reuse better than matched mobile
   cards.
3. **Dyadic-frame winner:** show that outer-Y positions systematically accept
   separately recurring left/right operand classes in other exact fixed-page
   frames, with direction or closure consequences not seen in generic `A–B–A`
   controls.
4. **Checklist winner:** show target-equivalent coordinates recurring at the
   same inherited cell position across multiple fixed-page records. Current
   line-tail versus field-head placement predicts failure.
5. **Ordinary-prose winner:** find independently sourced parallel wording for
   a whole authorized record whose formulaic phrase aligns to this exact path,
   without selecting the donor from Voynich strings.
6. **Renderer-artifact winner:** demonstrate that the exact triple disappears
   under a predeclared alternative segmentation while matched exact paths do
   not. Wrapper differences alone are insufficient.
7. **Negative prediction:** on the existing seven-page census, AIIN-only and
   Y-only environments should continue to outnumber the target formula; a
   model that makes either card obligatorily dyadic is already false.
