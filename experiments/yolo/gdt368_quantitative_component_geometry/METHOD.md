# GDT368 quantitative component geometry

Status: **POST-IMAGE-SELECTION YOLO EXTRACTION FROZEN BEFORE FORMAL ACCESS**.

GDT367 found that `BROAD_CLOSED_FORM` and `COLORED_FILL` were present on all
27 localized Pharma cells and that only two components lacked a visible fork.
GDT368 does not reinterpret those states. It extracts three coarse geometric
coordinates from the same unchanged nearest-component assignments and the
same cached official-image contexts.

This schema was chosen after the GDT367 images were viewed, so its visual
capacity is post-selected. It is frozen before any GDT368 source-native formal
row is loaded, joined, or scored.

## Fixed coordinates

1. `MAJOR_BODY_COUNT`: count spatially distinguishable broad closed bodies in
   the selected component. Bodies separated by a narrow waist or separate
   outline boundary count separately. Bin as `ONE`, `TWO`, `THREE_PLUS`, or
   `UNCERTAIN`.
2. `TERMINAL_ARM_COUNT`: count visible terminal narrow arms/tendrils leaving
   the major body/bodies inside the context. Bin as `ZERO_ONE`, `TWO_THREE`,
   `FOUR_PLUS`, or `UNCERTAIN`. Marginal teeth or paint scallops are not arms.
3. `DOMINANT_HUE`: classify filled area, ignoring dark outline ink, as
   `COOL_GREEN_BLUE`, `WARM_RED_ORANGE_TAN_BROWN`, `MIXED_COOL_WARM`, or
   `UNCERTAIN`. `MIXED` requires both hue groups to occupy substantial visible
   area rather than a tiny accent.

When the context clips the selected component or counting is insecure, use
`UNCERTAIN`; no row is dropped. Calls are source-aware, single-observer,
postexposure `AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION`. They are geometric observations,
not plant-part labels or ownership claims.

## Capacity and analysis routing

Before formal access, report state counts by array and physical folio. A later
exploratory formal atlas is allowed only for axes with at least two secure
states within at least two physical folios and at least two arrays. Hue must be
treated as a page/array confound unless it has within-array mobility.

If admitted, the formal scan must use an anonymous predeclared feature library,
within-array state permutations where possible, folio/array diagnostics,
leave-one-folio-out results, a max-search correction over every tested feature
and axis, and explicit post-selection labeling. ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b are alternate
readings, not samples.

No object, ownership, role, word, language, plaintext, meaning, or translation
may be inferred. No f84 row or image is eligible.
