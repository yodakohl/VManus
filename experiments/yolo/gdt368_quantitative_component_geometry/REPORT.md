# GDT368 quantitative component geometry report

Status: **ADJUSTED_ASSOCIATION_DIRECTIONALLY_UNSTABLE**.

## Panel and search

The post-image-selected acquisition placed all 27 canonical Pharma
CONTACT/GAP cells into three coarse visual coordinates. Every coordinate had
at least two secure states within at least two physical folios and two arrays.
Only then was the existing f84-free GDT002 formal join opened.

All 27 loci have family-level agreement across ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b. A
state-blind library of 174 family/construction predicates collapsed to 27
unique locus masks; 81 endpoint/mask rows were scored. The primary control was
4,096 within-array label permutations with a maximum over all three endpoints
and every unique mask.

## Strongest weird correlation

Within-array dependence between `FAMILY_3GRAM:ACA` and terminal-arm count is
far stronger than the frozen null expected:

- support: 8/27 loci;
- feature present: 5 `FOUR_PLUS`, 3 `TWO_THREE`;
- feature absent: 11 `FOUR_PLUS`, 4 `TWO_THREE`, 3 `ZERO_ONE`, 1 uncertain;
- array-stratified conditional mutual information: **0.666667 bits/row**;
- local permutation p: **0.000976**;
- complete three-endpoint/max-mask p: **0.002929**;
- secure-only CMI: 0.663274 bits/row;
- LOFO gain: only **+0.753 bits**, positive on f100 and f89 but negative on
  f99;
- selector-paid LOFO gain: **-6.586 bits**.

This is not a stable shared mapping. In ordered arm-count bins, the
feature-present minus feature-absent direction is +0.5 in f100r/L2, +2.0 in
f89r2/L4, -1.0 in f99v/L1, and +1.0 in f99v/L2. The two f99 arrays therefore
point in opposite directions. Conditional mutual information correctly finds
strong dependence while being indifferent to which category a feature selects
in each array. The direction correction is documented separately and was made
before publication; the CMI and p-values did not change.

No row satisfies the final `INTERESTING_EXPLORATORY` rule. Other leading rows
either lose held-folio bits, reverse across arrays, or are page/hue confounds.

## Interpretation

The result is a real exploratory anomaly in this small, postselected panel,
but it behaves like array-local pairing rather than a transferable visible
geometry code. It is compatible with page-specific vocabulary, source-family
ecology, the subjective count bins, or chance structure not represented by
the within-array null. It does not justify mining `ACA`, renaming a body/arm
class, or launching a gloss test.

The old CONTACT/GAP results are unchanged. ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b are alternate
readings, not replications. All new visual calls are source-aware,
single-observer `AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION`; no OCR or automated vision was
used. No f84 row or image was accessed.
