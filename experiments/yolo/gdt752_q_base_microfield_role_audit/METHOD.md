# GDT752 method

## Question

When the two members of each fixed direct complete-form pair are prevented from
anchoring one another, do their outward historical microfields independently
support a prefix-quality/base-preparation division more often than the direct
non-q controls?

## Inputs

- GDT751's 44 reader-exact direct q/base contacts and 160-pair non-q control
  deck, whose stored contact counts materialize exactly 42 control contacts;
- the ten GDT751 `okeey` carrier cards under review;
- the guarded cached line/cell context inherited through GDT751;
- GDT744's fixed two-channel historical field rules.

## Method

For each adjacent pair, treat the two complete surfaces as separate cells. For
each cell, the partner-facing side is a hard boundary. Walk only outwards for
at most five cells. Stop at the line edge, a strict initial-head boundary, a
known close field, or another occurrence of either pair surface. The pair
members are never eligible anchors for each other.

An outward anchor must be reader-exact, W2/W3, non-compositional and free of a
retired patient gloss under the inherited clean-whole loader. The union of
those complete-whole axes enters the unchanged GDT744 historical channel
rules. A side is fully bounded only if its outward scan reaches a real boundary
before radius five; radius-five censoring cannot produce an active role card.

The exact directional hypothesis requires:

1. quality or stage evidence on the prefix/q side;
2. `PREPARATION` evidence on the unprefixed/base side;
3. neither corresponding reverse pattern; and
4. complete outward boundaries on both sides.

A broad sensitivity substitutes any carrier (`MATERIAL`, `PREPARATION`,
`PART`, or `INGREDIENT`) for exact preparation. Raw censored candidates remain
visible but are not renderer-active. The identical procedure is applied to all
42 direct contacts belonging to the 160 non-q pairs.

Finally, intersect the thirteen `qokeey/okeey` contacts with the ten GDT751
carrier cards. Retain spoken `PREPARATION` only where an exact complete
directional split survives; otherwise preserve it as a background hypothesis
and render the GDT750 HOT|END_STAGE axes with the carrier open.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Promote no global split unless complete q contacts show a directional pattern
that is absent or clearly weaker in the control contacts. A censored field,
symmetric evidence on both sides, or a pattern also present in controls cannot
license an occurrence card.

The experiment can correct occurrence-local field rendering and retain a
whole-pair lead. It cannot assign a character, prefix, morpheme, sound,
abbreviation, substring, lexeme, ingredient, plant, disease, cure, person,
vessel, unit, plaintext, or universal whole meaning. External anchor cards are
working semantic evidence, not decoded historical words.
