# GDT1119 method

## Question

Can a constant 29-value candidate dictionary preserve all 26 GDT1118 N values and express a bounded f77r.25 preparation/residue construction under either `shedar` = deposits (S) or contains (H), while keeping the f77r.26 carrier and tail unresolved?

## Inputs and scope

Use the frozen native GDT928 source in ZL3b and IT2a. Include every complete native paragraph containing any newly assigned exact form (`qokal`, `shedar`, `daror`) and all five paragraphs selected for GDT1118. Retain all raw groups, variant readings and paragraph boundaries. Do not borrow RF1b paragraph boundaries. The source includes the GDT1118 N dictionary, EL decision, the bridge-author assumptions and predecessor audit. No new image, reserve, sealed folio, source admission or outside contact is used.

## Method

Build two constant whole-form readers. Each copies the 26 GDT1118 N assignments unchanged and adds `qokal` = in-basin, `daror` = residue, and one `shedar` value: S “deposits” or H “contains.” Apply only the exact adjacent `dal chedy` constructor as `NOT(PURE)` to the following `daror` at the nominated core; do not give `dal` a free meaning.

Represent the nominated six groups at f77r.25 as a conditional subject/location/predicate/property/object graph. Preserve the other two groups in that line; they remain outside the proposed core. At f77r.26 retain the fixed known values for portion, flows, and of-liquid, then preserve the three exact unread forms `chcthy`, `lchedy`, `qokaly`. A discontinuous backward genitive is not added to complete the sentence. Keep all unknown groups and all competing source differences in the full readers.

The run produces the fixed candidate readers, focal graphs and whole-form comparison; the independent validator reconstructs those outputs from frozen source and model inputs. A PASS concerns source selection, exact assignments, unknown retention and reproducibility, not the truth of a meaning.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Neither graph predicts that the other event/state is absent: “contains” does not rule out deposition, and “deposits” does not establish complete separation or purity. Current data do not bind the same residue, preparation and basin to an independently established transition that would distinguish S from H. Unknown scope or carrier is missing binding, not success or contradiction. Retain both only as exposed C0 proposals if mechanically expressible. No semantic winner, word translation, significance, independent confirmation, decoder, or repaired prior failure may be claimed.
