# GDT582 manual sense audit

Status: `PASS_NO_MATERIAL_CORRECTION`

The final generated GDT582 edition was inspected independently after the
compiler and validator had stabilized.  The audit read all twenty complete
passage cards, all twenty-five event cards and the complete local-name layer.
Awkward colon prose by itself was not treated as a semantic failure; the test
was loss, host drift, a clearly impossible register reading or leakage of a
specific learned substance into productive running text.

## Result

- All 15,889 ledger slots occur exactly once across running-event and local-card
  traces.  No slot ID, position or primary governor is lost or duplicated.
- The twenty passage cards and twenty-five event cards agree exactly with the
  corresponding rows in the complete 793-statement and 5,122-event editions.
- No register-foreign specific substance was found in the running, statement
  or local-card output.
- Water, wine, olive oil and salt occur thirteen times in total and only as
  owner-bound `PHARMA` learned-name slots:
  - water: P1003-E0554, P1003-E0557, P1008-E1040, P1008-E1176,
    P1008-E1180 and P1008-E1228;
  - wine: P1003-E0555 and P1008-E1177;
  - olive oil: P1003-E0458 and P1008-E1093;
  - salt: P1008-E1176 and P1008-E1182.
- `Krankheit oder Beschwerde` occurs only at
  `RUNNING:G515-E0410@2` in G515-S046 under owner `F66R_TEXT_BLOCK_02`.
- `Heilmittel oder Heilwirkung` occurs only at
  `RUNNING:G515-E0438@2` in G515-S050 under owner `F66R_TEXT_BLOCK_03`.

Two conspicuous readings were retained.  P1003-E0555 contains two fixed `y`
name slots and therefore says `Wein, Wein`; P1008-E1176 contains separate `s`
and `d` name slots and says `Salz, Wasser`.  The first is redundant and the
second is a plausible compound, but neither loses a slot or creates a clear
impossibility under the exploratory replacement rule.

## Remaining boundary

This audit establishes internal completeness and catches obvious whole-passage
or cross-register failures.  It does not make the learned ingredient palette
or the productive register glosses historically true.  Those defaults remain
replaceable working meanings.
