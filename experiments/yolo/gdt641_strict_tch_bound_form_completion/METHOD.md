# GDT641 method

## Question

Do the two reader-exact strict one-hole forms exposed in advance by GDT640,
`tcheor` and `chetchy`, admit concrete complete meanings from the already
bound TCH, E, OR and Y families, and do those meanings survive every allowed
occurrence without collapsing a written component?

## Inputs

- Frozen V17 dictionary, glossary, line coverage and strict one-hole list from
  GDT640.
- GDT624's 48-cell quality shell and the f29 Herbal part-quality contact.
- GDT625's terminal-quality occurrence table, especially the TCH block.
- GDT628's OL/OR carrier lattice.
- GDT633's outer `ch+e` dry-attributive shell.
- The existing guarded 179-page token and alternate-reader panel. No new page
  or image is opened; `f1r` is excluded and `f84*` is forbidden.

## Method

1. Freeze exactly the two GDT640 strict targets and their source loci before
   consulting their complete occurrence circuits.
2. Materialize only the 179-page allow-list through the guarded selector, then
   recover every exact target occurrence and its ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b line.
3. Compare the complete observed form families:
   `tchol/tchor/tcheol/tcheor`, the `tchy/tchey/tcheey` ladder, and the
   `che+tch+y` cell against its hot and `cho-` sisters.
4. Preserve every written field in an explicit composition. In particular,
   parse `chetchy` as `ch+e+tch+y`, never as a CTH/leaf form.
5. Insert one exact-whole trial gloss at a time, rebuild all 4,128 line states,
   and audit every occurrence before accepting the next target.
6. Publish the final V18 dictionary, glossary, full line coverage, complete and
   one-hole passages, family atlas, component audit and sequential ledger.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Accept a target only if its frozen source becomes a new strict-complete line,
every target occurrence is reader-exact, the observed family binds every
visible field, and no occurrence introduces a hard opposite value. Opaque
neighbours may remain neutral because this is an exploratory exact-whole
default, but they may not be presented as confirming contexts.

No free `e`, `or`, `che`, `tch`, `y`, wrapper, substring, naked body or absent
cell is added. The German values are replaceable technical-codebook readings;
they are not confirmed plaintext, sounds, historical words, a language or a
solution of the manuscript.
