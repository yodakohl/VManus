# Method

One concrete whole qokeey=physical fire, declared after initial frequency count
but before newly selected paragraph reading. No blind-test claim. Compare the
two inherited distinct raiin senses with all other executable glosses unchanged.

Selector-first projections cover the same39 admitted source selectors. Exact
raw group match in any edition selects a locus; all three readings survive.
P-stream partitioning expands hits to complete paragraphs with interleaved L
separate. Non-P records remain whole separate records. Union GDT821's65 contexts
to retain earlier unresolved raiin occurrences, not just the new fire contacts.

The runner preserves raw group IDs, separator states, extended entities,
alternatives and repeats. Source-order gloss vectors in two worlds consume
each group exactly once. An exact physical-fire trial is not a q/base or heat
class test. Corpus counts are navigation, not semantic scores.

The coordinator read all320 native context records with all three readings.
Content and historical checks are in src/CONTENT_READING.md. No second semantic
reviewer or subagent was used. The separate validator, also by the coordinator,
uses the older source-audit helper rather than the new runner and reconstructs
selection through nearest explicit paragraph boundaries. It checks groups,
contexts, literal vectors, counts and five modified-output cases. This offers
implementation cross-checking, not independent confirmation of a word meaning.

No dictionaries adopted, no source or legacy artifacts rewritten, no new pages
or images. No authorial word-to-picture relation or score-ready GDT388 edge.
