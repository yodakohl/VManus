## The concrete proposal and what it does not solve

qokeey=Feuer? means the physical substance/fire discussed in a natural account,
not a heating instruction, heat, warmth, medicinal material or an EVA q-stem.
It was declared after the initial count but before reading the newly selected
paragraphs. Its motivation is a possible named carrier for raiin, not historical
proof or the superseded HOT/END renderer in GDT751.

The two original raiin models stay separate. Under ASCENT, fire moves upward;
under LIGHTNESS, fire has low physical heaviness. Historical general statements
need not describe an event happening at that instant. This physical possibility
does not tell us which Voynich word names fire, or choose between these models.

## Why fire-to-water is not an automatic historical rejection

Aquinas's De anima II.9.3 [80618] explicitly allows reciprocal generation:
`ex igne generetur aqua, sicut e converso` (eight words). Its immediate topic
is the distinction between generation and nutrition, not a bath recipe. This
is a medieval primary text through a modern edition, not an original1420 ink
inspection or a Voynich lexical key.
[Primary text](https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/can2.html#80618).

This matters narrowly. It prevents a modern-chemistry objection from alone
discarding a possible medieval elemental-change statement. It does not show
that f75r actually has fire as subject, that chedy means becomes, or that the
manuscript follows Aquinas. The awkward clause remains unresolved, not rescued
as a demonstrated translation. Water extinguishes fire, warming produces
vapour, and fire becomes water are not silently interchangeable statements.

The already considered De caelo I.5.5 [70865] gives fire lightness and discusses
the motion of light bodies. It makes both fire-rises and fire-light conceptually
available; precisely because it accommodates both, it does not choose raiin.
[Primary text](https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/ccm1.html#70865).
Other search hits from anonymously attributed continuations were not used to
claim a securely dated1420 source; no symbolic element-letter table is adopted.

## Working decision and next useful move

Retain qokeey=physical fire as a concrete C0 trial with these full-context costs;
do not adopt it as an identified word or replace it by warmth. It changes the
content task usefully: both becoming-fire and possibly fire-to-water have to
fit the same model. It does not resolve raiin because fire plausibly permits
both ascent and lightness in the historical account.

The next question is narrower than another noun inventory: compare the actual
qol.chedy.qokeey contexts f81r.20/f82r.21 with f75r.33's
qokeey...qol.chedy.qokain. Work out one explicit connection between the named
material and the process/result, preserving all intermediate words. If a new
qol or lshedy meaning is tried, show it at every occurrence in these complete
paragraphs; do not retrofit a single clever sentence. No universal grammar,
four-element diagram map, q-prefix semantics or GDT388-scored edge is claimed.
