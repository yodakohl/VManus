## Required decisions

Each candidate must decide where the opaque Herbal payload most plausibly
lives among these broad possibilities:

1. plant identity/name;
2. plant part or visible property;
3. habitat, moisture or water relation;
4. preparation/action;
5. application/use/condition;
6. amount, quality or classification;
7. nonsemantic index, exemplar or copied code;
8. abbreviated ordinary prose;
9. an explicit mixture with a learnable rule.

Every proposed role must be checked against every occurrence of its exact card
on the four fixed Herbal pages. An image may supply silent arguments, but text
placement alone is not ownership. Water/moisture is allowed as a live content
hypothesis, not a privileged answer.

One architecture must win. The selection must state what the first complete
Herbal record means at source-class level, what remains opaque, which cards
carry reusable structure versus page-local content, and what observation would
make the leading reading lose.
