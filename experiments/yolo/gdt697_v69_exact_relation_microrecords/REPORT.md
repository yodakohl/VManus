## What the topology says

Only f86v6.25 is a real two-step transformation chain. The written action at
#4 both produces the measured drug share and supplies the exact object heated
at #5. That licenses a named intermediate product, but the material after
heating is not separately named.

f80v.35 is different. Its two additions share the hot wood-preparation share
at #3 as destination, but no edge carries the output of the first addition
into the second. The two sentences therefore remain separate, and the second
is visibly B-tier rather than being hidden in “add twice”.

The other five windows contain one concrete operation each. A following noun
is not automatically its result. This matters at f105v.1, where the later
“finished wood-extract powder” remains outside the heat window, and at f77r.38,
where the second `qol` at #9 does not inherit the object of `qol` #6.
