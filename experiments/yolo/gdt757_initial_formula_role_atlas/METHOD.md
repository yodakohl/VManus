# GDT757 method

## Question

Can the high-recipe-body, high-line-initial-purity forms found by GDT756 be
separated into concrete late-medieval formula roles—recipe opening, entry
heading, indication, Item continuation, process continuation, addition,
quantity and closure—using their complete-form placement and following body,
without turning the modern EVA characters into Latin initials?

## Inputs

- GDT756's fixed 113-form line-initial body ranking;
- the same guarded 179-page cached transcription and line metadata inherited
  by GDT756, with `f84` and `f84r` rejected before materialization;
- GDT754's 172 source-composed surfaces, excluded from independent body axes;
- 33 explicit candidates for eleven exact wholes;
- eight dated historical formula comparators covering `Recipe/Accipe/Take`,
  `Item`, purpose headings, preparation headings, `deinde`, closure, quantity
  and mixing.

No new page, image or transcription is opened.

## Target selection

The fixed mechanical gate is:

```text
at least five reader-exact initial lines
recipe content+amount+process triad rate >= 0.20
global complete-form line-initial purity >= 0.70
```

It selects exactly `pchor`, `ychor`, `polaiin`, `pol`, `ycheol`, `ychol`,
`dcheol`, `paiin`, `qokchor`, `tshol`, and `ycheor`. `ykar`, `yteedy`, `qotor`
and `dchey` are retained as high-body-score but low-purity comparators.

## Role ranking

For every target occurrence the builder records paragraph start/end, paragraph
line index, section, complete written line and independently licensed body
axes. Each candidate role receives an explicit 0–100 placement-fit score from
the features that role should occupy. For example, recipe opening rewards
paragraph start plus content/amount/process bodies; Item rewards paragraph
continuation plus the same body; closure rewards paragraph end; addition
rewards internal position followed by content.

The score orders the three candidates supplied for each whole. It does not
score spelling similarity to the historical expression. The historical source
register only establishes that the proposed formula type belongs to a relevant
late-medieval register.

Near complete forms are then compared at EVA Levenshtein distance one. This is
a predictive whole-form diagnostic only: it grants no meaning to the differing
character or to any substring.

## Interpretation boundary

The output may install short, concrete, replaceable working readings for the
eleven complete forms and may identify an opening-versus-continuation contrast
between `pchor` and `ychor`, plus an internal/closure neighborhood around
`ycheol`, `ychol`, `dcheol`, and `ycheor`. It identifies no confirmed lexeme,
language, plaintext clause, phonetic value, Latin initial, manuscript glyph
value, substring value, plant, ingredient, quantity, process or disease. The
79-line reader deliberately leaves bodies in EVA rather than filling them with
generic prose.
