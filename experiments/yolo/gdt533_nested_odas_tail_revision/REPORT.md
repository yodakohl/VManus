## Why `kod|as` loses

The previous working route was also built from real old cards:

```text
dair | y | kod          | as
      ... | K+O+D_ADDR  | A_ADDR+S
```

It therefore remains a legitimate alternate. But it cuts through the exact
`odas` suffix, and its card boundary creates `D_ADDR+A_ADDR`. That ordered pair
occurs only once in all 4,576 old running events and never as
`D_ADDR+A_ADDR+S`. By contrast, selected terminal pairs `O+DA` and `DA+S`
occur six and two times; exact `das` and `odas` carry the latter hierarchy
directly.

The semantic result is also less redundant. The old route read two successive
address controls, roughly “... Ausführung · hier · hier · wählen”. The revised
tail reads one coherent operation, “Ausführung · Stufe II · wählen”.
