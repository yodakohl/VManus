## V69 result in one sentence

The best ten-page simulation is a **domain-neutral, exemplar-dependent
whole-card/register system** that a small workshop could learn with a master
copy and code sheet; its visible formal architecture supports two equally
coherent content editions but identifies neither one.

```text
ordinary unknown technical source
  -> picture/register-supported ellipsis
  -> four record-local memory registers
  -> 14 reusable exact control cards + 159 local exact exemplar cards
  -> fields, closures, line reflow and hand-specific rendering
  -> visible Voynich groups
```

This is not a letter-by-letter cipher model. It is also not a claim that every
visible group is a word. A learned whole card could stand for a source word,
short formula, value, relation, instruction or purely notational operation.

## Small exact-card dictionary

Eleven exact joint-tuple identities retain short creative mnemonics, always
with an implicit question mark:

```text
MASS?       prescribed value or quantity
ANWENDEN?   execute/use the current item
BEREIT?     ready/released state
ANSATZ?     current preparation or working stock
ZIEL?       destination slot
KLAR?       required endpoint or clear-run state
VORIGES?    previous item within the same record
ANTEIL?     selected portion/item
TEMPERIEREN? adjust or hold the working condition
SPÜLEN?     wash/flush operation
ABLASSEN?   drain/discharge operation
```

These are compact source-class handles, not German translations. In
particular, `KLAR?` does **not** mean the sentence “bis die Flüssigkeit klar
abläuft”, and no visible `ey`, `ch`, `chy`, `cho`, prefix, suffix or PAGE_HOST
inherits any part of these meanings.

Four exact control cards also license formal prompts:

```text
VORGABEPARAMETER?
STANDARDSLOT_SETZEN
LOKALEN_RELATIONSSLOT_SETZEN
AKTIVEN_ARBEITSSTAND_VERKNÜPFEN
```

One is the same exact card as `MASS?`, leaving 14 distinct control types. The
remaining 159 exact types are `UNKNOWN_EXEMPLAR_WHOLE_CARD`. Surface spelling,
substring and coordinate decomposition are display/renderer evidence only.

## Statement and memory model

Physical line ends are layout and reflow events, not mandatory statement
ends. Across the 11 prose records, 46 internal line boundaries were resolved
as a mixture of same-clause continuation, active-item resumption, parallel
cell, new clause and one unresolved case. The particularly useful f82r.3→4
case remains a cross-line continuation with intentional repeated entry card as
the strongest rival.

Four anonymous registers operate only inside one record and reset at its
boundary:

- `OWNER`: pictured or dossier owner;
- `ACTIVE`: current working item;
- `TARGET`: requested destination slot;
- `PREVIOUS`: immediately prior completed item.

They are execution memory, not nouns, referents or semantic roles discovered
in the manuscript. `CLOSE` finishes a field; it need not finish a statement.

## Section-specific reading

### Herbal

The picture supplies a plausible silent owner, but not a recoverable plant
name. Only 29/100 events receive parser support; 71 are exemplar content. Water,
root, leaf, flower, decoction and ailment details are permitted creative fills,
not card meanings. `VORIGES?` is record-local and cannot reach into the prior
plant article.

### Biological

The figures and vessel/tube layouts make bathing, washing, circulation,
filtering and drainage the strongest shared practical vocabulary. Only 90/281
events receive parser support; 191 are exemplar content. The medical edition
is strongest in B2 and parts of B1–B4, while B5/B6 read better as technical
support or maintenance records. No internal anatomy or gynecological lexicon
is claimed.

### Astro

The conservative formal reading is:

- `f67r2`: 7×12 selector with local adjuncts;
- `f68r1`: centre plus 28 spatial addresses;
- `f69v`: independent ordered 28-rule sequence.

There is no licensed f68↔f69 join, no fixed start or rotation, and no import of
prose card values. The 395 visible groups are page-local diagram labels or
fragments, not translated words.

## Why a workshop could learn it

A master teaches nine bounded lessons: page/record ownership, exact card
copying, the small control deck, four memory registers, field closure,
cross-line carry, section-specific templates, hand/register rendering and
correction against the exemplar. Multiple scribes can share the same learned
cards while differing in wrapper and positional realization. What they cannot
do is reconstruct an unseen rare card or its source value from productive
components; those must be copied or memorized.

## Main unresolved contradiction

The architecture is deliberately stronger than its semantics. A generic
exemplar/form system explains the formal packet without deciding whether its
upstream content is medicine, material processing, scheduling, mixed practical
notes or another domain. The full German texts are consistency probes: their
ability to cover every group demonstrates that a world can be made coherent,
not that it is the historical world.
