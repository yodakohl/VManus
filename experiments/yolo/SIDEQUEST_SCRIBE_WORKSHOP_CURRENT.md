## Best current mechanism

The writing system is best reconstructed as a small three-layer workshop
codebook:

1. **Productive technical abbreviations** — 56 short values build rare or new
   cards. They account for 1,008 visible events in the current reading.
2. **Learned formula cards** — 47 common component recipes have 97 positional
   or hand-conditioned surfaces and account for 1,002 prose events.
3. **Local nomenclator and address cards** — 501 image-bound events name or
   locate a plant, ingredient, vessel, station, figure, ring place, value or
   grade.

This resembles a workshop mixture of productive brevigraphs, memorized
technical formulae and a local codebook. The longer learned card is spoken
first; otherwise the 56 abbreviations are composed from left to right. The
image owner determines whether a shared value is expanded as workshop action
or diagram address.

## Learned formula deck

The 47 memorized recipes cover 1,137 events in both channels, of which 1,002
are prose. Important cards are:

- `Y` → `dy/chey/y/chy/shy/sy` → **DIESER POSTEN**;
- `OL` → `ol/chol/qol/sol/ls` → **DAMIT WEITER**;
- `AIIN` → `daiin/aiin` → **NACH SOLLMASS**;
- `CHD+Y` → `chedy/chdy/chedchy` → **DIESEN POSTEN UMSETZEN**;
- `SHED+DY` → `shedy` → **ABSETZEN; ENDE**;
- `OK+E+DY` → `qokedy/okedy` → **KURZ ANSETZEN; ENDE**;
- `OK+EE+DY` → `qokeedy/okeedy` → **LÄNGER ANSETZEN; ENDE**;
- `OK+AIN` → `qokain/okain/chokain` → **EINE PORTION ANSETZEN**;
- `OK+AIIN` → `qokaiin/okaiin` → **NACH SOLLMASS ANSETZEN**;
- `L+CHD+DY` → `lchedy` → **WEITER UMSETZEN; ENDE**.

The surface is therefore not the dictionary. A q-, s-, ch- or d-form can be a
positional realization of the same learned card recipe.
