# GDT390 method — frozen Q20 inter-record pointer census

## Question

Do the 170 already established star-defined Q20 records on eight non-f84
physical folios contain an author-visible device that points from one complete
record/star to a different complete record/star?

This executes GDT388 batch B05. It is acquisition only. No Voynich group
surface, formal family, PAGE_HOST, tuple, record-template score, or semantic
label may be inspected or used.

## Frozen record frame

The exact frame is a metadata-only projection of the published GDT114 record
inventory. The projection retains only:

`unit_id, page, physical_folio, star_ordinal, OPEN locus, BODY line loci, record line count`.

The loader reads only the fixed metadata prefix, rejects any `f84*` page before
retention, and never parses, retains, or displays the later compiler/profile
fields. ZL3b supplies the one-record census; IT2a and RF1b are alternate
readings, not additional records. The expected frame is 170 records on 13 pages
and eight physical folios.

Page and record review order are deterministic SHA-256 orders fixed in the
pre-image artifact. No page or star record may be added or removed after image
review begins.

## Direct visual review

1. Map only the exact 13-page allow-list to official Yale IIIF canvases. Reject
   a mixed canvas carrying any f84 label before retaining other fields.
2. Inspect each complete page and record/star region without reading or
   transcribing glyph identities.
3. Record every visible line, arrow, pointer, tail, bracket, or other device
   that might link two different star-defined records. Absence is a valid page
   observation.
4. Admit direction only from a visible arrowhead or other unambiguous authorial
   direction device. Page order, star ordinal, physical proximity, common star
   rendering, OPEN/BODY hierarchy, and text reading order are forbidden.
5. A candidate is eligible only when both source and target are distinct exact
   frozen unit IDs, ownership is singular, direction is independent, and a
   matched alternative target remains mobile.

Allowed page states are:

- `NO_INTER_RECORD_POINTER`
- `AMBIGUOUS_CROSS_RECORD_GEOMETRY`
- `POINTER_WITH_UNRESOLVED_SOURCE_OR_TARGET`
- `POINTER_WITH_EXACT_SOURCE_AND_TARGET`
- `UNMAPPED_OR_UNREVIEWABLE`

## Frozen capacity and stop

- Formal scoring is prohibited in GDT390.
- All 13 pages and all candidates/ambiguities must be published.
- Fewer than 50 eligible directed edges or fewer than five physical folios
  closes this Q20 pointer application at acquisition capacity.
- Meeting capacity would authorize only a later separately frozen score.

## Reviewer provenance

The review is a single-AI exploratory direct visual census, not a human or
independent confirmation. The Q20 pages have prior repository exposure; this
is a procedural pre-image freeze, not a claim of observer-pristine blinding.
No OCR, automated image classification, CLIP/embedding retrieval, or captions
are permitted.

## Claim ceiling

GDT390 can establish only Q20 inter-record-pointer geometry and capacity. It
cannot establish a parent, reference, continuation meaning, heading, recipe,
syntax, POS, word, language, plaintext, or translation.

## Executed outcome

The frozen census was completed without modifying the design. All 13 pages and
170 record regions were reviewed. Every page received
`NO_INTER_RECORD_POINTER`: marginal star-like record markers are visible, but
no arrow, line, bracket, tail, path, or other authorial device connects one
record/marker to another. No ambiguous candidate required endpoint
localization. The eligible edge count is zero on zero folios, so the 50-edge /
five-folio capacity gate fails and formal scoring remains prohibited.
