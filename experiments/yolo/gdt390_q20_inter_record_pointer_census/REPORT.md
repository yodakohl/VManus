## Interpretation

The star markers organize Q20 records visually, but the current images provide
no independent directed record-to-record relation suitable for a parent,
reference, or continuation test. Page order, star ordinal, proximity, common
marker rendering, OPEN/BODY hierarchy, and text reading order remain forbidden
substitutes for an authorial pointer.

This is a complete negative acquisition result for the frozen Q20 pointer
frame, not evidence that Q20 records lack abstract relations in their unknown
content.

## Provenance and limits

The review is a single-AI exploratory direct visual census, not a human or
independent visual confirmation. The pages had prior repository exposure, so
the freeze was procedural rather than observer-pristine. The validator checks
frame/mapping coverage, record accounting, artifact hashes, access claims, and
the capacity decision; it does not independently reproduce the visual
judgments.

No OCR, automated image recognition, CLIP/embedding retrieval, or captioning
was used. No new f84 content was accessed. The separately disclosed earlier
metadata-only f84r display remains unchanged.

## Decision

Close the Q20 direct-pointer application at acquisition capacity. Do not score
the GDT387 relation instrument on Q20 and do not replace missing visible edges
with inferred order or recurrence.

## Claim ceiling

GDT390 establishes only the absence of eligible visible directed pointers in
this complete frozen Q20 census. It establishes no parent/reference relation,
record function, syntax, POS, meaning, language, plaintext, or translation.
