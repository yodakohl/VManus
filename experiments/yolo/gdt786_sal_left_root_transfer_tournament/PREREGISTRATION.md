# GDT786 exploratory registration

This is a throughput-oriented transfer tournament, not a blind decipherment
claim. All fourteen known `sal…` occurrences are admitted. The useful question
is whether the proposed root makes their outside geometry and their concrete
readings more predictable than learned-whole and matched-root alternatives.

The primary comparison is fixed to ten remainders with at least one
non-`sal` same-remainder control. `salf` and `salshcthdy` are sensitivity-only.
The candidate mechanisms are drug/material composite, root-only family,
remainder-only family, learned whole, orthographic/null family, and salt
composite. The visual resemblance of EVA `sal` to Latin *sal* scores zero.

A negative transfer result removes only portable component permission. It does
not delete `sal=Droge` as a standalone working card or form-specific readings
such as `saldal=abgemessene Rohdroge` if their own whole fields support them.
Every target retains a practical default and explicit counterreadings.

Ceiling: C2 observed complete-word boundaries, C1 formal left family and at
most form-specific whole roles, C0 concrete displays; zero plaintext,
specific-substance identity, EVA spelling/sound value, free component, unseen
form, new page, image, OCR, transcription, f84, or f84r.
