# GDT786 method — `sal…` left-family transfer tournament

## Question

Do the twelve observed complete words beginning with literal `sal` behave like
a productive **drug/material head plus a bound remainder**, or are they better
handled as a family of learned complete names whose internal resemblance does
not predict meaning?

## Inputs

The primary set is ten comparable remainder types at twelve reader-exact
positions: `salal`, `salar`, `saldal`, `saldam`, `saldy`, `salkeedy`, `salo`
twice, `salol`, `saltar`, and `saly` twice. `salf` and `salshcthdy` add two
sensitivity positions because their remainders have no same-remainder control.

All current-reader material is reconstructed through GDT782's guarded loader
and the inherited 179-page allow-list. Stolfi rows are requested through the
same guarded selector for the thirteen admitted target pages only. No new page,
image, OCR, or transcription is opened; f84 and f84r remain forbidden.

GDT785 supplies the standalone working card `sal=Droge`, the exact string
family, and the matched three-character roots. GDT734/GDT739 were locked during
candidate design, but no semantic neighbour label enters the scored geometry.
GDT515 and GDT585 supply older local-owner counterexamples;
GDT735 supplies period record architecture; GDT773 supplies the corrected
contextual `ol` role. None supplies an EVA sound or spelling identity.

## Method

1. Reconstruct all fourteen target occurrences and mask the target whole.
   Measure only outside-line formal geometry: register, line and paragraph
   placement, normalized position, distance to each edge, and the exactness,
   length bin and frequency bin of the immediate left and right token. Neither
   neighbour spelling nor a semantic renderer label enters the score.
2. For each of the ten primary remainders, construct a type-balanced
   same-remainder control from the eleven other GDT785 matched roots. A control
   whole counts once regardless of token frequency.
3. Compare fixed models: additive `sal + remainder`, standalone-root only,
   remainder only, leave-one-type-out `sal…` family, a learned-whole backoff,
   same-remainder null, and length/frequency null. Use target-masked Gower
   distance; smaller is better.
4. Run the inverse recognition test: for each target cell, ask whether the
   true three-character root is recovered from the candidate-root deck. Compare
   `sal` with the 29 scoreable non-`sal` control cells. Audit the two repeated
   wholes `salo` and `saly` against all equally frequent, equally long forms.
5. Count every raw and reader-exact separated `sal X`. Query the available
   Stolfi loci to see whether its legacy boundary actually falls at `sal|X`.
6. Independently read all fourteen fields under three historically possible
   mechanisms: drug composite, learned whole, and salt composite. Publish one
   practical C0 default plus explicit rivals for every complete target form.
7. Preserve assumptions at the level they survive: a failed portable transfer
   does not erase a useful form-specific composite, and no single countercase
   erases the exploratory `sal` hypothesis.

The primary Gower score weights fourteen root-facing/global fields and four
right-facing fields once each. Sensitivities balance the two sides, remove
register, isolate placement or flanks, enlarge the root deck, change type
weighting, remove singleton lines/types, remove repeated targets, and remove
the large `cho` control. The asymmetry, coarse bins, correlated placement
features, sparse same-remainder matrix and small ten-type cohort are explicit
limitations; this is a fast transfer discriminator, not a semantic decoder.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Portable semantic-root transfer requires more than an attractive phrase: the
additive model must materially beat both nulls, the true `sal` root must be
recoverable at least as well as control roots, the repeated wholes must be
coherent, and the result must survive removal of singleton lines, repeated
targets, and the large `cho` control. If those conditions fail, keep `sal` as
the standalone C1 working role and the observed `sal…` set as a C1 formal
family only. Form-specific complete-whole readings may remain at C0/C1 where
their own remainder and local field support them.

GDT786 may assign twelve observed complete wholes practical replaceable
defaults and distinguish learned names from three or more form-specific
composite leads. It may not split a written whole, export `sal` or any
remainder to unseen forms, identify salt or another substance, or claim a
plaintext lexeme, language, sound, glyph value, unit, plant, disease, or cure.
