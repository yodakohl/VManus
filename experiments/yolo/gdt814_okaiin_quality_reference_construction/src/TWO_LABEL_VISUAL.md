## f89r1.14: positive local grouping, unknown owner

Both words are visible together on one short baseline, with an internal
word gap and a much larger separation from neighbouring inscriptions. They
are above the broad, bent brown root/plant-part drawing in the middle band.
The preceding prose ends above this band; the following prose begins below
its drawings. Neither word is a continuation of those prose lines. This is
positive visual support for one two-whole short inscription.

The local scene contains an ornate vessel-like object and differentiated
plant/root fragments. The nearest large form has a curved thick body, pale
internal divisions, and branching extensions. Whole material, plant part,
property, amount or a reference to an entry are concrete possibilities.
There is no leader proving that the words name this one form rather than a
part or group. No powder pile, explicit preparation process or measurement
is depicted; "powder", a species and a numerical dan are not established.
