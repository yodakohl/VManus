## Historical bridge and the counterexample to overclaiming

Sloane332, probably around or soon after1420 according to its scholarly
catalogue, has a transcribed incipit describing Aries through fire, warmth
and dryness. This is a catalogue transcription, not our own original-folio
collation. [Dated manuscript and incipit](https://ptolemaeus.badw.de/ms/364).

Edited Circa-instans comparanda and the near-contemporary Latin9333 herbal
rubrics use corresponding qualities for medicinal plants. Edition layers,
written copulas/degree values and actual source dates remain explicit.
[Latin comparanda](https://celt.ucc.ie/published/G600005.html),
[Latin9333 transcription](https://medimanus.chs.harvard.edu/app/en/pharmantica/tacuinum_sanitatis).
These historical descriptions are not treatment advice or a Latin-language key.

The Archanum edition explicitly reports a horoscope-diagram form for its
sign/quality array. Crucially, it contains cold/dry as well as warm/dry.
Its manuscript witnesses are not both circa1420; the historical note gives
their distinct date ranges. [Edition, section4](https://ptolemaeus.badw.de/ms/140/210/text/1).
Thus the external material/astral bridge supports the possibility of a QUALITY
architecture but does not select warm over cold. The Aries example identifies
no Voynich figure, star or central pictogram. Calidus also does not fix the
modern German boundary between warm and hot.

The construction-level cold countervalue is a stated post-comparison limit,
not a hidden fifth scored model: replacing the property with cold would keep
the same word order and degree constructions. Without an independently known
thermal referent or a meaningful contrast, this exact identity stays open.
