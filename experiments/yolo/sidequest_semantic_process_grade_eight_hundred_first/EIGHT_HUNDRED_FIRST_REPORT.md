# Sidequest Pass 801: process verbs against grade and endpoint

This pass compares `SHED`, `CHK`, `P`, and `LSH` on the same fixed prose pages.
The four short meanings survive all 28 occurrences, but they do not share one
tail and therefore are not a four-way replacement deck.

## One root becomes genuinely productive

`CHK = WÄRMEN` now joins the productive core. It occupies three cells of a
small grade/endpoint board:

- `CHK + E + Y` = **dies kurz wärmen** (`cheky`, 3 occurrences);
- `CHK + EE + Y` = **dies länger wärmen** (`cheeky / chkeey`, 3);
- `CHK + EE + DY` = **länger wärmen; Schluss** (`chkeedy`, 1).

The board predicts the three remaining semantic cells without changing the
meaning of any component:

- `CHK + E + DY` = kurz wärmen; Schluss;
- `CHK + EEE + Y` = dies vollständig wärmen;
- `CHK + EEE + DY` = vollständig wärmen; Schluss.

Candidate spellings are recorded separately. None collides with an observed
surface on the fixed pages; the full-grade open cell has two possible renderer
forms and is intentionally not forced to one.

## Three values stay on the learned strip

- `SHED = ABSETZEN` occurs in 3 cards / 15 events, but its variants change
  endpoint or add cooling rather than form a controlled verb swap.
- `P = FÜLLEN` occurs in 3 cards / 3 events, once alone and twice inside a
  transfer construction. It may still be a recipient marker rather than a free
  verb.
- `LSH = WASCHEN` occurs in 2 cards / 3 events with unrelated tails.

They remain useful concrete workshop readings, just not freely generated roots
yet. The productive inventory is now core19; twelve recurrent strip values
remain.
