# Method

GDT1177 uses the frozen GDT1176 projector and counters with a new explicit
SPEC.json. Only the listed binding set, collection list and experiment label
change. GDT1176's files, original outcomes and rules are untouched.

No glyph encoding is implemented or claimed. Group identity is the full source
word tuple. Flattening must recover all source words and punctuation in order.
Use the predeclared joint gate and inspect each collection separately.
