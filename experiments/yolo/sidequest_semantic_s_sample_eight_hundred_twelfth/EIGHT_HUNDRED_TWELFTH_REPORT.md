# Sidequest Pass 812: S is a sample, not another portion

The singleton card `ches = CH+E+S` occurs at the small basin stations in f82r.
The next card is `aiin = SOLLMASS`, followed by a longer next item and measured
setting operations.

The old `S=TEIL` produced the awkward instruction “take briefly and divide.”
MENGE and ANTEIL duplicate the established `AIN=PORTION`; REST requires an
unseen prior depletion. The clean reading is:

> `S = PROBE`

Thus `ches` says:

> **Kurz eine Probe entnehmen.**

The three quantity-like terms now have distinct workshop jobs:

- `S` PROBE: material taken for checking;
- `AIN` PORTION: a working quantity used in the process;
- `AIIN` SOLLMASS: the prescribed amount or setting to reach.

The full statement becomes: guide and transfer from the source at the target;
take a quick sample; continue according to the prescribed measure; set briefly;
fill inward, transfer, and close.

`S` joins specialist core33. Four plausible semantic recipes are recorded
without invented surface spellings. Next, resolve `DA` in `daiiin=DA+IIN`:
does it mean SECOND, AGAIN, NEXT, or a numbered stage marker?
