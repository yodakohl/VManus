## Why the whole still fails

The author recorded three directly unused reference words: `dor` at .3G005,
`or` at .8G001 and `dor` at .9G003. Independent review also identified `dor`
at .7G004. Its source reference feeds .8G001, but that chain ends in the already
dead result. An intermediate consumer alone does not give the earlier word a
terminal contribution. All four gaps remain; the frozen author account retains
its original three-gap count, with this correction recorded separately.

The account's broad consumer lists recursively include nested history objects.
That is payload reachability, not proof that every listed field was actually
read. The five direct edges above have narrower executable field checks and
must not be conflated with the larger ancestry lists.

The frozen `ASSERT_OUTPUT_PROVENANCE` family promises common input/preparation
provenance. Its implementation checks distinct physical IDs and solid/liquid
phases, but does not compare origins or preparation identities. The baseline
constructor does generate common provenance; that extensional fact is not an
implemented provenance discriminator. No validator or author repair upgrades
this limitation to a pass.

The cost is23 function families,40 body unit assignments, nine constants,
63 finite whole/hardchunk licenses, two explicitly listed definition/reference
predicates inside `qol`, and the declared owner, state, scope and selection
policies. Many forms are aliases for repeated views or source assertions.
Their coherent execution supplies no independent support for that vocabulary
or for the historical plausibility of this whole repetitive account.

## Decision and limits

Retain the five direct component edges as conditional C0 construction results;
reject an unqualified whole-reading success. Do not patch the four dead words,
change DECOCTION, reinterpret `os`, split atomic units or reopen the older motif
and fixed-role failures. This finite `or` does not extend to the retained
`sain or or aiin` counterexample: its own output is not its required input type.
