3. Enumerate every other token on those eight lines, producing 65 neighbor
   records. A target can receive field evidence only from reader-exact complete

4. Reconstruct GDT734's 770-reading/769-surface W2/W3 axis pool for diagnostic
   continuity, but gate every displayed donor through later provenance. In
   particular, GDT754 blocks the obsolete source-built `okeol` sentence and

   genaue Funktion und Träger offen.” It lies at distance five and contributes

   its inherited MATERIAL axis is unchanged.
5. Match previously registered exact GDT759 constructions. Exactly one touches
   a target directly: `or aiin` before external `chedor`, with the working
   construction “drei Portionen” and 36 exact global pair occurrences.
6. Score all 23 enumerated candidate cards by proximity-weighted whole-form
   field axes, opposition, construction redundancy, content complement,

   scores organize the evidence; they are not lexical probabilities.
7. Preserve three differently framed manual assessments for each form: local
   field grammar, historical apothecary register practice and a surface-first

   all dissents visible.
8. Patch only the six original GDT781 target spans. The eight outside lines are
   emitted as aggregate card audits with explicit field delimiters, not as new
   renderer licences. All other 370 renderer rows, precedence decisions and
   token owners remain inherited.
9. Emit an eight-edge acquisition packet and run the GDT388 gate. These text
   relations are deliberately ineligible for scoring and remain
   `VALID_ACQUISITION_NOT_SCORE_READY`.
10. Independently validate source hashes, counts, coordinates, exactness,
    candidate/manual/final tables, provenance sanitization, renderer
    invariants, zero-credit fields and edge intake, then byte-replay every
    runner-owned artifact plus the report in a temporary directory.
