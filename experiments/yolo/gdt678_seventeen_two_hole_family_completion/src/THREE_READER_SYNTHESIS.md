# GDT678 three-reader synthesis

## Independent backgrounds

### Late-medieval recipe copyist and family compositor

The copyist treats the page as a compact workshop register rather than prose.
They expect learned drug heads, productive hot/cold/dry/moist additions, small
quantity endings and occasional repeated or bound signs. Their first duty is
to preserve a component across every occurrence; their weakness is a tendency
to make visible repetition semantic even when another reader restores a lost
middle sign.

### Practical apothecary and process reader

The apothecary asks what can physically be done with the named drug, fraction,
dose or preparation. They distinguish take, measure, heat, cool, dry, soak and
finish from the resulting material. Their first duty is a usable recipe
reading without generic filler; their weakness is a tendency to turn a compact
register into smoother procedural prose than the token order alone proves.

### Historical pharmacology and scribal-boundary reader

The historical reader compares the local information architecture with
near-period recipe books, antidotaries, degree tables, simples glossaries and
scribal abbreviation practice. They treat ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b as alternate
readings of one manuscript and prioritize shared letter sequences over one
reader's spaces. Their first duty is to catch false words created by a single
transcription boundary; their weakness is that genre analogies cannot identify
Voynich signs or substances.

## Agreement

All three readers independently counted 101 exact target positions on 82 lines
and 52 already admitted pages. None found a context that required a completely
different semantic core for any of the 34 ZL3b surfaces. They agreed most
strongly on:

- `olchey`: Holzdrogenansatz, bis Mittelstufe getrocknet;
- `chotar`: erste Fraktion des abgekühlten Trockenansatzes;
- `keeey`: stark erhitzt, letzte or Endstufe III;
- `oteor`: eine Portion kalten Ansatzes der Mittelstufe;
- `pchedaiin`: a measured three-dose dry-powder result;
- `oltain`: Holzdrogenansatz, kalt auf Stufe II;
- `okiin`: erhitzter Ansatz, Form III;
- the command cores in `qodor`, `qochar`, `qokeod` and `qotchody`.

The synthesis keeps eleven newly visible command cards in the 17 source lines:
`cphdor`, `dold`, `lldar`, `olkechy`, `qochar`, `qocho`, `qodor`, `qoin`,
`qokeod`, `qotchody` and `qy`. Nominal rivals remain explicit rather than
being silently discarded.

## Deliberate choices at disagreements

`qokeod` is composed from the already learned whole `qokeo`, “prepare a hot
extract”, plus terminal close `d`. This is more predictive than rebuilding the
same letters only from shorter heat-stage pieces. The middle-stage heated
preparation remains the rival because `qoked` and `qokeoy` support it.

`qoin` uses learned `oin`, “second preparation”, under a take command. The
fully productive `qo+in`, “take preparation in form II”, remains a nearly tied
rival. This choice tests the project-wide mixed model: learned wholes can sit
inside productive commands.

`qocho` similarly keeps learned `cho`, dry preparation, as the object of a take
command. “Dry and prepare” remains the process rival.

`qoeeo` stays nominal in the aligned deck as “second maceration preparation”,
using the learned `oeeo` whole. `lldar` supplies the following explicit measure
action. This avoids counting two commands merely because both words begin with
q/l-like command-compatible shapes.

`rr` is not accepted as an ordinary reduplicated word. ZL3b alone has `rr`;
both independent alternate readers have `rchr`. The practical default is
therefore “getrocknete Wurzeldroge”, composed from the existing root and dry
families. “Two root parts” remains the named ZL3b-only rival and receives no
productive reduplication rule.

`keo` is retained as a bound middle-heating preparation component for all five
ZL3b occurrences. At the three `keo r` boundaries, the practical renderer
honors the joined reader form `keor`; on the source line f7r.2 it outputs the
already learned whole “heiße Drogenportion”, not the misleading pair “Ansatz ·
Wurzel”.

At f86v6.4 both alternate readers join `l karchees`. The aligned deck preserves
both ZL3b positions, while the practical renderer prints one compound:
“vollständig getrocknete Charge der ersten heißen Holzfraktion”.

`yey`, `qy`, `lldar`, `rr`, `keo`, `aiindy`, `dolkain` and `otardy` remain the
lowest-confidence cards. They close explicit slots but carry exact replacement
conditions; none licenses a manuscript-wide phonetic or substring rule.

## Historical comparison limit

Royal MS 12 G IV, Harley MSS 2378/2374/2558 and Wellcome MSS 683/541 show that
recipes, learned substance names, synonym glossaries, weights, fractions,
qualities and ordered degrees could coexist in medical compilations close to
1420. The palaeographic comparison makes boundary variation unsurprising.
These sources support the proposed notation architecture only. They do not
show that the Voynich manuscript is Latin, German, Arabic, an antidotary, or a
direct copy of any cited work.
