## Exact feasibility without circular coordinates

For a selected sign set S, let c(w,s) count definite complete label w and
n(t,s) count source tuple t. There is an edge w->t exactly when, for every
s in S, c(w,s)<=n(t,s). Different definite label types must take distinct
tuples because the renderer is deterministic. Injectivity of the renderer
requires each known output to take only one tuple. A matching saturating all
known label types is necessary and sufficient for a complete assignment in
this relaxation: all remaining source occurrences can fill the retained
UNKNOWN slots. Per-sign totals are exactly30, making those leftovers equal
the unknown capacity. Source tuples with no observed known label receive
fresh distinct anonymous outputs wherever needed. The omitted degree order
is never reconstructed or claimed as evidence.

The runner must produce maximum matchings and, on failure, a verifiable Hall
certificate (set of label types with fewer candidate tuple neighbours). A
matched subset is not a successful reading. Report both full graphs and
compact certificates; no favorable singleton is selected from a failed model.

For each feasible discovery graph, force each edge in turn and test whether
it belongs to any saturating matching. Publish the entire allowed-edge set
and its additional-sign count vectors. These marginal sets are linked by
the original matching constraint; their Cartesian product is not the solution
set. Distinct tuples with identical count vectors are observationally
indistinguishable in this test and must be grouped/reported. No arbitrary
matching becomes a selected key and restart agreement is irrelevant.
Freeze discovery inputs, graphs and all candidate predictions in
DISCOVERY_FREEZE.json before additional labels are opened. If discovery
already fails, retain the failure; the additional and final all-ten-sign
censuses may still be reported as diagnostics, never as rescue/confirmation.
