can generate the admitted zodiac labels under the fixed deterministic injective

other historical attribute systems, homographic/contextual writing or distinct
contents in the labels.

neither source model has any tuple occurring in both signs. ZL also has

## Ambiguity, confirmation capacity and decision

All six discovery graphs are infeasible. Their supported discovery-edge sets
and additional selected-key predictions are therefore empty; no partial mapping
was promoted. The complete source predictions still cover the additional census.
T3 and T4 give the same discovery result and are both contradicted, so the test
does not choose whether domicile information is actually present. Nor does it
isolate which member of the content/renderer conjunction is false.

Discovery uses f70/f71; seven complete additional signs use f72/f73, with no
shared physical leaf. Taurus spans f71/f72 and enters only the final consistency
census. Thus there are two additional physical leaves with substantial diagnostic
coverage, but zero surviving candidate to confirm. The three transcriptions are
alternate readings of one manuscript. All pages were previously exposed and
there is **zero fresh independent meaning-confirmation capacity**. No reserve,
f84/f84r, f116v or new Voynich image was opened.
