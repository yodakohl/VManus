See research_registry/work_batches/seven_hours_20260914/ZODIAC_TUPLE_DECISION.md
and ZODIAC_UNORDERED_TUPLE_NOVELTY_AUDIT.md. Root read GDT337's report, the

The selected first test is narrower than the critic's prospective shared-code
decoder. It asks for feasibility of a deterministic **injective whole-tuple
renderer** on complete unordered per-sign label multisets. It builds no planet
code, computes no fitted circular coordinate and claims no score or significance.
A bipartite matching can decide the necessary consistency relation before any
compositional decoder is considered. Failure of this conjunction does not prove
that a homographic, context-sensitive or different-field representation is false.
Those alternatives are not to be introduced as repairs after results.

Whole-sign ownership comes from published metadata. In particular the two
Aries panels span physical f70/f71 and Taurus spans f71/f72. Ten signs cannot
be treated as ten independent physical leaves. Pisces has29 figures but30label
slots; the record hypothesis concerns all30slots, not precisely30human figures.
Gemini's one unlabeled slot is retained as unknown, not assigned a chosen degree.

Prior exposure includes published inventory sizes and the approximate80%label
hapax observation. No new label strings/count vectors were inspected during
source/novelty preparation. This is exploratory hypothesis testing on already
exposed material; no independent meaning confirmation follows from survival.
