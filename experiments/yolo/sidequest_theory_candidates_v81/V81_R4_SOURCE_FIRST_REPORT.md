## Frozen source object

Before generating or opening the V81 frequency-selected target manifest, R4
selected the Venetian cipher reproduced as figure 16 in William F. Friedman's
*Six Lectures on Cryptology*. Friedman dates the table to 1411 and states that
it was used in Venice. The facsimile heading names Michael Steno, then doge of
Venice. It combines a substitution alphabet, multiple equivalents, nulls and a
four-row word/sign table.
