# GDT676 — context-aware reader for the 51 V50 external lines

## Result

GDT676 renders every token of the 51 lines touched outside f81r by GDT675: 479
positions on 36 already admitted pages, with no new page opened. It assigns a
working value at the one GDT675 position on each line, raising assigned
positions from 292/479 (60.9603%) to 343/479 (71.6075%) and reducing explicit
gaps from 187 to 136.

This is **not a set of 51 complete translations**. There are 136 explicit
unknown positions and therefore 49 incomplete lines. Only f112v.10 and the
singleton f102v2.3 are complete in coverage.

Nor are all 343 assigned positions concrete meanings. The narrow carrier
screen finds 105 positions/106 matches in the literal token overlay and 113
positions/114 matches in the fluent working reader. An expanded
substance/grade/measure sensitivity screen matches 311/343 assigned literal
values (`0.906706`) as class-level readings. These values still carry local
distinctions such as hot/cold, dry/moist, grade, fraction or measure, but they
often do not yet tell us which actual substance or operation occupies the
slot. That is a specific next rendering problem, not a reason to discard the
reader. There are zero banned hard-generic work-item/work-cycle filler hits in
both the literal overlay and the fluent working reader.

## What survives, and the one correction

Fifty of the 51 GDT675 applications hold at full-line scale. In particular,
`qopchol` remains a concrete “getrockneten Pulverstoff nehmen” action in its
four external lines; `oror` behaves as a two-portion block; and `olkar` remains
a nominal heated first fraction rather than an implicit command.

The sole named line-scale override is f26r.2:

```text
dchey aiin adeeody ykecthey chedy ytedy dy checthedy ls
```

The isolated GDT675 default would start “eine Dosis bis Mittelstufe trocknen
und abschließen · Menge III”. In this nominal quantity/quality register that is
the wrong valency. GDT676 instead reads:

> Abgemessene Trockendroge der Mittelstufe, abgeschlossen · Menge III ·
> ⟦adeeody:?⟧ · ⟦ykecthey:?⟧ · trocken, Mittelstufe, abgeschlossen ·
> ⟦ytedy:?⟧ · Qualitäts-/Wertfeld geschlossen · ⟦checthedy:?⟧ · ⟦ls:?⟧.

Thus the `D+CH+E+Y` composition is retained, but adjacent `aiin` forces a
nominal measured result rather than an imperative. The token-overlay artifact
still preserves the upstream GDT675 literal default; the line-reader artifact
records the contextual correction.

The two decisive alternate-reader conflicts are also visible in the prose
rather than buried in metadata. At f85r2.5, ZL3b `olkar` is shown beside the
IT2a/RF1b rivals `olkor`/`ar`; at f95v1.7 both alternate readers have `oltar`.
The heated-fraction reading remains an explicit ZL3b working hypothesis at
those loci, not a reader-stable whole form.

Local quantities are likewise constrained. Ten visible head/value bindings
are accepted, three remain provisional and four tempting long-distance jumps
are rejected. `DY` closes the value field at f76v.10, while flanking `otaiin`
at f86v5.24 form a contrast series instead of values attached across the line.

## Limit

GDT676 demonstrates that the GDT675 cards can add local practical information
without degenerating into generic filler, and it exposes one real scope error
that isolated-token rendering missed. It does not prove the proposed German
values. The 51 fluent lines are manually authored contextual paraphrases;
`run.py` verifies token alignment, declared scope, counts and reproducibility,
but does not infer or historically validate those paraphrases. The manuscript
is not translated; no historical codebook, language,
phonetic system, named drug, plant, disease, patient or cure is established.
The complete source strings, literal token glosses, class-level carriers and all
136 unknowns remain visible in the published artifacts.

The independent validator reconstructs the 479-position edition and all
reported partitions in 18,770 checks and reproduces the fourteen generated
reader artifacts byte for byte.
