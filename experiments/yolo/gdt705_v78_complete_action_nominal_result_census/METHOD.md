## Complete comparison

Every action whose immediate right clause is nominal receives one explicit row. The comparison keeps four questions separate:

1. Does the right item reproduce a written operation or its resulting state?
2. Does it preserve the written degree or stage?
3. Does it preserve, relate, omit or contradict the material?
4. Does it reproduce completion, or merely place a possible patient/quantity/state after the action?

The outcome classes are intentionally practical rather than lexical rules: `ADMIT_NEW`, `HOLD_NEW`, earlier decisions replayed, partial open cases, and visible conflicts. A case remains usable while open; only the two admitted cases become new occurrence edges.
