# GDT792 preregistration — target-masked complete-whole host transfer

## Fixed question

Do the exact complete forms `otedy`, `okal`, `otchdy`, and `olaiin` carry a
portable physical host role from the 27 non-deep released pages into the three
deeply annotated image pages, once parser-created statement boundaries are
kept separate from physical line, paragraph, panel, and label evidence?

This is a successor test, not a claim of a previously unseen blind set.
GDT791 already exposed the target forms and their coarse counts.  The mask
means that no inherited gloss or component recipe may be used as an input
feature when the held deep-page roles are classified.

## Fixed scope

- exactly the 30 released pages and 35 guarded ZL3b selectors in GDT791;
- training pages: the 27 pages other than f77r, f82r, and f83r;
- held image pages: f77r, f82r, and f83r;
- no new page, image, transcription, or 179-page semantic output;
- f84 and f84r remain forbidden before row materialisation.

## Independent and circular channels

The primary channel contains only physical facts: running versus local scope,
word position on the written line, paragraph-start/end flags, page register,
visible topology, exact complete-form equality, and GDT790 panel/record owners.

The secondary channel contains GDT581 statement position, final recipe, owner
aliases, and H/grammar assignments.  These are useful diagnostics, but cannot
independently prove a meaning because the tested spellings helped construct
that parser.

The runner will also rank every complete form with 3–25 running occurrences,
so a favorable result cannot depend only on these hand-picked controls.

ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b exact-token stability is reported as one transcription
uncertainty diagnostic. These are alternate readings of the same writing and
never count as independent witnesses.

## Fixed transfer rules

1. A physical running role is predicted only when one of
   `LINE_INITIAL`, `LINE_INTERNAL`, or `LINE_FINAL` holds for at least 60% of
   the outside running occurrences.  The held set passes when at least 60% of
   its running occurrences occupy that predicted role.
2. Local-label membership transfers when the form occurs locally outside the
   deep pages and also locally on a held page.  Singleton-label status is
   scored separately.
3. Cross-topology transfer requires a held local occurrence in a topology not
   used by its outside local occurrences.
4. Statement-finality is explicitly parser-coupled and receives zero primary
   transfer credit.
5. A concrete object or substance gloss requires visually compatible owners
   across every local occurrence.  Mere reuse as an array label is insufficient.

## Working-gloss decision

`okal` may receive the replaceable exact-whole C0 default
`Ziel-/Bezugsstelle` only if all of the following hold:

- at least two outside singleton labels on at least two physical pages;
- at least one held singleton label in a new topology;
- running use is predominantly physical-line-internal both outside and held;
- its outside physical-line-internal rate strictly exceeds at least seven of
  eight controls in both deterministic control decks;
- it ranks first among all complete forms with 3–25 running occurrences by
  singleton-label page breadth, topology breadth, and count;
- at least one exact same-page cross-owner label-to-prose edge exists outside
  f82r and the known f82r edge family also survives;
- the result does not export `ok`, `al`, or any other component meaning.

The inherited `okal` heat/material/grade default is retired in the 30-page
overlay if those conditions hold.  No other target receives a selected gloss
from this experiment unless it meets an equally explicit complete-whole rule.

## Claim ceiling

The experiment may select occurrence-conditioned complete-whole renderer
defaults and reject earlier renderer cards.  It cannot confirm a lexeme,
plaintext, language, sound value, free root or affix, object identity, process
direction, number, substance, disease, plant, person, or unseen-page meaning.
