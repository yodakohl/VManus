# GDT579 method

## Question

Can all seventeen GDT575 outer/inner relation-modifier pairs receive a shorter
but fully reversible German voice, while factorizing only raw-adjacent pairs
and keeping every intervening atom visible in interrupted pairs?

## Inputs

GDT578 supplies the complete current 5,122-event, 793-statement, thirty-page
edition. GDT575 supplies exactly seventeen outer/inner pairs and their two
written atom positions. GDT407 and GDT515 supply the existing focus-head
bindings; GDT577 supplies the already used nearest-visible-action and
active-context rule for the four O and two D_ADDR slots that have no fixed
focus binding. GDT568 supplies the inherited action-head voices. The embedded
GDT574 fields in GDT576 are used only to recompute cumulative edition changes.

No new page, image, transcription, event, statement, recipe, atom, root value
or scope is admitted. Sealed f84 and f84r remain forbidden.

## Method

The split is decided from the written atom positions before German wording is
considered:

- seven pairs are raw-adjacent; their repeated base phrase is spoken once as
  `BASE im äußeren und im inneren Zweig`;
- ten pairs are interrupted by fifteen written atoms—ten actions and five
  modifiers—and therefore retain two complete scope phrases.

Every pair keeps two slot rows, one OUTER and one INNER. In a factorized pair,
the rows share one base-expression span but retain separate `äußeren` and
`inneren` spans and their original atom positions. The second basis phrase is
omitted only from the German surface, never from the inverse expansion.

The ten interrupted events are rebuilt once at event level. Their action and
argument block is retained, then every modifier is emitted in raw atom order.
The twenty scope slots name their selected action or active-context head;
seven other modifier fragments keep their current short voice. PRE_HEAD and
POST_HEAD remain structural geometry and are never spoken as process time.

The 34 head assignments divide into twenty GDT407 bindings, eight GDT515
bindings, four O nearest-visible-action candidates and two D_ADDR
active-context candidates. `G407-E4142` retains its fixed VISIBLE_OWNER head;
`G515-E0385` retains the active R/Kennzeichnen head from `G515-E0383` rather
than copying the intervening AR attachment.

Statements are rebuilt only from fixed event-ID sequences. This is necessary
because three of the seventeen scope statements also contain four different
events already improved by GDT578. Whole-statement replacement would regress
those voices.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all seventeen pairs and 34 scope slots survive, exactly seven
adjacent pairs factorize, all ten interrupted pairs retain both complete
scope phrases and all fifteen intervening atoms, and all 5,122 GDT578 event
clauses plus 793 statements reconstruct byte-for-byte through event-ID keyed
inverse cards. The three GDT578-overlap statements must retain their other four
changed events exactly.

This is an exploratory German coordination and attachment voice for already
present scope structure. `äußerer/innerer Zweig` is editorial wording, not a
new Voynich lexeme. The pass confirms no word meaning, conjunction, numeral,
plaintext, language, syntax, genre, chronology or object identity.
