## Perspectives and scope

Reuse R1–R4 unchanged from `SIDEQUEST_FOUR_AGENT_BACKGROUNDS.md`. Use only the
fixed Biological pages `f81v`, `f82r`, and `f83r`, their permitted images and

## Identity-hidden visual stage

Before joining any target value identity, each agent must freeze a role map
using only page geometry, paragraph/line location and visible drawings. The map
must list target occurrence loci or text-block ownership positions but may not
contain tuple IDs, surface strings or family labels.

Use a small fixed role vocabulary:

- `FIGURE_OR_BODY`;
- `VESSEL_POOL_OR_CONTAINER`;
- `CONDUIT_PATH_OR_FLOW`;
- `JUNCTION_OR_INTERMEDIATE_STATION`;
- `TERMINAL_OUTLET_OR_ENDPOINT`;
- `GENERAL_CONFIGURATION_OR_PAGE_OWNER`;
- `VISUALLY_UNOWNED`.

An agent may merge roles before reveal if its historical perspective makes a
distinction impossible, but may not split or reinterpret a role after seeing
value identities. Text-image proximity alone is weak evidence because drawings
preceded text and caused reflow. Every assignment needs an ownership rationale
and `HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW` confidence.

## Reveal and interpretation stage

After freezing the role map, join the four anonymous value families and ask:

1. Does one value recur disproportionately under one visible role?
2. Does the nonadjacent repeated C on f82r.27 occupy comparable roles?
3. Do values track physical role better than wrapper, field length, ordinal,
   page or copied stencil?
4. Can the four values be interpreted as state/process/application categories,
   medium/bath classes, station types, directions, results or generic form
   answers?
5. Which assignments remain meaningful when `VISUALLY_UNOWNED` is retained
   rather than redistributed attractively?

This is exploratory. A plausible mapping may remain live without proof, but a
value may not receive a visual meaning contradicted by its other occurrences.

## Required generative output

Each report must provide:

- complete accounting of all 38 occurrences;
- the pre-reveal visual-role map and its freeze hash/time;
- a value×role table after reveal;
- interpretation of f82r.27's seven cells;
- one continuous reading from each of f81v, f82r and f83r;
- one concrete meaning guess for each of the four families, or a principled
  shared-value taxonomy when individual meanings remain too unstable;
- comparison against generic stencil/cadence and ordinary abbreviated prose;
- strongest rival, confidences and fixed-page predictions.
