# V15 R2 — Medical-scribe reading of the Biological value deck

Status: **exploratory ten-page-sidequest candidate, not a GDT result and not a translation**.

## Decision

The four leading committed-value families do **not** follow a visible object
class strongly enough to be called BODY, POOL, PIPE or OUTLET. They are best
kept as **latent application/configuration answers** in a medical-practical
form. Page and stencil ecology explain more of their distribution than direct
picture ownership, but this does not force a semantically empty cadence model.
The drawing can supply the case or apparatus while the short cells record
unpictured conditions such as the normal setting, heat, manner of application
or immersion.

My concrete medical-scribe expansion, deliberately retained as a useful bet,
is:

| family | concrete source-class guess | working confidence | reason and main problem |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `VAL-A` | `STANDARD / AS USUAL` application setting | .38 | only family on all three pages and widest role spread; could instead be a common prose/cadence answer |
| `VAL-B` | `TEMPERED / WARM` medium or application | .34 | f82r/f83r category reused nonadjacently on f82r.27; no pictured temperature |
| `VAL-C` | `POUR / RINSE` application mode | .31 | slight figure/path concentration on f83r; still spans five roles |
| `VAL-D` | `IMMERSE / SOAK` application mode | .28 | present on communal-bath f81v and apparatus f83r; absent f82r despite its pool imagery |

These are not phonetic readings and not four demonstrated lexemes. The safer
shared taxonomy is `DEFAULT_SETTING / MEDIUM_CONDITION / ACTIVE_APPLICATION /
IMMERSION_APPLICATION`. It is more plausible than treating the four cards as
four apparatus nouns because each family moves across several visible roles.

Confidence for the class-level decision:

- exact committed categorical values: `.84` (inherited formal result);
- latent treatment/application configuration values: `.60`;
- the four concrete expansions above as a package: `.32`;
- direct visible-role vocabulary: `.20`;
- generic stencil/cadence values with no recoverable content: `.55`;
- ordinary abbreviated prose endings or predicates: `.43`.

Thus latent medical configuration narrowly remains the leading generative
interpretation, while generic cadence is the strongest rival and is better
supported by the internal distribution alone.

## Historical source practice

The medical frame is historically ordinary at the level claimed here, even
though no comparator identifies a Voynich card. British Library Harley MS
3407 is a late-fourteenth/early-fifteenth-century composite containing women's
medicine, recipes and prognostics; it shows that gynaecological practice and
short practical material could coexist in a compiled medical book
([catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002049238)). Sloane MS 6
contains surgical-instrument tables, cautery-point diagrams and scenes of women
using cupping glasses; it is a good comparator for pictures supplying body or
apparatus arguments while prose supplies the procedure
([catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002112343)). The
illustrated *De balneis Puteolorum* tradition ordered baths by their names and
virtues, showing that an illustrated bath sequence can carry medically useful
categorical distinctions
([Wellcome catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/stbvs45j)).

These comparators support three modest points only:

1. bath, body, application and women's-health material are plausible source
   domains around 1420;
2. drawings may supply an implicit case, site or apparatus while abbreviated
   text records a treatment distinction;
3. a miscellaneous medical codex need not use a modern database or fixed
   universal columns.

They do not support the four English glosses, a donor manuscript, an origin or
a language. `VAL-B = warm`, in particular, is a semantic wager motivated by
bath practice and repetition, not by a visible thermometer or readable word.

## Generic cadence and ordinary prose controls

The generic stencil account remains strong:

- sixteen of the 38 targets are complete one-card fields;
- all four families share the same attached commitment coordinate;
- families move among field ordinals and lengths;
- `VAL-A` has field ordinals F1/F2/F3 = 4/6/2;
- `VAL-B` has F1/F2/F3/F6 = 4/2/3/1;
- `VAL-C` has F1/F2/F3/F4/F5 = 1/2/2/2/1;
- `VAL-D` occurs exactly twice in each of F1–F4;
- page selectivity is sharper than the frozen visual-role separation.

A model-sheet scribe could therefore select common legal terminal cards by
page stencil and cadence without encoding temperature or treatment. That
model explains the evidence with fewer semantic assumptions.

Ordinary abbreviated prose is also viable. Common adjectives, imperatives or
recipe predicates can recur at clause ends and can form a one-card response
when the object and operation are inherited. It explains page-local vocabulary
and medical source practice better than pure cadence, but it does not by itself
explain why the same exact cards are so strongly attached to one committed
cell realization.

The medical configuration reading earns its lead only from the total book
model: costly bath/body/apparatus imagery, short committed cells, reuse of
values, and historically common implicit application arguments. The role test
alone does not select it.

## Strongest rival and contradictions

**Strongest rival:** `PAGE_CONDITIONED_GENERIC_ANSWER_DECK`. The four
identities are learned answers used to complete copied short-cell stencils;
their source meanings may be arbitrary or absent. This rival predicts exactly
the observed page selectivity, ordinal mobility and weak picture ownership.

Contradictions that cap the medical glosses:

- proposed `VAL-D = immersion` has zero frozen pool-owned occurrences and is
  absent from visually pool-rich f82r;
- proposed `VAL-B = warm` has no independent heat mark or degree qualifier;
- proposed `VAL-C = pour/rinse` has only 5/8 figure-or-path occurrences and no
  uniquely owned pouring station;
- `VAL-A = standard` is hard to distinguish from the default cadence value;
- f82r.27's repeated value shares one collective visual owner, so it cannot
  prove two equal-temperature depicted stations.

No contradiction makes the class-level application/configuration theory
impossible. They lower confidence and preserve the cadence rival.

## Fixed-page predictions

1. If `VAL-B` is a medium condition, its future occurrences on these three
   pages should remain compatible with degree/temperature qualifiers and may
   repeat across nonadjacent cells; a singular pool or pipe owner is not
   required.
2. If `VAL-C` is an active pour/rinse mode, complete all-occurrence reading
   should place it more often in clauses with relation/path or action
   qualifiers than `VAL-A`, even where the picture owner is general.
3. If `VAL-D` is immersion, the f81v double occurrence should continue to read
   as two inherited immersed phases/participants. A reading that requires two
   different drawn containers will fail because none is separately owned.
4. If `VAL-A` is the default setting, it should remain the most portable family
   and tolerate the broadest visible roles; that is already the observed
   pattern and is also the hardest prediction to distinguish from cadence.
5. The generic rival predicts that a within-page reshuffle preserving field
   ordinal, one-card status and copied line stencil will retain most apparent
   coherence. A true direct visible-role deck would not. The present role table
   favors the generic side of that contrast.
