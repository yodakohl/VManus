## Host construction and controls

Running events collapse to one physical paragraph. Local events would collapse
to one locus. Host domains are fixed as FIGURE_STATION_SYSTEM for radial and
pool/apparatus contexts, PLANT_DRUG_MATERIAL for plant/material contexts, and
TEXT_OR_OTHER for text blocks. Source-circle loci are removed before controls.

The builder reports:

- all `qS` controls generated from the 71 source surfaces;
- the subset with at least two external host units;
- the broad all-`q...` running/local base rate;
- exact-count and recurrent `q...dy` controls;
- complete edit-distance-one `qokaly` as the nearest whole control;
- the target-excluded host-cluster null, plus the page-exclusion sensitivity;
- all ten cached line positions and the two complete released paragraphs.

No substring decomposition, neighbour gloss or old component prose may score a
candidate. `DAQABBA` is retained only as a quarantined analyst shorthand: the
GDT795 source-native atlas contains no q-prefixed source label and no observed
DAQABBA row.

## Candidate adjudication

The frozen whole-form candidates are `davon`, `Heilmittel`,
`anwenden`, the same `Bade-/Behandlungseintrag` as `okaldy`, and opaque learned
form. Evidence axes are running/local deployment, paragraph/line position,
section breadth, within-paragraph repetition, direct whole-word adjacency to
`okaldy`, host-domain transfer and unsupported-assumption cost.

A concrete C0 card may be selected even when not proven, but every counterfact
must remain printed beside it. If no candidate wins on independent matched
axes, the tied displays remain explicit rather than being broken by raw prose
counts. Any selected or tied display is limited to the ten enumerated cached
ZL3b cells and may not license `q`, `okal`, `dy`, another surface or a new page.

## Claim ceiling

GDT798 may choose one explicit C0 whole-form display for the ten enumerated cached ZL3b cells,
correct the unsupported DAQABBA wording, and rank document-function
rivals. It cannot confirm a word, morpheme, remedy, action, reference, bath,
language, sound, cipher, plaintext clause or unseen-occurrence meaning.
