## Correction to GDT825

GDT825 said dar.chedy.qopchedy required wood/charcoal to work as a result.
That conclusion was too strong. It assumes dar is a noun/subject and the
postverbal material is a result. Neither role is decoded. A fronted property
would give another possible account with the SAME becomes meaning:

```text
f76r.51 ZL/IT: ... [opchdy] dar chedy qopchedy [tail]
EARTH:  ... [opchdy] Erde?    wird? Holz?/Holzkohle? [tail]
DRY:    ... [opchdy] trocken? wird? Holz?/Holzkohle? [tail]
```

“Trocken wird Holz” exemplifies the second parse: wood can be the subject,
dry the resulting property. This is an illustrative German arrangement, not
discovered Voynich syntax. It removes the claimed necessity of manufacturing
wood/charcoal, not all other possible source/result analyses. Old GDT825 files
remain reproducible; its **mandatory-result claim is superseded here**.

## Fixed meanings, and the difficult content

dar=Erde? means physical soil/earth, not generic matter or the planet.
dar=trocken? means low physical moisture, not humoral dryness, warm, thoroughly,
a dried drug, or the command dry. No relation to EVA d/ar or Latin initials.
Both were proposed before the new inventory; some motivating contexts had
already been seen in earlier readers. Old qopchedy and raiin models remain
separate, giving eight combinations, not eight independent tests.
