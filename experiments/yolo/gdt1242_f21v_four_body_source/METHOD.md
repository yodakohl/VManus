# Method and decision limits

Read one original photographic witness at its original pixel resolution after
freezing location, comparisons and outcomes. Preserve separation between:
(1) a compatible series of four drawn small bodies;
(2) the manuscript's unknown alphabet/segmentation;
(3) a hypothesized code's source expansion.
Only(1)is visually assessed. Mathematical code consequences remain conditional
on(2)and(3). Re-reading the same photo is not independent confirmation.

Nearby line2G001andG002are fixed before image access. No other favorable glyph
or rare word can replace the chosen examples. A full-page view locates line3;
one declared rectangle gives the single analytic view, without synthesis,
contrast processing, OCR or a second crop. Observed ambiguity is an outcome.
The program reduces fixed categorical judgments and validates input bookkeeping;
it cannot validate palaeographic accuracy. No native frequency is recomputed.
