# GDT713 method

## Question

Can the actual remaining three measure-family and six CKH/CPH-family weak
readings be made smaller and more compositionally useful without losing their
practical local realizations or pretending that bound fields are free words?

## Inputs

- GDT712 V85 active lexical, context, complete-confidence, held-census and
  bound-span artifacts;
- GDT711's thirteen admitted stem-family evidence rows;
- GDT638 for exact `dol = D+OL` whole-form evidence;
- GDT651/GDT652/GDT670/GDT681 for the admitted CKH/CPH whole-form families;
- GDT658/GDT681 for O/T/OR/AM measure-family compositions.

Only cached artifacts are consumed. No image, raw transcription or new page is
opened; `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Method

1. Select exactly the nine V85 rows still held in the `MEASURE_FAMILY` and
   `CKH_CPH_EXACT_FAMILY` queues, preserving their ten positions and seven
   pages.
2. Require an already admitted exact-family decomposition. Written pieces are
   family fields, not automatically free lexemes, and component export remains
   false.
3. Replace the unsupported learned head `Arzneikompositum` with the smaller
   exact-family head `Mischung`; retain dry/wet and base/start fields when the
   family card licenses them.
4. Keep E_ATTR and O_PREP as structural metadata. A practical `Ansatz`
   expression may appear in an occurrence realization but does not become a
   free E or O translation.
5. Split articles and contextual heads out of `oram` and `otam`. Preserve
   measure, cold-state and preparation-frame information actually licensed by
   OR+AM and O+T+AM.
6. Hold `dol = Materialmaß`: its lexical core is already minimal. Raise only
   its evidence score from 18 to 23 because GDT638 independently audited 76
   exact D+OL occurrences; do not invent a unit or measuring action.
7. Rebuild all 324 active readings, all 479 positions and the complete
   1,586-reading dictionary. Replay outputs and verify exact position parity,
   score formulas, inherited W3 rows, bound spans, export stops and H0 status.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A score rises only for a named semantic-debt removal or an explicit admitted
exact-family intersection. `dol` is the sole audit hold and receives only
exact-whole evidence credit. Frequency, fluent prose and relation edges add no
word points.

V86 is an exploratory working dictionary, not plaintext, a language
identification or a historical lexicon. Confidence is an internal evidence
index, not a probability. Every historical status remains `H0_NONE`; GDT713
adds zero relation-derived word credit.
