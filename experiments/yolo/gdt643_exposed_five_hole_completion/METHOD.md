# GDT643 method

## Question

Can the five exact surfaces left open by GDT642 be assigned practical,
compositionally differentiated meanings that complete their source lines and
remain usable over every occurrence in the inherited page panel?

## Inputs and scope

- V19 dictionary, glossary, 4,128-line coverage and one-hole frontier from
  GDT642.
- The quality, value, carrier, interfix and residual-head families from
  GDT624, GDT627–628, GDT633, GDT636 and GDT639.
- The unchanged 179-page guarded panel. `f1r` is excluded; `f84` and `f84r`
  are forbidden. No new page or image is opened.
- Every inherited executable helper from GDT631–642 is hash-bound.

## Method

1. Read the visible components of the five source holes and keep distinct
   practical slots: dose, preparation form, thermal quality, seed fraction
   and bound final state.
2. Materialize tokens and alternate readings only through the explicit-page
   guarded query.
3. Census every target occurrence, its page distribution, all-reader exactness
   and boundary-normalized survival. Repeated occurrences are counted by token
   ordinal, not merely by line presence.
4. Audit each candidate against the unchanged V19 context. The strong class
   requires an all-reader-exact target plus at least two already glossed
   companions that are neither ambiguous nor reader-unstable. Sparse or
   unstable contexts remain open rather than being counted as confirmation.
5. Add one exact card at a time, rebuild all line states, and record dictionary
   hashes, coverage changes, new complete lines and newly exposed holes.
6. Replay every output byte-for-byte and independently reconstruct the guarded
   occurrence, page, exact-reader and split-normalized counts.

## Reading rules

| Surface | Visible parse | Practical V20 reading |
|---|---|---|
| `cheodain` | `ch+e+o+d+ain` | Trockenansatz, Dosis II |
| `oiin` | `o+iin` | Zubereitungsform III |
| `choky` | `ch+o+k+y` | heiß-trockene Zubereitung, Grundform |
| `soysar` | `soy|sar = s+o+y | s+ar` | Samenzubereitung, Grundform; Samenfraktion I |
| `kcheedy` | `k+ch+ee+d+y` | heiß-trockene gebundene Abschlussform, Bindungsstufe II |

The first three require at least one all-reader-exact anchor. `soysar` requires
the same complete character package in all readers even when IT2a exposes the
boundary `soy sar`. `kcheedy` is accepted only as the ZL3b/IT2a card while
RF1b's `tcheedy` and its cold polarity remain explicit.

## Claim ceiling

The five whole-surface values are exploratory working defaults. Components are
shown to explain the composition but are not newly globalized. The audit's
zero collision column records that no manual same-slot contradiction was
flagged; it is not an independent semantic proof. No plaintext, phonetic
reading, language, ingredient identity or manuscript-wide solution is claimed.
