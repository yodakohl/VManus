## Why the large local inventory is not enough

The 1,059 local relation annotations are useful geometry: attachment, contact,
enclosure, and array/group membership. They do not provide an independent
second manuscript locus that a pivot points to. Recasting a local spatial tag,
a repeated tuple, a DY return, or an OPEN/BODY boundary as a “parent” would make
the target answer a restatement of the same structure used as predictor.

Similarly, the 40 cross-page referent pairs identify same/similar drawings, not
an authorial direction from one record to another. Five pairs have a locally
owned or provisional inscription, but none supplies the fixed ordered edge
required by this instrument.

## Gate result

All seven candidate families fail at least one frozen gate. The viable endpoint
freeze is intentionally empty. No model, null, association score, or semantic
label was produced.

## What new evidence would change this

A future target experiment requires a new, provenance-clean relation census
with:

1. an independently observable pivot and target;
2. authorial/editorial direction or fixed ownership not inferred from text;
3. singular mapping of both endpoints to manuscript records or inscriptions;
4. at least 50 edges spanning at least five physical folios; and
5. target identity not reconstructible from recurrence, placement, line/field
   structure, or the predictor itself.

Until such evidence is acquired, the parent-link route is closed at the target
capacity stage. The correct next action is acquisition/instrumentation, not an
internal grammar relabeling.
