## Surface-position caution

GDT475's boundary relation is the first functional root after learned name
material has been removed. It is not always the first visible substring.
`olkalaiin`, `oletal`, and `olkchs` carry OL before the learned name, whereas
`qkol`, `ykolairol`, and `doly` place the name before OL in the literal working
trace. Both orders can bind a continuation. The next pass should therefore ask
whether pre-name and post-name OL predict different *kinds* of continuation,
instead of treating OL as a simple prefix.
