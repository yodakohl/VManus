# GDT656 method

## Question

Can the observed AL surfaces inherit the existing dry/moist/hot/cold and
beginning/middle/end quality architecture while keeping every accepted value
bound to a complete reader-anchored surface?

## Inputs

- The byte-frozen GDT655 V32 dictionary, glossary, coverage, complete-line and
  one-hole artifacts.
- The GDT647 quality-position report and GDT652 preparation/material report.
- The same 179-page GDT655 allow-list.
- Guarded ZL3b token and ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b line inputs. `f1r` is excluded;
  `f84` and `f84r` are forbidden before row materialization.

## Method

1. Enumerate the complete 44-cell AL atlas: eight visible quality roots at
   zero/E/EE/EEE, the unqualified O and QO shells, and the unheaded rivals.
2. Count every target by complete ZL3b equality and independently record
   all-reader exact and split-normalized support.
3. Apply 21 exact-whole defaults sequentially: eighteen new surfaces and
   revisions of `okal`, `otal`, and `qokal`. No suffix match or substring
   inheritance is permitted.
4. Treat zero/E/EE as start/middle/end only in the visible quality-headed AL
   families. `oeeal` is a separately labelled exact-whole local analogy;
   `oeal`, `eeal`, `keeal`, `cheeeal`, absent cells and multi-quality shells
   do not acquire productive values.
5. Rebuild every one of the 4,128 inherited physical lines after each card,
   retaining literal unknowns, reader warnings and active rivals.
6. Store all 659 occurrence decisions, the 546 affected lines, newly complete
   lines, one-hole introductions, boundary witnesses and the compact
   historical architecture comparators.
7. Replay the builder in a temporary directory and independently recensus the
   raw data through `./vmanus-exp query-tsv`.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

An accepted card must be an observed complete ZL3b surface with at least one
all-reader exact occurrence and a concrete family-bound reading. Revision
cards may change an inherited gloss without adding coverage. `oeeal` requires
both exact occurrences plus its exact `oeear` sister and remains explicitly
nonproductive.

The output is an exploratory working translation of exact whole surfaces, not
plaintext, phonetics, language identification or an ingredient identification.
Structural tags remain distinct from English/German translations. No free
component, absent cell, f1r claim, new page or new image is licensed.
