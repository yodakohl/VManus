# GDT740 report — local-host attachment adjudication

## Outcome

GDT740 repairs the largest remaining source of false concreteness in GDT739:
a semantically compatible nearby field was being rendered as though it already
belonged to the target microentry.

The new working model is deliberately exploratory but no longer indiscriminate:

- direct contacts remain viable unless a boundary or incompatible flank blocks
  them;
- radius two is normally only neighborhood;
- two manually coherent three-cell relays remain live hypotheses;
- only one of eight former result/end overrides survives;
- 49 of 202 target renders change and 146 are now fully open.

This does not replace one generic sentence with another. It identifies exactly
which concrete slot survives at each occurrence and exactly why.

## Direct does not mean automatically bound

All target and host cells are single-cell practical units; zero share an
existing bound span. Direct adjacency is therefore evidence, not proof.

Fifty-five of the 62 direct contact rows remain renderer-active. Seven are
held:

- four contacts on two targets would build compound carriers from separate
  flankers;
- one direct preparation cue ends at a closure boundary;
- two opposite-flank contacts would fuse a left carrier with a right axis.

Among active unique direct targets, 32 match the formal H1/H2-right or
H3/H4-left diagnostic, thirteen reverse it, and three manual cases retain only
the carrier. The direction split is a confidence diagnostic; both directions
remain possible.

The historical direction prior is separate. In inspected pharmaceutical
comparators, names, named parts, or ingredients precede quality/degree or dose
fields. The witnesses attest field order, not token adjacency. As a qualitative
GDT740 model choice, a compatible immediate left host therefore receives
stronger microentry weight. This is not a measured frequency. Right contacts
may still be rubric-to-entry scope or register-conditioned inversion.

## New working basis

Renderer precedence after GDT740 is:

1. exact bound context/span;
2. licensed complete whole at its enumerated occurrence;
3. invariant complete-whole field class and ordered level;
4. retained direct host may fill an axis or broad carrier;
5. explicit boundary/flank conflicts reopen the slot;
6. radius two is silent unless an enumerated relay is licensed;
7. otherwise unknown.

The six repeated direct contacts were sent through the executable GDT388
intake. They correctly return `INVALID_PACKET`: formal semantic selection,
external-capacity, held-folio, independent-review, and mobile-null gates are
closed. The packet is published so later work cannot mistake recurrence for a
score-ready relation.

No page, image, or transcription was added. No head, body, substring, sound,
glyph value, global lexeme, literal patient, species, unseen form, language, or
plaintext clause is promoted.
