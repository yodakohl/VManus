## Atomic role rule

All radius-two decisions receive a whitelisted record without contact ID,
dispatch ID, page, locus, surface or predecessor outcome. A common frame
requires:

- distance two and at least one reader-exact occurrence of the full frame;
- a reader-exact, known middle cell that emits its own unit;
- no strict initial head and no second GDT738 target in the middle;
- `middle_barrier=OPEN`; and
- agreement with the inherited formal role direction.

The old strict AXIS relay remains unchanged: it additionally requires an
AXIS-only contact and one identical single quality axis in middle and host.

The carrier decision is now role-local. The requested nonempty carrier set must
be contained in both the host-carrier and middle-carrier sets. A CARRIER-only
contact then replays the old strict relay. A mixed AXIS+CARRIER contact may
relay the carrier independently only when `axis_continuity=NONE`. No partial
quality overlap is spoken.

This is a broad-field relation over current whole-cell tags. It is not an EVA
substring rule, a component value or a historical abbreviation expansion.

## Complete-deck dispatch and class census

The rule is applied to all 103 contacts. Direct contacts inherit GDT741; a
promotion-only gate stops the renderer if any old role would be deactivated.
The contact artifact carries the atomic target, host and middle fields needed
to recompute the derived barrier and continuity tags.

For diagnosis, the 41 radius-two contacts are also grouped by a declared
reduced signature: selected roles, formal direction, full-frame recurrence,
middle exactness/known/unit/head/target gates, boundary class and normalized
axis/carrier continuity. Identity, locus, surface, concrete carrier kind and
old/new decisions are excluded. Therefore a repeated class is a
role-isomorphic in-sample pattern, not an independent replication.
