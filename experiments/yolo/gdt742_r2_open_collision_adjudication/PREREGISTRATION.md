# GDT742 working protocol

## Proposition

The most economical next evolution of GDT741 is to remove role purity only for
carrier relay. A mixed AXIS+CARRIER target may inherit a complete carrier field
without inheriting a noncontinuous quality axis.

## Fixed material

- GDT741's 103 contact rows;
- GDT741's eight radius-two candidate-role rows;
- GDT741's 202-position renderer; and
- only the seven candidate loci from GDT734's existing compact cache for human
  display.

No page, image or transcription is newly opened. `f84` and `f84r` remain
forbidden.

## Decision map

1. Recompute the exact/known/unit/non-head/non-target middle-frame gate.
2. Require an open middle boundary and matching formal direction.
3. Preserve the old AXIS-only exact-single-axis relay.
4. Preserve the old CARRIER-only full-coverage relay.
5. For AXIS+CARRIER contacts, activate only CARRIER when requested carrier
   coverage is complete in both middle and host and axis continuity is `NONE`.
6. Leave reverse-direction, partial-axis, PROCESS/PASS, CLOSE, unknown and
   incomplete-carrier cases open.
7. Change no direct contact, axis, state mode, scalar level or inherited
   positional behavior.

The decision function may not receive an ID, locus, page, surface, GDT741 role
outcome or candidate status. Candidate IDs enter only after the complete deck
has been adjudicated for audit and rendering.

## Required audit products

- all 103 contact decisions with atomic reconstruction fields;
- a reduced outcome-free class census;
- all eight candidate-role outcomes;
- all 202 renderer rows;
- the seven admitted cached focus lines;
- the two provisional edge rows and their executable intake status;
- independent recomputation, exact cross-join parity and byte-identical replay.

## Interpretation boundary

`MATERIAL` and `PREPARATION` remain broad working carrier tags. Even a clean
two-case trigger does not identify `rain`, `sain`, `ain` or any other whole or
part as a historical word. A `HOLD` means the current rule does not license the
role; it does not prove the alternative meaning impossible.
