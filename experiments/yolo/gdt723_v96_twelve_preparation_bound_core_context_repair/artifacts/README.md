# GDT723/V96 artifacts

- `V96_12_TARGET_RENDERER.tsv`: the twelve portable cores beside their exact
  local readings.
- `V96_12_PREPARATION_CORE_CONTEXT_DELTA.tsv`: old/new decisions, evidence,
  rivals and open slots.
- `V96_12_LINEAGE_AUDIT.tsv`: GDT652 and GDT691 origin for every target.
- `V96_12_ACTION_HEAD_SEPARATION.tsv`: local product/action leakage audit.
- `V96_6_SCOPE_DICTIONARY.tsv`: exact-whole and no-component-export rules.
- `V96_36_RIVAL_MODEL_COMPARISON.tsv`: three explicit models per target.
- `V96_61_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_BINDINGS.tsv`: replayable exact-row fingerprints,
  including the explicit GDT713 CKH-family control for `chockhy`.
- `V96_324_ACTIVE_LEXICAL_READINGS.tsv` and
  `V96_479_CONTEXT_REALIZATIONS.tsv`: canonical active editions.
- `V96_47_HELD_READING_AUDIT.tsv`: twelve revised plus 35 still held.
- `V96_COMPLETE_WORD_CONFIDENCE.tsv`: all 1,586 readings with default,
  confidence, evidence, counterevidence and H0.
- `RESULT.json` and `VALIDATION.json`: compact machine summaries.

The large complete dictionary and active editions are retained because they
are required for full per-reading confidence/evidence and non-target parity.
