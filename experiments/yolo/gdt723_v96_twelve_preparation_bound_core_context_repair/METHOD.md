# GDT723 method — twelve bound preparation readings

## Question

Can the twelve remaining preparation-head holds receive concrete portable
defaults that retain their observed state, stage, quantity or material content,
while product heads, sources and actions appear only in the exact positions
that license them?

## Fixed inputs

The executable source is V95: 324 active lexical rows, 479 exact context
positions, the 47-row live repair queue, the 1,586-reading complete dictionary,
five bound spans, two one-shot directives and eight f7r.2 units. Exact lineage
is read from GDT691's token reader and rule table, from GDT652's line coverage,
and from GDT713's explicit CKH-family row. The twelve decisions are declared in
`src/V96_12_AUDIT_SPECS.tsv`.

GDT691 and GDT652 are inspected only at the ten explicitly allowed loci through
the guarded TSV loader. A row whose selector begins with `f84` is rejected
before its remaining columns are parsed. GDT652 is a line-level source, so its
machine selector is the real `locus` column; the token ordinal and expected
scope cell are recorded and validated separately as a position projection.

## Transformation

For each target the builder checks the exact surface, page, locus, ordinal,
left and right neighbour, action licence, V95 default, V95 score, GDT691 rule,
GDT691 formal route and GDT652 scope state. It then writes two channels:

1. a portable exact-whole core containing only the supported state, stage,
   quantity or material information;
2. a local renderer that may retain the practical preparation head, source or
   action licensed at that one position.

Ten targets retain later bound-whole/local-scope lineage. `chockhy` is kept
separate because GDT652 already classed it as an exact O-CKH grid cell.
`solchedy` is separate because GDT652 already supplied a boundary-unstable seed
head and GDT691 later marked it productive. `ykcho/ytcho` are only a paired
warm/cold whole contrast. None of these distinctions exports a free component.

All non-target lexical and context rows are preserved. The repair queue is
filtered to the 47 entries that were still held in V95, marks twelve as revised,
and leaves 35 for later work. Scores do not change.

## Outputs and checks

The run emits the complete V96 lexical/context editions, complete confidence
dictionary, target renderer, lineage audit, action/head separation table, three
rival models per target, exact evidence fingerprints and the inherited
span/directive outputs. The independent validator replays all 61 evidence
bindings, checks every dictionary row for a default, confidence level, positive
evidence, counterevidence and H0 field, and enforces non-target parity.

## Claim ceiling

These are concrete exploratory German renderer values for twelve exact
surfaces. They are not confirmed plaintext, lexemes, phonetics, a language or a
historical codebook. Exact-whole defaults are executable; productive substring
or component export, semantic score credit and historical confirmation are all
zero. No new page, image or transcription is used; `f84` and `f84r` remain
forbidden.
