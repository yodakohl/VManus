The most useful corrections are practical rather than cosmetic. `okeeeey` is
no longer rendered with the hidden action “geführt” at a nominal position.
`olord` gets a grammatical genitive locally without turning “Holzauszug” into
its universal word meaning. `qocho`, `ykcho` and `ytcho` retain their actual
instructions, but “nehmen”, “hieraus”, “bereiten” and “herstellen” no longer
contaminate the lexical default.

## The two lineage exceptions

The twelve forms do not have one uniform origin.

- Ten were still unknown at GDT652 and acquired later bound whole/local-scope
  readings in GDT691.
- `chockhy` was already a `KNOWN_EXACT_WHOLE` O-CKH grid cell. GDT713's later
  family repair supports the portable head “Mischung”, while the older
  “Arzneizubereitung” remains only the concrete local choice.
- `solchedy` already carried a boundary-unstable “Samen/Saatgutstoff/-ansatz”
  reading at GDT652 and a productive seed head in GDT691. V96 therefore keeps
  `Samenmaterial` portable but leaves `Samenmasse` local.

`ykcho` and `ytcho` form a useful warm/cold pair over “Trockenmischung”. This is
only a paired learned-whole contrast: it does not establish `k=warm`, `t=kalt`,
`cho=Mischung` or any other free substring value. Likewise `solchedy` exports
neither free `chedy` nor `dy`.

## Korrekturen aus den unabhängigen Nachprüfungen

The manual pass caught one grammatical overreach: the older `shotchey` output
made an invented source drug carry the cold/dry states. The final local reading
is therefore `kalt angefeuchtetes Mazerat, Trockenstufe II`; no source drug is
asserted. The adversarial pass also removed `geführt` from the list of locally
retained `okeeeey` words, because that hidden action is absent from both final
channels. The validator now requires every item labelled local-only to occur in
the local renderer and to be absent from the portable core.

The lineage pass corrected the evidence interface itself. GDT652 line rows are
selected only by their real `locus` field, with ordinal/scope checked as a
separate projection. The GDT713 `F_CKH` family row is now an explicit manifest
input and fingerprinted control for the neutral `chockhy` mixture head. The
final packet has 61 replayable bindings.

## What remains concrete, and what remains open

The new cores still say whether the item is seed or flower material, wood,
dry/wet, hot/cold, finished, measured, at an initial/end/intermediate stage, or
a warm/cold dry mixture. What moves out is only the replaceable product choice:
Mazerat versus Einweichung, Masse versus Rückstand, Zubereitung versus Ansatz,
and Droge versus other dry material.

All twelve remain W1. The round adds no confidence points merely for cleaner
wording. It allows all twelve exact surfaces to retain a default, but exports
zero components and predicts zero new surfaces.
