# Method

## Inputs

- GDT416's 4,576 exact component recipes, owners, and imperative clauses;
- GDT422's complete reduction of recipes with three or more action heads.

No new page, image, transcription, or dictionary value is used.

## Leave-one-page replay

For every one of the 24 stored physical-page keys, remove that page from the
teaching deck. The keys represent 26 admitted physical pages or panels. For
each clean one- or two-action recipe on the hidden page, build the slot cell

`ordered action head(s) | grade | argument | endpoint`.

Then assign one of three readings:

1. **green:** that exact slot cell occurs on another page;
2. **amber:** the head or ordered pair and every occupied margin occur on
   another page, but not in this exact combination;
3. **red:** the head/pair or at least one occupied margin is page-private.

Nonclean recipes remain explicitly outside the simple four-slot atlas. Long
chains inherit GDT422's pair reduction. Recipes with no action head are not
counted as failures of an action grammar.

## Validation

The validator rebuilds all outputs and requires byte identity, 4,576 unique
events, 1,129 page×slot cells, 2,662 clean slot events, 57 red cells, the
complete category distribution, and zero new pages or dictionary revisions.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Red means "not learnable after hiding its only page", not "semantically
wrong". It is the exact queue for a local-exception analysis. Amber is a real
prediction by old parts, but remains weaker than an already attested cell.
This tests compositional portability, not plaintext, language, or source.
