# GDT751 registration record

## Timing and purpose

GDT751 follows GDT750's post-result route diagnostic.  The fixed 51-pair deck,
the non-q prefix controls and the occurrence-local `okeey` carrier rule were
specified before the GDT751 builder was run.  The inherited semantic pattern
was already visible and is therefore never counted as independent evidence.

## Fixed question

Do complete `qX`/`X` pairs show a raw position or contact relation beyond the
ordinary effect of adding one initial EVA character, and can that relationship
support a preparation carrier at the GDT750 `okeey` occurrences directly
paired with `qokeey`?

## Fixed material

- every complete surface with a clean reader-exact W2/W3, non-compositional,
  non-retired current card in the inherited 179-page cache;
- all pairs for which both `qX` and `X` occur in that clean complete-surface
  deck;
- all other clean pairs made by removing exactly one initial character as the
  control universe;
- all reader-exact cached occurrences and guarded paragraph-line metadata;
- GDT750's nineteen active occurrence cards.

No new page, image or transcription is opened. `f84` and `f84r` remain
forbidden.

## Comparisons

For every pair, compare the prefix and base sides on pair-balanced normalized
line position, line/paragraph entry and exit, section-residual position and
reader-exact direct contact.  Report four groups: the 51 q pairs, a deterministic
51-pair non-q frequency/length match selected without outcome fields, all non-q
prefix pairs, and the non-q controls whose base also begins with `o`.

The q-side is a special entry shell only if its pair-balanced position shift is
more entry-biased than all three control summaries.  Direct contact density may
support a complete-pair relationship but cannot by itself identify the role of
either side.

The inherited quality/stage preservation and preparation asymmetry remain
model-internal even if numerically strong.  A local carrier-enriched card may
be issued only where a GDT750 active `okeey` card has `qokeey` as its immediate
contributing host and the complete pair has base-only `PREPARATION`.  The card
is occurrence-scoped and explicitly exploratory.

## Claim ceiling

No result assigns a value to EVA `q`, calls it a prefix or morpheme, or exports
a substring.  `PREPARATION` is a carrier role, not a literal historical word,
substance or vessel.  No language, sound, abbreviation, lexeme, plaintext,
ingredient, plant, disease, cure, person, unit or unseen form is identified.
