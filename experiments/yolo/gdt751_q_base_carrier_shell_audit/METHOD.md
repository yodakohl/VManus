# GDT751 method

## Question

Does the current complete-whole deck contain a genuine `qX`/`X` alternation,
and does raw placement independently support the inherited idea that the
unprefixed side carries a preparation while the q-side carries the same
quality/stage without that carrier?

## Guarded inputs

The builder imports GDT750's exact guarded token/cache loader and nineteen
active cards.  It also reads paragraph boundaries from the guarded line table
through the inherited allow-list-before-materialization loader.  A surface is
admitted to the pair universe only if at least one reader-exact current cache
cell has a W2/W3, non-compositional, non-retired complete-whole card.

When a surface has more than one scoped clean axis set, its canonical card is
the most frequent complete axis set; ties are resolved lexically.  This is a
description of the current renderer, not new semantic evidence.

## Pair and control decks

The q deck contains every clean `qX` whose clean `X` base also exists.  The
control deck applies the identical one-initial-character deletion to all other
clean surfaces.  Each pair receives counts and pair-balanced differences for:

- normalized line position;
- line-first and line-last;
- paragraph-first and paragraph-last token;
- paragraph-start and paragraph-end line;
- normalized position after subtracting the global mean of the occurrence's
  section;
- direct reader-exact adjacency of the two complete surfaces.

The matched control deck chooses one unique non-q pair per q pair.  Its cost
uses only base length, prefix-side frequency, base-side frequency and whether
the control base begins with `o`; no position, semantic or contact outcome may
enter matching.  The all-non-q and `o`-base control groups remain visible so
the greedy match cannot decide the conclusion by itself.

## Semantic audit and local renderer

For each complete pair, the builder reports whether canonical quality/stage
axes are identical and whether `PREPARATION` occurs on both sides, neither
side, prefix only or base only.  These fields are inherited outcomes and are
kept separate from the raw tests.

The only renderer addition intersects three existing constraints:

1. GDT750 already licensed a local `okeey` quality/stage card;
2. its immediate contributing host is the complete form `qokeey`;
3. the `qokeey/okeey` canonical pair preserves quality/stage and places
   `PREPARATION` only on `okeey`.

Such an occurrence renders as "heiße Zubereitung an der End-/Vollstufe".
Every other occurrence remains unchanged.  This is a C2 exploratory
whole-pair card, not `q = non-preparation`, `o = preparation` or
`okeey =` a decoded German word.

## Decision and ceiling

A q-specific position shell requires a stronger entry shift than the matched,
all-non-q and non-q-`o`-base controls.  Higher direct-contact density supports
only a real complete-pair relationship.  The preparation toggle may remain as
a model-internal working rule but gains no independent semantic status from
placement.  No EVA character, component, sound, abbreviation, substring,
lexeme, plaintext or literal materia is exported.
