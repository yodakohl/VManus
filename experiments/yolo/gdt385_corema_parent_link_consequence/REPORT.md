## What was learned

The repaired composite observation can carry a small, reproducible signal about
one genuinely external editor relation. `CMP_PARENT_02` saves 194.224 bits, or
only .00742 bits per eligible pivot, and changes exact target MRR by just
+.000140. This is instrument evidence, not a semantic bridge. The other raw
gains demonstrate why held calibration, collection stability, exact target
rank and the max-family null were all necessary.

The source-only relation model is already strong: edge AUC is .768339 and exact
target MRR is .781661. The passing role raises edge AUC to .775496 but barely
changes exact target rank. The experiment therefore localizes a weak
role-conditioned increment on top of strong record-position structure; it does
not recover a portable pointer vocabulary.

## Decision and next route

The frozen gate is not relaxed. No Voynich target experiment is authorized and
the readable labels `REF`, `TIME`, `ALTERNATIVE`, and exclusion do not transfer.
The one passing route may remain an anonymous comparator calibration, but a
future target experiment first needs a genuinely homologous, independently
observable parent/relation endpoint. Deriving a “parent” from Voynich tuple
recurrence, line position, or field structure would recycle the source-side
grammar and repeat the GDT384 overlap problem.

The next useful pass is therefore a score-blind target-homology/capacity audit,
not another operator scan: enumerate only authorial/external relation edges
already available for non-f84 Voynich records, deduplicate them against closed
resume/repeat/scope routes, and determine whether any can instantiate the
passing comparator relation without defining the answer from the predictor.
