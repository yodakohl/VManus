# Method

new census, after exposure to the full local context and predecessor reports.
Enumerate all exact new words in the six locked179-selector GDT915 snapshots;
retain all full target lines under four constant33-word lexicons. New word values
are shared by all four candidates, so occurrence rows are stored once with their
explicit four-candidate scope. Keep raw entities and separator types, including
an explicit marker for unaligned drawing interruptions.

Render the whole existing25-line f111v frame and explicit local subject/container
and motion bindings. ZL/IT paragraph completeness is separately verified from
GDT928; RF remains a comparison window. Export all alternate solkain locus lines,
including the ZL split reading without an exact target. Report all completely
lexically covered target lines, separating single-word labels from whole clauses.

The manual report assesses the local content and all solkain contexts. Global
exports are exact-word consequences, not a claimed translation of every paragraph.
The independent validator checks source coverage, inherited values, separator
fidelity and deterministic output, not meanings. No general scope parser.
