# GDT829 preregistration — literal context under changed wrapping

2026-09-05. Registered before extracting manuscript candidates. The user
authorized execution of the published repeated-passage proposal. This unit
establishes capacity only. No direction of terminal variation will be exposed
or scored in its output. `src/SPEC.json` is the executable contract.

## Fixed extraction

Use only the explicit 179 selectors inherited from GDT631, through the
selector-first guarded TSV query. f84 and f84r remain forbidden. Use the
source-group atlas, never legacy ASCII tokenization. ZL3b is primary; IT2a
and RF1b are alternative readings and receive separate sensitivity inventories.

The lexer retains every character and case. Each complete `@digits;` entity,
square alternative, brace form and angle annotation is one opaque transcription
atom; other characters are individual atoms. Unclosed or mismatched constructs,
unmatched closing delimiters and malformed @entities are errors.
These are comparison atoms, not established manuscript glyphs or phonemes.

A target is a nonempty group body followed by an explicit final literal `l`
or `m`. The key comprises that full body and exactly twelve transcription
atoms before and twelve after the terminal, with intervening separator kinds.
The terminal is masked. Spaces do not count toward twelve but remain in the
signature. No edit distance, inferred root or synonyms are allowed. Keys can
contain partial outer groups; they are extended local contexts, not necessarily
identical whole passages. No both-endings requirement is used for selection.

Only explicitly start/end bounded ZL paragraphs supply the layout scaffold.
Split their streams at non-P records, missing/nonconsecutive numeric loci or
source row indices, page/panel changes and drawing interruptions. Do not join
across these barriers. Each alternate segment must contain the exact ZL locus
sequence with consecutive native source rows; otherwise drop that segment.
Alternate paragraph-marker disagreement is counted, not silently treated as
independent paragraph evidence. P is a transcription class, not decoded prose.

For matching alone, a definite group gap and an admissible physical line join
both become GAP. This limited analytical convention makes a reflow comparison
possible; it is not proof a line break denotes a word space. Keep original
boundaries privately during extraction and reveal layout only after freezing
the recurrent-context inventory. Uncertain small spaces stay distinct. All
annotated contexts remain in the inventory; primary pairs require only ASCII
letter or complete extended-entity atoms and definite comparison gaps.

## Staged inventory and conservative independence

First write the masked eligible occurrence census and all recurrent context
families, without terminal values or line-final flags. Freeze their byte hashes.
This is procedural masking, not cryptographic blinding: neighbouring literal
endings remain in contexts and overlapping windows may permit reconstruction.
The cache was historically exposed. No target outcome column or directional
statistic is emitted. Only then disclose physical layout for those fixed families, retaining target
masking. A cross-layout pair has one line-final target group and one internal
target group, in identical extended contexts. Retain all qualifying occurrence
pairs descriptively, including unknown/different hands; only known equal hand
labels qualify for primary capacity.

Cluster recurrent families whenever any occurrence shares physical leaf fN,
including recto, verso and panels. This also collapses overlapping passages on
the same leaf. Each resulting component contributes at most one pair: the
lexicographically first eligible `(family_id, occurrence_id_1, occurrence_id_2)`.
Do not pick a pair after revealing terminal outcomes. Such conservative blocks
control obvious dependency, but cannot prove statistical independence.

## Capacity gate

The prospective statistic is a two-sided exact sign test at alpha .01 on
non-tied changes, with directional probability .8 as a design benchmark and
required power .8. Exact binomial calculation first attains this at n=32:
critical tails X<=8 or X>=24, size .0070003666915 and power .825395312300.
Power is discrete and nonmonotonic (n=33 is slightly below .8), so any later
test must recompute power at its actual informative n. No rounded shortcut.

Let U be the number of independent primary cross-layout components, before
terminal outcomes are known. U<32 is an upper-bound capacity failure: even
if every selected pair changed, this design could not meet the stated power.
U>=32 is only potential feasibility; ties and uncertainty can reduce n.
Unchanged pairs remain ties and are never replaced. Their abundance alone
would not prove a text-fixed mechanism or refute a probabilistic layout effect.

This unit ends after one extraction and a capacity report. It does not run
the direction test even if potential capacity exists. Any conditional next unit
must first validate the complete procedure on the controls in the proposal.
Failure does not license shorter contexts, edits, another terminal family,
additional pages or combining transcriptions to increase sample size.
