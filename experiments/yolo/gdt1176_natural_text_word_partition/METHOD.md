# Method

Test whether four declared, reversible source-word groupings can meet two
necessary word-frequency constraints before building a new writing system.
The bijection invariant means a different glyph key cannot repair these two
rates without changing word grouping or the source itself.

Source projection and partitions are fixed in PREREGISTRATION.md and SPEC.json.
The full source projection is preserved, including punctuation; original bytes
are pinned. We do not claim diplomatic spacing, medieval use of our grouping,
or natural-language identification for Voynich. Existing CoReMA words carry
meaning through knowledge of their source language; no invented semantic lexicon.

Runner records complete recipe tuples and source exclusions. Validator independently
flattens all tuples and recalculates the two metrics and joint necessary gate.
This cannot establish actual glyph counts, glyph transitions, full Voynich fit
or a viable22-glyph encoding. Use the result only to select or reject a next
construction task. Prior GDT1174 and GDT1175 outcomes stay unchanged.
