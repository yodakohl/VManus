## Decision before implementation

The source-owned two alternative mixtures have a real closed constraint:
their fixed weights imply relative grades A,B,target,C = 0,1,2,3 up to an
affine calibration. With six freely assigned quantity labels that spacing can
change: the complete explicit countermodel in ALLOY_VARIABLE_CONTENT_REVIEW
preserves both final totals. W94–96 and P06 already establish why a freely
balanced account is insufficient. GDT902 and GDT969 rejected their fixed target
formats before obtaining numeric interpretations; they do not answer this
source-only identification question. GDT882 excludes a fixed additive literal
whole-line mechanism, which is not used here.

Question: after treating arithmetic, roles and references as known, does a
single decimal digit permutation over the actual printed mixed quantities
leave different numerical meanings for these five complete final accounts?
The smallest adequate test enumerates all 10! digit permutations once, with
four cumulative layers below. If even these known roles leave different
complete numbers, this small source kernel is insufficient for numerical
calibration and no target code search follows from it. If the source kernel
uniquely fixes its numerical values, retain it as an identifying *conditional*
content kernel and examine the separately required complete writing grammar.
Uniqueness does not by itself authorize a target experiment: that still needs
a finite shared renderer, not fresh scalar labels or open commentary. Neither
outcome reopens a previous target failure.
