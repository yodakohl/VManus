# GDT925: complete-line word-multiset discovery

Registered 2026-09-12 before this comparison. Exploratory, not a meaning test.
Question: do two complete prose lines preserve exactly the same literal word
multiset but change its order? Retain identical-order repetitions separately.

Decision note: GDT838 tested recoded16-group windows, GDT918 reciprocal triples,
and ETR001 masked family-template records. Their negative results do not give
this complete literal-line census. IP036 requires independently bound equivalent
units and forbids using anagrams to infer that binding. This experiment does NOT
satisfy that proposal: it only inventories possible comparison sites, with zero
identity/meaning credit. No existing failure is reopened or threshold repaired.

Use only the six already guarded GDT915 SOURCE_DISCOVERY/EVALUATION JSONs and
its SPEC.json (179 admitted selectors). Prior project exposure is complete;
there is no unseen confirmation partition in this discovery. f84/f84r remain
sealed. No images, source-language text, decoding, normalization or glosses.

For each reading separately take every complete P line with at least two groups.
Require every original group to match [a-z]+, consecutive source group indices,
and DEFINITE_SPACE on both sides of every internal seam. Reject a whole line
if any requirement fails; do not shorten it. Group by the sorted list of words,
including multiplicities. Save ALL groups containing at least two lines,
including exact-order-only repetitions. Enumerate every pair and identify
changed order, exact reversal, same/cross physical leaf, and word count.
ZL3b is primary; IT2a/RF1b are alternate readings, not independent manuscripts.
Report all n>=2 cases; distinguish n=2 from n>=3 without selecting a new cutoff.

A changed-order pair is only a candidate for future examination. It does not
establish paraphrase, a transposition cipher, grammar or translation. No p-value
or significance claim. If none occurs, stop this exact census; do not loosen
seams, remove words, merge forms, or choose substring windows. If pairs occur,
list all and distinguish cross-reading stability by exact page/locus pair IDs;
no new image or semantic follow-up is automatically selected.

Smallest test: one hash-group census and separate direct Counter pair comparison
on the same admitted lines. Total budget20min including implementation,
validation and publication, then stop expansion. All outcomes decide whether
this exact carrier supplies any literal comparison pair, not a word meaning.
