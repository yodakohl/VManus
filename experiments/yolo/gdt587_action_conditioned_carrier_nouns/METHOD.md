# GDT587 method

## Question

Can the four portable carrier roots `Y/AIIN/AIN/OR` receive simple,
action-conditioned concrete nouns at all of their exact GDT584 action hosts,
and can multi-carrier hosts be rendered as coherent object packets without
changing the roots globally or importing local learned names into running
text?

## Inputs

- GDT582's complete 15,889-slot ledger, 793 running statements and 744 local
  cards.
- GDT584's 1,921 exact `T/SH/CHD/S` action revisions, 6,289 host phrases, 591
  polished statements and 158 polished local cards.
- GDT585's 109 exact name assignments and 89 grammar-aware local labels.
- GDT586's complete 793-statement and 744-local-card reader.
- No new image, page, transcription, surface, segmentation, root, owner,
  statement boundary, or primary-governor link. `f84` and `f84r` are forbidden.

## Method

### 1. Exact candidate population

The compiler selects complete-ledger rows whose written slot is one of
`Y/AIIN/AIN/OR` and whose unchanged `primary_governor_key` is the key of one
of GDT584's 1,921 target actions. The join yields 1,243 unique written carrier
slots at 953 target-action hosts:

| layer | slots | hosts | units |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| running | 1,163 | 883 | 379 statements |
| local | 80 | 70 | 70 cards |

Of the running slots, 384 are written in another event packet than their
action head. They are therefore joined only by the exact governor key, never
by adjacency. The population occupies 136 observed
`register × GDT584-rule × carrier-root` cells. Unobserved cells are not filled.

### 2. Four levels of noun choice

Every selected slot retains its short portable core:

```text
Y=POSTEN   AIIN=WERT   AIN=ANTEIL   OR=EINHEIT
```

The reader then chooses the German noun in this order:

1. an exact packet rule for a written multi-carrier host;
2. the fixed GDT584 action rule;
3. the manuscript register;
4. the existing GDT584 noun as fallback.

The main contextual contrasts are deliberately simple:

- Source `AIIN` is `Arbeitsflüssigkeit` under resting/sorting and
  `Flüssigkeitsmenge` under fixing; `AIN` is a `Teilmenge`.
- Herbal `Y` is `Pflanzenmaterial`; action state such as wet, dry, warm, grind,
  or soak remains in the verb. `AIIN` is normally `Pflanzenauszug`, but an
  explicit measure-setting head selects `Auszugsmenge`.
- Celestial keeps position/value/share and selects `Ringsegment` for `OR`.
- Biological clean bath/cool `Y` can be `Körper`; relation-, address-, form-,
  or new-batch-bearing hosts retain `Stationsansatz`. `AIIN` becomes
  `Badfüllung` only under bathing and otherwise `Stationsmaß`.
- Pharma `Y` is `Drogenmaterial`, `AIIN` is `Arzneiauszug` except at explicit
  dose setting, `AIN` is `Zutatenportion`, and `OR` selects a held material
  unit or medicinal batch by action.

Lemma, accusative object form and genitive form are stored separately for all
1,243 assignments. The action supplies condition or process; the noun does not
become permanently “dried material”, “soaked unit”, or another sentence-sized
dictionary entry.

### 3. Bounded packet composition

The renderer reuses GDT584's exact statement-wide governor partition but adds
only observed packet templates:

- Source `Y+AIN` under fixing: `Teilmenge des Arbeitsmaterials`.
- Source `Y+AIIN` under sorting: `Arbeitsflüssigkeit vom Arbeitsmaterial`.
- Celestial `Y+OR+AIIN`: `Ringposition des Ringsegments auf den Positionswert`.
- Biological `Y+AIN` under treatment: `Körperteil`.
- Biological diversion: stream, quantity, partial quantity and basin content
  compose into one flow packet.
- Biological bath `AIIN`: bath fill becomes a setting rather than a second
  equal object.
- Herbal/Pharma strain: extract and material receive the appropriate genitive
  or `aus der Portion` realization.
- Herbal/Pharma measure setting: extract quantity is set for the written
  material.

Every written slot remains in the exact trace even when two atoms are realized
as one German compound or relational phrase.

### 4. Full-reader integration

Only the 379 candidate statements and 70 candidate local cards are rerendered.
The other 414 statements and 674 local cards remain byte-identical to GDT586.
GDT585 name assignments are reapplied by exact slot before rendering.

There is no exact carrier/name slot or governor overlap. One local card,
`P1003-E0414`, contains both a separate learned name and a separate carrier.
Its bounded composer preserves
`dunkle Faserwurzeldroge [cheo]` and realizes the independent `Y` as
`Drogenmaterial`; it never derives the name from `Y`.

Twenty register passages and five stress cases are published side by side.
The stress pass caused one important correction: a first pure-`Y` bath rule
was too broad. Address, relation, form and new-batch signals now block the body
reading, leaving `G407-S572` and `G407-S115` byte-identical to GDT586.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass requires the exact 1,243-slot population, 953 action hosts, 136 observed
cells, unchanged portable core for every assignment, complete host and trace
projection, complete ordered 793/744 editions, exact preservation of all
noncandidate reader units, retained name separation, the named packet examples,
both body negative controls, and absence of forbidden pages. The independent
validator passes 69/69 checks.

GDT587 is an exploratory German reader layer. Its noun choices are conditioned
by action rules that were themselves inferred partly from these carrier
classes; they are useful editorial readings, not independent confirmation. It
confirms no manuscript lexeme, morpheme, plaintext, language, species,
substance, procedure, body part, patient, illness, remedy, astronomical value,
or historical codebook and licenses no unseen surface or new page.
