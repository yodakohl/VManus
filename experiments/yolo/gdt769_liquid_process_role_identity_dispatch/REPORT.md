## Two scoring corrections that must remain visible

### R03 requires both axes on the same target occurrence

The corrected R03 product/result expression is:

```text
(F01_AMOUNT_DIRECT OR F02_VALUE_DIRECT)
AND
(F06_TARGET_BEFORE_PROCESS OR F07_LINE_FINAL_OR_CLOSE)
ON_SAME_TARGET_OCCURRENCE
```

An amount-bearing occurrence on one page and an endpoint occurrence somewhere else may not be added together and called product evidence. The complete conjunction must first occur around one exact target token. After removing the strongest supporting locus, the same two-axis conjunction must recur on another page. This correction matters especially for common or multifunctional forms: the earlier aggregate treatment could manufacture a product role from unrelated uses.

### F14 alone never selects R05

F14 records only that a target is medial with usable material on both sides. That geometry is common and contributes no R05 support page, support locus, or ablation survival by itself. R05 requires:

```text
F14_MEDIAL_TWO_SIDED_LINKER
AND
(F15_STATE_TRANSITION_BRIDGE OR F16_RELATIONAL_AMOUNT_ORDER)
```

The conjunction must occur on at least three pages and remain on two pages after the strongest locus is removed. Thus a high F14 count cannot turn medial `ckhy` into a linker. For `otar`, mixed F15 directions remain compatible with a general `then/continue` marker; for `bis` or a named process, the right-hand target direction must be consistent.
