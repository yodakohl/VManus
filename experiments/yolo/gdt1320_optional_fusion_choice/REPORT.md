## What this changes

A content-independent coin with one probability per Currier/height is not an
adequate retained account of these choices under this panel's assumptions. We
stop that particular simple stochastic writer. Optional fusion in general remains
open. Word-specific spelling memory is one possible explanation of the predictive
effect; hand, leaf, internal position, local correlation, transcription or selection
differences can also violate the tested null. None is selected by this test.
No post-result expanded conditioning, new renderer or direction selection follows.
In particular HB's larger gain does NOT identify HB as the right reading: BH and
HB condition on different decoded words and have different opportunity sets.
