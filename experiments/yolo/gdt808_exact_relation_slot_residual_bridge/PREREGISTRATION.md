# GDT808 transparent design record

Recorded 2026-09-04 before the official builder and official four-deck scores
were produced.  This is outcome-aware, not blind: GDT807 already showed a provisional
`qokeol/qokol` paragraph split, and its exploratory red-team weights exposed
`qokeody/qokedy` as a possible parallel family.  Before this record we also
inspected raw and stable four-cell capacity, section/position distributions,
the old GDT632/GDT633 E/O/EO working hypotheses, and historical analogues.

Known at registration:

- 35 carriers are raw-complete, 28 have a stable occurrence in all cells, and
  CORE13 has useful capacity in every cell;
- after the fixed paragraph, reader, LCS, and own-family-singleton rules,
  CORE13 contains 1,777 focal events on 596 paragraphs and 169 physical
  folios;
- raw section distributions differ sharply between several cells, so a raw
  context classifier is not an adequate compositional test;
- the GDT633 defaults `e=attributive binding` and `o=preparation/form` are
  pre-existing working rivals, not evidence produced by GDT808;
- period comparators strongly support learned heads plus repeated record
  fields, but no inspected c.1390–1450 source establishes an analogous
  semantic character inserted inside many drug names.

Two read-only prototype implementations were then requested before this design
was published.  Both found strong held-carrier nuisance transfer within each
axis (`L_TO_L` macro AUC roughly 0.63–0.65 and `DY_TO_DY` roughly 0.75–0.79),
only a small and scorer-sensitive exact-neighbour increment, and weak or
inverted cross-axis transfer.  One prototype showed that mixing focal
position and anonymous quarantine-mask status into SLOT_HOLE can create an
artificial sign reversal.  Those fields were therefore moved to FORM_REGIME
or omitted; the registered local increment now uses stable exact neighbour
identity only.  These diagnostics have no official selection credit and must
be independently reconstructed.  They changed the registered question in one
justified way: within-axis portability is now primary, while cross-axis
transfer decides shared versus distinct relations.  Registering only the
initially hoped-for shared direction after seeing this diagnostic would have
thrown away the more informative rectangle.

Unknown at registration are all independently reconstructed official held
transfer scores, null ranks, carrier-level directions, sensitivity outcomes,
and historical-role ranking from the final feature artifacts.  Exploratory
scripts and notebook-like shell diagnostics have no selection credit.  The official builder must reconstruct
events from guarded sources, emit all predictions, and produce every stated
sensitivity and negative result.

The practical purpose is not to discard every imperfect hypothesis.  The
output always retains a ranked, replaceable working theory.  The thresholds in
`METHOD.md` control only whether that theory may be exported as a reusable
formal slot beyond the observed complete surfaces.
