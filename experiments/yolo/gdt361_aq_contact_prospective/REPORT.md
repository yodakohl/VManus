# GDT361 prospective AQ/contact report

Status: **FROZEN_DIRECTION_SUPPORTED_EXPLORATORILY**.

GDT361 gives the GDT360 AQ/contact lead a third-folio directional hit, but the
new evidence is sparse and statistically weak. It does not establish a visual
meaning for AQ.

## Frozen prospective result

The six scored f102v2/L2 loci had been visually frozen as 3 CONTACT, 2
CLEAR_GAP, and 1 UNCERTAIN before their family rows were opened.

| State | AQ/AQA | total |
|---|---:|---:|
| CONTACT | 1 | 3 |
| CLEAR_GAP | 0 | 2 |
| UNCERTAIN | 0 | 1 |

The CONTACT-minus-GAP prevalence difference is **+0.3333**. Conditional on
three CONTACT assignments and one AQ-positive row among the five hard calls,
the inclusive exact within-array one-sided probability is **6/10 = .60**.
Thus the predeclared direction is positive, but the array contains only one
prospective AQ occurrence and supplies little inferential leverage.

The single positive is `f102v2.12` (`AQACAKA`), visually graded CONTACT. The
two other CONTACT calls, `f102v2.13` (`APKA`) and `.14` (`CACAB`), are direct
counterexamples to any necessary AQ→CONTACT rule. The two hard GAP calls are
non-AQ. The uncertain crease-damaged `.16` is also non-AQ and carries the only
alternative site.

All seven first-group family predicates have the same AQ/non-AQ state in
ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b; those are alternate readings of one manuscript, not
replications. `AQ` and `AQA` happen to define the same mask on this six-row
panel.

## Excluded but disclosed row

`f102v2.10` is AQ/AQA and was visually graded CONTACT. It is attractive, but
its formal family was displayed during the pre-freeze source audit, so it is
excluded from the prospective score. Adding it descriptively gives 2/4 AQ
CONTACT versus 0/2 AQ GAP and an exact p of .40. It cannot repair the primary
result.

## Relationship to GDT360

GDT360's already exposed discovery panel had 5/8 AQ CONTACT versus 2/18 AQ
GAP. Adding only the six prospectively scored GDT361 rows gives the descriptive
combined counts 6/11 versus 2/20. The direction therefore persists across a
third physical folio, while the new array by itself remains weak.

This is not a clean validation:

- AQ was postselected from 306 GDT360 masks;
- the experiment has one new array on one Pharma folio;
- the observer was a single AI direct reviewer, not an independent human;
- the correct full canvas and source diplomatic surfaces were visible before
  the calls, although the six target family values were not;
- the initial freeze named the adjacent wrong canvas and required a published
  correction before crop-level review;
- the exact opportunity set has only five hard calls and ten possible state
  assignments.

## Next route

If this lead is pursued, the next acquisition should freeze a **panel** of
complete, mechanically localizable arrays on additional physical folios in one
source-only step, then grade every selected locus before any target family is
opened. Serially choosing one visually convenient array at a time would make
the postselection problem worse. One-sided and uncertain arrays should remain
in the discovery panel rather than becoming kill gates.

No existing array should be relabelled to strengthen the result, and the
pre-exposed `.10` row must remain outside the prospective denominator.

## Claim ceiling

The only retained claim is that the frozen AQ/AQA direction recurred weakly on
one new f102v2 label array. AQ is not CONTACT, attachment, a plant class, a
word, a morpheme, a part of speech, a sound, a language form, plaintext, or a
translation. No f84 material was accessed.
