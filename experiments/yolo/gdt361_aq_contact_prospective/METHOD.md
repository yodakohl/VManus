## Visual observation rule

For each complete inscription group, record one of:

- `CONTACT`: at least one visible inscription stroke touches, crosses, or is
  overlain by a non-writing drawn/painted contour;
- `CLEAR_GAP`: visible parchment separates every inscription stroke from every
  nearby non-writing contour;
- `UNCERTAIN`: fading, crease, ambiguous segmentation, or overlap prevents a
  reliable binary call.

The observer must first census whether the seven existing loci exhaust the
visible source-described row. `UNCERTAIN` is retained as missing evidence; it
is never forced to either class. A one-sided array remains an observation and
does not terminate the experiment.
