# GDT504 method

## Question

When each of the 46 GDT502 target phrases is placed beside its concrete old
clause, does every added or removed component have the expected small semantic
effect under the fixed dictionary and owner-local register expansions?

## Inputs

- the 46 supported GDT502 comparison cards;
- all 4,576 exact GDT416 clauses;
- the 46-component GDT413 working dictionary;
- all 95 GDT415 root×register expansions.

No new page, image, transcription, root value or target phrase is introduced.

## Method

1. Align each target recipe and selected carrier recipe by a stable longest
   ordered exact-token subsequence.
2. For a proper-partial card, require the complete carrier to be a target
   subsequence. Record every target-only token and its position.
3. For a pair-only card, require the exact ordered action pair in both recipes.
   Keep both target-only and carrier-only frame tokens; never treat the entire
   carrier sentence as the target frame.
4. Expand every changed token through GDT413 and, where available, GDT415's
   target or carrier register value.
5. Assign one of fourteen concrete phrase operations: destination, unit,
   second or inherited post, serial action, grade, counted repetition, or one
   of six pair-carrier frame edits.
6. Check all target additions for their German realization. A repeated action
   must surface as `zweimal`; a recipe-level explicitization may retain the
   already spoken inherited noun. Check every carrier-only token in the old
   clause and require its marker to be absent from the target phrase.
7. Publish three support depths separately: direct local delta,
   cross-register normalized delta and pair-backbone frame edit.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A card is phrase-consistent only when all of its changed-token effects pass.
This checks the internal reversibility and naturalness of the current working
German; it is not independent evidence that a composed target occurs in the
manuscript. Cross-register deltas transfer portable values, not owner nouns.
Pair cards transfer only the ordered actions and explicitly remove or replace
all foreign carrier context. Evidence labels remain `COMPOSED_WORKING` and no
surface or occurrence is predicted.
