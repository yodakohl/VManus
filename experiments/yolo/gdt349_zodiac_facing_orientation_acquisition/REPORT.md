direction, two folios for each direction, three mixed page-by-ring strata, and
two folios containing mixed strata.  Only `f70v2:INNER` contains both asserted
directions, and the sole right-facing assertion occurs on f70.  Completeness,
image provenance, and the uncertainty-fraction gate pass.

This is an acquisition-capacity failure, not evidence that pose is absent from
the manuscript.  It says that this strict *absolute image-left/right profile*
endpoint cannot support the planned transferable formal test.  No Voynich
surface, family, tuple, PAGE_HOST, or other formal payload was opened or joined,
and no formal score was run.
