# GDT349 source audit

The source frame is intentionally narrow.

- `zodiac_label_cycle_capacity.tsv` is the complete strict 235-position cyclic
  zodiac panel with page, physical folio, ring, Grove ordinal, source-record
  identifier, and current locus.  Its upstream capacity audit documents the
  65 public records excluded for incomplete/non-primary rings or non-ring
  placement.  It contains no f84 row.
- `public_zodiac_label_attribute_capacity.json` is the prior source-only
  capacity result that exposed the incomplete public facing annotations.  It
  did not inspect or score Voynich formal values.
- ZBV001 already completed the barrel/no-barrel native audit and stopped on
  ring confounding.  GDT349 must not present barrel state as new evidence or
  reopen that score.
- ZCV001 and ZST001 already tested clothing and star-tail states.  GDT349 does
  not reuse their formal outcomes or select rows from their winners.

The public Grove/Stolfi ordinal relation is a human indexing relation, not an
author-drawn leader.  It is retained for complete slot localization but cannot
by itself establish semantic ownership.

The new image review is limited to the official Yale canvases for f70v2,
f70v1, f71r, f71v, f72r1, f72r2, f72r3, f72v1, f72v2, f72v3, f73r, and f73v.
No f84 material is eligible.
