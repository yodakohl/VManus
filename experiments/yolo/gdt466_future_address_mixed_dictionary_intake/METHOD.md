# GDT466 method

## Question

Can the closed mixed codebook/nomenclator architecture be compiled into a
deterministic intake reader that gives every future visible address a default
reading while preserving unknown content as a learned owner-class name?

## Inputs

- GDT465's closed 107-label dictionary;
- GDT460's 27 direction-calibrated edges and seventeen strict owner-family
  markers;
- GDT461's nine strict-internal channels and `cheo` family bridge;
- GDT462's accepted `ar-` package exception;
- GDT463's four exact-card edge bridges;
- GDT464's three general prefix/suffix bridges. Its `yddy` result is a complete
  exact package, not a general substring channel.

## Method

The compiler emits 44 unique direction/stem function channels and eighteen
content-class-bound family markers. Intake precedence is fixed:

1. Return a known GDT466 label exactly.
2. On an unseen surface, select the longest calibrated prefix, then the longest
   nonoverlapping suffix, then longest nonoverlapping strict-internal channels
   inside the remaining center.
3. Report owner-family markers only when their frozen content class equals the
   supplied page/image owner class.
4. Wrap every uncovered substring—or the entire surface if nothing matches—as
   a learned `STERNSTELLENNAME`, `DROGENNAME`, `BADSTATIONSNAME`,
   `PFLANZENNAME`, or generic `LOKALNAME`.

Three replay layers exercise the order. Exact replay uses all 107 dictionary
cards. Cold replay suppresses exact identity and asks what the 44 general
channels alone recover. Finally, one synthetic `x` is inserted after the first
learned character in each of the 89 labels that has a name core. This makes a
new surface while leaving its old function substrings intact. Forty-four
single-channel probes, positive and wrong-class probes for all eighteen family
markers, and one whole-name fallback probe exercise every branch.

Compilation also checks whether a frozen general rule was missed by the source
dictionary. This found one propagation omission: GDT462's already accepted
prefix `ar=AR` also applies to `ararchodaiin`. GDT466 corrects that row without
adding a channel or value.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The reader may expose only frozen function values and frozen owner-family
classes. It may never assign an individual name-core meaning, transfer a family
across owner classes, generate a spelling, or predict that a surface occurs.
The owner class is supplied by context; it is not guessed from the word.

Synthetic `x` probes are mechanism tests, not Voynich surface predictions or
new manuscript material. No new page, component meaning, object identity,
plaintext, language or confirmed lexeme is added.
