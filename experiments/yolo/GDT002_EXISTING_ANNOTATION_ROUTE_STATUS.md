## Route decision

The present human/AI annotation corpus does not ground an anonymous formal
family, a stable distributed page vector, or a local multi-axis label tuple.
Do not mine GDT363–366 loadings, prefixes, or individual families for a new
gloss. Do not reinterpret the old binary stops as passes.

Reopen this route only with one of the following genuinely new capacities:

1. the same singular, source-bound external referent or visual contrast on at
   least three independent physical folios, with a complete held folio;
2. at least three visual axes observed on the same complete label cells, with
   both states mobile within multiple folios;
3. a readable external witness that fixes authorial slot ownership/order before
   Voynich formal values are inspected.

The next useful grounding experiment should predict a full anonymous formal
tuple or distributed profile on that held evidence. More page-wide substring,
prefix, PCA-loading, or catalogue mining is not warranted. f84 remains sealed.
