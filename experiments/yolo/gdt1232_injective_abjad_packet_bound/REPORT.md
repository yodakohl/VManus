The human rule being tested is precise: read one complete written group as one
additive source-letter packet; every working sign has its own distinct positive
value in {1..9,10..90by10,100..900by100,1000}; a packet's total must be in that
same pool. The historical example uses actual Arabic letters with this distinct
letter-value convention. Applying it to these22 working units is our declared
adaptation, not a historically identified native alphabet. Contextual forms,
different atomic units, shared-value signs, other sums, omissions or state are
outside this exact contract and are not automatically selected repairs.

Only19 working signs occur in the fixed packet. Any injective assignment to
those signs could be extended to22 by choosing unused values from the28-member
pool. The same argument permits the proof to ignore signs outside a core.
Thus the certificate is not relying on accidental omission of the remaining
working signs. It also does not assume that every plaintext letter can actually
be written, or that a paid message/word framing exists; these are necessary
conditions only. Failure already occurs before those additional obligations.
