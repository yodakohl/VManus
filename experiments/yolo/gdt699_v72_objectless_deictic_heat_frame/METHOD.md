# GDT699 method

## Question

Can the two admitted objectless `ykaiin`/grade-III heat frames license the sole
current `ykain`/grade-II sister to take one uniquely typed material share from
the immediately preceding complete nominal block, while leaving its separate
quantity register unbound?

## Inputs

- GDT388 supplies the frozen edge-packet and capacity contract used to audit
  whether the workshop edge is externally score-ready.
- GDT626 supplies the already registered formal `yka` family: `ykain` at value
  II and `ykaiin` at value III.  Its 11/43 counts are inherited lexical-card
  counts, not participant evidence.
- GDT664 and GDT665 supply the inherited whole-card working readings
  `Y_REFERENCE+K_HOT+...II/III`.  They do not prove `y` or `k` as free words.
- GDT689 supplies the exact five line-level action ordinals and surfaces.
  GDT693 supplies the bounded `oar` share slot and independently types the
  neighbouring `aiin` as a value-III head; GDT695 keeps `oar|aiin` as two
  semantic units with no binding.
- GDT696 supplies the five reference decisions and the held H002/H007 rivals;
  GDT697 supplies admitted prototype edges C001 and C006.
- GDT698 supplies the complete unchanged 479-token, 51-line and three-span V71
  scope.  The f86v5.24 surface is also checked against the existing exact
  ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b row.

## Method

`src/V72_HEAT_REFERENCE_CASE_SPECS.tsv` fixes five cases before scoring:

- C001 and C006 are replay-only `ykaiin` prototypes;
- R010 is an outside-family multi-donor control;
- R012 is an outside-family action that already writes `Krautdroge` as its
  material object;
- R021 is the sole YKA-II candidate.

The current 479-token scope is scanned exhaustively for action-licensed
`ykain|ykaiin`; exactly the two prototypes and R021 must occur.  R021 can pass
only because its immediately preceding complete V68 block contains `oar#1`,
whose GDT693 share slot and current gloss type it as a material share, followed
by `aiin#2`, whose current gloss is only `Menge III`.  V68 reports two semantic
units and `binding_ids=NONE`.  The new edge is therefore only #1→#3.  The old
H007 block edge #1–2→#3 and its phrase “in Menge III” remain rejected.

The relation is also emitted in the GDT388 packet schema and passed through
`./vmanus-exp check-edge-packet`.  It is intentionally not score-ready: it was
selected with formal access and has no visual provenance, capacity, held fold
or mobile null.  That does not prevent a workshop B-edge, but it prevents this
edge from masquerading as external grounding evidence.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass requires exactly five cases, three current YKA actions, two unchanged
prototype replays, one new `B_WORKING_LOCAL` edge C010 from f86v5.24#1 to #3,
two held outside-family controls and explicit exclusion of #2.  All 479 token
glosses, 51 inherited clause readings and three bound spans must remain
byte-identical, with no new word meaning or page.

V72 is one occurrence-bound exploratory German relation reading.  It is not a
portable YKAIN participant rule, a proof that YKA historically means heating,
or recovered plaintext, language or codebook.
