independent exceptions. They fall exactly into three secondary switches:

- the Q/SH habit can enter its short S/SH branch, generating `sol`, `sor`,

- the D habit can use its T branch, generating `taiin`, `tal`, `tchedy`.

registered card-surface pairs. There is no residual spelling exception, and
