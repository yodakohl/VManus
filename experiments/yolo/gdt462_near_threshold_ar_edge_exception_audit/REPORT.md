## Why the seventh form does not change the value

The sole mismatch is `arary = P+AR+AR`. It is not an isolated irregular
reading. A second surface, `parar`, carries the same exact recipe on another
page. In both cards:

- one `P=EINSETZEN` head governs the card;
- two separate `AR=AUSGANG` atoms occupy positions 2 and 3;
- both are recorded as peer members of the established
  `FREE_PLURAL_OR_REPEAT` package.

Thus the mismatch belongs to an exact repeated-relation whole-card hull. It
does not require a second value for `ar`. GDT462 accepts this one named package
exception and does not lower the general edge threshold.
