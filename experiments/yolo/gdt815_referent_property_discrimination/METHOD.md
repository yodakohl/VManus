## Method

Read primary reports via the index and screen duplicate routes. Three independent
agents review exact written carriers, astral ownership/history, and a nonthermal
property rival. Root reviews full relevant source paragraphs, cached pictures
and primary historical excerpts. Ten explicit cards preserve all three source
readings. Six fixed candidates share the unchanged seven whole guesses. The
runner stores literal one-item-per-token displays, not fluent hidden translation.
It stores three explicit label/prose/target chains and a nine-contact GDT388
packet. Agent notes retain additional contacts and counterexamples. The separate
validator checks the source and literal claims without invoking the runner.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

No frequency ranking, significance score or automatic semantic promotion.
Exact recurrence does not identify nouns, antecedents, owners or species. A
historical analogy motivates a candidate but does not decode it. Bitter is a
literal taste candidate and cannot silently become an astral medicinal effect.
Historical carrier and predicate frame remain separate from image recognition.
All13 word/model assignments are C0. Software checks are not semantic validation.
