# GDT739 method

## Question

Can the open dimension and patient of GDT738's twelve licensed complete wholes
be bound locally without reviving the discarded p/s/r/l patient model or
turning family analogies into unconditional word meanings?

## Inputs and boundary

- GDT738's 202 position-scoped renderer patches and twelve licensed cards;
- GDT737's cached occurrence geometry;
- GDT734's compact cell register and integrated line reader;
- the guarded alternate-reader exactness map already used by GDT738;
- explicit axis, whole, result-override, and passage-selection source tables in
  `src/`.

The inherited allow-list contains 179 cached pages, but the target positions
occupy only the already admitted GDT738 footprint. No page, image, or
transcription was added. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Host gate

For every target, the builder audits up to five cells on each side. A neighbor
is eligible only when it is alternate-reader exact, known in GDT734, W2 or W3,
zero-composition, not another GDT738 target, not an opaque p/s/r/l-head form,
free of the retired literal powder/seed/root/wood patients, and carries an
explicit axis tag.

Distance one and then distance two may dispatch. Distance three to five is a
sensitivity deck only. It cannot alter the renderer.

## Scalar dispatch

The seven `ain/kain/kaiin/kar` complete-whole forms contribute only their
current ordered level I, II, or III. The nearest typified ring may select:

- `QUALITY_DEGREE`: a hot/cold/dry/moist cue combined with a value/grade stem;
- `AMOUNT_DOSE`: amount, portion, measure, weighing/measuring, or an explicitly
  numbered part/fraction;
- `PROCESS_PASS`: a counted/repeated treatment, step, or ordinal preparation.

A bare operation does not create a process passage. If a ring contains rival
classes, the occurrence remains open. Nothing in this rule exports a body or
substring meaning.

## State and result dispatch

The 30 `cheol/cheedy/sheedy` occurrences receive a separate manual optical mode
audit. `pcheol/lcheol` remain descriptive state fields. Four `lcheedy`, three
`lsheedy`, and one `rsheedy` loci receive result/end mode; two have strong local
W2/W3 support and six are endpoint/closure best fits. Dry or moist is spoken
only when the corresponding favored axis has its own eligible host within
distance two. Otherwise the favored family orientation stays in a silent
candidate field.

## Carrier binding and renderer

The nearest preparation, material, or part host may supply only a broad carrier
class such as `Zubereitungsmaterial` or `Materialteil`. Its literal substance
name is never copied. The renderer emits a compact microentry, not an expanded
instructional sentence. Unsupported cells in the twenty-line reader appear as
`[surface:?]`.

## Claim ceiling

Every licence is attached to one complete observed surface at one enumerated
position. Global whole export, component export, H1–H4/body lexemes, unseen
forms, ingredient/species names, historical expansions, and plaintext claims
remain zero.
