# Third compact workshop grammar

## The 22 reusable cores

- Flow: `OK` ANSETZEN, `OT` DANACH, `OL` WEITER.
- Actions: `CH` ENTNEHMEN, `SH` HALTEN, `CTH` BEREITEN, `CHK` WÄRMEN,
  `SHED` STEHENLASSEN, `LSH` SPÜLEN.
- Transfer: `K` ZUGEBEN, `L` LEITEN, `CHD` UMSETZEN, `P` EINFÜLLEN.
- Quantity: `AIIN` SOLLMASS, `AIN` PORTION.
- Address: `AL` ZIELSTELLE, `AR` QUELLE.
- Grade: `E` KURZ, `EE` LANG, `EEE` VOLL.
- Endpoint: `Y` DIES / laufender Posten, `DY` SCHLUSS in its licensed card.

Read these in registered recipe order. Then copy the exact learned card rather
than spelling a new form ad hoc.

## The nine recurrent wall-strip values

`SOLK` SAMMELSTELLE, `T` ANWENDEN, `AIR` WASSER, `OR` ANSATZ, `HO` ZUTAT,
`CKH` DURCHLASS, `O` VORGANG, `IIN` ARBEITSSTUFE, `R` KÜHLEN.

They remain concrete and reusable, but do not yet have enough controlled slots
to be freely extended.

## Small remainder

`AN` is one bound variant; `CFH`, `S`, `DA`, and `LD` are local singleton
values. Only three complete command cards are still memorized without internal
composition: `os` FACH, `dchol/schol` WIEDERAUFNEHMEN, and `talam` VERWAHREN.

The core22 touches 166/173 card types and 363/381 events. More importantly,
105 cards / 292 events now consist entirely of core components. Sixty-four
unique unseen surfaces remain predictions only; `cheeeky` was proposed twice
by independent earlier boards.
