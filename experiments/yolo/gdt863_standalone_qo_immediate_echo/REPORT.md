**STRICT_ECHO_COUNTEREXAMPLE.** Seven exact coordinates agree across all three
readings on an eligible standalone `qo` followed immediately by a complete raw
group that does not start `qo`. They lie on six physical leaves: f76r/f76v
are two faces of the same leaf. Four other concordant coordinates do echo.

Thus concrete strings include `qo kaiin`, `qo olain` and `qo saly` under the

f108r.6#3→qokeey, f111v.21#12→qokain, f116r.42#1→qokain and
f76r.30#5→qokey. These demonstrate why the motivating positive example cannot
support an exceptionless immediate-echo rule.

motion. No automatic relaxed pairing, expanded source search or successor
model follows from this census.
