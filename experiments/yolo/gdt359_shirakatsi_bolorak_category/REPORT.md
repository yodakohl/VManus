# GDT359 report: *bolorak* is not an eight-compartment key

## Decision

`BOLORAK_CATEGORY_BROADENED_NO_FIGURE_KEY`

The 1958 scholarly monograph supplies a clean counterexample to the tempting
inference that the 1974 encyclopedia's *bolorak* caption identifies the Penn
wheel as a particular eight-phase construction. It prints a visibly different
concentric annular grid and captions it `Շիրակացու տոմարական բոլորակներից
մեկը`—one of Shirakatsi's calendrical boloraks.

The exact Penn surface remains genuinely associated in a secondary source with
a Shirakatsi *bolorak* tradition. But *bolorak* is now positively demonstrated
to cover distinct circular/table layouts. It does not fix topology,
cardinality, function, slot values, or reading order.

The complete 1958 scan contains another distinct concentric astronomical
figure and no visible exact Penn eight-compartment surface. The 1962 lunar
edition is bibliographically verified but was not acquired as a public scan.
Tumanian's 1971 attribution correction for two works in the same editorial
tradition adds a real caution without deciding the Penn figure's authorship.

Accordingly, the GDT354–359 lead remains a period-appropriate diagram-family
homologue only. There is still no legal endpoint for Voynich slot scoring.

## Claim ceiling

Distinct published diagram topologies share Shirakatsi *bolorak* vocabulary.
No eight-phase identification, exact diagram function, independent homologue,
Voynich alignment, Armenian origin/authorship, language, plaintext, meaning,
or translation follows.
