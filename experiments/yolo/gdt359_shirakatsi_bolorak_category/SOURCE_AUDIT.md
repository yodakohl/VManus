# GDT359 external source audit

## A second published *bolorak* topology

The complete public scan of the 1958 scholarly monograph *Anania Shirakatsi*
was inspected without OCR. Scan surface 43, containing printed pp.80–81,
reproduces a manuscript diagram made of several concentric annular grids. It is
visibly unlike the eight inward-curving compartments of LJS 443 f.209r.

The printed Armenian caption beneath the annular grid reads:

> Շիրակացու տոմարական բոլորակներից մեկը

Literal working translation: “One of Shirakatsi's calendrical boloraks.” The
important evidence is the source wording, not the translation. The 1974
encyclopedia caption audited in GDT358 calls the Penn image one of the
`բոլորակներից` composed by Shirakatsi. The shared vocabulary therefore spans
at least two visibly different published topologies.

Scan surface 34 (printed pp.64–65) contains another different concentric
astronomical manuscript diagram, with a central star/crescent sequence inside
large rings. A complete 75-surface census found no visible exact reproduction
of the Penn eight-compartment wheel.

## Edition and attribution limits

The National Library of Armenia bibliography records A. G. Abrahamyan's 1962
edition *Anania Shirakatsi's Lunar Cycles / Tables of the Lunar Circle* as a
110-page Armenian/Russian publication. A public scan of that exact edition was
not acquired in this pass. GDT358's 1979 collected-edition audit remains the
available visual proxy for the lunar works republished from that edition, and
it found several other lunar tables but no exact Penn wheel.

B. E. Tumanian's 1971 scholarly article examines two works attributed to
Shirakatsi in the 1962 and 1970 editions and argues that the spring-full-moon
tables reflect the later system of Hovhannes Sarkawag. This is material
evidence that attribution inside that editorial corpus is not automatically
secure. It concerns those two works, not the exact Penn figure, so it is
recorded as a warning rather than a refutation of the encyclopedia caption.

## Result

The new source evidence narrows rather than strengthens the prospective
alignment:

- *bolorak* vocabulary: securely attested on distinct diagram topologies;
- exact Penn function: still absent;
- eight slot values or phases: absent;
- authored start, direction, and order: absent;
- independent keyed witness: absent.

No Voynich target, image, transcription, tuple, or formal payload was opened or
scored.
