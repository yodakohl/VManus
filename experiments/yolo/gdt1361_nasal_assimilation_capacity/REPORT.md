## A complete rule with real ambiguity

IDEA1041uses22abstract source categories and a fixed22-unit bijection. For a
chosen disjoint ordered pair, original N before T writes T, otherwise it remains
N. The change is applied once using ORIGINAL neighbors. Boundaries and length
are preserved. Thus NT and TT both write TT. A reader enumerates and re-encodes
all possible source strings, retaining ambiguity instead of inventing one word.
The nasal/stop interpretation is only the proposed motivation; no actual sound
or language is assigned. This is a different contextual, information-losing
writer from1354's freelyUD covering code, not a phonetic decoding of that table.

The easy but wrong prediction would be that NT never occurs in the output.
In fact NNT writes NTT. The correct exact condition is that every written NT is
immediately followed by another T. Word-final NT and NTX with X≠T are forbidden.
Conversely, replacing the first T after an N-run by N gives a source preimage
for every word satisfying this condition. Disjoint pair rules do not interfere.
This characterizes the whole formal output language, not just these examples.

## What survival does and does not establish

Most active survivors have NO visible NT:40/45IT,45/48RF,44/46ZL. They have TT,
so a source NT is possible, but the data do not show it actually occurred.
Even visible NTT does not identify sounds; under this model it requires a source
NNT cluster, and a source-language account must support that obligation.
No phonological inventory, dictionary or frequency model was selected.

For T=e specifically, the allowed N labels are f/i/m/n in IT, f/m/n in RF,
and a/f/m/n in ZL. These are conditional table slots, NOT proposed sound values.
The reader differences are retained. There are no semantic or phonetic votes
among readers, and no key is selected from this list.

Every accepted word has a constructed abstract preimage. That is why mere
parseability cannot be positive evidence for this mechanism. Its reader also
loses genuine source distinctions. Ordinary contextual ambiguity is permitted;
unique source recovery is not asserted. The fixed source-word type/length
constraints retained in1041are not repaired by neutralization: this deterministic
writer cannot increase the number of types in a fixed source-token sample.

Decision: keep1041inconclusive and retain the exact pair exclusions for any
future independently grounded source test. Do not start a decoder, pick the
best-looking pair, call a working unit nasal/stop, or infer that repeated units
are phonetic. A different source-bearing consequence is required. Earlier967,
