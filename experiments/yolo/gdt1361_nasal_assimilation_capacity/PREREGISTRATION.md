## Frozen contract and evidence

Use1041as authored. The source has22abstract segment symbols and a fixed bijection
to the22working units. One or more disjoint N/T pairs may neutralize. All changes
consult the ORIGINAL source word simultaneously: N followed by its T becomes T;
everything else remains. Whole-word spaces and lengths are preserved. The reader
returns all exact source strings re-encoding to the word, not one favored reading.
No historical phonetic values, language or lexicon are supplied.

A written NT must have another T immediately after it: NNT→NTT is valid.
Word-final NT or NTX with X≠T is impossible. This is also sufficient: at each
visible NT change its T back to N; the following original T remains and the
forward rule reconstructs the word. Disjoint pairs do not interfere. Thus
single-pair failures reject every larger disjoint-pair model containing that pair.
Survivors are not evidence that the transformation actually occurred: TT can
originate in TT, and any accepted string has a formal inverse.
