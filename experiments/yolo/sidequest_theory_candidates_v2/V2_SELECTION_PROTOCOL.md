# Sidequest V2 theory-selection protocol

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: frozen before reading any V2 candidate outcome. This is a speculative
YOLO sidequest protocol, not a scientific test or a translation claim.

## Fixed scope

Only the ten pages in `SIDEQUEST_SCRIBE_WORKSHOP_CURRENT.md` are in scope.
No f84 or f84r image, transcription, formal row, or metadata may be accessed.
ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b are alternate readings rather than replications.

## Scoring rubric

| axis | points | question |
|---|---:|---|
| executable grammar | 20 | Can a 1420 workshop scribe generate well-formed records from a small teachable procedure? |
| real-page parse coverage | 20 | Does the same machinery parse many actual Herbal/Bio lines and address Astro honestly? |
| exact-evidence fit | 15 | Does it respect exact cards, wrapper variation, stencils, closure, carry and line reflow? |
| semantic leverage | 15 | Does it make concrete, mutually constrained guesses rather than renaming unknowns? |
| cross-page coherence | 10 | Do proposed functions remain useful across pages/registers without forced universality? |
| historical mechanism | 10 | Is there a documented scribal practice that could plausibly produce the system? |
| contradictions and predictions | 10 | Does it expose awkward facts and make risky discriminating predictions? |

Maximum: 100. Scores are abductive judgments, not probabilities.

## Mandatory comparison decisions

The final selection must answer:

1. Is the basic remembered unit primarily a whole card, a compositional form,
   or a mixture?
2. Is upstream content most plausibly abbreviated natural language, technical
   notation, or a hybrid?
3. Are attached closures punctuation-like, typed field values, or unresolved?
4. Does `Y–AIIN–Y` receive a coherent reusable reading, or should it be
   withdrawn as coincidental?
5. Is repeated `qokaiin` best modeled as copied-forward content, ordinary
   phrase repetition, or unresolved?
6. Which provisional functional readings of AIIN, Y, L/O and CTHY survive?
7. Can Astro join Herbal/Bio through an explicit mechanism, or must it remain
   a separate local namespace?
8. What would make the selected theory lose against its strongest alternative?

## Selection rule

Choose one new basis rather than averaging all candidates. Useful mechanisms
from losing candidates may be incorporated only when they do not make the
winner less executable or add an unsupported universal dictionary. Preserve
the strongest adversarial alternative and its decisive prediction.

No candidate may claim a confirmed word, sound, POS, language, plaintext, or
translation. Concrete guesses are encouraged but remain explicitly
provisional.
