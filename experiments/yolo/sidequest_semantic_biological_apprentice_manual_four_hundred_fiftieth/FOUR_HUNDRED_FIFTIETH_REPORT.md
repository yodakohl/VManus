# Pass 450 — das ausführbare Biological-Lehrbuch

All 124 exact Biological cards now have one of two teaching paths:

- 117 cards are generated from 33 listed components;
- 7 cards are memorized whole.

Thirty components recur in at least two exact cards. IIN, LDDY and LS occur in
only one card each and are honestly marked as learned bound signs. DAIIIN was
the only card still called productive without a reusable component; it is now
the seventh whole card.

## Repaired collisions

The manual forces several useful corrections:

- CHEEETY is EEE+T+Y, **dies vollständig füllen**, not „voll spülen“;
- AIR is Wasser in K+AIR, OK+AIR, CHD+AIR and AIR+Y+DY;
- equal CHD+DY and OT+CHD+DY parses now receive equal Umsetzen readings;
- R+SH+E+AL and AL+R+OR stay inside the Abkühlfamilie rather than inventing
  Waschflüssigkeit or an unrelated Auffangoperation.

The resulting load for an apprentice is forty learned units: thirty recurring
components, three bound technical signs and seven whole cards. Those units
generate 117 exact card types and 269 of the 281 Biological events. The twelve
whole-card events are simply copied from the small memorized deck.
