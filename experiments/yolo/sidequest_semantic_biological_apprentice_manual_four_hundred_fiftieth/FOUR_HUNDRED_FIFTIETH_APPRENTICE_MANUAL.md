# Biological apprentice manual

## What the apprentice memorizes

Learn thirty recurring components, three single-card bound signs, and seven
whole cards. Do not learn 124 independent words.

The seven whole cards are:

- CHAR/DAR/SAR — dasselbe;
- CHEEY/SHEY — Klarauszug;
- DAIN — Tuch;
- CHES — teilen;
- LOL — warm;
- QEKY — roh;
- DAIIIN — zweite Stufe.

The three bound signs are IIN=Sollstufe, LDDY=befestigen und schließen, and
LS=abführen. They occur in one exact card each and are taught as abbreviations,
not as freely productive roots.

## How a card is read

Read the registered components in five functional slots:

1. frame/order: O, OK, OL, OT;
2. operation/path: CH, CHD, CHK, CKH, CKHE, K, L, LS, LSH, P, R, SH, SHED,
   SOLK, T;
3. argument/address: AIN, AIIN, IIN, AIR, AR, AL, OR, Y;
4. grade: E, EE, EEE;
5. endpoint: Y leaves the current post active; DY closes; LDDY fastens and
   closes.

Surface order is copied from the registered card, while interpretation uses
these slots. Q, D, S and the short CHE/CH entry strokes can change the written
allograph without adding a separate word.

## Core examples

- OK+E+Y — kurz ansetzen;
- OK+EE+DY — länger ansetzen; Schluss;
- OK+EEE+DY — vollständig ansetzen; Schluss;
- T+E+Y — dies kurz füllen;
- EEE+T+Y — dies vollständig füllen;
- R+AL+Y — dies an der Stelle abkühlen;
- SOLK+AIIN — Auffangmaß;
- L+CHD+AR — von der Quelle hinausführen;
- OT+CHD+DY — danach umsetzen; Schluss.

Every one of the 117 productive exact cards has its registered parse in the
generator table. A scribe does not freely invent every mathematically possible
combination; the workshop teaches the attested card list plus this reading
grammar.
