# GDT834 method

The paired control removes only individual symbol-role information from the
successful mixed decoder's supplied architecture. This is a finite role
assignment problem, not unrestricted segmentation. Word boundaries, atomic
cipher symbols, paragraph boundaries, positional restrictions and nominal
26L/4S/8W capacities remain known. Fresh Epistolae I–VI supply discovery and
VII–XIII supply held text; the original normalized spelling remains gold.

## Prior results and new falsifier

GDT833 recovered its original held control completely with supplied roles.
GDT603 already recovered internal boundaries in a different public-capacity
Naibbe control; GDT604 did not produce a manuscript reading. GDT606 and GDT612
produced unstable or objectively preferred pseudokeys. GDT607 additionally
warned that frequency-stable W buckets are not independently observed words.
The unchanged GDT616 and CDA001 closures do not select a target run here.

A diagnostic on already published GDT833 discovery finds 219 complete role
assignments but only 67 assignments distinguishable on observed symbols.
Position alone fixes its 24 observed literals. That retrospective audit does
not establish fresh recovery. The new falsifier is paired original-spelling
held recovery with and without per-symbol role information, on a fresh work.
No manuscript data, new page admission or public decipherment search is used.

## Source and information boundaries

The independent source program fixes the natural whole-letter collection
split before support counts, preserves maximal citation runs, and retains all
representable words. Its pre-key source gates are in PREREGISTRATION.md.
Reference, candidate pools and encoder decks are unchanged from the native
GDT833 resources. Reference/control exact 20-word overlap is audited. No
orthography collapse, silent exclusion or alternate partition search occurs.

Three source-independent keys share this one content split. Each typed key is
aliased by a separate shuffle across all 38 X00–X37 identifiers, including
unobserved slots. Identifier prefixes/order do not encode roles. BLIND's C++
process receives only the reference model and an opaque discovery projection;
TYPED receives the corresponding typed projection. Neither receives gold,
held text, true values, alias maps or active-role counts. The orchestrator
checks aggregate source-gate status before launching these separate processes.
Source truth remains unopened by the decoder operator until all fits lock.

## Objective and search

The proper GDT832 OFF language objective is unchanged: paragraph-continuous
absolute-discount word bigrams, smoothed reference word probabilities, and the
same order-four within-word character backoff. There is no family factor.
The reference model and incremental likelihood calculation are inherited;
`build_engine.py` makes the explicit new role-search implementation reviewable.

Each of 38 anonymous atoms has one global (role, output) package. Exactly 26
packages are distinct literal letters; four are distinct entries of the
12-suffix pool; eight are distinct entries of the 128-word pool. Injection is
within roles. Roles and output lengths are not established manuscript facts.
All letters can occur anywhere. A suffix can occur only finally following at
least three literal atoms; a wholeword only alone. These domains are computed
from discovery occurrences alone. Unused atoms have all three role options.
A pre-fit exact enumeration requires at least two distinct observable role
partitions; permutations solely on unused slots do not meet that gate.

A random feasible bipartite matching initializes the nominal role slots.
Assigned literals are initialized by source/reference frequency ranks;
suffixes use seeded candidate samples; W values use reference-frequency ranks.
Other starts perturb literal and W mappings. Each fit uses 60,000 proposals,
temperature 80 to .05 nats, and four greedy sweeps. Twenty percent of proposals
attempt a complete package swap; the remainder use the inherited 72/14/14
literal/suffix/wholeword move mix. Domain-invalid or injection-invalid moves
are rejected. This same engine and budget run in both arms; TYPED's fixed
roles reject cross-role swaps. Equal seeds need not create equal initial keys.
Greedy sweeps cover every pair swap and S/W replacement.

There are eight restarts per arm for three keys: 48 fits. The largest discovery
objective selects each of six keys, with lower start resolving ties. All 48
restarts and six selections are hash-locked before held text or truth is
opened. No restarts, thresholds or reference adjustment follow recovery scores.

## Evaluation and decision

Both arms must reach at least 95% exact held words, 99% character similarity,
90% exact novel composed form occurrences and 90% exact unambiguous novel
composed lemma occurrences, in each of the three keys. Character similarity is
one minus total wordwise Levenshtein distance divided by total maximum paired
word lengths; known spaces are not scored. Novelty is against discovery gold.

BLIND must additionally recover every identifiable observed role and its
output exactly. Same-emission role alternatives under legal capacities,
position rules and candidate injection are reported separately. Inactive
slots are never key-accuracy evidence. Exact full paragraphs and the true-key
objective are secondary diagnostics, evaluated only after lock.

Source or ambiguity gate failures stop before fitting. If any typed recovery
floor fails, the result is BASELINE_RECOVERY_FAIL. Otherwise any BLIND floor
or identifiable role/output failure is ROLE_BLIND_RECOVERY_FAIL; all passing
is ROLE_BLIND_RECOVERY_PASS. Independent validation reconstructs source,
legalities, fit selection, likelihoods and every held prediction. A software
validation pass and a scientific recovery pass remain different statements.
