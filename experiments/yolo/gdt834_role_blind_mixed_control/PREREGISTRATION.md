# GDT834 prospective scope and gates

This continuation asks whether the successful GDT833 mixed-code decoder can
recover a fresh control when individual symbol roles are hidden. Exact word
and paragraph boundaries and nominal capacities 26 literal, four suffix and
eight whole-word cards remain supplied. Active role counts are not fitter
inputs; unused slots cannot contribute recovery evidence. No Voynich fit is
selected. GDT603 already recovered hidden boundaries in a different known
architecture; GDT604 then produced pseudotext on the manuscript. GDT606 and
GDT612 likewise do not establish a true mixed manuscript code.

Before new source support counts, the source auditor selected the natural
Epistolae I–VI discovery versus VII–XIII held split. All maximal citation runs
and representable words stay in their original order. The native Monarchia
reference and mixed encoder decks are inherited unchanged. Source-only gates
are assessed before keys and fitting: at least 20 citation runs in each split;
every active suffix/wholeword rule has eight discovery occurrences; every
held-active literal has one discovery occurrence; at least 100 held novel
composed form occurrences and 30 unambiguous novel composed lemma occurrences;
all planted wholeword values are in the fixed reference candidate pool.

Any failed source gate means SOURCE_CAPACITY_STOP, with no changed split,
encoding deck, threshold, or discarded rare words. No key generation, fit,
held recovery score or claimed role-blind decoder result follows that stop.
Source inspection itself is developmental, not a blinded recovery test.

A separate discovery-only structural audit tests whether role hiding is
nontrivial. At least two legal global role partitions must differ on an
observed symbol; permutations confined to unused slots do not count. Literal
cards may occur anywhere, suffix cards only last after at least three literal
cards, and wholeword cards only as singleton words. Opaque identifiers must
contain no role prefixes, role-sorted ranges, or source value information.

If source and ambiguity gates pass, complete fitter/evaluator code, exact fit
budgets, decision floors and commitments must be publicly registered before
any fresh control fit. This document alone does not authorize unregistered
fits. A recovery pass would concern only this supplied architecture and its
retained boundaries, not the Voynich manuscript or an inferred language.

The complete fit protocol is now specified in METHOD.md and SPEC.json:
three keys, both TYPED and BLIND, eight starts, 60,000 annealing proposals and
four greedy sweeps. Each arm must pass 95% held words, 99% characters, and
90% each novel composed forms and unambiguous novel composed lemmas. BLIND
also requires every identifiable observed role plus output to be exact.
Same-emission ambiguities and unused slots are excluded from that role gate.
All 48 fits and six discovery-selected keys lock before any truth evaluation.
Public registration hashes the implementation and evaluator before fits.
