# GDT851 — primitive whole-group tandem inventory

Root screened the route and GDT574/820 methods before selecting this census.
Those predecessors count adjacent single groups or inherited action slots,
not the complete primitive two-/three-group block census. Known motivator:
GDT651 f83r.27 contains shckhedy shckhedy. No semantic gloss is imported.

Fixed179selectors, all source kinds, ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b separately. Every physical
source line is identified by edition+locus. For each period p in1,2,3,
enumerate every start with2p stored source groups remaining in that line.
A window is eligible only if all source indices are consecutive and all
2p-1 intervening separators are DEFINITE_SPACE on both adjoining groups.
No line crossing, uncertain spaces, missing-index bridge or annotation
removal. Literal complete raw groups, including annotations/entities, are
the comparison units; these are not established words or phonemes.

A finite sequence w of length2p has period q when for every i with
q<=i<2p, w[i]=w[i-q]. A recorded tandem has period p and NO period q with
1<=q<p. Thus AAAA is not counted as primitive period2, and ABCABC is
period3 only when no smaller period exists under this finite-word definition.
Do not require period q to divide the sequence length. Retain overlapping
spans independently; do not combine into maximal runs or count a long run as
one occurrence. An exact tandem is a written pattern, not proof of copying.

Preserve all selected source lines in compact per-reading JSON with line
metadata and lossless raw group columns. Record every candidate window by
line/start/period plus eligibility, repetition and primitive flags; separate
candidate, eligible, nonprimitive tandem and primitive tandem denominators.
Publish all primitive hits, source IDs, counts and physical folios by reader
and period. Every p>1 hit has its complete original line in a dedicated
artifact. Full source files also permit independent denominator validation.

Decision: if p>1 patterns occur, retain concrete passage anchors. If absent,
stop this fixed census without longer periods, looser matches or broader
scope. Neither outcome identifies a copying mechanism, language, meaning,
null-model surprise or codebook. Readings are alternative observations of
one manuscript; folios and lines are not replaced by reader counts.

Smallest adequate test is one guarded extraction and exhaustive bounded
comparison, independent direct-loop validator, compact report and publication.
Budget preparation5min; end-to-end including root publication10min. Stop
expansion at the checkpoint. Root publishes preregistration and sends GO
before any new source query. f84 and f84r remain explicitly sealed.
