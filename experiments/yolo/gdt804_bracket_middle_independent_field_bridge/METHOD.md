## Preferred-side amount neighbours

rightward and an internal amount expression more often attaches leftward.

candidate neighbours only. The original `content_attachment_sides` and

explicitly post-hoc candidate family. Every raw ZL3b adjacency receives two

- token stability: both surface tokens pass the published occurrence-rank gate;

marked before counting external repeats.

outcome-blind K12 neighbours in both raw ZL3b and reader-stable views. A
control equals or exceeds `cheol` only from observed complete-surface counts and
rates; no semantic field enters this match.
