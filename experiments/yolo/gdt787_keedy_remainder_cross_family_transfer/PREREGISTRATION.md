# GDT787 exploratory registration

This is a throughput-oriented family tournament, not a blind decipherment
test.  It admits the complete observed `*keedy` family and asks whether its
very strong written lattice also predicts meaning.

The unit of evidence is the left-family type, not the token.  The primary nine
rows must be predicted from `Xkey`, `Xkeey` and `Xkedy` while all `*keedy`
meanings are masked.  Standalone X and clean learned wholes are the nulls.
HOT, END and CLOSE are audited separately; one successful field may not pull
the other two along.  Exact separated spans and Stolfi are boundary
sensitivities, not extra semantic votes.

Every observed complete form receives a nonempty exploratory display so that
failure of a portable remainder does not collapse the practical dictionary to
generic filler.  Reader-unstable forms remain visibly marked and are not
promoted into the active exact-whole renderer.

Those displays deliberately share one C0 HOT+END family prior.  They are not
counted as independent confirmation of that prior, and the experiment grants
zero new renderer licences even for reader-exact surfaces.

Ceiling: observed wholes, formal family, form-specific roles and replaceable
German displays only.  No free `keedy`, `k`, `ee`, `d` or `y` value is exported;
no plaintext, named substance, language, sound, glyph identity, unseen form or
sealed page can be claimed.
