# GDT1088 — complete repeated-head visual audit

**Decision: no plant name established; two organ overlaps and one unresolved pair.** The fixed GDT1070 census has four repeated exact ZL3b Herbal-A first heads on nine folios, giving six same-head pairs. All six were compared against official Yale images. None supplies two independent diagnostic traits for a same-taxon assignment. The visual inventory has a material observation disagreement for the lower structures of f2v and f29v, so the `kooiin` organ decision is **undecidable in the reconciled result**; the frozen first-blind score remains recorded as NO. This report makes no significance claim or confirmed translation.

| Exact first head | Complete same-head pair | Frozen first-blind same taxon / specific organ | Reconciled reading and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kooiin` | f2v–f29v | NO / NO | **Taxon NO; organ UNDECIDABLE.** Root's prior-exposed native review sees a long horizontal segmented brown basal structure on both. Neither independent page inventory described that feature as a shared rhizome, and anatomy/attachment is disputed. Distinct leaf and upper-organ architectures remain. `kooiin≈rootstock` is at most an observer-dependent C0 lead, not a positive fixed-test result. |
| `o` | f42r–f56r | NO / NO | **Taxon NO; organ NO.** Broad/fan-like blade architecture versus curved blue radial structure; independent inventories disagree on detailed f42r organ partition. A one-character first head is especially weak evidence for a separate lexical word. |

The primary image-only inventory (`artifacts/BLIND_VISUAL.tsv`) was frozen before the text-head join; the 36 all-pairs ratings in `artifacts/BLIND_PAIR_SIMILARITY.tsv` were based on it. A separate fresh nine-image inventory (`artifacts/BLIND_VISUAL_B.tsv`) and root's prior-exposed native review (`artifacts/ROOT_NATIVE_REVIEW.tsv`) were retained without changing the primary rows. The two independent inventories both omitted or did not identify the conspicuous lower horizontal f2v/f29v structures that root sees in the original-resolution images. In a **prompted, post-freeze diagnostic** (`artifacts/POST_FREEZE_DIAGNOSTIC.md`), the second image reader then saw both structures and an apparent stem connection, but declined to identify their anatomy. The inventories also differ on other organ partitions, notably f42r. The primary six-pair table is therefore a faithful, executable **record of the frozen observations**, not an error-free ground truth. The validator's PASS covers roster, sources and join integrity only. The result is reported with the disagreement rather than retroactively rescored as a successful `kooiin` organ prediction.

The preregistered route to an organ-class hypothesis required consistent discriminating architecture across the relevant repeated head. `pchor` has only one of three pairs sharing a basal form. `tshor` has one pair, already known, and GDT1069/W84 record a weak f53v ZL boundary, RF fusion `tshorshey`, and a medial f95v1 occurrence. In the already frozen GDT1087 **23-image** inventory, 11 other descriptions explicitly use *rootstock*, *rhizome* or *tuber* (f3v, f4v, f10v, f14r, f15r, f19v, f24v, f28r, f49r, f50v, f55r); three describe radiate/rayed heads (f14r, f15r, f53v). These are broad word-count backgrounds, not anatomical diagnoses or a selected control sample; f2v separately describes a beaded horizontal underground structure without those three words. Neither overlap uniquely assigns a word meaning. We did not select matching images after inspection, apply a multiple-search control, open reserves or claim a plant name. Prior project exposure to f2v/f29v and f15r/f53v prevents independent semantic confirmation. f84/f84r remain sealed.

The direct manuscript gain is a complete census of this specific image-to-head route: it makes a species-name reading implausible for the repeated heads as a group and keeps two narrow visual C0 leads visible, while locating the exact observation failure that blocks `kooiin`. A future test would need a **predeclared, independently identified organ and owner rule**, with a comparison set of other first heads; another unblinded view of these same two drawings cannot confirm the meaning.
