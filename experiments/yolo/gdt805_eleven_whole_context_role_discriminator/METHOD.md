# GDT805 method — discovery-subtracted whole-context discriminator

## Question

After removing the twelve exact GDT803 discovery occurrences, do the complete
left and right neighbours of the eleven bracket-middle wholes discriminate
among material/preparation, quality/state, process, general-carrier and opaque
record-entry rivals?

## Scope and guarded sources

The target set is exactly `chal, chedal, cheol, okail, okal, ol, otal,
qokeol, qokol, qotal, sail`. GDT800 supplies 1,098 paired-terminal occurrences;
the twelve GDT803 occurrence IDs are subtracted, leaving 1,086 external events
on 143 physical folios. No new page, image or transcription is opened.

Mixed raw transcription sources are queried only through `./vmanus-exp
query-tsv`, with the inherited 179-selector allow-list, explicit output columns
and `f84|f84r` rejection before materialization. Token stability and contiguous
sequence stability are checked separately across ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b, which are
alternate readings of one manuscript rather than independent witnesses.

## Exact context atlas

GDT802 provides the masked L1/R1 identities and GDT734 supplies full cached
ZL3b lines for parity. Every target occurrence is reconstructed at its exact
line ordinal. Primary profiles use L1 and R1; L2/R2 chains, two-sided frames and
five-token windows are retained as capacity diagnostics. The builder verifies
916 all-reader-stable target tokens and fixed sequence totals of 677 L1 pairs,
663 R1 pairs, 475 L2 chains, 461 R2 chains, 495 two-sided frames and 228 full
five-token windows.

The same GDT804 K12 control pool is used for every target. A projected-axis
profile lead requires at least three raw and three pair-stable physical folios,
rank at most 3/13 in both views, and a rate above the corresponding control
median in both views. Ties count against the target. A stricter diagnostic
marks a lead only when it exceeds every one of the twelve controls in both
views.

## Three evidence levels for neighbour axes

GDT739 contributes 230 locally selected neighbour contacts over 131 unique
complete surfaces. The source deck is audited before use:

- only radius-one or radius-two source assignments are active;
- GDT754-quarantined source-composed wholes are removed;
- GDT738 manual HOLD readings and all eleven target surfaces are masked;
- an axis at the identical page, locus, ordinal and surface is an exact active
  GDT739 cell;
- reuse of that axis at another occurrence of the same surface is a new GDT805
  exploratory surface projection.

This leaves 75 projection-eligible surfaces. The German source wording is not
imported, although the broad axes were derived from earlier German working
prose. Therefore every projection and every profile lead has semantic credit
zero and renderer licence zero. Of 184 mapped flanks in the external atlas,
only six are exact GDT739 source cells; 178 are explicitly marked projections.

## Candidate and frame diagnostics

Twenty predeclared whole-form rival cards specify expected left/right axes.
Full leads and near-only signals are disjoint. The diagnostic score is
`5 × dominant + 2 × non-dominant full + near-only`, divided by the number of
declared expected axes for display ranking. Field proximity, positional amount
candidates and inherited priors receive no score. Correlated axes from one
contact may still count separately, so this is a width-scaled,
axis-correlated diagnostic—not a probability or a fully normalized semantic
test.

Repeated exact L1–target–R1 frames must occur on at least two physical folios.
Boundary-anchored one-sided frames are counted separately and `NONE` is never a
token. Frames can order concrete rivals but cannot install a word meaning from
a single pattern.

## Decision and claim ceiling

Projected axes may expose context profiles and passage cards, but cannot select
a new target role. Only the already published `okal` opaque/system-entry prior
and `ol` general content/preparation-carrier prior may be retained. Specific
substances such as oil, water, wine, salt, root or leaf require a separate
direct bridge.

Every mapped relation is submitted to the executable GDT388 intake. Because
the text relations were formally accessed before sealing, the packet is
ineligible and not score-ready despite its descriptive capacity.

GDT805 may enumerate the external context atlas, audited projections, K12
profiles, repeated whole frames and concrete rival displays. It establishes no
lexeme, plaintext, component value, language, sound, glyph value, ingredient,
plant, disease, cure, unit or translation.
