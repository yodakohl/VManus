Date: 2026-09-04. This is an exploratory continuation of GDT804. No claim of
blinding or independent semantic confirmation is made.

## Fixed question

After removing the twelve exact GDT803 discovery occurrences, do complete
left/right context profiles distinguish the eleven bracket-middle whole forms
as material/preparation, quality/state, process, general carrier or opaque
record entries?

- Targets are exactly `chal, chedal, cheol, okail, okal, ol, otal, qokeol,
  qokol, qotal, sail`.

  allow-list is used, with `f84` and `f84r` rejected before materialization.

  sensitivity only.
- Every target uses the same code and GDT804 K12 control construction.

## Meaning boundary

GDT739's `eligible_local_anchor=1` rows are audited as source candidates. Only
distance one or two is active; distance three to five remains discovery-only.
GDT754 source-composition forms, GDT738 manual HOLD forms and the eleven targets
are masked. At the same enumerated source cell, a surviving broad axis may be
shown as local evidence. Assigning that tag to another occurrence of the same
surface is explicitly a new GDT805 surface projection with zero semantic and
renderer credit. The German working string is not copied, but the broad tags
were derived from earlier German working prose and are not independent
semantics. Unknown neighbours remain exact identities. Target-to-target
contacts receive no semantic credit. EVA substrings and edit distance are
excluded.

The concrete target rivals in `src/TARGET_CANDIDATE_MODELS.tsv` are questions
to discriminate, not answers. Oil, water, wine, salt, root, leaf, disease and
other literal identities receive no automatic slot.

## Primary decision rule

For each target, side and broad neighbour role, compare the target rate with
the same rate for its twelve fixed GDT804 controls. A role lead requires:

1. at least three physical folios;
2. target rank at most 3 of 13 in both raw and pair-stable views;
3. the target rate above the control median in both views.

GDT804 field proximity, an open positional amount candidate and an inherited
role prior are recorded separately; none is an independent semantic bridge.
Only a direct clean GDT760 CONTENT_PREP contact could serve as direct slot
evidence.

`okail` and any other target with fewer than three external folios must end as
`NO_EXTERNAL_CAPACITY`. Repeated exact two-sided frames are enumerated but are
not the primary discriminator because their capacity is expected to be sparse.

For every target with capacity, passage cards are selected mechanically by
mapped-role density, pair stability and new-folio coverage. They must show the
full ZL3b line, a safe role skeleton, the concrete candidate and its strongest
rival. A readable passage does not become plaintext.

Any new formal relation packet is submitted to the executable GDT388 intake
and cannot be called score-ready unless all of its gates pass.

## Pre-build process correction

During a read-only schema audit a subagent used `head` directly on the two
mixed raw TSVs. Only headers and early `f1r` rows were displayed; no `f84*`
row or value was displayed or retained. All counts and every builder access
must instead use `./vmanus-exp query-tsv` with the explicit allow-list and
output columns. The audit also corrected the expected repeated-frame capacity:
seven real two-sided multi-folio frames plus six boundary-anchored one-sided
frames, never thirteen real bidirectional frames.

## Post-build adversarial correction

The first exploratory builder pass exposed three coupled biases: broader
material models had more tested axes, a full lead was counted again as a near
lead, and field proximity was scored as direct content evidence. The retained
builder therefore makes full and near evidence disjoint, reports a
width-scaled, axis-correlated rival diagnostic without a breadth or field bonus,
treats ties against the target, and selects no new target role from the surface
projection. It does not claim full normalization because correlated axis tags
from one contact can still count separately.
Only the earlier `okal` opaque-entry and `ol` general-carrier priors may be
retained. Concrete candidate words remain questions for the next pass.
