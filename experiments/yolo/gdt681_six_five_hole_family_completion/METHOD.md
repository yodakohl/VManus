# GDT681 method

## Question

Can the 29 genuinely new residual surfaces in V54's six five-hole lines receive
concrete component-based or learned-whole meanings which preserve one practical
core at all 104 exact admitted occurrences, while published `chepy` is reused
unchanged, all six lines close and every reader repair stays explicit?

## Inputs

- GDT671's f84-free 4,128-line V48 panel and literal token coverage;
- the published GDT673, GDT674, GDT675, GDT677, GDT678, GDT679 and GDT680
  position overlays;
- GDT680's 51-line V54 reader and frozen result counts;
- twenty-nine manual card specifications, one inherited-card reuse, six source
  readings, forty-four reader decisions, seven historical records and the
  three-reader synthesis;
- the mixed transcription table, accessed only through `vmanus-exp query-tsv`
  with 77 explicit locus allow-values and `--forbid-prefix f84`.

## Method

1. Rebuild the current global overlay. The seven published occurrence passes
   contribute 503 disjoint V48 positions, leaving 7,677 globally open before
   GDT681.
2. Distinguish reader holes from global unknowns. V54 still prints `chepy` as a
   hole on f86v5.4 because external occurrence audits do not silently rewrite
   the 51-line deck; globally its six positions already carry the GDT680 card.
3. Locate every whitespace-delimited occurrence of the 29 new surfaces in the
   admitted panel. This yields 104 positions on 77 lines and 49 pages.
4. Materialize only those 77 loci from the mixed reader source. The guard
   selects 77 rows, rejects 98 f84-prefixed rows before materialization and
   skips 5,211 other rows.
5. Align ZL3b to IT2a and RF1b with exact tokens preferred over low-cost joins
   and splits. Record all 44 positions not exact in both readers. Bilateral
   repairs include `ytedy→ytchedy`, `ls→lr`, `cheop+ol→cheopol` and a local
   CKH-family replacement for `sheeey`.
6. Apply one replaceable card core to every exact new ZL3b occurrence. A local
   alternate-reader rendering may specialize the same core but may not become
   an unrecorded manuscript-wide rewrite.
7. Close five reader positions in each source line, including the inherited
   `chepy` application, and recalculate the 51-line deck and global overlay.
8. Generate eight forward family predictions and seven sourced historical
   architecture records. They constrain information type only, not language or
   sign values.
9. Independently rebuild eleven result files in a temporary directory and
   compare every byte; check counts, variants, token alignment, specificity,
   hashes, privacy markers and both seals.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The pass requires 29 unique new cards, all 104 exact positions represented,
one unchanged GDT680 reuse, no unrecorded non-both-reader position, all six
source lines closed, zero hard generic renderer terms, the predicted V55 and
global counts, and a byte-exact rebuild.

Cards remain exploratory defaults rather than confirmed plaintext. A concrete
candidate may stand with a named rival until a context makes it impossible or
a better reading explains the same occurrence circuit. GDT681 does not identify
the language, phonetics, historical codebook, exact plant or resin, disease,
patient, cure or carrier liquid.
