# GDT681 three-reader synthesis

Three independent readers used the same V54 frontier without editing the
experiment. Their recurring five-line backgrounds remained deliberately
different:

1. The workshop master read terse materia-medica notation as ordered
   operations, quantities and resulting substances, while refusing to export
   a free one-sign meaning from a single compound.
2. The travelling practitioner asked whether each line could actually guide a
   preparation: it had to name usable materials, quantities, states or acts,
   and it could retain an unproved concrete candidate until a better one won.
3. The circa-1420 scribe/glossator prioritized minim, abbreviation and boundary
   behaviour across ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b, then compared only the register type
   with near-period medical codices.

The workshop pass covered f104v.2 and f26r.2. Its strongest productive
extensions were `ychedaiin`, `qoteed`, `qokamdy`, `ytedy` and `checthedy`.
It recognized `oram=or+am` as a measured portion, `otarar=otar+ar` as a first
subfraction of the first cold preparation fraction, and `ls=l+s` as a learned
wood-drug charge. At f26r.2 both alternate readers instead write published
`lr`, so the local source rendering is Wurzelholz.

The practical pass covered f105r.31 and f115r.1. It found that `shx` is not a
damaged short token: all three readers preserve it, and the admitted `xol`,
`xor`, `xar`, `xoy`, `xoiin`, `shxam` and `chxar` family behaves like one
substance under material, portion, fraction, preparation and treatment fields.
The working identity is deliberately concrete but replaceable: soaked gum
resin. It also promoted `aiijy` from a nominal quantity to the only new action
on f105r.31, “fill into three bundles and close”, by extending published
`ajy`. On f115r.1, both alternate readers join `cheop` to following `ol`; the
local output therefore says middle-dried powder material rather than pretending
that the ZL3b boundary is secure.

The historical-boundary pass covered f114r.24 and f86v5.4, then audited all 29
new families plus reused `chepy`. Its decisive oppositions were:

- `aiir` retains the published third-fraction value at all twenty contexts;
- `chopo+sair` is joined by RF1b and naturally reads as seed fraction II of a
  powder preparation made from a dry preparation;
- `ypchesy` is seed powder in ZL3b and RF1b, but IT2a `ypchery` supplies a live
  root-powder rival;
- `sail/sain` changes an internal stroke while preserving at least the seed
  category;
- `chepy` is reused unchanged from GDT680 at all six published occurrences;
- at f115v.34 both alternate readers replace `sheeey` with a CKH-family form,
  so that locus renders locally as a moist medicinal composite.

## Adjudicated working choices

- Twenty-nine new cards cover 104 exact ZL3b positions. No source-only meaning
  is silently promoted; every occurrence and every non-both-exact reader case
  is present in the executable artifacts.
- `shx=eingeweichtes Gummiharz` is the most exploratory material identity. The
  x-family substance role is stronger than the resin identification.
- `kodeey` defaults to a completely heated, closed dose; RF1b `todeey` keeps
  the opposite cold reading visible.
- `fdar` retains the ZL3b measure marker, but flower plus first fraction is more
  secure than the action because neither alternate reader preserves the whole.
- `ytedy` is primarily an anaphoric cold-middle finish command and can render
  resultatively where syntax demands it. At f104v.14 bilateral `ytchedy` adds
  drying; at f26r.8 IT2a `otedy` replaces the reference by a preparation frame.
- `ls` keeps the general wood-charge card, with local `lr`, `lsaiin` and
  `olals/als` repairs rather than a manuscript-wide rewrite.

The combined system is a practical mixture of learned substance or measure
wholes and productive treatment, quantity and reference components. It makes
the six source lines concrete; it does not establish plaintext, language,
phonetics, a historical codebook identity, a specific resin or plant, disease,
patient, cure or carrier liquid.
