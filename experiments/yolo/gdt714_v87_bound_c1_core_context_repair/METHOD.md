# GDT714 method — V87 bound-C1 core/context repair

## Question

Can the eighteen `BOUND_C1_COMPOUNDS` still held by V86 receive shorter,
composition-consistent working cores and concrete local German realizations,
and can the predeclared f7r.2 `keo r` join be made executable without changing
any global lexeme or admitted position?

## Inputs

- GDT713 V86: 324 active readings, 479 context positions, complete dictionary,
  118-row cumulative audit and inherited one-shot span table.
- GDT711: thirteen declared family rules and their bonuses, caps and
  counterevidence.
- Exact primary TSV rows only: GDT647, GDT654, GDT661, GDT663, GDT664,
  GDT665, GDT668, GDT673, GDT678, GDT679, GDT681 and GDT690.

The target is exactly the eighteen V86 rows whose still-held queue is
`BOUND_C1_COMPOUNDS`. They occupy eighteen positions on twelve already
admitted pages. No image, raw transcription, new page, `f84` or `f84r` is
read.

## Method

1. Preserve every source reading, position, W3 card, scope and export flag.
2. For each target, separate a compact lexical core from its local practical
   realization. Keep only fields supported by the admitted exact family:
   state, stage, preparation frame, measured/value head, portion or carrier.
3. Remove unsupported object identities and unit claims (`Droge`, `Arznei`,
   `Dosis`, `Charge`) from both target channels. Bound S, O, D, OL and stage
   fields never become freely exportable German words.
4. Resolve every target through `V87_18_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_BINDINGS.tsv` against
   an exact older TSV row. Source decomposition, counts and reader class must
   match that row. Derive each score delta from the distinct credited family
   IDs and GDT711's bound `family_bonus`; never trust the authored numeric
   delta by itself. A prose/core-context/boundary cleanup alone earns no
   points: `cholkain`, `kc`, `keo`, `oteor`, `oty` and the exploratory
   `dshees = abgemessene feuchte Form, Endstufe` do not gain score. `os`
   receives only F_O's three points and remains W0.
5. Keep lexical and occurrence scope separate. GDT678's f7r.2 decision is
   installed only at P288/P289: LEFT `keo`, RIGHT `r`, render once as `heiße
   Portion`, export zero. `keo` retains its standalone reading as heiße
   Zubereitung auf Mittelstufe and
   `r` retains its global Wurzel reading.
6. Materialize two consumer directives: P288 consumes its source context and
   emits the span once; P289 consumes its source context and emits nothing.
   Reconstruct the complete nine-source f7r.2 locus as eight output units.
7. Rebuild the complete dictionary and cumulative queue, then independently
   replay and compare every inherited field, score, scope, position, span and
   historical status.

## Score interpretation

The 0–100 score is a non-probabilistic internal evidence index. W0–W3 are
working-theory levels, not translation probabilities. Historical status stays
`H0_NONE`; GDT714 contributes zero relation or historical word credit.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all eighteen old cores match V86 exactly, all eighteen new cores
and contexts match their explicit specifications, the active and complete
universes remain 324/479 and 1,582 surfaces/1,586 readings, all nineteen W3
readings remain unchanged, the held queue falls from 109 to 91, and the new
span is the only occurrence override. The validator must also prove from the
consumer output that f7r.2 emits `heiße Portion` exactly once and emits neither
the standalone `keo` context nor `Wurzel` there.

Claim ceiling: exploratory working dictionary only. This does not identify
plaintext, language, historical lexicon, free morphemes, drug species,
measurement units or a manuscript-wide translation.
