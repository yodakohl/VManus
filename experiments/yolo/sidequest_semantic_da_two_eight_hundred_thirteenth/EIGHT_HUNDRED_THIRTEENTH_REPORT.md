# Sidequest Pass 813: DA is bound TWO before STAGE

The only `DA` card is `daiiin = DA+IIN` late in the B5 sequence. The old prose
expanded it to “for the second pass up to the stage,” which said more than the
card needed.

The compact composition is:

> `DA = ZWEI` (bound)
> `IIN = STUFE`
> `daiiin = ZWEI · STUFE = zweite Stufe`

The full instruction now ends:

> … nach Sollmaß weiterarbeiten, bis zur zweiten Stufe führen und den Posten
> umsetzen.

ERNEUT overlaps the existing repetition/flow machinery; NÄCHST overlaps `OT`;
DOPPELT produces an awkward stage reading. Atomic ZWEI is the simplest value.

`DA` is not promoted to a free core. Naively composing it with AIN or AIIN would
produce `dain` and `daiin`, surfaces already used by the ordinary PORTION and
SOLLMASS cards. `DA` is therefore a bound numeral licensed only before IIN on
the current pages.

The inventory remains core33, now with two bound values and only one local
singleton. Next, resolve `LD` inside `qokylddy`: does it mean BINDEN,
BEFESTIGEN, VERSCHLIESSEN, or a purely graphic double-close instruction?
