# Sidequest pass 667: SH is the hold verb

The shortest useful reading of **SH** is **HALTEN**. Twenty exact cards carry
that contribution in 25 occurrences and 19 recipes. The root is mobile: it can
start a statement, sit medially, finish one, or occupy a complete short cell.

This removes several old overreadings. `cheey/shey` need not mean “clear
extract”, “visible result”, or a particular liquid. In the prose deck its
portable construction is simply **SH+EE+Y = den laufenden Posten länger
halten**. A clear extract can still be the local object in the f11r workshop
story, but clarity is not stored in SH.

## Duration and endpoint grid

Four central cells occur:

- SH+E+Y: hold briefly, keep the item active;
- SH+E+DY: hold briefly and close;
- SH+EE+Y: hold longer, keep the item active;
- SH+EE+DY: hold longer and close.

The zero-grade and full-grade active/closed cells remain useful predictions.
Bare `sh` occurs once as the compact command HALTEN. All eight observed closed
SH events are statement-final or occupy the whole statement.

## Valency

SH accepts the same workshop slots already seen around the other roots:

- `AIIN`: hold to the prescribed measure;
- `AL`: hold at the target;
- `OL`: continue holding;
- `CKH`: hold at/through a passage;
- `CTH`: hold until ready;
- `CHD`: hold and transfer;
- `K`: add while holding;
- `T`, `O`, and `R`: entry, work-cycle, and cooling modifiers.

This makes SH a genuine connecting verb rather than a special herbal noun.
Adding it to the current root dictionary raises coverage from 80 to 93 exact
cards and from 196 to 212 prose events.
