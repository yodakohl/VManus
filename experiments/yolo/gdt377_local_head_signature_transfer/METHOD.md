# GDT377 — frozen local-head signature transfer

GDT377 transfers the sole GDT376 comparator endpoint that passed every frozen
gate. It does not search for another detector.

The final CoReMA structure-only logistic model is fitted to all 27,349
observable comparator elements and serialized before Voynich scoring. The
Voynich mapping is fixed: a page is the comparator collection boundary, a
GDT327 record is a comparator record, each atomic joint-tuple event is an
element, and exact joint-tuple equality supplies only opaque recurrence.
PAGE_HOST, glyph strings, coordinates, semantics, and target-derived roles are
not used.

Every event receives nuisance and structural probabilities. Exact tuple
candidates require at least 12 events, three physical folios, mean structural
probability >= .5, at least 75% of folios with mean >= .5, and positive mean
structure-minus-nuisance. A 4,096-world null shuffles tuple IDs within section,
register, hand, record-length, and position strata.

Passing rows are named only `CMP_LOCAL_HEAD_SIGNATURE`. This is not a
predicate, verb, action, POS, valency, meaning, or translation assignment. The
high-valency and parented-dependent comparator endpoints failed and cannot be
inferred. All f84 rows are forbidden; the bound GDT327 input contains none.
