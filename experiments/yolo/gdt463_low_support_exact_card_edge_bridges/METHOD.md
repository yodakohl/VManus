# GDT463 method

## Question

Can GDT462's four perfect but low-support residual edges be admitted through
larger exact-card paradigms rather than through their one to three extensions
alone?

## Inputs

- GDT407's invariant running surface/recipe edition;
- GDT413's unchanged component values;
- GDT459's old minimal segmentations and factor-gate results;
- GDT462's 107-label hybrid dictionary.

## Method

For each of `-oin`, `kor-`, `yky-`, and `-cfhy`:

1. require every observed edge extension to retain the complete base recipe;
2. find every running surface containing that recipe as one contiguous atom
   sequence and require at least four physical pages;
3. keep a target hybrid unless all remaining pieces are independently exact
   running cards;
4. for a complete target, require GDT459's old minimal recipe to agree and its
   factor reader not to stop;
5. add no new atom value or letter-level split.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

All four edges pass. `korainy` and `ykyd` become complete formulas because they
split into `korain|y` and `yky|d`, respectively, and their old full recipes
already passed GDT459. `opoiiinoin` and `ykocfhy` retain learned left cores and
gain only exact suffix cards.

The result is a working codebook decomposition, not a plaintext, object-name,
language, or surface-generation claim.
