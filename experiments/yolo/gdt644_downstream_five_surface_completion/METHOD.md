# GDT644 method

## Question

Do the five exact surfaces newly exposed by GDT643 fit already populated
component families well enough to receive short practical meanings, close all
five source lines and remain usable at every occurrence?

## Inputs and scope

- The V20 dictionary, exact glossary, 4,128-line coverage edition, complete
  passages and one-hole frontier from GDT643.
- The quality, carrier, preparation, residual-head and exact completion
  families from GDT624, GDT627–643.
- The unchanged guarded 179-page panel. `f1r` is excluded; `f84` and `f84r`
  are forbidden. No new page or image is opened.
- Every inherited helper and every primary semantic source used by the builder
  is hash-bound in `RESULT.json`.

## Method

1. Consume the five GDT643 holes in their frozen order: `otal`, `cthol`,
   `chokchy`, `qotchod`, `ytchor`.
2. Require each target to occur exactly in all three manuscript readings at
   least once. Materialize the token and alternate-reading sources only through
   the explicit-page guarded query.
3. Build the complete observed comparison families rather than assign a value
   from one line: O/QO×K/T×AL/AR for `otal`; the CTH carrier row for `cthol`;
   the nested CHO/SHO quality square for `chokchy`; the 18-cell CHO/CHOD/CHODY
   state family for `qotchod`; and the eight-cell Y×quality×OL/OR family for
   `ytchor`.
4. Audit every target token. An all-reader-exact occurrence with at least two
   clean already rendered companions is concrete-context compatible. An exact
   token in a sparse or unstable line remains neutral. Every non-exact target
   receives an explicit reader warning.
5. Add one whole-surface card at a time, rebuild all line states and record
   dictionary hashes, coverage changes, new complete lines and newly exposed
   holes.
6. Replay all outputs byte-for-byte and independently reconstruct the guarded
   occurrence, page and reader-exact counts.

## Reading rules

| Surface | Scoped composition | Practical V21 reading |
|---|---|---|
| `otal` | `o+t+al` | Ansatz aus kaltem Rohstoff, Form I |
| `cthol` | `cth+ol` | CTH-Drogenstoff; im Kräuterbuch Blatt- oder Krautdroge |
| `chokchy` | `cho+(k+ch+y)` | Trockenansatz: heiß-trockene Grundform |
| `qotchod` | `qo+tch+o+d` | kalt-trockene Zubereitung, fertig gebunden |
| `ytchor` | `y+(tch+or)` | kalt-trockene Portion dieser Droge |

The nested brackets matter. `chokchy` is not flattened into unrelated atoms,
and the initial `y` of `ytchor` is licensed only inside that exact surface. It
does not create a free manuscript-wide word `y = aktuell`.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

All five cards are admitted because their complete target surfaces have exact
three-reader anchors, their relevant comparison cells are observed and no
audited occurrence forces an opposite slot value. A manual zero-collision
column is compatibility bookkeeping, not independent proof.

These are exploratory codebook defaults. CTH remains an unresolved drug class;
the herbal-book narrowing to leaf/herb material does not apply outside that
register. Components explain the selected whole values but are not newly
globalized. No plaintext language, phonetics, species, ingredient identity or
complete manuscript solution is claimed.
