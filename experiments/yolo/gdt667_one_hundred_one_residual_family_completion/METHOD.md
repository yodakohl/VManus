# GDT667 method

## Question

Can every one of the 101 distinct surfaces exposed by the V43 one-hole
frontier receive a concrete default that reuses the established workshop
roles wherever possible, adds only recurring full-panel blocks, closes all 102
source rows, and improves complete passage readings without opening another
page or using generic process prose?

## Inputs

- GDT666 V43 dictionary, glossary, full-line coverage, occurrence audits, and
  fixed 102-row one-hole frontier.
- The same guarded 179-page ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b panel. No new page is opened;
  `f1r`, `f84`, and `f84r` remain outside the material.
- Independent 101-card drafts from a recipe practitioner, stem compositor,
  and passage reader, plus twenty-five passage-first manual readings.
- The inherited 48-role stem sheet and the O+L and SOL practical renderers.

## Method

1. Preserve the 101 surfaces in first-frontier order and recount all 438 of
   their occurrences over the guarded panel.
2. Use the stem draft as the spelling spine, the recipe draft for concrete
   objects and operations, and the passage draft for sentence-local direction.
   Keep the strongest rejected reading on every card.
3. Prefer an inherited-role composition only when the named atoms reconstruct
   the visible surface exactly. Search the complete released panel before
   declaring a residue learned. This search adds four recurring blocks:
   `I_FORM_I`, `SO_SEED_PREP`, `OM_HANDFUL_PREP`, and `CPH_COMPOSITE`.
4. Keep the first role narrow: `i` fills only the missing Form-I cell in
   `oiees`. Keep `F_FLOWER` and `G_GRAIN_SIGLUM` on their enumerated cards.
   `so`, `om`, and `cph` have broader multi-form family support. Free `g`
   remains the inherited post-closing whole.
5. Use a sole `LEARNED_<SURFACE>_WHOLE` card when a productive split would
   export a restricted internal letter or contradict an established head. The
   result is 94 role-composed cards and seven learned exact wholes.
6. Dispatch only exact whitespace-delimited cards. Five short forms may use a
   local alternate-reader join only where IT2a or RF1b visibly concatenates
   that exact neighbor; otherwise they keep their free default.
7. Render every target position, recompute V44 coverage, and manually smooth
   twenty-five newly complete lines. Audit those passages again token by token,
   removing unlicensed verbs and restoring every omitted cooling, measuring,
   drying, preparation, or closing operation.
8. Publish the resulting 76-form next frontier with full-panel occurrence
   counts so the next round starts with recurring families rather than isolated
   first occurrences.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all 101 cards cover all 438 positions, all 102 starting rows are
closed, every composed card reconstructs its surface from one of the 52 scoped
roles, the seven learned wholes use their own exact namespaces, reader joins
have local alternate-transcription support, and the builder and validator
replay byte for byte.

The pass establishes a denser V44 working translation, not proven plaintext.
The German values are concrete and deliberately replaceable. It establishes
no language, phonetics, specific plant, disease, patient, cure, carrier liquid,
historical glyph identity, or unreleased page.
