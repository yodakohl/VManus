## The full panel reduces memorization

The conservative frontier-only composition found 85 decomposable forms and
would have memorized sixteen wholes. Searching the complete already released
panel instead reveals four recurring blocks and raises the final architecture
to 94 role-composed cards and seven learned exact wholes:

| visible block | scoped working value | supporting family |
|---|---|---|
| `i` | Zubereitungsform I | the missing cell before `ee+s` in `oiees`, alongside `oin/oiin/oiiin` |
| `so` | Saatgutzubereitung | `soaiin`, `sodaiin`, `sody`, `soin`, `soiin`, `soiiin`, plus two new cards |
| `om` | eine Handvoll Ansatz | naked `om`, `cthom`, `dom`, `olom`, plus `cholchom` and `qokom` |
| `cph` | Arzneikompositum | `cphol`, `cphedy`, `chcphedy`, `cpheol`, and the wider `cph` family |

This is the useful kind of growth: the dictionary gains meanings while its
reusable inventory grows by only four roles, from 48 to 52. Three caveats stay
explicit. `i` is a narrow Form-I ladder completion, not a free word;
`F_FLOWER` and `G_GRAIN_SIGLUM` remain named allow-lists; and formal
decomposability is not the same as historical proof.

## Passage audit: concrete does not mean free paraphrase

The first manual smoothing pass exposed a recurring failure mode: a line could
sound like a recipe while silently adding “take,” “add,” or “put into,” or
while dropping one of the visible operations. A second independent visual
reading corrected eighteen of the twenty-five selected passages. Examples:

`f17v.19`

> Nimm zwei Maße und trockne sie bis zur Mittelstufe. Nimm drei Teile einer
> Portion Blatt-/Krautdroge und eine Handvoll nachgetrockneten Drogenstoffs.

Here `dain` remains “zwei Maße”; it is not flattened into “Stufe II.”

`f18v.9`

> Eine Portion heiß-trockene Droge im Ansatz zunächst kalt, danach heiß
> antrocknen. Kühle hiervon den Drogenstoff; miss einen Ansatz ab, erhitze ihn
> leicht und schließe ab.

Here `doky` retains measuring, heating, and closure rather than becoming an
unspecified dose.

`f3r.2`

> Nimm hiervon eine bis zur Mittelstufe getrocknete Portion Pflanzenteil,
> Dosis I. Kühle ein Maß Trockendroge; dazu ein weiteres Maß Trockendroge.

Here `qotcham` is a cooling command, not another generic “take.”

`f44v.13`

> Holziger Pflanzenteil mit Feuchtgut; miss die zweite Fraktion der Holzdroge
> ab.

Here `dairl` remains an action instead of an inert material label.

`f9v.11`

> Nimm hiervon den Pflanzenteil. Trockne die Droge, weiche sie ein, setze sie
> an, kühle sie ab und schließe ab; erwärme den Ansatz anschließend leicht und
> dann auf Stufe III.

This line now preserves the complete dry–soak–prepare–cool–close sequence in
`chshoty`.

## What changed in the working theory

V44 strengthens the hybrid-codebook model. The working register is best read
as a mixture of reusable specialist abbreviations and learned whole cards, not
as either a universal one-letter vocabulary or 1,274 unrelated words. The
most useful compositional positions are now:

- command/reference heads such as `qo-`, `qol-`, and `y-`;
- material and preparation heads such as `ol`, `cth`, `chor`, `so`, `cph`;
- operations such as drying, moistening, heating, cooling, measuring, adding,
  straining, and closing;
- grade/form/quantity tails such as `e/ee/eee`, `an/ain/aiin`, and
  `in/iin/iiin`;
- a small exact vocabulary for forms whose internal spelling does not support
  a reusable split.

This gives the notation places for concrete nouns and actions. It does not yet
identify the exact plant species, carrier liquid, disease, patient, or cure.
