# GDT667 final card synthesis

## Decision

The stem compositor supplies the spelling spine for all 101 cards. The recipe
master supplies practical object and operation wording, and the passage reader
supplies direction and twenty-five complete-line renderings. A composition is
retained only when its named atoms concatenate to the surface exactly once;
otherwise the card stays an explicit learned whole.

The first conservative stem draft had 85 composed cards and sixteen learned
wholes. The full already-released panel revealed four recurring blocks that the
frontier-only pass had missed. Adding those blocks, extending the already named
flower family by three exact cards, and licensing one further terminal grain
card yields 94 role-composed cards and only seven learned wholes. No new page or
image is used.

## Four added blocks

### `i = I_FORM_I`

The inherited preparation-form ladder has `oin` seven times, `oiin` 26 times,
and `oiiin` eleven times for Forms II--IV. The internal `i` in singleton
`oiees` fills the visible Form-I cell before `ee+s`. The resulting reading is
“vollständig bereitete Ansatzcharge, Form I.” This is a narrow ladder repair,
not a free word.

### `so = SO_SEED_PREP`

The released panel already contains `soaiin`3, `sodaiin`3, `sody`4, `soin`5,
`soiin`15, and `soiiin`3 with seed-preparation readings. The new cards
`sotchdaiin` and `shsodaiin` each add one context. Treating `so` as one
Saatgutzubereitung head removes the illegal old use of internal `s-` before
`o` and predicts both new dose forms.

### `om = OM_HANDFUL_PREP`

Naked `om` occurs twenty times and already means “eine Handvoll Ansatz.” The
same practical value appears in `cthom`5, `dom`5, and `olom`3. The terminal
block therefore composes `cholchom` and `qokom`; it does not export free `m`
or rescue the incomplete internal `g` of learned `agairom`.

### `cph = CPH_COMPOSITE`

`cphol` occurs eleven times as a material-position noun. The wider family has
`cphedy`8, `chcphedy`4, `cpheol`2, and many further inflections; inherited
`chcphedy` already reads as an Arzneikompositum. The new narrow material head
therefore gives `cphol = CPH_COMPOSITE+OL_MATERIAL`, “zusammengesetzter
Drogenstoff.” It stays distinct from `cfh=Blütenfraktion` and `p=Pulver`.

## Existing roles extended without a new value

- `qopchcfhy = QO_COMMAND+P_POWDER+CH_DRY+F_FLOWER+Y_START_OR_CLOSE` reuses
  the already composed `chcfhy` flower card.
- `folaiin = F_FLOWER+OL_MATERIAL+AIIN_III` and
  `olfar = OL_MATERIAL+F_FLOWER+AR_FRACTION_I` remain inside the named flower
  family; no general `f/cfh` substring rule is created.
- `cheg = CH_DRY+E_MIDDLE+G_GRAIN_SIGLUM` is the fourth named terminal grain
  card. Free `g` remains the GDT666 post-close whole and `agairom` stays
  learned; the two uses are not merged.
- `qoctholy = QO_COMMAND+CTH_HERB+OLY_STRAIN_ACTION` is the clean new
  prediction of the V43 terminal `oly=abseihen` block.

## Seven learned wholes retained

`ytym`, `ykeeb`, `otydal`, `dairydy`, `okeeeykeey`, `olkaycthy`, and
`agairom` remain exact learned cards. Each would otherwise export restricted
free `m`, `b`, internal `y`, or internal `g`, or would require a reader boundary
that is not a general spelling rule. They still receive a concrete default;
“learned” means memorized workshop card, not untranslated.

## Practical corrections to the reader draft

The final passage channel changes `qoolkeey` from wood to the visible
`OL_MATERIAL` drug carrier, reads `oeoar` with one middle-stage `e`, restores
`qoctholy` as “seihe den Krautdrogenstoff ab,” treats `qokair` as a heating
command, and renders `olkees` as heated drug material rather than wood. A
second line-by-line audit then removes unlicensed additions such as “gib hinzu”
and restores omitted operations such as cooling, measuring, drying, and
closing. These changes keep every visible atom's contribution while preserving
readable workshop German.
