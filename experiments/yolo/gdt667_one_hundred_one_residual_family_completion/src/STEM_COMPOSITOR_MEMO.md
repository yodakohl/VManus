# GDT667 stem-compositor memo

## Working stance

1. I read the register as a circa-1420 writing master teaching a scribe to
   combine workshop abbreviations.
2. The reusable slots remain command, material, operation, grade, quantity,
   and close.
3. A visible piece keeps one narrow function across sister forms.
4. A learned whole is preferable to forcing a stem into a second job.
5. A new productive block requires several forms that it actually predicts.

## Outcome

The candidate contains exactly 101 surfaces in first-frontier order. Most are
fully reconstructible with the inherited V43 inventory. No new productive
role is proposed: the frontier supplies many new combinations, but no repeated
uncovered spelling block with a stable independent function.

The strongest family predictions are:

- `qoolkeey = qo + ol + k + ee + y`: command + drug material + heat + complete
  grade + close. It must not be read as `qol`, because the visible form is
  `qo|ol...`, not `qol...`.
- `olshy`, `sheoldy`, `olshees`, `olkees`, `olchdy`: `ol` remains the bounded
  material/result carrier while the following process and close change.
- `ypchedy`, `qopchcfhy`, `qocheor`: the command/reference frame precedes a
  powder or dry-process family; `ypchedy` is fully productive.
- `qolain`, `qolsheey`, `qolteedy`: initial `qol` remains the indivisible
  addition head. It is never simultaneously counted as `q+o+l`.
- `chokeeody`, `chokody`, `tcheody`: repeated visible `o` frames remain in the
  composition instead of being silently collapsed.
- `qoctholy` is the clean new use of inherited `OLY_STRAIN_ACTION`: terminal
  `oly` follows the complete material head `cth`, exactly as the V43 scope
  requires.

## Necessary whole forms

The following are retained as exact wholes because a productive parse would
violate the inherited scope or require an unattested atom:

- `oiees`, `cphol`: contain uncovered `i` or `cph` material.
- `qopchcfhy`, `folaiin`, `olfar`: the low-strength flower sign is confined to
  learned forms and cannot be exported as a general substring merely to close
  this frontier.
- `shsodaiin`: internal `s` is not immediately before its licensed grade,
  charge, or `dy` close.
- `cholchom`, `ytym`, `qokom`: free `m=Handvoll` cannot be exported into a
  longer surface.
- `ykeeb`: `b` has no productive value outside its named inherited whole.
- `otydal`, `dairydy`, `okeeeykeey`, `olkaycthy`: an internal `y` would require
  an unlicensed reader subboundary.
- `cheg`, `agairom`: `g=Gran` is not licensed in these longer forms.

Several other exact wholes (`oiees`, for example) may later acquire a family
analysis, but this frontier alone does not supply the repeated missing block
needed to do so.

## Explicit exclusions

- Do not turn `o` globally into water, oil, or another carrier liquid.
- Do not split `qol-` into command + preparation + wood.
- Do not read terminal `-s` as seed or internal `s-` as species.
- Do not read internal `d` as a close; terminal `-d` alone closes.
- Do not export free `m`, scoped compound `g`, restricted `b`, or low-support
  `f/cfh` merely because their letters occur inside a longer form.
- Do not treat `chor` automatically as `ch+or`; `shotchor` keeps the visible
  plant-part head, whereas `chorolk` is deliberately parsed `ch+or+ol+k`
  because `CHOR_PLANT_PART` is not licensed before material plus process.

The candidate therefore expands the combinatorial register without expanding
the stem inventory opportunistically.
