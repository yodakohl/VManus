# GDT465 method

## Question

Can the last wholly learned address label, `oiil`, be reduced through agreement
among the available manual readings plus exact `IIN`/`L` renderer families,
without inferring `O+IIN+L` from visual resemblance alone?

## Inputs

- GDT464's 107-label hybrid dictionary;
- GDT407's 4,576 running events and invariant component recipes;
- the f17r rows of the mixed cross-transcription source, obtained only through
  `./vmanus-exp query-tsv` with an explicit f17r allow-value and f84/f84r
  forbidden prefixes.

## Method

1. Select only locus f17r.13 and compare its ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b fields. A
   missing reading is kept missing rather than treated as disagreement.
2. Search the complete running recipe inventory for the ordered sequences
   `O+IIN`, `IIN+L`, `O+IIN+L`, `AIIN+L`, `O+AIIN+L` and `L+O+IIN`.
3. Inventory every running surface containing the visible strings `oii` or
   `iil`, retaining its exact old recipe, event count and pages.
4. Test the concrete candidate `o|ii|l` against exact whole cards and the full
   ordered recipe. Keep image evidence at owner level only: the admitted f17r
   image shows one pictured flowering-plant owner but no independently labelled
   inner parts corresponding to the proposed split.
5. Copy all 107 GDT464 readings unchanged and record only the disposition of
   `oiil`. Rebuild all compact artifacts deterministically.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Split `oiil` only if the middle is licensed by an exact card or if the complete
ordered package has running carriers. Agreement between alternate readings on
the unsplit spelling does not itself license components. Otherwise retain the
whole learned label.

ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b are alternate readings of one manuscript, not independent
observations. The audit adds no word meaning, object identity, plaintext,
surface prediction, language identification or confirmed lexeme. No new page
is admitted.
