# GDT862 — material-landmark mirror feasibility

Exploratory native material-only capacity screen on already admitted original
f104r Yale1006256 and f104v Yale1006257. Both images were previously viewed for
other questions; this is not an untouched-image test. Known raised strokes
at f104v.27 motivate possible future IP053, but no bleed-through is observed
or assumed. This screen does not inspect that target, classify glyphs or fit
text to a transformation. No new admission or image acquisition.

Before any directed target counterpart comparison, root A inspects only
material landmarks (holes, tears, creases), preferably outside writing, and
records distinctive candidate recto/verso pairs plus a proposed mirror mapping.
Generic corners, printed/photo framing and writing do not qualify. At least
three distinctive nonink material pairs are required. TGC001 excluded ink
f104r.17|C009 must not be inspected/classified. Root's prior guarded exclusion
check supplied that locator. A records explicit noninspection attestation;
software cannot verify gaze or material identity.

A schema: proposed_mirror_mapping string; landmarks list of unique IDs, kind
HOLE/TEAR/CREASE, recto_description, verso_description, distinctive boolean,
nonink boolean, note; excluded_ink_target_not_inspected=true; overall note.
A is saved then A_SEAL records its byte SHA256 and sealed_at_utc. Fewer than
three eligible (distinctive AND nonink) pairs => LANDMARK_CAPACITY_STOP,
no B or f104v.27 target comparison. Preserve rejected nominations as well.

Only if A reaches three, a separately assigned native B verifies proposed
material pairings without directed target annotation. B sees A's material
nominations, so agreement is not independent discovery. B records every A ID,
matching_material_pair boolean, note; excluded_ink_target_not_inspected=true;
overall note. At least three A-eligible pairs confirmed by B =>
MATERIAL_LANDMARK_CAPACITY_SUPPORTED_ONLY; fewer => LANDMARK_VERIFICATION_STOP.
Missing B after adequate A => A_CAPACITY_AWAITING_B. A's seal asserts unchanged
nomination bytes, not independently reconstructed collection chronology.

Even support only permits a later separately fixed mapping/prospective phase;
no automatic transformation, target counterpart search, glyph regrading,
show-through claim, pen chronology, decipherment or semantic inference.
Two native viewers still use the same two photographs of one leaf.

Runner checks schemas and fixed count decision from recorded native inputs;
independent validator checks actual original image SHA256/byte count/dimensions,
A seal and source metadata, and independently counts eligible/verified IDs.
No image recognition or material judgment is performed by software. Controls
cover two-versus-three threshold, ineligible nominations and rejected verification.
Public preregistration precedes this material-screen inspection. Cached replay
must be byte-identical. Implementation agent acquires/views no image.
