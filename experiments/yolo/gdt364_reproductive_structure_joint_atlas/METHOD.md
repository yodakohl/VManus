# GDT364 three-class Herbal reproductive-structure atlas

Status: **POSTEXPOSURE EXPLORATORY PANEL FROZEN BEFORE JOINT SOURCE-FAMILY AGGREGATION**.

## Question and provenance

The existing human Herbal catalogue contains three explicit positive
descriptions which do not turn silence into a negative:

- `FLOWER_SIDE`: `flower(s) seen from the side`;
- `BERRY_NO_CIRCLES`: `berries that have no added circles`;
- `NO_FRUIT_OR_FLOWER`: `no fruits or flowers`.

Use every non-f84 page carrying exactly one of those assertions: 19, 8, and 7
pages respectively. Five two-sided physical folios contribute two pages, so
all held evaluation removes the complete physical folio.

BERRY001 and FLOWER001 previously tested separate binary recurrent
literal/root feature families and were nonconfirming. Their results and
postexposure status are preserved. GDT364 is a genuinely different permissive
question: does a single anonymous source-native family distribution separate
the three explicitly described page types jointly? It is hypothesis
generation, not a prospective confirmation.

## Frozen formal representation and ranking

Use the same state-blind family-consensus page representation as GDT363:
family-component rates, within-source-group family bigrams/trigrams, first
group prefixes, last group suffixes, synchronized boundary classes, and
multi-group construction rates. Never cross a source-group boundary. Exclude
exact family expressions, source surfaces, member identities, roots,
PAGE_HOSTs, tuples, EVA, and meanings. Admit a feature before class labels are
used only when it is present on at least five of 34 pages and absent on at
least five.

Rank every admitted feature by:

- leave-one-physical-folio-out three-class codelength gain above a nuisance
  model (quire, page side, source volume, label rate, reading-alternative
  rate);
- class-conditional page-rate means and one-vs-rest contrasts;
- 4,096 deterministic whole-folio-vector permutations within
  `quire × pages-on-physical-folio`, preserving the paired-page state vector;
- complete-library maxT, leave-one-quire direction, and all-reading strict-row
  sensitivity.

The null is deliberately local and source-preserving. It is still exploratory:
only 29 physical folios exist and several late quires carry one class only.
Controls quantify suspicion and do not act as discovery kill gates.

## Claim ceiling and seal

All f84 selectors are discarded before any formal field is parsed. This test
may nominate only an exploratory association between an explicit human visual
page class and an anonymous source-family distribution. It assigns no plant,
flower, berry, lexeme, morpheme, part of speech, sound, language, plaintext,
meaning, or translation.
