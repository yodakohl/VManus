# Method

## Question

Can recurrent exact visible recipe frames connect all ten GDT549
higher-order-sequence cautions to stronger current cards in compatible observed
context modes, while preserving every complete working reading?

## Frame construction

Enumerate every exact old-alias full cover of all 145 GDT548 surfaces. Within
each cover, coalesce one to three adjacent alias segments. Retain a frame only
when it contains at least two visible characters and two recipe atoms.

A candidate must occur in at least one of the ten sequence targets and at
least one GDT548 non-default card. Record exact surface position, component
mapping, old alias realization, target cards, stronger peers and all
target/peer context-mode contacts. No frame is inferred from mere substring
resemblance; it must lie inside a complete synchronized surface/recipe cover.

## Cover selection

Only frames giving every touched target at least one stronger peer with an
overlapping observed GDT548 requirement mode may enter the cover. Exhaustively
enumerate finite frame sets by increasing size. Six five-frame sets are the
minimum complete covers of all ten targets.

Select among equal-size solutions by maximum mapped recipe atoms, then visible
length, same-mode peer contacts and lexical order. This chooses
`chor→CH+OR`, `ko→K+O`, `she→SH+E`, `shy→SH+Y`, and `ches→CH+E+S`.

## Working promotion

The new frame is additive to each target's existing GDT542 complete old tiles,
old seams, exact GDT549 visible route and unchanged GDT540 meaning. The absence
of an old complete whole-recipe carrier remains printed. Context and interface
residual cards are copied unchanged into the next queue.

## Validation

The validator independently regenerates all exact covers and frame
occurrences, rederives the 23 candidates and 20 context-compatible candidates,
exhausts the six minimum covers, checks the five selected frames, 31 exact
occurrences, 21 strong peers, 27 same-mode contacts, ten promotions, 4/5
residual split and deterministic regeneration.
