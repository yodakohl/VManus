# GDT477 — OL directional scope phrasebook

## Result

`OL=FORTSETZEN` now has one meaning and three predictable positional
realizations across all 28 local occurrences:

| function/name shape | working scope | occurrences |
|---|---|---:|
| `OL · X` | weiter mit X | 9 |
| `X · OL · Y` | X in Y weiterführen | 10 |
| `X · OL` | X weiterführen | 9 |

The distribution is almost perfectly balanced, but the jobs are not
interchangeable. All nine forward openings begin an event—eight whole bundles
and one later card. All sixteen event-internal OL slots are non-opening: eight
bridges and eight backward holds. The eleven cross-locus record binders contain
eight openings, two bridges and one backward hold.

## What this adds to the translation

The same root can now be spoken without inventing a long dictionary gloss:

- `olkalaiin`: `OL · Sternstelle k …` → “weiter mit Sternstelle k”;
- `qkol`: `Droge qk · OL` → “Droge qk weiterführen”;
- `ykolairol`: `Sternstelle yk · OL · BAHN · OL` → “Sternstelle yk in der
  Bahn weiterführen; die Bahn weiterführen”;
- `doly`: `Droge d · OL · POSTEN` → “Droge d als/in den Posten weiterführen”;
- `otolarol`: `DANACH · OL · AUSGANG · OL` → “den Folgeschritt zum Ausgang
  weiterführen; vom Ausgang aus weiter”.

These slot phrases sit beside, not instead of, the fluent GDT476 event reading.
Every one of the 28 occurrences therefore retains both its exact literal order
and a complete active default.

## Name direction

Thirteen OL slots are name-free, ten precede a learned name and five follow
one. A pre-name OL is never a backward hold; a post-name OL is never a forward
opening. This is the expected compositional behavior:

- before a name, continuation reaches toward or through the name;
- after a name, the name is already active and is held or carried onward;
- without a name, OL connects actions, relations or address values in the same
  way.

No OL lies between two learned names in this deck.

## Existing two-sided support

GDT460 already calibrated both surface directions independently. Prefix `ol-`
matches 54/56 running extension types, 138 events and twenty pages; suffix
`-ol` matches 102/111 types, 338 events and 24 pages. GDT477 does not reinterpret
those counts as semantics. It uses them to confirm that left and right OL
channels are both established parts of the current codebook rather than one
being an emergency spelling repair.

## Important precision

The three shapes are measured in the complete GDT474 function/name trace, not
by raw character offset alone. Renderer material can be present without its own
semantic token. The artifact therefore preserves surface, recipe, literal
trace, immediate neighbours and learned-name position separately.

## Next route

Complete the order grammar by applying the same literal left/right audit to all
41 `OT=DANACH` occurrences. The useful question is whether OT's function/name
position changes the way a fresh sibling is phrased, or whether it remains a
pure new-entry operator in every direction. This is a direct extension of
GDT475 made possible by GDT477; no page, root or name should change.

## Validation and ceiling

The validator passes 77/77 checks, including all 28 occurrence joins, all 26
events, exact 9/10/9 scope counts, exact 9/9 opening and 8+8 internal split,
name direction, both GDT460 edge channels, all five page totals, unchanged
GDT476 models and a byte-identical rebuild.

This is an exploratory positional renderer, not plaintext or confirmed syntax.
No component meaning, name, surface, recipe, selected model, event, page,
object identity, language or lexeme changes.
