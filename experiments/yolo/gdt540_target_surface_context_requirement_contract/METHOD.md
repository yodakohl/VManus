# GDT540 method

## Question

Which of the 149 target prose occurrences are self-contained, which use the
active same-statement action, argument, or both, and can those observations be
compiled into one practical future intake rule for all 145 surfaces?

## Inputs

- GDT539's 546 contextual prose-event rows, including the 149 target events,
  final recipes, explicit roots, inherited roots, source events, neutral
  phrases, and contextual clauses.

No page beyond f4r, f20v, f31r, and f66r is used.

## Method

1. Select the 149 rows whose recipe source is the final GDT538 surface layer.
2. Class each known occurrence from its actual GDT539 input state as
   self-contained, action-dependent, argument-dependent, or dependent on both.
3. Recompute every context-source distance from card ordinals and require the
   source to be earlier in the same statement.
4. Collapse occurrences onto 145 exact surfaces while preserving every recipe,
   contextual clause, page, event ID, and observed mode.
5. Separate observation from the future default. Visible actions and arguments
   win. A missing action uses the same-statement active action or falls back to
   a nonverbal fragment. A missing argument uses the active same-statement
   argument when available and otherwise remains objectless.
6. Expose that rule through `src/context_surface.py`, including explicit
   action/argument resolution and a safe unknown-surface delegation.

## Result conditions

- all 149 occurrences and 145 surfaces are represented exactly once;
- the occurrence classes are 92 self-contained, five action-dependent,
  41 argument-dependent, and eleven dependent on both;
- the three repeated surfaces retain their complete occurrence histories;
- `keody` and `shain` are consistent and `qokees` alone visibly switches;
- every inherited source remains earlier in the same statement;
- all recipes roundtrip and all executable reader cases pass.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The occurrence class states what the known GDT539 clause actually used; it is
not automatically a permanent property of a singleton surface. The future
intake contract is the compositional working default derived from visible
action and argument slots. It adds no page, recipe, root value, plaintext,
historical identification, or confirmed lexeme.
