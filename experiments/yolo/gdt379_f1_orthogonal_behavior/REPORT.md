# GDT379 — F1 orthogonal-behavior report

## Outcome

F1 has one real but unstable formal recurrence lead. It does **not** acquire a
functional label.

The source-freeze was published at commit `0f1dd89` before any F1 context was
enumerated. The run then tested 36 submetrics in eleven frozen diagnostic
families against one 4,096-world two-sided maxT family. The only source was the
already f84-free GDT327 interlinear: 8,448 groups, 2,400 fields, 288 records, 91
physical folios, and five registers. The identity null has 2,502 mobile events
in 199 nuisance strata and 3,333 distinct global maxima.

The final decision is:

`NO_STABLE_JOINTLY_ADJUSTED_ORTHOGONAL_CONSEQUENCE`

F1 remains the exposed, nonpromoted atomic tuple
`2f1c5e56e8f0ff459065`; its linked `d`-rendered source group is
`c502a1edfafbe3e54262`. The 435 atomic and 249 `d`-rendered occurrences are one
formal object at two resolutions, not independent evidence.

## The recurrence lead

Among 394 F1 events with two following groups available, 29 return to the exact
same atomic F1 tuple at distance two (`F1 X F1`), a rate of .073604. The matched
null mean is .043404 (z=3.646; local p=.000244; full 36-submetric maxT
p=.023188). Distance eight is also adjusted-positive (.086207 versus .050175;
maxT p=.035148); distance six narrowly misses the family correction
(p=.054918).

This is not one memorized `X`: the 29 distance-two events contain 25 distinct
opaque intervening tuple identities and occur on 27 physical folios, across all
five registers. It is therefore a useful description of F1's formal ecology.

It nevertheless fails the prospectively frozen transfer gate. After exact
residualization inside the mobile section/register/Currier/hand/record-length/
line-position/field-position/closure/frequency strata, the H2 direction is
positive on only 26 of 67 eligible folios (.388059), versus the required .60.
All five registers have the pooled positive direction, showing a broad but
minority-folio recurrence tendency rather than stable folio transfer. H8 is
positive on only 18 of 55 eligible folios (.327273). Accordingly the lead is
`UNSTABLE`, not `INTERESTING_EXPLORATORY` and not a promotion.

The neighboring operands also do not behave like a recovered coordination
class. Twenty-two complete multi-F1 fields were found, including two with
three F1 pivots, but their chain rate and operand homogeneity are ordinary under
the matched search. Left/right operand multiset similarity is exactly null-like
(combined score .276038 versus .275921). Thus `F1 X F1` recurrence does not
license a coordinator, list marker, or paired operator.

## Falsifiers and controls

- F1-linked left and right units are not unusually symmetric.
- Variable-arity F1 chains are not enriched after joint search charge.
- Linked operands are not more mutually exclusive than controls.
- F1 presence supplies **less**, not more, conditional information about the
  next-two-group state than the matched null (.147367 versus .156553 bits per
  event); this is only a local weak effect after family correction.
- No stable decay or termination at DY, B3, field, or line boundaries is found.
- The nested training-only F2 search makes held predictions on 84 folios but
  has no excess over its fully reselected null.
- The `d`-rendered and non-`d` F1 subsets have a high descriptive behavioral
  cosine (.975948), but matched forms also do (null mean .946418; p=.413717).
  This does not establish renderer equivalence.
- The three separate comparator-derived score routes do not transfer a new
  anonymous behavioral class. CMP01 and CMP02 are null; CMP03's local negative
  `d`-delta association does not survive maxT.

## Corrections and null design

Four prepublication corrections are retained. The first repaired a result-row
dictionary lookup after all worlds ran but before any output was written. The
second enforced the already frozen folio/register stability gate, converting
the initially misclassified H2 lead to `UNSTABLE`. The third made F2 support
strictly outer-fold-local, excluded F1 itself from F2, normalized observed and
null F2 scores by the same fold count, and computed stability on exact nuisance
residuals. The fourth added the complete retained 4,096×36 null matrix. Every
superseded hash is preserved; no candidate, threshold, or statistic was
retuned.

For future grammar-slot experiments, a candidate may not be an exact function
of variables conditioned on by the null. Such a candidate must be residualized
or randomized with membership mobile; otherwise it is `UNIDENTIFIABLE` and
excluded from maxT while its search remains charged. This correction does not
retroactively repair or promote GDT378.

## Claim ceiling

GDT379 establishes only that exposed anonymous formal object F1 has an
adjusted, cross-register but folio-unstable tendency to recur after one
intervening opaque tuple. It does not establish AND, OR, NOT, UNTIL, a function
word, POS, morpheme, meaning, language, plaintext, or translation. No f84 file,
row, image, text, or formal payload was opened, parsed, retained, or scored.
