# GDT379 — frozen F1 orthogonal-behavior test

## Status and scope

This is a prospective test of **new consequences** of one exposed formal lead.
GDT378's post-hoc `CMP_FUNCTION_04` identity diagnostic is frozen here as
anonymous candidate `F1`. It is not promoted, and the readable-comparator
endpoint name is not inherited. GDT378's failed primary null and primary
decision remain unchanged.

The only target input is the already f84-free
`gdt327_joint_tuple_interlinear.tsv`. Exact GDT327 joint tuples stay atomic.
No PAGE_HOST, glyph, substring, lexical, visual, or semantic feature is used.

`F1_ATOMIC` is joint tuple `2f1c5e56e8f0ff459065`. `F1_D_RENDERED` is source
group `c502a1edfafbe3e54262`, mechanically defined as that atomic tuple with
observed wrapper `d`. They are one formal object at two linked resolutions,
not independent evidence.

## Prospective F1 diagnostics

The following eight families are frozen before any F1 context is enumerated.

1. `F1_D01_COORDINATION_SYMMETRY`: for an interior F1 pivot, compare the
   maximal non-F1 operand immediately to its left and right inside the same
   field. The fixed symmetry score is the mean of length similarity and
   Jaccard similarity of opaque atomic-tuple multisets. Empty operands are
   ineligible.
2. `F1_D02_VARIABLE_ARITY_CHAIN`: find complete within-field patterns
   `operand (F1 operand){2,}`. Score operand homogeneity by mean pairwise tuple-
   multiset Jaccard and length similarity. Report chain arity and transfer by
   folio/register. One-sided or absent chains remain observations.
3. `F1_D03_MUTUAL_EXCLUSION`: for each F1-linked operand pair, learn from
   non-held folios whether either operand occupies the same exact structural
   slot in matched records and whether both co-occur less often than their
   marginals predict. Score held folios only. This is an anonymous
   exclusivity diagnostic, not an OR interpretation.
4. `F1_D04_DOWNSTREAM_DELTA`: match records on the two atomic tuples before a
   pivot, field position, record-size bucket, register, and closure context.
   Compare records with F1 against records with a non-F1 pivot. The target is
   the opaque next-two-tuple/closure state after the pivot. Test whether F1
   presence predicts a recurring downstream delta while the source-side host
   structure is fixed.
5. `F1_D05_SCOPE_HORIZON`: estimate F1-minus-control differences at fixed
   horizons 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 8 groups. Outcomes are downstream tuple return,
   tuple diversity, and boundary crossing. Separately report termination at
   DY, B3, field, and physical-line boundaries. The charged primary is the
   signed area under the six-horizon standardized effect curve; every horizon
   and boundary is included in maxT.
6. `F1_D06_PAIRED_OPERATOR`: in each outer held-folio fold, discover an opaque
   `F2` only in training folios from forms enriched 1–8 groups after F1.
   Minimum training support is 12 F1–F2 pairs, three folios, and two registers.
   Freeze the training-selected F2 and direction, then score held F1→F2
   occurrence against its training-estimated background. The entire candidate
   form × span search is rerun in every null world.
7. `F1_D07_RENDERER_EQUIVALENCE`: compare the seven-dimensional external
   behavior vector from D01–D06 for all atomic F1 events, the `d`-rendered
   subset, and the non-`d` subset. The primary renderer statistic is the
   minimum held-folio cosine between `d` and non-`d` effect vectors. Nested
   subsets are never counted as replication.
8. `F1_D08_POSITION_INDEPENDENCE`: every eligible endpoint is re-estimated
   after controlling section, register, Currier, hand, physical-line position,
   within-field position, closure, record-length bucket, and training-only
   tuple-frequency bucket. Report leave-one-register and leave-one-folio
   effects. A lead must retain its direction in at least 60% of eligible
   folios and at least three powered registers.

## Three separate anonymous signature routes

The other GDT378 signatures remain frozen and anonymous. They do not nominate
forms. Their scores are residualized against the nuisance panel in held-folio
cross-fitting, then tested on outcomes not used by their comparator detector:

- `R1_CMP01_BOUNDARY_RENDERER_PERSISTENCE`: does the residualized
  `CMP_FUNCTION_01` score predict the exact atomic renderer-state persistence
  across the next field/line boundary?
- `R2_CMP02_BRANCH_RECONVERGENCE`: does residualized `CMP_FUNCTION_02` score
  predict divergence of the two adjacent opaque spans followed by
  reconvergence to the same atomic tuple within four groups?
- `R3_CMP03_RECORD_DELTA_REVERSAL`: in source-side-matched record pairs with
  and without a high residualized `CMP_FUNCTION_03` event, is the downstream
  record-level atomic-state delta consistently reversed?

These are behavioral score tests, not identity atlases. The comparator names
remain provenance only; no Voynich function label is transferred.

## Controls, search charge, and thresholds

There are eleven charged diagnostic families: eight F1 families and three
anonymous signature routes. Every submetric, horizon, boundary, F2 identity,
span, renderer split, register deletion, and direction enters a single 4,096-
world two-sided maxT family.

For F1 identity tests, labels are permuted within mobile nuisance strata:
physical folio × section × register × Currier × hand × record-length bucket ×
line-position × within-field-position × closure × training-frequency bucket.
The candidate is an opaque identity and is not defined by those covariates.
Strata with no F1/control mobility contribute descriptively but not to the
inferential orbit.

For score-defined R1–R3, a held-folio nuisance model first removes section,
register, Currier, hand, line/field position, closure, record length, and tuple
frequency from both score and outcome. Freedman–Lane permutations shuffle only
the score residual within folio × register × coarse record-length strata. The
score-selection operation and every nested F2 search are replayed in each
world.

An endpoint is `INTERESTING_EXPLORATORY` only if it has inclusive joint maxT
`p <= .05`, the frozen direction survives at least 60% of eligible folios,
three powered registers, and both the atomic and nuisance-adjusted analyses.
Local `p <= .05` without the joint gate is `WEAK`; contradictory directions or
deletion failure are `UNSTABLE`; otherwise `NO_SIGNAL`. Because F1 was selected
post hoc, even `INTERESTING_EXPLORATORY` is not confirmation or promotion.

Minimum endpoint capacity is 12 eligible events, three physical folios, two
registers, and 256 mobile events across the charged family. A low-capacity
endpoint is `INSUFFICIENT_CAPACITY`, not negative evidence.

## Future null correction

GDT378 revealed a design error: a grammar-slot candidate was an exact function
of position/closure variables that the null held fixed. Future grammar-slot
tests must use cross-fitted nuisance residualization or a conditional
randomization that leaves candidate membership mobile. If candidate membership
is deterministic after conditioning, the endpoint is `UNIDENTIFIABLE` and is
excluded from the maxT statistic while its attempted search remains charged.
This correction is prospective only. It does not rescore, repair, or promote
GDT378 or F1.
