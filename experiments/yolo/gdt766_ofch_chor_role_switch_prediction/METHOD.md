# GDT766 method — `ofch` content class versus `chor` role switch

## Question

Can the already cached reader-exact occurrences distinguish two different
formal mechanisms?

1. Complete forms containing `ofch` may share a nominal drug/preparation
   class, with flower material retained only as a concrete family-bounded
   working hypothesis.
2. The four complete forms `chor`, `pchor`, `schor` and `lchor` may share
   visible spelling while occupying different record roles rather than naming
   one interchangeable plant part.

The experiment also asks whether those role assignments produce a useful
five-line, 46-token renderer without reviving component meanings or hiding
uncertain identities inside fluent prose.

## Status of this method

The executable builder now reproduces the source specifications and the
independent validator passes 5,294 checks. Counts described as expected below
are the registered reconstruction targets; all of them were met.

## Cohorts

### Complete forms containing `ofch`

Enumerate every reader-exact whitespace-delimited surface containing the
literal transcription sequence `ofch`. This is a formal selector, not a
morpheme claim. The expected complete cohort is 43 occurrences across 25
complete forms.

Keep the strict prefix subset separately: surfaces beginning with `ofch` are
expected to contribute 25 occurrences across thirteen complete forms. The
remaining twelve outer-shell forms contribute the expected eighteen
occurrences. The base-prefix and outer-shell layers may be compared, but no
substring receives a German word, sound or historical abbreviation expansion.

### Four exact `chor` wholes

Enumerate every reader-exact occurrence of exactly `chor`, `pchor`, `schor`
or `lchor`. Do not include longer lookalikes. The expected census is 191
occurrences: 176 `chor`, ten `pchor`, three `schor` and two `lchor`.

For each occurrence retain page, locus, section, ordinal, line length, line
position, paragraph-start and paragraph-end flags, exact left and right
neighbors, independent neighbor roles, direct selected-value contacts and any
already licensed exact spans. A true paragraph opener requires both ordinal 1
and a paragraph-start line; merely appearing later on the first line is kept
as a different feature.

### Matched controls

For each of the 22 strict-prefix target occurrences other than `ofchy`, use all
non-`ofch*` reader-exact occurrences from the same section whose complete
surface has the same global exact frequency. There is no sampling or
tie-breaking. Line-first and paragraph-start-line are outcomes, so neither is
matched. The output publishes each target's full-pool size/count/rate and a
reproducible aggregate; the validator independently reconstructs every pool.

The controls ask whether nominal/value-bearing neighborhoods and
paragraph-opening behavior are special to the target whole or merely typical
for a form of the same frequency and placement. Until that artifact exists,
the draft assigns no numerical control advantage to either family model.

## Evidence channels

1. **Geometry:** FIRST/MIDDLE/LAST, true paragraph opening or closing and
   section distribution.
2. **Exact local field:** independently typed exact neighbors, selected value
   followers and already licensed complete-word state spans.
3. **Whole recurrence:** the same complete surface must retain a compatible
   role across all its exact occurrences. Similar spelling alone supplies no
   semantic credit.
4. **Historical role analogy:** recipe opening, materia head, plant-part
   subentry, preparation/result and quality/value field are allowed role
   classes because such mixtures occur in the cached historical bank. This
   channel gives zero target-spelling and zero lexical-identity credit.
5. **Provenance:** any candidate derived from an old analyst composition is
   marked and cannot independently support the same family meaning.

The source-spec family score is

```text
2*geometry_fit + 2*context_fit + family_bridge + prior_anchor
+ portability_bonus - 2*contradiction_penalty - circularity_penalty
```

It compares the registered hypotheses without converting the score into a
probability. The reproduced totals are 15 for a generic nominal
drug/preparation complete-word class, 13 for the bold flower-material analogy,
-5 for a process/command class and -1 for an orthography-only account. For the
four `chor` wholes, the role-switched learned-whole model scores 18, a shared
plant noun 2 and a shared formula -2.

## Three renderer layers

The output must keep three fields distinct.

1. **Structural role:** record opener, item/subentry, internal preparation
   field, content head, result field, value field or transition. This is not
   spoken as a recovered word.
2. **Portable complete-word default:** the least specific useful semantic
   description supported across the admitted occurrences of that exact whole,
   such as “named drug in base form,” “reproductive plant part,” or “internal
   preparation field.”
3. **Bold replaceable reading:** one concrete renderer choice, such as
   `Blütenmasse`, `Blütenstand`, `Blütenauszug` or `nimm`. It is explicitly a
   working hypothesis and carries its own identity confidence and rivals.

Local German connective wording used to make a line readable belongs to a
fourth, occurrence-only syntax layer. It neither changes the portable
dictionary nor creates a lexeme.

## Five-line renderer

five registered lines f22r.4, f22v.1, f41v.2, f93r.2 and f107r.38. Expected

require one source row for every locus/ordinal, the written target surface at
that position, and `global_export=0` for all 46 rows.

Emit both the portable and bold line from
`PASSAGE_5_LINE_RENDER_SPECS.tsv`. The portable line speaks record function,
material/preparation class and independently retained state/value information.
The bold line substitutes the registered flower/leaf and process choices.
Neither channel is plaintext, and fluent word order or inserted connectives
must be labelled renderer syntax.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The mixed learned-whole/register model is retained because complete-word
geometry separates the four `chor` wholes and the `ofch` cohort is
predominantly early-but-noninitial. The matched control sharpens that result:
the 22 prefix targets are on paragraph-start lines 17/22 times versus a 26.39%
macro-control rate, yet are line-first 0/22 times versus 19.25% in controls.
The flower-material analogy remains the bold renderer but does not outrank the
portable generic drug/preparation class.

GDT766 may assign concrete, replaceable defaults to the 25 registered complete
`ofch` forms and the four exact `chor` wholes, and may render the five fixed
lines. It may not identify a Voynich word, sound, language, substance, plant,
unit or plaintext sentence; export `ofch`, `chor`, `p`, `s`, `l` or another
substring; treat EVA labels as Latin spelling; turn a same-line bridge into a
relation; or open a new page, image, transcription, f84 or f84r.
