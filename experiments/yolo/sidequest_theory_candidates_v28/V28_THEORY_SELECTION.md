# V28 selection — five-template workshop grammar

The complete prose translation no longer needs 135 unrelated syntactic rules.
Every field falls into one of five teachable constructions:

| template | fields |
|---|---:|
| one-card committed cell | 44 |
| short committed operation | 28 |
| multi-card committed operation | 18 |
| open Herbal article clause | 15 |
| open Bio worksheet continuation | 30 |

This gives a plausible learning mechanism for several scribes: memorize a
small common deck, copy rare payload cards from exemplars, select a field
template, then render it according to hand and physical placement. The drawing
and prior field supply silent arguments.

The grammar covers 135/135 fields without changing any of the 776 concrete
defaults. It is intentionally coarse: the five templates are production forms,
not proven source-language constituent structures. Full instructions are in
`V28_APPRENTICE_MANUAL.md`; f84 and f84r remained sealed.
