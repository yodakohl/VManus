# V28 apprentice manual — five field templates

Date: 2026-08-22

Status: executable reconstruction of how a small workshop could teach the
fixed-page system. It is not an historical transcription or decipherment.

## Before writing

1. Draw the plant, vessel system or diagram first.
2. Choose the register: illustrated article, application worksheet or Astro
   lookup.
3. The drawing becomes the silent owner of nearby text.
4. Select exact cards from the common teaching deck and the register-local
   exemplar tail.
5. Choose one of five field templates.

## The five templates

### T1 — SOLO COMMIT (44 fields)

```text
[one licensed operation/value card carrying closure]
```

Read: execute or record this one operation and close the cell. This is the most
common Bio form and explains the repeated one-card stencil rows.

### T2 — SHORT COMMIT (28 fields)

```text
[head or argument] [optional modifier] [close]
```

Read: instantiate one short operation with at most one explicit argument, then
commit it. Picture and preceding batch supply everything omitted.

### T3 — MULTI-CARD COMMIT (18 fields)

```text
[optional entry head] [arguments/relations]* [condition]? [action+close]
```

Read: fill a complete local operation—material, relation, measure, condition or
place as required—and commit the result.

### T4 — OPEN HERBAL ARTICLE (15 fields)

```text
[pictured owner inherited] [part/property] [preparation/use]* → continue
```

Read: continue describing or using the pictured simple. Do not add a terminator
merely because the remaining writing space ends.

### T5 — OPEN BIO WORKSHEET CONTINUATION (30 fields)

```text
[active cell/batch inherited] [arguments/relations]* → carry
```

Read: the application/configuration remains unfinished; continue it in the next
field or physical line.

## Repetition

Within any template, copying an action card again repeats the operation. Copying
an item, measure, relation or pointer opens another typed operand slot. This is
the V25 rule and needs no separate numeral for two.

## Rendering

Only after the source cards and template are selected does the hand choose
line-entry or post-close wrappers. A line may end because the pre-drawn picture
leaves no room. The reader therefore follows field closure and inherited state,
not the right edge of the page.

## Reading back

1. Identify register and pictured owner.
2. Segment by hierarchical spaces into fields.
3. If DY/B3 is present, commit that operation.
4. If open, carry owner/batch/state forward.
5. Interpret repeated cards according to their typed slot.
6. Expand omitted plant, vessel, channel, body site or current batch from the
   drawing and prior field.

All 135 fixed prose fields are assigned in `V28_ALL_135_FIELD_TEMPLATES.tsv`.
