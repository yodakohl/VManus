## Continuous reading

H4 measures, divides, cools and stores a preparation. H5 turns a measured addition or extract into repeated target applications. B2 moves those working portions through several visibly distinct upper, middle, lower and rim stations; each owner reset starts a new local cell rather than implying one global pipe direction.
