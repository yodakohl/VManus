# Hundred-ninety-ninth pass: normalized H4, H5 and B2

## H4: portion and store

H4 is a four-step preparation ledger:

1. measure the preparation, divide it into a first and second portion, cool;
2. transfer the prescribed amount to storage;
3. later take a prescribed portion from the source extract, work it longer and
   finish it;
4. set the prescribed amount at the target and form the following preparation
   from this batch and its preparation portion.

The text needs a working liquid or extract, but it does not itself force the
word WATER, WINE or OIL.

## H5: add and apply

H5 makes an additive preparation, brings the added material to measure, inserts
it into a following preparation, then repeats target operations: hold, add,
work briefly, reinsert, distribute extract and move to the next measured item.
The pictured plant remains the silent owner; the recurring cards are operations
and references, not plant-name fragments.

## B2: local station route

B2 contains 62 visible events in 26 fields and 22 statements. It moves working
portions through an upper pair station, a middle left device, an unresolved
middle-right line/platform, a lower multi-figure field and local rim stations.
The instructions are local: transfer, continue, add a portion, set at target,
pass, warm, settle, drain, divide and wash. No global flow direction is added.

One important source correction is preserved. At the line transition in
B2-S005, `qokaiin qokaiin` is two visible copies but one logical measure
instruction. The edition therefore contains 107 visible events but 106 logical
source tokens; it does not translate the pair as “measure twice.”

## Combined working reading

H4 supplies measured stored portions. H5 turns a portion or extract into a
target additive/application. B2 routes such working material through visible
stations in short closed cells. This remains compatible with both therapeutic
washing and technical bathhouse operation; the cards specify process more
strongly than domain.
