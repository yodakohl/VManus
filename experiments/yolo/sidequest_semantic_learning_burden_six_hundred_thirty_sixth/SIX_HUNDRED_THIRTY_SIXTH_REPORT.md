# Sidequest Pass 636: the apprentice's actual semantic burden

## Result

The 173 exact cards do not require 173 independent meanings. The complete
381-event prose inventory can be taught with 39 short semantic words plus exact
card surfaces and renderer habits.

## Four learning classes

| Class | Exact cards | Events | What is learned |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| fully compositional card | 33 | 159 | recurrent standalone word or one of the six productive tables |
| compositional meaning, learned exact surface | 132 | 213 | speak known components; copy the registered card body/wrapper |
| partial composition with one learned core | 5 | 5 | memorize one rare technical core; compose its productive edges |
| true learned whole card | 3 | 4 | memorize the complete card and its short value |

All 173 card parses use only the current 39 semantic words. Unknown components:
zero.

## The 39-word booklet

The words themselves split into:

- 31 recurrent productive components;
- 5 one-use embedded technical cores;
- 3 one-use whole-card words.

The five embedded cores are:

| Core | Card | Working value |
|---|---|---|
| `CFH` | `cfhy` | AUSWRINGEN + current item |
| `AN` | `ykan` | dose a subsequent portion of the current item |
| `S` | `ches` | take off briefly and divide |
| `LD` | `qokylddy` | set current item, fasten, close |
| `DA` | `daiiin` | mark the second work stage |

Their productive neighbours still read normally. For example `qokylddy` does
not need a sentence-sized whole gloss: only `LD=BEFESTIGEN` is a rare learned
core; `OK`, `Y`, and `DY` remain ordinary words.

## The three true whole cards

| Card surface | Working value | Events |
|---|---|---:|
| `os` | ARBEITSFACH | 1 |
| `dchol / schol` | WIEDERAUFNEHMEN | 2 |
| `talam` | VERWAHREN | 1 |

These are the only cards for which the whole card is itself the semantic word
and no smaller current composition is used.

## What “learned exact surface” means

The 132-card middle class is not semantically opaque. A card such as
`lchedal` reads `L + CHD + AL = WEITERLEITEN + UMSETZEN + ZIELSTELLE`.
However, the apprentice still learns or copies the exact registered visual body
that realizes that combination. Meaning and graphy therefore have different
burdens:

- semantic burden: 39 words and six little tables;
- exact-card inventory: 173 bodies;
- line realization: renderer rules plus a small residual exception deck.

This is precisely the desired mixture of Fachkürzel and learned whole forms.
