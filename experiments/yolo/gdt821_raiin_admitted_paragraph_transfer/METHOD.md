# Method

The design in PREREGISTRATION.md precedes the complete-context packet. The
ascent meaning is inherited from GDT820, not chosen after this inventory.
All39 admitted selectors are searched via selector-first guarded projections.
The primary selection criterion is an exact complete source group `raiin` in
any edition. All three readings at each resulting locus survive the union.
Each P hit expands to its complete source P block; non-P records remain separate.
Grouping and boundary disagreements are retained, not settled by majority vote.

`src/run.py` reuses existing guarded-query helpers without rerunning GDT819/820.
It emits the occurrence inventory, full source paragraph reader, raw group and
separator vectors, and one source-order C0 gloss vector per reader-locus. Every
unknown remains written. Paragraph flags do not establish sentence boundaries.

Two content readers independently read all65 selected loci with all3 editions:
a natural-philosophy physician evaluates physical ascent; an apothecary seeks
at most one short concrete rival. The coordinator separately reads the entire
packet and compares their content costs. These are exploratory readings, not
independent manuscript witnesses or decoded semantic measurements.

After that reading, LIGHT_TRIAL.json declares the single physical-lightness
rival. `src/light_probe.py` changes only the28 exact raiin gloss positions and
the hypothesis-confidence field in the same195 rows. It adds no copula or
subject and does not change the original ascent output. Historical parallels
only distinguish possible concepts; no Latin initials, sound key or word match.

The independent validator reconstructs selection and contexts from guarded
sources using a separately implemented P-stream partition. It checks all raw
groups, separators, alternatives, literal consumption, synthetic hit controls
and damaged-output cases. A PASS concerns reproduction, not either word sense.
No new scored semantic relation or image-owner edge is offered to GDT388.
