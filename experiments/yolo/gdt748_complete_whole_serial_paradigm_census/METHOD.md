# GDT748 method

## Question

Can written runs of three or four complete Voynich groups predict the
quality/stage role of exactly one open group, and what additional condition is
needed before that local prediction is useful as a whole-role hypothesis?

## Inputs

- GDT734's 32,339-cell compact cache on 179 already allowed pages;
- guarded ZL3b token order and the ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b agreement control;
- GDT743's 202 occurrence-specific complete-whole patches;
- GDT747's twelve held whole-card hypotheses;
- GDT739/GDT745's fixed HOT, COLD, DRY, MOIST and stage extractors.

No image, OCR, new page, f84 or f84r is opened.

## Complete-whole card hierarchy

A known card is either a safe GDT743 occurrence whole or a clean GDT734 W2/W3
whole with zero compositional/component credit and no retired literal patient.
A target is every other complete space-delimited group. No EVA substring,
initial, suffix, phonetic value or Latin-letter reading is used.

For every consecutive length-three and length-four window, retain cases with
exactly one target and at least two distinct known whole surfaces. The serial
prediction is only the intersection of the known cards' quality/stage axes.
Overlapping windows predicting the same physical target position collapse to
one evidence unit.

## Why serial context alone is not exported

The same rule is run leave-one-out over windows whose cards are all known. Each
known target is hidden and predicted from the remaining complete wholes. The
unconditioned rule is kept as an explicit failure/calibration layer; it cannot
issue a word value.

The productive condition is a whole-form bridge: the target must be at
Levenshtein distance one or two from at least one of the known whole surfaces
in its written frame. This compares complete surfaces only. It does not assign
a meaning to the edited position or any component.

Bridge evidence is weighted only for resolving repeated-surface ties:

- two or more known wholes within distance two: weight 3;
- one known whole at distance one: weight 2;
- one known whole at distance two: weight 1.

A recurrent consensus axis needs at least two physical target positions as
well as 60 percent of bridge weight. Thus one strong frame cannot manufacture
a repeated whole value. Forms of length one or two retain a control/function
rival and cannot receive recurrent semantic status.

## Decisions

- S3: at least three positions on at least two pages, weighted consensus, no
  within-dimension opposition;
- S2: at least two positions, weighted consensus, no opposition;
- S1: one position, conflicting recurrent evidence, or a short control rival;
- S0: mixed predictions without a repeat-supported axis.

An S2/S3 surface may reinforce a weak card, replace a retired literal only with
an axis role, or supply a new open-whole role lead. It does not become a
decoded lexeme. `qochey` is handled specially only in the report, not in the
builder: its strongest multi-bridge frame is retained while the full
three-position conflict prevents a global consensus.

## Claim ceiling

GDT748 may report written whole-series frames, complete-whole edit bridges,
leave-one-out calibration, recurrent quality/stage role leads, and explicit
conflicts. It identifies no plaintext, language, sound, EVA character or
component value, plant, ingredient, liquid, disease, cure, person, vessel,
unit, or unseen form.
