The proposed kernel is general standing/capacity, not royal office. Every meaning below is an invented C0 assumption. Language and encipherment stay unknown. Actual licenses are `d+aiin`, `qo+d+aiin`, `qo+t+aiin`, `or+aiin`, `ol+aiin`, `ch+ol`, `sho+d+aiin`, and `t+chedy`. aiin is standing; d is HOLD; qo is predicate negation; t is future; sho is past; chedy is rise. Bare aiin used predicatively takes an explicitly paid zero HOLD. or always refers to the block's salient bearer T; ol is another A, distinct from T. ch makes that other definite. Possessive oraiin/olaiin do not switch the subject. No unlicensed spelling or substring receives these senses.

The three complete actual contexts are:

- N.2 `sain or or aiin opchdy`: “As for that bearer, that bearer holds standing now.” Both or groups have precisely the same NP sense: sain consumes the first as topic, the second is subject. No speaker or lexical copula is supplied.
- N.4 `dair sheo oraiin chol daiin`: “Earlier, concerning that bearer's standing, the other bearer held standing.” dair scopes the clause; sheo consumes the possessive NP; chol is the distinct subject. Earlier holding makes no present-loss claim.
- S.16 `oteey qodaiin odain an chey`: “Now that bearer does not hold standing. That bearer waits and remains.” The written subject anchor is or at S.15 G004, retained as a nominated carrier; the rest of that line is unresolved in stage1. The omitted subjects and boundaries are paid. odain is an indivisible WAIT, not a repaired dain form.
