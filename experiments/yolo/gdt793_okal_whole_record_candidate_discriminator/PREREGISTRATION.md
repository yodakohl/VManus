# GDT793 preregistration — `okal` whole-record candidate discriminator

## Fixed question

On the thirty already released pages, which candidate makes a distinct
whole-record or whole-page prediction for the complete written form `okal`
and its mechanically defined prefix family `^okal.*$`?

The candidates are:

1. page-local address or reference;
2. unique addressed member or learned name;
3. strict number or ordinal;
4. visible quality grade;
5. upper-zone marker;
6. class, slot or system-entry code;
7. opaque productive renderer.

## Fixed material and masks

- Use only the GDT791 thirty-page occurrence and line spine, the already
  published local visual inventories, and the GDT792 `okal` correction.
- Do not open a new page or image.
- Mixed alternate-reader rows are acquired only through `vmanus-exp

  and `f84r` before row materialisation.
- The prefix family is literal and mechanical: the complete ZL3b surface

  other substring.
- Every owner-fingerprint comparison masks every `^okal.*$` whole before
  scoring. A target may not prove its own address by being repeated.

## Candidate-specific gates

### A — page-local address/reference

The alleged source owner must be recovered after the target family is masked
in at least two of the three informative whole-record cases: f72 ring E to
ring D, f82 P1 to the lower communal panel, and f82 P2 to that panel.

### B — unique member/name

Every maximum exact complete-whole label/prose matching must force the same
single local member in every informative case. Two different local members
with the same complete label are counterexamples.

### C — strict number/ordinal

Complete forms must admit one acyclic order on at least two multi-member
arrays and then predict the f82 top-row order. Reusing A before B and B before
A in the same visually ordered array is a decisive failure.

### D — visible quality grade

At least two independent arrays must supply an objectively annotated visible
quality and the same complete-form ordering. Missing visual traits make this
candidate untestable, not true.

### E — upper-zone marker

This is an explicitly post-exposure sensitivity lead. Report exact `okal`
positions over clock windows of ±1, ±1.5, ±2, ±2.5 and ±3 hours around 12,
plus the held f82 row. It cannot become a selected plaintext value in GDT793.

### F — class/slot/system-entry code

The family must show at least two same-array complete-form collisions on
distinct members and continue into a second visible topology. It does not
require a page-local label for every running use. Passing this gate permits a
C0 complete-whole working renderer only if address, unique-member and strict
ordinal gates fail.

### G — opaque renderer

This null remains alive unless an independently visible trait or an
owner-recovering companion fingerprint distinguishes the class/slot reading.

## Claim ceiling

GDT793 may choose a replaceable C0 complete-whole working renderer for exact
`okal` on the released thirty-page spine. It cannot confirm a word, plaintext,
language, sound, root, affix, number, direction, object, substance, person,
calendar value, disease or unseen-page meaning.
