are replaced by their difference modulo22. The public recipe reset makes
cumulative addition its unique inverse. Spaces retain whole source words and
do not reset the position. Since changing output glyph names bijectively
preserves all lengths and equality relations, every tested statistic is
invariant under a different22-glyph naming. This argument does not cover a
different source ring order, source projection, variable-length carrier or
context-sensitive grouping; none is fitted or repaired here.

preregistration and implementations before source measurement. Independent
