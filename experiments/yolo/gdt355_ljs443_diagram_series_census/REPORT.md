# GDT355 report: LJS 443 diagram-series census

## Result

**`EIGHT_BAND_SUBFAMILY_RECURRENT_RENDERING_NOT_EIGHT_SPECIFIC`**

The complete official 38-surface diagram run sharpens GDT354 in both
directions.

- The exact narrow spiral-band rendering is a real local subseries: three
  adjacent pages, all with eight curved text-bearing compartments.
- The broader eight-curved-compartment family contains five pages after adding
  two decorated petal-like predecessors.
- Curved compartments are not an eight-value key. A nearby page has twelve
  curved, text-bearing lobes around a central circle.
- Four other surfaces have a clear eightfold primary division rendered as
  straight sectors or interlaced circles.

Thus the f68v3 resemblance is not an isolated doodle, but neither is it a
specific semantic match. LJS 443 demonstrates a local design vocabulary in
which eightfold systems and curved radial compartments recur, while the same
general lobe idiom also carries a different cardinality.

## Consequence for the route

Retain LJS 443 as a strong period-appropriate diagram-family comparator. Demote
the inference “curved spiral = eight phases” unless a scholarly source keys the
exact folios. The next useful acquisition is a folio-specific edition or
catalogue that identifies the values and order of the 206v–210r sequence.

GDT355 does not authorize Voynich text exposure or slot alignment. The target
still lacks a fixed phase and an independent homologous folio.

## Provenance and limitations

All 38 observations are direct visual observations of official Penn images.
They are one local manuscript series, not 38 independent cultural witnesses.
The candidate was already known before this complete census, so the result is
descriptive and post-GDT354. The validator checks inventory integrity and
arithmetic, not the visual judgments themselves.

No Voynich image, source group, transcription, formal tuple, or f84 material
was accessed.

## Claim ceiling

This establishes only a recurrent external eight-curved-compartment subfamily
and a direct non-eight curved-lobe counterexample. It does not establish a
lunar phase table, slot meaning, start, direction, copying, Armenian origin,
language, plaintext, or translation.
