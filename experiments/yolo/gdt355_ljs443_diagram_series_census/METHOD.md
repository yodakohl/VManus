# GDT355 method: complete LJS 443 diagram-series census

Date: 2026-08-19

Status: `POST_GDT354_EXTERNAL_SERIES_DESCRIPTIVE_CENSUS`

## Question

Within the complete diagram run identified by the official OPenn metadata for
LJS 443, is the eight-curved-band rendering seen in GDT354 a coherent local
subfamily, and is the curved-compartment idiom itself specific to eight?

## Design and chronology

This is an external-only descriptive follow-up after GDT354 exposed the three
headline pages. It is not a preregistered test and is not an independent
manuscript replication. The population is every surface explicitly identified
as a diagram in the OPenn TEI between current folios 192r and 211v: 38 official
facsimile surfaces. The two intervening prose-only versos are outside that TEI
diagram set.

The census records only directly visible geometry:

- broad topology;
- whether a central circle is present;
- a compartment count only when the author-visible primary division is clear;
- whether the primary compartments are curved lobes;
- whether text occurs in those compartments;
- whether repeated crescent-like or ringed marks are visible; and
- membership in a broad eight-curved-compartment family and the narrower
  three-page spiral-band rendering from GDT354.

`UNKNOWN` is retained whenever a primary compartment count is not visually
secure. The census does not read Armenian, infer diagram semantics, or assign
slot values.

## Evidence policy

All 38 observations are `AI_DIRECT_EXTERNAL_VISUAL_OBSERVATION` from exact
official OPenn JPEGs. Images are not copied into the repository; their official
URLs, folio mappings, and SHA-256 digests are recorded. No OCR, image
similarity, object detection, automated captioning, or batch visual
classification was used.

No Voynich image, transcription, formal payload, or target row is opened in
GDT355. GDT354 remains the only source of the bounded f68v3 comparison. f84 is
forbidden.

## Fixed descriptive contrasts

1. Narrow spiral-band pages: the exact rendering used in GDT354.
2. Broad eight-curved pages: any central-circle wheel with eight curved,
   text-bearing compartments.
3. Non-eight curved counterexamples: the same broad curved-compartment idiom
   with a securely different cardinality.
4. Other exact-eight topologies: eightfold systems rendered without curved
   lobes.

These contrasts refine source-family specificity only. They do not authorize
Voynich text alignment.

## Reproducibility

The producer writes the fixed source and observation tables and derives all
counts mechanically. The nonimporting validator independently checks the
complete 38-surface mapping, byte digests, family membership, count arithmetic,
result bindings, and f84 exclusion. It does not claim an independent second
visual review or re-fetch remote bytes.

## Claim ceiling

At most GDT355 can establish that LJS 443 contains a recurrent local
eight-curved-compartment subfamily while the broader curved-lobe rendering is
not unique to eight. It cannot identify any diagram as a lunar phase table,
assign a slot value/start/direction, establish copying or an Armenian
connection, or infer a word, language, plaintext, or translation.
