# GDT729 method

## Question

Can the fourteen forms named in the current route receive one useful German
default each—cardinal amount, indexed-share amount, open value or quality
grade—without changing confidence/evidence or exporting meanings from their
internal letter sequences?

## Inputs

- V99R2's complete 1,586-reading confidence/evidence dictionary;
- GDT627's head-role atlas and explicit warning that `cth/chor+a+value` does
  not yet select amount, strength, size, maturity or class;
- GDT661/GDT663/GDT665 exact-whole family cards for `tdain`, `olain`,
  `olaiin`, `qoraiin`, `solaiin` and `arain`;
- GDT686's head-dependent D-value rule;
- GDT693/GDT724's distinction between indexed material `R/AR` and divided
  portion `OR`;
- the five GDT728 historical quantity-convention comparators, all retained at
  `H0_NONE` and zero Voynich relation credit.

No page, image, OCR, transcription, f84 or f84r source is opened.

## Method

1. Freeze the exact fourteen reading IDs in
   `src/V99R3_14_QUANTITY_DISPATCH_SPECS.tsv`; targets are never selected by a
   regex or by scanning for punctuation.
2. Preserve both inner indices in `arain`: `AR` is Anteil I while the outer
   amount is rendered as two parts.
3. Cardinalize only a learned material/portion/action whole whose inherited
   evidence already supplies a quantity reading: `olain`, `olaiin`, `oraiin`,
   `qoraiin`, `solaiin`.
4. Where the head is a plant part but the material axis remains unresolved,
   keep a concrete material default and speak only `Wertstufe N`:
   `chorain/choraiin`, `cthan/cthain/cthaiin`.
5. Keep naked `dan/daiiin` as open value levels because no visible outer head
   globally selects grade, amount or class. Let the visible cold-quality head
   in exact `tdain` select `Kältegrad II`.
6. Change only the two semantic fields and seven audit/lineage fields on the
   fourteen target rows. Preserve every score, confidence level, evidence
   field, scope, export permission, structural/action flag and source-reading
   identity.
7. Keep all 324 active V99 readings, all 1,248 non-target global readings, all
   sixty GDT728 targets, seven explicit scope controls and five active reader
   artifacts byte-identical.
8. Validate row hashes, exact provenance append, class/occurrence totals,
   historical H0 status and zero component/relation credit independently.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The practical main channel must contain exactly one default and no slash
ambiguity. A live rival belongs in the audit apparatus, not in the translated
phrase. This is an exploratory whole-reading renderer revision. It does not
prove plaintext, language, phonetics, historical unit, ingredient identity,
free `a/ain/aiin/ar/or/d` semantics or a universal numeral rule. Context-
licensed readings remain context-licensed and receive no new export right.
