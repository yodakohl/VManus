# V25 selection — repetition is construction grammar

Date: 2026-08-22

Status: **selected speculative construction rule, not decoded numerals**.

## Result

Exactly 15 prose loci repeat an exact card inside the same physical unit. Every
case is now read as a complete construction rather than duplicated English.

The useful rule is typed:

```text
ACTION ACTION       perform the action twice
ITEM ITEM           instantiate two portions/items
RELATION ... RELATION
                    attach two operands under the same active relation
POINTER ... POINTER preserve a two-slot reference frame
```

Thus repetition is not a single universal grammatical morpheme. The class of
the repeated card determines whether it counts actions, arguments or slots.

## Strongest action cases

- f81v.18 `qokedy qokedy`: rinse the indicated place twice.
- f81v.21 `lshedy lshedy`: wash twice, closing each pass.
- f82r.27 two separately closed `qokeedy` cells: perform two warm immersions.
- f83r.20 `qokedy ... first-rinse ... qokedy`: first rinse, then second rinse.
- f83r.27 `shckhedy shckhedy`: strain twice through cloth.

`LSHEDY` previously meant “wash twice” at the card level and then appeared
twice, accidentally producing four washes. V25 corrects the base card to
**wash once; close this pass**. Exact repetition supplies the count of two.

## Argument and relation cases

- f10r.6 uses three current-portion cards around one measure instruction:
  prepare three portions under the stated measure.
- f10r.9 repeats both prepared-liquid and present-portion cards: combine two
  portions of decoction and preserve the selected one.
- f55v.5 repeats the measure card: make two measured additions.
- f82r.19 repeats the add-portion card: add two measured portions from the same
  batch.
- f83r.3 repeats relation and current-portion cards: fill two parallel
  application slots.
- f83r.14 repeats the pictured-place card: apply at two depicted sites.

Repeated L/O-like relation cards on f10r.8, f81v.2 and f81v.7 are not read as
“with with.” They reopen the same active relation for a second operand.

## Consequence

This is the first explicit meaning-bearing construction above one exact card in
the complete sidequest translation. It is simple enough for the hypothesized
workshop: copy the card again to duplicate its active typed slot. No separate
written numeral is required for two; larger counts can be written by additional
instances.

`V25_REPEAT_CONSTRUCTION_READINGS.tsv` contains every one of the 15 cases.
`V25_COMPLETE_199_LOCUS_TRANSLATION.tsv` applies the constructional reading
while retaining all literal event glosses. The complete dictionary remains 569
entries and the ledger 776 events.

This does not prove plurality, a numeral system or a source-language rule.
Dittography remains a rival in individual cases, but it explains the staged
first/second rinse and the repeated closed action cells poorly. f84 and f84r
remained sealed.
