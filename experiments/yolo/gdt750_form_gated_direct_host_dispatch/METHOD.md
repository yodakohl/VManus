# GDT750 method

## Question

Can an independently supported complete-form prior make direct whole-card
neighbourhood useful at individual occurrences after GDT749 rejected global
distribution as a semantic dispatcher?

## Inputs

GDT750 imports the guarded token and current complete-cell cache through
GDT749, the forty-six known whole cards and their quality/stage axes, GDT749's
fixed seventeen targets and exact discovery coordinates, and GDT740's
attachment result.  The loader applies the inherited page allow-list before
materialization and requires exact agreement among the three alternate readers
for every scored target or host occurrence.

Only seven working axes are in scope: `HOT`, `COLD`, `DRY`, `MOIST`,
`BEGIN_STAGE`, `MIDDLE_STAGE` and `END_STAGE`.  They are structural renderer
tags extracted from already licensed complete-whole cards, not recovered words.

## Complete-form prior

For every target surface and maximum edit distance, the builder removes the
surface itself from the forty-six-reference deck, finds the remaining complete
surfaces within that Levenshtein distance and counts their axes.  It treats
thermal, moisture and stage as separate dimensions.  A dimension contributes
an axis only when one axis is the unique winner and at least two different
reference wholes support it.  Ties and singleton support stay open.

This is a relationship among complete surfaces.  No EVA character or substring
receives semantic credit.  In particular, a one-edit relationship does not say
which glyph caused the role similarity.

## Occurrence host dispatch

At each occurrence the builder inspects the nearest permitted ring.  A host is
usable only when:

- all three alternate readings agree on its complete surface;
- its current cache card is known, confidence W2/W3, non-compositional and has
  zero component export;
- the card contains at least one of the seven quality/stage axes and no retired
  literal patient;
- it is not a `CLOSE` card;
- target and host complete surfaces differ by exactly one edit;
- the host axis is admitted by the target's independent distance-one prior.

Within each dimension, exactly one surviving axis may be emitted.  Conflicting
flanks leave that dimension silent.  The primary renderer is radius one.  A
target card is issued only outside all GDT748 discovery coordinates.

## Calibration and alternatives

The same dispatcher is applied to 1,134 real occurrences of known whole cards,
while each target card is removed from its own form prior.  Axis precision is
`TP / (TP + FP)` and recall is `TP / (TP + FN)`, counted per axis rather than
per token.  Six variants preserve the rejected raw rule, the active narrow rule
and the wider search sensitivities in one table.

Radius two is not activated even when its calibration is clean: GDT740 already
demonstrated that distance alone does not establish ownership.  Edit distance
two remains sensitivity-only because it introduces contradictions.

## Decision and claim ceiling

Only the distance-one, radius-one, non-`CLOSE`, multi-reference form-gated rule
may emit an occurrence card.  It may render a local condition such as
"heißer Zustand an der End-/Vollstufe".  It may not globalize that phrase to
every occurrence of the surface or claim the surface literally means German
"heiß", "Ende" or any historical lexeme.  All ingredient, disease, cure,
person, vessel, unit, language, sound, abbreviation, component and plaintext
identities remain open.

## Post-result route diagnostic

After the primary result, a separate deterministic diagnostic asks which
successor is worth opening.  It is navigation, not part of the active rule's
calibration.  It applies the active quality/stage rule to all reader-exact
recurrent open surfaces, tries an explicitly broader thirteen-axis host rule on
the known deck, and inventories complete `qX`/`X` pairs plus their raw line
positions.  The diagnostic may close an unproductive successor or nominate a
bounded audit; it may not establish `q` or any other EVA character as a prefix,
morpheme, sound or word.
