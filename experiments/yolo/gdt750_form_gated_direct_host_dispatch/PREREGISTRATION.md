# GDT750 registration record

## Timing and purpose

GDT750 follows GDT749's decision that global distribution ranks are too noisy
to speak a role at every occurrence.  The rule family was designed and tested
inside this exploratory pass.  This record therefore fixes the implemented
comparison and claim boundary for reproducibility; it is not presented as an
independent unseen confirmation.

## Fixed question

Can a target occurrence inherit a quality or stage axis from an immediately
adjacent, already licensed complete-whole card when the target's own complete
surface has the same axis independently supported by multiple edit-distance-one
reference wholes?

## Fixed material

- the forty-six known complete-whole reference cards inherited through GDT749;
- their 1,134 reader-exact occurrences carrying at least one quality/stage axis;
- GDT749's fixed sixteen recurrent forms plus the conflicted `qochey` card;
- all 1,684 cached occurrences of those seventeen forms, with the 57 GDT748
  discovery positions still separated and only 1,311 reader-exact outside
  positions eligible for a new card;
- the inherited 179-page guarded allow-list.  No page, image or transcription
  is added, and `f84`/`f84r` remain forbidden.

## Compared rules

Six deterministic variants are retained in the result:

1. raw immediate host;
2. immediate host after excluding a `CLOSE` card;
3. distance-one complete-form prior plus immediate distance-one host;
4. the same form gate with radius two;
5. distance-two form/host sensitivity at radius one;
6. distance-two form/host sensitivity at radius two.

For a form prior, each known reference whole votes only for its recorded
quality/stage axes.  In each of THERMAL, MOISTURE and STAGE, an axis is admitted
only when it is the unique winner and is supported by at least two reference
wholes.  During known-whole calibration the target reference whole is omitted
from its own prior.

The active occurrence rule is variant 3 only if it introduces no false axis in
the 1,134-occurrence calibration.  A card then requires reader agreement, an
outside-discovery target, an immediate non-`CLOSE` host, complete-surface edit
distance one between target and host, agreement with the multi-reference form
prior, and no within-dimension conflict.  Radius two is retained only as a
search channel because GDT740 showed that proximity is not attachment.

## Outputs and ceiling

The primary output is an occurrence-specific axis card, never a global word
meaning.  German text renders the selected working axes only.  No result may
identify a language, sound, abbreviation, EVA component, substring, lexeme,
literal ingredient, plant, disease, cure, person, vessel, unit or plaintext.
No component or unseen-form value may be exported.
