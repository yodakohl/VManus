# Sidequest Pass 826: SOLK means “sammeln”

The old noun SAMMELSTELLE fit the visible basins, but every fluent translation
had to insert an unattested verb: “an der Sammelstelle halten”. The cards become
directly compositional if SOLK is an operation:

> `SOLK = SAMMELN`

The complete five-form grid is now:

- `SOLK+Y`: dies sammeln;
- `SOLK+E+Y`: kurz sammeln;
- `SOLK+EE+Y`: länger sammeln;
- `SOLK+AIIN`: bis zum Sollmaß sammeln;
- `SOLK+EE+DY`: länger sammeln; Schluss.

This covers five exact cards, seven events, and seven statements without a
hidden HALTEN. AUFFANGEN is a close rival but assumes incoming flow in every
case; SAMMELN also allows accumulation at a work site. BEHÄLTER and BECKEN are
owner nouns, not operations, and recreate the missing-verb problem.

The place ladder is consequently smaller and cleaner: AR=QUELLE, CKH=DURCHLASS,
AL=ZIELSTELLE. SOLK moves into the operation inventory as SAMMELN.
