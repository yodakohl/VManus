## A hand-checkable proof, with source support

Let C be a freely uniquely decodable code and M=C*. If four strings x,xy,yz,z
are in M, then y is also in M. The two concatenations x·yz and xy·z are identical.
Unique code-entry lists must align both cuts, so their intervening whole entries
spell y. If y is a single working unit, it must itself be a code entry.
One-sided cancellation would be invalid: {a,ab} is UD but does not contain b.

For RF1b GE2, the complete forms qo, qop, par and ar witness the obligation p:

    qo · par = qop · ar

respectively. This equation is an abstract concatenation identity, NOT a claim
that those groups are adjacent in the manuscript, a translation or a grammatical
phrase. p is a transcription label, not the sound /p/.

## What the countercodes establish

If no whole word ends in h, split each word into blocks h^k a, where a is not h.
These blocks are prefix-free and contain no singleton h. If no word begins h,
the reversed blocks a h^k give a suffix-free table. Retain all used blocks and
verify every original word has exactly one parse. These are actual finite
covering codes, not just failure to find a proof.

For example, IT2a FULL can omit n with25used entries; all22528groups parse,
and3855groups actually use a compound. ZL3b FULL can omit m with26entries or n
with24entries in two DIFFERENT tables. Old cfh/cph27entry and q32/33entry
possibilities reappear and are not credited as new historical writers.

Every GE2 sign is either forced or has such a countercode. This proves the
EXACT intersection of singleton membership over all admissible codebooks on
that panel. It does not prove one code can omit all nonforced signs together.
FULL retains the one unresolved i case; it is neither certified mandatory nor
certified optional. Closure completion alone never proves all logical
consequences have been found. Table sizes are witness sizes, not minima.

## Assumptions and research decision

The22working units and strict whole-group boundaries are supplied. A code may
group them but cannot split inside an existing unit such as ch or ckh. Every
source entry has one fixed nonempty spelling; all finite entry sequences must
be uniquely decodable. Words do not overlap, omit signs or cross visible spaces.
No codebook-size cap, sound labels, source language or meanings are inferred.
Grammar-assisted ambiguity, optional spellings and contextual/overlapping or
otherwise different writing rules remain outside this contract.

Within this model class, a joint phonetic/segmentation search cannot hide the
forced signs exclusively inside longer codes. This is a conditional restriction
on proposed writers, not evidence that the manuscript uses this class. The
image-only1352uncertainty and1351representation counterexample are not repaired:
working inside a specific code contract gives conditional units, not a physically
identified alphabet. No new decoder, key, dictionary or word reading selected.
Retain892capacity stop,906fixed-model failure,1235bounds, its later<=22exclusion,
1248prefix minimum and1267source bridge. No language is excluded by this report.
