# GDT745 method

## Question

When GDT744's 41 unresolved exact content-slot surfaces are followed through
all already cached occurrences, do they retain a descriptive lemma,
ingredient/process-complement, quantity/part or other concrete whole-form role?

## Inputs and boundary

The experiment uses only the inherited 179-page allow-list, the guarded ZL3b
token/cross-reader cache, GDT734's compact cell register and complete confidence
dictionary, GDT739's axis rules/windows, GDT743's 202 target positions and
GDT744's candidate cells, channel rules and whole-role supplements. It opens no
new page, image or transcription. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

EVA strings are compared as complete modern transcription surfaces. An edit
relation does not make an EVA character a sound, abbreviation or morpheme.

## Pass 1 — exact recurrence census

The 41 surfaces are selected only by exact whole identity in the safe GDT734
cell register. Every occurrence records page, locus, ordinal, section, line
position and alternate-reader exactness. This yields 136 positions on 53 pages;
123 positions are exact across ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b.

For each occurrence a radius-five context is clipped at line edge, another
enumerated GDT738 target, a strict opaque initial head and direction-aware
CLOSE, exactly as in GDT744. A strong anchor must be reader-exact, known,
W2/W3, composition-free, component-free and free of the retired literal
powder/seed/root/wood patients.

## Pass 2 — inherited field membership

All 202 GDT744 fields are rebuilt and searched for the 41 exact wholes. This
recovers the 42 seed cells and two additional memberships. Field membership is
kept distinct from a word meaning: a surface may occupy a content cell without
itself being a lemma or ingredient.

## Pass 3 — clean whole-neighbor analogy

The GDT734 dictionary is reduced to W2/W3 complete-whole readings with zero
composition credit, zero component export, no HOLD decision and no retired
literal powder/seed/root/wood patient. Multiple readings of one known surface
contribute only their shared semantic tags. The resulting pool contains 770
readings over 769 known wholes.

For each candidate, exact Levenshtein neighbors at distance one or two are
enumerated. If at least two closest neighbors exist, only that distance layer
is used; a single distance-one neighbor is supplemented by distance two.
Every known surface votes once with its shared tags. A candidate axis requires
at least two known wholes and support from at least 60% of the selected deck.
VALUE alone never promotes. The additional stage tags BEGIN, MIDDLE, END,
LEVEL_II and LEVEL_III come from explicit words in the inherited German whole
cards. `koch/ausgekocht` is treated as HOT.

The analogy tiers are:

- A3: multiple agreeing distance-one wholes;
- A2: distance-one plus radius-two consensus, or multiple distance-two wholes;
- A1: a single neighbor or a mixed neighborhood without consensus;
- A0: no clean known whole within distance two.

The 41 final wordings are manually consolidated in
`src/WORKING_DEFAULT_OVERRIDES.tsv`. They may combine consensus axes with the
nearest whole gloss while naming every material rival. They are complete-whole
working defaults only.

## Manual review and relation intake

Twenty ranked examples receive a manual assessment. The review explicitly
checks whether the local occurrence adds independent support; because all 136
centered contexts are OPEN, none is allowed to masquerade as such support.

One same-page candidate/known-whole analogy is passed through the executable
GDT388 packet checker. It is structurally valid but remains INVALID and not
score-ready because formal access, capacity, holdout and mobile-null gates are
not satisfied.

## Claim ceiling

GDT745 may assign replaceable complete-whole analogy defaults and functional
axes to these 41 observed surfaces. It identifies no plaintext, language,
sound, historical abbreviation, EVA character value, substring, component,
plant, ingredient, disease, cure, person, vessel, liquid, unit or unseen form.
Field roles and German working meanings are model labels, not translations.
