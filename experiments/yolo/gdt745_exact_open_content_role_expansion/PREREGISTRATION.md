# GDT745 exploratory working declaration

This is a throughput-oriented exploratory pass, not a blind or frozen test.

The starting question was whether the 41 GDT744 content-slot wholes would gain
stable roles from other exact cached occurrences. The first implementation
centered the GDT744 clipping/anchor grammar on each candidate occurrence. It
returned zero strong anchors and 136 OPEN contexts. That negative result is
retained rather than hidden.

The disclosed second route uses genuinely different evidence: edit-distance
relations between complete observed candidates and complete clean W2/W3 known
wholes. It does not split a candidate, assign letters or export a component.
Consensus requires two known wholes and 60% of the selected neighborhood;
single-neighbor and mixed cases remain separate low tiers.

The working output must include all 41 forms, confidence tier, positive
evidence, counterevidence and a default meaning, including explicitly mixed or
open defaults. No form may gain a literal plant/substance identity from analogy
alone.
