# GDT762 method

## Question

Can the three GDT761 candidates `ckhy`, `pcheey`, and `ol` be separated into a
specific moist medium, a preparation complement or record field, and a broad
carrier by extending the comparison from two discovery pairs to eleven
dry-side/moist-side whole-form pairs already present in the admitted cache?

The pair sides are working hypotheses, not decoded lexemes. In particular,
`DRY_SIDE` and `MOIST_SIDE` are analyst labels inherited from earlier
whole-form experiments; they do not assign meanings to EVA characters.

## Inputs

- GDT761's guarded cached context loader and target/candidate source decks;
- GDT760's 281 fused and separated amount-expression atlas;
- GDT736's 96-form training grid and GDT737's 273-form held-role bridge;
- GDT754/GDT737/GDT738 semantic quarantine inherited through GDT761;
- GDT755's 52-expression historical role bank, used only for category-sized
  comparisons;
- the fixed GDT762 source decks `STATE_PAIR_PRIORS.tsv`,
  `CANDIDATE_PRIORS.tsv`, and `ROLE_HYPOTHESES.tsv`.

No new Voynich page, image, transcription, or OCR output is opened. The loader
selects only the inherited allow-list before retaining row content and reports
4,137 selected rows, 98 forbidden rows skipped, and 1,150 non-allowed rows
skipped. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Procedure

1. Enumerate every reader-exact occurrence of `ckhy`, `pcheey`, and `ol` in
   the admitted cache. Clip exact whole neighbors at distance one and exactly
   distance two; line edges, non-exact readings, quarantined inherited
   compositions, state wholes, and candidate targets remain explicitly typed.
2. Count contacts with all eleven pair sides, both raw and normalized by the
   corresponding state exposure and right-neighbor opportunity count. Keep
   the nine outer-control strata separate from the two discovery strata.
3. Search fixed exact bigrams/trigrams and the gapped
   `sheo pcheey X daiin` frame. Written spans are counted once by locus and
   start position.
4. Compare `pcheey` with its same-body H1--H4 controls and with every H1 form
   from the GDT736 training and GDT737 held universes. A second null census
   tests all 1,571 quarantine-clean recurrent exact surfaces rather than only
   the selected H1 family.
5. Audit fused and separated CH/SH/O shell rivals so a spaced construction is
   not mistaken automatically for a word sequence with known syntax.
6. Join exact `ol` neighbors to GDT760's amount-expression positions. Preserve
   GDT760's positional rule: an amount at line start expects content to its
   right; a medial amount expects content to its left. Bilateral and opposite-
   side contacts remain unlicensed controls.
7. Compare each target role with nine attested historical categories
   (`siccum`, `misce`, `cola`, `temperando`, `preparatio`, `oleum`, `aqua`,
   `vinum`, and `vas distillatorium`). This is a role/ecology comparison only:
   EVA spelling receives zero Latin-match credit.
8. Repair a semantic-precedence error inherited from GDT761. Ninety-four
   GDT736 training wholes still received older GDT734 powder/seed/root/wood
   prose. GDT762 records both values and makes the later structural whole-form
   role override the retired head-noun mnemonic.

## Working decisions

- A candidate may become a specific water, wine, or oil identity only with an
  independent LIQUID/OIL/WATER/WINE or vessel/process signature, not merely a
  higher moist-side contact count.
- A complete `pcheey` predecessor pattern may license a local construction
  reading. The portable whole remains a record/form role unless an independent
  content relation distinguishes source, ingredient, or result.
- `ol` may become a quantity-bearing carrier when it repeatedly occupies the
  preferred content side of amount expressions. That does not by itself choose
  `Ansatz`, `Stoff`, or `Öl` as its identity.
- `ckhy` retains its learned nominal preparation/compositum prior unless a
  specific medium signature exceeds its small mixed direct-contact profile.

## Claim ceiling

GDT762 may revise three exact-whole working roles and license only enumerated
exact spans. German phrases are replaceable renderer hypotheses. It identifies
no confirmed lexeme, solvent, material identity, language, sound, glyph value,
EVA component, syntax, or plaintext clause and opens no new page.
