   form family or lacks an independent liquid, vessel, product, separation, or
   cooling signature.
2. Promote a `pcheey` construction only if all exact occurrences share the
   same directed moist-side predecessor pattern and same-body/full-H1 controls
   do not. Keep record field, dry source, result, and shell readings distinct.
3. Promote `ol` only to a quantity-bearing content/carrier role if multiple
   pages place it on GDT760's preferred content side. Do not infer oil from EVA
   spelling.
4. Retain `ckhy` as nominal preparation/compositum if the enlarged pair deck
   introduces a dry contact or fails to add an independent liquid signature.
5. Preserve every live rival and report discovery dependence explicitly.
