## The two strongest new contrasts

`ltaiin / oltaiin` is the cleanest material contrast:

```text
l + t + aiin       Holzdroge, kalt auf Stufe III
o + l + t + aiin   Holzdrogenansatz, kalt auf Stufe III
```

Only initial `o` changes; the cold degree remains fixed. This predicts that a
future exact `l/ol` sister pair should change preparation status rather than
temperature or quantity.

`ykcho / kchody` is the most useful grammar contrast:

```text
y + kcho       hieraus einen heiß-trockenen Ansatz bereiten
k + ch + o+dy  fertiggestellter heiß-trockener Ansatz
```

All four `ykcho` contexts tolerate the same anaphoric instruction and all four
`kchody` contexts tolerate the same nominal result. The choice is not merely
stylistic: f56r.6 changes from a nominal register to a mixed record and gains
one visible action, while f86v5.24 retains `kchody` as the result after the
earlier heating instruction.

## Quantity and reader-boundary gains

`aror = ar+or` holds six times as one portion of the first drug fraction. The
best internal comparison is f79r.17, where `air · or · aror` places second
fraction, portion and first-fraction portion in one visible sequence. At
f106r.23, `dar · aror` reads as measured fraction I followed by one portion of
that fraction. One alternate reader merges `aror` with the following word at
f104r.28; the other retains it exactly, so the boundary warning remains in the
occurrence artifact.

`losair` has two complete visible parses. ZL3b and IT2a write it unsplit, while
RF1b writes `los air`. Because V48 already has `los` = Drogenholzposten and
`air` = zweite Fraktion, the final default is **zweite Fraktion des
Drogenholzpostens**. The alternative `lo+sair` = **Holzabsud mit
Samenfraktion II** is compositionally and historically practical and remains
the explicit rival; it is not erased to make the line look certain.

`taiky` is deliberately narrower. The exact card reads **kalt angesetzte
Charge, leicht angewärmt**. Outer cold `t` and terminal light-hot `ky` remain
visible, but internal `ai` receives no new productive meaning. Its sole context
is now readable, yet the card remains the weakest of the nine.

miscellanies combine learned drug names, hot/cold/dry qualities, numbered
