## Decision

Keep the single compound trial C0. It is compatible with water contexts and
the known split/join air line while preserving its two components. There is
no independent identification of source, predicate, material names, or genre.

the repeated-predicate problem** at f77r.34, f81v.17 or f76r.19. Do not present
longer smooth German prose as progress where it would require deleting those
