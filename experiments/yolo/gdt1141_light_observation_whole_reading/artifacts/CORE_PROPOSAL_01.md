## Two composed contexts actually attempted

**Context A — f89v1.16 IT2a, complete nine groups.**

`qokaiin ykchol qockhy okalda otal dal chodar okaiin dalg`

Frozen C0 rendering: “Reference report of the Moon's received light; same-Moon frame; at greater Sun separation, with exposed lunar portion and its Earth-facing amount, this amount is related to an approaching-Sun condition; current report of Moon light; compare the reference and current reports for the same body.”

The intended source-compatible hypothesis is that the current Earth-facing amount is lower as the Sun approaches. The written amount/approach relation and the two report records make this a real, falsifiable claim. The final lower-than result is not forced by the word values: `dalg` returns a comparison whose result is still unknown unless the authored C0 binds `dal`'s amount-condition relation to its current record. That binding is an explicit **open grammar debt**, not a completed translation. I do not call `.16` a successful statement of “less”; the literal groups and their proposed values are frozen, while the output and link remain unresolved. No baseline was seeded from `.14` or from the Sun.

Source comparison: Morgan M.721 5r-C says greater separation brings more light shown above Earth, and approaching brings less; 5v-A adds a separate uncertain obstruction/eclipsing condition. M.721 is a normalized historical reading, not evidence that the Voynich copies it. This core uses only the distance-direction relation as an exploratory analogy and does not import phases, an Earth label, orbital numbers, or an eclipse rule.

Exposure account: I read the complete 48-line normalized source, all six eight-line stanzas (5r-A/B/C and 5v-A/B/C), including the final human-disposition stanza.
