# Method

## Inputs

- GDT416's 4,576 exact component recipes and owner-bound clauses;
- GDT413's unchanged 46-component working dictionary;
- GDT419's 52 unique missing one-atom recipes for a downstream gate.

No new page, image, or transcription is used.

## Construction

1. Keep exact recipes containing exactly one of the nine working action heads.
2. Inventory all such recipes, then isolate clean recipes with at most one
   grade, one argument, and one `DY` endpoint.
3. Fill every cell in the 9 heads × 4 grades × 5 arguments × 2 endpoints
   grid.
4. Mark a cell attested across registers, attested locally, an open combination
   gap, or blocked because its head has never licensed that grade, argument, or
   endpoint anywhere in the current deck.
5. Pass GDT419's missing forms through this stricter head gate.

"Blocked" is a working-deck prohibition, not proof that the manuscript could
never contain the form. It means a later occurrence may not be accepted as a
regular composition without revising the head card explicitly.
