# GDT686 method

## Question

Can every current `dain`, `daiin` and `qodaiin` value be assigned one concrete
degree or amount meaning from a visible local head without promoting either
axis globally or inventing an operation in practical prose?

## Inputs

- GDT626's complete minim-suffix occurrence ledger;
- GDT627's value-head atlas, fixed frames and direct plant-part contacts;
- GDT628's core-bearing OL quality and OR carrier value phrases;
- GDT630's separated naked-OL value expressions;
- GDT662's exact `qodaiin` card and alternate-reader audit;
- GDT671's current exact working glossary for neighbour descriptions;
- GDT684's position-level semantic-debt audit;
- GDT685's complete V58 reader, result and debt summary;
- eleven keyed V59 choices in `src/V59_VALUE_PATCH_SPECS.tsv`.

No page, image or raw transcription is opened. All evidence comes from
already admitted cached artifacts. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Method

1. Select every exact `dain`, `daiin` and `qodaiin` row from the GDT626
   occurrence ledger and retain its page, locus, ordinal, neighbours, register
   and reader stability.
2. Preserve the mechanical parses `d+a+II`, `d+a+III` and `qod+a+III`.
   Compare them with the complete `da` and `qoda` value families rather than
   treating the three surfaces as learned atomic words.
3. Join each bare-d target position by exact locus and ordinal to four mutually
   exclusive context classes, in priority order: core-bearing OL quality head,
   visible OR/part/material head, naked OL carrier, or outer head open.
4. Remove overlaps before counting. This yields 75 quality-grade positions,
   53 material/part amount positions, eleven naked-OL open-axis positions and
   775 d-values whose outer head is not immediately typed.
5. Audit all 41 `qodaiin` occurrences separately. Keep neighbour glosses and
   reader boundaries visible. Record the same-span rivals `qod | aiin` on
   f86v3.25 and `qo | daiin` on f95r2.1; neither split exports a global gloss.
6. Explode V58's 51 lines into exact token positions and locate its three
   `dain`, seven `daiin` and one `qodaiin` targets.
7. Give every current target one keyed working choice. A visible quality
   state selects grade; a visible material, fraction or measure head selects
   local amount. Direction and head are stored separately from the German
   rendering.
8. Rewrite the literal, aligned and practical layers together. A value card
   may supply level and axis but never `nehmen`, `abnehmen`, `verbinden` or any
   other operation.
9. Recompute strict, mechanical, broad and provenance-aware debt totals and
   preserve all 86 inherited action licenses.
10. Rebuild all generated artifacts byte-for-byte in a temporary directory
    and validate counts, partitions, changed positions, reader boundaries,
    hashes and sealed exclusions.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A global degree or amount gloss is rejected if the same surface occurs under
both directly written quality and material/part heads. The ordered value level
is portable. The semantic axis is portable only together with its visible or
keyed head.

V59 deliberately makes eleven exploratory local choices—four grade and seven
amount readings—so the current reader has one practical default at every
target. These are working defaults, not recovered plaintext. The experiment
does not identify an absolute unit, historical quality name, ingredient,
liquid, plant, disease, patient, cure, language, phonetics or codebook.
