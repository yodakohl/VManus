# Authored C0: a written head **of** the material

This is a new partial rival, not a repair or semantic reopening of AA. The
trial meaning is **OF**: s N H relates the actually following H to the written
N. schol H and schor H use this only for the two offered whole forms. The
thought is a genitive/source modifier, rather than a new proper portion.
Spaces remain real alternative evidence, not globally removable spelling.

The retained complete Anonis entry on native Lonitzer72r writes radicis
decoctum (root's decoction). Its exterior-use sentence writes decocta radix
(boiled root) instead. The relation and nominal head are different from the
material itself. This is a source construction analogy; it selects no Voynich
language, noun, plant or physical preparation. The following toy wording is
deliberately partial, with actual unknown heads rather than invented verbs.

The two N meanings (chor=HEAD-STOCK, chol=DRIED-DRUG-STOCK) are the old AA
**hypotheses only**. This does not transfer schor's image lead to chor or
validate a nominal chol. No bare occurrences are given individual identities
by spelling; H may denote another instance of the same material kind. Unlike
PORTION, this draft asserts no proper subset. Unlike ENTRY, it does not make
the marked item and following head the same reference. Its distinct concrete
obligation is to consume H, an actual written whole, at every application.

AA's whole277-group contexts and BM's whole301-group contexts remain linked
in the source dossier. The other three contexts were initially complete
physical lines only; GDT1104 retains their containing native whole paragraphs
before any full-scope decision. All additional literal s matches from the
fixed eight pages must be reported; this table cannot serve as a selected
success set. Untranslated continuations remain untranslated.

The amount/dose, noun/property and free-dy ambiguities can still destroy this
semantic proposal even if its written-argument capacity passes. A genitive
needs an actual interpreted relation; allocating adjacent strings cannot
confirm OF. Isolated s labels and unoffered compounds remain explicit limits
on any global export. SOURCE_HEAD is a C0 proposal, not a translated word.
