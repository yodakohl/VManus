# GDT659 method

## Question

Can the 270 currently unassigned, whitespace-delimited ZL3b `y` tokens be
given useful default readings without pretending that they are one global word
meaning *dieser*? In particular, does the reader alternation around
`y | rchey` at f80v.21 support the same entry-head architecture already visible
in GDT650's `y | cheol` family?

## Inputs

- GDT624 for bound final `-y` as a form edge inside its licensed quality shell,
  not a free suffix or word value;
- GDT650 for the line-initial `YCHOL/YCHEOL/YCHOR/YCHEOR` entry family and
  its split/fused reader witnesses;
- GDT658's frozen V35 dictionary, glossary, line coverage and one-hole deck;
- guarded ZL3b token and ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b line tables on the inherited explicit
  179-page allow-list; and
- a 38-context manual audit and six historical entry-marker analogies.

`f1r` is outside the allow-list. `f84` and `f84r` are forbidden explicitly.
Mixed transcription tables may be read only through `./vmanus-exp query-tsv`.

## Method

1. Rebuild every exact naked-`y` occurrence and classify its physical-line
   position, token type, neighbouring surfaces and three-reader boundaries.
2. Search the admitted ZL3b vocabulary for both visible fused sisters,
   `y+RIGHT` and `LEFT+y`. These are boundary evidence, not new words and not
   semantic proof.
3. Assign a structural role by a fixed hierarchy:
   label token; only token; line beginning; line end; then medial right-only,
   left-only, bidirectional or sisterless hinge. A medial right-bound instance
   before an already recognised materia/preparation head receives an additional
   local materia-reference subtype only when the mechanical class already
   attaches right. f80v.21 is the single narrow exception because IT2a itself
   joins `y+rchey` while the other readers preserve the boundary.
4. Render the roles in German only after the structural assignment:
   `Eintrag:`, `hierzu:`, punctuation for a left or final close,
   and a non-spoken label/divider note. The renderer never inserts the global
   lexical equation `Y = dieser`.
5. Replace every one of the 270 naked-`y` unknown positions in V35 coverage
   with its scoped V36 role, then rebuild complete passages, one-hole passages,
   the working glossary and the dictionary. All other V35 meanings remain
   byte-for-byte inherited.
6. Give f80v.21 a full practical reading and retain the narrower rival
   `zu derselben Droge` and `ferner` separately from the default `hierzu`.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Accept V36 if all 270 safe-corpus naked-`y` positions are replayable under the
fixed hierarchy; position-dependent fusion direction is recovered; all nine
single-token `y` lines remain label/divider cases; f80v.21 is classified as a
right-bound materia reference; and no non-`y` V35 position changes.

The result licenses scoped boundary roles and practical contextual renderings
only. It does not make `y` a confirmed lexeme, sound, pronoun or universal
deictic; it does not transfer the cards to bound terminal `-y`; and it does not
identify a plaintext language, cipher system, ingredient or disease. Historical
examples establish only that compact item/idem/entry and graphical divider
functions coexisted around 1420, never that EVA `y` is one of their glyphs.
