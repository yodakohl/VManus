- `Y_CONTEXT_CARDS.tsv`: eight scoped V36 cards, structural tags, renderings
  and live rivals.
- `Y_OCCURRENCE_CENSUS.tsv`: all 270 naked-`y` positions with neighbours,
  sister forms, role selection and both alternative-reader boundaries.
- `Y_CONTEXT_CLASS_SUMMARY.tsv`: mechanical and final card counts.
- `Y_DIMENSION_PROFILE.tsv`: section, language, hand, position and token-kind
  profiles.
- `Y_PAGE_COUNTS.tsv`: occurrence count on each of the 125 affected pages.
- `Y_READER_BOUNDARY_AUDIT.tsv`: IT2a/RF1b separate, right-, left-, both-bound
  and other outcomes for every position.
- `Y_READER_BOUNDARY_SUMMARY.tsv`: compact reader-by-position counts.
- `Y_NEIGHBOR_ATLAS.tsv`: left/right neighbours, V35 status and fused sisters.
- `Y_NEIGHBOR_SUMMARY.tsv`: aggregate neighbour and materia-head counts.
- `MANUAL_Y_CONTEXT_AUDIT.tsv`: 38 manually read contexts with deictic,
  entry and closure rivals plus replacement triggers.
- `HISTORICAL_ENTRY_MARKER_ANALOGIES.tsv`: six circa-1420 notation analogies
  and explicit limits.
- `Y_AFFECTED_LINE_TRANSLATIONS.tsv`: before/after rendering of all 257 lines.
- `F80V21_WORKING_TRANSLATION.tsv`: reader evidence and complete Wurzel passage.
- `NEWLY_COMPLETED_LINES.tsv`: the eight passages closed in V36.
- `NEWLY_EXPOSED_ONE_HOLE_LINES.tsv`: the 17 newly visible residuals, kept open
  without suffix or substring inheritance for the next experiment.
- `V36_WORKING_TOKEN_GLOSSARY.tsv`: the 495 inherited global surface rows;
  naked `y` stays occurrence-dispatched and is deliberately absent here.
- `WORKING_DICTIONARY_V36.tsv`: inherited V35 dictionary plus eight scoped
  naked-`y` entries.
- `ALL_LINE_CONCRETE_COVERAGE_V36.tsv`: complete guarded 4,128-line coverage.
- `COMPLETE_PASSAGES_V36.tsv`: all 146 complete multi-token passages.
- `ONE_UNKNOWN_PASSAGES_V36.tsv`: all 249 V36 one-hole passages.
- `ROUND_COVERAGE_COUNTS.tsv`: exact V35→V36 coverage change.
- `PAGE_ALLOWLIST.tsv`: inherited guarded 179-page allow-list.
- `RESULT.json`: machine-readable counts, guards, hashes and replay contract.
- `VALIDATION.json`: independent raw recensus and byte-identical replay.
