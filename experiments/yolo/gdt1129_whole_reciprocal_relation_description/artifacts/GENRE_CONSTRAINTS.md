## Complete counterexample: Md2's descriptive west-wind account

TübingenUB Md2, fifteenth-century Württemberg Hausbuch, [13v/canvas29](https://opendigi.ub.uni-tuebingen.de/opendigi/Md2/canvas/29) and [14r/canvas30](https://opendigi.ub.uni-tuebingen.de/opendigi/Md2/canvas/30). The owning complete source unit is the twelve-wind diagram plus the entire two-column prose, ending with knowledge/conduct/healing usefulness. The complete west paragraph at14r right upper/middle says: the third wind is west, from sunset; it has two companions; it is cold/moist by nature and always warm here; its passage through warm land explicitly explains the local warmth; it separately does not harm us.

Its minimum content is:

```text
one grammatical west-wind referent w
NaturalColdMoist(w)
Warm(land) + Traverse(w,land)
this passage through warm land explains LocalWarm(w,HERE)
AlwaysLocallyWarm(w,HERE)
NotHarm(w,US)
```

The source is descriptive and explanatory. It contains **dynamic traversal, a causal connection and a recipient-effect predicate**. A grammar treating “description” as excluding such predicates loses written content. Yet the text identifies no dated traversal event, unique air parcel, human performance instruction or before/after episode ledger. Generic relational propositions can preserve this content; a generic event schema can also preserve it if its scopes are paid and kept distinct from observed occurrences. The historical example therefore does not choose static graph encoding against event encoding.

It does constrain a claimant to this analogue: keep the same wind-description owner across intrinsic and local properties, keep land warmth separately owned, preserve the explicit explanation, and keep no-harm as a separate assertion. A new unreferenced wind, a single unqualified warmth slot, or a rule making warmth entail no harm changes the account. Same grammatical subject is not proof of one physically conserved parcel.

The whole-source context remains: four first winds and two companions with an explicit figure reference; all twelve diagram sectors/four triplets/four banners; uncertain crossed names; east hot/dry prose versus apparent moist/rain/growth orient inscription; south warm/moist travel with bodily harms particularly to sick people; north cold/dry owner and “one” companion attachment unresolved, with both benefit and harm; concluding useful knowledge, without a prescribed therapeutic algorithm or observed cure. These are not erased to make a favourable west example.

## Constraints for the finite grammar

- Preserve nominal designation, deictic/relational predication, generic dynamic relation, prescription and observed event as distinct contributions. A conversion needs an explicit rule and its cost.
- Union/conjunction must preserve every operand's arguments, polarity and scope. Returning a noun, new physical owner or action ID requires a declared construction; graph accumulation alone does not license it.
- Same subject/reference can preserve a description's owner without establishing one physical material token or unique episode. Spatial pairing is not a written identity assertion.
- A sequential prescription must retain actual participant references, temporal order and conditional/modal status. A descriptive encoding must retain any dynamic/cause/time predicates it claims to read.
- No genre rule requires prose to describe adjacent pictures literally. Neither source licenses target Latin words, anatomical names or implied sinks from drawing geometry.

These duties test a concrete complete authored account's claims. They do not impose medieval content on a target whose meaning remains unknown. The historical source does not mandate event IDs, disallow a relation graph, or confer nominal semantics on a union node.
