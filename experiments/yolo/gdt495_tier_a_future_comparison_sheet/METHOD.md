# GDT495 method

## Question

Can the 27 GDT494 Tier-A composed cells be published as a genuinely usable,
card-by-card comparison sheet in which every phrase, component, support head,
observed clause, old page and state warning remains visible?

## Inputs

- the fixed 27-card GDT494 Tier-A table;
- the complete GDT494 same-register non-T/R support-cell table;
- the complete GDT494 opposite-T/R support-cell table;
- the complete GDT494 same-action cross-register support-cell table.

## Method

1. Keep the GDT494 Tier-A order and assign `G495-F001` through `G495-F027`.
2. For each target cell, select every exact same-register non-T/R support cell,
   every available opposite-T/R cell and every same-action/frame cell in other
   registers.
3. Copy the working phrase, portable component trace, owner-local slot trace,
   evidence label and state warning without alteration.
4. Print a one-row machine card and a full readable card containing every
   support cell ID, action head, recipe, event count, observed German clauses
   and page list.
5. Print the union of old support pages and the literal guard
   `KEINE OBERFLÄCHENVORHERSAGE` on every card.
6. Preserve the three selected support subsets as exact row-for-row extracts
   with only future-card ID and priority rank prepended.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The sheet is complete only if all 27 Tier-A cells, all selected support rows
and every old page round-trip exactly; every card must retain
`COMPOSED_WORKING`, old slot values, its state warning and two explicit `NO`
prediction flags. This is a readable priority deck. It neither asserts that a
composed phrase was observed nor predicts a future manuscript surface or
occurrence. No root, meaning, frame, recipe, event or page is changed.
