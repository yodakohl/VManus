# Hundred-ninety-eighth pass: normalized H1, H2 and B1

## H1 and H2

The 38 f10r events now read as five open preparation statements rather than a
line-by-line herbal description.

- H1 makes the base preparation from the pictured plant: take the base part,
  prepare a portion in a receiving vessel, add working liquid, insert the next
  plant part and bring it to the prescribed measure.
- H1 then continues the first batch and holds it ready.
- H2 starts a following batch from that ready extract, chooses the next item and
  brings it to measure.
- H2 continues the same preparation, takes a prescribed amount from it and
  preserves the batch order.
- H2 runs the working preparation through the next stage and inserts the stated
  added measure.

The CH-frame fields F001, F004 and F005 are exactly the open build/preparation
mode predicted by the frame card.

## B1

B1 has 66 events in 24 fields and 21 statements. It is a basin/application
ledger, not a single prose paragraph. Its long second statement specifies the
main route: measure a charge, place it into the basin run, add portions, follow
the connection and target passages, set two measures, pass through and transfer.

The remaining cells are short operations: continue, transfer, settle, add a
portion, pass through, warm, wash, introduce and collect. Repeated singleton
`qokedy` cells mean the same short contact step repeated at separate positions.

O-mode fields F022, F030 and F041 carry continuing work in the active
preparation; Q-mode F038 selects an active target step. Their surfaces no longer
create spurious extra vocabulary after normalization.

## Current linked reading

H1 makes a base batch. H2 divides or renews it into measured following batches.
B1 applies those batches through the pictured shared basin field in short
closed cells. This is a cleaner WHAT→BATCH→APPLICATION relation than the former
repeated sentence preamble, while remaining compatible with a technical
bathhouse reading.
