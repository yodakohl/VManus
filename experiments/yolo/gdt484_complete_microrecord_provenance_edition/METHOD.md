# GDT484 method

## Question

Can every one of the 135 fixed GDT479 microrecords be printed with both a
concrete reading and a clear strongest support route, including the three
multi-event fragment tails omitted from GDT482's 45-event decomposition?

## Inputs

- GDT479: 135 definitive records and 183 definitive events;
- GDT480: strict whole-record templates and role shapes;
- GDT481: record fragment coverage and event templates;
- GDT482: complete component sequences, conditioned/model-free atlases, and
  45 single-event residual tiles;
- GDT483: the closed 45-event ledger and preferred `sodar` paraphrase.

## Strongest-support hierarchy

Each record receives the first applicable tier:

1. recurrent strict whole-record template;
2. recurrent whole-record role shape;
3. every event has a recurrent strict template;
4. at least one event has a recurrent strict template;
5. every event has a recurrent role shape;
6. at least one event has a recurrent role shape;
7. all remaining components recur under the same active model;
8. full component coverage needs marked model-free backoff;
9. an exact admitted running surface+recipe closes the event;
10. functional structure is complete and only a learned lexical slot remains.

The hierarchy is a navigation device. Lower rank does not mean untranslated;
every tier retains a concrete default.

## Three multi-event tails

GDT481 left three no-fragment records outside GDT482's single-event target:
`ochey|fydy`, `aral|oletal`, and `okchshy|qkol|oldam`. GDT484 applies the same
GDT482 length-one-to-three tiler to their seven events. A fragment must have a
donor event other than its target. All twenty semantic components recur under
the same active model, so all three enter tier 7.

## Reading policy and claim ceiling

All source readings remain byte-identical except record `G475-R125`, which
inherits GDT483's already published fluent `sodar` paraphrase. This
consolidation adds no root, component meaning, syntax, plaintext, language,
model, boundary, surface, recipe, event, or page.
