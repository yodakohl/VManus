## Interpretation and limits

M unparsed groups are displayed as residuals; they are not removed from the
stored panel. D head-free groups are necessarily parsable, so D/(N-M) describes
the identifiable content obligation in that hypothetical parsable subset. D/N
is separately stored as the original-panel forced-equal share. Neither fraction
provides readings for the M failures or for the other parsable groups. Allowing
further source/carrier constraints can only make realization harder.

The zero-residual forced-equal counts in1331numerically equal the old1236full
forced-singleton coverage counts. The new part was applying999's fixed BOTH-role
semantics and checking joint heads; it was not a new discovery of those word
frequencies.1236/1237/1262already distinguished rare-dependent formal exclusions
from human-error robustness. This experiment applies that distinction to999;
it neither repeats their measurements nor supersedes their fixed decisions.

Decision: retain1331with an explicit exact-panel premise and this discrepancy
frontier. Do not select a/ch/e as a key, discard residuals, call copying errors,
repair the role table, add heads or fit a source. No writer has been established
and no word translated. Any future error-aware writer needs its own independently
motivated error contract and source/carrier consequences; these fitted budgets
are not such a contract.
