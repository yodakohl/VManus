# GDT915: phrase-level terminal r/l co-variation

Decision, before new source query: GDT809 publishes `chor shor` at f17r.5 and
`chol shol` at f21r.11. Do two-position r/l variants recur as whole phrases,
and does their coupled ending transfer beyond leaf/stem/position margins?
GDT003 predicts dictionary cells,311 licensed single-form choice,419 inherited
recipe cells,345 next opaque states;914 required both edited pairs within ONE
four-group window. This test compares separate two-group occurrences. It does
not reopen generic adjacent edit-distance, allography, or old semantic glosses.
Primary predecessor review plus independent review found no exact duplicate.

The smallest test uses the unchanged179-selector atlas scope from851, split by
physical leaf number: odd discovery, even evaluation. All3 alternate readings
are retained; ZL3b alone nominates candidates. Whole-project prior exposure is
explicit: this is a new split for this test, not a historically untouched corpus.
The known seed examples are in discovery. f84/f84r remain forbidden. No images,
new admissions, decoder, lexicon, normalization or source rule changes.
Query each partition separately through the existing selector-first guard; do
not query even-leaf payload until discovery candidates and source/code hashes
are publicly frozen. Preserve whole source lines in compact per-reading caches.

An eligible token is a raw lowercase [a-z]+ group ending r or l, with nonempty
stem obtained by dropping that final character, in a P-kind source line. An
eligible pair consists of consecutive source indices on the same line with a
bilaterally DEFINITE_SPACE seam and DIFFERENT stems. No crossing a line, dropping
annotations, uncertain gaps or selecting only convenient source locations.

Discovery: enumerate every ordered stem pair. Nominate iff RR and LL both
occur and their union spans at least2 physical odd leaves. All RR,RL,LR,LL
occurrences and zeros retained. Freeze complete ZL3b candidate list before even
query; report alternate-reader discovery censuses without their own selection.
No semantic labels. No minimum number of candidates repaired after observation.
If zero candidates, stop before even query. Otherwise evaluate every nominated
pair in every eligible even-leaf occurrence, including mixed endings and zeros.

Primary statistic: for each even physical leaf having ANY eligible r/l pair,
sum +1 for a nominated same-ending pair, -1 for a nominated mixed-ending pair,
and0 for every other eligible pair; divide by ALL eligible pairs on that leaf.
T is the mean across these fixed evaluation leaves. Empty nomination gives T=0.
Report raw counts, per-candidate outcomes and leaf contributions as well.
At least5 nominated stem pairs,5 evaluation leaves with nominated occurrences,
and5 such leaves with a movable endpoint are required to call the comparison
adequately replicated; otherwise retain descriptive results/capacity limitation.

Conditional reference:1024 worlds, NumPy default_rng seeds915000+j for j0..1023.
Shuffle r/l endings among token positions within (physical leaf, exact stem,
physical position class FIRST/INTERNAL/LAST); a one-token line is SINGLE.
Only existing occurrences of each stem participate. Preserve all other source
fields and token order. One shared token receives ONE permuted ending even if
it occurs in two overlapping pairs. Recompute discovery nomination and even T
for EVERY world: control the full fixed r/l phrase search, not only lucky pairs.
Record movable strata/tokens/endpoints and all null scores/candidate counts.
This reference conditions on word stems, leaf and position; it does not control
all other lexical dependencies or the project's earlier search for operators.
Thus its tail rank is a conditional exploratory diagnostic, NOT a project-wide
significance claim or a semantic result. Alternate readings are not independent
replications; their evaluation uses the same real ZL3b nomination and no separate
best-reading selection. No pooled evidence count or combined p-value.

A strong replicated excess nominates phrase co-variation for a separate functional
contrast; absence/low capacity closes this fixed transfer test. Copying, formula
reuse and grammatical agreement remain distinct explanations even if it passes.
No noun, adjective, property or r/l meaning follows. No relation edge is scored.
Budget25min total including coding, query, verification and publication; stop
expansion at the checkpoint. Do not try different suffixes, partitions, word
lengths, pair distances or relaxed criteria within this experiment.
