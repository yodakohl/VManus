question is whether it extends to NEW stem combinations, rather than familiar
phrase families. That requires a separately fixed test; no new suffix, wider
window, extra decoder or meaning is inferred here. Copying/formula reuse and
linguistic agreement remain live explanations. Confirmed word meanings:0.
