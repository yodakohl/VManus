# GDT797 method

## Question

Does the complete source family `AQABBA` carry one portable status, or do its
two observed surfaces split into learned `okaldy` and `otaldy` entries whose
whole paragraphs and visible owners select different concrete domains?

## Inputs

The build joins the validated GDT795 source-family atlas to the lossless GDT791

## Method

The six primary exact-whole events are reconstructed from their canonical
sources. Running text is evaluated as a whole paragraph and local labels as a
visible-owner locus. The target strings are masked before candidate displays
are inserted.

For calibration, every single-token surface belonging to any of GDT795's
eleven recurrent boundary families is searched in the admitted occurrence
spine after all 101 source-register loci are removed. Repeated tokens inside
one paragraph collapse to one host unit. For each family the builder reports
surface-conditioned and family-pooled leave-one-host-out modal-domain credit.
The comparison tests learned complete surfaces, not substrings.

A separate branch sensitivity asks whether other `ok...` versus `ot...`
surfaces already show the same domain direction. This can support a formal
architecture rival but can never export `ok` or `ot` as a morpheme.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The fixed rule is in `PREREGISTRATION.md`. A successful split licenses only
enumerated C0 complete-whole displays. It may retire a conflicting inherited
working gloss on those positions. It cannot confirm a Voynich word, name,
plant, root, drug, figure, planet, facies, language, sound, cipher, plaintext
clause or reusable component.
