# GDT682 method

## Question

Can the seven genuinely new forms on V55's final open line receive concrete
component-based meanings which retain one practical core at every exact
admitted occurrence, close f105v.1 and produce a complete 51-line renderer
without generic filler or hidden reader repairs?

## Inputs

- GDT671's f84-free 4,128-line V48 panel;
- the published GDT673–GDT681 position overlays;
- GDT681's V55 reader and frozen result counts;
- seven manual card specifications, 17 keyed context-role verdicts, one source
  reading, one reader-boundary decision and seven historical register records;
- the mixed transcription table, accessed only through `vmanus-exp query-tsv`
  with eleven explicit locus allow-values and `--forbid-prefix f84`.

## Method

1. Rebuild the published overlay through GDT681. It assigns 607 disjoint V48
   positions and leaves 7,573 globally open.
2. Confirm that none of `polairy`, `oair`, `olpcheey`, `opchedaiin`, `dairody`,
   `ypcheddy` or `sairy` already has a published exact whole-form card.
3. Locate every exact occurrence of those seven surfaces in the admitted
   panel: 17 positions on eleven lines and ten pages.
4. Materialize only those eleven mixed-transcription loci. The guard selects
   eleven, rejects 98 f84-prefixed rows before materialization and skips 5,277
   other rows.
5. Prefer exact tokens in the ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b alignment, then low-cost joins and
   splits. Sixteen targets are exact in both alternate readers. At f111v.33,
   RF1b retains `oair` while IT2a reads the one token as `qoair`; that local
   graphemic rival is recorded rather than exported globally.
6. Compose each card from already published material, fraction, preparation,
   powder, dose, degree and completion fields. Preserve a named rival wherever
   action versus result or one extra `d` remains ambiguous.
7. Provisionally bind the final pair as `ypcheddy sairy`: the command acts on
   the following second seed fraction. Preserve the backward-reference rival
   to `dairody` and an independent `sairy` closure in the keyed context record.
   Keep the preceding powder, fraction and dose forms nominal in the portable
   default.
8. Recalculate the global overlay and the 51-line deck, emit seven forward
   family predictions and retain seven sourced historical architecture
   records.
9. Independently rebuild eleven result files in a temporary directory and compare
   every byte; check card counts, boundaries, token alignment, inherited-line
   preservation, hashes, privacy markers and both seals.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The pass requires seven unique cards, all 17 exact positions represented, the
single non-bilateral reader locus recorded, f105v.1 fully closed, all 479 reader
positions assigned, zero newly introduced hard generic renderer terms and a
byte-exact rebuild. Six inherited `ol` positions with the old
“Eigenschafts-/Zustands-/Materialträger” default are counted separately and
remain required follow-up rather than being called concrete.

Cards are concrete working defaults, not confirmed plaintext. In particular,
the extra `d` in `ypcheddy` may mark drawing off, a second closure or scribal
expansion; `dairody` may be read resultatively or as a measure-and-finish
command. GDT682 does not identify the language, phonetics, historical codebook,
exact plant, disease, patient, cure or carrier liquid.
