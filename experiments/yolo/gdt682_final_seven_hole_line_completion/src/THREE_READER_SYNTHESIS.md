# GDT682 three-reader synthesis

Three independent readers used the same seven-form frontier without editing
the experiment. Their recurring backgrounds remained deliberately different:

1. The late-medieval workshop master treated the line as a terse production
   record of materials, fractions, doses, operations and resulting substances.
2. The travelling circa-1420 practitioner required a reading usable as an
   actual recipe and tested every new whole against all of its admitted
   contexts.
3. The circa-1420 scribe/glossator prioritized abbreviation, minim and boundary
   behaviour across ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b, then compared only register architecture
   with near-period medical codices.

All three found zero reusable exact whole cards and agreed on six productive
decompositions: `pol+air+y`, `o+air`, `o+l+pcheey`, `o+pchedaiin`,
`dair+o+dy` and `sair+y`. For `ypcheddy`, the workshop reader preserved the
visible order as `y+pched+dy` and rejected a default draw operation; the other
two initially treated it as published `ypchedy` plus an extra `d`. The
adjudicated card expands the visible sequence as
`Y+P+CH+E+D_TERM_CLOSE+DY_FINISHED`; drawing off remains the rival. All three
also independently recovered 17 exact ZL3b positions on eleven loci. Sixteen
are exact in both alternate readers. Only f111v.33 differs: ZL3b/RF1b preserve
`oair`, while IT2a writes `qoair`; the nominal second-preparation-fraction
default therefore survives with a local take-command rival.

## Adjudicated working choices

- `polairy` is a closed second powder-material fraction. As a singleton it can
  still be read as an opening closure command.
- `oair` is the second preparation fraction. Its four contexts are nominal;
  only the local IT2a `qoair` supplies a command.
- `olpcheey` is dry-bound wood-drug powder, form II, and is the concrete object
  of the following grade-III heating command.
- `opchedaiin` is a three-dose quantity of middle-dried powder preparation. Its
  four contexts preserve the nominal dose core, including a position directly
  before published `ypchedy`.
- `dairody` defaults to a finished measured preparation fraction II. Two
  external registers favor the result; “measure the second fraction and finish
  it as a preparation” remains a live action rival.
- `ypcheddy` is the one new licensed command. Its visible `pched+dy` sequence
  says to dry to the middle stage, close the drying and finish the powder;
  drawing off remains a concrete contextual rival.
- `sairy` is the closed second seed fraction. Treating it as the right-hand
  object of `ypcheddy` is the current practical binding, but a backward
  reference to `dairody` plus independent `sairy` closure remains open.

The workshop reader preferred active readings for `polairy`, `dairody` and
`sairy`. The practical and scribal readers independently preferred nominal
defaults at their external contexts and proposed the local binding
`ypcheddy sairy`. That provisional two-to-one adjudication reduces the source
to two visible actions—published `ykaiin` and new `ypcheddy`—without deleting
the workshop or backward-reference rivals.

The combined line is a staged powder recipe with a second powder fraction,
second preparation fraction, wood-drug powder, heat grade III, three doses, a
finished measured fraction and powder of a second seed fraction dried to the
middle stage, closed and finished. It is not confirmed plaintext or a
historical codebook identification.
