## Frozen transcription contract

For every retained row, reader A and reader B independently submit the exact
Latin 6823 heading/rubric and the first twelve running-text tokens. They submit
before seeing one another's work. A comparison phase then records and resolves
all differences before the result is declared. The result packet retains both
original readings, the difference ledger, the reconciled diplomatic reading,
the declared normalization operations, and exact normalized scoring bytes.

The Clm 28531 reading is a control and remains in a separate field. Wording
from Clm, Masson, Sloane, Wagner, an edition, OCR, or a search result may not be
used to fill or silently normalize Latin 6823.
