# GDT412 method

## Question

Is `CHD` primarily a general process core, a transfer core, or a closing core?

## Inputs

GDT407's 4,576 running events and 715 complete statements, GDT405's atom
dictionary, and GDT411's nineteen-core dictionary. No new page, image, or
transcription is used.

## Method

Extract all `CHD`-bearing events and divide them into four directly visible
families: bare `CHD+Y`, terminal `...+DY`, open argument/relation-bearing, and
other open. Inventory all exact recipes, surfaces, pages, registers, and source
statements. Render every event under the three rival values and read one short
complete statement for every populated register×family cell.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Prefer the shortest value that reads the naked core, remains compatible with
open complements, and lets wrappers—not the core itself—supply direction or
closure. The selected German word is a portable working default, not a
confirmed lexical translation. Concrete operations still come from the card
hull and visible owner.
