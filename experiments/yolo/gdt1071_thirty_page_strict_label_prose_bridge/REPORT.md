# GDT1071 — exact label/prose capacity on the original visual pages

**Result: two operational strict-tag same-leaf whole-form matches; one of the
two is only group-owned on manual source inspection. No word translation.**
This is a complete source-first census of the 29 previously image-reviewed
GDT791 physical pages other than f1r, represented by 34 admitted selectors.
It uses an existing ZL3b occurrence spine, not a new transcription or image
admission. All target data are exposed development data. f84/f84r and reserves
remained closed.

The inherited RLO001 predicate classifies 34 of 313 single-group local loci as
UNHEDGED, OBJECT_BEARING and explicitly attached/enclosed/identical at either
the local or unit tag. Across the entire population, exact complete-form
matching found the following. The table is exhaustive for the operational
strict predicate, not a list of manually selected attractive hits.

The two same-page candidates were then checked against their complete
selector-guarded human comments and the already admitted f68r1 native image.
The f68r1.26 comment explicitly says “Label on star at 08:30 - next to border.
Six points, no core.” This is genuine local attachment of `otor` to one star.
The exact `otor` in f68r1.1 is in the upper running text, which addresses the
star field at page level. It has no author-visible pointer to that one star;
GDT791 has no deeper panel/record coordinates for this page. `otor` therefore
becomes a concrete candidate for local-star-label / general-star-text reuse,
but its value could be a name, type, property, number, address, or unrelated
reused form. The six rays are a visible feature, **not** a reading “six.”

The f89r2.19 comment says the label is near one light-rooted plant and another
dark leaf, explicitly using proximity. Its sole explicit-attachment tag is
in the **unit** description, “Labels on row of plants 3.” That description
does not attach `cheody` to one specific plant. Thus RLO001's inherited
OR-of-local-and-unit-tag rule overstates singular ownership for this case.
The preregistered operational count of two is preserved; a post-result
local-comment-only sensitivity leaves **one** same-leaf candidate, `otor`.
This correction is not a retrospective change to the selection rule or a
claim that all 34 inherited strict labels are truly singular.

Full tables contain all 313 single-group labels, including zeroes and
one-character forms, and all 1,041 exact label-to-running-occurrence edges.
The independent validator reconstructs every label, ownership-tag flag and
edge from the hash-bound GDT791 spine and a fresh selector-first annotation
query: PASS, 313 label rows, 1,041 edges. The 378 annotation rows were admitted
by 34 raw selector allow-values; the guard rejected 31 `f84*` rows **before**
materialization. No all-reader agreement or p-value is claimed.

This test **does** find one genuinely object-attached form that also appears
in prose on the same folio, `otor`; it does **not** prove the prose occurrence
refers to that star or identify a meaning. The next small task is a separately
registered complete-context and alternate-reading audit for `otor`, comparing
all its f68r1 contexts and other-page occurrences against name, star-class,
property, numeric and address rivals. A positive local match is a nominated
word candidate, not a confirmed word. Confirmed translated words: zero.
