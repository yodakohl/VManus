## What prevents a broader negative conclusion
For ZL ALL, the one-unit contexts have zero training support on174/9772 events,
versus635/9772 for the two-unit model. Under5 support occurs on664 versus1762.
For new interiors, zero-support counts are170 versus571 out of1978, and under5
counts551 versus1214. These frozen diagnostics show thinner conditioning tables;
they do not isolate the causal share of the loss or justify a support-selected
rescue. The unchanged smoothing rule gives uniform probabilities for unseen cells.
A loss under this estimator does NOT show that the second previous unit carries
no information, that first-order dependence is sufficient, or that the original
scribe used only one-unit memory. Latent whole-word/family structure remains open.

## Decision
Do not adopt this two-unit predictor as an improved construction component. Retain
1279's narrower demonstrated one-unit baseline without asserting sufficiency.
Do not automatically try order3/4, change smoothing, interpolate, filter support,
change splits, or lower gates. A different estimator would be a different proposal
requiring its own justified research decision, not a repair of this result.
