# Method

## Inputs

- GDT416's 4,576 exact component recipes;
- GDT413's unchanged working dictionary;
- GDT420's 52 gated GDT419 predictions.

No new page, image, or transcription is used.

## Procedure

1. Keep exact recipes containing exactly two action roots and preserve their
   visible order.
2. Build profiles for all 81 possible ordered action pairs.
3. For clean recipes with at most one grade, argument, and endpoint, fill the
   full 81×4×5×2 pair-slot atlas.
4. Compare both directions of all 36 unequal pairs plus nine self-pairs.
5. Gate all 31 multi-head gaps from GDT419 against the pair itself and its
   licensed grade, argument, and endpoint inventory.

Absent pairs and absent pair-specific slots remain blocked in the current
working deck. A later visible form must revise that card explicitly; it cannot
inherit permission merely because both action roots occur separately.
