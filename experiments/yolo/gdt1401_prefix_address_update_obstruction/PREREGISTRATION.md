## Exact source family

A valid plan is a finite active-leaf frontier of a rooted tree/forest: active
regions do not overlap, and the frontier covers each root. A fixed finite positive
branching count>=2is allowed; the proof works with variable local branching too.
At an addressed active leaf U, SPLIT replaces U by its immediate children.
At U whose immediate children are ALL active leaves, MERGE replaces them by U.
Otherwise that update is illegal. An empty child list admits no split or merge;
there is no vacuous empty-set coalescence. Each whole written word encodes exactly ONE
such local update; the current frontier determines split versus merge. Preserve
the full ordered update trace. No hidden update happens during navigation, inside
the word or between the adjacent words. Source roots/addresses/meanings are not
assigned to any manuscript word.

## General proof

After SPLIT(U), only immediate children of U are active below it. A descendant V
at distance>=2is not an active leaf; neither are its own children active leaves.
After MERGE(U), U itself is active, with no active proper descendants. Thus no
valid starting frontier allows U then such a V. Other roots and unrelated active
leaves cannot alter the argument. For known B Bdy, the two appended working units
force distance>=2under the declared encoding property. A verified adjacent pair
therefore contradicts every model in this family, independent of the unknown key.

Positive scope fixtures preserve Uthen immediate-child legality, and
U,child,child,Ureturning to the original frontier. The proof does not ban all
ancestor/descendant sequences. A spelling extension that adds onlyONEpath edge,
including a control/null, or names a different root need not contradict it.
Those are explicit limits, not proposed repairs of the source model.
