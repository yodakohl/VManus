# GDT358 external source audit

## The recovered printed attribution

Page 363 of volume 1 of the *Armenian Soviet Encyclopedia* (Yerevan, 1974)
prints a monochrome reproduction of the exact diagram on Penn LJS 443 current
f.209r (old f.210r). Direct comparison shows the same eight inward-curving
compartments, blank double-ring center, circular and crescent-like marks,
curved inscriptions, and manuscript lines below the wheel.

The printed caption is:

> Անանիա Շիրակացու կազմած բոլորակներից։

Literal working translation: “From among the boloraks/circular tables composed
by Anania Shirakatsi.” This supports a secondary-source Shirakatsi tradition
attribution for the exact Penn surface.

It does **not** say “eight phases of the Moon.” The English phrase “Phases of
the Moon” appears in metadata created by the Wikimedia Commons uploader in
2013. Commons cites the encyclopedia page, but that stronger English gloss is
not present in the printed caption and is excluded from semantic evidence.

## Context and conflict

The encyclopedia article separately states that Shirakatsi explained the
formation of lunar phases and compiled tables for a 19-year cycle of lunar
birth/fullness. These are article-level system facts; the article does not
explicitly attach either statement to this figure.

Penn's official catalogue places f.209r in the *Commentary on the Calendar* by
Hovhannes Vardapet/Sarkawag (fols.145v–212r). Its separately catalogued Anania
Shirakatsi *On Astronomy* begins at f.213r. This does not make the encyclopedia
caption impossible—a compilation may reproduce an older table—but it prevents
an unqualified individual-work or authorship claim. A secondary-source
misattribution also remains possible.

## Collected-edition check

The public 401-surface scan of the 1979 collected edition was inspected without
OCR. Printed p.143 begins a bracketed lunar-table section. Printed pp.144–146
show an overlapping numeric orbital construction and two annular/radial
manuscript facsimiles. A complete thumbnail census plus direct figure review
found no visible exact reproduction of the Penn eight-curved-band surface.

The Digital Library of Armenian Literature editorial note states that several
lunar works in the 1979 collection—including lunar-course tables, spring
full-moon tables, lunar forms, a zodiacal lunar-motion table, and a lunar
orbital cycle—were republished from Abrahamyan's 1962 edition based on
Matenadaran 1973. That supplies lunar-work provenance, not a Penn folio key.

## Capacity result

The new evidence is real but narrower than the circulating modern claim:

- exact Penn surface: identified;
- Shirakatsi bolorak/circular-table attribution: printed secondary support;
- “eight phases of the Moon”: unsupported by the printed caption;
- independent witness: absent;
- compartment values, start, direction, and order: absent.

Therefore no Voynich target scoring or slot alignment is licensed.
