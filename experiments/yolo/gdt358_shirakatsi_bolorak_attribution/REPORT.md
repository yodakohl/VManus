# GDT358 report: Shirakatsi bolorak attribution

## Result

**SAME_SURFACE_ANANIA_BOLORAK_ATTRIBUTION_NO_PHASE_OR_SLOT_KEY**

The 1974 *Armenian Soviet Encyclopedia* reproduces the exact LJS 443 f.209r
eight-compartment surface and captions it as one of the boloraks/circular
tables composed by Anania Shirakatsi. This is the strongest attribution found
for the exact page, not merely for its containing calendar manuscript.

The useful correction is equally important: the printed caption does not call
the wheel eight lunar phases. That phrase entered through a 2013 Commons upload
description. The encyclopedia article contains lunar context, but does not key
that context to the figure.

## What changed

GDT357's statement that no public folio-level attribution was found is
superseded narrowly: a secondary source now attributes the exact reproduced
surface to the Shirakatsi bolorak tradition. The missing-key conclusion is
unchanged.

The reproduction is not an independent homologue—it includes the same diagram
and lower manuscript writing. Penn's catalogue also places the surface inside
Sarkawag's commentary, while the manuscript's separately catalogued Anania
item starts four folios later. Borrowing, compilation, and secondary-source
misattribution remain live alternatives.

## Decision

Retain the exact-surface Shirakatsi attribution as a provenance lead. Do not
call the figure an eight-phase Moon diagram and do not expose or align a
Voynich target. Reopen only if scholarship or an independent witness supplies
the figure's function and eight ordered values.

No Voynich image, transcription, or formal payload was opened; no `f84*`
material was accessed.
