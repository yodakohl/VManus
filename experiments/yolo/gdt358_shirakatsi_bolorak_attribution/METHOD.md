# GDT358 method: Shirakatsi bolorak attribution audit

## Question

Does a newly recovered published reproduction identify the exact LJS 443
f.209r eight-compartment surface strongly enough to turn the GDT354–357
topological lead into an externally keyed endpoint?

This is an external-source audit. It does not inspect or score a Voynich
target. All `f84*` material remains forbidden.

## Fixed distinctions

The audit keeps four claims separate:

1. **surface identity**: is the published image the same manuscript surface?
2. **tradition attribution**: what does the printed source attribute it to?
3. **diagram function**: what do the eight compartments encode?
4. **slot key**: are values, start, direction, and order externally readable?

Evidence for an earlier item does not imply the later items. A modern upload
title is not substituted for the printed caption.

## Sources and procedure

- Directly compare the official OPenn f.209r facsimile with the figure printed
  on p.363 of the 1974 *Armenian Soviet Encyclopedia*.
- Retain five conservative visual landmarks: overall topology, circular marks,
  crescent-like marks, inscription placement, and the manuscript lines below
  the wheel. No automated image similarity, OCR, or text recognition is used.
- Record the printed Armenian caption verbatim and distinguish it from the
  English description added by the 2013 Wikimedia Commons uploader.
- Audit the public 1979 collected edition of Shirakatsi's writings by complete
  thumbnail census plus direct review of the lunar-section figures on printed
  pp.143–146. The AUA editorial note establishes that the listed lunar works
  reproduce Abrahamyan's 1962 edition.
- Preserve Penn's catalogue-level range attribution and treat its disagreement
  with the encyclopedia caption as unresolved provenance, not something to
  harmonize post hoc.

## Admission rule

Slot alignment would require all of the following: an exact figure function,
eight explicit values, an authored start/direction/order or invariant key, and
an independent readable witness. Same-surface reproduction and system-level
lunar context do not satisfy this rule.

## Claim ceiling

At most GDT358 may establish an exact-surface secondary attribution to a
Shirakatsi bolorak/circular-table tradition. It cannot establish eight lunar
phases, independent replication, a Voynich alignment, Armenian origin or
authorship, language, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
