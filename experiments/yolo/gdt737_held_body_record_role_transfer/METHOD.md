# GDT737 method

## Question

Do the record-location roles and both body-affinity pairs selected on GDT736's
24 complete bodies transfer to the remaining shared-remainder families, and
what part of the current renderer survives that test?

## Inputs and boundary

The panel is derived mechanically from GDT635's shared-remainder atlas after
removing GDT736's 24 bodies. Placeholder cells are excluded, leaving 120 held
bodies, 273 attested forms and 811 occurrences. All occurrence contexts are
reconstructed from the inherited 179-page allow-list through guarded,
column-projected queries. `f1r`, `f84` and `f84r` are absent; no new page or
image is opened. ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b are alternate readings of one manuscript.

## Transfer calculations

For every occurrence the builder records line position, normalized position,
paragraph status, section, cached language metadata and alternate-reader
agreement. It compares H1/H2 with H3/H4 unadjusted, in the reader-exact subset,
without one-token lines, by head occupancy, and with body+section and
body+section+language Mantel-Haenszel controls. Body- and page-controlled mean
positions expose reversals instead of hiding them.

For each of the six head pairs the builder recomputes, on the identical 120
body dimensions:

- raw occurrence-count cosine;
- reader-exact count cosine;
- body-balanced cosine, where each head count is divided by its body total;
- binary-presence cosine, phi, Jaccard and overlap coefficient.

Only raw occurrence-count cosine scores the frozen GDT736 affinity claim. The
other views are supporting diagnostics. Removing `ain`, then `ain|o|kar`, is a
dominance check, not a retuned score.

## Renderer correction

The V99R7 dictionary is joined only by exact complete surface. Any inherited
whole whose German working value contains a retired powder, seed, root or wood
head patient is quarantined. Clean current exact wholes may retain their
working default, still without becoming confirmed plaintext.

`BODY_WORKING_SPECS.tsv` records one explicit candidate for every held body,
including honest `UNKNOWN` and structure-only values. These candidates preserve
the best current state, value, material and preparation families, but are not
automatically inserted into headed forms. Form and occurrence artifacts keep
the candidate beside the executable structural default so later evidence can
promote it without losing provenance.

## Decision and claim ceiling

The location model passes if the direction and subroles recur across the held
panel and the controlled odds ratios remain strongly above one. The full
body-affinity 2x2 fails if its two selected pairs are not the top two raw-count
pairs. A failed affinity axis cannot be rescued by a binary or body-balanced
reranking.

GDT737 can generalize occurrence-conditioned structural roles, correct GDT736's
scope, quarantine invalid inherited renderer cards, and preserve explicit
semantic candidates. It cannot identify a head/body lexeme, component meaning,
plaintext, language, sound, physical glyph expansion, ingredient, unit or
species.
