# GDT737 design fix

The target is the 120 shared remainders in GDT635 that were not among the 24
complete four-head bodies used by GDT736. No new page, image, transcription or
meaning source is admitted.

The primary transfer checks are inherited rather than tuned to this panel:

1. H1/H2 should remain earlier and more often line-first than H3/H4.
2. H1 should remain more often paragraph-first than H2.
3. H3 should remain later or more often final than H4.
4. The frozen GDT736 body-affinity claim passes only if H1-H4 and H2-H3 remain
   the two strongest raw occurrence-count cosine pairs across held bodies.

Reader-exact, occupancy, section, body-balanced and binary-presence views are
diagnostics. They may explain a primary result but may not replace the frozen
raw-count test.

The semantic repair is separate. Existing V99R7 exact-whole cards containing
the retired p/s/r/l-derived powder, seed, root or wood patients are
quarantined. Concrete body roles are recorded as exploratory candidates with
confidence and counterevidence, but receive zero renderer licence and zero
component-export credit. The usable precedence is current clean exact whole,
then observed register-and-position role, then unknown.
