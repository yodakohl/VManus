# GDT510 method — target-register factor bridges for four cross-frame cards

## Question

Can the four GDT509 cards supported only by a complete carrier in another
register acquire concrete factor support inside their own target registers,
without changing any root value, phrase, target recipe or admitted page?

## Inputs

- GDT413's fixed 46-component working dictionary;
- GDT416's 4,576 owner-local imperative clauses;
- GDT425's 4,576 factor-portability records;
- GDT436's 4,576 oracle-free stream readings;
- GDT509's complete eleven-card evidence-strength deck.

## Method

1. Select exactly the four GDT509 cards in
   `B_CROSS_REGISTER_FRAME_REDUCTION`.
2. For Celestial `P+CH+E+Y`, scan every target-register component recipe for
   the exact four-atom sequence as a contiguous interval, including intervals
   inside longer multi-action recipes. Record the unique carrier, its removed
   prefix/suffix and its old factor/stream status.
3. For each `S+CHD+Y` target register, collect exact one-action-head clauses
   whose sole action head is `S` or `CHD` and whose active argument is `Y`,
   either explicit or inherited. Form the full `S` × `CHD` rectangle.
4. Choose one readable pair per register by a fixed local ranking: same owner,
   same page, same statement, `S` before `CHD`, smaller residual frames,
   smaller event distance, then event ID.
5. Replay each selected event through the old GDT425 and GDT436 records. Keep
   `G407-E1883` as the source of the directed `S>CHD` pair, because separate
   local head occurrences do not themselves instantiate the target recipe.
6. Emit one upgrade card for each of the four GDT509 cards and independently
   reconstruct all searches and selections in `src/validate.py`.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A card gains **some local factor support** if its complete ordered target is an
exact contiguous interval in a longer target-register recipe, or if both of its
action heads occur locally on the required `Y` argument. The second route does
not promote a local pair: its order support remains explicitly cross-register.

No exact target occurrence, word identity or new meaning is inferred. All four
GDT509 phrases and `COMPOSED_WORKING` labels stay unchanged. The pass makes no
surface, occurrence or page prediction and uses no page outside the fixed
GDT407 material; `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.
