rank u=(j-1)/(m-1); m<2 has no comparison. Raw-length z is normalized with the
fixed family minima/maxima. A block is paragraph/family/physical edge class/
previous-group-ends-dy/current-form-ends-dy. Whole-form multisets are preserved
within every block. Mobile means at least2positions and2distinct lengths.
LENTIL is analytically immobile under this strict dy control; it remains one
of six families. No replacement family or weaker control is permitted.

H0 uniformly distributes distinct assignments of the exact multiset. H1 assigns
weight exp[-ln(3)*sum(u*z)]. A count-state DP computes its exact partition sum,
without multiplying an extension by the remaining count of an identical form.
More than100000 count states in any mobile block stops computation. This is a
fixed effect: an extreme two-position long-then-short order has probability3/4.
All negative block gains remain. Per-leaf gain is total log2(P1/P0) divided by
mobile positions; G is the unweighted mean across mobile leaves.

with at least4mobile blocks on3leaves each. A failed reader is NO_CAPACITY and
