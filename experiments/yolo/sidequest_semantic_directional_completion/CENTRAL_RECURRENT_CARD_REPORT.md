This is the central creative review of the ten most frequent cards that still
entered the round as learned whole values. Existing meanings remain unless a
shorter selected component analysis explains both occurrences better.


The most important correction is `olkain`: its two occurrences do not share a
unique lower basin owner, so the old noun “unteres Becken” is less economical
than the already available `OL=continue` plus `KAIN=measured portion`.

`shedal` is the most creative promotion. The earlier exact rest family and the
new target grammar jointly predict a rest/settling station in two separate
f83r records. It remains a working meaning, not a universal translation of
every visible `sh` or `dal` substring.
