# Selected source–flow–target completion

Date: 2026-08-22

Mode: rapid creative ten-page scribe-workshop sidequest

Status: **selected working grammar, not deciphered plaintext**

## Result

The working script now has a small directional grammar that a workshop could
teach without a new sentence-sized meaning for every card:

```text
AR        SOURCE       from/out of a source or stock
AIR       FLOW         moving liquid in a run
AL        TARGET       at/to the intended work site

L+CHED    OUT          lead outward
P+CHED    IN           lead into a receiver
```

In compact German:

```text
AR    Quelle/Vorrat; aus/von
AIR   fließende Flüssigkeit im Lauf
AL    Ziel-/Arbeitsstelle; an/zu
L     hinaus       [only before CHED]
P     hinein       [only before CHED]
```

This reconciles the two useful rival readings of `AIR`: it is not forced to
mean pure water, but it is more concrete than an empty geometric path. It is
the liquid while moving through the local run.

## The central paradigm

```text
OK + AR       take something from the source into the work process
OK + AIR      put liquid into the run
OK + AL       put something at the target

L + CHED + AR lead outward from the source
L + CHED + AL outlet/exit station
P + CHED + AL inlet/filling station

L + CHED + DY lead outward; close
P + CHED + DY lead inward; close
```

The strongest exact contrast is therefore:

```text
lchedal   Auslassstelle      pchedal   Einfüllstelle
lchedy    hinaus; Schluss    pchedy    hinein; Schluss
```

No drawn arrow is claimed. The direction belongs to the creative card grammar,
while the image supplies the local basin, conduit, plant, body site, or other
owner.

## Five AIR cards now form a lifecycle

```text
chair       Flüssigkeitszulauf
kair        laufende Beckenflüssigkeit
okair       Flüssigkeit in den Lauf bringen
schedair    fließende Flüssigkeit durch den Lauf führen
dairydy     den Flüssigkeitslauf abschließen
```

This is a substantial improvement over assigning unrelated whole meanings such
as spring water, return flow, upper watercourse, clear outflow, and immediate
use. The local expansion may still be water, infusion, bath liquid, or another
working fluid.

## CHEO and LDDY

The two formerly opaque residues now receive concrete defaults:

```text
CHEO  Auszugs- oder Trägerflüssigkeit
LDDY  als Auflage befestigen und den Schritt schließen
```

They yield:

```text
chokcheo   Auszugsflüssigkeit zugeben
cheoar     Auszug daraus entnehmen
qokylddy   den laufenden Posten als Auflage befestigen; Schluss
```

`CHEO` deliberately does not decide among water, wine, oil, decoction, or
another carrier. `LDDY` is a learned single-card terminal core, not yet a
portable suffix.

## Seven recurrent whole cards become compositional

The ten most frequent remaining whole cards were reviewed separately. Three
keep their useful learned values (`chety|chty=zerkleinern`, `dain=durch Tuch`,
`dl=Badezusatz`). Seven now use the existing component inventory:

| Card | Selected composition | New default |
|---|---|---|
| `dchol|schol` | renderer + `OL` | vom vorigen Posten nehmen |
| `oiiin|soiiin` | renderer + `IIN` grade | vorgeschriebener Grad |
| `olkain|qolkain` | `OL+KAIN` | mit einer weiteren Portion fortfahren |
| `otchey` | `OT` + wrapped `Y` | den nächsten Posten wählen |
| `otchor|qotchor` | `OT` + renderer + `OR` | die nächste Zubereitung |
| `qcthey|shcthey` | ready + hold + current item | den laufenden Posten bereit halten |
| `shedal` | rest/settle + `AL` | Ruhe- oder Absetzstelle |

`olkain` is the clearest correction: its two occurrences do not share one
unique lower-basin owner, while `OL=continue` and `KAIN=measured portion`
already exist independently.
