showing that the continuous held role score acts as a broad calibration

## Null-centered effect

The raw +85.109-bit gain must not be read as 85 bits of role-specific relation
information. The 2,048 coupling-destruction worlds have mean +81.979 bits and
standard deviation 1.792 bits. The observed alignment is only +3.130 bits
above that mean (about 1.75 null standard deviations), with inclusive
`p=.042948`.

This is why the result is reported as narrow support. Most of the raw gain is a
generic calibration effect reproduced when the role probabilities are
reassigned within their exact source-side opportunity strata. The transferable
coupling-specific residue is real under the frozen test but very small.

## Interpretation

GDT385's passing CoReMA route is not purely an accident of that one collection.
After a different readable domain is Voynichified, a domain-local hidden
temporal/gate-derived class can still contribute to an external hierarchical
relation. No coefficient, surface form, prevalence, or local feature is shared
across the domains; only the higher-level role→relation architecture is shared.

The improvement is much too small to justify a semantic bridge by itself. Its
seven extra top-1 targets and +.000152 MRR show the scale honestly. It supports
continued use of independent relation consequences as a positive-control
instrument, while warning that raw codelength gains require a coupling null.
