## Exploratory rule

This remains the user's deliberately non-scientific workshop sidequest.
A concrete meaning may remain provisionally active without proof until it
becomes internally impossible or a more coherent alternative replaces it.
Every pass must nevertheless keep these levels separate:

```text
exact visible card
formal control or placement
short working mnemonic
local source expansion
complete record/diagram reading
```

No phrase-sized record expansion may silently become a stem value. Creative
risk is encouraged; category errors are not.

## Improvement rule

A revision beats V59 only when it does at least one of the following:

1. assigns a shorter and more reusable source value;
2. improves several full occurrences or records at once;
3. explains cross-line continuation without line=sentence;
4. restores a previously omitted referent, quantity, state, or relation;
5. reduces the number of unrelated local exceptions;
6. makes the circa-1420 writing/reading procedure easier to execute;
7. yields a more concrete full-page reading while publishing its cost;
8. defeats the strongest technical or ordinary-prose rival.

Merely replacing `UNKNOWN` with an attractive word is not improvement.
