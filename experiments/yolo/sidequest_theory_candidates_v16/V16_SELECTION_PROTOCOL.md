## Non-negotiable completeness rule

Every visible group and every multi-group construction on the ten fixed pages
must receive a default source meaning. No output cell may use `UNKNOWN`,
`OPAQUE`, `UNTRANSLATED`, `CONTENT`, `PAYLOAD`, `ITEM`, `VALUE`, `STATE` or an
empty gloss as its final default.

Where exact semantics are not recoverable, assign the most plausible concrete
contextual default—for example `plant name`, `leaf property`, `grows in moist
ground`, `take`, `mix with`, `warm`, `pour`, `immerse`, `apply`, `usual amount`,
`celestial governor 1`, `zodiac division 4`, or `lunar-station rule at the
upper-left locus`. Mark confidence; do not evade the assignment.

## Meaning policy

- One primary gloss per exact card is preferred.
- At most three explicitly defined senses per card are allowed, selected by
  register or construction—not ad hoc by attractive occurrence.
- Wrapper variants inherit the same core meaning unless a fixed renderer rule
  changes grammatical realization.
- A singleton may receive a page-context meaning even without cross-page
  support, but must be tagged `CONTEXT_DEFAULT` and low confidence.
- Pictures may supply silent subjects and arguments.
- Fluency is desired; every inserted silent argument must be marked in the
  ledger's inheritance rule.
- The result is a working translation dictionary that future iterations may
  revise, not a statement that these English words are decoded.
