# GDT675 method

## Question

Which of GDT674's twenty role-composed f81r cards and three learned exact
wholes preserve a practical meaning at every exact spelling outside f81r, and
which require a named action/result, scope, boundary, or reader split?

## Inputs

- GDT674's 23 candidate cards, review deck, and byte-level component traces;
- GDT671's 4,128-line V48 coverage table and exact 179-page allowlist;
- GDT673's 162-position transfer overlay;
- the cross-transcription table, materialized only through `./vmanus-exp
  query-tsv` with 179 explicit selector values, explicit output columns, and
  forbidden prefix `f84`;
- 23 explicit card decisions with external renderer defaults.

## Method

1. Match complete ZL3b token spellings. Substrings and merely similar stems do
   not count.
2. Separate the 24 f81r source positions from all exact positions on the other
   178 admitted sides. Reconstruct the GDT673 and GDT674 coverage layers before
   applying a new card.
3. Inspect the complete neighboring V49 fields for every external occurrence.
   Keep a card unless a concrete material, process, quantity, valence, or
   boundary conflict appears.
4. Keep all twenty P decompositions byte-complete. A changed grammatical use
   changes only the renderer: four cards select a nominal result externally,
   and five require an action/nominal, scope, or OL-boundary split.
5. Keep `lchl` as a learned exact whole. Its one external occurrence does not
   make `L-CH-L` a productive rule. Cards with no external occurrence remain
   f81r-local rather than being rejected.
6. Align every occurrence independently to IT2a and RF1b with the established
   exact-token/boundary dynamic program. Alternative readers expose boundary
   rivals; they are alternate readings of one manuscript, not independent
   votes.
7. Apply the accepted external renderings only to positions still open after
   GDT674, then rebuild every artifact byte-identically and validate from the
   guarded sources.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

This is an exploratory working transfer: a composition may remain in use while
its action/result realization or OL boundary stays explicitly open. A learned
whole remains a whole. Eleven source-only cards are neither promoted nor
discarded.

The result is not plaintext, a historical codebook, or proof of language,
phonetics, manuscript-wide lexemes, named substances, medical procedures,
plants, diseases, patients, or cures. No new page is opened, and f84/f84r stay
forbidden.
