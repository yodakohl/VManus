# GDT776 method — medial `ol` as a record-field construction

## Question

Does the 183-case medial remainder from GDT775 pattern with the already located
H4 internal-field or H3 later/reference record forms, and can that bridge turn
recurrent `ol X` outputs into content-bearing exact-whole fields instead of the
uninformative phrase “Ansatz-/Zubereitungsposten”?

## Inputs

The experiment reads only published cached artifacts from GDT734, GDT735,
GDT736, GDT737, GDT759, GDT769, and GDT775. GDT769 supplies the already guarded 4,137-line
environment so both members of every comparison bigram can be required to be
reader-exact. GDT736 and GDT737 supply opaque H3/H4 form and body membership;
GDT775 supplies the fixed 376-target atlas and predecessor renderer. GDT759
supplies the newer context rule that `s` is an amount/unit head before value
wholes but remains unresolved in other contexts.

All inputs are hash-locked in `src/SOURCE_LOCK.tsv`. No mixed TSV is parsed
directly and the sealed pages remain forbidden.

## Method

1. Select the 183 medial, novel, exact-right GDT775 targets.
2. Rebuild reader-exact adjacent bigrams and retain medial H3/H4 predecessors.
3. Compare complete right-word distributions in raw, surface-equalized,
   register-matched, register-plus-ordinal-standardized, shared-body, and
   `o...`-body-ablated views.
4. Produce an individual H3/H4 contact profile for every recurrent target
   whole. These profiles rank record-field context; they do not decode a word.
5. Apply the fixed 25-whole candidate deck. Existing spans receive a more
   informative field renderer and ten recurrent fallback surfaces are tested as
   extensions. `ol ol` remains a non-consuming chain link.
6. Emit a GDT388-compatible packet for the derived adjacency atlas. Because the
   relations are transcription-derived, all rows remain explicitly ineligible.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The renderer may publish exact contextual working defaults and a structural
internal/late-field bridge. It may not promote H3, H4, `ol`, or any right whole
to confirmed plaintext; may not infer free EVA components; and may not name a
specific liquid, plant, disease, or operation without independent whole-level
evidence. Exploratory candidates stay live until contradicted or replaced.

The practical success measure is information gain: fewer generic `ol` outputs,
unique right-token consumption, collision-safe `ol` chains, and a dictionary
row with confidence/evidence/counterevidence for every recurrent medial whole.
