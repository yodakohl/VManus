## Comparisons

The target right-word vector is compared with H3 and H4 in six deliberately
different views: pooled counts, predecessor-surface equalization, shared
`section|language|hand` support, shared register-plus-predecessor-ordinal
support with target-stratum standardization, shared-body raw counts, and
shared-body equalization. Removing `o...` bodies, `daiin`, the GDT775 fixed
family, and all recurrent targets are sensitivity views rather than vetoes.

H3 and H4 are structural classes. H4 means the previously transferred
internal-field position profile; H3 means the later/reference-or-close profile.
Neither class is an operator lexeme, Latin initial, plaintext word, or semantic
gold label.

## Renderer route

Every right whole recurring at least twice among the 183 medial targets receives
one explicit default candidate. Existing GDT775 spans are rewritten as
content-bearing fields; eligible recurrent fallbacks may become exact
two-whole extensions. The fixed candidate deck is
`src/MEDIAL_RIGHT_WHOLE_SPECS.tsv`.

`ol ol` is a chain, not a consumable two-token span: the first `ol` must leave
the second available to bind its own complement. Other selected `ol X` spans
consume the exact right-token occurrence once. Dispatch may use only cached
metadata, the exact whole surface, recurrence, and the general medial rule;
it may not use occurrence IDs or hand-picked loci.

  standardized views, call the construction **H4-leaning internal-field-like**.

  **internal/late record-field bridge** and do not export an H4 identity.

  confidence, positive evidence, and counterevidence. `r` and `s` may not
