## Result

The next incomplete component layer is now closed locally. The selected
edition preserves all 173 exact prose cards, all 381 occurrences and all 116
continuous statements while revising 21 cards, 83 occurrences and 60
statements.

The decisive correction is:

```text
Y      = der aktuell gemeinte Arbeitsposten; dies/es
CHY    = umhüllte Realisierung derselben Verweiskarte
DY     = gelernte lokale Abschlusskonstruktion, nicht D+Y
```

`Y` no longer means “open”. An occurrence is structurally open when no
licensed terminal construction closes it. This yields a much more plausible
workshop grammar: a short card can refer back to the active pictured or
previously introduced item without repeating its name.
