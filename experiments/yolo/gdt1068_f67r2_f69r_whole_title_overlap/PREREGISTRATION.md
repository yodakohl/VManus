# GDT1068 preregistration — exact complete-title transfer

Frozen before opening IT2a or RF1b f69r strings. ZL3b f69r and the inherited
f67r2 titles were already inspected during route selection: ZL3b has no exact
outer match and only `okar` on two radial loci. This is disclosed exploratory
exposure, not a blind validation set.

Question: does any **unchanged complete f67r2 title** occur as an exact
consecutive whole-group sequence within one f69r outer or radial text item?
Use all twelve `raw_title` values per edition from GDT958's fixed
`FROZEN_TITLE_VARIANTS.tsv` (`model=LM_ONLY`, one title per sector), without
LM alternatives, normalization, substring rules, or repair of uncertain
groups. Use exactly f69r.5–20 as sixteen outer items and f69r.21–42 as 22
radial items from `voynich_cross_transcription_lines.tsv`. Ignore the top
prose, circular text, and six centre items. Compare separately for ZL3b,
IT2a and RF1b; they are alternative readings of one manuscript.

Report every full-title match, all exact constituent whole-group overlaps,
and the item and sector at each match. Include negative results. A complete
multi-group title match would prioritize a **candidate** cross-page textual
link for later owner testing; one common group alone does not. If none occurs,
the unchanged literal-title bridge is unavailable. Neither outcome assigns a
wind name because f69r has no one-to-one twelve-title owner map. No p-value,
language or decipherment claim follows. Prior GDT952/958 and GDT1067 decisions
remain untouched.

Known dependencies: f67r2 sectors would have to be wind titles; the f69r
inscriptions would have to represent the same names; their exact spelling
would have to transfer across distinct diagrams. All are unproved. Known
counterexample: GDT958's fixed form variants produced zero new W.73 source
references. The Oxford MS17 f40v comparison supplies diagram family, not
the missing text ownership.

Decision note: the unknown is exact unchanged title recurrence between the
two already exposed diagrams. A complete multi-group recurrence would change
the next research priority to owner mapping; no recurrence parks this simple
literal bridge. The smallest adequate test is 12 × (16+22) complete-item
comparisons per reading. Budget for this small pass: preparation 10 minutes,
implementation 10, validation 10, publication 10; stop if it expands into
new spelling rules, decoder work, or source acquisition.
