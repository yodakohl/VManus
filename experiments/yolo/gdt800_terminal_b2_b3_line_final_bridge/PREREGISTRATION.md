# GDT800 preregistration

## Frozen question

Does the A09 source-member change B2→B3 recur as a complete `Xl/Xm` terminal
opposition outside the image labels, and is B3/terminal `m` better described as
a bound physical-line closing field than as a clothing value or a free
semantic suffix?

## Frozen primary tests

1. Enumerate all cross-array same-Kluge-member label pairs. A productive
   homolog transform requires at least two disjoint physical-folio pairs; A09
   alone fails it.
2. In the admitted running cache, require at least 20 exact stems possessing
   both `l` and `m` endings and at least 100 occurrences of each ending. A
   stem must contain at least one sign before the ending; bare `l`/`m` tokens
   are ineligible for the paired-stem model.
3. Under exact conditioning by stem, terminal `m` must have a multi-token
   physical-line-final odds ratio above 5 and one-sided `p < .001`.
4. The direction must remain above OR 3 and `p < .01` under
   stem×section×language×hand and stem×page conditioning and every
   leave-one-section-out analysis with estimable variance.
5. An obligatory allograph is rejected if either at least ten paired `m`
   occurrences are nonfinal or at least ten paired `l` occurrences are final.
6. A free semantic suffix is not selected without an independent repeated
   image or record-state contrast after physical line position is controlled.

## A09 holdout

The A09 label direction is not used to estimate the running-text position
model. Both labels are standalone label units, so physical finality alone
cannot predict which takes B2 or B3. Clothing is a negative control inherited
from GDT799; no new state is acquired.

## Claim ceiling

The pass may select a structural, boundary-favoured terminal field and a
learned-stem-plus-bound-ending architecture. It cannot establish allography,
inflection, abbreviation, grammatical case, sex, clothing, astronomical
status, word identity, language, sound, cipher, plaintext or translation.
