## Why these win

### OKEEY — temperature condition

R1 and R2 independently choose temperature/lukewarmness. The seven occurrences
neighbor immersion, rinse, application and other explicit heat cards. Reading
OKEEY as `mix thoroughly` duplicates open CHEDY; reading it as `add warm water`
inserts a new material at every occurrence. The selected phrase keeps it a
condition rather than an additional mixing or loading operation.

### LCHEDY — whole-charge drainage

All independent routes converge on downward drainage once the entire record is
read. The card follows bath, washing or mixing phases and repeatedly fits a
lower basin/outlet. It is now distinct from:

```text
QOKEDY   rinse the pictured place once
OLKEEDY  let the liquid settle
LCHEDY   drain the whole spent charge downward
```

### DCHEDY — apparatus wash-through

R2 and R3 independently prefer a single wash-through of the used basin or
route. This also makes the repeated pair readable as two actual washes rather
than two generic endings. `Finish treatment` remains the weaker rival, but
does not explain why this closer is distinct from the other terminal cards.

### OLCHEDY — reserve, not drain

R1 and R2 independently select putting the prepared mixed liquid aside. This
preserves a useful distinction from LCHEDY whole-charge drainage and
SHCKHEDY cloth straining. R3's attractive `draw off clear upper liquor` remains
the main concrete rival; the present ten pages do not yet force decanting over
covered reserve.

### CHAR — same prepared batch

R1 and R2 independently favor an antecedent relation. It explains Herbal and
Bio occurrences with one ordinary abbreviated source phrase and makes
`OKAIN–CHAR–OKAIN` read `add one portion, from the same batch, add another`.
The sequence reading `then continue` remains viable but loses the batch reuse.

### CHO — dossier continuation

R2 supplies the best bridge between the incompatible plant-part and page-topic
readings: `then take the following ingredient or plant part`. One recurring
card advances the f56r dossier; the following card, rather than CHO itself,
identifies root, wine, leaf or fresh preparation. This is concrete without
forcing all four occurrences to name flowering tops.

## Updated working process

The selected Bio reading is now more sharply ordered:

```text
load measured portion
→ combine and stir
→ temper/keep lukewarm
→ immerse or apply
→ let settle when required
→ wash the used route once
→ strain through cloth
→ reserve the useful mixed liquid
→ drain the spent whole charge into the lower receiver
→ proceed to the next local instruction
```

That is more informative than V17 because `wash`, `reserve`, `settle`, `strain`
and `drain` are no longer interchangeable closing glosses.
