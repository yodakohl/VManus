# GDT1059 method

The registered scope and decision rule are in [PREREGISTRATION.md](PREREGISTRATION.md).
`src/run.py` imports the unchanged GDT623 guard and `first_quality` function,
projects one first paragraph-opening head per Herbal-A page, and writes every
head's first strict q-prefix contact within three physical lines. This produces
one row per page, including `NONE`. It then computes the declared full and
leave-out summaries. `src/validate.py` independently re-aggregates the saved
row table and checks scope, the two exact target loci, and all reported rates.

The comparison is post-hoc relative to GDT623's candidate selection and has no
independent semantic referent. Whole physical folios cannot serve as blind
confirmation here because the head and image candidates were previously seen.
The pair fractions are descriptive base rates, not significance tests.
