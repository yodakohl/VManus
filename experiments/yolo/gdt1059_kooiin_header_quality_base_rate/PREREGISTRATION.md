2026-09-28. Registered before reading the new all-header projection.

Question: Does the already reported `kooiin` → TCH/TCH next-line agreement carry
useful specificity beyond ordinary Herbal-A page-head quality contacts? This is
a capacity check for the existing rootstock-class working gloss, not a search
for a new code orientation or a word meaning.

Inputs: the same guarded 179-page ZL3b token panel and unchanged `first_quality`
rule used by GDT623; published GDT623 repeated-header audit. No new image,
transcription rule, source name, gloss or sealed selector is admitted. `f1r`,
`f84` and `f84r` are excluded by the inherited guard. `koaiin` is a one-edit
singleton, not a third exact `kooiin` occurrence.

Selection: take the first paragraph-opening token on every admitted Herbal-A
page, preserving one head per page as in GDT623. Starting after that token,
take the first `^qo(k|t)(ch|sh)` surface at most three physical lines forward.
Record every head and family, including `NONE`, before scoring. The comparison
pool excludes f2v and f29v; report a second pool also excluding f3v. The primary
descriptive quantity is the TCH share among eligible comparison heads, with
`NONE` retained in the denominator; also report the share conditional on a
contact, the observed same-family pair share among all pairs, and the exact
`kooiin` TCH/TCH pair. Do not select a favorable alternate denominator later.

Decision: if TCH is common among comparison heads, the two-page match supplies
little independent leverage against a generic Herbal-entry head and `kooiin`
remains unselected. If TCH is rare, it strengthens a repeated formal head/code
association only; rootstock meaning still needs an independent owner and cannot
be declared confirmed. Any `kooiin` discrepancy is recorded, not repaired.
No significance claim: the target and code were chosen after prior inspection,
and heads on manuscript pages are not independent draws. The pair fractions are
descriptive sensitivity checks, not p-values.

Known predecessors/countercases: GDT623's four repeated heads select only
`kooiin` as nonempty same-family, after inspection; GDT768 says other
rootstock-rich heads lack `kooiin` and keeps the generic-head rival; GDT1058
leaves K/T temperature orientation unresolved. No thermometer gloss is tested.

Smallest adequate work: one guarded projection and a tabular replay validator.
Wall-time budget 40 minutes: preparation 10, implementation 15, validation 8,
publication 7. At the limit, stop expansion and reassess. No decoder or new
control corpus.
