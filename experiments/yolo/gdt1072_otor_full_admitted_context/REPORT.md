# GDT1072 — `otor` is not confined to the star page

**Decision:** The simple reading “`otor` names the one labelled f68r1 star”
is a poor working default: the exact whole is widespread in unrelated admitted
text. A general card, property, class, homonym, reused name or unrelated
orthographic recurrence remains possible. This census supplies **no word
translation** and cannot prove the referents of the running-text occurrences.

The frozen 179-selector corpus contains 33 exact ZL3b occurrences on 29 pages,
38 IT2a occurrences on 32 pages, and 29 RF1b occurrences on 25 pages. ZL3b's
33 split across H=17, S=8, B=4, P=2 and T=2; 31 are running text and two are
local inscriptions. IT2a has H=19, S=9, B=4, P=3, T=2, C=1. RF1b has H=17,
S=8, B=1, P=1, T=1, C=1. These are alternate transcriptions of one manuscript,
not additive occurrence counts or independent replications.

The two already exposed f68r1 loci add the exact star label f68r1.26 in all
three readings and the upper prose f68r1.1 in ZL3b/IT2a only. RF1b's prose
boundary differs, as the 2026-08-21 workshop primary had already noted. Across
the corpus plus these two loci, the complete table has 105 reader rows at 40
source loci: 25 loci contain exact `otor` in all three readings, 13 in two, and
two in one. Group indices were **not** aligned as if they were stable across
readers. The f68r1 star-label/prose match is a candidate page-local reuse in
two readings, with no author-visible pointer from the prose to that star.

The current census is broader than GDT1071's 29 visual pages and the older
workshop's micro-corpus, but does not newly discover the cross-genre existence
or the RF boundary difference. Its useful increment is their complete admitted
denominator and full position list. In particular, Herbal H alone has 17
ZL3b occurrences on many pages. An exclusive proper name for one specific
star would need a reason for this distribution that the available text and
images do not supply; the distribution does not logically rule out a name used
elsewhere or a homonym. The other exact local positions f88r.24, f89v1.22 and
f99v.3 are not independent object-name confirmations: existing annotations
for the first two are hedged, and the third states proximity only.

The independent validator matched the existing 179-selector word profile on
all three per-reader counts, section/kind strata and page counts, verified all
105 rows are exact `otor`, and excluded f84. Guard receipts report 2,122 f84*
rows rejected before materialization in each query. Previously exposed
development data and earlier microtheory are disclosed; f84/f84r, other reserve
text and new images remained closed. No p-value, source identity, stellar
numeral or confirmed meaning is claimed. Confirmed translated words: zero.

**Next decision:** park `otor` as a star-specific lexical anchor. Reopen only
with a source-bound object/reference relation or a whole-text reading that
predicts its cross-register uses, rather than another count or ray heuristic.
