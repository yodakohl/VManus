# GDT595 method

## Question

Can every one of GDT594's forty-four cold `Badegut` actions receive a concrete
occurrence-level patient without treating AIIN, Y, or SH as a global noun, and
can the two already accepted neutral episode carries inherit the resulting
type so that all 254 bath actions have a specific working object?

## Inputs

- GDT594's fixed 254 bath actions and 793-statement reader;
- the complete GDT581 written-slot ledger;
- GDT582/GDT590 occurrence nouns for exact carrier slots;
- a five-card historical analogy deck covering recipe chains, anaphora, and
  the separation of measure from continued material.

## Method

The exact target predicate is `gdt594_selection_route ==
COLD_BATH_OBJECT_DEFAULT`, which yields forty-four actions. It is cross-cut in
two independent ways.

The written-frame census is descriptive:

- twenty targets have written or carried AIIN fill context;
- eighteen have a Y later in the same written event;
- six have neither signal.

This census does not assign the patient. AIIN remains fill/medium, and a late Y
does not have one global noun value.

The occurrence-level object source ladder is:

1. Reuse the nearest visible non-medium participant after the last reader cut.
   These twenty-one cases are rendered anaphorically as `denselben` or
   `dieselbe`.
2. If there is no such left source and the same written event ends in an exact
   typed carrier, allow that carrier to close a short preceding verb chain.
   These eight rightward shared complements are rendered definitely, not as
   identity claims.
3. Resolve E2952's visible station/portion packet by its following station
   treatment.
4. Give fourteen remaining reset-bound targets the definite body-first working
   default. E2932 and E3218 retain their later station/portion competitors but
   do not treat them as same-event sources.
5. Propagate E3218's selected Body through the already accepted E3219 episode
   edge, and E3488's selected Station through E3489.

Three total assignments are retained in the comparison table: the first full
manual workshop model, a Body/Station source-precedence model, and the selected
hybrid. The selected hybrid differs from the workshop model only at E2863,
E3523, E3533, and E3664, where a same-event right-edge Y has a Station reading.
Every card retains Badegut, Body, Station, Portion, and Unit clauses.

Statement patches are keyed by statement, host ordinal, action slot, and the
original clause occurrence. All spans are located in the unchanged GDT594
reader before replacement and applied from right to left. This protects S502's
three patches, S565/S583's double patches, S583's duplicate old clauses, and
the E3664/E3673 repeated-clause hazard.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass requires exactly forty-four source cards, the 20/18/6 frame census, the
21/8/1/14 source-direction census, a direct 16 Body / 23 Station / 3 Portion /
2 Unit profile, two exact dependent carries, forty-six changed actions in
forty-two statements, and zero remaining generic bath objects. All retained
actions and statements must rebuild byte-identically, and all generated TSVs
must reproduce byte for byte.

This is an exploratory complete German working reader on six already admitted
pages. It may use historical recipe syntax as an analogy and may prefer a
right-edge shared-complement interpretation over the older narrow host
attachment. It does not establish a Voynich plaintext, language, global word
meaning, historical codebook, real patient, anatomy, substance, illness,
remedy, procedure, or new-page prediction. No surface, slot, root, parser,
segmentation, or page is changed.
