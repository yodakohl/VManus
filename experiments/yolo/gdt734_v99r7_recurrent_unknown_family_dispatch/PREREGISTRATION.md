# GDT734 design record

This is a source-informed pre-validation design record, not a blind
preregistration. The GDT733 cache, the missed active-whole population and the
top residual inventory had already been inspected when the two-pass design was
fixed. The candidate, role, editorial and historical specification decks were
then frozen before the final independent validation run.

## Fixed populations and decisions

1. Pass 1 repairs only unconditional `ACTIVE_WORKING_DEFAULT` whole-form
   projections that GDT733 left unknown; context- or span-bound readings remain
   excluded.
2. Pass 2 is limited to the 20 enumerated recurrent exact wholes with exactly
   one split among currently concrete V99R4 reading combinations. Split
   uniqueness earns no semantic score.
3. Every candidate is preclassified as compositional, role-constrained or a
   learned whole, with the score formula and component-export ceiling fixed in
   `CANDIDATE_SPECS.tsv` and `run.py`.
4. The 28-row editorial deck may change only the spoken renderer wording; it
   cannot alter stored semantics, scores, confidence, evidence, scope or
   export rights.
5. The role matrix and five historical comparators carry zero free-component,
   relation and Voynich-sign credit.
6. Exact contexts, render-once spans, structural punctuation and inherited
   cache cells retain higher precedence.
7. The fixed cache has 32,339 cells on 4,128 lines and 179 already admitted
   pages. No page, image, transcription, `f84` or `f84r` may be opened.

## Falsifiers and ceiling

The pass fails if the repair population is not exactly 71 surfaces/305 cells,
the candidate population is not exactly 20/226, any candidate exports a free
component, a higher-precedence cell changes, the complete dictionary loses
confidence/evidence fields, practical-unit structure changes, or independent
replay differs bytewise.

Even a pass supports only an exploratory exact-whole dictionary and cache
renderer. It cannot establish plaintext, language, phonetics, a plant,
ingredient, disease, cure, unit, or free Voynich component meaning.
