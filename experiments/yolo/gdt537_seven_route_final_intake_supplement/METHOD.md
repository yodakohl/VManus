# GDT537 method

## Question

Can every post-GDT516 working revision be compiled into one exact prose-surface
overlay so that the older GDT517 event lookup cannot silently restore a
superseded recipe on the next page batch?

## Inputs

- GDT536's complete 159-surface working edition and result;
- the selected route certificates from GDT530 through GDT536;
- the existing GDT517 role-aware intake command as fallback.

No page, transcription, root meaning, candidate, or recipe is added.

## Procedure

1. Mark every GDT536 row whose final recipe differs from the original GDT516
   recipe, plus every row whose resolution status was explicitly closed.
2. Require this set to equal the seven surfaces named in the GDT530–GDT536
   certificates.
3. Compile a slim exact dictionary for all 159 final prose surfaces.
4. Attach one route card to each of the seven revisions, including its exact
   visible split, recipe split, source experiment, evidence, and transfer
   scope.
5. Make the overlay prose-only. `LOCAL_RECORD` requests and surfaces absent
   from the final 159 delegate unchanged to GDT517.
6. Replay every exact final surface through the executable overlay.

## Pass conditions

- 159 unique final keys and zero unresolved rows;
- exactly seven named routes, six recipe changes, and one resolution-only card;
- all seven source experiments GDT530–GDT536 represented once;
- 159/159 executable recipe and route matches;
- local-domain, old-surface, and unseen-surface delegation works;
- deterministic replay and independent validation pass.

## Scope

The overlay locks exact final working prose readings. It does not extend any
special route to a new surface or turn a working meaning into confirmed text.
