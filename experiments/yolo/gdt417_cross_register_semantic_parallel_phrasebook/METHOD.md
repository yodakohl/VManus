# GDT417 method

## Question

Which exact component recipes genuinely recur across different content
registers, and do their imperative readings preserve one semantic skeleton?

## Inputs

GDT416 supplies all 4,576 clauses and 715 imperative statements. GDT412
supplies the nineteen core identities. No new manuscript material is read.

## Method

Group events by exact component recipe and retain groups present in at least
two registers. Preserve every occurrence and one example reading per register.
Separate fully self-contained recipes from cards whose imperative requires a
same-owner inherited action or argument. Independently group complete
statements by exact portable-core sequence and by template sequence. Finally,
measure for each of the nineteen roots how many occurrences lie inside a
cross-register recipe.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Shared recipes support compositional portability, not decoded words. Bare
argument or sequence cards may remain context-bound even when their exact form
occurs everywhere; their inherited verb is not part of the card meaning.
