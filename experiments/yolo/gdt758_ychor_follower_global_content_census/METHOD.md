# GDT758 method

## Question

When the eleven distinct forms immediately following the thirteen exact
line-initial `ychor` occurrences are followed globally, which concrete
complete-form meanings best survive, and which forced GDT756 patients or verbs
must change?

## Fixed target and scope

The target is exactly:

```text

```

The builder uses the already admitted guarded cache only. It opens no page,
image or transcription and rejects the sealed selectors before materializing
their remaining fields. ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b are agreement controls for one
manuscript, not independent witnesses.

Primary inputs are GDT756's thirteen-line/71-token reader, GDT757's formula
inventory, GDT754's 172-form suspect list, GDT745's clean exact-whole analogy
machinery, GDT755's historical expression bank, GDT625's `cthy` summary and
GDT629's exact `chor chol daiin` clauses.

## Global occurrence and field census

Every reader-exact target token is retained with page, locus, physical
position, immediate exact neighbours, formula predecessor and an outward
radius-five field. All 172 suspect source-composed surfaces and all eleven
targets are excluded as independent semantic anchors. Field channels therefore
describe the surroundings and never vote with the target's inherited gloss.

## Whole analogy

For each target, the existing GDT745 routine enumerates clean W2/W3 exact
wholes at edit distance one or two. Eligible neighbours have zero composition
credit, zero component-export permission and no retired literal powder/seed/
root/wood patient. Consensus axes rank whole-form candidates only. No character
or substring receives a meaning.

## Exact adjacency and the `s` correction

All exact-exact adjacent pairs in the guarded cache form the baseline. For each
target/side/neighbor relation the builder reports count, conditional rate,
background rate and descriptive lift. A separate comparator ranks every form
with at least five exact occurrences, four exact right contexts and at least
one follower in the inherited ordered-value family `an/ain/aiin/aiiin`.

That family is a structural working label: the test does not prove numbers,
units or phonetic values. `s=je/zu gleichen Teilen` is selected because the
quantity ecology and the historical `ana + amount` formula explain `s om` and
`s aiin` more economically than the forced content noun `Samen`. Drachm and
ounce remain live unit-sign rivals.

## Reader revision

All 71 post-`ychor` positions keep a default. The eleven audited whole forms
override GDT756; all other positions retain their published candidate. Eight
explicit, observed spans receive a one-time smoothed rendering, including
`s om = je eine Handvoll`, `cthy chol = trockenes Blattgut` and
`ar aiin = drei Anteile`. These span readings apply only to their observed
`ychor` lines and export no component value.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Every target receives a concrete default and two rivals. A candidate may remain
at C0 while it is still possible; evidence changes its ordering rather than
requiring premature rejection. The pass may revise renderer hypotheses and
identify global functional axes. It may not claim a confirmed lexeme, plaintext
clause, language, sound, historical graphic match, component value or unseen
form prediction.
