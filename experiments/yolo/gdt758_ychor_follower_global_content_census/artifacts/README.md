  least one member of the structural `an/ain/aiin/aiiin` family.

  the published historical register bank, including `ana`, measure signs,
  plant parts, dry/hot states, soaking and straining.

  and origin.
- `YCHOR_13_REVISED_READER.tsv`: complete transparent token maps plus eight
  exact-span smoothing rules.
- `GDT758_WORKING_DICTIONARY.md`: human-readable dictionary and all thirteen
  revised lines.

- `VALIDATION.json`: invariant and byte-replay certificate.

because they are the complete audit trails behind the eleven promoted global
defaults and remain well below the five-megabyte artifact limit.
