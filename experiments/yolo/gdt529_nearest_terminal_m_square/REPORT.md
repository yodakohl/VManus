## A useful correction to the preceding audit

GDT515 listed four weighted candidate recipes beside `cthom`, including
`CH+T+M_LOCAL+OL` and `CH+T+M_LOCAL+OR`. Those were not old manuscript
carriers. They were recipes mechanically produced by one-edit transformations
of `cthol`, `cthor`, `ctho`, and `chom`. They may suggest candidates, but they
cannot count as four independent attestations.

The actual old evidence is smaller and cleaner: eight exact `BASE/BASEm`
pairs, the exact `cth` right-slot triad, and the nearest one-edit pair
`cho/chom`. GDT529 uses only those real carriers.

## Why global frequency alone loses here

Across the old running compiler, visible final `m` generally favours
`AM_ADDR`; `om` likewise more often maps to `O+AM_ADDR` than to
`O+M_LOCAL`. That broad preference put `CH+T+O+AM_ADDR` first for `cthom`.

The local paradigm is more informative than that global count. The m-less
base `ctho` belongs to a complete two-action `O/OL/OR` slot family, and it is
only one visible edit from `cho`, whose exact `m` partner adds `M_LOCAL`.
The selected rule requires both facts. It does not say that Herbal context,
all `om`, or all visible `m` imply `M_LOCAL`.
