# GDT512 method — current eleven-card pair edition

## Question

Can GDT509's eleven-card deck be reissued without changing a translation while
incorporating GDT510's four target-register factor findings and GDT511's actual
linkage strength for the three `S+CHD+Y` cards?

## Inputs

- GDT509's complete eleven-card evidence-strength deck;
- GDT510's four target-register local-support upgrades;
- GDT511's three register linkage-strength cards.

## Method

1. Copy each GDT509 target identity, ordered pair, literal trace, handgrip,
   argument policy and German working translation exactly.
2. Retain its old local frame, context or repeated-package mechanism when no
   later experiment changes it.
3. Replace Celestial `P+CH+E+Y`'s old cross-register-only route with GDT510's
   exact contiguous local suffix carrier.
4. Replace the three `S+CHD+Y` cross-register-only labels with GDT511's long
   same-statement, same-owner/page and same-page/cross-owner head inventories.
   Keep their directed pair order explicitly on Biological `G407-E1883`.
5. Emit seven support buckets, one complete eleven-card edition and five
   handgrip coverage rows. Reconstruct every source mapping independently.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The seven `T1`–`T7` labels are a display and provenance order, not a calibrated
probability scale. `T1`–`T4` are different mechanisms and are not asserted to
be mutually stronger or weaker. Only `T5`–`T7` form an internal proximity
ordering among the three otherwise identical long head-inventory routes.

A card counts as having target-register pair order, an interval or a package
projection unless its `pair_order_locality` is
`CROSS_REGISTER_CARRIER_ONLY`. A local inventory of separate heads never
counts as a local pair.

Every target recipe remains unobserved and `COMPOSED_WORKING`. No component
value, phrase, evidence label, surface, occurrence or page is changed or
predicted. `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.
