# GDT1015 actual candidate contract, before consequence execution

MODEL_v01 and READING_v01 are the first complete saved exploratory draft. The
candidate lock freezes them and this contract before run/validate. This is not a
claim that meanings were assigned blind; the writer knew both text and Dio's
calculation. Every numerical value and construction was selected with that
knowledge. No score, search null or significance is defined.

The engine consumes the exact twelve construction patterns, checks all 80 raw
positions against one sixty-type lexicon, preserves the five inherited guesses,
and binds numerical arguments from their actual lexical values/positions. It
then evaluates the declared argument. Grammar templates are analyst-supplied;
passing a template check is bookkeeping, not evidence that this is Voynich syntax.

The finite model has one seven-member order. Offset0 is the first hour. The last
hour is offset23; the hour after the last is offset24. The first member after two
skipped following members is offset3. Compare rulers, not absolute hour objects.
C09 removes three complete seven-member cycles from24, obtaining a remainder that
must be nonnegative and less than7. C10 is methodA, C11 methodB; C11 SAME compares
B withA. C12 independently applies its declared reference convention. This second
occurrence of agreement is repetition inside one hypothesis, not new evidence.

Seven complete candidates are frozen. The five non-night variants each have one
setting; two night variants each have23 settings for unspecified daylight counts
1..23. Thus51 settings are checked at all7initial phases. No setting is selected
post hoc. A night variant with some passing settings remains possible when the
text supplies no daylight count; report all passing/failing values. Do not turn
failure at an invented12-hour night into exclusion of the whole variant.

Night-reverse convention: from each hour with offset h<d take a forward step;
from each with h>=d take a reverse step. This produces offset2d-24 at the next day.
Its NEXT_MEMBER uses a locally reversed order in the night clause. The additional
local order is a declared rival grammar assumption, absent from the explicit
seven-member definition; it is not silently supplied in the primary.
Night-reset convention: advance normally except the transition INTO hour d resets
to the initial ruler. Continue normally to offset24, without another reset at the
endpoint. This boundary reset is not written by a separate lexical operator; its
extra clause-scope assumption violates the primary's no-unspoken-operation rule.
Report that admissibility defect separately even where its arithmetic agrees.
Do not claim the manuscript excludes a reset on the basis of a guessed grammar.

For each setting: show actual ruler fromA, fromB, C09 remainder, C11 agreement,
C12 agreement, full source-hour-order compatibility, and declared grammar debt.
C01/C04/C05/C06/C07 type/reference checks must be retained even though they do not
add a second independent astronomical observation. All5040label permutations and
all7phases are tested as relabelings of the same abstract world. They cannot select
an actual planetary name. An independent validator walks literal named circles
and removes complete circles from a list of24hour slots; it does not import the
primary arithmetic routine. Counterexamples are saved, not explained away.

Equal length seasonal hours, absolute dawn and physical astronomy are not tested.
All positions were historically exposed, all interpretation is exploratory, and
there is no independent whole-leaf meaning confirmation capacity. f84/f84r stay
sealed, f116v stays unadmitted, no images/reserves/contact are used.
