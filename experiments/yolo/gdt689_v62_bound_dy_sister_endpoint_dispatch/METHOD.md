# GDT689 method

## Question

Can all sixty bound visible-`dy` working forms be rendered by preserving the
head, stage and base operation of an exact `...y` sister wherever one exists,
while separating result marking, semantically silent field closure, parser
controls and learned whole forms?

## Inputs

- GDT687's sixty-surface bound-`dy` inventory, 74 current positions and formal
  705-event closure prior;
- GDT688's complete 51-line V61 reader and exact practical-verb scanner;
- GDT671's V48 working glossary and full line atlas;
- GDT516's exact visible `dy/y` pair and parser-recipe atlas;
- GDT515's cached component recipes and the seven current exact statement
  joins;
- GDT624's complete quality-shell grid, including eight `d_bit`/state-binding
  controls whose semantics remain open;
- the explicit sixty-row V62 decision deck in
  `src/V62_DY_SISTER_DECISIONS.tsv`.

No image, new page or raw global transcription is opened. `f84` and `f84r`
remain forbidden.

## Method

1. Reconstruct the exact population of sixty visible bound-`dy` surfaces and
   74 current positions on 33 lines and 23 admitted pages.
2. Generate only the mechanical one-edit candidate `surface[:-2] + "y"`.
   This yields 39 card-backed neighbours, but two are not semantic controls:
   `dchedy/dchey` has different parsers and `ypcheddy/ypchedy` leaves a nested
   `dy` in the sister. Of the remaining 37, 36 have a sister meaning and
   `cheody/cheoy` is only a same-recipe null control.
3. Preserve three hard controls. `cheody/cheoy` has the same recipe
   `CH+E+O+Y`; `dchedy/dchey` is not a minimal parser pair; and the nested
   `ypcheddy/ypchedy` pair cannot isolate the ending.
4. Keep visible spelling and formal parser state orthogonal. Exact GDT515
   recipes classify 24 surfaces as definite `FORMAL_DY`, two as mixed-recipe,
   seventeen as `NO_FORMAL_DY`, and seventeen as unresolved. A visible ending
   therefore cannot inherit the formal statement-end prior automatically.
5. For each of 36 head-bearing sister pairs, copy its material head,
   degree/stage and base
   operation first. Assign `RESULT_PARTICIPLE` only when the target is the
   reached nominal state of that same core. Assign `FIELD_END` when the sister
   already contains its endpoint or action; this class adds no spoken value
   and inherits the sister's action/nonaction orientation.
6. Retain every parser-invalid, nested, unvalued-sister or absent-sister form
   as a learned whole form. Do not export an ending value from those 23
   controls.
7. Compare exactly the 36 head-bearing pairs whose sisters have V48 cards.
   The excluded nested sister is not allowed back into this score, and
   `cheoy`, which lacks a V48 working card, is not silently assigned one.
8. Match target and sister occurrences by section, language, hand and physical
   line position. Keep physical line position distinct from true statement
   position; only seven of 74 target positions currently have an exact GDT515
   statement join.
9. Apply the 36 sister-head decisions at 47 current occurrences and
   `cheody`'s own target whole-card value at three same-recipe null positions.
   Rerender the complete 51-line reader in source order and require every
   practical German verb span to fall inside exactly one action ordinal.
10. Rebuild fifteen primary artifacts byte-for-byte in a temporary directory
    and independently validate populations, recipes, controls, revisions,
    spans, action ordinals, page guards and hashes.

## Working decision and ceiling

The selected mixed code contains 25 result forms, twelve semantically silent
field-copy forms, zero productive action-telicity forms, one parser-invalid
pair and 22 whole-only forms. Its mechanical compression rule reproduces
36/37 selected result/field decisions; `ychedy` is the sole explicitly
in-sample whole-card override, not a held prediction. This is a replaceable
working theory,
not a claim that historical plaintext contains a universal `dy` suffix.

V62 may improve composition and practical prose, but it does not confirm any
German word, language, phonetic value, ingredient, disease, patient or cure.
