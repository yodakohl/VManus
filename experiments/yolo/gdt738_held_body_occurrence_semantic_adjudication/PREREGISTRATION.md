# GDT738 design note

This is an exploratory throughput pass, not a new blind holdout. Its
architectural constraint is simple: the result must improve concrete complete-whole
readings without reviving portable EVA initials or the retired four-material
head model.

The evidence channels remain separate:

1. exact bare-body whole card;
2. exact non-opaque adjacent whole card, reduced to a semantic axis;
3. observed head/location role;
4. manual full-line judgment for the six declared stress forms;
5. historical architecture only, with zero Voynich relation credit.

The historical architecture is itself split into two evidence decks before
interpretation:

1. `DESCRIPTIVE_MICROENTRY`: learned whole or rubric plus optional part/form,
   quality/state and locally bound degree;
2. `PRESCRIPTIVE_FORMULA`: recipe signal, ingredient or preparation plus
   amount, unit, process passage and result form.

A source may document both channels, but the channels are never pooled as
independent confirmations. They supply a typed pharmaceutical record prior
only. No historical initial, abbreviation, numeral or Latin word is mapped
onto an EVA label.

Automatic promotion requires all mechanical body and form gates described in
`METHOD.md`. Manual stress decisions cannot export a component and cannot
predict an unattested surface. A failure of neighbour-axis classification does
not erase a well-supported bare whole; it merely prevents claiming that local
neighbours independently identify the semantic axis.

All 17 scoped complete-whole candidates remain in an exploratory deck with
their rivals and counterevidence. The current executable subset is the final
quantitative W23 list of 12 cards:

`lain`, `lcheedy`, `lcheol`, `lkaiin`, `lkain`, `lkar`, `lsheedy`, `pcheol`,
`rain`, `rsheedy`, `sain`, `skaiin`.

The five cards `lcheor`, `lkeey`, `lkey`, `lky` and `pcheor` remain
`DISCOVERY_ONLY` and receive no occurrence-patch licence in this pass.
Confidence alone does not determine deck membership; `MEDIUM` is not a
blanket Discovery-only rule. Neither deck exports a head, body, suffix or
other component.

All 120 bodies and all 273 forms must receive a decision. The old literal salt
readings for `solaiin` and `sols` are specifically open to downgrade. Missing
forms are not generated, and absolute scalar values remain open wherever the
local host does not select degree, amount, dose or process passage.
