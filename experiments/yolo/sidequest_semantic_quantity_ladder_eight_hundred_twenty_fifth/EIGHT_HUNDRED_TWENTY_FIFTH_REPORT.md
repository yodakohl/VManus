# Sidequest Pass 825: the four-level quantity ladder

The 62 quantity/parameter memberships retain four concrete levels:

- `S = PROBE` — a small diagnostic object (1 event);
- `AIN = PORTION` — a working amount of material (18 events);
- `AIIN = SOLLMASS` — the prescribed value or setting to reach (39 events);
- `IIN = STUFE` — the current process stage (4 events).

The singleton S is the weak member, but its exact local order is useful:
`CH+E+S` says “kurz eine Probe entnehmen” and is followed immediately by the
independent AIIN card. A sample followed by a prescribed value forms a sensible
check; another generic PORTION would erase the distinction from AIN.

PROBE therefore remains a creative working assumption, not a forced fact. It
stays until a new page makes it impossible or offers a better repeated role.
