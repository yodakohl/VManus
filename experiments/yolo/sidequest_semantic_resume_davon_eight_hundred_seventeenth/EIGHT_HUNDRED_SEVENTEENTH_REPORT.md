# Sidequest Pass 817: dchol/schol means “davon”

The exact same card occurs twice, rendered as `dchol` on f11r and `schol` on
f56r. Both appearances begin a clause whose pictured plant owner is already
active. What follows is not a second topic heading but a concrete manipulation
of material taken from that owner.

The former value WIEDERAUFNEHMEN made the card a workshop meta-command and had
to invent an unnamed previous operation. The shorter invariant reading is:

> `dchol/schol = DAVON`

This is a learned whole-card anaphor: “from that / of it”, resolved locally to
the pictured plant or its currently active material. It is deliberately not
split into `d + chol` or `s + chol`.

The two continuous readings become:

> Bei der dicht bluehenden Kronenpflanze: **Davon** den aktuellen Posten
> beibehalten, ihm zugeben und ihn bis zum Sollmass weiterfuehren.

> Bei der mehrkoepfigen stacheligen Pflanze: **Davon** die Zutat als laufenden
> Posten fuehren und ansetzen; laenger im Arbeitsgang durch den Durchlass
> entnehmen und schliessen.

VON DIESER PFLANZE fits too, but merely expands the same anaphor by repeating
the visible owner. VOM GLEICHEN ANSATZ is too narrow for f11r; a paragraph/topic
marker is editorial metalanguage rather than a usable workshop value.
