# GDT756 working predictions

Before using the new `Item` comparator, the pass asks three practical
questions:

1. Does `ychor` remain an invariant line starter but a paragraph-continuation
   form?
2. Do its following fields contain content + amount + process more often than
   nearby same-register continuation lines?
3. Can every following written whole receive a short replaceable candidate and
   two rivals without falling back to generic work-item language?

`Item`, `Recipe/Accipe`, `Item take`, and `De/Ad/For` remain simultaneous
whole-form candidates. `Item` should lead only if the continuation geometry and
historical examples fit better than the alternatives. Literal nouns in the
body deck remain working guesses even when they improve a line's practical
readability.

No EVA letter resemblance, substring reading, new page, f84, or f84r may enter
the comparison.
