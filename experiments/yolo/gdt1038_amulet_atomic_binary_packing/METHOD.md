# GDT1038: complete fixed terminal streams under lexical packing

Question: does one atomic-or-binary lexical rule make each entire previously
owned amulet reading compatible with its unchanged authored terminal patterns?
This is a new postexposed representation test, not new meanings or a general
syntax model. See the bounded selection decision and src/SPEC.json.

Inputs are all 71 original entries and 17 disjoint extension entries, including
full meanings, all nine original clauses and four extension clauses. There are
five complete owned inputs: original P12 projection, ZL3b and IT2a; extension
ZL3b and IT2a. RF1b has no complete paragraph in the owned GDT928 packet. A
missing reader cannot be replaced. The original projection's three uncertain
raw ZL substitutions remain a distinct input, not corrected diplomatic text.
Known saiin and qokeedy fusion conflicts motivated the rule before selection.
All input units were exposed previously; independent confirmation capacity=0.

For every dictionary key and every raw group, enumerate its atomic entry if
present and every ordered pair of nonempty dictionary keys concatenating
exactly to that group. Retain all alternatives, including same-tag but
lexically different paths. No recursive split, three terminals, edited sign,
alias, cross-space fusion or contextual exception. All88 entries are available
in either unit. Freeze this complete table before any fit. The four models are:

- ATOMIC_ONLY: use only the exact whole-group entry.
- ATOMIC_OR_BINARY: retain the atomic entry and every exact two-entry split.
- ALWAYS_DECOMPOSE_WHEN_AVAILABLE: if any lexical binary split exists, remove
  the atomic entry, even when all splits fail the subsequent fixed pattern.
- EXACT_OLD_SPACE_ONLY: require the complete old canonical raw word sequence
  and only atomic entries. Different words with identical tags do not suffice.

Original C01-C09 remain tied to their respective entire physical lines.
Extension C10-C13 are a fixed concatenated 33-terminal stream; the old
1-6/7-18/19-24/25-33 spans are terminal coordinates after unpacking. Raw IDs,
line attribution and groups remain intact; a two-terminal group may straddle
an extension clause seam, but cannot fuse words from different groups/lines.
The original nine-line whole count is the product of its nine complete counts;
a fitting isolated clause does not rescue a failing paragraph.

Primary computation constructs lexical pairs by dictionary concatenation, then
uses forward dynamic programming and backward pruning. The independent
validator constructs alternatives by every raw character cut and recursively
counts completions. Every successful lexical derivation is represented by a
lossless DAG of group/terminal-index edges with exact arbitrary-precision path
counts. No path cap or best-parse selection. Empty lexical alternatives are
reported at every source ID. Counts/edges and the entire alternatives table
are reconstructed independently. Hand-checked synthetic ambiguities test
contraction, obligatory splitting, unknowns and preserved space boundaries.

A positive result means COMPLETE_FIXED_TAG_STREAM_FIT (or lexical-path
ambiguity if count>1). Zero means NO_COMPLETE_FIXED_TAG_STREAM; unknown groups
and original source flags are separate explanatory fields, not waived errors.
Source uncertainty persists even on a fit. All fixed pattern matches are
conditional; English meanings are not inferred by the matching algorithm.
No general type inference, recovered grammar or lossless source translation
follows. Same-tag alternative lexical meanings remain inspectable in the table.

All old physical/content rivals remain as before: original E/H material
branches, rejected1023countercases, extension stone-versus-assembly attributed
claims. No physical simulator rerun, new efficacy assertion or extra semantic
exclusion. Opaque relabelings remain equivalent. No search-wide null,
significance, independent meaning confirmation, new target, reserve or image.
A whole-reader failure closes this fixed binary rule for that reader: no third
component, spelling or clause repair follows automatically.

Code, method, specification and exact input hashes are publicly registered
before table construction. The table gets a separate hash lock before fitting.
Registered code and source hashes must remain unchanged. Run --prepare once,
then --execute, then the independent validator --execute. Report the full
five-input by four-model table, every unbound group and all retained limits.
