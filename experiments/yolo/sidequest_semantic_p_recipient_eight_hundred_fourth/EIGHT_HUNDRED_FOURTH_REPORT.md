# Sidequest Pass 804: P is a recipient-directed action

into an owner-local receiver:

- after squeezing the pictured plant preparation: `cphy = P+Y`;
- at the small basin stations: `pchedy = P+CHD+DY`;
- at the lower figure in the basket-like vessel: `pchedal = P+CHD+AL`.

Four readings were compared. `HINEIN` lacks a verb in the first card;
`EMPFAENGER` produces noun piles; plain `FUELLEN` omits the shared inward
direction. The compact portable value is:

> `P = EINFÜLLEN`

The three cards then read:

- `P+Y`: **dies einfüllen**;
- `P+CHD+DY`: **einfüllen, umsetzen; Schluss**;
- `P+CHD+AL`: **an der Zielstelle einfüllen und umsetzen**.

The recipient itself is not encoded as a universal word. It comes from the
local picture owner: a vessel, basin station, or the next container implied by
the preparation sequence. `P` supplies the directed action, while `CHD`, `AL`,
`Y`, and `DY` keep their existing values.
