# GDT411 method

## Question

Can process position and attached argument type separate the remaining five
working meanings without using a new manuscript page?

## Inputs

GDT407's complete 715 statements and 5,051 focus attachments, GDT405's locked
atom values, and GDT410's nineteen-core dictionary and ten target profiles.

## Method

Extract every occurrence of the nine action heads from each full statement and
build their complete 9×9 immediate-transition matrix. For `CH`, `K`, `S`, and
`CHD`, record first/middle/last/only position, mean relative phase, common
predecessors, and common successors. For the relation `AIR`, inventory every
governing head instead. Compare three short candidates per root on role,
process order, cross-register breadth, and avoidance of local overfit. Finally,
render one complete statement per target and register with the revised
dictionary.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

This is a creative working-meaning tournament, not a lexical proof. A value may
become `KEEP` when it is the smallest useful cross-register reading and a
process-order or complement contrast removes the strongest previous rival.
`CHD` stays provisional because its strong late/Biological bias conflicts with
its genuine first-position and non-Biological uses. Local nouns, materials,
body parts, and celestial names remain supplied by the owner rather than the
portable root.
