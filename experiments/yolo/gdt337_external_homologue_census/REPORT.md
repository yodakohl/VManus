## Why the zodiac still fails capacity

The zodiac is the only target with many slots and four physical folios, but it
contains seven incompatible panel topologies. Aries–Taurus, Leo–Virgo, and
Scorpius–Sagittarius are the only repeated topology pairs, and every pair shares
a physical folio. An external 1–30 series therefore cannot fix how the target's
inner, middle, outer, central, off-circle, and split-page bands continue.

Allowing one fitted rotation, reflection, band order, or phase per sign would
turn the missing answer into model flexibility. GDT337 freezes no such freedom.

## Acquisition requirements

A later joint-tuple grounding experiment becomes legitimate only after at least
one of these genuinely new observations exists:

1. an author-visible start/direction on a new 28-slot Voynich folio or a second
   independently ordered 28-slot target;
2. a target-side band/start key for the 30-position zodiac plus a repeated
   topology on disjoint physical folios;
3. a new homologous diagram family with singular owned slots on at least two
   discovery folios and one disjoint held folio.

External source searching should now prioritize those missing target relations,
not additional count analogies.
