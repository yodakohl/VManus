# GDT388 method

## Question

What exactly must be acquired before the externally calibrated GDT387
parent-link instrument can be applied to Voynich without manufacturing its
target from proximity, array order, or the frozen grammar?

## Inputs and access ceiling

The audit uses only compact, already published, f84-free artifacts from GDT337,
GDT360, GDT372, GDT386, and GDT387. It opens no manuscript image and reads no
Voynich surface form, family, PAGE_HOST, or GDT327 tuple. The GDT360 annotation
inventory is the complete permitted source for local geometry counts. Its four
human relation channels are alternate categorizations of source assertions,
not independent witnesses.

`PROXIMITY_ONLY` is retained as the comparison state. A row enters the positive
local-relation count only when the source explicitly records attachment,
contact, enclosure, or array/group membership. `HEDGED` remains hedged.

## Endpoint distinction

Two endpoints must not be conflated:

1. a local text-to-drawing relation, where a manuscript locus is near, on,
   inside, or touching a visible object; and
2. an ordered relation edge, where an independently observable device fixes a
   pivot inscription and a distinct target inscription.

The former can support a separate visual-grounding experiment. It cannot be
fed to the GDT387 relation instrument as if the drawing object were another
manuscript locus.

## Frozen eligibility gates

An edge may enter a later score only if all of the following are true before
formal access:

1. no formal identity has been opened during selection;
2. the pivot has one exact manuscript locus;
3. the target has one distinct exact manuscript locus;
4. direction follows an authorial pointer, an externally fixed order, or a
   source-authored ownership rule;
5. both ownership mappings are singular; proximity is not ownership;
6. target identity is not reconstructible from recurrence, placement,
   boundaries, source hierarchy, or predictor features;
7. at least 50 eligible edges exist;
8. they span at least five physical folios with a preassigned whole-folio
   holdout; and
9. each pivot retains a mobile matched alternative target under the null.

The 50-edge/five-folio rule is the minimum target-capacity gate inherited from
GDT386, not a claim of confirmation-level power. GDT372 separately shows that
a medium heterogeneous grounding test is better planned around one to three
externally frozen comparisons, multiple complete arrays, and roughly twelve
discovery plus four held folios. Even a single comparison needs a matched null.

## Primary acquisition batch

The first batch is fixed as
`B01_AUTHORIAL_CONNECTOR_BETWEEN_LABELED_ENDPOINTS`. Its universe is the exact
61-page/30-folio f84-free frame in `gdt388_page_frame.tsv`, comprising every
page already represented in the permitted GDT360 annotation inventory. Review
the complete page frame for explicit arrows, lines, ducts, paths, or pointers
with separately localizable inscriptions at both ends. Cases are admitted from
visible geometry only. Ambiguous direction is `UNRESOLVED`; it is never repaired
from text order or Voynich identity.

Source-aware localization and relation judgment are separate fields in the
packet. An exploratory AI review may nominate cases, but a later confirmation
claim must disclose reviewer history and use whatever independent review
standard is frozen for that validation.

The published packet is empty because no eligible edge currently exists.
Scoring remains unauthorized.

## Exclusions

- Catalogue cyclic order is not authorial direction.
- Center-spoke geometry is not a mobile target relation.
- OPEN/BODY membership is not an inter-record edge.
- A same/similar drawing pair is not ordered.
- A visual object ID is not a target transcription locus.
- ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate readings, not replications.
- f84 is forbidden.

## Claim ceiling

GDT388 may establish only a reproducible acquisition protocol and the current
capacity gap. It assigns no parent, antecedent, syntactic role, object meaning,
word, POS, language, plaintext, or translation.
