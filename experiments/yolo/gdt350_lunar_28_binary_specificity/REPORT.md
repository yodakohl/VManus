# GDT350 external 28-member binary-presentation specificity report

## Outcome

The exact `ordered 28 + alternating presentation` tuple is **not uniquely
Georgian in the audited evidence**. The official British Library catalogue for
German Add MS 25435 states both that the book has 28 owned prophet records keyed
to positions I–XXVIII and that every other miniature has a gold background.
That is an independent non-Georgian exact-alternation counterexample to A-65's
odd-red/even-black schedule.

This materially narrows KART001: the tuple remains a strong broad system
compatibility observation, but its **Georgian cultural specificity is low**.
The small purposive panel does not estimate how common the device was within
medieval lunar, divinatory, or technical manuscripts.

No Voynich target was opened or rescored.
