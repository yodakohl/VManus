## Research decision

Do not promote dry/moist, persistence, reset, causal action or an operator meaning.
The general forward-density motivation is unsupported by this descriptive panel.
The primary CH-side composition difference remains an exploratory observation,
with position, paragraph composition, formula reuse and reading sensitivity open.
Any follow-up must isolate one such rival with a new fixed contrast; widening
lags or changing the pair inventory to obtain a more attractive result is not
licensed. No confirmatory semantic experiment has been selected by these counts.
