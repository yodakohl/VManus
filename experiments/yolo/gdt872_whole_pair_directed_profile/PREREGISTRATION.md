## Pre-extraction implementation clarification

A path tests only separators traversed between successive groups: the right
separator of the left group and left separator of the right group. Untraversed
outer boundaries of endpoints do not disqualify it. Every raw group must keep
its position, including ineligible endpoints. A source-line gap not represented
in the line table must not be silently bridged.

Micro rate is hit opportunities / eligible opportunities within the selected
anchor-side, direction, band and boundary stratum. Macro-anchor rate is the
unweighted mean of each anchor occurrence's hit/opportunity ratio over anchors
with at least one opportunity in that stratum. Denominators and anchor counts
are always emitted. Same/opposite-side hit rates use the same denominator;
conditional same/(same+opposite) is separately labeled and undefined when zero.
An opportunity is one anchor plus signed lag, not nine independent opportunities.
Response-pair tables reuse that exposure; pooled counts never sum nine copies
of the denominator. Boundary strata are descriptive, not exchangeable controls.

The all-three diagnostic filters opportunities and hits together. Physical folio
support collapses recto/verso and panel selectors to f plus the manuscript leaf
number. Matching raw sequences certifies this positional diagnostic only, not
independent witnesses or an interpretation of spaces.

Raw line order uses the source atlas source_row_index for the primary edition,
then source_group_index; it does not depend on lexical locus sorting or which
alternate edition first appears in the atlas. Extra atlas loci remain barriers.
