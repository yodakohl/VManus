# GDT392 method — special-circle start/direction census

## Question

Do any of the 45 complete, text-blind special-circle arrays contain an
author-visible start and direction tied to separately owned manuscript labels?

## Inputs

The complete published special-circle inventory supplies 504 slots in 45
arrays on 23 pages/seven physical folios. Selection is complete rather than
feature-driven. Existing GDT389 canvas mappings and byte hashes are reused.
All inputs are f84-free. No formal-family, PAGE_HOST, joint-tuple, or renderer
identity is opened. `CORRECTION.md` records that the source catalogue rows
themselves include occasional diplomatic glyph notes and were materialized.

## Method

Before focused image review, freeze all arrays in their pre-existing inventory
order. Review each array for three distinct facts:

1. an author-visible, array-local start marker;
2. an author-visible direction marker; and
3. separately owned exact label slots to which that order applies.

A decorated sector may establish a start candidate without establishing
direction. An arrowhead or asymmetry may establish direction without a
singular start. Catalogue order, clock-face descriptions, conventional
clockwise reading, radial adjacency, spokes, or the center do not establish
either property.

The frozen outcome states are `NO_AUTHORIAL_START_OR_DIRECTION`,
`DISTINCT_START_MARKER_NO_DIRECTION`,
`DIRECTION_MARKER_NO_SINGULAR_START`,
`START_AND_DIRECTION_WITHOUT_ORDERED_OWNED_LABELS`,
`ELIGIBLE_AUTHORIAL_START_DIRECTION`, and `AMBIGUOUS_OR_UNRESOLVED`.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Formal scoring remains locked unless eligible ordered relations supply at
least 50 directed edges on five physical folios and retain mobile alternatives
under a topology-preserving null. Failure is a capacity result, not evidence
that the diagrams lack meaning or order.

The claim ceiling is complete text-blind special-circle start/direction
acquisition only. GDT392 cannot establish a direction name, number, reading
order, role, POS, meaning, language, plaintext, translation, or f84 result.

## Executed outcome

All 45 arrays were reviewed. Six retain an author-visible start-only marker or
boundary: f67r1 D1; f67r2 M1, M2, and M3; f67v1 X1; and f69r E1. None has an
independent arrowhead or other visible direction. The remaining 39 arrays have
neither a singular start nor a direction marker. Therefore zero arrays license
an ordered relation, all capacity gates fail, and formal scoring remains
closed.
