# GDT527 method

## Question

Can GDT526's single `cha` stem be generalized to independently certified old
stems with transparent terminal `s→S` or `l→L` extensions, and does the
old inventory decide the inherited `keeol=K+EE+O+L` versus `K+EE+OL`
working ambiguity?

## Inputs

- GDT407's 1,558 invariant old recipes and rotating four-fold rehearsal;
- GDT516's 159 current targets and current event contexts;
- GDT517's finite candidate compiler;
- the complete GDT526 score and working revisions.

No new page is admitted.

## Stem certificate

A target must be an old prefix stem plus one visible terminal `l` or `s`.
The candidate must add exactly atom `L` or `S` at the recipe's right edge,
and the matching right/right GDT522 signature must have positive support.

The old stem itself is eligible only when at least one of these independent
certificates is present in the training fold:

1. its complete recipe has at least three distinct old surface carriers; or
2. the exact old stem already has a different one-character right child that
   inserts a non-null atom on the right.

An exact old target surface with a conflicting recipe overrides the default.

## Model ladder and selection

The ladder separates `s→S`, `l→L`, and their union. It also compares
bonus-plus-one, bonus-only, and binary `s` features. The selected card is
`1+license_bonus` for certified `s→S`, weight `0.50`, after GDT526.

The corresponding high-weight `l→L` card is not selected. At weight 2.50 it
would force six old top-one errors and lower old rank one from 1,098 to 1,093.

## `OL` working revision

Every invariant old surface ending in visible `ol` is classified by its
recipe tail. Of 112 types, 103 end in atomic `OL`, six in `O+L`, and three
in another structure. This old predominance, the five concrete rehearsal
losses `alol/cphol/okeol/qokeeol/shol`, and GDT526's existing top choice
jointly revise `keeol` from `K+EE+O+L` to `K+EE+OL`.

## Decision and claim ceiling

The selected `s` rule must improve current rank one without a current loss and
must leave every old aggregate metric unchanged. GDT527 passes. The result is
an exploratory component-recipe and working-default update, not a confirmed
word, plaintext, language, or historical codebook reading.
