These sorted unique ASCII word-type lists are the exact finite inventories used by GDT1388 and GDT1412. They discard original sentence order, frequency and punctuation. They are supplied to reproduce the wordset-bound calculation without republishing the full prepared text caches. No new license or ownership is asserted over the underlying sources.
