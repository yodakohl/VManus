# GDT1069 — first-group boundaries of 23 published plant claims

**Decision: 16 of the 23 published candidate strings both match the first
prose group and have a definite following source space in all three current
readings.** This is a narrower written-unit eligibility result than GDT1062's
IT2a-only 23/23 first-position match. It does not establish that any candidate
is a plant name. In particular f9v `fochor` is a clearly bounded first group
in all three readings, but “Viola name” and “entry address” remain equivalent.

The [method](METHOD.md) and [registration](PREREGISTRATION.md) were published
before the complete separator query. f9v's definite boundary was already
known and disclosed. The 23 source claims were selected by an external
decoder and already project-exposed. The result is descriptive, not a blind
test or a p-value.

`=D` means the public form matches the first source group and is followed by
`DEFINITE_SPACE`; `=U` matches but is followed by `UNCERTAIN_SMALL_SPACE`;
`≠` means the exact public form does not match that reader's first source
group. The order in the last column is ZL3b / IT2a / RF1b. These are alternate
readings of one manuscript, not independent observations.

The complete [69-row table](artifacts/FIRST_HEAD_BOUNDARIES.tsv) preserves
the actual first group, both separator codes, the prior exact-match status and
raw-vs-clean disagreement for every reader. Counts: 64/69 source boundaries
are definite and 5/69 weak. Reader-specific matched-and-definite counts are
ZL3b **16/23**, IT2a **23/23**, RF1b **18/23**. Of 17 claims whose form matched
all three readers in GDT1062, f10v `paiin` loses all-reader definite-boundary
eligibility because ZL3b marks a weak space; the remaining 16 retain it.
The other four weak boundaries are ZL3b f14r `pcho`, ZL3b f46v `pody`,
ZL3b f53v `tshor`, and RF1b f42v `tcho`.

This result matters only to the unit claimed. A weak gap may join material
to a longer expression; it does not refute a plant name expressed by that
longer unit. A definite gap supports separating a written group, but neither
text position nor gap width proves the group's semantic role. The f9v
Viola-like picture and the historical Jacea correction from GDT1063–1064
remain exploratory evidence for the *owner*, not a translation of `fochor`.
GDT1059's same-head/different-plant counterexample remains intact.

The [saved-row validator](artifacts/VALIDATION.json) independently re-queried
the 23 explicit first-prose loci through the selector-first guard, rejected
sealed `f84*` rows before materializing contents and reconstructed all 69
results (PASS, 427 checks). No new image, reserve, transcription rule or
meaning was used. Confirmed translated words: **0**.
