# GDT775 method — complete right words, predecessor slots, and concrete spans

## Question

Can the 327 automatic GDT774 `ol` fallbacks be reduced without returning to a
generic action paraphrase? The active hypothesis is that a subset of `ol X`
acts as one compact expression: `ol` supplies a preparation/record head and
the exact complete word `X` supplies a state, value, amount, or product form.

## Cohorts and safe loading

The fixed universe is GDT774's 376 cached reader-exact `ol` targets. The
automatic fallback has 327 rows. Removing all rows with a direct signature
leaves 311; removing the six signalless GDT773 hybrid-calibration outputs by
the general condition `hybrid_contextual=0` leaves the 305-row novel cohort.
Of those, 203 have a reader-exact immediate right word.

Right words and exactness are reconstructed through GDT769's guarded cache
provider. Its admission count remains 4,137 lines with 98 forbidden rows
rejected. The mixed ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b TSV is queried only through
`./vmanus-exp query-tsv`, with the 98 already admitted page labels and six
explicit output columns. No script directly parses that mixed source.

## The 13-word family

`RIGHT_COMPLEMENT_SPECS.tsv` fixes thirteen complete words:

```text
chy chey cheey chdy chedy sheey shedy
aiin daiin kaiin okaiin oiin olaiin
```

The 305-row novel cohort contains 66 exact `ol X` spans from this family. A
primary surface must have a GDT734 W2/W3 complete-whole card, at least three
selected tokens, three physical folios, and two `(section,language,hand)`
registers. Eleven surfaces pass and contribute 57 spans. `sheey` (seven spans,
W1) and `chy` (two spans, one register) form a visible nine-span extension.
The extension is never counted as primary evidence.

Every displayed phrase consumes the whole `ol X` span. The right-side German
string is copied from the exact GDT734 whole card; the authored fluent form
only supplies German syntax. Two scope exceptions are deliberately exposed:
`daiin=Wert III` was restricted to named/bound contexts, so its five new uses
are C0 GDT775 extrapolations. The two-span throughput extension
`dain=Wert II` has the same named-context restriction and is also C0.
Substrings such as `o`, `ol`, `ch`, or `-iin` receive no meaning from this
experiment.

## Predecessor-slot comparison

All 16,657 reader-exact adjacent bigrams in the guarded cache are rebuilt.
GDT775 authors two working control decks from earlier published whole-word
cards; the class assignment itself is not an upstream gold label. In
particular, `NOMINAL_HEAD` is shorthand for a mixed content/record-head group:
`dair` is upstream a measured-fraction field, while `pol` and `polaiin` are
entry headings, not identified nouns:

- nominal 6: `chor shor cthy dair ofchy schor`;
- field/formula 6: `ychor ycheol ychol dcheol qokchor ycheor`;
- the symmetric 8+8 deck adds `pol polaiin` and `pchor tshol`.

The decks are balanced only by number of surface types, not by outgoing edge
count, and the 8+8 deck contains the 6+6 deck rather than independently
replicating it. The raw comparison pools outgoing right-word vectors. The surface-equalized
comparison first normalizes each anchor's outgoing vector to mass one and then
averages anchors, preventing frequent `chor` from determining the result.
Cosine similarity is reported for all 221 fallback right edges and separately
for the clean 203. Leave-one-physical-folio sensitivity rows remove both
target and control edges on the omitted folio before recomputing the raw
result. The 110 rows are 55 physical folios times two nested decks, not 110
independent replications or predictive held-folio scores. A separate target-drop
audit leaves the control vectors fixed while removing `daiin`, then
`daiin|aiin`, then all thirteen rendered family types from the target. The
equalized core result flips without `daiin`; both decks flip without the fixed
family. Thus the pooled lead cannot license the 66 constructions.

The comparison has a severe position confound: 183/203 clean target edges and
263/297 core nominal edges are medial, against only 4/42 core operator edges.
The expanded deck improves operator medial capacity only to 8/56. Therefore
the result is recorded as a nominal lean compatible with placement, not as an
identified semantic role. A later route needs medial field-operator controls.

The heuristic label `TIER_A_STABLE` is assigned only when a right word recurs at least
twice, keeps the same direction in both decks, has at least a 2:1
surface-equalized mass ratio, and the winning class is supported by at least
two anchor surfaces on two folios in both decks. This is a lead label, not
confirmation; in sparse cases it can rest on two positive and zero rival
edges. Four semantically usable
two-folio wholes outside the family become a separate throughput extension:
`al`, `dain`, and `or` take the nominal lead; `chol` takes the field-operator
lead. `dy` remains visible in the census but is not rendered because its
complete-whole card is only a structural placeholder.

## Boundary and frame checks

The 376 target positions contain 94 written family words immediately right of
`ol` versus 36 left; after reader-exact filtering the comparison is 77 versus
27. Across all 19 written `ol cheey/kaiin/oiin` cases, sixteen remain separated
in all three readings. At f107r.51, f86v5.33, and f80v.31 one alternate reading
fuses `olX`; all three have a nonexact right token and lie outside the selected
66. All fourteen selected boundary-audit cases stay separated in all three
readings. The three fusions therefore supply only local segmentation
uncertainty, not positive binding evidence. These readings belong to one
manuscript.

Among 167 fallbacks with two exact flanks, 166 complete frames are distinct.
The clean cohort has 151 such rows and 150 frames; only `chey|ol|aiin` repeats,
twice. This supplies almost no repeated-full-frame evidence. Occurrence-free
dispatch instead follows from the implemented clean/exact/RHS predicate and
is checked row by row by the validator. Three selected family spans do have an
immediately preceding `ol`; they remain
visible but their occurrence confidence is lowered to
`C0_ADJACENT_OL_COLLISION__FAMILY_RETAINED`.

## Relation intake and claim ceiling

The 203 clean target edges and 369 expanded-anchor edges are emitted as a
572-row GDT388 packet. Because they are transcription-derived rather than
visually acquired, every row is explicitly ineligible; a companion crosswalk
binds every edge back to its cached source row. `check-edge-packet`
returns `VALID_ACQUISITION_NOT_SCORE_READY` with zero eligible edges.

GDT775 may publish replaceable `ol X` complete-span defaults and the descriptive
baseline nominal lead together with its position and target-drop failures. It
may not promote that diagnostic to a resolved `ol` role or identify a liquid,
substance, unit, operation, language, lexeme, plaintext phrase, EVA character,
substring, or historical word. Historical sources provide architecture only.
