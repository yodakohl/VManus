# W10 — Semantics-light structured production

W10 is a deliberately adversarial semantics-light generator. It produces a
highly recurrent, structured written carrier without encoding a domain,
lexicon, entity inventory, reference system, meaningful function, or semantic
relation. Every semantic oracle field is therefore `NONE`.

The carrier is nevertheless generated rather than shuffled. Each record has a
real construction template and record schema. A seven-state stochastic
production process selects inherited form lineages, while register, hand,
line-position, neighbouring glyphs, and a few inert historical residues control
their surface rendering. Construction scope, production-state transitions,
renderer provenance, physical hierarchy, and historical lineage are genuine
oracle facts. They are not semantic facts.

The confound is intentional: construction slots favor overlapping lineage
classes, state persistence creates local runs, and Zipf-like sampling creates
stable high-frequency groups. Contextual clipping, fusion, hand substitution,
register divergence, and fossilized insertions create families that resemble
productive morphology even though no current component has meaning. Detached
and joined realizations, field-like breaks, line resets, four registers, and
three hands further support distributional analyses that could over-interpret
the carrier.

The genealogy records seven ordered stages of semantically inert form drift.
The stages document arbitrary prototype creation, copying errors, clipping,
fusion, frequency leveling, hand-specific substitutions, and register-specific
rendering. Historical stems are form-lineage identifiers only. Fossilized
component identifiers name inherited graphic residues and carry no denotation.

Carrier targets are an alphabet of 12 symbols, completed records of 8–16
events, physical lines of 4–8 groups, predominantly 4–6-symbol visible groups,
roughly 10–18% `JOIN` boundaries, and roughly 8–16% ambiguous boundaries.
