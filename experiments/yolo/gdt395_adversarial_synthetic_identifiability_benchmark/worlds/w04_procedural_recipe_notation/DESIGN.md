# W04 — Layered Workshop Recipe Notation

W04 records multi-step transformations of materials in workshop recipes. A
record introduces a primary material and vessel, executes a stateful chain of
operations, and closes with a result condition and sealing step. Four schemas
add guarded remedies, alternative branches, or corrective repetition. Event
references point to earlier operations or results; conditional signs delimit
explicit scope. The simulator tracks material states such as raw, ground,
combined, heated, settled, filtered, corrected, and sealed.

The visible system descends through eight historical stages. Frequent function
words and operations shorten unevenly; sound change creates register-dependent
mergers and formal splits; sequence, condition, and result words become
construction markers; frequent constructions trigger initial mutation and
lexical fusion; instrumental operations are remodeled by analogy; demonstrative,
body-part, locative, and imperative material fossilizes in scope, reference,
instrument, and audit signs. Workshop, archival, field, and ceremonial schools
diverge, while three hands and physical line position create further allography.
Water and chained *heat* use restricted suppletive forms, and bounded token
variants preserve copying exceptions.

The main identifiability confounds are deliberate polyfunctionality and opacity:
identical hidden items can have joined or detached spellings, constructional
mutation is not clean concatenation, short forms merge in rapid registers,
line-edge rendering competes with grammatical conditioning, and fossilized
components no longer have productive meanings. Recurring schemas and state
transitions nevertheless provide recoverable distributional evidence.
