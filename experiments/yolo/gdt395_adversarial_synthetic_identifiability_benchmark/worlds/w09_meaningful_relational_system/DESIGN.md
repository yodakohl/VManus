# W09: The Wayhouse Tallies

## Hidden domain

W09 is an organically evolved notation used by a chain of wayhouses to plan
routes and allocate scarce stores.  A record is not a word list: it is one of
six recurrent document schemas (dispatch, allocation, gate report,
contingency, amendment, or receipt).  It names real hubs, route segments,
teams, stores, quantities, and operations.  Relators point to source, goal,
route, beneficiary, cargo, condition, alternative, or a prior record.  Gate
clauses and alternatives have explicit scope.  Operations change persistent
hidden states such as a team's location, a segment's availability, or a
hub/resource stock band.  Later amendments and receipts can refer to an
earlier record.

## Historical development

The modern forms descend through eight stages.  Frequent route relators and
record markers shortened; goal and beneficiary particles partly merged;
analogy spread a route-order ending; one inherited motion stem split by
register while two inventory stems merged; a former “hold” verb bleached into
both a reservation operator and a state gate; obsolete path and vessel
components survive inside a few unanalyzable forms; a clause marker became
polyfunctional as condition and cross-record resumption; common motion verbs
developed suppletive archive and field forms; and four schools diverged in
their treatment of joins, endings, and abbreviations.  These changes leave
fossils, opaque alternations, exceptions, and homography.

## Visible carrier and adversarial confounds

The visible inventory has twelve symbols.  Most groups are four to six
symbols, with skewed recurrence, positional abbreviation, joined boundaries,
and occasional ambiguous segmentation.  Four registers and three hands
produce overlapping variants.  Semantically unrelated lexemes may merge on
the surface, while one entity may have suppletive or register-specific forms.
Productive construction material is often fused, but historical material may
look equally segmentable.  Consequently local glyph statistics and carrier
shape alone underdetermine the real relational/stateful system.
