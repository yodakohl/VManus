# W07: The Split-Bench Measure Ledgers

## Hidden system

W07 is an organically evolved hybrid workshop notation. A record can combine
ordinary lexical fragments for materials, opaque catalog codes for individual
stock lots, productive base-six quantities, measure words, participants,
locations, reference particles, and state-changing record operators. Receipt,
issue, measurement, transformation, inventory, transfer, and correction
records have recurrent constructions but optional glosses, qualifiers,
references, and repeated inventory fields prevent them from forming a simple
factor grid.

Operators scope over complete records. Their oracle state transitions modify
lot balances at physical benches, while quantity and unit events point back to
the lot they quantify. Catalog codes point to their previous occurrence,
corrections point to earlier records, transformation relates source and result
lots, and closing marks delimit posting scope. A carry-forward operator and a
same-as relation descend from the same stem but remain semantically distinct.

## History

The genealogy has eleven ordered stages (S0--S10). Spoken workshop words were
first compounded with rank marks to label bins. Frequency erosion shortened
common transactions, numbers, and codes; analogy reshaped the unit paradigm.
A compact-register merger made receipt and destination markers homographic,
while item nouns split into free and post-code reduced forms. Catalog
reanalysis fossilized old material classifiers, and a serial verb bleached
before developing operator/relation polyfunctionality. The numbers one, two,
and six and four frequent codes became suppletive exceptions. Counter, school,
and guild practices later diverged. Relation particles now alternate between
joined and detached realization, with line position and hand-specific
allographs adding further surface ambiguity.

## Carrier and confounds

Records occupy paragraphs, pages, and variable-length physical lines. R0 is a
compact counter register, R1 a conservative measurement-school register, and
R2 a guild-audit register; three hands overlap all three. Line-initial
allographs, line-final apocope, register-conditioned clipping, optional school
dividers, joined relations, reduced lexical glosses, and opaque codes mean that
visible recurrence does not map one-to-one to either words or fields.

The intended identifiability challenge is the coexistence of several genuine
signal types: lexical repetition, code identity, quantity composition,
transaction scope, state transitions, and long-distance reference. Apparent
prefix/suffix paradigms can instead be code fossils or positional allographs;
apparent identical words can be historical mergers or polyfunctionality; and
spacing cannot reliably distinguish an affix from a detached relation word.
