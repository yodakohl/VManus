# GDT395 — adversarial synthetic writing-system identifiability benchmark method

## Scientific question

Which lexical, genealogical, functional, relational, scope, register, and
semantic properties are identifiable from a Voynich-like internal observation
regime, and which are confused with renderer, position, recurrence, organic
codebook evolution, or semantics-light structure?

This is simulator/comparator calibration only. It reads and scores no Voynich
row. Whole f84 is forbidden.

## Independence and chronology

Ten primary worlds are authored by ten isolated Sol designer sessions. Each
receives only `WORLD_DESIGN_CONTRACT.md`, its assigned broad world family, and
the shared API. It receives no other world, decoder output, Voynich semantic
hypothesis, or benchmark result. Generator sources, manifests, genealogy
schemas, seeds, and the decoder contract are frozen before blind decoding.

Decoder agents are distinct from designers and may inspect only observation
packets, the public schema, and training-free decoder interface. Oracle,
genealogy, codebook, generator source, world-family labels, and other decoder
outputs are prohibited until all claims are frozen. Sol supplies a small
high-capacity panel; Luna supplies independent replication and robustness
decoders. No decoder analyzes a world it generated.

## World panel

The frozen panel spans technical shorthand over natural language, organically
evolved codebooks, procedural notation, mnemonic notation, catalogue/index
systems, hybrid words/codes/quantities, scribal shorthand, multi-school
divergence, conservative ritual/legacy notation, and a semantics-light
generative control. At least six worlds must implement explicit multi-stage
genealogy with abbreviation, analogy, merger/split, fossilization, and
register variation. Only a minority control may expose a clean component map.

Two predeclared adversarial comparisons use the same carrier envelope while
differing in hidden interpretation: meaningful versus semantics-light, and
organic versus engineered. Carrier matching constrains page/record/line-length,
alphabet, token-length, recurrence, and separator distributions without
forcing identical strings.

## Corpus panel

Each generator produces 20 deterministic independent corpus seeds, each
targeting 8,448 visible events. Generation uses only Python standard-library
randomness seeded by `GDT395:<WORLD>:<SEED>`. Observation, oracle, codebook, and
genealogy hashes are recorded independently. A seed is one corpus replicate,
not an independent writing system.

## Observation boundary

Blind packets expose only event IDs, pages, paragraphs, records, physical
lines, group order, visible group strings, hierarchical separators, layout
roles, register/hand IDs, coarse line/record position, and explicitly marked
boundary ambiguity. They do not expose world family, source words, semantic
labels, functions, relations, entity IDs, lexical IDs, ancestry, component
meaning, construction truth, or scope truth.

The sealed oracle records those hidden fields for every event plus the full
codebook and genealogy. Visible equality and position are legitimate evidence;
oracle identifiers are never passed to a decoder.

## Blind decoder claims

Every decoder emits the same anonymous claim schema at six observation levels:

1. complete visible group;
2. inferred stable host-like identity;
3. inferred composite state;
4. inferred components;
5. short construction span; and
6. record-level topology.

Claims include event-level entity, lexical, stem, function/operator,
construction, register-variant, and semantic-category cluster IDs; predicted
reference/relation target and scope; productive-versus-fossilized component
flags; record-schema class; and world-architecture family. Cluster labels are
arbitrary and are optimally aligned only during scoring.

## Identifiability metrics

The primary matrix is `property × world × representation × decoder × seed`.
Identity/class properties use adjusted Rand information, normalized mutual
information, and pairwise F1. Relation and reference targets use eligible
coverage, top-1, MRR, and target-distance error. Scope uses endpoint accuracy
and interval IoU. Binary productive/fossilized properties use balanced
accuracy, Matthews correlation, and false-discovery rate. Record schemas and
register variants use held-seed clustering metrics.

Reported world/property decisions are not one grand mean. A property is:

* `PROPERTY_IDENTIFIABLE_FROM_INTERNAL_STRUCTURE` only when the median blind
  decoder clears its frozen property threshold in at least seven worlds,
  including the semantics-light false-positive guard;
* `PROPERTY_ONLY_IDENTIFIABLE_UNDER_SPECIFIC_WORLD_FAMILIES` when it clears in
  at least two meaningful worlds but fewer than seven;
* `PROPERTY_CONFUSED_WITH_ORGANIC_CODEBOOK_EFFECTS` when organic and
  genealogical controls yield materially indistinguishable claims;
* `PROPERTY_REQUIRES_EXTERNAL_GROUNDING` when semantic category or actual
  lexical meaning remains at aligned chance despite structural recovery.

## Method stress tests

The fixed evaluation also measures exact-composite-as-word, universal versus
world-local coefficients, residualization of frequency/position/recurrence,
scalar-role bottlenecks, fixed short-horizon outcomes, and a multi-constraint
intersection replacement. A method is a false negative when it misses a known
positive that the best blind decoder recovers; it is a false positive when it
claims the corresponding property in the semantics-light world.

## Claim ceiling

GDT395 can validate or invalidate analytic instruments and characterize
identifiability under its synthetic world families. It cannot assign a
synthetic role, ontology, or recovered meaning to Voynich and cannot authorize
GDT396 unless the particular property transfers across multiple independent
worlds and survives adversarial/semantics-light controls.
