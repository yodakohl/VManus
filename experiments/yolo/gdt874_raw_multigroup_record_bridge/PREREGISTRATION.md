## Fixed scope and units

Use exactly the GDT791 thirty-page/thirty-five-selector roster. No179-selector
extension, new page or image admission. Obtain the GDT791 line-owner atlas and
raw source-separator atlas through selector-first query-tsv, explicit allow-values,
columns and f84/f84r exclusions. Preserve source selectors and physical leaf IDs.

LOCAL_LABEL_OR_MARKER and RUNNING_PROSE are inherited line classifications.
They do not establish a phrase, clause, authorial label or pictured owner.
Circular running text is reported separately by its raw source kind; it is not
silently treated as ordinary prose. Source records never cross physical lines.

A candidate is the COMPLETE raw local record, with at least two raw groups.
Every group must equal its single clean ASCII fragment and contain only a–z.
All raw groups remain in positional counting. Reject a candidate or running
match if an INTERNAL separator is UNCERTAIN_SMALL_SPACE or starts DRAWING_.
Outer record/window separators are not traversed. Do not split, drop, normalize,
reverse or shorten candidate groups. A split fragment is not a raw word.

Find every exact contiguous occurrence of each complete eligible candidate in
every running record within the same fixed selector roster. Same-page and
other-page/other-physical-leaf occurrences are retained separately. No cross-line
search, substring match or word-order relaxation. The f81v.28 record is reported
explicitly, including disagreement with its old cleaned description; its forms
are not a filter for the exploratory all-local-record census.

Run ZL3b, IT2a and RF1b separately as alternative readings of one manuscript.
Report each reading's local-record eligibility, patterns, running opportunities
and exact bridges, including zeroes. A same-index three-reading bridge requires
the same local locus, running locus, start group, length and equal raw group
counts of the whole running line in all three readings. Each reading must match
its own complete local string. This is a coordinate-concordance diagnostic,
not three independent witnesses or a claim of identical glyph segmentation.
Also mark the stricter subset with literally identical candidate strings.

primary metadata record must have raw ZL source groups; otherwise stop as an
incomplete input rather than interpreting absent matches. Alternate-reading
missing records are reported separately. Report raw loci outside the fixed791
metadata frame per reading; they lack an eligible inherited classification and
are excluded, not silently treated as empty records. This census covers that
metadata frame, not unclassified extra source records. Check raw group indices/
counts, unique row identities and joined-selector agreement before matching.
Blank metadata records remain visible.

Counts are descriptive availability evidence. Do not compute significance,
translation scores or a GDT388 mapped relation score. Repeated labels pointing
to one running occurrence are explicit repeated references, not independent
replications. Positive candidates require separate native/provenance review
before any semantic use. Zero exact bridges do not prove that the words, their
referents or differently written phrases cannot recur.
