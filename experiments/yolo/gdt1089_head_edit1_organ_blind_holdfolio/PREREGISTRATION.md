# GDT1089 preregistration — frozen before eleven new image accesses

## Decision and predecessor

GDT1088 found a **local descriptive** specific-organ overlap in 2/6 exact-repeat first-head pairs versus 2/30 other pairs of its nine selected drawings. Its f2v/f29v lower-form omission also showed that one observer can miss a key organ. GDT768/766 forbid free `chor`/`shor` component export, GDT1080 found no visual direction for `chor`/`shor`, and GDT360's annotation scan did not establish a multi-channel semantic world. The genuinely new question is whether **one-edit whole first-head neighbours** on other physical Herbal-A leaves share distinctive organ architecture more often than fixed, same-anchor non-neighbours. A positive result would prioritize one tightly scoped form-to-image semantic family; a negative result would park this extension. Either result leaves word translation unconfirmed.

The smallest adequate acquisition is the union of all GDT1070 one-edit first-head pair endpoints after excluding heads shorter than four signs, plus the already image-admitted GDT1087/GDT1088 Herbal-A pages as control opportunities. `src/FIXED_EDIT1_PAIRS.tsv` freezes **all 25** one-edit pairs on the fixed 89-page roster; exactly **14** contain at least one page not in the previous 29-image GDT1087/GDT1088 union. `src/BLIND_IMAGE_LIST.tsv` freezes **38** Herbal-A folios; `src/FIXED_CONTROLS.tsv` freezes one deterministic same-anchor control for each pair, selected without images by closest physical-folio distance, then closest comparison-head length, then folio ID among non-neighbours at edit distance ≥3. There is no post-image substitution of target/control pages. Three old image-only pages outside GDT1070 (f46v, f50v, f55r) are excluded. f6v was already image-admitted in GDT844. Eleven new visual admissions are listed in `src/NEW_IMAGE_ADMISSIONS.tsv` and the dated scope note. No new target text, f84/f84r, f116v or reserve access.

## Fixed visual inventory

Two independent annotators receive folio IDs and official Yale images but **no head strings, pair list, source-name hypotheses, GDT1088 visual tables or pair scores**. They record YES/NO/UNKNOWN for each visible architecture, with one short note per page. Codes: `HORIZONTAL_BEADS` = a lower chiefly horizontal axis with at least three successive rounded/knobby segments; `BASAL_SWOLLEN_BRANCHES` = enlarged basal mass splitting into at least two thick lobes/branches; `RADIATE_HEAD` = a rounded/disc terminal organ surrounded by repeated narrow ray-like elements; `SPINY_ROUND_HEAD` = a rounded terminal organ surrounded by sharp pointed bracts/spines; `BROAD_PETAL_FLOWER` = one discrete flower-like organ with about five broad petal-like lobes; `MULTI_UNIT_SPIKE` = many small discrete units arrayed along an elongated flowering/fruiting axis. `FIBROUS_FAN` and broad leaf forms may be described but **do not score** as specific matches. Unknown is mandatory when pigment, overlap, attachment or stylization prevents a secure visible classification. Neither annotator assigns botanical taxa or Voynich words. Their separate tables are hashed before seeing head/pair mapping. Root has prior project exposure and may adjudicate discrepancies only in a marked post-freeze sensitivity, never silently edit primary rows.

For a pair, primary YES requires at least one of the six listed architectures marked YES on **both pages by both annotators**. A cross-annotator disagreement affecting a possible match yields UNDECIDABLE. With no possible shared feature and no relevant unknown, mark NO. Apply identically to all 25 one-edit pairs and all 25 frozen controls, then separately report the 14 newly covered pairs and their controls. Keep dependent hub pairs and repeated physical folios visible; do not count pairs as independent evidence. Taxon identity, organ meaning and a `-shor` or `ko-` morpheme are not scoring endpoints.

## Decision rule and limitations

Exploratory positive for a form-to-organ **association** requires at least four YES among the 14 newly covered one-edit pairs, from at least three distinct unordered whole-form pairs, plus a YES fraction among adjudicable newly covered pairs at least twice its paired-control fraction. If controls have zero YES, require at least four target YES and at least three target-control discordant wins. A ≤1 YES target or target rate no greater than control is a negative extension. More than one-third undecidable target pairs makes the visual-capacity result invalid; other outcomes are inconclusive. These thresholds are decision heuristics, **not significance levels**. The form-neighbour family was selected after GDT1088, so no global p-value or prospective search-wide claim is permitted. The 11 newly admitted images are new to this fixed test but some may have historical project exposure; that is disclosed, not called independent confirmation.

If positive, the only next step is to choose one independently owned meaning contrast for a separately registered test. A positive pair rate alone never validates a specific plant name, organ word, morpheme, sentence, source copy or translation. If negative or invalid, do not expand automatically to all 89 images or retune the visual categories.

Wall-time budget **six hours** from registration: 45 minutes provenance/preparation, three hours two blind inventories, 90 minutes join/adjudication and source checks, 45 minutes validation/publication. At the limit stop expansion and report incomplete cells. No OCR, image embedding, machine-generated botanical ID or visual similarity model.
