# GDT730 method

## Question

Can all genuinely unresolved global V99R3 whole readings containing a slash,
a standalone `oder`, or a quantity-label use of `Menge` receive one practical
spoken default without changing their confidence, evidence, scope or export
rights?

## Inputs

- V99R3's complete 1,586-row confidence/evidence dictionary;
- GDT631–632's CTH plant-material lattice and explicit rule that the E/O
  interfixes remain semantically silent;
- GDT637's distinct form, share, quantity-class and measure ladders;
- GDT655's distinction between a measured DAL input and an OR portion;
- GDT660–661's Y structural wholes, value series and residual seed forms;
- GDT664's equal-part and flower-drug exact wholes;
- GDT693's later `R/AR` indexed-share interpretation;
- GDT727's five active-reader artifacts as byte-parity controls;
- GDT729's canonical dictionary and fourteen already closed quantity readings;
- three inherited historical-comparator decks from GDT631, GDT632 and GDT729.

No page, image, OCR, transcription, f84 or f84r source is opened.

## Method

1. Reproduce a deliberately broad case-insensitive technical selector over
   `GLOBAL_V48_DEFAULT`: slash, standalone `oder`, or substring `menge`.
2. Record its 95 rows and 1,050 occurrences, then classify `cphol#GLOBAL` as
   the sole lexical false positive because the letters occur only inside
   `zusammengesetzter`. Freeze the remaining 94 IDs and 1,039 occurrences in
   `src/V99R4_94_AMBIGUITY_DEFAULT_SPECS.tsv`.
3. Give every target exactly one main-channel whole reading. Keep its strongest
   displaced rival in the audit rather than joining alternatives with a slash
   or `oder`.
4. Apply coherent family defaults: globally neutral plant material for CTH;
   Wurzel, Samen, Holzdroge and Pulver for the material families; `Anteil` for
   later R-headed share forms; a measured `Rohstoffposten` for DAL rather than
   an OR portion; and a single entry/value-boundary function for Y wholes.
5. Keep GDT637's `saim/saiim` quantity-class ladder distinct from cardinal
   portions, and keep GDT632's E/O interfixes semantically silent.
6. Change the two semantic-rendering fields and seven provenance/audit fields
   on targets only. Preserve score, confidence level, positive evidence,
   counterevidence, semantic scope, span, source-reading identity, export
   permission and structural/action flags.
7. Verify all 1,586 identities in order, the 324 active readings, the 1,168
   non-target global rows, the fourteen GDT729 targets, the `cphol` control and
   five active-reader artifacts independently.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A target passes only if both its main and context default contain no slash,
standalone `oder`, or unresolved quantity-label `Menge`; audit rivals remain
visible. This is an exploratory German whole-reading renderer revision. It
does not prove plaintext, language, phonetics, historical units, ingredients,
species, a universal numeral system or productive component meanings. All
historical comparisons retain zero relation credit and `H0_NONE`.
