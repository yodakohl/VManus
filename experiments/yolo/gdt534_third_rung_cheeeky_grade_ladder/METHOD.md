# GDT534 method

## Question

Does `dalcheeeky` contain the missing third-grade member of the exact old
`cheky/cheeky` K-family, even though GDT529 did not generate that recipe?

## Inputs

- GDT407's 4,576 old running events supply invariant whole cards and the
  old-grade census.
- GDT516 supplies the exact f66r target and its complete owner block.
- GDT529 supplies all twelve generated `dalcheeeky` candidates.
- GDT533 supplies the inherited 159-form working edition and scorecard.

Only the already admitted thirty pages are used. `f84` and `f84r` remain
forbidden.

## Method

1. Collapse GDT407 by exact surface only where every occurrence has one recipe.
2. Deduplicate every surface pair separated by insertion of exactly one visible
   `e`; retain it only when the recipes differ at exactly one atom by `E→EE` or
   `EE→EEE`.
3. Join the two step tables through an identical middle surface, recipe and
   grade position to enumerate complete old three-rung ladders.
4. Inspect the exact `cheky→cheeky` pair, retain its atom order
   `CH+K+GRADE+Y`, and predict only its third rung `cheeeky=CH+K+EEE+Y`.
5. Combine that predicted embedded stem with exact old `dal=AL`; compare the
   result with all twelve GDT529 candidates and the long-`e` renderer controls.
6. Revise only `dalcheeeky`; inherit all other GDT533 decisions unchanged.

The finite candidate metric is not fabricated for an ungenerated recipe.
GDT534 reports the inherited 159-target metric unchanged, plus a separate
158-target in-space scorecard and one explicit outside-space working choice.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Select `AL+CH+K+EEE+Y` only if:

- `dal=AL`, `cheky=CH+K+E+Y`, and `cheeky=CH+K+EE+Y` are invariant old cards;
- both old grade steps occur generally and at least five complete old
  E/EE/EEE ladders exist;
- the selected recipe is absent from all twelve GDT529 candidates;
- the isolated `cheee→EEE` control remains represented rather than being
  globalized; and
- no other inherited working recipe changes.

This is an exploratory local family completion and concrete working reading.
It establishes neither a global `cheee` parse nor a confirmed lexeme,
plaintext, language, historical codebook, object identity, or unopened-page
translation.
