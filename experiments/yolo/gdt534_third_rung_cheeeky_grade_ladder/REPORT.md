# GDT534 — `cheeeky` is the missing third K-grade rung

## Result

`dalcheeeky` is better read as a composition that GDT529 never generated:

```text
dal | cheeeky
 AL | CH + K + EEE + Y
```

The working literal is:

```text
ZIELORT · NEHMEN · GEBEN · GRAD III · POSTEN
```

The short working translation is **“Am Zielort nehmen und geben; auf Grad III
posten.”**

## The local family

The decisive evidence is not `cheee` in isolation but the complete right frame
`ch...ky`:

| rung | visible form | recipe | old events | status |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I | `cheky` | `CH+K+E+Y` | 9 | observed on six pages/four registers |
| II | `cheeky` | `CH+K+EE+Y` | 5 | observed on five pages/two registers |

The visible grade is written before `k`, but the learned recipe order puts the
grade after `K`. GDT529's rank-12 candidate already captured that order at
grade II (`AL+CH+K+EE+Y`); it simply failed to generate the corresponding grade
III recipe.

## The third-rung operation is not invented for this word

Across all invariant old cards, inserting one visible `e` and changing exactly
one matching grade atom gives 57 deduplicated steps:

| step | old pairs |
|---|---:|
| `E→EE` | 49 |
| `EE→EEE` | 8 |

Five complete old ladders contain all three rungs:

```text
okey    -> okeey    -> okeeey
qokedy  -> qokeedy  -> qokeeedy
qokey   -> qokeey   -> qokeeey
qotedy  -> qoteedy  -> qoteeedy
tey     -> teey     -> teeey
```

Thus `cheky→cheeky→cheeeky` is a direct completion of an existing renderer
pattern. Exact old `dal=AL` occurs 44 times, so the outer composition needs no
new prefix meaning.

## Why the previous two leaders lose

- GDT529 rank 1, `AL+EEE+K+Y`, treats `cheee` as the old long-`e` renderer and
  deletes `CH`. That is possible elsewhere but ignores the recurrent
  `cheky/cheeky` K-family.
- The inherited rank 2, `AL+CH+EEE+K+Y`, preserves `CH` but places the grade
  before `K`, contrary to both observed members of that exact family.
- The selected `AL+CH+K+EEE+Y` keeps the family atom order and supplies exactly
  the missing grade.

The contrary control remains important: old `cheeety=EEE+T+Y` really does
collapse visible `cheee` to `EEE`. Therefore GDT534 does not announce a global
`cheee=CH+K+EEE` rule. The following `ky` frame decides this occurrence.
