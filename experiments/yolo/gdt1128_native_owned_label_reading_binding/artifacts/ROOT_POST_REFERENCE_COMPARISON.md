# Root post-reference comparison: diagnostic, not rescued prediction

The existing EVA reference chart page1 was inspected after all original native
records, after target-cache access, and after the separate decision note.
No original proposal, source convention, cached reading or crop was changed.

The reference exposes a limitation of our native prediction step: first C2
loop-plus-descending slant is compatible with standard EVA l, whereas standard
q has a4-like crossing. The third C2 curved arch/descending curl is compatible
with r (s remains a reader alternative), whereas standard k requires distinct
gallows stems. Our pre-cache q/k or q/d/t assignments were not sufficiently
calibrated to EVA notation. They are not robust evidence of a wrong locus.

On the unchanged C2 crop the sequential strokes are visually compatible with
l-o-r-a-l-o-d-y, the ZL/IT cached form. This is post-reference/cache compatibility,
not an independently recovered exact reading; l/r/s distinctions and handwriting
variation remain. RF losalody is not rejected by a confident native exclusion.
GB2 is likewise compatible with k-o-l-d-a-r-o-d on the original fixed crop;
GB3 remains too pale for a reliable distinction of odalydary versus odalydyry.
Their independent whole readings remain UNKNOWN literally.

Decision: no demonstrated source/locus contradiction. Three candidate joins may
be used conditionally in exploratory hypotheses with historical position evidence
and provisional native owners, but none is promoted to independent exact native
recovery or meaning. The C2 diagnostic cannot rescue pre-cache predictions or
older SNPL002/GDT1084 failures. Before any future such literal test, calibrate
standard notation before exposing its target strings; no new decoder needed.

Reference: Gabriel Landini, Eva font reference, page1 only:
https://www.voynich.nu/hist/gabriel/eva2_reference.pdf
