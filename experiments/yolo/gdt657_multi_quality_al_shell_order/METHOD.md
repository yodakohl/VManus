# GDT657 method

## Question

Do twenty observed multi-quality AL surfaces preserve the order and nesting of
the V33 quality/material model, or do the forms behave only as memorized or
order-neutral wholes?

## Inputs

- The byte-frozen GDT656 V33 dictionary, token glossary, all-line coverage,
  complete-passage and one-unknown-passage artifacts.
- The GDT624 productive two-quality lattice and the GDT640 preparation-head
  analysis. GDT640 takes precedence over a flat split of `CHO`, `CHEO`, `SHO`
  and `SHEO` into independent quality/degree characters.
- The unchanged 179-page allow-list and guarded ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b line inputs.
  `f1r` is excluded; `f84` and `f84r` are forbidden before row
  materialization.

## Method

1. Freeze twenty complete target surfaces before rebuilding coverage: thirteen
   multi-quality core shells and seven order/nesting sisters needed to compare
   the analyses directly.
2. Count complete ZL3b occurrences, occupied lines/pages, all-reader exact
   occurrences and split-normalized occurrences for every surface.
3. Keep `CHO/CHEO/SHO/SHEO` as bound preparation heads. In particular compare
   `CHO+KAL` with `O+KCH+AL`, and compare `CH+KAL` with `K+CHAL`.
4. Add meanings only by exact complete-surface equality. Do not inherit a card
   through suffixes, prefixes, substrings or reader-only fusion.
5. Rebuild all inherited physical lines after applying the twenty cards.
   Preserve literal residual surfaces, active rivals and reader warnings.
6. Census three deliberately separate, overlapping nonleak controls:
   complete target superforms; the global short KAL/TAL/OAL/OKAL/OTAL tail;
   and the open CH/SH...AL family frontier. Never sum these controls.
7. Record seven predicted but unattested core cells as HOLD, and retain the five
   previously known CH/SH...AL surfaces as protected rather than relabeling
   them.
8. Replay the builder in a temporary directory and independently recensus the
   raw reader rows through `./vmanus-exp query-tsv`.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Eighteen cards require at least one all-reader exact anchor. `choal` and
`qokchal` may enter only as explicitly reader-unstable exploratory analogies
because ZL3b and IT2a agree while RF1b changes or fuses the surface. Every card
must keep a concrete material/quality reading, an ordered composition, a live
rival and its complete occurrence circuit.

The result is an exploratory technical-codebook reader for exact wholes. It is
not plaintext, phonetics, a language identification, an exact ingredient, an
instructional recipe or a proof that the component labels are globally free.
Structural tags remain distinct from the German working translation. No HOLD
cell, f1r statement, new page or new image is licensed.
