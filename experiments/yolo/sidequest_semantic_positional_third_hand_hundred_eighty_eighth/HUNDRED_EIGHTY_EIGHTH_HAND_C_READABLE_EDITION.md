# Derselbe Mini-Abschnitt in positionsgewohnter Hand C

## A

`dchol shey daiin | cheky dain chedy qokeedy | shckhedy | shckhedy | kain al qoky lshedy`

## C

`dchol daiin cheky okal qokey kain chedy al shey daiin dain shckhedy | qotchy qotal qoky lshedy | kain talam oldy`

## B

`talam dchol ykaiin ches | ykain ody | talam oldy | ykan cheky okal qokedy | qotchy qotal qoky rshedy`

## D

`or char qokaiin dl cho qcthey ol | chey cheeky shedal shedy | qotchor okain oiiin chdal schedy | chey qokeey chdal lchedy | qotchor cho qcthey solkeey ldy`
