# GDT429 method

## Question

Do the ten non-action roots retain separate operational meanings when compared
inside otherwise identical component frames?

## Inputs

- GDT416's 4,576 complete component recipes;
- GDT425's 5,051 resolved focus attachments;
- GDT413's current component dictionary.

## Method

Profile each root by exact position, next atom, statement opening, register,
attached action, selector, and close co-occurrence. Then replace exactly one
argument, relation, or order root with `@CORE`, preserving every other atom and
its position.

Compare all six argument pairs among Y/AIIN/AIN/OR, all six relation pairs
among AL/AR/L/AIR, and OL~OT. A shared frame is counted only when both roots
actually occur in that same frozen slot.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Keep two meanings distinct when at least one exact frame is shared and a simple
operational asymmetry still separates them: active carry versus filled value,
target versus source, connection versus path, or continuation versus next
step. Do not promote a narrower substance, object, or geometry.

These are creative working meanings, not confirmed lexemes, plaintext,
language, or source identification.
