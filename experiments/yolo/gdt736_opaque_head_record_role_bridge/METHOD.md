# GDT736 method

## Question

Once the invalid EVA-as-Latin-initial assignment is removed, do the four opaque
heads H1–H4 carry reusable record roles over the same 24 bodies, and can those
roles repair the 96-form renderer without inventing four material nouns?

## Scope and guarded inputs

The target is the GDT735 96-cell grid: four opaque heads by 24 exact bodies,
with 1,166 inherited occurrences. GDT736 reuses the byte-identical GDT635
179-page allow-list. It queries token, line, and alternate-reader TSV sources
only through `vmanus-exp query-tsv`, with every allowed page passed explicitly,
only required columns projected, and both `f84` and `f84r` forbidden before row
materialization. No image, OCR pass, new page, or new transcription is used.

The three reader columns ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate readings of one
manuscript. Their agreement is a palaeographic control, never three witnesses.

## Reconstruction

For every target occurrence the builder records page, locus, token ordinal,
line length, first/middle/last position, normalized line position, paragraph
start, paragraph-first status, page-line-one status, immediate neighbors,
reader-exact and boundary-normalized agreement, opaque head, body, and all
claim ceilings. It independently checks the published 96 cell counts,
1,166-occurrence total, 875 reader-exact total, 141-page footprint, and
946-locus footprint.

## Record-location axis

The primary split contrasts H1/H2 with H3/H4. It is summarized as raw line
position, per-body normalized-position contrasts, section-specific contrasts,
an unadjusted first-position odds ratio, an exact-only residual check, and
Mantel–Haenszel odds ratios stratified by body+section and by
body+section+cached-language metadata.

H1 versus H2 is then resolved at the paragraph level. H1 is paragraph-first at
49/135 target positions; H2 at 3/440. This licenses `paragraph/record opener`
versus `line item/subentry` as formal roles, not words.

## Cross-cutting body-affinity axis

For each head the builder constructs a 24-dimensional vector of occurrence
counts and a parallel vector of all-reader-exact counts. It calculates all six
pairwise cosine similarities. H1–H4 and H2–H3 must be the two strongest pairs;
otherwise the 2×2 interpretation fails.

The labels `form/state-heavy` and `materia/value-heavy` summarize the inherited
working body roles. The quantitative fact is only that there are two strong
cross-pairs; their semantic orientation is replaceable.

Standalone-to-initial ratios and split-only alternate-reader normalization are
retained as a secondary free-form proxy. They are not direct measurements of
baseline joining, superscript position, or graphic attachment.

## Renderer repair

The four formal roles and 24 revised body defaults generate one structural and
one aggressive pharmaceutical role rendering for each of the 96 exact forms.
The aggressive rendering is always marked as low-confidence working language.
It cannot export a head or body meaning to an unseen form.

`air` receives the grid-wide formal default `part/fraction tier II`. Only the
five previously inspected Herbal H1–air page heads retain the GDT623 visual
rival `root or underground part`; that rival does not become a global lexeme.

The 24 inherited translation spans preserve their old text for audit and add a
corrected version with the head-derived powder, seed, root, and wood nouns
removed.

## Historical comparison

Eight architectures are retained in ranked form, including the selected 2×2
record model, bound abbreviation operators, an allograph/segmentation control,
atomic wholes, descriptive-grade and prescriptive-unit submodels, a shape-gated
suspension model, and the rejected EVA-initialism negative control. Twelve
checked historical sources establish only that learned wholes, contextual
abbreviations, record hierarchy, and pharmaceutical fields coexisted. None
identifies H1–H4.

## Decision and claim ceiling

The target result may select the formal record-location axis and the two opaque
body-affinity clusters, and may issue scoped working renderings for the 96
registered forms. It may not identify an H1–H4 word, an EVA letter or sound, a
Latin initial, a physical glyph shape, a confirmed body lexeme, an ingredient,
unit, plant species, language, plaintext sentence, or portable component rule.
No new page, `f84`, or `f84r` claim is permitted.
