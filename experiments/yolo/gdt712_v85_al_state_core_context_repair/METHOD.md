# GDT712 method

## Question

Can the next fifteen AL-class and eighteen state/carrier weak readings be
given smaller, more predictive lexical cores while keeping practical local
German, explicit confidence/evidence, exact occurrence scope and the existing
family restrictions?

## Inputs

- GDT711's V84 active lexical, occurrence, complete-confidence, weak-census
  and family-evidence artifacts;
- GDT655 and GDT656 for the admitted AL/DAL material lattice;
- GDT647 and GDT685 for quality, stage and OL-carrier dispatch;
- GDT683 for the already admitted local `cheop ol` compound.

Only cached artifacts are consumed. No image, raw transcription or new page is
opened; `f84` and `f84r` remain forbidden.

## Method

1. Take exactly the 15 `AL_CLASS_RETREAT` and 18
   `STATE_AND_NEUTRAL_CARRIER` readings still held by V84, preserving their 38
   exact positions and 23-page set.
2. Store a compact lexical core separately from the occurrence realization.
   Structural uncertainty goes in `open_semantic_slots`, never in the German
   translation.
3. Use `AL = Material I` and, only in atlas-licensed sister forms,
   `DAL = abgemessenes Material I`. Ten atlas forms receive the existing
   F_AL bonus; five later exact wholes receive no segmentation or score credit.
4. Preserve visible hot/cold/dry/wet and beginning/middle/end intersections.
   Remove named `Droge`, `Gut`, dose and class heads where the active evidence
   carries only material or state. Keep `keeey`, `qokeeey` and `okol` unchanged
   because their exact-whole readings remain more informative than a forced
   decomposition.
5. Correct f115r.1#5 `cheop` to the LEFT side of the already admitted
   `G683_CHEOP_OL` span; retain `ol#2` as RIGHT and publish one non-exportable
   span renderer.
6. Rebuild the complete 1,586-reading dictionary so every row still has a
   score, level, positive evidence, counterevidence and historical status.
7. Replay all outputs deterministically and verify source-position parity,
   target score formulas, family/decomposition visibility, scope/export
   metadata, the 19 W3 readings and absence of sealed selectors.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

A score may rise only for an explicit admitted family intersection or after a
named unsupported semantic atom is removed. Prose compression and the bound
span correction alone earn no points. Components remain non-exportable; only
the complete exact surface reading can be a default.

V85 is an exploratory working dictionary, not plaintext, a language
identification or a historical lexicon. Confidence is an internal evidence
index, not a probability. Every historical status remains `H0_NONE` and GDT712
adds zero relation-derived word credit.
