# Sidequest pass 675: short-fragment cleanup

The 91 short readings were inspected separately. Seventy-one needed only
capitalization and small German word-order normalization. Twenty were genuine
fragment cases: a target, source, measure, portion, ingredient, or current item
stood alone instead of attaching to the neighboring operation.

Those 20 are now hand-bound into executable workshop clauses. Examples:

- bare AL becomes “die Zielstelle festlegen”;
- AIIN before CHD+Y+TALAM becomes “nach Sollmaß umsetzen und verwahren”;
- OR before SHED+DY becomes “den Ansatz absetzen lassen und schließen”;
- AIN before CHD+Y+OK+EE+DY becomes “eine Portion umsetzen, länger ansetzen,
  schließen”;
- AIR+Y+DY becomes the moving liquid as current item, followed by closure.

All 116 statement readings now begin and end cleanly, contain no duplicated
“danach”, and have no detected dangling nominal slot. Card sequences,
components, and per-event phrases remain unchanged.
