# GDT666 method

## Question

Can every one of the 151 distinct surfaces exposed by the V42 one-hole
frontier receive a concrete default that primarily reuses the same 47 scoped
roles, permits only a recurrent and tightly bounded new block, closes all 156
source lines, and improves full-passage readings without opening another page
or falling back to generic process prose?

## Inputs

- GDT665 V42 dictionary, glossary, full-line coverage, occurrence audits and
  fixed 156-row one-hole frontier.
- The same guarded 179-page ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b panel. No new page is opened;
  `f1r`, `f84`, and `f84r` remain outside the material.
- Independent 151-card drafts from a stem compositor, recipe practitioner,
  passage reader, and historically informed workshop clerk.
- Thirty passage-first manual readings selected from lines closed by the new
  cards.
- The inherited 47-role stem sheet and the O+L and SOL practical renderers.

## Method

1. Require the fixed 151 surfaces in the first-occurrence order of the 156 V42
   frontier rows. Recount their 612 occurrences over the guarded panel.
2. Use the stem candidate as the spelling spine, the recipe candidate for
   concrete operations, the reader candidate for sentence-local direction,
   and the historical candidate for economical circa-1420 workshop phrasing.
   Preserve disagreements as explicit rivals.
3. Prefer an existing-role composition whenever its atoms reconstruct the
   exact surface and respect their scopes. Use a sole
   `LEARNED_<SURFACE>_WHOLE` card where an internal boundary would require a
   new or contradictory role. The final synthesis has 129 role-composed cards
   and 22 learned exact wholes. One repeatedly attested learned
   action block, `oly=abseihen`, extends the inventory to 48 roles.
4. Keep structural atoms separate from German renderings. `q/qo` supplies a
   command frame, `o` a preparation frame, `ch/sh/k/t` the dry/moist/hot/cold
   operations, `r/l/cth/chor/f/p/s` broad material classes, `e/ee/eee` stages,
   and the established quantity or closure tails their scoped contributions.
   These are working roles, not phonetic readings.
5. Dispatch only exact whitespace-delimited cards. Treat the ten forms of at
   most three characters as boundary-sensitive: use a local IT2a/RF1b join
   only when the aligned alternate reading visibly concatenates the adjacent
   token; otherwise keep the free default.
6. Render every target position, close the starting frontier, recompute the
   whole V43 coverage, and compare deterministic token prose with thirty
   independently smoothed workshop passages.
7. Require exact source/card order, exact composition spelling, licensed
   atoms, unchanged non-target structural assignments, concrete vocabulary,
   input/output hashes, and byte-identical replay.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all 151 cards cover all 612 positions, all 156 starting rows are
closed, every atom belongs to the final 48-role sheet (or is the sole
exact-whole namespace), the new `OLY_STRAIN_ACTION` stays inside its enumerated
whole/block scope, every reader join is locally visible, and the builder and
validator replay byte for byte.

The pass establishes a more complete V43 workshop translation. It does not
establish plaintext, language, phonetics, a specific plant, disease, patient,
therapeutic claim, carrier liquid, historical glyph identity, or an unreleased
page. Concrete values remain replaceable when a later passage supplies a
better reading.
