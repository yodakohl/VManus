# GDT469 — every address reading now carries its provenance

## Result

The live command now answers two questions at once:

1. What does the frozen working codebook do with this visible surface?
2. How familiar is that exact ordered component recipe in the old material?

All replay layers remain exact:

- 107/107 known label readings;
- 8,280/8,280 bounded shell readings, signatures, ranks and tiers;
- 81/81 individual function, family and fallback branches.

The provenance join never changes a component, learned core or German working
reading.

## Concrete example

For synthetic unseen `otxainy` in a stellar-position owner, the reader returns:

- reading: `DANACH · [STERNSTELLENNAME:x] · ANTEIL · POSTEN`;
- exact channels: GDT466 `ot` prefix + `ainy` suffix;
- shell: GDT467 `S0234`, `ot{NAME_CORE}ainy`;
- recipe: `OT+AIN+Y`;
- support: `ADDRESS_FULL_FORMULA_ONLY`, rank 86;
- old carrier: one complete address formula, no exact running carrier.

A nonmatching `zxqv` remains `[PFLANZENNAME:zxqv]` and explicitly receives
`OUTSIDE_BOUNDED_SHELL_ATLAS`.

## Exact labels retain priority

Among the 107 known labels, 25 function traces have running-exact support,
fifteen are complete-address-only, sixteen are hybrid-shell-only and 51 lie
outside the bounded prefix/suffix atlas. The last category is not a downgrade
of exact identity: those labels still replay from their exact cards, including
whole-package and shapes not represented by GDT467's two bounded templates.
