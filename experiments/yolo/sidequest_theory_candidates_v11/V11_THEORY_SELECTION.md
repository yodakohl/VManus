## Historical reason for the ambiguity

Readable Herbal and recipe articles can contain both genuine anaphoric
expressions—roughly *it*, *this herb* or *the same*—and repeatedly named
contents such as root, juice, powder, water or a process. Once words are hidden
behind opaque exact identities, both mechanisms produce page-local recurrence,
changing neighbors and mobile line placement. The historical comparators make
both interpretations plausible; they do not separate them.
