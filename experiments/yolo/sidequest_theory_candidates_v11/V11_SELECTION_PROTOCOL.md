# Sidequest V11 Herbal topic-carrier selection protocol

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: frozen before reading V11 outcomes. This is speculative theory
generation, not a GDT result or translation.

## Fixed question and scope

Test whether the two V10 whole-card leads behave as page-/article-topic
resumption devices or as ordinary recurring Herbal content:

- `OWNER-10` — exact tuple prefix `4d455901...`, twice on f10r;
- `O56` — exact tuple prefix `2cc054357...`, four times on f56r.

Use only `f10r`, `f11r`, `f55v`, and `f56r`, with f10r/f56r primary. No page
outside the ten-page sidequest may be admitted. `f84` and `f84r` remain sealed.
Exact whole-card identity and source-native record/line/layout context are
allowed. Substrings, edit similarity, phonetics, language identification and
imported Biological meanings are forbidden.

## Required comparisons

Every candidate must:

1. enumerate all six target occurrences in their complete line and paragraph
   contexts;
2. compare them with frequency- and page-locality-matched exact Herbal cards;
3. separate paragraph recurrence, physical-line entry and ordinary medial
   recurrence;
4. check whether removing the candidate makes the remaining article unusually
   coherent or merely deletes a frequent word;
5. test whether one anonymous functional description covers both cards without
   forcing identical surface placement;
6. publish the strongest counterexample and a controlled continuous reading;
7. avoid claiming a topic function merely because a card is page-local.

## Frozen candidate architectures

1. `PAGE_OWNER` — identifies or reactivates the pictured simple/article;
2. `TOPIC_RESUME` — resumes the current page-local entity or state without
   naming it;
3. `LOCAL_RELATION` — roughly OF_IT/FOR_IT/WITH_IT at source-function level;
4. `REPEATED_PART_OR_PROCESS` — ordinary content repeatedly discussed;
5. `ORDINARY_FREQUENT_PROSE` — common word/abbreviation without a special
   discourse function;
6. `RENDERER_OR_POSITION_EFFECT` — formal recurrence driven mainly by layout;
7. an explicit mixture, provided it gives a learnable rule.

No candidate may be called a pronoun, article, plant name, demonstrative,
preposition or verb unless a separate historical expansion is clearly marked
as speculation.

## Selection rubric

| criterion | points |
|---|---:|
| exhaustive six-occurrence context accounting | 20 |
| matched-card controls and anti-frequency reasoning | 20 |
| paragraph/line/record continuity | 15 |
| compatibility with V10 open Herbal prose | 15 |
| historical plausibility in abbreviated Herbal articles | 10 |
| controlled continuous reading | 10 |
| contradictions and fixed-page predictions | 10 |

Maximum: 100. One architecture must win. If the six events cannot distinguish
topic resumption from ordinary repeated content, the required decision is
`TOPIC_CARRIER_NOT_DISTINGUISHABLE_FROM_LOCAL_PROSE_RECURRENCE`, not a forced
gloss.
