# GDT1067 — the present f69r geometry cannot determine wind polarity

Decision: `POLARITY_NONIDENTIFIABLE_UNDER_EXISTING_GEOMETRY`.

The frozen calculation found **8 exact alignments among 32 rotations and
reflections** of the prior Matthew Paris class inventory to f69r's color
inventory. For either handedness, exact offsets are 2, 6, 10 and 14. Shifting
each exact mapping by 8 of 16 positions, the 180-degree difference between
where a wind comes **from** and where it travels **toward**, gives another
exact mapping: 2↔10 and 6↔14. All eight mappings therefore have an equally
fitting opposite-polarity partner. The independent validator reconstructed
all 32 comparisons and the eight pairings.

This is an identifiability result, not a negative test of the historical wind
analogy. The previously established **12+16 wind-system family resemblance**
remains a positive structural comparison. The calculation deliberately grants
an idealized spoke-to-sector overlay; the actual outer sixteen text sectors
still lack an independently established alignment to the inner colors. That
additional ownership uncertainty cannot resolve the displayed symmetry.

IDEA000649's source-bearing versus destination-bearing contrast has **no
meaning-discriminating capacity with current inputs**. A fixed label such as
North could be assigned differently under the paired maps, so no f69r word
receives a direction name. An independent authorial orientation and a
label-to-direction owner relation would both be needed to reopen a lexical
test. A better description of the same colors or another unlabeled wind rose
does not break the polarity symmetry.
