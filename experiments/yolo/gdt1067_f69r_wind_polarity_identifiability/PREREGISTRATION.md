already rejects its literal parsed-root compounding target. Neither result

exact class matches. For each match, shift every assigned target slot by eight
positions (180 degrees), keeping handedness, and ask whether that shifted map

wind-polarity readings are observationally equivalent under these inputs.
If any lacks a partner, inspect that asymmetry before proposing a lexical
test. This is a logical capacity result, with no p-value and no new manuscript
access. Even an apparent asymmetry would require an independently fixed
orientation and ownership before naming North or a Voynich word.

correspondence are inherited; no outer-label-to-spoke alignment, written
direction, sound value, or start point is assumed. Source-versus-destination
is a 180-degree reversal of the same wind vector. Readers are not independent
witnesses. The calculation cannot establish that f69r is actually a wind

Budget: at most 35 minutes total including preparation, code, validation and
publication. If equivalence holds, review IDEA000649 as lacking a present
