# GDT378 — cross-corpus construction-level functional transfer

## Question

Does the anonymous `PREDICATE_HEAD_WITH_DEPENDENTS` behavior calibrated in
GDT376 transfer at a construction level across independent readable medieval
domains, even though GDT377 found that exact GDT327 joint-tuple identity is not
a stable transfer unit?

GDT377 is not lowered, reinterpreted, or retuned.  Its exact-tuple result stays
`NO_STABLE_VOYNICH_TRANSFER_CANDIDATE`.

## Stage A: source-only freeze

The comparator sources are frozen before any GDT378 outcome is scored.  The
included panel is:

- the six existing CoReMA collections and their hidden editor oracle;
- PCEEC2 at one exact Git commit, using parsed syntax only as a hidden oracle;
- Cambridge Curious Cures manuscript MS Add. 9308, using its public diplomatic
  page transcriptions and physical page/line/paragraph structure;
- Harleian MSS 279 and 4016 through Thomas Austin's public-domain edition; and
- *The Book of Quinte Essence* through the public-domain Furnivall edition.

Cambridge transcriptions are HTR-assisted project transcriptions served as a
diplomatic TEI/HTML view.  The tracked experiment publishes hashes and derived
form-blind observations, not the transcription text.  The Internet Archive
Harleian text is an OCR derivative of the public-domain edition and is marked
as such; conclusions may not depend on it alone.

Regiomontanus/*Defensio Theonis* is source-audited but excluded because the
available Dartmouth edition states that its materials are proprietary and may
not be used without permission.  MEMT is excluded because straightforward
machine-readable access is commercial.  Neither exclusion blocks the declared
minimum independent-domain gate.

## Hidden-oracle boundary

The scored observation layer may contain only domain/collection/record order,
physical page or line boundaries where available, token/group length, salted
opaque equality identity, recurrence, anonymous neighboring-ID diversity,
return distance, local overlap, and scope/horizon measurements.  It may not
contain source words, translations, POS, parses, semantic/editor roles,
concept IDs, function labels, or parent links.

PCEEC2 constituent labels and source words are retained only in the hidden
oracle.  The three procedural editions use a frozen, high-precision lexical
oracle for procedural heads and fixed functional classes; this is explicitly
weaker than syntax gold.  Oracle construction happens before detector scoring,
and the detector never sees the lexical forms.

## Comparator calibration

Domains remain independent.  The main outer evaluation holds one complete
domain out.  CoREMA additionally retains its six-collection folds; PCEEC2
retains source-file folds; Curious Cures retains physical page blocks;
Harleian retains the two manuscript books; and Quinte Essence retains its
editorial sections.

The fixed seven representations are:

1. absolute comparator probability;
2. within-record rank;
3. structure-minus-nuisance delta;
4. domain-standardized score;
5. scope/horizon profile;
6. neighbor-diversity/recurrence profile; and
7. one fixed equal-weight rank combination of 2, 3, 5, and 6.

All seven are evaluated; no eighth representation may be invented after an
outcome is seen.  Search correction covers all representations and functional
families.  Position, record length, surface length, collection/domain, and
frequency are nuisance controls rather than evidence.

The HEAD signature can be frozen for Voynich only if it has positive
structure-over-nuisance held gain and AUC above 0.65 in CoReMA, at least one
independent medical/procedural domain, and PCEEC2 as the non-recipe syntax
domain.  A fixed representation and threshold are selected solely from held
comparator performance.

The remaining GDT375 families begin as comparator-only secondary endpoints:
state transition, valency, REF/anaphora, correlatives, NEXT/RESUME, UNTIL,
coordination, alternative/OR, polarity/exclusion, pointer+relation,
comparison, function-word bottleneck, scope horizon, and procedural automata.
A family failed by GDT376 is reconsidered only if the cross-corpus
representation passes held domains before any Voynich scoring. After the
comparator outcomes are available but before any Voynich access, secondary
transfer eligibility additionally requires AUC >= .65 and positive
structure-over-nuisance gain in at least three held domains, including one
syntax/non-recipe domain and one procedural domain, with max-family p <= .05.
Selected detector coefficients, normalization, score transform, and threshold
quantile are then frozen. This safeguard may remove comparator leads; it may
not add a detector that failed the held comparator panel.

## Conditional Voynich stage

Only a signature that passes its comparator gate may be frozen and applied,
unchanged, to the f84-free GDT327 representation at four resolutions. A HEAD
failure does not authorize lowering its gate; separately calibrated secondary
signatures remain distinct anonymous tests:

`atomic joint tuple -> source group -> field/construction span -> recurring
grammar slot/record position`.

The target hypothesis is constructional: a stable role can be occupied by
different exact tuples.  Whole-folio folds and matched nulls control section,
register, Currier, hand, physical-line position, within-field position, record
length, tuple frequency, recurrence, and layout.  Search cost includes all
resolutions, signatures, slots, and operator families.

Any surviving class is named only `HEAD_SLOT` or another anonymous functional
class.  It is not a verb, predicate, action, POS, meaning, sound, language,
plaintext, or translation.

## Seals and exclusions

No f84 row, file, image, text, or formal payload may be opened, parsed,
retained, or scored.  PAGE_HOST substring mining, minimal-pair synonymy,
GDT003 morphology, GDT345–347 manifolds, AQ/contact, and visual substring
atlases remain closed.
