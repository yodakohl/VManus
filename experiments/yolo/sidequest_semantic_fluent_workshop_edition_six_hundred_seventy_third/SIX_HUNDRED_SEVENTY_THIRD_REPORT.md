# Sidequest pass 673: fluent workshop edition

All 116 prose statements now have a complete record-local workshop reading.
The edition retains every one of the 381 visible events and every exact card in
source order. Thirteen representative or especially important statements are
hand-polished; the other 103 use the same compositional renderer.

The renderer turns root strings into compact infinitive instructions: active
item, material, quantity, source, target, grade, process, and endpoint are
spoken in their workshop order. Repeated Y references become “ihn” after the
first mention, and a licensed DY construction explicitly closes the step.

Examples:

- H3-S001: hold the preparation at the target, wring it, hold to the prescribed
  measure, fill the receiver, hold longer, remove, close.
- H4-S001: set to measure, add a portion and a subsequent portion, close.
- B1-S012: wash, set briefly, wash briefly again, close.
- B6-S001: catch longer, dose briefly, cool at the target, continue to measure,
  take a portion, forward the preparation to the target.

Physical lines are absent from the sentence rule. The 116 inherited statement
units govern continuity; a line break neither finishes nor restarts a reading.
This edition is intentionally practical and compact rather than literary.
