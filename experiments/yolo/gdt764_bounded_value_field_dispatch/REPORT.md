# GDT764 — `H1 X daiin` is a bounded value-field grammar

## Result

The five exact triples now have one shared parse:

```text
RECORD := H1  FIELD+
FIELD  := X   daiin
daiin  := written value III
axis   := bounded or selected by the complete X whole
```

This is materially more informative than rendering three unrelated labels.
H1 supplies the record/form scope; the immediate X whole bounds the kind of
field in which III occurs. One axis is selected strongly (`qoty`), one remains
a weak provisional choice (`oteody`), and three preserve an open axis while
still receiving a usable bounded rendering (`dal`, `qopchdy`, `ofchy`).

| Exact triple | Local axis | Portable reading | Stronger C1 reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pcheey qoty daiin` | quality/grade | dry Form II: cold grade III | dry Form II: cold in the third degree |
| `pcheey dal daiin` | measured material; axis open | dry Form II: raw material I, measured; value III | dry Form II: three local measures of raw material I |
| `pchedal qopchdy daiin` | nominal field; axis open | measured dry material, Form I: `qopchdy` field, value III | finished dry preparation on stage III |
| `pcheody oteody daiin` | result/stage | dry prepared result, Form I: cooling/result field, stage III | cooled finished preparation on stage III |
| `pchaiin ofchy daiin` | open | dry index/grade III: `ofchy` field, value III | if `ofchy` is a learned content name: content class III |

All five triples are reader-exact, stand on paragraph-start lines, and are not
paragraph-final. Three lines contain a second `Y daiin` cell. The clearest is:

```text
pchedal | qopchdy daiin | chedy daiin | ...
```

That is field parallelism, not a plausible place to import an imperative. It
selects nominal syntax; it does not identify the field axis.

## The decisive `qopchdy` repair

The inherited phrase “take finished dry powder” was not portable. GDT687's
only action license for the complete `qopchdy` whole belongs to f76v.10. The
target is f105v.5, where `qopchdy daiin` is immediately paralleled by
`chedy daiin`. GDT764 therefore renders the target portably as a nominal
`qopchdy` field on value III. “Powder” and the imperative disappear. Finished
dry preparation on stage III remains a concrete C1 rival, beside amount III;
neither is silently promoted by the parallel alone.

The two extra GDT686 `dal daiin` pairs have reader-unstable `dal` under the
current exact gate, so they remain recurrence evidence but not members of the
nine-pair primary atlas. Every one of the nine current pairs had been left
`D_VALUE_OUTER_HEAD_OPEN` by GDT686. GDT764 supplies the missing immediate-X
dispatch; it does not rewrite the entire 721-occurrence `daiin` inventory.

`qoty` belongs to the productive K/T and O/QO quality grid, so its III is a
working grade. `dal` belongs to the AL/DAL material-and-measure lattice, but
GDT711 explicitly replaced its overpacked quantity head with “raw material I,
measured.” Its portable III therefore stays a value; three local measures is
only the C1 rival. `oteody` is a weak learned whole joining cooling,
preparation, and end-state, so stage III remains deliberately provisional.
For `qopchdy`, the exact parallel selects only a nominal field. `ofchy` remains
the best place to search for a learned content name.

## The four recurrent `ol` amount orders

Twelve positions realize four recurring strings:

| Pattern | N | Slot dispatch | Portable construction |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `ol s aiin` | 4 | head 2, process-patient 1, bilateral 1 | preparation: three local measures |
| `sain ol` | 4 | head 4; one keeps a left context rival | two local measures of preparation |
| `saiin ol` | 2 | head 1, context 1 | three local measures of preparation |
| `or aiin ol` | 2 | head 1, context 1 | three portions of preparation |

The raw/exact gate first removes f82r.16 (`100`), f116r.6 (`01`), and
f112v.6 (`10`), leaving twelve exact positions from fifteen raw strings. The
rule is then positional record layout, not universal sentence order:

- an amount at line start expects its content on the right;
- a medial amount expects content on the left;
- an exact following process turns the licensed content head into its patient;
- a reader-exact competitor on the preferred side makes a nonpreferred `ol`
  a second/context field;
- the fourfold exact `sain ol` whole order overrides that default once at A09,
  while retaining its exact left candidate as a rival;
- `ol s aiin ol` stays bilateral.

The association profile explains why the rules are not flattened. Left `ol`
in `ol s aiin` occurs 4/18 times against 1/140 in other eligible left slots
(lift 31.11; descriptive Fisher p=0.000568). The other three orders have much
weaker lifts and therefore still need their local competitor checks.

The strongest concrete span remains:

```text
ol s aiin oly
→ preparation, amount III; process/close field
```

The aggressive C1 rendering is “three drachms of preparation; strain/filter.”
“Three drachms” is a practical historical unit candidate. It is not
forced: equal parts and ounces remain alternatives. `ol=oil` also remains a
whole-word rival because historical *oleum* is both quantifiable and
filterable, but the target has no liquid selection, no moist preference, and
only sixteen amount contacts among 376 exact `ol` occurrences. EVA spelling
does not supply Latin-initial evidence.

## Historical fit

The existing comparison bank supplies both needed record templates:

```text
descriptive materia: name/part → quality → degree
prescriptive recipe: ingredient/preparation → unit or amount marker → number → process
```

Early-fifteenth-century examples include explicit third-degree quality fields,
drachm formulae, *ana* equal-amount formulae, and learned *oleum*. They make
the mixed field architecture historically ordinary. They do not map a
Voynich whole to a Latin word.

## What changed and what remains

GDT764 gives one target a strong axis, one a provisional axis, and all five a
bounded field rendering. It removes one imported imperative, preserves the
later `dal` quantity-head repair, and replaces one generic `ol` gloss with
twelve position-specific renderings. It does not confirm a lexeme, ingredient,
unit, language, or plaintext clause. It opens no page and passes 714 checks
plus fourteen byte-identical builder outputs.

The next useful route is the unresolved content-name slot itself: follow all
three exact `ofchy` occurrences and the second `schor daiin` field on f22r.4
through already cached whole-family and paragraph parallels. The immediate
question is whether `ofchy` predicts a substance/preparation class or another
quality dimension. No new page is required.
