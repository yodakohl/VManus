# GDT764 working alternatives

## H1 value fields

The primary working grammar is `H1 [X daiin]`: H1 supplies record scope, the
complete X whole supplies a field type, and `daiin` supplies written value
III. Live alternatives are continuous prose, a universal amount III, a
universal grade III, and a sequence of learned content names plus an ordinal.

The useful outcome is not that every X receives the same meaning. It is that
one deterministic priority order renders all five triples while preserving an
open result where the X whole has no independent role.

Expected local choices:

- `qoty`: quality/grade;
- `dal`: material/amount proposal, but the GDT711 removal of its unsupported
  free quantity head must remain binding;
- `qopchdy`: result/stage proposal with foreign action license excluded; exact
  field parallelism may select only nominality and leave the axis open;
- `oteody`: preparation/result stage;
- `ofchy`: open value or learned content-name rival.

## Recurrent `ol` orders

The primary model is a position-dispatched preparation/content head. Live
rivals are a learned oil whole, second record field, unit, and source/relation.
The model must account for all four recurrent orders and preserve the
bilateral case rather than choosing its side arbitrarily.

## Limits

All readings are replaceable working meanings. Historical records calibrate
the field architecture but receive no target-spelling credit. No new page,
image, transcription, f84 or f84r may enter the pass.
