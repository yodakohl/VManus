# GDT764 method — bounded value fields and positional amount order

## Question

Can the immediate complete whole in the five exact `H1 X daiin` triples bound
or select a concrete grade, amount, result-stage or open-value reading for `daiin`? Can
the four recurrent `ol` amount orders be rendered by one positional record
grammar rather than one universal word order?

## Inputs

- GDT763's complete 199-occurrence H1 sequence atlas and sixteen-position `ol`
  dispatch;
- GDT686's 955-value census, which left every target `daiin` axis open;
- the GDT647 `qoty`, GDT655 `dal`, GDT687 `qopchdy`, and GDT734 `oteody`
  complete-whole evidence already present in the semantic cache, with
  GDT711's later removal of the unsupported free `dal` quantity head;
- GDT760/GDT762 amount contacts and five historical register templates from
  the GDT755 expression bank;
- two source decks in this directory recording the six X-role alternatives
  and five `ol` hypotheses.

The inherited guarded query materializes 4,137 allowed cached lines, rejects
98 forbidden f84 selectors before row materialization, and skips 1,150 rows
outside the allow-list.

## Method

1. Enumerate every reader-exact occurrence of `qoty`, `dal`, `qopchdy`,
   `ofchy`, `oteody`, and the strict-control whole `chofol`, retaining two
   neighbors on each side and the complete line.
2. Enumerate every exact `X daiin` bigram. Join the `daiin` cell back to
   GDT686 to show that its prior local axis was `OPEN`.
3. Consume the five H1 triples as bounded fields. H1 scopes the record; it
   does not choose the value axis. The complete X whole has priority over the
   outer H1 body:
   - quality X -> grade III;
   - material/measure X -> value III, with amount only if independent evidence
     survives later repairs;
   - result/preparation X -> local stage III only at C1 unless independently
     selected;
   - nominal X -> value III with the axis open;
   - open X -> value III.
4. Do not carry GDT687's `qopchdy` action from f76v.10 into f105v.5. At the
   target, the exact parallel `qopchdy daiin chedy daiin` licenses a nominal
   field but does not by itself license a result/stage axis.
5. Select the twelve positions belonging to the four recurrent `ol` orders.
   An initial amount expects its content on the right; a medial amount expects
   it on the left. An exact process immediately after a licensed content head
   makes it the patient. A competing exact preferred-side head makes a
   nonpreferred `ol` a context field; bilateral `ol` stays ambiguous.
   The fourfold exact `sain ol` construction may retain its right `ol` head at
   the single medial locus A09, while keeping the exact left candidate as a
   live context rival.
6. Scan the guarded cache for raw versus all-reader-exact occurrences and
   compare `ol` against every eligible GDT760 neighbor in the same side slot.
7. Replay all generated outputs byte for byte.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The pass may assign occurrence-local axes and concrete working renderers to
the five H1 fields and twelve `ol` orders. Where the evidence selects only a
field role, the renderer must keep a default value while exposing the
candidate axis as a rival. A complete-whole role may remain as a working
theory until a better role displaces it or a counterexample makes it
impossible.

The pass does not turn `daiin` globally into either “three” or “third degree.”
The full `s/or + value` formula selects amount in the `ol` construction. It
does not export EVA substrings, Latin initials, a fixed historical unit,
`ol=oil`, plaintext, language, ingredient identity, or a new page.
