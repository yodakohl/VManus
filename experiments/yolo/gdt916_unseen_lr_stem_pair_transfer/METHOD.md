# GDT916 — new combinations versus familiar phrase families

Decision before analysis: GDT915 found extra r/l concordance for22 known
odd-leaf phrase families on even leaves. Its result cannot distinguish familiar
phrase reuse from generalization to new combinations. GDT311 supplies known
single-form licenses, GDT345 predicts opaque next states, GDT003 dictionary
cells; none is this fixed literal two-word unseen-pair contrast. This follow-up
is justified by915's positive result and a different generalization criterion,
not an attempted rescue of914 or a new suffix selected for a better score.
Budget15min for implementation, cached computation, independent verification
and publication. No wider windows, new suffixes, new key or parser.

Use unchanged GDT915 six guarded source caches, partitioned odd/even physical
leaves, all179 admitted selectors. No new source or image query. Both partitions
were exposed in915. This is prospectively fixed analysis of exposed data, NOT
fresh held confirmation. f84/f84r remain sealed; alternate transcriptions are
readings of one manuscript. Reuse915's exact token, pair and position rules.

For EACH reading separately, collect every eligible ordered stem pair on odd
leaves, irrespective of its two endings. Also collect each stem's observed
r/l ending set over all eligible odd tokens. Classify every even eligible pair:
A_SEEN: same ordered stem pair occurs in odd in any of the four cells;
B_NEW_LICENSED: ordered pair absent in odd AND BOTH individual stems each have
both r and l attested in odd;
C_OTHER_NEW: ordered pair absent, but individual two-form licensing incomplete.
No ending from an even occurrence determines its category. Preserve every pair,
source ID, four-cell count and physical-leaf count in all categories/readings.
No semantic or same-meaning assumption is made by calling two forms licensed.

PRIMARY endpoint is B_NEW_LICENSED in ZL3b. For each even leaf with at least one
B pair, Tleaf=(same-ending minus mixed-ending count)/(all B pairs on that leaf).
T is their mean. Report A and C descriptively, not as alternate winner endpoints.
Capacity: at least5 distinct B stem pairs,5 B physical leaves and5 B leaves with
at least one movable endpoint under the fixed permutation. Missing capacity is
reported separately from a nonconfirming diagnostic. No capacity or score repair.

Use the identical1024 conditional worlds from915: NumPy default_rng915000+j,
shuffle mixed r/l strata in encounter order within(leaf,stem,physical position),
discovery first then evaluation. Each token gets one ending across overlapping
pairs. Category membership is unchanged because stem pairs and per-stem r/l
margins are preserved. Compute B T in every world. A and C observed results are
retained; neither changes the selected primary criterion. A positive diagnostic
requires capacity, observed B T above its reference mean, and tail rank<=.01.
Otherwise new-pair concordance is not established by this comparison; no general
morphology refutation or assumed power. The conditional reference does not account
for all lexical dependencies or earlier project selection; no project-wide
significance or semantic claim. Its worlds overlap915 and add no independent
replication credit. Alternate-reader populations may differ; report them plainly.

A positive B result motivates a future functional contrast beyond phrase memory;
a nonpositive result leaves915 limited to selected familiar phrase families.
Even a positive result does not identify agreement, copying, names or a word
meaning. No universal ending law; all mixed counterexamples remain visible.
