# Sidequest Pass 808: procedure, stage, and collection place

Three more wall-strip values behave as portable components when their recipes,
not raw substrings, are followed:

- `O = VORGANG` (18 cards / 19 events);
- `IIN = STUFE` (3 cards / 4 events);
- `SOLK = SAMMELSTELLE` (5 cards / 7 events).

`O` combines with preparation, continuation, current item, addition, batch,
measure, holding, target, application, closure, source, passage, rinsing,
transfer, and stage. This breadth is exactly why the value is the neutral noun
VORGANG rather than WATER, OIL, or a specific operation.

`IIN` remains STUFE after ZUGEBEN, VORGANG, and ZWEIT. The observed `O+IIN`
card says **Vorgang auf Stufe** and occurs twice. This is distinct from
`AIIN=SOLLMASS`: the former locates a process phase, the latter supplies a
quantity or prescribed setting.

`SOLK` has a compact place/grade/endpoint board. Four cells are observed:
bare/open, short/open, long/open, and long/closed. Four missing cells predict
`qolkdy`, `solkedy`, `solkeeey`, and `solkeeedy`; none collides with a fixed-page
surface. `SOLK+AIIN` separately says **Sammelstelle nach Sollmaß**.

All three join core28. Only three recurrent strip values remain: `T=ANWENDEN`,
`CKH=DURCHLASS`, and `R=KÜHLEN`. Next, attack those as the last portable-root
candidates, then rebuild a fourth grammar.
