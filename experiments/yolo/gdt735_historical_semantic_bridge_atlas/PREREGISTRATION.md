## Timing and status

This is **not a blind preregistration**. Broad historical source
reconnaissance had already been performed, source families inspected, and an
initial model drafted. The user then supplied the critical correction that
`p/s/r/l` are modern EVA transcription labels, not medieval Latin letters.
The design and role specs were corrected after that reconnaissance and user
correction but **before independent validation**.

The honest designation is a source-informed, pre-validation design fix.

## Interpretation and falsifiers

A positive result may attest only coexistence of learned names, bound
descriptive fields, and separate prescriptive fields. OCR winners, abstract
role order, distributional head associations, matching Latin initials, or the
presence of powder/seed/root/wood/liquor vocabulary remain nonidentifying.

The mixed prior must be demoted if the observations do not instantiate the
stated fields, the channels cannot be separated, MS.542 lacks either direct
channel, or the result needs EVA-initial or neighbor-derived semantic credit.
Role seeds remain unresolved when they reverse across supported families,
depend on one frequent head, or require an invented patient, unit, or process.
