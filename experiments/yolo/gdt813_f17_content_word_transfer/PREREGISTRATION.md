# GDT813 design and observation timing

2026-09-05. This file consolidates the design AFTER exploratory inspection.
It is not a claim of preregistered or blind semantic success. Existing f17r
text and earlier hypotheses were known; nothing is retrospectively made unseen.

The linguistic agent wrote the two fixed N/G mappings in
src/F17_CONTENT_MODELS.md before its own renewed page comparison and before
receiving the coordinating agent's external-label review. Its initial G-first
priority and later N-first priority remain separately recorded. Both stages
use the same mappings. The coordinator knew the selected source contexts
before assembling src/SPEC.json and the executable artifact selection.

Declared comparison: otchol=this?, cthar=root?, chol=dry? and the GDT812 A
family remain identical between models. Only okaiin differs: powder? versus
is?. Preserve every word and repetition; no old hot-III or cold-dry defaults
inside the new trial. Do not claim root from a cth component or EVA initials.

Observation unit: full f17r plus all complete loci containing any exact target
whole in any reading on the 39 already admitted selectors. This choice does
not guarantee complete external paragraphs or an unbiased lexical sample.
Manual image checks are limited to f17r and the admitted f88v region.
Historical construction comparisons identify possible architectures, not
aligned Voynich words or an assumed underlying Latin language.

Pre-existing duplicate screen: route-check queries included
`f17r cthar okaiin otchol subject plant content whole paragraph historical comparison`
and `okaiin copula powder cthar root otchol demonstrative`. Relevant old
GDT628 f17r defaults and GDT809/811/812 context findings were inspected.
Lexical navigation is not proof of novelty. The new question fixes actual
candidate identities across the local line and admitted external inscriptions;
it does not rerun the old two-slot inventory as a new discovery.

No numerical semantic threshold, withheld score, dictionary promotion or
confirmed translation is claimed. Three validator mutations check omissions
and invented words only. Relation intake is recorded even when ineligible;
that status neither proves nor disproves the exploratory readings.
