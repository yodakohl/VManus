# GDT617 — triple-herbal exact-plaintext transducer

Status: `SOURCE_REGISTERED_UNSCORED`

GDT617 prepares a new external grounding route without opening any Voynich
page. It binds the official catalogue records and IIIF manifests for three
physically different witnesses of one illustrated-herbal transmission chain:

1. BnF Latin 6823, the text-bearing source witness;
2. Beaux-Arts Paris, Masson 116, whose official description says that most of
   its plant iconography was copied from Latin 6823; and
3. British Library Sloane MS 4016, whose official catalogue describes it as a
   copy of Masson 116.

Only catalogue metadata and IIIF manifests are acquired. No canvas, image,
OCR, automatic image classification, Voynich transcription, target folio, or
plant-name-to-target pairing is present in this registration.
Redirects are rejected before any follow-up request, and the acquisition
artifact records the exact six allow-listed requests and derives its zero
canvas/image/target counters from that log.

The source bytes were fixed before a parallel search exposed published
three-witness concordance candidates, but publication had not yet occurred.
Those candidate entries are therefore developmental; the Voynich target stayed
unopened and requires a separately committed target-panel contract.

The future decisive test is deliberately concrete: five source-concordant
entries on five distinct physical Voynich folios, four discovery and one held,
must be read by one global primitive-to-0..3-Latin-letter transducer. There
are no macros, page keys, folio keys, entry keys, or held edits. The held span
must reproduce its exact Latin heading and the first twelve content words.

`artifacts/REGISTERED_SOURCE_BINDINGS.json`.
