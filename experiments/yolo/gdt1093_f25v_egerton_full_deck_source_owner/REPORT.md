# GDT1093 — complete Egerton 747 source-deck screen

**Decision: `NO_THREE_PART_NAMED_SOURCE_OWNER`.** The 309 official BL Egerton
747 canvases were inspected at thumbnail scale, and eight plausible or
previously named entries at 1500px. None gives a single named plant entry
with f25v's dominant nearly flat radial blade, orange fine-rayed lower fan,
and small outlined touching creature together. There is no Voynich word or
plant-name translation from this test.

The fixed rule and scope were recorded in `PREREGISTRATION.md` before the
full-deck images were fetched. `src/SOURCE_DECK.tsv` gives 309 official source
URLs and individual hashes. `src/NATIVE_SHORTLIST.tsv` gives all eight native
judgments and observations. `artifacts/RESULT.json` applies the fixed rule;
the validator replays the decision but does not validate visual judgment.

The **Rabarbarum** image at Egerton f84v is a disclosed, retrospective *partial*
resemblance: green leaves above an orange root. Its leaves are separate, its
orange root is solid rather than fine-rayed, and it lacks the small creature.
It cannot rescue the fixed result or bind f25v to rhubarb. A new comparison
would first need a separately declared historical witness and a way to test
the unusual features rather than the common green-above-brown layout.

This is an informed single-source screen; f25v was exposed in GDT1092 and
f89r/f93v before registration. At 320px, small marks on other pages might be
missed; the eight likely entries received native-scale inspection. The BL
source deck is an external medieval herbal, not independent Voynich reserve
data. Voynich f84/f84r and all reserved pages stayed closed. The original
GDT1092 negative Sloane result remains unchanged.
