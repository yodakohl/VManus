# GDT1137 frozen finite author reading

This is a newly authored, source-inspired C0 account of the entire inherited f83r.25–30 window, using IT2a's 32 groups as the primary semantic construction. It retains all 97 native groups and every native field from ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b. It does not translate the two alternate readings completely. The assigned meanings, word assemblies and clause attachments are hypotheses; no English word or source identity has been recovered.

The core was frozen separately and acknowledged before the remaining lexicon was extended. It gives CHE a powder-form, granular feedstock and SHE a sheet-form, laminar feedstock. These are two paid material-kind guesses, not historical metal names. One common nominal function combines either material with EY, an unconverted source-preparation recipe, or DY, a surface-converted preparation recipe. Thus literal CHE+EY = CHEEY, CHE+DY = CHEDY, SHE+EY = SHEEY, and SHE+DY = SHEDY compute the same sorts of material, stage, operation and prepared-form fields. Only these four local licenses are asserted. DY in OLDY, QOKYLDDY or SHCKHEDY receives no automatic decomposition.

QOKEDY takes a converted nominal recipe and returns a product TYPE with material and operation genealogy calculated from that recipe. It is exactly the same function at .25G004 and .28G004. A type is a prescription description, not an actual produced substance, a batch or a loading event. The first and second returns are the actual objects stored in the generation history. Later SOL+CHEDY selects the earlier matching generated type after the other type has intervened. CHEEY at .29G002 is a source assertion of the selected patient's actual material field; QODY then derives a further dry-setting prescription from the same patient's material and operation fields. No parallel powder-type register is seeded for the late consumer.

## Connected hypothesized reading

The following English gives the finite attachment choices; punctuation is editorial and physical lines are not claimed to be sentences.

- **.25**: Prescribe a closed chamber. Specify fine powder-form feedstock, with surface exposure; specify its surface-converted product type, and retain that preparation in the enclosure.
- **.26**: Describe the fine powder-form charge with surface exposure and a covered condition. Consider the contrasting converted sheet preparation recipe, prescribing prolonged covered treatment for it.
- **.27**: Resume the first product type and its converted powder recipe. Prescribe a closed chamber, and prescribe a renewed contact-treatment step, followed by another renewed contact-treatment step.
- **.28**: Turn to the other preparation case, with powder-form feedstock as the comparison material. Describe the sheet source preparation; specify its surface-converted product type, then set that specification aside.
- **.29**: Return to the earlier converted powder type. Its source preparation is powder-form feedstock. Prescribe further dry-setting of that returned type, require a dry friable finish, and set this further specification aside.
- **.30**: Then prescribe closed enclosure, reaffirm the closed-chamber condition and covered condition, and close this preparation specification.

This is a connected recipe/type account. It is not a claim that these instructions were performed, that the endpoint was achieved, or that the proposed materials possess these physical properties. Surface exposure means source surface available to the prescribed conversion within the enclosure; it does not require an open chamber or add an ambient-air/fluid event. Coverage concerns the enclosing chamber. Surface exposure, enclosure, coverage, duration, contact steps and finish are emitted requirements. A specification can mention a material or preparation before or after discussing another stage; it does not quietly supply a physical vessel, fluid, batch or event.

## Reference consequence with an unchanged lexicon

Under **explicit-earlier-type**, .29G001 returns the actual type generated by `IT2a|f83r.25|G004`: powder-form/granular material, operations `[surface-convert]`. Under **latest-product-only**, the same SOL call returns the actual type generated by `IT2a|f83r.28|G004`: sheet-form/laminar material, operations `[surface-convert]`. This already disregards SOL's embedded CHEDY material restriction (`written_type_matches=false`). The executed rival stops at .29G002; QODY and the remaining tail are recorded as not reached. This is a logical conflict with the paid written restriction/source assertion, not a claim that sheet material physically cannot be dry-set. It also is not an independent reason to prefer this English lexicon.

The selection restriction and the following source assertion both identify powder material in this candidate. The latter is partly redundant evidence within the authored semantics; nevertheless its computation genuinely reads the returned patient and would reject a different material. QODY additionally carries the actual returned operation path into its output. The reference difference is a consequence of the frozen lexical assumptions, not an empirical discrimination between meanings.

## Costs, dependence and remaining freedom

The account declares 23 distinct primary lexical rules: seven core words and sixteen extension words. There are two material-kind primitives, two nominal-state primitives, three core operator senses and one late source-predication rule. Seven further primitive senses license QO, SO, KEEDY, QOL, OT, Y and EKY locally. Eleven extension forms are whole residuals: QOKEEY, OTAL, QOKY, TOL, QOKYLDDY, DAIN, SHCKHEDY, SAIIN, OLDY, KESD and SAIRN. Ten literal whole assemblies are licensed (the four material/state nominals, SOLCHEDY, QOLCHEY, OTCHEY, CHEEKY, QOKEEDY and SOKEEDY). All 32 source-aligned clause attachments, two reference policies and twelve scope/default choices are recorded in AUTHOR_ACCOUNT.json. There are no spelling aliases or semantic overloads; changing the active attachment of QOKEEDY/OLDY/QOKY is expressly paid in the binding table.

These declaration counts are not a minimal economy score or calibrated cost of English choices. The senses, fine grade, material shapes, operation labels, discourse attachments, repeated-contact interpretation, opening shared-method scope and type-only ontology remain substantial author freedom. No broader grammar or compositional parser has been recovered. WORD_PRIORS is descriptive provenance only; CHEDY/SHEDY and other frequent forms do not thereby acquire these operation meanings elsewhere.

The chronology is explicitly asymmetric. CHEDY.25 precedes the first CHEEY at .29. SHEDY already appears at .26 before SHEEY.28 and its QOKEDY type declaration at .28. Neither is a supplied input-before-output physical event pattern. The late EY predicate names the source of the earlier type; it is not a late physical input. CHEY in QOLCHEY and OTCHEY uses the separately purchased Y fine-grade criterion, so there is no inserted E or donated CHEEY identity.

A fresh batch and a persistent batch of this same type remain equally compatible: no written instance identity rule was purchased. The program constructs types and prescriptions, and does not model actual material execution or establish consistency with physical chemistry. The finish requirement is not evaluated as a measured fact. Successful code execution proves only that the chosen finite semantic attachments can be evaluated as a connected account.

## Reproduction interface

`python src/author.py --output DESTINATION` deterministically emits the complete account from the bound native JSON and frozen local core. It does not query transcription, images, reserves, raw target sources or a corpus. `execute(policy, producer_overrides, materials, states, return_overrides)` supplies a small read-only diagnostic API. Part interventions can alter material/state inputs while holding remaining inputs fixed. Producer overrides alter only actual returned QOKEDY objects; return overrides alter those same objects immediately before the late selector, leaving later lexical expectations fixed. A rejection at the first affected source/recipe/selector check is preserved rather than manufacturing an executed tail. Such probes audit dependency; they are not new manuscript evidence.
