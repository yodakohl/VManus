# GDT360 existing-annotation joint grounding report

Status: **EXPLORATORY_SINGLE_CHANNEL_LEADS_ONLY**.

GDT360 reused the existing annotation corpus rather than producing another AI
description. It found one precise postselected lead in an already exposed
channel, but no independently supported multi-channel world and no semantic
role.

## What was joined

The guarded source audit retained 1,161 non-f84 exact human annotation rows.
Of these, 815 have source-native family-consensus coverage, while only 27 have
GDT327 exact joint-tuple coverage. The wider executable GDT327 representation
therefore cannot currently carry the label-grounding problem without an
unsupported backfill.

After expanding the explicit visual axes but canonicalizing each
channel×locus, the inventory has 4,607 cases and 4,536 formally covered case
rows on 771 unique loci. These are not 4,607 independent manuscript samples:
many are alternative visual questions asked of the same locus, and every exact
human relation/object row descends from the same Stolfi source layer.

Sixteen endpoints were scored against 306 globally distinct state-blind formal
masks. Per-endpoint identical masks were collapsed and retained as aliases.
The published atlas contains the top 25 of 2,699 scored endpoint/predicate
rows per endpoint. No image or catalogue was opened and no new visual judgment
was made.

  alternative-bearing locus in the 26-row panel;

Array detail matters. Both f89 CONTACT rows carry AQ/AQA. In f99v/L1 it occurs
on three of four CONTACT and one of four GAP rows; in f99v/L2 it occurs on zero
of one CONTACT and one of five GAP rows. Neither f100 array carries it. Thus the
held-f100 gain is a prediction from feature absence, not a new positive
occurrence.

This earns `INTERESTING_EXPLORATORY`, not confirmation. It is a newly isolated
correlation in the already exposed CONTACT/GAP data, not a prospective result;
it has only two supporting folios, one section/register, a tiny exact
opportunity orbit, and negative search-paid evidence. `AQ`/`AQA` is not assigned
CONTACT, attachment, plant, material, operation, or any other meaning.

## Why the joint worlds do not pass

Twenty-three compact multi-channel summaries survive the permissive screen;
16 have nominal positive selector-paid sums, but **zero** qualifies as an
independent interesting world.

The largest raw example combines family component `J` with the broad
STAR_OR_SKY context and the star-tail endpoint. It gains 44.61 raw held bits
and 27.45 after the literal rule selector, but all 41 scored star-tail loci are
already inside the wider star/sky object-context panel. It is therefore a
nested-target summary, not independent evidence. Other high-scoring worlds are
dominated by source length and overlapping zodiac/object panels. All are
labelled `WEAK_POSTSELECTED`; every latent role remains `UNASSIGNED`.
