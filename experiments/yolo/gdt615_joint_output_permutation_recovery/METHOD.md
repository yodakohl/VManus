# GDT615 method

## Question

Can the fixed GDT614 role/output deck be assigned to its same-role primitive
positions so that all 64 directed merge paths are train-coverable with no more
than eight hypothetical changed subtrees, can a full train world then be built,
does that committed world transfer unchanged to held, and can it pass the
oracle and recovery gates?

## Inputs

GDT608 supplies the 64 directed merges. GDT612 supplies the 98 recursive unit
records. GDT613 supplies the frozen Latin train, held, LM-fit, and LM-confirm
partitions. GDT614 supplies the fixed V2 role assignment, output deck,
historical macro licenses, transition catalog, and exact 18-versus-8 failure
for one arbitrary binding. No Voynich target or semantic material is an input.

## Method

The 34 primitive roles remain fixed. Complete output cards are permuted only
within role: 18 literal values, four syllabic values, three prefixes, three
suffixes, two connectors, two context values, one macro, and one empty layout
card. Length travels with the output card; no output string or role is added.

An exact finite solver represents these eight role-wise bijections and all 64
recursive raw renders using carrier train only. For each merge, either its raw
render must be a train substring, or one of at most eight selected bound nodes
must lie in that merge's inclusive recursive merge-DAG subtree. Mapping and its
canonical minimum-cover certificate are then frozen. This is the permissive
GDT614 necessary gate, now solved jointly rather than after an alphabetical
binding; it does not yet choose actual paid cards or locations.

The solver first maximizes the number of raw train-supported merges, then
minimizes the paid-subtree hitting number, then fixes the lexicographically
smallest complete primitive/output table and canonical minimum cover among tied
solutions. No frequency objective or adaptive fallback exists. A candidate is
accepted only after an independent solver replays its exact minimum, proves
the train `<=8` formula SAT, and excludes a better global objective value.

Only then does the unchanged GDT614 V2 machinery use train only to select the
actual eight paid locations and four-short/four-macro assignment, construct
ordered Latin parses, tile them by top-level 98-unit records, and cover all
cards, merges, paid children, macro licenses, focal families, and 21
transitions. W0 and the two seeded contrast worlds are all selected on train
alone and committed as one bundle before held is opened once. Any failure
terminates GDT615 without trying another mapping or world.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

`NO_EIGHT_HIT_BINDING` requires a complete exact UNSAT proof over every
role-wise permutation. A timeout or solver `unknown` is `SEARCH_INCOMPLETE`,
never a rejection. A mapping-level pass is not a truth-world pass; every later
GDT614 gate remains mandatory.

The experiment may select or reject only this synthetic binding/generator and
recovery stack. It assigns no Voynich unit, sound, language, word, plaintext,
object, operation, or meaning and forbids f84/f84r.
