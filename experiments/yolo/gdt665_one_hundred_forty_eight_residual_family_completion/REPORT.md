## The decisive split: the same shape can have a positional role

The strongest correction in this round is that `s` cannot carry one global
meaning. Initial or internal `s-`, before its own grade or closure, remains
`Saatgut`: `saiin`, `sain`, `chsey`, `kesey`, and `chsdy`. An *absolute final*
`-s` instead marks a finished charge or pharmaceutical species. This yields:

- `chos`: a charge of dry preparation, not seed;
- `okos`: a charge of hot preparation;
- `qokees` / `qokeees`: heat the preparation charge to the final / last stage;
- `oees`: preparation charge at the final stage;
- `oteos`: cold-preparation charge at the middle stage;
- `lols`: a charge of wood material in the preparation;
- `as`: an equal part of the pharmaceutical species.

This is not a convenient relabeling. `chos` occurs thirty times; five of those
lines mention seed separately, and none requires `chos` itself to mean seed.
The same positional discipline separates initial `d-` “measure a dose” from
absolute final `-d` “close”; “draw off” remains a contextual rival.

## A register of compounds plus learned wholes

The 148 cards do not behave like a simple substitution dictionary. V42 needs
both predictable constructions and learned entries:

| card class | forms | positions | role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| productive compounds | 55 | 442 | shared material/process/quantity parts |
| learned action wholes | 77 | 524 | recurring compact commands |
| learned nominal wholes | 10 | 14 | preparation or quantity entries |
| entry composites | 1 | 1 | anaphoric access to a preparation |
| boundary-sensitive forms | 5 | 13 | local reader join or free default |

The 47-row stem model now distinguishes, among other things:

- `d-` dose/measure versus `-d` close, with drawing off retained as a rival;
- `s-` seed versus `-s` charge/species;
- `qol-` learned “add drug material”, not mechanically `q + O + L`;
- `-ol` material/result carrier versus naked learned `ol=Grundansatz` and
  initial O+L wood-preparation forms;
- `y- + process` as an anaphoric imperative (“do this to it”);
- `aiir` as fraction III and `-in/-iin/-iiin` as forms II/III/IV;
- `ckh` as a composite-preparation head.

The final cross-role audit materially changed the first synthesis. It removed
an illegally embedded naked-`ol` reading from 60 `okol/okoldy` positions and
deleted unsupported “one portion” wording from all 92 `ykeey/ykaiin`
positions. It also replaced four nonexistent composition roles, removed three
rival annotations that had leaked into atom strings, and converted four
unsplit over-compositions to learned exact wholes. Every remaining declared
atom is licensed by the 47-role sheet and its spelling reconstructs the full
surface exactly once.

## The useful concrete ladder

Several frequent forms now occupy different practical slots rather than
collapsing into “process material”:

- `dor` — measure out a portion of drug material;
- `ykeey` — heat this to the final stage and close it;
- `ykaiin` — heat this to stage III;
- `dshedy` — soak a dose to the middle stage and close it;
- `ody` — finished preparation;
- `okol` — hot preparation material;
- `okoldy` — finish the heated preparation material;
- `okeeol` — preparation material heated to the final stage;
- `rain` — root drug in charge II;
- `lorain` — wood portion in charge II;
- `chory` — plant part in base form.

`ody` deliberately does **not** include straining. Two lines put a separate
`oly=seihe ab` after it, and the quantity frames `dal aiin ody`, `or aiin ody`,
and `am ody` treat it as a usable result. Likewise, `oreeeg` remains the learned
whole “long-extracted and strongly reduced root extract”; it does not export a
global `g=grain` rule.

## What the working translation now says

The automatic channel remains token-aligned. The manual channel turns the same
cards into readable workshop prose. Five representative complete passages are:

`f105v.22`

> Nimm drei Maße sowie drei Dosen Trockenansatz auf mittlerer Trockenstufe. Gib
> eine erste abgemessene, bis zur Mitte getrocknete Rohdrogenportion und die
> zweite Fraktion hinzu, erhitze den Ansatz auf Stufe III und trockne ihn
> vollständig.

`f116r.32`

> Erhitze den Holzdrogenansatz vollständig. Gib Wurzel der Charge II hinzu und
> weiche bis zur Mitte ein. Nimm eine Drogenportion mit drei Teilen, weiche
> nochmals ein und gib Grundansatz, getrocknetes Drogenholz sowie eingeweichte
> Wurzel hinzu. Erhitze vollständig, behandle kalt-trocken auf Stufe II und
> seihe den Holzansatz ab.

`f19r.7`

> Erhitze den Trockenansatz kurz. Nimm eine erste Blatt- oder Krautfraktion,
> miss eine Portion davon ab und trockne sie leicht an. Das Ergebnis ist die
> erste abgemessene Fraktion.

`f78r.31`

> Erhitze den Saatgutansatz vollständig. Nimm ein leicht angefeuchtetes
> Arzneikompositum und erwärme es bis zur Mitte. Gib Rohdroge I auf kalter
> Anfangsstufe hinzu, weiche bis zur Mitte ein und füge die erste Kaltfraktion
> sowie fertig getrocknete Holzdroge bei. Bereite die Krautdroge fertig auf.

`f81r.21`

> Weiche eine Dosis Droge bis zur Mittelstufe ein und schließe sie ab. Gib
> Rohdroge I auf heißer Anfangsstufe hinzu, erhitze den Holzdrogenansatz
> vollständig, kühle bis zur Endstufe, lasse die Holzdroge bis zur Mitte
> ziehen, kühle nochmals mäßig und gib Holzstoff hinzu.

These are concrete recipes in the working model: they contain seed, root,
wood, leaf or herb drugs, pharmaceutical composites, charges, fractions,
portions, measures, heating, drying, soaking, cooling, explicit straining, and
finished preparations. They still do not name the carrier
liquid, a specific botanical species, a disease, a patient, or a therapeutic
effect. Those meanings must occupy later cards; they are not hidden inside
generic “raw drug”.

## Reader boundaries

Five forms are boundary-sensitive over thirteen positions. Four local joins
are accepted because an alternate reader supplies the exact adjacent whole:
`chol|keechy→cholkeechy`, `ckhy|dom→ckhydom`, `shok|or→shokor`, and
`cho|kesey→chokesey`. The `otolaiin|o` candidate is a label and remains a label.
Every other new card dispatches only on the exact whitespace-delimited form.

## Working basis for the next round

V42 is not “the solution”, but it is a materially better basis than V41:

1. every new surface has a non-generic practical default;
2. positional homographs are explicit instead of silently contradictory;
3. high-frequency operations and preparation results now occupy different
   slots;
4. compounds coexist with learned commands and quantity sigla;
5. all candidate drafts, source choices, rivals, passages and replay artifacts
   are retained, so a better later meaning can replace one card without
   erasing the route that produced it.
