# GDT665 method

## Question

Can the 148 distinct surfaces exposed by the V41 one-hole frontier receive
concrete recipe meanings that agree with a single scoped stem inventory, close
all 156 source lines, and improve the practical reading without reverting to
generic process prose?

## Inputs

- GDT664 V41 dictionary, glossary, occurrence audit, full-line coverage and
  one-hole frontier.
- The inherited guarded ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b panel. No new page is opened; `f1r`,
  `f84`, and `f84r` remain outside the material.
- Three independently developed 148-card candidates: recipe-practitioner,
  stem-compositor and passage-reader.
- One independent 46-row stem candidate and one independent 30-passage reader
  draft.
- The synthesized `CARD_SPECS.tsv`, `STEM_MODEL_SPECS.tsv`, and
  `MANUAL_PASSAGE_SPECS.tsv` used by the builder.
- The inherited O+L and SOL practical-renderer specifications.

## Method

1. Verify the inherited safe panel and require the fixed 148 surfaces to occur
   exactly 994 times in the same order as the 156 V41 frontier rows.
2. Compare the three independent card sets. Use the stem-compositor set as the
   structural spine; prefer a recipe or reader value when it produces a more
   concrete operation and does not break the shared stem scopes.
3. Keep structural tags separate from German words. A composition records the
   current explanation of a whole card; it does not authorize arbitrary
   substring replacement elsewhere. Every declared atom must belong to the
   final 47-role stem sheet (or be the sole exact-whole namespace), and the
   declared atom spellings must reconstruct the surface exactly once.
4. Split only positionally licensed homographs: initial/internal `s-` is seed,
   absolute terminal `-s` is a charge or pharmaceutical species; initial `d-`
   measures a dose, absolute terminal `-d` closes or draws off. Keep learned
   wholes such as `ol`, `ody`, `qol-`, `dom`, `keem`, and `olom` explicit.
5. Dispatch cards by exact whitespace-delimited surface. For `keechy`, `dom`,
   `shok`, `otolaiin`, and `kesey`, accept a boundary join only when a locally
   aligned IT2a/RF1b reading has that exact concatenation. Otherwise use the
   free whole-card reading; labels remain labels.
6. Render the 994 occurrences across all 4,128 inherited physical lines, close
   all 156 starting lines, and compare the automatic token channel with thirty
   manual recipe readings.
7. Require concrete vocabulary, exact source-line identity, unchanged
   non-target structural assignments, positional `s/d/y/qol/ol` scopes,
   input/output hashes, and a byte-identical temporary-directory replay.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all 148 cards and 994 positions are present, every starting line
closes, all boundary joins have local reader support, the 47 stem rows and
thirty passage rows are exact, no generic work-language remains in a new card,
and the complete build replays byte for byte.

The pass establishes a coherent V42 working translation layer, not a decoded
plaintext. German values remain replaceable defaults. It does not identify a
language, phonetics, exact plant, disease, carrier liquid, historical glyph, or
unreleased page.
