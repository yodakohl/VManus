# Method

Repeat the GDT852 qualitative spacing question at a separately fixed, already
transcribed location in the same owned paragraph: f75v.47 groups5/6 versus the
internal split in group10. Preserve the original photograph and source indices.

A local difference can establish that a literal spacing contrast is visible.
It cannot establish that the split and joined expressions have the same
meaning, different meanings, or the same grammatical attachment. No whole-word
value or morpheme is assigned. The lexical worksheet remains an unselected
three-entry hypothesis whatever the image result.

Programs verify hashes, explicit records and the fixed conjunction only.
They do not verify palaeography. No numeric distance or statistical significance
is claimed. Prior whole-page exposure and root knowledge of the transcript
are retained as limitations.
