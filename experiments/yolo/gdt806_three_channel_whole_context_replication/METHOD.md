# GDT806 method — three-channel whole-context replication

## Question

Can six complete GDT805 wholes choose between fixed, equally wide practical
role rivals in two disjoint projected context decks, with exact local cells and
repeated whole frames supplying separate corroboration?

## Inputs and scope

GDT805 supplies the 1,086-event discovery-subtracted atlas, its audited
75-surface narrow projection deck and seven real repeated two-sided frames.
GDT734 supplies the broader exact-whole working dictionary; GDT739 supplies the
axis regexes and macro groups; GDT754 and GDT738 supply mandatory quarantine
and HOLD masks. No new page, image or transcription is read.

## Channel construction

The method partitions mapped immediate neighbours into exact GDT739 source
cells, non-source occurrences of the 75 narrow surfaces, and a global residual
deck that excludes those surfaces. The global deck is built fail-closed from
W2/W3 unconditional complete-whole readings with zero composition/component
credit, no retired literal patient words, at least one axis match and no later
quarantine, HOLD or target membership.

Each contact is binary within QUALITY, SCALAR, CARRIER and PROCESS. Raw and
pair-sequence-stable macro rates use only the mapped contacts in the relevant
channel and side as denominators. Each candidate contains one fixed L1 macro
and one fixed R1 macro, so no candidate receives a signature-width advantage.

## Decision and interpretation

Candidate scores are the mean of their L1 and R1 macro rates. Narrow and
disjoint-global residual deltas must agree in direction, exceed 0.05 in raw and
stable views, retain direction in at least 80 percent of leave-one-folio-out
stable folds and remain un-reversed in the recombined full-global sensitivity.
The exact channel can select only if it independently reaches bilateral
capacity; otherwise it is annotated but underpowered.

The seven fixed repeated frames are joined only after channel adjudication.
They can sharpen compatible whole-role wording but cannot create a lexeme.
Axes in every channel descend from prior German working renderers, so GDT806
may select a practical rival preference but no plaintext, historical word,
component, substance, process identity or translation.
