# GDT778 method

## Question

Can 29 legacy singleton-family complete right words be promoted only at every
reader-exact `ol + whole` occurrence, replacing generic output without reviving
the rejected letter-name/component dictionary?

## Inputs

The final 376-row GDT777 renderer is the fixed parent. GDT734 supplies only the
identity of legacy singleton cards; none of its retired literal patients is
support. GDT736/GDT737 supply corrected body candidates, GDT738 supplies their
transfer status, and GDT758/GDT759/GDT768/GDT769/GDT772 supply the five newer
complete-whole corrections. The authored 29-row deck freezes one default and
two rivals before the runner.

No mixed transcription source is parsed. Exactness, line text, page label,
physical folio, and token identity come only from the already guarded GDT777
renderer and its locked transitive cache.

## Method

1. Load and hash-check the fixed sources and require exactly 29 unique complete
   right surfaces in `EXACT_WHOLE_SPECS.tsv`.
2. Join the deck to all 376 GDT777 rows by the complete `right_surface`. Keep
   every reader-exact match and separately emit every nonexact exclusion.
3. Preserve an inherited structural frame when present. Compose the new whole
   meaning after `und` or within `Ansatz:` where appropriate; do not call an
   unchanged `chol` display a sharpening.
4. A selected fallback consumes its complete right token. Same-row ownership
   takeover is allowed for a token already consumed by that row; a cross-row
   collision is forbidden. Count unique token IDs across both layers.
5. Emit the 39-row atlas, exactness exclusions, 376-row renderer, working
   dictionary, changed passages, provenance audit, compact result, and an
   explicitly ineligible GDT388 acquisition packet plus crosswalk.
6. The validator independently reconstructs form-wide selection and counts,
   checks semantic/source quarantines, runs the relation-packet gates, and
   byte-replays every generated artifact and the report.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The fixed expectation is 39 exact loci/29 forms, 32 fallback replacements,
five actual contextual sharpenings, two contextual confirmations, coverage
163→195, fallback 213→181, and 155 unique consumed right tokens. Any
first-occurrence-only selection, nonexact admission, substring export, or
cross-row consumption collision fails.

Success licenses only replaceable exact-`ol + complete-whole` working meanings.
It does not identify EVA characters, substrings, language, plaintext,
historical abbreviations, a specific substance, disease, treatment, or recipe.
New pages, images, OCR, transcriptions, `f84`, and `f84r` remain forbidden.
