# GDT1164 prospective independent validation

Prepared from METHOD/SPEC before public-release permission and before reading source payloads, geometry, rankings or gold. No builder/fitter/scorer imports are permitted. This validator may inspect their serialization contracts after independent scientific implementation. Authoritative scientific rules remain METHOD/SPEC, not this plan.

## Phase 1: pins, source reconstruction and capacity

Verify public registration bytes and every declared input hash first. Reconstruct only permitted NUREMBERG books from the blinded diplomatic stream and blinded sites; reference expanded-line selection must test book/corpus before materializing payload. No held-book expanded text enters its reference panel, no unblinded site truth is read.

Independently reconstruct first-appearance record order, original line order, exact whitespace tokens, held-book first-overflow stop, and other-book round-robin first-overflow stop. Check complete-record conservation, panel sizes and record boundaries. Independently select frequency-supported vocabulary, opaque permutations, context vocabularies and selected marked-site identities. Verify marker/site ordinal joins before using exact-span eligibility. Count every excluded/unsupported class without gold. Check all four required per-book capacities and preserve a failing fold; never shrink vocabulary or select later records to repair it.

Geometry reconstruction counts every center and signed offset inside its own record. Verify context totals, full-center marginal denominators, PPMI rows, zero norms, cosine diagonal/clipping/mean normalization, sixteen off-diagonal quantiles and selected-node order. All selected rows must pass capacity. Compare anonymous published arrays and receipts; raw strings/metadata/marker flags must not enter the fitter.

Stop model authorization at NO_CAPACITY or INVALID_SOURCE_BINDING. A stopped study still receives a complete source/capacity validation, not a claimed ranking test.

## Phase 2: independent numerical fixture and fit audit if eligible

Before actual fits, use a small deterministic positive-mass fixture to compare the four-index GW definition with the matrix expression, row sums, column and joint KL terms, and gradients (finite differences or independent analytic derivatives). Independently reconstruct F from full-panel rates and B from unequal-size distance fingerprints; verify mean scaling, including zero mean. The fixture tests arithmetic, not recovery.

For observed folds and all19 worlds, reconstruct both global token permutations and unchanged record-length reinsertion. Check frequency/vocabulary/opaque-ID invariance and recomputed geometry. No world may be dropped. Reconstruct all three initial logits per model, fixed200-step Adam and post-step objective selection where feasible; at minimum independently replay a declared complete fold/model fit and audit every serialized restart objective/ranking against its saved parameters. Any uncompleted replay is reported explicitly, never certified as full independent optimization. F reuse requires identical input/output hashes.

Verify each row ranking and tie order, seed selection, finite values, all80 fold/world combinations, and prediction/code/payload lock preceding gold access. Raw text, lexical IDs, old expansion eligibility and gold labels may not enter inference.

## Phase 3: scoring and decision only after prediction lock

Only then read pinned unblinded site truth. Independently join every eligible selected marked site throughout each held book, including outside-fit records; preserve exact strings and count multiword/empty/OOV as errors. Reconstruct each form's gold mixture, every model/world top1/top5/MRR, equal-type then equal-book aggregation, marked coverage and the post-lock oracle ceiling. No best-sense selection or gold-based eligibility.

Recompute all19 G-minus-max(F,B) gains and inclusive null rank, plus the .50 top5, .10 gain,3/4-book strict-superiority and .05 rank gates. Check every retained failure and the final operational decision. The three baseline/geometry families and current source scope are fixed; no tuning or larger-source repair is allowed.

## Scope of a validation result

Implementation correctness does not identify a source language, an actual Voynich writer, any English word or calibrated target confidence. Previous project exposure and cross-book copying may persist. Capacity failure is not a semantic rejection; optimization success is not global identifiability. Record procedural deviations and incomplete independent checks explicitly.
