# GDT789 preregistration

Before scoring, GDT789 fixed the following route:

1. Target the longest-ending `*ar` family and assign `*dar` to its longer,
   already tested tail.
2. Use all 47 `Xar/Xor` pairs with at least two exact tokens on two physical
   folios per arm.
3. Report the 31-row high-support cohort and a separately defined, partly
   overlapping 31-row historical-exclusion sensitivity cohort.
4. Predict `Xar` only from complete-whole profiles as
   `Xor + ar - or`; compare with `Xor` and five mechanically selected clean
   learned wholes.
5. Do not reuse the GDT788 `al/dal/ar/dar` square as score evidence.
6. Require orthogonal R/N transfer on the fixed seven-row RN12 and six-row
   RN23 grids; these are separate surface contrasts, not disjoint samples.
7. Mask the complete AR/OR/R/N tail union, the GDT788 mask, and inherited AR
   semantic lineage from neighbours and donors.
8. Keep PART, AMOUNT, VALUE, MATERIAL, PREPARATION, PROCESS, and CLOSE separate;
   a concrete role classifier must recall every anchor class at least .50.
9. Treat separated/fused forms and Stolfi only as boundary evidence with zero
   semantic credit; validate the relation packet before reporting it.
10. Give every observed complete target form a replaceable working default,
    evidence, counterevidence, confidence, and three rivals, but export no
    substring or unseen form.

Portable transfer requires at least 21/31 wins against both primary controls
in both 31-row cohorts, plus at least 5/7 RN12 and 4/6 RN23 wins against all
controls.  The allowed outcomes are `PORTABLE_AR_ROLE_CANDIDATE`,
`AR_OR_SHELL_BOUND`, or `WHOLE_ONLY`.
