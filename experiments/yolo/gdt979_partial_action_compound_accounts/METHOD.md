# Fixed partial actions, not a fitted state inventory

The preregistration and SPEC define the entire family. A paragraph is a finite
program with exactly three semantic states and six declared partial actions.
One action has a different transition in the separately fitted rival. No new
state, action, exception or word value is learned to repair a passage.

A used atom is assigned a nonempty substring of length 1–3 when it is first
encountered at a genuine prospective atom boundary. Each subsequent occurrence
must use that exact substring. Pairwise prefix-incomparability makes parsing
under a completed dictionary deterministic. Enumerating every currently legal
atom and every 1–3-character prefix at first use therefore covers every legal
used-atom dictionary. Spaces require one to three complete atoms on each side;
line wraps do not reset state. The atom count and final OA/L conditions apply
to the whole paragraph. Limits produce UNKNOWN, never exhaustive rejection.

Every word must begin with a dictionary initial and end with a dictionary final.
Six dictionary entries can thus supply at most six distinct first characters
and at most six distinct last characters. Twenty-four nonempty atoms also imply
at most 24 groups and 72 characters; three atoms per group imply at most nine
characters per group. These are necessary conditions only. Nonliteral or
uncertain source paragraphs are not rejected as historical messages.

Two saved partial dictionaries merge only when every shared atom agrees and
all their codewords remain prefix-incomparable. Every unassigned atom can be
completed by a distinct unused initial letter: six entries cannot use all 26
initials. This establishes existence of a six-entry completion without
pretending that unused values have been recovered. Joint witnesses must use
distinct physical leaves and have opposite permission-presence flags.

Main and debt-canceling models each fit their own dictionary. More permissive
or isomorphic noninstitutional interpretations remain possible. The six state
labels, institutional account premise, complete paragraph boundaries and code
limits are assumptions; the source-inspired semantics are not identified
manuscript meanings. Source-only controls check computation and the model's
conditional contrast. They are not a null for the entire historical search.

The independent validator does not import the runner. Registration controls,
source/case coverage, full witness replay, separate bounded enumeration and
joint pair coverage have distinct outputs. Any validator cap stays unresolved.
No validation status is a translation success.
