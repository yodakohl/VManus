# GDT391 method — frozen local text-object relation normalization

## Question

Can the complete source-defined units containing existing strong local relation
observations be normalized into singular author-visible object IDs and
reproducible geometry states without consulting Voynich formal identities?

This executes GDT388 batch B02 as a separate grounding endpoint. It is not a
parent-link test and may not be imported into GDT387 as an inscription target.

## Complete-unit frame

The input is the f84-free, provenance-bound GDT360 exact-human relation
inventory. Four channels are used: explicit attachment, contact/overlap,
enclosure, and array/group membership. They are alternate categorizations of
one Stolfi source layer, not independent witnesses.

Selection is by complete source unit, not by attractive row. Every `array_id`
with at least one strong relation enters, and every relation locus in that unit
is retained, including `PROXIMITY_ONLY` and hedged rows. The resulting frozen
frame contains:

- 1,059 source relation rows collapsed to 335 unique loci;
- 94 loci with at least one strong source relation;
- 44 positive-bearing source units;
- all 180 loci in those units;
- 21 pages on 12 physical folios;
- 23 within-unit mixed relation/proximity units on eight folios.

The loader retains only the source/visual prefix of the GDT360 artifact. Formal
coverage suffix fields are not parsed, retained, or displayed. No Voynich
surface, source family, PAGE_HOST, tuple, or renderer value is available during
normalization.

## Normalization pass

For every frozen unit and locus, review the complete page and source-nominated
object region. Assign a neutral page-local object ID only when one visible
object or bounded region can be isolated. Record:

- singular, multiple/alternative, non-individuated region, proximity-only, or
  unresolved ownership;
- `ON_OR_ATTACHED`, `CONTACT_OR_OVERLAP`, `INSIDE_BOUNDED_REGION`,
  `ARRAY_OR_GROUP_MEMBER`, `PROXIMITY_ONLY`, or ambiguous geometry;
- a neutral topology class such as discrete point/star-like object, bounded
  region, linear contour, drawing unit, or array/group region;
- source evidence, crop/page provenance, hedging, and ambiguity.

The normalized ID is an audit handle, not an object name or meaning. Proximity
alone never becomes ownership. A source comment may nominate a region for
review, but the reviewer must retain ambiguity rather than force a singular
object.

## Capacity and stop

GDT391 performs acquisition only. Formal scoring remains prohibited.

A later separately frozen visual-formal test is authorized only if the census
contains at least 50 singular positive relations on at least five physical
folios, with at least ten mixed units supplying mobile within-unit comparators.
Every eligible row must have a neutral singular object ID, a visible or explicit
source-owned non-proximity relation, and a matched alternative in the same
source unit. Failing capacity closes only this normalized object-relation
application.

## Provenance and claim ceiling

The intended review is source-aware and exploratory. Many pages were viewed in
earlier experiments, so this is not observer-pristine blinding. A later
confirmation would require separately frozen reviewer standards. No OCR,
automated image recognition, CLIP/embeddings, or captions are permitted.

f84 remains forbidden. GDT391 can establish only local text-object geometry,
ownership normalization, and capacity. It establishes no object meaning,
label gloss, parent/reference relation, word, role, syntax, POS, language,
plaintext, or translation.

## Executed outcome

All 180 loci on 21 pages were reviewed under the frozen schema through 20
allow-listed official canvases. Sixty-one positive rows on eight folios retain
a singular neutral object/region, but 48 are the two f68 star arrays on one
physical folio. Only four positives in four units/four folios also have a
same-unit, same-topology singular `PROXIMITY_ONLY` comparator. The matched
minimums of 50 rows, ten mixed units, and five folios therefore fail. No formal
score was authorized or run.
