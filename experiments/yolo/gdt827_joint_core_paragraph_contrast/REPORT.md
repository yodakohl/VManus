# GDT827 — reconsider the semantic core, not only its neighbours

Status: C0_CORE_UNDERDETERMINED_NO_MODEL_WINNER. No dictionary replacement.

## Main outcome

Four distinct eight-whole models were compared over the same four known text
windows. The inherited physical-transformation account no longer has a
protected water/becomes core in this comparison. The exercise does not show
that it is false: it shows concretely why the polished f77r.35 sentence is
insufficient to prefer it. Two independent agents supplied countermodels;
required route reading exposed prior meanings, so this was NOT blind.

The motivating ZL/IT source is unchanged:

    solkeey okaiin chedy qokain sheedy qokaiin chedaiin chealy

Two illustrative grammatical German arrangements:

- TRANSFORMATION: Dessen Dampf wird Wasser, wenn die Luft kalt ist.
- FLOW: Dessen Wasser durchfließt das Becken, wenn das Rohr offen ist.

Both require the same speculative possession and clause arrangement; five
of eight exact glosses change together. Both are completions of a known
line, not recovered sentences. Articles and German reordering are explanatory,
not separately decoded groups. RF instead has qotaiin and che@152;aiin, so
neither complete sentence transfers unchanged to that reading.

NETWORK independently proposes outlet/its/joins/channel/where/basin and
leaves the last two wholes unknown. Its relation is contact between coexisting
physical endpoints, not a change of substance or physical liquid flow.
MANUAL instead proposes rub/press/stroke/hand/foot/skin/on/it. It leaves much
of the showcase line unknown and tries to explain repeated action sequences
elsewhere. These are different whole-core hypotheses, not eight synonyms for
generic work. Full definitions/debts are in the two agent notes and the
32-row CANDIDATES.tsv, with C0 confidence for every model/whole entry.
