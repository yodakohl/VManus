## Finding

A coherent *topic-level rival* to matter transformation is a description of
connected places: basins, their openings and the channels joining them.
Its crucial distinction is spatial contact between coexisting entities,
not conversion of one substance into another. This is worth stating as a
falsifiable alternative, but the present two text windows do not establish
its dictionary or supply a complete relational grammar. I do not recommend
promoting it above the matter-transformation model.

The most concrete eight-whole trial is:

## Strongest rival and an observation that would distinguish them

The strongest competitor is the inherited physical-material account:
qokain water?, qokaiin air?, chedy becomes?, solkeey vapour?, okaiin its?,
sheedy if?; it can attempt a state-change reading of f77r.35 and, with further
guesses, material-to-fire at f81r.20. Its appeal is an intelligible process
schema; its debts include exactly the unknown referents, genuine doublets,
alternate boundaries and completion-selected meanings recorded in GDT819.
The present network account does not dissolve those debts and adds new ones.
The visible conduit is compatible with transport of matter undergoing
transformation, so it is not a discriminating observation by itself.

A concrete distinguishing observation would be an independently author-owned,
repeated caption or explicit cross-reference linking the exact chedy relation
and its two written participants to a drawn connector between two simultaneously
depicted bounded structures, with those participant identities separately
anchored. Under this fixed account, chedy predicts *contact between distinct
coexisting endpoints*, qokain predicts the bounded connecting passage rather
than its contents, and qokaiin predicts the container/place rather than air.
Under a fixed material-becomes account the participants instead need evidence
of source/result material identity or temporal/state distinction; a literal
channel/container ownership relation would oppose those exact senses.

An especially useful contrast, if independently admitted and author-owned,
would distinguish a labeled empty connector from the liquid within a filled
connector: retaining qokain on the connector in both cases supports channel?
against water?; naming the liquid while the separately labeled connector is
unchanged supports water? against channel?. This is a proposed falsifier,
not an assertion that such a contrast exists on these images, nor permission
to obtain new pages. A new caption cannot be selected because its wording
fits the hypothesis; selection, capacities, held folios, provenance and mobile
nulls must precede scoring.

No currently inspected word has that authorial ownership. Current conclusion:
spatial-network description is a concrete alternative worth retaining at C0,
not a successful reading and not a reopened image-topology result. No complete
translation, semantic winner, finite POS or new edge follows.
