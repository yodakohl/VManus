## Fixed comparison
Within EACH reader, strata are section, Currier, hand, half length, last character
of U, first character of V, and physical line-position class (single/start/end/middle).
Within a stratum preserve the full multiset of U and the full multiset of V;
permute only their pairings. This preserves their inner strings, marginal counts,
the seam characters and the chosen nuisance metadata, but not arbitrary longer
spelling or lexical restrictions. Conditional exchangeability is an assumed
reference model, not an established manuscript production process.
Statistic T is the token count U=V. For each stratum compute exact rational
expectation and variance of T under all uniform token permutations. Report T,
E, Var, T-E and number of strata with positive variance, plus every diagonal
source group. No Monte Carlo p-value, significance claim or semantic score.
Variance is conditional permutation dispersion, not uncertainty of a population
estimate. Repeated tokens and physical leaves are not independent evidence.

Repeat the same arithmetic after dropping each physical leaf (f<number>, merging
recto/verso and panels), rebuilding margins in the same fixed strata. These are
sensitivity diagnostics, not held-out prediction. No parameters fit to outcomes.
