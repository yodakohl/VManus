# GDT473 method

## Question

Can the 76 formula-side GDT459 events and the 107 completed GDT472 label
readings be returned to one source-ordered local-address edition without
changing an old formula, losing an owner, or allowing one repeated surface to
carry conflicting working readings?

## Inputs

- GDT459's 183-event source-ordered address interlinear;
- GDT466's final 107-label character accounting;
- GDT472's complete 107-label empirical template assignments.

## Method

1. Preserve every GDT459 event ID, page, locus, source order, owner, surface and
   content class.
2. Preserve the 61 tier-A, seven tier-B and eight tier-C GDT459 recipes and
   readings byte-for-byte.
3. Replace only the 107 former tier-D whole-label defaults by the matching
   GDT472 source reading and recipe, joined through the unique source event ID.
4. Carry the exact GDT466 function/learned character counts for those labels.
   Formula-side events are fully functional by their existing GDT459 status.
5. Class each final event as a complete function formula, function/name hybrid,
   owner-family/name card, or wholly learned name.
6. Collapse all 183 events to 162 surface cards and require identical recipes,
   readings and coverage classes for every repeated surface.
7. Emit the complete TSV and a page-grouped human-readable edition.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The edition is complete only if all 183 source events occur once and in order,
all 107 label joins are exact, all 76 formula decisions are unchanged, every
event has a nonempty default, and all repeated surfaces are invariant. `ykyd`
and `yddy` remain exact-package-only even though their complete source readings
are printed.

This is a consolidated creative working translation over already admitted
pages. It adds no page, component meaning, surface spelling, occurrence,
individual object identity, plaintext, language or confirmed lexeme. Learned
name placeholders and structural family tags remain distinct from English or
German word translations.
