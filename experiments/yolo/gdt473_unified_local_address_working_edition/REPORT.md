# GDT473 — unified local address working edition

## Result

The address sidequest now has one complete, source-ordered working edition.
All 183 admitted local-address events receive a default reading:

| final architecture | events | distinct surfaces |
|---|---:|---:|
| complete function formula | 94 | 73 |
| function shell + learned name | 87 | 87 |
| owner-family marker + learned name | 1 | 1 |
| wholly learned name | 1 | 1 |

This is the important consolidation step. GDT459 originally kept 107 entries
whole because arbitrary segmentation was unsafe. GDT460–GDT472 subsequently
earned bounded internal readings for those exact entries. GDT473 returns those
later results to their original locations without retroactively changing any of
GDT459's 76 formula-side decisions.

## What is now readable

The unchanged formula side contributes 76 events and 332 fully assigned
characters. The completed label side contributes 107 events, of which eighteen
are now full formulae and 87 contain a functional shell around a learned name.
Across the complete edition, 774/1,045 surface characters (74.07%) belong to a
working function channel and 271 remain learned name material.

Only two events lack a translated function component:

- `cheosdy` retains the structural family statement
  `cheo:DROGENFAMILIE · [DROGENNAME:cheosdy]`;
- `oiil` remains `[PFLANZENNAME:oiil]`.

Those are useful learned defaults, not gaps in the edition. Structural family
membership is not silently promoted to a word meaning.

## Exact packages remain exact

`ykyd → POSTEN · GEBEN · POSTEN · HIER` and
`yddy → POSTEN · HIER · POSTEN` are printed as complete readings because their
whole cards are reconstructed. They remain the only two nontransferable
exact-package formulae. Their overlapping/package-specific segmentations are
not generalized to unseen spellings.

## Surface consistency

The 183 events collapse to 162 distinct surfaces. Sixteen surfaces recur in 37
events, including `otar` four times, `ar`, `ary` and `okaiin` three times each,
and twelve two-event forms. Every repeated surface retains one recipe, one
working reading and one coverage class. There are zero conflicts across pages
or registers.

## Practical consequence

The readable artifact is now the best compact answer to “what does our current
translation say?” It preserves the manuscript order and makes every residual
name visible instead of hiding it behind the old generic `STERNSTELLENNAME` or
`DROGENNAME` defaults.

The next useful creative pass is grammatical rather than another stem hunt:
collapse adjacent entries into their visible locus/owner bundles and try three
plain-language readings of each bundle—as coordinate, ordered instruction, or
catalogue qualifier—while keeping the nineteen roots and all learned cores
unchanged. That can tell us what kind of sentence the address strings are
trying to be without opening another page.

## Validation and ceiling

The validator passes 48/48 checks, including exact source order, exact 76/107
route joins, all character accounts, page totals, the 16 repeated-surface
families, both exact-package restrictions and a byte-identical rebuild.

No new page, component meaning, surface spelling, occurrence, object identity,
plaintext, language or confirmed lexeme is claimed.
