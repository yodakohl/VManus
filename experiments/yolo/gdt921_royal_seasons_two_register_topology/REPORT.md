**PARTIAL_SOURCE_TOPOLOGY_NO_OWNED_VALUES.** The fixed Royal19CI f54v source does contain four seasonal personifications, four head-adjacent labels and four angularly offset inscribed spokes. Two separately frozen observations agree. This is a more specific external comparison than the earlier BnF15171 diagram without people. Complete readable, uniquely co-owned label values and a Voynich mapping are not established.

The exact catalogue-selected IIIF canvas, image URL and SHA256 are in src/SOURCE.json and src/CANVAS.json. Public preregistration79e0c7a6 preceded visual inspection.

Root initially called this a month ring; that description is **withdrawn**, with the original frozen packet preserved and POSTFREEZE_AUDIT.json recording the correction.

A separately frozen same-source regional reading by B finds the repeated date-like frame `le [numeral] iorn de [ending]` on the four spokes. Its terminal-word attempts are set[?], iu[?], ma[?] and dece[?]; exact numerals and expansions remain unresolved. Root's independent NW attempt also recognized the iorn-like phrase but did not certify a date. The source supports boundary-date inscriptions as a plausible interpretation, not four exclusively owned intrinsic qualities. This distinction is worth retaining; it does not assign day, month, season, quality or direction to any Voynich word.
