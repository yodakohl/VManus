# GDT669 method

## Question

Can all 37 distinct surfaces in the fixed GDT668/V45 one-hole frontier receive
concrete workshop defaults while preserving exact surface order, the guarded
179-page panel, alternate-reader evidence, and the distinction between
productive roles and learned whole cards?

## Inputs

- GDT668 `REPORT.md`, `RESULT.json`, page allow-list, V45 glossary,
  dictionary, coverage, complete passages, one-hole passages, and the fixed
  37-row newly exposed frontier.
- The same guarded ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b panel: 32,339 materialized tokens on 4,128
  physical lines. `f1r`, `f84`, and `f84r` remain excluded.
- The 37-card final specification, the inherited O+L and SOL render revisions,
  a 56-row stem sheet, and twenty manually smoothed complete passages.
- Independent stem, apothecary, and historical-scribe candidate readings.
- Independent final card and twenty-passage audits; the passage reader supplied
  six narrow corrections, and the root consistency pass added two instances
  of the same process-direction repair.

## Method

1. Deduplicate target surfaces in first-occurrence order from GDT668
   `NEWLY_EXPOSED_ONE_HOLE_LINES.tsv`; require exactly 37 surfaces and recount
   exactly 165 occurrences on the guarded panel.
2. Dispatch every new card only on exact whitespace-delimited surface identity.
   The three short forms `sh`, `ro`, and `odl` may use a local alternate-reader
   merge only where the aligned reader visibly supplies it.
3. Prefer the inherited 54 scoped roles. Add only the recurrent preparation
   blocks `OY_PREP_BASE` and `OKY_HOT_PREP_BASE`, producing a 56-row V46 sheet.
4. Keep `lkchdal` and `eeckhy` as learned exact aliases. At f105v.13 both
   alternate readers write `lkeedal` for ZL3b `lkchdal`; at f51r.7 both write
   `chckhy` for ZL3b `eeckhy`. Their meanings may follow the known reader card,
   but their ZL3b spellings may not be assigned invisible atoms.
5. Preserve the high-frequency moisture spine:
   `sh=SH_MOIST`, `shek=SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+K_HOT`,
   `shear=SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+AR_FRACTION_I`, and
   `shedaiin=SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+D_MEASURE+AIIN_III`.
6. Require `olkair` to begin `O_PREP+L_WOOD`, not bound `OL_MATERIAL`.
   Preserve the visible `tcheodal` order as cold → dry → prepare → measure raw
   drug I.
7. Compare all twenty manual passages independently with the deterministic
   renderer and apply only explicit token-order or process-direction repairs.
8. Rebuild glossary, dictionary, occurrence audits, complete passages, and the
   next frontier; then replay every artifact in a fresh temporary directory.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all 37 cards fill all 165 previously open positions, all source
frontier rows close, every productive composition is drawn from the 56-role
sheet, the learned set is exactly `{eeckhy, lkchdal}`, non-target structural
projections remain byte-stable, and the temporary replay is identical.

The result is an exploratory recipe-register work theory. It does not identify
plaintext, language, phonetics, a plant species, carrier liquid, disease,
patient, cure, or historical glyph expansion. Concrete German phrases remain
replaceable defaults.
