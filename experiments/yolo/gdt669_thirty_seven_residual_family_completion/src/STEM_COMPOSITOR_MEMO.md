# GDT669 stem-compositor memo

## Fixed lens

1. You are a workshop teaching-master who trains scribes to build compact cards from a stable repertory of heads, operations, measures, grades, and endings.
2. You require each proposed role sequence to spell the complete visible surface exactly once and in order.
3. You search the entire released panel for recurring blocks before memorizing a whole card.
4. You tolerate provisional semantics, but a shared stem must carry one scoped practical core across all its licensed compounds.
5. You prefer a small predictive mixed codebook over either universal one-letter values or a dictionary of unrelated wholes.

## Result

All 37 surfaces are covered in the exact first-frontier order. Thirty-three use
the inherited 54-role sheet directly, two use narrowly proposed recurrent
blocks, `OY_PREP_BASE` and `OKY_HOT_PREP_BASE`, and two are exact
reader-alias wholes. No card is left open.

## The requested family investigations

### `she` versus `sh`

`shear`, `shek`, and `shedaiin` preserve the same construction:

`SH_MOIST + E_MIDDLE + remainder`

Naked `sh` remains the free process imperative “einweichen.” The panel's large
`she-` family therefore supports `sh` as the operation and `e` as its middle
grade; it does not require a competing `SHE` lexeme.

### `dal`

`dalar` and `dals` join the already broad `dal-` family. The stable parse is
`D_MEASURE+AL_RAW_I`, followed by fraction, charge, process, or close. A new
`DAL` role would merely hide two already predictive roles and is rejected.

### `ro`

Naked `ro` and the panel forms `roaiin`, `rodaiin`, `rodam`, `rody`, `roiin`,
`roiiin`, `rol...`, `rom`, and `ror...` support `R_ROOT+O_PREP`. The existing
roots already predict the family, so no redundant `RO` head is added.

### `oeee`

`oeees` joins `oeeeody` and `oeeeos` as `O_PREP+EEE_LONG_OR_FINAL+tail`.
The `eee` value remains the long/last stage, while `-s`, `-dy`, or another
preparation frame determines the ending. No opaque `OEEE` whole is needed.

### Initial `ol-`

`olkair` follows the inherited initial-`ol*` practical rule already used by
`olkain` and `olkaiin`: `O_PREP+L_WOOD+K_HOT+AIR_FRACTION_II`. It is not an
initial `OL_MATERIAL` card. The latter remains the bound material/result
carrier after a complete head.

## Two useful extensions

The guarded full-panel census gives `oy` 12 occurrences, plus one `oychey`
and one `oytor`. `oytor` cannot be rendered as
`O_PREP+Y_START_OR_CLOSE+T_COLD+OR_PORTION`
without turning terminal `y` into an internal linker. The panel already has
naked `oy` = “Ansatz in Grundform” and `oychey` in the same preparation
family. `OY_PREP_BASE+T_COLD+OR_PORTION` is therefore narrower and more
predictive than either an internal-y exception or a memorized `oytor` card.

The same problem occurs in `okytaiin`. Naked `oky` occurs 89 times in the
guarded panel and already means “heißer Ansatz am Anfang des Grades”; the new
compound occurs once. `OKY_HOT_PREP_BASE+T_COLD+AIIN_III` preserves that exact
practical core. Compound support is still low, so the extension remains LOW
and may not export into arbitrary `oky` substrings.

## Exact two-reader aliases

All 37 target loci were compared with IT2a and RF1b. Exactly two targets have
the same differently spelled target card in both alternate readers:

- ZL `eeckhy` at f51r.7 is `chckhy` in both readers. The known consensus card
  is “Arzneikompositum: trocken, Gradanfang,” so ZL `eeckhy` is retained as
  sole `LEARNED_EECKHY_WHOLE`; it is not falsely decomposed as `ee+ckh+y`.
- ZL `lkchdal` at f105v.13 is `lkeedal` in both readers. Its default follows
  the consensus spelling `l+k+ee+d+al`, but ZL dispatch remains the sole
  `LEARNED_LKCHDAL_WHOLE`; no `ee` atom is pretended to occur in ZL.

`shedaiin` is split `she daiin` by both readers, but this concatenation has the
same exact inherited atom sequence `SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+D_MEASURE+AIIN_III` and
therefore needs no alias. Other reader disagreements either preserve the target
surface or lack two-reader agreement.

## Compound endings retained

- final `-d` closes in `pched`;
- final `-y` closes or marks the base form in `dcheey`, `orshy`, and `sary`;
- final `-dy` finishes in `chordy`, `alchdy`, `ckhody`, and `psheedy`;
- final `-s` is charge/species in `loeees`, `chodys`, `dals`, `ytchas`, and
  `oeees`;
- `ain/aiin`, `air`, `ar`, and `am` remain distinct stage, fraction, and
  measure tails.

No `o` is promoted to water/oil, no `sh` becomes a material, and no internal
`y` is licensed outside the two named preparation blocks.
