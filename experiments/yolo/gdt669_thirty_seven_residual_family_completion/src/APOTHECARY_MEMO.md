# GDT669 apothecary memo

## Lens

1. You are a practical apothecary trained in a circa-1420 herb and compound-drug workshop.
2. You think in ingredients, preparation states, measures, heat, cooling, soaking, drying, finishing, and batch order.
3. You judge a reading by whether an assistant could actually perform or inventory it.
4. You accept provisional meanings until a context makes them impossible, but you never hide uncertainty behind generic work prose.
5. You preserve every visible operation and do not invent water, wine, oil, salt, vessels, disease, or cure without a card.

## Decision

All 37 frontier surfaces receive a concrete default in the exact frequency-ranked frontier order. Every proposed composition uses only V45 roles and reconstructs the visible surface by concatenating the corresponding V45 stems. No new primitive role is required and no surface is left as an unknown whole.

The deck reads as an apothecary register of ingredients, fractions, doses, preparation states and ordered operations. Nominal cards remain nominal: `cthal` is a raw-herb ingredient, `ro` a root preparation, and `oeees` a final-stage batch. Imperative or executable cards preserve every written process: `kchokchy` retains both heating/drying cycles, while `tcheodal` retains cooling, drying, preparation and measuring.

## The recurrent SH family

The full released panel favors one productive process family:

- `sh = SH_MOIST`: soak or moisten. Its fourteen positions fit a process/state bridge before additions, cooling stages and material cards better than a named substance.
- `shear = SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+AR_FRACTION_I`: soak the first fraction to the middle stage. Across 22 positions it is compatible with fraction, heat, material and batch neighbors; reading `ar` as the established first fraction is more useful than memorizing the form.
- `shedaiin = SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+D_MEASURE+AIIN_III`: three doses of drug soaked to the middle stage. Ten positions repeatedly place it beside measured, heated, dried or compound preparations. The rival remains three measures rather than three doses.
- `shek = SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+K_HOT`: soak to the middle stage, then heat. Ten positions support a transition between moist and hot states. Reversing it to “heat, then soak” contradicts the visible role order.

This family is a practical gain: one stable moisture operation predicts a fraction card, a measured-dose card and a following heat operation without inventing a liquid.

## Other high-value decisions

- `dcheey` retains measuring, full drying and closure; it is not merely “dry material.”
- `dals`, `dalar`, `odl`, `daiinol` and `lkchdal` keep their written measuring component.
- `okeedal` reads directionally: first a fully heated preparation, then Rohdroge I is measured from it.
- `alchdy`, `chordy`, `ckhody`, `chodys` and `psheedy` retain finalization rather than becoming inert ingredient labels.
- `ytair`, `ykeor`, `ytchas` and `yteain` keep the initial reference direction “hiervon.”
- `pched` uses terminal `-d` as closure, while `psheedy` uses the already distinct `dy` finalization role.

## Uncertainty kept visible

The strongest unresolved choices concern whether `d+AIIN` is best rendered as three doses, three measures or three parts; whether terminal `-y` is light degree or closure in individual lines; and whether some nominal preparation cards should be read as immediate commands under an inherited head. The TSV records one operational default and one rival for each surface. None of these alternatives licenses water, wine, oil, salt, a vessel, disease or cure.
