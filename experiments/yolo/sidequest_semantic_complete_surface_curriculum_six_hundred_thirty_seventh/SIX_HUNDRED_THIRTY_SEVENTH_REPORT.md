## A learnable 1420 workshop routine

1. Identify the picture owner and case record.
2. Speak the short semantic components.
3. Use one of six paradigm tables where applicable.
4. Select the exact registered card body.
5. Apply desk/register/position and previous-wrapper habits.
6. Apply the three small special rules.
7. Consult the seventeen-entry local exception strip only if named.
8. Read the written card sequence backward to verify the instruction.

The multiple scribes need share meanings and card identities. They may retain
their own wrapper habits because the renderer handles those separately.
