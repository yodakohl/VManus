## Complete bundle population

For each of the 42 delayed nominal windows, punctuation is excluded and the
semantic items remain in written order. A window with `n` semantic items
contributes `n-2` consecutive triples. This yields exactly 119 triples:

- 32 begin at the first nominal item and are therefore adjacent to the action;
- 87 begin later and remain enumerated no-skip controls because a result edge
  may not hop over the earlier written items; they are not individually
  classified as result bundles;
- ten additional delayed windows contain exactly two semantic items and are
  explicit two-item length controls, not all short contexts among 83 actions.

The 32 leading triples are read as complete prefixes, never as isolated target
words. One is admitted, four stay open, and 27 stop before becoming a complete
three-item result bundle. Only A083 receives an explicit right-stop boundary;
the other rows are complete population decisions, not claimed exact stop-point
annotations. A `STOP` closes only that longer path. It does not erase a shorter
earlier hold, redefine a surface, or reject a broader exploratory hypothesis.
