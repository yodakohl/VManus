# Sidequest Pass 820: semantic collision atlas

The sixth grammar has no literally duplicated German component value, but it
has six neighborhoods that can easily collapse during fluent translation.
Reading every occurrence in its complete statement gives a simple workshop
distinction for each.

## Movement and action

- OK ANSETZEN activates the current work step.
- T ANWENDEN uses the item on or for its owner.
- CHD UMSETZEN moves or converts it inside the work chain.

- K ZUGEBEN makes material join the active mixture.
- P EINFUELLEN puts material into a receiving place.
- L LEITEN conducts it along a path.

These are not synonyms. A writer can teach them with three questions: start or
use or transfer; then add or fill or guide.

## State, order, quantity, and place

- SH HALTEN is active retention; SHED STEHENLASSEN is unattended waiting.
- OL WEITER continues the same relation; OT DANACH advances; OS DAZU adds a
  linked clause without asserting temporal succession.
- S PROBE is a diagnostic sample; AIN PORTION is material; AIIN SOLLMASS is a
  prescribed value; IIN STUFE is a process setting.
- AR QUELLE is origin; CKH DURCHLASS is path; AL ZIELSTELLE is destination;
  SOLK SAMMELSTELLE is a destination that receives and retains output.

No merge or split is needed in this pass. The apparent collisions are exactly
the compact contrasts that make the proposed workshop notation useful.
