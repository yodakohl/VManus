# GDT627 method

## Question

Which heads turn GDT626's written I–IV slot into a quality degree, a material
amount, or an unresolved grammatical value? In particular, can the open `d`
of `dan/dain/daiin/daiiin` receive a useful local meaning?

## Scope and inputs

GDT627 opens no page, transcription source, or image. It inherits GDT626's
5,176 safe `^(.*a)(i*)n$` occurrences on 179 pages, its family summary and its
same-head mixed lines. `f1r` and every `f84*` selector remain absent. The
quality meanings and productive segmentations are inherited from GDT623 and
GDT624. Six manually recorded historical syntax comparators and eight already
available visual/layout judgments are retained as explicit source artifacts.

## Construction

1. Remove the final value slot from each GDT626 head and classify the remaining
   root. Inherited `k/t/ch/sh` and their `o/qo` scopes select quality degree;
   known `cth/chor/shor/dair/sair` endings select an unresolved part value;
   `d` is tested as a free value head. Everything else stays open.
2. Materialize the complete eight-head quality grid across I–IV and retain all
   occurrences of the four simple axes plus their `o/qo` scopes.
3. Find thermal and moisture values within three token positions on the same
   physical line. Record order, distance, degree agreement and alternate-
   reading stability.
4. For every head, group occurrences under the exact immediate left and right
   token. A frame is informative when the same head has at least two values in
   that unchanged frame.
5. Isolate `d` under those frames, its exact contact with known part terms, and
   bounded part–`d`–part brackets. Use the repeated syntax to choose a practical
   left-binding default.
6. Emit a compact working dictionary and ten concrete line readings. Unknown
   heads and the absolute `d` unit remain explicit.

All generated artifacts are deterministic. The validator reruns the builder,
requires byte-identical output, checks the inherited meaning rows, counts every
promoted family and witness, and scans the retained files for forbidden pages
and private machine material.

## Working interpretation

The shared surface architecture is:

```text
HEAD + a + (n|in|iin|iiin)
         -> value I/II/III/IV
```

`a` is therefore read as a value-slot linker—usually null or a colon in German
rendering—not as a substance. The head supplies the value type:

```text
k/t/ch/sh + value  -> hot/cold/dry/moist, degree I–IV
d + value          -> free measure/grade I–IV
part d+value       -> part, one/two/three/four local measures (default)
```

Historical materia medica supplies both relevant syntaxes: paired qualities in
first through fourth degree, and an ingredient followed by a unit plus Roman
quantity. It does not identify the unknown unit or prove that every value head
is numerical.

## Claim ceiling

This is an exploratory compositional reader, not a manuscript solution. It
promotes degree readings only on the inherited quality heads and a local
measure/portion default for free `d` beside known part terms. Outside those
contexts `d` may still express a grade, table value, or another technical unit.
The direction of I–IV, the absolute unit, the language and the remaining 494
heads are not identified.
