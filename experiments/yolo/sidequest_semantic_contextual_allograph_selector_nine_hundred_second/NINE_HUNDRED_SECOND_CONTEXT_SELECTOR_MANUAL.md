# Kontextselektor für Allographen

Nur 16 der 190 Wurzelrezepte haben mehr als eine belegte Oberfläche.
Der Lehrling wählt zuerst nach Register und Kartenposition, dann nach Auftrag/Seite/Stufe; nur zuletzt nach dem lokalen Mini-Deck.

## Die 16 Familien

- `AIIN` → **aiin | daiin**: MASTER_SECTION (SHARED_WORKFLOW_SELECTOR; 19 Marken).
- `AL` → **cheal | dal**: MEMORIZED_IDENTITY (MEMORIZED_IDENTITY_SELECTOR; 14 Marken).
- `AR` → **ar | char**: MASTER_SECTION (SHARED_WORKFLOW_SELECTOR; 7 Marken).
- `CHD+DY` → **dchdy | schedy**: UNIT (LOCAL_MINI_DECK_SELECTOR; 5 Marken).
- `CHD+Y` → **chdy | chedchy | chedy**: STAGE_PLUS_POSITION (STAGE_SELECTOR; 13 Marken).
- `CHK+EE+Y` → **cheeky | chkeey**: ORDER (ORDER_OR_PAGE_SELECTOR; 3 Marken).
- `NONE` → **daiial | iokeeor**: MEMORIZED_IDENTITY (MEMORIZED_IDENTITY_SELECTOR; 2 Marken).
- `OK+CHD+DY` → **okchedy | qokchdy**: UNIT_POSITION (SHARED_WORKFLOW_SELECTOR; 5 Marken).
- `OK+OL` → **okchol | qokol**: MASTER_SECTION (SHARED_WORKFLOW_SELECTOR; 2 Marken).
- `OK+Y` → **choky | qokchy | qoky**: SECTION_PLUS_POSITION (SHARED_WORKFLOW_SELECTOR; 14 Marken).
- `OL` → **chol | ls | ol**: MEMORIZED_IDENTITY (MEMORIZED_IDENTITY_SELECTOR; 22 Marken).
- `OL+Y` → **choly | qolchey**: MASTER_SECTION (SHARED_WORKFLOW_SELECTOR; 2 Marken).
- `OT+CHD+DY` → **otchdy | otchedy | qotchedy**: ORDER_PLUS_POSITION (ORDER_OR_PAGE_SELECTOR; 3 Marken).
- `OT+Y` → **otchey | qotchy**: MASTER_SECTION (SHARED_WORKFLOW_SELECTOR; 2 Marken).
- `SH+EE+Y` → **cheey | sheey**: UNIT (LOCAL_MINI_DECK_SELECTOR; 7 Marken).
- `Y` → **chey | chy | dy | y**: MEMORIZED_IDENTITY (MEMORIZED_IDENTITY_SELECTOR; 23 Marken).

## q-Träger

- HOW / FIRST: q 3, ohne q 0 → Q.
- HOW / LAST: q 2, ohne q 3 → NO_Q.
- HOW / MIDDLE: q 3, ohne q 1 → Q.
- HOW / ONLY: q 5, ohne q 1 → Q.
- WHAT / FIRST: q 3, ohne q 0 → Q.
- WHAT / MIDDLE: q 4, ohne q 1 → Q.
- WHEN / MIDDLE: q 0, ohne q 2 → NO_Q.
