# GDT955: the recovered source headings contradict R16

The predeclared consequence was specific: if GDT953's P R16 alignment is the
28-purpose list, source22 must repeat “to acquire every good,” because f69v.27
has the same `okeod` label as the proposed source3/7 at f69v.18/.14.
The recovered supplementary Latin versions instead specify safe escape and
protection against hostile speech. Neither supplies the predicted heading.

The [candidate/prediction table](artifacts/CANDIDATE_PREDICTION_TABLE.tsv) binds each
outcome to its precise source and dependence limit. All consulted evidence and
failed retrievals are retained in [EVIDENCE.json](src/EVIDENCE.json).

Both Latin supplements explicitly refer to IV.ix50, the missing22nd entry, with
21Albelda and23Zaadebola in the unchanged main text. Thus this is the correct
series, not the separate I.iv purpose roster. Main-text3/7 and their modern
translation both retain the universal-good heading. F² andM/N disagree with one
another about the complete heading while both disagree with our prediction.
The modern translation is not a third independent medieval witness.

The supplements were already present on printed253 of the same cached1986
edition. They had been overlooked in GDT953's main-text extraction. This is a
newly inspected source consequence, not newly acquired manuscript evidence.
Public preregistration `f096cd536` preceded the witness search; the source
selection and target pattern were previously exposed. No target was reopened.

The historical limit matters: F's copying date is1536; the F² layer is not dated
independently here. M is early17thcentury andN is described as a copy ofM, probably
1647. The editor characterizes their class as eclectic and innovative. These
later witnesses do not establish the original missing heading or a1420 reading.
See the independent model [source-reading audit](src/SOURCE_READING_AUDIT.json)
and its small literal [root collation notes](src/ROOT_SOURCE_NOTES.md).

Decision: do not develop R16 on the hope that the lacuna supplies a third all-good
purpose. Both actually recovered Latin versions contradict that consequence.
This is conditional on the same-content witness comparison; the unknown Latin
archetype has not been reconstructed. GDT953's source, complete outputs and report
remain byte-unchanged, its original lacuna remains unknown, and its failed
component model stays closed. No adopted `okeod` meaning, language identification,
independent Voynich confirmation, reserve access, or search-wide significance.

The two executable manifest commands check input immutability and the recorded
manual source classifications. They do not make the translation judgment
independent. A separate model read the native appendix page and manuscript
provenance; this was not an external human expert review. RESULT/VALIDATION are
compact reproducibility receipts, not semantic scores.
