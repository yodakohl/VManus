## Frozen scope and rule

All eight, including candidates with only one prose reuse, are listed in
`src/LOCI.tsv`. Inspect both complete official Yale original images, then
each of the eight positions. Record a row for every locus even if illegible
or physically unlocatable. Use native visual inspection, with exact crops
allowed; no OCR, paint removal, image generation or transcription repair.

`SINGULAR` requires a visible uninterrupted ink leader from label to **one**
bounded depicted object with a clear endpoint, or the label physically
inscribed *inside* one bounded object. A line that is itself part of drawing
without a label endpoint, mere proximity, row membership, enclosure by a
whole multi-object panel, or a reconstructed damaged boundary gives
`AMBIGUOUS` or `NONE`. `UNLOCATABLE` cannot pass. No post-result loosening.
The fixed outcome is `NATIVE_SINGULAR_OWNER_FOUND` if at least one of all
eight meets `SINGULAR`; otherwise `NO_NATIVE_SINGULAR_OWNER`. Report exact
object and rival ownership for every row. If positive, only nominate the
complete form for a separate rival whole-passage test; no suffix export.

Selection is by exact reuse seen in GDT1082, so there is no whole-search
significance claim. The same two exposed physical pages cannot confirm a
reading independently. Three transcriptions are alternate readings of one
manuscript.

Total wall budget 35 minutes from first image fetch/view through registration,
assessment, validator and publication. Stop at budget rather than adding
pages or adjusting the rule.
