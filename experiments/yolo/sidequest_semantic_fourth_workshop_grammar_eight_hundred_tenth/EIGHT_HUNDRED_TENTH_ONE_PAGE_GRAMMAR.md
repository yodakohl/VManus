# Fourth compact workshop grammar

The reusable inventory now has 31 semantic components:

- control: `OK` ANSETZEN, `OT` DANACH, `OL` WEITER;
- action: `CH` ENTNEHMEN, `SH` HALTEN, `CTH` BEREITEN, `T` ANWENDEN;
- transfer: `K` ZUGEBEN, `L` LEITEN, `CHD` UMSETZEN, `P` EINFÜLLEN;
- process: `CHK` WÄRMEN, `SHED` STEHENLASSEN, `LSH` SPÜLEN, `R` KÜHLEN;
- material: `AIR` WASSER, `OR` ANSATZ, `HO` ZUTAT;
- place/path/procedure: `SOLK` SAMMELSTELLE, `CKH` DURCHLASS, `O` VORGANG;
- measure/stage: `AIIN` SOLLMASS, `AIN` PORTION, `IIN` STUFE;
- address: `AL` ZIELSTELLE, `AR` QUELLE;
- grade: `E` KURZ, `EE` LANG, `EEE` VOLL;
- endpoint: `Y` DIES / current item, licensed `DY` SCHLUSS.

The workshop speaks a registered component recipe, then copies one exact whole
card surface. It does not spell freely from visible letters.

Only eight cards remain outside full core composition: `ykan` with bound `AN`;
four singleton-bearing cards `cfhy`, `ches`, `qokylddy`, `daiiin`; and the three
whole commands `os`, `dchol/schol`, `talam`.

Coverage: core31 touches 170/173 cards and 377/381 events; 165 cards and 372
events are entirely core-composed. Sixty-nine unique unseen surfaces remain
predictions, never inserted into the observed edition; the 70 input rows
contained one repeated `solkeeey` proposal.
