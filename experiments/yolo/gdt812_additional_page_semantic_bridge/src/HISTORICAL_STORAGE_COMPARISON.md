# Historical storage-time comparator

Status: `SOURCE_ATTESTED_STORAGE_RIVAL__NO_VOYNICH_TEXTUAL_MATCH__NO_LEXICAL_PROMOTION`.

## Accessible primary text and edition boundary

[Tadhg Ó Cuinn, *An Irish Materia Medica*, chapter 234, “Rosa rubia”](https://celt.ucc.ie/published/G600006.html): English translation p.612; edited Irish text pp.237–238. CELT dates the work to 1415. This is an accessible edited primary text, not a catalogue description.

> When the flower of the rose or the rose itself are dried, they retain their efficacy for three years

The same entry assigns cold degree I and dry degree II, then describes rose-honey and rose-oil preparations. Its three-year duration therefore differs from its dryness degree. These are historical assertions, not present-day treatment or storage advice.

The modern editor separately assembles Latin comparanda, principally from Venice 1497, supplemented by other witnesses. Those excerpts are not a diplomatic Latin transcription of the 1415 Irish work; the rose storage sentence is directly present in the edited Irish text and its English translation. No claim is made that Voynich copied this source or used Irish.

## What this changes

The previous degree-versus-dose framing is incomplete as a list of historical possibilities: a written value can also specify retained efficacy or keeping time. This does not select that interpretation in Voynich.

Keep **dry**, a humoral property, separate from **dried**, a material state. A drying instruction, an already-dried substance, and the duration for which it remains usable are three different claims. The comparator cannot license collapsing them.

## Candidate-discriminating prediction

A genuinely source-linked passage about the same substance must distinguish its dryness-degree field from its keeping-time field. For this particular comparator, the contrasting values are II and III. Preserving the shared subject and identifying an independent time/storage construction would favor the proposed duration reading over a degree or dose reading. Positive evidence that the target span instead belongs to an explicit degree-III or dosage construction for that same subject would contradict it. Failure to recover a time word alone is not disproof.

The current passages supply no independently identified substance, time unit, storage predicate, or source-specific chain of parallel preparations. Repeated `daiin` elsewhere in the f32v paragraph remains unexplained. Thus this is a concrete falsifiable rival, not a usable historical textual match or a translation gain.
