The fresh botanical context requires any portable reading to explain more:

- Same medicinal substance: a botanical label identifies a drug also named
  at its preparation/application in the bodily diagram. Substance unknown.
- Same property/effect: for example **warming**, **moistening**, **drying**,
  or **diuretic** could concern a drug and its application. All are speculative;
  none is selected by the drawing or the written form.
- Context-specific senses: bath sense in one place, botanical sense in another.
  This is possible but adds freedom; it is not established polysemy.

The first two are more economical shared-meaning hypotheses than silently
switching the word's translation with the illustration. Neither is proven.
