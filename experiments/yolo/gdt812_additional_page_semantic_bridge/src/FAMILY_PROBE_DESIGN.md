# Whole-family continuation: design before the 39-selector extraction

This is a post-result GDT812 extension, not an unseen experiment. The four
new pages and the f32v repetitions are already known. GDT626/GDT686 established
the recurring written series; their ordinal/quantity glosses are not facts.
No repeat of their corpus-wide frequency ranking will select a meaning here.

Question: can one explicitly specified family interpretation account for
`dan`, `dain`, `daiin`, `daiiin`, including their combinations, rather than
improving only the most frequent member? See FAMILY_MODEL_PROPOSAL.md for
independently proposed fixed scalar alternatives. The numeric I–IV reading
remains a comparator, not a decoded answer. A written family alone does not
logically rule out grammatical inflection or related function words.

## Scope and mechanical selection

Use precisely the original GDT791 35 source selectors plus the four recorded
GDT812 admissions: 34 visual page keys / 39 selectors. This is a text-context
extension within existing admissions, not 39 freshly inspected pictures.
Both f84 and f84r stay sealed. Do not use the inherited 179/190-selector union.
Query the cached source and alternate readings through the selector-first
guard. All three readings of selected contexts remain separate.

Extract all exact standalone members of the four-form family. Do not split
cthodaiin, treat EVA i as a decoded number, or merge alternate boundaries.
Select complete source prose paragraphs (or individual non-prose loci) if
ANY reading contains (a) rare endpoint dan/daiiin, (b) immediately adjacent
family members, including across a physical line inside a paragraph, or
(c) a family member at a source prose paragraph's beginning. Keep all three
readings of every selected block, even when a triggering form is absent.
Record overall per-reader counts only to expose coverage, not as new meaning
evidence. Preserve all unknown heads and the source paragraph segmentation.

## Predictions and interpretation

An obligatory postfix modifier needs an explicit host inside its paragraph;
a genuine paragraph-initial occurrence would require an explicitly different
construction, not attachment across the boundary. Adjacent different family
members must receive their declared composition, not be silently deduplicated
or supplied with separate invisible subjects. Repetition may be emphatic,
enumerative or scribal; none is assumed from repetition alone.

The fixed ordinal comparator also owes a construction for adjacent values;
numbers cannot win merely because arbitrary sequences of numbers are printable.
A free numeral list and a postfix property grade are different constructions.
Endpoint absence or an unknown host does not refute a proposed meaning.
This source-based comparison may leave multiple candidates alive. Report what
each says literally, where extra grammar is required, and any genuinely
useful next lexical prediction. No automatic fluency or semantic score.

Reproduction: src/family_probe.py writes source-bound context/result artifacts;
its --check mode replays them without writing. This tests extraction only,
not meanings, historical equivalence, or all 34 images' interpretation.
