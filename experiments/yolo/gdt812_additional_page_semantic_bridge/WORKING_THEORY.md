# Working interpretation after the four extra pages

The best broad reading remains a compendium of medicinal substances, their
properties and applications, with diagrammatic bodily/celestial material.
Different record genres remain possible. This is a subject-matter hypothesis,
not a decoded language. No concrete lexeme or complete clause is confirmed.

## What became more concrete

**Keeping time is distinct from quality and dose.** An actual1415 pharmacological
text can give a substance a dryness degree of II and then a three-year useful
life when dried. Thus the trial “flower-material, dried: keeps three years?”
for the whole chor chol daiin sequence is historically meaningful. It remains
one unsupported candidate beside “dry in degree III?” and “dried, three
portions?”; even flower and three are assumptions. The complete f21r/f32v
pages identify neither the substance nor a time unit, and the images do not
establish a direct rose-entry match. See the historical/source comparison.

**Shared labels need a portable account of their subject.** dchdy occurs as an
inscription in a botanical field on f100v and beneath the arch on f77r. A
portable “steam” or “drainage outlet” does not explain the plant-adjacent use
without an extra story. More economical trial accounts are a medicinal
substance named in both its botanical and applied setting, or a common effect
such as warming/drying. Context-specific senses remain possible, but are not
an established escape hatch. No particular substance or effect is selected.

**A vessel is not an automatic paragraph delimiter.** f100v combines13 local
inscriptions with one prose paragraph. f101r has no local inscriptions and
three source paragraphs, though its text occupies two broad visual bands.
Its third vessel stands among the lower plants, below both bands. Therefore
a compulsory forward vessel→captioned-row→paragraph rule is unsupported.
Three vessels and three paragraphs can still be aligned hypothetically;
their equal count does not identify the attachment.

## What should not be repaired by nicer German

The f88r trial qokol chol qokol qokol chol → “hot, dry / hot / hot, dry”
preserves its written repetitions. However, it invents two subject boundaries
and three substance references. Its three nearby inscription records are not
three certified pictured owners. The trial is retained, but has no advantage
until those subject references are independently explained. Do not promote
qokol=hot by repeating this display.

## Nonnumerical intensity rival added in the follow-up

Exact daiin can alternatively be tried as **very / sehr?**. The doubled form
then intensifies one expression, while chol daiin could conditionally mean
very dry rather than dry in degree III. Macer attests postposed intensity,
not the doubled modifier. The full f32v trial preserves twelve exact ZL3b
occurrences and all unknowns, including dain and cthaiin. It cannot keep
inherited nominal/numerical readings of every preceding whole: shor daiin
and dain daiin make that cost especially clear. This is a concrete rival,
not a new dictionary winner. The recurring written family remains a separate
obligation; its minim counts do not independently identify numbers.

## Next discriminating question

For the concrete shared-label hypothesis, ask whether a named substance is
introduced on a botanical page and referred to at an application, or whether
the same label instead predicates a shared property. That requires a source-
bound construction retaining the same subject across the explanatory text,
not another word-frequency ranking or a nearby picture substituted as a noun.
For the value/intensity alternatives, work from complete constructions with
explicit preceding expressions: can a fixed modifier explain them without
continually changing their noun/property roles? A number/unit, time/storage
or gradable-predicate interpretation must account for the same occurrences.
Do not count the two known chor chol daiin spans again as new support.

The source follow-up sharpens rather than resolves this choice. Historical
baths can promote or arrest flow; a shared drug does not imply a shared effect.
Fribourg L52 supplies a real Latin abbreviation comparator, not a bilingual
key. No f116v admission was spent to repeat the archived marginalia route.

The relocated-label test on f101r is not pursued further: there are no local
labels to relocate. The16 unspent page admissions remain available for a
specific next acquisition; they are not implicitly released as a new corpus.
The complete reader and source memos preserve this pass so it need not be
repeated. Current dictionary and renderer remain unchanged.
