# GDT743 method

## Question

Can a longer local carrier-coverage run justify overriding the inherited H4
formal direction at D0075, and can the bilateral neighborhood at D0040 license
its inherited HOT candidate, without introducing a new page, transcription,
word meaning or component value?

## Inputs

GDT743 reads six byte-bound cached artifacts:

- the complete GDT742 103-contact role-separation deck;
- the eight GDT742 candidate-role audit rows;
- the complete 202-position GDT742 renderer;
- the seven GDT742 cached focus lines;
- the complete GDT739 202-target dimension dispatch; and
- the 1,373 GDT739 cached token-window rows.

The experiment uses no new page, image, OCR or transcription. `f84` and `f84r`
remain forbidden.

## Method

For each of the 202 target occurrences, the builder takes the carrier candidate
already requested by GDT739 and walks outward on both sides. A cell contributes
to requested-carrier coverage when its carrier-tag set contains that candidate.
The run terminates on the first non-covering cell or after a CLOSE-tagged cell.

`screened` is a mechanical audit label: the cell is reader-exact, known, has no
retired-patient tag, is not a strict initial head or another target, and receives
no composition credit. It does not mean carrier-pure or semantically decoded.
The builder also records the contiguous exact-carrier-set prefix and variants
that exclude PROCESS, PASS and CLOSE tags.

The requested-carrier census is supplemented by an unconditioned scan: each of
PREPARATION, MATERIAL and PART is tested independently at every target. This
makes runs hidden by the inherited carrier request visible rather than treating
the request as a discovered fact.

For each of the 41 radius-two contacts, the outcome-free dispatcher first
recomputes the GDT742 open-frame, direction and carrier-continuity features. The
exploratory reverse branch requires:

1. a common open R2 frame;
2. formal-direction mismatch;
3. CARRIER among the selected roles;
4. the complete requested carrier in both middle and host cells;
5. at least three screened requested-carrier covering cells on the selected
   side, fewer than three on the opposite side, and no CLOSE in the first three.

The dispatcher cannot address a target ID, locus, surface or predecessor
outcome. Candidate labels and old outcomes are joined only after all 41
decisions exist.

The validator also removes item 5 as an ablation. Both the relaxed and full
rules select one contact, so the coverage gate excludes zero contacts. The
resulting D0075 carrier tag is consequently classified as a provisional
composite analogy, not as discrimination supplied by run length.

For the axis lead, the census is explicitly conditioned on each target's
inherited favored-axis candidate. It records bilateral and two-by-two local
coverage of that tag. A singleton host-middle intersection is retained only as
a lead when the reverse partial-R2 pattern has no second trigger; it does not
license a renderer axis.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The exploratory working renderer may add the requested carrier at an existing
occurrence only when the complete reverse branch above fires. The new rendering
must expose the carrier, open dimension and provisional rule inside brackets.
No natural-language genitive may make the tag look like decoded plaintext.

GDT743 may report the complete coverage censuses, strict sensitivities, R2
ablation, one occurrence-scoped provisional carrier tag and one conditioned
axis lead. It may not identify a word, stem, glyph value, language, historical
abbreviation, plaintext clause, ingredient, patient, plant, species, disease,
cure, unit or unseen-form meaning. Structural/model tags remain distinct from
translations.
