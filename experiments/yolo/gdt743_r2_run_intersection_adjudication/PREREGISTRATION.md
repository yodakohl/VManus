reproducibility protocol, not a claim that the exploratory sidequest was
externally or temporally preregistered.

  outcomes.

sensitivities from the cached windows. A reverse R2 CARRIER role receives an
exploratory occurrence-local licence only if the open/full-carrier frame and
the unique selected-side screened coverage threshold `>=3` both hold. Do not
license an AXIS from a partial intersection.

count. Independently scan all three carrier tags over all 202 targets and
publish every qualifying pair, including pairs that disagree with the inherited
requested carrier. Compare predecessor outcomes only after these feature decks
are complete.

Any retained D0075 carrier must be marked `PROVISIONAL_COMPOSITE_RULE` and shown
in a bracketed model render. D0040 may remain a visible conditioned HOT lead but
