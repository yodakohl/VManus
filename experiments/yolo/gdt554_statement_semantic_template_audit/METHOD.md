# GDT554 method

## Question

When the GDT553 exact-key deck is placed back into all 78 already admitted
GDT539 statements, which event and multi-card semantic templates actually
recur, and does any repeated exact surface/context or recipe/context state
force two incompatible portable readings?

## Inputs

- GDT539's 546 contextual prose events and 78 complete statements;
- GDT553's final 145-card zero-rest reader.

No new page, image, transcription or local-record row is opened.

## Method

For every prose event, retain the GDT539 recipe, clause and same-statement
incoming action/argument state. Resolve a compact semantic macro from:

1. visible or inherited action roots;
2. visible or inherited argument roots;
3. the ordered control layer (grade, execution, relation, address, continuation,
   ordering, carrier and closure marks).

Also replace portable action, argument and relation identities by role symbols
to obtain an abstract event template. Count the resulting slot-transition and
event templates across statements, pages and registers. Mine contiguous
two-to-four-event frames twice: once with the actual portable roots and once
with abstract role symbols. Frames never cross a statement boundary.

Reinsert all 149 target events through their exact GDT553 keys. Their recipes
must match exactly and each event clause must be one of the card's already
known contextual readings. Record the GDT553 provenance generation, but do not
treat it as a semantic class.

Build two repeated-state ledgers: exact surface plus incoming state, and exact
recipe plus incoming state. A register-specific German wording may differ;
the portable semantic macro may not. Finally isolate every event with no
resolved action as an explicit nominal/control fragment instead of inventing a
verb.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass as a compilation/audit if all 546 events partition the 78 source
statements exactly, all 149 GDT553 events retain exact recipes and admitted
context readings, every recurrent frame remains statement-bounded, the known
16 no-action fragments remain explicit, and no repeated exact state has more
than one portable semantic macro.

This is an exploratory horizontal working grammar over four already admitted
pages. Repetition can nominate a reusable template, but does not establish
plaintext, syntax, a language, a historical codebook, an object or a future
page reading. A zero contradiction count applies only to repeated exact states
in this edition. No root value, recipe, segmentation or German reading changes.
