# GDT755 method

## Question

Can the fixed GDT754 top-24 complete-form deck be given concrete, concise working meanings by crossing whole-role bundles and independent cached placement against real late-medieval pharmaceutical expression classes, without reviving EVA-letter initialisms or hiding uncertainty behind generic process prose?

## Inputs

- GDT754's fixed 24-form historical bridge deck and its complete 172-form `PRODUCTIVE_COMPOUND` provenance inventory;
- the existing guarded, reader-exact 179-page cache and GDT753/GDT744 field-boundary functions;
- 52 historical expression classes from 13 registered period, near-period or explicitly weaker edited/corpus sources;
- a 24-row analyst candidate-prior deck containing one primary and two rivals for every complete form.

No new Voynich page, image or transcription is opened. The source boundary is unchanged and f84/f84r remain forbidden.

## Method

### Independent occurrence fields

Locate every reader-exact cached occurrence of the 24 complete surfaces. Clip the same radius-five local fields used in the recent whole-role audits. The target never anchors its own field. More importantly, all 172 GDT754 suspect productive-compound forms lose their semantic axes whenever they occur as neighbours. They remain visible as neutral written tokens, so the field is not silently shortened, but none of their old analyst-composed meanings can vote for a historical candidate.

For each target occurrence retain page, locus, section, normalized position, line-first/last status, boundaries, independent anchor identities and tags, channel, and every excluded suspect neighbour. Aggregate these into one 24-row placement/channel census.

As a semantics-free position control, rank every reader-exact surface with at least ten cached occurrences by its line-initial rate. This prevents an apparently striking target rate from being judged without its full recurrent-form baseline.

### Historical expression bank

The expression bank separates:

- formulaic command and measure macros (`Recipe`, `Accipe`, `ana`, drachm, ounce, `semis`, `parte`);
- Galenic quality/degree expressions (`calide in primo/2o/3o gradu`, `frigida in primo gradu`);
- practical process verbs (`misce`, `cola`, `repone`, `refrigeretur`, `fac bullire`, `sicca`);
- preparation nouns (`preparatio`, `unguentum`, `electuarium`);
- concrete content nouns and slots (`aqua`, `vinum`, `oleum`, `sal`, `radix`, `folia`, `semen`, `lignum`, `pulvis`, `vas`, `mulier`, `morbus`, `balneum`).

Each row records the required role axes, preferred external channels and layout, evidence date tier, exact attested or normalized expression, source and caveat. A normalized expression class is labeled as such and is not passed off as a diplomatic quotation.

### Candidate assignment

Every target receives three complete-whole candidates. The primary must satisfy its declared required-axis gate. Placement, complete external-field channels, historical date and register agreement are retained as diagnostics. The primary order is an explicit exploratory analyst choice, not a hidden optimization or a blind identification test.

The selected concise German default is rendered only at the target's 448 enumerated reader-exact positions. Other tokens remain written as EVA in the hybrid reader; they are not filled with phrases such as “work item” or “perform the work step.”

Confidence labels mean:

- `C2_STRONG_EXPLORATORY`: a distinctive complete-form distribution and/or later whole-form evidence combines with a period expression class;
- `C1_CONSTRAINED_CANDIDATE`: the whole-role bundle plus some placement, field or historical evidence constrain the reading, but rivals remain substantial;
- `C0_FORCED_DEFAULT`: a concrete default is supplied to keep the exploratory renderer total, while the evidence is too weak or too nonspecific to privilege it strongly.

These are working-theory levels, not calibrated probabilities.

## Concrete-slot audit

For each requested practical concept, compare the historical expression's required axes with all top-24 role bundles. Report separately:

1. a broad structural slot (for example MATERIAL plus PREPARATION), and
2. a literal-specific axis (for example OINTMENT, WATER, ROOT or VESSEL).

This distinction permits a bold `cthody=Salbe` C0 candidate while recording that eleven forms could carry a generic preparation noun and zero currently carry an independent OINTMENT axis. It also shows where a concept must be sought in other learned wholes rather than forcing it into this deck.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

All 24 forms must receive a nonempty candidate and two nonempty rivals. A candidate remains live until contradicted or replaced by a better whole-form account. Historical attestation demonstrates that the expression belongs to an appropriate technical register; it does not demonstrate graphic identity with the target.

GDT755 may establish a 24-form exploratory candidate layer, compare complete-form placement and independent field channels, and prioritize the `ychor=Recipe/Accipe` lead. It identifies no confirmed lexeme, plaintext clause, language, glyph, sound, Latin initial, literal ingredient, plant, patient, vessel or component value. No EVA spelling resemblance is scored and no substring inherits a meaning.
