# GDT668 stem-compositor memo

## Lehrmeister-Regel

The 76 cards were read as a workshop register of fixed technical words and
reusable multi-sign blocks. The inherited V44 roles were tested against the
full released-panel counts before any new role was considered. A narrow role
was not widened merely to avoid a whole card.

## Result

All 76 frontier forms receive a concrete default in first-frontier order. The
high-frequency spine needs no new stem:

- `okeor = O_PREP+K_HOT+E_MIDDLE+OR_PORTION` (15 panel positions);
- `cheeor = CH_DRY+EE_END+OR_PORTION` (13);
- `shedain = SH_MOIST+E_MIDDLE+D_MEASURE+AIN_II` (12);
- `cheeol = CH_DRY+EE_END+OL_MATERIAL` (9);
- `okees = O_PREP+K_HOT+EE_END+S_TERM_SPECIES` (9);
- `qolchey = QOL_ADD+CH_DRY+E_MIDDLE+Y_START_OR_CLOSE` (9);
- `qotcho = QO_COMMAND+T_COLD+CH_DRY+O_PREP` (9);
- `tody = T_COLD+O_PREP+DY_FINISHED` (9).

These sister forms are useful because swapping the tail changes one narrow
slot: `-or` portion, `-ol` material/result, `-s` charge, `-y` close, and `-dy`
finish. No fresh vocabulary is needed to explain that behavior.

## Reused multi-sign heads

- `qol-` remains the indivisible addition command in `qolchey`.
- `so-` remains Saatgutzubereitung in `soshy`, distinct from `sol-` in
  `solkedy` and from initial `s-` in `san`, `skain`, and `sqokeodaiin`.
- terminal `om` remains the scoped handful-preparation block in `rom`,
  `ykchom`, and `dalchom`; free `m` is never exported.
- `chor` stays a plant-part head in `dorchory` and `ykchor`. In contrast,
  `chkor` visibly uses `ch+k+or`, because its operation-plus-portion family is
  already productive.
- `cph` requires no new occurrence in this frontier; `ckh` remains the
  composite head in `cheeckhody` and `lcheckhy`.

## Necessary learned wholes

- `otchyd` and `rokyd`: a literal atom split would place `Y_START_OR_CLOSE`
  internally before terminal `D_TERM_CLOSE`, contradicting both scopes.
- `dairin`: `IN_FORM_II` is licensed after a preparation or material head,
  not directly after the fraction-II block `air`.
- `eaiin`: a bare grade marker does not provide the process/material head
  required to decide whether `aiin` is quantity or stage.

These four remain concrete cards but do not create new roles.

## Exclusions retained

- No global water, oil, or carrier-liquid value is assigned to `o`.
- Terminal `-s` is never seed; initial `s-` is never a charge.
- Terminal `-d` closes; nonterminal `d-` measures only before a licensed
  quantity, material, or form head.
- Internal `y` is not silently converted into a linker.
- `qol`, `om`, `chor`, `sol`, and `so` remain distinct visible blocks even
  where a character-by-character split could be fabricated.

The resulting candidate grows the surface dictionary by 76 cards while
leaving the reusable stem inventory unchanged.
