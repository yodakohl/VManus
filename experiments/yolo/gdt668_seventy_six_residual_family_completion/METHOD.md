# GDT668 method

## Question

Can every one of the 76 distinct surfaces exposed by the V44 one-hole frontier
receive a concrete default that reuses the 52-role workshop sheet wherever
possible, adds only narrowly supported blocks, closes every starting row, and
improves complete passage readings without opening another page?

## Inputs

- GDT667's V44 dictionary, glossary, full-line coverage, occurrence audit, and
  fixed 76-row one-hole frontier.
- The same guarded 179-page ZL3b/IT2a/RF1b panel. `f1r`, `f84`, and `f84r`
  remain outside the material.
- Independent drafts from a practical recipe reader, a stem compositor, and a
  passage reader, followed by a second token-by-token passage audit.
- The inherited 52-role sheet and inherited O+L and SOL render revisions.

## Construction

1. Preserve the 76 surfaces in first-frontier order and recount all 230 of
   their positions over the already released panel.
2. Require every named role sequence to reproduce the complete visible surface
   exactly. Keep a learned whole when a tempting split would violate a role's
   scope.
3. Retain the recurring high-frequency spine directly: `okeor`, `cheeor`,
   `shedain`, `cheeol`, `okees`, `qolchey`, `qotcho`, and `tody` all compose
   from inherited roles.
4. Add terminal `YD_POST_CLOSE` only for naked `yd` or an absolute final `yd`
   after a complete head. Add `SQOKEO_SEED_HEAT_PREP` only for the left card
   independently separated by IT2a and RF1b at f88v.20.
5. Keep `dairin` and `eaiin` as concrete learned wholes. The result is 74
   role-composed surfaces, two learned wholes, and 54 total roles.
6. Render every target position, recompute V45 coverage, and manually read
   twenty complete passages. Recheck each token for action direction, object,
   grade, measure, and finish; do not turn free `dy` into an extra command.
7. Publish the next 37-form frontier ranked by its full-panel count.

## Completion conditions

The package completes only when all 76 cards cover all 230 positions, all 76
starting rows close, every composition spells its surface exactly, the two
learned wholes use exact namespaces, the f88v.20 split agrees with both
alternate readers, all twenty manual passages are complete, and a clean replay
reproduces every artifact byte for byte.

The result is an exploratory concrete workshop translation, not a claimed
historical decipherment. It asserts no language, phonetics, exact plant,
disease, patient, cure, liquid, new page, or image.
