## One production workflow

```text
1. Select inherited article, application schema or lookup diagram.
2. Draw the visual address before text.
3. Choose register program:
     Herbal open article | Bio committed cells | Astro spatial slots.
4. Omit material supplied by picture, active heading and familiar routine.
5. Copy exact learned cards and page/register-local payload cards.
6. Use qokaiin to activate/advance an entry where licensed.
7. Use L/O to continue under the active relation.
8. Use FORMULA_F3 where the exemplar calls for one shared-reference formula.
9. In Bio choose an exact value card and commit the cell.
10. Apply hand/position wrappers, JOIN/SPACE and physical reflow.
```

The same workshop can therefore maintain ordinary abbreviated prose, compact
technical notation and diagram labels without using three languages.

## Why the generic-register rival remains important

The writing machine itself does not prove medicine. It could preserve a broader
technical miscellany. The corrector's model better explains the absence of a
direct cross-register pointer and warns against making every Bio figure a bath
or every Astro cycle a treatment calendar. The medical-purpose model remains
ahead because an expensive sequence of plants, human/apparatus scenes and
astronomical selectors has a coherent late-medieval practical ecology that a
content-free form book does not explain.
