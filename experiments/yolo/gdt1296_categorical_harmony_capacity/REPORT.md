## Exact finite argument
Join two distinct units when they coexist anywhere in an eligible whole group.
For any proposed A, all possible B lie in Bmax: units outside A and outside every
cooccurrence neighbor of A. Enlarging B to Bmax cannot introduce a mixed group
and cannot reduce B's group coverage. Consequently the maximum of the smaller
coverage over these reduced candidates is the true maximum over every permissible
A/B/neutral assignment. The search does NOT explicitly list every smaller B.

The recursive scan safely prunes a partial A when fewer than two B units remain:
adding units to A can only shrink Bmax. Exactly10/7/13eligibleA subsets remain in
ZL/IT/RF. No rare or awkward group is discarded. Tie-breaking applies only to the
reduced candidates; its scope was clarified before the executable/source lock and
before native enumeration. The globally exact quantity is the coverage optimum,
not a lexicographic optimum over all smaller B variants.

The exact threshold is checked by20*count>=N. Group occurrences, not glyph counts,
form the denominator: repeated letters in a word contribute only one touch, while
repeated words remain multiple observations. A/B touch sets are disjoint, so the
smaller share cannot exceed1/2. Neutral-only groups remain in N.
