# GDT717 method — V90 quality/stage core-context repair

## Question

Can five repeated quality-grid cells and two weaker result-sister wholes share
compact state/stage cores without treating `Ansatz`, `Zubereitung` or `Mazerat`
as encoded product identities?

## Inputs

- canonical V89 lexical, position, confidence, weak-queue and span tables;
- GDT647 exact quality-grid cells and exact whole glossary;
- GDT689 visible-dy inventory, sister comparison, grid controls and exact
  position revisions;
- GDT691 exact preparation-renderer rules and 479-position reader;
- GDT711 family bonuses and caps.

## Method

1. Freeze all 324 V89 active readings, 479 positions, 1,586 complete dictionary
   readings and both occurrence-local spans.
2. Select exactly `otchy`, `otchey`, `otchdy`, `okchedy`, `otshey`, `chody`
   and `sheody`, each at its single admitted V89 position.
3. For the five GDT647 grid cells, keep only hot/cold, dry/moist and the named
   start/middle/reached stage in the portable core. Bind both `F_STATE` and
   `F_STAGE` once, for `+8` total.
4. For `chody` and `sheody`, preserve the exact GDT689 sister-result reading
   but credit only `F_STATE +4`: both have visible `dy` without a formal DY
   parse, so their result/stage prose is not separately rewarded.
5. Put a replaceable local preparation head only in the exact occurrence
   realization. Do not import a left neighbour as patient and do not award
   points for deleting a noun or improving German fluency.
6. Rebuild the complete confidence dictionary and the f7r.2 one-shot renderer;
   independently replay every evidence selector, score, non-target field and
   default/evidence requirement.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass only if all declared primary selectors match one exact source row, each
score family is credited at most once per target, all seven readings remain
W1, 317 non-target lexical rows and 472 non-target contexts retain parity,
both spans remain exact, and all 1,586 complete readings retain a nonempty
default, confidence, evidence, counterevidence and `H0_NONE`.

This is an exploratory whole-reading composition. It does not establish free
Voynich morphemes, product identities, plaintext, language or historical
lexicon, and it opens no page, image or transcription.
