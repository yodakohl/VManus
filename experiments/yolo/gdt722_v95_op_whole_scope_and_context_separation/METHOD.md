# GDT722 method

## Question

Can the five remaining OP/P powder-preparation holds be given concrete portable
cores without extending GDT635's strictly token-initial `p=Pulver` value to
unlicensed internal positions, and can action wording be kept at the exact
position where it is licensed?

## Inputs

- V94 active lexical, exact-position, held-reading, complete-confidence, span,
  directive and f7r.2 artifacts from GDT721
- GDT691 exact token rules and 479-position preparation renderer
- GDT635 initial-p scope profile
- GDT652 earlier exact-position unknown controls
- GDT633 `opchey/opcheey` formal E-run family

Every selected source row is listed in
`src/V95_36_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_BINDINGS.tsv`. f84 and f84r are forbidden.

## Method

1. Locate each of the five V94 holds at its exact active position and retain its
   left and right written neighbours.
2. Compare three readings for every form: bound unsegmented renderer,
   internal-p composition, and unrelated lexeme/formula marker. GDT691's
   `LEARNED_WHOLE_OR_UNEXPORTED` origin remains unresolved.
3. Keep the concrete GDT691 powder value whenever it remains useful, but do not
   inherit GDT635's initial-p value inside a longer token.
4. Separate a local action from the portable word core whenever the exact
   position, rather than every occurrence of the surface, carries the action.
5. Treat GDT633's 26 `opchey` plus four `opcheey` occurrences as a formal
   sister family only; do not derive free OP/CH/E/Y meanings from it.
6. Preserve every non-target lexical row, context, bound span, one-shot
   directive and f7r.2 output unit, then regenerate the complete dictionary.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

Pass requires five exact target decisions, zero internal-p selections, zero
component or substring export or score credit, exact preservation of 319 non-target lexical
rows and 474 non-target contexts, and a complete 1,586-reading dictionary with
a default, confidence, evidence and counterevidence on every row.

Each selected exact whole surface retains its working default; that does not
license `op` or `p` as productive prefixes or substrings. The selected meanings are exploratory German renderer values, not recovered
plaintext. The internal-p model remains open for future evidence. No language,
phonetics, historical codebook, ingredient identity, new page, image,
transcription, f84 or f84r is asserted.
