# Sidequest Pass 638: the complete ten-page apprentice handbook

## Result

The fixed ten pages now fit one learnable workshop handbook with two explicitly
different reading modes:

1. seven Herbal/Biological prose pages use productive semantic components,
   exact cards, and multiscribe rendering;
2. three celestial pages use local picture addresses and copied whole labels.

The two modes are kept separate. No prose root is imported into Astro and no
Astro label is used as a prose word.

## Prose lesson

- identify visible owner and case;
- speak 39 short semantic words;
- use six productive tables;
- select one of 173 exact cards;
- write its surface through card/desk and body/wrapper rules;
- consult seventeen local surface entries if named;
- read the result backward.

Coverage: 381/381.

## Celestial lesson

- identify one of thirteen local namespaces;
- point to the exact picture locus;
- copy the complete local label in its visible order;
- retrieve it only within that namespace;
- optionally attach it as an external condition/address in the master's spoken
  workflow, never through a hidden prose pointer.

Coverage: 395 groups at 142 loci, represented by 301 distinct visible group
forms.

Each Astro group receives a default workshop reading of the form:

> local celestial/address label at this named picture position

That is a usable codebook value without pretending to know whether the label
means Mars, Tuesday, the head, a disease, or a star name.

## Thirteen celestial namespaces

- f67 right condition/sector wheel;
- f67 left star/aspect field wheel;
- f67 unresolved paired legend;
- f68 left, middle, and right panel headers;
- f68 twenty-eight local star positions;
- f68 multipanel headers, central legend, and centre-key quarantines;
- f69 left wheel with twenty-eight local positions;
- f69 middle cloud/wave/celestial-state wheel;
- f69 right face/light/planet-like wheel.

The last descriptions are visual workshop handles, not decoded astronomical
names.

## Deliberate non-rules

- Astro required by a prose case: 0/395 groups.
- Prose dictionary imports: 0.
- Fixed start or direction: 0.
- f68-f69 keys: 0.
- Universal 28-step schedule: none.

This protects the useful image-address function while avoiding the old mistake
of forcing the three diagrams into one linear calendar.

## Current best working architecture

The workshop book is an illustrated practical register with a productive
prose-command layer and a separate celestial exemplar/lookup appendix. The
content lead remains practical plant preparation and bath/application work;
the nonmedical plant-material/bathhouse rival remains possible. The writing
technology itself no longer depends on choosing between those two contents.

## Next move

Run an apprentice examination entirely inside the ten pages:

- one complete C3 forward-write/back-read exercise using a non-source legal
  order and a short-hold substitution;
- one f69 local-locus copy/retrieval exercise;
- one deliberate error in each, followed by the master's correction.

This tests whether the combined handbook is actually teachable without adding
another page or meaning.
