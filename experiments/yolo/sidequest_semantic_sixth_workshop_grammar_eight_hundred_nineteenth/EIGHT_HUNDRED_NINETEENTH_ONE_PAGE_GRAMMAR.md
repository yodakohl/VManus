Grade and endpoint: E KURZ; EE LAENGER; EEE VOLL; Y DIES; licensed DY SCHLUSS.

## Narrow learned material

Bound: AN=NACHGABE only in its learned frame; DA=ZWEI only before IIN;
LD=BEFESTIGEN only before DY.

Whole words: OS=DAZU; DCHOL/SCHOL=DAVON; TALAM=BEISEITESTELLEN.

## Reading order

1. Load the pictured or local owner.
2. Read registered components from left to right.
3. Resolve DIES and DAVON to the active local material.
4. Apply KURZ/LAENGER/VOLL only to the adjacent operation.
5. Treat a licensed DY construction as the end of the local step, not
   automatically the end of a physical line or sentence.
6. Copy the exact learned surface; do not invent a surface from the German
   mnemonic.
