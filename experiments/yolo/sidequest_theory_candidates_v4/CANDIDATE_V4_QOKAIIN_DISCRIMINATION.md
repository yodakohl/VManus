## Model A — HEAD with TAKE/USE-like source expansion

Frozen role: Q introduces an executable entry, expanded loosely as
`TAKE/USE/ENTER/APPLY current item`. The expansion must remain one class; it
cannot alternate freely among verbs merely to improve fluency.

Consecutive pseudo-translation of all events:

1. f55v.5: `[TAKE/USE] AIIN, X, X; set X and CLOSE. Then AIIN, X, X and CLOSE.`
2. f81v.2: `CLOSE X. [TAKE/USE] X, X, relation, X, relation, X, X, relation, X, X.`
3–4. f82r.3–4: `CLOSE X. X, X, X; write the pending [TAKE/USE] head at the
   line edge; at the next line repeat [TAKE/USE], add X, X and CLOSE; then X,
   X, X, X.`
5. f82r.26: `CLOSE X. X, X, X, AIIN, X; [TAKE/USE] X.`
6. f83r.3: `CLOSE X; X X CLOSE; Y AIIN Y X CLOSE; [TAKE/USE] X, X.`
7. f83r.6: `CLOSE X; X X X CLOSE; [TAKE/USE] X X CLOSE; CLOSE X; X.`
8. f83r.11: `X X CLOSE; [TAKE/USE] X X X CLOSE; X X CLOSE; CLOSE X; X.`
9. f83r.14: `CLOSE X four times; X X CLOSE; [TAKE/USE] CTHY, X, Y.`

This is coherent if fields are short instructions. It forces most immediate
right neighbors to be operands, specifications or subordinate actions, while
AIIN becomes an argument/parameter, CTHY a state/quality, L/O a relation, and
attached close the completion of the introduced operation. The internal
f82r.26 event must be a serial embedded operation (“then take/use X”), and the
f82r.3 LAST event must be an anticipatory copied head, not its logical source
position. Those two repairs are possible but make literal TAKE/USE less clean
than anonymous HEAD.

Boundary account: the scribe copied the next operation head before discovering
or choosing the physical break, then recopied it in executable position at the
new line. This explains the local repeat but does not predict a manuscript-wide
RESUME sign.

## Model B — ADDRESS/ACTIVATE current cell or subrecord

Frozen role: Q selects or reactivates a local formula-card address. It is an
anonymous control card, not necessarily a spoken verb. “Address” here means a
form location/topic pointer, not proven PAGE_HOST or external referent.

Consecutive pseudo-translation of all events:

1. f55v.5: `[ACTIVATE Q-slot]: AIIN, X, X, then X and CLOSE. Continue with
   AIIN, X, X and CLOSE.`
2. f81v.2: `CLOSE the first cell. [ACTIVATE Q-slot]: X, X, relation, X,
   relation, X, X, relation, X, X.`
3–4. f82r.3–4: `CLOSE cell X. Fill X, X, X, then place the pending Q-address

   the next cell X, X, X, X.`
5. f82r.26: `CLOSE the first cell. In the open specification X, X, X, AIIN,
   X, switch/reactivate [Q-subaddress], then X.`
6. f83r.3: `Commit three cells, including Y–AIIN–Y; [ACTIVATE Q-slot] and
   enter X, X.`
7. f83r.6: `Commit two cells; [ACTIVATE Q-slot], enter X, X and CLOSE; commit
   X; leave X open.`
8. f83r.11: `Commit X X; [ACTIVATE Q-slot], enter X, X, X and CLOSE; commit
   two further cells; leave X open.`
9. f83r.14: `Commit five compact cells; [ACTIVATE Q-slot], enter CTHY, X, Y.`

This model requires neighboring cards to carry almost all substantive payload.
AIIN can remain a broad parameter/index, CTHY a state/qualifier, Y an item or
reference slot, L/O a relation, and exact terminal cards payload-bearing
commits. It does **not** require any successor to be an object of a verb. The
varied right context is expected because the address licenses a slot whose
payload varies. The one medial occurrence is a nested subaddress/reactivation,
not a new material or imperative.

Boundary account: f82r.3 Q is a catchword-like anticipatory address; f82r.4 Q
is the executable copy at field entry. The exact duplication is meaningful at
both graphic positions but corresponds to one logical activation. This is a
local reflow repair, not a universal translation of Q as CONTINUE.

6. f83r.3: `Commit three cells; [MEDIUM] with X, X.`
7. f83r.6: `Commit two cells; [MEDIUM] with X, X; CLOSE; commit X; X.`

9. f83r.14: `Commit five cells; [MEDIUM] with CTHY, X, Y.`

This reading makes adjacent cards qualities, ingredients, operations or
amounts of the medium. In particular it pressures AIIN toward quantity at
f55v.5 and CTHY toward a medium-state at f83r.14, resurrecting narrower glosses
that the current theory has deliberately weakened. The f82r.26 internal event
is natural as “add medium,” but the six post-close field starts then require
the material to be restated at five selected new cells. Nothing in the
allowed evidence independently owns a single liquid referent shared by f55v,
f81v, f82r and f83r.

Boundary account: the two copies either state continuous material on both
sides of a line break or are dittography. Continuity is plausible, but it does
not explain why this is the only exact boundary-card repeat in the current
fixed evidence. Calling it a catchword weakens the lexical-material model by
borrowing the address model's mechanism.

## Model D — ordinary content/card repetition

Frozen role: Q is a frequent ordinary payload card (a source noun, phrase or
formula fragment) with no fixed field-control function. No English expansion
is chosen.

Consecutive pseudo-translation of all events:

1. f55v.5: `Q-content, AIIN, X, X, X; CLOSE. AIIN, X, X; CLOSE.`
2. f81v.2: `CLOSE X. Q-content, X, X, relation, X, relation, X, X, relation,
   X, X.`
3–4. f82r.3–4: `CLOSE X. X, X, X, Q-content / Q-content, X, X; CLOSE. Then
   X, X, X, X.`
5. f82r.26: `CLOSE X. X, X, X, AIIN, X, Q-content, X.`
6. f83r.3: `Commit three cells; Q-content, X, X.`
7. f83r.6: `Commit two cells; Q-content, X, X; CLOSE; commit X; X.`
8. f83r.11: `Commit X X; Q-content, X, X, X; CLOSE; commit two cells; X.`
9. f83r.14: `Commit five cells; Q-content, CTHY, X, Y.`

This model forces almost nothing about other cards, which is its main virtue
and its main explanatory weakness. Q's preferred initial placement becomes a
distributional property of that content item, comparable to a frequent noun
or formula onset; closes retain their formal role; AIIN/Y/CTHY/L/O keep their
broad current classes. The boundary pair is ordinary repetition, scribal
dittography or a repeated phrase element. Because the two copies straddle a
field/line boundary and no other exact boundary repeat is known here, “ordinary
repetition” names the event but does not explain its exceptional placement.

## Selection

**Leading theory: Q is an ADDRESS/ACTIVATE card for a B-register cell or local
subrecord.** The safest structural label is `Q-ADDRESS_HEAD`; the best source
expansion class is ACTIVATE/ENTER CURRENT SLOT. TAKE/USE remains a plausible
downstream wording of some source instances, but it is too verb-specific to be
the primary identity. WORKING_MEDIUM loses decisively because it has no owned
shared referent and converts the strongest fact—five ordinary post-close
starts—into unexplained material restatement. Ordinary content is the serious
null: it preserves ignorance well but leaves both the closure-conditioned
entry ecology and the unique boundary duplication largely accidental.

The f82r.3–4 pair is counted as two physical card events but one logical Q-head:

```text
... payload Q[anticipatory edge copy]
Q[executable field head] payload CLOSE ...
```

This choice neither translates Q as RESUME nor generalizes the mechanism
beyond that single transition.

## Required implications and falsifiers

Under the selected model:

- the cards after Q must be variable payload/specification, not one stable
  semantic object class;
- AIIN can remain PARAMETER/INDEX/REFERENCE and need not become AMOUNT;
- CTHY can remain a state/qualifier and need not become a liquid quality;
- Y and L/O retain item/reference and relation-like structural classes;
- attached closers commit the local cell but do not define Q's lexical sense;
- the B register must permit nested reactivation, accounting for f82r.26.

The next discriminating test should count the f82r carry both twice (physical
events) and once (logical head), then compare Q against frequency-, page- and
register-matched exact cards on two frozen endpoints: probability of FIRST
immediately after a committed predecessor, and right-neighbor diversity. The
ADDRESS model loses if matched common content cards reproduce both properties,
or if Q's successors form a materially homogeneous ingredient/medium frame.
The MEDIUM model gains only if an independently owned visual or readable
endpoint identifies one shared material across the Q-bearing Herbal-B and
Biological-B contexts. Internal fluency alone cannot supply that referent.
