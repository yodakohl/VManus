# Sidequest V4 ambiguity-resolution protocol

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: frozen before reading V4 candidate outcomes. Speculative YOLO work,
not a scientific test or translation.

## Scope

Only the ten fixed pages may be used. f84 and f84r remain sealed. Exact-card
identity, field/record structure and permitted pictures are the evidence base;
alternate transcriptions are not independent witnesses.

## Rubric

| criterion | points |
|---|---:|
| all-occurrence coverage of proposed cards | 25 |
| record-level continuity and inherited-state economy | 20 |
| fewest anonymous role changes | 15 |
| explanatory discrimination among competing lexicons | 15 |
| historically plausible source expansion | 10 |
| fluent but provenance-controlled translation | 10 |
| risky fixed-page predictions | 5 |

Maximum: 100. Scores are abductive judgments.

## Required decisions

The final selection must choose among, or explicitly replace:

- `qokaiin`: entry head, address activation, working medium, or unresolved
  content;
- L/O: relation, portion/slot, medium relation, or unresolved;
- AIIN: parameter/value/index, process/contact, or unresolved;
- Y: item/tag, pointer/anaphor, or unresolved;
- CTHY: state/property, process/treatment, or unresolved;
- terminal cards: payload plus COMMIT, typed semantic values, or punctuation.

Every winning role must account for awkward positions as well as attractive
ones. The selection must include a record-length pseudo-translation and the
strongest losing lexicon.
