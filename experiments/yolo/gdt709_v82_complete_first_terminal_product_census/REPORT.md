## Result

The exhaustive scan changes the shape of the problem. “Terminal product” is
not one binary category. The same 42 windows form a stable narrowing funnel:

| reader | windows | purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| compound-aware completion+material marker | 25 | high-throughput candidate generation |
| operation-aware workshop reading | 22 | distinguish products from completed states/quantities |
| complete manual endpoint reading | 9 | retain locally readable exploratory endpoints |
| admitted relation edge | 2 | inherited C019 and C021 only |

The broad marker finds 30 terminal-material fields in total. Five are later
fields in a window that already contains an earlier terminal value; the
no-skip rule blocks them. Two additional completion fields are hard controls:
A038 is state-only (“vollständig erhitzt, Stufe III”) and A070 quantity-only
(“drei fertig abgeteilte Teile”).

The manual partition of all 42 windows is exact: nine locally readable endpoints,
seven earlier completions that block longer paths, twelve reset/break cases,
thirteen with no coherent product path, and C020/A083 as one coherent but
non-finished result-state exception.

## Best new working reading

> **A048 / f77r.38#3-5:** Endportion hinzugeben. Danach steht: heiß, Grad III.
> Mögliches terminales Produkt: abgeschlossenes, bis zur Mittelstufe
> getrocknetes Arzneikompositum.

This is not generic renderer filler. It names an input portion, a thermal and
degree attribute, and a material-bearing terminal product. It is the strongest
new exploratory hold because the block is short and ends exactly at #5.

It is not yet an edge. `Hinzunehmen` does not itself produce drying or closure;
Grad III changes to Mittelstufe; and the action surface `qoeedy` is a singleton
in the frozen 479-token layer. The useful conclusion is therefore
`HIGH_HOLD_NO_EDGE`, not rejection and not a word default.

## Why the reader disagreement matters

The three readers disagree in controlled, interpretable places. The lexical
marker additionally accepts fully heated or soaked materials such as A004,
A015 and A053; the operation-aware reader does not call those terminal products
in context. The complete path reader then removes cases whose completion is
real but belongs to a changed material, operation or degree. This is evidence
about where the current renderer loses patient continuity, not permission to
erase the concrete readings.

The populations are nested only at window level. In 21 cases the broad and
operation-aware readers select the same first field. A047 is the one exception:
#7 `lsheetal` is the first broad completion+material field, while the
operation-aware reader names later #8 `shecphy`. #8 is still blocked by #7;
A047 remains STOP and adds no edge.

Likewise, “nine endpoints” does not mean nine blocker-free causal paths. A005,
A014, A041 and A048 have their first unresolved operation or degree mismatch
in the selected terminal field itself. They remain visible because the local
product phrase is readable; the mismatch is precisely why they stay holds or
STOPs.
