# GDT709 method

## Question

When every semantic item in all 42 delayed nominal windows is inspected
without skipping, how many first terminal products survive three different
levels of reading?

## Frozen inputs

The 42 first items come from the V79 action dispositions; the 161 later items
come from the V79 delayed-pair universe. Together they reconstruct 203
semantic items in uninterrupted rank order. The V81 graph, 479-token reader,
51-line reader and three bound spans are inherited by hash. No new page is
opened and f84/f84r remain forbidden.

## Three readers

R1 is deliberately broad and executable. A field is a terminal-material
candidate when its frozen German gloss contains at least one completion stem
(`fertig`, `vollständig`, `abgeschlossen`) and at least one compound-aware
material head (`Ansatz`, `Arzneikompositum`, `Auszug`, `Charge`, `Droge`,
`Gut`, `Holz`, `Masse`, `Mazerat`, `Portion`, `Pulver`, `Zubereitung`). Only
the first hit in a window is selectable; later hits are visibly blocked.

R2 reads workshop flow and distinguishes a terminal material or stage from a
merely complete state or quantity. It is more permissive about historically
plausible process ellipsis than the final patient test.

R3 selects the shortest locally readable product endpoint. An earlier
completion, new material, new operation, degree reset or thermal reversal
blocks every later attraction. A mismatch may occur in the selected terminal
field itself; that makes the endpoint a hold or STOP, not an edge. In A005,
A014, A041 and A048 this is exactly what happens. The nine selections are
therefore exploratory endpoints, not nine blocker-free causal paths.

The 25 and 22 populations are nested only by window identity. Twenty-one
operation-aware terminal values are also the broad reader's first field. A047
is the sole exception: its operation-aware terminal is later than its first
lexical completion and cannot rescue the stopped path.

## Decision rule

The three populations are reported separately instead of choosing one
definition after seeing the result. A new edge would require the terminal
field to restore the action's patient/material and degree and to express an
operation productively licensed by the action. This pass is a census: it
replays C019 and C021 but adds no edge. Strict STOP labels apply only to the
long terminal route and do not cancel shorter holds or C020.

## Claim ceiling

The practical German readings are working renderings of the current atomic
codebook. They are not recovered plaintext or confirmed Voynich lexemes. No
surface default, patient identity outside its occurrence, historical language
or general recipe grammar is asserted.
