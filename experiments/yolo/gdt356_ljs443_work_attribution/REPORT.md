# GDT356 report: LJS 443 work attribution

## Result

**`WORK_ATTRIBUTION_NARROWED_FOLIO_KEY_STILL_ABSENT`**

The three narrow eight-band pages are part of the catalogued Hovhannēs
Sarkawag *Commentary on the Calendar* (fols. 145v–212r). They are not part of
the Anania Shirakatsʻi astronomy work that begins at fol. 213r, nor of Hakob
Ghrimetsʻi's earlier commentary at fols. 3r–54v.

That correction materially narrows the source search. Hovhannēs' collection
does contain calendrical exemplars, a `Kharnakhoran`, lunar computation, a
lunar indicator, and 532-year tables. But none of the audited scholarship maps
those components onto LJS 443 fols. 209r–210r or specifies the diagrams' eight
values, start, or direction.

## Consequence

The next acquisition should target an edition or specialist catalogue that
provides a folio concordance for Hovhannēs' commentary, especially the end of
the practical collection. Searching general Anania astronomy or treating the
pages as generic “eight lunar phases” is now lower value.

No Voynich target alignment is authorized. GDT355's topology remains a
period-appropriate system-family homologue only.

## Counterevidence retained

- The broader LJS 443 series includes a twelve-curved-lobe page.
- The scholarly lunar discussion is primarily 19-year-cycle and lunar
  birth/fullness computation, not a documented eight-phase slot list.
- The `Kharnakhoran` is described as a set of large comparative calendar
  tables, not as these three keyed spiral pages.
- There is still one manuscript series and no independent external folio with
  fixed corresponding values.

## Claim ceiling

GDT356 establishes only the containing catalogued work and the absence of a
usable folio key in the audited sources. It does not establish a lunar-table
identity, slot values, Armenian influence on the Voynich manuscript, copying,
language, plaintext, or translation.

No Voynich image, transcription, formal payload, or f84 material was accessed.
