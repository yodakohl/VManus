# GDT680 three-reader synthesis

Three independent readers worked from the same V53 frontier without editing
the experiment. Their stable five-line backgrounds were deliberately unlike:

1. The workshop master treated the forms as terse executable materia-medica
   notation and prioritized visible component order.
2. The travelling practitioner required each target passage to name concrete
   materials, quantities, states and operations that could actually be used.
3. The circa-1420 scribe/glossator prioritized abbreviation, minim and word-
   boundary evidence, then checked the register against manuscript catalogues.

The workshop pass concentrated on f105v.14, f23r.6 and f80r.17. It found 33
new-card positions plus the inherited `pchedaiin` source use without a forced
context conflict. Its strongest proposals were `tshol` as cold-soaked drug
material and `chetain` as the ordered operation dry-to-middle then cool-to-II.
It also quarantined `da` as an exact-short-card measure command and retained
the real `tail/kail` cold/hot reader rival.

The practical pass concentrated on f10r.2, f114v.36 and f86v6.25. It tested 34
new-card occurrences and supplied four corrections to the preliminary root
synthesis. All four were adopted:

- `ofchedy` retains published `fchedy`, the middle-dried and closed flower
  fraction, under an O preparation wrapper. It is not powder merely because it
  sits near powder cards.
- RF1b `ar aram` supports one measure of fraction I, not an invented double
  measure.
- `qopaiin` preserves the wider QOP take/measure family: take three parts of
  powder preparation. A local addition remains prose scope, not token value.
- `oidal` preserves known `odal`, measured raw material I in preparation. The
  internal I is a local base-form field; the AIR fraction-II ladder is absent.

The historical-boundary pass audited all 31 source surfaces, including four
already assigned `pchedaiin` occurrences: 92 positions, 67 loci and 52 admitted
pages. Removing that inherited family gives GDT680's actual new-card circuit of
88 positions, 64 loci and 50 pages. It found exactly two bilateral repairs:
f47r.1 `chair→pchair`, which specializes the second dry fraction as powder, and
f78v.20 `op chedy→opchedy` (RF1b additionally joins `lchey`), which binds the
short powder-preparation head to the following finished result. The pass also
identified the especially clean six-position `qockhol=qo+ckhol` command family
and the two immediately repeated, bilateral-exact `qoekol` commands.

## Adjudicated working choices

- `pchedaiin` is not a new card. Its exact GDT678 value, three doses of powder
  dried to the middle stage, is reused and recorded separately.
- `qockhol` renders as “add composite-drug material” because the published
  `ckhol` object survives intact under the productive QO command wrapper.
  “Prepare composite-drug material” remains the strongest practical rival.
- `qoekol qoekol` executes the same hot-middle-material operation twice. The
  repetition is not collapsed into an unparsed doubled name.
- `orom` uses the concrete learned-whole default “one handful of preparation
  as a portion”, but unit I remains its explicit rival and no free `m` value is
  exported.
- `solky` defaults to lightly heated seed preparation from the productive seed
  family; a learned salt-preparation reading remains equally visible.
- `da` keeps only the part-measure core across eleven positions. The worst
  locus, f29r.5 (`da/do/e`), is rendered nominally as one part rather than used
  to manufacture a letter value.

The combined choice therefore mixes productive forms, reader-controlled local
repairs and learned wholes. It closes the eight lines with concrete prose, but
does not claim confirmed plaintext, phonetics, language, a codebook identity,
an exact plant, disease, patient, cure or carrier liquid.
