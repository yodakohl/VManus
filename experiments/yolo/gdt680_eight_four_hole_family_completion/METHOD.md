# GDT680 method

## Question

Can the 30 genuinely new residual surfaces in V53's eight four-hole lines
receive concrete component-based or learned-whole meanings which preserve one
practical core at all 88 exact admitted occurrences, while the already known
`pchedaiin` card is reused unchanged, all eight lines close and no generic
filler or hidden reader repair is introduced?

## Inputs

- GDT671's f84-free 4,128-line V48 panel and literal token coverage;
- the published GDT673, GDT674, GDT675, GDT677, GDT678 and GDT679 position
  overlays;
- GDT679's complete 51-line V53 reader and frozen result counts;
- thirty new manual card specifications, one inherited-card reuse, eight
  source readings, eighteen reader decisions, seven historical architecture
  records and the three-reader synthesis;
- the mixed transcription table, accessed only through `vmanus-exp query-tsv`
  with 64 explicit locus allow-values and `--forbid-prefix f84`.

## Method

1. Rebuild the exact current global overlay. The six published occurrence
   passes contribute 415 disjoint V48 positions, leaving 7,765 globally open
   positions before GDT680.
2. Distinguish reader holes from globally unresolved positions. V53 still
   prints `pchedaiin` as a hole because earlier external occurrence audits did
   not silently rewrite the 51-line deck; globally, all four positions already
   carry the exact GDT678 card. Record that reuse instead of inventing a value.
3. Locate whitespace-delimited occurrences of the thirty new surfaces in the
   admitted panel. This yields 88 positions on 64 lines and 50 pages.
4. Materialize only those 64 loci from the mixed reader source. The guarded
   command selects 64 rows, rejects 98 `f84*` rows before materialization and
   skips 5,224 other rows.
5. Align ZL3b to IT2a and RF1b with exact tokens preferred over low-cost joins
   and splits. Every non-both-exact target has an explicit decision. The two
   bilateral repairs are `chair→pchair` and `op chedy→opchedy`.
6. Apply one card core to every exact new ZL3b occurrence. Reader-conditioned
   renderings may specialize the same material or operation but may not replace
   it with an unrelated gloss.
7. Close exactly four reader positions in each source line, including the one
   inherited card, and record all action ordinals. Recalculate the complete
   51-line deck and the global overlay independently; do not assume that only
   source lines close.
8. Generate eight forward family predictions and a sourced historical atlas.
   Historical parallels constrain only the mixed recipe/degree/measure
   architecture; they do not identify a Voynich sign or language.
9. Independently rebuild eleven result files in a temporary directory and
   compare every byte. The validator checks all counts, reader variants, reuse,
   token alignment, prose specificity, hashes, privacy markers and both seals.

## Decision rule and claim ceiling

The pass requires thirty unique new cards, all 88 exact positions represented,
one unchanged GDT678 reuse, no unrecorded non-both-reader position, all eight
source lines closed, zero hard generic renderer terms, the predicted V54 and
global counts, and a byte-exact rebuild.

Cards remain exploratory defaults rather than confirmed plaintext. A card may
stand despite a named rival until a context makes it impossible or a better
reading explains the same occurrence circuit. GDT680 does not identify the
language, phonetics, historical codebook, exact plant, disease, patient, cure
or carrier liquid.
