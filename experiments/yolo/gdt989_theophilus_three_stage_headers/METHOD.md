# Three named preparation families under one stage marker

Freeze SOURCE.json before the first bundle/header census. We test two compact
header hypotheses for the complete Theophilus I.1–9 content sequence. Paragraphs
1,2,6 have headers M, P, V. Paragraphs3,4,5 have respectively O, R, L plus FIRST;
paragraphs7,8,9 have O, R, L plus SECOND. The six names identify source recipes,
not presumed Latin letter values or known Voynich words. STAGE_PREFIX renders
stage+name; STAGE_SUFFIX renders name+stage. Both stage strings and all name
strings are nonempty. Names are pairwise distinct, stages distinct; cross-type
coincidences/prefix overlaps are allowed. Nine complete header strings must be
distinct, so different meanings cannot have the same complete header here.
No per-name stage allomorph, silent stage, title particle, word resegmentation,
word omission, reordered chapter, or heading position shift.

Every complete paragraph body is retained by frozen input ID. SOURCE.json fixes
all ingredients, qualifications, body parts, branches and references as future
obligations. This test checks only necessary header equations. It cannot claim
a body translation, formal full source-code fit, recipe identification or meaning
from a header pass. No old model or key is altered.

## Scope and header-source rule

Use all GDT928 complete paragraphs (659ZL3b,690IT2a,RF0), on the179admitted text
selectors. On each exact selector and in source-row order, enumerate every
sliding bundle of nine consecutive complete paragraphs, without crossing a
selector boundary. A source-locus gap between successive paragraphs is an
UNKNOWN_GAP case, retained in the denominator. Do not silently skip the gap.
Within-paragraph completeness/boundaries are exactly the frozen928construction.
This tests a nine-chapter bundle on one selector; it does not test distribution
across multiple pages or an unmarked chapter sequence.

Read first-group provenance from the six guarded915JSONs. The header is the
first cached group of the paragraph's first source line. Require source group
index1, literal lowercase[a-z]+ and the exact first928source ID. If the source
line has a second group, require its index2 and matching DEFINITE_SPACE on the
first group's right and second group's left boundary. A sole source group is
eligible only when metadata source_group_count=1. Other body words/lines may
be uncertain; they are retained but do not determine this header-only eligibility.
Never turn a dubious split or other reader's head into the current reading.
Record every header's group ID, raw spelling, boundaries and eligibility reason.
No raw mixed TSV or new page/image is accessed. f84/f84r, f116v and reserves closed.

All nine heads must be eligible for a complete equation verdict. Otherwise the
bundle is UNKNOWN_HEADER and no full code is claimed. This conservative source
policy is fixed before fitting; it may leave a bundle unknown even if a partial
known subset could separately refute it. No such partial refutation is counted.
Gap priority is before header eligibility; complete readable bundles receive
an exhaustive equation result, never a sampled/restart decision.
