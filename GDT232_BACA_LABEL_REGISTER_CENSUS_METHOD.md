# GDT232 — BACA family-prefix register census

GDT232 follows the exposed GDT231 `BACA-` lead through every non-f84 strict
family-consensus locus.  It asks whether the prefix behaves more like a local
waterfall/content marker or a graphical-label-register construction.

The prefix and question are postselected.  The script first rejects every raw
row whose page begins `f84`, then parses the remaining first consensus group
per locus.  It reports every `BACA-` occurrence by section and editorial kind.
No semantic, phonetic, or display-string match is scored.

A prefix may be called `LABEL_REGISTER_ENRICHED` if it is concentrated in
editorial label loci across more than one section.  This does not make the
prefix an authorial word for “label,” a grammatical marker, or a meaning.
