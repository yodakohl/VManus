## What the result means

The stable patterns are real descriptive facts.  For example, `cthy` occurs as
`chcthy`, `checthy`, and `shcthy` and lands in an instruction-clause-like
field 23/24 times on eight folios.  `che` occurs under five wrappers and lands
in a short-argument-like field 13/14 times on six folios.  These are precisely
the kinds of units a content-bearing address system might create.

But those fields are mechanically distinguishable before the PAGE_HOST is
seen.  In every one of the nine top stable cases, the placement baseline meets
or beats exact-host lookup.  The atlas therefore supplies **prioritized opaque
handles**, not lexical evidence.

This also explains why the earlier CKHY visual gloss was tempting: `ckhy` is
strongly and recurrently clause-positioned.  That fact survives; the proposed
parallel/fused plant-configuration meaning does not.
