# GDT075 — nested section-held predicate discovery

Status: **YOLO nested discovery/transfer audit**

Use only `REL_ENCLOSURE` in sections A and Z.  In each direction, completely
hold one section's relation labels out.  Build all host behavior rates from
source events outside the held section and admit only hosts occurring on at
least two outside-section physical folios.  Generate the unchanged GDT069
state-blind library of `.10/.25/.50/.75` thresholds over wrapper, frame,
right-family, DY/B3, position, and neighbouring-state rates.

A candidate must have at least five training feature loci, three held feature
loci, at least five training nonfeature loci, three held nonfeature loci, two
feature folios in both sections, and at least one informative
physical-folio×human-unit stratum in both sections.  Rank candidates by
training conditional z, then effect, then canonical predicate name.  Freeze
the top candidate and score it on the unseen section.  Also report the
pre-existing `RATE:R=aiin>=0.25` candidate's training and held ranks/effects;
it is not substituted for a failing top candidate.

This tests whether a behavior predicate is rediscovered from one section and
transfers to another.  It is still an archived-label experiment, not a fresh
visual validation.  No semantic class or meaning is assigned.  f84r is
excluded.
