# GDT063 — PAGE_HOST local-annotation candidate atlas

Status: **YOLO postselected hypothesis generation**

GDT059 did not localize aggregate external information specifically to
PAGE_HOST.  That does not rule out a small number of useful hosts.  GDT063
therefore searches aggressively for exact PAGE_HOSTs and reusable host
fragments associated with the eight archived exact-local object/relation axes.
The archived annotations are outcomes for generating leads only.

The candidate library is mechanically generated without annotation values:
exact hosts, one-to-three-character prefixes and suffixes, within-host
bigrams/trigrams, host-length buckets, group count, and exact multi-group host
expressions.  Require at least five annotated loci on three physical folios.

For each candidate/outcome pair report pooled risk difference and a conditional
score that fixes physical folio × human annotation unit.  The conditional null
preserves each stratum's candidate and annotation margins; its expectation and
hypergeometric variance give an exploratory z score.  Report leave-one-folio
sign stability and repeat on the unhedged subset.  Bonferroni values cover the
complete eligible candidate×axis atlas, but they rank rather than stop YOLO
discovery.

`INTERESTING_EXPLORATORY` requires at least three informative strata, local
p<.01, and a stable leave-folio direction.  `LIKELY_PAGE_CONFOUND` marks
features without sufficient within-page/unit mobility.  No feature is assigned
a semantic role or gloss.  f84r remains sealed.
