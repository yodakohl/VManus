# GDT183 — f57 start-construction audit

## Question

The sole label outside the f57 circular diagram is human-localized at the
common ring-start position and is read `dairal` (ZL3b) / `dairol` (IT2a,
RF1b). Is this evidence for a translated title/start word, or for a reusable
formal entry construction?

## Frozen scope

This audit uses the already published f57 inventory and the f84r-free GDT016
HPR2 prose inventory. It does not inspect an image, query a new target, or use
f84r. The externally nominated source-native family is BAFAB. The comparison
population is all HPR2-covered `d-`-wrapped prose groups; the look-elsewhere
sensitivity is all family types with the same four-occurrence support.

Positions are mechanical: group 1 of a multi-group line is FIRST, the final
group is LAST, intervening groups are CORE, and a one-group line is SINGLE.
The exact raw statistic is the without-replacement probability that four
groups drawn from the `d-` wrapper population are all FIRST. It is descriptive:
the family was nominated after f57's start position was already known and the
HPR2-covered rows are a parseable subset.

## Falsification rule

`dairal/dairol = start/title` fails if the whole form is not stable across
readings or if its prose family realizes heterogeneous HPR2 states. A weaker
`START_MARKER_LIKE entry construction` remains permissible if the family is
entry-enriched, but it must be parsed as compiler structure plus opaque host,
not promoted to a lexical translation.

## Claim ceiling

At most this can identify the structural use of one exposed diagram label and
an entry-enriched formal family. It cannot establish a word meaning, morpheme,
sound, language, title, plaintext, or translation.
