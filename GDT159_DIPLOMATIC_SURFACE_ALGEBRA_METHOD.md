# GDT159 — diplomatic surface-algebra residual

Status: `METHOD_AND_EXTERNAL_SOURCES_FROZEN_BEFORE_SCORING`

## Question

GDT158 showed that authentic medieval record layout can generate the boundary
half of the residual.  GDT159 asks whether a real abbreviation-preserving
natural-language manuscript system reaches the remaining Voynich surface
regime on exactly three already-defined GDT003 coordinates:

1. mean retained operation count per held fold;
2. compatible-operation-pair density;
3. right/left log2 edge-support ratio (negative is left-dominant).

This pass does not add another opening, closing, reset, or generic-closer
control.  It does not tune an encoder or transformation inventory toward
Voynich.

## Frozen fingerprint

`run_gdt003_structural_fingerprint_comparator.evaluate_corpus` is called
unchanged.  Therefore every training-fold operation rule, support gate,
32-rule stratum cap, rectangle/compatibility criterion, and held-fold split is
identical to GDT003.  The source normalizer and 12 x 1,000 matched design are
also unchanged.  Lower-capacity sources remain visibly unranked sensitivities.

Only the three residual coordinates are interpreted.  Other values produced
by the frozen function are exported for integrity but are not used to rescue
or redefine the question.

## Comparison rule

The published f84r-free `VOYNICH_MATCHED` aggregate from GDT003 is the sole
Voynich numerical target.  Every corpus receives:

- operation-scale ratio to Voynich;
- compatible-density ratio to Voynich;
- edge-direction agreement;
- a transparent log-ratio distance on the first two coordinates plus absolute
  edge-ratio difference.

`VOYNICH_ALGEBRAIC_REGIME` is a descriptive, pre-score rubric, not a p-value:
both positive magnitudes must lie within a factor of two of Voynich and edge
support must be left-dominant.  Continuous values and Pareto relations are
primary; the factor-two label cannot turn a near miss into identification.

Nuremberg diplomatic and Augsburg accounting fingerprints from GDT158 are
reproduced as published anchors, not refitted.  The new corpora are never
selected or removed after looking at their fingerprint.

## Separate B3 stability audit

The B3 comparison uses only the published f84r-free GDT045 output and GDT158
closer-fold artifact.  For each powered Voynich register and hand, it reports
the fixed B3 class's endpoint precision, exact within-line expectation,
effect, and leave-one-folio minimum.  Hand-level expectation is reconstructed
as the sum of `1/group_count` over B3 occurrences; the one-row unknown-hand
stratum is descriptive only.

This measures **fixed-class effect stability**.  GDT158's generic closers are
training-selected predicates, so their number of selected identities and
modal-fold fraction measure **selector identity stability**.  The two are
shown side by side but are not falsely presented as the same estimator.

No claim that B3 is punctuation, syntax, or a linguistic morpheme is made.

## Decisions

- `REAL_DIPLOMATIC_SYSTEM_REACHES_VOYNICH_ALGEBRAIC_REGIME` only if at least
  one capacity-matched newly admitted corpus meets all three descriptive
  factor-two/sign conditions.
- `SURFACE_ALGEBRA_RESIDUAL_REMAINS_UNEXPLAINED` if none does.
- `INSUFFICIENT_NEW_DIPLOMATIC_CAPACITY` if no newly admitted corpus is
  capacity matched.

B3 receives a separate result:

- `B3_FIXED_CLASS_STABLE_ACROSS_POWERED_STRATA` if every powered hand and
  register has positive effect and positive leave-folio minimum where
  computable;
- otherwise `B3_EFFECT_REGISTER_OR_HAND_UNSTABLE`.

## Claim ceiling

At most this experiment can show whether authentic graphematic medieval text
occupies the same three-coordinate surface-algebra regime, and whether the
formal B3 class has more stable positional effect than generic
training-selected medieval closers.  It cannot establish a language,
linguistic morphology, abbreviation expansion, punctuation, word, sound,
meaning, plaintext, origin, or translation.

f84r remains sealed.  No f84r source row or image is an admissible input.
