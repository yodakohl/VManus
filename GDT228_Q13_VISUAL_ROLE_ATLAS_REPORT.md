## Interpretation

The weak pattern is nevertheless coherent enough to preserve as a hypothesis:
pages depicting several separate bounded regions may serialize more short
component-, parameter-, state-, or case-like items and less continuous long
material than pages organized around one region.  Those alternatives are not
distinguished.  The result does not show that a pool is a process stage, an
argument is a material, or any field belongs to a particular drawing.

This is explicitly postselected: both visual axes and their scratch effects
were inspected before the method was published.  The page annotations are
human catalogue statements normalized to visible geometry, but the join is
page-level and supplies no authorial field ownership.  The role endpoint is
still the position/length likeness from GDT176.

The lead should therefore guide acquisition rather than interpretation.  A
fresh comparable page set would need independently counted bounded regions
and source-bound prose blocks before their abstract role profile is opened.
