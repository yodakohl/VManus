# GDT043 — OD + AIN/AIIN variant attribution

## Question

GDT037 isolated `ODAIN` as a Herbal-B/Stars candidate and GDT038 found that
its wrapper/state context changes sharply by section. This pass asks whether
`ODAIN` is better treated as an indivisible residual host or as the short
member of an exact `OD + {AIN, AIIN}` construction family.

No sound, language, word, morpheme, POS, referent, or meaning is assigned.

## Frozen inventory and decomposition

The input is the f84r-free, all-reading-agreeing GDT016 physical/manual group
inventory. Existing outer wrappers `q`, `ch`, `che`, `sh`, `s`, and `t` are
retained. Existing outer `d` is restored to the residual string for this test.
The remaining string is classified, in order, as `OD+BASE`, `O+BASE`,
`D+BASE`, or bare `BASE`. Empty bases and base `y` are excluded; the latter
would alias the already-established right-edge `DY` construction.

The exact short/long test uses only `OD+AIN` and `OD+AIIN`, across every
retained outer wrapper. It reports HB, S/B, Herbal-A, and other-Currier-B
counts; exact one-sided Fisher tests; target-folio deletion; wrapper and hand
breakdowns; and every physical occurrence.

Separately, O/D compatibility is tested across all remaining bases. Within
each exact base host × outer wrapper × physical folio stratum, the O and D
margins are fixed and the exact hypergeometric null is convolved. This asks
whether the two operations themselves combine more often than expected; raw
abundance of `OD...` cannot answer that question.

## Decision logic

`ODAIN` is attributed to a short/long construction choice only if the short
member is enriched in both target/control contrasts directionally, recurs on
multiple target folios, and remains positive after deletion of every target
folio. This is exploratory attribution, not semantic identification.

f84r is not opened, retained, queried, joined, or scored.
