# GDT104 — HPR2 object-axis versus relation-axis decomposition

## Question

Does the GDT103 external-signal pattern decompose into a PAGE_HOST channel for
the four pre-existing object/content axes and DY/RIGHT channels for the four
pre-existing relation/layout axes?

This is an exploratory synthesis of an already exposed archive, not a new
external validation. It assigns no semantic role or gloss to any Voynich form.

## Frozen axis partition

The partition follows the literal GDT068/GDT103 axis schema, not score signs:

- `OBJECT_AXIS`: STAR_OR_SKY, FIGURE, PLANT, WATER_OR_APPARATUS.
- `RELATION_AXIS`: REL_PROXIMITY, REL_EXPLICIT_ATTACHMENT, REL_ENCLOSURE,
  REL_ARRAY_OR_GROUP.

Use only `ACTIVE_ONLY` GDT103 scores. For PAGE_HOST, sum gain versus nuisance.
For WRAPPER, FRAME, RIGHT, DY, and B3, sum incremental gain versus PAGE_HOST.

## Diagnostic partition null

Enumerate all 70 ways to assign four of the eight axes to channel A. The fixed
architecture statistic rewards PAGE_HOST gain on A and DY+RIGHT incremental
gain on its complement. This exact rank is a post-hoc diagnostic: axes are
correlated and the architecture was noticed after inspecting GDT103.

## Frozen next prediction

Before any genuinely new non-f84 external panel is acquired, freeze:

1. PAGE_HOST beats raw/compiler primarily on object/content axes.
2. Adding DY or RIGHT improves relation/layout axes more than object axes.
3. Adding B3 improves neither channel materially.
4. Wrapper and O/OT additions do not improve the object channel over PAGE_HOST.

Failure on a fresh panel downgrades this two-channel interpretation without
altering the established formal parser.
