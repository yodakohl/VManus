# GDT017 prefix-conditioned post-resolution audit

GDT017 asks whether GDT015/GDT016's `DY_RESOLUTION -> OT_*_LOCAL` tendency is
only the already-known `dy | qo...` source-boundary pattern in disguise.

The corpus is the same strict, all-reading-agreeing, non-f84r
`CONFIRMED_PROSE` group inventory used by GDT014--016.  Candidate hosts are
frozen as `AR/OTAR`, `AL/OTAL`, and `OL/OTOL`.  The binary outcome is whether
the immediately preceding physical source group has terminal `DY`.

Five exact conditional tests are run:

1. a pooled comparison conditioned on core, page, normalized position
   quartile, and recovered prefix class;
2. the same pooled comparison restricted to groups with no recovered prefix;
3. three no-prefix core-specific comparisons.

Each informative stratum fixes the numbers of OT forms and previous-DY
outcomes.  Their overlap has the hypergeometric distribution; stratum
distributions are convolved exactly.  Raw two-sided p values and a conservative
five-test Bonferroni value are reported.  The first two tests are nested
confound audits, not independent replications.

All observed `DY -> OT_*_LOCAL` examples are exported, including the target
prefix.  This makes the q-bearing fraction and the no-prefix counterexamples
inspectable.  No visual data or semantic labels are used.  f84r is excluded
before grouping.

Claim ceiling: a prefix-conditioned formal sequence relation only; no
morpheme, word, syntax, sound, language, plaintext, meaning, or translation.
