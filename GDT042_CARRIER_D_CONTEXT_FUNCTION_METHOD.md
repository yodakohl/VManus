# GDT042 — carrier+D local-context function test

## Question

Given the real HB/S carrier+D host stack in GDT041, does inner literal D have
a stable anonymous positional function across exact hosts and physical folios?

Only complete strict-consensus physical lines are used. Carrier-wrapped groups
with ambiguous base `y` are excluded as in GDT041. Six predeclared binary
contexts are tested: later line half, field start, preclose/close, immediately
after DY, immediately before DY, and physical line end.

Within every exact base-host × physical-folio stratum, inner-D labels are
permuted exactly among carrier-wrapped occurrences. Hypergeometric
distributions are convolved. Herbal-B, S/B, and pooled HB+S/B are scored, with
18-test Bonferroni values.

A stable local function requires a common direction in HB and S/B, a pooled
absolute rate effect of at least 0.10, and adjusted p at most 0.05. Failure
does not remove the stack; it means these coarse local positions do not name
its function.

No meaning, morpheme, POS, sound, language, plaintext, or translation is
assigned. f84r remains sealed.
