# GDT206 — f82v/f83r arched-channel referent audit

## Purpose

Test whether the public human descriptions of rainbow-shaped tubes on f82v and
f83r supply the new repeated, separately owned referent required after GDT202.
This is a direct native-visual acquisition audit, not a formal-string score.

The exact official Yale IIIF derivatives for f82v and f83r were inspected
directly at 1,800-pixel width.  No OCR, text recognition, computer vision,
embedding, segmentation, or automatic captioning was used.  The observations
are marked `AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION` and kept distinct from the existing
human catalogue descriptions.

## Frozen bridge gate

A usable repeated-referent bridge requires:

1. the same author-visible component class on both physical folios;
2. a singular inscription owner on each occurrence, established by a leader,
   enclosure, or unambiguous isolated placement; and
3. no reinterpretation based on the Voynich strings.

A broad arch/rainbow motif or proximity to several labels and figures is not
enough.  The audit does not query or compare the formal payload.

No f84 material is accessed.
