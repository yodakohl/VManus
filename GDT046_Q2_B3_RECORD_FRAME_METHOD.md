# GDT046 — Q2...B3 record-frame compatibility

## Question

Given the transferable B3 record-closing marker from GDT045, is there a
source-native opening-member class that co-selects B3 closers across complete
physical lines?

The input is the same f84r-free complete-line GDT016 inventory. First and last
STA members must agree in ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b. The line-level exact null fixes
opening-member and B3-closing margins inside physical folio × ZL editorial
paragraph-start state × line-length bucket (`1`...`9`, `10PLUS`). Editorial
paragraph state is a nuisance control, not an authorial semantic fact.

Every stable opening member with at least 20 lines is included in a ten-class
max-search atlas. HB+S/B are the exposed discovery registers; HA, other-B, and
other-A are the transfer set. Whole-folio deletion is reported for Q2.

Q2 is source-native member nomenclature. On this inventory it corresponds
exactly to the display `t-` entry class. A positive association supports only
a possible probabilistic line-frame template, not START/END words, syntax,
language, plaintext, or translation.
