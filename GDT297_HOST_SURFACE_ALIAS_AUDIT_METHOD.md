# GDT297 — exact-host renderer/surface alias audit

## Purpose

Audit the strongest alternative explanation for GDT293--296: an exact opaque
host may predict its renderer because, conditional on that host, each renderer
tuple is merely another name for one deterministic whole source form.  This is
a post-hoc mechanistic audit of an exposed atlas, not a new confirmation test.

## Fixed inventory and join

Use the complete 59-host GDT296 population.  Join every GDT278 Voynich event
to the already published f84-free GDT276 event by exact `observation_id` and
recover only its stored `raw_token`.  Do not parse new source files, inspect
host substrings, or select hosts from their spelling.

For each exact host, enumerate the relation between:

- the exact renderer tuple `wrapper|frame|inner-D|right|DY|B3`; and
- the complete raw source group.

Report whether renderer-to-raw and raw-to-renderer are functions, the number
and frequency of variants, and whether held-folio top-1 renderer prediction is
exactly equivalent to predicting the corresponding whole source form.  Preserve
the frozen GDT296 descriptive classes; do not refit thresholds.

## Interpretation

If the relation is bijective within every host, GDT296 remains a useful formal
normalizer but its exact-host predictability cannot by itself distinguish a
reusable compositional renderer from memorized whole-form alternants.  Evidence
for independent operations must then come from cross-host transfer, for which
GDT289--290 are already negative.

## Claim ceiling

This audit can identify an exact formal aliasing relation only.  It cannot
establish or reject lexicality in general, a word, morpheme, code value, sound,
language, meaning, plaintext, or translation.  No f84 row may be opened,
parsed, retained, joined, or scored.
