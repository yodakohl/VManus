# GDT008 hybrid-register theory synthesis

Status: `YOLO_ABDUCTIVE_THEORY_GENERATION`

Date: 2026-08-14

Branch: `yolo/gdt002-visual-grammar-constraints`

## Purpose

This pass deliberately constructs the strongest concrete generative theory
compatible with the accumulated branch evidence. It is not a confirmation
test and does not require every component to be independently identifiable.
Post-hoc synthesis and weak evidence are allowed, but every observation,
inference, and speculative value must remain visibly distinct.

The three compared architectures are:

1. compressed/abbreviated natural language;
2. semantic or technical notation;
3. a hybrid natural-language-derived technical register with abbreviation and
   notation-like fields.

The selected architecture must explain the manuscript hierarchy, page and
Currier effects, line reset, productive formal reuse, split/join spellings,
label compression, recurrent edge operations, repeated formulae, and the
failure of simple language/cipher mappings with one coherent generator.

## Evidence admitted

Only compact current reports and structured results are read:

- `experiments/semantic_assumptions/grammar/CONFIRMED_GRAMMAR.md`;
- `GDT001_CURRENT_SUMMARY.md` and `gdt001_current_summary.json`;
- `GDT002_MORPHOLOGY_FALSIFICATION_REPORT.md` and its result/occurrence tables;
- `GDT003_PARADIGM_PREDICTION_REPORT.md` and `gdt003_results.json`;
- `GDT003_NESTED_HELDOUT_REPORT.md` and `gdt003_nested_result.json`;
- `GDT003_STRUCTURAL_FINGERPRINT_COMPARATOR_REPORT.md` and its result;
- `Q20OB001_OPEN_BODY_REPORT.md` and `q20ob001_result.json`.

ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate readings of one manuscript. Nearest-basic
EVA strings are display forms, not phonetic transcriptions. No image, OCR,
embedding, language lexicon, plaintext, or f84r formal artifact is opened.

## Abductive comparison

Thirteen predeclared observations receive transparent importance weights.
Each architecture receives fit 0, 0.5, or 1 for each observation. The weighted
sum minus an explicit complexity penalty is a theory-selection aid, not a
likelihood, Bayes factor, p-value, or confirmation statistic. The hybrid is
selected only if it has the largest net score and supplies one explicit
generator rather than an unconstrained mixture of explanations.

## Claim classes

- `OBSERVATION`: reproduced current result or source occurrence.
- `ABDUCTIVE_INFERENCE`: the selected theory's explanation of observations.
- `PROVISIONAL_FUNCTION`: a latent functional class assigned to a recurrent
  form; this is not a translation.
- `SPECULATIVE_VALUE`: an English mnemonic for a latent contrast, included so
  the theory is concrete and falsifiable.
- `FROZEN_NOVEL_PREDICTION`: a prediction not used in architecture scoring.

## Holdout policy

f84r remains completely sealed. The builder reads no f84r formal payload. A
small prediction packet is frozen for a later surprise test, but the target is
not opened, scored, summarized, or joined in this pass.

## Claim ceiling

The result is an explicit exploratory world model. It may propose functional
classes, but it does not establish a language, sound system, morpheme, part of
speech, word meaning, plaintext, translation, author, date, or origin.
