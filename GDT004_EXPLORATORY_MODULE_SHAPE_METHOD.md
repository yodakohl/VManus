# GDT004 exploratory physical-module atlas

GDT004 is a YOLO discovery follow-up to GDT003. It asks whether the formal
cuts used in nine exact folio-held fourth-cell completions correspond to
visibly reusable edge sequences or to independently marked physical units.
It does not retest predictive significance and does not assign language,
morphology, sound, part of speech, meaning, or semantic role.

The sample is deliberately postselected: it contains the nine GDT003
`FOLIO_HELD_NOVEL_FORM` exact completions, one per physical folio. Successful,
low-ranked, and multiply factorable cases are retained. This is a physical
audit of the strongest formal examples, not an unbiased estimate of the
manuscript.

Exact Yale IIIF full-page images are inspected directly at native resolution.
Every new statement is `AI_DIRECT_VISUAL_OBSERVATION`. No OCR, image model,
segmentation model, embedding, object detector, or automated visual
classification is used. Cropping and display enlargement are mechanical only.
The registered full-image SHA256, canvas dimensions, context rectangle, and
target rectangle bind every inspection. Images are not committed.

For each target the source-aware observer records whether the complete target
is a visible manual source group and whether a distinct physical separator is
visible at each analytic cut. `NO_DISTINCT_PHYSICAL_SEPARATOR` means only that
there is no word-sized or delimiter-like mark at that cut. It does not deny a
fused orthographic module. Ordinary inter-sign spacing, touching strokes, and
ligaturing are not converted into linguistic boundaries.

ZL3b, IT2a, and RF1b are alternate readings of one object. Their agreement is
recorded once per physical group. RF markup and ambiguous readings remain in
the source audit; nearest-basic display strings are never treated as new
observations.

The f84r formal holdout is excluded before source records are retained. No
f84r image, formal row, prediction, or transcription is opened, joined, or
scored by GDT004.

## Discovery labels

- `PROVISIONAL`: repeated physical edge sequence, but no independent boundary.
- `WEAK`: too few examples or factorisation is non-unique.
- `FAILED`: the specific physical-boundary claim has no supporting target.

These labels rank exploratory hypotheses. They are not validation gates. The
GDT003 conclusion (`NOT DISTINGUISHABLE FROM STRING STATISTICS`) remains
unchanged and is an explicit counterweight to every visual lead.

## Claim ceiling

After correction, GDT004 establishes only that two postselected target strings
were securely bound to the inspected physical groups and that three proposed
cuts in those groups lack a coarse separator. It cannot establish cross-folio
physical recurrence, linguistic morphemes, independent slots, author-intended
segmentation, plaintext, language, semantic role, meaning, or translation.
